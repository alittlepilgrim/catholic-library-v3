# Blesseds Angelus Maria Prat Hostench and Lucas of St Joseph, Tristany Pujol and Companions

## Biography

*The paragraph below is our English translation of the two Vatican beatification briefs cited at the foot of this page. The facts and names are theirs.*

Ángel María Prat Hostench and his sixteen companions placed their hope in the Lord alone and, strengthened by grace, bore witness to their faith in him even in the midst of persecution. When religious persecution broke out in Spain in the years 1936–1939, they chose to die rather than deny Christ, and so gained the palm of martyrdom. Like true Christians, they went to meet the Lord forgiving their executioners. Father Ángel María was born on April 29, 1896, at Bañolas, in the diocese of Gerona. He made solemn profession on September 2, 1916, was ordained a priest on December 21, 1918, and served as rector of the Marian College of Olot and several times as prior at Olot and at Tárrega. Revolutionary militiamen seized the Tárrega community on July 28, 1936, and the next day shot and burned them at a place called Clot dels Aubens, not far from the city of Cervera. Five others of the group died elsewhere: two of the community of Tarrasa, two of the community of Olot, and a Carmelite nun of the monastery of Vic. Father Lucas of St Joseph Tristany Pujol, a priest of the Order of Discalced Carmelites, was killed at Barcelona on July 20, 1936, at the age of sixty-four; he was one of fourteen friars of his Order among sixty-four martyrs of Barcelona beatified together. Benedict XVI beatified both groups at Rome on October 28, 2007.
