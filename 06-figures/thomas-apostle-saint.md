# Saint Thomas the Apostle

## Biography

One of the Twelve Apostles, remembered chiefly for doubting the Resurrection until he could see and touch Christ's wounds, prompting Jesus's words "blessed are those who have not seen and yet believe" ([[john-20#^john-20-24|Jn 20:24-29]]). Tradition holds he carried the Gospel to India, where the Saint Thomas Christians trace their origin to his mission, and was martyred there. Feast: July 3.
