# Gabriel of St. Mary Magdalen, O.C.D

## Biography

Discalced Carmelite friar and theologian (Gabriele di Santa Maria Maddalena), author of *Divine Intimacy*, a comprehensive guide to daily spiritual reading organized by liturgical season and week.

## Notes

- See the *Divine Intimacy* notes in `pursuits-texts/03-spiritual-theology/04-divine-intimacy/`.

## Works

- *Divine Intimacy*

## Attribution Note

Often incorrectly attributed to Raoul Plus, S.J., but the correct author is Fr. Gabriel of St. Mary Magdalen, OCD.
