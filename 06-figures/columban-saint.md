# Saint Columban

## Biography

Irish missionary monk and abbot (c. 543-615), one of the great figures of Hiberno-Scottish monastic missionary activity on the Continent. Founded monasteries at Annegray, Luxeuil, and Fontaine in Gaul, and finally Bobbio in Italy, where he died. Feast: November 23.
