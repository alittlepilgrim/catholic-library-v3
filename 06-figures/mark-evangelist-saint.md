# Saint Mark the Evangelist

## Biography

Author of the Gospel of Mark, traditionally understood to record the preaching of St. Peter. A companion of both St. Paul (on his early missionary journeys) and St. Peter (who calls him "my son" in [[1-peter-05#^1-peter-05-13|1 Pt 5:13]]). Tradition holds he founded the Church of Alexandria and was martyred there. Symbolized by the lion among the four Evangelists. Feast: April 25.
