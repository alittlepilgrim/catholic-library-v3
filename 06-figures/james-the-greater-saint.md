# Saint James the Greater

## Biography

One of the Twelve Apostles, son of Zebedee and brother of St. John the Evangelist, called "the Greater" to distinguish him from James the Less. Present at the Transfiguration and the Agony in the Garden. The first apostle to be martyred, beheaded by Herod Agrippa I ([[acts-of-the-apostles-12#^acts-of-the-apostles-12-2|Acts 12:2]]). Patron of Spain; his traditional burial site at Santiago de Compostela is one of Christendom's great pilgrimage destinations. Feast: July 25.
