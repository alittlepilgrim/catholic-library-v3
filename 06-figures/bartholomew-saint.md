# Saint Bartholomew

## Biography

One of the Twelve Apostles ([[matthew-10#^matthew-10-3|Mt 10:3]]), traditionally identified with Nathanael of [[john-01#^john-01-45|Jn 1:45-51]], whom Jesus called "an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile." Tradition holds he preached in Armenia and India and was martyred by flaying, hence his common iconography. Feast: August 24.
