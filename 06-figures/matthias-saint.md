# Saint Matthias

## Biography

Disciple who had followed Jesus from His baptism by John through the Ascension, chosen by lot after the Ascension to take the place among the Twelve left vacant by the betrayal and death of Judas Iscariot ([[acts-of-the-apostles-01#^acts-of-the-apostles-01-15|Acts 1:15-26]]). Feast: May 14.
