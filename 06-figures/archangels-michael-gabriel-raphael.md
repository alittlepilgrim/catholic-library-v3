# Saints Michael, Gabriel, and Raphael, Archangels

## Biography

The three archangels named in Scripture, commemorated on a shared feast (formerly Michael's alone; Gabriel and Raphael's separate feasts were merged into it in the 1969 calendar reform). **Michael**, whose name means "Who is like God?", is the great prince and warrior who cast down Satan ([[revelation-12#^revelation-12-7|Rev 12:7-9]]). **Gabriel**, "God is my strength," announced the births of John the Baptist and Christ ([[luke-01#^luke-01-19|Lk 1:19, 26-38]]). **Raphael**, "God heals," guided and healed Tobias in the Book of Tobit. Feast: September 29.
