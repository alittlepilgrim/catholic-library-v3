# Saint Louis Martin

## Biography

Louis Martin was born in Bordeaux on 22 August 1823. A clockmaker in Alençon, he met Marie-Azélie (Zélie) Guérin, a lacemaker. Their marriage, celebrated on 13 July 1858, gave birth to nine children, including the future Saint Thérèse of the Child Jesus, but four died in infancy. An exemplary spouse and father, an entrepreneur caring for the poor and driven by a missionary spirit, he drew his strength from frequenting the Eucharist and a deep Marian devotion. After Zélie's death in 1877, Louis moved to Lisieux to ensure a better life for his five daughters, four of whom entered the Carmelite Order and one the Visitation Order. After offering all his daughters to God, this loving father experienced suffering and illness. He died near Évreux on 29 July 1894. He was canonized with his wife on 18 October 2015 by Pope Francis.
