# Saints Simon and Jude

## Biography

Two of the Twelve Apostles, commemorated on a shared feast day. Simon is called "the Zealot" or "the Cananaean" ([[luke-06#^luke-06-15|Lk 6:15]]); Jude, also called Thaddeus, is traditionally credited with the New Testament Epistle of Jude and asked Jesus a question at the Last Supper recorded in [[john-14#^john-14-22|Jn 14:22]]. Tradition holds both were martyred on missionary journeys, sometimes said to be together in Persia. Feast: October 28.
