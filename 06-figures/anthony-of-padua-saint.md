# Saint Anthony of Padua

## Biography

Portuguese-born Franciscan friar and Doctor of the Church (1195-1231), celebrated for his preaching and his knowledge of Scripture. Declared a Doctor of the Church in 1946. Popularly invoked as the patron saint of finding lost things and lost persons. Feast: June 13.
