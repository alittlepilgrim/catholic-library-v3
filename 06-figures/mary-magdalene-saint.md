# Saint Mary Magdalene

## Biography

A woman from Magdala healed by Jesus and among His most devoted followers, present at the Crucifixion and burial. The Gospel of John records her as the first witness of the risen Christ, whom she was sent to announce to the other disciples ([[john-20#^john-20-11|Jn 20:11-18]]) — earning her the traditional title "Apostle to the Apostles." In 2016 Pope Francis raised her memorial to the rank of Feast. Feast: July 22.
