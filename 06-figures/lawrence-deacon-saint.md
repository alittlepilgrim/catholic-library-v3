# Saint Lawrence

## Biography

Deacon of Rome under Pope St. Sixtus II, entrusted with care of the Church's material goods and the poor. Martyred in 258 during the persecution of Valerian, traditionally by being roasted alive on a gridiron — the instrument that became his iconographic attribute. One of the most venerated martyrs of the Roman Church. Feast: August 10. *(Distinct from [[lawrence-of-the-resurrection-brother|Brother Lawrence of the Resurrection]], the 17th-century Discalced Carmelite of the *Practice of the Presence of God*.)*
