# Saint Stephen, Protomartyr

## Biography

One of the first seven men chosen by the apostles to serve as deacons in the early Jerusalem church ([[acts-of-the-apostles-06#^acts-of-the-apostles-06-5|Acts 6:5]]). Renowned for his preaching and wisdom, he was seized, tried before the Sanhedrin, and stoned to death while praying for his executioners' forgiveness — the first Christian martyr ([[acts-of-the-apostles-07#^acts-of-the-apostles-07-54|Acts 7:54-60]]). Feast: December 26, in the Octave of Christmas.
