# Saint Zélie Martin

## Biography

Marie-Azélie (Zélie) Guérin was born in Gandelain on 23 December 1831. A lacemaker in Alençon, she met Louis Martin, a clockmaker. Their marriage, celebrated on 13 July 1858, gave birth to nine children, including the future Saint Thérèse of the Child Jesus, but four died in infancy. An exemplary spouse and mother, an entrepreneur caring for the poor and driven by a missionary spirit, she drew her strength from frequenting the Eucharist and a deep Marian devotion. After a long illness, Zélie died in Alençon on 28 August 1877, leaving five daughters, four of whom entered the Carmelite Order and one the Visitation Order. She was canonized with her husband on 18 October 2015 by Pope Francis.
