# Saints Peter and Paul

## Biography

The two great pillar-apostles of the early Church, commemorated on a shared solemnity. St. Peter, the fisherman chosen by Christ as the rock on which He would build His Church ([[matthew-16#^matthew-16-18|Mt 16:18]]) and the first Pope; St. Paul, the former persecutor converted on the road to Damascus who became the Apostle to the Gentiles. Tradition holds both were martyred in Rome under Nero — Peter crucified upside-down, Paul beheaded. Feast: June 29.
