# Isaiah, Chapter 12

### Song of Thanksgiving

1 On that day, you will say: ^isaiah-12-1

I give you thanks, O LORD;

though you have been angry with me,

your anger has abated, and you have consoled me.

2 God indeed is my salvation; ^isaiah-12-2

I am confident and unafraid.

For the LORD is my strength and my might,

and he has been my salvation.

3 With joy you will draw water ^isaiah-12-3

from the fountains of salvation,

4 And you will say on that day: ^isaiah-12-4

give thanks to the LORD, acclaim his name;

Among the nations make known his deeds,

proclaim how exalted is his name.

5 Sing praise to the LORD for he has done glorious things; ^isaiah-12-5

let this be known throughout all the earth.

6 Shout with exultation, City of Zion, ^isaiah-12-6

for great in your midst

is the Holy One of Israel!

---

\* (12:1 – 6) Israel’s thanksgiving to the Lord, expressed in language like that of the Psalms.

---

a. (12:2) [[exodus-15#^exodus-15-2|Ex 15:2]]; Ps 118:14.

b. (12:3) Jgs 5:11; Jer 2:13; Jn 4:10, 14.

c. (12:4) Ps 105:1; 148:13.

d. (12:5) [[isaiah-11#^isaiah-11-9|Is 11:9]]; [[exodus-15#^exodus-15-1|Ex 15:1]].

e. (12:6) Is 52:8 – 9; 54:1; Zep 3:14 – 15.
