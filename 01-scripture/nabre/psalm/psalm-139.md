# Psalm 139

## The All-knowing and Ever-present God

1 For the leader. A psalm of David. ^psalm-139-1

### I

LORD, you have probed me, you know me:

2 you know when I sit and stand; ^psalm-139-2

you understand my thoughts from afar.

3 You sift through my travels and my rest; ^psalm-139-3

with all my ways you are familiar.

4 Even before a word is on my tongue, ^psalm-139-4

LORD, you know it all.

5 Behind and before you encircle me ^psalm-139-5

and rest your hand upon me.

6 Such knowledge is too wonderful for me, ^psalm-139-6

far too lofty for me to reach.

7 Where can I go from your spirit? ^psalm-139-7

From your presence, where can I flee?

8 If I ascend to the heavens, you are there; ^psalm-139-8

if I lie down in Sheol, there you are.

9 If I take the wings of dawn ^psalm-139-9

and dwell beyond the sea,

10 Even there your hand guides me, ^psalm-139-10

your right hand holds me fast.

11 If I say, “Surely darkness shall hide me, ^psalm-139-11

and night shall be my light”—

12 Darkness is not dark for you, ^psalm-139-12

and night shines as the day.

Darkness and light are but one.

### II

13 You formed my inmost being; ^psalm-139-13

you knit me in my mother’s womb.

14 I praise you, because I am wonderfully made; ^psalm-139-14

wonderful are your works!

My very self you know.

15 My bones are not hidden from you, ^psalm-139-15

When I was being made in secret,

fashioned in the depths of the earth.

16 Your eyes saw me unformed; ^psalm-139-16

in your book all are written down;

my days were shaped, before one came to be.

### III

17 How precious to me are your designs, O God; ^psalm-139-17

how vast the sum of them!

18 Were I to count them, they would outnumber the sands; ^psalm-139-18

when I complete them, still you are with me.

19 When you would destroy the wicked, O God, ^psalm-139-19

the bloodthirsty depart from me!

20 Your foes who conspire a plot against you ^psalm-139-20

are exalted in vain.

### IV

21 Do I not hate, LORD, those who hate you? ^psalm-139-21

Those who rise against you, do I not loathe?

22 With fierce hatred I hate them, ^psalm-139-22

enemies I count as my own.

23 Probe me, God, know my heart; ^psalm-139-23

try me, know my thoughts.

24 See if there is a wicked path in me; ^psalm-139-24

lead me along an ancient path.

---

\* (Psalm 139) A hymnic meditation on God’s omnipresence and omniscience. The psalmist is keenly aware of God’s all-knowing gaze (Ps 139:1 – 6), of God’s presence in every part of the universe (Ps 139:7 – 12), and of God’s control over the psalmist’s very self (Ps 139:13 – 16). Summing up Ps 139:1 – 16, 17 – 18 express wonder. There is only one place hostile to God’s rule—wicked people. The psalmist prays to be removed from their company (Ps 139:19 – 24).

\* (139:2) When I sit and stand: in all my physical movement.

\* (139:9) Take the wings of dawn: go to the extremities of the east. Beyond the sea: uttermost bounds of the west; the sea is the Mediterranean.

\* (139:11) Night shall be my light: night to me is what day is to others.

\* (139:15) The depths of the earth: figurative language for the womb, stressing the hidden and mysterious operations that occur there.

\* (139:24) Lead me along an ancient path: the manner of living of our ancestors, who were faithful to God’s will, cf. Jer 6:16.
