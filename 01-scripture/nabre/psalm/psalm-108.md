# Psalm 108

## Prayer for Victory

1 A song; a psalm of David. ^psalm-108-1

### I

2 My heart is steadfast, God; ^psalm-108-2

my heart is steadfast.

Let me sing and chant praise.

3 Awake, lyre and harp! ^psalm-108-3

I will wake the dawn.

4 I will praise you among the peoples, LORD; ^psalm-108-4

I will chant your praise among the nations.

5 For your mercy is greater than the heavens; ^psalm-108-5

your faithfulness, to the skies.

### II

6 Appear on high over the heavens, God; ^psalm-108-6

your glory above all the earth.

7 Help with your right hand and answer us ^psalm-108-7

that your loved ones may escape.

8 God speaks in his holiness: ^psalm-108-8

“I will exult, I will apportion Shechem;

the valley of Succoth I will measure out.

9 Gilead is mine, mine is Manasseh; ^psalm-108-9

Ephraim is the helmet for my head,

Judah, my scepter.

10 Moab is my washbowl; ^psalm-108-10

upon Edom I cast my sandal;

I will shout in triumph over Philistia.”

11 Who will bring me to the fortified city? ^psalm-108-11

Who will lead me into Edom?

12 Was it not you who rejected us, God? ^psalm-108-12

Do you no longer march with our armies?

13 Give us aid against the foe; ^psalm-108-13

worthless is human help.

14 We will triumph with the help of God, ^psalm-108-14

who will trample down our foes.

---

\* (Psalm 108) A prayer compiled from two other Psalms: Ps 108:2 – 6 are virtually the same as Ps 57:8 – 12; Ps 108:7 – 14 are the same as Ps 60:7 – 14. An old promise of salvation (Ps 108:8 – 10) is combined with a confident assurance (Ps 108:2 – 6, 13) and petition (Ps 108:7, 12 – 13).

\* (108:8) Holiness: may also be translated as “sanctuary” or as referring to God’s heavenly abode.
