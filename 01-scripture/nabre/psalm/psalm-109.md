# Psalm 109

## Prayer of a Person Falsely Accused

1 For the leader. A psalm of David. ^psalm-109-1

### I

2 O God, whom I praise, do not be silent, ^psalm-109-2

for wicked and treacherous mouths attack me.

They speak against me with lying tongues;

3 with hateful words they surround me, ^psalm-109-3

attacking me without cause.

4 In return for my love they slander me, ^psalm-109-4

even though I prayed for them.

5 They repay me evil for good, ^psalm-109-5

hatred for my love.

### II

6 Appoint an evil one over him, ^psalm-109-6

an accuser to stand at his right hand,

7 That he may be judged and found guilty, ^psalm-109-7

that his plea may be in vain.

8 May his days be few; ^psalm-109-8

may another take his office.

9 May his children be fatherless, ^psalm-109-9

his wife, a widow.

10 May his children wander and beg, ^psalm-109-10

driven from their hovels.

11 May the usurer snare all he owns, ^psalm-109-11

strangers plunder all he earns.

12 May no one treat him with mercy ^psalm-109-12

or pity his fatherless children.

13 May his posterity be destroyed, ^psalm-109-13

their name rooted out in the next generation.

14 May his fathers’ guilt be mentioned to the LORD; ^psalm-109-14

his mother’s sin not rooted out.

15 May their guilt be always before the LORD, ^psalm-109-15

till their memory is banished from the earth,

16 For he did not remember to show mercy, ^psalm-109-16

but hounded the wretched poor

and brought death to the brokenhearted.

17 He loved cursing; may it come upon him; ^psalm-109-17

he hated blessing; may none come to him.

18 May cursing clothe him like a robe; ^psalm-109-18

may it enter his belly like water,

his bones like oil.

19 May it be near as the clothes he wears, ^psalm-109-19

as the belt always around him.

20 May this be the reward for my accusers from the LORD, ^psalm-109-20

for those speaking evil against me.

### III

21 But you, LORD, are my Lord, ^psalm-109-21

deal kindly with me for your name’s sake;

in your great mercy rescue me.

22 For I am poor and needy; ^psalm-109-22

my heart is pierced within me.

23 Like a lengthening shadow I am gone, ^psalm-109-23

I am shaken off like the locust.

24 My knees totter from fasting; ^psalm-109-24

my flesh has wasted away.

25 I have become a mockery to them; ^psalm-109-25

when they see me, they shake their heads.

26 Help me, LORD, my God; ^psalm-109-26

save me in your mercy.

27 Make them know this is your hand, ^psalm-109-27

that you, LORD, have done this.

28 Though they curse, may you bless; ^psalm-109-28

arise, shame them, that your servant may rejoice.

29 Clothe my accusers with disgrace; ^psalm-109-29

make them wear their shame like a mantle.

30 I will give fervent thanks to the LORD; ^psalm-109-30

before a crowd I will praise him.

31 For he stands at the right hand of the poor ^psalm-109-31

to save him from those who pass judgment on him.

---

\* (Psalm 109) A lament notable for the length and vehemence of its prayer against evildoers (Ps 109:6 – 20); the cry to God (Ps 109:1) and the complaint (Ps 109:22 – 25) are brief in comparison. The psalmist is apparently the victim of a slander campaign, potentially devastating in a society where reputation and honor are paramount. In the emotional perspective of the Psalm, there are only two types of people: the wicked and their poor victims. The psalmist is a poor victim (Ps 109:22, 31) and by that fact a friend of God and enemy of the wicked. The psalmist seeks vindication not on the basis of personal virtue but because of God’s promise to protect the poor.

\* (109:6) An accuser: Hebrew *satan*, a word occurring in Jb 1 – 2 and Zec 3:1 – 2. In the latter passage Satan stands at the right hand of the high priest to bring false accusations against him before God. Here the accuser is human.
