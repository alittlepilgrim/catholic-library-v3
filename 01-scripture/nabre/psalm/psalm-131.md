# Psalm 131

## Humble Trust in God

1 A song of ascents. Of David. ^psalm-131-1

LORD, my heart is not proud;

nor are my eyes haughty.

I do not busy myself with great matters,

with things too sublime for me.

2 Rather, I have stilled my soul, ^psalm-131-2

Like a weaned child to its mother,

weaned is my soul.

3 Israel, hope in the LORD, ^psalm-131-3

now and forever.

---

\* (Psalm 131) A song of trust, in which the psalmist gives up self-sufficiency (Ps 131:1), like a babe enjoying the comfort of its mother’s lap (Ps 131:2), thus providing a model for Israel’s faith (Ps 131:3).

---

a. (131:1) Ps 139:6.

b. (131:2) Is 66:12 – 13.
