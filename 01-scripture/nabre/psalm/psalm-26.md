# Psalm 26

## Prayer of Innocence

1 Of David. ^psalm-26-1

### I

Judge me, LORD!

For I have walked in my integrity.

In the LORD I trust;

I do not falter.

2 Examine me, Lord, and test me; ^psalm-26-2

search my heart and mind.

3 Your mercy is before my eyes; ^psalm-26-3

I walk guided by your faithfulness.

### II

4 I do not sit with worthless men, ^psalm-26-4

nor with hypocrites do I mingle.

5 I hate an evil assembly; ^psalm-26-5

with the wicked I do not sit.

6 I will wash my hands in innocence ^psalm-26-6

so that I may process around your altar, Lord,

7 To hear the sound of thanksgiving, ^psalm-26-7

and recount all your wondrous deeds.

8 Lord, I love the refuge of your house, ^psalm-26-8

the site of the dwelling-place of your glory.

### III

9 Do not take me away with sinners, ^psalm-26-9

nor my life with the men of blood,

10 In whose hands there is a plot, ^psalm-26-10

their right hands full of bribery.

11 But I walk in my integrity; ^psalm-26-11

redeem me, be gracious to me!

12 My foot stands on level ground; ^psalm-26-12

in assemblies I will bless the LORD.

---

\* (Psalm 26) Like a priest washing before approaching the altar (Ex 30:17 – 21), the psalmist seeks God’s protection upon entering the Temple. [[psalm-26#^psalm-26-1|Ps 26:1 – 3]], matched by [[psalm-26#^psalm-26-11|Ps 26:11 – 12]], remind God of past integrity while asking for purification; [[psalm-26#^psalm-26-4|Ps 26:4 – 5]], matched by [[psalm-26#^psalm-26-9|Ps 26:9 – 10]], pray for inclusion among the just; [[psalm-26#^psalm-26-6|Ps 26:6 – 8]], the center of the poem, express the joy in God at the heart of all ritual.

\* (26:6) I will wash my hands: the washing of hands was a liturgical act (Ex 30:19, 21; 40:31 – 32), symbolic of inner as well as outer cleanness, cf. Is 1:16.

\* (26:12) On level ground: in safety, where there is no danger of tripping and falling. In assemblies: at the Temple. Having walked around the altar, the symbol of God’s presence, the psalmist blesses God.
