# Psalm 105

## God’s Fidelity to the Promise

### I

1 Give thanks to the LORD, invoke his name; ^psalm-105-1

make known among the peoples his deeds!

2 Sing praise to him, play music; ^psalm-105-2

proclaim all his wondrous deeds!

3 Glory in his holy name; ^psalm-105-3

let hearts that seek the LORD rejoice!

4 Seek out the LORD and his might; ^psalm-105-4

constantly seek his face.

5 Recall the wondrous deeds he has done, ^psalm-105-5

his wonders and words of judgment,

6 You descendants of [[abraham|Abraham]] his servant, ^psalm-105-6

offspring of Jacob the chosen one!

### II

7 He the LORD, is our God ^psalm-105-7

whose judgments reach through all the earth.

8 He remembers forever his covenant, ^psalm-105-8

the word he commanded for a thousand generations,

9 Which he made with [[abraham|Abraham]], ^psalm-105-9

and swore to Isaac,

10 And ratified in a statute for Jacob, ^psalm-105-10

an everlasting covenant for Israel:

11 “To you I give the land of Canaan, ^psalm-105-11

your own allotted inheritance.”

### III

12 When they were few in number, ^psalm-105-12

a handful, and strangers there,

13 Wandering from nation to nation, ^psalm-105-13

from one kingdom to another people,

14 He let no one oppress them; ^psalm-105-14

for their sake he rebuked kings:

15 “Do not touch my anointed ones, ^psalm-105-15

to my prophets do no harm.”

### IV

16 Then he called down a famine on the land, ^psalm-105-16

destroyed the grain that sustained them.

17 He had sent a man ahead of them, ^psalm-105-17

Joseph, sold as a slave.

18 They shackled his feet with chains; ^psalm-105-18

collared his neck in iron,

19 Till his prediction came to pass, ^psalm-105-19

and the word of the LORD proved him true.

20 The king sent and released him; ^psalm-105-20

the ruler of peoples set him free.

21 He made him lord over his household, ^psalm-105-21

ruler over all his possessions,

22 To instruct his princes as he desired, ^psalm-105-22

to teach his elders wisdom.

### V

23 Then Israel entered Egypt; ^psalm-105-23

Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

24 God greatly increased his people, ^psalm-105-24

made them more numerous than their foes.

25 He turned their hearts to hate his people, ^psalm-105-25

to treat his servants deceitfully.

26 He sent his servant Moses, ^psalm-105-26

and Aaron whom he had chosen.

27 They worked his signs in Egypt ^psalm-105-27

and wonders in the land of Ham.

28 He sent darkness and it grew dark, ^psalm-105-28

but they rebelled against his word.

29 He turned their waters into blood ^psalm-105-29

and killed their fish.

30 Their land swarmed with frogs, ^psalm-105-30

even the chambers of their kings.

31 He spoke and there came swarms of flies, ^psalm-105-31

gnats through all their country.

32 For rain he gave them hail, ^psalm-105-32

flashes of lightning throughout their land.

33 He struck down their vines and fig trees, ^psalm-105-33

shattered the trees of their country.

34 He spoke and the locusts came, ^psalm-105-34

grasshoppers without number.

35 They devoured every plant in the land; ^psalm-105-35

they devoured the crops of their fields.

36 He struck down every firstborn in the land, ^psalm-105-36

the first fruits of all their vigor.

37 He brought his people out, ^psalm-105-37

laden with silver and gold;

no one among the tribes stumbled.

38 Egypt rejoiced when they left, ^psalm-105-38

for fear had seized them.

### VI

39 He spread a cloud out as a cover, ^psalm-105-39

and made a fire to light up the night.

40 They asked and he brought them quail; ^psalm-105-40

with bread from heaven he filled them.

41 He split the rock and water gushed forth; ^psalm-105-41

it flowed through the desert like a river.

42 For he remembered his sacred promise ^psalm-105-42

to [[abraham|Abraham]] his servant.

43 He brought his people out with joy, ^psalm-105-43

his chosen ones with shouts of triumph.

44 He gave them the lands of the nations, ^psalm-105-44

they took possession of the wealth of the peoples,

45 That they might keep his statutes ^psalm-105-45

and observe his teachings.

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 105) A hymn to God who promised the land of Canaan to the holy people, cf. Ps 78; 106; 136. Israel is invited to praise and seek the presence of God (Ps 105:1 – 6), who is faithful to the promise of land to the ancestors (Ps 105:7 – 11). In every phase of the national story—the ancestors in the land of Canaan (Ps 105:12 – 15), Joseph in Egypt (Ps 105:16 – 22), Israel in Egypt (Ps 105:23 – 38), Israel in the desert on the way to Canaan (Ps 105:39 – 45)—God remained faithful, reiterating the promise of the land to successive servants.

\* (105:14) Kings: Pharaoh and Abimelech of Gerar, cf. [[genesis-12#^genesis-12-17|Gen 12:17]]; 20:6 – 7.

\* (105:15) My anointed ones my prophets: the patriarchs [[abraham|Abraham]], Isaac, and Jacob, who were “anointed” in the sense of being consecrated and recipients of God’s revelation.

\* (105:16) The grain that sustained them: lit., every “staff of bread.”

\* (105:23, 27) The land of Ham: a synonym for Egypt, cf. [[genesis-10#^genesis-10-6|Gen 10:6]].

\* (105:27 – 38) This Psalm and Ps 78:43 – 51 have an account of the plagues differing in number or in order from [[exodus-07#^exodus-07-14|Ex 7:14 – 12:30]]. Several versions of the exodus story were current.
