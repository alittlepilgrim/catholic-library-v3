# Psalm 146

## Trust in God the Creator and Redeemer

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-146-1

2 Praise the LORD, my soul; ^psalm-146-2

I will praise the LORD all my life,

sing praise to my God while I live.

### I

3 Put no trust in princes, ^psalm-146-3

in children of Adam powerless to save.

4 Who breathing his last, returns to the earth; ^psalm-146-4

that day all his planning comes to nothing.

### II

5 Blessed the one whose help is the God of Jacob, ^psalm-146-5

whose hope is in the LORD, his God,

6 The maker of heaven and earth, ^psalm-146-6

the seas and all that is in them,

Who keeps faith forever,

7 secures justice for the oppressed, ^psalm-146-7

who gives bread to the hungry.

The LORD sets prisoners free;

8 the LORD gives sight to the blind. ^psalm-146-8

The LORD raises up those who are bowed down;

the LORD loves the righteous.

9 The LORD protects the resident alien, ^psalm-146-9

comes to the aid of the orphan and the widow,

but thwarts the way of the wicked.

10 The LORD shall reign forever, ^psalm-146-10

your God, Zion, through all generations!

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 146) A hymn of someone who has learned there is no other source of strength except the merciful God. Only God, not mortal human beings (Ps 146:3 – 4), can help vulnerable and oppressed people (Ps 146:5 – 9). The first of the five hymns that conclude the Psalter.
