# Psalm 147

## God’s Word Restores Jerusalem

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-147-1

### I

How good to sing praise to our God;

how pleasant to give fitting praise.

2 The LORD rebuilds Jerusalem, ^psalm-147-2

and gathers the dispersed of Israel,

3 Healing the brokenhearted, ^psalm-147-3

and binding up their wounds.

4 He numbers the stars, ^psalm-147-4

and gives to all of them their names.

5 Great is our Lord, vast in power, ^psalm-147-5

with wisdom beyond measure.

6 The LORD gives aid to the poor, ^psalm-147-6

but casts the wicked to the ground.

### II

7 Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; ^psalm-147-7

with the lyre make music to our God,

8 Who covers the heavens with clouds, ^psalm-147-8

provides rain for the earth,

makes grass sprout on the mountains,

9 Who gives animals their food ^psalm-147-9

and young ravens what they cry for.

10 He takes no delight in the strength of horses, ^psalm-147-10

no pleasure in the runner’s stride.

11 Rather the LORD takes pleasure in those who fear him, ^psalm-147-11

those who put their hope in his mercy.

### III

12 Glorify the LORD, Jerusalem; ^psalm-147-12

Zion, offer praise to your God,

13 For he has strengthened the bars of your gates, ^psalm-147-13

blessed your children within you.

14 He brings peace to your borders, ^psalm-147-14

and satisfies you with finest wheat.

15 He sends his command to earth; ^psalm-147-15

his word runs swiftly!

16 Thus he makes the snow like wool, ^psalm-147-16

and spreads the frost like ash;

17 He disperses hail like crumbs. ^psalm-147-17

Who can withstand his cold?

18 Yet when again he issues his command, it melts them; ^psalm-147-18

he raises his winds and the waters flow.

19 He proclaims his word to Jacob, ^psalm-147-19

his statutes and laws to Israel.

20 He has not done this for any other nation; ^psalm-147-20

of such laws they know nothing.

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 147) The hymn is divided into three sections by the calls to praise in Ps 147:1, 7, 12. The first section praises the powerful creator who restores exiled Judah (Ps 147:1 – 6); the second section, the creator who provides food to animals and human beings; the third and climactic section exhorts the holy city to recognize it has been re-created and made the place of disclosure for God’s word, a word as life-giving as water.

\* (147:8 – 9) God clothes the fields and feeds the birds, cf. [[matthew-06#^matthew-06-26|Mt 6:26, 30]].

\* (147:10 – 11) Acknowledging one’s dependence upon God rather than claiming self-sufficiency pleases God, cf. [[psalm-20#^psalm-20-8|Ps 20:8]]; 33:16 – 19.

\* (147:15 – 19) God speaks through the thunder of nature and the word of revealed law, cf. Is 55:10 – 11. The weather phenomena are well known in Jerusalem: a blizzard of snow and hail followed by a thunderstorm that melts the ice.
