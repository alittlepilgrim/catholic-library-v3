# Psalm 148

## All Creation Summoned to Praise

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-148-1

### I

Praise the LORD from the heavens;

praise him in the heights.

2 Praise him, all you his angels; ^psalm-148-2

give praise, all you his hosts.

3 Praise him, sun and moon; ^psalm-148-3

praise him, all shining stars.

4 Praise him, highest heavens, ^psalm-148-4

you waters above the heavens.

5 Let them all praise the LORD’s name; ^psalm-148-5

for he commanded and they were created,

6 Assigned them their station forever, ^psalm-148-6

set an order that will never change.

### II

7 Praise the LORD from the earth, ^psalm-148-7

you sea monsters and all the deeps of the sea;

8 Lightning and hail, snow and thick clouds, ^psalm-148-8

storm wind that fulfills his command;

9 Mountains and all hills, ^psalm-148-9

fruit trees and all cedars;

10 Animals wild and tame, ^psalm-148-10

creatures that crawl and birds that fly;

11 Kings of the earth and all peoples, ^psalm-148-11

princes and all who govern on earth;

12 Young men and women too, ^psalm-148-12

old and young alike.

13 Let them all praise the LORD’s name, ^psalm-148-13

for his name alone is exalted,

His majesty above earth and heaven.

14 He has lifted high the horn of his people; ^psalm-148-14

to the praise of all his faithful,

the Israelites, the people near to him.

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 148) A hymn inviting the beings of heaven (Ps 148:1 – 6) and of earth (Ps 148:7 – 14) to praise God. The hymn does not distinguish between inanimate and animate (and rational) nature.

\* (148:4) Highest heavens: lit., “the heavens of the heavens,” i.e., the space above the firmament, where the “upper waters” are stored, cf. Gn 1:6 – 7; Dt 10:14; 1 Kgs 8:27; Ps 104:3, 13.

\* (148:14) Has lifted high the horn of his people: the horn symbolizes strength, the concrete noun for the abstract. Of all peoples, God has chosen Israel to return praise and thanks in a special way.
