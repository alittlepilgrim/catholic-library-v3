# Psalm 107

## God the Savior of Those in Distress

1 “Give thanks to the LORD for he is good, ^psalm-107-1

his mercy endures forever!”

2 Let that be the prayer of the LORD’s redeemed, ^psalm-107-2

those redeemed from the hand of the foe,

3 Those gathered from foreign lands, ^psalm-107-3

from east and west, from north and south.

### I

4 Some had lost their way in a barren desert; ^psalm-107-4

found no path toward a city to live in.

5 They were hungry and thirsty; ^psalm-107-5

their life was ebbing away.

6 In their distress they cried to the LORD, ^psalm-107-6

who rescued them in their peril,

7 Guided them by a direct path ^psalm-107-7

so they reached a city to live in.

8 Let them thank the LORD for his mercy, ^psalm-107-8

such wondrous deeds for the children of Adam.

9 For he satisfied the thirsty, ^psalm-107-9

filled the hungry with good things.

### II

10 Some lived in darkness and gloom, ^psalm-107-10

imprisoned in misery and chains.

11 Because they rebelled against God’s word, ^psalm-107-11

and scorned the counsel of the Most High,

12 He humbled their hearts through hardship; ^psalm-107-12

they stumbled with no one to help.

13 In their distress they cried to the LORD, ^psalm-107-13

who saved them in their peril;

14 He brought them forth from darkness and the shadow of death ^psalm-107-14

and broke their chains asunder.

15 Let them thank the LORD for his mercy, ^psalm-107-15

such wondrous deeds for the children of Adam.

16 For he broke down the gates of bronze ^psalm-107-16

and snapped the bars of iron.

### III

17 Some fell sick from their wicked ways, ^psalm-107-17

afflicted because of their sins.

18 They loathed all manner of food; ^psalm-107-18

they were at the gates of death.

19 In their distress they cried to the LORD, ^psalm-107-19

who saved them in their peril,

20 Sent forth his word to heal them, ^psalm-107-20

and snatched them from the grave.

21 Let them thank the LORD for his mercy, ^psalm-107-21

such wondrous deeds for the children of Adam.

22 Let them offer a sacrifice in thanks, ^psalm-107-22

recount his works with shouts of joy.

### IV

23 Some went off to sea in ships, ^psalm-107-23

plied their trade on the deep waters.

24 They saw the works of the LORD, ^psalm-107-24

the wonders of God in the deep.

25 He commanded and roused a storm wind; ^psalm-107-25

it tossed the waves on high.

26 They rose up to the heavens, sank to the depths; ^psalm-107-26

their hearts trembled at the danger.

27 They reeled, staggered like drunkards; ^psalm-107-27

their skill was of no avail.

28 In their distress they cried to the LORD, ^psalm-107-28

who brought them out of their peril;

29 He hushed the storm to silence, ^psalm-107-29

the waves of the sea were stilled.

30 They rejoiced that the sea grew calm, ^psalm-107-30

that God brought them to the harbor they longed for.

31 Let them thank the LORD for his mercy, ^psalm-107-31

such wondrous deeds for the children of Adam.

32 Let them extol him in the assembly of the people, ^psalm-107-32

and praise him in the council of the elders.

### V

33 God changed rivers into desert, ^psalm-107-33

springs of water into thirsty ground,

34 Fruitful land into a salty waste, ^psalm-107-34

because of the wickedness of its people.

35 He changed the desert into pools of water, ^psalm-107-35

arid land into springs of water,

36 And settled the hungry there; ^psalm-107-36

they built a city to live in.

37 They sowed fields and planted vineyards, ^psalm-107-37

brought in an abundant harvest.

38 God blessed them, and they increased greatly, ^psalm-107-38

and their livestock did not decrease.

39 But he poured out contempt on princes, ^psalm-107-39

made them wander trackless wastes,

40 Where they were diminished and brought low ^psalm-107-40

through misery and cruel oppression.

41 While he released the poor man from affliction, ^psalm-107-41

and increased their families like flocks.

42 The upright saw this and rejoiced; ^psalm-107-42

all wickedness shut its mouth.

43 Whoever is wise will take note of these things, ^psalm-107-43

and ponder the merciful deeds of the LORD.

---

\* (Psalm 107) A hymn inviting those who have been rescued by God to give praise (Ps 107:1 – 3). Four archetypal divine rescues are described, each ending in thanksgiving: from the sterile desert (Ps 107:4 – 9), from imprisonment in gloom (Ps 107:10 – 16), from mortal illness (Ps 107:17 – 22), and from the angry sea (Ps 107:23 – 32). The number four connotes totality, all the possible varieties of rescue. The same saving activity of God is shown in Israel’s history (Ps 107:33 – 41); whenever the people were endangered God rescued them. The last verses invite people to ponder the persistent saving acts of God (Ps 107:42 – 43).

\* (107:33 – 41) God destroyed Sodom and Gomorrah in Gn 18 – 19, which the Psalm sees as the destruction of the wicked inhabitants of Canaan to prepare the way for Israel (Ps 107:33 – 34). God then led Israel through the desert to give them a fertile land (Ps 107:35 – 38) and protected them from every danger (Ps 107:39 – 41).
