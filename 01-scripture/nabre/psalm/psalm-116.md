# Psalm 116

## Thanksgiving to God Who Saves from Death

### I

1 I love the LORD, who listened ^psalm-116-1

to my voice in supplication,

2 Who turned an ear to me ^psalm-116-2

on the day I called.

3 I was caught by the cords of death; ^psalm-116-3

the snares of Sheol had seized me;

I felt agony and dread.

4 Then I called on the name of the LORD, ^psalm-116-4

“O LORD, save my life!”

### II

5 Gracious is the LORD and righteous; ^psalm-116-5

yes, our God is merciful.

6 The LORD protects the simple; ^psalm-116-6

I was helpless, but he saved me.

7 Return, my soul, to your rest; ^psalm-116-7

the LORD has been very good to you.

8 For my soul has been freed from death, ^psalm-116-8

my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling.

9 I shall walk before the LORD ^psalm-116-9

in the land of the living.

### III

10 I kept faith, even when I said, ^psalm-116-10

“I am greatly afflicted!”

11 I said in my alarm, ^psalm-116-11

“All men are liars!”

12 How can I repay the LORD ^psalm-116-12

for all the great good done for me?

13 I will raise the cup of salvation ^psalm-116-13

and call on the name of the LORD.

14 I will pay my vows to the LORD ^psalm-116-14

in the presence of all his people.

15 Dear in the eyes of the LORD ^psalm-116-15

is the death of his devoted.

16 LORD, I am your servant, ^psalm-116-16

your servant, the child of your maidservant;

you have loosed my bonds.

17 I will offer a sacrifice of praise ^psalm-116-17

and call on the name of the LORD.

18 I will pay my vows to the LORD ^psalm-116-18

in the presence of all his people,

19 In the courts of the house of the LORD, ^psalm-116-19

in your midst, O Jerusalem.

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 116) A thanksgiving in which the psalmist responds to divine rescue from mortal danger (Ps 116:3 – 4) and from near despair (Ps 116:10 – 11) with vows and Temple sacrifices (Ps 116:13 – 14, 17 – 19). The Greek and Latin versions divide the Psalm into two parts: Ps 116:1 – 9 and Ps 116:10 – 19, corresponding to its two major divisions.

\* (116:3) The cords of death: death is personified here; it attempts to capture the psalmist with snares and nets, cf. [[psalm-18#^psalm-18-6|Ps 18:6]].

\* (116:9) The land of the living: the phrase elsewhere is an epithet of the Jerusalem Temple (cf. [[psalm-27#^psalm-27-13|Ps 27:13]]; 52:5; Is 38:11). Hence the psalmist probably refers to being present to God in the Temple.

\* (116:10) I kept faith, even when I said: even in the days of despair, the psalmist did not lose all hope.

\* (116:13) The cup of salvation: probably the libation of wine poured out in gratitude for rescue, cf. [[exodus-25#^exodus-25-29|Ex 25:29]]; Nm 15:5, 7, 10.

\* (116:15) Dear in the eyes of the LORD: the meaning is that the death of God’s faithful is grievous to God, not that God is pleased with the death, cf. Ps 72:14. In [[wisdom-03#^wisdom-03-5|Wis 3:5 – 6]], God accepts the death of the righteous as a sacrificial burnt offering.
