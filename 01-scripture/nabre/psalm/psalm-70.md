# Psalm 70

## Prayer for Divine Help

1 For the leader; of David. For remembrance. ^psalm-70-1

2 Graciously rescue me, God! ^psalm-70-2

Come quickly to help me, LORD!

3 Let those who seek my life ^psalm-70-3

be confused and put to shame.

Let those who desire my ruin

turn back in disgrace.

4 Let those who say “Aha!” ^psalm-70-4

turn back in their shame.

5 But may all who seek you ^psalm-70-5

rejoice and be glad in you,

Those who long for your help

always say, “God be glorified!”

6 I am miserable and poor. ^psalm-70-6

God, come to me quickly!

You are my help and deliverer.

LORD, do not delay!

---

\* (Psalm 70) A lament of a poor and afflicted person (Ps 70:6) who has no resource except God, and who cries out to be saved from the enemy. The Psalm is almost identical to Ps 40:14 – 17.

---

a. (70:2 – 6) Ps 40:14 – 18.

b. (70:2) Ps 71:12.

c. (70:3) Ps 35:4, 26.

d. (70:4) Ps 35:21, 25.

e. (70:5) Ps 35:27.
