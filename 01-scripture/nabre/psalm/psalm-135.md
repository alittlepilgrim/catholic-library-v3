# Psalm 135

## Praise of God, the Ruler and Benefactor of Israel

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-135-1

### I

Praise the name of the LORD!

Praise, you servants of the LORD,

2 Who stand in the house of the LORD, ^psalm-135-2

in the courts of the house of our God!

3 Praise the LORD, for the LORD is good! ^psalm-135-3

Sing to his name, for it brings joy!

4 For the LORD has chosen Jacob for himself, ^psalm-135-4

Israel as his treasured possession.

### II

5 For I know that the LORD is great, ^psalm-135-5

that our Lord is greater than all gods.

6 Whatever the LORD desires ^psalm-135-6

he does in heaven and on earth,

in the seas and all the depths.

7 It is he who raises storm clouds from the end of the earth, ^psalm-135-7

makes lightning for the rain,

and brings forth wind from his storehouse.

### III

8 He struck down Egypt’s firstborn, ^psalm-135-8

of human being and beast alike,

9 And sent signs and wonders against you, Egypt, ^psalm-135-9

against Pharaoh and all his servants.

10 It is he who struck down many nations, ^psalm-135-10

and slew mighty kings—

11 Sihon, king of the Amorites, ^psalm-135-11

and Og, king of Bashan,

all the kings of Canaan—

12 And made their land a heritage, ^psalm-135-12

a heritage for Israel his people.

13 O LORD, your name is forever, ^psalm-135-13

your renown, from generation to generation!

14 For the LORD defends his people, ^psalm-135-14

shows mercy to his servants.

### IV

15 The idols of the nations are silver and gold, ^psalm-135-15

the work of human hands.

16 They have mouths but do not speak; ^psalm-135-16

they have eyes but do not see;

17 They have ears but do not hear; ^psalm-135-17

nor is there breath in their mouths.

18 Their makers will become like them, ^psalm-135-18

and anyone who trusts in them.

### V

19 House of Israel, bless the LORD! ^psalm-135-19

House of Aaron, bless the LORD!

20 House of Levi, bless the LORD! ^psalm-135-20

You who fear the LORD, bless the LORD!

21 Blessed be the LORD from Zion, ^psalm-135-21

who dwells in Jerusalem!

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 135) The hymn begins and ends with an invitation to praise God (Ps 135:1 – 3, 19 – 20) for the great act of choosing Israel (Ps 135:4). The story of Israel’s emergence as a people is told in Ps 135:5 – 14; God created and redeemed the people, easily conquering all opposition. God’s defeat of hostile powers means that the powers themselves and their images are useless (Ps 135:15 – 18). The last three verses appear also in Ps 115:4 – 8.

\* (135:4) Though all nations are God’s, Israel has a special status as God’s “treasured” people: [[exodus-19#^exodus-19-5|Ex 19:5]]; Dt 7:6; 14:2; 26:18; Mal 3:17.
