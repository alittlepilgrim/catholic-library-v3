# Psalm 103

## Praise of Divine Goodness

1 Of David. ^psalm-103-1

### I

Bless the LORD, my soul;

all my being, bless his holy name!

2 Bless the LORD, my soul; ^psalm-103-2

and do not forget all his gifts,

3 Who pardons all your sins, ^psalm-103-3

and heals all your ills,

4 Who redeems your life from the pit, ^psalm-103-4

and crowns you with mercy and compassion,

5 Who fills your days with good things, ^psalm-103-5

so your youth is renewed like the eagle’s.

### II

6 The LORD does righteous deeds, ^psalm-103-6

brings justice to all the oppressed.

7 He made known his ways to Moses, ^psalm-103-7

to the Israelites his deeds.

8 Merciful and gracious is the LORD, ^psalm-103-8

slow to anger, abounding in mercy.

9 He will not always accuse, ^psalm-103-9

and nurses no lasting anger;

10 He has not dealt with us as our sins merit, ^psalm-103-10

nor requited us as our wrongs deserve.

### III

11 For as the heavens tower over the earth, ^psalm-103-11

so his mercy towers over those who fear him.

12 As far as the east is from the west, ^psalm-103-12

so far has he removed our sins from us.

13 As a father has compassion on his children, ^psalm-103-13

so the LORD has compassion on those who fear him.

14 For he knows how we are formed, ^psalm-103-14

remembers that we are dust.

15 As for man, his days are like the grass; ^psalm-103-15

he blossoms like a flower in the field.

16 A wind sweeps over it and it is gone; ^psalm-103-16

its place knows it no more.

17 But the LORD’s mercy is from age to age, ^psalm-103-17

toward those who fear him.

His salvation is for the children’s children

18 of those who keep his covenant, ^psalm-103-18

and remember to carry out his precepts.

### IV

19 The LORD has set his throne in heaven; ^psalm-103-19

his dominion extends over all.

20 Bless the LORD, all you his angels, ^psalm-103-20

mighty in strength, acting at his behest,

obedient to his command.

21 Bless the LORD, all you his hosts, ^psalm-103-21

his ministers who carry out his will.

22 Bless the LORD, all his creatures, ^psalm-103-22

everywhere in his domain.

Bless the LORD, my soul!

---

\* (Psalm 103) The speaker in this hymn begins by praising God for personal benefits (Ps 103:1 – 5), then moves on to God’s mercy toward all the people (Ps 103:6 – 18). Even sin cannot destroy that mercy (Ps 103:11 – 13), for the eternal God is well aware of the people’s human fragility (Ps 103:14 – 18). The psalmist invites the heavenly beings to join in praise (Ps 103:19 – 22).

\* (103:5) Your youth is renewed like the eagle’s: because of the eagle’s long life it was a symbol of perennial youth and vigor, cf. Is 40:31.
