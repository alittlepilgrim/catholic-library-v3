# Psalm 111

## Praise of God for Goodness to Israel

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-111-1

I will praise the LORD with all my heart

in the assembled congregation of the upright.

2 Great are the works of the LORD, ^psalm-111-2

studied by all who delight in them.

3 Majestic and glorious is his work, ^psalm-111-3

his righteousness endures forever.

4 He won renown for his wondrous deeds; ^psalm-111-4

gracious and merciful is the LORD.

5 He gives food to those who fear him, ^psalm-111-5

he remembers his covenant forever.

6 He showed his powerful deeds to his people, ^psalm-111-6

giving them the inheritance of the nations.

7 The works of his hands are true and just, ^psalm-111-7

reliable all his decrees,

8 Established forever and ever, ^psalm-111-8

to be observed with truth and equity.

9 He sent release to his people, ^psalm-111-9

decreed his covenant forever;

holy and fearsome is his name.

10 The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; ^psalm-111-10

prudent are all who practice it.

His praise endures forever.

---

\* (Psalm 111) A Temple singer (Ps 111:1) tells how God is revealed in Israel’s history (Ps 111:2 – 10). The deeds reveal God’s very self, powerful, merciful, faithful. The poem is an acrostic, each verse beginning with a successive letter of the Hebrew alphabet.

\* (111:1) In the assembled congregation of the upright: in the Temple, cf. Ps 149:1.

\* (111:5) Food to those who fear him: probably a reference to the manna in the desert, which elsewhere is seen as a type of the Eucharist, cf. [[john-06#^john-06-31|Jn 6:31 – 33, 49 – 51]].

\* (111:10) The fear of the LORD: reverence for God.
