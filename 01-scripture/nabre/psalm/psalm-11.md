# Psalm 11

## Confidence in the Presence of God

1 For the leader. Of David. ^psalm-11-1

### I

In the LORD I take refuge;

how can you say to me,

“Flee like a bird to the mountains!

2 See how the wicked string their bows, ^psalm-11-2

fit their arrows to the string

to shoot from the shadows at the upright of heart.

3 If foundations are destroyed, ^psalm-11-3

what can the just one do?”

### II

4 The LORD is in his holy temple; ^psalm-11-4

the LORD’s throne is in heaven.

God’s eyes keep careful watch;

they test the children of Adam.

5 The LORD tests the righteous and the wicked, ^psalm-11-5

hates those who love violence,

6 And rains upon the wicked ^psalm-11-6

fiery coals and brimstone,

a scorching wind their allotted cup.

7 The LORD is just and loves just deeds; ^psalm-11-7

the upright will see his face.

---

\* (Psalm 11) A song of trust. Though friends counsel flight to the mountain country (a traditional hideout) to escape trouble ([[psalm-11#^psalm-11-1|Ps 11:1 – 3]]), the innocent psalmist reaffirms confidence in God, who protects those who seek asylum in the Temple ([[psalm-11#^psalm-11-4|Ps 11:4 – 7]]).

\* (11:3) Foundations: usually understood of public order, cf. Ps 82:5.

\* (11:6) Their allotted cup: the cup that God gives people to drink is a common figure for their destiny, cf. [[psalm-16#^psalm-16-5|Ps 16:5]]; 75:9; [[matthew-20#^matthew-20-22|Mt 20:22]]; 26:39; Rv 14:10.
