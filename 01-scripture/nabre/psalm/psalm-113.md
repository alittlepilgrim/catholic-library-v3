# Psalm 113

## Praise of God’s Care for the Poor

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-113-1

### I

Praise, you servants of the LORD,

praise the name of the LORD.

2 Blessed be the name of the LORD ^psalm-113-2

both now and forever.

3 From the rising of the sun to its setting ^psalm-113-3

let the name of the LORD be praised.

### II

4 High above all nations is the LORD; ^psalm-113-4

above the heavens his glory.

5 Who is like the LORD our God, ^psalm-113-5

enthroned on high,

6 looking down on heaven and earth? ^psalm-113-6

7 He raises the needy from the dust, ^psalm-113-7

lifts the poor from the ash heap,

8 Seats them with princes, ^psalm-113-8

the princes of the people,

9 Gives the childless wife a home, ^psalm-113-9

the joyful mother of children.

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 113) A hymn exhorting the congregation to praise God’s name, i.e., the way in which God is present in the world; the name is mentioned three times in Ps 113:1 – 3. The divine name is especially honored in the Temple (Ps 113:1) but its recognition is not limited by time (Ps 113:2) and space (Ps 113:3), for God is everywhere active (Ps 113:4 – 5) especially in rescuing the lowly faithful (Ps 113:7 – 9).
