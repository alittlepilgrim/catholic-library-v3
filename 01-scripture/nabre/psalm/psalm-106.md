# Psalm 106

## Israel’s Confession of sin

1 Hallelujah! ^psalm-106-1

### A

Give thanks to the LORD, who is good,

whose mercy endures forever.

2 Who can recount the mighty deeds of the LORD, ^psalm-106-2

proclaim in full God’s praise?

3 Blessed those who do what is right, ^psalm-106-3

whose deeds are always just.

4 Remember me, LORD, as you favor your people; ^psalm-106-4

come to me with your saving help,

5 That I may see the prosperity of your chosen ones, ^psalm-106-5

rejoice in the joy of your people,

and glory with your heritage.

### B

6 We have sinned like our ancestors; ^psalm-106-6

we have done wrong and are guilty.

#### I

7 Our ancestors in Egypt ^psalm-106-7

did not attend to your wonders.

They did not remember your manifold mercy;

they defied the Most High at the Red Sea.

8 Yet he saved them for his name’s sake ^psalm-106-8

to make his power known.

9 He roared at the Red Sea and it dried up. ^psalm-106-9

He led them through the deep as through a desert.

10 He rescued them from hostile hands, ^psalm-106-10

freed them from the power of the enemy.

11 The waters covered their oppressors; ^psalm-106-11

not one of them survived.

12 Then they believed his words ^psalm-106-12

and sang his praise.

#### II

13 But they soon forgot all he had done; ^psalm-106-13

they had no patience for his plan.

14 In the desert they gave in to their cravings, ^psalm-106-14

tempted God in the wasteland.

15 So he gave them what they asked ^psalm-106-15

and sent a wasting disease against them.

#### III

16 In the camp they challenged Moses ^psalm-106-16

and Aaron, the holy one of the LORD.

17 The earth opened and swallowed Dathan, ^psalm-106-17

it closed on the followers of Abiram.

18 Against their company the fire blazed; ^psalm-106-18

flames consumed the wicked.

#### IV

19 At Horeb they fashioned a calf, ^psalm-106-19

worshiped a metal statue.

20 They exchanged their glory ^psalm-106-20

for the image of a grass-eating bull.

21 They forgot the God who had saved them, ^psalm-106-21

who had done great deeds in Egypt,

22 Amazing deeds in the land of Ham, ^psalm-106-22

fearsome deeds at the Red Sea.

23 He would have decreed their destruction, ^psalm-106-23

had not Moses, his chosen one,

Withstood him in the breach

to turn back his destroying anger.

#### V

24 Next they despised the beautiful land; ^psalm-106-24

they did not believe the promise.

25 In their tents they complained; ^psalm-106-25

they did not heed the voice of the LORD.

26 So with raised hand he swore ^psalm-106-26

he would destroy them in the desert,

27 And scatter their descendants among the nations, ^psalm-106-27

disperse them in foreign lands.

#### VI

28 They joined in the rites of Baal of Peor, ^psalm-106-28

ate food sacrificed to the dead.

29 They provoked him by their actions, ^psalm-106-29

and a plague broke out among them.

30 Then Phinehas rose to intervene, ^psalm-106-30

and the plague was brought to a halt.

31 This was counted for him as a righteous deed ^psalm-106-31

for all generations to come.

#### VII

32 At the waters of Meribah they angered God, ^psalm-106-32

and Moses suffered because of them.

33 They so embittered his spirit ^psalm-106-33

that rash words crossed his lips.

#### VIII

34 They did not destroy the peoples ^psalm-106-34

as the LORD had commanded them,

35 But mingled with the nations ^psalm-106-35

and imitated their ways.

36 They served their idols ^psalm-106-36

and were ensnared by them.

37 They sacrificed to demons ^psalm-106-37

their own sons and daughters,

38 Shedding innocent blood, ^psalm-106-38

the blood of their own sons and daughters,

Whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan,

desecrating the land with bloodshed.

39 They defiled themselves by their actions, ^psalm-106-39

became adulterers by their conduct.

40 So the LORD grew angry with his people, ^psalm-106-40

abhorred his own heritage.

41 He handed them over to the nations, ^psalm-106-41

and their adversaries ruled over them.

42 Their enemies oppressed them, ^psalm-106-42

kept them under subjection.

43 Many times did he rescue them, ^psalm-106-43

but they kept rebelling and scheming

and were brought low by their own guilt.

44 Still God had regard for their affliction ^psalm-106-44

when he heard their wailing.

45 For their sake he remembered his covenant ^psalm-106-45

and relented in his abundant mercy,

46 Winning for them compassion ^psalm-106-46

from all who held them captive.

### C

47 Save us, LORD, our God; ^psalm-106-47

gather us from among the nations

That we may give thanks to your holy name

and glory in praising you.

48 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, ^psalm-106-48

from everlasting to everlasting!

Let all the people say, Amen!

Hallelujah!

---

\* (Psalm 106) Israel is invited to praise the God whose mercy has always tempered judgment of Israel (Ps 106:1 – 3). The speaker, on behalf of all, seeks solidarity with the people, who can always count on God’s fidelity despite their sin (Ps 106:4 – 5). Confident of God’s mercy, the speaker invites national repentance (Ps 106:6) by reciting from Israel’s history eight instances of sin, judgment, and forgiveness. The sins are the rebellion at the Red Sea (Ps 106:6 – 12; see Ex 14 – 15), the craving for meat in the desert (Ps 106:13 – 15; see Nm 11), the challenge to Moses’ authority (Ps 106:16 – 18; see Nm 16), the golden calf episode (Ps 106:19 – 23; see Ex 32 – 34), the refusal to take Canaan by the southern route (Ps 106:24 – 27; see Nm 13 – 14 and Dt 1 – 2), the rebellion at Baal-Peor (Ps 106:28 – 31; see Nm 25:1 – 10), the anger of Moses (Ps 106:32 – 33; see Nm 20:1 – 13), and mingling with the nations (Ps 106:34 – 47). The last, as suggested by its length and generalized language, may be the sin that invites the repentance of the present generation. The text gives the site of each sin: Egypt (Ps 106:7), the desert (Ps 106:14), the camp (Ps 106:16), Horeb (Ps 106:19), in their tents (Ps 106:25), Baal-Peor (Ps 106:28), the waters of Meribah (Ps 106:32), Canaan (Ps 106:38).

\* (106:20) Their glory: meant as a reference to God.

\* (106:23) Withstood him in the breach: the image is that of Moses standing in a narrow break made in the wall to keep anyone from entering.

\* (106:32) Moses suffered because of them: Moses was not allowed to enter the promised land because of his rash words (Nm 20:12). According to Dt 1:37, Moses was not allowed to cross because of the people’s sin, not his own.

\* (106:37) Demons: Hebrew *shedim* occurs in parallelism with “gods” in an important inscription from Transjordan and hence can also be translated “the gods.”

\* (106:48) A doxology ending Book IV of the Psalter. It is not part of the Psalm.
