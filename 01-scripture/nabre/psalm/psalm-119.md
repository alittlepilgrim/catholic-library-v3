# Psalm 119

## A Prayer to God, the Lawgiver

### Aleph

1 Blessed those whose way is blameless, ^psalm-119-1

who walk by the law of the LORD.

2 Blessed those who keep his testimonies, ^psalm-119-2

who seek him with all their heart.

3 They do no wrong; ^psalm-119-3

they walk in his ways.

4 You have given them the command ^psalm-119-4

to observe your precepts with care.

5 May my ways be firm ^psalm-119-5

in the observance of your statutes!

6 Then I will not be ashamed ^psalm-119-6

to ponder all your commandments.

7 I will praise you with sincere heart ^psalm-119-7

as I study your righteous judgments.

8 I will observe your statutes; ^psalm-119-8

do not leave me all alone.

### Beth

9 How can the young keep his way without fault? ^psalm-119-9

Only by observing your words.

10 With all my heart I seek you; ^psalm-119-10

do not let me stray from your commandments.

11 In my heart I treasure your promise, ^psalm-119-11

that I may not sin against you.

12 Blessed are you, O LORD; ^psalm-119-12

teach me your statutes.

13 With my lips I recite ^psalm-119-13

all the judgments you have spoken.

14 I find joy in the way of your testimonies ^psalm-119-14

more than in all riches.

15 I will ponder your precepts ^psalm-119-15

and consider your paths.

16 In your statutes I take delight; ^psalm-119-16

I will never forget your word.

### Gimel

17 Be kind to your servant that I may live, ^psalm-119-17

that I may keep your word.

18 Open my eyes to see clearly ^psalm-119-18

the wonders of your law.

19 I am a sojourner in the land; ^psalm-119-19

do not hide your commandments from me.

20 At all times my soul is stirred ^psalm-119-20

with longing for your judgments.

21 With a curse you rebuke the proud ^psalm-119-21

who stray from your commandments.

22 Free me from disgrace and contempt, ^psalm-119-22

for I keep your testimonies.

23 Though princes meet and talk against me, ^psalm-119-23

your servant meditates on your statutes.

24 Your testimonies are my delight; ^psalm-119-24

they are my counselors.

### Daleth

25 My soul clings to the dust; ^psalm-119-25

give me life in accord with your word.

26 I disclosed my ways and you answered me; ^psalm-119-26

teach me your statutes.

27 Make me understand the way of your precepts; ^psalm-119-27

I will ponder your wondrous deeds.

28 My soul is depressed; ^psalm-119-28

lift me up according to your word.

29 Lead me from the way of deceit; ^psalm-119-29

favor me with your law.

30 The way of loyalty I have chosen; ^psalm-119-30

I have kept your judgments.

31 I cling to your testimonies, LORD; ^psalm-119-31

do not let me come to shame.

32 I will run the way of your commandments, ^psalm-119-32

for you will broaden my heart.

### He

33 LORD, teach me the way of your statutes; ^psalm-119-33

I shall keep them with care.

34 Give me understanding to keep your law, ^psalm-119-34

to observe it with all my heart.

35 Lead me in the path of your commandments, ^psalm-119-35

for that is my delight.

36 Direct my heart toward your testimonies ^psalm-119-36

and away from gain.

37 Avert my eyes from what is worthless; ^psalm-119-37

by your way give me life.

38 For your servant, fulfill your promise ^psalm-119-38

made to those who fear you.

39 Turn away from me the taunts I dread, ^psalm-119-39

for your judgments are good.

40 See how I long for your precepts; ^psalm-119-40

in your righteousness give me life.

### Waw

41 Let your mercy come to me, LORD, ^psalm-119-41

salvation in accord with your promise.

42 Let me answer my taunters with a word, ^psalm-119-42

for I trust in your word.

43 Do not take the word of truth from my mouth, ^psalm-119-43

for in your judgments is my hope.

44 I will keep your law always, ^psalm-119-44

for all time and forever.

45 I will walk freely in an open space ^psalm-119-45

because I cherish your precepts.

46 I will speak openly of your testimonies ^psalm-119-46

without fear even before kings.

47 I delight in your commandments, ^psalm-119-47

which I dearly love.

48 I lift up my hands to your commandments; ^psalm-119-48

I study your statutes, which I love.

### Zayin

49 Remember your word to your servant ^psalm-119-49

by which you give me hope.

50 This is my comfort in affliction, ^psalm-119-50

your promise that gives me life.

51 Though the arrogant utterly scorn me, ^psalm-119-51

I do not turn from your law.

52 When I recite your judgments of old ^psalm-119-52

I am comforted, LORD.

53 Rage seizes me because of the wicked; ^psalm-119-53

they forsake your law.

54 Your statutes become my songs ^psalm-119-54

wherever I make my home.

55 Even at night I remember your name ^psalm-119-55

in observance of your law, LORD.

56 This is my good fortune, ^psalm-119-56

for I have kept your precepts.

### Heth

57 My portion is the LORD; ^psalm-119-57

I promise to observe your words.

58 I entreat you with all my heart: ^psalm-119-58

have mercy on me in accord with your promise.

59 I have examined my ways ^psalm-119-59

and turned my steps to your testimonies.

60 I am prompt, I do not hesitate ^psalm-119-60

in observing your commandments.

61 Though the snares of the wicked surround me, ^psalm-119-61

your law I do not forget.

62 At midnight I rise to praise you ^psalm-119-62

because of your righteous judgments.

63 I am the friend of all who fear you, ^psalm-119-63

of all who observe your precepts.

64 The earth, LORD, is filled with your mercy; ^psalm-119-64

teach me your statutes.

### Teth

65 You have treated your servant well, ^psalm-119-65

according to your word, O LORD.

66 Teach me wisdom and knowledge, ^psalm-119-66

for in your commandments I trust.

67 Before I was afflicted I went astray, ^psalm-119-67

but now I hold to your promise.

68 You are good and do what is good; ^psalm-119-68

teach me your statutes.

69 The arrogant smear me with lies, ^psalm-119-69

but I keep your precepts with all my heart.

70 Their hearts are gross and fat; ^psalm-119-70

as for me, your law is my delight.

71 It was good for me to be afflicted, ^psalm-119-71

in order to learn your statutes.

72 The law of your mouth is more precious to me ^psalm-119-72

than heaps of silver and gold.

### Yodh

73 Your hands made me and fashioned me; ^psalm-119-73

give me understanding to learn your commandments.

74 Those who fear you rejoice to see me, ^psalm-119-74

because I hope in your word.

75 I know, LORD, that your judgments are righteous; ^psalm-119-75

though you afflict me, you are faithful.

76 May your mercy comfort me ^psalm-119-76

in accord with your promise to your servant.

77 Show me compassion that I may live, ^psalm-119-77

for your law is my delight.

78 Shame the proud for leading me astray with falsehood, ^psalm-119-78

that I may study your testimonies.

79 Let those who fear you turn to me, ^psalm-119-79

those who acknowledge your testimonies.

80 May I be wholehearted toward your statutes, ^psalm-119-80

that I may not be put to shame.

### Kaph

81 My soul longs for your salvation; ^psalm-119-81

I put my hope in your word.

82 My eyes long to see your promise. ^psalm-119-82

When will you comfort me?

83 I am like a wineskin shriveled by smoke, ^psalm-119-83

but I have not forgotten your statutes.

84 How long can your servant survive? ^psalm-119-84

When will your judgment doom my foes?

85 The arrogant have dug pits for me; ^psalm-119-85

defying your law.

86 All your commandments are steadfast. ^psalm-119-86

Help me! I am pursued without cause.

87 They have almost put an end to me on earth, ^psalm-119-87

but I do not forsake your precepts.

88 In your mercy give me life, ^psalm-119-88

to observe the testimonies of your mouth.

### Lamedh

89 Your word, LORD, stands forever; ^psalm-119-89

it is firm as the heavens.

90 Through all generations your truth endures; ^psalm-119-90

fixed to stand firm like the earth.

91 By your judgments they stand firm to this day, ^psalm-119-91

for all things are your servants.

92 Had your law not been my delight, ^psalm-119-92

I would have perished in my affliction.

93 I will never forget your precepts; ^psalm-119-93

through them you give me life.

94 I am yours; save me, ^psalm-119-94

for I cherish your precepts.

95 The wicked hope to destroy me, ^psalm-119-95

but I seek to understand your testimonies.

96 I have seen the limits of all perfection, ^psalm-119-96

but your commandment is without bounds.

### Mem

97 How I love your law, Lord! ^psalm-119-97

I study it all day long.

98 Your commandment makes me wiser than my foes, ^psalm-119-98

as it is forever with me.

99 I have more insight than all my teachers, ^psalm-119-99

because I ponder your testimonies.

100 I have more understanding than my elders, ^psalm-119-100

because I keep your precepts.

101 I keep my steps from every evil path, ^psalm-119-101

that I may observe your word.

102 From your judgments I do not turn, ^psalm-119-102

for you have instructed me.

103 How sweet to my tongue is your promise, ^psalm-119-103

sweeter than honey to my mouth!

104 Through your precepts I gain understanding; ^psalm-119-104

therefore I hate all false ways.

### Nun

105 Your word is a lamp for my feet, ^psalm-119-105

a light for my path.

106 I make a solemn vow ^psalm-119-106

to observe your righteous judgments.

107 I am very much afflicted, LORD; ^psalm-119-107

give me life in accord with your word.

108 Accept my freely offered praise; ^psalm-119-108

LORD, teach me your judgments.

109 My life is always at risk, ^psalm-119-109

but I do not forget your law.

110 The wicked have set snares for me, ^psalm-119-110

but from your precepts I do not stray.

111 Your testimonies are my heritage forever; ^psalm-119-111

they are the joy of my heart.

112 My heart is set on fulfilling your statutes; ^psalm-119-112

they are my reward forever.

### Samekh

113 I hate every hypocrite; ^psalm-119-113

your law I love.

114 You are my refuge and shield; ^psalm-119-114

in your word I hope.

115 Depart from me, you wicked, ^psalm-119-115

116 Sustain me by your promise that I may live; ^psalm-119-116

134 Free me from human oppression, ^psalm-119-134

136 My eyes shed streams of tears ^psalm-119-136

161 Princes persecute me without reason, ^psalm-119-161

162 I rejoice at your promise, ^psalm-119-162

163 Falsehood I hate and abhor; ^psalm-119-163

166 I look for your salvation, LORD, ^psalm-119-166

167 I observe your testimonies; ^psalm-119-167

168 I observe your precepts and testimonies; ^psalm-119-168

### Taw

169 Let my cry come before you, LORD; ^psalm-119-169

in keeping with your word, give me understanding.

170 Let my prayer come before you; ^psalm-119-170

rescue me according to your promise.

171 May my lips pour forth your praise, ^psalm-119-171

172 May my tongue sing of your promise, ^psalm-119-172

---

\* (Psalm 119) This Psalm, the longest by far in the Psalter, praises God for giving such splendid laws and instruction for people to live by. The author glorifies and thanks God for the Torah, prays for protection from sinners enraged by others’ fidelity to the law, laments the cost of obedience, delights in the law’s consolations, begs for wisdom to understand the precepts, and asks for the rewards of keeping them. Several expected elements do not appear in the Psalm: Mount Sinai with its story of God’s revelation and gift to Israel of instruction and commandments, the Temple and other institutions related to revelation and laws (frequent in other Psalms). The Psalm is fascinated with God’s word directing and guiding human life. The poem is an acrostic; its twenty-two stanzas (of eight verses each) are in the order of the Hebrew alphabet. Each of the eight verses within a stanza begins with the same letter. Each verse contains one word for “instruction.” The translation here given attempts to translate each Hebrew word for “instruction” with the same English word. There are, however, nine words for “instruction,” not eight, so the principle of a different word for “instruction” in each verse cannot be maintained with perfect consistency. The nine words for “instruction” in the translation are: law, statute, commandment, precept, testimony, word, judgment, way, and promise.

\* (119:19) A sojourner in the land: like someone without the legal protection of a native inhabitant, the psalmist has a special need for the guidance of God’s teaching.

\* (119:48) I lift up my hands to your commandments: to lift up the hands was an ancient gesture of reverence to God. Here the picture is applied to God’s law.

\* (119:89 – 91) God’s word creates the world, which manifests that word by its permanence and reliability.

\* (119:97) Lord: the inclusion of “Lord” follows the tradition of the Septuagint and the Vulgate.
