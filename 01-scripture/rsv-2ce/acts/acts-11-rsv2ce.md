# Acts 11 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Peter's Report to the Church at Jerusalem**

1 Now the apostles and the brethren who were in Judea heard that the Gentiles also had received the word of God. ^acts-11-1

2 So when Peter went up to Jerusalem, the circumcision party criticized him, ^acts-11-2

3 saying, "Why did you go to uncircumcised men and eat with them?" ^acts-11-3

4 But Peter began and explained to them in order: ^acts-11-4

5 "I was in the city of Joppa praying; and in a trance I saw a vision, something descending, like a great sheet, let down from heaven by four corners; and it came down to me. ^acts-11-5

6 Looking at it closely I observed animals and beasts of prey and reptiles and birds of the air. ^acts-11-6

7 And I heard a voice saying to me, 'Rise, Peter; kill and eat.' ^acts-11-7

8 But I said, 'No, Lord; for nothing common or unclean has ever entered my mouth.' ^acts-11-8

9 But the voice answered a second time from heaven, 'What God has cleansed you must not call common.' ^acts-11-9

10 This happened three times, and all was drawn up again into heaven. ^acts-11-10

11 At that very moment three men arrived at the house in which we were, sent to me from Caesare'a. ^acts-11-11

12 And the Spirit told me to go with them, making no distinction. These six brethren also accompanied me, and we entered the man's house. ^acts-11-12

13 And he told us how he had seen the angel standing in his house and saying, 'Send to Joppa and bring Simon called Peter; ^acts-11-13

14 he will declare to you a message by which you will be saved, you and all your household.' ^acts-11-14

15 As I began to speak, the Holy Spirit fell on them just as on us at the beginning. ^acts-11-15

16 And I remembered the word of the Lord, how he said, 'John baptized with water, but you shall be baptized with the Holy Spirit.' ^acts-11-16

17 If then God gave the same gift to them as he gave to us when we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ, who was I that I could withstand God?" ^acts-11-17

18 When they heard this they were silenced. And they glorified God, saying, "Then to the Gentiles also God has granted repentance unto life." ^acts-11-18

**The Church in Antioch**

19 Now those who were scattered because of the persecution that arose over Stephen traveled as far as Phoeni'cia and Cyprus and Antioch, speaking the word to none except Jews. ^acts-11-19

20 But there were some of them, men of Cyprus and Cyre'ne, who on coming to Antioch spoke to the Greeks<sup>i</sup> also, preaching the Lord Jesus. ^acts-11-20

21 And the hand of the Lord was with them, and a great number that believed turned to the Lord. ^acts-11-21

22 News of this came to the ears of the Church in Jerusalem, and they sent Barnabas to Antioch. ^acts-11-22

23 When he came and saw the grace of God, he was glad; and he exhorted them all to remain faithful to the Lord with steadfast purpose; ^acts-11-23

24 for he was a good man, full of the Holy Spirit and of faith. And a large company was added to the Lord. ^acts-11-24

25 So Barnabas went to Tarsus to look for Saul; ^acts-11-25

26 and when he had found him, he brought him to Antioch. For a whole year they met with<sup>j</sup> the Church, and taught a large company of people; and in Antioch the disciples were for the first time called Christians. ^acts-11-26

27 Now in these days prophets came down from Jerusalem to Antioch. ^acts-11-27

28 And one of them named Ag'abus stood up and foretold by the Spirit that there would be a great famine over all the world; and this took place in the days of Claudius. ^acts-11-28

29 And the disciples determined, every one according to his ability, to send relief to the brethren who lived in Judea; ^acts-11-29

30 and they did so, sending it to the elders by the hand of Barnabas and Saul. ^acts-11-30
