# Acts 8 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 And Saul was consenting to his death. ^acts-08-1

**Saul Persecutes the Church**

And on that day a great persecution arose against the Church in Jerusalem; and they were all scattered throughout the region of Judea and Sama'ria, except the apostles. ^acts-08-1c

2 Devout men buried Stephen, and made great lamentation over him. ^acts-08-2

3 But Saul laid waste the Church, and entering house after house, he dragged off men and women and committed them to prison. ^acts-08-3

**Philip Preaches in Samaria [Map]**

4 Now those who were scattered went about preaching the word. ^acts-08-4

5 Philip went down to a city of Samar'ia, and proclaimed to them the Christ. ^acts-08-5

6 And the multitudes with one accord gave heed to what was said by Philip, when they heard him and saw the signs which he did. ^acts-08-6

7 For unclean spirits came out of many who were possessed, crying with a loud voice; and many who were paralyzed or lame were healed. ^acts-08-7

8 So there was much joy in that city. ^acts-08-8

**Simon the Magician**

9 But there was a man named Simon who had previously practiced magic in the city and amazed the nation of Samar'ia, saying that he himself was somebody great. ^acts-08-9

10 They all listened to him, from the least to the greatest, saying, "This man is that power of God which is called Great." ^acts-08-10

11 And they listened to him, because for a long time he had amazed them with his magic. ^acts-08-11

12 But when they believed Philip as he preached good news about the kingdom of God and the name of Jesus Christ, they were baptized, both men and women. ^acts-08-12

13 Even Simon himself believed, and after being baptized he continued with Philip. And seeing signs and great miracles performed, he was amazed. ^acts-08-13

14 Now when the apostles at Jerusalem heard that Samar'ia had received the word of God, they sent to them Peter and John, ^acts-08-14

15 who came down and prayed for them that they might receive the Holy Spirit; ^acts-08-15

16 for the Spirit had not yet fallen on any of them, but they had only been baptized in the name of the Lord Jesus. ^acts-08-16

17 Then they laid their hands on them and they received the Holy Spirit. ^acts-08-17

18 Now when Simon saw that the Spirit was given through the laying on of the apostles' hands, he offered them money, ^acts-08-18

19 saying, "Give me also this power, that any one on whom I lay my hands may receive the Holy Spirit." ^acts-08-19

20 But Peter said to him, "Your silver perish with you, because you thought you could obtain the gift of God with money! \* ^acts-08-20

21 You have neither part nor lot in this matter, for your heart is not right before God. ^acts-08-21

22 Repent therefore of this wickedness of yours, and pray to the Lord that, if possible, the intent of your heart may be forgiven you. ^acts-08-22

23 For I see that you are in the gall of bitterness and in the bond of iniquity." ^acts-08-23

24 And Simon answered, "Pray for me to the Lord, that nothing of what you have said may come upon me." ^acts-08-24

25 Now when they had testified and spoken the word of the Lord, they returned to Jerusalem, preaching the gospel to many villages of the Samaritans. ^acts-08-25

**Philip and the Ethiopian Eunuch**

26 But an angel of the Lord said to Philip, "Rise and go toward the south<sup>g</sup> to the road that goes down from Jerusalem to Gaza." This is a desert road. ^acts-08-26

27 And he rose and went. And behold, an Ethiopian, a eunuch, a minister of Canda'ce the queen of the Ethiopians, in charge of all her treasure, had come to Jerusalem to worship ^acts-08-27

28 and was returning; seated in his chariot, he was reading the prophet Isaiah. ^acts-08-28

29 And the Spirit said to Philip, "Go up and join this chariot." ^acts-08-29

30 So Philip ran to him, and heard him reading Isaiah the prophet, and asked, "Do you understand what you are reading?" ^acts-08-30

31 And he said, "How can I, unless some one guides me?" And he invited Philip to come up and sit with him. ^acts-08-31

32 Now the passage of the Scripture which he was reading was this:

"As a sheep led to the slaughter
or a lamb before its shearer is silent,
so he opens not his mouth. ^acts-08-32

33 In his humiliation justice was denied him.
  Who can describe his generation?
  For his life is taken up from the earth." ^acts-08-33

34 And the eunuch said to Philip, "Please, about whom does the prophet say this, about himself or about some one else?" ^acts-08-34

35 Then Philip opened his mouth, and beginning with this Scripture he told him the good news of Jesus. ^acts-08-35

36 And as they went along the road they came to some water, and the eunuch said, "See, here is water! What is to prevent my being baptized?"<sup>h</sup> ^acts-08-36

38 And he commanded the chariot to stop, and they both went down into the water, Philip and the eunuch, and he baptized him. ^acts-08-38

39 And when they came up out of the water, the Spirit of the Lord caught up Philip; and the eunuch saw him no more, and went on his way rejoicing. ^acts-08-39

40 But Philip was found at Azo'tus, and passing on he preached the gospel to all the towns till he came to Caesare'a. ^acts-08-40
