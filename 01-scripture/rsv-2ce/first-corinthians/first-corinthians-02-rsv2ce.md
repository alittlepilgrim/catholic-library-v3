# 1 Corinthians 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Proclaiming Christ Crucified**

1 When I came to you, brethren, I did not come proclaiming to you the testimony<sup>c</sup> of God in lofty words or wisdom. ^first-corinthians-02-1

2 For I decided to know nothing among you except Jesus Christ and him crucified. \* ^first-corinthians-02-2

3 And I was with you in weakness and in much fear and trembling; ^first-corinthians-02-3

4 and my speech and my message were not in plausible words of wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power, ^first-corinthians-02-4

5 that your faith might not rest in the wisdom of men but in the power of God. ^first-corinthians-02-5

**The True Wisdom of God**

6 Yet among the mature we do impart wisdom, although it is not a wisdom of this age or of the rulers of this age, who are doomed to pass away. ^first-corinthians-02-6

7 But we impart a secret and hidden wisdom of God, which God decreed before the ages for our glorification. ^first-corinthians-02-7

8 None of the rulers of this age understood this; for if they had, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory. ^first-corinthians-02-8

9 But, as it is written,

"What no eye has seen, nor ear heard,
nor the heart of man conceived,
what God has prepared for those who love him," ^first-corinthians-02-9

10 God has revealed to us through the Spirit. For the Spirit searches everything, even the depths of God. ^first-corinthians-02-10

11 For what person knows a man's thoughts except the spirit of the man which is in him? So also no one comprehends the thoughts of God except the Spirit of God. ^first-corinthians-02-11

12 Now we have received not the spirit of the world, but the Spirit which is from God, that we might understand the gifts bestowed on us by God. ^first-corinthians-02-12

13 And we impart this in words not taught by human wisdom but taught by the Spirit, interpreting spiritual truths to those who possess the Spirit.<sup>d</sup> ^first-corinthians-02-13

14 The unspiritual<sup>e</sup> man does not receive the gifts of the Spirit of God, for they are folly to him, and he is not able to understand them because they are spiritually discerned. ^first-corinthians-02-14

15 The spiritual man judges all things, but is himself to be judged by no one. ^first-corinthians-02-15

16 "For who has known the mind of the Lord so as to instruct him?" But we have the mind of Christ. ^first-corinthians-02-16


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (2:1–2) Paul’s failure at Athens convinced him that lofty words and worldly wisdom were less effective than Jesus crucified. ^first-corinthians-02-dn-1
