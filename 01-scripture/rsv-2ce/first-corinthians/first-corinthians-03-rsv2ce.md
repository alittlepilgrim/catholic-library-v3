# 1 Corinthians 3 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**On Dissension in the Corinthian Church**

1 But I, brethren, could not address you as spiritual men, but as men of the flesh, as infants in Christ. ^first-corinthians-03-1

2 I fed you with milk, not solid food; for you were not ready for it; and even yet you are not ready, ^first-corinthians-03-2

3 for you are still of the flesh. For while there is jealousy and strife among you, are you not of the flesh, and behaving like ordinary men? ^first-corinthians-03-3

4 For when one says, "I belong to Paul," and another, "I belong to Apol'los," are you not merely men? ^first-corinthians-03-4

5 What then is Apol'los? What is Paul? Servants through whom you believed, as the Lord assigned to each. ^first-corinthians-03-5

6 I planted, Apol'los watered, but God gave the growth. ^first-corinthians-03-6

7 So neither he who plants nor he who waters is anything, but only God who gives the growth. ^first-corinthians-03-7

8 He who plants and he who waters are equal, and each shall receive his wages according to his labor. ^first-corinthians-03-8

9 For we are God's fellow workers;<sup>f</sup> you are God's field, God's building. ^first-corinthians-03-9

10 According to the commission of God given to me, like a skilled master builder I laid a foundation, and another man is building upon it. Let each man take care how he builds upon it. ^first-corinthians-03-10

11 For no other foundation can any one lay than that which is laid, which is Jesus Christ. ^first-corinthians-03-11

12 Now if any one builds on the foundation with gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, straw— ^first-corinthians-03-12

13 each man's work will become manifest; for the Day \* will disclose it, because it will be revealed with fire, and the fire will test what sort of work each one has done. ^first-corinthians-03-13

14 If the work which any man has built on the foundation survives, he will receive a reward. ^first-corinthians-03-14

15 If any man's work is burned up, he will suffer loss, though he himself will be saved, but only as through fire. ^first-corinthians-03-15

16 Do you not know that you are God's temple \* and that God's Spirit dwells in you? ^first-corinthians-03-16

17 If any one destroys God's temple, God will destroy him. For God's temple is holy, and that temple you are. ^first-corinthians-03-17

18 Let no one deceive himself. If any one among you thinks that he is wise in this age, let him become a fool that he may become wise. ^first-corinthians-03-18

19 For the wisdom of this world is folly with God. For it is written, "He catches the wise in their craftiness," ^first-corinthians-03-19

20 and again, "The Lord knows that the thoughts of the wise are futile." ^first-corinthians-03-20

21 So let no one boast of men. For all things are yours, ^first-corinthians-03-21

22 whether Paul or Apol'los or Ce'phas or the world or life or death or the present or the future, all are yours; ^first-corinthians-03-22

23 and you are Christ's; and Christ is God's. ^first-corinthians-03-23


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (3:13) the Day: i.e., the day of the Lord, God’s searching judgment. ^first-corinthians-03-dn-1

\* (3:16) God’s temple: The dignity of the Christians. ^first-corinthians-03-dn-2
