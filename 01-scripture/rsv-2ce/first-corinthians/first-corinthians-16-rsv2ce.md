# 1 Corinthians 16 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Contribution for the Saints**

1 Now concerning the contribution for the saints: \* as I directed the churches of Galatia, so you also are to do. ^first-corinthians-16-1

2 On the first day of every week, each of you is to put something aside and store it up, as he may prosper, so that contributions need not be made when I come. ^first-corinthians-16-2

3 And when I arrive, I will send those whom you accredit by letter to carry your gift to Jerusalem. ^first-corinthians-16-3

4 If it seems advisable that I should go also, they will accompany me. ^first-corinthians-16-4

**Plans for Travel**

5 I will visit you after passing through Macedonia, for I intend to pass through Macedonia, ^first-corinthians-16-5

6 and perhaps I will stay with you or even spend the winter, so that you may speed me on my journey, wherever I go. ^first-corinthians-16-6

7 For I do not want to see you now just in passing; I hope to spend some time with you, if the Lord permits. ^first-corinthians-16-7

8 But I will stay in Ephesus until Pentecost, ^first-corinthians-16-8

9 for a wide door for effective work has opened to me, and there are many adversaries. ^first-corinthians-16-9

10 When Timothy comes, see that you put him at ease among you, for he is doing the work of the Lord, as I am. ^first-corinthians-16-10

11 So let no one despise him. Speed him on his way in peace, that he may return to me; for I am expecting him with the brethren. ^first-corinthians-16-11

12 As for our brother Apol'los, I strongly urged him to visit you with the other brethren, but it was not at all his will<sup>b</sup> to come now. He will come when he has opportunity. ^first-corinthians-16-12

**Final Message and Greeting**

13 Be watchful, stand firm in your faith, be courageous, be strong. ^first-corinthians-16-13

14 Let all that you do be done in love. ^first-corinthians-16-14

15 Now, brethren, you know that the household of Steph'anas were the first converts in Acha'ia, and they have devoted themselves to the service of the saints; ^first-corinthians-16-15

16 I urge you to be subject to such men and to every fellow worker and laborer. ^first-corinthians-16-16

17 I rejoice at the coming of Steph'anas and Fortuna'tus and Acha'icus, because they have made up for your absence; ^first-corinthians-16-17

18 for they refreshed my spirit as well as yours. Give recognition to such men. ^first-corinthians-16-18

19 The churches of Asia send greetings. Aqui'la and Prisca, together with the church in their house, send you hearty greetings in the Lord. ^first-corinthians-16-19

20 All the brethren send greetings. Greet one another with a holy kiss. ^first-corinthians-16-20

21 I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. ^first-corinthians-16-21

22 If any one has no love for the Lord, let him be accursed. Our Lord, come!<sup>c</sup> ^first-corinthians-16-22

23 The grace of the Lord Jesus be with you. ^first-corinthians-16-23

24 My love be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen. ^first-corinthians-16-24


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (16:1) The collection to be made everywhere for the poor Christians in Jerusalem. ^first-corinthians-16-dn-1
