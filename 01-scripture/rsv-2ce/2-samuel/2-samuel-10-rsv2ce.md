# 2 Samuel 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[2-samuel-10|NABRE]]

**The Ammonites and Syrians Are Defeated**

1 After this the king of the Am'monites died, and Ha'nun his son reigned in his stead. ^2-samuel-10-1

2 And David said, “I will deal loyally with Ha'nun the son of Na'hash, as his father dealt loyally with me.” So David sent by his servants to console him concerning his father. And David’s servants came into the land of the Am'monites. ^2-samuel-10-2

3 But the princes of the Am'monites said to Ha'nun their lord, “Do you think, because David has sent comforters to you, that he is honoring your father? Has not David sent his servants to you to search the city, and to spy it out, and to overthrow it?” ^2-samuel-10-3

4 So Ha'nun took David’s servants, and shaved off half the beard of each, and cut off their garments in the middle, at their hips, and sent them away. ^2-samuel-10-4

5 When it was told David, he sent to meet them, for the men were greatly ashamed. And the king said, “Remain at Jericho until your beards have grown, and then return.” ^2-samuel-10-5

6 When the Am'monites saw that they had become odious to David, the Ammonites sent and hired the Syrians of Beth-re'hob, and the Syrians of Zobah, twenty thousand foot soldiers, and the king of Ma'acah with a thousand men, and the men of Tob, twelve thousand men. ^2-samuel-10-6

7 And when David heard of it, he sent Jo'ab and all the host of the mighty men. ^2-samuel-10-7

8 And the Am'monites came out and drew up in battle array at the entrance of the gate; and the Syrians of Zobah and of Re'hob, and the men of Tob and Ma'acah, were by themselves in the open country. ^2-samuel-10-8

9 When Jo'ab saw that the battle was set against him both in front and in the rear, he chose some of the picked men of Israel, and arrayed them against the Syrians; ^2-samuel-10-9

10 the rest of his men he put in the charge of Abi'shai his brother, and he arrayed them against the Am'monites. ^2-samuel-10-10

11 And he said, “If the Syrians are too strong for me, then you shall help me; but if the Am'monites are too strong for you, then I will come and help you. ^2-samuel-10-11

12 Be of good courage, and let us play the man for our people, and for the cities of our God; and may the LORD do what seems good to him.” ^2-samuel-10-12

13 So Jo'ab and the people who were with him drew near to battle against the Syrians; and they fled before him. ^2-samuel-10-13

14 And when the Am'monites saw that the Syrians fled, they likewise fled before Abi'shai, and entered the city. Then Jo'ab returned from fighting against the Ammonites, and came to Jerusalem. ^2-samuel-10-14

15 But when the Syrians saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they gathered themselves together. ^2-samuel-10-15

16 And Hadade'zer sent, and brought out the Syrians who were beyond the Euphra'tes; and they came to He'lam, with Shobach the commander of the army of Hadadezer at their head. ^2-samuel-10-16

17 And when it was told David, he gathered all Israel together, and crossed the Jordan, and came to He'lam. And the Syrians arrayed themselves against David, and fought with him. ^2-samuel-10-17

18 And the Syrians fled before Israel; and David slew of the Syrians the men of seven hundred chariots, and forty thousand horsemen, and wounded Shobach the commander of their army, so that he died there. ^2-samuel-10-18

19 And when all the kings who were servants of Hadade'zer saw that they had been defeated by Israel, they made peace with Israel, and became subject to them. So the Syrians feared to help the Am'monites any more. ^2-samuel-10-19
