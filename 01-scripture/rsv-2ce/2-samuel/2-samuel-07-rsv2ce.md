# 2 Samuel 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[2-samuel-07|NABRE]]

**God’s Promise to David**

1 Now when the king dwelt in his house, and the LORD had given him rest from all his enemies round about, ^2-samuel-07-1

2 the king said to Nathan the prophet, “See now, I dwell in a house of cedar, but the ark of God dwells in a tent.” ^2-samuel-07-2

3 And Nathan said to the king, “Go, do all that is in your heart; for the LORD is with you.” ^2-samuel-07-3

4 But that same night the word of the LORD came to Nathan, ^2-samuel-07-4

5 “Go and tell my servant David, ‘Thus says the LORD: Would you build me a house to dwell in? ^2-samuel-07-5

6 I have not dwelt in a house since the day I brought up the sons of Israel from Egypt to this day, but I have been moving about in a tent for my dwelling. ^2-samuel-07-6

7 In all places where I have moved with all the sons of Israel, did I speak a word with any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to shepherd my people Israel, saying, “Why have you not built me a house of cedar?” ’ ^2-samuel-07-7

8 Now therefore thus you shall say to my servant David, ‘Thus says the LORD of hosts, I took you from the pasture, from following the sheep, that you should be prince over my people Israel; ^2-samuel-07-8

9 and I have been with you wherever you went, and have cut off all your enemies from before you; and I will make for you a great name, like the name of the great ones of the earth. ^2-samuel-07-9

10 And I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in their own place, and be disturbed no more; and violent men shall afflict them no more, as formerly, ^2-samuel-07-10

11 from the time that I appointed judges over my people Israel; and I will give you rest from all your enemies. Moreover the LORD declares to you that the LORD will make you a house. ^2-samuel-07-11

12 When your days are fulfilled and you lie down with your fathers, I will raise up your offspring after you, who shall come forth from your body, and I will establish his kingdom. ^2-samuel-07-12

13 He shall build a house for my name, and I will establish the throne of his kingdom for ever. ^2-samuel-07-13

14 I will be his father, and he shall be my son. When he commits iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, with the stripes of the sons of men; ^2-samuel-07-14

15 but I will not take my merciful love from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away from before you. ^2-samuel-07-15

16 And your house and your kingdom shall be made sure for ever before me; your throne shall be established for ever.’ ” ^2-samuel-07-16

17 In accordance with all these words, and in accordance with all this vision, Nathan spoke to David. ^2-samuel-07-17

**David’s Prayer**

18 Then King David went in and sat before the LORD, and said, “Who am I, O Lord GOD, and what is my house, that you have brought me thus far? ^2-samuel-07-18

19 And yet this was a small thing in your eyes, O Lord GOD; you have spoken also of your servant’s house for a great while to come, and have shown me future generations, O Lord GOD! ^2-samuel-07-19

20 And what more can David say to you? For you know your servant, O Lord GOD! ^2-samuel-07-20

21 Because of your promise, and according to your own heart, you have wrought all this greatness, to make your servant know it. ^2-samuel-07-21

22 Therefore you are great, O LORD God; for there is none like you, and there is no God besides you, according to all that we have heard with our ears. ^2-samuel-07-22

23 What other nation on earth is like your people Israel, whom God went to redeem to be his people, making himself a name, and doing for them great and terrible things, by driving out before his people a nation and its gods? ^2-samuel-07-23

24 And you established for yourself your people Israel to be your people for ever; and you, O LORD, became their God. ^2-samuel-07-24

25 And now, O LORD God, confirm for ever the word which you have spoken concerning your servant and concerning his house, and do as you have spoken; ^2-samuel-07-25

26 and your name will be magnified for ever, saying, ‘The LORD of hosts is God over Israel,’ and the house of your servant David will be established before you. ^2-samuel-07-26

27 For you, O LORD of hosts, the God of Israel, have made this revelation to your servant, saying, ‘I will build you a house’; therefore your servant has found courage to pray this prayer to you. ^2-samuel-07-27

28 And now, O Lord GOD, you are God, and your words are true, and you have promised this good thing to your servant; ^2-samuel-07-28

29 now therefore may it please you to bless the house of your servant, that it may continue for ever before you; for you, O Lord GOD, have spoken, and with your blessing shall the house of your servant be blessed for ever.” ^2-samuel-07-29

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (7:1–29) The point of this prophecy is the establishment of David’s dynasty. Hence God says he will build David a house (verse 11). David thanks him suitably in a formal prayer (verses 18–29). ^2-samuel-07-dn-1
