# 2 Samuel 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[2-samuel-08|NABRE]]

**David’s Wars**

1 After this David defeated the Philis'tines and subdued them, and David took Meth'eg-am'mah out of the hand of the Philistines. ^2-samuel-08-1

2 And he defeated Moab, and measured them with a line, making them lie down on the ground; two lines he measured to be put to death, and one full line to be spared. And the Moabites became servants to David and brought tribute. ^2-samuel-08-2

3 David also defeated Hadade'zer the son of Re'hob, king of Zobah, as he went to restore his power at the river Euphrates. ^2-samuel-08-3

4 And David took from him a thousand and seven hundred horsemen, and twenty thousand foot soldiers; and David hamstrung all the chariot horses, but left enough for a hundred chariots. ^2-samuel-08-4

5 And when the Syrians of Damascus came to help Hadade'zer king of Zobah, David slew twenty-two thousand men of the Syrians. ^2-samuel-08-5

6 Then David put garrisons in Ar'am of Damascus; and the Syrians became servants to David and brought tribute. And the LORD gave victory to David wherever he went. ^2-samuel-08-6

7 And David took the shields of gold which were carried by the servants of Hadade'zer, and brought them to Jerusalem. ^2-samuel-08-7

8 And from Betah and from Bero'thai, cities of Hadade'zer, King David took very much bronze. ^2-samuel-08-8

9 When To'i king of Ha'math heard that David had defeated the whole army of Hadade'zer, ^2-samuel-08-9

10 To'i sent his son Jo'ram to King David, to greet him, and to congratulate him because he had fought against Hadade'zer and defeated him; for Hadadezer had often been at war with Toi. And Joram brought with him articles of silver, of gold, and of bronze; ^2-samuel-08-10

11 these also King David dedicated to the LORD, together with the silver and gold which he dedicated from all the nations he subdued, ^2-samuel-08-11

12 from E'dom, Moab, the Am'monites, the Philis'tines, Am'alek, and from the spoil of Hadade'zer the son of Re'hob, king of Zobah. ^2-samuel-08-12

13 And David won a name for himself. When he returned, he slew eighteen thousand E'domites in the Valley of Salt. ^2-samuel-08-13

14 And he put garrisons in E'dom; throughout all Edom he put garrisons, and all the E'domites became David’s servants. And the LORD gave victory to David wherever he went. ^2-samuel-08-14

**David’s Officers**

15 So David reigned over all Israel; and David administered justice and equity to all his people. ^2-samuel-08-15

16 And Jo'ab the son of Zeru'iah was over the army; and Jehosh'aphat the son of Ahi'lud was recorder; ^2-samuel-08-16

17 and Zad'ok the son of Ahi'tub and Ahim'elech the son of Abi'athar were priests; and Serai'ah was secretary; ^2-samuel-08-17

18 and Bena'iah the son of Jehoi'ada was over the Cher'ethites and the Pel'ethites; and David’s sons were priests. ^2-samuel-08-18
