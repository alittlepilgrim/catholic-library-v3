# Revelation 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Scroll and the Lamb**

1 And I saw in the right hand of him who was seated on the throne a scroll \* written within and on the back, sealed with seven seals; ^revelation-05-1

2 and I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, "Who is worthy to open the scroll and break its seals?" ^revelation-05-2

3 And no one in heaven or on earth or under the earth was able to open the scroll or to look into it, ^revelation-05-3

4 and I wept much that no one was found worthy to open the scroll or to look into it. ^revelation-05-4

5 Then one of the elders said to me, "Weep not; behold, the Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has conquered, so that he can open the scroll and its seven seals." ^revelation-05-5

6 And between the throne and the four living creatures and among the elders, I saw a Lamb standing, as though it had been slain, with seven horns and with seven eyes, \* which are the seven spirits of God sent out into all the earth; ^revelation-05-6

7 and he went and took the scroll from the right hand of him who was seated on the throne. ^revelation-05-7

8 And when he had taken the scroll, the four living creatures and the twenty- four elders fell down before the Lamb, each holding a harp, and with golden bowls full of incense, which are the prayers of the saints; ^revelation-05-8

9 and they sang a new song, saying,

  "Worthy are you to take the scroll and to open its
  seals,
  for you were slain and by your blood you ransomed
  men for God
from every tribe and tongue and people and nation, ^revelation-05-9

10 and have made them a kingdom and priests to our
  God,
  and they shall reign on earth." ^revelation-05-10

11 Then I looked, and I heard around the throne and the living creatures and the elders the voice of many angels, numbering myriads of myriads and thousands of thousands, ^revelation-05-11

12 saying with a loud voice, "Worthy is the Lamb who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!" ^revelation-05-12

13 And I heard every creature in heaven and on earth and under the earth and in the sea, and all therein, saying, "To him who sits upon the throne and to the Lamb be blessing and honor and glory and might for ever and ever!" ^revelation-05-13

14 And the four living creatures said, "Amen!" and the elders fell down and worshiped. ^revelation-05-14


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (5:1) a scroll: This contained God’s designs, kept secret till now; being written on both sides, nothing could be added. ^revelation-05-dn-1

\* (5:6) The seven horns and seven eyes symbolize Christ’s full power and knowledge. ^revelation-05-dn-2
