# Lamentations 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[lamentations-05|NABRE]]

**A Prayer for Mercy**

1 Remember, O LORD, what has befallen us; behold, and see our disgrace! ^lamentations-05-1

2 Our inheritance has been turned over to strangers, our homes to aliens. ^lamentations-05-2

3 We have become orphans, fatherless; our mothers are like widows. ^lamentations-05-3

4 We must pay for the water we drink, the wood we get must be bought. ^lamentations-05-4

5 With a yoke on our necks we are hard driven; we are weary, we are given no rest. ^lamentations-05-5

6 We have given the hand to Egypt, and to Assyria, to get bread enough. ^lamentations-05-6

7 Our fathers sinned, and are no more; and we bear their iniquities. ^lamentations-05-7

8 Slaves rule over us; there is none to deliver us from their hand. ^lamentations-05-8

9 We get our bread at the peril of our lives, because of the sword in the wilderness. ^lamentations-05-9

10 Our skin is hot as an oven with the burning heat of famine. ^lamentations-05-10

11 Women are ravished in Zion, virgins in the towns of Judah. ^lamentations-05-11

12 Princes are hung up by their hands; no respect is shown to the elders. ^lamentations-05-12

13 Young men are compelled to grind at the mill; and boys stagger under loads of wood. ^lamentations-05-13

14 The old men have quit the city gate, the young men their music. ^lamentations-05-14

15 The joy of our hearts has ceased; our dancing has been turned to mourning. ^lamentations-05-15

16 The crown has fallen from our head; woe to us, for we have sinned! ^lamentations-05-16

17 For this our heart has become sick, for these things our eyes have grown dim, ^lamentations-05-17

18 for Mount Zion which lies desolate; jackals prowl over it. ^lamentations-05-18

19 But you, O LORD, reign for ever; your throne endures to all generations. ^lamentations-05-19

20 Why do you forget us for ever, why do you so long forsake us? ^lamentations-05-20

21 Restore us to yourself, O LORD, that we may be restored! Renew our days as of old! ^lamentations-05-21

22 Or have you utterly rejected us? Are you exceedingly angry with us? ^lamentations-05-22
