# Lamentations 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[lamentations-03|NABRE]]

**God’s Merciful Love Endures**

1 I am the man who has seen affliction under the rod of his wrath; ^lamentations-03-1

2 he has driven and brought me into darkness without any light; ^lamentations-03-2

3 surely against me he turns his hand again and again the whole day long. ^lamentations-03-3

4 He has made my flesh and my skin waste away, and broken my bones; ^lamentations-03-4

5 he has besieged and enveloped me with bitterness and tribulation; ^lamentations-03-5

6 he has made me dwell in darkness like the dead of long ago. ^lamentations-03-6

7 He has walled me about so that I cannot escape; he has put heavy chains on me; ^lamentations-03-7

8 though I call and cry for help, he shuts out my prayer; ^lamentations-03-8

9 he has blocked my ways with hewn stones, he has made my paths crooked. ^lamentations-03-9

10 He is to me like a bear lying in wait, like a lion in hiding; ^lamentations-03-10

11 he led me off my way and tore me to pieces; he has made me desolate; ^lamentations-03-11

12 he bent his bow and set me as a mark for his arrow. ^lamentations-03-12

13 He drove into my heart the arrows of his quiver; ^lamentations-03-13

14 I have become the laughingstock of all peoples, the burden of their songs all day long. ^lamentations-03-14

15 He has filled me with bitterness, he has sated me with wormwood. ^lamentations-03-15

16 He has made my teeth grind on gravel, and made me cower in ashes; ^lamentations-03-16

17 my soul is bereft of peace, I have forgotten what happiness is; ^lamentations-03-17

18 so I say, “Gone is my glory, and my expectation from the LORD.” ^lamentations-03-18

19 Remember my affliction and my bitterness, the wormwood and the gall! ^lamentations-03-19

20 My soul continually thinks of it and is bowed down within me. ^lamentations-03-20

21 But this I call to mind, and therefore I have hope: ^lamentations-03-21

22 The steadfast love of the LORD never ceases, his mercies never come to an end; ^lamentations-03-22

23 they are new every morning; great is your faithfulness. ^lamentations-03-23

24 “The LORD is my portion,” says my soul, “therefore I will hope in him.” ^lamentations-03-24

25 The LORD is good to those who wait for him, to the soul that seeks him. ^lamentations-03-25

26 It is good that one should wait quietly for the salvation of the LORD. ^lamentations-03-26

27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth. ^lamentations-03-27

28 Let him sit alone in silence when he has laid it on him; ^lamentations-03-28

29 let him put his mouth in the dust— there may yet be hope; ^lamentations-03-29

30 let him give his cheek to the one who strikes him, and be filled with insults. ^lamentations-03-30

31 For the Lord will not cast off for ever, ^lamentations-03-31

32 but, though he cause grief, he will have compassion according to the abundance of his steadfast love; ^lamentations-03-32

33 for he does not willingly afflict or grieve the sons of men. ^lamentations-03-33

34 To crush under foot all the prisoners of the earth, ^lamentations-03-34

35 to turn aside the right of a man in the presence of the Most High, ^lamentations-03-35

36 to subvert a man in his cause, the Lord does not approve. ^lamentations-03-36

37 Who has commanded and it came to pass, unless the Lord has ordained it? ^lamentations-03-37

38 Is it not from the mouth of the Most High that good and evil come? ^lamentations-03-38

39 Why should a living man complain, a man, about the punishment of his sins? ^lamentations-03-39

40 Let us test and examine our ways, and return to the LORD! ^lamentations-03-40

41 Let us lift up our hearts and hands to God in heaven: ^lamentations-03-41

42 “We have transgressed and rebelled, and you have not forgiven. ^lamentations-03-42

43 “You have wrapped yourself with anger and pursued us, slaying without pity; ^lamentations-03-43

44 you have wrapped yourself with a cloud so that no prayer can pass through. ^lamentations-03-44

45 You have made us offscouring and refuse among the peoples. ^lamentations-03-45

46 “All our enemies rail against us; ^lamentations-03-46

47 panic and pitfall have come upon us, devastation and destruction; ^lamentations-03-47

48 my eyes flow with rivers of tears because of the destruction of the daughter of my people. ^lamentations-03-48

49 “My eyes will flow without ceasing, without respite, ^lamentations-03-49

50 until the LORD from heaven looks down and sees; ^lamentations-03-50

51 my eyes cause me grief at the fate of all the maidens of my city. ^lamentations-03-51

52 “I have been hunted like a bird by those who were my enemies without cause; ^lamentations-03-52

53 they flung me alive into the pit and cast stones on me; ^lamentations-03-53

54 water closed over my head; I said, ‘I am lost.’ ^lamentations-03-54

55 “I called on your name, O LORD, from the depths of the pit; ^lamentations-03-55

56 you heard my plea, ‘Do not close your ear to my cry for help!’ ^lamentations-03-56

57 You came near when I called on you; you said, ‘Do not fear!’ ^lamentations-03-57

58 “You have taken up my cause, O Lord, you have redeemed my life. ^lamentations-03-58

59 You have seen the wrong done to me, O LORD; do judge my cause. ^lamentations-03-59

60 You have seen all their vengeance, all their devices against me. ^lamentations-03-60

61 “You have heard their taunts, O LORD, all their devices against me. ^lamentations-03-61

62 The lips and thoughts of my assailants are against me all the day long. ^lamentations-03-62

63 Behold their sitting and their rising; I am the burden of their songs. ^lamentations-03-63

64 “You will repay them, O LORD, according to the work of their hands. ^lamentations-03-64

65 You will give them dullness of heart; your curse will be on them. ^lamentations-03-65

66 You will pursue them in anger and destroy them from under your heavens, O LORD.” ^lamentations-03-66
