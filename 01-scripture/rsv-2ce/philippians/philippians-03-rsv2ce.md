# Philippians 3 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Loss of All to Gain Christ**

1 Finally, my brethren, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you is not irksome to me, and is safe for you. ^philippians-03-1

2 Look out for the dogs, look out for the evil-workers, look out for those who mutilate the flesh. ^philippians-03-2

3 For we are the true circumcision, who worship God in spirit,<sup>e</sup> and glory in Christ Jesus, and put no confidence in the flesh. ^philippians-03-3

4 Though I myself have reason for confidence in the flesh also. If any other man thinks he has reason for confidence in the flesh, I have more: ^philippians-03-4

5 circumcised on the eighth day, of the people of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, a Hebrew born of Hebrews; as to the law a Pharisee, ^philippians-03-5

6 as to zeal a persecutor of the Church, as to righteousness under the law blameless. ^philippians-03-6

7 But whatever gain I had, I counted as loss for the sake of Christ. ^philippians-03-7

8 Indeed I count everything as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord. For his sake I have suffered the loss of all things, and count them as refuse, in order that I may gain Christ ^philippians-03-8

9 and be found in him, not having a righteousness of my own, based on law, but that which is through faith in Christ, the righteousness from God that depends on faith; ^philippians-03-9

10 that I may know him and the power of his resurrection, and may share his sufferings, becoming like him in his death, ^philippians-03-10

11 that if possible I may attain the resurrection from the dead. ^philippians-03-11

**Pressing toward the Goal**

12 Not that I have already obtained this or am already perfect; but I press on to make it my own, because Christ Jesus has made me his own. \* ^philippians-03-12

13 Brethren, I do not consider that I have made it my own; but one thing I do, forgetting what lies behind and straining forward to what lies ahead, ^philippians-03-13

14 I press on toward the goal for the prize of the upward call of God in Christ Jesus. ^philippians-03-14

15 Let those of us who are mature be thus minded; and if in anything you are otherwise minded, God will reveal that also to you. ^philippians-03-15

16 Only let us hold true to what we have attained. ^philippians-03-16

17 Brethren, join in imitating me, and mark those who so walk as you have an example in us. ^philippians-03-17

18 For many, of whom I have often told you and now tell you even with tears, walk as enemies of the cross of Christ. ^philippians-03-18

19 Their end is destruction, their god is the belly, and they glory in their shame, with minds set on earthly things. Â§ ^philippians-03-19

20 But our commonwealth is in heaven, and from it we await a Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, ^philippians-03-20

21 who will change our lowly body to be like his glorious body, by the power which enables him even to subject all things to himself. ^philippians-03-21

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (3:12) made me his own: On the road to Damascus. ^philippians-03-dn-1

\* (3:19) These Judaizers made holiness a question of distinction of foods and set great store by circumcision. ^philippians-03-dn-2
