# Philippians 1 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Salutation**

1 Paul and Timothy, servants<sup>a</sup> of Christ Jesus,

To all the saints in Christ Jesus who are at Philip'pi, with the bishops<sup>b</sup> and deacons: ^philippians-01-1

2 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^philippians-01-2

**Paul's Prayer for the Philippians**

3 I thank my God in all my remembrance of you, ^philippians-01-3

4 always in every prayer of mine for you all making my prayer with joy, ^philippians-01-4

5 thankful for your partnership in the gospel from the first day until now. ^philippians-01-5

6 And I am sure that he who began a good work in you will bring it to completion at the day of Jesus Christ. ^philippians-01-6

7 It is right for me to feel this way about you all, because I hold you in my heart, for you are all partakers with me of grace, both in my imprisonment and in the defense and confirmation of the gospel. ^philippians-01-7

8 For God is my witness, how I yearn for you all with the affection of Christ Jesus. ^philippians-01-8

9 And it is my prayer that your love may abound more and more, with knowledge and all discernment, ^philippians-01-9

10 so that you may approve what is excellent, and may be pure and blameless for the day of Christ, ^philippians-01-10

11 filled with the fruits of righteousness which come through Jesus Christ, to the glory and praise of God. ^philippians-01-11

**Paul's Present Circumstances**

12 I want you to know, brethren, that what has happened to me has really served to advance the gospel, ^philippians-01-12

13 so that it has become known throughout the whole praetorian guard<sup>c</sup> and to all the rest that my imprisonment is for Christ; ^philippians-01-13

14 and most of the brethren have been made confident in the Lord because of my imprisonment, \* and are much more bold to speak the word of God without fear. ^philippians-01-14

15 Some indeed preach Christ from envy and rivalry, but others from good will. ^philippians-01-15

16 The latter do it out of love, knowing that I am put here for the defense of the gospel; ^philippians-01-16

17 the former proclaim Christ out of partisanship, not sincerely but thinking to afflict me in my imprisonment. ^philippians-01-17

18 What then? Only that in every way, whether in pretense or in truth, Christ is proclaimed; and in that I rejoice. ^philippians-01-18

**To Live Is Christ**

19 Yes, and I shall rejoice. For I know that through your prayers and the help of the Spirit of Jesus Christ this will turn out for my deliverance, ^philippians-01-19

20 as it is my eager expectation and hope that I shall not be at all ashamed, but that with full courage now as always Christ will be honored in my body, \* whether by life or by death. ^philippians-01-20

21 For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain. ^philippians-01-21

22 If it is to be life in the flesh, that means fruitful labor for me. Yet which I shall choose I cannot tell. ^philippians-01-22

23 I am hard pressed between the two. My desire is to depart and be with Christ, for that is far better. ^philippians-01-23

24 But to remain in the flesh is more necessary on your account. ^philippians-01-24

25 Convinced of this, I know that I shall remain and continue with you all, for your progress and joy in the faith, ^philippians-01-25

26 so that in me you may have ample cause to glory in Christ Jesus, because of my coming to you again. ^philippians-01-26

27 Only let your manner of life be worthy of the gospel of Christ, so that whether I come and see you or am absent, I may hear of you that you stand firm in one spirit, with one mind striving side by side for the faith of the gospel, ^philippians-01-27

28 and not frightened in anything by your opponents. This is a clear omen to them of their destruction, but of your salvation, and that from God. ^philippians-01-28

29 For it has been granted to you that for the sake of Christ you should not only believe in him but also suffer for his sake, ^philippians-01-29

30 engaged in the same conflict which you saw and now hear to be mine. ^philippians-01-30


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:14) because of my imprisonment: i.e., because I continue to preach in their midst, though in prison. ^philippians-01-dn-1

\* (1:20) honored in my body: i.e., through my sufferings. ^philippians-01-dn-2
