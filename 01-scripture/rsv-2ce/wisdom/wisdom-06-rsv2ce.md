# Wisdom 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[wisdom-06|NABRE]]

**Admonition to Rulers**

1 Listen therefore, O kings, and understand; learn, O judges of the ends of the earth. ^wisdom-06-1

2 Give ear, you that rule over multitudes, and boast of many nations. ^wisdom-06-2

3 For your dominion was given you from the Lord, and your sovereignty from the Most High, who will search out your works and inquire into your plans. ^wisdom-06-3

4 Because as servants of his kingdom you did not rule rightly, nor keep the law, nor walk according to the purpose of God, ^wisdom-06-4

5 he will come upon you terribly and swiftly, because severe judgment falls on those in high places. ^wisdom-06-5

6 For the lowliest man may be pardoned in mercy, but mighty men will be mightily tested. ^wisdom-06-6

7 For the Lord of all will not stand in awe of any one, nor show deference to greatness; because he himself made both small and great, and he takes thought for all alike. ^wisdom-06-7

8 But a strict inquiry is in store for the mighty. ^wisdom-06-8

9 To you then, O monarchs, my words are directed, that you may learn wisdom and not transgress. ^wisdom-06-9

10 For they will be made holy who observe holy things in holiness, and those who have been taught them will find a defense. ^wisdom-06-10

11 Therefore set your desire on my words; long for them, and you will be instructed. ^wisdom-06-11

12 Wisdom is radiant and unfading, and she is easily discerned by those who love her, and is found by those who seek her. ^wisdom-06-12

13 She hastens to make herself known to those who desire her. ^wisdom-06-13

14 He who rises early to seek her will have no difficulty, for he will find her sitting at his gates. ^wisdom-06-14

15 To fix one’s thought on her is perfect understanding, and he who is vigilant on her account will soon be free from care, ^wisdom-06-15

16 because she goes about seeking those worthy of her, and she graciously appears to them in their paths, and meets them in every thought. ^wisdom-06-16

17 The beginning of wisdom is the most sincere desire for instruction, and concern for instruction is love of her, ^wisdom-06-17

18 and love of her is the keeping of her laws, and giving heed to her laws is assurance of immortality, ^wisdom-06-18

19 and immortality brings one near to God; ^wisdom-06-19

20 so the desire for wisdom leads to a kingdom. ^wisdom-06-20

21 Therefore if you delight in thrones and scepters, O monarchs over the peoples, honor wisdom, that you may reign for ever. ^wisdom-06-21

22 I will tell you what wisdom is and how she came to be, and I will hide no secrets from you, but I will trace her course from the beginning of creation, and make knowledge of her clear, and I will not pass by the truth; ^wisdom-06-22

23 neither will I travel in the company of sickly envy, for envy does not associate with wisdom. ^wisdom-06-23

24 A multitude of wise men is the salvation of the world, and a sensible king is the stability of his people. ^wisdom-06-24

25 Therefore be instructed by my words, and you will profit. ^wisdom-06-25
