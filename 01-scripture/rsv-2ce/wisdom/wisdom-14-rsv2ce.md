# Wisdom 14 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[wisdom-14|NABRE]]

1 Again, one preparing to sail and about to voyage over raging waves calls upon a piece of wood more fragile than the ship which carries him. ^wisdom-14-1

2 For it was desire for gain that planned that vessel, and wisdom was the craftsman who built it; ^wisdom-14-2

3 but it is your providence, O Father, that steers its course, because you have given it a path in the sea, and a safe way through the waves, ^wisdom-14-3

4 showing that you can save from every danger, so that even if a man lacks skill, he may put to sea. ^wisdom-14-4

5 It is your will that works of your wisdom should not be without effect; therefore men trust their lives even to the smallest piece of wood, and passing through the billows on a raft they come safely to land. ^wisdom-14-5

6 For even in the beginning, when arrogant giants were perishing, the hope of the world took refuge on a raft, and guided by your hand left to the world the seed of a new generation. ^wisdom-14-6

7 For blessed is the wood by which righteousness comes. ^wisdom-14-7

8 But the idol made with hands is accursed, and so is he who made it; because he did the work, and the perishable thing was named a god. ^wisdom-14-8

9 For equally hateful to God are the ungodly man and his ungodliness, ^wisdom-14-9

10 for what was done will be punished together with him who did it. ^wisdom-14-10

11 Therefore there will be a visitation also upon the heathen idols, because, though part of what God created, they became an abomination, and became traps for the souls of men and a snare to the feet of the foolish. ^wisdom-14-11

12 For the idea of making idols was the beginning of fornication, and the invention of them was the corruption of life, ^wisdom-14-12

13 for neither have they existed from the beginning nor will they exist for ever. ^wisdom-14-13

14 For through the vanity of men they entered the world, and therefore their speedy end has been planned. ^wisdom-14-14

15 For a father, consumed with grief at an untimely bereavement, made an image of his child, who had been suddenly taken from him; and he now honored as a god what was once a dead human being, and handed on to his dependents secret rites and initiations. ^wisdom-14-15

16 Then the ungodly custom, grown strong with time, was kept as a law, and at the command of monarchs graven images were worshiped. ^wisdom-14-16

17 When men could not honor monarchs in their presence, since they lived at a distance, they imagined their appearance far away, and made a visible image of the king whom they honored, so that by their zeal they might flatter the absent one as though present. ^wisdom-14-17

18 Then the ambition of the craftsman impelled even those who did not know the king to intensify their worship. ^wisdom-14-18

19 For he, perhaps wishing to please his ruler, skilfully forced the likeness to take more beautiful form, ^wisdom-14-19

20 and the multitude, attracted by the charm of his work, now regarded as an object of worship the one whom shortly before they had honored as a man. ^wisdom-14-20

21 And this became a hidden trap for mankind, because men, in bondage to misfortune or to royal authority, bestowed on objects of stone or wood the name that ought not to be shared. ^wisdom-14-21

22 Afterward it was not enough for them to err about the knowledge of God, but they live in great strife due to ignorance, and they call such great evils peace. ^wisdom-14-22

23 For whether they kill children in their initiations, or celebrate secret mysteries, or hold frenzied revels with strange customs, ^wisdom-14-23

24 they no longer keep either their lives or their marriages pure, but they either treacherously kill one another, or grieve one another by adultery, ^wisdom-14-24

25 and all is a raging riot of blood and murder, theft and deceit, corruption, faithlessness, tumult, perjury, ^wisdom-14-25

26 confusion over what is good, forgetfulness of favors, pollution of souls, sex perversion, disorder in marriage, adultery, and debauchery. ^wisdom-14-26

27 For the worship of idols not to be named is the beginning and cause and end of every evil. ^wisdom-14-27

28 For their worshipers either rave in exultation, or prophesy lies, or live unrighteously, or readily commit perjury; ^wisdom-14-28

29 for because they trust in lifeless idols they swear wicked oaths and expect to suffer no harm. ^wisdom-14-29

30 But just penalties will overtake them on two counts: because they thought wickedly of God in devoting themselves to idols, and because in deceit they swore unrighteously through contempt for holiness. ^wisdom-14-30

31 For it is not the power of the things by which men swear, but the just penalty for those who sin, that always pursues the transgression of the unrighteous. ^wisdom-14-31
