# Wisdom 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[wisdom-02|NABRE]]

1 For they reasoned unsoundly, saying to themselves, “Short and sorrowful is our life, and there is no remedy when a man comes to his end, and no one has been known to return from Hades. ^wisdom-02-1

2 Because we were born by mere chance, and hereafter we shall be as though we had never been; because the breath in our nostrils is smoke, and reason is a spark kindled by the beating of our hearts. ^wisdom-02-2

3 When it is extinguished, the body will turn to ashes, and the spirit will dissolve like empty air. ^wisdom-02-3

4 Our name will be forgotten in time, and no one will remember our works; our life will pass away like the traces of a cloud, and be scattered like mist that is chased by the rays of the sun and overcome by its heat. ^wisdom-02-4

5 For our allotted time is the passing of a shadow, and there is no return from our death, because it is sealed up and no one turns back. ^wisdom-02-5

6 “Come, therefore, let us enjoy the good things that exist, and make use of the creation to the full as in youth. ^wisdom-02-6

7 Let us take our fill of costly wine and perfumes, and let no flower of spring pass by us. ^wisdom-02-7

8 Let us crown ourselves with rosebuds before they wither. ^wisdom-02-8

9 Let none of us fail to share in our revelry, everywhere let us leave signs of enjoyment, because this is our portion, and this our lot. ^wisdom-02-9

10 Let us oppress the righteous poor man; let us not spare the widow nor regard the gray hairs of the aged. ^wisdom-02-10

11 But let our might be our law of right, for what is weak proves itself to be useless. ^wisdom-02-11

12 “Let us lie in wait for the righteous man, because he is inconvenient to us and opposes our actions; he reproaches us for sins against the law, and accuses us of sins against our training. ^wisdom-02-12

13 He professes to have knowledge of God, and calls himself a child of the Lord. ^wisdom-02-13

14 He became to us a reproof of our thoughts; ^wisdom-02-14

15 the very sight of him is a burden to us, because his manner of life is unlike that of others, and his ways are strange. ^wisdom-02-15

16 We are considered by him as something base, and he avoids our ways as unclean; he calls the last end of the righteous happy, and boasts that God is his father. ^wisdom-02-16

17 Let us see if his words are true, and let us test what will happen at the end of his life; ^wisdom-02-17

18 for if the righteous man is God’s son, he will help him, and will deliver him from the hand of his adversaries. ^wisdom-02-18

19 Let us test him with insult and torture, that we may find out how gentle he is, and make trial of his forbearance. ^wisdom-02-19

20 Let us condemn him to a shameful death, for, according to what he says, he will be protected.” ^wisdom-02-20

21 Thus they reasoned, but they were led astray, for their wickedness blinded them, ^wisdom-02-21

22 and they did not know the secret purposes of God, nor hope for the wages of holiness, nor discern the prize for blameless souls; ^wisdom-02-22

23 for God created man for incorruption, and made him in the image of his own eternity, ^wisdom-02-23

24 but through the devil’s envy death entered the world, and those who belong to his party experience it. ^wisdom-02-24
