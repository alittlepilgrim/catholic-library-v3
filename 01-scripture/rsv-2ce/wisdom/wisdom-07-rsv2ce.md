# Wisdom 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[wisdom-07|NABRE]]

**Solomon’s Prayer for and Love of Wisdom**

1 I also am mortal, like all men, a descendant of the first-formed child of earth; and in the womb of a mother I was molded into flesh, ^wisdom-07-1

2 within the period of ten months, compacted with blood, from the seed of a man and the pleasure of marriage. ^wisdom-07-2

3 And when I was born, I began to breathe the common air, and fell upon the kindred earth, and my first sound was a cry, like that of all. ^wisdom-07-3

4 I was nursed with care in swaddling cloths. ^wisdom-07-4

5 For no king has had a different beginning of existence; ^wisdom-07-5

6 there is for all mankind one entrance into life, and a common departure. ^wisdom-07-6

7 Therefore I prayed, and understanding was given me; I called upon God, and the spirit of wisdom came to me. ^wisdom-07-7

8 I preferred her to scepters and thrones, and I accounted wealth as nothing in comparison with her. ^wisdom-07-8

9 Neither did I liken to her any priceless gem, because all gold is but a little sand in her sight, and silver will be accounted as clay before her. ^wisdom-07-9

10 I loved her more than health and beauty, and I chose to have her rather than light, because her radiance never ceases. ^wisdom-07-10

11 All good things came to me along with her, and in her hands uncounted wealth. ^wisdom-07-11

12 I rejoiced in them all, because wisdom leads them; but I did not know that she was their mother. ^wisdom-07-12

13 I learned without guile and I impart without grudging; I do not hide her wealth, ^wisdom-07-13

14 for it is an unfailing treasure for men; those who get it obtain friendship with God, commended for the gifts that come from instruction. ^wisdom-07-14

15 May God grant that I speak with judgment and have thoughts worthy of what I have received, for he is the guide even of wisdom and the corrector of the wise. ^wisdom-07-15

16 For both we and our words are in his hand, as are all understanding and skill in crafts. ^wisdom-07-16

17 For it is he who gave me unerring knowledge of what exists, to know the structure of the world and the activity of the elements; ^wisdom-07-17

18 the beginning and end and middle of times, the alternations of the solstices and the changes of the seasons, ^wisdom-07-18

19 the cycles of the year and the constellations of the stars, ^wisdom-07-19

20 the natures of animals and the tempers of wild beasts, the powers of spirits and the reasonings of men, the varieties of plants and the virtues of roots; ^wisdom-07-20

21 I learned both what is secret and what is manifest, ^wisdom-07-21

22 for wisdom, the fashioner of all things, taught me. For in her there is a spirit that is intelligent, holy, unique, manifold, subtle, mobile, clear, unpolluted, distinct, invulnerable, loving the good, keen, irresistible, ^wisdom-07-22

23 beneficent, humane, steadfast, sure, free from anxiety, all-powerful, overseeing all, and penetrating through all spirits that are intelligent and pure and most subtle. ^wisdom-07-23

24 For wisdom is more mobile than any motion; because of her pureness she pervades and penetrates all things. ^wisdom-07-24

25 For she is a breath of the power of God, and a pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty; therefore nothing defiled gains entrance into her. ^wisdom-07-25

26 For she is a reflection of eternal light, a spotless mirror of the working of God, and an image of his goodness. ^wisdom-07-26

27 Though she is but one, she can do all things, and while remaining in herself, she renews all things; in every generation she passes into holy souls and makes them friends of God, and prophets; ^wisdom-07-27

28 for God loves nothing so much as the man who lives with wisdom. ^wisdom-07-28

29 For she is more beautiful than the sun, and excels every constellation of the stars. Compared with the light she is found to be superior, ^wisdom-07-29

30 for it is succeeded by the night, but against wisdom evil does not prevail. ^wisdom-07-30
