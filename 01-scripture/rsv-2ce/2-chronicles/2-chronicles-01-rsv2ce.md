# 2 Chronicles 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[2-chronicles-01|NABRE]]

**Solomon Requests Wisdom**

1 Solomon the son of David established himself in his kingdom, and the LORD his God was with him and made him exceedingly great. ^2-chronicles-01-1

2 Solomon spoke to all Israel, to the commanders of thousands and of hundreds, to the judges, and to all the leaders in all Israel, the heads of fathers’ houses. ^2-chronicles-01-2

3 And Solomon, and all the assembly with him, went to the high place that was at Gib'eon; for the tent of meeting of God, which Moses the servant of the LORD had made in the wilderness, was there. ^2-chronicles-01-3

4 (But David had brought up the ark of God from Kir'iath-je'arim to the place that David had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it in Jerusalem.) ^2-chronicles-01-4

5 Moreover the bronze altar that Bez'alel the son of U'ri, son of Hur, had made, was there before the tabernacle of the LORD. And Solomon and the assembly sought the LORD. ^2-chronicles-01-5

6 And Solomon went up there to the bronze altar before the LORD, which was at the tent of meeting, and offered a thousand burnt offerings upon it. ^2-chronicles-01-6

7 In that night God appeared to Solomon, and said to him, “Ask what I shall give you.” ^2-chronicles-01-7

8 And Solomon said to God, “You have shown great and merciful love to David my father, and have made me king in his stead. ^2-chronicles-01-8

9 O LORD God, let your promise to David my father be now fulfilled, for you have made me king over a people as many as the dust of the earth. ^2-chronicles-01-9

10 Give me now wisdom and knowledge to go out and come in before this people, for who can rule this your people, that is so great?” ^2-chronicles-01-10

11 God answered Solomon, “Because this was in your heart, and you have not asked possessions, wealth, honor, or the life of those who hate you, and have not even asked long life, but have asked wisdom and knowledge for yourself that you may rule my people over whom I have made you king, ^2-chronicles-01-11

12 wisdom and knowledge are granted to you. I will also give you riches, possessions, and honor, such as none of the kings had who were before you, and none after you shall have the like.” ^2-chronicles-01-12

13 So Solomon came from the high place at Gib'eon, from before the tent of meeting, to Jerusalem. And he reigned over Israel. ^2-chronicles-01-13

**Solomon’s Acquisitions**

14 Solomon gathered together chariots and horsemen; he had fourteen hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, whom he stationed in the chariot cities and with the king in Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-01-14

15 And the king made silver and gold as common in Jerusalem as stone, and he made cedar as plentiful as the sycamore of the Shephe'lah. ^2-chronicles-01-15

16 And Solomon’s import of horses was from Egypt and Ku'e, and the king’s traders received them from Kue for a price. ^2-chronicles-01-16

17 They imported a chariot from Egypt for six hundred shekels of silver, and a horse for a hundred and fifty; likewise through them these were exported to all the kings of the Hittites and the kings of Syria. ^2-chronicles-01-17

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:1) The Chronicler makes no mention of the rival claims of Adonijah and of his being put to death by Solomon; cf. 1 Kings 2:13–25. He concentrates on the favorable aspects of the reign and enlarges on the wisdom Solomon received from God. ^2-chronicles-01-dn-1
