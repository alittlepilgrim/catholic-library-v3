# 2 Chronicles 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[2-chronicles-08|NABRE]]

**Solomon Builds Many Cities**

1 At the end of twenty years, in which Solomon had built the house of the LORD and his own house, ^2-chronicles-08-1

2 Solomon rebuilt the cities which Hu'ram had given to him, and settled the sons of Israel in them. ^2-chronicles-08-2

3 And Solomon went to Ha'math-zo'bah, and took it. ^2-chronicles-08-3

4 He built Tadmor in the wilderness and all the store-cities which he built in Ha'math. ^2-chronicles-08-4

5 He also built Upper Beth-ho'ron and Lower Beth-horon, fortified cities with walls, gates, and bars, ^2-chronicles-08-5

6 and Ba'alath, and all the store-cities that Solomon had, and all the cities for his chariots, and the cities for his horsemen, and whatever Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and in all the land of his dominion. ^2-chronicles-08-6

7 All the people who were left of the Hittites, the Am'orites, the Per'izzites, the Hi'vites, and the Jeb'usites, who were not of Israel, ^2-chronicles-08-7

8 from their descendants who were left after them in the land, whom the sons of Israel had not destroyed—these Solomon made a forced levy and so they are to this day. ^2-chronicles-08-8

9 But of the sons of Israel Solomon made no slaves for his work; they were soldiers, and his officers, the commanders of his chariots, and his horsemen. ^2-chronicles-08-9

10 And these were the chief officers of King Solomon, two hundred and fifty, who exercised authority over the people. ^2-chronicles-08-10

11 Solomon brought Pharaoh’s daughter up from the city of David to the house which he had built for her, for he said, “My wife shall not live in the house of David king of Israel, for the places to which the ark of the LORD has come are holy.” ^2-chronicles-08-11

12 Then Solomon offered up burnt offerings to the LORD upon the altar of the LORD which he had built before the vestibule, ^2-chronicles-08-12

13 as the duty of each day required, offering according to the commandment of Moses for the sabbaths, the new moons, and the three annual feasts—the feast of unleavened bread, the feast of weeks, and the feast of tabernacles. ^2-chronicles-08-13

14 According to the ordinance of David his father, he appointed the divisions of the priests for their service, and the Levites for their offices of praise and ministry before the priests as the duty of each day required, and the gatekeepers in their divisions for the several gates; for so David the man of God had commanded. ^2-chronicles-08-14

15 And they did not turn aside from what the king had commanded the priests and Levites concerning any matter and concerning the treasuries. ^2-chronicles-08-15

16 Thus was accomplished all the work of Solomon from the day the foundation of the house of the LORD was laid until it was finished. So the house of the LORD was completed. ^2-chronicles-08-16

17 Then Solomon went to E'zion-ge'ber and E'loth on the shore of the sea, in the land of E'dom. ^2-chronicles-08-17

18 And Hu'ram sent him by his servants ships and servants familiar with the sea, and they went to O'phir together with the servants of Solomon, and fetched from there four hundred and fifty talents of gold and brought it to King Solomon. ^2-chronicles-08-18
