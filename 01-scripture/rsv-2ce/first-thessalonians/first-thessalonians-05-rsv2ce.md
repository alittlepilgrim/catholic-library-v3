# 1 Thessalonians 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 But as to the times and the seasons, brethren, you have no need to have anything written to you. ^first-thessalonians-05-1

2 For you yourselves know well that the day of the Lord will come like a thief in the night. ^first-thessalonians-05-2

3 When people say, "There is peace and security," then sudden destruction will come upon them as labor pains come upon a woman with child, and there will be no escape. ^first-thessalonians-05-3

4 But you are not in darkness, brethren, for that day to surprise you like a thief. ^first-thessalonians-05-4

5 For you are all sons of light and sons of the day; we are not of the night or of darkness. ^first-thessalonians-05-5

6 So then let us not sleep, as others do, but let us keep awake and be sober. ^first-thessalonians-05-6

7 For those who sleep sleep at night, and those who get drunk are drunk at night. ^first-thessalonians-05-7

8 But, since we belong to the day, let us be sober, and put on the breastplate of faith and love, and for a helmet the hope of salvation. ^first-thessalonians-05-8

9 For God has not destined us for wrath, but to obtain salvation through our Lord Jesus Christ, ^first-thessalonians-05-9

10 who died for us so that whether we wake or sleep we might live with him. ^first-thessalonians-05-10

11 Therefore encourage one another and build one another up, just as you are doing. ^first-thessalonians-05-11

**Final Exhortations, Greetings, and Benediction**

12 But we beg you, brethren, to respect those who labor among you and are over you in the Lord and admonish you, ^first-thessalonians-05-12

13 and to esteem them very highly in love because of their work. Be at peace among yourselves.<sup>c2</sup> ^first-thessalonians-05-13

14 And we exhort you, brethren, admonish the idle, encourage the fainthearted, help the weak, be patient with them all. ^first-thessalonians-05-14

15 See that none of you repays evil for evil, but always seek to do good to one another and to all. ^first-thessalonians-05-15

16 Rejoice always, ^first-thessalonians-05-16

17 pray constantly, ^first-thessalonians-05-17

18 give thanks in all circumstances; for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you. ^first-thessalonians-05-18

19 Do not quench the Spirit, ^first-thessalonians-05-19

20 do not despise prophesying, ^first-thessalonians-05-20

21 but test everything; hold fast what is good, ^first-thessalonians-05-21

22 abstain from every form of evil. ^first-thessalonians-05-22

23 May the God of peace himself sanctify you wholly; and may your spirit and soul and body be kept sound and blameless at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. ^first-thessalonians-05-23

24 He who calls you is faithful, and he will do it. ^first-thessalonians-05-24

25 Brethren, pray for us. ^first-thessalonians-05-25

26 Greet all the brethren with a holy kiss. ^first-thessalonians-05-26

27 I adjure you by the Lord that this letter be read to all the brethren. ^first-thessalonians-05-27

28 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. ^first-thessalonians-05-28
