## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* The Books of Ezra and Nehemiah (The First and Second Books of Esdras): These two books were originally one and were written probably by the same author as Chronicles. They deal with the century following the return from the Exile, but it is by no means a complete history that they give. The author’s main purpose is to describe the religious and political reorganization after the return, and to underline the workings of God’s providence. There is some uncertainty as to the chronology and order of the events described. Two main sources, the memoirs of Ezra and those of Nehemiah, are here intermingled. ^ezra-00-dn-1
