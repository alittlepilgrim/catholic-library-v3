# Jonah 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[jonah-02|NABRE]]

**Jonah’s Prayer and Deliverance**

1 Then Jonah prayed to the LORD his God from the belly of the fish, ^jonah-02-1

2 saying, “I called to the LORD, out of my distress, and he answered me; out of the belly of Sheol I cried, and you heard my voice. ^jonah-02-2

3 For you cast me into the deep, into the heart of the seas, and the flood was round about me; all your waves and your billows passed over me. ^jonah-02-3

4 Then I said, ‘I am cast out from your presence; how shall I again look upon your holy temple?’ ^jonah-02-4

5 The waters closed in over me, the deep was round about me; weeds were wrapped about my head ^jonah-02-5

6 at the roots of the mountains. I went down to the land whose bars closed upon me for ever; yet you brought up my life from the Pit, O LORD my God. ^jonah-02-6

7 When my soul fainted within me, I remembered the LORD; and my prayer came to you, into your holy temple. ^jonah-02-7

8 Those who pay regard to vain idols forsake their true loyalty. ^jonah-02-8

9 But I with the voice of thanksgiving will sacrifice to you; what I have vowed I will pay. Deliverance belongs to the LORD!” ^jonah-02-9

10 And the LORD spoke to the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land. ^jonah-02-10
