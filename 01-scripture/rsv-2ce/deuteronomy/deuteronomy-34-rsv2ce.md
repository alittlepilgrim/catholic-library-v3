# Deuteronomy 34 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[deuteronomy-34|NABRE]]

**The Death of Moses**

1 And Moses went up from the plains of Moab to Mount Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, which is opposite Jericho. And the LORD showed him all the land, Gilead as far as Dan, ^deuteronomy-34-1

2 all Naph'tali, the land of E'phraim and Manas'seh, all the land of Judah as far as the western sea, ^deuteronomy-34-2

3 the Neg'eb, and the Plain, that is, the valley of Jericho the city of palm trees, as far as Zoar. ^deuteronomy-34-3

4 And the LORD said to him, “This is the land of which I swore to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, ‘I will give it to your descendants.’ I have let you see it with your eyes, but you shall not go over there.” ^deuteronomy-34-4

5 So Moses the servant of the LORD died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the LORD, ^deuteronomy-34-5

6 and he buried him in the valley in the land of Moab opposite Beth-pe'or; but no man knows the place of his burial to this day. ^deuteronomy-34-6

7 Moses was a hundred and twenty years old when he died; his eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated. ^deuteronomy-34-7

8 And the sons of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days; then the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were ended. ^deuteronomy-34-8

9 And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom, for Moses had laid his hands upon him; so the sons of Israel obeyed him, and did as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^deuteronomy-34-9

10 And there has not arisen a prophet since in Israel like Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face, ^deuteronomy-34-10

11 none like him for all the signs and the wonders which the LORD sent him to do in the land of Egypt, to Pharaoh and to all his servants and to all his land, ^deuteronomy-34-11

12 and for all the mighty power and all the great and terrible deeds which Moses wrought in the sight of all Israel. ^deuteronomy-34-12
