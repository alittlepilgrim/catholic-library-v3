# James 4 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Friendship with the World**

1 What causes wars, and what causes fightings among you? Is it not your passions that are at war in your members? ^james-04-1

2 You desire and do not have; so you kill. And you covet<sup>d</sup> and cannot obtain; so you fight and wage war. You do not have, because you do not ask. ^james-04-2

3 You ask and do not receive, because you ask wrongly, to spend it on your passions. ^james-04-3

4 Unfaithful creatures! Do you not know that friendship with the world is enmity with God? Therefore whoever wishes to be a friend of the world makes himself an enemy of God. ^james-04-4

5 Or do you suppose it is in vain that the Scripture says, "He yearns jealously over the spirit which he has made to dwell in us"? ^james-04-5

6 But he gives more grace; therefore it says, "God opposes the proud, but gives grace to the humble." ^james-04-6

7 Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you. ^james-04-7

8 Draw near to God and he will draw near to you. Cleanse your hands, you sinners, and purify your hearts, you men of double mind. ^james-04-8

9 Be wretched and mourn and weep. Let your laughter be turned to mourning and your joy to dejection. ^james-04-9

10 Humble yourselves before the Lord and he will exalt you. ^james-04-10

**Warning against Judging Another**

11 Do not speak evil against one another, brethren. He that speaks evil against a brother or judges his brother, speaks evil against the law and judges the law. But if you judge the law, you are not a doer of the law but a judge. ^james-04-11

12 There is one lawgiver and judge, he who is able to save and to destroy. But who are you that you judge your neighbor? ^james-04-12

**Boasting about Tomorrow**

13 Come now, you who say, "Today or tomorrow we will go into such and such a town and spend a year there and trade and get gain"; ^james-04-13

14 whereas you do not know about tomorrow. What is your life? For you are a mist that appears for a little time and then vanishes. ^james-04-14

15 Instead you ought to say, "If the Lord wills, we shall live and we shall do this or that." ^james-04-15

16 As it is, you boast in your arrogance. All such boasting is evil. ^james-04-16

17 Whoever knows what is right to do and fails to do it, for him it is sin. ^james-04-17
