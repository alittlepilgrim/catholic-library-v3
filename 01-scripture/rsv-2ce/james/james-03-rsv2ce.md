# James 3 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Taming the Tongue**

1 Let not many of you become teachers, my brethren, for you know that we who teach shall be judged with greater strictness. ^james-03-1

2 For we all make many mistakes, and if any one makes no mistakes in what he says he is a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body also. ^james-03-2

3 If we put bits into the mouths of horses that they may obey us, we guide their whole bodies. ^james-03-3

4 Look at the ships also; though they are so great and are driven by strong winds, they are guided by a very small rudder wherever the will of the pilot directs. ^james-03-4

5 So the tongue is a little member and boasts of great things. How great a forest is set ablaze by a small fire! ^james-03-5

6 And the tongue is a fire. The tongue is an unrighteous world among our members, staining the whole body, setting on fire the cycle of nature,<sup>b</sup> and set on fire by hell.<sup>c</sup> ^james-03-6

7 For every kind of beast and bird, of reptile and sea creature, can be tamed and has been tamed by mankind, ^james-03-7

8 but no human being can tame the tongue—a restless evil, full of deadly poison. ^james-03-8

9 With it we bless the Lord and Father, and with it we curse men, who are made in the likeness of God. ^james-03-9

10 From the same mouth come blessing and cursing. My brethren, this ought not to be so. ^james-03-10

11 Does a spring pour forth from the same opening fresh water and brackish? ^james-03-11

12 Can a fig tree, my brethren, yield olives, or a grapevine figs? No more can salt water yield fresh. ^james-03-12

**Two Kinds of Wisdom**

13 Who is wise and understanding among you? By his good life let him show his works in the meekness of wisdom. ^james-03-13

14 But if you have bitter jealousy and selfish ambition in your hearts, do not boast and be false to the truth. ^james-03-14

15 This wisdom is not such as comes down from above, but is earthly, unspiritual, devilish. ^james-03-15

16 For where jealousy and selfish ambition exist, there will be disorder and every vile practice. ^james-03-16

17 But the wisdom from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, open to reason, full of mercy and good fruits, without uncertainty or insincerity. ^james-03-17

18 And the harvest of righteousness is sown in peace by those who make peace. ^james-03-18
