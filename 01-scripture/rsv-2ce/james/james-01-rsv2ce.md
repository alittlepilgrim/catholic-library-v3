# James 1 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Salutation**

1 James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ,

To the twelve tribes \* in the Dispersion:

Greeting. ^james-01-1

**Faith and Wisdom**

2 Count it all joy, my brethren, when you meet various trials, ^james-01-2

3 for you know that the testing of your faith produces steadfastness. ^james-01-3

4 And let steadfastness have its full effect, that you may be perfect and complete, lacking in nothing. ^james-01-4

5 If any of you lacks wisdom, let him ask God, who gives to all men generously and without reproaching, and it will be given him. ^james-01-5

6 But let him ask in faith, with no doubting, for he who doubts is like a wave of the sea that is driven and tossed by the wind. ^james-01-6

7 , ^james-01-7

8 For that person must not suppose that a double-minded man, unstable in all his ways, will receive anything from the Lord. ^james-01-8

**Poverty and Riches**

9 Let the lowly brother boast in his exaltation, ^james-01-9

10 and the rich in his humiliation, because like the flower of the grass he will pass away. ^james-01-10

11 For the sun rises with its scorching heat and withers the grass; its flower falls, and its beauty perishes. So will the rich man fade away in the midst of his pursuits. ^james-01-11

**Trial and Temptation**

12 Blessed is the man who endures trial, for when he has stood the test he will receive the crown of life which God has promised to those who love him. ^james-01-12

13 Let no one say when he is tempted, "I am tempted by God"; for God cannot be tempted with evil and he himself tempts no one; ^james-01-13

14 but each person is tempted when he is lured and enticed by his own desire. ^james-01-14

15 Then desire when it has conceived gives birth to sin; and sin when it is full-grown brings forth death. ^james-01-15

16 Do not be deceived, my beloved brethren. ^james-01-16

17 Every good endowment and every perfect gift is from above, coming down from the Father of lights with whom there is no variation or shadow due to change.<sup>a</sup> ^james-01-17

18 Of his own will he brought us forth by the word of truth that we should be a kind of first fruits of his creatures. ^james-01-18

19 Know this, my beloved brethren. Let every man be quick to hear, slow to speak, slow to anger, ^james-01-19

20 for the anger of man does not work the righteousness of God. ^james-01-20

21 Therefore put away all filthiness and rank growth of wickedness and receive with meekness the implanted word, which is able to save your souls. ^james-01-21

22 But be doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving yourselves. \* ^james-01-22

23 For if any one is a hearer of the word and not a doer, he is like a man who observes his natural face in a mirror; ^james-01-23

24 for he observes himself and goes away and at once forgets what he was like. ^james-01-24

25 But he who looks into the perfect law, the law of liberty, and perseveres, being no hearer that forgets but a doer that acts, he shall be blessed in his doing. ^james-01-25

26 If any one thinks he is religious, and does not bridle his tongue but deceives his heart, this man's religion is vain. ^james-01-26

27 Religion that is pure and undefiled before God and the Father is this: to visit orphans and widows in their affliction, and to keep oneself unstained from the world. ^james-01-27


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:1) twelve tribes: i.e., Jewish Christians outside Palestine. ^james-01-dn-1

\* (1:22) This is the main theme of the letter. ^james-01-dn-2
