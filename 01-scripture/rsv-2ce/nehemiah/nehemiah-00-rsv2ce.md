## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* Some years after the arrival of Ezra (cf. Ezra 7), Nehemiah had himself appointed governor of Jerusalem by the Persian king and came to that city to build up its walls (445 B.C.). As before, the project was opposed by the surrounding peoples (cf. chapters 4 and 6), but this time the work was not interrupted. ^nehemiah-00-dn-1
