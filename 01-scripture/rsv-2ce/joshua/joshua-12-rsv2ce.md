# Joshua 12 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[joshua-12|NABRE]]

**The Kings Conquered by Moses**

1 Now these are the kings of the land, whom the sons of Israel defeated, and took possession of their land beyond the Jordan toward the sunrising, from the valley of the Arnon to Mount Hermon, with all the Ar'abah eastward: ^joshua-12-1

2 Si'hon king of the Am'orites who dwelt at Heshbon, and ruled from Aro'er, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and from the middle of the valley as far as the river Jabbok, the boundary of the Am'monites, that is, half of Gilead, ^joshua-12-2

3 and the Ar'abah to the Sea of Chin'neroth eastward, and in the direction of Beth-jesh'imoth, to the sea of the Arabah, the Salt Sea, southward to the foot of the slopes of Pisgah; ^joshua-12-3

4 and Og king of Bashan, one of the remnant of the Reph'aim, who dwelt at Ash'taroth and at Ed're-i ^joshua-12-4

5 and ruled over Mount Hermon and Sal'ecah and all Bashan to the boundary of the Gesh'urites and the Ma-ac'athites, and over half of Gilead to the boundary of Si'hon king of Heshbon. ^joshua-12-5

6 Moses, the servant of the LORD, and the sons of Israel defeated them; and Moses the servant of the LORD gave their land for a possession to the Reubenites and the Gadites and the half-tribe of Manas'seh. ^joshua-12-6

**The Kings Conquered by Joshua**

7 And these are the kings of the land whom Joshua and the sons of Israel defeated on the west side of the Jordan, from Ba'al-gad in the valley of Lebanon to Mount Ha'lak, that rises toward Se'ir (and Joshua gave their land to the tribes of Israel as a possession according to their allotments, ^joshua-12-7

8 in the hill country, in the lowland, in the Ar'abah, in the slopes, in the wilderness, and in the Neg'eb, the land of the Hittites, the Am'orites, the Canaanites, the Per'izzites, the Hi'vites, and the Jeb'usites): ^joshua-12-8

9 the king of Jericho, one; the king of Ai, which is beside Bethel, one; ^joshua-12-9

10 the king of Jerusalem, one; the king of He'bron, one; ^joshua-12-10

11 the king of Jarmuth, one; the king of La'chish, one; ^joshua-12-11

12 the king of Eg'lon, one; the king of Gezer, one; ^joshua-12-12

13 the king of De'bir, one; the king of Geder, one; ^joshua-12-13

14 the king of Hormah, one; the king of Ar'ad, one; ^joshua-12-14

15 the king of Libnah, one; the king of Adul'lam, one; ^joshua-12-15

16 the king of Makke'dah, one; the king of Bethel, one; ^joshua-12-16

17 the king of Tap'pu-ah, one; the king of He'pher, one; ^joshua-12-17

18 the king of A'phek, one; the king of Lashar'on, one; ^joshua-12-18

19 the king of Madon, one; the king of Ha'zor, one; ^joshua-12-19

20 the king of Shim'ron-me'ron, one; the king of Ach'shaph, one; ^joshua-12-20

21 the king of Ta'anach, one; the king of Megid'do, one; ^joshua-12-21

22 the king of Ke'desh, one; the king of Jok'ne-am in Carmel, one; ^joshua-12-22

23 the king of Dor in Na'phath-dor, one; the king of Goi'im in Galilee, one; ^joshua-12-23

24 the king of Tirzah, one: in all, thirty-one kings. ^joshua-12-24
