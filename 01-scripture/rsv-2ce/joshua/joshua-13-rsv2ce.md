# Joshua 13 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[joshua-13|NABRE]]

**Still Unconquered Parts of Canaan**

1 Now Joshua was old and advanced in years; and the LORD said to him, “You are old and advanced in years, and there remains yet very much land to be possessed. ^joshua-13-1

2 This is the land that yet remains: all the regions of the Philis'tines, and all those of the Gesh'urites ^joshua-13-2

3 (from the Shi'hor, which is east of Egypt, northward to the boundary of Ek'ron, it is reckoned as Canaanite; there are five rulers of the Philis'tines, those of Gaza, Ash'dod, Ash'kelon, Gath, and Ek'ron), and those of the Avvim, ^joshua-13-3

4 in the south, all the land of the Canaanites, and Me-ar'ah which belongs to the Sido'nians, to A'phek, to the boundary of the Am'orites, ^joshua-13-4

5 and the land of the Geb'alites, and all Lebanon, toward the sunrising, from Ba'al-gad below Mount Hermon to the entrance of Ha'math, ^joshua-13-5

6 all the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon to Mis'rephoth-ma'im, even all the Sido'nians. I will myself drive them out from before the sons of Israel; only allot the land to Israel for an inheritance, as I have commanded you. ^joshua-13-6

7 Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance to the nine tribes and half the tribe of Manas'seh.” ^joshua-13-7

8 With the other half of the tribe of Manas'seh the Reubenites and the Gadites received their inheritance, which Moses gave them, beyond the Jordan eastward, as Moses the servant of the LORD gave them: ^joshua-13-8

9 from Aro'er, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the valley, and all the tableland of Med'eba as far as Di'bon; ^joshua-13-9

10 and all the cities of Si'hon king of the Am'orites, who reigned in Heshbon, as far as the boundary of the Am'monites; ^joshua-13-10

11 and Gilead, and the region of the Gesh'urites and Ma-ac'athites, and all Mount Hermon, and all Bashan to Sal'ecah; ^joshua-13-11

12 all the kingdom of Og in Bashan, who reigned in Ash'taroth and in Ed're-i (he alone was left of the remnant of the Reph'aim); these Moses had defeated and driven out. ^joshua-13-12

13 Yet the sons of Israel did not drive out the Gesh'urites or the Ma-ac'athites; but Ge'shur and Ma'acath dwell in the midst of Israel to this day. ^joshua-13-13

14 To the tribe of Levi alone Moses gave no inheritance; the offerings by fire to the LORD God of Israel are their inheritance, as he said to him. ^joshua-13-14

**The Territory of Reuben**

15 And Moses gave an inheritance to the tribe of the Reubenites according to their families. ^joshua-13-15

16 So their territory was from Aro'er, which is on the edge of the valley of the Arnon, and the city that is in the middle of the valley, and all the tableland by Med'eba; ^joshua-13-16

17 with Heshbon, and all its cities that are in the tableland; Di'bon, and Ba'moth-ba'al, and Beth-ba'al-me'on, ^joshua-13-17

18 and Ja'haz, and Ked'emoth, and Meph'a-ath, ^joshua-13-18

19 and Kir''iatha'im, and Sibmah, and Ze'reth-sha'har on the hill of the valley, ^joshua-13-19

20 and Beth-pe'or, and the slopes of Pisgah, and Beth-jesh'imoth, ^joshua-13-20

21 that is, all the cities of the tableland, and all the kingdom of Si'hon king of the Am'orites, who reigned in Heshbon, whom Moses defeated with the leaders of Mid'ian, E'vi and Re'kem and Zur and Hur and Reba, the princes of Si'hon, who dwelt in the land. ^joshua-13-21

22 Balaam also, the son of Beor, the soothsayer, the sons of Israel killed with the sword among the rest of their slain. ^joshua-13-22

23 And the border of the people of Reuben was the Jordan as a boundary. This was the inheritance of the Reubenites, according to their families with their cities and villages. ^joshua-13-23

**The Territory of Gad**

24 And Moses gave an inheritance also to the tribe of the Gadites, according to their families. ^joshua-13-24

25 Their territory was Ja'zer, and all the cities of Gilead, and half the land of the Am'monites, to Aro'er, which is east of Rabbah, ^joshua-13-25

26 and from Heshbon to Ra'math-miz'peh and Bet'onim, and from Ma''hana'im to the territory of De'bir, ^joshua-13-26

27 and in the valley Beth-ha'ram, Beth-nim'rah, Succoth, and Za'phon, the rest of the kingdom of Si'hon king of Heshbon, having the Jordan as a boundary, to the lower end of the Sea of Chin'nereth, eastward beyond the Jordan. ^joshua-13-27

28 This is the inheritance of the Gadites according to their families, with their cities and villages. ^joshua-13-28

**The Territory of the Half-tribe of Manasseh (East)**

29 And Moses gave an inheritance to the half-tribe of Manas'seh; it was allotted to the half-tribe of the Manas'sites according to their families. ^joshua-13-29

30 Their region extended from Ma''hana'im, through all Bashan, the whole kingdom of Og king of Bashan, and all the towns of Ja'ir, which are in Bashan, sixty cities, ^joshua-13-30

31 and half Gilead, and Ash'taroth, and Ed're-i, the cities of the kingdom of Og in Bashan; these were allotted to the people of Ma'chir the son of Manas'seh for the half of the Ma'chirites according to their families. ^joshua-13-31

32 These are the inheritances which Moses distributed in the plains of Moab, beyond the Jordan east of Jericho. ^joshua-13-32

33 But to the tribe of Levi Moses gave no inheritance; the LORD God of Israel is their inheritance, as he said to them. ^joshua-13-33
