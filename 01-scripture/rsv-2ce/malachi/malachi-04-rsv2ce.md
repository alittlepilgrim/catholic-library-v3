# Malachi 4 (RSV-2CE)

**The Great Day of the Lord**

1 “For behold, the day comes, burning like an oven, when all the arrogant and all evildoers will be stubble; the day that comes shall burn them up, says the LORD of hosts, so that it will leave them neither root nor branch. ^malachi-04-1

2 But for you who fear my name the sun of righteousness shall rise, with healing in its wings. You shall go forth leaping like calves from the stall. ^malachi-04-2

3 And you shall tread down the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet, on the day when I act, says the LORD of hosts. ^malachi-04-3

4 “Remember the law of my servant Moses, the statutes and ordinances that I commanded him at Horeb for all Israel. ^malachi-04-4

5 “Behold, I will send you Eli'jah the prophet before the great and awesome day of the LORD comes. ^malachi-04-5

6 And he will turn the hearts of fathers to their children and the hearts of children to their fathers, lest I come and strike the land with a curse.” ^malachi-04-6
