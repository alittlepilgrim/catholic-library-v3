# Ecclesiastes 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ecclesiastes-01|NABRE]]

**All Is Vanity**

1 The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem. ^ecclesiastes-01-1

2 Vanity of vanities, says the Preacher, vanity of vanities! All is vanity. ^ecclesiastes-01-2

3 What does man gain by all the toil at which he toils under the sun? ^ecclesiastes-01-3

4 A generation goes, and a generation comes, but the earth remains for ever. ^ecclesiastes-01-4

5 The sun rises and the sun goes down, and hastens to the place where it rises. ^ecclesiastes-01-5

6 The wind blows to the south, and goes round to the north; round and round goes the wind, and on its circuits the wind returns. ^ecclesiastes-01-6

7 All streams run to the sea, but the sea is not full; to the place where the streams flow, there they flow again. ^ecclesiastes-01-7

8 All things are full of weariness; a man cannot utter it; the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing. ^ecclesiastes-01-8

9 What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done; and there is nothing new under the sun. ^ecclesiastes-01-9

10 Is there a thing of which it is said, “See, this is new”? It has been already, in the ages before us. ^ecclesiastes-01-10

11 There is no remembrance of former things, nor will there be any remembrance of later things yet to happen among those who come after. ^ecclesiastes-01-11

**Vanity in Seeking Wisdom**

12 I the Preacher have been king over Israel in Jerusalem. ^ecclesiastes-01-12

13 And I applied my mind to seek and to search out by wisdom all that is done under heaven; it is an unhappy business that God has given to the sons of men to be busy with. ^ecclesiastes-01-13

14 I have seen everything that is done under the sun; and behold, all is vanity and a striving after wind. ^ecclesiastes-01-14

15 What is crooked cannot be made straight, and what is lacking cannot be numbered. ^ecclesiastes-01-15

16 I said to myself, “I have acquired great wisdom, surpassing all who were over Jerusalem before me; and my mind has had great experience of wisdom and knowledge.” ^ecclesiastes-01-16

17 And I applied my mind to know wisdom and to know madness and folly. I perceived that this also is but a striving after wind. ^ecclesiastes-01-17

18 For in much wisdom is much vexation, and he who increases knowledge increases sorrow. ^ecclesiastes-01-18
