# Ecclesiastes 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ecclesiastes-10|NABRE]]

**Observations of Wisdom**

1 Dead flies make the perfumer’s ointment give off an evil odor; so a little folly outweighs wisdom and honor. ^ecclesiastes-10-1

2 A wise man’s heart inclines him toward the right, but a fool’s heart toward the left. ^ecclesiastes-10-2

3 Even when the fool walks on the road, he lacks sense, and he says to every one that he is a fool. ^ecclesiastes-10-3

4 If the anger of the ruler rises against you, do not leave your place, for deference will make amends for great offenses. ^ecclesiastes-10-4

5 There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, as it were an error proceeding from the ruler: ^ecclesiastes-10-5

6 folly is set in many high places, and the rich sit in a low place. ^ecclesiastes-10-6

7 I have seen slaves on horses, and princes walking on foot like slaves. ^ecclesiastes-10-7

8 He who digs a pit will fall into it; and a serpent will bite him who breaks through a wall. ^ecclesiastes-10-8

9 He who quarries stones is hurt by them; and he who splits logs is endangered by them. ^ecclesiastes-10-9

10 If the iron is blunt, and one does not whet the edge, he must put forth more strength; but wisdom helps one to succeed. ^ecclesiastes-10-10

11 If the serpent bites before it is charmed, there is no advantage in a charmer. ^ecclesiastes-10-11

12 The words of a wise man’s mouth win him favor, but the lips of a fool consume him. ^ecclesiastes-10-12

13 The beginning of the words of his mouth is foolishness, and the end of his talk is wicked madness. ^ecclesiastes-10-13

14 A fool multiplies words, though no man knows what is to be, and who can tell him what will be after him? ^ecclesiastes-10-14

15 The toil of a fool wearies him, so that he does not know the way to the city. ^ecclesiastes-10-15

16 Woe to you, O land, when your king is a child, and your princes feast in the morning! ^ecclesiastes-10-16

17 Happy are you, O land, when your king is the son of free men, and your princes feast at the proper time, for strength, and not for drunkenness! ^ecclesiastes-10-17

18 Through sloth the roof sinks in, and through indolence the house leaks. ^ecclesiastes-10-18

19 Bread is made for laughter, and wine gladdens life, and money answers everything. ^ecclesiastes-10-19

20 Even in your thought, do not curse the king, nor in your bedchamber curse the rich; for a bird of the air will carry your voice, or some winged creature tell the matter. ^ecclesiastes-10-20
