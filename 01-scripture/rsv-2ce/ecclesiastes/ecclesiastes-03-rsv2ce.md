# Ecclesiastes 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ecclesiastes-03|NABRE]]

**Everything Has a Season**

1 For everything there is a season, and a time for every matter under heaven: ^ecclesiastes-03-1

2 a time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pluck up what is planted; ^ecclesiastes-03-2

3 a time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to break down, and a time to build up; ^ecclesiastes-03-3

4 a time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance; ^ecclesiastes-03-4

5 a time to cast away stones, and a time to gather stones together; a time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing; ^ecclesiastes-03-5

6 a time to seek, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to cast away; ^ecclesiastes-03-6

7 a time to tear, and a time to sew; a time to keep silence, and a time to speak; ^ecclesiastes-03-7

8 a time to love, and a time to hate; a time for war, and a time for peace. ^ecclesiastes-03-8

9 What gain has the worker from his toil? ^ecclesiastes-03-9

**God-given Tasks**

10 I have seen the business that God has given to the sons of men to be busy with. ^ecclesiastes-03-10

11 He has made everything beautiful in its time; also he has put eternity into man’s mind, yet so that he cannot find out what God has done from the beginning to the end. ^ecclesiastes-03-11

12 I know that there is nothing better for them than to be happy and enjoy themselves as long as they live; ^ecclesiastes-03-12

13 also that it is God’s gift to man that every one should eat and drink and take pleasure in all his toil. ^ecclesiastes-03-13

14 I know that whatever God does endures for ever; nothing can be added to it, nor anything taken from it; God has made it so, in order that men should fear before him. ^ecclesiastes-03-14

15 That which is, already has been; that which is to be, already has been; and God seeks what has been driven away. ^ecclesiastes-03-15

**Judgment and the Future Belong to God**

16 Moreover I saw under the sun that in the place of justice, even there was wickedness, and in the place of righteousness, even there was wickedness. ^ecclesiastes-03-16

17 I said in my heart, God will judge the righteous and the wicked, for he has appointed a time for every matter, and for every work. ^ecclesiastes-03-17

18 I said in my heart with regard to the sons of men that God is testing them to show them that they are but beasts. ^ecclesiastes-03-18

19 For the fate of the sons of men and the fate of beasts is the same; as one dies, so dies the other. They all have the same breath, and man has no advantage over the beasts; for all is vanity. ^ecclesiastes-03-19

20 All go to one place; all are from the dust, and all turn to dust again. ^ecclesiastes-03-20

21 Who knows whether the spirit of man goes upward and the spirit of the beast goes down to the earth? ^ecclesiastes-03-21

22 So I saw that there is nothing better than that a man should enjoy his work, for that is his lot; who can bring him to see what will be after him? ^ecclesiastes-03-22
