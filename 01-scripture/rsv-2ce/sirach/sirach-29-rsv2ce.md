# Sirach 29 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-29|NABRE]]

**On Lending and Borrowing, Home, and Hospitality**

1 He that shows mercy will lend to his neighbor, and he that strengthens him with his hand keeps the commandments. ^sirach-29-1

2 Lend to your neighbor in the time of his need; and in turn, repay your neighbor promptly. ^sirach-29-2

3 Confirm your word and keep faith with him, and on every occasion you will find what you need. ^sirach-29-3

4 Many persons regard a loan as a windfall, and cause trouble to those who help them. ^sirach-29-4

5 A man will kiss another’s hands until he gets a loan, and will lower his voice in speaking of his neighbor’s money; but at the time for repayment he will delay, and will pay in words of unconcern, and will find fault with the time. ^sirach-29-5

6 If the lender exerts pressure, he will hardly get back half, and will regard that as a windfall. If he does not, the borrower has robbed him of his money, and he has needlessly made him his enemy; he will repay him with curses and reproaches, and instead of glory will repay him with dishonor. ^sirach-29-6

7 Because of such wickedness, therefore, many have refused to lend; they have been afraid of being defrauded needlessly. ^sirach-29-7

8 Nevertheless, be patient with a man in humble circumstances, and do not make him wait for your alms. ^sirach-29-8

9 Help a poor man for the commandment’s sake, and because of his need do not send him away empty. ^sirach-29-9

10 Lose your silver for the sake of a brother or a friend, and do not let it rust under a stone and be lost. ^sirach-29-10

11 Lay up your treasure according to the commandments of the Most High, and it will profit you more than gold. ^sirach-29-11

12 Store up almsgiving in your treasury, and it will rescue you from all affliction; ^sirach-29-12

13 more than a mighty shield and more than a heavy spear, it will fight on your behalf against your enemy. ^sirach-29-13

14 A good man will be surety for his neighbor, but a man who has lost his sense of shame will fail him. ^sirach-29-14

15 Do not forget all the kindness of your surety, for he has given his life for you. ^sirach-29-15

16 A sinner will overthrow the prosperity of his surety, ^sirach-29-16

17 and one who does not feel grateful will abandon his rescuer. ^sirach-29-17

18 Being surety has ruined many men who were prosperous, and has shaken them like a wave of the sea; it has driven men of power into exile, and they have wandered among foreign nations. ^sirach-29-18

19 The sinner who has fallen into suretyship and pursues gain will fall into lawsuits. ^sirach-29-19

20 Assist your neighbor according to your ability, but take heed to yourself lest you fall. ^sirach-29-20

21 The essentials for life are water and bread and clothing and a house to cover one’s nakedness. ^sirach-29-21

22 Better is the life of a poor man under the shelter of his roof than sumptuous food in another man’s house. ^sirach-29-22

23 Be content with little or much. ^sirach-29-23

24 It is a miserable life to go from house to house, and where you are a stranger you may not open your mouth; ^sirach-29-24

25 you will play the host and provide drink without being thanked, and besides this you will hear bitter words: ^sirach-29-25

26 “Come here, stranger, prepare the table, and if you have anything at hand, let me have it to eat.” ^sirach-29-26

27 “Give place, stranger, to an honored person; my brother has come to stay with me; I need my house.” ^sirach-29-27

28 These things are hard to bear for a man who has feeling: scolding about lodging and the reproach of the moneylender. ^sirach-29-28
