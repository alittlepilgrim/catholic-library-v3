# Sirach 11 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-11|NABRE]]

1 The wisdom of a humble man will lift up his head, and will seat him among the great. ^sirach-11-1

2 Do not praise a man for his good looks, nor loathe a man because of his appearance. ^sirach-11-2

3 The bee is small among flying creatures, but her product is the best of sweet things. ^sirach-11-3

4 Do not boast about wearing fine clothes, nor exalt yourself in the day that you are honored; for the works of the Lord are wonderful, and his works are concealed from men. ^sirach-11-4

5 Many kings have had to sit on the ground, but one who was never thought of has worn a crown. ^sirach-11-5

6 Many rulers have been greatly disgraced, and illustrious men have been handed over to others. ^sirach-11-6

7 Do not find fault before you investigate; first consider, and then reprove. ^sirach-11-7

8 Do not answer before you have heard, nor interrupt a speaker in the midst of his words. ^sirach-11-8

9 Do not argue about a matter which does not concern you, nor sit with sinners when they judge a case. ^sirach-11-9

10 My son, do not busy yourself with many matters; if you multiply activities you will not go unpunished, and if you pursue you will not overtake, and by fleeing you will not escape. ^sirach-11-10

11 There is a man who works, and toils, and presses on, but is so much the more in want. ^sirach-11-11

12 There is another who is slow and needs help, who lacks strength and abounds in poverty; but the eyes of the Lord look upon him for his good; he lifts him out of his low estate ^sirach-11-12

13 and raises up his head, so that many are amazed at him. ^sirach-11-13

14 Good things and bad, life and death, poverty and wealth, come from the Lord. ^sirach-11-14

17 The gift of the Lord endures for those who are godly, and what he approves will have lasting success. ^sirach-11-17

18 There is a man who is rich through his diligence and self-denial, and this is the reward allotted to him: ^sirach-11-18

19 when he says, “I have found rest, and now I shall enjoy my goods!” he does not know how much time will pass until he leaves them to others and dies. ^sirach-11-19

20 Stand by your covenant and attend to it, and grow old in your work. ^sirach-11-20

21 Do not wonder at the works of a sinner, but trust in the Lord and keep at your toil; for it is easy in the sight of the Lord to enrich a poor man quickly and suddenly. ^sirach-11-21

22 The blessing of the Lord is the reward of the godly, and quickly God causes his blessing to flourish. ^sirach-11-22

23 Do not say, “What do I need, and what prosperity could be mine in the future?” ^sirach-11-23

24 Do not say, “I have enough, and what calamity could happen to me in the future?” ^sirach-11-24

25 In the day of prosperity, adversity is forgotten, and in the day of adversity, prosperity is not remembered. ^sirach-11-25

26 For it is easy in the sight of the Lord to reward a man on the day of death according to his conduct. ^sirach-11-26

27 The misery of an hour makes one forget luxury, and at the close of a man’s life his deeds will be revealed. ^sirach-11-27

28 Call no one happy before his death; a man will be known through his children. ^sirach-11-28

29 Do not bring every man into your home, for many are the wiles of the crafty. ^sirach-11-29

30 Like a decoy partridge in a cage, so is the mind of a proud man, and like a spy he observes your weakness; ^sirach-11-30

31 for he lies in wait, turning good into evil, and to worthy actions he will attach blame. ^sirach-11-31

32 From a spark of fire come many burning coals, and a sinner lies in wait to shed blood. ^sirach-11-32

33 Beware of a scoundrel, for he devises evil, lest he give you a lasting blemish. ^sirach-11-33

34 Receive a stranger into your home and he will upset you with commotion, and will estrange you from your family. ^sirach-11-34
