# Sirach 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-02|NABRE]]

**Duties toward God**

1 My son, if you come forward to serve the Lord, remain in justice and in fear, and prepare yourself for temptation. ^sirach-02-1

2 Set your heart right and be steadfast, incline your ear, and receive words of understanding, and do not be hasty in time of calamity. ^sirach-02-2

3 Await God’s patience, cling to him and do not depart, that you may be wise in all your ways. ^sirach-02-3

4 Accept whatever is brought upon you, and endure it in sorrow; in changes that humble you be patient. ^sirach-02-4

5 For gold and silver are tested in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of humiliation. ^sirach-02-5

6 Trust in God, and he will help you; hope in him, and he will make your ways straight. Stay in fear of him, and grow old in him. ^sirach-02-6

7 You who fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and turn not aside, lest you fall. ^sirach-02-7

8 You who fear the Lord, trust in him, and your reward will not fail; ^sirach-02-8

9 you who fear the Lord, hope for good things, for everlasting joy and mercy. You who fear the Lord, love him, and your hearts will be made radiant. ^sirach-02-9

10 Consider the ancient generations and see: who ever trusted in the Lord and was put to shame? Or who ever persevered in his commandments and was forsaken? Or who ever called upon him and was overlooked? ^sirach-02-10

11 For the Lord is compassionate and merciful; he forgives sins and saves in time of affliction, and he is the shield of all who seek him in truth. ^sirach-02-11

12 Woe to timid hearts and to slack hands, and to the sinner who walks along two ways! ^sirach-02-12

13 Woe to the faint heart, for it has no trust! Therefore it will not be sheltered. ^sirach-02-13

14 Woe to you who have lost your endurance! What will you do when the Lord punishes you? ^sirach-02-14

15 Those who fear the Lord will not disobey his words, and those who love him will keep his ways. ^sirach-02-15

16 Those who fear the Lord will seek his approval, and those who love him will be filled with the law. ^sirach-02-16

17 Those who fear the Lord will prepare their hearts, and will humble themselves before him. ^sirach-02-17

18 Let us fall into the hands of the Lord, but not into the hands of men; for as his majesty is, so also is his mercy. ^sirach-02-18
