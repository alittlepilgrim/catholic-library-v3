# Sirach 26 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-26|NABRE]]

1 Happy is the husband of a good wife; the number of his days will be doubled. ^sirach-26-1

2 A loyal wife rejoices her husband, and he will complete his years in peace. ^sirach-26-2

3 A good wife is a great blessing; she will be granted among the blessings of the man who fears the Lord. ^sirach-26-3

4 Whether rich or poor, his heart is glad, and at all times his face is cheerful. ^sirach-26-4

5 Of three things my heart is afraid, and of a fourth I am frightened: The slander of a city, the gathering of a mob, and false accusation—all these are worse than death. ^sirach-26-5

6 There is grief of heart and sorrow when a wife is envious of a rival, and a tongue-lashing makes it known to all. ^sirach-26-6

7 An evil wife is an ox yoke which chafes; taking hold of her is like grasping a scorpion. ^sirach-26-7

8 There is great anger when a wife is drunken; she will not hide her shame. ^sirach-26-8

9 A wife’s harlotry shows in her lustful eyes, and she is known by her eyelids. ^sirach-26-9

10 Keep strict watch over a headstrong daughter, lest, when she finds liberty, she use it to her hurt. ^sirach-26-10

11 Be on guard against her impudent eye, and do not wonder if she sins against you. ^sirach-26-11

12 As a thirsty wayfarer opens his mouth and drinks from any water near him, so will she sit in front of every post and open her quiver to the arrow. ^sirach-26-12

13 A wife’s charm delights her husband, and her skill puts fat on his bones. ^sirach-26-13

14 A sensible and silent wife is a gift of the Lord, and there is nothing so precious as a disciplined soul. ^sirach-26-14

15 A modest wife adds charm to charm, and no balance can weigh the value of a chaste soul. ^sirach-26-15

16 Like the sun rising in the heights of the Lord, so is the beauty of a good wife in her well-ordered home. ^sirach-26-16

17 Like the shining lamp on the holy lampstand, so is a beautiful face on a stately figure. ^sirach-26-17

18 Like pillars of gold on a base of silver, so are beautiful feet with a steadfast heart. ^sirach-26-18

28 At two things my heart is grieved, and because of a third anger comes over me: a warrior in want through poverty, and intelligent men who are treated contemptuously; a man who turns back from righteousness to sin— the Lord will prepare him for the sword! ^sirach-26-28

29 A merchant can hardly keep from wrongdoing, and a tradesman will not be declared innocent of sin. ^sirach-26-29
