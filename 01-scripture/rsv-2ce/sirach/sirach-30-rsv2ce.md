# Sirach 30 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-30|NABRE]]

**Discipline of Children, Right Attitudes**

1 He who loves his son will whip him often, in order that he may rejoice at the way he turns out. ^sirach-30-1

2 He who disciplines his son will profit by him, and will boast of him among acquaintances. ^sirach-30-2

3 He who teaches his son will make his enemies envious, and will glory in him in the presence of friends. ^sirach-30-3

4 The father may die, and yet he is not dead, for he has left behind him one like himself; ^sirach-30-4

5 while alive he saw and rejoiced, and when he died he was not grieved; ^sirach-30-5

6 he has left behind him an avenger against his enemies, and one to repay the kindness of his friends. ^sirach-30-6

7 He who spoils his son will bind up his wounds, and his feelings will be troubled at every cry. ^sirach-30-7

8 A horse that is untamed turns out to be stubborn, and a son unrestrained turns out to be wilful. ^sirach-30-8

9 Pamper a child, and he will frighten you; play with him, and he will give you grief. ^sirach-30-9

10 Do not laugh with him, lest you have sorrow with him, and in the end you will gnash your teeth. ^sirach-30-10

11 Give him no authority in his youth, and do not ignore his errors. ^sirach-30-11

12 Bow down his neck in his youth, and beat his sides while he is young, lest he become stubborn and disobey you, and you have sorrow of soul from him. ^sirach-30-12

13 Discipline your son and take pains with him, that you may not be offended by his shamelessness. ^sirach-30-13

14 Better off is a poor man who is well and strong in constitution than a rich man who is severely afflicted in body. ^sirach-30-14

15 Health and soundness are better than all gold, and a robust body than countless riches. ^sirach-30-15

16 There is no wealth better than health of body, and there is no gladness above joy of heart. ^sirach-30-16

17 Death is better than a miserable life, and eternal rest than chronic sickness. ^sirach-30-17

18 Good things poured out upon a mouth that is closed are like offerings of food placed upon a grave. ^sirach-30-18

19 Of what use to an idol is an offering of fruit? For it can neither eat nor smell. So is he who is afflicted by the Lord; ^sirach-30-19

20 he sees with his eyes and groans, like a eunuch who embraces a maiden and groans. ^sirach-30-20

21 Do not give yourself over to sorrow, and do not afflict yourself deliberately. ^sirach-30-21

22 Gladness of heart is the life of man, and the rejoicing of a man is length of days. ^sirach-30-22

23 Delight your soul and comfort your heart, and remove sorrow far from you, for sorrow has destroyed many, and there is no profit in it. ^sirach-30-23

24 Jealousy and anger shorten life, and anxiety brings on old age too soon. ^sirach-30-24

25 A man of cheerful and good heart will give heed to the food he eats. ^sirach-30-25
