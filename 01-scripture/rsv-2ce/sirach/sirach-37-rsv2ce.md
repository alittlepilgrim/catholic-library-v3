# Sirach 37 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-37|NABRE]]

**Concerning Good Council, Reason, and Moderation**

1 Every friend will say, “I too am a friend”; but some friends are friends only in name. ^sirach-37-1

2 Is it not a grief to the death when a companion and friend turns to enmity? ^sirach-37-2

3 O evil imagination, why were you formed to cover the land with deceit? ^sirach-37-3

4 Some companions rejoice in the happiness of a friend, but in time of trouble are against him. ^sirach-37-4

5 Some companions help a friend for their stomachs’ sake, and in the face of battle take up the shield. ^sirach-37-5

6 Do not forget a friend in your heart, and be not unmindful of him in your wealth. ^sirach-37-6

7 Every counselor praises counsel, but some give counsel in their own interest. ^sirach-37-7

8 Be wary of a counselor, and learn first what is his interest— for he will take thought for himself— lest he cast the lot against you ^sirach-37-8

9 and tell you, “Your way is good,” and then stand aloof to see what will happen to you. ^sirach-37-9

10 Do not consult with one who looks at you suspiciously; hide your counsel from those who are jealous of you. ^sirach-37-10

11 Do not consult with a woman about her rival or with a coward about war, with a merchant about barter or with a buyer about selling, with a grudging man about gratitude or with a merciless man about kindness, with an idler about any work or with a man hired for a year about completing his work, with a lazy servant about a big task— pay no attention to these in any matter of counsel. ^sirach-37-11

12 But stay constantly with a godly man whom you know to be a keeper of the commandments, whose soul is in accord with your soul, and who will sorrow with you if you fail. ^sirach-37-12

13 And establish the counsel of your own heart, for no one is more faithful to you than it is. ^sirach-37-13

14 For a man’s soul sometimes keeps him better informed than seven watchmen sitting high on a watchtower. ^sirach-37-14

15 And besides all this pray to the Most High that he may direct your way in truth. ^sirach-37-15

16 Reason is the beginning of every work, and counsel precedes every undertaking. ^sirach-37-16

17 As a clue to changes of heart ^sirach-37-17

18 four turns of fortune appear, good and evil, life and death; and it is the tongue that continually rules them. ^sirach-37-18

19 A man may be shrewd and the teacher of many, and yet be unprofitable to himself. ^sirach-37-19

20 A man skilled in words may be hated; he will be destitute of all food, ^sirach-37-20

21 for grace was not given him by the Lord, since he is lacking in all wisdom. ^sirach-37-21

22 A man may be wise to his own advantage, and the fruits of his understanding may be trustworthy on his lips. ^sirach-37-22

23 A wise man will instruct his own people, and the fruits of his understanding will be trustworthy. ^sirach-37-23

24 A wise man will have praise heaped upon him, and all who see him will call him happy. ^sirach-37-24

25 The life of a man is numbered by days, but the days of Israel are without number. ^sirach-37-25

26 He who is wise among his people will inherit confidence, and his name will live for ever. ^sirach-37-26

27 My son, test your soul while you live; see what is bad for it and do not give it that. ^sirach-37-27

28 For not everything is good for every one, and not every person enjoys everything. ^sirach-37-28

29 Do not have an insatiable appetite for any luxury, and do not give yourself up to food; ^sirach-37-29

30 for overeating brings sickness, and gluttony leads to nausea. ^sirach-37-30

31 Many have died of gluttony, but he who is careful to avoid it prolongs his life. ^sirach-37-31
