# Sirach 49 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-49|NABRE]]

1 The memory of Josi'ah is like a blending of incense prepared by the art of the perfumer; it is sweet as honey to every mouth, and like music at a banquet of wine. ^sirach-49-1

2 He was led aright in converting the people, and took away the abominations of iniquity. ^sirach-49-2

3 He set his heart upon the Lord; in the days of wicked men he strengthened godliness. ^sirach-49-3

4 Except David and Hezeki'ah and Josi'ah they all sinned greatly, for they forsook the law of the Most High; the kings of Judah came to an end; ^sirach-49-4

5 for they gave their power to others, and their glory to a foreign nation, ^sirach-49-5

6 who set fire to the chosen city of the sanctuary, and made her streets desolate, according to the word of Jeremi'ah. ^sirach-49-6

7 For they had afflicted him; yet he had been consecrated in the womb as prophet, to pluck up and afflict and destroy, and likewise to build and to plant. ^sirach-49-7

8 It was Ezek'iel who saw the vision of glory which God showed him above the chariot of the cherubim. ^sirach-49-8

9 For God remembered his enemies with storm, and did good to those who directed their ways rightly. ^sirach-49-9

10 May the bones of the twelve prophets revive from where they lie, for they comforted the people of Jacob and delivered them with confident hope. ^sirach-49-10

11 How shall we magnify Zerub'babel? He was like a signet on the right hand, ^sirach-49-11

12 and so was Jesh'ua the son of Jo'zadak; in their days they built the house and raised a temple holy to the Lord, prepared for everlasting glory. ^sirach-49-12

13 The memory of Nehemi'ah also is lasting; he raised for us the walls that had fallen, and set up the gates and bars and rebuilt our ruined houses. ^sirach-49-13

14 No one like E'noch has been created on earth, for he was taken up from the earth. ^sirach-49-14

15 And no man like Joseph has been born, and his bones are cared for. ^sirach-49-15

16 Shem and Seth were honored among men, and Adam above every living being in the creation. ^sirach-49-16
