# Sirach 19 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-19|NABRE]]

**True Wisdom Contrasted to Cleverness and Evil**

1 A workman who is a drunkard will not become rich; he who despises small things will fail little by little. ^sirach-19-1

2 Wine and women lead intelligent men astray, and the man who consorts with harlots is very reckless. ^sirach-19-2

3 Decay and worms will inherit him, and the reckless soul will be snatched away. ^sirach-19-3

4 One who trusts others too quickly is lightminded, and one who sins does wrong to himself. ^sirach-19-4

5 One who rejoices in wickedness will be condemned, ^sirach-19-5

6 and for one who hates gossip evil is lessened. ^sirach-19-6

7 Never repeat a conversation, and you will lose nothing at all. ^sirach-19-7

8 With friend or foe do not report it, and unless it would be a sin for you, do not disclose it; ^sirach-19-8

9 for some one has heard you and watched you, and when the time comes he will hate you. ^sirach-19-9

10 Have you heard a word? Let it die with you. Be brave! It will not make you burst! ^sirach-19-10

11 With such a word a fool will suffer pangs like a woman in labor with a child. ^sirach-19-11

12 Like an arrow stuck in the flesh of the thigh, so is a word inside a fool. ^sirach-19-12

13 Question a friend, perhaps he did not do it; but if he did anything, so that he may do it no more. ^sirach-19-13

14 Question a neighbor, perhaps he did not say it; but if he said it, so that he may not say it again. ^sirach-19-14

15 Question a friend, for often it is slander; so do not believe everything you hear. ^sirach-19-15

16 A person may make a slip without intending it. Who has never sinned with his tongue? ^sirach-19-16

17 Question your neighbor before you threaten him; and let the law of the Most High take its course. ^sirach-19-17

20 All wisdom is the fear of the Lord, and in all wisdom there is the fulfilment of the law. ^sirach-19-20

22 But the knowledge of wickedness is not wisdom, nor is there prudence where sinners take counsel. ^sirach-19-22

23 There is a cleverness which is abominable, but there is a fool who merely lacks wisdom. ^sirach-19-23

24 Better is the God-fearing man who lacks intelligence, than the highly prudent man who transgresses the law. ^sirach-19-24

25 There is a cleverness which is scrupulous but unjust, and there are people who distort kindness to gain a verdict. ^sirach-19-25

26 There is a rascal bowed down in mourning, but inwardly he is full of deceit. ^sirach-19-26

27 He hides his face and pretends not to hear; but where no one notices, he will forestall you. ^sirach-19-27

28 And if by lack of strength he is prevented from sinning, he will do evil when he finds an opportunity. ^sirach-19-28

29 A man is known by his appearance, and a sensible man is known by his face, when you meet him. ^sirach-19-29

30 A man’s attire and open-mouthed laughter, and a man’s manner of walking, show what he is. ^sirach-19-30
