# Sirach 15 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-15|NABRE]]

**Freedom of Choice and Its Consequences**

1 The man who fears the Lord will do this, and he who holds to the law will obtain wisdom. ^sirach-15-1

2 She will come to meet him like a mother, and like the wife of his youth she will welcome him. ^sirach-15-2

3 She will feed him with the bread of understanding, and give him the water of wisdom to drink. ^sirach-15-3

4 He will lean on her and will not fall, and he will rely on her and will not be put to shame. ^sirach-15-4

5 She will exalt him above his neighbors, and will open his mouth in the midst of the assembly; she will fill him with a spirit of wisdom and understanding, and clothe him with a robe of glory. ^sirach-15-5

6 He will find gladness and a crown of rejoicing, and will acquire an everlasting name. ^sirach-15-6

7 Foolish men will not obtain her, and sinful men will not see her. ^sirach-15-7

8 She is far from men of pride, and liars will never think of her. ^sirach-15-8

9 A hymn of praise is not fitting on the lips of a sinner, for it has not been sent from the Lord. ^sirach-15-9

10 For a hymn of praise should be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it. ^sirach-15-10

11 Do not say, “Because of the Lord I left the right way”; for he will not do what he hates. ^sirach-15-11

12 Do not say, “It was he who led me astray”; for he has no need of a sinful man. ^sirach-15-12

13 The Lord hates all abominations, and they are not loved by those who fear him. ^sirach-15-13

14 It was he who created man in the beginning, and he left him in the power of his own inclination. ^sirach-15-14

15 If you will, you can keep the commandments, they will save you; if you trust in God, you too shall live. ^sirach-15-15

16 He has placed before you fire and water: stretch out your hand for whichever you wish. ^sirach-15-16

17 Before a man are life and death, good and evil, and whichever he chooses will be given to him. ^sirach-15-17

18 For great is the wisdom of the Lord; he is mighty in power and sees everything. ^sirach-15-18

19 The eyes of the Lord are on those who fear him, and he knows every deed of man. ^sirach-15-19

20 He has not commanded any one to be ungodly, and he has not given any one permission to sin. ^sirach-15-20
