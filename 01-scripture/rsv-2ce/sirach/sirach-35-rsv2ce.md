# Sirach 35 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-35|NABRE]]

**The Law and Sacrifice—Divine Justice**

1 He who keeps the law makes many offerings; he who heeds the commandments sacrifices a peace offering. ^sirach-35-1

2 He who returns a kindness offers fine flour, and he who gives alms sacrifices a thank offering. ^sirach-35-2

3 To keep from wickedness is pleasing to the Lord, and to forsake unrighteousness is atonement. ^sirach-35-3

4 Do not appear before the Lord empty-handed, ^sirach-35-4

5 for all these things are to be done because of the commandment. ^sirach-35-5

6 The offering of a righteous man anoints the altar, and its pleasing odor rises before the Most High. ^sirach-35-6

7 The sacrifice of a righteous man is acceptable, and the memory of it will not be forgotten. ^sirach-35-7

8 Glorify the Lord generously, and do not stint the first fruits of your hands. ^sirach-35-8

9 With every gift show a cheerful face, and dedicate your tithe with gladness. ^sirach-35-9

10 Give to the Most High as he has given, and as generously as your hand has found. ^sirach-35-10

11 For the Lord is the one who repays, and he will repay you sevenfold. ^sirach-35-11

12 Do not offer him a bribe, for he will not accept it; and do not trust to an unrighteous sacrifice; for the Lord is the judge, and with him is no partiality. ^sirach-35-12

13 He will not show partiality in the case of a poor man; and he will listen to the prayer of one who is wronged. ^sirach-35-13

14 He will not ignore the supplication of the fatherless, nor the widow when she pours out her story. ^sirach-35-14

15 Do not the tears of the widow run down her cheek as she cries out against him who has caused them to fall? ^sirach-35-15

16 He whose service is pleasing to the Lord will be accepted, and his prayer will reach to the clouds. ^sirach-35-16

17 The prayer of the humble pierces the clouds, and he will not be consoled until it reaches the Lord; he will not desist until the Most High visits him, and the just judge executes judgment. ^sirach-35-17

18 And the Lord will not delay, neither will he be patient with them, till he crushes the loins of the unmerciful and repays vengeance on the nations; till he takes away the multitude of the insolent, and breaks the scepters of the unrighteous; ^sirach-35-18

19 till he repays man according to his deeds, and the works of men according to their devices; till he judges the case of his people and makes them rejoice in his mercy. ^sirach-35-19

20 Mercy is as welcome when he afflicts them as clouds of rain in the time of drought. ^sirach-35-20
