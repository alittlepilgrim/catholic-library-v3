# Sirach 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-05|NABRE]]

1 Do not set your heart on your wealth, nor say, “I have enough.” ^sirach-05-1

2 Do not follow your inclination and strength, walking according to the desires of your heart. ^sirach-05-2

3 Do not say, “Who will have power over me?” or “Who will bring me down because of my deeds?” for God will surely punish you. ^sirach-05-3

4 Do not say, “I sinned, and what happened to me?” for the Most High is slow to anger. ^sirach-05-4

5 Do not be so confident of atonement that you add sin to sin. ^sirach-05-5

6 Do not say, “His mercy is great, he will forgive the multitude of my sins,” for both mercy and wrath are with him, and his anger rests on sinners. ^sirach-05-6

7 Do not delay to turn to the Lord, nor postpone it from day to day; for suddenly the wrath of the Lord will go forth, and at the time of punishment you will perish. ^sirach-05-7

8 Do not depend on dishonest wealth, for it will not benefit you in the day of calamity. ^sirach-05-8

9 Do not winnow with every wind, nor follow every path: the double-tongued sinner does that. ^sirach-05-9

10 Be steadfast in your understanding, and let your speech be consistent. ^sirach-05-10

11 Be quick to hear, and be deliberate in answering. ^sirach-05-11

12 If you have understanding, answer your neighbor; but if not, put your hand on your mouth. ^sirach-05-12

13 Glory and dishonor come from speaking, and a man’s tongue is his downfall. ^sirach-05-13

14 Do not be called a slanderer, and do not lie in ambush with your tongue; for shame comes to the thief, and severe condemnation to the double-tongued. ^sirach-05-14

15 In great or small matters do not act amiss, and do not become an enemy instead of a friend; ^sirach-05-15
