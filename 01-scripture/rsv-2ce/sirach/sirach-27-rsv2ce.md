# Sirach 27 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-27|NABRE]]

1 Many have committed sin for a trifle, and whoever seeks to get rich will avert his eyes. ^sirach-27-1

2 As a stake is driven firmly into a fissure between stones, so sin is wedged in between selling and buying. ^sirach-27-2

3 If a man is not steadfast and zealous in the fear of the Lord, his house will be quickly overthrown. ^sirach-27-3

4 When a sieve is shaken, the refuse remains; so a man’s filth remains in his thoughts. ^sirach-27-4

5 The kiln tests the potter’s vessels; so the test of just men is in tribulation. ^sirach-27-5

6 The fruit discloses the cultivation of a tree; so the expression of a thought discloses the cultivation of a man’s mind. ^sirach-27-6

7 Do not praise a man before you hear him speak, for this is the test of men. ^sirach-27-7

8 If you pursue justice, you will attain it and wear it as a glorious robe. ^sirach-27-8

9 Birds flock with their kind; so truth returns to those who practice it. ^sirach-27-9

10 A lion lies in wait for prey; so does sin for the workers of iniquity. ^sirach-27-10

11 The talk of the godly man is always wise, but the fool changes like the moon. ^sirach-27-11

12 Among stupid people watch for a chance to leave, but among thoughtful people stay on. ^sirach-27-12

13 The talk of fools is offensive, and their laughter is wantonly sinful. ^sirach-27-13

14 The talk of men given to swearing makes one’s hair stand on end, and their quarrels make a man stop his ears. ^sirach-27-14

15 The strife of the proud leads to bloodshed, and their abuse is grievous to hear. ^sirach-27-15

16 Whoever betrays secrets destroys confidence, and he will never find a congenial friend. ^sirach-27-16

17 Love your friend and keep faith with him; but if you betray his secrets, do not run after him. ^sirach-27-17

18 For as a man destroys his enemy, so you have destroyed the friendship of your neighbor. ^sirach-27-18

19 And as you allow a bird to escape from your hand, so you have let your neighbor go, and will not catch him again. ^sirach-27-19

20 Do not go after him, for he is too far off, and has escaped like a gazelle from a snare. ^sirach-27-20

21 For a wound may be bandaged, and there is reconciliation after abuse, but whoever has betrayed secrets is without hope. ^sirach-27-21

22 Whoever winks his eye plans evil deeds, and no one can keep him from them. ^sirach-27-22

23 In your presence his mouth is all sweetness, and he admires your words; but later he will twist his speech and with your own words he will give offense. ^sirach-27-23

24 I have hated many things, but none to be compared to him; even the Lord will hate him. ^sirach-27-24

25 Whoever throws a stone straight up throws it on his own head; and a treacherous blow opens up wounds. ^sirach-27-25

26 He who digs a pit will fall into it, and he who sets a snare will be caught in it. ^sirach-27-26

27 If a man does evil, it will roll back upon him, and he will not know where it came from. ^sirach-27-27

28 Mockery and abuse issue from the proud man, but vengeance lies in wait for him like a lion. ^sirach-27-28

29 Those who rejoice in the fall of the godly will be caught in a snare, and pain will consume them before their death. ^sirach-27-29

30 Anger and wrath, these also are abominations, and the sinful man will possess them. ^sirach-27-30
