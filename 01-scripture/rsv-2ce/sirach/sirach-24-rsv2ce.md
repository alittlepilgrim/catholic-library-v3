# Sirach 24 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-24|NABRE]]

**The Praise of Wisdom**

1 Wisdom will praise herself and is honored in God, and will glory in the midst of her people. ^sirach-24-1

2 In the assembly of the Most High she will open her mouth, and in the presence of his host she will glory. In the midst of her people she is exalted; in holy fulness she is admired. In the multitude of the chosen she finds praise, and among the blessed she is blessed, saying: ^sirach-24-2

3 “I came forth from the mouth of the Most High, the first-born before all creatures. I ordained that an unfailing light should arise in the heavens, and I covered the earth like a mist. ^sirach-24-3

4 I dwelt in high places, and my throne was in a pillar of cloud. ^sirach-24-4

5 Alone I have made the circuit of the vault of heaven and have walked in the depths of the abyss. ^sirach-24-5

6 In the waves of the sea, in the whole earth, and in every people and nation I have gotten a possession. ^sirach-24-6

7 Among all these I sought a resting place; I sought in whose territory I might lodge. ^sirach-24-7

8 “Then the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and the one who created me assigned a place for my tent. And he said, ‘Make your dwelling in Jacob, and in Israel receive your inheritance, and among my chosen put down your roots.’ ^sirach-24-8

9 From eternity, in the beginning, he created me, and for eternity I shall not cease to exist. ^sirach-24-9

10 In the holy tabernacle I ministered before him, and so I was established in Zion. ^sirach-24-10

11 In the beloved city likewise he gave me a resting place, and in Jerusalem was my dominion. ^sirach-24-11

12 So I took root in an honored people, in the portion of the Lord, who is their inheritance, and my abode was in the full assembly of the saints. ^sirach-24-12

13 “I grew tall like a cedar in Lebanon, and like a cypress on the heights of Hermon. ^sirach-24-13

14 I grew tall like a palm tree in En-ge'di, and like rose plants in Jericho; like a beautiful olive tree in the field, and like a plane tree I grew tall. ^sirach-24-14

15 Like cassia and camel’s thorn I gave forth the aroma of spices, and like choice myrrh I spread a pleasant odor, like galbanum, onycha, and stacte, and like the fragrance of frankincense in the tabernacle. ^sirach-24-15

16 Like a terebinth I spread out my branches, and my branches are glorious and graceful. ^sirach-24-16

17 Like a vine I caused loveliness to bud, and my blossoms became glorious and abundant fruit. ^sirach-24-17

19 “Come to me, you who desire me, and eat your fill of my produce. ^sirach-24-19

20 For my teaching is sweeter than honey, and my inheritance sweeter than the honeycomb, and my remembrance lasts throughout all generations. ^sirach-24-20

21 Those who eat me will hunger for more, and those who drink me will thirst for more. ^sirach-24-21

22 Whoever obeys me will not be put to shame, and those who work with my help will not sin.” ^sirach-24-22

23 All this is the book of the covenant of the Most High God, the law which Moses commanded us as an inheritance for the congregations of Jacob. ^sirach-24-23

25 It fills men with wisdom, like the Pi'shon, and like the Tigris at the time of the first fruits. ^sirach-24-25

26 It makes them full of understanding, like the Euphrates, and like the Jordan at harvest time. ^sirach-24-26

27 It makes instruction shine forth like light, like the Gi'hon at the time of vintage. ^sirach-24-27

28 Just as the first man did not know her perfectly, the last one has not fathomed her; ^sirach-24-28

29 for her thought is more abundant than the sea, and her counsel deeper than the great abyss. ^sirach-24-29

30 I went forth like a canal from a river and like a water channel into a garden. ^sirach-24-30

31 I said, “I will water my orchard and drench my garden plot”; and behold, my canal became a river, and my river became a sea. ^sirach-24-31

32 I will again make instruction shine forth like the dawn, and I will make it shine afar; ^sirach-24-32

33 I will again pour out teaching like prophecy, and leave it to all future generations. ^sirach-24-33

34 Observe that I have not labored for myself alone, but for all who seek instruction. ^sirach-24-34
