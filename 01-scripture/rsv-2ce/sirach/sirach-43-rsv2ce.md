# Sirach 43 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-43|NABRE]]

**God’s Greatness in Creation**

1 The pride of the heavenly heights is the clear firmament, the appearance of heaven in a spectacle of glory. ^sirach-43-1

2 The sun, when it appears, making proclamation as it goes forth, is a marvelous instrument, the work of the Most High. ^sirach-43-2

3 At noon it parches the land; and who can withstand its burning heat? ^sirach-43-3

4 A man tending a furnace works in burning heat, but the sun burns the mountains three times as much; it breathes out fiery vapors, and with bright beams it blinds the eyes. ^sirach-43-4

5 Great is the Lord who made it; and at his command it hastens on its course. ^sirach-43-5

6 He made the moon also, to serve in its season to mark the times and to be an everlasting sign. ^sirach-43-6

7 From the moon comes the sign for feast days, a light that wanes when it has reached the full. ^sirach-43-7

8 The month is named for the moon, increasing marvelously in its phases, an instrument of the hosts on high shining forth in the firmament of heaven. ^sirach-43-8

9 The glory of the stars is the beauty of heaven, a gleaming array in the heights of the Lord. ^sirach-43-9

10 At the command of the Holy One they stand as ordered, they never relax in their watches. ^sirach-43-10

11 Look upon the rainbow, and praise him who made it, exceedingly beautiful in its brightness. ^sirach-43-11

12 It encircles the heaven with its glorious arc; the hands of the Most High have stretched it out. ^sirach-43-12

13 By his command he sends the driving snow and speeds the lightning of his judgment. ^sirach-43-13

14 Therefore the storehouses are opened, and the clouds fly forth like birds. ^sirach-43-14

15 In his majesty he amasses the clouds, and the hailstones are broken in pieces. ^sirach-43-15

16 At his appearing the mountains are shaken; at his will the south wind blows. ^sirach-43-16

17 The voice of his thunder rebukes the earth; so do the tempest from the north and the whirlwind. He scatters the snow like birds flying down, and its descent is like locusts alighting. ^sirach-43-17

18 The eye marvels at the beauty of its whiteness, and the mind is amazed at its falling. ^sirach-43-18

19 He pours the hoarfrost upon the earth like salt, and when it freezes, it becomes pointed thorns. ^sirach-43-19

20 The cold north wind blows, and ice freezes over the water; it rests upon every pool of water, and the water puts it on like a breastplate. ^sirach-43-20

21 He consumes the mountains and burns up the wilderness, and withers the tender grass like fire. ^sirach-43-21

22 A mist quickly heals all things; when the dew appears, it refreshes from the heat. ^sirach-43-22

23 By his counsel he stilled the great deep and planted islands in it. ^sirach-43-23

24 Those who sail the sea tell of its dangers, and we marvel at what we hear. ^sirach-43-24

25 For in it are strange and marvelous works, all kinds of living things, and huge creatures of the sea. ^sirach-43-25

26 Because of him his messenger finds the way, and by his word all things hold together. ^sirach-43-26

27 Though we speak much we cannot reach the end, and the sum of our words is: “He is the all.” ^sirach-43-27

28 Where shall we find strength to praise him? For he is greater than all his works. ^sirach-43-28

29 Terrible is the Lord and very great, and marvelous is his power. ^sirach-43-29

30 When you praise the Lord, exalt him as much as you can; for he will surpass even that. When you exalt him, put forth all your strength, and do not grow weary, for you cannot praise him enough. ^sirach-43-30

31 Who has seen him and can describe him? Or who can extol him as he is? ^sirach-43-31

32 Many things greater than these lie hidden, for we have seen but few of his works. ^sirach-43-32

33 For the Lord has made all things, and to the godly he has granted wisdom. ^sirach-43-33
