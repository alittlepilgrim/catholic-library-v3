# Sirach 21 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-21|NABRE]]

**Various Sins and Foolishness**

1 Have you sinned, my son? Do so no more, but pray about your former sins. ^sirach-21-1

2 Flee from sin as from a snake; for if you approach sin, it will bite you. Its teeth are lion’s teeth, and destroy the souls of men. ^sirach-21-2

3 All lawlessness is like a two-edged sword; there is no healing for its wound. ^sirach-21-3

4 Terror and violence will lay waste riches; thus the house of the proud will be laid waste. ^sirach-21-4

5 The prayer of a poor man goes from his lips to the ears of God, and his judgment comes speedily. ^sirach-21-5

6 Whoever hates reproof walks in the steps of the sinner, but he that fears the Lord will repent in his heart. ^sirach-21-6

7 He who is mighty in speech is known from afar; but the sensible man, when he slips, is aware of it. ^sirach-21-7

8 A man who builds his house with other people’s money is like one who gathers stones for his burial mound. ^sirach-21-8

9 An assembly of the wicked is like tow gathered together, and their end is a flame of fire. ^sirach-21-9

10 The way of sinners is smoothly paved with stones, but at its end is the pit of Hades. ^sirach-21-10

11 Whoever keeps the law controls his thoughts, and wisdom is the fulfilment of the fear of the Lord. ^sirach-21-11

12 He who is not clever cannot be taught, but there is a cleverness which increases bitterness. ^sirach-21-12

13 The knowledge of a wise man will increase like a flood, and his counsel like a flowing spring. ^sirach-21-13

14 The mind of a fool is like a broken jar; it will hold no knowledge. ^sirach-21-14

15 When a man of understanding hears a wise saying, he will praise it and add to it; when a reveler hears it, he dislikes it and casts it behind his back. ^sirach-21-15

16 A fool’s narration is like a burden on a journey, but delight will be found in the speech of the intelligent. ^sirach-21-16

17 The utterance of a sensible man will be sought in the assembly, and they will ponder his words in their minds. ^sirach-21-17

18 Like a house that has vanished, so is wisdom to a fool; and the knowledge of the ignorant is unexamined talk. ^sirach-21-18

19 To a senseless man education is chains on his feet, and like manacles on his right hand. ^sirach-21-19

20 A fool raises his voice when he laughs, but a clever man smiles quietly. ^sirach-21-20

21 To a sensible man education is like a golden ornament, and like a bracelet on the right arm. ^sirach-21-21

22 The foot of a fool rushes into a house, but a man of experience stands respectfully before it. ^sirach-21-22

23 A boor peers into the house from the door, but a cultivated man remains outside. ^sirach-21-23

24 It is ill-mannered for a man to listen at a door, and a discreet man is grieved by the disgrace. ^sirach-21-24

25 The lips of strangers will speak of these things, but the words of the prudent will be weighed in the balance. ^sirach-21-25

26 The mind of fools is in their mouth, but the mouth of wise men is in their mind. ^sirach-21-26

27 When an ungodly man curses his adversary, he curses his own soul. ^sirach-21-27

28 A whisperer defiles his own soul and is hated in his neighborhood. ^sirach-21-28
