# Sirach 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-08|NABRE]]

**Prudence**

1 Do not contend with a powerful man, lest you fall into his hands. ^sirach-08-1

2 Do not quarrel with a rich man, lest his resources outweigh yours; for gold has ruined many, and has perverted the minds of kings. ^sirach-08-2

3 Do not argue with a chatterer, nor heap wood on his fire. ^sirach-08-3

4 Do not jest with an ill-bred person, lest your ancestors be disgraced. ^sirach-08-4

5 Do not reproach a man who is turning away from sin; remember that we all deserve punishment. ^sirach-08-5

6 Do not disdain a man when he is old, for some of us are growing old. ^sirach-08-6

7 Do not rejoice over any one’s death; remember that we all must die. ^sirach-08-7

8 Do not slight the discourse of the sages, but busy yourself with their maxims; because from them you will gain instruction and learn how to serve great men. ^sirach-08-8

9 Do not disregard the discourse of the aged, for they themselves learned from their fathers; because from them you will gain understanding and learn how to give an answer in time of need. ^sirach-08-9

10 Do not kindle the coals of a sinner, lest you be burned in his flaming fire. ^sirach-08-10

11 Do not get up and leave an insolent fellow, lest he lie in ambush against your words. ^sirach-08-11

12 Do not lend to a man who is stronger than you; but if you do lend anything, be as one who has lost it. ^sirach-08-12

13 Do not give surety beyond your means, but if you give surety, be concerned as one who must pay. ^sirach-08-13

14 Do not go to law against a judge, for the decision will favor him because of his standing. ^sirach-08-14

15 Do not travel on the road with a foolhardy fellow, lest he be burdensome to you; for he will act as he pleases, and through his folly you will perish with him. ^sirach-08-15

16 Do not fight with a wrathful man, and do not cross the wilderness with him; because blood is as nothing in his sight, and where no help is at hand, he will strike you down. ^sirach-08-16

17 Do not consult with a fool, for he will not be able to keep a secret. ^sirach-08-17

18 In the presence of a stranger do nothing that is to be kept secret, for you do not know what he will divulge. ^sirach-08-18

19 Do not reveal your thoughts to every one, lest you drive away your good luck. ^sirach-08-19
