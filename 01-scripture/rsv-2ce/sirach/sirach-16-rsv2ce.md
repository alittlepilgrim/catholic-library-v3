# Sirach 16 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-16|NABRE]]

1 Do not desire a multitude of useless children, nor rejoice in ungodly sons. ^sirach-16-1

2 If they multiply, do not rejoice in them, unless the fear of the Lord is in them. ^sirach-16-2

3 Do not trust in their survival, and do not rely on their multitude; for one is better than a thousand, and to die childless is better than to have ungodly children. ^sirach-16-3

4 For through one man of understanding a city will be filled with people, but through a tribe of lawless men it will be made desolate. ^sirach-16-4

5 Many such things my eye has seen, and my ear has heard things more striking than these. ^sirach-16-5

6 In an assembly of sinners a fire will be kindled, and in a disobedient nation wrath was kindled. ^sirach-16-6

7 He was not propitiated for the ancient giants who revolted in their might. ^sirach-16-7

8 He did not spare the neighbors of Lot, whom he loathed on account of their insolence. ^sirach-16-8

9 He showed no pity for a nation devoted to destruction, for those destroyed in their sins; ^sirach-16-9

10 nor for the six hundred thousand men on foot, who rebelliously assembled in their stubbornness. ^sirach-16-10

11 Even if there is only one stiffnecked person, it will be a wonder if he remains unpunished. For mercy and wrath are with the Lord; he is mighty to forgive, and he pours out wrath. ^sirach-16-11

12 As great as his mercy, so great is also his reproof; he judges a man according to his deeds. ^sirach-16-12

13 The sinner will not escape with his plunder, and the patience of the godly will not be frustrated. ^sirach-16-13

14 He will make room for every act of mercy; every one will receive in accordance with his deeds. ^sirach-16-14

17 Do not say, “I shall be hidden from the Lord, and who from on high will remember me? Among so many people I shall not be known, for what is my soul in the boundless creation? ^sirach-16-17

18 Behold, heaven and the highest heaven, the abyss and the earth, will tremble at his visitation. ^sirach-16-18

19 The mountains also and the foundations of the earth shake with trembling when he looks upon them. ^sirach-16-19

20 And no mind will reflect on this. Who will ponder his ways? ^sirach-16-20

21 Like a tempest which no man can see, so most of his works are concealed. ^sirach-16-21

22 Who will announce his acts of justice? Or who will await them? For the covenant is far off.” ^sirach-16-22

23 This is what one devoid of understanding thinks; a senseless and misguided man thinks foolishly. ^sirach-16-23

24 Listen to me, my son, and acquire knowledge, and pay close attention to my words. ^sirach-16-24

25 I will impart instruction by weight, and declare knowledge accurately. ^sirach-16-25

26 The works of the Lord have existed from the beginning by his creation, and when he made them, he determined their divisions. ^sirach-16-26

27 He arranged his works in an eternal order, and their dominion for all generations; they neither hunger nor grow weary, and they do not cease from their labors. ^sirach-16-27

28 They do not crowd one another aside, and they will never disobey his word. ^sirach-16-28

29 After this the Lord looked upon the earth, and filled it with his good things; ^sirach-16-29

30 with all kinds of living beings he covered its surface, and to it they return. ^sirach-16-30
