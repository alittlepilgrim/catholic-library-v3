# Sirach 34 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-34|NABRE]]

**Fear of the Lord, Sacrifices, Justice, and Prayer**

1 A man of no understanding has vain and false hopes, and dreams give wings to fools. ^sirach-34-1

2 As one who catches at a shadow and pursues the wind, so is he who gives heed to dreams. ^sirach-34-2

3 The vision of dreams is this against that, the likeness of a face confronting a face. ^sirach-34-3

4 From an unclean thing what will be made clean? And from something false what will be true? ^sirach-34-4

5 Divinations and omens and dreams are folly, and like a woman with labor pains the mind has fancies. ^sirach-34-5

6 Unless they are sent from the Most High as a visitation, do not give your mind to them. ^sirach-34-6

7 For dreams have deceived many, and those who put their hope in them have failed. ^sirach-34-7

8 Without such deceptions the law will be fulfilled, and wisdom is made perfect in truthful lips. ^sirach-34-8

9 An educated man knows many things, and one with much experience will speak with understanding. ^sirach-34-9

10 He that is inexperienced knows few things, but he that has traveled acquires much cleverness. ^sirach-34-10

11 I have seen many things in my travels, and I understand more than I can express. ^sirach-34-11

12 I have often been in danger of death, but have escaped because of these experiences. ^sirach-34-12

13 The spirit of those who fear the Lord will live, for their hope is in him who saves them. ^sirach-34-13

14 He who fears the Lord will not be timid, nor play the coward, for he is his hope. ^sirach-34-14

15 Blessed is the soul of the man who fears the Lord! To whom does he look? And who is his support? ^sirach-34-15

16 The eyes of the Lord are upon those who love him, a mighty protection and strong support, a shelter from the hot wind and a shade from noonday sun, a guard against stumbling and a defense against falling. ^sirach-34-16

17 He lifts up the soul and gives light to the eyes; he grants healing, life, and blessing. ^sirach-34-17

18 If one sacrifices from what has been wrongfully obtained, the offering is blemished; the gifts of the lawless are not acceptable. ^sirach-34-18

19 The Most High is not pleased with the offerings of the ungodly; and he is not propitiated for sins by a multitude of sacrifices. ^sirach-34-19

20 Like one who kills a son before his father’s eyes is the man who offers a sacrifice from the property of the poor. ^sirach-34-20

21 The bread of the needy is the life of the poor; whoever deprives them of it is a man of blood. ^sirach-34-21

22 To take away a neighbor’s living is to murder him; to deprive an employee of his wages is to shed blood. ^sirach-34-22

23 When one builds and another tears down, what do they gain but toil? ^sirach-34-23

24 When one prays and another curses, to whose voice will the Lord listen? ^sirach-34-24

25 If a man washes after touching a dead body, and touches it again, what has he gained by his washing? ^sirach-34-25

26 So if a man fasts for his sins, and goes again and does the same things, who will listen to his prayer? And what has he gained by humbling himself? ^sirach-34-26
