# Sirach 33 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-33|NABRE]]

**Practical Advice**

1 No evil will befall the man who fears the Lord, but in trial he will deliver him again and again. ^sirach-33-1

2 A wise man will not hate the law, but he who is hypocritical about it is like a boat in a storm. ^sirach-33-2

3 A man of understanding will trust in the law; for him the law is as dependable as an inquiry by means of Urim. ^sirach-33-3

4 Prepare what to say, and thus you will be heard; bind together your instruction, and make your answer. ^sirach-33-4

5 The heart of a fool is like a cart wheel, and his thoughts like a turning axle. ^sirach-33-5

6 A stallion is like a mocking friend; he neighs under every one who sits on him. ^sirach-33-6

7 Why is any day better than another, when all the daylight in the year is from the sun? ^sirach-33-7

8 By the Lord’s decision they were distinguished, and he appointed the different seasons and feasts; ^sirach-33-8

9 some of them he exalted and hallowed, and some of them he made ordinary days. ^sirach-33-9

10 All men are from the ground, and Adam was created of the dust. ^sirach-33-10

11 In the fulness of his knowledge the Lord distinguished them and appointed their different ways; ^sirach-33-11

12 some of them he blessed and exalted, and some of them he made holy and brought near to himself; but some of them he cursed and brought low, and he turned them out of their place. ^sirach-33-12

13 As clay in the hand of the potter— for all his ways are as he pleases— so men are in the hand of him who made them, to give them as he decides. ^sirach-33-13

14 Good is the opposite of evil, and life the opposite of death; so the sinner is the opposite of the godly. ^sirach-33-14

15 Look upon all the works of the Most High; they likewise are in pairs, one the opposite of the other. ^sirach-33-15

16 I was the last on watch; I was like one who gleans after the grape-gatherers; by the blessing of the Lord I excelled, and like a grape-gatherer I filled my wine press. ^sirach-33-16

17 Consider that I have not labored for myself alone, but for all who seek instruction. ^sirach-33-17

18 Hear me, you who are great among the people, and you leaders of the congregation, listen. ^sirach-33-18

19 To son or wife, to brother or friend, do not give power over yourself, as long as you live; and do not give your property to another, lest you change your mind and must ask for it. ^sirach-33-19

20 While you are still alive and have breath in you, do not let any one take your place. ^sirach-33-20

21 For it is better that your children should ask from you than that you should look to the hand of your sons. ^sirach-33-21

22 Excel in all that you do; bring no stain upon your honor. ^sirach-33-22

23 At the time when you end the days of your life, in the hour of death, distribute your inheritance. ^sirach-33-23

24 Fodder and a stick and burdens for a donkey; bread and discipline and work for a servant. ^sirach-33-24

25 Set your slave to work, and you will find rest; leave his hands idle, and he will seek liberty. ^sirach-33-25

26 Yoke and thong will bow the neck, and for a wicked servant there are racks and tortures. ^sirach-33-26

27 Put him to work, that he may not be idle, for idleness teaches much evil. ^sirach-33-27

28 Set him to work, as is fitting for him, and if he does not obey, make his chains heavy. ^sirach-33-28

29 Do not act immoderately toward anybody, and do nothing without discretion. ^sirach-33-29

30 If you have a servant, let him be as yourself, because you have bought him with blood. ^sirach-33-30

31 If you have a servant, treat him as a brother, for as your own soul you will need him. If you ill-treat him, and he leaves and runs away, which way will you go to seek him? ^sirach-33-31
