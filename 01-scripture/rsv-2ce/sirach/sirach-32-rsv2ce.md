# Sirach 32 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-32|NABRE]]

1 If they make you master of the feast, do not exalt yourself; be among them as one of them; take good care of them and then be seated; ^sirach-32-1

2 when you have fulfilled your duties, take your place, that you may be merry on their account and receive a wreath for your excellent leadership. ^sirach-32-2

3 Speak, you who are older, for it is fitting that you should, but with accurate knowledge, and do not interrupt the music. ^sirach-32-3

4 Where there is entertainment, do not pour out talk; do not display your cleverness out of season. ^sirach-32-4

5 A ruby seal in a setting of gold is a concert of music at a banquet of wine. ^sirach-32-5

6 A seal of emerald in a rich setting of gold is the melody of music with good wine. ^sirach-32-6

7 Speak, young man, if there is need of you, but no more than twice, and only if asked. ^sirach-32-7

8 Speak concisely, say much in few words; be as one who knows and yet holds his tongue. ^sirach-32-8

9 Among the great do not act as their equal; and when another is speaking, do not babble. ^sirach-32-9

10 Lightning speeds before the thunder, and approval precedes a modest man. ^sirach-32-10

11 Leave in good time and do not be the last; go home quickly and do not linger. ^sirach-32-11

12 Amuse yourself there, and do what you have in mind, but do not sin through proud speech. ^sirach-32-12

13 And for these things bless him who made you and satisfies you with his good gifts. ^sirach-32-13

14 He who fears the Lord will accept his discipline, and those who rise early to seek him will find favor. ^sirach-32-14

15 He who seeks the law will be filled with it, but the hypocrite will stumble at it. ^sirach-32-15

16 Those who fear the Lord will form true judgments, and like a light they will kindle righteous deeds. ^sirach-32-16

17 A sinful man will shun reproof, and will find a decision according to his liking. ^sirach-32-17

18 A man of judgment will not overlook an idea, and an insolent and proud man will not cower in fear. ^sirach-32-18

19 Do nothing without deliberation; and when you have acted, do not regret it. ^sirach-32-19

20 Do not go on a path full of hazards, and do not stumble over stony ground. ^sirach-32-20

21 Do not be overconfident on a smooth way, ^sirach-32-21

22 and give good heed to your paths. ^sirach-32-22

23 Guard yourself in every act, for this is the keeping of the commandments. ^sirach-32-23

24 He who believes the law gives heed to the commandments, and he who trusts the Lord will not suffer loss. ^sirach-32-24
