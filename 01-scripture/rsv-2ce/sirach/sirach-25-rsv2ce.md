# Sirach 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-25|NABRE]]

**The Good and the Evil in Daily Life**

1 My soul takes pleasure in three things, and they are beautiful in the sight of the Lord and of men: agreement between brothers, friendship between neighbors, and a wife and husband who live in harmony. ^sirach-25-1

2 My soul hates three kinds of men, and I am greatly offended at their life: a beggar who is proud, a rich man who is a liar, and an adulterous old man who lacks good sense. ^sirach-25-2

3 You have gathered nothing in your youth; how then can you find anything in your old age? ^sirach-25-3

4 What an attractive thing is judgment in gray-haired men, and for the aged to possess good counsel! ^sirach-25-4

5 How attractive is wisdom in the aged, and understanding and counsel in honorable men! ^sirach-25-5

6 Rich experience is the crown of the aged, and their boast is the fear of the Lord. ^sirach-25-6

7 With nine thoughts I have gladdened my heart, and a tenth I shall tell with my tongue: a man rejoicing in his children; a man who lives to see the downfall of his foes; ^sirach-25-7

8 happy is he who lives with an intelligent wife, and he who has not made a slip with his tongue, and he who has not served a man inferior to himself; ^sirach-25-8

9 happy is he who has gained good sense, and he who speaks to attentive listeners. ^sirach-25-9

10 How great is he who has gained wisdom! But there is no one superior to him who fears the Lord. ^sirach-25-10

11 The fear of the Lord surpasses everything; to whom shall be likened the one who holds it fast? ^sirach-25-11

13 Any wound, but not a wound of the heart! Any wickedness, but not the wickedness of a wife! ^sirach-25-13

14 Any attack, but not an attack from those who hate! And any vengeance, but not the vengeance of enemies! ^sirach-25-14

15 There is no venom worse than a snake’s venom, and no wrath worse than an enemy’s wrath. ^sirach-25-15

16 I would rather dwell with a lion and a dragon than dwell with an evil wife. ^sirach-25-16

17 The wickedness of a wife changes her appearance, and darkens her face like that of a bear. ^sirach-25-17

18 Her husband takes his meals among the neighbors, and he cannot help sighing bitterly. ^sirach-25-18

19 Any iniquity is insignificant compared to a wife’s iniquity; may a sinner’s lot befall her! ^sirach-25-19

20 A sandy ascent for the feet of the aged— such is a garrulous wife for a quiet husband. ^sirach-25-20

21 Do not be ensnared by a woman’s beauty, and do not desire a woman for her possessions. ^sirach-25-21

22 There is wrath and impudence and great disgrace when a wife supports her husband. ^sirach-25-22

23 A dejected mind, a gloomy face, and a wounded heart are caused by an evil wife. Drooping hands and weak knees are caused by the wife who does not make her husband happy. ^sirach-25-23

24 From a woman sin had its beginning, and because of her we all die. ^sirach-25-24

25 Allow no outlet to water, and no boldness of speech in an evil wife. ^sirach-25-25

26 If she does not go as you direct, separate her from yourself. ^sirach-25-26
