# Sirach 14 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[sirach-14|NABRE]]

1 Blessed is the man who does not blunder with his lips and need not suffer grief for sin. ^sirach-14-1

2 Blessed is he whose heart does not condemn him, and who has not given up his hope. ^sirach-14-2

3 Riches are not seemly for a stingy man; and of what use is property to an envious man? ^sirach-14-3

4 Whoever accumulates by depriving himself, accumulates for others; and others will live in luxury on his goods. ^sirach-14-4

5 If a man is mean to himself, to whom will he be generous? He will not enjoy his own riches. ^sirach-14-5

6 No one is meaner than the man who is grudging to himself, and this is the retribution for his baseness; ^sirach-14-6

7 even if he does good, he does it unintentionally, and betrays his baseness in the end. ^sirach-14-7

8 Evil is the man with a grudging eye; he averts his face and disregards people. ^sirach-14-8

9 A greedy man’s eye is not satisfied with a portion, and mean injustice withers the soul. ^sirach-14-9

10 A stingy man’s eye begrudges bread, and it is lacking at his table. ^sirach-14-10

11 My son, treat yourself well, according to your means, and present worthy offerings to the Lord. ^sirach-14-11

12 Remember that death will not delay, and the decree of Hades has not been shown to you. ^sirach-14-12

13 Do good to a friend before you die, and reach out and give to him as much as you can. ^sirach-14-13

14 Do not deprive yourself of a happy day; let not your share of desired good pass by you. ^sirach-14-14

15 Will you not leave the fruit of your labors to another, and what you acquired by toil to be divided by lot? ^sirach-14-15

16 Give, and take, and beguile yourself, because in Hades one cannot look for luxury. ^sirach-14-16

17 All living beings become old like a garment, for the decree from of old is, “You must surely die!” ^sirach-14-17

18 Like flourishing leaves on a spreading tree which sheds some and puts forth others, so are the generations of flesh and blood: one dies and another is born. ^sirach-14-18

19 Every product decays and ceases to exist, and the man who made it will pass away with it. ^sirach-14-19

20 Blessed is the man who meditates on wisdom and who reasons intelligently. ^sirach-14-20

21 He who reflects in his mind on her ways will also ponder her secrets. ^sirach-14-21

22 Pursue wisdom like a hunter, and lie in wait on her paths. ^sirach-14-22

23 He who peers through her windows will also listen at her doors; ^sirach-14-23

24 he who encamps near her house will also fasten his tent peg to her walls; ^sirach-14-24

25 he will pitch his tent near her, and will lodge in an excellent lodging place; ^sirach-14-25

26 he will place his children under her shelter, and will camp under her boughs; ^sirach-14-26

27 he will be sheltered by her from the heat, and will dwell in the midst of her glory. ^sirach-14-27
