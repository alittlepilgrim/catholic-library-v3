# John 14 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Jesus the Way, the Truth, and the Life**

1 "Let not your hearts be troubled; believe<sup>e</sup> in God, believe also in me. ^john-14-1

2 In my Father's house are many rooms; if it were not so, would I have told you that I go to prepare a place for you? ^john-14-2

3 And when I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and will take you to myself, that where I am you may be also. ^john-14-3

4 And you know the way where I am going."<sup>f</sup> ^john-14-4

5 Thomas said to him, "Lord, we do not know where you are going; how can we know the way?" ^john-14-5

6 Jesus said to him, "I am the way, and the truth, and the life; no one comes to the Father, but by me. ^john-14-6

7 If you had known me, you would have known my Father also; henceforth you know him and have seen him." ^john-14-7

8 Philip said to him, "Lord, show us the Father, and we shall be satisfied." ^john-14-8

9 Jesus said to him, "Have I been with you so long, and yet you do not know me, Philip? He who has seen me has seen the Father; how can you say, 'Show us the Father'? ^john-14-9

10 Do you not believe that I am in the Father and the Father is in me? The words that I say to you I do not speak on my own authority; but the Father who dwells in me does his works. ^john-14-10

11 Believe me that I am in the Father and the Father is in me; or else believe me for the sake of the works themselves. ^john-14-11

12 "Truly, truly, I say to you, he who believes in me will also do the works that I do; and greater works than these will he do, because I go to the Father. ^john-14-12

13 Whatever you ask in my name, I will do it, that the Father may be glorified in the Son; ^john-14-13

14 if you ask<sup>g</sup> anything in my name, I will do it. ^john-14-14

**The Promise of the Holy Spirit**

15 "If you love me, you will keep my commandments. ^john-14-15

16 And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another Counselor, to be with you for ever, ^john-14-16

17 even the Spirit of truth, whom the world cannot receive, because it neither sees him nor knows him; you know him, for he dwells with you, and will be in you. ^john-14-17

18 "I will not leave you desolate; I will come to you. ^john-14-18

19 Yet a little while, and the world will see me no more, but you will see me; because I live, you will live also. ^john-14-19

20 In that day you will know that I am in my Father, and you in me, and I in you. ^john-14-20

21 He who has my commandments and keeps them, he it is who loves me; and he who loves me will be loved by my Father, and I will love him and manifest myself to him." ^john-14-21

22 Judas (not Iscariot) said to him, "Lord, how is it that you will manifest yourself to us, and not to the world?" ^john-14-22

23 Jesus answered him, "If a man loves me, he will keep my word, and my Father will love him, and we will come to him and make our home with him. ^john-14-23

24 He who does not love me does not keep my words; and the word which you hear is not mine but the Father's who sent me. ^john-14-24

25 "These things I have spoken to you, while I am still with you. ^john-14-25

26 But the Counselor, the Holy Spirit, whom the Father will send in my name, he will teach you all things, \* and bring to your remembrance all that I have said to you. ^john-14-26

27 Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your hearts be troubled, neither let them be afraid. ^john-14-27

28 You heard me say to you, 'I go away, and I will come to you.' If you loved me, you would have rejoiced, because I go to the Father; for the Father is greater than I. ^john-14-28

29 And now I have told you before it takes place, so that when it does take place, you may believe. ^john-14-29

30 I will no longer talk much with you, for the ruler of this world is coming. He has no power over me; ^john-14-30

31 but I do as the Father has commanded me, so that the world may know that I love the Father. Rise, let us go from here. ^john-14-31


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (14:26) all things: After Jesus has gone to his Father, the Holy Spirit will complete his revelation to the world. ^john-14-dn-1
