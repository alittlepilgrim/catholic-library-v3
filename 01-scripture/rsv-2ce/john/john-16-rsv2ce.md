# John 16 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 "I have said all this to you to keep you from falling away. ^john-16-1

2 They will put you out of the synagogues; indeed, the hour is coming when whoever kills you will think he is offering service to God. ^john-16-2

3 And they will do this because they have not known the Father, nor me. ^john-16-3

4 But I have said these things to you, that when their hour comes you may remember that I told you of them. ^john-16-4

**The Work of the Spirit**

"I did not say these things to you from the beginning, because I was with you. ^john-16-4b

5 But now I am going to him who sent me; yet none of you asks me, 'Where are you going?' ^john-16-5

6 But because I have said these things to you, sorrow has filled your hearts. ^john-16-6

7 Nevertheless I tell you the truth: it is to your advantage that I go away, for if I do not go away, the Counselor will not come to you; but if I go, I will send him to you. ^john-16-7

8 And when he comes, he will convince the world of sin and of righteousness and of judgment: ^john-16-8

9 of sin, because they do not believe in me; ^john-16-9

10 of righteousness, because I go to the Father, and you will see me no more; \* ^john-16-10

11 of judgment, because the ruler of this world is judged. ^john-16-11

12 "I have yet many things to say to you, but you cannot bear them now. ^john-16-12

13 When the Spirit of truth comes, he will guide you into all the truth; for he will not speak on his own authority, but whatever he hears he will speak, and he will declare to you the things that are to come. ^john-16-13

14 He will glorify me, for he will take what is mine and declare it to you. ^john-16-14

15 All that the Father has is mine; therefore I said that he will take what is mine and declare it to you. ^john-16-15

**Sorrow Will Turn into Joy**

16 "A little while, and you will see me no more; again a little while, and you will see me." ^john-16-16

17 Some of his disciples said to one another, "What is this that he says to us, 'A little while, and you will not see me, and again a little while, and you will see me'; and, 'because I go to the Father'?" ^john-16-17

18 They said, "What does he mean by 'a little while'? We do not know what he means." ^john-16-18

19 Jesus knew that they wanted to ask him; so he said to them, "Is this what you are asking yourselves, what I meant by saying, 'A little while, and you will not see me, and again a little while, and you will see me'? ^john-16-19

20 Truly, truly, I say to you, you will weep and lament, but the world will rejoice; you will be sorrowful, but your sorrow will turn into joy. ^john-16-20

21 When a woman is in labor, she has pain, because her hour has come; but when she is delivered of the child, she no longer remembers the anguish, for joy that a child<sup>j</sup> is born into the world. ^john-16-21

22 So you have sorrow now, but I will see you again and your hearts will rejoice, and no one will take your joy from you. ^john-16-22

23 In that day you will ask nothing of me. Truly, truly, I say to you, if you ask anything of the Father, he will give it to you in my name. ^john-16-23

24 Until now you have asked nothing in my name; ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be full. ^john-16-24

**Peace for the Disciples**

25 "I have said this to you in figures; the hour is coming when I shall no longer speak to you in figures but tell you plainly of the Father. ^john-16-25

26 In that day you will ask in my name; and I do not say to you that I shall ask the Father for you; ^john-16-26

27 for the Father himself loves you, because you have loved me and have believed that I came from the Father. ^john-16-27

28 I came from the Father and have come into the world; again, I am leaving the world and going to the Father." ^john-16-28

29 His disciples said, "Ah, now you are speaking plainly, not in any figure! ^john-16-29

30 Now we know that you know all things, and need none to question you; by this we believe that you came from God." ^john-16-30

31 Jesus answered them, "Do you now believe? ^john-16-31

32 The hour is coming, indeed it has come, when you will be scattered, every man to his home, and will leave me alone; yet I am not alone, for the Father is with me. ^john-16-32

33 I have said this to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you have tribulation; but be of good cheer, I have overcome the world." ^john-16-33

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (16:10) Jesus is taken from them because they did not receive him. ^john-16-dn-1
