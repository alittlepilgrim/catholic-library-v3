# John 10 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Jesus the Good Shepherd**

1 "Truly, truly, I say to you, he who does not enter the sheepfold by the door but climbs in by another way, that man is a thief and a robber; ^john-10-1

2 but he who enters by the door is the shepherd of the sheep. ^john-10-2

3 To him the gatekeeper opens; the sheep hear his voice, and he calls his own sheep by name and leads them out. ^john-10-3

4 When he has brought out all his own, he goes before them, and the sheep follow him, for they know his voice. ^john-10-4

5 A stranger they will not follow, but they will flee from him, for they do not know the voice of strangers." ^john-10-5

6 This figure Jesus used with them, but they did not understand what he was saying to them. ^john-10-6

7 So Jesus again said to them, "Truly, truly, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep. ^john-10-7

8 All who came before me are thieves and robbers; but the sheep did not heed them. ^john-10-8

9 I am the door; if any one enters by me, he will be saved, and will go in and out and find pasture. ^john-10-9

10 The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy; I came that they may have life, and have it abundantly. ^john-10-10

11 I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd lays down his life for the sheep. ^john-10-11

12 He who is a hireling and not a shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, sees the wolf coming and leaves the sheep and flees; and the wolf snatches them and scatters them. ^john-10-12

13 He flees because he is a hireling and cares nothing for the sheep. ^john-10-13

14 I am the good shepherd; \* I know my own and my own know me, ^john-10-14

15 as the Father knows me and I know the Father; and I lay down my life for the sheep. ^john-10-15

16 And I have other sheep, that are not of this fold; I must bring them also, and they will heed my voice. So there shall be one flock, one shepherd. ^john-10-16

17 For this reason the Father loves me, because I lay down my life, that I may take it again. ^john-10-17

18 No one takes it from me, but I lay it down of my own accord. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again; this charge I have received from my Father." \* ^john-10-18

19 There was again a division among the Jews because of these words. ^john-10-19

20 Many of them said, "He has a demon, and he is mad; why listen to him?" ^john-10-20

21 Others said, "These are not the sayings of one who has a demon. Can a demon open the eyes of the blind?" ^john-10-21

**Jesus Is Rejected by the Jews**

22 It was the feast of the Dedication at Jerusalem; ^john-10-22

23 it was winter, and Jesus was walking in the temple, in the portico of Solomon. ^john-10-23

24 So the Jews gathered round him and said to him, "How long will you keep us in suspense? If you are the Christ, tell us plainly." ^john-10-24

25 Jesus answered them, "I told you, and you do not believe. The works that I do in my Father's name, they bear witness to me; ^john-10-25

26 but you do not believe, because you do not belong to my sheep. ^john-10-26

27 My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me; ^john-10-27

28 and I give them eternal life, and they shall never perish, and no one shall snatch them out of my hand. ^john-10-28

29 My Father, who has given them to me,<sup>w</sup> is greater than all, and no one is able to snatch them out of the Father's hand. ^john-10-29

30 I and the Father are one." ^john-10-30

31 The Jews took up stones again to stone him. ^john-10-31

32 Jesus answered them, "I have shown you many good works from the Father; for which of these do you stone me?" ^john-10-32

33 The Jews answered him, "We stone you for no good work but for blasphemy; because you, being a man, make yourself God." ^john-10-33

34 Jesus answered them, "Is it not written in your law, 'I said, you are gods'? ^john-10-34

35 If he called them gods to whom the word of God came (and Scripture cannot be nullified), ^john-10-35

36 do you say of him whom the Father consecrated and sent into the world, 'You are blaspheming,' because I said, 'I am the Son of God'? ^john-10-36

37 If I am not doing the works of my Father, then do not believe me; ^john-10-37

38 but if I do them, even though you do not believe me, believe the works, that you may know and understand that the Father is in me and I am in the Father." ^john-10-38

39 Again they tried to arrest him, but he escaped from their hands. ^john-10-39

40 He went away again across the Jordan to the place where John at first baptized, and there he remained. ^john-10-40

41 And many came to him; and they said, "John did no sign, but everything that John said about this man was true." ^john-10-41

42 And many believed in him there. ^john-10-42

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (10:14) the good shepherd: The name has Messianic significance; cf. Ezek 34. ^john-10-dn-1

\* (10:18) Throughout the Gospel, Jesus insists that he is master of his own life and no one takes it from him; cf. 18:6 (at his arrest); 19:11 (before Pilate); 19:30 (on the cross). ^john-10-dn-2
