# John 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Jesus Heals on the Sabbath**

1 After this there was a feast of the Jews, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem. ^john-05-1

2 Now there is in Jerusalem by the Sheep Gate a pool, in Hebrew called Beth-za'tha,<sup>j</sup> which has five porticoes. ^john-05-2

3 In these lay a multitude of invalids, blind, lame, paralyzed.<sup>k</sup> ^john-05-3

5 One man was there, who had been ill for thirty-eight years. ^john-05-5

6 When Jesus saw him and knew that he had been lying there a long time, he said to him, "Do you want to be healed?" ^john-05-6

7 The sick man answered him, "Sir, I have no man to put me into the pool when the water is troubled, and while I am going another steps down before me." ^john-05-7

8 Jesus said to him, "Rise, take up your pallet, and walk." ^john-05-8

9 And at once the man was healed, and he took up his pallet and walked.

Now that day was the sabbath. ^john-05-9

10 So the Jews said to the man who was cured, "It is the sabbath, it is not lawful for you to carry your pallet." ^john-05-10

11 But he answered them, "The man who healed me said to me, 'Take up your pallet, and walk.' " ^john-05-11

12 They asked him, "Who is the man who said to you, 'Take up your pallet, and walk'?" ^john-05-12

13 Now the man who had been healed did not know who it was, for Jesus had withdrawn, as there was a crowd in the place. ^john-05-13

14 Afterward, Jesus found him in the temple, and said to him, "See, you are well! Sin no more, that nothing worse befall you." ^john-05-14

15 The man went away and told the Jews that it was Jesus who had healed him. ^john-05-15

16 And this was why the Jews persecuted Jesus, because he did this on the sabbath. ^john-05-16

17 But Jesus answered them, "My Father is working still, and I am working." ^john-05-17

18 This was why the Jews sought all the more to kill him, because he not only broke the sabbath \* but also called God his Father, making himself equal with God. ^john-05-18

**The Authority of the Son**

19 Jesus said to them, "Truly, truly, I say to you, the Son can do nothing of his own accord, but only what he sees the Father doing; for whatever he does, that the Son does likewise. ^john-05-19

20 For the Father loves the Son, and shows him all that he himself is doing; and greater works than these will he show him, that you may marvel. ^john-05-20

21 For as the Father raises the dead and gives them life, so also the Son gives life to whom he will. ^john-05-21

22 The Father judges no one, but has given all judgment to the Son, ^john-05-22

23 that all may honor the Son, even as they honor the Father. He who does not honor the Son does not honor the Father who sent him. ^john-05-23

24 Truly, truly, I say to you, he who hears my word and believes him who sent me, has eternal life; he does not come into judgment, but has passed from death to life. ^john-05-24

25 "Truly, truly, I say to you, the hour is coming, and now is, when the dead will hear the voice of the Son of God, and those who hear will live. ^john-05-25

26 For as the Father has life in himself, so he has granted the Son also to have life in himself, ^john-05-26

27 and has given him authority to execute judgment, because he is the Son of man. ^john-05-27

28 Do not marvel at this; for the hour is coming when all who are in the tombs will hear his voice ^john-05-28

29 and come forth, those who have done good, to the resurrection of life, and those who have done evil, to the resurrection of judgment. ^john-05-29

**The Testimony to Jesus**

30 "I can do nothing on my own authority; as I hear, I judge; and my judgment is just, because I seek not my own will but the will of him who sent me. ^john-05-30

31 If I bear witness to myself, my testimony is not true; ^john-05-31

32 there is another who bears witness to me, and I know that the testimony which he bears to me is true. ^john-05-32

33 You sent to John, and he has borne witness to the truth. ^john-05-33

34 Not that the testimony which I receive is from man; but I say this that you may be saved. ^john-05-34

35 He was a burning and shining lamp, and you were willing to rejoice for a while in his light. ^john-05-35

36 But the testimony which I have is greater than that of John; for the works which the Father has granted me to accomplish, these very works which I am doing, bear me witness that the Father has sent me. ^john-05-36

37 And the Father who sent me has himself borne witness to me. His voice you have never heard, his form you have never seen; ^john-05-37

38 and you do not have his word abiding in you, for you do not believe him whom he has sent. ^john-05-38

39 You search the Scriptures, because you think that in them you have eternal life; and it is they that bear witness to me; ^john-05-39

40 yet you refuse to come to me that you may have life. ^john-05-40

41 I do not receive glory from men. ^john-05-41

42 But I know that you have not the love of God within you. ^john-05-42

43 I have come in my Father's name, and you do not receive me; if another comes in his own name, him you will receive. ^john-05-43

44 How can you believe, who receive glory from one another and do not seek the glory that comes from the only God? ^john-05-44

45 Do not think that I shall accuse you to the Father; it is Moses who accuses you, on whom you set your hope. ^john-05-45

46 If you believed Moses, you would believe me, for he wrote of me. ^john-05-46

47 But if you do not believe his writings, how will you believe my words?" ^john-05-47
