# John 15 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Jesus the True Vine**

1 "I am the true vine, and my Father is the vinedresser. ^john-15-1

2 Every branch of mine that bears no fruit, he takes away, and every branch that does bear fruit he prunes, that it may bear more fruit. ^john-15-2

3 You are already made clean by the word which I have spoken to you. ^john-15-3

4 Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit by itself, unless it abides in the vine, neither can you, unless you abide in me. ^john-15-4

5 I am the vine, you are the branches. He who abides in me, and I in him, he it is that bears much fruit, for apart from me you can do nothing. ^john-15-5

6 If a man does not abide in me, he is cast forth as a branch and withers; and the branches are gathered, thrown into the fire and burned. ^john-15-6

7 If you abide in me, and my words abide in you, ask whatever you will, and it shall be done for you. ^john-15-7

8 By this my Father is glorified, that you bear much fruit, and so prove to be my disciples. ^john-15-8

9 As the Father has loved me, so have I loved you; abide in my love. ^john-15-9

10 If you keep my commandments, you will abide in my love, just as I have kept my Father's commandments and abide in his love. ^john-15-10

11 These things I have spoken to you, that my joy may be in you, and that your joy may be full. ^john-15-11

12 "This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you. ^john-15-12

13 Greater love has no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends. ^john-15-13

14 You are my friends if you do what I command you. ^john-15-14

15 No longer do I call you servants,<sup>h</sup> for the servant<sup>i</sup> does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends, for all that I have heard from my Father I have made known to you. ^john-15-15

16 You did not choose me, but I chose you and appointed you that you should go and bear fruit and that your fruit should abide; so that whatever you ask the Father in my name, he may give it to you. ^john-15-16

17 This I command you, to love one another. ^john-15-17

**The World's Hatred**

18 "If the world hates you, know that it has hated me before it hated you. \* ^john-15-18

19 If you were of the world, the world would love its own; but because you are not of the world, but I chose you out of the world, therefore the world hates you. ^john-15-19

20 Remember the word that I said to you, 'A servant<sup>i</sup> is not greater than his master.' If they persecuted me, they will persecute you; if they kept my word, they will keep yours also. ^john-15-20

21 But all this they will do to you on my account, because they do not know him who sent me. ^john-15-21

22 If I had not come and spoken to them, they would not have sin; but now they have no excuse for their sin. ^john-15-22

23 He who hates me hates my Father also. ^john-15-23

24 If I had not done among them the works which no one else did, they would not have sin; but now they have seen and hated both me and my Father. ^john-15-24

25 It is to fulfil the word that is written in their law, 'They hated me without a cause.' ^john-15-25

26 But when the Counselor comes, whom I shall send to you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, who proceeds from the Father, he will bear witness to me; ^john-15-26

27 and you also are witnesses, because you have been with me from the beginning. ^john-15-27


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (15:18) Jesus contrasts the love his disciples have with the hatred the world bears them. ^john-15-dn-1
