# 2 Corinthians 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 For we know that if the earthly tent we live in is destroyed, we have a building from God, a house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens. ^second-corinthians-05-1

2 Here indeed we groan, and long to put on our heavenly dwelling, ^second-corinthians-05-2

3 so that by putting it on we may not be found naked. ^second-corinthians-05-3

4 For while we are still in this tent, we sigh with anxiety; not that we would be unclothed, but that we would be further clothed, so that what is mortal may be swallowed up by life. ^second-corinthians-05-4

5 He who has prepared us for this very thing is God, who has given us the Spirit as a guarantee. ^second-corinthians-05-5

6 So we are always of good courage; we know that while we are at home in the body we are away from the Lord, ^second-corinthians-05-6

7 for we walk by faith, not by sight. ^second-corinthians-05-7

8 We are of good courage, and we would rather be away from the body and at home with the Lord. ^second-corinthians-05-8

9 So whether we are at home or away, we make it our aim to please him. ^second-corinthians-05-9

10 For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ, so that each one may receive good or evil, according to what he has done in the body. ^second-corinthians-05-10

**The Ministry of Reconciliation**

11 Therefore, knowing the fear of the Lord, we persuade men; but what we are is known to God, and I hope it is known also to your conscience. ^second-corinthians-05-11

12 We are not commending ourselves to you again but giving you cause to be proud of us, so that you may be able to answer those who pride themselves on a man's position and not on his heart. ^second-corinthians-05-12

13 For if we are beside ourselves, it is for God; if we are in our right mind, it is for you. ^second-corinthians-05-13

14 For the love of Christ urges us on, because we are convinced that one has died for all; therefore all have died. ^second-corinthians-05-14

15 And he died for all, that those who live might live no longer for themselves but for him who for their sake died and was raised. ^second-corinthians-05-15

16 From now on, therefore, we regard no one according to the flesh; even though we once regarded Christ according to the flesh, we regard him thus no longer. ^second-corinthians-05-16

17 Therefore, if any one is in Christ, he is a new creation;<sup>g</sup> the old has passed away, behold, the new has come. ^second-corinthians-05-17

18 All this is from God, who through Christ reconciled us to himself and gave us the ministry of reconciliation; ^second-corinthians-05-18

19 that is, in Christ God was reconciling<sup>h</sup> the world to himself, \* not counting their trespasses against them, and entrusting to us the message of reconciliation. ^second-corinthians-05-19

20 So we are ambassadors for Christ, God making his appeal through us. We beg you on behalf of Christ, be reconciled to God. ^second-corinthians-05-20

21 For our sake he made him to be sin \* who knew no sin, so that in him we might become the righteousness of God. ^second-corinthians-05-21


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (5:19) Or, “God was reconciling the world to himself through Christ.” ^second-corinthians-05-dn-1

\* (5:21) made him to be sin: i.e., “sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh and for sin, he condemned sin in the flesh” (Rom 8:3). ^second-corinthians-05-dn-2
