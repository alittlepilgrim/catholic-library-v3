# 2 Corinthians 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 For I made up my mind not to make you another painful visit. ^second-corinthians-02-1

2 For if I cause you pain, who is there to make me glad but the one whom I have pained? ^second-corinthians-02-2

3 And I wrote as I did, so that when I came I might not suffer pain from those who should have made me rejoice, for I felt sure of all of you, that my joy would be the joy of you all. ^second-corinthians-02-3

4 For I wrote you out of much affliction and anguish of heart and with many tears, not to cause you pain but to let you know the abundant love that I have for you. ^second-corinthians-02-4

**Forgiveness for the Offender**

5 But if any one has caused pain, he has caused it not to me, but in some measure—not to put it too severely—to you all. ^second-corinthians-02-5

6 For such a one this punishment by the majority is enough; ^second-corinthians-02-6

7 so you should rather turn to forgive and comfort him, or he may be overwhelmed by excessive sorrow. ^second-corinthians-02-7

8 So I beg you to reaffirm your love for him. ^second-corinthians-02-8

9 For this is why I wrote, that I might test you and know whether you are obedient in everything. ^second-corinthians-02-9

10 Any one whom you forgive, I also forgive. What I have forgiven, if I have forgiven anything, has been for your sake in the presence of Christ, ^second-corinthians-02-10

11 to keep Satan from gaining the advantage over us; for we are not ignorant of his designs. ^second-corinthians-02-11

**Paul's Anxiety in Troas**

12 When I came to Troas to preach the gospel of Christ, a door was opened for me in the Lord; ^second-corinthians-02-12

13 but my mind could not rest because I did not find my brother Titus there. So I took leave of them and went on to Macedonia. ^second-corinthians-02-13

14 But thanks be to God, who in Christ always leads us in triumph, and through us spreads the fragrance of the knowledge of him everywhere. ^second-corinthians-02-14

15 For we are the aroma of Christ to God among those who are being saved and among those who are perishing, ^second-corinthians-02-15

16 to one a fragrance from death to death, to the other a fragrance from life to life. Who is sufficient for these things? ^second-corinthians-02-16

17 For we are not, like so many, peddlers of God's word; but as men of sincerity, as commissioned by God, in the sight of God we speak in Christ. ^second-corinthians-02-17
