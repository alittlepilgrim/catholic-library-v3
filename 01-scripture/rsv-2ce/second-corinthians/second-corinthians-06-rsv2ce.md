# 2 Corinthians 6 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 Working together with him, then, we entreat you not to accept the grace of God in vain. ^second-corinthians-06-1

2 For he says,

"At the acceptable time I have listened to you,
and helped you on the day of salvation."

Behold, now is the acceptable time; behold, now is the day of salvation. ^second-corinthians-06-2

3 We put no obstacle in any one's way, so that no fault may be found with our ministry, ^second-corinthians-06-3

4 but as servants of God we commend ourselves in every way: through great endurance, in afflictions, hardships, calamities, ^second-corinthians-06-4

5 beatings, imprisonments, tumults, labors, watching, hunger; ^second-corinthians-06-5

6 by purity, knowledge, forbearance, kindness, the Holy Spirit, genuine love, ^second-corinthians-06-6

7 truthful speech, and the power of God; with the weapons of righteousness for the right hand and for the left; ^second-corinthians-06-7

8 in honor and dishonor, in ill repute and good repute. We are treated as impostors, and yet are true; ^second-corinthians-06-8

9 as unknown, and yet well known; as dying, and behold we live; as punished, and yet not killed; ^second-corinthians-06-9

10 as sorrowful, yet always rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, and yet possessing everything. ^second-corinthians-06-10

11 Our mouth is open to you, Corinthians; our heart is wide. ^second-corinthians-06-11

12 You are not restricted by us, but you are restricted in your own affections. ^second-corinthians-06-12

13 In return—I speak as to children— widen your hearts also. ^second-corinthians-06-13

**The Temple of the Living God**

14 Do not be mismated with unbelievers. For what partnership have righteousness and iniquity? Or what fellowship has light with darkness? ^second-corinthians-06-14

15 What accord has Christ with Be'lial?<sup>i</sup> Or what has a believer in common with an unbeliever? ^second-corinthians-06-15

16 What agreement has the temple of God with idols? For we are the temple of the living God; as God said,

"I will live in them and move among them,
and I will be their God,
and they shall be my people. ^second-corinthians-06-16

17 Therefore come out from them,
  and be separate from them, says the Lord,
  and touch nothing unclean;
  then I will welcome you, ^second-corinthians-06-17

18 and I will be a father to you,
  and you shall be my sons and daughters,
  says the Lord Almighty." ^second-corinthians-06-18
