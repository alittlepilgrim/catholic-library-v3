# 2 Kings 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[2-kings-08|NABRE]]

**The Shunammite Woman’s Land Restored**

1 Now Eli'sha had said to the woman whose son he had restored to life, “Arise, and depart with your household, and sojourn wherever you can; for the LORD has called for a famine, and it will come upon the land for seven years.” ^2-kings-08-1

2 So the woman arose, and did according to the word of the man of God; she went with her household and sojourned in the land of the Philis'tines seven years. ^2-kings-08-2

3 And at the end of the seven years, when the woman returned from the land of the Philis'tines, she went forth to appeal to the king for her house and her land. ^2-kings-08-3

4 Now the king was talking with Geha'zi the servant of the man of God, saying, “Tell me all the great things that Eli'sha has done.” ^2-kings-08-4

5 And while he was telling the king how Eli'sha had restored the dead to life, behold, the woman whose son he had restored to life appealed to the king for her house and her land. And Geha'zi said, “My lord, O king, here is the woman, and here is her son whom Elisha restored to life.” ^2-kings-08-5

6 And when the king asked the woman, she told him. So the king appointed an official for her, saying, “Restore all that was hers, together with all the produce of the fields from the day that she left the land until now.” ^2-kings-08-6

**The Death of Ben-hadad**

7 Now Eli'sha came to Damascus. Ben-ha'dad the king of Syria was sick; and when it was told him, “The man of God has come here,” ^2-kings-08-7

8 the king said to Haz'ael, “Take a present with you and go to meet the man of God, and inquire of the LORD through him, saying, ‘Shall I recover from this sickness?’ ” ^2-kings-08-8

9 So Haz'ael went to meet him, and took a present with him, all kinds of goods of Damascus, forty camel loads. When he came and stood before him, he said, “Your son Ben-ha'dad king of Syria has sent me to you, saying, ‘Shall I recover from this sickness?’ ” ^2-kings-08-9

10 And Eli'sha said to him, “Go, say to him, ‘You shall certainly recover’; but the LORD has shown me that he shall certainly die.” ^2-kings-08-10

11 And he fixed his gaze and stared at him, until he was ashamed. And the man of God wept. ^2-kings-08-11

12 And Haz'ael said, “Why does my lord weep?” He answered, “Because I know the evil that you will do to the sons of Israel; you will set on fire their fortresses, and you will slay their young men with the sword, and dash in pieces their little ones, and rip up their women with child.” ^2-kings-08-12

13 And Haz'ael said, “What is your servant, who is but a dog, that he should do this great thing?” Eli'sha answered, “The LORD has shown me that you are to be king over Syria.” ^2-kings-08-13

14 Then he departed from Eli'sha, and came to his master, who said to him, “What did Elisha say to you?” And he answered, “He told me that you would certainly recover.” ^2-kings-08-14

15 But the next day he took the coverlet and dipped it in water and spread it over his face, till he died. And Haz'ael became king in his stead. ^2-kings-08-15

**Jehoram Reigns over Judah**

16 In the fifth year of Jo'ram the son of A'hab, king of Israel, Jeho'ram the son of Jehosh'aphat, king of Judah, began to reign. ^2-kings-08-16

17 He was thirty-two years old when he became king, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem. ^2-kings-08-17

18 And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as the house of A'hab had done, for the daughter of Ahab was his wife. And he did what was evil in the sight of the LORD. ^2-kings-08-18

19 Yet the LORD would not destroy Judah, for the sake of David his servant, since he promised to give a lamp to him and to his sons for ever. ^2-kings-08-19

20 In his days E'dom revolted from the rule of Judah, and set up a king of their own. ^2-kings-08-20

21 Then Jo'ram passed over to Za'ir with all his chariots, and rose by night, and he and his chariot commanders struck the E'domites who had surrounded him; but his army fled home. ^2-kings-08-21

22 So E'dom revolted from the rule of Judah to this day. Then Libnah revolted at the same time. ^2-kings-08-22

23 Now the rest of the acts of Jo'ram, and all that he did, are they not written in the Book of the Chronicles of the Kings of Judah? ^2-kings-08-23

24 So Jo'ram slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David; and Ahazi'ah his son reigned in his stead. ^2-kings-08-24

**Ahaziah Reigns over Judah**

25 In the twelfth year of Jo'ram the son of A'hab, king of Israel, Ahazi'ah the son of Jeho'ram, king of Judah, began to reign. ^2-kings-08-25

26 Ahazi'ah was twenty-two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother’s name was Athali'ah; she was a granddaughter of Omri king of Israel. ^2-kings-08-26

27 He also walked in the way of the house of A'hab, and did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, as the house of Ahab had done, for he was son-in-law to the house of Ahab. ^2-kings-08-27

28 He went with Jo'ram the son of A'hab to make war against Haz'ael king of Syria at Ra'moth-gil'ead, where the Syrians wounded Joram. ^2-kings-08-28

29 And King Jo'ram returned to be healed in Jezre'el of the wounds which the Syrians had given him at Ra'mah, when he fought against Haz'ael king of Syria. And Ahazi'ah the son of Jeho'ram king of Judah went down to see Joram the son of A'hab in Jezre'el, because he was sick. ^2-kings-08-29
