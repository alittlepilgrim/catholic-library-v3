# Judges 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[judges-04|NABRE]]

**Deborah and Barak**

1 And the sons of Israel again did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, after E'hud died. ^judges-04-1

2 And the LORD sold them into the hand of Ja'bin king of Canaan, who reigned in Ha'zor; the commander of his army was Sis'era, who dwelt in Haro'sheth-ha-goi'im. ^judges-04-2

3 Then the sons of Israel cried to the LORD for help; for he had nine hundred chariots of iron, and oppressed the sons of Israel cruelly for twenty years. ^judges-04-3

4 Now Deborah, a prophetess, the wife of Lap'pidoth, was judging Israel at that time. ^judges-04-4

5 She used to sit under the palm of Deborah between Ra'mah and Bethel in the hill country of E'phraim; and the sons of Israel came up to her for judgment. ^judges-04-5

6 She sent and summoned Barak the son of Abin'o-am from Ke'desh in Naph'tali, and said to him, “The LORD, the God of Israel, commands you, ‘Go, gather your men at Mount Ta'bor, taking ten thousand from the tribe of Naphtali and the tribe of Zeb'ulun. ^judges-04-6

7 And I will draw out Sis'era, the general of Ja'bin’s army, to meet you by the river Ki'shon with his chariots and his troops; and I will give him into your hand.’ ” ^judges-04-7

8 Barak said to her, “If you will go with me, I will go; but if you will not go with me, I will not go.” ^judges-04-8

9 And she said, “I will surely go with you; nevertheless, the road on which you are going will not lead to your glory, for the LORD will sell Sis'era into the hand of a woman.” Then Deborah arose, and went with Barak to Ke'desh. ^judges-04-9

10 And Barak summoned Zeb'ulun and Naph'tali to Ke'desh; and ten thousand men went up at his heels; and Deborah went up with him. ^judges-04-10

11 Now He'ber the Kenite had separated from the Kenites, the descendants of Ho'bab the father-in-law of Moses, and had pitched his tent as far away as the oak in Za-anan'nim, which is near Ke'desh. ^judges-04-11

12 When Sis'era was told that Barak the son of Abin'o-am had gone up to Mount Ta'bor, ^judges-04-12

13 Sis'era called out all his chariots, nine hundred chariots of iron, and all the men who were with him, from Haro'sheth-ha-goi'im to the river Ki'shon. ^judges-04-13

14 And Deborah said to Barak, “Up! For this is the day in which the LORD has given Sis'era into your hand. Does not the LORD go out before you?” So Barak went down from Mount Ta'bor with ten thousand men following him. ^judges-04-14

15 And the LORD routed Sis'era and all his chariots and all his army before Barak at the edge of the sword; and Sisera alighted from his chariot and fled away on foot. ^judges-04-15

16 And Barak pursued the chariots and the army to Haro'sheth-ha-goi'im, and all the army of Sis'era fell by the edge of the sword; not a man was left. ^judges-04-16

**Jael Kills Sisera**

17 But Sis'era fled away on foot to the tent of Ja'el, the wife of He'ber the Kenite; for there was peace between Ja'bin the king of Ha'zor and the house of Heber the Kenite. ^judges-04-17

18 And Ja'el came out to meet Sis'era, and said to him, “Turn aside, my lord, turn aside to me; have no fear.” So he turned aside to her into the tent, and she covered him with a rug. ^judges-04-18

19 And he said to her, “Please, give me a little water to drink; for I am thirsty.” So she opened a skin of milk and gave him a drink and covered him. ^judges-04-19

20 And he said to her, “Stand at the door of the tent, and if any man comes and asks you, ‘Is any one here?’ say, No.” ^judges-04-20

21 But Ja'el the wife of He'ber took a tent peg, and took a hammer in her hand, and went softly to him and drove the peg into his temple, till it went down into the ground, as he was lying fast asleep from weariness. So he died. ^judges-04-21

22 And behold, as Barak pursued Sis'era, Ja'el went out to meet him, and said to him, “Come, and I will show you the man whom you are seeking.” So he went in to her tent; and there lay Sisera dead, with the tent peg in his temple. ^judges-04-22

23 So on that day God subdued Ja'bin the king of Canaan before the sons of Israel. ^judges-04-23

24 And the hand of the sons of Israel bore harder and harder on Ja'bin the king of Canaan, until they destroyed Jabin king of Canaan. ^judges-04-24
