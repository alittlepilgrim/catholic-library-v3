# Judges 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[judges-10|NABRE]]

**Tola and Jair**

1 After Abim'elech there arose to deliver Israel Tola the son of Puah, son of Dodo, a man of Is'sachar; and he lived at Sha'mir in the hill country of E'phraim. ^judges-10-1

2 And he judged Israel twenty-three years. Then he died, and was buried at Sha'mir. ^judges-10-2

3 After him arose Ja'ir the Gileadite, who judged Israel twenty-two years. ^judges-10-3

4 And he had thirty sons who rode on thirty donkeys; and they had thirty cities, called Hav'voth-ja'ir to this day, which are in the land of Gilead. ^judges-10-4

5 And Ja'ir died, and was buried in Kamon. ^judges-10-5

**Oppression by the Philistines and Ammonites**

6 And the sons of Israel again did what was evil in the sight of the LORD, and served the Ba'als and the Ash'taroth, the gods of Syria, the gods of Sidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the Am'monites, and the gods of the Philis'tines; and they forsook the LORD, and did not serve him. ^judges-10-6

7 And the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of the Philis'tines and into the hand of the Am'monites, ^judges-10-7

8 and they crushed and oppressed the children of Israel that year. For eighteen years they oppressed all the sons of Israel that were beyond the Jordan in the land of the Am'orites, which is in Gilead. ^judges-10-8

9 And the Am'monites crossed the Jordan to fight also against Judah and against Benjamin and against the house of E'phraim; so that Israel was sorely distressed. ^judges-10-9

10 And the sons of Israel cried to the LORD, saying, “We have sinned against you, because we have forsaken our God and have served the Ba'als.” ^judges-10-10

11 And the LORD said to the sons of Israel, “Did I not deliver you from the Egyptians and from the Am'orites, from the Am'monites and from the Philis'tines? ^judges-10-11

12 The Sido'nians also, and the Amal'ekites, and the Ma'onites, oppressed you; and you cried to me, and I delivered you out of their hand. ^judges-10-12

13 Yet you have forsaken me and served other gods; therefore I will deliver you no more. ^judges-10-13

14 Go and cry to the gods whom you have chosen; let them deliver you in the time of your distress.” ^judges-10-14

15 And the sons of Israel said to the LORD, “We have sinned; do to us whatever seems good to you; only deliver us, we beg you, this day.” ^judges-10-15

16 So they put away the foreign gods from among them and served the LORD; and he became indignant over the misery of Israel. ^judges-10-16

17 Then the Am'monites were called to arms, and they encamped in Gilead; and the sons of Israel came together, and they encamped at Mizpah. ^judges-10-17

18 And the people, the leaders of Gilead, said one to another, “Who is the man that will begin to fight against the Am'monites? He shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.” ^judges-10-18
