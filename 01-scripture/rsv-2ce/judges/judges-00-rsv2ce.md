## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* This book, which has been edited at least twice, fills in the period from the settlement to the monarchy. This was a period of crisis that gave rise to “saviors” raised up by God to meet these critical situations. The deeds of six of these are recounted at some length: Othniel, Ehud, Deborah (and Barak), Gideon, Jephthah, Samson; there are also six “minor” judges who are given only a short notice: Shamgar, Tola, Jair, Ibzan, Elon, Abdon. The book ends with two appendixes (chapters 17–21). ^judges-00-dn-1
