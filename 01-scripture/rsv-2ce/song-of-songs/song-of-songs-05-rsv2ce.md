# Song of Solomon 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[song-of-songs-05|NABRE]]

**The Groom’s Song of Love; and the Torment of Separation**

1 I come to my garden, my sister, my bride, I gather my myrrh with my spice, I eat my honeycomb with my honey, I drink my wine with my milk. Eat, O friends, and drink: drink deeply, O lovers! ^song-of-songs-05-1

2 I slept, but my heart was awake. Hark! my beloved is knocking. “Open to me, my sister, my love, my dove, my perfect one; for my head is wet with dew, my locks with the drops of the night.” ^song-of-songs-05-2

3 I had put off my garment, how could I put it on? I had bathed my feet, how could I soil them? ^song-of-songs-05-3

4 My beloved put his hand to the latch, and my heart was thrilled within me. ^song-of-songs-05-4

5 I arose to open to my beloved, and my hands dripped with myrrh, my fingers with liquid myrrh, upon the handles of the bolt. ^song-of-songs-05-5

6 I opened to my beloved, but my beloved had turned and gone. My soul failed me when he spoke. I sought him, but found him not; I called him, but he gave no answer. ^song-of-songs-05-6

7 The watchmen found me, as they went about in the city; they beat me, they wounded me, they took away my mantle, those watchmen of the walls. ^song-of-songs-05-7

8 I adjure you, O daughters of Jerusalem, if you find my beloved, that you tell him I am sick with love. ^song-of-songs-05-8

9 What is your beloved more than another beloved, O fairest among women? What is your beloved more than another beloved, that you thus adjure us? ^song-of-songs-05-9

10 My beloved is all radiant and ruddy, distinguished among ten thousand. ^song-of-songs-05-10

11 His head is the finest gold; his locks are wavy, black as a raven. ^song-of-songs-05-11

12 His eyes are like doves beside springs of water, bathed in milk, fitly set. ^song-of-songs-05-12

13 His cheeks are like beds of spices, yielding fragrance. His lips are lilies, distilling liquid myrrh. ^song-of-songs-05-13

14 His arms are rounded gold, set with jewels. His body is ivory work, encrusted with sapphires. ^song-of-songs-05-14

15 His legs are alabaster columns, set upon bases of gold. His appearance is like Lebanon, choice as the cedars. ^song-of-songs-05-15

16 His speech is most sweet, and he is altogether desirable. This is my beloved and this is my friend, O daughters of Jerusalem. ^song-of-songs-05-16
