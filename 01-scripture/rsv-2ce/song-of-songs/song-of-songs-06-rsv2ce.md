# Song of Solomon 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[song-of-songs-06|NABRE]]

**Colloquy of the Friends and the Bride; the Bridegroom’s Praise of the Bride**

1 Where has your beloved gone, O fairest among women? Where has your beloved turned, that we may seek him with you? ^song-of-songs-06-1

2 My beloved has gone down to his garden, to the beds of spices, to pasture his flock in the gardens, and to gather lilies. ^song-of-songs-06-2

3 I am my beloved’s and my beloved is mine; he pastures his flock among the lilies. ^song-of-songs-06-3

4 You are beautiful as Tirzah, my love, comely as Jerusalem, terrible as an army with banners. ^song-of-songs-06-4

5 Turn away your eyes from me, for they disturb me— Your hair is like a flock of goats, moving down the slopes of Gilead. ^song-of-songs-06-5

6 Your teeth are like a flock of ewes, that have come up from the washing, all of them bear twins, not one among them is bereaved. ^song-of-songs-06-6

7 Your cheeks are like halves of a pomegranate behind your veil. ^song-of-songs-06-7

8 There are sixty queens and eighty concubines, and maidens without number. ^song-of-songs-06-8

9 My dove, my perfect one, is only one, the darling of her mother, flawless to her that bore her. The maidens saw her and called her happy; the queens and concubines also, and they praised her. ^song-of-songs-06-9

10 “Who is this that looks forth like the dawn, fair as the moon, bright as the sun, terrible as an army with banners?” ^song-of-songs-06-10

11 I went down to the nut orchard, to look at the blossoms of the valley, to see whether the vines had budded, whether the pomegranates were in bloom. ^song-of-songs-06-11

12 Before I was aware, my fancy set me in a chariot beside my prince. ^song-of-songs-06-12

13 Return, return, O Shu'lammite, return, return, that we may look upon you. Why should you look upon the Shulammite, as upon a dance before two armies? ^song-of-songs-06-13
