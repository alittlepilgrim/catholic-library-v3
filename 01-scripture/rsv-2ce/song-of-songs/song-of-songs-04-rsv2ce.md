# Song of Solomon 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[song-of-songs-04|NABRE]]

**The Bride’s Beauty Extolled**

1 Behold, you are beautiful, my love, behold, you are beautiful! Your eyes are doves behind your veil. Your hair is like a flock of goats, moving down the slopes of Gilead. ^song-of-songs-04-1

2 Your teeth are like a flock of shorn ewes that have come up from the washing, all of which bear twins, and not one among them is bereaved. ^song-of-songs-04-2

3 Your lips are like a scarlet thread, and your mouth is lovely. Your cheeks are like halves of a pomegranate behind your veil. ^song-of-songs-04-3

4 Your neck is like the tower of David, built for an arsenal, whereon hang a thousand bucklers, all of them shields of warriors. ^song-of-songs-04-4

5 Your two breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle, that feed among the lilies. ^song-of-songs-04-5

6 Until the day breathes and the shadows flee, I will hasten to the mountain of myrrh and the hill of frankincense. ^song-of-songs-04-6

7 You are all fair, my love; there is no flaw in you. ^song-of-songs-04-7

8 Come with me from Lebanon, my bride; come with me from Lebanon. Depart from the peak of Ama'na, from the peak of Se'nir and Hermon, from the dens of lions, from the mountains of leopards. ^song-of-songs-04-8

9 You have ravished my heart, my sister, my bride, you have ravished my heart with a glance of your eyes, with one jewel of your necklace. ^song-of-songs-04-9

10 How sweet is your love, my sister, my bride! how much better is your love than wine, and the fragrance of your oils than any spice! ^song-of-songs-04-10

11 Your lips distil nectar, my bride; honey and milk are under your tongue; the scent of your garments is like the scent of Lebanon. ^song-of-songs-04-11

12 A garden locked is my sister, my bride, a garden locked, a fountain sealed. ^song-of-songs-04-12

13 Your shoots are an orchard of pomegranates with all choicest fruits, henna with nard, ^song-of-songs-04-13

14 nard and saffron, calamus and cinnamon, with all trees of frankincense, myrrh and aloes, with all chief spices— ^song-of-songs-04-14

15 a garden fountain, a well of living water, and flowing streams from Lebanon. ^song-of-songs-04-15

16 Awake, O north wind, and come, O south wind! Blow upon my garden, let its fragrance be wafted abroad. Let my beloved come to his garden, and eat its choicest fruits. ^song-of-songs-04-16
