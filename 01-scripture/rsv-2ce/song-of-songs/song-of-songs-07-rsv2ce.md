# Song of Solomon 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[song-of-songs-07|NABRE]]

**Colloquy of the Bride and the Groom**

1 How graceful are your feet in sandals, O queenly maiden! Your rounded thighs are like jewels, the work of a master hand. ^song-of-songs-07-1

2 Your navel is a rounded bowl that never lacks mixed wine. Your belly is a heap of wheat, encircled with lilies. ^song-of-songs-07-2

3 Your two breasts are like two fawns, twins of a gazelle. ^song-of-songs-07-3

4 Your neck is like an ivory tower. Your eyes are pools in Hesh'bon, by the gate of Bath-rab'bim. Your nose is like a tower of Lebanon, overlooking Damascus. ^song-of-songs-07-4

5 Your head crowns you like Car'mel, and your flowing locks are like purple; a king is held captive in the tresses. ^song-of-songs-07-5

6 How fair and pleasant you are, O loved one, delectable maiden! ^song-of-songs-07-6

7 You are stately as a palm tree, and your breasts are like its clusters. ^song-of-songs-07-7

8 I say I will climb the palm tree and lay hold of its branches. Oh, may your breasts be like clusters of the vine, and the scent of your breath like apples, ^song-of-songs-07-8

9 and your kisses like the best wine that goes down smoothly, gliding over lips and teeth. ^song-of-songs-07-9

10 I am my beloved’s, and his desire is for me. ^song-of-songs-07-10

11 Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the fields, and lodge in the villages; ^song-of-songs-07-11

12 let us go out early to the vineyards, and see whether the vines have budded, whether the grape blossoms have opened and the pomegranates are in bloom. There I will give you my love. ^song-of-songs-07-12

13 The mandrakes give forth fragrance, and over our doors are all choice fruits, new as well as old, which I have laid up for you, O my beloved. ^song-of-songs-07-13
