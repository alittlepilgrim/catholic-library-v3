# Proverbs 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-08|NABRE]]

**Eternity of Wisdom**

1 Does not wisdom call, does not understanding raise her voice? ^proverbs-08-1

2 On the heights beside the way, in the paths she takes her stand; ^proverbs-08-2

3 beside the gates in front of the town, at the entrance of the portals she cries aloud: ^proverbs-08-3

4 “To you, O men, I call, and my cry is to the sons of men. ^proverbs-08-4

5 O simple ones, learn prudence; O foolish men, pay attention. ^proverbs-08-5

6 Hear, for I will speak noble things, and from my lips will come what is right; ^proverbs-08-6

7 for my mouth will utter truth; wickedness is an abomination to my lips. ^proverbs-08-7

8 All the words of my mouth are righteous; there is nothing twisted or crooked in them. ^proverbs-08-8

9 They are all straight to him who understands and right to those who find knowledge. ^proverbs-08-9

10 Take my instruction instead of silver, and knowledge rather than choice gold; ^proverbs-08-10

11 for wisdom is better than jewels, and all that you may desire cannot compare with her. ^proverbs-08-11

12 I, wisdom, dwell in prudence, and I find knowledge and discretion. ^proverbs-08-12

13 The fear of the LORD is hatred of evil. Pride and arrogance and the way of evil and perverted speech I hate. ^proverbs-08-13

14 I have counsel and sound wisdom, I have insight, I have strength. ^proverbs-08-14

15 By me kings reign, and rulers decree what is just; ^proverbs-08-15

16 by me princes rule, and nobles govern the earth. ^proverbs-08-16

17 I love those who love me, and those who seek me diligently find me. ^proverbs-08-17

18 Riches and honor are with me, enduring wealth and prosperity. ^proverbs-08-18

19 My fruit is better than gold, even fine gold, and my yield than choice silver. ^proverbs-08-19

20 I walk in the way of righteousness, in the paths of justice, ^proverbs-08-20

21 endowing with wealth those who love me, and filling their treasuries. ^proverbs-08-21

22 The LORD created me at the beginning of his work, the first of his acts of old. ^proverbs-08-22

23 Ages ago I was set up, at the first, before the beginning of the earth. ^proverbs-08-23

24 When there were no depths I was brought forth, when there were no springs abounding with water. ^proverbs-08-24

25 Before the mountains had been shaped, before the hills, I was brought forth; ^proverbs-08-25

26 before he had made the earth with its fields, or the first of the dust of the world. ^proverbs-08-26

27 When he established the heavens, I was there, when he drew a circle on the face of the deep, ^proverbs-08-27

28 when he made firm the skies above, when he established the fountains of the deep, ^proverbs-08-28

29 when he assigned to the sea its limit, so that the waters might not transgress his command, when he marked out the foundations of the earth, ^proverbs-08-29

30 then I was beside him, like a master workman; and I was daily his delight, rejoicing before him always, ^proverbs-08-30

31 rejoicing in his inhabited world and delighting in the sons of men. ^proverbs-08-31

32 And now, my sons, listen to me: happy are those who keep my ways. ^proverbs-08-32

33 Hear instruction and be wise, and do not neglect it. ^proverbs-08-33

34 Happy is the man who listens to me, watching daily at my gates, waiting beside my doors. ^proverbs-08-34

35 For he who finds me finds life and obtains favor from the LORD; ^proverbs-08-35

36 but he who misses me injures himself; all who hate me love death.” ^proverbs-08-36
