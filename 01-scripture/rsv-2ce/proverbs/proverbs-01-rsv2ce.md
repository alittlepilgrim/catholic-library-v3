# Proverbs 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-01|NABRE]]

**The Call of Wisdom**

1 The proverbs of Solomon, son of David, king of Israel: ^proverbs-01-1

2 That men may know wisdom and instruction, understand words of insight, ^proverbs-01-2

3 receive instruction in wise dealing, righteousness, justice, and equity; ^proverbs-01-3

4 that prudence may be given to the simple, knowledge and discretion to the youth— ^proverbs-01-4

5 the wise man also may hear and increase in learning, and the man of understanding acquire skill, ^proverbs-01-5

6 to understand a proverb and a figure, the words of the wise and their riddles. ^proverbs-01-6

7 The fear of the LORD is the beginning of knowledge; fools despise wisdom and instruction. ^proverbs-01-7

8 Hear, my son, your father’s instruction, and reject not your mother’s teaching; ^proverbs-01-8

9 for they are a fair garland for your head, and pendants for your neck. ^proverbs-01-9

10 My son, if sinners entice you, do not consent. ^proverbs-01-10

11 If they say, “Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us wantonly ambush the innocent; ^proverbs-01-11

12 like Sheol let us swallow them alive and whole, like those who go down to the Pit; ^proverbs-01-12

13 we shall find all precious goods, we shall fill our houses with spoil; ^proverbs-01-13

14 throw in your lot among us, we will all have one purse”— ^proverbs-01-14

15 my son, do not walk in the way with them, hold back your foot from their paths; ^proverbs-01-15

16 for their feet run to evil, and they make haste to shed blood. ^proverbs-01-16

17 For in vain is a net spread in the sight of any bird; ^proverbs-01-17

18 but these men lie in wait for their own blood, they set an ambush for their own lives. ^proverbs-01-18

19 Such are the ways of all who get gain by violence; it takes away the life of its possessors. ^proverbs-01-19

20 Wisdom cries aloud in the street; in the markets she raises her voice; ^proverbs-01-20

21 on the top of the walls she cries out; at the entrance of the city gates she speaks: ^proverbs-01-21

22 “How long, O simple ones, will you love being simple? How long will scoffers delight in their scoffing and fools hate knowledge? ^proverbs-01-22

23 Give heed to my reproof; behold, I will pour out my thoughts to you; I will make my words known to you. ^proverbs-01-23

24 Because I have called and you refused to listen, have stretched out my hand and no one has heeded, ^proverbs-01-24

25 and you have ignored all my counsel and would have none of my reproof, ^proverbs-01-25

26 I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when panic strikes you, ^proverbs-01-26

27 when panic strikes you like a storm, and your calamity comes like a whirlwind, when distress and anguish come upon you. ^proverbs-01-27

28 Then they will call upon me, but I will not answer; they will seek me diligently but will not find me. ^proverbs-01-28

29 Because they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of the LORD, ^proverbs-01-29

30 would have none of my counsel, and despised all my reproof, ^proverbs-01-30

31 therefore they shall eat the fruit of their way and be sated with their own devices. ^proverbs-01-31

32 For the simple are killed by their turning away, and the complacence of fools destroys them; ^proverbs-01-32

33 but he who listens to me will dwell secure and will be at ease, without dread of evil.” ^proverbs-01-33
