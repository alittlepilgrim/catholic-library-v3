# Proverbs 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-25|NABRE]]

**Further Wise Sayings of Solomon**

1 These also are proverbs of Solomon which the men of Hezeki'ah king of Judah copied. ^proverbs-25-1

2 It is the glory of God to conceal things, but the glory of kings is to search things out. ^proverbs-25-2

3 As the heavens for height, and the earth for depth, so the mind of kings is unsearchable. ^proverbs-25-3

4 Take away the dross from the silver, and the smith has material for a vessel; ^proverbs-25-4

5 take away the wicked from the presence of the king, and his throne will be established in righteousness. ^proverbs-25-5

6 Do not put yourself forward in the king’s presence or stand in the place of the great; ^proverbs-25-6

7 for it is better to be told, “Come up here,” than to be put lower in the presence of the prince. What your eyes have seen ^proverbs-25-7

8 do not hastily bring into court; for what will you do in the end, when your neighbor puts you to shame? ^proverbs-25-8

9 Argue your case with your neighbor himself, and do not disclose another’s secret; ^proverbs-25-9

10 lest he who hears you bring shame upon you, and your ill repute have no end. ^proverbs-25-10

11 A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in a setting of silver. ^proverbs-25-11

12 Like a gold ring or an ornament of gold is a wise reprover to a listening ear. ^proverbs-25-12

13 Like the cold of snow in the time of harvest is a faithful messenger to those who send him, he refreshes the spirit of his masters. ^proverbs-25-13

14 Like clouds and wind without rain is a man who boasts of a gift he does not give. ^proverbs-25-14

15 With patience a ruler may be persuaded, and a soft tongue will break a bone. ^proverbs-25-15

16 If you have found honey, eat only enough for you, lest you be sated with it and vomit it. ^proverbs-25-16

17 Let your foot be seldom in your neighbor’s house, lest he become weary of you and hate you. ^proverbs-25-17

18 A man who bears false witness against his neighbor is like a war club, or a sword, or a sharp arrow. ^proverbs-25-18

19 Trust in a faithless man in time of trouble is like a bad tooth or a foot that slips. ^proverbs-25-19

20 He who sings songs to a heavy heart is like one who takes off a garment on a cold day, and like vinegar on a wound. ^proverbs-25-20

21 If your enemy is hungry, give him bread to eat; and if he is thirsty, give him water to drink; ^proverbs-25-21

22 for you will heap coals of fire on his head, and the LORD will reward you. ^proverbs-25-22

23 The north wind brings forth rain; and a backbiting tongue, angry looks. ^proverbs-25-23

24 It is better to live in a corner of the housetop than in a house shared with a contentious woman. ^proverbs-25-24

25 Like cold water to a thirsty soul, so is good news from a far country. ^proverbs-25-25

26 Like a muddied spring or a polluted fountain is a righteous man who gives way before the wicked. ^proverbs-25-26

27 It is not good to eat much honey, so be sparing of complimentary words. ^proverbs-25-27

28 A man without self-control is like a city broken into and left without walls. ^proverbs-25-28
