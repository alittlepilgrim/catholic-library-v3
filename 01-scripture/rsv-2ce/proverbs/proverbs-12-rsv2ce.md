# Proverbs 12 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-12|NABRE]]

1 Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge, but he who hates reproof is stupid. ^proverbs-12-1

2 A good man obtains favor from the LORD, but a man of evil devices he condemns. ^proverbs-12-2

3 A man is not established by wickedness, but the root of the righteous will never be moved. ^proverbs-12-3

4 A good wife is the crown of her husband, but she who brings shame is like rottenness in his bones. ^proverbs-12-4

5 The thoughts of the righteous are just; the counsels of the wicked are treacherous. ^proverbs-12-5

6 The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood, but the mouth of the upright delivers men. ^proverbs-12-6

7 The wicked are overthrown and are no more, but the house of the righteous will stand. ^proverbs-12-7

8 A man is commended according to his good sense, but one of perverse mind is despised. ^proverbs-12-8

9 Better is a man of humble standing who works for himself than one who plays the great man but lacks bread. ^proverbs-12-9

10 A righteous man has regard for the life of his beast, but the mercy of the wicked is cruel. ^proverbs-12-10

11 He who tills his land will have plenty of bread, but he who follows worthless pursuits has no sense. ^proverbs-12-11

12 The strong tower of the wicked comes to ruin, but the root of the righteous stands firm. ^proverbs-12-12

13 An evil man is ensnared by the transgression of his lips, but the righteous escapes from trouble. ^proverbs-12-13

14 From the fruit of his words a man is satisfied with good, and the work of a man’s hand comes back to him. ^proverbs-12-14

15 The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but a wise man listens to advice. ^proverbs-12-15

16 The vexation of a fool is known at once, but the prudent man ignores an insult. ^proverbs-12-16

17 He who speaks the truth gives honest evidence, but a false witness utters deceit. ^proverbs-12-17

18 There is one whose rash words are like sword thrusts, but the tongue of the wise brings healing. ^proverbs-12-18

19 Truthful lips endure for ever, but a lying tongue is but for a moment. ^proverbs-12-19

20 Deceit is in the heart of those who devise evil, but those who plan good have joy. ^proverbs-12-20

21 No ill befalls the righteous, but the wicked are filled with trouble. ^proverbs-12-21

22 Lying lips are an abomination to the LORD, but those who act faithfully are his delight. ^proverbs-12-22

23 A prudent man conceals his knowledge, but fools proclaim their folly. ^proverbs-12-23

24 The hand of the diligent will rule, while the slothful will be put to forced labor. ^proverbs-12-24

25 Anxiety in a man’s heart weighs him down, but a good word makes him glad. ^proverbs-12-25

26 A righteous man turns away from evil, but the way of the wicked leads them astray. ^proverbs-12-26

27 A slothful man will not catch his prey, but the diligent man will get precious wealth. ^proverbs-12-27

28 In the path of righteousness is life, but the way of error leads to death. ^proverbs-12-28
