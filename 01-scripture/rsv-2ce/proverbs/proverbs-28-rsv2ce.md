# Proverbs 28 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-28|NABRE]]

**The Wicked and the Righteous Contrasted**

1 The wicked flee when no one pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion. ^proverbs-28-1

2 When a land transgresses it has many rulers; but with men of understanding and knowledge its stability will long continue. ^proverbs-28-2

3 A poor man who oppresses the poor is a beating rain that leaves no food. ^proverbs-28-3

4 Those who forsake the law praise the wicked, but those who keep the law strive against them. ^proverbs-28-4

5 Evil men do not understand justice, but those who seek the LORD understand it completely. ^proverbs-28-5

6 Better is a poor man who walks in his integrity than a rich man who is perverse in his ways. ^proverbs-28-6

7 He who keeps the law is a wise son, but a companion of gluttons shames his father. ^proverbs-28-7

8 He who augments his wealth by interest and increase gathers it for him who is kind to the poor. ^proverbs-28-8

9 If one turns away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer is an abomination. ^proverbs-28-9

10 He who misleads the upright into an evil way will fall into his own pit; but the blameless will have an excellent inheritance. ^proverbs-28-10

11 A rich man is wise in his own eyes, but a poor man who has understanding will find him out. ^proverbs-28-11

12 When the righteous triumph, there is great glory; but when the wicked rise, men hide themselves. ^proverbs-28-12

13 He who conceals his transgressions will not prosper, but he who confesses and forsakes them will obtain mercy. ^proverbs-28-13

14 Blessed is the man who fears the LORD always; but he who hardens his heart will fall into calamity. ^proverbs-28-14

15 Like a roaring lion or a charging bear is a wicked ruler over a poor people. ^proverbs-28-15

16 A ruler who lacks understanding is a cruel oppressor; but he who hates unjust gain will prolong his days. ^proverbs-28-16

17 If a man is burdened with the blood of another, let him be a fugitive until death; let no one help him. ^proverbs-28-17

18 He who walks in integrity will be delivered, but he who is perverse in his ways will fall into a pit. ^proverbs-28-18

19 He who tills his land will have plenty of bread, but he who follows worthless pursuits will have plenty of poverty. ^proverbs-28-19

20 A faithful man will abound with blessings, but he who hastens to be rich will not go unpunished. ^proverbs-28-20

21 To show partiality is not good; but for a piece of bread a man will do wrong. ^proverbs-28-21

22 A miserly man hastens after wealth, and does not know that want will come upon him. ^proverbs-28-22

23 He who rebukes a man will afterward find more favor than he who flatters with his tongue. ^proverbs-28-23

24 He who robs his father or his mother and says, “That is no transgression,” is the companion of a man who destroys. ^proverbs-28-24

25 A greedy man stirs up strife, but he who trusts in the LORD will be enriched. ^proverbs-28-25

26 He who trusts in his own mind is a fool; but he who walks in wisdom will be delivered. ^proverbs-28-26

27 He who gives to the poor will not want, but he who hides his eyes will get many a curse. ^proverbs-28-27

28 When the wicked rise, men hide themselves, but when they perish, the righteous increase. ^proverbs-28-28
