# Proverbs 15 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-15|NABRE]]

1 A soft answer turns away wrath, but a harsh word stirs up anger. ^proverbs-15-1

2 The tongue of the wise dispenses knowledge, but the mouths of fools pour out folly. ^proverbs-15-2

3 The eyes of the LORD are in every place, keeping watch on the evil and the good. ^proverbs-15-3

4 A gentle tongue is a tree of life, but perverseness in it breaks the spirit. ^proverbs-15-4

5 A fool despises his father’s instruction, but he who heeds admonition is prudent. ^proverbs-15-5

6 In the house of the righteous there is much treasure, but trouble befalls the income of the wicked. ^proverbs-15-6

7 The lips of the wise spread knowledge; not so the minds of fools. ^proverbs-15-7

8 The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but the prayer of the upright is his delight. ^proverbs-15-8

9 The way of the wicked is an abomination to the LORD, but he loves him who pursues righteousness. ^proverbs-15-9

10 There is severe discipline for him who forsakes the way; he who hates reproof will die. ^proverbs-15-10

11 Sheol and Abad'don lie open before the LORD, how much more the hearts of men! ^proverbs-15-11

12 A scoffer does not like to be reproved; he will not go to the wise. ^proverbs-15-12

13 A glad heart makes a cheerful countenance, but by sorrow of heart the spirit is broken. ^proverbs-15-13

14 The mind of him who has understanding seeks knowledge, but the mouths of fools feed on folly. ^proverbs-15-14

15 All the days of the afflicted are evil, but a cheerful heart has a continual feast. ^proverbs-15-15

16 Better is a little with the fear of the LORD than great treasure and trouble with it. ^proverbs-15-16

17 Better is a dinner of herbs where love is than a fatted ox and hatred with it. ^proverbs-15-17

18 A hot-tempered man stirs up strife, but he who is slow to anger quiets contention. ^proverbs-15-18

19 The way of a sluggard is overgrown with thorns, but the path of the upright is a level highway. ^proverbs-15-19

20 A wise son makes a glad father, but a foolish man despises his mother. ^proverbs-15-20

21 Folly is a joy to him who has no sense, but a man of understanding walks aright. ^proverbs-15-21

22 Without counsel plans go wrong, but with many advisers they succeed. ^proverbs-15-22

23 To make an apt answer is a joy to a man, and a word in season, how good it is! ^proverbs-15-23

24 The wise man’s path leads upward to life, that he may avoid Sheol beneath. ^proverbs-15-24

25 The LORD tears down the house of the proud, but maintains the widow’s boundaries. ^proverbs-15-25

26 The thoughts of the wicked are an abomination to the LORD, the words of the pure are pleasing to him. ^proverbs-15-26

27 He who is greedy for unjust gain makes trouble for his household, but he who hates bribes will live. ^proverbs-15-27

28 The mind of the righteous ponders how to answer, but the mouth of the wicked pours out evil things. ^proverbs-15-28

29 The LORD is far from the wicked, but he hears the prayer of the righteous. ^proverbs-15-29

30 The light of the eyes rejoices the heart, and good news refreshes the bones. ^proverbs-15-30

31 He whose ear heeds wholesome admonition will abide among the wise. ^proverbs-15-31

32 He who ignores instruction despises himself, but he who heeds admonition gains understanding. ^proverbs-15-32

33 The fear of the LORD is instruction in wisdom, and humility goes before honor. ^proverbs-15-33
