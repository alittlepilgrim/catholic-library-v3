# Proverbs 13 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-13|NABRE]]

1 A wise son hears his father’s instruction, but a scoffer does not listen to rebuke. ^proverbs-13-1

2 From the fruit of his mouth a good man eats good, but the desire of the treacherous is for violence. ^proverbs-13-2

3 He who guards his mouth preserves his life; he who opens wide his lips comes to ruin. ^proverbs-13-3

4 The soul of the sluggard craves, and gets nothing, while the soul of the diligent is richly supplied. ^proverbs-13-4

5 A righteous man hates falsehood, but a wicked man acts shamefully and disgracefully. ^proverbs-13-5

6 Righteousness guards him whose way is upright, but sin overthrows the wicked. ^proverbs-13-6

7 One man pretends to be rich, yet has nothing; another pretends to be poor, yet has great wealth. ^proverbs-13-7

8 The ransom of a man’s life is his wealth, but a poor man has no means of redemption. ^proverbs-13-8

9 The light of the righteous rejoices, but the lamp of the wicked will be put out. ^proverbs-13-9

10 By insolence the heedless make strife, but with those who take advice is wisdom. ^proverbs-13-10

11 Wealth hastily gotten will dwindle, but he who gathers little by little will increase it. ^proverbs-13-11

12 Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but a desire fulfilled is a tree of life. ^proverbs-13-12

13 He who despises the word brings destruction on himself, but he who respects the commandment will be rewarded. ^proverbs-13-13

14 The teaching of the wise is a fountain of life, that one may avoid the snares of death. ^proverbs-13-14

15 Good sense wins favor, but the way of the faithless is their ruin. ^proverbs-13-15

16 In everything a prudent man acts with knowledge, but a fool flaunts his folly. ^proverbs-13-16

17 A bad messenger plunges men into trouble, but a faithful envoy brings healing. ^proverbs-13-17

18 Poverty and disgrace come to him who ignores instruction, but he who heeds reproof is honored. ^proverbs-13-18

19 A desire fulfilled is sweet to the soul; but to turn away from evil is an abomination to fools. ^proverbs-13-19

20 He who walks with wise men becomes wise, but the companion of fools will suffer harm. ^proverbs-13-20

21 Misfortune pursues sinners, but prosperity rewards the righteous. ^proverbs-13-21

22 A good man leaves an inheritance to his children’s children, but the sinner’s wealth is laid up for the righteous. ^proverbs-13-22

23 The fallow ground of the poor yields much food, but it is swept away through injustice. ^proverbs-13-23

24 He who spares the rod hates his son, but he who loves him is diligent to discipline him. ^proverbs-13-24

25 The righteous has enough to satisfy his appetite, but the belly of the wicked suffers want. ^proverbs-13-25
