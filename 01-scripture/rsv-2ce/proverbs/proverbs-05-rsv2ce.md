# Proverbs 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-05|NABRE]]

**Warning against Loose Women**

1 My son, be attentive to my wisdom, incline your ear to my understanding; ^proverbs-05-1

2 that you may keep discretion, and your lips may guard knowledge. ^proverbs-05-2

3 For the lips of a loose woman drip honey, and her speech is smoother than oil; ^proverbs-05-3

4 but in the end she is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a two-edged sword. ^proverbs-05-4

5 Her feet go down to death; her steps follow the path to Sheol; ^proverbs-05-5

6 she does not take heed to the path of life; her ways wander, and she does not know it. ^proverbs-05-6

7 And now, O sons, listen to me, and do not depart from the words of my mouth. ^proverbs-05-7

8 Keep your way far from her, and do not go near the door of her house; ^proverbs-05-8

9 lest you give your honor to others and your years to the merciless; ^proverbs-05-9

10 lest strangers take their fill of your strength, and your labors go to the house of an alien; ^proverbs-05-10

11 and at the end of your life you groan, when your flesh and body are consumed, ^proverbs-05-11

12 and you say, “How I hated discipline, and my heart despised reproof! ^proverbs-05-12

13 I did not listen to the voice of my teachers or incline my ear to my instructors. ^proverbs-05-13

14 I was at the point of utter ruin in the assembled congregation.” ^proverbs-05-14

15 Drink water from your own cistern, flowing water from your own well. ^proverbs-05-15

16 Should your springs be scattered abroad, streams of water in the streets? ^proverbs-05-16

17 Let them be for yourself alone, and not for strangers with you. ^proverbs-05-17

18 Let your fountain be blessed, and rejoice in the wife of your youth, ^proverbs-05-18

19 a lovely deer, a graceful doe. Let her affection fill you at all times with delight, be infatuated always with her love. ^proverbs-05-19

20 Why should you be infatuated, my son, with a loose woman and embrace the bosom of an adventuress? ^proverbs-05-20

21 For a man’s ways are before the eyes of the LORD, and he watches all his paths. ^proverbs-05-21

22 The iniquities of the wicked ensnare him, and he is caught in the toils of his sin. ^proverbs-05-22

23 He dies for lack of discipline, and because of his great folly he is lost. ^proverbs-05-23
