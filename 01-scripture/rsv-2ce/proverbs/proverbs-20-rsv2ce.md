# Proverbs 20 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-20|NABRE]]

1 Wine is a mocker, strong drink a brawler; and whoever is led astray by it is not wise. ^proverbs-20-1

2 The dread wrath of a king is like the growling of a lion; he who provokes him to anger forfeits his life. ^proverbs-20-2

3 It is an honor for a man to keep aloof from strife; but every fool will be quarreling. ^proverbs-20-3

4 The sluggard does not plow in the autumn; he will seek at harvest and have nothing. ^proverbs-20-4

5 The purpose in a man’s mind is like deep water, but a man of understanding will draw it out. ^proverbs-20-5

6 Many a man proclaims his own loyalty, but a faithful man who can find? ^proverbs-20-6

7 A righteous man who walks in his integrity— blessed are his sons after him! ^proverbs-20-7

8 A king who sits on the throne of judgment winnows all evil with his eyes. ^proverbs-20-8

9 Who can say, “I have made my heart clean; I am pure from my sin”? ^proverbs-20-9

10 Diverse weights and diverse measures are both alike an abomination to the LORD. ^proverbs-20-10

11 Even a child makes himself known by his acts, whether what he does is pure and right. ^proverbs-20-11

12 The hearing ear and the seeing eye, the LORD has made them both. ^proverbs-20-12

13 Love not sleep, lest you come to poverty; open your eyes, and you will have plenty of bread. ^proverbs-20-13

14 “It is bad, it is bad,” says the buyer; but when he goes away, then he boasts. ^proverbs-20-14

15 There is gold, and abundance of costly stones; but the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel. ^proverbs-20-15

16 Take a man’s garment when he has given surety for a stranger, and hold him in pledge when he gives surety for foreigners. ^proverbs-20-16

17 Bread gained by deceit is sweet to a man, but afterward his mouth will be full of gravel. ^proverbs-20-17

18 Plans are established by counsel; by wise guidance wage war. ^proverbs-20-18

19 He who goes about gossiping reveals secrets; therefore do not associate with one who speaks foolishly. ^proverbs-20-19

20 If one curses his father or his mother, his lamp will be put out in utter darkness. ^proverbs-20-20

21 An inheritance gotten hastily in the beginning will in the end not be blessed. ^proverbs-20-21

22 Do not say, “I will repay evil”; wait for the LORD, and he will help you. ^proverbs-20-22

23 Diverse weights are an abomination to the LORD, and false scales are not good. ^proverbs-20-23

24 A man’s steps are ordered by the LORD; how then can man understand his way? ^proverbs-20-24

25 It is a snare for a man to say rashly, “It is holy,” and to reflect only after making his vows. ^proverbs-20-25

26 A wise king winnows the wicked, and drives the wheel over them. ^proverbs-20-26

27 The spirit of man is the lamp of the LORD, searching all his innermost parts. ^proverbs-20-27

28 Loyalty and faithfulness preserve the king and his throne is upheld by righteousness. ^proverbs-20-28

29 The glory of young men is their strength, but the beauty of old men is their gray hair. ^proverbs-20-29

30 Blows that wound cleanse away evil; strokes make clean the innermost parts. ^proverbs-20-30
