# Proverbs 14 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-14|NABRE]]

1 Wisdom builds her house, but folly with her own hands tears it down. ^proverbs-14-1

2 He who walks in uprightness fears the LORD, but he who is devious in his ways despises him. ^proverbs-14-2

3 The talk of a fool is a rod for his back, but the lips of the wise will preserve them. ^proverbs-14-3

4 Where there are no oxen, there is no grain; but abundant crops come by the strength of the ox. ^proverbs-14-4

5 A faithful witness does not lie, but a false witness breathes out lies. ^proverbs-14-5

6 A scoffer seeks wisdom in vain, but knowledge is easy for a man of understanding. ^proverbs-14-6

7 Leave the presence of a fool, for there you do not meet words of knowledge. ^proverbs-14-7

8 The wisdom of a prudent man is to discern his way, but the folly of fools is deceiving. ^proverbs-14-8

9 God scorns the wicked, but the upright enjoy his favor. ^proverbs-14-9

10 The heart knows its own bitterness, and no stranger shares its joy. ^proverbs-14-10

11 The house of the wicked will be destroyed, but the tent of the upright will flourish. ^proverbs-14-11

12 There is a way which seems right to a man, but its end is the way to death. ^proverbs-14-12

13 Even in laughter the heart is sad, and the end of joy is grief. ^proverbs-14-13

14 A perverse man will be filled with the fruit of his ways, and a good man with the fruit of his deeds. ^proverbs-14-14

15 The simple believes everything, but the prudent looks where he is going. ^proverbs-14-15

16 A wise man is cautious and turns away from evil, but a fool throws off restraint and is careless. ^proverbs-14-16

17 A man of quick temper acts foolishly, but a man of discretion is patient. ^proverbs-14-17

18 The simple acquire folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge. ^proverbs-14-18

19 The evil bow down before the good, the wicked at the gates of the righteous. ^proverbs-14-19

20 The poor is disliked even by his neighbor, but the rich has many friends. ^proverbs-14-20

21 He who despises his neighbor is a sinner, but happy is he who is kind to the poor. ^proverbs-14-21

22 Do they not err that devise evil? Those who devise good meet loyalty and faithfulness. ^proverbs-14-22

23 In all toil there is profit, but mere talk tends only to want. ^proverbs-14-23

24 The crown of the wise is their wisdom, but folly is the garland of fools. ^proverbs-14-24

25 A truthful witness saves lives, but one who utters lies is a betrayer. ^proverbs-14-25

26 In the fear of the LORD one has strong confidence, and his children will have a refuge. ^proverbs-14-26

27 The fear of the LORD is a fountain of life, that one may avoid the snares of death. ^proverbs-14-27

28 In a multitude of people is the glory of a king, but without people a prince is ruined. ^proverbs-14-28

29 He who is slow to anger has great understanding, but he who has a hasty temper exalts folly. ^proverbs-14-29

30 A tranquil mind gives life to the flesh, but passion makes the bones rot. ^proverbs-14-30

31 He who oppresses a poor man insults his Maker, but he who is kind to the needy honors him. ^proverbs-14-31

32 The wicked is overthrown through his evil-doing, but the righteous finds refuge through his integrity. ^proverbs-14-32

33 Wisdom abides in the mind of a man of understanding, but it is not known in the heart of fools. ^proverbs-14-33

34 Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a reproach to any people. ^proverbs-14-34

35 A servant who deals wisely has the king’s favor, but his wrath falls on one who acts shamefully. ^proverbs-14-35
