# Proverbs 30 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-30|NABRE]]

**Sayings of Agur**

1 The words of Agur son of Ja'keh of Mas'sa. The man says to Ith'iel, to Ithiel and U'cal: ^proverbs-30-1

2 Surely I am too stupid to be a man. I have not the understanding of a man. ^proverbs-30-2

3 I have not learned wisdom, nor have I knowledge of the Holy One. ^proverbs-30-3

4 Who has ascended to heaven and come down? Who has gathered the wind in his fists? Who has wrapped up the waters in a garment? Who has established all the ends of the earth? What is his name, and what is his son’s name? Surely you know! ^proverbs-30-4

5 Every word of God proves true; he is a shield to those who take refuge in him. ^proverbs-30-5

6 Do not add to his words, lest he rebuke you, and you be found a liar. ^proverbs-30-6

7 Two things I ask of you; deny them not to me before I die: ^proverbs-30-7

8 Remove far from me falsehood and lying; give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with the food that is needful for me, ^proverbs-30-8

9 lest I be full, and deny you, and say, “Who is the LORD?” or lest I be poor, and steal, and profane the name of my God. ^proverbs-30-9

10 Do not slander a servant to his master, lest he curse you, and you be held guilty. ^proverbs-30-10

11 There are those who curse their fathers and do not bless their mothers. ^proverbs-30-11

12 There are those who are pure in their own eyes but are not cleansed of their filth. ^proverbs-30-12

13 There are those—how lofty are their eyes, how high their eyelids lift! ^proverbs-30-13

14 There are those whose teeth are swords, whose teeth are knives, to devour the poor from off the earth, the needy from among men. ^proverbs-30-14

15 The leech has two daughters; “Give, give,” they cry. Three things are never satisfied; four never say, “Enough”: ^proverbs-30-15

16 Sheol, the barren womb, the earth ever thirsty for water, and the fire which never says, “Enough.” ^proverbs-30-16

17 The eye that mocks a father and scorns to obey a mother will be picked out by the ravens of the valley and eaten by the vultures. ^proverbs-30-17

18 Three things are too wonderful for me; four I do not understand: ^proverbs-30-18

19 the way of an eagle in the sky, the way of a serpent on a rock, the way of a ship on the high seas, and the way of a man with a maiden. ^proverbs-30-19

20 This is the way of an adulteress: she eats, and wipes her mouth, and says, “I have done no wrong.” ^proverbs-30-20

21 Under three things the earth trembles; under four it cannot bear up: ^proverbs-30-21

22 a slave when he becomes king, and a fool when he is filled with food; ^proverbs-30-22

23 an unloved woman when she gets a husband, and a maid when she succeeds her mistress. ^proverbs-30-23

24 Four things on earth are small, but they are exceedingly wise: ^proverbs-30-24

25 the ants are a people not strong, yet they provide their food in the summer; ^proverbs-30-25

26 the badgers are a people not mighty, yet they make their homes in the rocks; ^proverbs-30-26

27 the locusts have no king, yet all of them march in rank; ^proverbs-30-27

28 the lizard you can take in your hands, yet it is in kings’ palaces. ^proverbs-30-28

29 Three things are stately in their tread; four are stately in their stride: ^proverbs-30-29

30 the lion, which is mightiest among beasts and does not turn back before any; ^proverbs-30-30

31 the strutting cock, the he-goat, and a king striding before his people. ^proverbs-30-31

32 If you have been foolish, exalting yourself, or if you have been devising evil, put your hand on your mouth. ^proverbs-30-32

33 For pressing milk produces curds, pressing the nose produces blood, and pressing anger produces strife. ^proverbs-30-33
