# Proverbs 19 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-19|NABRE]]

1 Better is a poor man who walks in his integrity than a man who is perverse in speech, and is a fool. ^proverbs-19-1

2 It is not good for a man to be without knowledge, and he who makes haste with his feet misses his way. ^proverbs-19-2

3 When a man’s folly brings his way to ruin, his heart rages against the LORD. ^proverbs-19-3

4 Wealth brings many new friends, but a poor man is deserted by his friend. ^proverbs-19-4

5 A false witness will not go unpunished, and he who utters lies will not escape. ^proverbs-19-5

6 Many seek the favor of a generous man, and every one is a friend to a man who gives gifts. ^proverbs-19-6

7 All a poor man’s brothers hate him; how much more do his friends go far from him! He pursues them with words, but does not have them. ^proverbs-19-7

8 He who gets wisdom loves himself; he who keeps understanding will prosper. ^proverbs-19-8

9 A false witness will not go unpunished, and he who utters lies will perish. ^proverbs-19-9

10 It is not fitting for a fool to live in luxury, much less for a slave to rule over princes. ^proverbs-19-10

11 Good sense makes a man slow to anger, and it is his glory to overlook an offense. ^proverbs-19-11

12 A king’s wrath is like the growling of a lion, but his favor is like dew upon the grass. ^proverbs-19-12

13 A foolish son is ruin to his father, and a wife’s quarreling is a continual dripping of rain. ^proverbs-19-13

14 House and wealth are inherited from fathers, but a prudent wife is from the LORD. ^proverbs-19-14

15 Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep, and an idle person will suffer hunger. ^proverbs-19-15

16 He who keeps the commandment keeps his life; he who despises the word will die. ^proverbs-19-16

17 He who is kind to the poor lends to the LORD, and he will repay him for his deed. ^proverbs-19-17

18 Discipline your son while there is hope; do not set your heart on his destruction. ^proverbs-19-18

19 A man of great wrath will pay the penalty; for if you deliver him, you will only have to do it again. ^proverbs-19-19

20 Listen to advice and accept instruction, that you may gain wisdom for the future. ^proverbs-19-20

21 Many are the plans in the mind of a man, but it is the purpose of the LORD that will be established. ^proverbs-19-21

22 What is desired in a man is loyalty, and a poor man is better than a liar. ^proverbs-19-22

23 The fear of the LORD leads to life; and he who has it rests satisfied; he will not be visited by harm. ^proverbs-19-23

24 The sluggard buries his hand in the dish, and will not even bring it back to his mouth. ^proverbs-19-24

25 Strike a scoffer, and the simple will learn prudence; reprove a man of understanding, and he will gain knowledge. ^proverbs-19-25

26 He who does violence to his father and chases away his mother is a son who causes shame and brings reproach. ^proverbs-19-26

27 Cease, my son, to hear instruction only to stray from the words of knowledge. ^proverbs-19-27

28 A worthless witness mocks at justice, and the mouth of the wicked devours iniquity. ^proverbs-19-28

29 Condemnation is ready for scoffers, and flogging for the backs of fools. ^proverbs-19-29
