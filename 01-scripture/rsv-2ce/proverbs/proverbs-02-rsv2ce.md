# Proverbs 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-02|NABRE]]

**The Treasure of Wisdom**

1 My son, if you receive my words and treasure up my commandments with you, ^proverbs-02-1

2 making your ear attentive to wisdom and inclining your heart to understanding; ^proverbs-02-2

3 yes, if you cry out for insight and raise your voice for understanding, ^proverbs-02-3

4 if you seek it like silver and search for it as for hidden treasures; ^proverbs-02-4

5 then you will understand the fear of the LORD and find the knowledge of God. ^proverbs-02-5

6 For the LORD gives wisdom; from his mouth come knowledge and understanding; ^proverbs-02-6

7 he stores up sound wisdom for the upright; he is a shield to those who walk in integrity, ^proverbs-02-7

8 guarding the paths of justice and preserving the way of his saints. ^proverbs-02-8

9 Then you will understand righteousness and justice and equity, every good path; ^proverbs-02-9

10 for wisdom will come into your heart, and knowledge will be pleasant to your soul; ^proverbs-02-10

11 discretion will watch over you; understanding will guard you; ^proverbs-02-11

12 delivering you from the way of evil, from men of perverted speech, ^proverbs-02-12

13 who forsake the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness, ^proverbs-02-13

14 who rejoice in doing evil and delight in the perverseness of evil; ^proverbs-02-14

15 men whose paths are crooked, and who are devious in their ways. ^proverbs-02-15

16 You will be saved from the loose woman, from the adventuress with her smooth words, ^proverbs-02-16

17 who forsakes the companion of her youth and forgets the covenant of her God; ^proverbs-02-17

18 for her house sinks down to death, and her paths to the shades; ^proverbs-02-18

19 none who go to her come back nor do they regain the paths of life. ^proverbs-02-19

20 So you will walk in the way of good men and keep to the paths of the righteous. ^proverbs-02-20

21 For the upright will inhabit the land, and men of integrity will remain in it; ^proverbs-02-21

22 but the wicked will be cut off from the land, and the treacherous will be rooted out of it. ^proverbs-02-22
