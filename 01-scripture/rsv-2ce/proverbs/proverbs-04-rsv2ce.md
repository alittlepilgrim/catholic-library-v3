# Proverbs 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-04|NABRE]]

**A Father’s Advice**

1 Hear, O sons, a father’s instruction, and be attentive, that you may gain insight; ^proverbs-04-1

2 for I give you good precepts: do not forsake my teaching. ^proverbs-04-2

3 When I was a son with my father, tender, the only one in the sight of my mother, ^proverbs-04-3

4 he taught me, and said to me, “Let your heart hold fast my words; keep my commandments, and live; ^proverbs-04-4

5 do not forget, and do not turn away from the words of my mouth. Get wisdom; get insight. ^proverbs-04-5

6 Do not forsake her, and she will keep you; love her, and she will guard you. ^proverbs-04-6

7 The beginning of wisdom is this: Get wisdom, and whatever you get, get insight. ^proverbs-04-7

8 Prize her highly, and she will exalt you; she will honor you if you embrace her. ^proverbs-04-8

9 She will place on your head a fair garland; she will bestow on you a beautiful crown.” ^proverbs-04-9

10 Hear, my son, and accept my words, that the years of your life may be many. ^proverbs-04-10

11 I have taught you the way of wisdom; I have led you in the paths of uprightness. ^proverbs-04-11

12 When you walk, your step will not be hampered; and if you run, you will not stumble. ^proverbs-04-12

13 Keep hold of instruction, do not let go; guard her, for she is your life. ^proverbs-04-13

14 Do not enter the path of the wicked, and do not walk in the way of evil men. ^proverbs-04-14

15 Avoid it; do not go on it; turn away from it and pass on. ^proverbs-04-15

16 For they cannot sleep unless they have done wrong; they are robbed of sleep unless they have made someone stumble. ^proverbs-04-16

17 For they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence. ^proverbs-04-17

18 But the path of the righteous is like the light of dawn, which shines brighter and brighter until full day. ^proverbs-04-18

19 The way of the wicked is like deep darkness; they do not know over what they stumble. ^proverbs-04-19

20 My son, be attentive to my words; incline your ear to my sayings. ^proverbs-04-20

21 Let them not escape from your sight; keep them within your heart. ^proverbs-04-21

22 For they are life to him who finds them, and healing to all his flesh. ^proverbs-04-22

23 Keep your heart with all vigilance; for from it flow the springs of life. ^proverbs-04-23

24 Put away from you crooked speech, and put devious talk far from you. ^proverbs-04-24

25 Let your eyes look directly forward, and your gaze be straight before you. ^proverbs-04-25

26 Take heed to the path of your feet, then all your ways will be sure. ^proverbs-04-26

27 Do not swerve to the right or to the left; turn your foot away from evil. ^proverbs-04-27
