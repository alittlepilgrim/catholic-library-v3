# Proverbs 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-03|NABRE]]

**Exhortation to Be Wise and to Honor God**

1 My son, do not forget my teaching, but let your heart keep my commandments; ^proverbs-03-1

2 for length of days and years of life and abundant welfare will they give you. ^proverbs-03-2

3 Let not loyalty and faithfulness forsake you; bind them about your neck, write them on the tablet of your heart. ^proverbs-03-3

4 So you will find favor and good repute in the sight of God and man. ^proverbs-03-4

5 Trust in the LORD with all your heart, and do not rely on your own insight. ^proverbs-03-5

6 In all your ways acknowledge him, and he will make straight your paths. ^proverbs-03-6

7 Be not wise in your own eyes; fear the LORD, and turn away from evil. ^proverbs-03-7

8 It will be healing to your flesh and refreshment to your bones. ^proverbs-03-8

9 Honor the LORD with your substance and with the first fruits of all your produce; ^proverbs-03-9

10 then your barns will be filled with plenty, and your vats will be bursting with wine. ^proverbs-03-10

11 My son, do not despise the LORD’s discipline or be weary of his reproof, ^proverbs-03-11

12 for the LORD reproves him whom he loves, as a father the son in whom he delights. ^proverbs-03-12

13 Happy is the man who finds wisdom, and the man who gets understanding, ^proverbs-03-13

14 for the gain from it is better than gain from silver and its profit better than gold. ^proverbs-03-14

15 She is more precious than jewels, and nothing you desire can compare with her. ^proverbs-03-15

16 Long life is in her right hand; in her left hand are riches and honor. ^proverbs-03-16

17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. ^proverbs-03-17

18 She is a tree of life to those who lay hold of her; those who hold her fast are called happy. ^proverbs-03-18

19 The LORD by wisdom founded the earth; by understanding he established the heavens; ^proverbs-03-19

20 by his knowledge the deeps broke forth, and the clouds drop down the dew. ^proverbs-03-20

21 My son, keep sound wisdom and discretion; let them not escape from your sight, ^proverbs-03-21

22 and they will be life for your soul and adornment for your neck. ^proverbs-03-22

23 Then you will walk on your way securely and your foot will not stumble. ^proverbs-03-23

24 If you sit down, you will not be afraid; when you lie down, your sleep will be sweet. ^proverbs-03-24

25 Do not be afraid of sudden panic, or of the ruin of the wicked, when it comes; ^proverbs-03-25

26 for the LORD will be your confidence and will keep your foot from being caught. ^proverbs-03-26

27 Do not withhold good from those to whom it is due, when it is in your power to do it. ^proverbs-03-27

28 Do not say to your neighbor, “Go, and come again, tomorrow I will give it”—when you have it with you. ^proverbs-03-28

29 Do not plan evil against your neighbor who dwells trustingly beside you. ^proverbs-03-29

30 Do not contend with a man for no reason, when he has done you no harm. ^proverbs-03-30

31 Do not envy a man of violence and do not choose any of his ways; ^proverbs-03-31

32 for the perverse man is an abomination to the LORD, but the upright are in his confidence. ^proverbs-03-32

33 The LORD’s curse is on the house of the wicked, but he blesses the abode of the righteous. ^proverbs-03-33

34 Toward the scorners he is scornful, but to the humble he shows favor. ^proverbs-03-34

35 The wise will inherit honor, but fools get disgrace. ^proverbs-03-35
