# Proverbs 26 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-26|NABRE]]

1 Like snow in summer or rain in harvest, so honor is not fitting for a fool. ^proverbs-26-1

2 Like a sparrow in its flitting, like a swallow in its flying, a curse that is causeless does not alight. ^proverbs-26-2

3 A whip for the horse, a bridle for the donkey, and a rod for the back of fools. ^proverbs-26-3

4 Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest you be like him yourself. ^proverbs-26-4

5 Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own eyes. ^proverbs-26-5

6 He who sends a message by the hand of a fool cuts off his own feet and drinks violence. ^proverbs-26-6

7 Like a lame man’s legs, which hang useless, is a proverb in the mouth of fools. ^proverbs-26-7

8 Like one who binds the stone in the sling is he who gives honor to a fool. ^proverbs-26-8

9 Like a thorn that goes up into the hand of a drunkard is a proverb in the mouth of fools. ^proverbs-26-9

10 Like an archer who wounds everybody is he who hires a passing fool or drunkard. ^proverbs-26-10

11 Like a dog that returns to his vomit is a fool who repeats his folly. ^proverbs-26-11

12 Do you see a man who is wise in his own eyes? There is more hope for a fool than for him. ^proverbs-26-12

13 The sluggard says, “There is a lion in the road! There is a lion in the streets!” ^proverbs-26-13

14 As a door turns on its hinges, so does a sluggard on his bed. ^proverbs-26-14

15 The sluggard buries his hand in the dish; it wears him out to bring it back to his mouth. ^proverbs-26-15

16 The sluggard is wiser in his own eyes than seven men who can answer discreetly. ^proverbs-26-16

17 He who meddles in a quarrel not his own is like one who takes a passing dog by the ears. ^proverbs-26-17

18 Like a madman who throws firebrands, arrows, and death, ^proverbs-26-18

19 is the man who deceives his neighbor and says, “I am only joking!” ^proverbs-26-19

20 For lack of wood the fire goes out; and where there is no whisperer, quarreling ceases. ^proverbs-26-20

21 As charcoal to hot embers and wood to fire, so is a quarrelsome man for kindling strife. ^proverbs-26-21

22 The words of a whisperer are like delicious morsels; they go down into the inner parts of the body. ^proverbs-26-22

23 Like the glaze covering an earthen vessel are smooth lips with an evil heart. ^proverbs-26-23

24 He who hates, dissembles with his lips and harbors deceit in his heart; ^proverbs-26-24

25 when he speaks graciously, believe him not, for there are seven abominations in his heart; ^proverbs-26-25

26 though his hatred be covered with guile, his wickedness will be exposed in the assembly. ^proverbs-26-26

27 He who digs a pit will fall into it, and a stone will come back upon him who starts it rolling. ^proverbs-26-27

28 A lying tongue hates its victims, and a flattering mouth works ruin. ^proverbs-26-28
