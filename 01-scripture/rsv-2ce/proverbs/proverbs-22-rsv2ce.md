# Proverbs 22 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-22|NABRE]]

1 A good name is to be chosen rather than great riches, and favor is better than silver or gold. ^proverbs-22-1

2 The rich and the poor meet together; the LORD is the maker of them all. ^proverbs-22-2

3 A prudent man sees danger and hides himself; but the simple go on, and suffer for it. ^proverbs-22-3

4 The reward for humility and fear of the LORD is riches and honor and life. ^proverbs-22-4

5 Thorns and snares are in the way of the perverse; he who guards himself will keep far from them. ^proverbs-22-5

6 Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it. ^proverbs-22-6

7 The rich rules over the poor, and the borrower is the slave of the lender. ^proverbs-22-7

8 He who sows injustice will reap calamity, and the rod of his fury will fail. ^proverbs-22-8

9 He who has a bountiful eye will be blessed, for he shares his bread with the poor. ^proverbs-22-9

10 Drive out a scoffer, and strife will go out, and quarreling and abuse will cease. ^proverbs-22-10

11 He who loves purity of heart, and whose speech is gracious, will have the king as his friend. ^proverbs-22-11

12 The eyes of the LORD keep watch over knowledge, but he overthrows the words of the faithless. ^proverbs-22-12

13 The sluggard says, “There is a lion outside! I shall be slain in the streets!” ^proverbs-22-13

14 The mouth of a loose woman is a deep pit; he with whom the LORD is angry will fall into it. ^proverbs-22-14

15 Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, but the rod of discipline drives it far from him. ^proverbs-22-15

16 He who oppresses the poor to increase his own wealth, or gives to the rich, will only come to want. ^proverbs-22-16

**Sayings of the Wise**

17 Incline your ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply your mind to my knowledge; ^proverbs-22-17

18 for it will be pleasant if you keep them within you, if all of them are ready on your lips. ^proverbs-22-18

19 That your trust may be in the LORD, I have made them known to you today, even to you. ^proverbs-22-19

20 Have I not written for you thirty sayings of admonition and knowledge, ^proverbs-22-20

21 to show you what is right and true, that you may give a true answer to those who sent you? ^proverbs-22-21

22 Do not rob the poor, because he is poor, or crush the afflicted at the gate; ^proverbs-22-22

23 for the LORD will plead their cause and despoil of life those who despoil them. ^proverbs-22-23

24 Make no friendship with a man given to anger, nor go with a wrathful man, ^proverbs-22-24

25 lest you learn his ways and entangle yourself in a snare. ^proverbs-22-25

26 Be not one of those who give pledges, who become surety for debts. ^proverbs-22-26

27 If you have nothing with which to pay, why should your bed be taken from under you? ^proverbs-22-27

28 Remove not the ancient landmark which your fathers have set. ^proverbs-22-28

29 Do you see a man skilful in his work? he will stand before kings; he will not stand before obscure men. ^proverbs-22-29
