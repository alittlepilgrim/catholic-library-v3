# Proverbs 31 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-31|NABRE]]

**Sayings of Lemuel’s Mother; Praise of Good Wife**

1 The words of Lem'uel, king of Massa, which his mother taught him: ^proverbs-31-1

2 What, my son? What, son of my womb? What, son of my vows? ^proverbs-31-2

3 Give not your strength to women, your ways to those who destroy kings. ^proverbs-31-3

4 It is not for kings, O Lem'uel, it is not for kings to drink wine, or for rulers to desire strong drink; ^proverbs-31-4

5 lest they drink and forget what has been decreed, and pervert the rights of all the afflicted. ^proverbs-31-5

6 Give strong drink to him who is perishing, and wine to those in bitter distress; ^proverbs-31-6

7 let them drink and forget their poverty, and remember their misery no more. ^proverbs-31-7

8 Open your mouth for the mute, for the rights of all who are left desolate. ^proverbs-31-8

9 Open your mouth, judge righteously, maintain the rights of the poor and needy. ^proverbs-31-9

10 Who can find a good wife? She is far more precious than jewels. ^proverbs-31-10

11 The heart of her husband trusts in her, and he will have no lack of gain. ^proverbs-31-11

12 She does him good, and not harm, all the days of her life. ^proverbs-31-12

13 She seeks wool and flax, and works with willing hands. ^proverbs-31-13

14 She is like the ships of the merchant, she brings her food from afar. ^proverbs-31-14

15 She rises while it is yet night and provides food for her household and tasks for her maidens. ^proverbs-31-15

16 She considers a field and buys it; with the fruit of her hands she plants a vineyard. ^proverbs-31-16

17 She clothes her loins with strength and makes her arms strong. ^proverbs-31-17

18 She perceives that her merchandise is profitable. Her lamp does not go out at night. ^proverbs-31-18

19 She puts her hands to the distaff, and her hands hold the spindle. ^proverbs-31-19

20 She opens her hand to the poor, and reaches out her hands to the needy. ^proverbs-31-20

21 She is not afraid of snow for her household, for all her household are clothed in scarlet. ^proverbs-31-21

22 She makes herself coverings; her clothing is fine linen and purple. ^proverbs-31-22

23 Her husband is known in the gates, when he sits among the elders of the land. ^proverbs-31-23

24 She makes linen garments and sells them; she delivers sashes to the merchant. ^proverbs-31-24

25 Strength and dignity are her clothing, and she laughs at the time to come. ^proverbs-31-25

26 She opens her mouth with wisdom, and the teaching of kindness is on her tongue. ^proverbs-31-26

27 She looks well to the ways of her household, and does not eat the bread of idleness. ^proverbs-31-27

28 Her children rise up and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praises her: ^proverbs-31-28

29 “Many women have done excellently, but you surpass them all.” ^proverbs-31-29

30 Charm is deceitful, and beauty is vain, but a woman who fears the LORD is to be praised. ^proverbs-31-30

31 Give her of the fruit of her hands, and let her works praise her in the gates. ^proverbs-31-31
