# Proverbs 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-07|NABRE]]

**False Attractions of Adultery**

1 My son, keep my words and treasure up my commandments with you; ^proverbs-07-1

2 keep my commandments and live, keep my teachings as the apple of your eye; ^proverbs-07-2

3 bind them on your fingers, write them on the tablet of your heart. ^proverbs-07-3

4 Say to wisdom, “You are my sister,” and call insight your intimate friend; ^proverbs-07-4

5 to preserve you from the loose woman, from the adventuress with her smooth words. ^proverbs-07-5

6 For at the window of my house I have looked out through my lattice, ^proverbs-07-6

7 and I have seen among the simple, I have perceived among the youths, a young man without sense, ^proverbs-07-7

8 passing along the street near her corner, taking the road to her house ^proverbs-07-8

9 in the twilight, in the evening, at the time of night and darkness. ^proverbs-07-9

10 And behold, a woman meets him, dressed as a harlot, wily of heart. ^proverbs-07-10

11 She is loud and wayward, her feet do not stay at home; ^proverbs-07-11

12 now in the street, now in the market, and at every corner she lies in wait. ^proverbs-07-12

13 She seizes him and kisses him, and with impudent face she says to him: ^proverbs-07-13

14 “I had to offer sacrifices, and today I have paid my vows; ^proverbs-07-14

15 so now I have come out to meet you, to seek you eagerly, and I have found you. ^proverbs-07-15

16 I have decked my couch with coverings, colored spreads of Egyptian linen; ^proverbs-07-16

17 I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. ^proverbs-07-17

18 Come, let us take our fill of love till morning; let us delight ourselves with love. ^proverbs-07-18

19 For my husband is not at home; he has gone on a long journey; ^proverbs-07-19

20 he took a bag of money with him; at full moon he will come home.” ^proverbs-07-20

21 With much seductive speech she persuades him; with her smooth talk she compels him. ^proverbs-07-21

22 All at once he follows her, as an ox goes to the slaughter, or as a stag is caught fast ^proverbs-07-22

23 till an arrow pierces its entrails; as a bird rushes into a snare; he does not know that it will cost him his life. ^proverbs-07-23

24 And now, O sons, listen to me, and be attentive to the words of my mouth. ^proverbs-07-24

25 Let not your heart turn aside to her ways, do not stray into her paths; ^proverbs-07-25

26 for many a victim has she laid low; yes, all her slain are a mighty host. ^proverbs-07-26

27 Her house is the way to Sheol, going down to the chambers of death. ^proverbs-07-27
