# Proverbs 16 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-16|NABRE]]

**Solomon’s Proverbs on Life and Conduct**

1 The plans of the mind belong to man, but the answer of the tongue is from the LORD. ^proverbs-16-1

2 All the ways of a man are pure in his own eyes, but the LORD weighs the spirit. ^proverbs-16-2

3 Commit your work to the LORD, and your plans will be established. ^proverbs-16-3

4 The LORD has made everything for its purpose, even the wicked for the day of trouble. ^proverbs-16-4

5 Every one who is arrogant is an abomination to the LORD; be assured, he will not go unpunished. ^proverbs-16-5

6 By loyalty and faithfulness iniquity is atoned for, and by the fear of the LORD a man avoids evil. ^proverbs-16-6

7 When a man’s ways please the LORD, he makes even his enemies to be at peace with him. ^proverbs-16-7

8 Better is a little with righteousness than great revenues with injustice. ^proverbs-16-8

9 A man’s mind plans his way, but the LORD directs his steps. ^proverbs-16-9

10 Inspired decisions are on the lips of a king; his mouth does not sin in judgment. ^proverbs-16-10

11 A just balance and scales are the LORD’s; all the weights in the bag are his work. ^proverbs-16-11

12 It is an abomination to kings to do evil, for the throne is established by righteousness. ^proverbs-16-12

13 Righteous lips are the delight of a king, and he loves him who speaks what is right. ^proverbs-16-13

14 A king’s wrath is a messenger of death, and a wise man will appease it. ^proverbs-16-14

15 In the light of a king’s face there is life, and his favor is like the clouds that bring the spring rain. ^proverbs-16-15

16 To get wisdom is better than gold; to get understanding is to be chosen rather than silver. ^proverbs-16-16

17 The highway of the upright turns aside from evil; he who guards his way preserves his life. ^proverbs-16-17

18 Pride goes before destruction, and a haughty spirit before a fall. ^proverbs-16-18

19 It is better to be of a lowly spirit with the poor than to divide the spoil with the proud. ^proverbs-16-19

20 He who gives heed to the word will prosper, and happy is he who trusts in the LORD. ^proverbs-16-20

21 The wise of heart is called a man of discernment, and pleasant speech increases persuasiveness. ^proverbs-16-21

22 Wisdom is a fountain of life to him who has it, but folly is the chastisement of fools. ^proverbs-16-22

23 The mind of the wise makes his speech judicious, and adds persuasiveness to his lips. ^proverbs-16-23

24 Pleasant words are like a honeycomb, sweetness to the soul and health to the body. ^proverbs-16-24

25 There is a way which seems right to a man, but its end is the way to death. ^proverbs-16-25

26 A worker’s appetite works for him; his mouth urges him on. ^proverbs-16-26

27 A worthless man plots evil, and his speech is like a scorching fire. ^proverbs-16-27

28 A perverse man spreads strife, and a whisperer separates close friends. ^proverbs-16-28

29 A man of violence entices his neighbor and leads him in a way that is not good. ^proverbs-16-29

30 He who winks his eyes plans perverse things, he who compresses his lips brings evil to pass. ^proverbs-16-30

31 A hoary head is a crown of glory; it is gained in a righteous life. ^proverbs-16-31

32 He who is slow to anger is better than the mighty, and he who rules his spirit than he who takes a city. ^proverbs-16-32

33 The lot is cast into the lap, but the decision is wholly from the LORD. ^proverbs-16-33
