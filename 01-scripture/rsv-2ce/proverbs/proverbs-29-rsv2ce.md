# Proverbs 29 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-29|NABRE]]

1 He who is often reproved, yet stiffens his neck, will suddenly be broken beyond healing. ^proverbs-29-1

2 When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice; but when the wicked rule, the people groan. ^proverbs-29-2

3 He who loves wisdom makes his father glad, but one who keeps company with harlots squanders his substance. ^proverbs-29-3

4 By justice a king gives stability to the land, but one who exacts gifts ruins it. ^proverbs-29-4

5 A man who flatters his neighbor spreads a net for his feet. ^proverbs-29-5

6 An evil man is ensnared in his transgression, but a righteous man sings and rejoices. ^proverbs-29-6

7 A righteous man knows the rights of the poor; a wicked man does not understand such knowledge. ^proverbs-29-7

8 Scoffers set a city aflame, but wise men turn away wrath. ^proverbs-29-8

9 If a wise man has an argument with a fool, the fool only rages and laughs, and there is no quiet. ^proverbs-29-9

10 Bloodthirsty men hate one who is blameless, and the wicked seek his life. ^proverbs-29-10

11 A fool gives full vent to his anger, but a wise man quietly holds it back. ^proverbs-29-11

12 If a ruler listens to falsehood, all his officials will be wicked. ^proverbs-29-12

13 The poor man and the oppressor meet together; the LORD gives light to the eyes of both. ^proverbs-29-13

14 If a king judges the poor with equity his throne will be established for ever. ^proverbs-29-14

15 The rod and reproof give wisdom, but a child left to himself brings shame to his mother. ^proverbs-29-15

16 When the wicked are in authority, transgression increases; but the righteous will look upon their downfall. ^proverbs-29-16

17 Discipline your son, and he will give you rest; he will give delight to your heart. ^proverbs-29-17

18 Where there is no prophecy the people cast off restraint, but blessed is he who keeps the law. ^proverbs-29-18

19 By mere words a servant is not disciplined, for though he understands, he will not give heed. ^proverbs-29-19

20 Do you see a man who is hasty in his words? There is more hope for a fool than for him. ^proverbs-29-20

21 He who pampers his servant from childhood, will in the end find him his heir. ^proverbs-29-21

22 A man of wrath stirs up strife, and a man given to anger causes much transgression. ^proverbs-29-22

23 A man’s pride will bring him low, but he who is lowly in spirit will obtain honor. ^proverbs-29-23

24 The partner of a thief hates his own life; he hears the curse, but discloses nothing. ^proverbs-29-24

25 The fear of man lays a snare, but he who trusts in the LORD is safe. ^proverbs-29-25

26 Many seek the favor of a ruler, but from the LORD a man gets justice. ^proverbs-29-26

27 An unjust man is an abomination to the righteous, but he whose way is straight is an abomination to the wicked. ^proverbs-29-27
