# Proverbs 11 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-11|NABRE]]

1 A false balance is an abomination to the LORD, but a just weight is his delight. ^proverbs-11-1

2 When pride comes, then comes disgrace; but with the humble is wisdom. ^proverbs-11-2

3 The integrity of the upright guides them, but the crookedness of the treacherous destroys them. ^proverbs-11-3

4 Riches do not profit in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivers from death. ^proverbs-11-4

5 The righteousness of the blameless keeps his way straight, but the wicked falls by his own wickedness. ^proverbs-11-5

6 The righteousness of the upright delivers them, but the treacherous are taken captive by their lust. ^proverbs-11-6

7 When the wicked dies, his hope perishes, and the expectation of the godless comes to nothing. ^proverbs-11-7

8 The righteous is delivered from trouble, and the wicked gets into it instead. ^proverbs-11-8

9 With his mouth the godless man would destroy his neighbor, but by knowledge the righteous are delivered. ^proverbs-11-9

10 When it goes well with the righteous, the city rejoices; and when the wicked perish there are shouts of gladness. ^proverbs-11-10

11 By the blessing of the upright a city is exalted, but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked. ^proverbs-11-11

12 He who belittles his neighbor lacks sense, but a man of understanding remains silent. ^proverbs-11-12

13 He who goes about as a talebearer reveals secrets, but he who is trustworthy in spirit keeps a thing hidden. ^proverbs-11-13

14 Where there is no guidance, a people falls; but in an abundance of counselors there is safety. ^proverbs-11-14

15 He who gives surety for a stranger will smart for it, but he who hates suretyship is secure. ^proverbs-11-15

16 A gracious woman gets honor, and violent men get riches. ^proverbs-11-16

17 A man who is kind benefits himself, but a cruel man hurts himself. ^proverbs-11-17

18 A wicked man earns deceptive wages, but one who sows righteousness gets a sure reward. ^proverbs-11-18

19 He who is steadfast in righteousness will live, but he who pursues evil will die. ^proverbs-11-19

20 Men of perverse mind are an abomination to the LORD, but those of blameless ways are his delight. ^proverbs-11-20

21 Be assured, an evil man will not go unpunished, but those who are righteous will be delivered. ^proverbs-11-21

22 Like a gold ring in a swine’s snout is a beautiful woman without discretion. ^proverbs-11-22

23 The desire of the righteous ends only in good; the expectation of the wicked in wrath. ^proverbs-11-23

24 One man gives freely, yet grows all the richer; another withholds what he should give, and only suffers want. ^proverbs-11-24

25 A liberal man will be enriched, and one who waters will himself be watered. ^proverbs-11-25

26 The people curse him who holds back grain, but a blessing is on the head of him who sells it. ^proverbs-11-26

27 He who diligently seeks good seeks favor, but evil comes to him who searches for it. ^proverbs-11-27

28 He who trusts in his riches will wither, but the righteous will flourish like a green leaf. ^proverbs-11-28

29 He who troubles his household will inherit wind, and the fool will be servant to the wise. ^proverbs-11-29

30 The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life, but lawlessness takes away lives. ^proverbs-11-30

31 If the righteous is repaid on earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner! ^proverbs-11-31
