# Proverbs 18 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-18|NABRE]]

1 He who is estranged seeks pretexts to break out against all sound judgment. ^proverbs-18-1

2 A fool takes no pleasure in understanding, but only in expressing his opinion. ^proverbs-18-2

3 When wickedness comes, contempt comes also; and with dishonor comes disgrace. ^proverbs-18-3

4 The words of a man’s mouth are deep waters; the fountain of wisdom is a gushing stream. ^proverbs-18-4

5 It is not good to be partial to a wicked man, or to deprive a righteous man of justice. ^proverbs-18-5

6 A fool’s lips bring strife, and his mouth invites a flogging. ^proverbs-18-6

7 A fool’s mouth is his ruin, and his lips are a snare to himself. ^proverbs-18-7

8 The words of a whisperer are like delicious morsels; they go down into the inner parts of the body. ^proverbs-18-8

9 He who is slack in his work is a brother to him who destroys. ^proverbs-18-9

10 The name of the LORD is a strong tower; the righteous man runs into it and is safe. ^proverbs-18-10

11 A rich man’s wealth is his strong city, and like a high wall protecting him. ^proverbs-18-11

12 Before destruction a man’s heart is haughty, but humility goes before honor. ^proverbs-18-12

13 If one gives answer before he hears, it is his folly and shame. ^proverbs-18-13

14 A man’s spirit will endure sickness; but a broken spirit who can bear? ^proverbs-18-14

15 An intelligent mind acquires knowledge, and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge. ^proverbs-18-15

16 A man’s gift makes room for him and brings him before great men. ^proverbs-18-16

17 He who states his case first seems right, until the other comes and examines him. ^proverbs-18-17

18 The lot puts an end to disputes and decides between powerful contenders. ^proverbs-18-18

19 A brother helped is like a strong city, but quarreling is like the bars of a castle. ^proverbs-18-19

20 From the fruit of his mouth a man is satisfied; he is satisfied by the yield of his lips. ^proverbs-18-20

21 Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and those who love it will eat its fruits. ^proverbs-18-21

22 He who finds a wife finds a good thing, and obtains favor from the LORD. ^proverbs-18-22

23 The poor use entreaties, but the rich answer roughly. ^proverbs-18-23

24 There are friends who pretend to be friends, but there is a friend who sticks closer than a brother. ^proverbs-18-24
