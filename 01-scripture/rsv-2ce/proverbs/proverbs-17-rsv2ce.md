# Proverbs 17 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-17|NABRE]]

1 Better is a dry morsel with quiet than a house full of feasting with strife. ^proverbs-17-1

2 A slave who deals wisely will rule over a son who acts shamefully, and will share the inheritance as one of the brothers. ^proverbs-17-2

3 The crucible is for silver, and the furnace is for gold, and the LORD tries hearts. ^proverbs-17-3

4 An evildoer listens to wicked lips; and a liar gives heed to a mischievous tongue. ^proverbs-17-4

5 He who mocks the poor insults his Maker; he who is glad at calamity will not go unpunished. ^proverbs-17-5

6 Grandchildren are the crown of the aged, and the glory of sons is their fathers. ^proverbs-17-6

7 Fine speech is not becoming to a fool; still less is false speech to a prince. ^proverbs-17-7

8 A bribe is like a magic stone in the eyes of him who gives it; wherever he turns he prospers. ^proverbs-17-8

9 He who forgives an offense seeks love, but he who repeats a matter alienates a friend. ^proverbs-17-9

10 A rebuke goes deeper into a man of understanding than a hundred blows into a fool. ^proverbs-17-10

11 An evil man seeks only rebellion, and a cruel messenger will be sent against him. ^proverbs-17-11

12 Let a man meet a she-bear robbed of her cubs, rather than a fool in his folly. ^proverbs-17-12

13 If a man returns evil for good, evil will not depart from his house. ^proverbs-17-13

14 The beginning of strife is like letting out water; so quit before the quarrel breaks out. ^proverbs-17-14

15 He who justifies the wicked and he who condemns the righteous are both alike an abomination to the LORD. ^proverbs-17-15

16 Why should a fool have a price in his hand to buy wisdom, when he has no mind? ^proverbs-17-16

17 A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity. ^proverbs-17-17

18 A man without sense gives a pledge, and becomes surety in the presence of his neighbor. ^proverbs-17-18

19 He who loves transgression loves strife; he who makes his door high seeks destruction. ^proverbs-17-19

20 A man of crooked mind does not prosper, and one with a perverse tongue falls into calamity. ^proverbs-17-20

21 A stupid son is a grief to a father; and the father of a fool has no joy. ^proverbs-17-21

22 A cheerful heart is a good medicine, but a downcast spirit dries up the bones. ^proverbs-17-22

23 A wicked man accepts a bribe from the bosom to pervert the ways of justice. ^proverbs-17-23

24 A man of understanding sets his face toward wisdom, but the eyes of a fool are on the ends of the earth. ^proverbs-17-24

25 A foolish son is a grief to his father and bitterness to her who bore him. ^proverbs-17-25

26 To impose a fine on a righteous man is not good; to flog noble men is wrong. ^proverbs-17-26

27 He who restrains his words has knowledge, and he who has a cool spirit is a man of understanding. ^proverbs-17-27

28 Even a fool who keeps silent is considered wise; when he closes his lips, he is deemed intelligent. ^proverbs-17-28
