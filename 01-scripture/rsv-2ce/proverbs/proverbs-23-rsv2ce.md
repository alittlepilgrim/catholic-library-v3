# Proverbs 23 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-23|NABRE]]

**Precepts and Warnings**

1 When you sit down to eat with a ruler, observe carefully what is before you; ^proverbs-23-1

2 and put a knife to your throat if you are a man given to appetite. ^proverbs-23-2

3 Do not desire his delicacies, for they are deceptive food. ^proverbs-23-3

4 Do not toil to acquire wealth; be wise enough to desist. ^proverbs-23-4

5 When your eyes light upon it, it is gone; for suddenly it takes to itself wings, flying like an eagle toward heaven. ^proverbs-23-5

6 Do not eat the bread of a man who is stingy; do not desire his delicacies; ^proverbs-23-6

7 for he is like one who is inwardly reckoning. “Eat and drink!” he says to you; but his heart is not with you. ^proverbs-23-7

8 You will vomit up the morsels which you have eaten, and waste your pleasant words. ^proverbs-23-8

9 Do not speak in the hearing of a fool, for he will despise the wisdom of your words. ^proverbs-23-9

10 Do not remove an ancient landmark or enter the fields of the fatherless; ^proverbs-23-10

11 for their Redeemer is strong; he will plead their cause against you. ^proverbs-23-11

12 Apply your mind to instruction and your ear to words of knowledge. ^proverbs-23-12

13 Do not withhold discipline from a child; if you beat him with a rod, he will not die. ^proverbs-23-13

14 If you beat him with the rod you will save his life from Sheol. ^proverbs-23-14

15 My son, if your heart is wise, my heart too will be glad. ^proverbs-23-15

16 My soul will rejoice when your lips speak what is right. ^proverbs-23-16

17 Let not your heart envy sinners, but continue in the fear of the LORD all the day. ^proverbs-23-17

18 Surely there is a future, and your hope will not be cut off. ^proverbs-23-18

19 Hear, my son, and be wise, and direct your mind in the way. ^proverbs-23-19

20 Be not among winebibbers, or among gluttonous eaters of meat; ^proverbs-23-20

21 for the drunkard and the glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness will clothe a man with rags. ^proverbs-23-21

22 Listen to your father who begot you, and do not despise your mother when she is old. ^proverbs-23-22

23 Buy truth, and do not sell it; buy wisdom, instruction, and understanding. ^proverbs-23-23

24 The father of the righteous will greatly rejoice; he who begets a wise son will be glad in him. ^proverbs-23-24

25 Let your father and mother be glad, let her who bore you rejoice. ^proverbs-23-25

26 My son, give me your heart, and let your eyes observe my ways. ^proverbs-23-26

27 For a harlot is a deep pit; an adventuress is a narrow well. ^proverbs-23-27

28 She lies in wait like a robber and increases the faithless among men. ^proverbs-23-28

29 Who has woe? Who has sorrow? Who has strife? Who has complaining? Who has wounds without cause? Who has redness of eyes? ^proverbs-23-29

30 Those who tarry long over wine, those who go to try mixed wine. ^proverbs-23-30

31 Do not look at wine when it is red, when it sparkles in the cup and goes down smoothly. ^proverbs-23-31

32 At the last it bites like a serpent, and stings like an adder. ^proverbs-23-32

33 Your eyes will see strange things, and your mind utter perverse things. ^proverbs-23-33

34 You will be like one who lies down in the midst of the sea, like one who lies on the top of a mast. ^proverbs-23-34

35 “They struck me,” you will say, “but I was not hurt; they beat me, but I did not feel it. When shall I awake? I will seek another drink.” ^proverbs-23-35
