# Proverbs 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-06|NABRE]]

**Practical Admonitions and Warnings**

1 My son, if you have become surety for your neighbor, have given your pledge for a stranger; ^proverbs-06-1

2 if you are snared in the utterance of your lips, caught in the words of your mouth; ^proverbs-06-2

3 then do this, my son, and save yourself, for you have come into your neighbor’s power: go, hasten, and importune your neighbor. ^proverbs-06-3

4 Give your eyes no sleep and your eyelids no slumber; ^proverbs-06-4

5 save yourself like a gazelle from the hunter, like a bird from the hand of the fowler. ^proverbs-06-5

6 Go to the ant, O sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise. ^proverbs-06-6

7 Without having any chief, officer or ruler, ^proverbs-06-7

8 she prepares her food in summer, and gathers her sustenance in harvest. ^proverbs-06-8

9 How long will you lie there, O sluggard? When will you arise from your sleep? ^proverbs-06-9

10 A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to rest, ^proverbs-06-10

11 and poverty will come upon you like a vagabond, and want like an armed man. ^proverbs-06-11

12 A worthless person, a wicked man, goes about with crooked speech, ^proverbs-06-12

13 winks with his eyes, scrapes with his feet, points with his finger, ^proverbs-06-13

14 with perverted heart devises evil, continually sowing discord; ^proverbs-06-14

15 therefore calamity will come upon him suddenly; in a moment he will be broken beyond healing. ^proverbs-06-15

16 There are six things which the LORD hates, seven which are an abomination to him: ^proverbs-06-16

17 haughty eyes, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood, ^proverbs-06-17

18 a heart that devises wicked plans, feet that make haste to run to evil, ^proverbs-06-18

19 a false witness who breathes out lies, and a man who sows discord among brothers. ^proverbs-06-19

20 My son, keep your father’s commandment, and forsake not your mother’s teaching. ^proverbs-06-20

21 Bind them upon your heart always; tie them about your neck. ^proverbs-06-21

22 When you walk, they will lead you; when you lie down, they will watch over you; and when you awake, they will talk with you. ^proverbs-06-22

23 For the commandment is a lamp and the teaching a light, and the reproofs of discipline are the way of life, ^proverbs-06-23

24 to preserve you from the evil woman, from the smooth tongue of the adventuress. ^proverbs-06-24

25 Do not desire her beauty in your heart, and do not let her capture you with her eyelashes; ^proverbs-06-25

26 for a harlot may be hired for a loaf of bread, but an adulteress stalks a man’s very life. ^proverbs-06-26

27 Can a man carry fire in his bosom and his clothes not be burned? ^proverbs-06-27

28 Or can one walk upon hot coals and his feet not be scorched? ^proverbs-06-28

29 So is he who goes in to his neighbor’s wife; none who touches her will go unpunished. ^proverbs-06-29

30 Men do not despise a thief if he steals to satisfy his appetite when he is hungry. ^proverbs-06-30

31 And if he is caught, he will pay sevenfold; he will give all the goods of his house. ^proverbs-06-31

32 He who commits adultery has no sense; he who does it destroys himself. ^proverbs-06-32

33 Wounds and dishonor will he get, and his disgrace will not be wiped away. ^proverbs-06-33

34 For jealousy makes a man furious, and he will not spare when he takes revenge. ^proverbs-06-34

35 He will accept no compensation, nor be appeased though you multiply gifts. ^proverbs-06-35
