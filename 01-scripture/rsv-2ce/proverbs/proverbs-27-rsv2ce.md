# Proverbs 27 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-27|NABRE]]

1 Do not boast about tomorrow, for you do not know what a day may bring forth. ^proverbs-27-1

2 Let another praise you, and not your own mouth; a stranger, and not your own lips. ^proverbs-27-2

3 A stone is heavy, and sand is weighty, but a fool’s provocation is heavier than both. ^proverbs-27-3

4 Wrath is cruel, anger is overwhelming; but who can stand before jealousy? ^proverbs-27-4

5 Better is open rebuke than hidden love. ^proverbs-27-5

6 Faithful are the wounds of a friend; profuse are the kisses of an enemy. ^proverbs-27-6

7 He who is sated loathes honey, but to one who is hungry everything bitter is sweet. ^proverbs-27-7

8 Like a bird that strays from its nest, is a man who strays from his home. ^proverbs-27-8

9 Oil and perfume make the heart glad, but the soul is torn by trouble. ^proverbs-27-9

10 Your friend, and your father’s friend, do not forsake; and do not go to your brother’s house in the day of your calamity. Better is a neighbor who is near than a brother who is far away. ^proverbs-27-10

11 Be wise, my son, and make my heart glad, that I may answer him who reproaches me. ^proverbs-27-11

12 A prudent man sees danger and hides himself; but the simple go on, and suffer for it. ^proverbs-27-12

13 Take a man’s garment when he has given surety for a stranger, and hold him in pledge when he gives surety for foreigners. ^proverbs-27-13

14 He who blesses his neighbor with a loud voice, rising early in the morning, will be counted as cursing. ^proverbs-27-14

15 A continual dripping on a rainy day and a contentious woman are alike; ^proverbs-27-15

16 to restrain her is to restrain the wind or to grasp oil in one’s right hand. ^proverbs-27-16

17 Iron sharpens iron, and one man sharpens another. ^proverbs-27-17

18 He who tends a fig tree will eat its fruit, and he who guards his master will be honored. ^proverbs-27-18

19 As in water face answers to face, so the mind of man reflects the man. ^proverbs-27-19

20 Sheol and Abad'don are never satisfied, and never satisfied are the eyes of man. ^proverbs-27-20

21 The crucible is for silver, and the furnace is for gold, and a man is judged by his praise. ^proverbs-27-21

22 Crush a fool in a mortar with a pestle along with crushed grain, yet his folly will not depart from him. ^proverbs-27-22

23 Know well the condition of your flocks, and give attention to your herds; ^proverbs-27-23

24 for riches do not last for ever; and does a crown endure to all generations? ^proverbs-27-24

25 When the grass is gone, and the new growth appears, and the herbage of the mountains is gathered, ^proverbs-27-25

26 the lambs will provide your clothing, and the goats the price of a field; ^proverbs-27-26

27 there will be enough goats’ milk for your food, for the food of your household and maintenance for your maidens. ^proverbs-27-27
