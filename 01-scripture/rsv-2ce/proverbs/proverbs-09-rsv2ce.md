# Proverbs 9 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[proverbs-09|NABRE]]

**The Feast of Wisdom; and General Maxims**

1 Wisdom has built her house, she has set up her seven pillars. ^proverbs-09-1

2 She has slaughtered her beasts, she has mixed her wine, she has also set her table. ^proverbs-09-2

3 She has sent out her maids to call from the highest places in the town, ^proverbs-09-3

4 “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” To him who is without sense she says, ^proverbs-09-4

5 “Come, eat of my bread and drink of the wine I have mixed. ^proverbs-09-5

6 Leave simpleness, and live, and walk in the way of insight.” ^proverbs-09-6

7 He who corrects a scoffer gets himself abuse, and he who reproves a wicked man incurs injury. ^proverbs-09-7

8 Do not reprove a scoffer, or he will hate you; reprove a wise man, and he will love you. ^proverbs-09-8

9 Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be still wiser; teach a righteous man and he will increase in learning. ^proverbs-09-9

10 The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom, and the knowledge of the Holy One is insight. ^proverbs-09-10

11 For by me your days will be multiplied, and years will be added to your life. ^proverbs-09-11

12 If you are wise, you are wise for yourself; if you scoff, you alone will bear it. ^proverbs-09-12

13 A foolish woman is noisy; she is wanton and knows no shame. ^proverbs-09-13

14 She sits at the door of her house, she takes a seat on the high places of the town, ^proverbs-09-14

15 calling to those who pass by, who are going straight on their way, ^proverbs-09-15

16 “Whoever is simple, let him turn in here!” And to him who is without sense she says, ^proverbs-09-16

17 “Stolen water is sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant.” ^proverbs-09-17

18 But he does not know that the dead are there, that her guests are in the depths of Sheol. ^proverbs-09-18
