# Ephesians 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 Therefore be imitators of God, as beloved children. ^ephesians-05-1

2 And walk in love, as Christ loved us and gave himself up for us, a fragrant offering and sacrifice to God. ^ephesians-05-2

**Renounce Pagan Ways**

3 But immorality and all impurity or covetousness must not even be named among you, as is fitting among saints. ^ephesians-05-3

4 Let there be no filthiness, nor silly talk, nor levity, which are not fitting; but instead let there be thanksgiving. ^ephesians-05-4

5 Be sure of this, that no immoral or impure man, or one who is covetous (that is, an idolater), has any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God. ^ephesians-05-5

6 Let no one deceive you with empty words, for it is because of these things that the wrath of God comes upon the sons of disobedience. ^ephesians-05-6

7 Therefore do not associate with them, ^ephesians-05-7

8 for once you were darkness, but now you are light in the Lord; walk as children of light ^ephesians-05-8

9 (for the fruit of light is found in all that is good and right and true), ^ephesians-05-9

10 and try to learn what is pleasing to the Lord. ^ephesians-05-10

11 Take no part in the unfruitful works of darkness, but instead expose them. ^ephesians-05-11

12 For it is a shame even to speak of the things that they do in secret; ^ephesians-05-12

13 but when anything is exposed by the light it becomes visible, for anything that becomes visible is light. ^ephesians-05-13

14 Therefore it is said,

"Awake, O sleeper, and arise from the dead,
and Christ shall give you light." \* ^ephesians-05-14

15 Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise men but as wise, ^ephesians-05-15

16 making the most of the time, because the days are evil. ^ephesians-05-16

17 Therefore do not be foolish, but understand what the will of the Lord is. ^ephesians-05-17

18 And do not get drunk with wine, for that is debauchery; but be filled with the Spirit, ^ephesians-05-18

19 addressing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing and making melody to the Lord with all your heart, ^ephesians-05-19

20 always and for everything giving thanks in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ to God the Father. ^ephesians-05-20

**The Christian Household**

21 Be subject to one another out of reverence for Christ. ^ephesians-05-21

22 Wives, be subject to your husbands, as to the Lord. ^ephesians-05-22

23 For the husband is the head of the wife as Christ is the head of the Church, his body, and is himself its Savior. ^ephesians-05-23

24 As the Church is subject to Christ, so let wives also be subject in everything to their husbands. ^ephesians-05-24

25 Husbands, love your wives, as Christ loved the Church and gave himself up for her, ^ephesians-05-25

26 that he might sanctify her, having cleansed her by the washing of water with the word, ^ephesians-05-26

27 that he might present the Church to himself in splendor, without spot or wrinkle or any such thing, that she might be holy and without blemish. ^ephesians-05-27

28 Even so husbands should love their wives as their own bodies. He who loves his wife loves himself. ^ephesians-05-28

29 For no man ever hates his own flesh, but nourishes and cherishes it, as Christ does the Church, ^ephesians-05-29

30 because we are members of his body. ^ephesians-05-30

31 "For this reason a man shall leave his father and mother and be joined to his wife, and the two shall become one flesh." ^ephesians-05-31

32 This is a great mystery, and I mean in reference to Christ and the Church; ^ephesians-05-32

33 however, let each one of you love his wife as himself, and let the wife see that she respects her husband. ^ephesians-05-33

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (5:14) Apparently a fragment of an early Christian hymn; cf. 1 Tim 3:16. ^ephesians-05-dn-1
