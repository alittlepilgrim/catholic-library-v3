# Ephesians 3 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Paul's Ministry to the Gentiles**

1 For this reason I, Paul, a prisoner for Christ Jesus on behalf of you Gentiles— ^ephesians-03-1

2 assuming that you have heard of the stewardship of God's grace that was given to me for you, ^ephesians-03-2

3 how the mystery \* was made known to me by revelation, as I have written briefly. ^ephesians-03-3

4 When you read this you can perceive my insight into the mystery of Christ, ^ephesians-03-4

5 which was not made known to the sons of men in other generations as it has now been revealed to his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit; ^ephesians-03-5

6 that is, how the Gentiles are fellow heirs, members of the same body, and partakers of the promise in Christ Jesus through the gospel. ^ephesians-03-6

7 Of this gospel I was made a minister according to the gift of God's grace which was given me by the working of his power. ^ephesians-03-7

8 To me, though I am the very least of all the saints, this grace was given, to preach to the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ, ^ephesians-03-8

9 and to make all men see what is the plan of the mystery hidden for ages in<sup>d</sup> God who created all things; ^ephesians-03-9

10 that through the Church the manifold wisdom of God might now be made known to the principalities and powers in the heavenly places. ^ephesians-03-10

11 This was according to the eternal purpose which he has realized in Christ Jesus our Lord, ^ephesians-03-11

12 in whom we have boldness and confidence of access through our faith in him. ^ephesians-03-12

13 So I ask you not to<sup>e</sup> lose heart over what I am suffering for you, which is your glory. ^ephesians-03-13

**Prayer for the Ephesians**

14 For this reason I bow my knees before the Father, ^ephesians-03-14

15 from whom every family in heaven and on earth is named, ^ephesians-03-15

16 that according to the riches of his glory he may grant you to be strengthened with might through his Spirit in the inner man, ^ephesians-03-16

17 and that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith; that you, being rooted and grounded in love, ^ephesians-03-17

18 may have power to comprehend with all the saints what is the breadth and length and height and depth, ^ephesians-03-18

19 and to know the love of Christ which surpasses knowledge, that you may be filled with all the fulness of God. ^ephesians-03-19

20 Now to him who by the power at work within us is able to do far more abundantly than all that we ask or think, ^ephesians-03-20

21 to him be glory in the Church and in Christ Jesus to all generations, for ever and ever. Amen. ^ephesians-03-21


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (3:3) the mystery: i.e., that the Gentiles were to be admitted to the Church on the basis of equality. ^ephesians-03-dn-1
