# Job 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-06|NABRE]]

**Job Replies to Eliphaz**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-06-1

2 “O that my vexation were weighed, and all my calamity laid in the balances! ^job-06-2

3 For then it would be heavier than the sand of the sea; therefore my words have been rash. ^job-06-3

4 For the arrows of the Almighty are in me; my spirit drinks their poison; the terrors of God are arrayed against me. ^job-06-4

5 Does the wild donkey bray when he has grass, or the ox low over his fodder? ^job-06-5

6 Can that which is tasteless be eaten without salt, or is there any taste in the slime of the purslane? ^job-06-6

7 My appetite refuses to touch them; they are as food that is loathsome to me. ^job-06-7

8 “O that I might have my request, and that God would grant my desire; ^job-06-8

9 that it would please God to crush me, that he would let loose his hand and cut me off! ^job-06-9

10 This would be my consolation; I would even exult in pain unsparing; for I have not denied the words of the Holy One. ^job-06-10

11 What is my strength, that I should wait? And what is my end, that I should be patient? ^job-06-11

12 Is my strength the strength of stones, or is my flesh bronze? ^job-06-12

13 In truth I have no help in me, and any resource is driven from me. ^job-06-13

14 “He who withholds kindness from a friend forsakes the fear of the Almighty. ^job-06-14

15 My brethren are treacherous as a torrent-bed, as freshets that pass away, ^job-06-15

16 which are dark with ice, and where the snow hides itself. ^job-06-16

17 In time of heat they disappear; when it is hot, they vanish from their place. ^job-06-17

18 The caravans turn aside from their course; they go up into the waste, and perish. ^job-06-18

19 The caravans of Te'ma look, the travelers of Sheba hope. ^job-06-19

20 They are disappointed because they were confident; they come there and are confounded. ^job-06-20

21 Such you have now become to me; you see my calamity, and are afraid. ^job-06-21

22 Have I said, ‘Make me a gift’? Or, ‘From your wealth offer a bribe for me’? ^job-06-22

23 Or, ‘Deliver me from the adversary’s hand’? Or, ‘Ransom me from the hand of oppressors’? ^job-06-23

24 “Teach me, and I will be silent; make me understand how I have erred. ^job-06-24

25 How forceful are honest words! But what does reproof from you reprove? ^job-06-25

26 Do you think that you can reprove words, when the speech of a despairing man is wind? ^job-06-26

27 You would even cast lots over the fatherless, and bargain over your friend. ^job-06-27

28 “But now, be pleased to look at me; for I will not lie to your face. ^job-06-28

29 Turn, I beg, let no wrong be done. Turn now, my vindication is at stake. ^job-06-29

30 Is there any wrong on my tongue? Cannot my taste discern calamity? ^job-06-30
