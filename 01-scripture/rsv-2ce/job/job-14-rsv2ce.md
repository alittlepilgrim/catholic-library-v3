# Job 14 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-14|NABRE]]

**The Frailty of Man**

1 “Man that is born of a woman is of few days, and full of trouble. ^job-14-1

2 He comes forth like a flower, and withers; he flees like a shadow, and continues not. ^job-14-2

3 And do you open your eyes upon such a one and bring him into judgment with you? ^job-14-3

4 Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? There is not one. ^job-14-4

5 Since his days are determined, and the number of his months is with you, and you have appointed his bounds that he cannot pass, ^job-14-5

6 look away from him, and desist, that he may enjoy, like a hireling, his day. ^job-14-6

7 “For there is hope for a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that its shoots will not cease. ^job-14-7

8 Though its root grow old in the earth, and its stump die in the ground, ^job-14-8

9 yet at the scent of water it will bud and put forth branches like a young plant. ^job-14-9

10 But man dies, and is laid low; man breathes his last, and where is he? ^job-14-10

11 As waters fail from a lake, and a river wastes away and dries up, ^job-14-11

12 so man lies down and rises not again; till the heavens are no more he will not awake, or be roused out of his sleep. ^job-14-12

13 Oh, that you would hide me in Sheol, that you would conceal me until your wrath be past, that you would appoint me a set time, and remember me! ^job-14-13

14 If a man die, shall he live again? All the days of my service I would wait, till my release should come. ^job-14-14

15 You would call, and I would answer you; you would long for the work of your hands. ^job-14-15

16 For then you would number my steps, you would not keep watch over my sin; ^job-14-16

17 my transgression would be sealed up in a bag, and you would cover over my iniquity. ^job-14-17

18 “But the mountain falls and crumbles away, and the rock is removed from its place; ^job-14-18

19 the waters wear away the stones; the torrents wash away the soil of the earth; so you destroy the hope of man. ^job-14-19

20 You prevail for ever against him, and he passes; you change his countenance, and send him away. ^job-14-20

21 His sons come to honor, and he does not know it; they are brought low, and he perceives it not. ^job-14-21

22 He feels only the pain of his own body, and he mourns only for himself.” ^job-14-22
