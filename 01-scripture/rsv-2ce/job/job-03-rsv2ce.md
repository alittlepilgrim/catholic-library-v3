# Job 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-03|NABRE]]

**Job Curses the Day of His Birth**

1 After this Job opened his mouth and cursed the day of his birth. ^job-03-1

2 And Job said: ^job-03-2

3 “Let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night which said, ‘A man-child is conceived.’ ^job-03-3

4 Let that day be darkness! May God above not seek it, nor light shine upon it. ^job-03-4

5 Let gloom and deep darkness claim it. Let clouds dwell upon it; let the blackness of the day terrify it. ^job-03-5

6 That night—let thick darkness seize it! let it not rejoice among the days of the year, let it not come into the number of the months. ^job-03-6

7 Yes, let that night be barren; let no joyful cry be heard in it. ^job-03-7

8 Let those curse it who curse the day, who are skilled to rouse up Levi'athan. ^job-03-8

9 Let the stars of its dawn be dark; let it hope for light, but have none, nor see the eyelids of the morning; ^job-03-9

10 because it did not shut the doors of my mother’s womb, nor hide trouble from my eyes. ^job-03-10

11 “Why did I not die at birth, come forth from the womb and expire? ^job-03-11

12 Why did the knees receive me? Or why the breasts, that I should suck? ^job-03-12

13 For then I should have lain down and been quiet; I should have slept; then I should have been at rest, ^job-03-13

14 with kings and counselors of the earth who rebuilt ruins for themselves, ^job-03-14

15 or with princes who had gold, who filled their houses with silver. ^job-03-15

16 Or why was I not as a hidden untimely birth, as infants that never see the light? ^job-03-16

17 There the wicked cease from troubling, and there the weary are at rest. ^job-03-17

18 There the prisoners are at ease together; they hear not the voice of the taskmaster. ^job-03-18

19 The small and the great are there, and the slave is free from his master. ^job-03-19

20 “Why is light given to him who is in misery, and life to the bitter in soul, ^job-03-20

21 who long for death, but it comes not, and dig for it more than for hidden treasures; ^job-03-21

22 who rejoice exceedingly, and are glad, when they find the grave? ^job-03-22

23 Why is light given to a man whose way is hidden, whom God has hedged in? ^job-03-23

24 For my sighing comes as my bread, and my groanings are poured out like water. ^job-03-24

25 For the thing that I fear comes upon me, and what I dread befalls me. ^job-03-25

26 I am not at ease, nor am I quiet; I have no rest; but trouble comes.” ^job-03-26
