# Job 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-04|NABRE]]

**Eliphaz Speaks: Suffering Is a Result of Sin**

1 Then Eli'phaz the Te'manite answered: ^job-04-1

2 “If one ventures a word with you, will you be offended? Yet who can keep from speaking? ^job-04-2

3 Behold, you have instructed many, and you have strengthened the weak hands. ^job-04-3

4 Your words have upheld him who was stumbling, and you have made firm the feeble knees. ^job-04-4

5 But now it has come to you, and you are impatient; it touches you, and you are dismayed. ^job-04-5

6 Is not your fear of God your confidence, and the integrity of your ways your hope? ^job-04-6

7 “Think now, who that was innocent ever perished? Or where were the upright cut off? ^job-04-7

8 As I have seen, those who plow iniquity and sow trouble reap the same. ^job-04-8

9 By the breath of God they perish, and by the blast of his anger they are consumed. ^job-04-9

10 The roar of the lion, the voice of the fierce lion, the teeth of the young lions, are broken. ^job-04-10

11 The strong lion perishes for lack of prey, and the whelps of the lioness are scattered. ^job-04-11

12 “Now a word was brought to me stealthily, my ear received the whisper of it. ^job-04-12

13 Amid thoughts from visions of the night, when deep sleep falls on men, ^job-04-13

14 dread came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones shake. ^job-04-14

15 A spirit glided past my face; the hair of my flesh stood up. ^job-04-15

16 It stood still, but I could not discern its appearance. A form was before my eyes; there was silence, then I heard a voice: ^job-04-16

17 ‘Can mortal man be righteous before God? Can a man be pure before his Maker? ^job-04-17

18 Even in his servants he puts no trust, and his angels he charges with error; ^job-04-18

19 how much more those who dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, who are crushed before the moth. ^job-04-19

20 Between morning and evening they are destroyed; they perish for ever without any regarding it. ^job-04-20

21 If their tent-cord is plucked up within them, do they not die, and that without wisdom?’ ^job-04-21
