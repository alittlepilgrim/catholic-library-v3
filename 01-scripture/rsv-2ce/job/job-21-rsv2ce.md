# Job 21 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-21|NABRE]]

**Job Replies: The Wicked Often Prosper**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-21-1

2 “Listen carefully to my words, and let this be your consolation. ^job-21-2

3 Bear with me, and I will speak, and after I have spoken, mock on. ^job-21-3

4 As for me, is my complaint against man? Why should I not be impatient? ^job-21-4

5 Look at me, and be appalled, and lay your hand upon your mouth. ^job-21-5

6 When I think of it I am dismayed, and shuddering seizes my flesh. ^job-21-6

7 Why do the wicked live, reach old age, and grow mighty in power? ^job-21-7

8 Their children are established in their presence, and their offspring before their eyes. ^job-21-8

9 Their houses are safe from fear, and no rod of God is upon them. ^job-21-9

10 Their bull breeds without fail; their cow calves, and does not cast her calf. ^job-21-10

11 They send forth their little ones like a flock, and their children dance. ^job-21-11

12 They sing to the tambourine and the lyre, and rejoice to the sound of the pipe. ^job-21-12

13 They spend their days in prosperity, and in peace they go down to Sheol. ^job-21-13

14 They say to God, ‘Depart from us! We do not desire the knowledge of your ways. ^job-21-14

15 What is the Almighty, that we should serve him? And what profit do we get if we pray to him?’ ^job-21-15

16 Behold, is not their prosperity in their hand? The counsel of the wicked is far from me. ^job-21-16

17 “ How often is it that the lamp of the wicked is put out? That their calamity comes upon them? That God distributes pains in his anger? ^job-21-17

18 That they are like straw before the wind, and like chaff that the storm carries away? ^job-21-18

19 You say, ‘God stores up their iniquity for their sons.’ Let him recompense it to themselves, that they may know it. ^job-21-19

20 Let their own eyes see their destruction, and let them drink of the wrath of the Almighty. ^job-21-20

21 For what do they care for their houses after them, when the number of their months is cut off? ^job-21-21

22 Will any teach God knowledge, seeing that he judges those that are on high? ^job-21-22

23 One dies in full prosperity, being wholly at ease and secure, ^job-21-23

24 his body full of fat and the marrow of his bones moist. ^job-21-24

25 Another dies in bitterness of soul, never having tasted of good. ^job-21-25

26 They lie down alike in the dust, and the worms cover them. ^job-21-26

27 “Behold, I know your thoughts, and your schemes to wrong me. ^job-21-27

28 For you say, ‘Where is the house of the prince? Where is the tent in which the wicked dwelt?’ ^job-21-28

29 Have you not asked those who travel the roads, and do you not accept their testimony ^job-21-29

30 that the wicked man is spared in the day of calamity, that he is rescued in the day of wrath? ^job-21-30

31 Who declares his way to his face, and who repays him for what he has done? ^job-21-31

32 When he is borne to the grave, watch is kept over his tomb. ^job-21-32

33 The clods of the valley are sweet to him; all men follow after him, and those who go before him are innumerable. ^job-21-33

34 How then will you comfort me with empty nothings? There is nothing left of your answers but falsehood.” ^job-21-34
