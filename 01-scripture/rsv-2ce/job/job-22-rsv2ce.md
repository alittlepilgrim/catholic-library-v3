# Job 22 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-22|NABRE]]

**Eliphaz Speaks: Job’s Wickedness Must Be Great**

1 Then Eli'phaz the Te'manite answered: ^job-22-1

2 “Can a man be profitable to God? Surely he who is wise is profitable to himself. ^job-22-2

3 Is it any pleasure to the Almighty if you are righteous, or is it gain to him if you make your ways blameless? ^job-22-3

4 Is it for your fear of him that he reproves you, and enters into judgment with you? ^job-22-4

5 Is not your wickedness great? There is no end to your iniquities. ^job-22-5

6 For you have exacted pledges of your brothers for nothing, and stripped the naked of their clothing. ^job-22-6

7 You have given no water to the weary to drink, and you have withheld bread from the hungry. ^job-22-7

8 The man with power possessed the land, and the favored man dwelt in it. ^job-22-8

9 You have sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless were crushed. ^job-22-9

10 Therefore snares are round about you, and sudden terror overwhelms you; ^job-22-10

11 your light is darkened, so that you cannot see, and a flood of water covers you. ^job-22-11

12 “Is not God high in the heavens? See the highest stars, how lofty they are! ^job-22-12

13 Therefore you say, ‘What does God know? Can he judge through the deep darkness? ^job-22-13

14 Thick clouds enwrap him, so that he does not see, and he walks on the vault of heaven.’ ^job-22-14

15 Will you keep to the old way which wicked men have trod? ^job-22-15

16 They were snatched away before their time; their foundation was washed away. ^job-22-16

17 They said to God, ‘Depart from us,’ and ‘What can the Almighty do to us?’ ^job-22-17

18 Yet he filled their houses with good things— but the counsel of the wicked is far from me. ^job-22-18

19 The righteous see it and are glad; the innocent laugh them to scorn, ^job-22-19

20 saying, ‘Surely our adversaries are cut off, and what they left the fire has consumed.’ ^job-22-20

21 “Agree with God, and be at peace; thereby good will come to you. ^job-22-21

22 Receive instruction from his mouth, and lay up his words in your heart. ^job-22-22

23 If you return to the Almighty and humble yourself, if you remove unrighteousness far from your tents, ^job-22-23

24 if you lay gold in the dust, and gold of O'phir among the stones of the torrent bed, ^job-22-24

25 and if the Almighty is your gold, and your precious silver; ^job-22-25

26 then you will delight yourself in the Almighty, and lift up your face to God. ^job-22-26

27 You will make your prayer to him, and he will hear you; and you will pay your vows. ^job-22-27

28 You will decide on a matter, and it will be established for you, and light will shine on your ways. ^job-22-28

29 For God abases the proud, but he saves the lowly. ^job-22-29

30 He delivers the innocent man; you will be delivered through the cleanness of your hands.” ^job-22-30
