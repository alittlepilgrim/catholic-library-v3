# Job 34 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-34|NABRE]]

**Elihu Proclaims God’s Justice**

1 Then Eli'hu said: ^job-34-1

2 “Hear my words, you wise men, and give ear to me, you who know; ^job-34-2

3 for the ear tests words as the palate tastes food. ^job-34-3

4 Let us choose what is right; let us determine among ourselves what is good. ^job-34-4

5 For Job has said, ‘I am innocent, and God has taken away my right; ^job-34-5

6 in spite of my right I am counted a liar; my wound is incurable, though I am without transgression.’ ^job-34-6

7 What man is like Job, who drinks up scoffing like water, ^job-34-7

8 who goes in company with evildoers and walks with wicked men? ^job-34-8

9 For he has said, ‘It profits a man nothing that he should take delight in God.’ ^job-34-9

10 “Therefore, hear me, you men of understanding, far be it from God that he should do wickedness, and from the Almighty that he should do wrong. ^job-34-10

11 For according to the work of a man he will repay him, and according to his ways he will make it befall him. ^job-34-11

12 Of a truth, God will not do wickedly, and the Almighty will not pervert justice. ^job-34-12

13 Who gave him charge over the earth and who laid on him the whole world? ^job-34-13

14 If he should take back his spirit to himself, and gather to himself his breath, ^job-34-14

15 all flesh would perish together, and man would return to dust. ^job-34-15

16 “If you have understanding, hear this; listen to what I say. ^job-34-16

17 Shall one who hates justice govern? Will you condemn him who is righteous and mighty, ^job-34-17

18 who says to a king, ‘Worthless one,’ and to nobles, ‘Wicked man’; ^job-34-18

19 who shows no partiality to princes, nor regards the rich more than the poor, for they are all the work of his hands? ^job-34-19

20 In a moment they die; at midnight the people are shaken and pass away, and the mighty are taken away by no human hand. ^job-34-20

21 “For his eyes are upon the ways of a man, and he sees all his steps. ^job-34-21

22 There is no gloom or deep darkness where evildoers may hide themselves. ^job-34-22

23 For he has not appointed a time for any man to go before God in judgment. ^job-34-23

24 He shatters the mighty without investigation, and sets others in their place. ^job-34-24

25 Thus, knowing their works, he overturns them in the night, and they are crushed. ^job-34-25

26 He strikes them for their wickedness in the sight of men, ^job-34-26

27 because they turned aside from following him, and had no regard for any of his ways, ^job-34-27

28 so that they caused the cry of the poor to come to him, and he heard the cry of the afflicted— ^job-34-28

29 When he is quiet, who can condemn? When he hides his face, who can behold him, whether it be a nation or a man? — ^job-34-29

30 that a godless man should not reign, that he should not ensnare the people. ^job-34-30

31 “For has any one said to God, ‘I have borne chastisement; I will not offend any more; ^job-34-31

32 teach me what I do not see; if I have done iniquity, I will do it no more’? ^job-34-32

33 Will he then make repayment to suit you, because you reject it? For you must choose, and not I; therefore declare what you know. ^job-34-33

34 Men of understanding will say to me, and the wise man who hears me will say: ^job-34-34

35 ‘Job speaks without knowledge, his words are without insight.’ ^job-34-35

36 Would that Job were judged to the end, because he answers like wicked men. ^job-34-36

37 For he adds rebellion to his sin; he claps his hands among us, and multiplies his words against God.” ^job-34-37
