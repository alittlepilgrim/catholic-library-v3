# Job 32 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-32|NABRE]]

**Young Elihu Rebukes Job’s Friends**

1 So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes. ^job-32-1

2 Then Eli'hu the son of Bar'achel the Buzite, of the family of Ram, became angry. He was angry at Job because he justified himself rather than God; ^job-32-2

3 he was angry also at Job’s three friends because they had found no answer, although they had declared Job to be in the wrong. ^job-32-3

4 Now Eli'hu had waited to speak to Job because they were older than he. ^job-32-4

5 And when Eli'hu saw that there was no answer in the mouths of these three men, he became angry. ^job-32-5

6 And Eli'hu the son of Bar'achel the Buzite answered: “I am young in years, and you are aged; therefore I was timid and afraid to declare my opinion to you. ^job-32-6

7 I said, ‘Let days speak, and many years teach wisdom.’ ^job-32-7

8 But it is the spirit in a man, the breath of the Almighty, that makes him understand. ^job-32-8

9 It is not the old that are wise, nor the aged that understand what is right. ^job-32-9

10 Therefore I say, ‘Listen to me; let me also declare my opinion.’ ^job-32-10

11 “Behold, I waited for your words. I listened for your wise sayings, while you searched out what to say. ^job-32-11

12 I gave you my attention, and, behold, there was none that confuted Job, or that answered his words, among you. ^job-32-12

13 Beware lest you say, ‘We have found wisdom; God may vanquish him, not man.’ ^job-32-13

14 He has not directed his words against me, and I will not answer him with your speeches. ^job-32-14

15 “They are discomfited, they answer no more; they have not a word to say. ^job-32-15

16 And shall I wait, because they do not speak, because they stand there, and answer no more? ^job-32-16

17 I also will give my answer; I also will declare my opinion. ^job-32-17

18 For I am full of words, the spirit within me constrains me. ^job-32-18

19 Behold, my heart is like wine that has no vent; like new wineskins, it is ready to burst. ^job-32-19

20 I must speak, that I may find relief; I must open my lips and answer. ^job-32-20

21 I will not show partiality to any person or use flattery toward any man. ^job-32-21

22 For I do not know how to flatter, else would my Maker soon put an end to me. ^job-32-22
