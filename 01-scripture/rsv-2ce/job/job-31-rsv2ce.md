# Job 31 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-31|NABRE]]

**Job Asserts His Integrity**

1 “I have made a covenant with my eyes; how then could I look upon a virgin? ^job-31-1

2 What would be my portion from God above, and my heritage from the Almighty on high? ^job-31-2

3 Does not calamity befall the unrighteous, and disaster the workers of iniquity? ^job-31-3

4 Does not he see my ways, and number all my steps? ^job-31-4

5 “If I have walked with falsehood, and my foot has hastened to deceit; ^job-31-5

6 (Let me be weighed in a just balance, and let God know my integrity!) ^job-31-6

7 if my step has turned aside from the way, and my heart has gone after my eyes, and if any spot has clung to my hands; ^job-31-7

8 then let me sow, and another eat; and let what grows for me be rooted out. ^job-31-8

9 “If my heart has been enticed to a woman, and I have lain in wait at my neighbor’s door; ^job-31-9

10 then let my wife grind for another, and let others bow down upon her. ^job-31-10

11 For that would be a heinous crime; that would be an iniquity to be punished by the judges; ^job-31-11

12 for that would be a fire which consumes unto Abad'don, and it would burn to the root all my increase. ^job-31-12

13 “If I have rejected the cause of my manservant or my maidservant, when they brought a complaint against me; ^job-31-13

14 what then shall I do when God rises up? When he makes inquiry, what shall I answer him? ^job-31-14

15 Did not he who made me in the womb make him? And did not one fashion us in the womb? ^job-31-15

16 “If I have withheld anything that the poor desired, or have caused the eyes of the widow to fail, ^job-31-16

17 or have eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless has not eaten of it ^job-31-17

18 (for from his youth I reared him as a father, and from his mother’s womb I guided him ); ^job-31-18

19 if I have seen any one perish for lack of clothing, or a poor man without covering; ^job-31-19

20 if his loins have not blessed me, and if he was not warmed with the fleece of my sheep; ^job-31-20

21 if I have raised my hand against the fatherless, because I saw help in the gate; ^job-31-21

22 then let my shoulder blade fall from my shoulder, and let my arm be broken from its socket. ^job-31-22

23 For I was in terror of calamity from God, and I could not have faced his majesty. ^job-31-23

24 “If I have made gold my trust, or called fine gold my confidence; ^job-31-24

25 if I have rejoiced because my wealth was great, or because my hand had gotten much; ^job-31-25

26 if I have looked at the sun when it shone, or the moon moving in splendor, ^job-31-26

27 and my heart has been secretly enticed, and my mouth has kissed my hand; ^job-31-27

28 this also would be an iniquity to be punished by the judges, for I should have been false to God above. ^job-31-28

29 “If I have rejoiced at the ruin of him that hated me, or exulted when evil overtook him ^job-31-29

30 (I have not let my mouth sin by asking for his life with a curse); ^job-31-30

31 if the men of my tent have not said, ‘Who is there that has not been filled with his meat?’ ^job-31-31

32 (the sojourner has not lodged in the street; I have opened my doors to the wayfarer); ^job-31-32

33 if I have concealed my transgressions from men, by hiding my iniquity in my bosom, ^job-31-33

34 because I stood in great fear of the multitude, and the contempt of families terrified me, so that I kept silence, and did not go out of doors— ^job-31-34

35 Oh, that I had one to hear me! (Here is my signature! let the Almighty answer me!) Oh, that I had the indictment written by my adversary! ^job-31-35

36 Surely I would carry it on my shoulder; I would bind it on me as a crown; ^job-31-36

37 I would give him an account of all my steps; like a prince I would approach him. ^job-31-37

38 “If my land has cried out against me, and its furrows have wept together; ^job-31-38

39 if I have eaten its yield without payment, and caused the death of its owners; ^job-31-39

40 let thorns grow instead of wheat, and foul weeds instead of barley.” The words of Job are ended. ^job-31-40
