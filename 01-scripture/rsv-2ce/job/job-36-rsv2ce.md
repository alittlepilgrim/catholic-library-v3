# Job 36 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-36|NABRE]]

**Elihu Speaks on God’s Behalf**

1 And Eli'hu continued, and said: ^job-36-1

2 “Bear with me a little, and I will show you, for I have yet something to say on God’s behalf. ^job-36-2

3 I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and ascribe righteousness to my Maker. ^job-36-3

4 For truly my words are not false; one who is perfect in knowledge is with you. ^job-36-4

5 “Behold, God is mighty, and does not despise any; he is mighty in strength of understanding. ^job-36-5

6 He does not keep the wicked alive, but gives the afflicted their right. ^job-36-6

7 He does not withdraw his eyes from the righteous, but with kings upon the throne he sets them for ever, and they are exalted. ^job-36-7

8 And if they are bound in fetters and caught in the cords of affliction, ^job-36-8

9 then he declares to them their work and their transgressions, that they are behaving arrogantly. ^job-36-9

10 He opens their ears to instruction, and commands that they return from iniquity. ^job-36-10

11 If they listen and serve him, they complete their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasantness. ^job-36-11

12 But if they do not listen, they perish by the sword, and die without knowledge. ^job-36-12

13 “The godless in heart cherish anger; they do not cry for help when he binds them. ^job-36-13

14 They die in youth, and their life ends in shame. ^job-36-14

15 He delivers the afflicted by their affliction, and opens their ear by adversity. ^job-36-15

16 He also allured you out of distress into a broad place where there was no cramping, and what was set on your table was full of rich food. ^job-36-16

17 “But you are full of the judgment on the wicked; judgment and justice seize you. ^job-36-17

18 Beware lest wrath entice you into scoffing; and let not the greatness of the ransom turn you aside. ^job-36-18

19 Will your cry avail to keep you from distress, or all the force of your strength? ^job-36-19

20 Do not long for the night, when peoples are cut off in their place. ^job-36-20

21 Take heed, do not turn to iniquity, for this you have chosen rather than affliction. ^job-36-21

22 Behold, God is exalted in his power; who is a teacher like him? ^job-36-22

23 Who has prescribed for him his way, or who can say, ‘You have done wrong’? ^job-36-23

24 “Remember to extol his work, of which men have sung. ^job-36-24

25 All men have looked on it; man beholds it from afar. ^job-36-25

26 Behold, God is great, and we know him not; the number of his years is unsearchable. ^job-36-26

27 For he draws up the drops of water, he distils his mist in rain ^job-36-27

28 which the skies pour down, and drop upon man abundantly. ^job-36-28

29 Can any one understand the spreading of the clouds, the thunderings of his pavilion? ^job-36-29

30 Behold, he scatters his lightning about him, and covers the roots of the sea. ^job-36-30

31 For by these he judges peoples; he gives food in abundance. ^job-36-31

32 He covers his hands with the lightning, and commands it to strike the mark. ^job-36-32

33 Its crashing declares concerning him, who is jealous with anger against iniquity. ^job-36-33
