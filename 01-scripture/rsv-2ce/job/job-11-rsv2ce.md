# Job 11 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-11|NABRE]]

**Zophar Speaks: Job’s Guilt Deserves Punishment**

1 Then Zo'phar the Na'amathite answered: ^job-11-1

2 “Should a multitude of words go unanswered, and a man full of talk be vindicated? ^job-11-2

3 Should your babble silence men, and when you mock, shall no one shame you? ^job-11-3

4 For you say, ‘My doctrine is pure, and I am clean in God’s eyes.’ ^job-11-4

5 But oh, that God would speak, and open his lips to you, ^job-11-5

6 and that he would tell you the secrets of wisdom! For he is manifold in understanding. Know then that God exacts of you less than your guilt deserves. ^job-11-6

7 “Can you find out the deep things of God? Can you find out the limit of the Almighty? ^job-11-7

8 It is higher than heaven —what can you do? Deeper than Sheol—what can you know? ^job-11-8

9 Its measure is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea. ^job-11-9

10 If he passes through, and imprisons, and calls to judgment, who can hinder him? ^job-11-10

11 For he knows worthless men; when he sees iniquity, will he not consider it? ^job-11-11

12 But a stupid man will get understanding, when a wild donkey’s colt is born a man. ^job-11-12

13 “If you set your heart aright, you will stretch out your hands toward him. ^job-11-13

14 If iniquity is in your hand, put it far away, and let not wickedness dwell in your tents. ^job-11-14

15 Surely then you will lift up your face without blemish; you will be secure, and will not fear. ^job-11-15

16 You will forget your misery; you will remember it as waters that have passed away. ^job-11-16

17 And your life will be brighter than the noonday; its darkness will be like the morning. ^job-11-17

18 And you will have confidence, because there is hope; you will be protected and take your rest in safety. ^job-11-18

19 You will lie down, and none will make you afraid; many will entreat your favor. ^job-11-19

20 But the eyes of the wicked will fail; all way of escape will be lost to them, and their hope is to breathe their last.” ^job-11-20
