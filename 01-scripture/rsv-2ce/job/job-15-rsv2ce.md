# Job 15 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-15|NABRE]]

**Eiphaz Rebukes Job**

1 Then Eli'phaz the Te'manite answered: ^job-15-1

2 “Should a wise man answer with windy knowledge, and fill himself with the east wind? ^job-15-2

3 Should he argue in unprofitable talk, or in words with which he can do no good? ^job-15-3

4 But you are doing away with the fear of God, and hindering meditation before God. ^job-15-4

5 For your iniquity teaches your mouth, and you choose the tongue of the crafty. ^job-15-5

6 Your own mouth condemns you, and not I; your own lips testify against you. ^job-15-6

7 “Are you the first man that was born? Or were you brought forth before the hills? ^job-15-7

8 Have you listened in the council of God? And do you limit wisdom to yourself? ^job-15-8

9 What do you know that we do not know? What do you understand that is not clear to us? ^job-15-9

10 Both the gray-haired and the aged are among us, older than your father. ^job-15-10

11 Are the consolations of God too small for you, or the word that deals gently with you? ^job-15-11

12 Why does your heart carry you away, and why do your eyes flash, ^job-15-12

13 that you turn your spirit against God, and let such words go out of your mouth? ^job-15-13

14 What is man, that he can be clean? Or he who is born of a woman, that he can be righteous? ^job-15-14

15 Behold, God puts no trust in his holy ones, and the heavens are not clean in his sight; ^job-15-15

16 how much less one who is abominable and corrupt, a man who drinks iniquity like water! ^job-15-16

17 “I will show you, hear me; and what I have seen I will declare ^job-15-17

18 (what wise men have told, and their fathers have not hidden, ^job-15-18

19 to whom alone the land was given, and no stranger passed among them). ^job-15-19

20 The wicked man writhes in pain all his days, through all the years that are laid up for the ruthless. ^job-15-20

21 Terrifying sounds are in his ears; in prosperity the destroyer will come upon him. ^job-15-21

22 He does not believe that he will return out of darkness, and he is destined for the sword. ^job-15-22

23 He wanders abroad for bread, saying, ‘Where is it?’ He knows that a day of darkness is ready at his hand; ^job-15-23

24 distress and anguish terrify him; they prevail against him, like a king prepared for battle. ^job-15-24

25 Because he has stretched forth his hand against God, and bids defiance to the Almighty, ^job-15-25

26 running stubbornly against him with a thick-bossed shield; ^job-15-26

27 because he has covered his face with his fat, and gathered fat upon his loins, ^job-15-27

28 and has lived in desolate cities, in houses which no man should inhabit, which were destined to become heaps of ruins; ^job-15-28

29 he will not be rich, and his wealth will not endure, nor will he strike root in the earth; ^job-15-29

30 he will not escape from darkness; the flame will dry up his shoots, and his blossom will be swept away by the wind. ^job-15-30

31 Let him not trust in emptiness, deceiving himself; for emptiness will be his recompense. ^job-15-31

32 It will be paid in full before his time, and his branch will not be green. ^job-15-32

33 He will shake off his unripe grape, like the vine, and cast off his blossom, like the olive tree. ^job-15-33

34 For the company of the godless is barren, and fire consumes the tents of bribery. ^job-15-34

35 They conceive mischief and bring forth evil and their heart prepares deceit.” ^job-15-35
