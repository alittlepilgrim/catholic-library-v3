# Job 16 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-16|NABRE]]

**Job Reaffirms His Innocence**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-16-1

2 “I have heard many such things; miserable comforters are you all. ^job-16-2

3 Shall windy words have an end? Or what provokes you that you answer? ^job-16-3

4 I also could speak as you do, if you were in my place; I could join words together against you, and shake my head at you. ^job-16-4

5 I could strengthen you with my mouth, and the solace of my lips would assuage your pain. ^job-16-5

6 “If I speak, my pain is not assuaged, and if I forbear, how much of it leaves me? ^job-16-6

7 Surely now God has worn me out; he has made desolate all my company. ^job-16-7

8 And he has shriveled me up, which is a witness against me; and my leanness has risen up against me, it testifies to my face. ^job-16-8

9 He has torn me in his wrath, and hated me; he has gnashed his teeth at me; my adversary sharpens his eyes against me. ^job-16-9

10 Men have gaped at me with their mouth, they have struck me insolently upon the cheek, they mass themselves together against me. ^job-16-10

11 God gives me up to the ungodly, and casts me into the hands of the wicked. ^job-16-11

12 I was at ease, and he broke me asunder; he seized me by the neck and dashed me to pieces; he set me up as his target, ^job-16-12

13 his archers surround me. He slashes open my kidneys, and does not spare; he pours out my gall on the ground. ^job-16-13

14 He breaks me with breach upon breach; he runs upon me like a warrior. ^job-16-14

15 I have sewn sackcloth upon my skin, and have laid my strength in the dust. ^job-16-15

16 My face is red with weeping, and on my eyelids is deep darkness; ^job-16-16

17 although there is no violence in my hands, and my prayer is pure. ^job-16-17

18 “O earth, cover not my blood, and let my cry find no resting place. ^job-16-18

19 Even now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and he that vouches for me is on high. ^job-16-19

20 My friends scorn me; my eye pours out tears to God, ^job-16-20

21 that he would maintain the right of a man with God, like that of a man with his neighbor. ^job-16-21

22 For when a few years have come I shall go the way from where I shall not return. ^job-16-22
