# Job 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-08|NABRE]]

**Bildad Speaks: God Is Just**

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: ^job-08-1

2 “How long will you say these things, and the words of your mouth be a great wind? ^job-08-2

3 Does God pervert justice? Or does the Almighty pervert the right? ^job-08-3

4 If your children have sinned against him, he has delivered them into the power of their transgression. ^job-08-4

5 If you will seek God and make supplication to the Almighty, ^job-08-5

6 if you are pure and upright, surely then he will rouse himself for you and reward you with a rightful habitation. ^job-08-6

7 And though your beginning was small, your latter days will be very great. ^job-08-7

8 “For inquire, I beg you, of bygone ages, and consider what the fathers have found; ^job-08-8

9 for we are but of yesterday, and know nothing, for our days on earth are a shadow. ^job-08-9

10 Will they not teach you, and tell you, and utter words out of their understanding? ^job-08-10

11 “Can papyrus grow where there is no marsh? Can reeds flourish where there is no water? ^job-08-11

12 While yet in flower and not cut down, they wither before any other plant. ^job-08-12

13 Such are the paths of all who forget God; the hope of the godless man shall perish. ^job-08-13

14 His confidence breaks in sunder, and his trust is a spider’s web. ^job-08-14

15 He leans against his house, but it does not stand; he lays hold of it, but it does not endure. ^job-08-15

16 He thrives before the sun, and his shoots spread over his garden. ^job-08-16

17 His roots twine about the stone-heap; he lives among the rocks. ^job-08-17

18 If he is destroyed from his place, then it will deny him, saying, ‘I have never seen you.’ ^job-08-18

19 Behold, this is the joy of his way; and out of the earth others will spring. ^job-08-19

20 “Behold, God will not reject a blameless man, nor take the hand of evildoers. ^job-08-20

21 He will yet fill your mouth with laughter, and your lips with shouting. ^job-08-21

22 Those who hate you will be clothed with shame, and the tent of the wicked will be no more.” ^job-08-22
