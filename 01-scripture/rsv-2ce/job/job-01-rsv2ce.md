# Job 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-01|NABRE]]

**Job and His Family**

1 There was a man in the land of Uz, whose name was Job; and that man was blameless and upright, one who feared God, and turned away from evil. ^job-01-1

2 There were born to him seven sons and three daughters. ^job-01-2

3 He had seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she-donkeys, and very many servants; so that this man was the greatest of all the people of the east. ^job-01-3

4 His sons used to go and hold a feast in the house of each on his day; and they would send and invite their three sisters to eat and drink with them. ^job-01-4

5 And when the days of the feast had run their course, Job would send and sanctify them, and he would rise early in the morning and offer burnt offerings according to the number of them all; for Job said, “It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts.” Thus Job did continually. ^job-01-5

**Satan Is Allowed to Tempt Job**

6 Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the LORD, and Satan also came among them. ^job-01-6

7 The LORD said to Satan, “From where have you come?” Satan answered the LORD, “From going back and forth on the earth, and from walking up and down on it.” ^job-01-7

8 And the LORD said to Satan, “Have you considered my servant Job, that there is none like him on the earth, a blameless and upright man, who fears God and turns away from evil?” ^job-01-8

9 Then Satan answered the LORD, “Does Job fear God for nothing? ^job-01-9

10 Have you not put a hedge about him and his house and all that he has, on every side? You have blessed the work of his hands, and his possessions have increased in the land. ^job-01-10

11 But put forth your hand now, and touch all that he has, and he will curse you to your face.” ^job-01-11

12 And the LORD said to Satan, “Behold, all that he has is in your power; only upon himself do not put forth your hand.” So Satan went forth from the presence of the LORD. ^job-01-12

**Job Loses His Property and Children**

13 Now there was a day when his sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house; ^job-01-13

14 and there came a messenger to Job, and said, “The oxen were plowing and the donkeys feeding beside them; ^job-01-14

15 and the Sabe'ans fell upon them and took them, and slew the servants with the edge of the sword; and I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^job-01-15

16 While he was yet speaking, there came another, and said, “The fire of God fell from heaven and burned up the sheep and the servants, and consumed them; and I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^job-01-16

17 While he was yet speaking, there came another, and said, “The Chalde'ans formed three companies, and made a raid upon the camels and took them, and slew the servants with the edge of the sword; and I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^job-01-17

18 While he was yet speaking, there came another, and said, “Your sons and daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house; ^job-01-18

19 and behold, a great wind came across the wilderness, and struck the four corners of the house, and it fell upon the young people, and they are dead; and I alone have escaped to tell you.” ^job-01-19

20 Then Job arose, and tore his robe, and shaved his head, and fell upon the ground, and worshiped. ^job-01-20

21 And he said, “Naked I came from my mother’s womb, and naked shall I return; the LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD.” ^job-01-21

22 In all this Job did not sin or charge God with wrong. ^job-01-22
