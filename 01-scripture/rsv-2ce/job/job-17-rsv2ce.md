# Job 17 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-17|NABRE]]

**Job Prays for Relief**

1 My spirit is broken, my days are extinct, the grave is ready for me. ^job-17-1

2 Surely there are mockers about me, and my eye dwells on their provocation. ^job-17-2

3 “Lay down a pledge for me with yourself; who is there that will give surety for me? ^job-17-3

4 Since you have closed their minds to understanding, therefore you will not let them triumph. ^job-17-4

5 He who informs against his friends to get a share of their property, the eyes of his children will fail. ^job-17-5

6 “He has made me a byword of the peoples, and I am one before whom men spit. ^job-17-6

7 My eye has grown dim from grief, and all my members are like a shadow. ^job-17-7

8 Upright men are appalled at this, and the innocent stirs himself up against the godless. ^job-17-8

9 Yet the righteous holds to his way, and he that has clean hands grows stronger and stronger. ^job-17-9

10 But you, come on again, all of you, and I shall not find a wise man among you. ^job-17-10

11 My days are past, my plans are broken off, the desires of my heart. ^job-17-11

12 They make night into day; ‘The light,’ they say, ‘is near to the darkness.’ ^job-17-12

13 If I look for Sheol as my house, if I spread my couch in darkness, ^job-17-13

14 if I say to the pit, ‘You are my father,’ and to the worm, ‘My mother,’ or ‘My sister,’ ^job-17-14

15 where then is my hope? Who will see my hope? ^job-17-15

16 Will it go down to the bars of Sheol? Shall we descend together into the dust?” ^job-17-16
