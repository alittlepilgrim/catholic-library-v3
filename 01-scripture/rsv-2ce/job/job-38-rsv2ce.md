# Job 38 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-38|NABRE]]

**The Lord Answers Job**

1 Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind: ^job-38-1

2 “Who is this that darkens counsel by words without knowledge? ^job-38-2

3 Gird up your loins like a man, I will question you, and you shall declare to me. ^job-38-3

4 “Where were you when I laid the foundation of the earth? Tell me, if you have understanding. ^job-38-4

5 Who determined its measurements—surely you know! Or who stretched the line upon it? ^job-38-5

6 On what were its bases sunk, or who laid its cornerstone, ^job-38-6

7 when the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy? ^job-38-7

8 “Or who shut in the sea with doors, when it burst forth from the womb; ^job-38-8

9 when I made clouds its garment, and thick darkness its swaddling band; ^job-38-9

10 and prescribed bounds for it, and set bars and doors, ^job-38-10

11 and said, ‘Thus far shall you come, and no farther, and here shall your proud waves be stayed’? ^job-38-11

12 “Have you commanded the morning since your days began, and caused the dawn to know its place, ^job-38-12

13 that it might take hold of the skirts of the earth, and the wicked be shaken out of it? ^job-38-13

14 It is changed like clay under the seal, and it is dyed like a garment. ^job-38-14

15 From the wicked their light is withheld, and their uplifted arm is broken. ^job-38-15

16 “Have you entered into the springs of the sea, or walked in the recesses of the deep? ^job-38-16

17 Have the gates of death been revealed to you, or have you seen the gates of deep darkness? ^job-38-17

18 Have you comprehended the expanse of the earth? Declare, if you know all this. ^job-38-18

19 “Where is the way to the dwelling of light, and where is the place of darkness, ^job-38-19

20 that you may take it to its territory and that you may discern the paths to its home? ^job-38-20

21 You know, for you were born then, and the number of your days is great! ^job-38-21

22 “Have you entered the storehouses of the snow, or have you seen the storehouses of the hail, ^job-38-22

23 which I have reserved for the time of trouble, for the day of battle and war? ^job-38-23

24 What is the way to the place where the light is distributed, or where the east wind is scattered upon the earth? ^job-38-24

25 “Who has cleft a channel for the torrents of rain, and a way for the thunderbolt, ^job-38-25

26 to bring rain on a land where no man is, on the desert in which there is no man; ^job-38-26

27 to satisfy the waste and desolate land, and to make the ground put forth grass? ^job-38-27

28 “Has the rain a father, or who has begotten the drops of dew? ^job-38-28

29 From whose womb did the ice come forth, and who has given birth to the hoarfrost of heaven? ^job-38-29

30 The waters become hard like stone, and the face of the deep is frozen. ^job-38-30

31 “Can you bind the chains of the Plei'ades, or loose the cords of Ori'on? ^job-38-31

32 Can you lead forth the Maz'zaroth in their season, or can you guide the Bear with its children? ^job-38-32

33 Do you know the ordinances of the heavens? Can you establish their rule on the earth? ^job-38-33

34 “Can you lift up your voice to the clouds, that a flood of waters may cover you? ^job-38-34

35 Can you send forth bolts of lightning, that they may go and say to you, ‘Here we are’? ^job-38-35

36 Who has put wisdom in the clouds, or given understanding to the mists? ^job-38-36

37 Who can number the clouds by wisdom? Or who can tilt the waterskins of the heavens, ^job-38-37

38 when the dust runs into a mass and the clods cling tightly together? ^job-38-38

39 “Can you hunt the prey for the lion, or satisfy the appetite of the young lions, ^job-38-39

40 when they crouch in their dens, or lie in wait in their hiding places? ^job-38-40

41 Who provides for the raven its prey, when its young ones cry to God, and wander about for lack of food? ^job-38-41
