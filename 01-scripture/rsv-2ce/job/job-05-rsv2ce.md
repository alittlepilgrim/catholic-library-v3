# Job 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-05|NABRE]]

1 “Call now; is there any one who will answer you? To which of the holy ones will you turn? ^job-05-1

2 Surely vexation kills the fool, and jealousy slays the simple. ^job-05-2

3 I have seen the fool taking root, but suddenly I cursed his dwelling. ^job-05-3

4 His sons are far from safety, they are crushed in the gate, and there is no one to deliver them. ^job-05-4

5 His harvest the hungry eat, and he takes it even out of thorns; and the thirsty pant after his wealth. ^job-05-5

6 For affliction does not come from the dust, nor does trouble sprout from the ground; ^job-05-6

7 but man is born to trouble as the sparks fly upward. ^job-05-7

8 “As for me, I would seek God, and to God would I commit my cause; ^job-05-8

9 who does great things and unsearchable, marvelous things without number: ^job-05-9

10 he gives rain upon the earth and sends waters upon the fields; ^job-05-10

11 he sets on high those who are lowly, and those who mourn are lifted to safety. ^job-05-11

12 He frustrates the devices of the crafty, so that their hands achieve no success. ^job-05-12

13 He takes the wise in their own craftiness; and the schemes of the wily are brought to a quick end. ^job-05-13

14 They meet with darkness in the daytime, and grope at noonday as in the night. ^job-05-14

15 But he saves the fatherless from their mouth, the needy from the hand of the mighty. ^job-05-15

16 So the poor have hope, and injustice shuts her mouth. ^job-05-16

17 “Behold, happy is the man whom God reproves; therefore despise not the chastening of the Almighty. ^job-05-17

18 For he wounds, but he binds up; he strikes, but his hands heal. ^job-05-18

19 He will deliver you from six troubles; in seven there shall no evil touch you. ^job-05-19

20 In famine he will redeem you from death, and in war from the power of the sword. ^job-05-20

21 You shall be hidden from the scourge of the tongue, and shall not fear destruction when it comes. ^job-05-21

22 At destruction and famine you shall laugh, and shall not fear the beasts of the earth. ^job-05-22

23 For you shall be in league with the stones of the field, and the beasts of the field shall be at peace with you. ^job-05-23

24 You shall know that your tent is safe, and you shall inspect your fold and miss nothing. ^job-05-24

25 You shall know also that your descendants shall be many, and your offspring as the grass of the earth. ^job-05-25

26 You shall come to your grave in ripe old age, as a shock of grain comes up to the threshing floor in its season. ^job-05-26

27 Behold, this we have searched out; it is true. Hear, and know it for your good.” ^job-05-27
