# Job 26 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-26|NABRE]]

**Job Speaks of God’s Power**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-26-1

2 “How you have helped him who has no power! How you have saved the arm that has no strength! ^job-26-2

3 How you have counseled him who has no wisdom, and plentifully declared sound knowledge! ^job-26-3

4 With whose help have you uttered words, and whose spirit has come forth from you? ^job-26-4

5 The shades below tremble, the waters and their inhabitants. ^job-26-5

6 Sheol is naked before God, and Abad'don has no covering. ^job-26-6

7 He stretches out the north over the void, and hangs the earth upon nothing. ^job-26-7

8 He binds up the waters in his thick clouds, and the cloud is not torn under them. ^job-26-8

9 He covers the face of the moon, and spreads over it his cloud. ^job-26-9

10 He has described a circle upon the face of the waters at the boundary between light and darkness. ^job-26-10

11 The pillars of heaven tremble, and are astounded at his rebuke. ^job-26-11

12 By his power he stilled the sea; by his understanding he struck Ra'hab. ^job-26-12

13 By his wind the heavens were made fair; his hand pierced the fleeing serpent. ^job-26-13

14 Behold, these are but the outskirts of his ways; and how small a whisper do we hear of him! But the thunder of his power who can understand?” ^job-26-14
