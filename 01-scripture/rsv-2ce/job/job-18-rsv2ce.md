# Job 18 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-18|NABRE]]

**Bildad Speaks: God Punishes the Wicked**

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: ^job-18-1

2 “How long will you hunt for words? Consider, and then we will speak. ^job-18-2

3 Why are we counted as cattle? Why are we stupid in your sight? ^job-18-3

4 You who tear yourself in your anger, shall the earth be forsaken for you, or the rock be removed out of its place? ^job-18-4

5 “Yes, the light of the wicked is put out, and the flame of his fire does not shine. ^job-18-5

6 The light is dark in his tent, and his lamp above him is put out. ^job-18-6

7 His strong steps are shortened and his own schemes throw him down. ^job-18-7

8 For he is cast into a net by his own feet, and he walks on a pitfall. ^job-18-8

9 A trap seizes him by the heel, a snare lays hold of him. ^job-18-9

10 A rope is hid for him in the ground, a trap for him in the path. ^job-18-10

11 Terrors frighten him on every side, and chase him at his heels. ^job-18-11

12 His strength is hunger-bitten, and calamity is ready for his stumbling. ^job-18-12

13 By disease his skin is consumed, the first-born of death consumes his limbs. ^job-18-13

14 He is torn from the tent in which he trusted, and is brought to the king of terrors. ^job-18-14

15 In his tent dwells that which is none of his; brimstone is scattered upon his habitation. ^job-18-15

16 His roots dry up beneath, and his branches wither above. ^job-18-16

17 His memory perishes from the earth, and he has no name in the street. ^job-18-17

18 He is thrust from light into darkness, and driven out of the world. ^job-18-18

19 He has no offspring or descendant among his people, and no survivor where he used to live. ^job-18-19

20 They of the west are appalled at his day, and horror seizes them of the east. ^job-18-20

21 Surely such are the dwellings of the ungodly, such is the place of him who knows not God.” ^job-18-21
