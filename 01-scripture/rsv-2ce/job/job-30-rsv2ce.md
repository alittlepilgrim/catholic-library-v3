# Job 30 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-30|NABRE]]

**Job Speaks of His Present Suffering**

1 “But now they make sport of me, men who are younger than I, whose fathers I would have disdained to set with the dogs of my flock. ^job-30-1

2 What could I gain from the strength of their hands, men whose vigor is gone? ^job-30-2

3 Through want and hard hunger they gnaw the dry and desolate ground; ^job-30-3

4 they pick mallow and the leaves of bushes, and to warm themselves the roots of the broom. ^job-30-4

5 They are driven out from among men; they shout after them as after a thief. ^job-30-5

6 In the gullies of the torrents they must dwell, in holes of the earth and of the rocks. ^job-30-6

7 Among the bushes they bray; under the nettles they huddle together. ^job-30-7

8 A senseless, a disreputable brood, they have been whipped out of the land. ^job-30-8

9 “And now I have become their song, I am a byword to them. ^job-30-9

10 They abhor me, they keep aloof from me; they do not hesitate to spit at the sight of me. ^job-30-10

11 Because God has loosed my cord and humbled me, they have cast off restraint in my presence. ^job-30-11

12 On my right hand the rabble rise, they drive me forth, they cast up against me their ways of destruction. ^job-30-12

13 They break up my path, they promote my calamity; no one restrains them. ^job-30-13

14 As through a wide breach they come; amid the crash they roll on. ^job-30-14

15 Terrors are turned upon me; my honor is pursued as by the wind, and my prosperity has passed away like a cloud. ^job-30-15

16 “And now my soul is poured out within me; days of affliction have taken hold of me. ^job-30-16

17 The night racks my bones, and the pain that gnaws me takes no rest. ^job-30-17

18 With violence it seizes my garment; it binds me about like the collar of my tunic. ^job-30-18

19 God has cast me into the mire, and I have become like dust and ashes. ^job-30-19

20 I cry to you and you do not answer me; I stand, and you do not heed me. ^job-30-20

21 You have turned cruel to me; with the might of your hand you persecute me. ^job-30-21

22 You lift me up on the wind, you make me ride on it, and you toss me about in the roar of the storm. ^job-30-22

23 Yes, I know that you will bring me to death, and to the house appointed for all living. ^job-30-23

24 “Yet does not one in a heap of ruins stretch out his hand, and in his disaster cry for help? ^job-30-24

25 Did not I weep for him whose day was hard? Was not my soul grieved for the poor? ^job-30-25

26 But when I looked for good, evil came; and when I waited for light, darkness came. ^job-30-26

27 My heart is in turmoil, and is never still; days of affliction come to meet me. ^job-30-27

28 I go about blackened, but not by the sun; I stand up in the assembly, and cry for help. ^job-30-28

29 I am a brother of jackals, and a companion of ostriches. ^job-30-29

30 My skin turns black and falls from me, and my bones burn with heat. ^job-30-30

31 My lyre is turned to mourning, and my pipe to the voice of those who weep. ^job-30-31
