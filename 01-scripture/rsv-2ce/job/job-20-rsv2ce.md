# Job 20 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-20|NABRE]]

**Zophar Speaks: Wickedness Receives Retribution**

1 Then Zo'phar the Na'amathite answered: ^job-20-1

2 “Therefore my thoughts answer me, because of my haste within me. ^job-20-2

3 I hear censure which insults me, and out of my understanding a spirit answers me. ^job-20-3

4 Do you not know this from of old, since man was placed upon earth, ^job-20-4

5 that the exulting of the wicked is short, and the joy of the godless but for a moment? ^job-20-5

6 Though his height mount up to the heavens, and his head reach to the clouds, ^job-20-6

7 he will perish for ever like his own dung; those who have seen him will say, ‘Where is he?’ ^job-20-7

8 He will fly away like a dream, and not be found; he will be chased away like a vision of the night. ^job-20-8

9 The eye which saw him will see him no more, nor will his place any more behold him. ^job-20-9

10 His children will seek the favor of the poor, and his hands will give back his wealth. ^job-20-10

11 His bones are full of youthful vigor, but it will lie down with him in the dust. ^job-20-11

12 “Though wickedness is sweet in his mouth, though he hides it under his tongue, ^job-20-12

13 though he is loath to let it go, and holds it in his mouth, ^job-20-13

14 yet his food is turned in his stomach; it is the gall of asps within him. ^job-20-14

15 He swallows down riches and vomits them up again; God casts them out of his belly. ^job-20-15

16 He will suck the poison of asps; the tongue of a viper will kill him. ^job-20-16

17 He will not look upon the rivers, the streams flowing with honey and curds. ^job-20-17

18 He will give back the fruit of his toil, and will not swallow it down; from the profit of his trading he will get no enjoyment. ^job-20-18

19 For he has crushed and abandoned the poor, he has seized a house which he did not build. ^job-20-19

20 “Because his greed knew no rest, he will not save anything in which he delights. ^job-20-20

21 There was nothing left after he had eaten; therefore his prosperity will not endure. ^job-20-21

22 In the fulness of his sufficiency he will be in straits; all the force of misery will come upon him. ^job-20-22

23 To fill his belly to the full God will send his fierce anger into him, and rain it upon him as his food. ^job-20-23

24 He will flee from an iron weapon; a bronze arrow will strike him through. ^job-20-24

25 It is drawn forth and comes out of his body, the glittering point comes out of his gall; terrors come upon him. ^job-20-25

26 Utter darkness is laid up for his treasures; a fire not blown upon will devour him; what is left in his tent will be consumed. ^job-20-26

27 The heavens will reveal his iniquity, and the earth will rise up against him. ^job-20-27

28 The possessions of his house will be carried away, dragged off in the day of God’s wrath. ^job-20-28

29 This is the wicked man’s portion from God, the heritage decreed for him by God.” ^job-20-29
