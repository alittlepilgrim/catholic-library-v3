# Job 28 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-28|NABRE]]

**Where Shall Wisdom Be Found?**

1 “Surely there is a mine for silver, and a place for gold which they refine. ^job-28-1

2 Iron is taken out of the earth, and copper is smelted from the ore. ^job-28-2

3 Men put an end to darkness, and search out to the farthest bound the ore in gloom and deep darkness. ^job-28-3

4 They open shafts in a valley away from where men live; they are forgotten by travelers, they hang afar from men, they swing back and forth. ^job-28-4

5 As for the earth, out of it comes bread; but underneath it is turned up as by fire. ^job-28-5

6 Its stones are the place of sapphires, and it has dust of gold. ^job-28-6

7 “That path no bird of prey knows, and the falcon’s eye has not seen it. ^job-28-7

8 The proud beasts have not trodden it; the lion has not passed over it. ^job-28-8

9 “Man puts his hand to the flinty rock, and overturns mountains by the roots. ^job-28-9

10 He cuts out channels in the rocks, and his eye sees every precious thing. ^job-28-10

11 He binds up the streams so that they do not trickle, and the thing that is hidden he brings forth to light. ^job-28-11

12 “But where shall wisdom be found? And where is the place of understanding? ^job-28-12

13 Man does not know the way to it, and it is not found in the land of the living. ^job-28-13

14 The deep says, ‘It is not in me,’ and the sea says, ‘It is not with me.’ ^job-28-14

15 It cannot be gotten for gold, and silver cannot be weighed as its price. ^job-28-15

16 It cannot be valued in the gold of O'phir, in precious onyx or sapphire. ^job-28-16

17 Gold and glass cannot equal it, nor can it be exchanged for jewels of fine gold. ^job-28-17

18 No mention shall be made of coral or of crystal; the price of wisdom is above pearls. ^job-28-18

19 The topaz of Ethiopia cannot compare with it, nor can it be valued in pure gold. ^job-28-19

20 “From where does wisdom come? And where is the place of understanding? ^job-28-20

21 It is hidden from the eyes of all living, and concealed from the birds of the air. ^job-28-21

22 Abad'don and Death say, ‘We have heard a rumor of it with our ears.’ ^job-28-22

23 “God understands the way to it, and he knows its place. ^job-28-23

24 For he looks to the ends of the earth, and sees everything under the heavens. ^job-28-24

25 When he gave to the wind its weight, and meted out the waters by measure; ^job-28-25

26 when he made a decree for the rain, and a way for the lightning of the thunder; ^job-28-26

27 then he saw it and declared it; he established it, and searched it out. ^job-28-27

28 And he said to man, ‘Behold, the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom; and to depart from evil is understanding.’ ” ^job-28-28
