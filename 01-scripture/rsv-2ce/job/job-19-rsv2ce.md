# Job 19 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-19|NABRE]]

**Job Answers: I Know That My Redeemer Lives**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-19-1

2 “How long will you torment me, and break me in pieces with words? ^job-19-2

3 These ten times you have cast reproach upon me; are you not ashamed to wrong me? ^job-19-3

4 And even if it be true that I have erred, my error remains with myself. ^job-19-4

5 If indeed you magnify yourselves against me, and make my humiliation an argument against me, ^job-19-5

6 know then that God has put me in the wrong, and closed his net about me. ^job-19-6

7 Behold, I cry out, ‘Violence!’ but I am not answered; I call aloud, but there is no justice. ^job-19-7

8 He has walled up my way, so that I cannot pass, and he has set darkness upon my paths. ^job-19-8

9 He has stripped from me my glory, and taken the crown from my head. ^job-19-9

10 He breaks me down on every side, and I am gone, and my hope has he pulled up like a tree. ^job-19-10

11 He has kindled his wrath against me, and counts me as his adversary. ^job-19-11

12 His troops come on together; they have cast up siegeworks against me, and encamp round about my tent. ^job-19-12

13 “He has put my brethren far from me, and my acquaintances are wholly estranged from me. ^job-19-13

14 My kinsfolk and my close friends have failed me; ^job-19-14

15 the guests in my house have forgotten me; my maidservants count me as a stranger; I have become an alien in their eyes. ^job-19-15

16 I call to my servant, but he gives me no answer; I must beseech him with my mouth. ^job-19-16

17 I am repulsive to my wife, loathsome to the sons of my own mother. ^job-19-17

18 Even young children despise me; when I rise they talk against me. ^job-19-18

19 All my intimate friends abhor me, and those whom I loved have turned against me. ^job-19-19

20 My bones cling to my skin and to my flesh, and I have escaped by the skin of my teeth. ^job-19-20

21 Have pity on me, have pity on me, O you my friends, for the hand of God has touched me! ^job-19-21

22 Why do you, like God, pursue me? Why are you not satisfied with my flesh? ^job-19-22

23 “Oh, that my words were written! Oh, that they were inscribed in a book! ^job-19-23

24 Oh, that with an iron pen and lead they were graven in the rock for ever! ^job-19-24

25 For I know that my Redeemer lives, and at last he will stand upon the earth; ^job-19-25

26 and after my skin has been thus destroyed, then from my flesh I shall see God, ^job-19-26

27 whom I shall see on my side, and my eyes shall behold, and not another. My heart faints within me! ^job-19-27

28 If you say, ‘How we will pursue him!’ and, ‘The root of the matter is found in him’; ^job-19-28

29 be afraid of the sword, for wrath brings the punishment of the sword, that you may know there is a judgment.” ^job-19-29
