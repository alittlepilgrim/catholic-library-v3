# Job 39 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-39|NABRE]]

**The Lord Questions Job**

1 “Do you know when the mountain goats bring forth? Do you observe the deer bringing forth their young? ^job-39-1

2 Can you number the months that they fulfil, and do you know the time when they bring forth, ^job-39-2

3 when they crouch, bring forth their offspring, and are delivered of their young? ^job-39-3

4 Their young ones become strong, they grow up in the open; they go forth, and do not return to them. ^job-39-4

5 “Who has let the wild donkey go free? Who has loosed the bonds of the swift donkey, ^job-39-5

6 to whom I have given the steppe for his home, and the salt land for his dwelling place? ^job-39-6

7 He scorns the tumult of the city; he hears not the shouts of the driver. ^job-39-7

8 He ranges the mountains as his pasture, and he searches after every green thing. ^job-39-8

9 “Is the wild ox willing to serve you? Will he spend the night at your crib? ^job-39-9

10 Can you bind him in the furrow with ropes, or will he harrow the valleys after you? ^job-39-10

11 Will you depend on him because his strength is great, and will you leave to him your labor? ^job-39-11

12 Do you have faith in him that he will return, and bring your grain to your threshing floor? ^job-39-12

13 “The wings of the ostrich wave proudly; but are they the pinions and plumage of love? ^job-39-13

14 For she leaves her eggs to the earth, and lets them be warmed on the ground, ^job-39-14

15 forgetting that a foot may crush them, and that the wild beast may trample them. ^job-39-15

16 She deals cruelly with her young, as if they were not hers; though her labor be in vain, yet she has no fear; ^job-39-16

17 because God has made her forget wisdom, and given her no share in understanding. ^job-39-17

18 When she rouses herself to flee, she laughs at the horse and his rider. ^job-39-18

19 “Do you give the horse his might? Do you clothe his neck with strength? ^job-39-19

20 Do you make him leap like the locust? His majestic snorting is terrible. ^job-39-20

21 He paws in the valley, and exults in his strength; he goes out to meet the weapons. ^job-39-21

22 He laughs at fear, and is not dismayed; he does not turn back from the sword. ^job-39-22

23 Upon him rattle the quiver, the flashing spear and the javelin. ^job-39-23

24 With fierceness and rage he swallows the ground; he cannot stand still at the sound of the trumpet. ^job-39-24

25 When the trumpet sounds, he says ‘Aha!’ He smells the battle from afar, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting. ^job-39-25

26 “Is it by your wisdom that the hawk soars, and spreads his wings toward the south? ^job-39-26

27 Is it at your command that the eagle mounts up and makes his nest on high? ^job-39-27

28 On the rock he dwells and makes his home in the fastness of the rocky crag. ^job-39-28

29 From there he spies out the prey; his eyes behold it afar off. ^job-39-29

30 His young ones suck up blood; and where the slain are, there is he.” ^job-39-30
