# Job 33 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-33|NABRE]]

**Elihu Addresses Job**

1 “But now, hear my speech, O Job, and listen to all my words. ^job-33-1

2 Behold, I open my mouth; the tongue in my mouth speaks. ^job-33-2

3 My words declare the uprightness of my heart, and what my lips know they speak sincerely. ^job-33-3

4 The spirit of God has made me, and the breath of the Almighty gives me life. ^job-33-4

5 Answer me, if you can; set your words in order before me; take your stand. ^job-33-5

6 Behold, I am toward God as you are; I too was formed from a piece of clay. ^job-33-6

7 Behold, no fear of me need terrify you; my pressure will not be heavy upon you. ^job-33-7

8 “Surely, you have spoken in my hearing, and I have heard the sound of your words. ^job-33-8

9 You say, ‘I am clean, without transgression; I am pure, and there is no iniquity in me. ^job-33-9

10 Behold, he finds occasions against me, he counts me as his enemy; ^job-33-10

11 he puts my feet in the stocks, and watches all my paths.’ ^job-33-11

12 “Behold, in this you are not right. I will answer you. God is greater than man. ^job-33-12

13 Why do you contend against him, saying, ‘He will answer none of my words’? ^job-33-13

14 For God speaks in one way, and in two, though man does not perceive it. ^job-33-14

15 In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls upon men, while they slumber on their beds, ^job-33-15

16 then he opens the ears of men, and terrifies them with warnings, ^job-33-16

17 that he may turn man aside from his deed, and cut off pride from man; ^job-33-17

18 he keeps back his soul from the Pit, his life from perishing by the sword. ^job-33-18

19 “Man is also chastened with pain upon his bed, and with continual strife in his bones; ^job-33-19

20 so that his life loathes bread, and his appetite dainty food. ^job-33-20

21 His flesh is so wasted away that it cannot be seen; and his bones which were not seen stick out. ^job-33-21

22 His soul draws near the Pit, and his life to those who bring death. ^job-33-22

23 If there be for him an angel, a mediator, one of the thousand, to declare to man what is right for him; ^job-33-23

24 and he is gracious to him, and says, ‘Deliver him from going down into the Pit, I have found a ransom; ^job-33-24

25 let his flesh become fresh with youth; let him return to the days of his youthful vigor’; ^job-33-25

26 then man prays to God, and he accepts him, he comes into his presence with joy. He recounts to men his salvation, ^job-33-26

27 and he sings before men, and says: ‘I sinned and perverted what was right, and it was not repaid to me. ^job-33-27

28 He has redeemed my soul from going down into the Pit, and my life shall see the light.’ ^job-33-28

29 “Behold, God does all these things, twice, three times, with a man, ^job-33-29

30 to bring back his soul from the Pit, that he may see the light of life. ^job-33-30

31 Give heed, O Job, listen to me; be silent, and I will speak. ^job-33-31

32 If you have anything to say, answer me; speak, for I desire to justify you. ^job-33-32

33 If not, listen to me; be silent, and I will teach you wisdom.” ^job-33-33
