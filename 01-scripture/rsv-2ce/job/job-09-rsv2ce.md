# Job 9 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-09|NABRE]]

**Job Replies to Bildad**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-09-1

2 “Truly I know that it is so: But how can a man be just before God? ^job-09-2

3 If one wished to contend with him, one could not answer him once in a thousand times. ^job-09-3

4 He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength —who has hardened himself against him, and succeeded?— ^job-09-4

5 he who removes mountains, and they know it not, when he overturns them in his anger; ^job-09-5

6 who shakes the earth out of its place, and its pillars tremble; ^job-09-6

7 who commands the sun, and it does not rise; who seals up the stars; ^job-09-7

8 who alone stretched out the heavens, and trampled the waves of the sea; ^job-09-8

9 who made the Bear and Ori'on, the Plei'ades and the chambers of the south; ^job-09-9

10 who does great things beyond understanding, and marvelous things without number. ^job-09-10

11 Behold, he passes by me, and I see him not; he moves on, but I do not perceive him. ^job-09-11

12 Behold, he snatches away; who can hinder him? Who will say to him, ‘What are you doing?’ ^job-09-12

13 “God will not turn back his anger; beneath him bowed the helpers of Ra'hab. ^job-09-13

14 How then can I answer him, choosing my words with him? ^job-09-14

15 Though I am innocent, I cannot answer him; I must appeal for mercy to my accuser. ^job-09-15

16 If I summoned him and he answered me, I would not believe that he was listening to my voice. ^job-09-16

17 For he crushes me with a tempest, and multiplies my wounds without cause; ^job-09-17

18 he will not let me get my breath, but fills me with bitterness. ^job-09-18

19 If it is a contest of strength, behold him! If it is a matter of justice, who can summon him? ^job-09-19

20 Though I am innocent, my own mouth would condemn me; though I am blameless, he would prove me perverse. ^job-09-20

21 I am blameless; I regard not myself; I loathe my life. ^job-09-21

22 It is all one; therefore I say, he destroys both the blameless and the wicked. ^job-09-22

23 When disaster brings sudden death, he mocks at the calamity of the innocent. ^job-09-23

24 The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; he covers the faces of its judges— if it is not he, who then is it? ^job-09-24

25 “My days are swifter than a runner; they flee away, they see no good. ^job-09-25

26 They go by like skiffs of reed, like an eagle swooping on the prey. ^job-09-26

27 If I say, ‘I will forget my complaint, I will put off my sad countenance, and be of good cheer,’ ^job-09-27

28 I become afraid of all my suffering, for I know you will not hold me innocent. ^job-09-28

29 I shall be condemned; why then do I labor in vain? ^job-09-29

30 If I wash myself with snow, and cleanse my hands with lye, ^job-09-30

31 yet you will plunge me into a pit, and my own clothes will abhor me. ^job-09-31

32 For he is not a man, as I am, that I might answer him, that we should come to trial together. ^job-09-32

33 There is no umpire between us, who might lay his hand upon us both. ^job-09-33

34 Let him take his rod away from me, and let not dread of him terrify me. ^job-09-34

35 Then I would speak without fear of him, for I am not so in myself. ^job-09-35
