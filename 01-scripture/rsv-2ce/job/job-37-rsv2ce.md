# Job 37 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-37|NABRE]]

**The Greatness of God**

1 “At this also my heart trembles, and leaps out of its place. ^job-37-1

2 Listen to the thunder of his voice and the rumbling that comes from his mouth. ^job-37-2

3 Under the whole heaven he lets it go, and his lightning to the corners of the earth. ^job-37-3

4 After it his voice roars; he thunders with his majestic voice and he does not restrain the bolts of lightning when his voice is heard. ^job-37-4

5 God thunders wondrously with his voice; he does great things which we cannot comprehend. ^job-37-5

6 For to the snow he says, ‘Fall on the earth’; and to the shower and the rain, ‘Be strong.’ ^job-37-6

7 He seals up the hand of every man, that all men may know his work. ^job-37-7

8 Then the beasts go into their lairs, and remain in their dens. ^job-37-8

9 From its chamber comes the whirlwind, and cold from the scattering winds. ^job-37-9

10 By the breath of God ice is given, and the broad waters are frozen fast. ^job-37-10

11 He loads the thick cloud with moisture; the clouds scatter his lightning. ^job-37-11

12 They turn round and round by his guidance, to accomplish all that he commands them on the face of the habitable world. ^job-37-12

13 Whether for correction, or for his land, or for love, he causes it to happen. ^job-37-13

14 “Hear this, O Job; stop and consider the wondrous works of God. ^job-37-14

15 Do you know how God lays his command upon them, and causes the lightning of his cloud to shine? ^job-37-15

16 Do you know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him who is perfect in knowledge, ^job-37-16

17 you whose garments are hot when the earth is still because of the south wind? ^job-37-17

18 Can you, like him, spread out the skies, hard as a molten mirror? ^job-37-18

19 Teach us what we shall say to him; we cannot draw up our case because of darkness. ^job-37-19

20 Shall it be told him that I would speak? Did a man ever wish that he would be swallowed up? ^job-37-20

21 “And now men cannot look on the light when it is bright in the skies, when the wind has passed and cleared them. ^job-37-21

22 Out of the north comes golden splendor; God is clothed with awesome majesty. ^job-37-22

23 The Almighty—we cannot find him; he is great in power and justice, and abundant righteousness he will not violate. ^job-37-23

24 Therefore men fear him; he does not regard any who are wise in their own conceit.” ^job-37-24
