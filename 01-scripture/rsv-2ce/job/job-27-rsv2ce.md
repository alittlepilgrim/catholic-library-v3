# Job 27 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-27|NABRE]]

**Job Maintains His Integrity**

1 And Job again took up his discourse, and said: ^job-27-1

2 “As God lives, who has taken away my right, and the Almighty, who has made my soul bitter; ^job-27-2

3 as long as my breath is in me, and the spirit of God is in my nostrils; ^job-27-3

4 my lips will not speak falsehood, and my tongue will not utter deceit. ^job-27-4

5 Far be it from me to say that you are right; till I die I will not put away my integrity from me. ^job-27-5

6 I hold fast my righteousness, and will not let it go; my heart does not reproach me for any of my days. ^job-27-6

7 “Let my enemy be as the wicked, and let him that rises up against me be as the unrighteous. ^job-27-7

8 For what is the hope of the godless when God cuts him off, when God takes away his life? ^job-27-8

9 Will God hear his cry, when trouble comes upon him? ^job-27-9

10 Will he take delight in the Almighty? Will he call upon God at all times? ^job-27-10

11 I will teach you concerning the hand of God; what is with the Almighty I will not conceal. ^job-27-11

12 Behold, all of you have seen it yourselves; why then have you become altogether vain? ^job-27-12

13 “This is the portion of a wicked man with God, and the heritage which oppressors receive from the Almighty: ^job-27-13

14 If his children are multiplied, it is for the sword; and his offspring have not enough to eat. ^job-27-14

15 Those who survive him the pestilence buries, and their widows make no lamentation. ^job-27-15

16 Though he heap up silver like dust, and pile up clothing like clay; ^job-27-16

17 he may pile it up, but the just will wear it, and the innocent will divide the silver. ^job-27-17

18 The house which he builds is like a spider’s web, like a booth which a watchman makes. ^job-27-18

19 He goes to bed rich, but will do so no more; he opens his eyes, and his wealth is gone. ^job-27-19

20 Terrors overtake him like a flood; in the night a whirlwind carries him off. ^job-27-20

21 The east wind lifts him up and he is gone; it sweeps him out of his place. ^job-27-21

22 It hurls at him without pity; he flees from its power in headlong flight. ^job-27-22

23 It claps its hands at him, and hisses at him from its place. ^job-27-23
