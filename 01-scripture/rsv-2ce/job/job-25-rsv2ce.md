# Job 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-25|NABRE]]

**Bildad Speaks: How Can Man Be Righteous?**

1 Then Bildad the Shuhite answered: ^job-25-1

2 “Dominion and fear are with God; he makes peace in his high heaven. ^job-25-2

3 Is there any number to his armies? Upon whom does his light not arise? ^job-25-3

4 How then can man be righteous before God? How can he who is born of woman be clean? ^job-25-4

5 Behold, even the moon is not bright and the stars are not clean in his sight; ^job-25-5

6 how much less man, who is a maggot, and the son of man, who is a worm!” ^job-25-6
