# Job 35 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-35|NABRE]]

**Elihu Condemns Self-righteousness**

1 And Eli'hu said: ^job-35-1

2 “Do you think this to be just? Do you say, ‘It is my right before God,’ ^job-35-2

3 that you ask, ‘What advantage have I? How am I better off than if I had sinned?’ ^job-35-3

4 I will answer you and your friends with you. ^job-35-4

5 Look at the heavens, and see; and behold the clouds, which are higher than you. ^job-35-5

6 If you have sinned, what do you accomplish against him? And if your transgressions are multiplied, what do you do to him? ^job-35-6

7 If you are righteous, what do you give to him; or what does he receive from your hand? ^job-35-7

8 Your wickedness concerns a man like yourself, and your righteousness a son of man. ^job-35-8

9 “Because of the multitude of oppressions people cry out; they call for help because of the arm of the mighty. ^job-35-9

10 But none says, ‘Where is God my Maker, who gives songs in the night, ^job-35-10

11 who teaches us more than the beasts of the earth, and makes us wiser than the birds of the air?’ ^job-35-11

12 There they cry out, but he does not answer, because of the pride of evil men. ^job-35-12

13 Surely God does not hear an empty cry, nor does the Almighty regard it. ^job-35-13

14 How much less when you say that you do not see him, that the case is before him, and you are waiting for him! ^job-35-14

15 And now, because his anger does not punish, and he does not greatly heed transgression, ^job-35-15

16 Job opens his mouth in empty talk, he multiplies words without knowledge.” ^job-35-16
