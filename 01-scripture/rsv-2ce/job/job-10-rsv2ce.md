# Job 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-10|NABRE]]

**Job Says He Loathes His Life**

1 “I loathe my life; I will give free utterance to my complaint; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^job-10-1

2 I will say to God, Do not condemn me; let me know why you contend against me. ^job-10-2

3 Does it seem good to you to oppress, to despise the work of your hands and favor the designs of the wicked? ^job-10-3

4 Do you have eyes of flesh? Do you see as man sees? ^job-10-4

5 Are your days as the days of man, or your years as man’s years, ^job-10-5

6 that you seek out my iniquity and search for my sin, ^job-10-6

7 although you know that I am not guilty, and there is none to deliver out of your hand? ^job-10-7

8 Your hands fashioned and made me; and now you turn about and destroy me. ^job-10-8

9 Remember that you have made me of clay; and will you turn me to dust again? ^job-10-9

10 Did you not pour me out like milk and curdle me like cheese? ^job-10-10

11 You clothed me with skin and flesh, and knit me together with bones and sinews. ^job-10-11

12 You have granted me life and mercy; and your care has preserved my spirit. ^job-10-12

13 Yet these things you hid in your heart; I know that this was your purpose. ^job-10-13

14 If I sin, you mark me, and do not acquit me of my iniquity. ^job-10-14

15 If I am wicked, woe to me! If I am righteous, I cannot lift up my head, for I am filled with disgrace and look upon my affliction. ^job-10-15

16 And if I lift myself up, you hunt me like a lion, and again work wonders against me; ^job-10-16

17 you renew your witnesses against me, and increase your vexation toward me; you bring fresh hosts against me. ^job-10-17

18 “Why did you bring me forth from the womb? Would that I had died before any eye had seen me, ^job-10-18

19 and were as though I had not been, carried from the womb to the grave. ^job-10-19

20 Are not the days of my life few? Let me alone, that I may find a little comfort ^job-10-20

21 before I go from where I shall not return, to the land of gloom and deep darkness, ^job-10-21

22 the land of gloom and chaos, where light is as darkness.” ^job-10-22
