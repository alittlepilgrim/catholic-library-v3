# Job 24 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-24|NABRE]]

**Job Complains of Violence**

1 “Why are not times of judgment kept by the Almighty, and why do those who know him never see his days? ^job-24-1

2 Men remove landmarks; they seize flocks and pasture them. ^job-24-2

3 They drive away the donkey of the fatherless; they take the widow’s ox for a pledge. ^job-24-3

4 They thrust the poor off the road; the poor of the earth all hide themselves. ^job-24-4

5 Behold, like wild donkeys in the desert they go forth to their toil, seeking prey in the wilderness as food for their children. ^job-24-5

6 They gather their fodder in the field and they glean the vineyard of the wicked man. ^job-24-6

7 They lie all night naked, without clothing, and have no covering in the cold. ^job-24-7

8 They are wet with the rain of the mountains, and cling to the rock for want of shelter. ^job-24-8

9 (There are those who snatch the fatherless child from the breast, and take in pledge the infant of the poor.) ^job-24-9

10 They go about naked, without clothing; hungry, they carry the sheaves; ^job-24-10

11 among the olive rows of the wicked they make oil; they tread the wine presses, but suffer thirst. ^job-24-11

12 From out of the city the dying groan, and the soul of the wounded cries for help; yet God pays no attention to their prayer. ^job-24-12

13 “There are those who rebel against the light, who are not acquainted with its ways, and do not stay in its paths. ^job-24-13

14 The murderer rises in the dark, that he may kill the poor and needy; and in the night he is as a thief. ^job-24-14

15 The eye of the adulterer also waits for the twilight, saying, ‘No eye will see me’; and he disguises his face. ^job-24-15

16 In the dark they dig through houses; by day they shut themselves up; they do not know the light. ^job-24-16

17 For deep darkness is morning to all of them; for they are friends with the terrors of deep darkness. ^job-24-17

18 “You say, ‘They are swiftly carried away upon the face of the waters; their portion is cursed in the land; no treader turns toward their vineyards. ^job-24-18

19 Drought and heat snatch away the snow waters; so does Sheol those who have sinned. ^job-24-19

20 The squares of the town forget them; their name is no longer remembered; so wickedness is broken like a tree.’ ^job-24-20

21 “They feed on the barren childless woman, and do no good to the widow. ^job-24-21

22 Yet God prolongs the life of the mighty by his power; they rise up when they despair of life. ^job-24-22

23 He gives them security, and they are supported; and his eyes are upon their ways. ^job-24-23

24 They are exalted a little while, and then are gone; they wither and fade like the mallow; they are cut off like the heads of grain. ^job-24-24

25 If it is not so, who will prove me a liar, and show that there is nothing in what I say?” ^job-24-25
