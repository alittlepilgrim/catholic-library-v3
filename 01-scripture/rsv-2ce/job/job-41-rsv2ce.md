# Job 41 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-41|NABRE]]

1 “Can you draw out Levi'athan with a fishhook, or press down his tongue with a cord? ^job-41-1

2 Can you put a rope in his nose, or pierce his jaw with a hook? ^job-41-2

3 Will he make many supplications to you? Will he speak to you soft words? ^job-41-3

4 Will he make a covenant with you to take him for your servant for ever? ^job-41-4

5 Will you play with him as with a bird, or will you put him on leash for your maidens? ^job-41-5

6 Will traders bargain over him? Will they divide him up among the merchants? ^job-41-6

7 Can you fill his skin with harpoons, or his head with fishing spears? ^job-41-7

8 Lay hands on him; think of the battle; you will not do it again! ^job-41-8

9 Behold, the hope of a man is disappointed; he is laid low even at the sight of him. ^job-41-9

10 No one is so fierce that he dares to stir him up. Who then is he that can stand before me? ^job-41-10

11 Who has given to me, that I should repay him? Whatever is under the whole heaven is mine. ^job-41-11

12 “I will not keep silence concerning his limbs, or his mighty strength, or his large frame. ^job-41-12

13 Who can strip off his outer garment? Who can penetrate his double coat of mail? ^job-41-13

14 Who can open the doors of his face? Round about his teeth is terror. ^job-41-14

15 His back is made of rows of shields, shut up closely as with a seal. ^job-41-15

16 One is so near to another that no air can come between them. ^job-41-16

17 They are joined one to another; they clasp each other and cannot be separated. ^job-41-17

18 His sneezings flash forth light, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the dawn. ^job-41-18

19 Out of his mouth go flaming torches; sparks of fire leap forth. ^job-41-19

20 Out of his nostrils comes forth smoke, as from a boiling pot and burning rushes. ^job-41-20

21 His breath kindles coals, and a flame comes forth from his mouth. ^job-41-21

22 In his neck abides strength, and terror dances before him. ^job-41-22

23 The folds of his flesh cling together, firmly cast upon him and immovable. ^job-41-23

24 His heart is hard as a stone, hard as the nether millstone. ^job-41-24

25 When he raises himself up the mighty are afraid; at the crashing they are beside themselves. ^job-41-25

26 Though the sword reaches him, it does not avail; nor the spear, the dart, or the javelin. ^job-41-26

27 He counts iron as straw, and bronze as rotten wood. ^job-41-27

28 The arrow cannot make him flee; for him slingstones are turned to stubble. ^job-41-28

29 Clubs are counted as stubble; he laughs at the rattle of javelins. ^job-41-29

30 His underparts are like sharp potsherds; he spreads himself like a threshing sledge on the mire. ^job-41-30

31 He makes the deep boil like a pot; he makes the sea like a pot of ointment. ^job-41-31

32 Behind him he leaves a shining wake; one would think the deep to be hoary. ^job-41-32

33 Upon earth there is not his like, a creature without fear. ^job-41-33

34 He beholds everything that is high; he is king over all the sons of pride.” ^job-41-34
