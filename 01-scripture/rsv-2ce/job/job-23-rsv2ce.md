# Job 23 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-23|NABRE]]

**Job Replies: I Am Hemmed In**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-23-1

2 “Today also my complaint is bitter, his hand is heavy in spite of my groaning. ^job-23-2

3 Oh, that I knew where I might find him, that I might come even to his seat! ^job-23-3

4 I would lay my case before him and fill my mouth with arguments. ^job-23-4

5 I would learn what he would answer me, and understand what he would say to me. ^job-23-5

6 Would he contend with me in the greatness of his power? No; he would give heed to me. ^job-23-6

7 There an upright man could reason with him, and I should be acquitted for ever by my judge. ^job-23-7

8 “Behold, I go forward, but he is not there; and backward, but I cannot perceive him; ^job-23-8

9 on the left hand I seek him, but I cannot behold him; I turn to the right hand, but I cannot see him. ^job-23-9

10 But he knows the way that I take; when he has tried me, I shall come forth as gold. ^job-23-10

11 My foot has held fast to his steps; I have kept his way and have not turned aside. ^job-23-11

12 I have not departed from the commandment of his lips; I have treasured in my bosom the words of his mouth. ^job-23-12

13 But he is unchangeable and who can turn him? What he desires, that he does. ^job-23-13

14 For he will complete what he appoints for me; and many such things are in his mind. ^job-23-14

15 Therefore I am terrified at his presence; when I consider, I am in dread of him. ^job-23-15

16 God has made my heart faint; the Almighty has terrified me; ^job-23-16

17 for I am hemmed in by darkness, and thick darkness covers my face. ^job-23-17
