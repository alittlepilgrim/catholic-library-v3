# Job 13 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-13|NABRE]]

1 “Behold, my eye has seen all this, my ear has heard and understood it. ^job-13-1

2 What you know, I also know; I am not inferior to you. ^job-13-2

3 But I would speak to the Almighty, and I desire to argue my case with God. ^job-13-3

4 As for you, you whitewash with lies; worthless physicians are you all. ^job-13-4

5 Oh, that you would keep silent, and it would be your wisdom! ^job-13-5

6 Hear now my reasoning, and listen to the pleadings of my lips. ^job-13-6

7 Will you speak falsely for God, and speak deceitfully for him? ^job-13-7

8 Will you show partiality toward him, will you plead the case for God? ^job-13-8

9 Will it be well with you when he searches you out? Or can you deceive him, as one deceives a man? ^job-13-9

10 He will surely rebuke you if in secret you show partiality. ^job-13-10

11 Will not his majesty terrify you, and the dread of him fall upon you? ^job-13-11

12 Your maxims are proverbs of ashes, your defenses are defenses of clay. ^job-13-12

13 “Let me have silence, and I will speak, and let come on me what may. ^job-13-13

14 I will take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in my hand. ^job-13-14

15 Behold, he will slay me; I have no hope; yet I will defend my ways to his face. ^job-13-15

16 This will be my salvation, that a godless man shall not come before him. ^job-13-16

17 Listen carefully to my words, and let my declaration be in your ears. ^job-13-17

18 Behold, I have prepared my case; I know that I shall be vindicated. ^job-13-18

19 Who is there that will contend with me? For then I would be silent and die. ^job-13-19

20 Only grant two things to me, then I will not hide myself from your face: ^job-13-20

21 withdraw your hand far from me, and let not dread of you terrify me. ^job-13-21

22 Then call, and I will answer; or let me speak, and do reply to me. ^job-13-22

23 How many are my iniquities and my sins? Make me know my transgression and my sin. ^job-13-23

24 Why do you hide your face, and count me as your enemy? ^job-13-24

25 Will you frighten a driven leaf and pursue dry chaff? ^job-13-25

26 For you write bitter things against me, and make me inherit the iniquities of my youth. ^job-13-26

27 You put my feet in the stocks, and watch all my paths; you set a bound to the soles of my feet. ^job-13-27

28 Man wastes away like a rotten thing, like a garment that is moth-eaten. ^job-13-28
