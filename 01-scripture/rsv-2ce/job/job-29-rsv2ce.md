# Job 29 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-29|NABRE]]

**Job Recalls Past Happiness**

1 And Job again took up his discourse, and said: ^job-29-1

2 “Oh, that I were as in the months of old, as in the days when God watched over me; ^job-29-2

3 when his lamp shone upon my head, and by his light I walked through darkness; ^job-29-3

4 as I was in my autumn days, when the friendship of God was upon my tent; ^job-29-4

5 when the Almighty was yet with me, when my children were about me; ^job-29-5

6 when my steps were washed with milk, and the rock poured out for me streams of oil! ^job-29-6

7 When I went out to the gate of the city, when I prepared my seat in the square, ^job-29-7

8 the young men saw me and withdrew, and the aged rose and stood; ^job-29-8

9 the princes refrained from talking, and laid their hand on their mouth; ^job-29-9

10 the voice of the nobles was hushed, and their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth. ^job-29-10

11 When the ear heard, it called me blessed, and when the eye saw, it approved; ^job-29-11

12 because I delivered the poor who cried, and the fatherless who had none to help him. ^job-29-12

13 The blessing of him who was about to perish came upon me, and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy. ^job-29-13

14 I put on righteousness, and it clothed me; my justice was like a robe and a turban. ^job-29-14

15 I was eyes to the blind, and feet to the lame. ^job-29-15

16 I was a father to the poor, and I searched out the cause of him whom I did not know. ^job-29-16

17 I broke the fangs of the unrighteous, and made him drop his prey from his teeth. ^job-29-17

18 Then I thought, ‘I shall die in my nest, and I shall multiply my days as the sand, ^job-29-18

19 my roots spread out to the waters, with the dew all night on my branches, ^job-29-19

20 my glory fresh with me, and my bow ever new in my hand.’ ^job-29-20

21 “Men listened to me, and waited, and kept silence for my counsel. ^job-29-21

22 After I spoke they did not speak again, and my word dropped upon them. ^job-29-22

23 They waited for me as for the rain; and they opened their mouths as for the spring rain. ^job-29-23

24 I smiled on them when they had no confidence; and the light of my countenance they did not cast down. ^job-29-24

25 I chose their way, and sat as chief, and I dwelt like a king among his troops, like one who comforts mourners. ^job-29-25
