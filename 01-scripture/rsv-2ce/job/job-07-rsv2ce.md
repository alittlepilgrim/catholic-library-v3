# Job 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-07|NABRE]]

**Job Speaks of His Sufferings**

1 “Has not man a hard service upon earth, and are not his days like the days of a hireling? ^job-07-1

2 Like a slave who longs for the shadow, and like a hireling who looks for his wages, ^job-07-2

3 so I am allotted months of emptiness, and nights of misery are apportioned to me. ^job-07-3

4 When I lie down I say, ‘When shall I arise?’ But the night is long, and I am full of tossing till the dawn. ^job-07-4

5 My flesh is clothed with worms and dirt; my skin hardens, then breaks out afresh. ^job-07-5

6 My days are swifter than a weaver’s shuttle, and come to their end without hope. ^job-07-6

7 “Remember that my life is a breath; my eye will never again see good. ^job-07-7

8 The eye of him who sees me will behold me no more; while your eyes are upon me, I shall be gone. ^job-07-8

9 As the cloud fades and vanishes, so he who goes down to Sheol does not come up; ^job-07-9

10 he returns no more to his house, nor does his place know him any more. ^job-07-10

11 “Therefore I will not restrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul. ^job-07-11

12 Am I the sea, or a sea monster, that you set a guard over me? ^job-07-12

13 When I say, ‘My bed will comfort me, my couch will ease my complaint,’ ^job-07-13

14 then you scare me with dreams and terrify me with visions, ^job-07-14

15 so that I would choose strangling and death rather than my bones. ^job-07-15

16 I loathe my life; I would not live for ever. Let me alone, for my days are a breath. ^job-07-16

17 What is man, that you make so much of him, and that you set your mind upon him, ^job-07-17

18 visit him every morning, and test him every moment? ^job-07-18

19 How long will you not look away from me, nor let me alone till I swallow my spittle? ^job-07-19

20 If I sin, what do I do to you, you watcher of men? Why have you made me your mark? Why have I become a burden to you? ^job-07-20

21 Why do you not pardon my transgression and take away my iniquity? For now I shall lie in the earth; you will seek me, but I shall not be.” ^job-07-21
