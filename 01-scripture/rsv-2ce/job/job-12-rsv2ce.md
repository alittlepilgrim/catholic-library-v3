# Job 12 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-12|NABRE]]

**Job Replies to Zophar**

1 Then Job answered: ^job-12-1

2 “No doubt you are the people, and wisdom will die with you. ^job-12-2

3 But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you. Who does not know such things as these? ^job-12-3

4 I am a laughingstock to my friends; I, who called upon God and he answered me, a just and blameless man, am a laughingstock. ^job-12-4

5 In the thought of one who is at ease there is contempt for misfortune; it is ready for those whose feet slip. ^job-12-5

6 The tents of robbers are at peace, and those who provoke God are secure, who bring their god in their hand. ^job-12-6

7 “But ask the beasts, and they will teach you; the birds of the air, and they will tell you; ^job-12-7

8 or the plants of the earth, and they will teach you; and the fish of the sea will declare to you. ^job-12-8

9 Who among all these does not know that the hand of the LORD has done this? ^job-12-9

10 In his hand is the life of every living thing and the breath of all mankind. ^job-12-10

11 Does not the ear try words as the palate tastes food? ^job-12-11

12 Wisdom is with the aged, and understanding in length of days. ^job-12-12

13 “With God are wisdom and might; he has counsel and understanding. ^job-12-13

14 If he tears down, none can rebuild; if he shuts a man in, none can open. ^job-12-14

15 If he withholds the waters, they dry up; if he sends them out, they overwhelm the land. ^job-12-15

16 With him are strength and wisdom; the deceived and the deceiver are his. ^job-12-16

17 He leads counselors away stripped, and judges he makes fools. ^job-12-17

18 He looses the bonds of kings, and binds a waistcloth on their loins. ^job-12-18

19 He leads priests away stripped, and overthrows the mighty. ^job-12-19

20 He deprives of speech those who are trusted, and takes away the discernment of the elders. ^job-12-20

21 He pours contempt on princes, and looses the belt of the strong. ^job-12-21

22 He uncovers the deeps out of darkness, and brings deep darkness to light. ^job-12-22

23 He makes nations great, and he destroys them: he enlarges nations, and leads them away. ^job-12-23

24 He takes away understanding from the chiefs of the people of the earth, and makes them wander in a pathless waste. ^job-12-24

25 They grope in the dark without light; and he makes them stagger like a drunken man. ^job-12-25
