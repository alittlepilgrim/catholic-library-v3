# Job 40 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[job-40|NABRE]]

**Dialogue: God Challenges Job**

1 And the LORD said to Job: ^job-40-1

2 “Shall a faultfinder contend with the Almighty? He who argues with God, let him answer it.” ^job-40-2

3 Then Job answered the LORD: ^job-40-3

4 “Behold, I am of small account; what shall I answer you? I lay my hand on my mouth. ^job-40-4

5 I have spoken once, and I will not answer; twice, but I will proceed no further.” ^job-40-5

6 Then the LORD answered Job out of the whirlwind: ^job-40-6

7 “Gird up your loins like a man; I will question you, and you declare to me. ^job-40-7

8 Will you even put me in the wrong? Will you condemn me that you may be justified? ^job-40-8

9 Have you an arm like God, and can you thunder with a voice like his? ^job-40-9

10 “Deck yourself with majesty and dignity; clothe yourself with glory and splendor. ^job-40-10

11 Pour forth the overflowings of your anger, and look on every one that is proud, and abase him. ^job-40-11

12 Look on every one that is proud, and bring him low; and tread down the wicked where they stand. ^job-40-12

13 Hide them all in the dust together; bind their faces in the world below. ^job-40-13

14 Then will I also acknowledge to you, that your own right hand can give you victory. ^job-40-14

15 “Behold, Be'hemoth, which I made as I made you; he eats grass like an ox. ^job-40-15

16 Behold, his strength in his loins, and his power in the muscles of his belly. ^job-40-16

17 He makes his tail stiff like a cedar; the sinews of his thighs are knit together. ^job-40-17

18 His bones are tubes of bronze, his limbs like bars of iron. ^job-40-18

19 “He is the first of the works of God; let him who made him bring near his sword! ^job-40-19

20 For the mountains yield food for him where all the wild beasts play. ^job-40-20

21 Under the lotus plants he lies, in the hiding place of the reeds and in the marsh. ^job-40-21

22 For his shade the lotus trees cover him; the willows of the brook surround him. ^job-40-22

23 Behold, if the river is turbulent he is not frightened; he is confident though Jordan rushes against his mouth. ^job-40-23

24 Can one take him with hooks, or pierce his nose with a snare? ^job-40-24
