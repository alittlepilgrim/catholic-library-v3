# Luke 15 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Parable of the Lost Sheep**

1 Now the tax collectors and sinners were all drawing near to hear him. ^luke-15-1

2 And the Pharisees and the scribes murmured, saying, "This man receives sinners and eats with them." ^luke-15-2

3 So he told them this parable: ^luke-15-3

4 "What man of you, having a hundred sheep, if he has lost one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness, and go after the one which is lost, until he finds it? ^luke-15-4

5 And when he has found it, he lays it on his shoulders, rejoicing. ^luke-15-5

6 And when he comes home, he calls together his friends and his neighbors, saying to them, 'Rejoice with me, for I have found my sheep which was lost.' ^luke-15-6

7 Just so, I tell you, there will be more joy in heaven over one sinner who repents than over ninety-nine righteous persons who need no repentance. ^luke-15-7

**The Parable of the Lost Coin**

8 "Or what woman, having ten silver coins,<sup>t</sup> if she loses one coin, does not light a lamp and sweep the house and seek diligently until she finds it? ^luke-15-8

9 And when she has found it, she calls together her friends and neighbors, saying, 'Rejoice with me, for I have found the coin which I had lost.' ^luke-15-9

10 Just so, I tell you, there is joy before the angels of God over one sinner who repents." ^luke-15-10

**The Parable of the Prodigal and His Brother**

11 And he said, "There was a man who had two sons; ^luke-15-11

12 and the younger of them said to his father, 'Father, give me the share of property that falls to me.' And he divided his living between them. ^luke-15-12

13 Not many days later, the younger son gathered all he had and took his journey into a far country, and there he squandered his property in loose living. ^luke-15-13

14 And when he had spent everything, a great famine arose in that country, and he began to be in want. ^luke-15-14

15 So he went and joined himself to one of the citizens of that country, who sent him into his fields to feed swine. ^luke-15-15

16 And he would gladly have fed on<sup>u</sup> the pods that the swine ate; and no one gave him anything. ^luke-15-16

17 But when he came to himself he said, 'How many of my father's hired servants have bread enough and to spare, but I perish here with hunger! ^luke-15-17

18 I will arise and go to my father, and I will say to him, "Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; ^luke-15-18

19 I am no longer worthy to be called your son; treat me as one of your hired servants." ' ^luke-15-19

20 And he arose and came to his father. But while he was yet at a distance, his father saw him and had compassion, and ran and embraced him and kissed him. ^luke-15-20

21 And the son said to him, 'Father, I have sinned against heaven and before you; I am no longer worthy to be called your son.'<sup>v</sup> ^luke-15-21

22 But the father said to his servants, 'Bring quickly the best robe, and put it on him; and put a ring on his hand, and shoes on his feet; ^luke-15-22

23 and bring the fatted calf and kill it, and let us eat and make merry; ^luke-15-23

24 for this my son was dead, and is alive again; he was lost, and is found.' And they began to make merry. ^luke-15-24

25 "Now his elder son was in the field; and as he came and drew near to the house, he heard music and dancing. ^luke-15-25

26 And he called one of the servants and asked what this meant. ^luke-15-26

27 And he said to him, 'Your brother has come, and your father has killed the fatted calf, because he has received him safe and sound.' ^luke-15-27

28 But he was angry and refused to go in. His father came out and entreated him, ^luke-15-28

29 but he answered his father, 'Behold, these many years I have served you, and I never disobeyed your command; yet you never gave me a kid, that I might make merry with my friends. ^luke-15-29

30 But when this son of yours came, who has devoured your living with harlots, you killed for him the fatted calf!' ^luke-15-30

31 And he said to him, 'Son, you are always with me, and all that is mine is yours. ^luke-15-31

32 It was fitting to make merry and be glad, for this your brother was dead, and is alive; he was lost, and is found.' " ^luke-15-32
