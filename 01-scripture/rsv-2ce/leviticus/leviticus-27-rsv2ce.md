# Leviticus 27 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[leviticus-27|NABRE]]

**Votive Offerings**

1 The LORD said to Moses, ^leviticus-27-1

2 “Say to the sons of Israel, When a man makes a special vow of persons to the LORD at your valuation, ^leviticus-27-2

3 then your valuation of a male from twenty years old up to sixty years old shall be fifty shekels of silver, according to the shekel of the sanctuary. ^leviticus-27-3

4 If the person is a female, your valuation shall be thirty shekels. ^leviticus-27-4

5 If the person is from five years old up to twenty years old, your valuation shall be for a male twenty shekels, and for a female ten shekels. ^leviticus-27-5

6 If the person is from a month old up to five years old, your valuation shall be for a male five shekels of silver, and for a female your valuation shall be three shekels of silver. ^leviticus-27-6

7 And if the person is sixty years old and upward, then your valuation for a male shall be fifteen shekels, and for a female ten shekels. ^leviticus-27-7

8 And if a man is too poor to pay your valuation, then he shall bring the person before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to the ability of him who vowed the priest shall value him. ^leviticus-27-8

9 “If it is an animal such as men offer as an offering to the LORD, all of such that any man gives to the LORD is holy. ^leviticus-27-9

10 He shall not substitute anything for it or exchange it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good; and if he makes any exchange of beast for beast, then both it and that for which it is exchanged shall be holy. ^leviticus-27-10

11 And if it is an unclean animal such as is not offered as an offering to the LORD, then the man shall bring the animal before the priest, ^leviticus-27-11

12 and the priest shall value it as either good or bad; as you, the priest, value it, so it shall be. ^leviticus-27-12

13 But if he wishes to redeem it, he shall add a fifth to the valuation. ^leviticus-27-13

14 “When a man dedicates his house to be holy to the LORD, the priest shall value it as either good or bad; as the priest values it, so it shall stand. ^leviticus-27-14

15 And if he who dedicates it wishes to redeem his house, he shall add a fifth of the valuation in money to it, and it shall be his. ^leviticus-27-15

16 “If a man dedicates to the LORD part of the land which is his by inheritance, then your valuation shall be according to the seed for it; a sowing of a homer of barley shall be valued at fifty shekels of silver. ^leviticus-27-16

17 If he dedicates his field from the year of jubilee, it shall stand at your full valuation; ^leviticus-27-17

18 but if he dedicates his field after the jubilee, then the priest shall compute the money-value for it according to the years that remain until the year of jubilee, and a deduction shall be made from your valuation. ^leviticus-27-18

19 And if he who dedicates the field wishes to redeem it, then he shall add a fifth of the valuation in money to it, and it shall remain his. ^leviticus-27-19

20 But if he does not wish to redeem the field, or if he has sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any more; ^leviticus-27-20

21 but the field, when it is released in the jubilee, shall be holy to the LORD, as a field that has been devoted; the priest shall be in possession of it. ^leviticus-27-21

22 If he dedicates to the LORD a field which he has bought, which is not a part of his possession by inheritance, ^leviticus-27-22

23 then the priest shall compute the valuation for it up to the year of jubilee, and the man shall give the amount of the valuation on that day as a holy thing to the LORD. ^leviticus-27-23

24 In the year of jubilee the field shall return to him from whom it was bought, to whom the land belongs as a possession by inheritance. ^leviticus-27-24

25 Every valuation shall be according to the shekel of the sanctuary: twenty gerahs shall make a shekel. ^leviticus-27-25

26 “But a firstling of animals, which as a firstling belongs to the LORD, no man may dedicate; whether ox or sheep, it is the LORD’s. ^leviticus-27-26

27 And if it is an unclean animal, then he shall buy it back at your valuation, and add a fifth to it; or, if it is not redeemed, it shall be sold at your valuation. ^leviticus-27-27

28 “But no devoted thing that a man devotes to the LORD, of anything that he has, whether of man or beast, or of his inherited field, shall be sold or redeemed; every devoted thing is most holy to the LORD. ^leviticus-27-28

29 No one devoted, who is to be utterly destroyed from among men, shall be ransomed; he shall be put to death. ^leviticus-27-29

30 “All the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land or of the fruit of the trees, is the LORD’s; it is holy to the LORD. ^leviticus-27-30

31 If a man wishes to redeem any of his tithe, he shall add a fifth to it. ^leviticus-27-31

32 And all the tithe of herds and flocks, every tenth animal of all that pass under the herdsman’s staff, shall be holy to the LORD. ^leviticus-27-32

33 A man shall not inquire whether it is good or bad, neither shall he exchange it; and if he exchanges it, then both it and that for which it is exchanged shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.” ^leviticus-27-33

34 These are the commandments which the LORD commanded Moses for the sons of Israel on Mount Sinai. ^leviticus-27-34
