# Habakkuk 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[habakkuk-01|NABRE]]

**The Prophet’s Complaint**

1 The oracle of God which Habak'kuk the prophet saw. ^habakkuk-01-1

2 O LORD, how long shall I cry for help, and you will not hear? Or cry to you “Violence!” and you will not save? ^habakkuk-01-2

3 Why do you make me see wrongs and look upon trouble? Destruction and violence are before me; strife and contention arise. ^habakkuk-01-3

4 So the law is slacked and justice never goes forth. For the wicked surround the righteous, so justice goes forth perverted. ^habakkuk-01-4

5 Look among the nations, and see; wonder and be astounded. For I am doing a work in your days that you would not believe if told. ^habakkuk-01-5

6 For behold, I am rousing the Chalde'ans, that bitter and hasty nation, who march through the breadth of the earth, to seize habitations not their own. ^habakkuk-01-6

7 Dread and fearsome are they; their justice and dignity proceed from themselves. ^habakkuk-01-7

8 Their horses are swifter than leopards, more fierce than the evening wolves; their horsemen press proudly on. Yes, their horsemen come from afar; they fly like an eagle swift to devour. ^habakkuk-01-8

9 They all come for violence; terror of them goes before them. They gather captives like sand. ^habakkuk-01-9

10 At kings they scoff, and of rulers they make sport. They laugh at every fortress, for they heap up earth and take it. ^habakkuk-01-10

11 Then they sweep by like the wind and go on, guilty men, whose own might is their god! ^habakkuk-01-11

12 Are you not from everlasting, O LORD my God, my Holy One? We shall not die. O LORD, you have ordained them as a judgment; and you, O Rock, have established them for chastisement. ^habakkuk-01-12

13 You who are of purer eyes than to behold evil and cannot look on wrong, why do you look on faithless men, and are silent when the wicked swallows up the man more righteous than he? ^habakkuk-01-13

14 For you make men like the fish of the sea, like crawling things that have no ruler. ^habakkuk-01-14

15 He brings all of them up with a hook, he drags them out with his net, he gathers them in his seine; so he rejoices and exults. ^habakkuk-01-15

16 Therefore he sacrifices to his net and burns incense to his seine; for by them he lives in luxury, and his food is rich. ^habakkuk-01-16

17 Is he then to keep on emptying his net, and mercilessly slaying nations for ever? ^habakkuk-01-17
