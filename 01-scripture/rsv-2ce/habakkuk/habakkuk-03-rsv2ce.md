# Habakkuk 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[habakkuk-03|NABRE]]

**Habakkuk’s Prayer**

1 A prayer of Habak'kuk the prophet, accord-ing to Shigion'oth. ^habakkuk-03-1

2 O LORD, I have heard the report of you, and your work, O LORD, I fear. In the midst of the years renew it; in the midst of the years make it known; in wrath remember mercy. ^habakkuk-03-2

3 God came from Te'man, and the Holy One from Mount Par'an. His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise. Selah ^habakkuk-03-3

4 His brightness was like the light, rays flashed from his hand; and there he veiled his power. ^habakkuk-03-4

5 Before him went pestilence, and plague followed close behind. ^habakkuk-03-5

6 He stood and measured the earth; he looked and shook the nations; Then the eternal mountains were scattered, the everlasting hills sank low. His ways were as of old. ^habakkuk-03-6

7 I saw the tents of Cush'an in affliction; the curtains of the land of Mid'ian trembled. ^habakkuk-03-7

8 Was your wrath against the rivers, O LORD? Was your anger against the rivers, or your indignation against the sea, when you rode upon your horses, upon your chariot of victory? ^habakkuk-03-8

9 You stripped the sheath from your bow, and put the arrows to the string. Selah You split the earth with rivers. ^habakkuk-03-9

10 The mountains saw you, and writhed; the raging waters swept on; the deep gave forth its voice, it lifted its hands on high. ^habakkuk-03-10

11 The sun and moon stood still in their habitation at the light of your arrows as they sped, at the flash of your glittering spear. ^habakkuk-03-11

12 You bestrode the earth in fury, you trampled the nations in anger. ^habakkuk-03-12

13 You went forth for the salvation of your people, for the salvation of your anointed. You crushed the head of the wicked, laying him bare from thigh to neck. Selah ^habakkuk-03-13

14 You pierced with your shafts the head of his warriors, who came like a whirlwind to scatter me, rejoicing as if to devour the poor in secret. ^habakkuk-03-14

15 You trampled the sea with your horses, the surging of mighty waters. ^habakkuk-03-15

16 I hear, and my body trembles, my lips quiver at the sound; rottenness enters into my bones, my steps totter beneath me. I will quietly wait for the day of trouble to come upon people who invade us. ^habakkuk-03-16

17 Though the fig tree does not blossom, nor fruit be on the vines, the produce of the olive fail and the fields yield no food, the flock be cut off from the fold and there be no herd in the stalls, ^habakkuk-03-17

18 yet I will rejoice in the LORD, I will joy in the God of my salvation. ^habakkuk-03-18

19 GOD, the Lord, is my strength; he makes my feet like deer’s feet, he makes me tread upon my high places. To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. ^habakkuk-03-19
