# Psalm 139 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-139|NABRE]]

**The Inescapable God**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 O LORD, you have searched me and known me! ^psalm-139-1

2 You know when I sit down and when I rise up; you discern my thoughts from afar. ^psalm-139-2

3 You search out my path and my lying down, and are acquainted with all my ways. ^psalm-139-3

4 Even before a word is on my tongue, behold, O LORD, you know it altogether. ^psalm-139-4

5 You beset me behind and before, and lay your hand upon me. ^psalm-139-5

6 Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is high, I cannot attain it. ^psalm-139-6

7 Where shall I go from your Spirit? Or where shall I flee from your presence? ^psalm-139-7

8 If I ascend to heaven, you are there! If I make my bed in Sheol, you are there! ^psalm-139-8

9 If I take the wings of the morning and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea, ^psalm-139-9

10 even there your hand shall lead me, and your right hand shall hold me. ^psalm-139-10

11 If I say, “Let only darkness cover me, and the light about me be night,” ^psalm-139-11

12 even the darkness is not dark to you, the night is bright as the day; for darkness is as light with you. ^psalm-139-12

13 For you formed my inward parts, you knitted me together in my mother’s womb. ^psalm-139-13

14 I praise you, for I am wondrously made. Wonderful are your works! You know me right well; ^psalm-139-14

15 my frame was not hidden from you, when I was being made in secret, intricately wrought in the depths of the earth. ^psalm-139-15

16 Your eyes beheld my unformed substance; in your book were written, every one of them, the days that were formed for me, when as yet there was none of them. ^psalm-139-16

17 How precious to me are your thoughts, O God! How vast is the sum of them! ^psalm-139-17

18 If I would count them, they are more than the sand. When I awake, I am still with you. ^psalm-139-18

19 O that you would slay the wicked, O God, and that men of blood would depart from me, ^psalm-139-19

20 men who maliciously defy you, who lift themselves up against you for evil! ^psalm-139-20

21 Do I not hate them that hate you, O LORD? And do I not loathe them that rise up against you? ^psalm-139-21

22 I hate them with perfect hatred; I count them my enemies. ^psalm-139-22

23 Search me, O God, and know my heart! Try me and know my thoughts! ^psalm-139-23

24 And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting! To the choirmaster. ^psalm-139-24
