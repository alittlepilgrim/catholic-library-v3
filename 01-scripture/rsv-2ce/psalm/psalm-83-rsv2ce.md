# Psalm 83 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-83|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Judgment on Israel’s Foes**

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

1 O God, do not keep silence; do not hold your peace or be still, O God! ^psalm-83-1

2 For behold, your enemies are in tumult; those who hate you have raised their heads. ^psalm-83-2

3 They lay crafty plans against your people; they consult together against your protected ones. ^psalm-83-3

4 They say, “Come, let us wipe them out as a nation; let the name of Israel be remembered no more!” ^psalm-83-4

5 Yes, they conspire with one accord; against you they make a covenant— ^psalm-83-5

6 the tents of Edom and the Ish'maelites, Moab and the Hagrites, ^psalm-83-6

7 Gebal and Ammon and Am'alek, Philistia with the inhabitants of Tyre; ^psalm-83-7

8 Assyria also has joined them; they are the strong arm of the children of Lot. Selah ^psalm-83-8

9 Do to them as you did to Mid'ian, as to Sis'era and Jabin at the river Kishon, ^psalm-83-9

10 who were destroyed at En-dor, who became dung for the ground. ^psalm-83-10

11 Make their nobles like Oreb and Zeeb, all their princes like Zebah and Zalmun'na, ^psalm-83-11

12 who said, “Let us take possession for ourselves of the pastures of God.” ^psalm-83-12

13 O my God, make them like whirling dust, like chaff before the wind. ^psalm-83-13

14 As fire consumes the forest, as the flame sets the mountains ablaze, ^psalm-83-14

15 so you pursue them with your tempest and terrify them with your hurricane! ^psalm-83-15

16 Fill their faces with shame, that they may seek your name, O LORD. ^psalm-83-16

17 Let them be put to shame and dismayed for ever; let them perish in disgrace. ^psalm-83-17

18 Let them know that you alone, whose name is the LORD, are the Most High over all the earth. ^psalm-83-18
