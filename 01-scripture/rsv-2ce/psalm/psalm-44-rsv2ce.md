# Psalm 44 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-44|NABRE]]

**National Lament and Prayer for Help**

*To the choirmaster. A Maskil of the Sons of Korah.*

1 We have heard with our ears, O God, our fathers have told us, what deeds you performed in their days, in the days of old: ^psalm-44-1

2 you with your own hand drove out the nations, but you planted them; you afflicted the peoples, but you set them free; ^psalm-44-2

3 for not by their own sword did they win the land, nor did their own arm give them victory; but your right hand, and your arm, and the light of your countenance; for you delighted in them. ^psalm-44-3

4 You are my King and my God, who ordain victories for Jacob. ^psalm-44-4

5 Through you we push down our foes; through your name we tread down our assailants. ^psalm-44-5

6 For not in my bow do I trust, nor can my sword save me. ^psalm-44-6

7 But you have saved us from our foes, and have put to confusion those who hate us. ^psalm-44-7

8 In God we have boasted continually, and we will give thanks to your name for ever. Selah ^psalm-44-8

9 Yet you have cast us off and abased us, and have not gone out with our armies. ^psalm-44-9

10 You have made us turn back from the foe; and our enemies have gotten spoil. ^psalm-44-10

11 You have made us like sheep for slaughter, and have scattered us among the nations. ^psalm-44-11

12 You have sold your people for a trifle, demanding no high price for them. ^psalm-44-12

13 You have made us the taunt of our neighbors, the derision and scorn of those about us. ^psalm-44-13

14 You have made us a byword among the nations, a laughingstock among the peoples. ^psalm-44-14

15 All day long my disgrace is before me, and shame has covered my face, ^psalm-44-15

16 at the words of the taunters and revilers, at the sight of the enemy and the avenger. ^psalm-44-16

17 All this has come upon us, though we have not forgotten you, or been false to your covenant. ^psalm-44-17

18 Our heart has not turned back, nor have our steps departed from your way, ^psalm-44-18

19 that you should have broken us in the place of jackals, and covered us with deep darkness. ^psalm-44-19

20 If we had forgotten the name of our God, or spread forth our hands to a strange god, ^psalm-44-20

21 would not God discover this? For he knows the secrets of the heart. ^psalm-44-21

22 No, for your sake we are slain all the day long, and accounted as sheep for the slaughter. ^psalm-44-22

23 Rouse yourself! Why do you sleep, O Lord? Awake! Do not cast us off for ever! ^psalm-44-23

24 Why do you hide your face? Why do you forget our affliction and oppression? ^psalm-44-24

25 For our soul is bowed down to the dust; our body clings to the ground. ^psalm-44-25

26 Rise up, come to our help! Deliver us for the sake of your merciful love! ^psalm-44-26
