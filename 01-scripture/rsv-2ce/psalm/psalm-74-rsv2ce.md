# Psalm 74 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-74|NABRE]]

**Plea for Help in Time of National Humiliation**

1 O God, why do you cast us off for ever? Why does your anger smoke against the sheep of your pasture? ^psalm-74-1

2 Remember your congregation, which you have gotten of old, which you have redeemed to be the tribe of your heritage! Remember Mount Zion, where you have dwelt. ^psalm-74-2

3 Direct your steps to the perpetual ruins; the enemy has destroyed everything in the sanctuary! ^psalm-74-3

4 Your foes have roared in the midst of your holy place; they set up their own signs for signs. ^psalm-74-4

5 At the upper entrance they hacked the wooden trellis with axes. ^psalm-74-5

6 And then all its carved wood they broke down with hatchets and hammers. ^psalm-74-6

7 They set your sanctuary on fire; to the ground they desecrated the dwelling place of your name. ^psalm-74-7

8 They said to themselves, “We will utterly subdue them”; they burned all the meeting places of God in the land. ^psalm-74-8

9 We do not see our signs; there is no longer any prophet, and there is none among us who knows how long. ^psalm-74-9

10 How long, O God, is the foe to scoff? Is the enemy to revile your name for ever? ^psalm-74-10

11 Why do you hold back your hand, why do you keep your right hand in your bosom? ^psalm-74-11

12 Yet God my King is from of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth. ^psalm-74-12

13 You divided the sea by your might; you broke the heads of the dragons on the waters. ^psalm-74-13

14 You crushed the heads of Leviathan, you gave him as food for the creatures of the wilderness. ^psalm-74-14

15 You cut open springs and brooks; you dried up ever-flowing streams. ^psalm-74-15

16 Yours is the day, yours also the night; you have established the luminaries and the sun. ^psalm-74-16

17 You have fixed all the bounds of the earth; you have made summer and winter. ^psalm-74-17

18 Remember this, O LORD, how the enemy scoffs, and an impious people reviles your name. ^psalm-74-18

19 Do not deliver the soul of your dove to the wild beasts; do not forget the souls of your poor for ever. ^psalm-74-19

20 Have regard for your covenant; for the dark places of the land are full of the habitations of violence. ^psalm-74-20

21 Let not the humble be put to shame; let the poor and needy praise your name. ^psalm-74-21

22 Arise, O God, plead your cause; remember how the impious scoff at you all the day! ^psalm-74-22

23 Do not forget the clamor of your foes, the uproar of your adversaries which goes up continually! To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. ^psalm-74-23
