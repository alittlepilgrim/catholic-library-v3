# Psalm 128 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-128|NABRE]]

**The Happy Home of the Faithful**

*A Song of Ascents.*

1 Blessed is every one who fears the LORD, who walks in his ways! ^psalm-128-1

2 You shall eat the fruit of the labor of your hands; you shall be happy, and it shall be well with you. ^psalm-128-2

3 Your wife will be like a fruitful vine within your house; your children will be like olive shoots around your table. ^psalm-128-3

4 Behold, thus shall the man be blessed who fears the LORD. ^psalm-128-4

5 The LORD bless you from Zion! May you see the prosperity of Jerusalem all the days of your life! ^psalm-128-5

6 May you see your children’s children! Peace be upon Israel! ^psalm-128-6
