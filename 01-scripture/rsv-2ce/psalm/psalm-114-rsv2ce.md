# Psalm 114 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-114|NABRE]]

**God’s Wonders at the Exodus**

1 When Israel went forth from Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language, ^psalm-114-1

2 Judah became his sanctuary, Israel his dominion. ^psalm-114-2

3 The sea looked and fled, Jordan turned back. ^psalm-114-3

4 The mountains skipped like rams, the hills like lambs. ^psalm-114-4

5 What ails you, O sea, that you flee? O Jordan, that you turn back? ^psalm-114-5

6 O mountains, that you skip like rams? O hills, like lambs? ^psalm-114-6

7 Tremble, O earth, at the presence of the LORD, at the presence of the God of Jacob, ^psalm-114-7

8 who turns the rock into a pool of water, the flint into a spring of water. ^psalm-114-8
