# Psalm 40 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-40|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Deliverance; and Prayer for Help**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.*

1 I waited patiently for the LORD; he inclined to me and heard my cry. ^psalm-40-1

2 He drew me up from the desolate pit, out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure. ^psalm-40-2

3 He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. Many will see and fear, and put their trust in the LORD. ^psalm-40-3

4 Blessed is the man who makes the LORD his trust, who does not turn to the proud, to those who go astray after false gods! ^psalm-40-4

5 You have multiplied, O LORD my God, your wondrous deeds and your thoughts toward us; none can compare with you! Were I to proclaim and tell of them, they would be more than can be numbered. ^psalm-40-5

6 Sacrifice and offering you do not desire; but you have given me an open ear. Burnt offering and sin offering you have not required. ^psalm-40-6

7 Then I said, “Behold, I come; in the roll of the book it is written of me; ^psalm-40-7

8 I delight to do your will, O my God; your law is within my heart.” ^psalm-40-8

9 I have told the glad news of deliverance in the great congregation; behold, I have not restrained my lips, as you know, O LORD. ^psalm-40-9

10 I have not hidden your saving help within my heart, I have spoken of your faithfulness and your salvation; I have not concealed your mercy and your faithfulness from the great congregation. ^psalm-40-10

11 Do not, O LORD, withhold your compassion from me, let your mercy and your faithfulness ever preserve me! ^psalm-40-11

12 For evils have encompassed me without number; my iniquities have overtaken me, till I cannot see; they are more than the hairs of my head; my heart fails me. ^psalm-40-12

13 Be pleased, O LORD, to deliver me! O LORD, make haste to help me! ^psalm-40-13

14 Let them be put to shame and confusion altogether who seek to snatch away my life; let them be turned back and brought to dishonor who desire my hurt! ^psalm-40-14

15 Let them be appalled because of their shame who say to me, “Aha, Aha!” ^psalm-40-15

16 But may all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you; may those who love your salvation say continually, “Great is the LORD!” ^psalm-40-16

17 As for me, I am poor and needy; but the Lord takes thought for me. You are my help and my deliverer; do not delay, O my God! ^psalm-40-17
