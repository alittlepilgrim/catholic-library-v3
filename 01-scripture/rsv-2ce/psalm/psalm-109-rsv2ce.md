# Psalm 109 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-109|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Vindication and Vengeance**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.*

1 Be not silent, O God of my praise! ^psalm-109-1

2 For wicked and deceitful mouths are opened against me, speaking against me with lying tongues. ^psalm-109-2

3 They beset me with words of hate, and attack me without cause. ^psalm-109-3

4 In return for my love they accuse me, even as I make prayer for them. ^psalm-109-4

5 So they reward me evil for good, and hatred for my love. ^psalm-109-5

6 Appoint a wicked man against him; let an accuser bring him to trial. ^psalm-109-6

7 When he is tried, let him come forth guilty; let his prayer be counted as sin! ^psalm-109-7

8 May his days be few; may another seize his goods! ^psalm-109-8

9 May his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow! ^psalm-109-9

10 May his children wander about and beg; may they be driven out of the ruins they inhabit! ^psalm-109-10

11 May the creditor seize all that he has; may strangers plunder the fruits of his toil! ^psalm-109-11

12 Let there be none to extend kindness to him, nor any to pity his fatherless children! ^psalm-109-12

13 May his posterity be cut off; may his name be blotted out in the second generation! ^psalm-109-13

14 May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered before the LORD, and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out! ^psalm-109-14

15 Let them be before the LORD continually; and may his memory be cut off from the earth! ^psalm-109-15

16 For he did not remember to show kindness, but pursued the poor and needy and the brokenhearted to their death. ^psalm-109-16

17 He loved to curse; let curses come on him! He did not like blessing; may it be far from him! ^psalm-109-17

18 He clothed himself with cursing as his coat, may it soak into his body like water, like oil into his bones! ^psalm-109-18

19 May it be like a garment which he wraps round him, like a belt with which he daily girds himself! ^psalm-109-19

20 May this be the reward of my accusers from the LORD, of those who speak evil against my life! ^psalm-109-20

21 But you, O GOD my Lord, deal on my behalf for your name’s sake; because your mercy is good, deliver me! ^psalm-109-21

22 For I am poor and needy, and my heart is stricken within me. ^psalm-109-22

23 I am gone, like a shadow at evening; I am shaken off like a locust. ^psalm-109-23

24 My knees are weak through fasting; my body has become gaunt. ^psalm-109-24

25 I am an object of scorn to my accusers; when they see me, they wag their heads. ^psalm-109-25

26 Help me, O LORD my God! Save me according to your merciful love! ^psalm-109-26

27 Let them know that this is your hand; you, O LORD, have done it! ^psalm-109-27

28 Let them curse, but do bless! Let my assailants be put to shame; may your servant be glad! ^psalm-109-28

29 May my accusers be clothed with dishonor; may they be wrapped in their own shame as in a mantle! ^psalm-109-29

30 With my mouth I will give great thanks to the LORD; I will praise him in the midst of the throng. ^psalm-109-30

31 For he stands at the right hand of the needy, to save him from those who condemn him to death. ^psalm-109-31
