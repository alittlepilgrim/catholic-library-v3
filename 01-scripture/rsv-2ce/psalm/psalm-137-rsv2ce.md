# Psalm 137 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-137|NABRE]]

**Lament over the Destruction of Jerusalem**

1 By the waters of Babylon, there we sat down and wept, when we remembered Zion. ^psalm-137-1

2 On the willows there we hung up our lyres. ^psalm-137-2

3 For there our captors required of us songs, and our tormentors, mirth, saying, “Sing us one of the songs of Zion!” ^psalm-137-3

4 How shall we sing the LORD’s song in a foreign land? ^psalm-137-4

5 If I forget you, O Jerusalem, let my right hand wither! ^psalm-137-5

6 Let my tongue cleave to the roof of my mouth, if I do not remember you, if I do not set Jerusalem above my highest joy! ^psalm-137-6

7 Remember, O LORD, against the E'domites the day of Jerusalem, how they said, “Raze it, raze it! Down to its foundations!” ^psalm-137-7

8 O daughter of Babylon, you devastator! Happy shall he be who repays you with what you have done to us! ^psalm-137-8

9 Happy shall he be who takes your little ones and dashes them against the rock! A Psalm of David. ^psalm-137-9

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (137:1) This psalm breathes the spirit of sadness and homesickness of the Israelite in exile in Babylon. ^psalm-137-dn-1
