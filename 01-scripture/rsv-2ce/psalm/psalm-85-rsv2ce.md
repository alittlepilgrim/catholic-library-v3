# Psalm 85 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-85|NABRE]]

**Prayer for the Restoration of God’s Favor**

*A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.*

1 LORD, you were favorable to your land; you brought back the captives of Jacob. ^psalm-85-1

2 You forgave the iniquity of your people; you pardoned all their sin. Selah ^psalm-85-2

3 You withdrew all your wrath; you turned from your hot anger. ^psalm-85-3

4 Restore us again, O God of our salvation, and put away your indignation toward us! ^psalm-85-4

5 Will you be angry with us for ever? Will you prolong your anger to all generations? ^psalm-85-5

6 Will you not revive us again, that your people may rejoice in you? ^psalm-85-6

7 Show us your merciful love, O LORD, and grant us your salvation. ^psalm-85-7

8 Let me hear what God the LORD will speak, for he will speak peace to his people, to his saints, to those who turn to him in their hearts. ^psalm-85-8

9 Surely his salvation is at hand for those who fear him, that glory may dwell in our land. ^psalm-85-9

10 Mercy and faithfulness will meet; righteousness and peace will kiss each other. ^psalm-85-10

11 Faithfulness will spring up from the ground, and righteousness will look down from heaven. ^psalm-85-11

12 Yes, the LORD will give what is good, and our land will yield its increase. ^psalm-85-12

13 Righteousness will go before him, and make his footsteps a way. ^psalm-85-13
