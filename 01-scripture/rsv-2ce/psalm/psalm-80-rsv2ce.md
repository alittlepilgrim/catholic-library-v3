# Psalm 80 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-80|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Israel’s Restoration**

*To the choirmaster: according to Lilies. A Testimony of Asaph. A Psalm.*

1 Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, you who lead Joseph like a flock! You who are enthroned upon the cherubim, shine forth ^psalm-80-1

2 before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manas'seh! Stir up your might, and come to save us! ^psalm-80-2

3 Restore us, O God; let your face shine, that we may be saved! ^psalm-80-3

4 O LORD God of hosts, how long will you be angry with your people’s prayers? ^psalm-80-4

5 You have fed them with the bread of tears, and given them tears to drink in full measure. ^psalm-80-5

6 You make us the scorn of our neighbors; and our enemies laugh among themselves. ^psalm-80-6

7 Restore us, O God of hosts; let your face shine, that we may be saved! ^psalm-80-7

8 You brought a vine out of Egypt; you drove out the nations and planted it. ^psalm-80-8

9 You cleared the ground for it; it took deep root and filled the land. ^psalm-80-9

10 The mountains were covered with its shade, the mighty cedars with its branches; ^psalm-80-10

11 it sent out its branches to the sea, and its shoots to the River. ^psalm-80-11

12 Why then have you broken down its walls, so that all who pass along the way pluck its fruit? ^psalm-80-12

13 The boar from the forest ravages it, and all that move in the field feed on it. ^psalm-80-13

14 Turn again, O God of hosts! Look down from heaven, and see; have regard for this vine, ^psalm-80-14

15 the stock which your right hand planted. ^psalm-80-15

16 They have burned it with fire, they have cut it down; may they perish at the rebuke of your countenance! ^psalm-80-16

17 But let your hand be upon the man of your right hand, the son of man whom you have made strong for yourself! ^psalm-80-17

18 Then we will never turn back from you; give us life, and we will call on your name! ^psalm-80-18

19 Restore us, O LORD God of hosts! let your face shine, that we may be saved! To the choirmaster: according to The Gittith. ^psalm-80-19
