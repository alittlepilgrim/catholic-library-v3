# Psalm 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-05|NABRE]]

**Trust in God for Deliverance from Enemies**

*To the choirmaster: for the flutes. A Psalm of David.*

1 Give ear to my words, O LORD; give heed to my groaning. ^psalm-05-1

2 Listen to the sound of my cry, my King and my God, for to you do I pray. ^psalm-05-2

3 O LORD, in the morning you hear my voice; in the morning I prepare a sacrifice for you, and watch. ^psalm-05-3

4 For you are not a God who delights in wickedness; evil may not sojourn with you. ^psalm-05-4

5 The boastful may not stand before your eyes; you hate all evildoers. ^psalm-05-5

6 You destroy those who speak lies; the LORD abhors bloodthirsty and deceitful men. ^psalm-05-6

7 But I through the abundance of your merciful love will enter your house, I will worship toward your holy temple in the fear of you. ^psalm-05-7

8 Lead me, O LORD, in your righteousness because of my enemies; make your way straight before me. ^psalm-05-8

9 For there is no truth in their mouth; their heart is destruction, their throat is an open sepulchre, they flatter with their tongue. ^psalm-05-9

10 Make them bear their guilt, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; because of their many transgressions cast them out, for they have rebelled against you. ^psalm-05-10

11 But let all who take refuge in you rejoice, let them ever sing for joy; and do defend them, that those who love your name may exult in you. ^psalm-05-11

12 For you bless the righteous, O LORD; you cover him with favor as with a shield. ^psalm-05-12
