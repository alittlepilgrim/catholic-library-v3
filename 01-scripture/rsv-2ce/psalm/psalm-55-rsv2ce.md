# Psalm 55 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-55|NABRE]]

**Complaint about a Friend’s Treachery**

*To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David.*

1 Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not yourself from my supplication! ^psalm-55-1

2 Attend to me, and answer me; I am overcome by my trouble. I am distraught ^psalm-55-2

3 by the noise of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked. For they bring trouble upon me, and in anger they cherish enmity against me. ^psalm-55-3

4 My heart is in anguish within me, the terrors of death have fallen upon me. ^psalm-55-4

5 Fear and trembling come upon me, and horror overwhelms me. ^psalm-55-5

6 And I say, “O that I had wings like a dove! I would fly away and be at rest; ^psalm-55-6

7 yes, I would wander afar, I would lodge in the wilderness, Selah ^psalm-55-7

8 I would wait for him who saves me from the raging wind and tempest.” ^psalm-55-8

9 Destroy their plans, O Lord, confuse their tongues; for I see violence and strife in the city. ^psalm-55-9

10 Day and night they go around it on its walls; and mischief and trouble are within it, ^psalm-55-10

11 ruin is in its midst; oppression and fraud do not depart from its market place. ^psalm-55-11

12 It is not an enemy who taunts me— then I could bear it; it is not an adversary who deals insolently with me— then I could hide from him. ^psalm-55-12

13 But it is you, my equal, my companion, my familiar friend. ^psalm-55-13

14 We used to hold sweet converse together; within God’s house we walked in fellowship. ^psalm-55-14

15 Let death come upon them; let them go down to Sheol alive; let them go away in terror into their graves. ^psalm-55-15

16 But I call upon God; and the LORD will save me. ^psalm-55-16

17 Evening and morning and at noon I utter my complaint and moan, and he will hear my voice. ^psalm-55-17

18 He will deliver my soul in safety from the battle that I wage, for many are arrayed against me. ^psalm-55-18

19 God will give ear, and humble them, he who is enthroned from of old; because they keep no law, and do not fear God. Selah ^psalm-55-19

20 My companion stretched out his hand against his friends, he violated his covenant. ^psalm-55-20

21 His speech was smoother than butter, yet war was in his heart; his words were softer than oil, yet they were drawn swords. ^psalm-55-21

22 Cast your burden on the LORD, and he will sustain you; he will never permit the righteous to be moved. ^psalm-55-22

23 But you, O God, will cast them down into the lowest pit; men of blood and treachery shall not live out half their days. But I will trust in you. ^psalm-55-23
