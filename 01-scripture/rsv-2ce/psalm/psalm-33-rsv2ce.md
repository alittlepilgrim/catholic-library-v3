# Psalm 33 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-33|NABRE]]

**The Greatness and Goodness of God**

1 Rejoice in the LORD, O you righteous! Praise befits the upright. ^psalm-33-1

2 Praise the LORD with the lyre, make melody to him with the harp of ten strings! ^psalm-33-2

3 Sing to him a new song, play skillfully on the strings, with loud shouts. ^psalm-33-3

4 For the word of the LORD is upright; and all his work is done in faithfulness. ^psalm-33-4

5 He loves righteousness and justice; the earth is full of the mercy of the LORD. ^psalm-33-5

6 By the word of the LORD the heavens were made, and all their host by the breath of his mouth. ^psalm-33-6

7 He gathered the waters of the sea as in a bottle; he put the deeps in storehouses. ^psalm-33-7

8 Let all the earth fear the LORD, let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him! ^psalm-33-8

9 For he spoke, and it came to be; he commanded, and it stood forth. ^psalm-33-9

10 The LORD brings the counsel of the nations to nought; he frustrates the plans of the peoples. ^psalm-33-10

11 The counsel of the LORD stands for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations. ^psalm-33-11

12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the LORD, the people whom he has chosen as his heritage! ^psalm-33-12

13 The LORD looks down from heaven, he sees all the sons of men; ^psalm-33-13

14 from where he sits enthroned he looks forth on all the inhabitants of the earth, ^psalm-33-14

15 he who fashions the hearts of them all, and observes all their deeds. ^psalm-33-15

16 A king is not saved by his great army; a warrior is not delivered by his great strength. ^psalm-33-16

17 The war horse is a vain hope for victory, and by its great might it cannot save. ^psalm-33-17

18 Behold, the eye of the LORD is on those who fear him, on those who hope in his merciful love, ^psalm-33-18

19 that he may deliver their soul from death, and keep them alive in famine. ^psalm-33-19

20 Our soul waits for the LORD; he is our help and shield. ^psalm-33-20

21 Yes, our heart is glad in him, because we trust in his holy name. ^psalm-33-21

22 Let your mercy, O LORD, be upon us, even as we hope in you. ^psalm-33-22
