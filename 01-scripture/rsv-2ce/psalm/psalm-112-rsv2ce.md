# Psalm 112 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-112|NABRE]]

**Blessings of the Righteous**

1 Praise the LORD! Blessed is the man who fears the LORD, who greatly delights in his commandments! ^psalm-112-1

2 His descendants will be mighty in the land; the generation of the upright will be blessed. ^psalm-112-2

3 Wealth and riches are in his house; and his righteousness endures for ever. ^psalm-112-3

4 Light rises in the darkness for the upright; the LORD is gracious, merciful, and righteous. ^psalm-112-4

5 It is well with the man who deals generously and lends, who conducts his affairs with justice. ^psalm-112-5

6 For the righteous will never be moved; he will be remembered for ever. ^psalm-112-6

7 He is not afraid of evil tidings; his heart is firm, trusting in the LORD. ^psalm-112-7

8 His heart is steady, he will not be afraid, until he sees his desire on his adversaries. ^psalm-112-8

9 He has distributed freely, he has given to the poor; his righteousness endures for ever; his horn is exalted in honor. ^psalm-112-9

10 The wicked man sees it and is angry; he gnashes his teeth and melts away; the desire of the wicked man comes to nought. ^psalm-112-10
