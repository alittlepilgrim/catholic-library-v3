# Psalm 71 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-71|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Lifelong Protection and Help**

1 In you, O LORD, I take refuge; let me never be put to shame! ^psalm-71-1

2 In your righteousness deliver me and rescue me; incline your ear to me, and save me! ^psalm-71-2

3 Be to me a rock of refuge, a strong fortress, to save me, for you are my rock and my fortress. ^psalm-71-3

4 Rescue me, O my God, from the hand of the wicked, from the grasp of the unjust and cruel man. ^psalm-71-4

5 For you, O Lord, are my hope, my trust, O LORD, from my youth. ^psalm-71-5

6 Upon you I have leaned from my birth; from my mother’s womb, you have been my strength. My praise is continually of you. ^psalm-71-6

7 I have been as a portent to many; but you are my strong refuge. ^psalm-71-7

8 My mouth is filled with your praise, and with your glory all the day. ^psalm-71-8

9 Do not cast me off in the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength is spent. ^psalm-71-9

10 For my enemies speak concerning me, those who watch for my life consult together, ^psalm-71-10

11 and say, “God has forsaken him; pursue and seize him, for there is none to deliver him.” ^psalm-71-11

12 O God, be not far from me; O my God, make haste to help me! ^psalm-71-12

13 May my accusers be put to shame and consumed; with scorn and disgrace may they be covered who seek my harm. ^psalm-71-13

14 But I will hope continually, and will praise you yet more and more. ^psalm-71-14

15 My mouth will tell of your righteous acts, of your deeds of salvation all the day, for their number is past my knowledge. ^psalm-71-15

16 With the mighty deeds of the Lord GOD I will come, I will praise your righteousness, yours alone. ^psalm-71-16

17 O God, from my youth you have taught me, and I still proclaim your wondrous deeds. ^psalm-71-17

18 So even to old age and gray hairs, O God, do not forsake me, till I proclaim your might to all the generations to come. Your power ^psalm-71-18

19 and your righteousness, O God, reach the high heavens. You who have done great things, O God, who is like you? ^psalm-71-19

20 You who have made me see many sore troubles will revive me again; from the depths of the earth you will bring me up again. ^psalm-71-20

21 You will increase my honor, and comfort me again. ^psalm-71-21

22 I will also praise you with the harp for your faithfulness, O my God; I will sing praises to you with the lyre, O Holy One of Israel. ^psalm-71-22

23 My lips will shout for joy, when I sing praises to you; my soul also, which you have rescued. ^psalm-71-23

24 And my tongue will talk of your righteous help all the day long, for they have been put to shame and disgraced who sought to do me harm. ^psalm-71-24
