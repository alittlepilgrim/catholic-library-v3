# Psalm 32 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-32|NABRE]]

**The Joy of Forgiveness**

*A Maskil.*

1 Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered. ^psalm-32-1

2 Blessed is the man to whom the LORD imputes no iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no deceit. ^psalm-32-2

3 When I declared not my sin, my body wasted away through my groaning all day long. ^psalm-32-3

4 For day and night your hand was heavy upon me; my strength was dried up as by the heat of summer. Selah ^psalm-32-4

5 I acknowledged my sin to you, and I did not hide my iniquity; I said, “I will confess my transgressions to the LORD”; then you forgave the guilt of my sin. Selah ^psalm-32-5

6 Therefore let every one who is godly offer prayer to you; at a time of distress, in the rush of great waters, they shall not reach him. ^psalm-32-6

7 You are a hiding place for me, you preserve me from trouble; you surround me with deliverance. Selah ^psalm-32-7

8 I will instruct you and teach you the way you should go; I will counsel you with my eye upon you. ^psalm-32-8

9 Be not like a horse or a mule, without understanding, which must be curbed with bit and bridle, else it will not keep with you. ^psalm-32-9

10 Many are the pangs of the wicked; but steadfast love surrounds him who trusts in the LORD. ^psalm-32-10

11 Be glad in the LORD, and rejoice, O righteous, and shout for joy, all you upright in heart! ^psalm-32-11
