# Psalm 70 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-70|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David, for the memorial offering.*

1 Be pleased, O God, to deliver me! O LORD, make haste to help me! ^psalm-70-1

2 Let them be put to shame and confusion who seek my life! Let them be turned back and brought to dishonor who desire my harm! ^psalm-70-2

3 Let them be appalled because of their shame who say, “Aha, Aha!” ^psalm-70-3

4 May all who seek you rejoice and be glad in you! May those who love your salvation say evermore, “God is great!” ^psalm-70-4

5 But I am poor and needy; hasten to me, O God! You are my help and my deliverer; O LORD, do not delay! ^psalm-70-5
