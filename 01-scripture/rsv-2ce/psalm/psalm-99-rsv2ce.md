# Psalm 99 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-99|NABRE]]

**Praise to God for His Holiness**

1 The LORD reigns; let the peoples tremble! He sits enthroned upon the cherubim; let the earth quake! ^psalm-99-1

2 The LORD is great in Zion; he is exalted over all the peoples. ^psalm-99-2

3 Let them praise your great and awesome name! Holy is he! ^psalm-99-3

4 Mighty King, lover of justice, you have established equity; you have executed justice and righteousness in Jacob. ^psalm-99-4

5 Extol the LORD our God; worship at his footstool! Holy is he! ^psalm-99-5

6 Moses and Aaron were among his priests, Samuel also was among those who called on his name. They cried to the LORD, and he answered them. ^psalm-99-6

7 He spoke to them in the pillar of cloud; they kept his testimonies, and the statutes that he gave them. ^psalm-99-7

8 O LORD our God, you answered them; you were a forgiving God to them, but an avenger of their wrongdoings. ^psalm-99-8

9 Extol the LORD our God, and worship at his holy mountain; for the LORD our God is holy! ^psalm-99-9
