# Psalm 122 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-122|NABRE]]

**Song of Praise; and a Prayer for Jerusalem**

*Of David.*

1 I was glad when they said to me, “Let us go to the house of the LORD!” ^psalm-122-1

2 Our feet have been standing within your gates, O Jerusalem! ^psalm-122-2

3 Jerusalem, built as a city which is bound firmly together, ^psalm-122-3

4 to which the tribes go up, the tribes of the LORD, as was decreed for Israel, to give thanks to the name of the LORD. ^psalm-122-4

5 There thrones for judgment were set, the thrones of the house of David. ^psalm-122-5

6 Pray for the peace of Jerusalem! “May they prosper who love you! ^psalm-122-6

7 Peace be within your walls, and security within your towers!” ^psalm-122-7

8 For my brethren and companions’ sake I will say, “Peace be within you!” ^psalm-122-8

9 For the sake of the house of the LORD our God, I will seek your good. A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-122-9
