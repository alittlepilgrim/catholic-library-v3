# Psalm 115 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-115|NABRE]]

**The Impotence of Idols and the Greatness of God**

1 Not to us, O LORD, not to us, but to your name give glory, for the sake of your mercy and your faithfulness! ^psalm-115-1

2 Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?” ^psalm-115-2

3 Our God is in the heavens; he does whatever he pleases. ^psalm-115-3

4 Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands. ^psalm-115-4

5 They have mouths, but do not speak; eyes, but do not see. ^psalm-115-5

6 They have ears, but do not hear; noses, but do not smell. ^psalm-115-6

7 They have hands, but do not feel; feet, but do not walk; and they do not make a sound in their throat. ^psalm-115-7

8 Those who make them are like them; so are all who trust in them. ^psalm-115-8

9 O Israel, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^psalm-115-9

10 O house of Aaron, put your trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^psalm-115-10

11 You who fear the LORD, trust in the LORD! He is their help and their shield. ^psalm-115-11

12 The LORD has been mindful of us; he will bless us; he will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron; ^psalm-115-12

13 he will bless those who fear the LORD, both small and great. ^psalm-115-13

14 May the LORD give you increase, you and your children! ^psalm-115-14

15 May you be blessed by the LORD, who made heaven and earth! ^psalm-115-15

16 The heavens are the LORD’s heavens, but the earth he has given to the sons of men. ^psalm-115-16

17 The dead do not praise the LORD, nor do any that go down into silence. ^psalm-115-17

18 But we will bless the LORD from this time forth and for evermore. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-115-18
