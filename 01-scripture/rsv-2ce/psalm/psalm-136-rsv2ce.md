# Psalm 136 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-136|NABRE]]

**God’s Work in Creation and in History**

1 O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good, for his mercy endures for ever. ^psalm-136-1

2 O give thanks to the God of gods, for his mercy endures for ever. ^psalm-136-2

3 O give thanks to the Lord of lords, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-3

4 to him who alone does great wonders, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-4

5 to him who by understanding made the heavens, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-5

6 to him who spread out the earth upon the waters, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-6

7 to him who made the great lights, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-7

8 the sun to rule over the day, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-8

9 the moon and stars to rule over the night, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-9

10 to him who struck the first-born of Egypt, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-10

11 and brought Israel out from among them, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-11

12 with a strong hand and an outstretched arm, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-12

13 to him who divided the Red Sea in two, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-13

14 and made Israel pass through the midst of it, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-14

15 but overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red Sea, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-15

16 to him who led his people through the wilderness, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-16

17 to him who struck great kings, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-17

18 and slew famous kings, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-18

19 Sihon, king of the Amorites, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-19

20 and Og, king of Bashan, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-20

21 and gave their land as a heritage, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-21

22 a heritage to Israel his servant, for his mercy endures for ever. ^psalm-136-22

23 It is he who remembered us in our low estate, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-23

24 and rescued us from our foes, for his mercy endures for ever; ^psalm-136-24

25 he who gives food to all flesh, for his mercy endures for ever. ^psalm-136-25

26 O give thanks to the God of heaven, for his mercy endures for ever. ^psalm-136-26
