# Psalm 106 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-106|NABRE]]

**A Confession of Israel’s Sins**

1 Praise the LORD! O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; for his mercy endures for ever! ^psalm-106-1

2 Who can utter the mighty doings of the LORD, or show forth all his praise? ^psalm-106-2

3 Blessed are they who observe justice, who do righteousness at all times! ^psalm-106-3

4 Remember me, O LORD, when you show favor to your people; help me when you deliver them; ^psalm-106-4

5 that I may see the prosperity of your chosen ones, that I may rejoice in the gladness of your nation, that I may glory with your heritage. ^psalm-106-5

6 Both we and our fathers have sinned; we have committed iniquity, we have done wickedly. ^psalm-106-6

7 Our fathers, when they were in Egypt, did not consider your wonderful works; they did not remember the abundance of your mercy, but rebelled against the Most High at the Red Sea. ^psalm-106-7

8 Yet he saved them for his name’s sake, that he might make known his mighty power. ^psalm-106-8

9 He rebuked the Red Sea, and it became dry; and he led them through the deep as through a desert. ^psalm-106-9

10 So he saved them from the hand of the foe, and delivered them from the power of the enemy. ^psalm-106-10

11 And the waters covered their adversaries; not one of them was left. ^psalm-106-11

12 Then they believed his words; they sang his praise. ^psalm-106-12

13 But they soon forgot his works; they did not wait for his counsel. ^psalm-106-13

14 But they had a wanton craving in the wilderness, and put God to the test in the desert; ^psalm-106-14

15 he gave them what they asked, but sent a wasting disease among them. ^psalm-106-15

16 When men in the camp were jealous of Moses and Aaron, the holy one of the LORD, ^psalm-106-16

17 the earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abi'ram. ^psalm-106-17

18 Fire also broke out in their company; the flame burned up the wicked. ^psalm-106-18

19 They made a calf in Horeb and worshiped a molten image. ^psalm-106-19

20 They exchanged the glory of God for the image of an ox that eats grass. ^psalm-106-20

21 They forgot God, their Savior, who had done great things in Egypt, ^psalm-106-21

22 wondrous works in the land of Ham, and awesome things by the Red Sea. ^psalm-106-22

23 Therefore he said he would destroy them— had not Moses, his chosen one, stood in the breach before him, to turn away his wrath from destroying them. ^psalm-106-23

24 Then they despised the pleasant land, having no faith in his promise. ^psalm-106-24

25 They murmured in their tents, and did not obey the voice of the LORD. ^psalm-106-25

26 Therefore he raised his hand and swore to them that he would make them fall in the wilderness, ^psalm-106-26

27 and would disperse their descendants among the nations, scattering them over the lands. ^psalm-106-27

28 Then they attached themselves to the Ba'al of Peor, and ate sacrifices offered to the dead; ^psalm-106-28

29 they provoked the LORD to anger with their doings, and a plague broke out among them. ^psalm-106-29

30 Then Phin'ehas stood up and interposed, and the plague was stayed. ^psalm-106-30

31 And that has been reckoned to him as righteousness from generation to generation for ever. ^psalm-106-31

32 They angered him at the waters of Mer'ibah, and it went ill with Moses on their account; ^psalm-106-32

33 for they made his spirit bitter, and he spoke words that were rash. ^psalm-106-33

34 They did not destroy the peoples, as the LORD commanded them, ^psalm-106-34

35 but they mingled with the nations and learned to do as they did. ^psalm-106-35

36 They served their idols, which became a snare to them. ^psalm-106-36

37 They sacrificed their sons and their daughters to the demons; ^psalm-106-37

38 they poured out innocent blood, the blood of their sons and daughters, whom they sacrificed to the idols of Canaan; and the land was polluted with blood. ^psalm-106-38

39 Thus they became unclean by their acts, and played the harlot in their doings. ^psalm-106-39

40 Then the anger of the LORD was kindled against his people, and he abhorred his heritage; ^psalm-106-40

41 he gave them into the hand of the nations, so that those who hated them ruled over them. ^psalm-106-41

42 Their enemies oppressed them, and they were brought into subjection under their power. ^psalm-106-42

43 Many times he delivered them, but they were rebellious in their purposes, and were brought low through their iniquity. ^psalm-106-43

44 Nevertheless he regarded their distress, when he heard their cry. ^psalm-106-44

45 He remembered for their sake his covenant, and relented according to the abundance of his mercy. ^psalm-106-45

46 He caused them to be pitied by all those who held them captive. ^psalm-106-46

47 Save us, O LORD our God, and gather us from among the nations, that we may give thanks to your holy name and glory in your praise. ^psalm-106-47

48 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting! And let all the people say, “Amen!” Praise the LORD! BOOK V ^psalm-106-48
