# Psalm 148 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-148|NABRE]]

**Praise for God’s Universal Glory**

1 Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD from the heavens, praise him in the heights! ^psalm-148-1

2 Praise him, all his angels, praise him, all his host! ^psalm-148-2

3 Praise him, sun and moon, praise him, all you shining stars! ^psalm-148-3

4 Praise him, you highest heavens, and you waters above the heavens! ^psalm-148-4

5 Let them praise the name of the LORD! For he commanded and they were created. ^psalm-148-5

6 And he established them for ever and ever; he fixed their bounds which cannot be passed. ^psalm-148-6

7 Praise the LORD from the earth, you sea monsters and all deeps, ^psalm-148-7

8 fire and hail, snow and frost, stormy wind fulfilling his command! ^psalm-148-8

9 Mountains and all hills, fruit trees and all cedars! ^psalm-148-9

10 Beasts and all cattle, creeping things and flying birds! ^psalm-148-10

11 Kings of the earth and all peoples, princes and all rulers of the earth! ^psalm-148-11

12 Young men and maidens together, old men and children! ^psalm-148-12

13 Let them praise the name of the LORD, for his name alone is exalted; his glory is above earth and heaven. ^psalm-148-13

14 He has raised up a horn for his people, praise for all his saints, for the people of Israel who are near to him. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-148-14
