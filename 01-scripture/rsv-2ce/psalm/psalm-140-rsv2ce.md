# Psalm 140 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-140|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Deliver me, O LORD, from evil men; preserve me from violent men, ^psalm-140-1

2 who plan evil things in their heart, and stir up wars continually. ^psalm-140-2

3 They make their tongue sharp as a serpent’s, and under their lips is the poison of vipers. Selah ^psalm-140-3

4 Guard me, O LORD, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from violent men, who have planned to trip up my feet. ^psalm-140-4

5 Arrogant men have hidden a trap for me, and with cords they have spread a net, by the wayside they have set snares for me. Selah ^psalm-140-5

6 I say to the LORD, You are my God; give ear to the voice of my supplications, O LORD! ^psalm-140-6

7 O LORD, my Lord, my strong deliverer, you have covered my head in the day of battle. ^psalm-140-7

8 Grant not, O LORD, the desires of the wicked; do not further his evil plot! Selah ^psalm-140-8

9 Those who surround me lift up their head, let the mischief of their lips overwhelm them! ^psalm-140-9

10 Let burning coals fall upon them! Let them be cast into pits, no more to rise! ^psalm-140-10

11 Let not the slanderer be established in the land; let evil hunt down the violent man speedily! ^psalm-140-11

12 I know that the LORD maintains the cause of the afflicted, and executes justice for the needy. ^psalm-140-12

13 Surely the righteous shall give thanks to your name; the upright shall dwell in your presence. A Psalm of David. ^psalm-140-13
