# Psalm 86 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-86|NABRE]]

**Supplication for Help against Enemies**

*A Prayer of David.*

1 Incline your ear, O LORD, and answer me, for I am poor and needy. ^psalm-86-1

2 Preserve my life, for I am godly; save your servant who trusts in you. You are my God; ^psalm-86-2

3 have mercy on me, O Lord, for to you do I cry all the day. ^psalm-86-3

4 Gladden the soul of your servant, for to you, O Lord, do I lift up my soul. ^psalm-86-4

5 For you, O Lord, are good and forgiving, abounding in mercy to all who call on you. ^psalm-86-5

6 Give ear, O LORD, to my prayer; listen to my cry of supplication. ^psalm-86-6

7 In the day of my trouble I call on you, for you do answer me. ^psalm-86-7

8 There is none like you among the gods, O Lord, nor are there any works like yours. ^psalm-86-8

9 All the nations you have made shall come and bow down before you, O Lord, and shall glorify your name. ^psalm-86-9

10 For you are great and do wondrous things, you alone are God. ^psalm-86-10

11 Teach me your way, O LORD, that I may walk in your truth; unite my heart to fear your name. ^psalm-86-11

12 I give thanks to you, O Lord my God, with my whole heart, and I will glorify your name for ever. ^psalm-86-12

13 For great is your merciful love toward me; you have delivered my soul from the depths of Sheol. ^psalm-86-13

14 O God, insolent men have risen up against me; a band of ruthless men seek my life, and they do not set you before them. ^psalm-86-14

15 But you, O Lord, are a God merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in mercy and faithfulness. ^psalm-86-15

16 Turn to me and take pity on me; give your strength to your servant, and save the son of your handmaid. ^psalm-86-16

17 Show me a sign of your favor, that those who hate me may see and be put to shame because you, LORD, have helped me and comforted me. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah. ^psalm-86-17
