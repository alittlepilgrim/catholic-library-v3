# Psalm 107 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-107|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Deliverance from Many Troubles**

1 O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; for his mercy endures for ever! ^psalm-107-1

2 Let the redeemed of the LORD say so, whom he has redeemed from trouble ^psalm-107-2

3 and gathered in from the lands, from the east and from the west, from the north and from the south. ^psalm-107-3

4 Some wandered in desert wastes, finding no way to a city to dwell in; ^psalm-107-4

5 hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted within them. ^psalm-107-5

6 Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them from their distress; ^psalm-107-6

7 he led them by a straight way, till they reached a city to dwell in. ^psalm-107-7

8 Let them thank the LORD for his merciful love, for his wonderful works to the sons of men! ^psalm-107-8

9 For he satisfies him who is thirsty, and the hungry he fills with good things. ^psalm-107-9

10 Some sat in darkness and in gloom, prisoners in affliction and in irons, ^psalm-107-10

11 for they had rebelled against the words of God, and spurned the counsel of the Most High. ^psalm-107-11

12 Their hearts were bowed down with hard labor; they fell down, with none to help. ^psalm-107-12

13 Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them from their distress; ^psalm-107-13

14 he brought them out of darkness and gloom, and broke their bonds asunder. ^psalm-107-14

15 Let them thank the LORD for his steadfast love, for his wonderful works to the sons of men! ^psalm-107-15

16 For he shatters the doors of bronze, and cuts in two the bars of iron. ^psalm-107-16

17 Some were sick through their sinful ways, and because of their iniquities suffered affliction; ^psalm-107-17

18 they loathed any kind of food, and they drew near to the gates of death. ^psalm-107-18

19 Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them from their distress; ^psalm-107-19

20 he sent forth his word, and healed them, and delivered them from destruction. ^psalm-107-20

21 Let them thank the LORD for his steadfast love, for his wonderful works to the sons of men! ^psalm-107-21

22 And let them offer sacrifices of thanksgiving, and tell of his deeds in songs of joy! ^psalm-107-22

23 Some went down to the sea in ships, doing business on the great waters; ^psalm-107-23

24 they saw the deeds of the LORD, his wondrous works in the deep. ^psalm-107-24

25 For he commanded, and raised the stormy wind, which lifted up the waves of the sea. ^psalm-107-25

26 They mounted up to heaven, they went down to the depths; their courage melted away in their evil plight; ^psalm-107-26

27 they reeled and staggered like drunken men, and were at their wits’ end. ^psalm-107-27

28 Then they cried to the LORD in their trouble, and he delivered them from their distress; ^psalm-107-28

29 he made the storm be still, and the waves of the sea were hushed. ^psalm-107-29

30 Then they were glad because they had quiet, and he brought them to their desired haven. ^psalm-107-30

31 Let them thank the LORD for his merciful love, for his wonderful works to the sons of men! ^psalm-107-31

32 Let them extol him in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the elders. ^psalm-107-32

33 He turns rivers into a desert, springs of water into thirsty ground, ^psalm-107-33

34 a fruitful land into a salty waste, because of the wickedness of its inhabitants. ^psalm-107-34

35 He turns a desert into pools of water, a parched land into springs of water. ^psalm-107-35

36 And there he lets the hungry dwell, and they establish a city to live in; ^psalm-107-36

37 they sow fields, and plant vineyards, and get a fruitful yield. ^psalm-107-37

38 By his blessing they multiply greatly; and he does not let their cattle decrease. ^psalm-107-38

39 When they are diminished and brought low through oppression, trouble, and sorrow, ^psalm-107-39

40 he pours contempt upon princes and makes them wander in trackless wastes; ^psalm-107-40

41 but he raises up the needy out of affliction, and makes their families like flocks. ^psalm-107-41

42 The upright see it and are glad; and all wickedness stops its mouth. ^psalm-107-42

43 Whoever is wise, let him give heed to these things; let men consider the steadfast love of the LORD. A Song. ^psalm-107-43
