# Psalm 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-02|NABRE]]

**God’s Promise to His Anointed**

1 Why do the nations conspire and the peoples plot in vain? ^psalm-02-1

2 The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against the LORD and his anointed, saying, ^psalm-02-2

3 “Let us burst their bonds asunder, and cast their cords from us.” ^psalm-02-3

4 He who sits in the heavens laughs; the LORD has them in derision. ^psalm-02-4

5 Then he will speak to them in his wrath, and terrify them in his fury, saying, ^psalm-02-5

6 “I have set my king on Zion, my holy mountain.” ^psalm-02-6

7 I will tell of the decree of the LORD: He said to me, “You are my son, today I have begotten you. ^psalm-02-7

8 Ask of me, and I will make the nations your heritage, and the ends of the earth your possession. ^psalm-02-8

9 You shall break them with a rod of iron, and dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.” ^psalm-02-9

10 Now therefore, O kings, be wise; be warned, O rulers of the earth. ^psalm-02-10

11 Serve the LORD with fear, with trembling ^psalm-02-11

12 rejoice, lest he be angry, and you perish in the way; for his wrath is quickly kindled. Blessed are all who take refuge in him. ^psalm-02-12

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (2:1) Together with Ps 110 a royal, Messianic psalm, often applied to Jesus as Messiah in the New Testament. ^psalm-02-dn-1
