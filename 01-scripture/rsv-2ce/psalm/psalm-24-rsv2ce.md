# Psalm 24 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-24|NABRE]]

**Entrance into the Temple**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 The earth is the LORD’s and the fulness thereof, the world and those who dwell therein; ^psalm-24-1

2 for he has founded it upon the seas, and established it upon the rivers. ^psalm-24-2

3 Who shall ascend the hill of the LORD? And who shall stand in his holy place? ^psalm-24-3

4 He who has clean hands and a pure heart, who does not lift up his soul to what is false, and does not swear deceitfully. ^psalm-24-4

5 He will receive blessing from the LORD, and vindication from the God of his salvation. ^psalm-24-5

6 Such is the generation of those who seek him, who seek the face of the God of Jacob. Selah ^psalm-24-6

7 Lift up your heads, O gates! and be lifted up, O ancient doors! that the King of glory may come in. ^psalm-24-7

8 Who is the King of glory? The LORD, strong and mighty, the LORD, mighty in battle! ^psalm-24-8

9 Lift up your heads, O gates! and be lifted up, O ancient doors! that the King of glory may come in. ^psalm-24-9

10 Who is this King of glory? The LORD of hosts, he is the King of glory! Selah ^psalm-24-10
