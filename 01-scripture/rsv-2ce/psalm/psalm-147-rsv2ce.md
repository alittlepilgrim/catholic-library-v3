# Psalm 147 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-147|NABRE]]

**Praise for God’s Care for Jerusalem**

1 Praise the LORD! For it is good to sing praises to our God; for he is gracious, and a song of praise is seemly. ^psalm-147-1

2 The LORD builds up Jerusalem; he gathers the outcasts of Israel. ^psalm-147-2

3 He heals the brokenhearted, and binds up their wounds. ^psalm-147-3

4 He determines the number of the stars, he gives to all of them their names. ^psalm-147-4

5 Great is our LORD, and abundant in power; his understanding is beyond measure. ^psalm-147-5

6 The LORD lifts up the downtrodden, he casts the wicked to the ground. ^psalm-147-6

7 Sing to the LORD with thanksgiving; make melody to our God upon the lyre! ^psalm-147-7

8 He covers the heavens with clouds, he prepares rain for the earth, he makes grass grow upon the hills. ^psalm-147-8

9 He gives to the beasts their food, and to the young ravens which cry. ^psalm-147-9

10 His delight is not in the strength of the horse, nor his pleasure in the legs of a man; ^psalm-147-10

11 but the LORD takes pleasure in those who fear him, in those who hope in his steadfast love. ^psalm-147-11

12 Praise the LORD, O Jerusalem! Praise your God, O Zion! ^psalm-147-12

13 For he strengthens the bars of your gates; he blesses your sons within you. ^psalm-147-13

14 He makes peace in your borders; he fills you with the finest of the wheat. ^psalm-147-14

15 He sends forth his command to the earth; his word runs swiftly. ^psalm-147-15

16 He gives snow like wool; he scatters hoarfrost like ashes. ^psalm-147-16

17 He casts forth his ice like morsels; who can stand before his cold? ^psalm-147-17

18 He sends forth his word, and melts them; he makes his wind blow, and the waters flow. ^psalm-147-18

19 He declares his word to Jacob, his statutes and ordinances to Israel. ^psalm-147-19

20 He has not dealt thus with any other nation; they do not know his ordinances. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-147-20
