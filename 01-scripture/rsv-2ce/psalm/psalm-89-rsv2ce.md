# Psalm 89 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-89|NABRE]]

**God’s Covenant with David**

*A Maskil of Ethan the Ezrahite.*

1 I will sing of your mercies, O LORD, for ever; with my mouth I will proclaim your faithfulness to all generations. ^psalm-89-1

2 For your merciful love was established for ever, your faithfulness is firm as the heavens. ^psalm-89-2

3 You have said, “I have made a covenant with my chosen one, I have sworn to David my servant: ^psalm-89-3

4 ‘I will establish your descendants for ever, and build your throne for all generations.’ ” Selah ^psalm-89-4

5 Let the heavens praise your wonders, O LORD, your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones! ^psalm-89-5

6 For who in the skies can be compared to the LORD? Who among the heavenly beings is like the LORD, ^psalm-89-6

7 a God feared in the council of the holy ones, great and awesome above all that are round about him? ^psalm-89-7

8 O LORD God of hosts, who is mighty as you are, O LORD, with your faithfulness round about you? ^psalm-89-8

9 You rule the raging of the sea; when its waves rise, you still them. ^psalm-89-9

10 You crushed Rahab like a carcass, you scattered your enemies with your mighty arm. ^psalm-89-10

11 The heavens are yours, the earth also is yours; the world and all that is in it, you have founded them. ^psalm-89-11

12 The north and the south, you have created them; Tabor and Hermon joyously praise your name. ^psalm-89-12

13 You have a mighty arm; strong is your hand, high your right hand. ^psalm-89-13

14 Righteousness and justice are the foundation of your throne; steadfast love and faithfulness go before you. ^psalm-89-14

15 Blessed are the people who know the festal shout, who walk, O LORD, in the light of your countenance, ^psalm-89-15

16 who exult in your name all the day, and extol your righteousness. ^psalm-89-16

17 For you are the glory of their strength; by your favor our horn is exalted. ^psalm-89-17

18 For our shield belongs to the LORD, our king to the Holy One of Israel. ^psalm-89-18

19 Of old you spoke in a vision to your faithful one, and said: “I have set the crown upon one who is mighty, I have exalted one chosen from the people. ^psalm-89-19

20 I have found David, my servant; with my holy oil I have anointed him; ^psalm-89-20

21 so that my hand shall ever abide with him, my arm also shall strengthen him. ^psalm-89-21

22 The enemy shall not outwit him, the wicked shall not humble him. ^psalm-89-22

23 I will crush his foes before him and strike down those who hate him. ^psalm-89-23

24 My faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him, and in my name shall his horn be exalted. ^psalm-89-24

25 I will set his hand on the sea and his right hand on the rivers. ^psalm-89-25

26 He shall cry to me, ‘You are my Father, my God, and the Rock of my salvation.’ ^psalm-89-26

27 And I will make him the first-born, the highest of the kings of the earth. ^psalm-89-27

28 My merciful love I will keep for him for ever, and my covenant will stand firm for him. ^psalm-89-28

29 I will establish his line for ever and his throne as the days of the heavens. ^psalm-89-29

30 If his children forsake my law and do not walk according to my ordinances, ^psalm-89-30

31 if they violate my statutes and do not keep my commandments, ^psalm-89-31

32 then I will punish their transgression with the rod and their iniquity with scourges; ^psalm-89-32

33 but I will not remove from him my merciful love, or be false to my faithfulness. ^psalm-89-33

34 I will not violate my covenant, or alter the word that went forth from my lips. ^psalm-89-34

35 Once for all I have sworn by my holiness; I will not lie to David. ^psalm-89-35

36 His line shall endure for ever, his throne as long as the sun before me. ^psalm-89-36

37 Like the moon it shall be established for ever; it shall stand firm while the skies endure.” Selah ^psalm-89-37

38 But now you have cast off and rejected, you are full of wrath against your anointed. ^psalm-89-38

39 You have renounced the covenant with your servant; you have defiled his crown in the dust. ^psalm-89-39

40 You have breached all his walls; you have laid his strongholds in ruins. ^psalm-89-40

41 All that pass by despoil him; he has become the scorn of his neighbors. ^psalm-89-41

42 You have exalted the right hand of his foes; you have made all his enemies rejoice. ^psalm-89-42

43 Yes, you have turned back the edge of his sword, and you have not made him stand in battle. ^psalm-89-43

44 You have removed the scepter from his hand, and cast his throne to the ground. ^psalm-89-44

45 You have cut short the days of his youth; you have covered him with shame. Selah ^psalm-89-45

46 How long, O LORD? Will you hide yourself for ever? How long will your wrath burn like fire? ^psalm-89-46

47 Remember, O Lord, what the measure of life is, for what vanity you have created all the sons of men! ^psalm-89-47

48 What man can live and never see death? Who can deliver his soul from the power of Sheol? Selah ^psalm-89-48

49 Lord, where is your steadfast love of old, which by your faithfulness you swore to David? ^psalm-89-49

50 Remember, O Lord, how your servant is scorned; how I bear in my bosom the insults of the peoples, ^psalm-89-50

51 with which your enemies taunt, O LORD, with which they mock the footsteps of your anointed. ^psalm-89-51

52 Blessed be the LORD for ever! Amen and Amen. BOOK IV ^psalm-89-52
