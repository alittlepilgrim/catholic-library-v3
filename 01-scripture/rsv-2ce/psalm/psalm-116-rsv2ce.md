# Psalm 116 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-116|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Recovery from Illness**

1 I love the LORD, because he has heard my voice and my supplications. ^psalm-116-1

2 Because he inclined his ear to me, therefore I will call on him as long as I live. ^psalm-116-2

3 The snares of death encompassed me; the pangs of Sheol laid hold on me; I suffered distress and anguish. ^psalm-116-3

4 Then I called on the name of the LORD: “O LORD, I beg you, save my life!” ^psalm-116-4

5 Gracious is the LORD, and righteous; our God is merciful. ^psalm-116-5

6 The LORD preserves the simple; when I was brought low, he saved me. ^psalm-116-6

7 Return, O my soul, to your rest; for the LORD has dealt bountifully with you. ^psalm-116-7

8 For you have delivered my soul from death, my eyes from tears, my feet from stumbling; ^psalm-116-8

9 I walk before the LORD in the land of the living. ^psalm-116-9

10 I kept my faith, even when I said, “I am greatly afflicted”; ^psalm-116-10

11 I said in my consternation, “Men are all a vain hope.” ^psalm-116-11

12 What shall I render to the LORD for all his bounty to me? ^psalm-116-12

13 I will lift up the chalice of salvation and call on the name of the LORD, ^psalm-116-13

14 I will pay my vows to the LORD in the presence of all his people. ^psalm-116-14

15 Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints. ^psalm-116-15

16 O LORD, I am your servant; I am your servant, the son of your handmaid. You have loosed my bonds. ^psalm-116-16

17 I will offer to you the sacrifice of thanksgiving and call on the name of the LORD. ^psalm-116-17

18 I will pay my vows to the LORD in the presence of all his people, ^psalm-116-18

19 in the courts of the house of the LORD, in your midst, O Jerusalem. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-116-19
