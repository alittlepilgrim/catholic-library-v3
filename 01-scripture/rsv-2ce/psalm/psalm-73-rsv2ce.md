# Psalm 73 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-73|NABRE]]

**Plea for Relief from Oppressors**

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

1 Truly God is good to the upright, to those who are pure in heart. ^psalm-73-1

2 But as for me, my feet had almost stumbled, my steps had well nigh slipped. ^psalm-73-2

3 For I was envious of the arrogant, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked. ^psalm-73-3

4 For they have no pangs; their bodies are sound and sleek. ^psalm-73-4

5 They are not in trouble as other men are; they are not stricken like other men. ^psalm-73-5

6 Therefore pride is their necklace; violence covers them as a garment. ^psalm-73-6

7 Their eyes swell out with fatness, their hearts overflow with follies. ^psalm-73-7

8 They scoff and speak with malice; loftily they threaten oppression. ^psalm-73-8

9 They set their mouths against the heavens, and their tongue struts through the earth. ^psalm-73-9

10 Therefore the people turn and praise them; and find no fault in them. ^psalm-73-10

11 And they say, “How can God know? Is there knowledge in the Most High?” ^psalm-73-11

12 Behold, these are the wicked; always at ease, they increase in riches. ^psalm-73-12

13 All in vain have I kept my heart clean and washed my hands in innocence. ^psalm-73-13

14 For all the day long I have been stricken, and chastened every morning. ^psalm-73-14

15 If I had said, “I will speak thus,” I would have been untrue to the generation of your children. ^psalm-73-15

16 But when I thought how to understand this, it seemed to me a wearisome task, ^psalm-73-16

17 until I went into the sanctuary of God; then I perceived their end. ^psalm-73-17

18 Truly you set them in slippery places; you make them fall to ruin. ^psalm-73-18

19 How they are destroyed in a moment, swept away utterly by terrors! ^psalm-73-19

20 They are like a dream when one awakes, on awaking you despise their phantoms. ^psalm-73-20

21 When my soul was embittered, when I was pricked in heart, ^psalm-73-21

22 I was stupid and ignorant, I was like a beast toward you. ^psalm-73-22

23 Nevertheless I am continually with you; you hold my right hand. ^psalm-73-23

24 You guide me with your counsel, and afterward you will receive me to glory. ^psalm-73-24

25 Whom have I in heaven but you? And there is nothing upon earth that I desire besides you. ^psalm-73-25

26 My flesh and my heart may fail, but God is the strength of my heart and my portion for ever. ^psalm-73-26

27 For behold, those who are far from you shall perish; you put an end to those who are false to you. ^psalm-73-27

28 But for me it is good to be near God; I have made the Lord GOD my refuge, that I may tell of all your works. A Maskil of Asaph. ^psalm-73-28
