# Psalm 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-25|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Guidance and for Deliverance**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 To you, O LORD, I lift up my soul. ^psalm-25-1

2 O my God, in you I trust, let me not be put to shame; let not my enemies exult over me. ^psalm-25-2

3 Yes, let none that wait for you be put to shame; let them be ashamed who are wantonly treacherous. ^psalm-25-3

4 Make me to know your ways, O LORD; teach me your paths. ^psalm-25-4

5 Lead me in your truth, and teach me, for you are the God of my salvation; for you I wait all the day long. ^psalm-25-5

6 Be mindful of your compassion, O LORD, and of your merciful love, for they have been from of old. ^psalm-25-6

7 Remember not the sins of my youth, or my transgressions; according to your mercy remember me, for your goodness’ sake, O LORD! ^psalm-25-7

8 Good and upright is the LORD; therefore he instructs sinners in the way. ^psalm-25-8

9 He leads the humble in what is right, and teaches the humble his way. ^psalm-25-9

10 All the paths of the LORD are mercy and faithfulness, for those who keep his covenant and his testimonies. ^psalm-25-10

11 For your name’s sake, O LORD, pardon my guilt, for it is great. ^psalm-25-11

12 Who is the man that fears the LORD? Him will he instruct in the way that he should choose. ^psalm-25-12

13 He himself shall abide in prosperity, and his children shall possess the land. ^psalm-25-13

14 The friendship of the LORD is for those who fear him, and he makes known to them his covenant. ^psalm-25-14

15 My eyes are ever toward the LORD, for he will pluck my feet out of the net. ^psalm-25-15

16 Turn to me, and be gracious to me; for I am lonely and afflicted. ^psalm-25-16

17 Relieve the troubles of my heart, and bring me out of my distresses. ^psalm-25-17

18 Consider my affliction and my trouble, and forgive all my sins. ^psalm-25-18

19 Consider how many are my foes, and with what violent hatred they hate me. ^psalm-25-19

20 Oh, guard my life, and deliver me; let me not be put to shame, for I take refuge in you. ^psalm-25-20

21 May integrity and uprightness preserve me, for I wait for you. ^psalm-25-21

22 Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles. ^psalm-25-22
