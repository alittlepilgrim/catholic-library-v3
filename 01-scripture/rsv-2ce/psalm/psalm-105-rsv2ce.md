# Psalm 105 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-105|NABRE]]

**God’s Faithfulness to Israel**

1 O give thanks to the LORD, call on his name, make known his deeds among the peoples! ^psalm-105-1

2 Sing to him, sing praises to him, tell of all his wonderful works! ^psalm-105-2

3 Glory in his holy name; let the hearts of those who seek the LORD rejoice! ^psalm-105-3

4 Seek the LORD and his strength, seek his presence continually! ^psalm-105-4

5 Remember the wonderful works that he has done, his miracles, and the judgments he uttered, ^psalm-105-5

6 O offspring of Abraham his servant, sons of Jacob, his chosen ones! ^psalm-105-6

7 He is the LORD our God; his judgments are in all the earth. ^psalm-105-7

8 He is mindful of his covenant for ever, of the word that he commanded, for a thousand generations, ^psalm-105-8

9 the covenant which he made with Abraham, his sworn promise to Isaac, ^psalm-105-9

10 which he confirmed to Jacob as a statute, to Israel as an everlasting covenant, ^psalm-105-10

11 saying, “To you I will give the land of Canaan as your portion for an inheritance.” ^psalm-105-11

12 When they were few in number, of little account, and sojourners in it, ^psalm-105-12

13 wandering from nation to nation, from one kingdom to another people, ^psalm-105-13

14 he allowed no one to oppress them; he rebuked kings on their account, ^psalm-105-14

15 saying, “Touch not my anointed ones, do my prophets no harm!” ^psalm-105-15

16 When he summoned a famine on the land, and broke every staff of bread, ^psalm-105-16

17 he had sent a man ahead of them, Joseph, who was sold as a slave. ^psalm-105-17

18 His feet were hurt with shackles, his neck was put in a collar of iron; ^psalm-105-18

19 until what he had said came to pass the word of the LORD tested him. ^psalm-105-19

20 The king sent and released him, the ruler of the peoples set him free; ^psalm-105-20

21 he made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his possessions, ^psalm-105-21

22 to instruct his princes at his pleasure, and to teach his elders wisdom. ^psalm-105-22

23 Then Israel came to Egypt; Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. ^psalm-105-23

24 And the LORD made his people very fruitful, and made them stronger than their foes. ^psalm-105-24

25 He turned their hearts to hate his people, to deal craftily with his servants. ^psalm-105-25

26 He sent Moses his servant, and Aaron whom he had chosen. ^psalm-105-26

27 They wrought his signs among them, and miracles in the land of Ham. ^psalm-105-27

28 He sent darkness, and made the land dark; they rebelled against his words. ^psalm-105-28

29 He turned their waters into blood, and caused their fish to die. ^psalm-105-29

30 Their land swarmed with frogs, even in the chambers of their kings. ^psalm-105-30

31 He spoke, and there came swarms of flies, and gnats throughout their country. ^psalm-105-31

32 He gave them hail for rain, and lightning that flashed through their land. ^psalm-105-32

33 He struck their vines and fig trees, and shattered the trees of their country. ^psalm-105-33

34 He spoke, and the locusts came, and young locusts without number; ^psalm-105-34

35 which devoured all the vegetation in their land, and ate up the fruit of their ground. ^psalm-105-35

36 He struck all the first-born in their land, the first issue of all their strength. ^psalm-105-36

37 Then he led forth Israel with silver and gold, and there was none among his tribes who stumbled. ^psalm-105-37

38 Egypt was glad when they departed, for dread of them had fallen upon it. ^psalm-105-38

39 He spread a cloud for a covering, and fire to give light by night. ^psalm-105-39

40 They asked, and he brought quails, and gave them bread from heaven in abundance. ^psalm-105-40

41 He opened the rock, and water gushed forth; it flowed through the desert like a river. ^psalm-105-41

42 For he remembered his holy promise, and Abraham his servant. ^psalm-105-42

43 So he led forth his people with joy, his chosen ones with singing. ^psalm-105-43

44 And he gave them the lands of the nations; and they took possession of the fruit of the peoples’ toil, ^psalm-105-44

45 to the end that they should keep his statutes, and observe his laws. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-105-45
