# Psalm 104 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-104|NABRE]]

**God the Creator and Provider**

1 Bless the LORD, O my soul! O LORD my God, you are very great! You are clothed with honor and majesty, ^psalm-104-1

2 who cover yourself with light as with a garment, who have stretched out the heavens like a tent, ^psalm-104-2

3 who have laid the beams of your chambers on the waters, who make the clouds your chariot, who ride on the wings of the wind, ^psalm-104-3

4 who make the winds your messengers, fire and flame your ministers. ^psalm-104-4

5 You set the earth on its foundations, so that it should never be shaken. ^psalm-104-5

6 You covered it with the deep as with a garment; the waters stood above the mountains. ^psalm-104-6

7 At your rebuke they fled; at the sound of your thunder they took to flight. ^psalm-104-7

8 The mountains rose, the valleys sank down to the place which you appointed for them. ^psalm-104-8

9 You set a bound which they should not pass, so that they might not again cover the earth. ^psalm-104-9

10 You make springs gush forth in the valleys; they flow between the hills, ^psalm-104-10

11 they give drink to every beast of the field; the wild donkeys quench their thirst. ^psalm-104-11

12 By them the birds of the air have their habitation; they sing among the branches. ^psalm-104-12

13 From your lofty abode you water the mountains; the earth is satisfied with the fruit of your work. ^psalm-104-13

14 You cause the grass to grow for the cattle, and plants for man to cultivate, that he may bring forth food from the earth, ^psalm-104-14

15 and wine to gladden the heart of man, oil to make his face shine, and bread to strengthen man’s heart. ^psalm-104-15

16 The trees of the LORD are watered abundantly, the cedars of Lebanon which he planted. ^psalm-104-16

17 In them the birds build their nests; the stork has her home in the fir trees. ^psalm-104-17

18 The high mountains are for the wild goats; the rocks are a refuge for the badgers. ^psalm-104-18

19 You have made the moon to mark the seasons; the sun knows its time for setting. ^psalm-104-19

20 You make darkness, and it is night, when all the beasts of the forest creep forth. ^psalm-104-20

21 The young lions roar for their prey, seeking their food from God. ^psalm-104-21

22 When the sun rises, they get them away and lie down in their dens. ^psalm-104-22

23 Man goes forth to his work and to his labor until the evening. ^psalm-104-23

24 O LORD, how manifold are your works! In wisdom you have made them all; the earth is full of your creatures. ^psalm-104-24

25 Yonder is the sea, great and wide, which teems with things innumerable, living things both small and great. ^psalm-104-25

26 There go the ships, and Leviathan which you formed to sport in it. ^psalm-104-26

27 These all look to you, to give them their food in due season. ^psalm-104-27

28 When you give to them, they gather it up; when you open your hand, they are filled with good things. ^psalm-104-28

29 When you hide your face, they are dismayed; when you take away their spirit, they die and return to their dust. ^psalm-104-29

30 When you send forth your Spirit, they are created; and you renew the face of the earth. ^psalm-104-30

31 May the glory of the LORD endure for ever, may the LORD rejoice in his works, ^psalm-104-31

32 who looks on the earth and it trembles, who touches the mountains and they smoke! ^psalm-104-32

33 I will sing to the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praise to my God while I have being. ^psalm-104-33

34 May my meditation be pleasing to him, for I rejoice in the LORD. ^psalm-104-34

35 Let sinners be consumed from the earth, and let the wicked be no more! Bless the LORD, O my soul! Praise the LORD! ^psalm-104-35

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (104:1) A magnificent hymn of creation, it should be compared with Gen 1. It is remarkably similar to an Egyptian hymn to Aten, the sun god. ^psalm-104-dn-1
