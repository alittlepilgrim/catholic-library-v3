# Psalm 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-03|NABRE]]

**Trust in God under Adversity**

*A Psalm of David, when he fled from Absalom his son.*

1 O LORD, how many are my foes! Many are rising against me; ^psalm-03-1

2 many are saying of me, there is no help for him in God. Selah ^psalm-03-2

3 But you, O LORD, are a shield about me, my glory, and the lifter of my head. ^psalm-03-3

4 I cry aloud to the LORD, and he answers me from his holy mountain. Selah ^psalm-03-4

5 I lie down and sleep; I wake again, for the LORD sustains me. ^psalm-03-5

6 I am not afraid of ten thousands of people who have set themselves against me round about. ^psalm-03-6

7 Arise, O LORD! Deliver me, O my God! For you strike all my enemies on the cheek, you break the teeth of the wicked. ^psalm-03-7

8 Deliverance belongs to the LORD; your blessing be upon your people! Selah ^psalm-03-8
