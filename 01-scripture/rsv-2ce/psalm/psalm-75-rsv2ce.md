# Psalm 75 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-75|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for God’s Wondrous Deeds**

*A Psalm of Asaph. A Song.*

1 We give thanks to you, O God; we give thanks; we call on your name and recount your wondrous deeds. ^psalm-75-1

2 At the set time which I appoint I will judge with equity. ^psalm-75-2

3 When the earth totters, and all its inhabitants, it is I who keep steady its pillars. Selah ^psalm-75-3

4 I say to the boastful, “Do not boast,” and to the wicked, “Do not lift up your horn; ^psalm-75-4

5 do not lift up your horn on high, or speak with insolent neck.” ^psalm-75-5

6 For not from the east or from the west and not from the wilderness comes lifting up; ^psalm-75-6

7 but it is God who executes judgment, putting down one and lifting up another. ^psalm-75-7

8 For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup, with foaming wine, well mixed; and he will pour a draught from it, and all the wicked of the earth shall drain it down to the dregs. ^psalm-75-8

9 But I will rejoice for ever, I will sing praises to the God of Jacob. ^psalm-75-9

10 All the horns of the wicked he will cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted. To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. ^psalm-75-10
