# Psalm 37 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-37|NABRE]]

**Exhortation to Patience and Trust**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Do not fret because of the wicked, be not envious of wrongdoers! ^psalm-37-1

2 For they will soon fade like the grass, and wither like the green herb. ^psalm-37-2

3 Trust in the LORD, and do good; so you will dwell in the land, and be nourished in safety. ^psalm-37-3

4 Take delight in the LORD, and he will give you the desires of your heart. ^psalm-37-4

5 Commit your way to the LORD; trust in him, and he will act. ^psalm-37-5

6 He will bring forth your vindication as the light, and your right as the noonday. ^psalm-37-6

7 Be still before the LORD, and wait patiently for him; do not fret over him who prospers in his way, over the man who carries out evil devices! ^psalm-37-7

8 Refrain from anger, and forsake wrath! Do not fret; it tends only to evil. ^psalm-37-8

9 For the wicked shall be cut off; but those who wait for the LORD shall possess the land. ^psalm-37-9

10 Yet a little while, and the wicked will be no more; though you look well at his place, he will not be there. ^psalm-37-10

11 But the meek shall possess the land, and delight themselves in abundant prosperity. ^psalm-37-11

12 The wicked plots against the righteous, and gnashes his teeth at him; ^psalm-37-12

13 but the LORD laughs at the wicked, for he sees that his day is coming. ^psalm-37-13

14 The wicked draw the sword and bend their bows, to bring down the poor and needy, to slay those who walk uprightly; ^psalm-37-14

15 their sword shall enter their own heart, and their bows shall be broken. ^psalm-37-15

16 Better is a little that the righteous has than the abundance of many wicked. ^psalm-37-16

17 For the arms of the wicked shall be broken; but the LORD upholds the righteous. ^psalm-37-17

18 The LORD knows the days of the blameless, and their heritage will abide for ever; ^psalm-37-18

19 they are not put to shame in evil times, in the days of famine they have abundance. ^psalm-37-19

20 But the wicked perish; the enemies of the LORD are like the glory of the pastures, they vanish—like smoke they vanish away. ^psalm-37-20

21 The wicked borrows, and cannot pay back, but the righteous is generous and gives; ^psalm-37-21

22 for those blessed by the LORD shall possess the land, but those cursed by him shall be cut off. ^psalm-37-22

23 The steps of a man are from the LORD, and he establishes him in whose way he delights; ^psalm-37-23

24 though he fall, he shall not be cast headlong, for the LORD is the stay of his hand. ^psalm-37-24

25 I have been young, and now am old; yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken or his children begging bread. ^psalm-37-25

26 He is ever giving liberally and lending, and his children become a blessing. ^psalm-37-26

27 Depart from evil, and do good; so shall you abide for ever. ^psalm-37-27

28 For the LORD loves justice; he will not forsake his saints. The righteous shall be preserved for ever, but the children of the wicked shall be cut off. ^psalm-37-28

29 The righteous shall possess the land, and dwell upon it for ever. ^psalm-37-29

30 The mouth of the righteous utters wisdom, and his tongue speaks justice. ^psalm-37-30

31 The law of his God is in his heart; his steps do not slip. ^psalm-37-31

32 The wicked watches the righteous, and seeks to slay him. ^psalm-37-32

33 The LORD will not abandon him to his power, or let him be condemned when he is brought to trial. ^psalm-37-33

34 Wait for the LORD, and keep to his way, and he will exalt you to possess the land; you will look on the destruction of the wicked. ^psalm-37-34

35 I have seen a wicked man overbearing, and towering like a cedar of Lebanon. ^psalm-37-35

36 Again I passed by, and, behold, he was no more; though I sought him, he could not be found. ^psalm-37-36

37 Mark the blameless man, and behold the upright, for there is posterity for the man of peace. ^psalm-37-37

38 But transgressors shall be altogether destroyed; the posterity of the wicked shall be cut off. ^psalm-37-38

39 The salvation of the righteous is from the LORD; he is their refuge in the time of trouble. ^psalm-37-39

40 The LORD helps them and delivers them; he delivers them from the wicked, and saves them, because they take refuge in him. ^psalm-37-40
