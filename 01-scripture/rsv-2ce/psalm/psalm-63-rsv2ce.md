# Psalm 63 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-63|NABRE]]

**Comfort and Assurance in God’s Presence**

*A Psalm of David, when he was in the Wilderness of Judah.*

1 O God, you are my God, I seek you, my soul thirsts for you; my flesh faints for you, as in a dry and weary land where no water is. ^psalm-63-1

2 So I have looked upon you in the sanctuary, beholding your power and glory. ^psalm-63-2

3 Because your merciful love is better than life, my lips will praise you. ^psalm-63-3

4 So I will bless you as long as I live; I will lift up my hands and call on your name. ^psalm-63-4

5 My soul is feasted as with marrow and fat, and my mouth praises you with joyful lips, ^psalm-63-5

6 when I think of you upon my bed, and meditate on you in the watches of the night; ^psalm-63-6

7 for you have been my help, and in the shadow of your wings I sing for joy. ^psalm-63-7

8 My soul clings to you; your right hand upholds me. ^psalm-63-8

9 But those who seek to destroy my life shall go down into the depths of the earth; ^psalm-63-9

10 they shall be given over to the power of the sword, they shall be prey for jackals. ^psalm-63-10

11 But the king shall rejoice in God; all who swear by him shall glory; for the mouths of liars will be stopped. To the choirmaster. ^psalm-63-11
