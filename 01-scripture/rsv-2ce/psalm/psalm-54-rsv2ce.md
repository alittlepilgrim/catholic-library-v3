# Psalm 54 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-54|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Vindication**

*To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Maskil of David, when the Ziphites went and told Saul, “David is in hiding among us.”*

1 Save me, O God, by your name, and vindicate me by your might. ^psalm-54-1

2 Hear my prayer, O God; give ear to the words of my mouth. ^psalm-54-2

3 For insolent men have risen against me, ruthless men seek my life; they do not set God before them. Selah ^psalm-54-3

4 Behold, God is my helper; the Lord is the upholder of my life. ^psalm-54-4

5 He will repay my enemies with evil; in your faithfulness put an end to them. ^psalm-54-5

6 With a freewill offering I will sacrifice to you; I will give thanks to your name, O LORD, for it is good. ^psalm-54-6

7 For you have delivered me from every trouble, and my eye has looked in triumph on my enemies. ^psalm-54-7
