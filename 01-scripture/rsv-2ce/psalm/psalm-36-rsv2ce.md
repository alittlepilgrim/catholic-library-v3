# Psalm 36 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-36|NABRE]]

**Human Wickedness and Divine Goodness**

*A Psalm of David, the servant of the LORD.*

1 Transgression speaks to the wicked deep in his heart; there is no fear of God before his eyes. ^psalm-36-1

2 For he flatters himself in his own eyes that his iniquity cannot be found out and hated. ^psalm-36-2

3 The words of his mouth are mischief and deceit; he has ceased to act wisely and do good. ^psalm-36-3

4 He plots mischief while on his bed; he sets himself in a way that is not good; he spurns not evil. ^psalm-36-4

5 Your mercy, O LORD, extends to the heavens, your faithfulness to the clouds. ^psalm-36-5

6 Your righteousness is like the mountains of God, your judgments are like the great deep; man and beast you save, O LORD. ^psalm-36-6

7 How precious is your mercy, O God! The children of men take refuge in the shadow of your wings. ^psalm-36-7

8 They feast on the abundance of your house, and you give them drink from the river of your delights. ^psalm-36-8

9 For with you is the fountain of life; in your light do we see light. ^psalm-36-9

10 O continue your steadfast love to those who know you, and your salvation to the upright of heart! ^psalm-36-10

11 Let not the foot of arrogance come upon me, nor the hand of the wicked drive me away. ^psalm-36-11

12 There the evildoers lie prostrate, they are thrust down, unable to rise. ^psalm-36-12
