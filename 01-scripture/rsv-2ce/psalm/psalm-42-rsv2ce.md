# Psalm 42 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-42|NABRE]]

**Longing for God and His Help in Distress**

*To the choirmaster. A Maskil of the Sons of Korah.*

1 As a deer longs for flowing streams, so longs my soul for you, O God. ^psalm-42-1

2 My soul thirsts for God, for the living God. When shall I come and behold the face of God? ^psalm-42-2

3 My tears have been my food day and night, while men say to me continually, “Where is your God?” ^psalm-42-3

4 These things I remember, as I pour out my soul: how I went with the throng, and led them in procession to the house of God, with glad shouts and songs of thanksgiving, a multitude keeping festival. ^psalm-42-4

5 Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God; for I shall again praise him, my savior ^psalm-42-5

6 and my God. My soul is cast down within me, therefore I remember you from the land of Jordan and of Hermon, from Mount Mizar. ^psalm-42-6

7 Deep calls to deep at the thunder of your cataracts; all your waves and your billows have gone over me. ^psalm-42-7

8 By day the LORD commands his steadfast love; and at night his song is with me, a prayer to the God of my life. ^psalm-42-8

9 I say to God, my rock: “Why have you forgotten me? Why do I go mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?” ^psalm-42-9

10 As with a deadly wound in my body, my adversaries taunt me, while they say to me continually, “Where is your God?” ^psalm-42-10

11 Why are you cast down, O my soul, and why are you disquieted within me? Hope in God; for I shall again praise him, my help and my God. ^psalm-42-11

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (42:1) This and the following psalm were originally one, as can be seen from the refrain repeated three times. ^psalm-42-dn-1
