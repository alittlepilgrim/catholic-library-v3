# Psalm 22 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-22|NABRE]]

**Plea for Deliverance from Suffering and Hostility**

*To the choirmaster: according to The Hind of the Dawn. A Psalm of David.*

1 My God, my God, why have you forsaken me? Why are you so far from helping me, from the words of my groaning? ^psalm-22-1

2 O my God, I cry by day, but you do not answer; and by night, but find no rest. ^psalm-22-2

3 Yet you are holy, enthroned on the praises of Israel. ^psalm-22-3

4 In you our fathers trusted; they trusted, and you delivered them. ^psalm-22-4

5 To you they cried, and were saved; in you they trusted, and were not disappointed. ^psalm-22-5

6 But I am a worm, and no man; scorned by men, and despised by the people. ^psalm-22-6

7 All who see me mock at me, they make mouths at me, they wag their heads; ^psalm-22-7

8 “He committed his cause to the LORD; let him deliver him, let him rescue him, for he delights in him!” ^psalm-22-8

9 Yet you are he who took me from the womb; you kept me safe upon my mother’s breasts. ^psalm-22-9

10 Upon you was I cast from my birth, and since my mother bore me you have been my God. ^psalm-22-10

11 Be not far from me, for trouble is near and there is none to help. ^psalm-22-11

12 Many bulls encompass me, strong bulls of Bashan surround me; ^psalm-22-12

13 they open wide their mouths at me, like a ravening and roaring lion. ^psalm-22-13

14 I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint; my heart is like wax, it is melted within my breast; ^psalm-22-14

15 my strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue cleaves to my jaws; you lay me in the dust of death. ^psalm-22-15

16 Yes, dogs are round about me; a company of evildoers encircle me; they have pierced my hands and feet— ^psalm-22-16

17 I can count all my bones— they stare and gloat over me; ^psalm-22-17

18 they divide my garments among them, and for my clothing they cast lots. ^psalm-22-18

19 But you, O LORD, be not far off! O my help, hasten to my aid! ^psalm-22-19

20 Deliver my soul from the sword, my life from the power of the dog! ^psalm-22-20

21 Save me from the mouth of the lion, my afflicted soul from the horns of the wild oxen! ^psalm-22-21

22 I will tell of your name to my brethren; in the midst of the congregation I will praise you: ^psalm-22-22

23 You who fear the LORD, praise him! all you sons of Jacob, glorify him, and stand in awe of him, all you sons of Israel! ^psalm-22-23

24 For he has not despised or abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; and he has not hidden his face from him, but has heard, when he cried to him. ^psalm-22-24

25 From you comes my praise in the great congregation; my vows I will pay before those who fear him. ^psalm-22-25

26 The afflicted shall eat and be satisfied; those who seek him shall praise the LORD! May your hearts live for ever! ^psalm-22-26

27 All the ends of the earth shall remember and turn to the LORD; and all the families of the nations shall worship before him. ^psalm-22-27

28 For dominion belongs to the LORD, and he rules over the nations. ^psalm-22-28

29 Yes, to him shall all the proud of the earth bow down; before him shall bow all who go down to the dust, and he who cannot keep himself alive. ^psalm-22-29

30 Posterity shall serve him; men shall tell of the Lord to the coming generation, ^psalm-22-30

31 and proclaim his deliverance to a people yet unborn, that he has wrought it. ^psalm-22-31

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (22:1) This lamentation of the righteous man in his sufferings has profoundly influenced the Gospel passion story. See especially Mt 27:46. ^psalm-22-dn-1
