# Psalm 13 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-13|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 How long, O LORD? Will you forget me for ever? How long will you hide your face from me? ^psalm-13-1

2 How long must I bear pain in my soul, and have sorrow in my heart all the day? How long shall my enemy be exalted over me? ^psalm-13-2

3 Consider and answer me, O LORD my God; lighten my eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death; ^psalm-13-3

4 lest my enemy say, “I have prevailed over him”; lest my foes rejoice because I am shaken. ^psalm-13-4

5 But I have trusted in your merciful love; my heart shall rejoice in your salvation. ^psalm-13-5

6 I will sing to the LORD, because he has dealt bountifully with me. ^psalm-13-6
