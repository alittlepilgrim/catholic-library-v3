# Psalm 45 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-45|NABRE]]

**Ode for a Royal Wedding**

*To the choirmaster: according to Lilies. A Maskil of the Sons of Korah; a love song.*

1 My heart overflows with a goodly theme; I address my verses to the king; my tongue is like the pen of a ready scribe. ^psalm-45-1

2 You are the fairest of the sons of men; grace is poured upon your lips; therefore God has blessed you for ever. ^psalm-45-2

3 Gird your sword upon your thigh, O mighty one, in your glory and majesty! ^psalm-45-3

4 In your majesty ride forth victoriously for the cause of truth and to defend the right; let your right hand teach you dread deeds! ^psalm-45-4

5 Your arrows are sharp in the heart of the king’s enemies; the peoples fall under you. ^psalm-45-5

6 Your divine throne endures for ever and ever. Your royal scepter is a scepter of equity; ^psalm-45-6

7 you love righteousness and hate wickedness. Therefore God, your God, has anointed you with the oil of gladness above your fellows; ^psalm-45-7

8 your robes are all fragrant with myrrh and aloes and cassia. From ivory palaces stringed instruments make you glad; ^psalm-45-8

9 daughters of kings are among your ladies of honor; at your right hand stands the queen in gold of Ophir. ^psalm-45-9

10 Hear, O daughter, consider, and incline your ear; forget your people and your father’s house; ^psalm-45-10

11 and the king will desire your beauty. Since he is your lord, bow to him; ^psalm-45-11

12 the people of Tyre will court your favor with gifts, the richest of the people ^psalm-45-12

13 with all kinds of wealth. The daughter of the king is decked in her chamber with gold-woven robes; ^psalm-45-13

14 in many-colored robes she is led to the king, with her virgin companions, her escort, in her train. ^psalm-45-14

15 With joy and gladness they are led along as they enter the palace of the king. ^psalm-45-15

16 Instead of your fathers shall be your sons; you will make them princes in all the earth. ^psalm-45-16

17 I will cause your name to be celebrated in all generations; therefore the peoples will praise you for ever and ever. To the choirmaster. ^psalm-45-17
