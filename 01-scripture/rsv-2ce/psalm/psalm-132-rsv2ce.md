# Psalm 132 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-132|NABRE]]

**The Eternal Dwelling of God in Zion**

1 Remember, O LORD, in David’s favor, his humility; ^psalm-132-1

2 how he swore to the LORD and vowed to the Mighty One of Jacob, ^psalm-132-2

3 “I will not enter my house or get into my bed; ^psalm-132-3

4 I will not give sleep to my eyes or slumber to my eyelids, ^psalm-132-4

5 until I find a place for the LORD, a dwelling place for the Mighty One of Jacob.” ^psalm-132-5

6 Behold, we heard of it in Eph'rathah, we found it in the fields of Ja'ar. ^psalm-132-6

7 “Let us go to his dwelling place; let us worship at his footstool!” ^psalm-132-7

8 Arise, O LORD, and go to your resting place, you and the ark of your might. ^psalm-132-8

9 Let your priests be clothed with righteousness, and let your saints shout for joy. ^psalm-132-9

10 For your servant David’s sake do not turn away the face of your anointed one. ^psalm-132-10

11 The LORD swore to David a sure oath from which he will not turn back: “One of the sons of your body I will set on your throne. ^psalm-132-11

12 If your sons keep my covenant and my testimonies which I shall teach them, their sons also for ever shall sit upon your throne.” ^psalm-132-12

13 For the LORD has chosen Zion; he has desired it for his habitation: ^psalm-132-13

14 “This is my resting place for ever; here I will dwell, for I have desired it. ^psalm-132-14

15 I will abundantly bless her provisions; I will satisfy her poor with bread. ^psalm-132-15

16 Her priests I will clothe with salvation, and her saints will shout for joy. ^psalm-132-16

17 There I will make a horn to sprout for David; I have prepared a lamp for my anointed. ^psalm-132-17

18 His enemies I will clothe with shame, but upon himself his crown will shed its luster.” A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-132-18
