# Psalm 100 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-100|NABRE]]

**All Lands Summoned to Praise God**

*A Psalm for the thank offering.*

1 Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the lands! ^psalm-100-1

2 Serve the LORD with gladness! Come into his presence with singing! ^psalm-100-2

3 Know that the LORD is God! It is he that made us, and we are his; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture. ^psalm-100-3

4 Enter his gates with thanksgiving, and his courts with praise! Give thanks to him, bless his name! ^psalm-100-4

5 For the LORD is good; his mercy endures for ever, and his faithfulness to all generations. ^psalm-100-5
