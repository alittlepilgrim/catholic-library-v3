# Psalm 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-10|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies**

1 Why do you stand afar off, O LORD? Why do you hide yourself in times of trouble? ^psalm-10-1

2 In arrogance the wicked hotly pursue the poor; let them be caught in the schemes which they have devised. ^psalm-10-2

3 For the wicked boasts of the desires of his heart, and the man greedy for gain curses and renounces the LORD. ^psalm-10-3

4 In the pride of his countenance the wicked does not seek him; all his thoughts are, “There is no God.” ^psalm-10-4

5 His ways prosper at all times; your judgments are on high, out of his sight; as for all his foes, he puffs at them. ^psalm-10-5

6 He thinks in his heart, “I shall not be moved; throughout all generations I shall not meet adversity.” ^psalm-10-6

7 His mouth is filled with cursing and deceit and oppression; under his tongue are mischief and iniquity. ^psalm-10-7

8 He sits in ambush in the villages; in hiding places he murders the innocent. His eyes stealthily watch for the hapless, ^psalm-10-8

9 he lurks in secret like a lion in his den; he lurks that he may seize the poor, he seizes the poor when he draws him into his net. ^psalm-10-9

10 The hapless is crushed, sinks down, and falls by his might. ^psalm-10-10

11 He thinks in his heart, “God has forgotten, he has hidden his face, he will never see it.” ^psalm-10-11

12 Arise, O LORD; O God, lift up your hand; forget not the poor. ^psalm-10-12

13 Why does the wicked renounce God, and say in his heart, “You will not call to account”? ^psalm-10-13

14 You see; yes, you note trouble and vexation, that you may take it into your hands; the hapless commits himself to you; you have been the helper of the fatherless. ^psalm-10-14

15 Break the arm of the wicked and evildoer; seek out his wickedness till you find none. ^psalm-10-15

16 The LORD is king for ever and ever; the nations shall perish from his land. ^psalm-10-16

17 O LORD, you will hear the desire of the meek; you will strengthen their heart, you will incline your ear ^psalm-10-17

18 to do justice to the fatherless and the oppressed, so that man who is of the earth may strike terror no more. ^psalm-10-18
