# Psalm 81 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-81|NABRE]]

**God’s Appeal to Stubborn Israel**

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

1 Sing aloud to God our strength; shout for joy to the God of Jacob! ^psalm-81-1

2 Raise a song, sound the timbrel, the sweet lyre with the harp. ^psalm-81-2

3 Blow the trumpet at the new moon, at the full moon, on our feast day. ^psalm-81-3

4 For it is a statute for Israel, an ordinance of the God of Jacob. ^psalm-81-4

5 He made it a decree in Joseph, when he went out over the land of Egypt. I hear a voice I had not known: ^psalm-81-5

6 “I relieved your shoulder of the burden; your hands were freed from the basket. ^psalm-81-6

7 In distress you called, and I delivered you; I answered you in the secret place of thunder; I tested you at the waters of Mer'ibah. Selah ^psalm-81-7

8 Hear, O my people, while I admonish you! O Israel, if you would but listen to me! ^psalm-81-8

9 There shall be no strange god among you; you shall not bow down to a foreign god. ^psalm-81-9

10 I am the LORD your God, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt. Open your mouth wide, and I will fill it. ^psalm-81-10

11 “But my people did not listen to my voice; Israel would have none of me. ^psalm-81-11

12 So I gave them over to their stubborn hearts, to follow their own counsels. ^psalm-81-12

13 O that my people would listen to me, that Israel would walk in my ways! ^psalm-81-13

14 I would soon subdue their enemies, and turn my hand against their foes. ^psalm-81-14

15 Those who hate the LORD would cringe toward him, and their fate would last for ever. ^psalm-81-15

16 I would feed you with the finest of the wheat, and with honey from the rock I would satisfy you.” A Psalm of Asaph. ^psalm-81-16
