# Psalm 78 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-78|NABRE]]

**God’s Goodness and Israel’s Ingratitude**

1 Give ear, O my people, to my teaching; incline your ears to the words of my mouth! ^psalm-78-1

2 I will open my mouth in a parable; I will utter dark sayings from of old, ^psalm-78-2

3 things that we have heard and known, that our fathers have told us. ^psalm-78-3

4 We will not hide them from their children, but tell to the coming generation the glorious deeds of the LORD, and his might, and the wonders which he has wrought. ^psalm-78-4

5 He established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers to teach to their children; ^psalm-78-5

6 that the next generation might know them, the children yet unborn, and arise and tell them to their children, ^psalm-78-6

7 so that they should set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments; ^psalm-78-7

8 and that they should not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation, a generation whose heart was not steadfast, whose spirit was not faithful to God. ^psalm-78-8

9 The E'phraimites, armed with the bow, turned back on the day of battle. ^psalm-78-9

10 They did not keep God’s covenant, but refused to walk according to his law. ^psalm-78-10

11 They forgot what he had done, and the miracles that he had shown them. ^psalm-78-11

12 In the sight of their fathers he wrought marvels in the land of Egypt, in the fields of Zo'an. ^psalm-78-12

13 He divided the sea and let them pass through it, and made the waters stand like a heap. ^psalm-78-13

14 In the daytime he led them with a cloud, and all the night with a fiery light. ^psalm-78-14

15 He cleft rocks in the wilderness, and gave them drink abundantly as from the deep. ^psalm-78-15

16 He made streams come out of the rock, and caused waters to flow down like rivers. ^psalm-78-16

17 Yet they sinned still more against him, rebelling against the Most High in the desert. ^psalm-78-17

18 They tested God in their heart by demanding the food they craved. ^psalm-78-18

19 They spoke against God, saying, “Can God spread a table in the wilderness? ^psalm-78-19

20 He struck the rock so that water gushed out and streams overflowed. Can he also give bread, or provide meat for his people?” ^psalm-78-20

21 Therefore, when the LORD heard, he was full of wrath; a fire was kindled against Jacob, his anger mounted against Israel; ^psalm-78-21

22 because they had no faith in God, and did not trust his saving power. ^psalm-78-22

23 Yet he commanded the skies above, and opened the doors of heaven; ^psalm-78-23

24 and he rained down upon them manna to eat, and gave them the bread of heaven. ^psalm-78-24

25 Man ate of the bread of the angels; he sent them food in abundance. ^psalm-78-25

26 He caused the east wind to blow in the heavens, and by his power he led out the south wind; ^psalm-78-26

27 he rained flesh upon them like dust, winged birds like the sand of the seas; ^psalm-78-27

28 he let them fall in the midst of their camp, all around their habitations. ^psalm-78-28

29 And they ate and were well filled, for he gave them what they craved. ^psalm-78-29

30 But before they had sated their craving, while the food was still in their mouths, ^psalm-78-30

31 the anger of God rose against them and he slew the strongest of them, and laid low the picked men of Israel. ^psalm-78-31

32 In spite of all this they still sinned; despite his wonders they did not believe. ^psalm-78-32

33 So he made their days vanish like a breath, and their years in terror. ^psalm-78-33

34 When he slew them, they sought for him; they repented and sought God earnestly. ^psalm-78-34

35 They remembered that God was their rock, the Most High God their redeemer. ^psalm-78-35

36 But they flattered him with their mouths; they lied to him with their tongues. ^psalm-78-36

37 Their heart was not steadfast toward him; they were not true to his covenant. ^psalm-78-37

38 Yet he, being compassionate, forgave their iniquity, and did not destroy them; he restrained his anger often, and did not stir up all his wrath. ^psalm-78-38

39 He remembered that they were but flesh, a wind that passes and comes not again. ^psalm-78-39

40 How often they rebelled against him in the wilderness and grieved him in the desert! ^psalm-78-40

41 They tested him again and again, and provoked the Holy One of Israel. ^psalm-78-41

42 They did not keep in mind his power, or the day when he redeemed them from the foe; ^psalm-78-42

43 when he wrought his signs in Egypt, and his miracles in the fields of Zo'an. ^psalm-78-43

44 He turned their rivers to blood, so that they could not drink of their streams. ^psalm-78-44

45 He sent among them swarms of flies, which devoured them, and frogs, which destroyed them. ^psalm-78-45

46 He gave their crops to the caterpillar, and the fruit of their labor to the locust. ^psalm-78-46

47 He destroyed their vines with hail, and their sycamores with frost. ^psalm-78-47

48 He gave over their cattle to the hail, and their flocks to thunderbolts. ^psalm-78-48

49 He let loose on them his fierce anger, wrath, indignation, and distress, a company of destroying angels. ^psalm-78-49

50 He made a path for his anger; he did not spare them from death, but gave their lives over to the plague. ^psalm-78-50

51 He struck all the first-born in Egypt, the first issue of their strength in the tents of Ham. ^psalm-78-51

52 Then he led forth his people like sheep, and guided them in the wilderness like a flock. ^psalm-78-52

53 He led them in safety, so that they were not afraid; but the sea overwhelmed their enemies. ^psalm-78-53

54 And he brought them to his holy land, to the mountain which his right hand had won. ^psalm-78-54

55 He drove out nations before them; he apportioned them for a possession and settled the tribes of Israel in their tents. ^psalm-78-55

56 Yet they tested and rebelled against the Most High God, and did not observe his decrees, ^psalm-78-56

57 but turned away and acted treacherously like their fathers; they twisted like a deceitful bow. ^psalm-78-57

58 For they provoked him to anger with their high places; they moved him to jealousy with their graven images. ^psalm-78-58

59 When God heard, he was full of wrath, and he utterly rejected Israel. ^psalm-78-59

60 He forsook his dwelling at Shiloh, the tent where he dwelt among men, ^psalm-78-60

61 and delivered his power to captivity, his glory to the hand of the foe. ^psalm-78-61

62 He gave his people over to the sword, and vented his wrath on his heritage. ^psalm-78-62

63 Fire devoured their young men, and their maidens had no marriage song. ^psalm-78-63

64 Their priests fell by the sword, and their widows made no lamentation. ^psalm-78-64

65 Then the Lord awoke as from sleep, like a strong man shouting because of wine. ^psalm-78-65

66 And he put his adversaries to rout; he put them to everlasting shame. ^psalm-78-66

67 He rejected the tent of Joseph, he did not choose the tribe of E'phraim; ^psalm-78-67

68 but he chose the tribe of Judah, Mount Zion, which he loves. ^psalm-78-68

69 He built his sanctuary like the high heavens, like the earth, which he has founded for ever. ^psalm-78-69

70 He chose David his servant, and took him from the sheepfolds; ^psalm-78-70

71 from tending the ewes that had young he brought him to be the shepherd of Jacob his people, of Israel his inheritance. ^psalm-78-71

72 With upright heart he tended them, and guided them with skilful hand. ^psalm-78-72
