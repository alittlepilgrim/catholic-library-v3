# Psalm 88 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-88|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Help in Despondency**

*To the choirmaster: according to Mahalath Leannoth. A Maskil of Heman the Ezrahite.*

1 O LORD, my God, I call for help by day; I cry out in the night before you. ^psalm-88-1

2 Let my prayer come before you, incline your ear to my cry! ^psalm-88-2

3 For my soul is full of troubles, and my life draws near to Sheol. ^psalm-88-3

4 I am reckoned among those who go down to the Pit; I am a man who has no strength, ^psalm-88-4

5 like one forsaken among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, like those whom you remember no more, for they are cut off from your hand. ^psalm-88-5

6 You have put me in the depths of the Pit, in the regions dark and deep. ^psalm-88-6

7 Your wrath lies heavy upon me, and you overwhelm me with all your waves. Selah ^psalm-88-7

8 You have caused my companions to shun me; you have made me a thing of horror to them. I am shut in so that I cannot escape; ^psalm-88-8

9 my eye grows dim through sorrow. Every day I call upon you, O LORD; I spread out my hands to you. ^psalm-88-9

10 Do you work wonders for the dead? Do the shades rise up to praise you? Selah ^psalm-88-10

11 Is your mercy declared in the grave, or your faithfulness in Abad'don? ^psalm-88-11

12 Are your wonders known in the darkness, or your saving help in the land of forgetfulness? ^psalm-88-12

13 But I, O LORD, cry to you; in the morning my prayer comes before you. ^psalm-88-13

14 O LORD, why do you cast me off? Why do you hide your face from me? ^psalm-88-14

15 Afflicted and close to death from my youth, I suffer your terrors; I am helpless. ^psalm-88-15

16 Your wrath has swept over me; your dread assaults destroy me. ^psalm-88-16

17 They surround me like a flood all day long; they close in upon me together. ^psalm-88-17

18 You have caused loved one and friend to shun me; my companions are in darkness. ^psalm-88-18
