# Psalm 56 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-56|NABRE]]

**Trust in God under Persecution**

*To the choirmaster: according to The Dove on Far-off Terebinths. A Miktam of David, when the Philistines seized him in Gath.*

1 Have mercy on me, O God, for men trample upon me; all day long foes oppress me; ^psalm-56-1

2 my enemies trample upon me all day long, for many fight against me proudly. ^psalm-56-2

3 When I am afraid, I put my trust in you. ^psalm-56-3

4 In God, whose word I praise, in God I trust without a fear. What can flesh do to me? ^psalm-56-4

5 All day long they seek to injure my cause; all their thoughts are against me for evil. ^psalm-56-5

6 They band themselves together, they lurk, they watch my steps. As they have waited for my life, ^psalm-56-6

7 so recompense them for their crime; in wrath cast down the peoples, O God! ^psalm-56-7

8 You have kept count of my tossings; put my tears in your bottle! Are they not in your book? ^psalm-56-8

9 Then my enemies will be turned back in the day when I call. This I know, that God is for me. ^psalm-56-9

10 In God, whose word I praise, in the LORD, whose word I praise, ^psalm-56-10

11 in God I trust without a fear. What can man do to me? ^psalm-56-11

12 My vows to you I must perform, O God; I will render thank offerings to you. ^psalm-56-12

13 For you have delivered my soul from death, yes, my feet from falling, that I may walk before God in the light of life. ^psalm-56-13
