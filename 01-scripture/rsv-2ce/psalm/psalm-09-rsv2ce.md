# Psalm 9 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-09|NABRE]]

**God’s Power and Justice**

*To the choirmaster: according to Muth-labben. A Psalm of David.*

1 I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart; I will tell of all your wonderful deeds. ^psalm-09-1

2 I will be glad and exult in you, I will sing praise to your name, O Most High. ^psalm-09-2

3 When my enemies turned back, they stumbled and perished before you. ^psalm-09-3

4 For you have maintained my just cause; you have sat on the throne giving righteous judgment. ^psalm-09-4

5 You have rebuked the nations, you have destroyed the wicked; you have blotted out their name for ever and ever. ^psalm-09-5

6 The enemies have vanished in everlasting ruins; their cities you have rooted out; the very memory of them has perished. ^psalm-09-6

7 But the LORD sits enthroned for ever, he has established his throne for judgment; ^psalm-09-7

8 and he judges the world with righteousness, he judges the peoples with equity. ^psalm-09-8

9 The LORD is a stronghold for the oppressed, a stronghold in times of trouble. ^psalm-09-9

10 And those who know your name put their trust in you, for you, O LORD, have not forsaken those who seek you. ^psalm-09-10

11 Sing praises to the LORD, who dwells in Zion! Tell among the peoples his deeds! ^psalm-09-11

12 For he who avenges blood is mindful of them; he does not forget the cry of the poor. ^psalm-09-12

13 Be gracious to me, O LORD! Behold what I suffer from those who hate me, O you who lift me up from the gates of death, ^psalm-09-13

14 that I may recount all your praises, that in the gates of the daughter of Zion I may rejoice in your deliverance. ^psalm-09-14

15 The nations have sunk in the pit which they made; in the net which they hid has their own foot been caught. ^psalm-09-15

16 The LORD has made himself known, he has executed judgment; the wicked are snared in the work of their own hands. Higgaion. Selah ^psalm-09-16

17 The wicked shall depart to Sheol, all the nations that forget God. ^psalm-09-17

18 For the needy shall not always be forgotten, and the hope of the poor shall not perish for ever. ^psalm-09-18

19 Arise, O LORD! Let not man prevail; let the nations be judged before you! ^psalm-09-19

20 Put them in fear, O LORD! Let the nations know that they are but men! Selah ^psalm-09-20
