# Psalm 103 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-103|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for God’s Goodness**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Bless the LORD, O my soul; and all that is within me, bless his holy name! ^psalm-103-1

2 Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits, ^psalm-103-2

3 who forgives all your iniquity, who heals all your diseases, ^psalm-103-3

4 who redeems your life from the Pit, who crowns you with mercy and compassion, ^psalm-103-4

5 who satisfies you with good as long as you live so that your youth is renewed like the eagle’s. ^psalm-103-5

6 The LORD works vindication and justice for all who are oppressed. ^psalm-103-6

7 He made known his ways to Moses, his acts to the people of Israel. ^psalm-103-7

8 The LORD is merciful and gracious, slow to anger and abounding in mercy. ^psalm-103-8

9 He will not always chide, nor will he keep his anger for ever. ^psalm-103-9

10 He does not deal with us according to our sins, nor repay us according to our iniquities. ^psalm-103-10

11 For as the heavens are high above the earth, so great is his mercy toward those who fear him; ^psalm-103-11

12 as far as the east is from the west, so far does he remove our transgressions from us. ^psalm-103-12

13 As a father pities his children, so the LORD pities those who fear him. ^psalm-103-13

14 For he knows our frame; he remembers that we are dust. ^psalm-103-14

15 As for man, his days are like grass; he flourishes like a flower of the field; ^psalm-103-15

16 for the wind passes over it, and it is gone, and its place knows it no more. ^psalm-103-16

17 But the mercy of the LORD is from everlasting to everlasting upon those who fear him, and his righteousness to children’s children, ^psalm-103-17

18 to those who keep his covenant and remember to do his commandments. ^psalm-103-18

19 The LORD has established his throne in the heavens, and his kingdom rules over all. ^psalm-103-19

20 Bless the LORD, O you his angels, you mighty ones who do his word, hearkening to the voice of his word! ^psalm-103-20

21 Bless the LORD, all his hosts, his ministers that do his will! ^psalm-103-21

22 Bless the LORD, all his works, in all places of his dominion. Bless the LORD, O my soul! ^psalm-103-22
