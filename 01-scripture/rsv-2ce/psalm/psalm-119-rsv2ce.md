# Psalm 119 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-119|NABRE]]

**The Glories of God’s Law**

1 Blessed are those whose way is blameless, who walk in the law of the LORD! ^psalm-119-1

2 Blessed are those who keep his testimonies, who seek him with their whole heart, ^psalm-119-2

3 who also do no wrong, but walk in his ways! ^psalm-119-3

4 You have commanded your precepts to be kept diligently. ^psalm-119-4

5 O that my ways may be steadfast in keeping your statutes! ^psalm-119-5

6 Then I shall not be put to shame, having my eyes fixed on all your commandments. ^psalm-119-6

7 I will praise you with an upright heart, when I learn your righteous ordinances. ^psalm-119-7

8 I will observe your statutes; O forsake me not utterly! ^psalm-119-8

9 How can a young man keep his way pure? By guarding it according to your word. ^psalm-119-9

10 With my whole heart I seek you; let me not wander from your commandments! ^psalm-119-10

11 I have laid up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you. ^psalm-119-11

12 Blessed are you, O LORD; teach me your statutes! ^psalm-119-12

13 With my lips I declare all the ordinances of your mouth. ^psalm-119-13

14 In the way of your testimonies I delight as much as in all riches. ^psalm-119-14

15 I will meditate on your precepts, and fix my eyes on your ways. ^psalm-119-15

16 I will delight in your statutes; I will not forget your word. ^psalm-119-16

17 Deal bountifully with your servant, that I may live and observe your word. ^psalm-119-17

18 Open my eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of your law. ^psalm-119-18

19 I am a sojourner on earth; hide not your commandments from me! ^psalm-119-19

20 My soul is consumed with longing for your ordinances at all times. ^psalm-119-20

21 You rebuke the insolent, accursed ones, who wander from your commandments; ^psalm-119-21

22 take away from me their scorn and contempt, for I have kept your testimonies. ^psalm-119-22

23 Even though princes sit plotting against me, your servant will meditate on your statutes. ^psalm-119-23

24 Your testimonies are my delight, they are my counselors. ^psalm-119-24

25 My soul clings to the dust; revive me according to your word! ^psalm-119-25

26 When I told of my ways, you answered me; teach me your statutes! ^psalm-119-26

27 Make me understand the way of your precepts, and I will meditate on your wondrous works. ^psalm-119-27

28 My soul melts away for sorrow; strengthen me according to your word! ^psalm-119-28

29 Put false ways far from me; and graciously teach me your law! ^psalm-119-29

30 I have chosen the way of faithfulness, I set your ordinances before me. ^psalm-119-30

31 I cling to your testimonies, O LORD; let me not be put to shame! ^psalm-119-31

32 I will run in the way of your commandments when you enlarge my understanding! ^psalm-119-32

33 Teach me, O LORD, the way of your statutes; and I will keep it to the end. ^psalm-119-33

34 Give me understanding, that I may keep your law and observe it with my whole heart. ^psalm-119-34

35 Lead me in the path of your commandments, for I delight in it. ^psalm-119-35

36 Incline my heart to your testimonies, and not to gain! ^psalm-119-36

37 Turn my eyes from looking at vanities; and give me life in your ways. ^psalm-119-37

38 Confirm to your servant your promise, which is for those who fear you. ^psalm-119-38

39 Turn away the reproach which I dread; for your ordinances are good. ^psalm-119-39

40 Behold, I long for your precepts; in your righteousness give me life! ^psalm-119-40

41 Let your mercy come to me, O LORD, your salvation according to your promise; ^psalm-119-41

42 then shall I have an answer for those who taunt me, for I trust in your word. ^psalm-119-42

43 And take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth, for my hope is in your ordinances. ^psalm-119-43

44 I will keep your law continually, for ever and ever; ^psalm-119-44

45 and I shall walk at liberty, for I have sought your precepts. ^psalm-119-45

46 I will also speak of your testimonies before kings, and shall not be put to shame; ^psalm-119-46

47 for I find my delight in your commandments, which I love. ^psalm-119-47

48 I revere your commandments, which I love, and I will meditate on your statutes. ^psalm-119-48

49 Remember your word to your servant, in which you have made me hope. ^psalm-119-49

50 This is my comfort in my affliction that your promise gives me life. ^psalm-119-50

51 Godless men utterly deride me, but I do not turn away from your law. ^psalm-119-51

52 When I think of your ordinances from of old, I take comfort, O LORD. ^psalm-119-52

53 Hot indignation seizes me because of the wicked, who forsake your law. ^psalm-119-53

54 Your statutes have been my songs in the house of my pilgrimage. ^psalm-119-54

55 I remember your name in the night, O LORD, and keep your law. ^psalm-119-55

56 This blessing has fallen to me, that I have kept your precepts. ^psalm-119-56

57 The LORD is my portion; I promise to keep your words. ^psalm-119-57

58 I entreat your favor with all my heart; be gracious to me according to your promise. ^psalm-119-58

59 When I think of your ways, I turn my feet to your testimonies; ^psalm-119-59

60 I hasten and do not delay to keep your commandments. ^psalm-119-60

61 Though the cords of the wicked ensnare me, I do not forget your law. ^psalm-119-61

62 At midnight I rise to praise you, because of your righteous ordinances. ^psalm-119-62

63 I am a companion of all who fear you, of those who keep your precepts. ^psalm-119-63

64 The earth, O LORD, is full of your steadfast love; teach me your statutes! ^psalm-119-64

65 You have dealt well with your servant, O LORD, according to your word. ^psalm-119-65

66 Teach me good judgment and knowledge, for I believe in your commandments. ^psalm-119-66

67 Before I was afflicted I went astray; but now I keep your word. ^psalm-119-67

68 You are good and do good; teach me your statutes. ^psalm-119-68

69 The godless besmear me with lies, but with my whole heart I keep your precepts; ^psalm-119-69

70 their heart is gross like fat, but I delight in your law. ^psalm-119-70

71 It is good for me that I was afflicted, that I might learn your statutes. ^psalm-119-71

72 The law of your mouth is better to me than thousands of gold and silver pieces. ^psalm-119-72

73 Your hands have made and fashioned me; give me understanding that I may learn your commandments. ^psalm-119-73

74 Those who fear you shall see me and rejoice, because I have hoped in your word. ^psalm-119-74

75 I know, O LORD, that your judgments are right, and that in faithfulness you have afflicted me. ^psalm-119-75

76 Let your mercy be ready to comfort me according to your promise to your servant. ^psalm-119-76

77 Let your compassion come to me, that I may live; for your law is my delight. ^psalm-119-77

78 Let the godless be put to shame, because they have subverted me with guile; as for me, I will meditate on your precepts. ^psalm-119-78

79 Let those who fear you turn to me, that they may know your testimonies. ^psalm-119-79

80 May my heart be blameless in your statutes, that I may not be put to shame! ^psalm-119-80

81 My soul languishes for your salvation; I hope in your word. ^psalm-119-81

82 My eyes fail with watching for your promise; I ask, “When will you comfort me?” ^psalm-119-82

83 For I have become like a wineskin in the smoke, yet I have not forgotten your statutes. ^psalm-119-83

84 How long must your servant endure? When will you judge those who persecute me? ^psalm-119-84

85 Godless men have dug pitfalls for me, men who do not conform to your law. ^psalm-119-85

86 All your commandments are sure; they persecute me with falsehood; help me! ^psalm-119-86

87 They have almost made an end of me on earth; but I have not forsaken your precepts. ^psalm-119-87

88 In your mercy spare my life, that I may keep the testimonies of your mouth. ^psalm-119-88

89 For ever, O LORD, your word is firmly fixed in the heavens. ^psalm-119-89

90 Your faithfulness endures to all generations; you have established the earth, and it stands fast. ^psalm-119-90

91 By your appointment they stand this day; for all things are your servants. ^psalm-119-91

92 If your law had not been my delight, I should have perished in my affliction. ^psalm-119-92

93 I will never forget your precepts; for by them you have given me life. ^psalm-119-93

94 I am yours, save me; for I have sought your precepts. ^psalm-119-94

95 The wicked lie in wait to destroy me; but I consider your testimonies. ^psalm-119-95

96 I have seen a limit to all perfection, but your commandment is exceedingly broad. ^psalm-119-96

97 Oh, how I love your law! It is my meditation all the day. ^psalm-119-97

98 Your commandment makes me wiser than my enemies, for it is ever with me. ^psalm-119-98

99 I have more understanding than all my teachers, for your testimonies are my meditation. ^psalm-119-99

100 I understand more than the aged, for I keep your precepts. ^psalm-119-100

101 I hold back my feet from every evil way, in order to keep your word. ^psalm-119-101

102 I do not turn aside from your ordinances, for you have taught me. ^psalm-119-102

103 How sweet are your words to my taste, sweeter than honey to my mouth! ^psalm-119-103

104 Through your precepts I get understanding; therefore I hate every false way. ^psalm-119-104

105 Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path. ^psalm-119-105

106 I have sworn an oath and confirmed it, to observe your righteous ordinances. ^psalm-119-106

107 I am sorely afflicted; give me life, O LORD, according to your word! ^psalm-119-107

108 Accept my offerings of praise, O LORD, and teach me your ordinances. ^psalm-119-108

109 I hold my life in my hand continually, but I do not forget your law. ^psalm-119-109

110 The wicked have laid a snare for me, but I do not stray from your precepts. ^psalm-119-110

111 Your testimonies are my heritage for ever; yes, they are the joy of my heart. ^psalm-119-111

112 I incline my heart to perform your statutes for ever, to the end. ^psalm-119-112

113 I hate double-minded men, but I love your law. ^psalm-119-113

114 You are my hiding place and my shield; I hope in your word. ^psalm-119-114

115 Depart from me, you evildoers, that I may keep the commandments of my God. ^psalm-119-115

116 Uphold me according to your promise, that I may live, and let me not be put to shame in my hope! ^psalm-119-116

117 Hold me up, that I may be safe and have regard for your statutes continually! ^psalm-119-117

118 You spurn all who go astray from your statutes; yes, their cunning is in vain. ^psalm-119-118

119 All the wicked of the earth you count as dross; therefore I love your testimonies. ^psalm-119-119

120 My flesh trembles for fear of you, and I am afraid of your judgments. ^psalm-119-120

121 I have done what is just and right; do not leave me to my oppressors. ^psalm-119-121

122 Be surety for your servant for good; let not the godless oppress me. ^psalm-119-122

123 My eyes fail with watching for your salvation, and for the fulfilment of your righteous promise. ^psalm-119-123

124 Deal with your servant according to your steadfast love, and teach me your statutes. ^psalm-119-124

125 I am your servant; give me understanding, that I may know your testimonies! ^psalm-119-125

126 It is time for the LORD to act, for your law has been broken. ^psalm-119-126

127 Therefore I love your commandments above gold, above fine gold. ^psalm-119-127

128 Therefore I direct my steps by all your precepts; I hate every false way. ^psalm-119-128

129 Your testimonies are wonderful; therefore my soul keeps them. ^psalm-119-129

130 The unfolding of your words gives light; it imparts understanding to the simple. ^psalm-119-130

131 With open mouth I pant, because I long for your commandments. ^psalm-119-131

132 Turn to me and be gracious to me, as you always do toward those who love your name. ^psalm-119-132

133 Keep steady my steps according to your promise, and let no iniquity get dominion over me. ^psalm-119-133

134 Redeem me from man’s oppression, that I may keep your precepts. ^psalm-119-134

135 Make your face shine upon your servant, and teach me your statutes. ^psalm-119-135

136 My eyes shed streams of tears, because men do not keep your law. ^psalm-119-136

137 You are righteous, O LORD, and right are your judgments. ^psalm-119-137

138 You have appointed your testimonies in righteousness and in all faithfulness. ^psalm-119-138

139 My zeal consumes me, because my foes forget your words. ^psalm-119-139

140 Your promise is well tried, and your servant loves it. ^psalm-119-140

141 I am small and despised, yet I do not forget your precepts. ^psalm-119-141

142 Your righteousness is righteous for ever, and your law is true. ^psalm-119-142

143 Trouble and anguish have come upon me, but your commandments are my delight. ^psalm-119-143

144 Your testimonies are righteous for ever; give me understanding that I may live. ^psalm-119-144

145 With my whole heart I cry; answer me, O LORD! I will keep your statutes. ^psalm-119-145

146 I cry to you; save me, that I may observe your testimonies. ^psalm-119-146

147 I rise before dawn and cry for help; I hope in your words. ^psalm-119-147

148 My eyes are awake before the watches of the night, that I may meditate upon your promise. ^psalm-119-148

149 Hear my voice in your steadfast love; O LORD, in your justice preserve my life. ^psalm-119-149

150 They draw near who persecute me with evil purpose; they are far from your law. ^psalm-119-150

151 But you are near, O LORD, and all your commandments are true. ^psalm-119-151

152 Long have I known from your testimonies that you have founded them for ever. ^psalm-119-152

153 Look on my affliction and deliver me, for I do not forget your law. ^psalm-119-153

154 Plead my cause and redeem me; give me life according to your promise! ^psalm-119-154

155 Salvation is far from the wicked, for they do not seek your statutes. ^psalm-119-155

156 Great is your mercy, O LORD; give me life according to your justice. ^psalm-119-156

157 Many are my persecutors and my adversaries, but I do not swerve from your testimonies. ^psalm-119-157

158 I look at the faithless with disgust, because they do not keep your commands. ^psalm-119-158

159 Consider how I love your precepts! Preserve my life according to your steadfast love. ^psalm-119-159

160 The sum of your word is truth; and every one of your righteous ordinances endures for ever. ^psalm-119-160

161 Princes persecute me without cause, but my heart stands in awe of your words. ^psalm-119-161

162 I rejoice at your word like one who finds great spoil. ^psalm-119-162

163 I hate and abhor falsehood, but I love your law. ^psalm-119-163

164 Seven times a day I praise you for your righteous ordinances. ^psalm-119-164

165 Great peace have those who love your law; nothing can make them stumble. ^psalm-119-165

166 I hope for your salvation, O LORD, and I do your commandments. ^psalm-119-166

167 My soul keeps your testimonies; I love them exceedingly. ^psalm-119-167

168 I keep your precepts and testimonies, for all my ways are before you. ^psalm-119-168

169 Let my cry come before you, O LORD; give me understanding according to your word! ^psalm-119-169

170 Let my supplication come before you; deliver me according to your word. ^psalm-119-170

171 My lips will pour forth praise that you teach me your statutes. ^psalm-119-171

172 My tongue will sing of your word, for all your commandments are right. ^psalm-119-172

173 Let your hand be ready to help me, for I have chosen your precepts. ^psalm-119-173

174 I long for your salvation, O LORD, and your law is my delight. ^psalm-119-174

175 Let my soul live, that I may praise you, and let your ordinances help me. ^psalm-119-175

176 I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek your servant, for I do not forget your commandments. ^psalm-119-176

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (119:1) This great hymn of praise for the Torah, the law of God, is typical of the best Jewish piety after the Exile. ^psalm-119-dn-1
