# Psalm 20 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-20|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Victory**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.*

1 The LORD answer you in the day of trouble! The name of the God of Jacob protect you! ^psalm-20-1

2 May he send you help from the sanctuary, and give you support from Zion! ^psalm-20-2

3 May he remember all your offerings, and regard with favor your burnt sacrifices! Selah ^psalm-20-3

4 May he grant you your heart’s desire, and fulfil all your plans! ^psalm-20-4

5 May we shout for joy over your victory, and in the name of our God set up our banners! May the LORD fulfil all your petitions! ^psalm-20-5

6 Now I know that the LORD will help his anointed; he will answer him from his holy heaven with mighty victories by his right hand. ^psalm-20-6

7 Some boast of chariots, and some of horses; but we boast of the name of the LORD our God. ^psalm-20-7

8 They will collapse and fall; but we shall rise and stand upright. ^psalm-20-8

9 Give victory to the king, O LORD; answer us when we call. ^psalm-20-9
