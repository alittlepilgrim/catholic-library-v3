# Psalm 141 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-141|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Preservation from Evil**

1 I call upon you, O LORD; make haste to me! Give ear to my voice, when I call to you! ^psalm-141-1

2 Let my prayer be counted as incense before you, and the lifting up of my hands as an evening sacrifice! ^psalm-141-2

3 Set a guard over my mouth, O LORD, keep watch over the door of my lips! ^psalm-141-3

4 Incline not my heart to any evil, to busy myself with wicked deeds in company with men who work iniquity; and let me not eat of their dainties! ^psalm-141-4

5 Let a good man strike or rebuke me in kindness, but let the oil of the wicked never anoint my head; for my prayer is continually against their wicked deeds. ^psalm-141-5

6 When they are given over to those who shall condemn them, then they shall learn that the word of the LORD is true. ^psalm-141-6

7 As a rock which one cleaves and shatters on the land, so shall their bones be strewn at the mouth of Sheol. ^psalm-141-7

8 But my eyes are toward you, O LORD God; in you I seek refuge; leave me not defenseless! ^psalm-141-8

9 Keep me from the trap which they have laid for me, and from the snares of evildoers! ^psalm-141-9

10 Let the wicked together fall into their own nets, while I escape. ^psalm-141-10
