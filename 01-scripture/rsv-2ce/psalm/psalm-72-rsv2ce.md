# Psalm 72 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-72|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Guidance and Support for the King**

*A Psalm of Solomon.*

1 Give the king your justice, O God, and your righteousness to the royal son! ^psalm-72-1

2 May he judge your people with righteousness, and your poor with justice! ^psalm-72-2

3 Let the mountains bear prosperity for the people, and the hills, in righteousness! ^psalm-72-3

4 May he defend the cause of the poor of the people, give deliverance to the needy, and crush the oppressor! ^psalm-72-4

5 May he live while the sun endures, and as long as the moon, throughout all generations! ^psalm-72-5

6 May he be like rain that falls on the mown grass, like showers that water the earth! ^psalm-72-6

7 In his days may righteousness flourish, and peace abound, till the moon be no more! ^psalm-72-7

8 May he have dominion from sea to sea, and from the River to the ends of the earth! ^psalm-72-8

9 May his foes bow down before him, and his enemies lick the dust! ^psalm-72-9

10 May the kings of Tarshish and of the isles render him tribute, may the kings of Sheba and Seba bring gifts! ^psalm-72-10

11 May all kings fall down before him, all nations serve him! ^psalm-72-11

12 For he delivers the needy when he calls, the poor and him who has no helper. ^psalm-72-12

13 He has pity on the weak and the needy, and saves the lives of the needy. ^psalm-72-13

14 From oppression and violence he redeems their souls; and precious is their blood in his sight. ^psalm-72-14

15 Long may he live, may gold of Sheba be given to him! May prayer be made for him continually, and blessings invoked for him all the day! ^psalm-72-15

16 May there be abundance of grain in the land; on the tops of the mountains may it wave; may its fruit be like Lebanon; and may men blossom forth from the cities like the grass of the field! ^psalm-72-16

17 May his name endure for ever, his fame continue as long as the sun! May men bless themselves by him, all nations call him blessed! ^psalm-72-17

18 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, who alone does wondrous things. ^psalm-72-18

19 Blessed be his glorious name for ever; may his glory fill the whole earth! Amen and Amen! ^psalm-72-19

20 The prayers of David, the son of Jesse, are ended. BOOK III ^psalm-72-20
