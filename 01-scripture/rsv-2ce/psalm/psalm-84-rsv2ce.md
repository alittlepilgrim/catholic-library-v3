# Psalm 84 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-84|NABRE]]

**The Joy of Worship in the Temple**

*To the choirmaster: according to The Gittith. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.*

1 How lovely is your dwelling place, O LORD of hosts! ^psalm-84-1

2 My soul longs, yes, faints for the courts of the LORD; my heart and flesh sing for joy to the living God. ^psalm-84-2

3 Even the sparrow finds a home, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, at your altars, O LORD of hosts, my King and my God. ^psalm-84-3

4 Blessed are those who dwell in your house, ever singing your praise! Selah ^psalm-84-4

5 Blessed are the men whose strength is in you, in whose heart are the highways to Zion. ^psalm-84-5

6 As they go through the valley of Baca they make it a place of springs; the early rain also covers it with pools. ^psalm-84-6

7 They go from strength to strength; the God of gods will be seen in Zion. ^psalm-84-7

8 O LORD God of hosts, hear my prayer; give ear, O God of Jacob! Selah ^psalm-84-8

9 Behold our shield, O God; look upon the face of your anointed! ^psalm-84-9

10 For a day in your courts is better than a thousand elsewhere. I would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God than dwell in the tents of wickedness. ^psalm-84-10

11 For the LORD God is a sun and shield; he bestows favor and honor. No good thing does the LORD withhold from those who walk uprightly. ^psalm-84-11

12 O LORD of hosts, blessed is the man who trusts in you! To the choirmaster. ^psalm-84-12
