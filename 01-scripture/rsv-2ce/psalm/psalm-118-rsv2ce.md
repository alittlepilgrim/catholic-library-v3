# Psalm 118 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-118|NABRE]]

**A Song of Victory**

1 O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; his mercy endures for ever! ^psalm-118-1

2 Let Israel say, “His mercy endures for ever.” ^psalm-118-2

3 Let the house of Aaron say, “His mercy endures for ever.” ^psalm-118-3

4 Let those who fear the LORD say, “His mercy endures for ever.” ^psalm-118-4

5 Out of my distress I called on the LORD; the LORD answered me and set me free. ^psalm-118-5

6 With the LORD on my side I do not fear. What can man do to me? ^psalm-118-6

7 The LORD is on my side to help me; I shall look in triumph on those who hate me. ^psalm-118-7

8 It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to put confidence in man. ^psalm-118-8

9 It is better to take refuge in the LORD than to put confidence in princes. ^psalm-118-9

10 All nations surrounded me; in the name of the LORD I cut them off! ^psalm-118-10

11 They surrounded me, surrounded me on every side; in the name of the LORD I cut them off! ^psalm-118-11

12 They surrounded me like bees, they blazed like a fire of thorns; in the name of the LORD I cut them off! ^psalm-118-12

13 I was pushed hard, so that I was falling, but the LORD helped me. ^psalm-118-13

14 The LORD is my strength and my song; he has become my salvation. ^psalm-118-14

15 Listen, glad songs of victory in the tents of the righteous: “The right hand of the LORD does valiantly! ^psalm-118-15

16 The right hand of the LORD is exalted; the right hand of the LORD does valiantly!” ^psalm-118-16

17 I shall not die, but I shall live, and recount the deeds of the LORD. ^psalm-118-17

18 The LORD has chastened me sorely, but he has not given me over to death. ^psalm-118-18

19 Open to me the gates of righteousness, that I may enter through them and give thanks to the LORD. ^psalm-118-19

20 This is the gate of the LORD; the righteous shall enter through it. ^psalm-118-20

21 I thank you that you have answered me and have become my salvation. ^psalm-118-21

22 The stone which the builders rejected has become the cornerstone. ^psalm-118-22

23 This is the LORD’s doing; it is marvelous in our eyes. ^psalm-118-23

24 This is the day which the LORD has made; let us rejoice and be glad in it. ^psalm-118-24

25 Save us, we beg you, O LORD! O LORD, we beg you, give us success! ^psalm-118-25

26 Blessed be he who enters in the name of the LORD! We bless you from the house of the LORD. ^psalm-118-26

27 The LORD is God, and he has given us light. Bind the festal procession with branches, up to the horns of the altar! ^psalm-118-27

28 You are my God, and I will give thanks to you; you are my God, I will extol you. ^psalm-118-28

29 O give thanks to the LORD, for he is good; for his mercy endures for ever! ^psalm-118-29
