# Psalm 66 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-66|NABRE]]

**Praise for God’s Goodness to Israel**

1 Make a joyful noise to God, all the earth; ^psalm-66-1

2 sing the glory of his name; give to him glorious praise! ^psalm-66-2

3 Say to God, “How awesome are your deeds! So great is your power that your enemies cringe before you. ^psalm-66-3

4 All the earth worships you; they sing praises to you, sing praises to your name.” Selah ^psalm-66-4

5 Come and see what God has done: he is awesome in his deeds among men. ^psalm-66-5

6 He turned the sea into dry land; men passed through the river on foot. There did we rejoice in him, ^psalm-66-6

7 who rules by his might for ever, whose eyes keep watch on the nations— let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah ^psalm-66-7

8 Bless our God, O peoples, let the sound of his praise be heard, ^psalm-66-8

9 who has kept us among the living, and has not let our feet slip. ^psalm-66-9

10 For you, O God, have tested us; you have tried us as silver is tried. ^psalm-66-10

11 You brought us into the net; you laid affliction on our backs; ^psalm-66-11

12 you let men ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water; yet you have brought us forth to a spacious place. ^psalm-66-12

13 I will come into your house with burnt offerings; I will pay you my vows, ^psalm-66-13

14 that which my lips uttered and my mouth promised when I was in trouble. ^psalm-66-14

15 I will offer to you burnt offerings of fatlings, with the smoke of the sacrifice of rams; I will make an offering of bulls and goats. Selah ^psalm-66-15

16 Come and hear, all you who fear God, and I will tell what he has done for me. ^psalm-66-16

17 I cried aloud to him, and he was extolled with my tongue. ^psalm-66-17

18 If I had cherished iniquity in my heart, the Lord would not have listened. ^psalm-66-18

19 But truly God has listened; he has given heed to the voice of my prayer. ^psalm-66-19

20 Blessed be God, because he has not rejected my prayer or removed his merciful love from me! ^psalm-66-20
