# Psalm 121 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-121|NABRE]]

**Assurance of God’s Protection**

*A Song of Ascents.*

1 I lift up my eyes to the hills. From where does my help come? ^psalm-121-1

2 My help comes from the LORD, who made heaven and earth. ^psalm-121-2

3 He will not let your foot be moved, he who keeps you will not slumber. ^psalm-121-3

4 Behold, he who keeps Israel will neither slumber nor sleep. ^psalm-121-4

5 The LORD is your keeper; the LORD is your shade on your right hand. ^psalm-121-5

6 The sun shall not strike you by day, nor the moon by night. ^psalm-121-6

7 The LORD will keep you from all evil; he will keep your life. ^psalm-121-7

8 The LORD will keep your going out and your coming in from this time forth and for evermore. A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-121-8
