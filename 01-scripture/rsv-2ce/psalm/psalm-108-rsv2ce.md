# Psalm 108 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-108|NABRE]]

**Praise and Prayer for Victory**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast! I will sing and make melody! Awake, my soul! ^psalm-108-1

2 Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awake the dawn! ^psalm-108-2

3 I will give thanks to you, O LORD, among the peoples, I will sing praises to you among the nations. ^psalm-108-3

4 For your steadfast love is great above the heavens, your faithfulness reaches to the clouds. ^psalm-108-4

5 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens! Let your glory be over all the earth! ^psalm-108-5

6 That your beloved may be delivered, give help by your right hand, and answer me! ^psalm-108-6

7 God has promised in his sanctuary: “With exultation I will divide up She'chem, and portion out the Vale of Succoth. ^psalm-108-7

8 Gilead is mine; Manas'seh is mine; E'phraim is my helmet; Judah my scepter. ^psalm-108-8

9 Moab is my washbasin; upon Edom I cast my shoe; over Philistia I shout in triumph.” ^psalm-108-9

10 Who will bring me to the fortified city? Who will lead me to Edom? ^psalm-108-10

11 Have you not rejected us, O God? You do not go forth, O God, with our armies. ^psalm-108-11

12 O grant us help against the foe, for vain is the help of man! ^psalm-108-12

13 With God we shall do valiantly; it is he who will tread down our foes. ^psalm-108-13
