# Psalm 64 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-64|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Protection from Enemies**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Hear my voice, O God, in my complaint; preserve my life from dread of the enemy, ^psalm-64-1

2 hide me from the secret plots of the wicked, from the scheming of evildoers, ^psalm-64-2

3 who whet their tongues like swords, who aim bitter words like arrows, ^psalm-64-3

4 shooting from ambush at the blameless, shooting at him suddenly and without fear. ^psalm-64-4

5 They hold fast to their evil purpose; they talk of laying snares secretly, thinking, “Who can see us? ^psalm-64-5

6 Who can search out our crimes? We have thought out a cunningly conceived plot.” For the inward mind and heart of a man are deep! ^psalm-64-6

7 But God will shoot his arrow at them; they will be wounded suddenly. ^psalm-64-7

8 Because of their tongue he will bring them to ruin; all who see them will wag their heads. ^psalm-64-8

9 Then all men will fear; they will tell what God has wrought, and ponder what he has done. ^psalm-64-9

10 Let the righteous rejoice in the LORD, and take refuge in him! Let all the upright in heart glory! ^psalm-64-10
