# Psalm 125 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-125|NABRE]]

**The Security of God’s People**

*A Song of Ascents.*

1 Those who trust in the LORD are like Mount Zion, which cannot be moved, but abides for ever. ^psalm-125-1

2 As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the LORD is round about his people, from this time forth and for evermore. ^psalm-125-2

3 For the scepter of wickedness shall not rest upon the land allotted to the righteous, lest the righteous put forth their hands to do wrong. ^psalm-125-3

4 Do good, O LORD, to those who are good, and to those who are upright in their hearts! ^psalm-125-4

5 But those who turn aside upon their crooked ways the LORD will lead away with evildoers! Peace be in Israel! ^psalm-125-5
