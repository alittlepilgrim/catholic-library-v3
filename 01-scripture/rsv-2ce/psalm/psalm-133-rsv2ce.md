# Psalm 133 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-133|NABRE]]

**The Blessedness of Unity**

1 Behold, how good and pleasant it is when brothers dwell in unity! ^psalm-133-1

2 It is like the precious oil upon the head, running down upon the beard, upon the beard of Aaron, running down on the collar of his robes! ^psalm-133-2

3 It is like the dew of Hermon, which falls on the mountains of Zion! For there the LORD has commanded the blessing, life for evermore. ^psalm-133-3
