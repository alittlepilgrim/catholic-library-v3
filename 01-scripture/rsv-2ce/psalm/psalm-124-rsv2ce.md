# Psalm 124 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-124|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Israel’s Deliverance**

*Of David.*

1 If it had not been the LORD who was on our side, let Israel now say— ^psalm-124-1

2 if it had not been the LORD who was on our side, when men rose up against us, ^psalm-124-2

3 then they would have swallowed us up alive, when their anger was kindled against us; ^psalm-124-3

4 then the flood would have swept us away, the torrent would have gone over us; ^psalm-124-4

5 then over us would have gone the raging waters. ^psalm-124-5

6 Blessed be the LORD, who has not given us as prey to their teeth! ^psalm-124-6

7 We have escaped as a bird from the snare of the fowlers; the snare is broken, and we have escaped! ^psalm-124-7

8 Our help is in the name of the LORD, who made heaven and earth. ^psalm-124-8
