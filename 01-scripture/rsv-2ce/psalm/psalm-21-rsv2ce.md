# Psalm 21 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-21|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Victory**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.*

1 In your strength the king rejoices, O LORD; and in your help how greatly he exults! ^psalm-21-1

2 You have given him his heart’s desire, and have not withheld the request of his lips. Selah ^psalm-21-2

3 For you meet him with goodly blessings; you set a crown of fine gold upon his head. ^psalm-21-3

4 He asked life of you; you gave it to him, length of days for ever and ever. ^psalm-21-4

5 His glory is great through your help; splendor and majesty you bestow upon him. ^psalm-21-5

6 Yes, you make him most blessed for ever; you make him glad with the joy of your face. ^psalm-21-6

7 For the king trusts in the LORD; and through the steadfast love of the Most High he shall not be moved. ^psalm-21-7

8 Your hand will find out all your enemies; your right hand will find out those who hate you. ^psalm-21-8

9 You will make them as a blazing oven when you appear. The LORD will swallow them up in his wrath; and fire will consume them. ^psalm-21-9

10 You will destroy their offspring from the earth, and their children from among the sons of men. ^psalm-21-10

11 If they plan evil against you, if they devise mischief, they will not succeed. ^psalm-21-11

12 For you will put them to flight; you will aim at their faces with your bows. ^psalm-21-12

13 Be exalted, O LORD, in your strength! We will sing and praise your power. ^psalm-21-13
