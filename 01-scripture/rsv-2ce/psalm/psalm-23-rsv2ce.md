# Psalm 23 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-23|NABRE]]

**The Divine Shepherd**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 The LORD is my shepherd, I shall not want; ^psalm-23-1

2 he makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside still waters; ^psalm-23-2

3 he restores my soul. He leads me in paths of righteousness for his name’s sake. ^psalm-23-3

4 Even though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I fear no evil; for you are with me; your rod and your staff, they comfort me. ^psalm-23-4

5 You prepare a table before me in the presence of my enemies; you anoint my head with oil, my cup overflows. ^psalm-23-5

6 Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life; and I shall dwell in the house of the LORD for ever. ^psalm-23-6
