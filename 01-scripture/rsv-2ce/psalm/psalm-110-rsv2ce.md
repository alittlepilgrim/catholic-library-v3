# Psalm 110 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-110|NABRE]]

**Assurance of Victory for God’s Priest-King**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 The LORD says to my lord: “Sit at my right hand, till I make your enemies your footstool.” ^psalm-110-1

2 The LORD sends forth from Zion your mighty scepter. Rule in the midst of your foes! ^psalm-110-2

3 Yours is dominion on the day you lead your host in holy splendor. From the womb of the morning I begot you. ^psalm-110-3

4 The LORD has sworn and will not change his mind, “You are a priest for ever according to the order of Melchiz'edek.” ^psalm-110-4

5 The Lord is at your right hand; he will shatter kings on the day of his wrath. ^psalm-110-5

6 He will execute judgment among the nations, filling them with corpses; he will shatter chiefs over the wide earth. ^psalm-110-6

7 He will drink from the brook by the way; therefore he will lift up his head. ^psalm-110-7
