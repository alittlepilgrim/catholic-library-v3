# Psalm 131 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-131|NABRE]]

**Song of Quiet Trust**

*Of David.*

1 O LORD, my heart is not lifted up, my eyes are not raised too high; I do not occupy myself with things too great and too marvelous for me. ^psalm-131-1

2 But I have calmed and quieted my soul, like a child quieted at its mother’s breast; like a child that is quieted is my soul. ^psalm-131-2

3 O Israel, hope in the LORD from this time forth and for evermore. A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-131-3
