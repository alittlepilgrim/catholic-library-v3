# Psalm 16 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-16|NABRE]]

**Song of Trust and Security in God**

*A Miktam of David.*

1 Preserve me, O God, for in you I take refuge. ^psalm-16-1

2 I say to the Lord, “You are my Lord; I have no good apart from you.” ^psalm-16-2

3 As for the saints in the land, they are the noble, in whom is all my delight. ^psalm-16-3

4 Those who choose another god multiply their sorrows; their libations of blood I will not pour out or take their names upon my lips. ^psalm-16-4

5 The LORD is my chosen portion and my cup; you hold my lot. ^psalm-16-5

6 The lines have fallen for me in pleasant places; yes, I have a goodly heritage. ^psalm-16-6

7 I bless the LORD who gives me counsel; in the night also my heart instructs me. ^psalm-16-7

8 I keep the LORD always before me; because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved. ^psalm-16-8

9 Therefore my heart is glad, and my soul rejoices; my body also dwells secure. ^psalm-16-9

10 For you do not give me up to Sheol, or let your godly one see the Pit. ^psalm-16-10

11 You show me the path of life; in your presence there is fulness of joy, in your right hand are pleasures for evermore. ^psalm-16-11
