# Psalm 38 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-38|NABRE]]

**A Penitent Sufferer’s Plea for Healing**

*A Psalm of David, for the memorial offering.*

1 O LORD, rebuke me not in your anger, nor chasten me in your wrath! ^psalm-38-1

2 For your arrows have sunk into me, and your hand has come down on me. ^psalm-38-2

3 There is no soundness in my flesh because of your indignation; there is no health in my bones because of my sin. ^psalm-38-3

4 For my iniquities have gone over my head; they weigh like a burden too heavy for me. ^psalm-38-4

5 My wounds grow foul and fester because of my foolishness, ^psalm-38-5

6 I am utterly bowed down and prostrate; all the day I go about mourning. ^psalm-38-6

7 For my loins are filled with burning, and there is no soundness in my flesh. ^psalm-38-7

8 I am utterly spent and crushed; I groan because of the tumult of my heart. ^psalm-38-8

9 Lord, all my longing is known to you, my sighing is not hidden from you. ^psalm-38-9

10 My heart throbs, my strength fails me; and the light of my eyes—it also has gone from me. ^psalm-38-10

11 My friends and companions stand aloof from my plague, and my kinsmen stand afar off. ^psalm-38-11

12 Those who seek my life lay their snares, those who seek my hurt speak of ruin, and meditate treachery all the day long. ^psalm-38-12

13 But I am like a deaf man, I do not hear, like a mute man who does not open his mouth. ^psalm-38-13

14 Yes, I am like a man who does not hear, and in whose mouth are no rebukes. ^psalm-38-14

15 But for you, O LORD, do I wait; it is you, O LORD my God, who will answer. ^psalm-38-15

16 For I pray, “Only let them not rejoice over me, who boast against me when my foot slips!” ^psalm-38-16

17 For I am ready to fall, and my pain is ever with me. ^psalm-38-17

18 I confess my iniquity, I am sorry for my sin. ^psalm-38-18

19 Those who are my foes without cause are mighty, and many are those who hate me wrongfully. ^psalm-38-19

20 Those who render me evil for good are my adversaries because I follow after good. ^psalm-38-20

21 Do not forsake me, O LORD! O my God, be not far from me! ^psalm-38-21

22 Make haste to help me, O Lord, my salvation! ^psalm-38-22
