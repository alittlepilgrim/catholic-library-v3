# Psalm 41 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-41|NABRE]]

**Assurance of God’s Help; and a Plea for Healing**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.*

1 Blessed is he who considers the poor! The LORD delivers him in the day of trouble; ^psalm-41-1

2 the LORD protects him and keeps him alive; he is called blessed in the land; you do not give him up to the will of his enemies. ^psalm-41-2

3 The LORD sustains him on his sickbed; in his illness you heal all his infirmities. ^psalm-41-3

4 As for me, I said, “O LORD, be gracious to me; heal me, for I have sinned against you!” ^psalm-41-4

5 My enemies say of me in malice: “When will he die, and his name perish?” ^psalm-41-5

6 And when one comes to see me, he utters empty words, while his heart gathers mischief; when he goes out, he tells it abroad. ^psalm-41-6

7 All who hate me whisper together about me; they imagine the worst for me. ^psalm-41-7

8 They say, “A deadly thing has fastened upon him; he will not rise again from where he lies.” ^psalm-41-8

9 Even my bosom friend in whom I trusted, who ate of my bread, has lifted his heel against me. ^psalm-41-9

10 But you, O LORD, be gracious to me, and raise me up, that I may repay them! ^psalm-41-10

11 By this I know that you are pleased with me, in that my enemy has not triumphed over me. ^psalm-41-11

12 But you have upheld me because of my integrity, and set me in your presence for ever. ^psalm-41-12

13 Blessed be the LORD, the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting! Amen and Amen. BOOK II ^psalm-41-13
