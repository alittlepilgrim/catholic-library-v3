# Psalm 79 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-79|NABRE]]

**Plea for Mercy for Jerusalem**

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

1 O God, the heathen have come into your inheritance; they have defiled your holy temple; they have laid Jerusalem in ruins. ^psalm-79-1

2 They have given the bodies of your servants to the birds of the air for food, the flesh of your saints to the beasts of the earth. ^psalm-79-2

3 They have poured out their blood like water round about Jerusalem, and there was none to bury them. ^psalm-79-3

4 We have become a taunt to our neighbors, mocked and derided by those round about us. ^psalm-79-4

5 How long, O LORD? Will you be angry for ever? Will your jealous wrath burn like fire? ^psalm-79-5

6 Pour out your anger on the nations that do not know you, and on the kingdoms that do not call on your name! ^psalm-79-6

7 For they have devoured Jacob, and laid waste his habitation. ^psalm-79-7

8 Do not remember against us the iniquities of our forefathers; let your compassion come speedily to meet us, for we are brought very low. ^psalm-79-8

9 Help us, O God of our salvation, for the glory of your name; deliver us, and forgive our sins, for your name’s sake! ^psalm-79-9

10 Why should the nations say, “Where is their God?” Let the avenging of the outpoured blood of your servants be known among the nations before our eyes! ^psalm-79-10

11 Let the groans of the prisoners come before you; according to your great power preserve those doomed to die! ^psalm-79-11

12 Return sevenfold into the bosom of our neighbors the taunts with which they have taunted you, O Lord! ^psalm-79-12

13 Then we your people, the flock of your pasture, will give thanks to you for ever; from generation to generation we will recount your praise. ^psalm-79-13
