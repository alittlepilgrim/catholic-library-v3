# Psalm 27 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-27|NABRE]]

**Triumphant Song of Confidence**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 The LORD is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? The LORD is the stronghold of my life; of whom shall I be afraid? ^psalm-27-1

2 When evildoers assail me, to devour my flesh, my adversaries and foes, they shall stumble and fall. ^psalm-27-2

3 Though a host encamp against me, my heart shall not fear; though war arise against me, yet I will be confident. ^psalm-27-3

4 One thing have I asked of the LORD, that will I seek after; that I may dwell in the house of the LORD all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the LORD, and to inquire in his temple. ^psalm-27-4

5 For he will hide me in his shelter in the day of trouble; he will conceal me under the cover of his tent, he will set me high upon a rock. ^psalm-27-5

6 And now my head shall be lifted up above my enemies round about me; and I will offer in his tent sacrifices with shouts of joy; I will sing and make melody to the LORD. ^psalm-27-6

7 Hear, O LORD, when I cry aloud, be gracious to me and answer me! ^psalm-27-7

8 You have said, “Seek my face.” My heart says to you, “Your face, LORD, do I seek.” ^psalm-27-8

9 Hide not your face from me. Turn not your servant away in anger, you who have been my help. Cast me not off, forsake me not, O God of my salvation! ^psalm-27-9

10 For my father and my mother have forsaken me, but the LORD will take me up. ^psalm-27-10

11 Teach me your way, O LORD; and lead me on a level path because of my enemies. ^psalm-27-11

12 Give me not up to the will of my adversaries; for false witnesses have risen against me, and they breathe out violence. ^psalm-27-12

13 I believe that I shall see the goodness of the LORD in the land of the living! ^psalm-27-13

14 Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage; yes, wait for the LORD! A Psalm of David. ^psalm-27-14
