# Psalm 48 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-48|NABRE]]

**The Glory and Strength of Zion**

*A Song. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.*

1 Great is the LORD and greatly to be praised in the city of our God! His holy mountain, ^psalm-48-1

2 beautiful in elevation, is the joy of all the earth, Mount Zion, in the far north, the city of the great King. ^psalm-48-2

3 Within her citadels God has shown himself a sure defense. ^psalm-48-3

4 For behold, the kings assembled, they came on together. ^psalm-48-4

5 As soon as they saw it, they were astounded, they were in panic, they took to flight; ^psalm-48-5

6 trembling took hold of them there, anguish as of a woman with labor pains. ^psalm-48-6

7 By the east wind you shattered the ships of Tarshish. ^psalm-48-7

8 As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God, which God establishes for ever. Selah ^psalm-48-8

9 We have thought on your mercy, O God, in the midst of your temple. ^psalm-48-9

10 As your name, O God, so your praise reaches to the ends of the earth. Your right hand is filled with victory; ^psalm-48-10

11 let Mount Zion be glad! Let the daughters of Judah rejoice because of your judgments! ^psalm-48-11

12 Walk about Zion, go round about her, number her towers, ^psalm-48-12

13 consider well her ramparts, go through her citadels; that you may tell the next generation ^psalm-48-13

14 that this is God, our God for ever and ever. He will be our guide for ever. To the choirmaster. ^psalm-48-14
