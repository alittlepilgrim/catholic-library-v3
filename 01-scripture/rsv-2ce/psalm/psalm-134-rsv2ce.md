# Psalm 134 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-134|NABRE]]

**Praise in the Night**

*A Song of Ascents.*

1 Come, bless the LORD, all you servants of the LORD, who stand by night in the house of the LORD! ^psalm-134-1

2 Lift up your hands to the holy place, and bless the LORD! ^psalm-134-2

3 May the LORD bless you from Zion, he who made heaven and earth! ^psalm-134-3
