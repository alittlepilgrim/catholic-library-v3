# Psalm 130 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-130|NABRE]]

**Waiting for Divine Redemption**

1 Out of the depths I cry to you, O LORD! ^psalm-130-1

2 Lord, hear my voice! Let your ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications! ^psalm-130-2

3 If you, O LORD, should mark iniquities, Lord, who could stand? ^psalm-130-3

4 But there is forgiveness with you, that you may be feared. ^psalm-130-4

5 I wait for the LORD, my soul waits, and in his word I hope; ^psalm-130-5

6 my soul waits for the LORD more than watchmen for the morning, more than watchmen for the morning. ^psalm-130-6

7 O Israel, hope in the LORD! For with the LORD there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption. ^psalm-130-7

8 And he will redeem Israel from all his iniquities. A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-130-8
