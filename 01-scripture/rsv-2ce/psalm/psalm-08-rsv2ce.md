# Psalm 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-08|NABRE]]

**Divine Majesty and Human Dignity**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth! You whose glory above the heavens is chanted ^psalm-08-1

2 by the mouth of babies and infants, you have founded a bulwark because of your foes, to still the enemy and the avenger. ^psalm-08-2

3 When I look at your heavens, the work of your fingers, the moon and the stars which you have established; ^psalm-08-3

4 what is man that you are mindful of him, and the son of man that you care for him? ^psalm-08-4

5 Yet you have made him little less than the angels, and you have crowned him with glory and honor. ^psalm-08-5

6 You have given him dominion over the works of your hands; you have put all things under his feet, ^psalm-08-6

7 all sheep and oxen, and also the beasts of the field, ^psalm-08-7

8 the birds of the air, and the fish of the sea, whatever passes along the paths of the sea. ^psalm-08-8

9 O LORD, our Lord, how majestic is your name in all the earth! ^psalm-08-9

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (8:1) A magnificent hymn to the glory of God as seen in creation and reflected in man. ^psalm-08-dn-1
