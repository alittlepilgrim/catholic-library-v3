# Psalm 50 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-50|NABRE]]

**The Acceptable Sacrifice**

*A Psalm of Asaph.*

1 The Mighty One, God the LORD, speaks and summons the earth from the rising of the sun to its setting. ^psalm-50-1

2 Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God shines forth. ^psalm-50-2

3 Our God comes, he does not keep silence, before him is a devouring fire, round about him a mighty tempest. ^psalm-50-3

4 He calls to the heavens above and to the earth, that he may judge his people: ^psalm-50-4

5 “Gather to me my faithful ones, who made a covenant with me by sacrifice!” ^psalm-50-5

6 The heavens declare his righteousness, for God himself is judge! Selah ^psalm-50-6

7 “Hear, O my people, and I will speak, O Israel, I will testify against you. I am God, your God. ^psalm-50-7

8 I do not reprove you for your sacrifices; your burnt offerings are continually before me. ^psalm-50-8

9 I will accept no bull from your house, nor he-goat from your folds. ^psalm-50-9

10 For every beast of the forest is mine, the cattle on a thousand hills. ^psalm-50-10

11 I know all the birds of the air, and all that moves in the field is mine. ^psalm-50-11

12 “If I were hungry, I would not tell you; for the world and all that is in it is mine. ^psalm-50-12

13 Do I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats? ^psalm-50-13

14 Offer to God a sacrifice of thanksgiving, and pay your vows to the Most High; ^psalm-50-14

15 and call upon me in the day of trouble; I will deliver you, and you shall glorify me.” ^psalm-50-15

16 But to the wicked God says: “What right have you to recite my statutes, or take my covenant on your lips? ^psalm-50-16

17 For you hate discipline, and you cast my words behind you. ^psalm-50-17

18 If you see a thief, you are a friend of his; and you keep company with adulterers. ^psalm-50-18

19 “You give your mouth free rein for evil, and your tongue frames deceit. ^psalm-50-19

20 You sit and speak against your brother; you slander your own mother’s son. ^psalm-50-20

21 These things you have done and I have been silent; you thought that I was one like yourself. But now I rebuke you, and lay the charge before you. ^psalm-50-21

22 “Mark this, then, you who forget God, lest I tear, and there be none to deliver! ^psalm-50-22

23 He who brings thanksgiving as his sacrifice honors me; to him who orders his way aright I will show the salvation of God!” ^psalm-50-23
