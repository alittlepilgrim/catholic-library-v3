# Psalm 97 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-97|NABRE]]

**The Glory of God’s Reign**

1 The LORD reigns; let the earth rejoice; let the many islands be glad! ^psalm-97-1

2 Clouds and thick darkness are round about him; righteousness and justice are the foundation of his throne. ^psalm-97-2

3 Fire goes before him, and burns up his adversaries round about. ^psalm-97-3

4 His lightnings lighten the world; the earth sees and trembles. ^psalm-97-4

5 The mountains melt like wax before the LORD, before the Lord of all the earth. ^psalm-97-5

6 The heavens proclaim his righteousness; and all the peoples behold his glory. ^psalm-97-6

7 All worshipers of images are put to shame, who make their boast in worthless idols; let all his angels bow down before him. ^psalm-97-7

8 Zion hears and is glad, and the daughters of Judah rejoice, because of your judgments, O God. ^psalm-97-8

9 For you, O LORD, are most high over all the earth; you are exalted far above all gods. ^psalm-97-9

10 The LORD loves those who hate evil; he preserves the lives of his saints; he delivers them from the hand of the wicked. ^psalm-97-10

11 Light dawns for the righteous, and joy for the upright in heart. ^psalm-97-11

12 Rejoice in the LORD, O you righteous, and give thanks to his holy name! ^psalm-97-12
