# Psalm 111 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-111|NABRE]]

**Praise for God’s Wonderful Works**

1 Praise the LORD! I will give thanks to the LORD with my whole heart, in the company of the upright, in the congregation. ^psalm-111-1

2 Great are the works of the LORD, studied by all who have pleasure in them. ^psalm-111-2

3 Full of honor and majesty is his work, and his righteousness endures for ever. ^psalm-111-3

4 He has caused his wonderful works to be remembered; the LORD is gracious and merciful. ^psalm-111-4

5 He provides food for those who fear him; he is ever mindful of his covenant. ^psalm-111-5

6 He has shown his people the power of his works, in giving them the heritage of the nations. ^psalm-111-6

7 The works of his hands are faithful and just; all his precepts are trustworthy, ^psalm-111-7

8 they are established for ever and ever, to be performed with faithfulness and uprightness. ^psalm-111-8

9 He sent redemption to his people; he has commanded his covenant for ever. Holy and awesome is his name! ^psalm-111-9

10 The fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom; a good understanding have all those who practice it. His praise endures for ever! ^psalm-111-10
