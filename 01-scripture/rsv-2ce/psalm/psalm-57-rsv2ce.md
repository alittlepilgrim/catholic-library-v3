# Psalm 57 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-57|NABRE]]

**Praise and Assurance under Persecution**

*To the choirmaster: according to Do Not Destroy. A Miktam of David, when he fled from Saul, in the cave.*

1 Be merciful to me, O God, be merciful to me, for in you my soul takes refuge; in the shadow of your wings I will take refuge, till the storms of destruction pass by. ^psalm-57-1

2 I cry to God Most High, to God who fulfils his purpose for me. ^psalm-57-2

3 He will send from heaven and save me, he will put to shame those who trample upon me. Selah God will send forth his mercy and his faithfulness! ^psalm-57-3

4 I lie in the midst of lions that greedily devour the sons of men; their teeth are spears and arrows, their tongues sharp swords. ^psalm-57-4

5 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens! Let your glory be over all the earth! ^psalm-57-5

6 They set a net for my steps; my soul was bowed down. They dug a pit in my way, but they have fallen into it themselves. Selah ^psalm-57-6

7 My heart is steadfast, O God, my heart is steadfast! I will sing and make melody! ^psalm-57-7

8 Awake, my soul! Awake, O harp and lyre! I will awake the dawn! ^psalm-57-8

9 I will give thanks to you, O Lord, among the peoples; I will sing praises to you among the nations. ^psalm-57-9

10 For your mercy is great to the heavens, your faithfulness to the clouds. ^psalm-57-10

11 Be exalted, O God, above the heavens! Let your glory be over all the earth! ^psalm-57-11
