# Psalm 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-04|NABRE]]

**Confident Plea for Deliverance from Enemies**

*To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Psalm of David.*

1 Answer me when I call, O God of my right! You have given me room when I was in distress. Be gracious to me, and hear my prayer. ^psalm-04-1

2 O sons of men, how long will you be dull of heart? How long will you love vain words, and seek after lies? Selah ^psalm-04-2

3 But know that the LORD has set apart the godly for himself; the LORD hears when I call to him. ^psalm-04-3

4 Be angry, but sin not; commune with your own hearts on your beds, and be silent. Selah ^psalm-04-4

5 Offer right sacrifices, and put your trust in the LORD. ^psalm-04-5

6 There are many who say, “O that we might see some good! Lift up the light of your countenance upon us, O LORD!” ^psalm-04-6

7 You have put more joy in my heart than they have when their grain and wine abound. ^psalm-04-7

8 In peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety. ^psalm-04-8
