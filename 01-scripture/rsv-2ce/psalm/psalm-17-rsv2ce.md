# Psalm 17 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-17|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Persecutors**

*A Prayer of David.*

1 Hear a just cause, O LORD; attend to my cry! Give ear to my prayer from lips free of deceit! ^psalm-17-1

2 From you let my vindication come! Let your eyes see the right! ^psalm-17-2

3 If you try my heart, if you visit me by night, if you test me, you will find no wickedness in me; my mouth does not transgress. ^psalm-17-3

4 With regard to the works of men, by the word of your lips I have avoided the ways of the violent. ^psalm-17-4

5 My steps have held fast to your paths, my feet have not slipped. ^psalm-17-5

6 I call upon you, for you will answer me, O God; incline your ear to me, hear my words. ^psalm-17-6

7 Wondrously show your mercies, O savior of those who seek refuge from their adversaries at your right hand. ^psalm-17-7

8 Keep me as the apple of the eye; hide me in the shadow of your wings, ^psalm-17-8

9 from the wicked who despoil me, my deadly enemies who surround me. ^psalm-17-9

10 They close their hearts to pity; with their mouths they speak arrogantly. ^psalm-17-10

11 They track me down; now they surround me; they set their eyes to cast me to the ground. ^psalm-17-11

12 They are like a lion eager to tear, as a young lion lurking in ambush. ^psalm-17-12

13 Arise, O LORD! confront them, overthrow them! Deliver my life from the wicked by your sword, ^psalm-17-13

14 from men by your hand, O LORD, from men whose portion in life is of the world. May their belly be filled with what you have stored up for them; may their children have more than enough; may they leave something over to their babies. ^psalm-17-14

15 As for me, I shall behold your face in righteousness; when I awake, I shall be satisfied with beholding your form. ^psalm-17-15
