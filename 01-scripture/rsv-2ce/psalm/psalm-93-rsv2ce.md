# Psalm 93 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-93|NABRE]]

**The Majesty of God’s Rule**

1 The LORD reigns; he is robed in majesty; the LORD is robed, he is girded with strength. Yes, the world is established; it shall never be moved; ^psalm-93-1

2 your throne is established from of old; you are from everlasting. ^psalm-93-2

3 The floods have lifted up, O LORD, the floods have lifted up their voice, the floods lift up their roaring. ^psalm-93-3

4 Mightier than the thunders of many waters, mightier than the waves of the sea, the LORD on high is mighty! ^psalm-93-4

5 Your decrees are very sure; holiness befits your house, O LORD, for evermore. ^psalm-93-5

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (93:1) The first of several psalms which represent Yahweh as enthroned in heaven. ^psalm-93-dn-1
