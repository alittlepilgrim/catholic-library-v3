# Psalm 47 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-47|NABRE]]

**God’s Rule over the Nations**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.*

1 Clap your hands, all peoples! Shout to God with loud songs of joy! ^psalm-47-1

2 For the LORD, the Most High, is awesome, a great king over all the earth. ^psalm-47-2

3 He subdued peoples under us, and nations under our feet. ^psalm-47-3

4 He chose our heritage for us, the pride of Jacob whom he loves. Selah ^psalm-47-4

5 God has gone up with a shout, the LORD with the sound of a trumpet. ^psalm-47-5

6 Sing praises to God, sing praises! Sing praises to our King, sing praises! ^psalm-47-6

7 For God is the king of all the earth; sing praises with a psalm! ^psalm-47-7

8 God reigns over the nations; God sits on his holy throne. ^psalm-47-8

9 The princes of the peoples gather as the people of the God of Abraham. For the shields of the earth belong to God; he is highly exalted! ^psalm-47-9
