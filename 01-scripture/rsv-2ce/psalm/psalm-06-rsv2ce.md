# Psalm 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-06|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Recovery from Grave Illness**

*To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments; according to The Sheminith. A Psalm of David.*

1 O LORD, rebuke me not in your anger, nor chasten me in your wrath. ^psalm-06-1

2 Have mercy on me, O LORD, for I am languishing; O LORD, heal me, for my bones are troubled. ^psalm-06-2

3 My soul also is sorely troubled. But you, O LORD—how long? ^psalm-06-3

4 Turn, O Lord, save my life; deliver me for the sake of your merciful love. ^psalm-06-4

5 For in death there is no remembrance of you; in Sheol who can give you praise? ^psalm-06-5

6 I am weary with my moaning; every night I flood my bed with tears; I drench my couch with my weeping. ^psalm-06-6

7 My eye wastes away because of grief, it grows weak because of all my foes. ^psalm-06-7

8 Depart from me, all you workers of evil; for the LORD has heard the sound of my weeping. ^psalm-06-8

9 The LORD has heard my supplication; the LORD accepts my prayer. ^psalm-06-9

10 All my enemies shall be ashamed and sorely troubled; they shall turn back, and be put to shame in a moment. ^psalm-06-10
