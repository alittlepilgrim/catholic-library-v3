# Psalm 12 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-12|NABRE]]

**Plea for Help in Evil Times**

*To the choirmaster: according to The Sheminith. A Psalm of David.*

1 Help, LORD; for there is no longer any one that is godly; for the faithful have vanished from among the sons of men. ^psalm-12-1

2 Every one utters lies to his neighbor; with flattering lips and a double heart they speak. ^psalm-12-2

3 May the LORD cut off all flattering lips, the tongue that makes great boasts, ^psalm-12-3

4 those who say, “With our tongue we will prevail, our lips are with us; who is our master?” ^psalm-12-4

5 “Because the poor are despoiled, because the needy groan, I will now arise,” says the LORD; “I will place him in the safety for which he longs.” ^psalm-12-5

6 The promises of the LORD are promises that are pure, silver refined in a furnace on the ground, purified seven times. ^psalm-12-6

7 Do, O LORD, protect us, guard us for ever from this generation. ^psalm-12-7

8 On every side the wicked prowl, as vileness is exalted among the sons of men. To the choirmaster. ^psalm-12-8
