# Psalm 143 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-143|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Hear my prayer, O LORD; give ear to my supplications! In your faithfulness answer me, in your righteousness! ^psalm-143-1

2 Enter not into judgment with your servant; for no man living is righteous before you. ^psalm-143-2

3 For the enemy has pursued me; he has crushed my life to the ground; he has made me sit in darkness like those long dead. ^psalm-143-3

4 Therefore my spirit faints within me; my heart within me is appalled. ^psalm-143-4

5 I remember the days of old, I meditate on all that you have done; I muse on what your hands have wrought. ^psalm-143-5

6 I stretch out my hands to you; my soul thirsts for you like a parched land. Selah ^psalm-143-6

7 Make haste to answer me, O LORD! My spirit fails! Hide not your face from me, lest I be like those who go down to the Pit. ^psalm-143-7

8 Let me hear in the morning of your merciful love, for in you I put my trust. Teach me the way I should go, for to you I lift up my soul. ^psalm-143-8

9 Deliver me, O LORD, from my enemies! I have fled to you for refuge! ^psalm-143-9

10 Teach me to do your will, for you are my God! Let your good spirit lead me on a level path! ^psalm-143-10

11 For your name’s sake, O LORD, preserve my life! In your righteousness bring me out of trouble! ^psalm-143-11

12 And in your steadfast love cut off my enemies, and destroy all my adversaries, for I am your servant. A Psalm of David. ^psalm-143-12
