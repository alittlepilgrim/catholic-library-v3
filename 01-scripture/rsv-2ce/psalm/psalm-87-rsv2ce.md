# Psalm 87 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-87|NABRE]]

**The Joy of Living in Zion**

*A Song.*

1 On the holy mount stands the city he founded; ^psalm-87-1

2 the LORD loves the gates of Zion more than all the dwelling places of Jacob. ^psalm-87-2

3 Glorious things are spoken of you, O city of God. Selah ^psalm-87-3

4 Among those who know me I mention Rahab and Babylon; behold, Philis'tia and Tyre, with Ethiopia— “This one was born there,” they say. ^psalm-87-4

5 And of Zion it shall be said, “This one and that one were born in her”; for the Most High himself will establish her. ^psalm-87-5

6 The LORD records as he registers the peoples, “This one was born there.” Selah ^psalm-87-6

7 Singers and dancers alike say, “All my springs are in you.” A Song. A Psalm of the Sons of Korah. ^psalm-87-7
