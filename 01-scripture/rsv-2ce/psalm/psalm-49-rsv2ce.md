# Psalm 49 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-49|NABRE]]

**The Folly of Trust in Riches**

*A Psalm of the Sons of Korah.*

1 Hear this, all peoples! Give ear, all inhabitants of the world, ^psalm-49-1

2 both low and high, rich and poor together! ^psalm-49-2

3 My mouth shall speak wisdom; the meditation of my heart shall be understanding. ^psalm-49-3

4 I will incline my ear to a proverb; I will solve my riddle to the music of the lyre. ^psalm-49-4

5 Why should I fear in times of trouble, when the iniquity of my persecutors surrounds me, ^psalm-49-5

6 men who trust in their wealth and boast of the abundance of their riches? ^psalm-49-6

7 Truly no man can ransom himself, or give to God the price of his life, ^psalm-49-7

8 for the ransom of his life is costly, and can never suffice, ^psalm-49-8

9 that he should continue to live on for ever, and never see the Pit. ^psalm-49-9

10 Yes, he shall see that even the wise die, the fool and the stupid alike must perish and leave their wealth to others. ^psalm-49-10

11 Their graves are their homes for ever, their dwelling places to all generations, though they named lands their own. ^psalm-49-11

12 Man cannot abide in his pomp, he is like the beasts that perish. ^psalm-49-12

13 This is the fate of those who have foolish confidence, the end of those who are pleased with their portion. Selah ^psalm-49-13

14 Like sheep they are appointed for Sheol; Death shall be their shepherd; straight to the grave they descend, and their form shall waste away; Sheol shall be their home. ^psalm-49-14

15 But God will ransom my soul from the power of Sheol, for he will receive me. Selah ^psalm-49-15

16 Be not afraid when one becomes rich, when the glory of his house increases. ^psalm-49-16

17 For when he dies he will carry nothing away; his glory will not go down after him. ^psalm-49-17

18 Though, while he lives, he counts himself happy, and though a man gets praise when he does well for himself, ^psalm-49-18

19 he will go to the generation of his fathers, who will never more see the light. ^psalm-49-19

20 Man cannot abide in his pomp, he is like the beasts that perish. ^psalm-49-20
