# Psalm 101 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-101|NABRE]]

**A Sovereign’s Pledge of Integrity and Justice**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 I will sing of mercy and of justice; to you, O LORD, I will sing. ^psalm-101-1

2 I will give heed to the way that is blameless. Oh, when will you come to me? I will walk with integrity of heart within my house; ^psalm-101-2

3 I will not set before my eyes anything that is base. I hate the work of those who fall away; it shall not cling to me. ^psalm-101-3

4 Perverseness of heart shall be far from me; I will know nothing of evil. ^psalm-101-4

5 Him who slanders his neighbor secretly I will destroy. The man of haughty looks and arrogant heart I will not endure. ^psalm-101-5

6 I will look with favor on the faithful in the land, that they may dwell with me; he who walks in the way that is blameless shall minister to me. ^psalm-101-6

7 No man who practices deceit shall dwell in my house; no man who utters lies shall continue in my presence. ^psalm-101-7

8 Morning by morning I will destroy all the wicked in the land, cutting off all the evildoers from the city of the LORD. ^psalm-101-8
