# Psalm 19 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-19|NABRE]]

**God’s Glory in Creation and the Law**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David.*

1 The heavens are telling the glory of God; and the firmament proclaims his handiwork. ^psalm-19-1

2 Day to day pours forth speech, and night to night declares knowledge. ^psalm-19-2

3 There is no speech, nor are there words; their voice is not heard; ^psalm-19-3

4 yet their voice goes out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them he has set a tent for the sun, ^psalm-19-4

5 which comes forth like a bridegroom leaving his chamber, and like a strong man runs its course with joy. ^psalm-19-5

6 Its rising is from the end of the heavens, and its circuit to the end of them; and there is nothing hidden from its heat. ^psalm-19-6

7 The law of the LORD is perfect, reviving the soul; the testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple; ^psalm-19-7

8 the precepts of the LORD are right, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the LORD is pure, enlightening the eyes; ^psalm-19-8

9 the fear of the LORD is clean, enduring for ever; the ordinances of the LORD are true, and righteous altogether. ^psalm-19-9

10 More to be desired are they than gold, even much fine gold; sweeter also than honey and drippings of the honeycomb. ^psalm-19-10

11 Moreover by them is your servant warned; in keeping them there is great reward. ^psalm-19-11

12 But who can discern his errors? Clear me from hidden faults. ^psalm-19-12

13 Keep back your servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me! Then I shall be blameless, and innocent of great transgression. ^psalm-19-13

14 Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in your sight, O LORD, my rock and my redeemer. ^psalm-19-14
