# Psalm 129 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-129|NABRE]]

**Prayer for the Downfall of Israel’s Enemies**

*A Song of Ascents.*

1 “Sorely have they afflicted me from my youth,” let Israel now say— ^psalm-129-1

2 “Sorely have they afflicted me from my youth, yet they have not prevailed against me. ^psalm-129-2

3 The plowers plowed upon my back; they made long their furrows.” ^psalm-129-3

4 The LORD is righteous; he has cut the cords of the wicked. ^psalm-129-4

5 May all who hate Zion be put to shame and turned backward! ^psalm-129-5

6 Let them be like the grass on the housetops, which withers before it grows up, ^psalm-129-6

7 with which the reaper does not fill his hand or the binder of sheaves his arms, ^psalm-129-7

8 while those who pass by do not say, “The blessing of the LORD be upon you! We bless you in the name of the LORD!” A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-129-8
