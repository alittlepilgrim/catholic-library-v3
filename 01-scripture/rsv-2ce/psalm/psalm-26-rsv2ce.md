# Psalm 26 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-26|NABRE]]

**Plea for Justice; and Declaration of Righteousness**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Vindicate me, O LORD, for I have walked in my integrity, and I have trusted in the LORD without wavering. ^psalm-26-1

2 Prove me, O LORD, and try me; test my heart and my mind. ^psalm-26-2

3 For your mercy is before my eyes, and I walk in faithfulness to you. ^psalm-26-3

4 I do not sit with false men, nor do I consort with dissemblers; ^psalm-26-4

5 I hate the company of evildoers, and I will not sit with the wicked. ^psalm-26-5

6 I wash my hands in innocence, and go about your altar, O LORD, ^psalm-26-6

7 singing aloud a song of thanksgiving, and telling all your wondrous deeds. ^psalm-26-7

8 O LORD, I love the habitation of your house, and the place where your glory dwells. ^psalm-26-8

9 Sweep me not away with sinners, nor my life with bloodthirsty men, ^psalm-26-9

10 men in whose hands are evil devices, and whose right hands are full of bribes. ^psalm-26-10

11 But as for me, I walk in my integrity; redeem me, and have mercy on me. ^psalm-26-11

12 My foot stands on level ground; in the great congregation I will bless the LORD. ^psalm-26-12
