# Psalm 39 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-39|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Wisdom and Forgiveness**

*To the choirmaster: to Jeduthun. A Psalm of David.*

1 I said, “I will guard my ways, that I may not sin with my tongue; I will bridle my mouth, so long as the wicked are in my presence.” ^psalm-39-1

2 I was mute and silent, I held my peace to no avail; my distress grew worse, ^psalm-39-2

3 my heart became hot within me. As I mused, the fire burned; then I spoke with my tongue: ^psalm-39-3

4 “LORD, let me know my end, and what is the measure of my days; let me know how fleeting my life is! ^psalm-39-4

5 Behold, you have made my days a few handbreadths, and my lifetime is as nothing in your sight. Surely every man stands as a mere breath! Selah ^psalm-39-5

6 Surely man goes about as a shadow! Surely for nought are they in turmoil; man heaps up, and knows not who will gather! ^psalm-39-6

7 “And now, Lord, for what do I wait? My hope is in you. ^psalm-39-7

8 Deliver me from all my transgressions. Make me not the scorn of the fool! ^psalm-39-8

9 I am silent, I do not open my mouth; for it is you who have done it. ^psalm-39-9

10 Remove your stroke from me; I am spent by the blows of your hand. ^psalm-39-10

11 When you chasten man with rebukes for sin, you consume like a moth what is dear to him; surely every man is a mere breath! Selah ^psalm-39-11

12 “Hear my prayer, O LORD, and give ear to my cry; hold not your peace at my tears! For I am your passing guest, a sojourner, like all my fathers. ^psalm-39-12

13 Look away from me, that I may know gladness, before I depart and be no more!” ^psalm-39-13
