# Psalm 69 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-69|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Persecution**

*To the choirmaster: according to Lilies. A Psalm of David.*

1 Save me, O God! For the waters have come up to my neck. ^psalm-69-1

2 I sink in deep mire, where there is no foothold; I have come into deep waters, and the flood sweeps over me. ^psalm-69-2

3 I am weary with my crying; my throat is parched. My eyes grow dim with waiting for my God. ^psalm-69-3

4 More in number than the hairs of my head are those who hate me without cause; mighty are those who would destroy me, those who attack me with lies. What I did not steal must I now restore? ^psalm-69-4

5 O God, you know my folly; the wrongs I have done are not hidden from you. ^psalm-69-5

6 Let not those who hope in you be put to shame through me, O Lord GOD of hosts; let not those who seek you be brought to dishonor through me, O God of Israel. ^psalm-69-6

7 For it is for your sake that I have borne reproach, that shame has covered my face. ^psalm-69-7

8 I have become a stranger to my brethren, an alien to my mother’s sons. ^psalm-69-8

9 For zeal for your house has consumed me, and the insults of those who insult you have fallen on me. ^psalm-69-9

10 When I humbled my soul with fasting, it became my reproach. ^psalm-69-10

11 When I made sackcloth my clothing, I became a byword to them. ^psalm-69-11

12 I am the talk of those who sit in the gate, and the drunkards make songs about me. ^psalm-69-12

13 But as for me, my prayer is to you, O LORD. At an acceptable time, O God, in the abundance of your mercy answer me. With your faithful help ^psalm-69-13

14 rescue me from sinking in the mire; let me be delivered from my enemies and from the deep waters. ^psalm-69-14

15 Let not the flood sweep over me, or the deep swallow me up, or the pit close its mouth over me. ^psalm-69-15

16 Answer me, O LORD, for your merciful love is good; according to your abundant compassion, turn to me. ^psalm-69-16

17 Hide not your face from your servant; for I am in distress, make haste to answer me. ^psalm-69-17

18 Draw near to me, redeem me, set me free because of my enemies! ^psalm-69-18

19 You know my reproach, and my shame and my dishonor; my foes are all known to you. ^psalm-69-19

20 Insults have broken my heart, so that I am in despair. I looked for pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none. ^psalm-69-20

21 They gave me gall for food, and for my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. ^psalm-69-21

22 Let their own table before them become a snare; let their sacrificial feasts be a trap. ^psalm-69-22

23 Let their eyes be darkened, so that they cannot see; and make their loins tremble continually. ^psalm-69-23

24 Pour out your indignation upon them, and let your burning anger overtake them. ^psalm-69-24

25 May their camp be a desolation, let no one dwell in their tents. ^psalm-69-25

26 For they persecute him whom you have struck down, and him whom you have wounded, they afflict still more. ^psalm-69-26

27 Add to them punishment upon punishment; may they have no acquittal from you. ^psalm-69-27

28 Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; let them not be enrolled among the righteous. ^psalm-69-28

29 But I am afflicted and in pain; let your salvation, O God, set me on high! ^psalm-69-29

30 I will praise the name of God with a song; I will magnify him with thanksgiving. ^psalm-69-30

31 This will please the LORD more than an ox or a bull with horns and hoofs. ^psalm-69-31

32 Let the humble see it and be glad; you who seek God, let your hearts revive. ^psalm-69-32

33 For the LORD hears the needy, and does not despise his own that are in bonds. ^psalm-69-33

34 Let heaven and earth praise him, the seas and everything that moves therein. ^psalm-69-34

35 For God will save Zion and rebuild the cities of Judah; and his servants shall dwell there and possess it; ^psalm-69-35

36 the children of his servants shall inherit it, and those who love his name shall dwell in it. ^psalm-69-36
