# Psalm 51 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-51|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Cleansing and Pardon**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David, when Nathan the prophet came to him, after he had gone in to Bathsheba.*

1 Have mercy on me, O God, according to your merciful love; according to your abundant mercy blot out my transgressions. ^psalm-51-1

2 Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin! ^psalm-51-2

3 For I know my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me. ^psalm-51-3

4 Against you, you only, have I sinned, and done that which is evil in your sight, so that you are justified in your sentence and blameless in your judgment. ^psalm-51-4

5 Behold, I was brought forth in iniquity, and in sin did my mother conceive me. ^psalm-51-5

6 Behold, you desire truth in the inward being; therefore teach me wisdom in my secret heart. ^psalm-51-6

7 Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean; wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. ^psalm-51-7

8 Make me hear joy and gladness; let the bones which you have broken rejoice. ^psalm-51-8

9 Hide your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities. ^psalm-51-9

10 Create in me a clean heart, O God, and put a new and right spirit within me. ^psalm-51-10

11 Cast me not away from your presence, and take not your holy Spirit from me. ^psalm-51-11

12 Restore to me the joy of your salvation, and uphold me with a willing spirit. ^psalm-51-12

13 Then I will teach transgressors your ways, and sinners will return to you. ^psalm-51-13

14 Deliver me from bloodguilt, O God, O God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing aloud of your deliverance. ^psalm-51-14

15 O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth shall show forth your praise. ^psalm-51-15

16 For you take no delight in sacrifice; were I to give a burnt offering, you would not be pleased. ^psalm-51-16

17 The sacrifice acceptable to God is a broken spirit; a broken and contrite heart, O God, you will not despise. ^psalm-51-17

18 Do good to Zion in your good pleasure; rebuild the walls of Jerusalem, ^psalm-51-18

19 then will you delight in right sacrifices, in burnt offerings and whole burnt offerings; then bulls will be offered on your altar. ^psalm-51-19

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (51:1) This psalm, known as the “Miserere,” is the one most used in the Liturgy of the Hours to express repentance for sin. ^psalm-51-dn-1
