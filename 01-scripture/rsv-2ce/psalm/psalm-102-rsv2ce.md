# Psalm 102 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-102|NABRE]]

**Prayer to the Eternal King for Help**

*A prayer of one afflicted, when he is faint and pours out his complaint before the LORD.*

1 Hear my prayer, O LORD; let my cry come to you! ^psalm-102-1

2 Do not hide your face from me in the day of my distress! Incline your ear to me; answer me speedily in the day when I call! ^psalm-102-2

3 For my days pass away like smoke, and my bones burn like a furnace. ^psalm-102-3

4 My heart is struck down like grass, and withered; I forget to eat my bread. ^psalm-102-4

5 Because of my loud groaning my bones cling to my flesh. ^psalm-102-5

6 I am like a vulture of the wilderness, like an owl of the waste places; ^psalm-102-6

7 I lie awake, I am like a lonely bird on the housetop. ^psalm-102-7

8 All the day my enemies taunt me, those who deride me use my name for a curse. ^psalm-102-8

9 For I eat ashes like bread, and mingle tears with my drink, ^psalm-102-9

10 because of your indignation and anger; for you have taken me up and thrown me away. ^psalm-102-10

11 My days are like an evening shadow; I wither away like grass. ^psalm-102-11

12 But you, O LORD, are enthroned for ever; your name endures to all generations. ^psalm-102-12

13 You will arise and have pity on Zion; it is the time to favor her; the appointed time has come. ^psalm-102-13

14 For your servants hold her stones dear, and have pity on her dust. ^psalm-102-14

15 The nations will fear the name of the LORD, and all the kings of the earth your glory. ^psalm-102-15

16 For the LORD will build up Zion, he will appear in his glory; ^psalm-102-16

17 he will regard the prayer of the destitute, and will not despise their supplication. ^psalm-102-17

18 Let this be recorded for a generation to come, so that a people yet unborn may praise the LORD: ^psalm-102-18

19 that he looked down from his holy height, from heaven the LORD looked at the earth, ^psalm-102-19

20 to hear the groans of the prisoners, to set free those who were doomed to die; ^psalm-102-20

21 that men may declare in Zion the name of the LORD, and in Jerusalem his praise, ^psalm-102-21

22 when peoples gather together, and kingdoms, to worship the LORD. ^psalm-102-22

23 He has broken my strength in mid-course; he has shortened my days. ^psalm-102-23

24 “O my God,” I say, “do not take me from here in the midst of my days, you whose years endure throughout all generations!” ^psalm-102-24

25 Of old you laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the work of your hands. ^psalm-102-25

26 They will perish, but you endure; they will all wear out like a garment. You change them like clothing, and they pass away; ^psalm-102-26

27 but you are the same, and your years have no end. ^psalm-102-27

28 The children of your servants shall dwell secure; their posterity shall be established before you. ^psalm-102-28
