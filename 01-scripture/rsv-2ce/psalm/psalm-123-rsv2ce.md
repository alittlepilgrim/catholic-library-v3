# Psalm 123 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-123|NABRE]]

**Supplication for Mercy**

1 To you I lift up my eyes, O you who are enthroned in the heavens! ^psalm-123-1

2 Behold, as the eyes of servants look to the hand of their master, as the eyes of a maid to the hand of her mistress, so our eyes look to the LORD our God, till he have mercy upon us. ^psalm-123-2

3 Have mercy upon us, O LORD, have mercy upon us, for we have had more than enough of contempt. ^psalm-123-3

4 Too long our soul has been sated with the scorn of those who are at ease, the contempt of the proud. A Song of Ascents. ^psalm-123-4
