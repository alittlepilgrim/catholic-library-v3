# Psalm 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-01|NABRE]]

**The Two Ways**

1 Blessed is the man who walks not in the counsel of the wicked, nor stands in the way of sinners, nor sits in the seat of scoffers; ^psalm-01-1

2 but his delight is in the law of the LORD, and on his law he meditates day and night. ^psalm-01-2

3 He is like a tree planted by streams of water, that yields its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither. In all that he does, he prospers. ^psalm-01-3

4 The wicked are not so, but are like chaff which the wind drives away. ^psalm-01-4

5 Therefore the wicked will not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous; ^psalm-01-5

6 for the LORD knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the wicked will perish. ^psalm-01-6
