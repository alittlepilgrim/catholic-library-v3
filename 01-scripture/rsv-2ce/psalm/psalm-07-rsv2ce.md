# Psalm 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-07|NABRE]]

**Plea for Help against Persecution**

*A Shiggaion of David, which he sang to the LORD concerning Cush a Benjaminite.*

1 O LORD my God, in you I take refuge; save me from all my pursuers, and deliver me, ^psalm-07-1

2 lest like a lion they rend me, dragging me away, with none to rescue. ^psalm-07-2

3 O LORD my God, if I have done this, if there is wrong in my hands, ^psalm-07-3

4 if I have repaid my friend with evil or plundered my enemy without cause, ^psalm-07-4

5 let the enemy pursue me and overtake me, and let him trample my life to the ground, and lay my soul in the dust. Selah ^psalm-07-5

6 Arise, O LORD, in your anger, lift yourself up against the fury of my enemies; awake, O my God; you have appointed a judgment. ^psalm-07-6

7 Let the assembly of the peoples be gathered about you; and over it take your seat on high. ^psalm-07-7

8 The LORD judges the peoples; judge me, O LORD, according to my righteousness and according to the integrity that is in me. ^psalm-07-8

9 O let the evil of the wicked come to an end, but establish the righteous, you who try the minds and hearts, O righteous God. ^psalm-07-9

10 My shield is with God, who saves the upright in heart. ^psalm-07-10

11 God is a righteous judge, and a God who has indignation every day. ^psalm-07-11

12 If a man does not repent, God will whet his sword; he has bent and strung his bow; ^psalm-07-12

13 he has prepared his deadly weapons, making his arrows fiery shafts. ^psalm-07-13

14 Behold, the wicked man conceives evil, and is pregnant with mischief, and brings forth lies. ^psalm-07-14

15 He makes a pit, digging it out, and falls into the hole which he has made. ^psalm-07-15

16 His mischief returns upon his own head, and on his own skull his violence descends. ^psalm-07-16

17 I will give to the LORD the thanks due to his righteousness, and I will sing praise to the name of the LORD, the Most High. To the choirmaster: according to The Gittith. ^psalm-07-17
