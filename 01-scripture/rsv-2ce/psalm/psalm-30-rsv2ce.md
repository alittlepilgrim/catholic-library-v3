# Psalm 30 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-30|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Recovery from Grave Illness**

*A Song at the dedication of the Temple.*

1 I will extol you, O LORD, for you have drawn me up, and have not let my foes rejoice over me. ^psalm-30-1

2 O LORD my God, I cried to you for help, and you have healed me. ^psalm-30-2

3 O LORD, you have brought up my soul from Sheol, restored me to life from among those gone down to the Pit. ^psalm-30-3

4 Sing praises to the LORD, O you his saints, and give thanks to his holy name. ^psalm-30-4

5 For his anger is but for a moment, and his favor is for a lifetime. Weeping may last for the night, but joy comes with the morning. ^psalm-30-5

6 As for me, I said in my prosperity, “I shall never be moved.” ^psalm-30-6

7 By your favor, O LORD, you had established me as a strong mountain; you hid your face, I was dismayed. ^psalm-30-7

8 To you, O LORD, I cried; and to the LORD I made supplication: ^psalm-30-8

9 “What profit is there in my death, if I go down to the Pit? Will the dust praise you? Will it tell of your faithfulness? ^psalm-30-9

10 Hear, O LORD, and be gracious to me! O LORD, be my helper!” ^psalm-30-10

11 You have turned my mourning into dancing; you have loosed my sackcloth and clothed me with gladness, ^psalm-30-11

12 that my soul may praise you and not be silent. O LORD my God, I will give thanks to you for ever. ^psalm-30-12
