# Psalm 146 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-146|NABRE]]

**Praise for God’s Help**

1 Praise the LORD! Praise the LORD, O my soul! ^psalm-146-1

2 I will praise the LORD as long as I live; I will sing praises to my God while I have being. ^psalm-146-2

3 Put not your trust in princes, in a son of man, in whom there is no help. ^psalm-146-3

4 When his breath departs he returns to his earth; on that very day his plans perish. ^psalm-146-4

5 Happy is he whose help is the God of Jacob, whose hope is in the LORD his God, ^psalm-146-5

6 who made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them; who keeps faith for ever; ^psalm-146-6

7 who executes justice for the oppressed; who gives food to the hungry. The LORD sets the prisoners free; ^psalm-146-7

8 the LORD opens the eyes of the blind. The LORD lifts up those who are bowed down; the LORD loves the righteous. ^psalm-146-8

9 The LORD watches over the sojourners, he upholds the widow and the fatherless; but the way of the wicked he brings to ruin. ^psalm-146-9

10 The LORD will reign for ever, your God, O Zion, to all generations. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-146-10
