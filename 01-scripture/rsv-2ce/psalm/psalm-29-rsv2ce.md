# Psalm 29 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-29|NABRE]]

**The Voice of God in a Great Storm**

*A Psalm of David.*

1 Ascribe to the LORD, O sons of God, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength. ^psalm-29-1

2 Ascribe to the LORD the glory of his name; worship the LORD in holy attire. ^psalm-29-2

3 The voice of the LORD is upon the waters; the God of glory thunders, the LORD, upon many waters. ^psalm-29-3

4 The voice of the LORD is powerful, the voice of the LORD is full of majesty. ^psalm-29-4

5 The voice of the LORD breaks the cedars, the LORD breaks the cedars of Lebanon. ^psalm-29-5

6 He makes Lebanon to skip like a calf, and Sir'ion like a young wild ox. ^psalm-29-6

7 The voice of the LORD flashes forth flames of fire. ^psalm-29-7

8 The voice of the LORD shakes the wilderness, the LORD shakes the wilderness of Kadesh. ^psalm-29-8

9 The voice of the LORD makes the oaks to whirl, and strips the forests bare; and in his temple all cry, “Glory!” ^psalm-29-9

10 The LORD sits enthroned over the flood; the LORD sits enthroned as king for ever. ^psalm-29-10

11 May the LORD give strength to his people! May the LORD bless his people with peace! A Psalm of David. ^psalm-29-11
