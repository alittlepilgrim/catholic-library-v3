# Psalm 59 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-59|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Deliverance from Enemies**

*A Miktam of David, when Saul sent men to watch his house in order to kill him.*

1 Deliver me from my enemies, O my God, protect me from those who rise up against me, ^psalm-59-1

2 deliver me from those who work evil, and save me from bloodthirsty men. ^psalm-59-2

3 For behold, they lie in wait for my life; fierce men band themselves against me. For no transgression or sin of mine, O LORD, ^psalm-59-3

4 for no fault of mine, they run and make ready. Rouse yourself, come to my help, and see! ^psalm-59-4

5 You, LORD God of hosts, are God of Israel. Awake to punish all the nations; spare none of those who treacherously plot evil. Selah ^psalm-59-5

6 Each evening they come back, howling like dogs and prowling about the city. ^psalm-59-6

7 There they are, bellowing with their mouths, and snarling with their lips— for “Who,” they think, “will hear us?” ^psalm-59-7

8 But you, O LORD, laugh at them; you hold all the nations in derision. ^psalm-59-8

9 O my Strength, I will sing praises to you; for you, O God, are my fortress. ^psalm-59-9

10 My God in his mercy will meet me; my God will let me look in triumph on my enemies. ^psalm-59-10

11 Slay them not, lest my people forget; make them totter by your power, and bring them down, O Lord, our shield! ^psalm-59-11

12 For the sin of their mouths, the words of their lips, let them be trapped in their pride. For the cursing and lies which they utter, ^psalm-59-12

13 consume them in wrath, consume them till they are no more, that men may know that God rules over Jacob to the ends of the earth. Selah ^psalm-59-13

14 Each evening they come back, howling like dogs and prowling about the city. ^psalm-59-14

15 They roam about for food, and growl if they do not get their fill. ^psalm-59-15

16 But I will sing of your might; I will sing aloud of your mercy in the morning. For you have been to me a fortress and a refuge in the day of my distress. ^psalm-59-16

17 O my Strength, I will sing praises to you, for you, O God, are my fortress, the God who shows me mercy. ^psalm-59-17
