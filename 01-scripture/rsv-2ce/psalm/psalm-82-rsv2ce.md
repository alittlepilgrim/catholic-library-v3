# Psalm 82 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-82|NABRE]]

**A Plea for Justice**

1 God has taken his place in the divine council; in the midst of the angels he holds judgment: ^psalm-82-1

2 “How long will you judge unjustly and show partiality to the wicked? Selah ^psalm-82-2

3 Give justice to the weak and the fatherless; maintain the right of the afflicted and the destitute. ^psalm-82-3

4 Rescue the weak and the needy; deliver them from the hand of the wicked.” ^psalm-82-4

5 They have neither knowledge nor understanding, they walk about in darkness; all the foundations of the earth are shaken. ^psalm-82-5

6 I say, “You are gods, sons of the Most High, all of you; ^psalm-82-6

7 nevertheless, you shall die like men, and fall like any prince.” ^psalm-82-7

8 Arise, O God, judge the earth; for to you belong all the nations! A Song. ^psalm-82-8
