# Psalm 98 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-98|NABRE]]

**Praise the Judge of the World**

*A Psalm.*

1 O sing to the LORD a new song, for he has done marvelous things! His right hand and his holy arm have gotten him victory. ^psalm-98-1

2 The LORD has made known his victory, he has revealed his vindication in the sight of the nations. ^psalm-98-2

3 He has remembered his mercy and faithfulness to the house of Israel. All the ends of the earth have seen the victory of our God. ^psalm-98-3

4 Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth; break forth into joyous song and sing praises! ^psalm-98-4

5 Sing praises to the LORD with the lyre, with the lyre and the sound of melody! ^psalm-98-5

6 With trumpets and the sound of the horn make a joyful noise before the King, the LORD! ^psalm-98-6

7 Let the sea roar, and all that fills it; the world and those who dwell in it! ^psalm-98-7

8 Let the floods clap their hands; let the hills sing for joy together ^psalm-98-8

9 before the LORD, for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, and the peoples with equity. ^psalm-98-9
