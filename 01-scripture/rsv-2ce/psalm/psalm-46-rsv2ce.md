# Psalm 46 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-46|NABRE]]

**God’s Defense of His City and People**

*A Psalm of the Sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A Song.*

1 God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. ^psalm-46-1

2 Therefore we will not fear though the earth should change, though the mountains shake in the heart of the sea; ^psalm-46-2

3 though its waters roar and foam, though the mountains tremble with its tumult. Selah ^psalm-46-3

4 There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the holy habitation of the Most High. ^psalm-46-4

5 God is in the midst of her, she shall not be moved; God will help her when morning dawns. ^psalm-46-5

6 The nations rage, the kingdoms totter; he utters his voice, the earth melts. ^psalm-46-6

7 The LORD of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah ^psalm-46-7

8 Come, behold the works of the LORD, how he has wrought desolations in the earth. ^psalm-46-8

9 He makes wars cease to the end of the earth; he breaks the bow, and shatters the spear, he burns the chariots with fire! ^psalm-46-9

10 “Be still, and know that I am God. I am exalted among the nations, I am exalted in the earth!” ^psalm-46-10

11 The LORD of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah ^psalm-46-11
