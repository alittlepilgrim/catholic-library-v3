# Psalm 34 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-34|NABRE]]

**Praise for Deliverance from Trouble**

*A Psalm of David, when he feigned madness before Abimelech, so that he drove him out, and he went away.*

1 I will bless the LORD at all times; his praise shall continually be in my mouth. ^psalm-34-1

2 My soul makes its boast in the LORD; let the humble hear and be glad. ^psalm-34-2

3 O magnify the LORD with me, and let us exalt his name together! ^psalm-34-3

4 I sought the LORD, and he answered me, and delivered me from all my fears. ^psalm-34-4

5 Look to him, and be radiant; so your faces shall never be ashamed. ^psalm-34-5

6 This poor man cried, and the LORD heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles. ^psalm-34-6

7 The angel of the LORD encamps around those who fear him, and delivers them. ^psalm-34-7

8 O taste and see that the LORD is good! Blessed is the man who takes refuge in him! ^psalm-34-8

9 O fear the LORD, you his saints, for those who fear him have no want! ^psalm-34-9

10 The young lions suffer want and hunger; but those who seek the LORD lack no good thing. ^psalm-34-10

11 Come, O sons, listen to me, I will teach you the fear of the LORD. ^psalm-34-11

12 What man is there who desires life, and covets many days, that he may enjoy good? ^psalm-34-12

13 Keep your tongue from evil, and your lips from speaking deceit. ^psalm-34-13

14 Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it. ^psalm-34-14

15 The eyes of the LORD are toward the righteous, and his ears toward their cry. ^psalm-34-15

16 The face of the LORD is against evildoers, to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth. ^psalm-34-16

17 When the righteous cry for help, the LORD hears, and delivers them out of all their troubles. ^psalm-34-17

18 The LORD is near to the brokenhearted, and saves the crushed in spirit. ^psalm-34-18

19 Many are the afflictions of the righteous; but the LORD delivers him out of them all. ^psalm-34-19

20 He keeps all his bones; not one of them is broken. ^psalm-34-20

21 Evil shall slay the wicked; and those who hate the righteous will be condemned. ^psalm-34-21

22 The LORD redeems the life of his servants; none of those who take refuge in him will be condemned. ^psalm-34-22
