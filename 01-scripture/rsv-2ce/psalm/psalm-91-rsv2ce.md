# Psalm 91 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-91|NABRE]]

**Assurance of God’s Protection**

1 He who dwells in the shelter of the Most High, who abides in the shadow of the Almighty, ^psalm-91-1

2 will say to the LORD, “My refuge and my fortress; my God, in whom I trust.” ^psalm-91-2

3 For he will deliver you from the snare of the fowler and from the deadly pestilence; ^psalm-91-3

4 he will cover you with his pinions, and under his wings you will find refuge; his faithfulness is a shield and buckler. ^psalm-91-4

5 You will not fear the terror of the night, nor the arrow that flies by day, ^psalm-91-5

6 nor the pestilence that stalks in darkness, nor the destruction that wastes at noonday. ^psalm-91-6

7 A thousand may fall at your side, ten thousand at your right hand; but it will not come near you. ^psalm-91-7

8 You will only look with your eyes and see the recompense of the wicked. ^psalm-91-8

9 Because you have made the LORD your refuge, the Most High your habitation, ^psalm-91-9

10 no evil shall befall you, no scourge come near your tent. ^psalm-91-10

11 For he will give his angels charge of you to guard you in all your ways. ^psalm-91-11

12 On their hands they will bear you up, lest you dash your foot against a stone. ^psalm-91-12

13 You will tread on the lion and the adder, the young lion and the serpent you will trample under foot. ^psalm-91-13

14 Because he clings to me in love, I will deliver him; I will protect him, because he knows my name. ^psalm-91-14

15 When he calls to me, I will answer him; I will be with him in trouble, I will rescue him and honor him. ^psalm-91-15

16 With long life I will satisfy him, and show him my salvation. ^psalm-91-16
