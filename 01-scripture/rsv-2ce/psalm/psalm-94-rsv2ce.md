# Psalm 94 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-94|NABRE]]

**God the Avenger of the Righteous**

1 O LORD, you God of vengeance, you God of vengeance, shine forth! ^psalm-94-1

2 Rise up, O judge of the earth; render to the proud their deserts! ^psalm-94-2

3 O LORD, how long shall the wicked, how long shall the wicked exult? ^psalm-94-3

4 They pour out their arrogant words, they boast, all the evildoers. ^psalm-94-4

5 They crush your people, O LORD, and afflict your heritage. ^psalm-94-5

6 They slay the widow and the sojourner, and murder the fatherless; ^psalm-94-6

7 and they say, “The LORD does not see; the God of Jacob does not perceive.” ^psalm-94-7

8 Understand, O dullest of the people! Fools, when will you be wise? ^psalm-94-8

9 He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see? ^psalm-94-9

10 He who chastens the nations, does he not chastise? He who teaches men knowledge, ^psalm-94-10

11 the LORD, knows the thoughts of man, that they are but a breath. ^psalm-94-11

12 Blessed is the man whom you chasten, O LORD, and whom you teach out of your law ^psalm-94-12

13 to give him respite from days of trouble, until a pit is dug for the wicked. ^psalm-94-13

14 For the LORD will not forsake his people; he will not abandon his heritage; ^psalm-94-14

15 for justice will return to the righteous, and all the upright in heart will follow it. ^psalm-94-15

16 Who rises up for me against the wicked? Who stands up for me against evildoers? ^psalm-94-16

17 If the LORD had not been my help, my soul would soon have dwelt in the land of silence. ^psalm-94-17

18 When I thought, “My foot slips,” your mercy, O LORD, held me up. ^psalm-94-18

19 When the cares of my heart are many, your consolations cheer my soul. ^psalm-94-19

20 Can wicked rulers be allied with you, who frame mischief by statute? ^psalm-94-20

21 They band together against the life of the righteous, and condemn the innocent to death. ^psalm-94-21

22 But the LORD has become my stronghold, and my God the rock of my refuge. ^psalm-94-22

23 He will bring back on them their iniquity and wipe them out for their wickedness; the LORD our God will wipe them out. ^psalm-94-23
