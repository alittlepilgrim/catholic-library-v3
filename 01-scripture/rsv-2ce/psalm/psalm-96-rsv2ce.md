# Psalm 96 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-96|NABRE]]

**Praise to God Who Comes in Judgment**

1 O sing to the LORD a new song; sing to the LORD, all the earth! ^psalm-96-1

2 Sing to the LORD, bless his name; tell of his salvation from day to day. ^psalm-96-2

3 Declare his glory among the nations, his marvelous works among all the peoples! ^psalm-96-3

4 For great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised; he is to be feared above all gods. ^psalm-96-4

5 For all the gods of the peoples are idols; but the LORD made the heavens. ^psalm-96-5

6 Honor and majesty are before him; strength and beauty are in his sanctuary. ^psalm-96-6

7 Ascribe to the LORD, O families of the peoples, ascribe to the LORD glory and strength! ^psalm-96-7

8 Ascribe to the LORD the glory due his name; bring an offering, and come into his courts! ^psalm-96-8

9 Worship the LORD in holy attire; tremble before him, all the earth! ^psalm-96-9

10 Say among the nations, “The LORD reigns! Yes, the world is established, it shall never be moved; he will judge the peoples with equity.” ^psalm-96-10

11 Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice; let the sea roar, and all that fills it; ^psalm-96-11

12 let the field exult, and everything in it! Then shall all the trees of the wood sing for joy ^psalm-96-12

13 before the LORD, for he comes, for he comes to judge the earth. He will judge the world with righteousness, and the peoples with his truth. ^psalm-96-13
