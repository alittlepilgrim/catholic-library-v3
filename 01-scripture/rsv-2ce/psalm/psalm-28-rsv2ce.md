# Psalm 28 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-28|NABRE]]

**Prayer for Help; and Thanksgiving for It**

1 To you, O LORD, I call; my rock, be not deaf to me, lest, if you be silent to me, I become like those who go down to the Pit. ^psalm-28-1

2 Hear the voice of my supplication, as I cry to you for help, as I lift up my hands toward your most holy sanctuary. ^psalm-28-2

3 Take me not off with the wicked, with those who are workers of evil, who speak peace with their neighbors, while mischief is in their hearts. ^psalm-28-3

4 Repay them according to their work, and according to the evil of their deeds; repay them according to the work of their hands; render them their due reward. ^psalm-28-4

5 Because they do not regard the works of the LORD, or the work of his hands, he will break them down and build them up no more. ^psalm-28-5

6 Blessed be the LORD! for he has heard the voice of my supplications. ^psalm-28-6

7 The LORD is my strength and my shield; in him my heart trusts; so I am helped, and my heart exults, and with my song I give thanks to him. ^psalm-28-7

8 The LORD is the strength of his people, he is the saving refuge of his anointed. ^psalm-28-8

9 O save your people, and bless your heritage; be their shepherd, and carry them for ever. ^psalm-28-9
