# Psalm 92 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-92|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving for Vindication**

*A Psalm. A Song for the Sabbath.*

1 It is good to give thanks to the LORD, to sing praises to your name, O Most High; ^psalm-92-1

2 to declare your merciful love in the morning, and your faithfulness by night, ^psalm-92-2

3 to the music of the lute and the harp, to the melody of the lyre. ^psalm-92-3

4 For you, O LORD, have made me glad by your work; at the works of your hands I sing for joy. ^psalm-92-4

5 How great are your works, O LORD! Your thoughts are very deep! ^psalm-92-5

6 The dull man cannot know, the stupid cannot understand this: ^psalm-92-6

7 that, though the wicked sprout like grass and all evildoers flourish, they are doomed to destruction for ever, ^psalm-92-7

8 but you, O LORD, are on high for ever. ^psalm-92-8

9 For, behold, your enemies, O LORD, for, behold, your enemies shall perish; all evildoers shall be scattered. ^psalm-92-9

10 But you have exalted my horn like that of the wild ox; you have poured over me fresh oil. ^psalm-92-10

11 My eyes have seen the downfall of my enemies, my ears have heard the doom of my evil assailants. ^psalm-92-11

12 The righteous flourish like the palm tree, and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. ^psalm-92-12

13 They are planted in the house of the LORD, they flourish in the courts of our God. ^psalm-92-13

14 They still bring forth fruit in old age, they are ever full of sap and green, ^psalm-92-14

15 to show that the LORD is upright; he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him. ^psalm-92-15
