# Psalm 149 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-149|NABRE]]

**Praise for God’s Goodness to Israel**

1 Praise the LORD! Sing to the LORD a new song, his praise in the assembly of the faithful! ^psalm-149-1

2 Let Israel be glad in his Maker, let the sons of Zion rejoice in their King! ^psalm-149-2

3 Let them praise his name with dancing, making melody to him with timbrel and lyre! ^psalm-149-3

4 For the LORD takes pleasure in his people; he adorns the humble with victory. ^psalm-149-4

5 Let the faithful exult in glory; let them sing for joy on their couches. ^psalm-149-5

6 Let the high praises of God be in their throats and two-edged swords in their hands, ^psalm-149-6

7 to wreak vengeance on the nations and chastisement on the peoples, ^psalm-149-7

8 to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with fetters of iron, ^psalm-149-8

9 to execute on them the judgment written! This is glory for all his faithful ones. Praise the LORD! ^psalm-149-9
