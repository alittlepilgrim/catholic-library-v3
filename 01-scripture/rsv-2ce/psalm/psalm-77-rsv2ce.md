# Psalm 77 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-77|NABRE]]

**God’s Mighty Deeds Recalled**

*To the choirmaster: according to Jeduthun. A Psalm of Asaph.*

1 I cry aloud to God, aloud to God, that he may hear me. ^psalm-77-1

2 In the day of my trouble I seek the Lord; in the night my hand is stretched out without wearying; my soul refuses to be comforted. ^psalm-77-2

3 I think of God, and I moan; I meditate, and my spirit faints. Selah ^psalm-77-3

4 You keep my eyelids from closing; I am so troubled that I cannot speak. ^psalm-77-4

5 I consider the days of old, I remember the years long ago. ^psalm-77-5

6 I commune with my heart in the night; I meditate and search my spirit: ^psalm-77-6

7 “Will the Lord spurn for ever, and never again be favorable? ^psalm-77-7

8 Has his steadfast love for ever ceased? Are his promises at an end for all time? ^psalm-77-8

9 Has God forgotten to be gracious? Has he in anger shut up his compassion?” Selah ^psalm-77-9

10 And I say, “It is my grief that the right hand of the Most High has changed.” ^psalm-77-10

11 I will call to mind the deeds of the LORD; yes, I will remember your wonders of old. ^psalm-77-11

12 I will meditate on all your work, and muse on your mighty deeds. ^psalm-77-12

13 Your way, O God, is holy. What god is great like our God? ^psalm-77-13

14 You are the God who works wonders, who have manifested your might among the peoples. ^psalm-77-14

15 With your arm you redeemed your people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah ^psalm-77-15

16 When the waters saw you, O God, when the waters saw you, they were afraid, yes, the deep trembled. ^psalm-77-16

17 The clouds poured out water; the skies gave forth thunder; your arrows flashed on every side. ^psalm-77-17

18 The crash of your thunder was in the whirlwind; your lightnings lighted up the world; the earth trembled and shook. ^psalm-77-18

19 Your way was through the sea, your path through the great waters; yet your footprints were unseen. ^psalm-77-19

20 You led your people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron. A Maskil of Asaph. ^psalm-77-20
