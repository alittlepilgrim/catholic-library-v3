# Psalm 15 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-15|NABRE]]

**Who Shall Abide in God’s Sanctuary?**

1 O LORD, who shall sojourn in your tent? Who shall dwell on your holy mountain? ^psalm-15-1

2 He who walks blamelessly, and does what is right, and speaks truth from his heart; ^psalm-15-2

3 who does not slander with his tongue, and does no evil to his friend, nor takes up a reproach against his neighbor; ^psalm-15-3

4 in whose eyes a reprobate is despised, but who honors those who fear the LORD; who swears to his own hurt and does not change; ^psalm-15-4

5 who does not put out his money at interest, and does not take a bribe against the innocent. He who does these things shall never be moved. ^psalm-15-5
