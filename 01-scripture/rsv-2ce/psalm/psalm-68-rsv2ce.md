# Psalm 68 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-68|NABRE]]

**Praise and Thanksgiving**

*To the choirmaster. A Psalm of David. A Song.*

1 Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered; let those who hate him flee before him! ^psalm-68-1

2 As smoke is driven away, so drive them away; as wax melts before fire, let the wicked perish before God! ^psalm-68-2

3 But let the righteous be joyful; let them exult before God; let them be jubilant with joy! ^psalm-68-3

4 Sing to God, sing praises to his name; lift up a song to him who rides upon the clouds; his name is the LORD, exult before him! ^psalm-68-4

5 Father of the fatherless and protector of widows is God in his holy habitation. ^psalm-68-5

6 God gives the desolate a home to dwell in; he leads out the prisoners to prosperity; but the rebellious dwell in a parched land. ^psalm-68-6

7 O God, when you went forth before your people, when you marched through the wilderness, Selah ^psalm-68-7

8 the earth quaked, the heavens poured down rain, at the presence of God; yon Sinai quaked at the presence of God, the God of Israel. ^psalm-68-8

9 Rain in abundance, O God, you showered abroad; you restored your heritage as it languished; ^psalm-68-9

10 your flock found a dwelling in it; in your goodness, O God, you provided for the needy. ^psalm-68-10

11 The Lord gives the command; great is the host of those who bore the tidings: ^psalm-68-11

12 “The kings of the armies, they flee, they flee!” The women at home divide the spoil, ^psalm-68-12

13 though they stay among the sheepfolds— the wings of a dove covered with silver, its pinions with green gold. ^psalm-68-13

14 When the Almighty scattered kings there, snow fell on Zalmon. ^psalm-68-14

15 O mighty mountain, mountain of Bashan; O many-peaked mountain, mountain of Bashan! ^psalm-68-15

16 Why do you look with envy, O many-peaked mountain, at the mount which God desired for his abode, yes, where the LORD will dwell for ever? ^psalm-68-16

17 With mighty chariotry, twice ten thousand, thousands upon thousands, the Lord came from Sinai into the holy place. ^psalm-68-17

18 You ascended the high mount, leading captives in your train, and receiving gifts among men, even among the rebellious, that the LORD God may dwell there. ^psalm-68-18

19 Blessed be the Lord, who daily bears us up; God is our salvation. Selah ^psalm-68-19

20 Our God is a God of salvation; and to GOD, the Lord, belongs escape from death. ^psalm-68-20

21 But God will shatter the heads of his enemies, the hairy crown of him who walks in his guilty ways. ^psalm-68-21

22 The Lord said, “I will bring them back from Bashan, I will bring them back from the depths of the sea, ^psalm-68-22

23 that you may bathe your feet in blood, that the tongues of your dogs may have their portion from the foe.” ^psalm-68-23

24 Your solemn processions are seen, O God, the processions of my God, my King, into the sanctuary— ^psalm-68-24

25 the singers in front, the minstrels last, between them maidens playing timbrels: ^psalm-68-25

26 “Bless God in the great congregation, the LORD, O you who are of Israel’s fountain!” ^psalm-68-26

27 There is Benjamin, the least of them, in the lead, the princes of Judah in their throng, the princes of Zeb'ulun, the princes of Naph'tali. ^psalm-68-27

28 Summon your might, O God; show your strength, O God, you who have wrought for us. ^psalm-68-28

29 Because of your temple at Jerusalem kings bear gifts to you. ^psalm-68-29

30 Rebuke the beasts that dwell among the reeds, the herd of bulls with the calves of the peoples. Trample under foot those who lust after tribute; scatter the peoples who delight in war. ^psalm-68-30

31 Let bronze be brought from Egypt; let Ethiopia hasten to stretch out her hands to God. ^psalm-68-31

32 Sing to God, O kingdoms of the earth; sing praises to the Lord, Selah ^psalm-68-32

33 to him who rides in the heavens, the ancient heavens; behold, he sends forth his voice, his mighty voice. ^psalm-68-33

34 Ascribe power to God, whose majesty is over Israel, and his power is in the skies. ^psalm-68-34

35 Awesome is God in his sanctuary, the God of Israel, he gives power and strength to his people. Blessed be God! ^psalm-68-35

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (68:1) This is one of the most ancient compositions in the Psalter and has been influenced by Canaanite motifs and representations. ^psalm-68-dn-1
