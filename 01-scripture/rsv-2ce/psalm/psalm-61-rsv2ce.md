# Psalm 61 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[psalm-61|NABRE]]

**Assurance of God’s Protection**

*To the choirmaster: with stringed instruments. A Psalm of David.*

1 Hear my cry, O God, listen to my prayer; ^psalm-61-1

2 from the end of the earth I call to you, when my heart is faint. Lead me to the rock that is higher than I; ^psalm-61-2

3 for you are my refuge, a strong tower against the enemy. ^psalm-61-3

4 Let me dwell in your tent for ever! Oh, to be safe under the shelter of your wings! Selah ^psalm-61-4

5 For you, O God, have heard my vows, you have given me the heritage of those who fear your name. ^psalm-61-5

6 Prolong the life of the king; may his years endure to all generations! ^psalm-61-6

7 May he be enthroned for ever before God; bid steadfast love and faithfulness watch over him! ^psalm-61-7

8 So will I ever sing praises to your name, as I pay my vows day after day. ^psalm-61-8
