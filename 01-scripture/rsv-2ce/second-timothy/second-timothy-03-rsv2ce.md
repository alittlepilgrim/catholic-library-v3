# 2 Timothy 3 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Godlessness in the Last Days**

1 But understand this, that in the last days there will come times of stress. ^second-timothy-03-1

2 For men will be lovers of self, lovers of money, proud, arrogant, abusive, disobedient to their parents, ungrateful, unholy, ^second-timothy-03-2

3 inhuman, implacable, slanderers, profligates, fierce, haters of good, ^second-timothy-03-3

4 treacherous, reckless, swollen with conceit, lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God, ^second-timothy-03-4

5 holding the form of religion but denying the power of it. Avoid such people. ^second-timothy-03-5

6 For among them are those who make their way into households and capture weak women, burdened with sins and swayed by various impulses, ^second-timothy-03-6

7 who will listen to anybody and can never arrive at a knowledge of the truth. ^second-timothy-03-7

8 As Jan'nes and Jam'bres opposed Moses, so these men also oppose the truth, men of corrupt mind and counterfeit faith; ^second-timothy-03-8

9 but they will not get very far, for their folly will be plain to all, as was that of those two men. ^second-timothy-03-9

**Paul's Charge to Timothy**

10 Now you have observed my teaching, my conduct, my aim in life, my faith, my patience, my love, my steadfastness, ^second-timothy-03-10

11 my persecutions, my sufferings, what befell me at Antioch, at Ico'nium, and at Lystra, what persecutions I endured; yet from them all the Lord rescued me. ^second-timothy-03-11

12 Indeed all who desire to live a godly life in Christ Jesus will be persecuted, ^second-timothy-03-12

13 while evil men and impostors will go on from bad to worse, deceivers and deceived. ^second-timothy-03-13

14 But as for you, continue in what you have learned and have firmly believed, knowing from whom you learned it ^second-timothy-03-14

15 and how from childhood you have been acquainted with the Sacred Writings which are able to instruct you for salvation through faith in Christ Jesus. ^second-timothy-03-15

16 All Scripture is inspired by God and<sup>d</sup> profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness, \* ^second-timothy-03-16

17 that the man of God may be complete, equipped for every good work. ^second-timothy-03-17


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (3:16) Paul refers to the Old Testament Scriptures. ^second-timothy-03-dn-1
