# 2 Timothy 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**A Good Soldier of Christ**

1 You then, my son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus, ^second-timothy-02-1

2 and what you have heard from me before many witnesses entrust to faithful men who will be able to teach others also. ^second-timothy-02-2

3 Take your share of suffering as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. ^second-timothy-02-3

4 No soldier on service gets entangled in civilian pursuits, since his aim is to satisfy the one who enlisted him. ^second-timothy-02-4

5 An athlete is not crowned unless he competes according to the rules. ^second-timothy-02-5

6 It is the hard-working farmer who ought to have the first share of the crops. ^second-timothy-02-6

7 Think over what I say, for the Lord will grant you understanding in everything. ^second-timothy-02-7

8 Remember Jesus Christ, risen from the dead, descended from David, as preached in my gospel, ^second-timothy-02-8

9 the gospel for which I am suffering and wearing chains like a criminal. But the word of God is not chained. ^second-timothy-02-9

10 Therefore I endure everything for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation which in Christ Jesus goes with eternal glory. ^second-timothy-02-10

11 The saying is sure:

If we have died with him, we shall also live with him; ^second-timothy-02-11

12 if we endure, we shall also reign with him;
  if we deny him, he also will deny us; ^second-timothy-02-12

13 if we are faithless, he remains faithful—
  for he cannot deny himself. ^second-timothy-02-13

**A Workman Approved by God**

14 Remind them of this, and charge them before the Lord<sup>b</sup> to avoid disputing about words, which does no good, but only ruins the hearers. ^second-timothy-02-14

15 Do your best to present yourself to God as one approved, a workman who has no need to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth. ^second-timothy-02-15

16 Avoid such godless chatter, for it will lead people into more and more ungodliness, ^second-timothy-02-16

17 and their talk will eat its way like gangrene. Among them are Hymenae'us and Phile'tus, ^second-timothy-02-17

18 who have swerved from the truth by holding that the resurrection is past already. \* They are upsetting the faith of some. ^second-timothy-02-18

19 But God's firm foundation stands, bearing this seal: "The Lord knows those who are his," and, "Let every one who names the name of the Lord depart from iniquity." ^second-timothy-02-19

20 In a great house there are not only vessels of gold and silver but also of wood and earthenware, and some for noble use, some for ignoble. ^second-timothy-02-20

21 If any one purifies himself from what is ignoble, then he will be a vessel for noble use, consecrated and useful to the master of the house, ready for any good work. ^second-timothy-02-21

22 So shun youthful passions and aim at righteousness, faith, love, and peace, along with those who call upon the Lord from a pure heart. ^second-timothy-02-22

23 Have nothing to do with stupid, senseless controversies; you know that they breed quarrels. ^second-timothy-02-23

24 And the Lord's servant must not be quarrelsome but kindly to every one, an apt teacher, forbearing, ^second-timothy-02-24

25 correcting his opponents with gentleness. God may perhaps grant that they will repent and come to know the truth, ^second-timothy-02-25

26 and they may escape from the snare of the devil, after being captured by him to do his will.<sup>c</sup> ^second-timothy-02-26


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (2:18) They explained the resurrection by saying it was the rising to newness of life in baptism, thus ignoring a bodily resurrection, a doctrine the Greeks found very hard to accept; cf. Acts 17:32. ^second-timothy-02-dn-1
