# Joel 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[joel-03|NABRE]]

1 “For behold, in those days and at that time, when I restore the fortunes of Judah and Jerusalem, ^joel-03-1

2 I will gather all the nations and bring them down to the valley of Jehosh’aphat, and I will enter into judgment with them there, on account of my people and my heritage Israel, because they have scattered them among the nations, and have divided up my land, ^joel-03-2

3 and have cast lots for my people, and have given a boy for a harlot, and have sold a girl for wine, and have drunk it. ^joel-03-3

4 “What are you to me, O Tyre and Si'don, and all the regions of Philis'tia? Are you paying me back for something? If you are paying me back, I will repay your deed upon your own head swiftly and speedily. ^joel-03-4

5 For you have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried my rich treasures into your temples. ^joel-03-5

6 You have sold the people of Judah and Jerusalem to the Greeks, removing them far from their own border. ^joel-03-6

7 But now I will stir them up from the place to which you have sold them, and I will repay your deed upon your own head. ^joel-03-7

8 I will sell your sons and your daughters into the hand of the sons of Judah, and they will sell them to the Sabe'ans, to a nation far off; for the LORD has spoken.” ^joel-03-8

9 Proclaim this among the nations: Prepare war, stir up the mighty men. Let all the men of war draw near, let them come up. ^joel-03-9

10 Beat your plowshares into swords, and your pruning hooks into spears; let the weak say, “I am a warrior.” ^joel-03-10

11 Hasten and come, all you nations round about, gather yourselves there. Bring down your warriors, O LORD. ^joel-03-11

12 Let the nations bestir themselves, and come up to the valley of Jehosh'aphat; for there I will sit to judge all the nations round about. ^joel-03-12

13 Put in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe. Go in, tread, for the wine press is full. The vats overflow, for their wickedness is great. ^joel-03-13

14 Multitudes, multitudes, in the valley of decision! For the day of the LORD is near in the valley of decision. ^joel-03-14

15 The sun and the moon are darkened, and the stars withdraw their shining. ^joel-03-15

16 And the LORD roars from Zion, and utters his voice from Jerusalem, and the heavens and the earth shake. But the LORD is a refuge to his people, a stronghold to the people of Israel. ^joel-03-16

17 “So you shall know that I am the LORD your God, who dwell in Zion, my holy mountain. And Jerusalem shall be holy and strangers shall never again pass through it. ^joel-03-17

18 “And in that day the mountains shall drip sweet wine, and the hills shall flow with milk, and all the stream beds of Judah shall flow with water; and a fountain shall come forth from the house of the LORD and water the valley of Shittim. ^joel-03-18

19 “Egypt shall become a desolation and E'dom a desolate wilderness, for the violence done to the people of Judah, because they have shed innocent blood in their land. ^joel-03-19

20 But Judah shall be inhabited for ever, and Jerusalem to all generations. ^joel-03-20

21 I will avenge their blood, and I will not clear the guilty, for the LORD dwells in Zion.” ^joel-03-21
