# Numbers 13 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[numbers-13|NABRE]]

**Spies Sent into Canaan**

1 The LORD said to Moses, ^numbers-13-1

2 “Send men to spy out the land of Canaan, which I give to the sons of Israel; from each tribe of their fathers shall you send a man, every one a leader among them.” ^numbers-13-2

3 So Moses sent them from the wilderness of Par'an, according to the command of the LORD, all of them men who were heads of the sons of Israel. ^numbers-13-3

4 And these were their names: From the tribe of Reuben, Sham'mu-a the son of Zaccur; ^numbers-13-4

5 from the tribe of Simeon, Sha'phat the son of Ho'ri; ^numbers-13-5

6 from the tribe of Judah, Caleb the son of Jephun'neh; ^numbers-13-6

7 from the tribe of Is'sachar, I'gal the son of Joseph; ^numbers-13-7

8 from the tribe of E'phraim, Hoshe'a the son of Nun; ^numbers-13-8

9 from the tribe of Benjamin, Palti the son of Ra'phu; ^numbers-13-9

10 from the tribe of Zeb'ulun, Gad'diel the son of Sodi; ^numbers-13-10

11 from the tribe of Joseph (that is from the tribe of Manas'seh), Gaddi the son of Susi; ^numbers-13-11

12 from the tribe of Dan, Am'miel the son of Gemal'li; ^numbers-13-12

13 from the tribe of Asher, Seth'ur the son of Michael; ^numbers-13-13

14 from the tribe of Naph'tali, Nahbi the son of Voph'si; ^numbers-13-14

15 from the tribe of Gad, Geu'el the son of Machi. ^numbers-13-15

16 These were the names of the men whom Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses called Hoshe'a the son of Nun Joshua. ^numbers-13-16

17 Moses sent them to spy out the land of Canaan, and said to them, “Go up into the Neg'eb yonder, and go up into the hill country, ^numbers-13-17

18 and see what the land is, and whether the people who dwell in it are strong or weak, whether they are few or many, ^numbers-13-18

19 and whether the land that they dwell in is good or bad, and whether the cities that they dwell in are camps or strongholds, ^numbers-13-19

20 and whether the land is rich or poor, and whether there is wood in it or not. Be of good courage, and bring some of the fruit of the land.” Now the time was the season of the first ripe grapes. ^numbers-13-20

21 So they went up and spied out the land from the wilderness of Zin to Re'hob, near the entrance of Ha'math. ^numbers-13-21

22 They went up into the Neg'eb, and came to He'bron; and Ahi'man, Sheshai, and Talmai, the descendants of A'nak, were there. (Hebron was built seven years before Zoan in Egypt.) ^numbers-13-22

23 And they came to the Valley of Eshcol, and cut down from there a branch with a single cluster of grapes, and they carried it on a pole between two of them; they brought also some pomegranates and figs. ^numbers-13-23

24 That place was called the Valley of Eshcol, because of the cluster which the men of Israel cut down from there. ^numbers-13-24

25 At the end of forty days they returned from spying out the land. ^numbers-13-25

26 And they came to Moses and Aaron and to all the congregation of the sons of Israel in the wilderness of Par'an, at Ka'desh; they brought back word to them and to all the congregation, and showed them the fruit of the land. ^numbers-13-26

27 And they told him, “We came to the land to which you sent us; it flows with milk and honey, and this is its fruit. ^numbers-13-27

28 Yet the people who dwell in the land are strong, and the cities are fortified and very large; and besides, we saw the descendants of A'nak there. ^numbers-13-28

29 The Amal'ekites dwell in the land of the Neg'eb; the Hittites, the Jeb'usites, and the Am'orites dwell in the hill country; and the Canaanites dwell by the sea, and along the Jordan.” ^numbers-13-29

30 But Caleb quieted the people before Moses, and said, “Let us go up at once, and occupy it; for we are well able to overcome it.” ^numbers-13-30

31 Then the men who had gone up with him said, “We are not able to go up against the people; for they are stronger than we.” ^numbers-13-31

32 So they brought to the sons of Israel an evil report of the land which they had spied out, saying, “The land, through which we have gone to spy it out, is a land that devours its inhabitants; and all the people that we saw in it are men of great stature. ^numbers-13-32

33 And there we saw the Neph'ilim (the sons of A'nak, who come from the Nephilim); and we seemed to ourselves like grasshoppers, and so we seemed to them.” ^numbers-13-33
