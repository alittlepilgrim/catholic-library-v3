# Numbers 9 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[numbers-09|NABRE]]

**The Passover Kept at Sinai**

1 And the LORD spoke to Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they had come out of the land of Egypt, saying, ^numbers-09-1

2 “Let the sons of Israel keep the Passover at its appointed time. ^numbers-09-2

3 On the fourteenth day of this month, in the evening, you shall keep it at its appointed time; according to all its statutes and all its ordinances you shall keep it.” ^numbers-09-3

4 So Moses told the sons of Israel that they should keep the Passover. ^numbers-09-4

5 And they kept the Passover in the first month, on the fourteenth day of the month, in the evening, in the wilderness of Sinai; according to all that the LORD commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel did. ^numbers-09-5

6 And there were certain men who were unclean through touching the dead body of a man, so that they could not keep the Passover on that day; and they came before Moses and Aaron on that day; ^numbers-09-6

7 and those men said to him, “We are unclean through touching the dead body of a man; why are we kept from offering the LORD’s offering at its appointed time among the sons of Israel?” ^numbers-09-7

8 And Moses said to them, “Wait, that I may hear what the LORD will command concerning you.” ^numbers-09-8

9 The LORD said to Moses, ^numbers-09-9

10 “Say to the sons of Israel, If any man of you or of your descendants is unclean through touching a dead body, or is afar off on a journey, he shall still keep the Passover to the LORD. ^numbers-09-10

11 In the second month on the fourteenth day in the evening they shall keep it; they shall eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs. ^numbers-09-11

12 They shall leave none of it until the morning, nor break a bone of it; according to all the statute for the Passover they shall keep it. ^numbers-09-12

13 But the man who is clean and is not on a journey, yet refrains from keeping the Passover, that person shall be cut off from his people, because he did not offer the LORD’s offering at its appointed time; that man shall bear his sin. ^numbers-09-13

14 And if a stranger sojourns among you, and will keep the Passover to the LORD, according to the statute of the Passover and according to its ordinance, so shall he do; you shall have one statute, both for the sojourner and for the native.” ^numbers-09-14

**The Cloud and the Fire**

15 On the day that the tabernacle was set up, the cloud covered the tabernacle, the tent of the covenant; and at evening it was over the tabernacle like the appearance of fire until morning. ^numbers-09-15

16 So it was continually; the cloud covered it by day, and the appearance of fire by night. ^numbers-09-16

17 And whenever the cloud was taken up from over the tent, after that the sons of Israel set out; and in the place where the cloud settled down, there the sons of Israel encamped. ^numbers-09-17

18 At the command of the LORD the sons of Israel set out, and at the command of the LORD they encamped; as long as the cloud rested over the tabernacle, they remained in camp. ^numbers-09-18

19 Even when the cloud continued over the tabernacle many days, the sons of Israel kept the charge of the LORD, and did not set out. ^numbers-09-19

20 Sometimes the cloud was a few days over the tabernacle, and according to the command of the LORD they remained in camp; then according to the command of the LORD they set out. ^numbers-09-20

21 And sometimes the cloud remained from evening until morning; and when the cloud was taken up in the morning, they set out, or if it continued for a day and a night, when the cloud was taken up they set out. ^numbers-09-21

22 Whether it was two days, or a month, or a longer time, that the cloud continued over the tabernacle, abiding there, the sons of Israel remained in camp and did not set out; but when it was taken up they set out. ^numbers-09-22

23 At the command of the LORD they encamped, and at the command of the LORD they set out; they kept the charge of the LORD, at the command of the LORD by Moses. ^numbers-09-23
