# Numbers 20 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[numbers-20|NABRE]]

**The Waters of Meribah**

1 And the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came into the wilderness of Zin in the first month, and the people stayed in Ka'desh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there. ^numbers-20-1

2 Now there was no water for the congregation; and they assembled themselves together against Moses and against Aaron. ^numbers-20-2

3 And the people contended with Moses, and said, “Would that we had died when our brethren died before the LORD! ^numbers-20-3

4 Why have you brought the assembly of the LORD into this wilderness, that we should die here, both we and our cattle? ^numbers-20-4

5 And why have you made us come up out of Egypt, to bring us to this evil place? It is no place for grain, or figs, or vines, or pomegranates; and there is no water to drink.” ^numbers-20-5

6 Then Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly to the door of the tent of meeting, and fell on their faces. And the glory of the LORD appeared to them, ^numbers-20-6

7 and the LORD said to Moses, ^numbers-20-7

8 “Take the rod, and assemble the congregation, you and Aaron your brother, and tell the rock before their eyes to yield its water; so you shall bring water out of the rock for them; so you shall give drink to the congregation and their cattle.” ^numbers-20-8

9 And Moses took the rod from before the LORD, as he commanded him. ^numbers-20-9

10 And Moses and Aaron gathered the assembly together before the rock, and he said to them, “Hear now, you rebels; shall we bring forth water for you out of this rock?” ^numbers-20-10

11 And Moses lifted up his hand and struck the rock with his rod twice; and water came forth abundantly, and the congregation drank, and their cattle. ^numbers-20-11

12 And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron, “Because you did not believe in me, to sanctify me in the eyes of the sons of Israel, therefore you shall not bring this assembly into the land which I have given them.” ^numbers-20-12

13 These are the waters of Mer'ibah, where the sons of Israel contended with the LORD, and he showed himself holy among them. ^numbers-20-13

**Passage through Edom Refused**

14 Moses sent messengers from Ka'desh to the king of E'dom, “Thus says your brother Israel: You know all the adversity that has befallen us: ^numbers-20-14

15 how our fathers went down to Egypt, and we dwelt in Egypt a long time; and the Egyptians dealt harshly with us and our fathers; ^numbers-20-15

16 and when we cried to the LORD, he heard our voice, and sent an angel and brought us forth out of Egypt; and here we are in Ka'desh, a city on the edge of your territory. ^numbers-20-16

17 Now let us pass through your land. We will not pass through field or vineyard, neither will we drink water from a well; we will go along the King’s Highway, we will not turn aside to the right hand or to the left, until we have passed through your territory.” ^numbers-20-17

18 But E'dom said to him, “You shall not pass through, lest I come out with the sword against you.” ^numbers-20-18

19 And the sons of Israel said to him, “We will go up by the highway; and if we drink of your water, I and my cattle, then I will pay for it; let me only pass through on foot, nothing more.” ^numbers-20-19

20 But he said, “You shall not pass through.” And E'dom came out against them with many men, and with a strong force. ^numbers-20-20

21 Thus E'dom refused to give Israel passage through his territory; so Israel turned away from him. ^numbers-20-21

**The Death of Aaron**

22 And they journeyed from Ka'desh, and the sons of Israel, the whole congregation, came to Mount Hor. ^numbers-20-22

23 And the LORD said to Moses and Aaron at Mount Hor, on the border of the land of E'dom, ^numbers-20-23

24 “Aaron shall be gathered to his people; for he shall not enter the land which I have given to the sons of Israel, because you rebelled against my command at the waters of Mer'ibah. ^numbers-20-24

25 Take Aaron and Elea'zar his son, and bring them up to Mount Hor; ^numbers-20-25

26 and strip Aaron of his garments, and put them upon Elea'zar his son; and Aaron shall be gathered to his people, and shall die there.” ^numbers-20-26

27 Moses did as the LORD commanded; and they went up Mount Hor in the sight of the congregation. ^numbers-20-27

28 And Moses stripped Aaron of his garments, and put them upon Elea'zar his son; and Aaron died there on the top of the mountain. Then Moses and Eleazar came down from the mountain. ^numbers-20-28

29 And when all the congregation saw that Aaron was dead, all the house of Israel wept for Aaron thirty days. ^numbers-20-29
