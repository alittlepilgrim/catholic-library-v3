# Numbers 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[numbers-25|NABRE]]

**Worship of Baal of Peor**

1 While Israel dwelt in Shittim the people began to play the harlot with the daughters of Moab. ^numbers-25-1

2 These invited the people to the sacrifices of their gods, and the people ate, and bowed down to their gods. ^numbers-25-2

3 So Israel yoked himself to Ba'al of Peor. And the anger of the LORD was kindled against Israel; ^numbers-25-3

4 and the LORD said to Moses, “Take all the chiefs of the people, and hang them in the sun before the LORD, that the fierce anger of the LORD may turn away from Israel.” ^numbers-25-4

5 And Moses said to the judges of Israel, “Every one of you slay his men who have yoked themselves to Ba'al of Peor.” ^numbers-25-5

6 And behold, one of the sons of Israel came and brought a Mid'ianite woman to his family, in the sight of Moses and in the sight of the whole congregation of the sons of Israel, while they were weeping at the door of the tent of meeting. ^numbers-25-6

7 When Phin'ehas the son of Elea'zar, son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose and left the congregation, and took a spear in his hand ^numbers-25-7

8 and went after the man of Israel into the inner room, and pierced both of them, the man of Israel and the woman, through her body. Thus the plague was stayed from the sons of Israel. ^numbers-25-8

9 Nevertheless those that died by the plague were twenty-four thousand. ^numbers-25-9

10 And the LORD said to Moses, ^numbers-25-10

11 “Phin'ehas the son of Elea'zar, son of Aaron the priest, has turned back my wrath from the sons of Israel, in that he was jealous with my jealousy among them, so that I did not consume the sons of Israel in my jealousy. ^numbers-25-11

12 Therefore say, ‘Behold, I give to him my covenant of peace; ^numbers-25-12

13 and it shall be to him, and to his descendants after him, the covenant of a perpetual priesthood, because he was jealous for his God, and made atonement for the sons of Israel.’ ” ^numbers-25-13

14 The name of the slain man of Israel, who was slain with the Mid'ianite woman, was Zimri the son of Sa'lu, head of a fathers’ house belonging to the Simeonites. ^numbers-25-14

15 And the name of the Mid'ianite woman who was slain was Cozbi the daughter of Zur, who was the head of the people of a fathers’ house in Mid'ian. ^numbers-25-15

16 And the LORD said to Moses, ^numbers-25-16

17 “Harass the Mid'ianites, and strike them; ^numbers-25-17

18 for they have harassed you with their wiles, with which they beguiled you in the matter of Peor, and in the matter of Cozbi, the daughter of the prince of Mid'ian, their sister, who was slain on the day of the plague on account of Peor.” ^numbers-25-18
