# 1 Chronicles 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-08|NABRE]]

**Descendants of Benjamin in Detail**

1 Benjamin was the father of Be'la his first-born, Ash'bel the second, A'harah the third, ^1-chronicles-08-1

2 No'hah the fourth, and Ra'pha the fifth. ^1-chronicles-08-2

3 And Be'la had sons: Addar, Gera, Abi'hud, ^1-chronicles-08-3

4 Abishu'a, Na'aman, Aho'ah, ^1-chronicles-08-4

5 Gera, Shephu'phan, and Hu'ram. ^1-chronicles-08-5

6 These are the sons of E'hud (they were heads of fathers’ houses of the inhabitants of Ge'ba, and they were carried into exile to Mana'hath): ^1-chronicles-08-6

7 Na'aman, Ahi'jah, and Gera, that is, Heglam, who was the father of Uzza and Ahi'hud. ^1-chronicles-08-7

8 And Sha''hara'im had sons in the country of Moab after he had sent away Hu'shim and Ba'ara his wives. ^1-chronicles-08-8

9 He had sons by Hodesh his wife: Jo'bab, Zib'ia, Me'sha, Malcam, ^1-chronicles-08-9

10 Je'uz, Sachi'a, and Mirmah. These were his sons, heads of fathers’ houses. ^1-chronicles-08-10

11 He also had sons by Hu'shim: Abi'tub and Elpa'al. ^1-chronicles-08-11

12 The sons of Elpa'al: E'ber, Misham, and She'med, who built Ono and Lod with its towns, ^1-chronicles-08-12

13 and Beri'ah and She'ma (they were heads of fathers’ houses of the inhabitants of Ai'jalon, who put to flight the inhabitants of Gath); ^1-chronicles-08-13

14 and Ahi'o, Shashak, and Jer'emoth. ^1-chronicles-08-14

15 Zebadi'ah, Ar'ad, E'der, ^1-chronicles-08-15

16 Michael, Ishpah, and Joha were sons of Beri'ah. ^1-chronicles-08-16

17 Zebadi'ah, Meshul'lam, Hizki, He'ber, ^1-chronicles-08-17

18 Ish'merai, Izli'ah, and Jo'bab were the sons of Elpa'al. ^1-chronicles-08-18

19 Ja'kim, Zich'ri, Zabdi, ^1-chronicles-08-19

20 E'li-e'nai, Zil'le-thai, Eli'el, ^1-chronicles-08-20

21 Adai'ah, Bera'iah, and Shimrath were the sons of Shim'e-i. ^1-chronicles-08-21

22 Ishpan, E'ber, Eli'el, ^1-chronicles-08-22

23 Abdon, Zich'ri, Ha'nan, ^1-chronicles-08-23

24 Hanani'ah, E'lam, Anthothi'jah, ^1-chronicles-08-24

25 Iphde'iah, and Penu'el were the sons of Shashak. ^1-chronicles-08-25

26 Sham'sherai, Shehari'ah, Athali'ah, ^1-chronicles-08-26

27 Ja-areshi'ah, Eli'jah, and Zich'ri were the sons of Jero'ham. ^1-chronicles-08-27

28 These were the heads of fathers’ houses, according to their generations, chief men. These dwelt in Jerusalem. ^1-chronicles-08-28

29 Je-i'el the father of Gib'eon dwelt in Gibeon, and the name of his wife was Ma'acah. ^1-chronicles-08-29

30 His first-born son: Abdon, then Zur, Kish, Ba'al, Na'dab, ^1-chronicles-08-30

31 Gedor, Ahi'o, Zecher, ^1-chronicles-08-31

32 and Mikloth (he was the father of Shim'e-ah). Now these also dwelt opposite their kinsmen in Jerusalem, with their kinsmen. ^1-chronicles-08-32

33 Ner was the father of Kish, Kish of Saul, Saul of Jonathan, Mal''chishu'a, Abin'adab, and Eshba'al; ^1-chronicles-08-33

34 and the son of Jonathan was Mer'ib-ba'al; and Merib-baal was the father of Micah. ^1-chronicles-08-34

35 The sons of Micah: Pithon, Melech, Tare'a, and A'haz. ^1-chronicles-08-35

36 A'haz was the father of Jeho'addah; and Jehoaddah was the father of Al'emeth, Az'maveth, and Zimri; Zimri was the father of Moza. ^1-chronicles-08-36

37 Moza was the father of Bin'e-a; Ra'phah was his son, El-e-a'sah his son, A'zel his son. ^1-chronicles-08-37

38 A'zel had six sons, and these are their names: Azri'kam, Bo'cheru, Ish'mael, Sheari'ah, Obadi'ah, and Ha'nan. All these were the sons of Azel. ^1-chronicles-08-38

39 The sons of E'shek his brother: Ulam his first-born, Je'ush the second, and Eliph'elet the third. ^1-chronicles-08-39

40 The sons of Ulam were men who were mighty warriors, bowmen, having many sons and grandsons, one hundred and fifty. All these were Benjaminites. ^1-chronicles-08-40
