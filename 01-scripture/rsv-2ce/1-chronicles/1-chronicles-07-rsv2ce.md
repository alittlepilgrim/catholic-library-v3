# 1 Chronicles 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-07|NABRE]]

**Descendants of Issachar**

1 The sons of Is'sachar: To'la, Puah, Jash'ub, and Shim'ron, four. ^1-chronicles-07-1

2 The sons of To'la: Uzzi, Rephai'ah, Je'ri-el, Jahmai, Ibsam, and Shemu'el, heads of their fathers’ houses, namely of Tola, mighty warriors of their generations, their number in the days of David being twenty-two thousand six hundred. ^1-chronicles-07-2

3 The sons of Uzzi: Izrahi'ah. And the sons of Izrahiah: Michael, Obadi'ah, Joel, and Isshi'ah, five, all of them chief men; ^1-chronicles-07-3

4 and along with them, by their generations, according to their fathers’ houses, were units of the army for war, thirty-six thousand, for they had many wives and sons. ^1-chronicles-07-4

5 Their kinsmen belonging to all the families of Is'sachar were in all eighty-seven thousand mighty warriors, enrolled by genealogy. ^1-chronicles-07-5

**Descendants of Benjamin**

6 The sons of Benjamin: Be'la, Be'cher, and Jedi'a-el, three. ^1-chronicles-07-6

7 The sons of Be'la: Ezbon, Uzzi, Uz'ziel, Jer'imoth, and Iri, five, heads of fathers’ houses, mighty warriors; and their enrollment by genealogies was twenty-two thousand and thirty-four. ^1-chronicles-07-7

8 The sons of Be'cher: Zemi'rah, Jo'ash, Elie'zer, El'i-o-e'nai, Omri, Jer'emoth, Abi'jah, An'athoth, and Al'emeth. All these were the sons of Becher; ^1-chronicles-07-8

9 and their enrollment by genealogies, according to their generations, as heads of their fathers’ houses, mighty warriors, was twenty thousand two hundred. ^1-chronicles-07-9

10 The sons of Jedi'a-el: Bilhan. And the sons of Bilhan: Je'ush, Benjamin, E'hud, Chena'anah, Zethan, Tar'shish, and Ahish'ahar. ^1-chronicles-07-10

11 All these were the sons of Jedi'a-el according to the heads of their fathers’ houses, mighty warriors, seventeen thousand and two hundred, ready for service in war. ^1-chronicles-07-11

12 And Shuppim and Huppim were the sons of Ir, Hu'shim the sons of A'her. ^1-chronicles-07-12

**Descendants of Naphtali**

13 The sons of Naph'tali: Jah'zi-el, Gu'ni, Je'zer, and Shallum, the offspring of Bilhah. ^1-chronicles-07-13

**Descendants of Manasseh**

14 The sons of Manas'seh: As'ri-el, whom his Arame'an concubine bore; she bore Ma'chir the father of Gilead. ^1-chronicles-07-14

15 And Ma'chir took a wife for Huppim and for Shuppim. The name of his sister was Ma'acah. And the name of the second was Zeloph'ehad; and Zelophehad had daughters. ^1-chronicles-07-15

16 And Ma'acah the wife of Ma'chir bore a son, and she called his name Peresh; and the name of his brother was Sheresh; and his sons were Ulam and Rakem. ^1-chronicles-07-16

17 The sons of Ulam: Be'dan. These were the sons of Gilead the son of Ma'chir, son of Manas'seh. ^1-chronicles-07-17

18 And his sister Hammo'lecheth bore Ish'hod, Abie'zer, and Mahlah. ^1-chronicles-07-18

19 The sons of Shemi'da were Ahi'an, She'chem, Likhi, and Ani'am. ^1-chronicles-07-19

**Descendants of Ephraim**

20 The sons of E'phraim: Shuthe'lah, and Be'red his son, Ta'hath his son, Ele-a'dah his son, Tahath his son, ^1-chronicles-07-20

21 Zabad his son, Shuthe'lah his son, and E'zer and E'le-ad, whom the men of Gath who were born in the land slew, because they came down to raid their cattle. ^1-chronicles-07-21

22 And E'phraim their father mourned many days, and his brothers came to comfort him. ^1-chronicles-07-22

23 And E'phraim went in to his wife, and she conceived and bore a son; and he called his name Beri'ah, because evil had befallen his house. ^1-chronicles-07-23

24 His daughter was She'erah, who built both Lower and Upper Beth-ho'ron, and Uz'zen-she'erah. ^1-chronicles-07-24

25 Rephah was his son, Resheph his son, Te'lah his son, Ta'han his son, ^1-chronicles-07-25

26 Ladan his son, Ammi'hud his son, Elish'ama his son, ^1-chronicles-07-26

27 Nun his son, Joshua his son. ^1-chronicles-07-27

28 Their possessions and settlements were Bethel and its towns, and eastward Na'aran, and westward Gezer and its towns, She'chem and its towns, and Ayyah and its towns; ^1-chronicles-07-28

29 also along the borders of the Manas'sites, Beth-she'an and its towns, Ta'anach and its towns, Megid'do and its towns, Dor and its towns. In these dwelt the sons of Joseph the son of Israel. ^1-chronicles-07-29

**Descendants of Asher**

30 The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beri'ah, and their sister Se'rah. ^1-chronicles-07-30

31 The sons of Beri'ah: He'ber and Mal'chi-el, who was the father of Bir'zaith. ^1-chronicles-07-31

32 He'ber was the father of Japhlet, Shomer, Ho'tham, and their sister Shua. ^1-chronicles-07-32

33 The sons of Japhlet: Pasach, Bimhal, and Ashvath. These are the sons of Japhlet. ^1-chronicles-07-33

34 The sons of She'mer his brother: Rohgah, Jehub'bah, and Ar'am. ^1-chronicles-07-34

35 The sons of He'lem his brother: Zo'phah, Imna, Shelesh, and A'mal. ^1-chronicles-07-35

36 The sons of Zo'phah: Su'ah, Har'nepher, Shual, Beri, Imrah, ^1-chronicles-07-36

37 Bezer, Hod, Shamma, Shilshah, Ithran, and Be-e'ra. ^1-chronicles-07-37

38 The sons of Je'ther: Jephun'neh, Pispa, and Ar'a. ^1-chronicles-07-38

39 The sons of Ulla: A'rah, Han'niel, and Rizi'a. ^1-chronicles-07-39

40 All of these were men of Asher, heads of fathers’ houses, approved, mighty warriors, chief of the princes. Their number enrolled by genealogies, for service in war, was twenty-six thousand men. ^1-chronicles-07-40
