# 1 Chronicles 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-03|NABRE]]

**Descendants of David and Solomon**

1 These are the sons of David that were born to him in He'bron: the first-born Amnon, by Ahin'o-am the Jezre'elitess; the second Daniel, by Ab'igail the Car'melitess, ^1-chronicles-03-1

2 the third Ab'salom, whose mother was Ma'acah, the daughter of Talmai, king of Ge'shur; the fourth Adoni'jah, whose mother was Haggith; ^1-chronicles-03-2

3 the fifth Shephati'ah, by Abi'tal; the sixth Ith're-am, by his wife Eglah; ^1-chronicles-03-3

4 six were born to him in He'bron, where he reigned for seven years and six months. And he reigned thirty-three years in Jerusalem. ^1-chronicles-03-4

5 These were born to him in Jerusalem: Shim'e-a, Sho'bab, Nathan, and Solomon, four by Bath-shu'a, the daughter of Am'mi-el; ^1-chronicles-03-5

6 then Ibhar, Elish'ama, Eliph'elet, ^1-chronicles-03-6

7 No'gah, Ne'pheg, Japhi'a, ^1-chronicles-03-7

8 Elish'ama, Eli'ada, and Eliph'elet, nine. ^1-chronicles-03-8

9 All these were David’s sons, besides the sons of the concubines; and Ta'mar was their sister. ^1-chronicles-03-9

10 The descendants of Solomon: Rehobo'am, Abi'jah his son, Asa his son, Jehosh'aphat his son, ^1-chronicles-03-10

11 Jo'ram his son, Ahazi'ah his son, Jo'ash his son, ^1-chronicles-03-11

12 Amazi'ah his son, Azari'ah his son, Jo'tham his son, ^1-chronicles-03-12

13 A'haz his son, Hezeki'ah his son, Manas'seh his son, ^1-chronicles-03-13

14 A'mon his son, Josi'ah his son. ^1-chronicles-03-14

15 The sons of Josi'ah: Joha'nan the first-born, the second Jehoi'akim, the third Zedeki'ah, the fourth Shallum. ^1-chronicles-03-15

16 The descendants of Jehoi'akim: Jeconi'ah his son, Zedeki'ah his son; ^1-chronicles-03-16

17 and the sons of Jeconi'ah, the captive: She-al'ti-el his son, ^1-chronicles-03-17

18 Malchi'ram, Pedai'ah, Shenaz'zar, Jekami'ah, Hosh'ama, and Nedabi'ah; ^1-chronicles-03-18

19 and the sons of Pedai'ah: Zerub'babel and Shim'e-i; and the sons of Zerubbabel: Meshul'lam and Hanani'ah, and Shelo'mith was their sister; ^1-chronicles-03-19

20 and Hashu'bah, O'hel, Berechi'ah, Hasadi'ah, and Ju'shab-he'sed, five. ^1-chronicles-03-20

21 The sons of Hanani'ah: Pelati'ah and Jesha'iah, his son Rephai'ah, his son Arnan, his son Obadi'ah, his son Shecani'ah. ^1-chronicles-03-21

22 The sons of Shecani'ah: Shemai'ah. And the sons of Shemaiah: Hattush, I'gal, Bari'ah, Neari'ah, and Sha'phat, six. ^1-chronicles-03-22

23 The sons of Neari'ah: El'i-o-e'nai, Hizki'aj, and Azri'kam, three. ^1-chronicles-03-23

24 The sons of El'i-o-e'nai: Hod''avi'ah, Eli'ashib, Pelai'ah, Akkub, Joha'nan, Delai'ah, and Ana'ni, seven. ^1-chronicles-03-24
