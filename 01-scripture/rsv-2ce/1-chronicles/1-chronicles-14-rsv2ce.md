# 1 Chronicles 14 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-14|NABRE]]

**David Established in Jerusalem**

1 And Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and cedar trees, also masons and carpenters to build a house for him. ^1-chronicles-14-1

2 And David perceived that the LORD had established him king over Israel, and that his kingdom was highly exalted for the sake of his people Israel. ^1-chronicles-14-2

3 And David took more wives in Jerusalem, and David begot more sons and daughters. ^1-chronicles-14-3

4 These are the names of the children whom he had in Jerusalem: Sham'mu-a, Sho'bab, Nathan, Solomon, ^1-chronicles-14-4

5 Ibhar, Eli'shu-a, El'pelet, ^1-chronicles-14-5

6 No'gah, Ne'pheg, Japhi'a, ^1-chronicles-14-6

7 Elish'ama, Beeli'ada, and Eliph'elet. ^1-chronicles-14-7

**Defeat of the Philistines**

8 When the Philis'tines heard that David had been anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up in search of David; and David heard of it and went out against them. ^1-chronicles-14-8

9 Now the Philis'tines had come and made a raid in the valley of Reph'aim. ^1-chronicles-14-9

10 And David inquired of God, “Shall I go up against the Philis'tines? Will you give them into my hand?” And the LORD said to him, “Go up, and I will give them into your hand.” ^1-chronicles-14-10

11 And he went up to Ba'al-pera'zim, and David defeated them there; and David said, “God has broken through my enemies by my hand, like a bursting flood.” Therefore the name of that place is called Baal-perazim. ^1-chronicles-14-11

12 And they left their gods there, and David gave command, and they were burned. ^1-chronicles-14-12

13 And the Philis'tines yet again made a raid in the valley. ^1-chronicles-14-13

14 And when David again inquired of God, God said to him, “You shall not go up after them; go around and come upon them opposite the balsam trees. ^1-chronicles-14-14

15 And when you hear the sound of marching in the tops of the balsam trees, then go out to battle; for God has gone out before you to strike the army of the Philis'tines.” ^1-chronicles-14-15

16 And David did as God commanded him, and they struck the Philis'tine army from Gib'eon to Gezer. ^1-chronicles-14-16

17 And the fame of David went out into all lands, and the LORD brought the fear of him upon all nations. ^1-chronicles-14-17
