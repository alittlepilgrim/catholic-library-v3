# 1 Chronicles 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-04|NABRE]]

**Descendants of Judah**

1 The sons of Judah: Per'ez, Hezron, Carmi, Hur, and Sho'bal. ^1-chronicles-04-1

2 Re-ai'ah the son of Sho'bal was the father of Jahath, and Jahath was the father of Ahu'mai and Lahad. These were the families of the Zo'rathites. ^1-chronicles-04-2

3 These were the sons of E'tam: Jezre'el, Ishma, and Idbash; and the name of their sister was Hazzelelpo'ni, ^1-chronicles-04-3

4 and Penu'el was the father of Gedor, and E'zer the father of Hu'shah. These were the sons of Hur, the first-born of Eph'rathah, the father of Bethlehem. ^1-chronicles-04-4

5 Ash'hur, the father of Teko'a, had two wives, He'lah and Na'arah; ^1-chronicles-04-5

6 Na'arah bore him Ahuz'zam, He'pher, Te'meni, and Ha-a-hash'tari. These were the sons of Naarah. ^1-chronicles-04-6

7 The sons of He'lah: Ze'reth, Izhar, and Ethnan. ^1-chronicles-04-7

8 Koz was the father of A'nub, Zobe'bah, and the families of Ahar'hel the son of Harum. ^1-chronicles-04-8

9 Ja'bez was more honorable than his brothers; and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, “Because I bore him in pain.” ^1-chronicles-04-9

10 Ja'bez called on the God of Israel, saying, “Oh that you would bless me and enlarge my border, and that your hand might be with me, and that you would keep me from harm so that it might not hurt me!” And God granted what he asked. ^1-chronicles-04-10

11 Che'lub, the brother of Shu'hah, was the father of Me'hir, who was the father of Eshton. ^1-chronicles-04-11

12 Eshton was the father of Bethra'pha, Pase'ah, and Tehin'nah the father of Irna'hash. These are the men of Re'cah. ^1-chronicles-04-12

13 The sons of Ke'naz: Oth'ni-el and Serai'ah; and the sons of Othni-el: Ha'thath and Meo'nothai. ^1-chronicles-04-13

14 Meo'nothai was the father of Oph'rah; and Serai'ah was the father of Jo'ab the father of Ge-har'ashim, so-called because they were craftsmen. ^1-chronicles-04-14

15 The sons of Caleb the son of Jephun'neh: Iru, E'lah, and Na'am; and the sons of Elah: Ke'naz. ^1-chronicles-04-15

16 The sons of Jehal'lelel: Ziph, Ziphah, Tir'i-a, and As'arel. ^1-chronicles-04-16

17 The sons of Ezrah: Je'ther, Me'red, E'pher, and Ja'lon. These are the sons of Bith'i-ah, the daughter of Pharaoh, whom Mered married; and she conceived and bore Miriam, Sham'mai, and Ish'bah, the father of Eshtemo'a. ^1-chronicles-04-17

18 And his Jewish wife bore Je'red the father of Gedor, He'ber the father of Soco, and Jeku'thiel the father of Zano'ah. ^1-chronicles-04-18

19 The sons of the wife of Hodi'ah, the sister of Na'ham, were the fathers of Kei'lah the Garmite and Eshtemo'a the Ma-ac'athite. ^1-chronicles-04-19

20 The sons of Shimon: Amnon, Rinnah, Ben-ha'nan, and Ti'lon. The sons of Ishi: Zoheth and Ben-zo'heth. ^1-chronicles-04-20

21 The sons of She'lah the son of Judah: Er the father of Lecah, La'adah the father of Mare'shah, and the families of the house of linen workers at Beth-ashbe'a; ^1-chronicles-04-21

22 and Jokim, and the men of Coze'ba, and Jo'ash, and Saraph, who ruled in Moab and returned to Lehem (now the records are ancient). ^1-chronicles-04-22

23 These were the potters and inhabitants of Neta'im and Gede'rah; they dwelt there with the king for his work. ^1-chronicles-04-23

**Descendants of Simeon**

24 The sons of Simeon: Nem'u-el, Ja'min, Ja'rib, Ze'rah, Sha'ul; ^1-chronicles-04-24

25 Shallum was his son, Mib'sam his son, Mishma his son. ^1-chronicles-04-25

26 The sons of Mishma: Ham'mu-el his son, Zaccur his son, Shim'e-i his son. ^1-chronicles-04-26

27 Shim'e-i had sixteen sons and six daughters; but his brothers had not many children, nor did all their family multiply like the men of Judah. ^1-chronicles-04-27

28 They dwelt in Be'er-she'ba, Mo'ladah, Ha'zar-shu'al, ^1-chronicles-04-28

29 Bilhah, E'zem, To'lad, ^1-chronicles-04-29

30 Bethu'el, Hormah, Zik'lag, ^1-chronicles-04-30

31 Beth-mar'caboth, Ha'zar-su'sim, Beth-bir'i, and Sha-ara'im. These were their cities until David reigned. ^1-chronicles-04-31

32 And their villages were E'tam, A'in, Rimmon, Tochen, and A'shan, five cities, ^1-chronicles-04-32

33 along with all their villages which were round about these cities as far as Ba'al. These were their settlements, and they kept a genealogical record. ^1-chronicles-04-33

34 Mesho'bab, Jamlech, Joshah the son of Amazi'ah, ^1-chronicles-04-34

35 Joel, Je'hu the son of Joshibi'ah, son of Serai'ah, son of As'i-el, ^1-chronicles-04-35

36 Eli-o-e'nai, Ja''ako'bah, Jeshohai'ah, Asai'ah, Ad'i-el, Jesim'i-el, Bena'iah, ^1-chronicles-04-36

37 Ziza the son of Shi'phi, son of Allon, son of Jedai'ah, son of Shimri, son of Shemai'ah— ^1-chronicles-04-37

38 these mentioned by name were princes in their families, and their fathers’ houses increased greatly. ^1-chronicles-04-38

39 They journeyed to the entrance of Gedor, to the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks, ^1-chronicles-04-39

40 where they found rich, good pasture, and the land was very broad, quiet, and peaceful; for the former inhabitants there belonged to Ham. ^1-chronicles-04-40

41 These, registered by name, came in the days of Hezeki'ah, king of Judah, and destroyed their tents and the Me-u'nim who were found there, and exterminated them to this day, and settled in their place, because there was pasture there for their flocks. ^1-chronicles-04-41

42 And some of them, five hundred men of the Simeonites, went to Mount Se'ir, having as their leaders Pelati'ah, Ne-ari'ah, Rephai'ah, and Uz'ziel, the sons of Ishi; ^1-chronicles-04-42

43 and they destroyed the remnant of the Amal'ekites that had escaped, and they have dwelt there to this day. ^1-chronicles-04-43
