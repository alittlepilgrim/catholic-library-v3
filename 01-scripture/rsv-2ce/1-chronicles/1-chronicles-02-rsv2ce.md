# 1 Chronicles 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-02|NABRE]]

**Sons of Israel and Descendants of Judah**

1 These are the sons of Israel: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Is'sachar, Zeb'ulun, ^1-chronicles-02-1

2 Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naph'tali, Gad, and Ash'er. ^1-chronicles-02-2

3 The sons of Judah: Er, O'nan, and She'lah; these three Bath-shu'a the Canaanitess bore to him. Now Er, Judah’s first-born, was wicked in the sight of the LORD, and he slew him. ^1-chronicles-02-3

4 His daughter-in-law Ta'mar also bore him Per'ez and Ze'rah. Judah had five sons in all. ^1-chronicles-02-4

5 The sons of Per'ez: Hezron and Ha'mul. ^1-chronicles-02-5

6 The sons of Ze'rah: Zimri, Ethan, He'man, Calcol, and Dara, five in all. ^1-chronicles-02-6

7 The sons of Carmi: A'char, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the matter of the devoted thing; ^1-chronicles-02-7

8 and Ethan’s son was Azari'ah. ^1-chronicles-02-8

9 The sons of Hezron, that were born to him: Jerah'meel, Ram, and Chelu'bai. ^1-chronicles-02-9

10 Ram was the father of Ammin'adab, and Amminadab was the father of Nahshon, prince of the sons of Judah. ^1-chronicles-02-10

11 Nahshon was the father of Salma, Salma of Boaz, ^1-chronicles-02-11

12 Boaz of O'bed, Obed of Jesse. ^1-chronicles-02-12

13 Jesse was the father of Eli'ab his first-born, Abin'adab the second, Shim'e-a the third, ^1-chronicles-02-13

14 Nethan'el the fourth, Raddai the fifth, ^1-chronicles-02-14

15 Ozem the sixth, David the seventh; ^1-chronicles-02-15

16 and their sisters were Zeru'iah and Ab'igail. The sons of Zeruiah: Abi'shai, Jo'ab, and As'ahel, three. ^1-chronicles-02-16

17 Ab'igail bore Ama'sa, and the father of Amasa was Je'ther the Ish'maelite. ^1-chronicles-02-17

18 Caleb the son of Hezron had children by his wife Azu'bah, and by Jer'ioth; and these were her sons: Jesher, Shobab, and Ardon. ^1-chronicles-02-18

19 When Azu'bah died, Caleb married Eph'rath, who bore him Hur. ^1-chronicles-02-19

20 Hur was the father of U'ri, and Uri was the father of Bez'alel. ^1-chronicles-02-20

21 Afterward Hezron went in to the daughter of Ma'chir the father of Gilead, whom he married when he was sixty years old; and she bore him Segub; ^1-chronicles-02-21

22 and Segub was the father of Ja'ir, who had twenty-three cities in the land of Gilead. ^1-chronicles-02-22

23 But Ge'shur and Ar'am took from them Hav'voth-ja'ir, Ke'nath and its villages, sixty towns. All these were descendants of Ma'chir, the father of Gilead. ^1-chronicles-02-23

24 After the death of Hezron, Caleb went in to Eph'rathah, the wife of Hezron his father, and she bore him Ash'hur, the father of Teko'a. ^1-chronicles-02-24

25 The sons of Jerah'meel, the first-born of Hezron: Ram, his first-born, Bunah, Oren, Ozem, and Ahi'jah. ^1-chronicles-02-25

26 Jerah'meel also had another wife, whose name was At'arah; she was the mother of O'nam. ^1-chronicles-02-26

27 The sons of Ram, the first-born of Jerah'meel: Ma'az, Ja'min, and E'ker. ^1-chronicles-02-27

28 The sons of O'nam: Shammai and Jada. The sons of Shammai: Na'dab and Abi'shur. ^1-chronicles-02-28

29 The name of Abi'shur’s wife was Ab'ihail, and she bore him Ahban and Molid. ^1-chronicles-02-29

30 The sons of Na'dab: Se'led and Ap'pa-im; and Seled died childless. ^1-chronicles-02-30

31 The sons of Ap'pa-im: Ishi. The sons of Ishi: Sheshan. The sons of Sheshan: Ahlai. ^1-chronicles-02-31

32 The sons of Jada, Shammai’s brother: Je'ther and Jonathan; and Jether died childless. ^1-chronicles-02-32

33 The sons of Jonathan: Pe'leth and Zaza. These were the descendants of Jerah'meel. ^1-chronicles-02-33

34 Now Sheshan had no sons, only daughters; but Sheshan had an Egyptian slave, whose name was Jarha. ^1-chronicles-02-34

35 So Sheshan gave his daughter in marriage to Jarha his slave; and she bore him Attai. ^1-chronicles-02-35

36 Attai was the father of Nathan, and Nathan of Zabad. ^1-chronicles-02-36

37 Zabad was the father of Ephlal, and Ephlal of O'bed. ^1-chronicles-02-37

38 O'bed was the father of Je'hu, and Jehu of Azari'ah. ^1-chronicles-02-38

39 Azari'ah was the father of He'lez, and Helez of E'le-a'sah. ^1-chronicles-02-39

40 Ele-a'sah was the father of Sismai, and Sismai of Shallum. ^1-chronicles-02-40

41 Shallum was the father of Jekami'ah, and Jekamiah of Elish'ama. ^1-chronicles-02-41

42 The sons of Caleb the brother of Jerah'meel: Mare'shah his first-born, who was the father of Ziph. The sons of Mareshah: He'bron. ^1-chronicles-02-42

43 The sons of He'bron: Ko'rah, Tap'pu-ah, Re'kem, and Shema. ^1-chronicles-02-43

44 Shema was the father of Raham, the father of Jor'ke-am; and Re'kem was the father of Shammai. ^1-chronicles-02-44

45 The son of Shammai: Maon; and Maon was the father of Beth-zur. ^1-chronicles-02-45

46 E'phah also, Caleb’s concubine, bore Haran, Moza, and Gazez; and Haran was the father of Gazez. ^1-chronicles-02-46

47 The sons of Jahdai: Re'gem, Jo'tham, Geshan, Pe'let, E'phah, and Sha'aph. ^1-chronicles-02-47

48 Ma'acah, Caleb’s concubine, bore Sheber and Tir'hanah. ^1-chronicles-02-48

49 She also bore Sha'aph the father of Madman'nah, Sheva the father of Machbe'nah and the father of Gib'e-a; and the daughter of Caleb was Achsah. ^1-chronicles-02-49

50 These were the descendants of Caleb. The sons of Hur the first-born of Eph'rathah: Sho'bal the father of Kir'iath-je'arim, ^1-chronicles-02-50

51 Salma, the father of Bethlehem, and Hareph the father of Beth-ga'der. ^1-chronicles-02-51

52 Sho'bal the father of Kir'iath-je'arim had other sons: Haro'eh, half of the Menu'hoth. ^1-chronicles-02-52

53 And the families of Kir'iath-je'arim: the Ithrites, the Puthites, the Shu'mathites, and the Mish'raites; from these came the Zorathites and the Esh'taolites. ^1-chronicles-02-53

54 The sons of Salma: Bethlehem, the Netoph'athites, At'roth-beth-jo'ab, and half of the Man''aha'thites, the Zorites. ^1-chronicles-02-54

55 The families also of the scribes that dwelt at Ja'bez: the Ti'rathites, the Shim'e-athites, and the Su'cathites. These are the Kenites who came from Hammath, the father of the house of Re'chab. ^1-chronicles-02-55
