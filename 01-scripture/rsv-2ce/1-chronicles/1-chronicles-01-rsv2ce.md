# 1 Chronicles 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-chronicles-01|NABRE]]

**From Adam to Abraham**

1 Adam, Seth, E'nosh; ^1-chronicles-01-1

2 Ke'nan, Ma-hal'alel, Jar'ed; ^1-chronicles-01-2

3 E'noch, Methu'selah, La'mech; ^1-chronicles-01-3

4 Noah, Shem, Ham, and Ja'pheth. ^1-chronicles-01-4

5 The sons of Ja'pheth: Gomer, Ma'gog, Ma'dai, Ja'van, Tu'bal, Me'shech, and Ti'ras. ^1-chronicles-01-5

6 The sons of Gomer: Ash'kenaz, Di'phath, and Togar'mah. ^1-chronicles-01-6

7 The sons of Ja'van: Eli'shah, Tar'shish, Kittim, and Ro'danim. ^1-chronicles-01-7

8 The sons of Ham: Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan. ^1-chronicles-01-8

9 The sons of Cush: Seba, Hav'ilah, Sabta, Ra'ama, and Sab'teca. The sons of Ra'amah: Sheba and De'dan. ^1-chronicles-01-9

10 Cush was the father of Nimrod; he began to be a mighty one in the earth. ^1-chronicles-01-10

11 Egypt was the father of Lu'dim, An'amim, Leha'bim, Naph'tuhim, ^1-chronicles-01-11

12 Pathru'sim, Caslu'him (from whom came the Philis'tines), and Caph'torim. ^1-chronicles-01-12

13 Canaan was the father of Si'don his first-born, and Heth, ^1-chronicles-01-13

14 and the Jeb'usites, the Am'orites, the Gir'gashites, ^1-chronicles-01-14

15 the Hi'vites, the Arkites, the Si'nites, ^1-chronicles-01-15

16 the Ar'vadites, the Zem'arites, and the Ha'mathites. ^1-chronicles-01-16

17 The sons of Shem: E'lam, Asshur, Arpach'shad, Lud, Ar'am, Uz, Hul, Ge'ther, and Me'shech. ^1-chronicles-01-17

18 Arpach'shad was the father of She'lah; and Shelah was the father of E'ber. ^1-chronicles-01-18

19 To E'ber were born two sons: the name of the one was Pe'leg (for in his days the earth was divided), and the name of his brother Joktan. ^1-chronicles-01-19

20 Joktan was the father of Almo'dad, She'leph, Ha''zarma'veth, Je'rah, ^1-chronicles-01-20

21 Hador'am, U'zal, Diklah, ^1-chronicles-01-21

22 E'bal, Abim'a-el, Sheba, ^1-chronicles-01-22

23 O'phir, Hav'ilah, and Jo'bab; all these were the sons of Joktan. ^1-chronicles-01-23

24 Shem, Arpach'shad, She'lah; ^1-chronicles-01-24

25 E'ber, Pe'leg, Re'u; ^1-chronicles-01-25

26 Se'rug, Na'hor, Te'rah; ^1-chronicles-01-26

27 Abram, that is, Abraham. ^1-chronicles-01-27

**Descendants of Abraham**

28 The sons of Abraham: Isaac and Ish'mael. ^1-chronicles-01-28

29 These are their genealogies: the first-born of Ish'mael, Neba'ioth; and Ke'dar, Ad'beel, Mibsam, ^1-chronicles-01-29

30 Mishma, Du'mah, Massa, Ha'dad, Te'ma, ^1-chronicles-01-30

31 Je'tur, Naphish, and Ked'emah. These are the sons of Ish'mael. ^1-chronicles-01-31

32 The sons of Ketu'rah, Abraham’s concubine: she bore Zimran, Jok'shan, Me'dan, Mid'ian, Ishbak, and Shuah. The sons of Jokshan: Sheba and De'dan. ^1-chronicles-01-32

33 The sons of Mid'ian: E'phah, E'pher, Ha'noch, Abi'da, and Elda'ah. All these were the descendants of Ketu'rah. ^1-chronicles-01-33

34 Abraham was the father of Isaac. The sons of Isaac: Esau and Israel. ^1-chronicles-01-34

35 The sons of Esau: Eli'phaz, Reu'el, Je'ush, Ja'lam, and Ko'rah. ^1-chronicles-01-35

36 The sons of Eli'phaz: Te'man, Omar, Zephi, Ga'tam, Ke'naz, Timna, and Am'alek. ^1-chronicles-01-36

37 The sons of Reu'el: Na'hath, Ze'rah, Shammah, and Mizzah. ^1-chronicles-01-37

38 The sons of Se'ir: Lo'tan, Sho'bal, Zib'eon, An'ah, Di'shon, E'zer, and Di'shan. ^1-chronicles-01-38

39 The sons of Lo'tan: Ho'ri and Homam; and Lotan’s sister was Timna. ^1-chronicles-01-39

40 The sons of Sho'bal: Al'ian, Man'ahath, E'bal, She'phi, and O'nam. The sons of Zib'eon: Ai'ah and An'ah. ^1-chronicles-01-40

41 The sons of An'ah: Di'shon. The sons of Dishon: Hamran, Eshban, Ithran, and Che'ran. ^1-chronicles-01-41

42 The sons of E'zer: Bilhan, Za'avan, and Ja'akan. The sons of Di'shan: Uz and A'ran. ^1-chronicles-01-42

43 These are the kings who reigned in the land of E'dom before any king reigned over the Israelites: Be'la the son of Beor, the name of whose city was Din'habah. ^1-chronicles-01-43

44 When Be'la died, Jobab the son of Ze'rah of Bozrah reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-44

45 When Jobab died, Hu'sham of the land of the Te'manites reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-45

46 When Hu'sham died, Ha'dad the son of Be'dad, who defeated Mid'ian in the country of Moab, reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was A'vith. ^1-chronicles-01-46

47 When Ha'dad died, Samlah of Masre'kah reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-47

48 When Samlah died, Sha'ul of Reho'both on the Euphra'tes reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-48

49 When Sha'ul died, Ba'al-ha'nan, the son of Achbor, reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-49

50 When Ba'al-ha'nan died, Ha'dad reigned in his stead; and the name of his city was Pa'i, and his wife’s name Mehet'abel the daughter of Ma'tred, the daughter of Me'zahab. ^1-chronicles-01-50

51 And Ha'dad died. The chiefs of E'dom were: chiefs Timna, Al'iah, Je'theth, ^1-chronicles-01-51

52 Oholiba'mah, E'lah, Pi'non, ^1-chronicles-01-52

53 Ke'naz, Te'man, Mibzar, ^1-chronicles-01-53

54 Mag'di-el, and I'ram; these are the chiefs of E'dom. ^1-chronicles-01-54

---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:1) Much space is given to genealogies in Chronicles. Postexilic Judaism was greatly interested in these in its efforts to preserve the race. ^1-chronicles-01-dn-1
