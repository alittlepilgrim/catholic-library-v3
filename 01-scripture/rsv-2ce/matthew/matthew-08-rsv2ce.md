# Matthew 8 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Jesus Cleanses a Leper**

1 When he came down from the mountain, great crowds followed him; ^matthew-08-1

2 and behold, a leper came to him and knelt before him, saying, "Lord, if you will, you can make me clean." ^matthew-08-2

3 And he stretched out his hand and touched him, saying, "I will; be clean." And immediately his leprosy was cleansed. \* ^matthew-08-3

4 And Jesus said to him, "See that you say nothing to any one; but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer the gift that Moses commanded, for a proof to the people." <sup>r</sup> ^matthew-08-4

**Jesus Heals a Centurion's Servant**

5 As he entered Caper'na-um, a centurion came forward to him, begging him ^matthew-08-5

6 and saying, "Lord, my servant is lying paralyzed at home, in terrible distress." ^matthew-08-6

7 And he said to him, "I will come and heal him." ^matthew-08-7

8 But the centurion answered him, "Lord, I am not worthy to have you come under my roof; but only say the word, and my servant will be healed. ^matthew-08-8

9 For I am a man under authority, with soldiers under me; and I say to one, 'Go,' and he goes, and to another, 'Come,' and he comes, and to my slave, 'Do this,' and he does it." ^matthew-08-9

10 When Jesus heard him, he marveled, and said to those who followed him, "Truly, I say to you, not even <sup>s</sup> in Israel have I found such faith. ^matthew-08-10

11 I tell you, many will come from east and west and sit at table with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob in the kingdom of heaven, ^matthew-08-11

12 while the sons of the kingdom will be thrown into the outer darkness; there men will weep and gnash their teeth." ^matthew-08-12

13 And to the centurion Jesus said, "Go; let it be done for you as you have believed." And the servant was healed at that very moment. ^matthew-08-13

**Jesus Heals Many at Peter's House**

14 And when Jesus entered Peter's house, he saw his mother-in-law lying sick with a fever; ^matthew-08-14

15 he touched her hand, and the fever left her, and she rose and served him. ^matthew-08-15

16 That evening they brought to him many who were possessed with demons; and he cast out the spirits with a word, and healed all who were sick. ^matthew-08-16

17 This was to fulfil what was spoken by the prophet Isaiah, "He took our infirmities and bore our diseases." ^matthew-08-17

**Would-be Followers of Jesus**

18 Now when Jesus saw great crowds around him, he gave orders to go over to the other side. ^matthew-08-18

19 And a scribe came up and said to him, "Teacher, I will follow you wherever you go." ^matthew-08-19

20 And Jesus said to him, "Foxes have holes, and birds of the air have nests; but the Son of man has nowhere to lay his head." ^matthew-08-20

21 Another of the disciples said to him, "Lord, let me first go and bury my father." ^matthew-08-21

22 But Jesus said to him, "Follow me, and leave the dead to bury their own dead." ^matthew-08-22

**Jesus Calms the Storm at Sea**

23 And when he got into the boat, his disciples followed him. ^matthew-08-23

24 And behold, there arose a great storm on the sea, so that the boat was being swamped by the waves; but he was asleep. ^matthew-08-24

25 And they went and woke him, saying, "Save us, Lord; we are perishing." ^matthew-08-25

26 And he said to them, "Why are you afraid, O men of little faith?" Then he rose and rebuked the winds and the sea; and there was a great calm. ^matthew-08-26

27 And the men marveled, saying, "What sort of man is this, that even winds and sea obey him?" ^matthew-08-27

**Jesus Heals the Gadarene Demoniacs**

28 And when he came to the other side, to the country of the Gad'arenes, <sup>t</sup> two demoniacs met him, coming out of the tombs, so fierce that no one could pass that way. ^matthew-08-28

29 And behold, they cried out, "What have you to do with us, O Son of God? Have you come here to torment us before the time?" \* ^matthew-08-29

30 Now a herd of many swine was feeding at some distance from them. ^matthew-08-30

31 And the demons begged him, "If you cast us out, send us away into the herd of swine." ^matthew-08-31

32 And he said to them, "Go." So they came out and went into the swine; and behold, the whole herd rushed down the steep bank into the sea, and perished in the waters. ^matthew-08-32

33 The herdsmen fled, and going into the city they told everything, and what had happened to the demoniacs. ^matthew-08-33

34 And behold, all the city came out to meet Jesus; and when they saw him, they begged him to leave their neighborhood. ^matthew-08-34

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (8:3) The miracles of Jesus were never performed to amaze people and shock them into belief. They were worked with a view to a real strengthening of faith in the recipient or beholder, from whom the proper dispositions were required. ^matthew-08-dn-1

\* (8:29) before the time: Before the day of judgment the demons are permitted by God to tempt men and even to possess them. ^matthew-08-dn-2
