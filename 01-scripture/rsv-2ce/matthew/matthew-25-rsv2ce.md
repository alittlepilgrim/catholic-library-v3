# Matthew 25 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Parable of the Wise and Foolish Maidens**

1 "Then the kingdom of heaven shall be compared to ten maidens who took their lamps and went to meet the bridegroom. <sup>c</sup> ^matthew-25-1

2 Five of them were foolish, and five were wise. ^matthew-25-2

3 For when the foolish took their lamps, they took no oil with them; ^matthew-25-3

4 but the wise took flasks of oil with their lamps. ^matthew-25-4

5 As the bridegroom was delayed, they all slumbered and slept. ^matthew-25-5

6 But at midnight there was a cry, 'Behold, the bridegroom! Come out to meet him.' ^matthew-25-6

7 Then all those maidens rose and trimmed their lamps. ^matthew-25-7

8 And the foolish said to the wise, 'Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out.' ^matthew-25-8

9 But the wise replied, 'Perhaps there will not be enough for us and for you; go rather to the dealers and buy for yourselves.' ^matthew-25-9

10 And while they went to buy, the bridegroom came, and those who were ready went in with him to the marriage feast; and the door was shut. ^matthew-25-10

11 Afterward the other maidens came also, saying, 'Lord, lord, open to us.' ^matthew-25-11

12 But he replied, 'Truly, I say to you, I do not know you.' ^matthew-25-12

13 Watch therefore, for you know neither the day nor the hour. ^matthew-25-13

**The Parable of the Talents**

14 "For it will be as when a man going on a journey called his servants and entrusted to them his property; ^matthew-25-14

15 to one he gave five talents, <sup>d</sup> to another two, to another one, to each according to his ability. Then he went away. ^matthew-25-15

16 He who had received the five talents went at once and traded with them; and he made five talents more. ^matthew-25-16

17 So also, he who had the two talents made two talents more. ^matthew-25-17

18 But he who had received the one talent went and dug in the ground and hid his master's money. ^matthew-25-18

19 Now after a long time the master of those servants came and settled accounts with them. ^matthew-25-19

20 And he who had received the five talents came forward, bringing five talents more, saying, 'Master, you delivered to me five talents; here I have made five talents more.' ^matthew-25-20

21 His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful servant; you have been faithful over a little, I will set you over much; enter into the joy of your master.' ^matthew-25-21

22 And he also who had the two talents came forward, saying, 'Master, you delivered to me two talents; here I have made two talents more.' ^matthew-25-22

23 His master said to him, 'Well done, good and faithful servant; you have been faithful over a little, I will set you over much; enter into the joy of your master.' ^matthew-25-23

24 He also who had received the one talent came forward, saying, 'Master, I knew you to be a hard man, reaping where you did not sow, and gathering where you did not winnow; ^matthew-25-24

25 so I was afraid, and I went and hid your talent in the ground. Here you have what is yours.' ^matthew-25-25

26 But his master answered him, 'You wicked and slothful servant! You knew that I reap where I have not sowed, and gather where I have not winnowed? ^matthew-25-26

27 Then you ought to have invested my money with the bankers, and at my coming I should have received what was my own with interest. ^matthew-25-27

28 So take the talent from him, and give it to him who has the ten talents. ^matthew-25-28

29 For to every one who has will more be given, and he will have abundance; but from him who has not, even what he has will be taken away. \* ^matthew-25-29

30 And cast the worthless servant into the outer darkness, where there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.' ^matthew-25-30

**The Judgment of the Nations**

31 "When the Son of man comes in his glory, and all the angels with him, then he will sit on his glorious throne. ^matthew-25-31

32 Before him will be gathered all the nations, and he will separate them one from another as a shepherd separates the sheep from the goats, ^matthew-25-32

33 and he will place the sheep at his right hand, but the goats at the left. ^matthew-25-33

34 Then the King will say to those at his right hand, 'Come, O blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world; ^matthew-25-34

35 for I was hungry and you gave me food, I was thirsty and you gave me drink, I was a stranger and you welcomed me, ^matthew-25-35

36 I was naked and you clothed me, I was sick and you visited me, I was in prison and you came to me.' ^matthew-25-36

37 Then the righteous will answer him, 'Lord, when did we see you hungry and feed you, or thirsty and give you drink? ^matthew-25-37

38 And when did we see you a stranger and welcome you, or naked and clothe you? ^matthew-25-38

39 And when did we see you sick or in prison and visit you?' ^matthew-25-39

40 And the King will answer them, 'Truly, I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these my brethren, you did it to me.' ^matthew-25-40

41 Then he will say to those at his left hand, 'Depart from me, you cursed, into the eternal fire prepared for the devil and his angels; ^matthew-25-41

42 for I was hungry and you gave me no food, I was thirsty and you gave me no drink, ^matthew-25-42

43 I was a stranger and you did not welcome me, naked and you did not clothe me, sick and in prison and you did not visit me.' ^matthew-25-43

44 Then they also will answer, 'Lord, when did we see you hungry or thirsty or a stranger or naked or sick or in prison, and did not minister to you?' ^matthew-25-44

45 Then he will answer them, 'Truly, I say to you, as you did it not to one of the least of these, you did it not to me.' ^matthew-25-45

46 And they will go away into eternal punishment, but the righteous into eternal life." ^matthew-25-46
