# Matthew 10 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Twelve Disciples**

1 And he called to him his twelve disciples and gave them authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every disease and every infirmity. ^matthew-10-1

2 The names of the twelve apostles are these: first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James the son of Zeb'edee, and John his brother; ^matthew-10-2

3 Philip and Bartholomew; Thomas and Matthew the tax collector; James the son of Alphae'us, and Thaddae'us; <sup>x</sup> ^matthew-10-3

4 Simon the Cananaean, and Judas Iscariot, who betrayed him. ^matthew-10-4

**The Mission of the Twelve**

5 These Twelve Jesus sent out, charging them, "Go nowhere among the Gentiles, and enter no town of the Samaritans, \* ^matthew-10-5

6 but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel. ^matthew-10-6

7 And preach as you go, saying, 'The kingdom of heaven is at hand.' ^matthew-10-7

8 Heal the sick, raise the dead, cleanse lepers, cast out demons. You received without pay, give without pay. ^matthew-10-8

9 Take no gold, nor silver, nor copper in your belts, ^matthew-10-9

10 no bag for your journey, nor two tunics, nor sandals, nor a staff; for the laborer deserves his food. ^matthew-10-10

11 And whatever town or village you enter, find out who is worthy in it, and stay with him until you depart. ^matthew-10-11

12 As you enter the house, salute it. ^matthew-10-12

13 And if the house is worthy, let your peace come upon it; but if it is not worthy, let your peace return to you. ^matthew-10-13

14 And if any one will not receive you or listen to your words, shake off the dust from your feet as you leave that house or town. ^matthew-10-14

15 Truly, I say to you, it shall be more tolerable on the day of judgment for the land of Sodom and Gomor'rah than for that town. ^matthew-10-15

**Coming Persecutions**

16 "Behold, I send you out as sheep in the midst of wolves; so be wise as serpents and innocent as doves. ^matthew-10-16

17 Beware of men; for they will deliver you up to councils, and flog you in their synagogues, ^matthew-10-17

18 and you will be dragged before governors and kings for my sake, to bear testimony before them and the Gentiles. ^matthew-10-18

19 When they deliver you up, do not be anxious about how you are to speak or what you are to say; for what you are to say will be given to you in that hour; ^matthew-10-19

20 for it is not you who speak, but the Spirit of your Father speaking through you. ^matthew-10-20

21 Brother will deliver up brother to death, and the father his child, and children will rise against parents and have them put to death; ^matthew-10-21

22 and you will be hated by all for my name's sake. But he who endures to the end will be saved. ^matthew-10-22

23 When they persecute you in one town, flee to the next; for truly, I say to you, you will not have gone through all the towns of Israel, before the Son of man comes. ^matthew-10-23

24 "A disciple is not above his teacher, nor a servant <sup>y</sup> above his master; ^matthew-10-24

25 it is enough for the disciple to be like his teacher, and the servant <sup>y</sup> like his master. If they have called the master of the house Be-el'zebul, how much more will they malign those of his household. ^matthew-10-25

**Whom to Fear**

26 "So have no fear of them; for nothing is covered that will not be revealed, or hidden that will not be known. ^matthew-10-26

27 What I tell you in the dark, utter in the light; and what you hear whispered, proclaim upon the housetops. ^matthew-10-27

28 And do not fear those who kill the body but cannot kill the soul; rather fear him who can destroy both soul and body in hell. <sup>z</sup> ^matthew-10-28

29 Are not two sparrows sold for a penny? And not one of them will fall to the ground without your Father's will. ^matthew-10-29

30 But even the hairs of your head are all numbered. ^matthew-10-30

31 Fear not, therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows. ^matthew-10-31

32 So every one who acknowledges me before men, I also will acknowledge before my Father who is in heaven; ^matthew-10-32

33 but whoever denies me before men, I also will deny before my Father who is in heaven. ^matthew-10-33

**Taking Up One's Cross**

34 "Do not think that I have come to bring peace on earth; I have not come to bring peace, but a sword. ^matthew-10-34

35 For I have come to set a man against his father, and a daughter against her mother, and a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law; ^matthew-10-35

36 and a man's foes will be those of his own household. ^matthew-10-36

37 He who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me; and he who loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me; ^matthew-10-37

38 and he who does not take his cross and follow me is not worthy of me. ^matthew-10-38

39 He who finds his life will lose it, and he who loses his life for my sake will find it. ^matthew-10-39

**Rewards**

40 "He who receives you receives me, and he who receives me receives him who sent me. ^matthew-10-40

41 He who receives a prophet because he is a prophet shall receive a prophet's reward, and he who receives a righteous man because he is a righteous man shall receive a righteous man's reward. ^matthew-10-41

42 And whoever gives to one of these little ones even a cup of cold water because he is a disciple, truly, I say to you, he shall not lose his reward." ^matthew-10-42

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (10:5) The gospel, the Messianic salvation, had first to be preached and offered to the chosen people, Israel. Later it would be offered to the Gentiles. ^matthew-10-dn-1
