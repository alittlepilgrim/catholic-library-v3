# Philemon – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Salutation**

1 Paul, a prisoner for Christ Jesus, and Timothy our brother,

To Phile'mon our beloved fellow worker ^philemon-01-1

2 and Ap'phia our sister and Archip'pus our fellow soldier, and the church in your house: ^philemon-01-2

3 Grace to you and peace from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^philemon-01-3

**Philemon's Love and Faith**

4 I thank my God always when I remember you in my prayers, ^philemon-01-4

5 because I hear of your love and of the faith which you have toward the Lord Jesus and all the saints, ^philemon-01-5

6 and I pray that the sharing of your faith may promote the knowledge of all the good that is ours in Christ. ^philemon-01-6

7 For I have derived much joy and comfort from your love, my brother, because the hearts of the saints have been refreshed through you. ^philemon-01-7

**Paul's Plea for Onesimus**

8 Accordingly, though I am bold enough in Christ to command you to do what is required, ^philemon-01-8

9 yet for love's sake I prefer to appeal to you—I, Paul, an ambassador<sup>a</sup> and now a prisoner also for Christ Jesus— ^philemon-01-9

10 I appeal to you for my child, Ones'imus,<sup>b</sup> whose father I have become in my imprisonment. ^philemon-01-10

11 (Formerly he was useless to you, but now he is indeed useful to you and to me.) ^philemon-01-11

12 I am sending him back to you, sending my very heart. ^philemon-01-12

13 I would have been glad to keep him with me, in order that he might serve me on your behalf during my imprisonment for the gospel; ^philemon-01-13

14 but I preferred to do nothing without your consent in order that your goodness might not be by compulsion but of your own free will. ^philemon-01-14

15 Perhaps this is why he was parted from you for a while, that you might have him back for ever, ^philemon-01-15

16 no longer as a slave but more than a slave, as a beloved brother, especially to me but how much more to you, both in the flesh and in the Lord. ^philemon-01-16

17 So if you consider me your partner, receive him as you would receive me. ^philemon-01-17

18 If he has wronged you at all, or owes you anything, charge that to my account. ^philemon-01-18

19 I, Paul, write this with my own hand, I will repay it—to say nothing of your owing me even your own self. ^philemon-01-19

20 Yes, brother, I want some benefit from you in the Lord. Refresh my heart in Christ. ^philemon-01-20

21 Confident of your obedience, I write to you, knowing that you will do even more than I say. ^philemon-01-21

22 At the same time, prepare a guest room for me, for I am hoping through your prayers to be granted to you. ^philemon-01-22

**Final Greetings and Benediction**

23 Ep'aphras, my fellow prisoner in Christ Jesus, sends greetings to you, ^philemon-01-23

24 and so do Mark, Aristar'chus, Demas, and Luke, my fellow workers. ^philemon-01-24

25 The grace of the Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. ^philemon-01-25
