# Exodus 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[exodus-04|NABRE]]

**God Gives Moses Help for His Mission**

1 Then Moses answered, “But behold, they will ot believe me or listen to my voice, for they will say, ‘The LORD did not appear to you.’ ” ^exodus-04-1

2 The LORD said to him, “What is that in your hand?” He said, “A rod.” ^exodus-04-2

3 And he said, “Cast it on the ground.” So he cast it on the ground, and it became a serpent; and Moses fled from it. ^exodus-04-3

4 But the LORD said to Moses, “Put out your hand, and take it by the tail”—so he put out his hand and caught it, and it became a rod in his hand— ^exodus-04-4

5 “that they may believe that the LORD, the God of their fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, has appeared to you.” ^exodus-04-5

6 Again, the LORD said to him, “Put your hand into your bosom.” And he put his hand into his bosom; and when he took it out, behold, his hand was leprous, as white as snow. ^exodus-04-6

7 Then God said, “Put your hand back into your bosom.” So he put his hand back into his bosom; and when he took it out, behold, it was restored like the rest of his flesh. ^exodus-04-7

8 “If they will not believe you,” God said, “or heed the first sign, they may believe the latter sign. ^exodus-04-8

9 If they will not believe even these two signs or heed your voice, you shall take some water from the Nile and pour it upon the dry ground; and the water which you shall take from the Nile will become blood upon the dry ground.” ^exodus-04-9

10 But Moses said to the LORD, “Oh, my Lord, I am not eloquent, either heretofore or since you have spoken to your servant; but I am slow of speech and of tongue.” ^exodus-04-10

11 Then the LORD said to him, “Who has made man’s mouth? Who makes him mute, or deaf, or seeing, or blind? Is it not I, the LORD? ^exodus-04-11

12 Now therefore go, and I will be with your mouth and teach you what you shall speak.” ^exodus-04-12

13 But he said, “Oh, my Lord, send, I pray, some other person.” ^exodus-04-13

14 Then the anger of the LORD was kindled against Moses and he said, “Is there not Aaron, your brother, the Levite? I know that he can speak well; and behold, he is coming out to meet you, and when he sees you he will be glad in his heart. ^exodus-04-14

15 And you shall speak to him and put the words in his mouth; and I will be with your mouth and with his mouth, and will teach you what you shall do. ^exodus-04-15

16 He shall speak for you to the people; and he shall be a mouth for you, and you shall be to him as God. ^exodus-04-16

17 And you shall take in your hand this rod, with which you shall do the signs.” ^exodus-04-17

**Moses Returns to Egypt**

18 Moses went back to Jethro his father-in-law and said to him, “Let me go back, I beg, to my kinsmen in Egypt and see whether they are still alive.” And Jethro said to Moses, “Go in peace.” ^exodus-04-18

19 And the LORD said to Moses in Mid'ian, “Go back to Egypt; for all the men who were seeking your life are dead.” ^exodus-04-19

20 So Moses took his wife and his sons and set them on a donkey, and went back to the land of Egypt; and in his hand Moses took the rod of God. ^exodus-04-20

21 And the LORD said to Moses, “When you go back to Egypt, see that you do before Pharaoh all the miracles which I have put in your power; but I will harden his heart, so that he will not let the people go. ^exodus-04-21

22 And you shall say to Pharaoh, ‘Thus says the LORD, Israel is my first-born son, ^exodus-04-22

23 and I say to you, “Let my son go that he may serve me”; if you refuse to let him go, behold, I will slay your first-born son.' " ^exodus-04-23

24 At a lodging place on the way the LORD met him and sought to kill him. ^exodus-04-24

25 Then Zippo'rah took a flint and cut off her son’s foreskin, and touched Moses’ feet with it, and said, “Surely you are a bridegroom of blood to me!” ^exodus-04-25

26 So he let him alone. Then it was that she said, “You are a bridegroom of blood,” because of the circumcision. ^exodus-04-26

27 The LORD said to Aaron, “Go into the wilderness to meet Moses.” So he went, and met him at the mountain of God and kissed him. ^exodus-04-27

28 And Moses told Aaron all the words of the LORD with which he had sent him, and all the signs which he had charged him to do. ^exodus-04-28

29 Then Moses and Aaron went and gathered together all the elders of the sons of Israel. ^exodus-04-29

30 And Aaron spoke all the words which the LORD had spoken to Moses, and did the signs in the sight of the people. ^exodus-04-30

31 And the people believed; and when they heard that the LORD had visited the sons of Israel and that he had seen their affliction, they bowed their heads and worshiped. ^exodus-04-31
