# Exodus 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[exodus-06|NABRE]]

1 But the Lord said to Moses, “Now you shall see what I will do to Pharaoh; for with a strong hand he will send them out, yes, with a strong hand he will drive them out of his land.” ^exodus-06-1

**God Promises Deliverance**

2 And God said to Moses, “I am the LORD. ^exodus-06-2

3 I appeared to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, as God Almighty, but by my name the LORD I did not make myself known to them. ^exodus-06-3

4 I also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land in which they dwelt as sojourners. ^exodus-06-4

5 Moreover I have heard the groaning of the sons of Israel whom the Egyptians hold in bondage and I have remembered my covenant. ^exodus-06-5

6 Say therefore to the sons of Israel, ‘I am the LORD, and I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, and I will deliver you from their bondage, and I will redeem you with an outstretched arm and with great acts of judgment, ^exodus-06-6

7 and I will take you for my people, and I will be your God; and you shall know that I am the LORD your God, who has brought you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians. ^exodus-06-7

8 And I will bring you into the land which I swore to give to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; I will give it to you for a possession. I am the LORD.’ ” ^exodus-06-8

9 Moses spoke thus to the sons of Israel; but they did not listen to Moses, because of their broken spirit and their cruel bondage. ^exodus-06-9

10 And the LORD said to Moses, ^exodus-06-10

11 “Go in, tell Pharaoh king of Egypt to let the sons of Israel go out of his land.” ^exodus-06-11

12 But Moses said to the LORD, “Behold, the sons of Israel have not listened to me; how then shall Pharaoh listen to me, who am a man of uncircumcised lips?” ^exodus-06-12

13 But the LORD spoke to Moses and Aaron, and gave them a charge to the sons of Israel and to Pharaoh king of Egypt to bring the sons of Israel out of the land of Egypt. ^exodus-06-13

**The Genealogy of Moses and Aaron**

14 These are the heads of their fathers’ houses: the sons of Reuben, the first-born of Israel: Ha'noch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi; these are the families of Reuben. ^exodus-06-14

15 The sons of Simeon: Jemu'el, Ja'min, O'had, Ja'chin, Zo'har, and Sha'ul, the son of a Canaanite woman; these are the families of Simeon. ^exodus-06-15

16 These are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations: Gershon, Ko'hath, and Merar'i, the years of the life of Levi being a hundred and thirty-seven years. ^exodus-06-16

17 The sons of Gershon: Libni and Shim'e-i, by their families. ^exodus-06-17

18 The sons of Ko'hath: Amram, Izhar, He'bron, and Uz'ziel, the years of the life of Kohath being a hundred and thirty-three years. ^exodus-06-18

19 The sons of Merar'i: Mah'li and Mu'shi. These are the families of the Levites according to their generations. ^exodus-06-19

20 Amram took to wife Joch'ebed his father’s sister and she bore him Aaron and Moses, the years of the life of Amram being one hundred and thirty-seven years. ^exodus-06-20

21 The sons of Izhar: Ko'rah, Ne'pheg, and Zich'ri. ^exodus-06-21

22 And the sons of Uz'ziel: Mish'a-el, El'zaphan, and Sithri. ^exodus-06-22

23 Aaron took to wife Eli'sheba, the daughter of Ammin'adab and the sister of Nahshon; and she bore him Na'dab, Abi'hu, Elea'zar, and Ith'amar. ^exodus-06-23

24 The sons of Ko'rah: Assir, Elka'nah, and Abi'asaph; these are the families of the Ko'rahites. ^exodus-06-24

25 Elea'zar, Aaron’s son, took to wife one of the daughters of Pu'ti-el; and she bore him Phin'ehas. These are the heads of the fathers’ houses of the Levites by their families. ^exodus-06-25

26 These are the Aaron and Moses to whom the LORD said: “Bring out the sons of Israel from the land of Egypt by their hosts.” ^exodus-06-26

27 It was they who spoke to Pharaoh king of Egypt about bringing out the sons of Israel from Egypt, this Moses and this Aaron. ^exodus-06-27

**Moses and Aaron Obey God’s Commands**

28 On the day when the LORD spoke to Moses in the land of Egypt, ^exodus-06-28

29 the LORD said to Moses, “I am the LORD; tell Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I say to you.” ^exodus-06-29

30 But Moses said to the LORD, “Behold, I am of uncircumcised lips; how then shall Pharaoh listen to me?” ^exodus-06-30
