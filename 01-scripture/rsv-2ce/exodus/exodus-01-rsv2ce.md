# Exodus 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[exodus-01|NABRE]]

**The Sons of Israel**

1 These are the names of the sons of Israel who came to Egypt with Jacob, each with his household: ^exodus-01-1

2 Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, ^exodus-01-2

3 Is'sachar, Zeb'ulun, and Benjamin, ^exodus-01-3

4 Dan and Naph'tali, Gad and Asher. ^exodus-01-4

5 All the offspring of Jacob were seventy persons; Joseph was already in Egypt. ^exodus-01-5

6 Then Joseph died, and all his brothers, and all that generation. ^exodus-01-6

7 But the descendants of Israel were fruitful and increased greatly; they multiplied and grew exceedingly strong; so that the land was filled with them. ^exodus-01-7

**The Israelites Are Oppressed by the Egyptians**

8 Now there arose a new king over Egypt, who did not know Joseph. ^exodus-01-8

9 And he said to his people, “Behold, the sons of Israel are too many and too mighty for us. ^exodus-01-9

10 Come, let us deal shrewdly with them, lest they multiply, and, if war befall us, they join our enemies and fight against us and escape from the land.” ^exodus-01-10

11 Therefore they set taskmasters over them to afflict them with heavy burdens; and they built for Pharaoh store-cities, Pithom and Ra-am'ses. ^exodus-01-11

12 But the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied and the more they spread abroad. And the Egyptians were in dread of the sons of Israel. ^exodus-01-12

13 So they made the sons of Israel serve with rigor, ^exodus-01-13

14 and made their lives bitter with hard service, in mortar and brick, and in all kinds of work in the field; in all their work they made them serve with rigor. ^exodus-01-14

15 Then the king of Egypt said to the Hebrew midwives, one of whom was named Shiphrah and the other Puah, ^exodus-01-15

16 “When you serve as midwife to the Hebrew women, and see them upon the birthstool, if it is a son, you shall kill him; but if it is a daughter, she shall live.” ^exodus-01-16

17 But the midwives feared God, and did not do as the king of Egypt commanded them, but let the male children live. ^exodus-01-17

18 So the king of Egypt called the midwives, and said to them, “Why have you done this, and let the male children live?” ^exodus-01-18

19 The midwives said to Pharaoh, “Because the Hebrew women are not like the Egyptian women; for they are vigorous and are delivered before the midwife comes to them.” ^exodus-01-19

20 So God dealt well with the midwives; and the people multiplied and grew very strong. ^exodus-01-20

21 And because the midwives feared God he gave them families. ^exodus-01-21

22 Then Pharaoh commanded all his people, “Every son that is born to the Hebrews you shall cast into the Nile, but you shall let every daughter live.” ^exodus-01-22
