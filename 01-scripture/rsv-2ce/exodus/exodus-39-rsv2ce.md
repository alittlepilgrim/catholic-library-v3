# Exodus 39 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[exodus-39|NABRE]]

**Making the Priests’ Garments**

1 And of the blue and purple and scarlet stuff they made finely wrought garments, for ministering in the holy place; they made the holy garments for Aaron; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-1

2 And he made the ephod of gold, blue and purple and scarlet stuff, and fine twined linen. ^exodus-39-2

3 And gold leaf was hammered out and cut into threads to work into the blue and purple and the scarlet stuff, and into the fine twined linen, in skilled design. ^exodus-39-3

4 They made for the ephod shoulder-pieces, joined to it at its two edges. ^exodus-39-4

5 And the skilfully woven band upon it, to belt it on, was of the same materials and workmanship, of gold, blue and purple and scarlet stuff, and fine twined linen; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-5

6 The onyx stones were prepared, enclosed in settings of gold filigree and engraved like the engravings of a signet, according to the names of the sons of Israel. ^exodus-39-6

7 And he set them on the shoulder-pieces of the ephod, to be stones of remembrance for the sons of Israel; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-7

8 He made the breastpiece, in skilled work, like the work of the ephod, of gold, blue and purple and scarlet stuff, and fine twined linen. ^exodus-39-8

9 It was square; the breastpiece was made double, a span its length and a span its breadth when doubled. ^exodus-39-9

10 And they set in it four rows of stones. A row of sardius, topaz, and carbuncle was the first row; ^exodus-39-10

11 and the second row, an emerald, a sapphire, and a diamond; ^exodus-39-11

12 and the third row, a jacinth, an agate, and an amethyst; ^exodus-39-12

13 and the fourth row, a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper; they were enclosed in settings of gold filigree. ^exodus-39-13

14 There were twelve stones with their names according to the names of the sons of Israel; they were like signets, each engraved with its name, for the twelve tribes. ^exodus-39-14

15 And they made on the breastpiece twisted chains like cords, of pure gold; ^exodus-39-15

16 and they made two settings of gold filigree and two gold rings, and put the two rings on the two edges of the breastpiece; ^exodus-39-16

17 and they put the two cords of gold in the two rings at the edges of the breastpiece. ^exodus-39-17

18 Two ends of the two cords they had attached to the two settings of filigree; thus they attached it in front to the shoulder-pieces of the ephod. ^exodus-39-18

19 Then they made two rings of gold, and put them at the two ends of the breastpiece, on its inside edge next to the ephod. ^exodus-39-19

20 And they made two rings of gold, and attached them in front to the lower part of the two shoulder-pieces of the ephod, at its joining above the skilfully woven band of the ephod. ^exodus-39-20

21 And they bound the breastpiece by its rings to the rings of the ephod with a lace of blue, so that it should lie upon the skilfully woven band of the ephod, and that the breastpiece should not come loose from the ephod; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-21

22 He also made the robe of the ephod woven all of blue; ^exodus-39-22

23 and the opening of the robe in it was like the opening in a garment, with a binding around the opening, that it might not be torn. ^exodus-39-23

24 On the skirts of the robe they made pomegranates of blue and purple and scarlet stuff and fine twined linen. ^exodus-39-24

25 They also made bells of pure gold, and put the bells between the pomegranates upon the skirts of the robe round about, between the pomegranates; ^exodus-39-25

26 a bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate round about upon the skirts of the robe for ministering; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-26

27 They also made the coats, woven of fine linen, for Aaron and his sons, ^exodus-39-27

28 and the turban of fine linen, and the caps of fine linen, and the linen breeches of fine twined linen, ^exodus-39-28

29 and the sash of fine twined linen and of blue and purple and scarlet stuff, embroidered with needlework; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-29

30 And they made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold, and wrote upon it an inscription, like the engraving of a signet, “Holy to the LORD.” ^exodus-39-30

31 And they tied to it a lace of blue, to fasten it on the turban above; as the LORD had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-31

**The Work Finished**

32 Thus all the work of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting was finished; and the sons of Israel had done according to all that the LORD had commanded Moses; so had they done. ^exodus-39-32

33 And they brought the tabernacle to Moses, the tent and all its utensils, its hooks, its frames, its bars, its pillars, and its bases; ^exodus-39-33

34 the covering of tanned rams’ skins and goatskins, and the veil of the screen; ^exodus-39-34

35 the ark of the covenant with its poles and the mercy seat; ^exodus-39-35

36 the table with all its utensils, and the bread of the Presence; ^exodus-39-36

37 the lampstand of pure gold and its lamps with the lamps set and all its utensils, and the oil for the light; ^exodus-39-37

38 the golden altar, the anointing oil and the fragrant incense, and the screen for the door of the tent; ^exodus-39-38

39 the bronze altar, and its grating of bronze, its poles, and all its utensils; the laver and its base; ^exodus-39-39

40 the hangings of the court, its pillars, and its bases, and the screen for the gate of the court, its cords, and its pegs; and all the utensils for the service of the tabernacle, for the tent of meeting; ^exodus-39-40

41 the finely worked garments for ministering in the holy place, the holy garments for Aaron the priest, and the garments of his sons to serve as priests. ^exodus-39-41

42 According to all that the LORD had commanded Moses, so the sons of Israel had done all the work. ^exodus-39-42

43 And Moses saw all the work, and behold, they had done it; as the LORD had commanded, so had they done it. And Moses blessed them. ^exodus-39-43
