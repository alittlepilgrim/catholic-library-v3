# Exodus 20 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[exodus-20|NABRE]]

**The Ten Commandments**

1 And God spoke all these words, saying, ^exodus-20-1

2 “I am the LORD your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. ^exodus-20-2

3 “You shall have no other gods before me. ^exodus-20-3

4 You shall not make for yourself a graven image, or any likeness of anything that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth; ^exodus-20-4

5 you shall not bow down to them or serve them; for I the LORD your God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children to the third and the fourth generation of those who hate me, ^exodus-20-5

6 but showing mercy to thousands of those who love me and keep my commandments. ^exodus-20-6

7 “You shall not take the name of the LORD your God in vain; for the LORD will not hold him guiltless who takes his name in vain. ^exodus-20-7

8 “Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy. ^exodus-20-8

9 Six days you shall labor, and do all your work; ^exodus-20-9

10 but the seventh day is a sabbath to the LORD your God; in it you shall not do any work, you, or your son, or your daughter, your manservant, or your maidservant, or your cattle, or the sojourner who is within your gates; ^exodus-20-10

11 for in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested the seventh day; therefore the LORD blessed the sabbath day and hallowed it. ^exodus-20-11

12 “Honor your father and your mother, that your days may be long in the land which the LORD your God gives you. ^exodus-20-12

13 “You shall not kill. ^exodus-20-13

14 “You shall not commit adultery. ^exodus-20-14

15 “You shall not steal. ^exodus-20-15

16 “You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor. ^exodus-20-16

17 “You shall not covet your neighbor’s house; you shall not covet your neighbor’s wife, or his manservant, or his maidservant, or his ox, or his donkey, or anything that is your neighbor’s.” ^exodus-20-17

18 Now when all the people perceived the thunder and the lightning and the sound of the trumpet and the mountain smoking, the people were afraid and trembled; and they stood afar off, ^exodus-20-18

19 and said to Moses, “You speak to us, and we will hear; but let not God speak to us, lest we die.” ^exodus-20-19

20 And Moses said to the people, “Do not fear; for God has come to test you, and that the fear of him may be before your eyes, that you may not sin.” ^exodus-20-20

**God Gives Moses Laws about Sacrifice**

21 And the people stood afar off, while Moses drew near to the thick darkness where God was. ^exodus-20-21

22 And the LORD said to Moses, “Thus you shall say to the people of Israel: ‘You have seen for yourselves that I have talked with you from heaven. ^exodus-20-22

23 You shall not make gods of silver to be with me, nor shall you make for yourselves gods of gold. ^exodus-20-23

24 An altar of earth you shall make for me and sacrifice on it your burnt offerings and your peace offerings, your sheep and your oxen; in every place where I cause my name to be remembered I will come to you and bless you. ^exodus-20-24

25 And if you make me an altar of stone, you shall not build it of hewn stones; for if you wield your tool upon it you profane it. ^exodus-20-25

26 And you shall not go up by steps to my altar, that your nakedness be not exposed on it.’ ^exodus-20-26

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (20:1–17) The Ten Commandments, in their original form even briefer than here, are found in a different version in Deut 5:6–21. ^exodus-20-dn-1
