# Ezekiel 15 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ezekiel-15|NABRE]]

**The Useless Vine**

1 And the word of the LORD came to me: ^ezekiel-15-1

2 “Son of man, how does the wood of the vine surpass any wood, the vine branch which is among the trees of the forest? ^ezekiel-15-2

3 Is wood taken from it to make anything? Do men take a peg from it to hang any vessel on? ^ezekiel-15-3

4 Behold, it is given to the fire for fuel; when the fire has consumed both ends of it, and the middle of it is charred, is it useful for anything? ^ezekiel-15-4

5 Behold, when it was whole, it was used for nothing; how much less, when the fire has consumed it and it is charred, can it ever be used for anything! ^ezekiel-15-5

6 Therefore thus says the Lord GOD: Like the wood of the vine among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give up the inhabitants of Jerusalem. ^ezekiel-15-6

7 And I will set my face against them; though they escape from the fire, the fire shall yet consume them; and you will know that I am the LORD, when I set my face against them. ^ezekiel-15-7

8 And I will make the land desolate, because they have acted faithlessly, says the Lord GOD.” ^ezekiel-15-8
