# Ezekiel 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ezekiel-25|NABRE]]

**Prophecy against the Ammonites**

1 The word of the LORD came to me: ^ezekiel-25-1

2 “Son of man, set your face toward the Am'monites, and prophesy against them. ^ezekiel-25-2

3 Say to the Am'monites, Hear the word of the Lord GOD: Thus says the Lord GOD, Because you said, ‘Aha!’ over my sanctuary when it was profaned, and over the land of Israel when it was made desolate, and over the house of Judah when it went into exile; ^ezekiel-25-3

4 therefore I am handing you over to the people of the East for a possession, and they shall set their encampments among you and make their dwellings in your midst; they shall eat your fruit, and they shall drink your milk. ^ezekiel-25-4

5 I will make Rabbah a pasture for camels and the cities of the Am'monites a fold for flocks. Then you will know that I am the LORD. ^ezekiel-25-5

6 For thus says the Lord GOD: Because you have clapped your hands and stamped your feet and rejoiced with all the malice within you against the land of Israel, ^ezekiel-25-6

7 therefore, behold, I have stretched out my hand against you, and will hand you over as spoil to the nations; and I will cut you off from the peoples and will make you perish out of the countries; I will destroy you. Then you will know that I am the LORD. ^ezekiel-25-7

**Prophecy against Moab**

8 “Thus says the Lord GOD: Because Moab said, Behold, the house of Judah is like all the other nations, ^ezekiel-25-8

9 therefore I will lay open the flank of Moab from the cities on its frontier, the glory of the country, Beth-jesh'imoth, Ba'al-me'on, and Kir''iatha'im. ^ezekiel-25-9

10 I will give it along with the Am'monites to the people of the East as a possession, that it may be remembered no more among the nations, ^ezekiel-25-10

11 and I will execute judgments upon Moab. Then they will know that I am the LORD. ^ezekiel-25-11

**Prophecy against Edom**

12 “Thus says the Lord GOD: Because E'dom acted revengefully against the house of Judah and has grievously offended in taking vengeance upon them, ^ezekiel-25-12

13 therefore thus says the Lord GOD, I will stretch out my hand against E'dom, and cut off from it man and beast; and I will make it desolate; from Te'man even to De'dan they shall fall by the sword. ^ezekiel-25-13

14 And I will lay my vengeance upon E'dom by the hand of my people Israel; and they shall do in Edom according to my anger and according to my wrath; and they shall know my vengeance, says the Lord GOD. ^ezekiel-25-14

**Prophecy against the Philistines**

15 “Thus says the Lord GOD: Because the Philis'tines acted revengefully and took vengeance with malice of heart to destroy in never-ending enmity; ^ezekiel-25-15

16 therefore thus says the Lord GOD, Behold, I will stretch out my hand against the Philis'tines, and I will cut off the Cher'ethites, and destroy the rest of the seacoast. ^ezekiel-25-16

17 I will execute great vengeance upon them with wrathful chastisements. Then they will know that I am the LORD, when I lay my vengeance upon them.” ^ezekiel-25-17
