# Ezekiel 9 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ezekiel-09|NABRE]]

**Vision of the Executioners**

1 Then he cried in my ears with a loud voice, saying, “Draw near, you executioners of the city, each with his destroying weapon in his hand.” ^ezekiel-09-1

2 And behold, six men came from the direction of the upper gate, which faces north, every man with his weapon for slaughter in his hand, and with them was a man clothed in linen, with a writing case at his side. And they went in and stood beside the bronze altar. ^ezekiel-09-2

3 Now the glory of the God of Israel had gone up from the cherubim on which it rested to the threshold of the house; and he called to the man clothed in linen, who had the writing case at his side. ^ezekiel-09-3

4 And the LORD said to him, “Go through the city, through Jerusalem, and put a mark upon the foreheads of the men who sigh and groan over all the abominations that are committed in it.” ^ezekiel-09-4

5 And to the others he said in my hearing, “Pass through the city after him, and kill; your eye shall not spare, and you shall show no pity; ^ezekiel-09-5

6 slay old men outright, young men and maidens, little children and women, but touch no one upon whom is the mark. And begin at my sanctuary.” So they began with the elders who were before the house. ^ezekiel-09-6

7 Then he said to them, “Defile the house, and fill the courts with the slain. Go forth.” So they went forth, and killed in the city. ^ezekiel-09-7

8 And while they were killing, and I was left alone, I fell upon my face, and cried, “Ah, Lord GOD! will you destroy all that remains of Israel in the outpouring of your wrath upon Jerusalem?” ^ezekiel-09-8

9 Then he said to me, “The guilt of the house of Israel and Judah is exceedingly great; the land is full of blood, and the city full of injustice; for they say, ‘The LORD has forsaken the land, and the LORD does not see.’ ^ezekiel-09-9

10 As for me, my eye will not spare, nor will I have pity, but I will repay their deeds upon their heads.” ^ezekiel-09-10

11 And behold, the man clothed in linen, with the writing case at his side, brought back word, saying, “I have done as you commanded me.” ^ezekiel-09-11
