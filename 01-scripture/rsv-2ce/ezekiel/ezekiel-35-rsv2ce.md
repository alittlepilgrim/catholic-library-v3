# Ezekiel 35 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[ezekiel-35|NABRE]]

**Prophecy against Mount Seir**

1 The word of the LORD came to me: ^ezekiel-35-1

2 “Son of man, set your face against Mount Se'ir, and prophesy against it, ^ezekiel-35-2

3 and say to it, Thus says the Lord GOD: Behold, I am against you, Mount Se'ir, and I will stretch out my hand against you, and I will make you a desolation and a waste. ^ezekiel-35-3

4 I will lay your cities waste, and you shall become a desolation; and you shall know that I am the LORD. ^ezekiel-35-4

5 Because you cherished perpetual enmity, and gave over the people of Israel to the power of the sword at the time of their calamity, at the time of their final punishment; ^ezekiel-35-5

6 therefore, as I live, says the Lord GOD, I will prepare you for blood, and blood shall pursue you; because you are guilty of blood, therefore blood shall pursue you. ^ezekiel-35-6

7 I will make Mount Se'ir a waste and a desolation; and I will cut off from it all who come and go. ^ezekiel-35-7

8 And I will fill your mountains with the slain; on your hills and in your valleys and in all your ravines those slain with the sword shall fall. ^ezekiel-35-8

9 I will make you a perpetual desolation, and your cities shall not be inhabited. Then you will know that I am the LORD. ^ezekiel-35-9

10 “Because you said, ‘These two nations and these two countries shall be mine, and we will take possession of them,’—although the LORD was there— ^ezekiel-35-10

11 therefore, as I live, says the Lord GOD, I will deal with you according to the anger and envy which you showed because of your hatred against them; and I will make myself known among you, when I judge you. ^ezekiel-35-11

12 And you shall know that I, the LORD, have heard all the revilings which you uttered against the mountains of Israel, saying, ‘They are laid desolate, they are given us to devour.’ ^ezekiel-35-12

13 And you magnified yourselves against me with your mouth, and multiplied your words against me; I heard it. ^ezekiel-35-13

14 Thus says the Lord GOD: For the rejoicing of the whole earth I will make you desolate. ^ezekiel-35-14

15 As you rejoiced over the inheritance of the house of Israel, because it was desolate, so I will deal with you; you shall be desolate, Mount Se'ir, and all E'dom, all of it. Then they will know that I am the LORD. ^ezekiel-35-15
