# Hebrews 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 For every high priest chosen from among men is appointed to act on behalf of men in relation to God, to offer gifts and sacrifices for sins. ^hebrews-05-1

2 He can deal gently with the ignorant and wayward, since he himself is beset with weakness. ^hebrews-05-2

3 Because of this he is bound to offer sacrifice for his own sins as well as for those of the people. ^hebrews-05-3

4 And one does not take the honor upon himself, but he is called by God, just as Aaron was. ^hebrews-05-4

5 So also Christ did not exalt himself to be made a high priest, but was appointed by him who said to him,

"You are my Son,
today I have begotten you"; \* ^hebrews-05-5

6 as he says also in another place,

"You are a priest for ever,
according to the order of Melchiz'edek." ^hebrews-05-6

7 In the days of his flesh, Jesus<sup>j</sup> offered up prayers and supplications, with loud cries and tears, to him who was able to save him from death, and he was heard for his godly fear. ^hebrews-05-7

8 Although he was a Son, he learned obedience through what he suffered; ^hebrews-05-8

9 and being made perfect he became the source of eternal salvation to all who obey him, ^hebrews-05-9

10 being designated by God a high priest according to the order of Melchiz'edek. ^hebrews-05-10

**Spiritual Growth**

11 About this we have much to say which is hard to explain, since you have become dull of hearing. ^hebrews-05-11

12 For though by this time you ought to be teachers, you need some one to teach you again the first principles of God's word. You need milk, not solid food; ^hebrews-05-12

13 for every one who lives on milk is unskilled in the word of righteousness, for he is a child. ^hebrews-05-13

14 But solid food is for the mature, for those who have their faculties trained by practice to distinguish good from evil. ^hebrews-05-14


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (5:1–5) If Jesus was to be mediator, he had to have a human nature like ours, and, moreover, he could not appoint himself, but had to be appointed by God. ^hebrews-05-dn-1
