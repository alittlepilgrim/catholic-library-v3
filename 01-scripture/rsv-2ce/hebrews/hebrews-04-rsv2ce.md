# Hebrews 4 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Rest That God Promised**

1 Therefore, while the promise of entering his rest remains, let us fear lest any of you be judged to have failed to reach it. ^hebrews-04-1

2 For good news came to us just as to them; but the message which they heard did not benefit them, because it did not meet with faith in the hearers.<sup>h</sup> ^hebrews-04-2

3 For we who have believed enter that rest, as he has said,

"As I swore in my wrath,
'They shall never enter my rest,' "

although his works were finished from the foundation of the world. ^hebrews-04-3

4 For he has somewhere spoken of the seventh day in this way, "And God rested on the seventh day from all his works." ^hebrews-04-4

5 And again in this place he said,

"They shall never enter my rest." ^hebrews-04-5

6 Since therefore it remains for some to enter it, and those who formerly received the good news failed to enter because of disobedience, ^hebrews-04-6

7 again he sets a certain day, "Today," saying through David so long afterward, in the words already quoted,

"Today, when you hear his voice,
do not harden your hearts." ^hebrews-04-7

8 For if Joshua had given them rest, God<sup>i</sup> would not speak later of another day. ^hebrews-04-8

9 So then, there remains a sabbath rest for the people of God; ^hebrews-04-9

10 for whoever enters God's rest also ceases from his labors as God did from his. ^hebrews-04-10

11 Let us therefore strive to enter that rest, that no one fall by the same sort of disobedience. ^hebrews-04-11

12 For the word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and spirit, of joints and marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart. ^hebrews-04-12

13 And before him no creature is hidden, but all are open and laid bare to the eyes of him with whom we have to do. ^hebrews-04-13

**Jesus the Great High Priest**

14 Since then we have a great high priest who has passed through the heavens, Jesus, the Son of God, let us hold fast our confession. ^hebrews-04-14

15 For we have not a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but one who in every respect has been tempted as we are, yet without sinning. ^hebrews-04-15

16 Let us then with confidence draw near to the throne of grace, that we may receive mercy and find grace to help in time of need. ^hebrews-04-16
