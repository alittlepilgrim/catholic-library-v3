# Hebrews 7 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Priestly Order of Melchizedek**

1 For this Melchiz'edek, king of Salem, priest of the Most High God, met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings and blessed him; ^hebrews-07-1

2 and to him Abraham apportioned a tenth part of everything. He is first, by translation of his name, king of righteousness, and then he is also king of Salem, that is, king of peace. ^hebrews-07-2

3 He is without father \* or mother or genealogy, and has neither beginning of days nor end of life, \* but resembling the Son of God he continues a priest for ever. ^hebrews-07-3

4 See how great he is! Abraham the patriarch gave him a tithe of the spoils. ^hebrews-07-4

5 And those descendants of Levi who receive the priestly office have a commandment in the law to take tithes from the people, that is, from their brethren, though these also are descended from Abraham. ^hebrews-07-5

6 But this man who has not their genealogy received tithes from Abraham and blessed him who had the promises. ^hebrews-07-6

7 It is beyond dispute that the inferior is blessed by the superior. ^hebrews-07-7

8 Here tithes are received by mortal men; there, by one of whom it is testified that he lives. ^hebrews-07-8

9 One might even say that Levi himself, who receives tithes, paid tithes through Abraham, ^hebrews-07-9

10 for he was still in the loins of his ancestor when Melchiz'edek met him. ^hebrews-07-10

**Another Priest, according to the Order of Melchizedek**

11 Now if perfection had been attainable through the Levitical priesthood (for under it the people received the law), what further need would there have been for another priest to arise according to the order of Melchiz'edek, rather than one named according to the order of Aaron? ^hebrews-07-11

12 For when there is a change in the priesthood, there is necessarily a change in the law as well. ^hebrews-07-12

13 For the one of whom these things are spoken belonged to another tribe, from which no one has ever served at the altar. ^hebrews-07-13

14 For it is evident that our Lord was descended from Judah, and in connection with that tribe Moses said nothing about priests. ^hebrews-07-14

15 This becomes even more evident when another priest arises in the likeness of Melchiz'edek, ^hebrews-07-15

16 who has become a priest, not according to a legal requirement concerning bodily descent but by the power of an indestructible life. ^hebrews-07-16

17 For it is witnessed of him,

"You are a priest for ever,
according to the order of Melchiz'edek." ^hebrews-07-17

18 On the one hand, a former commandment is set aside because of its weakness and uselessness ^hebrews-07-18

19 (for the law made nothing perfect); on the other hand, a better hope is introduced, through which we draw near to God. ^hebrews-07-19

20 And it was not without an oath. ^hebrews-07-20

21 Those who formerly became priests took their office without an oath, but this one was addressed with an oath,

"The Lord has sworn
and will not change his mind,
'You are a priest for ever.' " ^hebrews-07-21

22 This makes Jesus the surety of a better covenant. ^hebrews-07-22

23 The former priests were many in number, because they were prevented by death from continuing in office; ^hebrews-07-23

24 but he holds his priesthood permanently, because he continues for ever. ^hebrews-07-24

25 Consequently he is able for all time to save those who draw near to God through him, since he always lives to make intercession for them. ^hebrews-07-25

26 For it was fitting that we should have such a high priest, holy, blameless, unstained, separated from sinners, exalted above the heavens. ^hebrews-07-26

27 He has no need, like those high priests, to offer sacrifices daily, first for his own sins and then for those of the people; he did this once for all when he offered up himself. ^hebrews-07-27

28 Indeed, the law appoints men in their weakness as high priests, but the word of the oath, which came later than the law, appoints a Son who has been made perfect for ever. ^hebrews-07-28

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (7:3) without father: i.e., the father is not mentioned in Scripture. ^hebrews-07-dn-1

\* (7:3) neither beginning of days nor end of life: So too here, they are not mentioned in Scripture either. Thus his priesthood can be taken to foreshadow or symbolize the Christian priesthood. “You are a priest for ever according to the order of Melchizedek” (Ps 110:4; cf. Heb 7:17). ^hebrews-07-dn-2
