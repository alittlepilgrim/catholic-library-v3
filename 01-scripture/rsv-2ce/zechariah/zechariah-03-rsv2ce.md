# Zechariah 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[zechariah-03|NABRE]]

**Fourth Vision: Joshua and Satan**

1 Then he showed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the LORD, and Satan standing at his right hand to accuse him. ^zechariah-03-1

2 And the LORD said to Satan, “The LORD rebuke you, O Satan! The LORD who has chosen Jerusalem rebuke you! Is not this a brand plucked from the fire?” ^zechariah-03-2

3 Now Joshua was standing before the angel, clothed with filthy garments. ^zechariah-03-3

4 And the angel said to those who were standing before him, “Remove the filthy garments from him.” And to him he said, “Behold, I have taken your iniquity away from you, and I will clothe you with rich apparel.” ^zechariah-03-4

5 And I said, “Let them put a clean turban on his head.” So they put a clean turban on his head and clothed him with garments; and the angel of the LORD was standing by. ^zechariah-03-5

6 And the angel of the LORD enjoined Joshua, ^zechariah-03-6

7 “Thus says the LORD of hosts: If you will walk in my ways and keep my charge, then you shall rule my house and have charge of my courts, and I will give you the right of access among those who are standing here. ^zechariah-03-7

8 Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, you and your friends who sit before you, for they are men of good omen: behold, I will bring my servant the Branch. ^zechariah-03-8

9 For behold, upon the stone which I have set before Joshua, upon a single stone with seven facets, I will engrave its inscription, says the LORD of hosts, and I will remove the guilt of this land in a single day. ^zechariah-03-9

10 In that day, says the LORD of hosts, every one of you will invite his neighbor under his vine and under his fig tree.” ^zechariah-03-10
