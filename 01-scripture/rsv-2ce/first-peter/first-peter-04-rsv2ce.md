# 1 Peter 4 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Good Stewards of God's Grace**

1 Since therefore Christ suffered in the flesh,<sup>i</sup> arm yourselves with the same thought, for whoever has suffered in the flesh has ceased from sin, \* ^first-peter-04-1

2 so as to live for the rest of the time in the flesh no longer by human passions but by the will of God. ^first-peter-04-2

3 Let the time that is past suffice for doing what the Gentiles like to do, living in licentiousness, passions, drunkenness, revels, carousing, and lawless idolatry. ^first-peter-04-3

4 They are surprised that you do not now join them in the same wild debauchery, and they abuse you; ^first-peter-04-4

5 but they will give account to him who is ready to judge the living and the dead. ^first-peter-04-5

6 For this is why the gospel was preached even to the dead, that though judged in the flesh like men, they might live in the spirit like God. ^first-peter-04-6

7 The end of all things is at hand; therefore keep sane and sober for your prayers. ^first-peter-04-7

8 Above all hold unfailing your love for one another, since love covers a multitude of sins. ^first-peter-04-8

9 Practice hospitality ungrudgingly to one another. ^first-peter-04-9

10 As each has received a gift, employ it for one another, as good stewards of God's varied grace: ^first-peter-04-10

11 whoever speaks, as one who utters oracles of God; whoever renders service, as one who renders it by the strength which God supplies; in order that in everything God may be glorified through Jesus Christ. To him belong glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen. ^first-peter-04-11

**Suffering as a Christian**

12 Beloved, do not be surprised at the fiery ordeal which comes upon you to prove you, as though something strange were happening to you. ^first-peter-04-12

13 But rejoice in so far as you share Christ's sufferings, that you may also rejoice and be glad when his glory is revealed. ^first-peter-04-13

14 If you are reproached for the name of Christ, you are blessed, because the spirit of glory<sup>j</sup> and of God rests upon you. ^first-peter-04-14

15 But let none of you suffer as a murderer, or a thief, or a wrongdoer, or a mischief-maker; ^first-peter-04-15

16 yet if one suffers as a Christian, let him not be ashamed, but under that name let him glorify God. ^first-peter-04-16

17 For the time has come for judgment to begin with the household of God; and if it begins with us, what will be the end of those who do not obey the gospel of God? ^first-peter-04-17

18 And

"If the righteous man is scarcely saved,
where will the impious and sinner appear?" ^first-peter-04-18

19 Therefore let those who suffer according to God's will do right and entrust their souls to a faithful Creator. ^first-peter-04-19


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (4:1) ceased from sin: Peter means that a continual acceptance of suffering is incompatible with a proneness to sin. ^first-peter-04-dn-1
