# Romans 11 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Israel's Rejection Is Not Final**

1 I ask, then, has God rejected his people? By no means! I myself am an Israelite, a descendant of Abraham, a member of the tribe of Benjamin. ^romans-11-1

2 God has not rejected his people whom he foreknew. Do you not know what the Scripture says of Eli'jah, how he pleads with God against Israel? ^romans-11-2

3 "Lord, they have killed your prophets, they have demolished your altars, and I alone am left, and they seek my life." ^romans-11-3

4 But what is God's reply to him? "I have kept for myself seven thousand men who have not bowed the knee to Ba'al." ^romans-11-4

5 So too at the present time there is a remnant, chosen by grace. ^romans-11-5

6 But if it is by grace, it is no longer on the basis of works; otherwise grace would no longer be grace. ^romans-11-6

7 What then? Israel failed to obtain what it sought. The elect obtained it, but the rest were hardened, ^romans-11-7

8 as it is written,

"God gave them a spirit of stupor,
eyes that should not see and ears that should not hear,
down to this very day." ^romans-11-8

9 And David says,

"Let their feast become a snare and a trap,
a pitfall and a retribution for them; ^romans-11-9

10 let their eyes be darkened so that they cannot see,
  and bend their backs for ever." ^romans-11-10

**The Salvation of the Gentiles**

11 So I ask, have they stumbled so as to fall? By no means! But through their trespass salvation has come to the Gentiles, so as to make Israel jealous. ^romans-11-11

12 Now if their trespass means riches for the world, and if their failure means riches for the Gentiles, how much more will their full inclusion mean! ^romans-11-12

13 Now I am speaking to you Gentiles. Inasmuch then as I am an apostle to the Gentiles, I magnify my ministry ^romans-11-13

14 in order to make my fellow Jews jealous, and thus save some of them. ^romans-11-14

15 For if their rejection means the reconciliation of the world, what will their acceptance mean but life from the dead? ^romans-11-15

16 If the dough offered as first fruits is holy, so is the whole batch; and if the root is holy, so are the branches. ^romans-11-16

17 But if some of the branches were broken off, and you, a wild olive shoot, were grafted in their place to share the richness<sup>o</sup> of the olive tree, ^romans-11-17

18 do not boast over the branches. If you do boast, remember it is not you that support the root, but the root that supports you. ^romans-11-18

19 You will say, "Branches were broken off so that I might be grafted in." ^romans-11-19

20 That is true. They were broken off because of their unbelief, but you stand fast only through faith. So do not become proud, but stand in awe. ^romans-11-20

21 For if God did not spare the natural branches, neither will he spare you. ^romans-11-21

22 Note then the kindness and the severity of God: severity toward those who have fallen, but God's kindness to you, provided you continue in his kindness; otherwise you too will be cut off. ^romans-11-22

23 And even the others, if they do not persist in their unbelief, will be grafted in, for God has the power to graft them in again. ^romans-11-23

24 For if you have been cut from what is by nature a wild olive tree, and grafted, contrary to nature, into a cultivated olive tree, how much more will these natural branches be grafted back into their own olive tree. ^romans-11-24

**Israel Will Be Saved**

25 Lest you be wise in your own conceits, I want you to understand this mystery, brethren: a hardening has come upon part of Israel, until the full number of the Gentiles come in, ^romans-11-25

26 and so all Israel will be saved; as it is written,

"The Deliverer will come from Zion,
he will banish ungodliness from Jacob"; ^romans-11-26

27 "and this will be my covenant with them
  when I take away their sins." ^romans-11-27

28 As regards the gospel they are enemies of God, for your sake; but as regards election they are beloved for the sake of their forefathers. ^romans-11-28

29 For the gifts and the call of God are irrevocable. ^romans-11-29

30 Just as you were once disobedient to God but now have received mercy because of their disobedience, ^romans-11-30

31 so they have now been disobedient in order that by the mercy shown to you they also may<sup>p</sup> receive mercy. ^romans-11-31

32 For God has consigned all men to disobedience, that he may have mercy upon all. ^romans-11-32

33 O the depth of the riches and wisdom and knowledge of God! How unsearchable are his judgments and how inscrutable his ways! ^romans-11-33

34 "For who has known the mind of the Lord,
  or who has been his counselor?" ^romans-11-34

35 "Or who has given a gift to him
  that he might be repaid?" ^romans-11-35

36 For from him and through him and to him are all things. To him be glory for ever. Amen. ^romans-11-36
