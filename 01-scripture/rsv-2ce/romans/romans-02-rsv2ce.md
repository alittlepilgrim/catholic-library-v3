# Romans 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Righteous Judgment of God**

1 Therefore you have no excuse, O man, whoever you are, when you judge another; for in passing judgment upon him you condemn yourself, because you, the judge, are doing the very same things. ^romans-02-1

2 We know that the judgment of God rightly falls upon those who do such things. ^romans-02-2

3 Do you suppose, O man, that when you judge those who do such things and yet do them yourself, you will escape the judgment of God? ^romans-02-3

4 Or do you presume upon the riches of his kindness and forbearance and patience? Do you not know that God's kindness is meant to lead you to repentance? ^romans-02-4

5 But by your hard and impenitent heart you are storing up wrath for yourself on the day of wrath when God's righteous judgment will be revealed. ^romans-02-5

6 For he will render to every man according to his works: ^romans-02-6

7 to those who by patience in well-doing seek for glory and honor and immortality, he will give eternal life; ^romans-02-7

8 but for those who are factious and do not obey the truth, but obey wickedness, there will be wrath and fury. ^romans-02-8

9 There will be tribulation and distress for every human being who does evil, the Jew first and also the Greek, ^romans-02-9

10 but glory and honor and peace for every one who does good, the Jew first and also the Greek. ^romans-02-10

11 For God shows no partiality. ^romans-02-11

12 All who have sinned without the law will also perish without the law, and all who have sinned under the law will be judged by the law. ^romans-02-12

13 For it is not the hearers of the law who are righteous before God, but the doers of the law who will be justified. ^romans-02-13

14 When Gentiles who have not the law do by nature what the law requires, they are a law to themselves, even though they do not have the law. ^romans-02-14

15 They show that what the law requires is written on their hearts, while their conscience also bears witness and their conflicting thoughts accuse or perhaps excuse them ^romans-02-15

16 on that day when, according to my gospel, God judges the secrets of men by Christ Jesus. ^romans-02-16

**The Jews and the Law**

17 But if you call yourself a Jew and rely upon the law and boast of your relation to God ^romans-02-17

18 and know his will and approve what is excellent, because you are instructed in the law, ^romans-02-18

19 and if you are sure that you are a guide to the blind, a light to those who are in darkness, ^romans-02-19

20 a corrector of the foolish, a teacher of children, having in the law the embodiment of knowledge and truth— ^romans-02-20

21 you then who teach others, will you not teach yourself? While you preach against stealing, do you steal? ^romans-02-21

22 You who say that one must not commit adultery, do you commit adultery? You who abhor idols, do you rob temples? ^romans-02-22

23 You who boast in the law, do you dishonor God by breaking the law? ^romans-02-23

24 For, as it is written, "The name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles because of you." ^romans-02-24

25 Circumcision indeed is of value if you obey the law; but if you break the law, your circumcision becomes uncircumcision. ^romans-02-25

26 So, if a man who is uncircumcised keeps the precepts of the law, will not his uncircumcision be regarded as circumcision? ^romans-02-26

27 Then those who are physically uncircumcised but keep the law will condemn you who have the written code and circumcision but break the law. ^romans-02-27

28 For he is not a real Jew who is one outwardly, nor is true circumcision something external and physical. ^romans-02-28

29 He is a Jew who is one inwardly, and real circumcision is a matter of the heart, spiritual and not literal. His praise is not from men but from God. ^romans-02-29
