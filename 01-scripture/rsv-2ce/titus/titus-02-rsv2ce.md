# Titus 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Teach Sound Doctrine**

1 But as for you, teach what befits sound doctrine. ^titus-02-1

2 Bid the older men be temperate, serious, sensible, sound in faith, in love, and in steadfastness. ^titus-02-2

3 Bid the older women likewise to be reverent in behavior, not to be slanderers or slaves to drink; they are to teach what is good, ^titus-02-3

4 and so train the young women to love their husbands and children, ^titus-02-4

5 to be sensible, chaste, domestic, kind, and submissive to their husbands, that the word of God may not be discredited. ^titus-02-5

6 Likewise urge the younger men to control themselves. ^titus-02-6

7 Show yourself in all respects a model of good deeds, and in your teaching show integrity, gravity, ^titus-02-7

8 and sound speech that cannot be censured, so that an opponent may be put to shame, having nothing evil to say of us. ^titus-02-8

9 Bid slaves to be submissive to their masters and to give satisfaction in every respect; they are not to talk back, ^titus-02-9

10 nor to pilfer, but to show entire and true fidelity, so that in everything they may adorn the doctrine of God our Savior. ^titus-02-10

11 For the grace of God has appeared for the salvation of all men, ^titus-02-11

12 training us to renounce irreligion and worldly passions, and to live sober, upright, and godly lives in this world, ^titus-02-12

13 awaiting our blessed hope, the appearing of the glory of our great God and Savior<sup>c</sup> \* Jesus Christ, ^titus-02-13

14 who gave himself for us to redeem us from all iniquity and to purify for himself a people of his own who are zealous for good deeds. ^titus-02-14

15 Declare these things; exhort and reprove with all authority. Let no one disregard you. ^titus-02-15


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (2:13) God and Savior: Both terms appear to refer to Jesus Christ. ^titus-02-dn-1
