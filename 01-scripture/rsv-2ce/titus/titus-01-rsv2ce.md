# Titus 1 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Salutation**

1 Paul, a servant<sup>a</sup> of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, to further the faith of God's elect and their knowledge of the truth which accords with godliness, ^titus-01-1

2 in hope of eternal life which God, who never lies, promised ages ago ^titus-01-2

3 and at the proper time manifested in his word through the preaching with which I have been entrusted by command of God our Savior; ^titus-01-3

4 To Titus, my true child in a common faith:

Grace and peace from God the Father and Christ Jesus our Savior. ^titus-01-4

**Titus in Crete**

5 This is why I left you in Crete, that you might amend what was defective, and appoint elders \* in every town as I directed you, ^titus-01-5

6 if any man is blameless, the husband of one wife, and his children are believers and not open to the charge of debauchery and not being insubordinate. ^titus-01-6

7 For a bishop, as God's steward, must be blameless; he must not be arrogant or quick-tempered or a drunkard or violent or greedy for gain, ^titus-01-7

8 but hospitable, a lover of goodness, master of himself, upright, holy, and self-controlled; ^titus-01-8

9 he must hold firm to the sure word as taught, so that he may be able to give instruction in sound doctrine and also to confute those who contradict it. ^titus-01-9

10 For there are many insubordinate men, empty talkers and deceivers, especially the circumcision party; ^titus-01-10

11 they must be silenced, since they are upsetting whole families by teaching for base gain what they have no right to teach. ^titus-01-11

12 One of themselves, a prophet of their own, said, "Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, lazy gluttons." ^titus-01-12

13 This testimony is true. Therefore rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith, ^titus-01-13

14 instead of giving heed to Jewish myths or to commands of men who reject the truth. ^titus-01-14

15 To the pure all things are pure, but to the corrupt and unbelieving nothing is pure; their very minds and consciences are corrupted. ^titus-01-15

16 They profess to know God, but they deny him by their deeds; they are detestable, disobedient, unfit for any good deed. ^titus-01-16


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:5) elders: Each Christian community was ruled by a body of elders. ^titus-01-dn-1
