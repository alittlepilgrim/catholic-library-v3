# Colossians 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 For I want you to know how greatly I strive for you, and for those at La-odice'a, and for all who have not seen my face, ^colossians-02-1

2 that their hearts may be encouraged as they are knit together in love, to have all the riches of assured understanding and the knowledge of God's mystery, of Christ, ^colossians-02-2

3 in whom are hidden all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge. ^colossians-02-3

4 I say this in order that no one may delude you with beguiling speech. ^colossians-02-4

5 For though I am absent in body, yet I am with you in spirit, rejoicing to see your good order and the firmness of your faith in Christ. ^colossians-02-5

**Fulness of Life in Christ**

6 As therefore you received Christ Jesus the Lord, so live in him, ^colossians-02-6

7 rooted and built up in him and established in the faith, just as you were taught, abounding in thanksgiving. ^colossians-02-7

**Warnings against False Teachers**

8 See to it that no one makes a prey of you by philosophy and empty deceit, according to human tradition, according to the elemental spirits of the universe, and not according to Christ. ^colossians-02-8

9 For in him the whole fulness of deity dwells bodily, ^colossians-02-9

10 and you have come to fulness of life in him, who is the head of all rule and authority. ^colossians-02-10

11 In him also you were circumcised with a circumcision made without hands, by putting off the body of flesh in the circumcision of Christ; ^colossians-02-11

12 and you were buried with him in baptism, in which you were also raised with him through faith in the working of God, who raised him from the dead. ^colossians-02-12

13 And you, who were dead in trespasses and the uncircumcision of your flesh, God made alive together with him, having forgiven us all our trespasses, ^colossians-02-13

14 having canceled the bond which stood against us with its legal demands; this he set aside, nailing it to the cross. ^colossians-02-14

15 He disarmed the principalities and powers and made a public example of them, triumphing over them in him.<sup>d</sup> ^colossians-02-15

16 Therefore let no one pass judgment on you in questions of food and drink or with regard to a festival or a new moon or a sabbath. ^colossians-02-16

17 These are only a shadow of what is to come; but the substance belongs to Christ. ^colossians-02-17

18 Let no one disqualify you, insisting on self-abasement and worship of angels, taking his stand on visions, puffed up without reason by his sensuous mind, ^colossians-02-18

19 and not holding fast to the Head, from whom the whole body, nourished and knit together through its joints and ligaments, grows with a growth that is from God. ^colossians-02-19

20 If with Christ you died to the elemental spirits of the universe, why do you live as if you still belonged to the world? Why do you submit to regulations, ^colossians-02-20

21 "Do not handle, Do not taste, Do not touch" ^colossians-02-21

22 (referring to things which all perish as they are used), according to human precepts and doctrines? ^colossians-02-22

23 These have indeed an appearance of wisdom in promoting rigor of devotion and self-abasement and severity to the body, but they are of no value in checking the indulgence of the flesh.<sup>e</sup> ^colossians-02-23
