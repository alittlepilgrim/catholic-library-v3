# Colossians 4 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 Masters, treat your slaves justly and fairly, knowing that you also have a Master in heaven. ^colossians-04-1

**Further Instructions**

2 Continue steadfastly in prayer, being watchful in it with thanksgiving; ^colossians-04-2

3 and pray for us also, that God may open to us a door for the word, to declare the mystery of Christ, on account of which I am in prison, ^colossians-04-3

4 that I may make it clear, as I ought to speak. ^colossians-04-4

5 Conduct yourselves wisely toward outsiders, making the most of the time. ^colossians-04-5

6 Let your speech always be gracious, seasoned with salt, so that you may know how you ought to answer every one. ^colossians-04-6

**Final Greetings and Benediction**

7 Tych'icus will tell you all about my affairs; he is a beloved brother and faithful minister and fellow servant in the Lord. ^colossians-04-7

8 I have sent him to you for this very purpose, that you may know how we are and that he may encourage your hearts, ^colossians-04-8

9 and with him Ones'imus, the faithful and beloved brother, who is one of yourselves. They will tell you of everything that has taken place here. ^colossians-04-9

10 Aristar'chus my fellow prisoner greets you, and Mark the cousin of Barnabas \* (concerning whom you have received instructions—if he comes to you, receive him), ^colossians-04-10

11 and Jesus who is called Justus. These are the only men of the circumcision among my fellow workers for the kingdom of God, and they have been a comfort to me. ^colossians-04-11

12 Ep'aphras, who is one of yourselves, a servant<sup>g</sup> of Christ Jesus, greets you, always remembering you earnestly in his prayers, that you may stand mature and fully assured in all the will of God. ^colossians-04-12

13 For I bear him witness that he has worked hard for you and for those in La-odice'a and in Hi-erap'olis. ^colossians-04-13

14 Luke the beloved physician \* and Demas greet you. ^colossians-04-14

15 Give my greetings to the brethren at La-odice'a, and to Nympha and the church in her house. ^colossians-04-15

16 And when this letter has been read among you, have it read also in the Church of the La- odice'ans; and see that you read also the letter from La- odice'a. ^colossians-04-16

17 And say to Archip'pus, "See that you fulfil the ministry which you have received in the Lord." ^colossians-04-17

18 I, Paul, write this greeting with my own hand. Remember my chains. Grace be with you. ^colossians-04-18


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (4:10) Mark, the evangelist, and, probably, the John Mark of Acts 12:12, 25. ^colossians-04-dn-1

\* (4:14) Luke, the evangelist. ^colossians-04-dn-2
