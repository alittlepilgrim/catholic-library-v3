# Isaiah 1 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-01|NABRE]]

**The Sinfulness of Judah**

1 The vision of Isai'ah the son of A'moz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzzi'ah, Jo'tham, A'haz, and Hezeki'ah, kings of Judah. ^isaiah-01-1

2 Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth; for the LORD has spoken: “Sons have I reared and brought up, but they have rebelled against me. ^isaiah-01-2

3 The ox knows its owner, and the donkey its master’s crib; but Israel does not know, my people does not understand.” ^isaiah-01-3

4 Ah, sinful nation, a people laden with iniquity, offspring of evildoers, sons who deal corruptly! They have forsaken the LORD, they have despised the Holy One of Israel, they are utterly estranged. ^isaiah-01-4

5 Why will you still be struck down, that you continue to rebel? The whole head is sick, and the whole heart faint. ^isaiah-01-5

6 From the sole of the foot even to the head, there is no soundness in it, but bruises and sores and bleeding wounds; they are not pressed out, or bound up, or softened with oil. ^isaiah-01-6

7 Your country lies desolate, your cities are burned with fire; in your very presence strangers devour your land; it is desolate, as overthrown by strangers. ^isaiah-01-7

8 And the daughter of Zion is left like a booth in a vineyard, like a lodge in a cucumber field, like a besieged city. ^isaiah-01-8

9 If the LORD of hosts had not left us a few survivors, we should have been like Sodom, and become like Gomor'rah. ^isaiah-01-9

10 Hear the word of the LORD, you rulers of Sodom! Give ear to the teaching of our God, you people of Gomor'rah! ^isaiah-01-10

11 “What to me is the multitude of your sacrifices? says the LORD; I have had enough of burnt offerings of rams and the fat of fed beasts; I do not delight in the blood of bulls, or of lambs, or of he-goats. ^isaiah-01-11

12 “When you come to appear before me, who requires of you this trampling of my courts? ^isaiah-01-12

13 Bring no more vain offerings; incense is an abomination to me. New moon and sabbath and the calling of assemblies— I cannot endure iniquity and solemn assembly. ^isaiah-01-13

14 Your new moons and your appointed feasts my soul hates; they have become a burden to me, I am weary of bearing them. ^isaiah-01-14

15 When you spread forth your hands, I will hide my eyes from you; even though you make many prayers, I will not listen; your hands are full of blood. ^isaiah-01-15

16 Wash yourselves; make yourselves clean; remove the evil of your doings from before my eyes; cease to do evil, ^isaiah-01-16

17 learn to do good; seek justice, correct oppression; defend the fatherless, plead for the widow. ^isaiah-01-17

18 “Come now, let us reason together, says the LORD: though your sins are like scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; though they are red like crimson, they shall become like wool. ^isaiah-01-18

19 If you are willing and obedient, you shall eat the good of the land; ^isaiah-01-19

20 but if you refuse and rebel, you shall be devoured by the sword; for the mouth of the LORD has spoken.” ^isaiah-01-20

21 How the faithful city has become a harlot, she that was full of justice! Righteousness lodged in her, but now murderers. ^isaiah-01-21

22 Your silver has become dross, your wine mixed with water. ^isaiah-01-22

23 Your princes are rebels and companions of thieves. Every one loves a bribe and runs after gifts. They do not defend the fatherless, and the widow’s cause does not come to them. ^isaiah-01-23

24 Therefore the Lord says, the LORD of hosts, the Mighty One of Israel: “Ah, I will vent my wrath on my enemies, and avenge myself on my foes. ^isaiah-01-24

25 I will turn my hand against you and will smelt away your dross as with lye and remove all your alloy. ^isaiah-01-25

26 And I will restore your judges as at the first, and your counselors as at the beginning. Afterward you shall be called the city of righteousness, the faithful city.” ^isaiah-01-26

27 Zion shall be redeemed by justice, and those in her who repent, by righteousness. ^isaiah-01-27

28 But rebels and sinners shall be destroyed together, and those who forsake the LORD shall be consumed. ^isaiah-01-28

29 For you shall be ashamed of the oaks in which you delighted; and you shall blush for the gardens which you have chosen. ^isaiah-01-29

30 For you shall be like an oak whose leaf withers, and like a garden without water. ^isaiah-01-30

31 And the strong shall become tow, and his work a spark, and both of them shall burn together, with none to quench them. ^isaiah-01-31
