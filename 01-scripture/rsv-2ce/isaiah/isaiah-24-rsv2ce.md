# Isaiah 24 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-24|NABRE]]

**Impending Judgment on the Earth**

1 Behold, the LORD will lay waste the earth and make it desolate, and he will twist its surface and scatter its inhabitants. ^isaiah-24-1

2 And it shall be, as with the people, so with the priest; as with the slave, so with his master; as with the maid, so with her mistress; as with the buyer, so with the seller; as with the lender, so with the borrower; as with the creditor, so with the debtor. ^isaiah-24-2

3 The earth shall be utterly laid waste and utterly despoiled; for the LORD has spoken this word. ^isaiah-24-3

4 The earth mourns and withers, the world languishes and withers; the heavens languish together with the earth. ^isaiah-24-4

5 The earth lies polluted under its inhabitants; for they have transgressed the laws, violated the statutes, broken the everlasting covenant. ^isaiah-24-5

6 Therefore a curse devours the earth, and its inhabitants suffer for their guilt; therefore the inhabitants of the earth are scorched, and few men are left. ^isaiah-24-6

7 The wine mourns, the vine languishes, all the merry-hearted sigh. ^isaiah-24-7

8 The mirth of the timbrels is stilled, the noise of the jubilant has ceased, the mirth of the lyre is stilled. ^isaiah-24-8

9 No more do they drink wine with singing; strong drink is bitter to those who drink it. ^isaiah-24-9

10 The city of chaos is broken down, every house is shut up so that none can enter. ^isaiah-24-10

11 There is an outcry in the streets for lack of wine; all joy has reached its eventide; the gladness of the earth is banished. ^isaiah-24-11

12 Desolation is left in the city, the gates are battered into ruins. ^isaiah-24-12

13 For thus it shall be in the midst of the earth among the nations, as when an olive tree is beaten, as at the gleaning when the vintage is done. ^isaiah-24-13

14 They lift up their voices, they sing for joy; over the majesty of the LORD they shout from the west. ^isaiah-24-14

15 Therefore in the east give glory to the LORD; in the islands of the sea, to the name of the LORD, the God of Israel. ^isaiah-24-15

16 From the ends of the earth we hear songs of praise, of glory to the Righteous One. But I say, “I pine away, I pine away. Woe is me! For the treacherous deal treacherously, the treacherous deal very treacherously.” ^isaiah-24-16

17 Terror, and the pit, and the snare are upon you, O inhabitant of the earth! ^isaiah-24-17

18 He who flees at the sound of the terror shall fall into the pit; and he who climbs out of the pit shall be caught in the snare. For the windows of heaven are opened, and the foundations of the earth tremble. ^isaiah-24-18

19 The earth is utterly broken, the earth is torn apart, the earth is violently shaken. ^isaiah-24-19

20 The earth staggers like a drunken man, it sways like a hut; its transgression lies heavy upon it, and it falls, and will not rise again. ^isaiah-24-20

21 On that day the LORD will punish the host of heaven, in heaven, and the kings of the earth, on the earth. ^isaiah-24-21

22 They will be gathered together as prisoners in a pit; they will be shut up in a prison, and after many days they will be punished. ^isaiah-24-22

23 Then the moon will be confounded, and the sun ashamed; for the LORD of hosts will reign on Mount Zion and in Jerusalem and before his elders he will manifest his glory. ^isaiah-24-23
