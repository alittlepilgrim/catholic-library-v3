# Isaiah 26 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-26|NABRE]]

**Judah’s Song of Praise to God**

1 In that day this song will be sung in the land of Judah: “We have a strong city; he sets up salvation as walls and bulwarks. ^isaiah-26-1

2 Open the gates, that the righteous nation which keeps faith may enter in. ^isaiah-26-2

3 You keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on you, because he trusts in you. ^isaiah-26-3

4 Trust in the LORD for ever, for the LORD GOD is an everlasting rock. ^isaiah-26-4

5 For he has brought low the inhabitants of the height, the lofty city. He lays it low, lays it low to the ground, casts it to the dust. ^isaiah-26-5

6 The foot tramples it, the feet of the poor, the steps of the needy.” ^isaiah-26-6

7 The way of the righteous is level; you make smooth the path of the righteous. ^isaiah-26-7

8 In the path of your judgments, O LORD, we wait for you; your memorial name is the desire of our soul. ^isaiah-26-8

9 My soul yearns for you in the night, my spirit within me earnestly seeks you. For when your judgments are in the earth, the inhabitants of the world learn righteousness. ^isaiah-26-9

10 If favor is shown to the wicked, he does not learn righteousness; in the land of uprightness he deals perversely and does not see the majesty of the LORD. ^isaiah-26-10

11 O LORD, your hand is lifted up, but they see it not. Let them see your zeal for your people, and be ashamed. Let the fire for your adversaries consume them. ^isaiah-26-11

12 O LORD, you will ordain peace for us, you have wrought for us all our works. ^isaiah-26-12

13 O LORD our God, other lords besides you have ruled over us, but your name alone we acknowledge. ^isaiah-26-13

14 They are dead, they will not live; they are shades, they will not arise; to that end you have visited them with destruction and wiped out all remembrance of them. ^isaiah-26-14

15 But you have increased the nation, O LORD, you have increased the nation; you are glorified; you have enlarged all the borders of the land. ^isaiah-26-15

16 O LORD, in distress they sought you, they poured out a prayer when your chastening was upon them. ^isaiah-26-16

17 Like a woman with child, who writhes and cries out in her pangs, when she is near her time, so were we because of you, O LORD; ^isaiah-26-17

18 we were with child, we writhed, but we gave birth only to wind. We have wrought no deliverance in the earth, and the inhabitants of the world have not fallen. ^isaiah-26-18

19 Your dead shall live, their bodies shall rise. O dwellers in the dust, awake and sing for joy! For your dew is a dew of light, and on the land of the shades you will let it fall. ^isaiah-26-19

20 Come, my people, enter your chambers, and shut your doors behind you; hide yourselves for a little while until the wrath is past. ^isaiah-26-20

21 For behold, the LORD is coming forth out of his place to punish the inhabitants of the earth for their iniquity, and the earth will disclose the blood shed upon her, and will no more cover her slain. ^isaiah-26-21
