# Isaiah 47 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-47|NABRE]]

**The Humiliation of Babylon**

1 Come down and sit in the dust, O virgin daughter of Babylon; sit on the ground without a throne, O daughter of the Chalde'ans! For you shall no more be called tender and delicate. ^isaiah-47-1

2 Take the millstones and grind meal, put off your veil, strip off your robe, uncover your legs, pass through the rivers. ^isaiah-47-2

3 Your nakedness shall be uncovered, and your shame shall be seen. I will take vengeance, and I will spare no man. ^isaiah-47-3

4 Our Redeemer—the LORD of hosts is his name— is the Holy One of Israel. ^isaiah-47-4

5 Sit in silence, and go into darkness, O daughter of the Chalde'ans; for you shall no more be called the mistress of kingdoms. ^isaiah-47-5

6 I was angry with my people, I profaned my heritage; I gave them into your hand, you showed them no mercy; on the aged you made your yoke exceedingly heavy. ^isaiah-47-6

7 You said, “I shall be mistress for ever,” so that you did not lay these things to heart or remember their end. ^isaiah-47-7

8 Now therefore hear this, you lover of pleasures, who sit securely, who say in your heart, “I am, and there is no one besides me; I shall not sit as a widow or know the loss of children”: ^isaiah-47-8

9 These two things shall come to you in a moment, in one day; the loss of children and widowhood shall come upon you in full measure, in spite of your many sorceries and the great power of your enchantments. ^isaiah-47-9

10 You felt secure in your wickedness, you said, “No one sees me”; your wisdom and your knowledge led you astray, and you said in your heart, “I am, and there is no one besides me.” ^isaiah-47-10

11 But evil shall come upon you, for which you cannot atone; disaster shall fall upon you, which you will not be able to expiate; and ruin shall come on you suddenly, of which you know nothing. ^isaiah-47-11

12 Stand fast in your enchantments and your many sorceries, with which you have labored from your youth; perhaps you may be able to succeed, perhaps you may inspire terror. ^isaiah-47-12

13 You are wearied with your many counsels; let them stand forth and save you, those who divide the heavens, who gaze at the stars, who at the new moons predict what shall befall you. ^isaiah-47-13

14 Behold, they are like stubble, the fire consumes them; they cannot deliver themselves from the power of the flame. No coal for warming oneself is this, no fire to sit before! ^isaiah-47-14

15 Such to you are those with whom you have labored, who have trafficked with you from your youth; they wander about each in his own direction; there is no one to save you. ^isaiah-47-15
