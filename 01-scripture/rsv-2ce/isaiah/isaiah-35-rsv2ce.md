# Isaiah 35 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-35|NABRE]]

**The Lord Will Come to Save**

1 The wilderness and the dry land shall be glad, the desert shall rejoice and blossom; like the lily ^isaiah-35-1

2 it shall blossom abundantly, and rejoice with joy and singing. The glory of Lebanon shall be given to it, the majesty of Car'mel and Sharon. They shall see the glory of the LORD, the majesty of our God. ^isaiah-35-2

3 Strengthen the weak hands, and make firm the feeble knees. ^isaiah-35-3

4 Say to those who are of a fearful heart, “Be strong, fear not! Behold, your God will come with vengeance, with the recompense of God. He will come and save you.” ^isaiah-35-4

5 Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf unstopped; ^isaiah-35-5

6 then shall the lame man leap like a deer, and the tongue of the mute sing for joy. For waters shall break forth in the wilderness, and streams in the desert; ^isaiah-35-6

7 the burning sand shall become a pool, and the thirsty ground springs of water; the haunt of jackals shall become a swamp, the grass shall become reeds and rushes. ^isaiah-35-7

8 And a highway shall be there, and it shall be called the Holy Way; the unclean shall not pass over it, and fools shall not err therein. ^isaiah-35-8

9 No lion shall be there, nor shall any ravenous beast come up on it; they shall not be found there, but the redeemed shall walk there. ^isaiah-35-9

10 And the ransomed of the LORD shall return, and come to Zion with singing; everlasting joy shall be upon their heads; they shall obtain joy and gladness, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away. ^isaiah-35-10
