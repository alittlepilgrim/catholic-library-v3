# Isaiah 3 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-03|NABRE]]

**The Lord’s Judgment**

1 For behold, the Lord, the LORD of hosts, is taking away from Jerusalem and from Judah stay and staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water; ^isaiah-03-1

2 the mighty man and the soldier, the judge and the prophet, the diviner and the elder, ^isaiah-03-2

3 the captain of fifty and the man of rank, the counselor and the skilful magician and the expert in charms. ^isaiah-03-3

4 And I will make boys their princes, and infants shall rule over them. ^isaiah-03-4

5 And the people will oppress one another, every man his fellow and every man his neighbor; the youth will be insolent to the elder, and the base fellow to the honorable. ^isaiah-03-5

6 When a man takes hold of his brother in the house of his father, saying: “You have a mantle; you shall be our leader, and this heap of ruins shall be under your rule”; ^isaiah-03-6

7 in that day he will speak out, saying: “I will not be a healer; in my house there is neither bread nor mantle; you shall not make me leader of the people.” ^isaiah-03-7

8 For Jerusalem has stumbled, and Judah has fallen; because their speech and their deeds are against the LORD, defying his glorious presence. ^isaiah-03-8

9 Their partiality witnesses against them; they proclaim their sin like Sodom, they do not hide it. Woe to them! For they have brought evil upon themselves. ^isaiah-03-9

10 Tell the righteous that it shall be well with them, for they shall eat the fruit of their deeds. ^isaiah-03-10

11 Woe to the wicked! It shall be ill with him, for what his hands have done shall be done to him. ^isaiah-03-11

12 My people—children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. O my people, your leaders mislead you, and confuse the course of your paths. ^isaiah-03-12

13 The LORD has taken his place to contend, he stands to judge his people. ^isaiah-03-13

14 The LORD enters into judgment with the elders and princes of his people: “It is you who have devoured the vineyard, the spoil of the poor is in your houses. ^isaiah-03-14

15 What do you mean by crushing my people, by grinding the face of the poor?” says the Lord GOD of hosts. ^isaiah-03-15

16 The LORD said: Because the daughters of Zion are haughty and walk with outstretched necks, glancing wantonly with their eyes, mincing along as they go, tinkling with their feet; ^isaiah-03-16

17 the Lord will strike with a scab the heads of the daughters of Zion, and the LORD will lay bare their secret parts. ^isaiah-03-17

18 In that day the Lord will take away the finery of the anklets, the headbands, and the crescents; ^isaiah-03-18

19 the pendants, the bracelets, and the scarfs; ^isaiah-03-19

20 the headdresses, the armlets, the sashes, the perfume boxes, and the amulets; ^isaiah-03-20

21 the signet rings and nose rings; ^isaiah-03-21

22 the festal robes, the mantles, the cloaks, and the handbags; ^isaiah-03-22

23 the garments of gauze, the linen garments, the turbans, and the veils. ^isaiah-03-23

24 Instead of perfume there will be rottenness; and instead of a belt, a rope; and instead of well-set hair, baldness; and instead of a rich robe, a putting on of sackcloth; instead of beauty, shame. ^isaiah-03-24

25 Your men shall fall by the sword and your mighty men in battle. ^isaiah-03-25

26 And her gates shall lament and mourn; ravaged, she shall sit upon the ground. ^isaiah-03-26
