# Isaiah 32 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-32|NABRE]]

**A Reign of Righteousness and Justice**

1 Behold, a king will reign in righteousness, and princes will rule in justice. ^isaiah-32-1

2 Each will be like a hiding place from the wind, a covert from the tempest, like streams of water in a dry place, like the shade of a great rock in a weary land. ^isaiah-32-2

3 Then the eyes of those who see will not be closed, and the ears of those who hear will listen. ^isaiah-32-3

4 The mind of the rash will have good judgment, and the tongue of the stammerers will speak readily and distinctly. ^isaiah-32-4

5 The fool will no more be called noble, nor the knave said to be honorable. ^isaiah-32-5

6 For the fool speaks folly, and his mind plots iniquity: to practice ungodliness, to utter error concerning the LORD, to leave the craving of the hungry unsatisfied, and to deprive the thirsty of drink. ^isaiah-32-6

7 The knaveries of the knave are evil; he devises wicked devices to ruin the poor with lying words, even when the plea of the needy is right. ^isaiah-32-7

8 But he who is noble devises noble things, and by noble things he stands. ^isaiah-32-8

9 Rise up, you women who are at ease, hear my voice; you complacent daughters, give ear to my speech. ^isaiah-32-9

10 In little more than a year you will shudder, you complacent women; for the vintage will fail, the fruit harvest will not come. ^isaiah-32-10

11 Tremble, you women who are at ease, shudder, you complacent ones; strip, and make yourselves bare, and put sackcloth upon your loins. ^isaiah-32-11

12 Beat upon your breasts for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine, ^isaiah-32-12

13 for the soil of my people growing up in thorns and briers; yes, for all the joyous houses in the joyful city. ^isaiah-32-13

14 For the palace will be forsaken, the populous city deserted; the hill and the watchtower will become dens for ever, a joy of wild donkeys, a pasture of flocks; ^isaiah-32-14

15 until the Spirit is poured upon us from on high, and the wilderness becomes a fruitful field, and the fruitful field is deemed a forest. ^isaiah-32-15

16 Then justice will dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness abide in the fruitful field. ^isaiah-32-16

17 And the effect of righteousness will be peace, and the result of righteousness, quietness and trust for ever. ^isaiah-32-17

18 My people will abide in a peaceful habitation, in secure dwellings, and in quiet resting places. ^isaiah-32-18

19 And the forest will utterly go down, and the city will be utterly laid low. ^isaiah-32-19

20 Happy are you who sow beside all waters, who let the feet of the ox and the donkey range free. ^isaiah-32-20
