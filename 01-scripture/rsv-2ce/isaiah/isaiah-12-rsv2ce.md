# Isaiah 12 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[isaiah-12|NABRE]]

**Thanksgiving and Praise**

1 You will say in that day: “I will give thanks to you, O LORD, for though you were angry with me, your anger turned away, and you did comfort me. ^isaiah-12-1

2 “Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and will not be afraid; for the LORD GOD is my strength and my song, and he has become my salvation.” ^isaiah-12-2

3 With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation. ^isaiah-12-3

4 And you will say in that day: “Give thanks to the LORD, call upon his name; make known his deeds among the nations, proclaim that his name is exalted. ^isaiah-12-4

5 “Sing praises to the LORD, for he has done gloriously; let this be known in all the earth. ^isaiah-12-5

6 Shout, and sing for joy, O inhabitant of Zion, for great in your midst is the Holy One of Israel.” ^isaiah-12-6
