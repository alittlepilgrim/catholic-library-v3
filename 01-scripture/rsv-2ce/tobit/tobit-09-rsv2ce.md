# Tobit 9 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[tobit-09|NABRE]]

**The Money Recovered from Gabael**

1 Then Tobi'as called Ra'phael and said to him, ^tobit-09-1

2 “Brother Azari'as, take a servant and two camels with you and go to Gab'ael at Ra'ges in Med'ia and get the money for me; and bring him to the wedding feast. ^tobit-09-2

3 For Rag'uel has sworn that I should not leave; ^tobit-09-3

4 but my father is counting the days, and if I delay long he will be greatly distressed.” ^tobit-09-4

5 So Ra'phael made the journey and stayed overnight with Gab'ael. He gave him the receipt, and Gabael brought out the money bags with their seals intact and gave them to him. ^tobit-09-5

6 In the morning they both got up early and came to the wedding feast. And Gab'ael blessed Tobi'as and his wife. ^tobit-09-6
