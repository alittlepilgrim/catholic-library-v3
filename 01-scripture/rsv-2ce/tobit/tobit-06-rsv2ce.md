# Tobit 6 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[tobit-06|NABRE]]

**A Miraculous Fish**

1 Now as they proceeded on their way they came at evening to the Tigris river and camped there. ^tobit-06-1

2 Then the young man went down to wash himself. A fish leaped up from the river and would have swallowed the young man; ^tobit-06-2

3 and the angel said to him, “Catch the fish.” So the young man seized the fish and threw it up on the land. ^tobit-06-3

4 Then the angel said to him, “Cut open the fish and take the heart and liver and gall and put them away safely.” ^tobit-06-4

5 So the young man did as the angel told him; and they roasted and ate the fish. And they both continued on their way until they came near to Ecbat'ana. ^tobit-06-5

6 Then the young man said to the angel, “Brother Azari'as, of what use is the liver and heart and gall of the fish?” ^tobit-06-6

7 He replied, “As for the heart and liver, if a demon or evil spirit gives trouble to any one, you make a smoke from these before the man or woman, and that person will never be troubled again. ^tobit-06-7

8 And as for the gall, anoint with it a man who has white films in his eyes, and he will be cured.” ^tobit-06-8

**Raphael’s Instructions**

9 When he entered Med'ia and was already approaching Ecbat'ana, ^tobit-06-9

10 Ra'phael said to the young man, “Brother Tobi'as!” “Here I am,” he answered. Then Raphael said to him, “We must stay this night in the home of Ra'guel. He is your relative, and he has a daughter named Sarah. He has no male heir and no daughter except Sarah only, and you, as next of kin to her, have before all other men a hereditary claim on her. ^tobit-06-10

11 Also, it is right for you to inherit her father’s possessions. ^tobit-06-11

12 Moreover, the girl is sensible, brave, and very beautiful, and her father is a good man.” ^tobit-06-12

13 Then the young man said to the angel, “Brother Azari'as, I have heard that the girl has been given to seven husbands and that each died in the bridal chamber. ^tobit-06-13

14 Now I am the only son my father has, and I am afraid that if I go in I will die as those before me did, for a demon is in love with her, and he harms no one except those who approach her. So now I fear that I may die and bring the lives of my father and mother to the grave in sorrow on my account. And they have no other son to bury them.” ^tobit-06-14

15 But the angel said to him, “Do you not remember the words with which your father commanded you to take a wife from among your own people? Now listen to me, brother, for she will become your wife; and do not worry about the demon, for this very night she will be given to you in marriage. ^tobit-06-15

16 When you enter the bridal chamber, you shall take live ashes of incense and lay upon them some of the heart and liver of the fish so as to make a smoke. ^tobit-06-16

17 Then the demon will smell it and flee away, and will never again return. And when you approach her, rise up, both of you, and cry out to the merciful God, and he will save you and have mercy on you. Do not be afraid, for she was destined for you from eternity. You will save her, and she will go with you, and I suppose that you will have children by her.” When Tobi'as heard these things, he fell in love with her and yearned deeply for her. ^tobit-06-17
