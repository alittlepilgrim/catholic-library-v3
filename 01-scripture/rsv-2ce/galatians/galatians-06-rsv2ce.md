# Galatians 6 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Bear One Another's Burdens**

1 Brethren, if a man is overtaken in any trespass, you who are spiritual should restore him in a spirit of gentleness. Look to yourself, lest you too be tempted. ^galatians-06-1

2 Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ. ^galatians-06-2

3 For if any one thinks he is something, when he is nothing, he deceives himself. ^galatians-06-3

4 But let each one test his own work, and then his reason to boast will be in himself alone and not in his neighbor. ^galatians-06-4

5 For each man will have to bear his own load. ^galatians-06-5

6 Let him who is taught the word share all good things with him who teaches. ^galatians-06-6

7 Do not be deceived; God is not mocked, for whatever a man sows, that he will also reap. ^galatians-06-7

8 For he who sows to his own flesh will from the flesh reap corruption; but he who sows to the Spirit will from the Spirit reap eternal life. ^galatians-06-8

9 And let us not grow weary in well-doing, for in due season we shall reap, if we do not lose heart. ^galatians-06-9

10 So then, as we have opportunity, let us do good to all men, and especially to those who are of the household of faith. ^galatians-06-10

**Final Admonitions and Benediction**

11 See with what large letters I am writing to you with my own hand. ^galatians-06-11

12 It is those who want to make a good showing in the flesh that would compel you to be circumcised, and only in order that they may not be persecuted for the cross of Christ. ^galatians-06-12

13 For even those who receive circumcision do not themselves keep the law, but they desire to have you circumcised that they may glory in your flesh. ^galatians-06-13

14 But far be it from me to glory except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by which<sup>l</sup> the world has been crucified to me, and I to the world. ^galatians-06-14

15 For neither circumcision counts for anything, nor uncircumcision, but a new creation. ^galatians-06-15

16 Peace and mercy be upon all who walk by this rule, upon the Israel of God. ^galatians-06-16

17 Henceforth let no man trouble me; for I bear on my body the marks of Jesus. ^galatians-06-17

18 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit, brethren. Amen. ^galatians-06-18
