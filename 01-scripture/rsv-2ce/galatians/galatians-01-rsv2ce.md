# Galatians 1 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Salutation**

1 Paul an apostle—not from men nor through man, but through Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead— ^galatians-01-1

2 and all the brethren who are with me,

To the churches of Galatia: ^galatians-01-2

3 Grace to you and peace from God the Father and our Lord Jesus Christ, ^galatians-01-3

4 who gave himself for our sins to deliver us from the present evil age, according to the will of our God and Father; ^galatians-01-4

5 to whom be the glory for ever and ever. Amen. ^galatians-01-5

**There Is No Other Gospel**

6 I am astonished that you are so quickly deserting him who called you in the grace of Christ and turning to a different gospel— \* ^galatians-01-6

7 not that there is another gospel, but there are some who trouble you and want to pervert the gospel of Christ. ^galatians-01-7

8 But even if we, or an angel from heaven, should preach to you a gospel contrary to that which we preached to you, let him be accursed. ^galatians-01-8

9 As we have said before, so now I say again, If any one is preaching to you a gospel contrary to that which you received, let him be accursed. ^galatians-01-9

10 Am I now seeking the favor of men, or of God? Or am I trying to please men? If I were still pleasing men, I should not be a servant<sup>a</sup> of Christ. \* ^galatians-01-10

**Paul's Vindication of His Apostleship**

11 Brethren, I would have you know that the gospel which was preached by me is not man's<sup>b</sup> gospel. ^galatians-01-11

12 For I did not receive it from man, nor was I taught it, but it came through a revelation of Jesus Christ. ^galatians-01-12

13 For you have heard of my former life in Judaism, how I persecuted the Church of God violently and tried to destroy it; ^galatians-01-13

14 and I advanced in Judaism beyond many of my own age among my people, so extremely zealous was I for the traditions of my fathers. ^galatians-01-14

15 But when he who had set me apart before I was born, and had called me through his grace, ^galatians-01-15

16 was pleased to reveal his Son to<sup>c</sup> me, in order that I might preach him among the Gentiles, I did not confer with flesh and blood, ^galatians-01-16

17 nor did I go up to Jerusalem to those who were apostles before me, but I went away into Arabia; and again I returned to Damascus. ^galatians-01-17

18 Then after three years I went up to Jerusalem to visit Ce'phas, and remained with him fifteen days. ^galatians-01-18

19 But I saw none of the other apostles except James the Lord's brother. \* ^galatians-01-19

20 (In what I am writing to you, before God, I do not lie!) ^galatians-01-20

21 Then I went into the regions of Syria and Cili'cia. ^galatians-01-21

22 And I was still not known by sight to the churches of Christ in Judea; ^galatians-01-22

23 they only heard it said, "He who once persecuted us is now preaching the faith he once tried to destroy." ^galatians-01-23

24 And they glorified God because of me. ^galatians-01-24

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:6) After the greeting there is no commendation, as was usual, but rather strong rebuke. ^galatians-01-dn-1

\* (1:10) No doubt Paul was accused of exempting Gentile converts from the law of Moses in order to curry favor. ^galatians-01-dn-2

\* (1:19) Lord’s brother: See the note on brethren at Mt 12:46 above. ^galatians-01-dn-3
