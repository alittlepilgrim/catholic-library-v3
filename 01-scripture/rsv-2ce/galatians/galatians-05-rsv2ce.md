# Galatians 5 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Christian Freedom**

1 For freedom Christ has set us free; stand fast therefore, and do not submit again to a yoke of slavery. ^galatians-05-1

2 Now I, Paul, say to you that if you receive circumcision, Christ will be of no advantage to you. ^galatians-05-2

3 I testify again to every man who receives circumcision that he is bound to keep the whole law. ^galatians-05-3

4 You are severed from Christ, you who would be justified by the law; you have fallen away from grace. ^galatians-05-4

5 For through the Spirit, by faith, we wait for the hope of righteousness. ^galatians-05-5

6 For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision nor uncircumcision is of any avail, but faith working through love. ^galatians-05-6

7 You were running well; who hindered you from obeying the truth? ^galatians-05-7

8 This persuasion is not from him who called you. ^galatians-05-8

9 A little leaven leavens all the dough. ^galatians-05-9

10 I have confidence in the Lord that you will take no other view than mine; and he who is troubling you will bear his judgment, whoever he is. ^galatians-05-10

11 But if I, brethren, still preach circumcision, why am I still persecuted? In that case the stumbling block of the cross \* has been removed. ^galatians-05-11

12 I wish those who unsettle you would mutilate themselves! ^galatians-05-12

13 For you were called to freedom, brethren; only do not use your freedom as an opportunity for the flesh, but through love be servants of one another. ^galatians-05-13

14 For the whole law is fulfilled in one word, "You shall love your neighbor as yourself." ^galatians-05-14

15 But if you bite and devour one another take heed that you are not consumed by one another. ^galatians-05-15

**The Works of the Flesh and the Fruit of the Spirit**

16 But I say, walk by the Spirit, and do not gratify the desires of the flesh. ^galatians-05-16

17 For the desires of the flesh are against the Spirit, and the desires of the Spirit are against the flesh; for these are opposed to each other, to prevent you from doing what you would. ^galatians-05-17

18 But if you are led by the Spirit you are not under the law. ^galatians-05-18

19 Now the works of the flesh are plain: immorality, impurity, licentiousness, ^galatians-05-19

20 idolatry, sorcery, enmity, strife, jealousy, anger, selfishness, dissension, party spirit, ^galatians-05-20

21 envy,<sup>k</sup> drunkenness, carousing, and the like. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God. ^galatians-05-21

22 But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, ^galatians-05-22

23 gentleness, self-control; against such there is no law. ^galatians-05-23

24 And those who belong to Christ Jesus have crucified the flesh with its passions and desires. ^galatians-05-24

25 If we live by the Spirit, let us also walk by the Spirit. ^galatians-05-25

26 Let us have no self-conceit, no provoking of one another, no envy of one another. ^galatians-05-26


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (5:11) stumbling block of the cross: So far as the Jews were concerned, this would consist largely in the exemption of converts from the obligations of the law of Moses. ^galatians-05-dn-1
