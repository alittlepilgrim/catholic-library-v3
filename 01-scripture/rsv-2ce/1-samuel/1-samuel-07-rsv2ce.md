# 1 Samuel 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[1-samuel-07|NABRE]]

1 And the men of Kir'iath-je'arim came and took up the ark of the LORD, and brought it to the house of Abin'adab on the hill; and they consecrated his son, Elea'zar, to have charge of the ark of the LORD. ^1-samuel-07-1

2 From the day that the ark was lodged at Kir'iath-je'arim, a long time passed, some twenty years, and all the house of Israel lamented after the LORD. ^1-samuel-07-2

**Samuel as Judge of Israel**

3 Then Samuel said to all the house of Israel, “If you are returning to the LORD with all your heart, then put away the foreign gods and the Ash'taroth from among you, and direct your heart to the LORD, and serve him only, and he will deliver you out of the hand of the Philis'tines.” ^1-samuel-07-3

4 So Israel put away the Ba'als and the Ash'taroth, and they served the LORD only. ^1-samuel-07-4

5 Then Samuel said, “Gather all Israel at Mizpah, and I will pray to the Lord for you.” ^1-samuel-07-5

6 So they gathered at Mizpah, and drew water and poured it out before the Lord, and fasted on that day, and said there, “We have sinned against the Lord.” And Samuel judged the sons of Israel at Mizpah. ^1-samuel-07-6

7 Now when the Philis'tines heard that the sons of Israel had gathered at Mizpah, the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel. And when the sons of Israel heard of it they were afraid of the Philistines. ^1-samuel-07-7

8 And the sons of Israel said to Samuel, “Do not cease to cry to the Lord our God for us, that he may save us from the hand of the Philis'tines.” ^1-samuel-07-8

9 So Samuel took a suckling lamb and offered it as a whole burnt offering to the Lord; and Samuel cried to the Lord for Israel, and the Lord answered him. ^1-samuel-07-9

10 As Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philis'tines drew near to attack Israel; but the Lord thundered with a mighty voice that day against the Philistines and threw them into confusion; and they were routed before Israel. ^1-samuel-07-10

11 And the men of Israel went out of Mizpah and pursued the Philis'tines, and struck them, as far as below Beth-car. ^1-samuel-07-11

12 Then Samuel took a stone and set it up between Mizpah and Jesha'nah, and called its name Ebenezer; for he said, “Hitherto the LORD has helped us.” ^1-samuel-07-12

13 So the Philis'tines were subdued and did not again enter the territory of Israel. And the hand of the LORD was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel. ^1-samuel-07-13

14 The cities which the Philis'tines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ek'ron to Gath; and Israel rescued their territory from the hand of the Philistines. There was peace also between Israel and the Am'orites. ^1-samuel-07-14

15 Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life. ^1-samuel-07-15

16 And he went on a circuit year by year to Bethel, Gilgal, and Mizpah; and he judged Israel in all these places. ^1-samuel-07-16

17 Then he would come back to Ra'mah, for his home was there, and there also he administered justice to Israel. And he built there an altar to the LORD. ^1-samuel-07-17
