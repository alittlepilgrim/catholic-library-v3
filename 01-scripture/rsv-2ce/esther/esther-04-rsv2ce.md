# Esther 4 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[esther-04|NABRE]]

**Esther Agrees to Help the Jews**

1 When Mor'decai learned all that had been done, Mordecai tore his clothes and put on sackcloth and ashes, and went out into the midst of the city, wailing with a loud and bitter cry; ^esther-04-1

2 he went up to the entrance of the king’s gate, for no one might enter the king’s gate clothed with sackcloth. ^esther-04-2

3 And in every province, wherever the king’s command and his decree came, there was great mourning among the Jews, with fasting and weeping and lamenting, and most of them lay in sackcloth and ashes. ^esther-04-3

4 When Esther’s maids and her eunuchs came and told her, the queen was deeply distressed; she sent garments to clothe Mor'decai, so that he might take off his sackcloth, but he would not accept them. ^esther-04-4

5 Then Esther called for Ha'thach, one of the king’s eunuchs, who had been appointed to attend her, and ordered him to go to Mor'decai to learn what this was and why it was. ^esther-04-5

6 Ha'thach went out to Mor'decai in the open square of the city in front of the king’s gate, ^esther-04-6

7 and Mor'decai told him all that had happened to him, and the exact sum of money that Ha'man had promised to pay into the king’s treasuries for the destruction of the Jews. ^esther-04-7

8 Mor'decai also gave him a copy of the written decree issued in Susa for their destruction, that he might show it to Esther and explain it to her and charge her to go to the king to make supplication to him and entreat him for her people, “Remembering the days of your lowliness, when you were cared for by me, because Haman, who is next to the king, spoke against us for our destruction. Beseech the Lord and speak to the king concerning us and deliver us from death.” ^esther-04-8

9 And Ha'thach went and told Esther what Mor'decai had said. ^esther-04-9

10 Then Esther spoke to Ha'thach and gave him a message for Mor'decai, saying, ^esther-04-10

11 “All the king’s servants and the people of the king’s provinces know that if any man or woman goes to the king inside the inner court without being called, there is but one law; all alike are to be put to death, except the one to whom the king holds out the golden scepter that he may live. And I have not been called to come in to the king these thirty days.” ^esther-04-11

12 And they told Mor'decai what Esther had said. ^esther-04-12

13 Then Mor'decai told them to return answer to Esther, “Think not that in the king’s palace you will escape any more than all the other Jews. ^esther-04-13

14 For if you keep silence at such a time as this, relief and deliverance will rise for the Jews from another quarter, but you and your father’s house will perish. And who knows whether you have not come to the kingdom for such a time as this?” ^esther-04-14

15 Then Esther told them to reply to Mor'decai, ^esther-04-15

16 “Go, gather all the Jews to be found in Susa, and hold a fast on my behalf, and neither eat nor drink for three days, night or day. I and my maids will also fast as you do. Then I will go to the king, though it is against the law; and if I perish, I perish.” ^esther-04-16

17 Mor'decai then went away and did everything as Esther had ordered him. ^esther-04-17
