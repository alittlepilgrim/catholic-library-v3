# Genesis 16 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-16|NABRE]]

**The Birth of Ishmael**

1 Now Sar'ai, Abram’s wife, bore him no children. She had an Egyptian maid whose name was Hagar; ^genesis-16-1

2 and Sar'ai said to Abram, “Behold now, the LORD has prevented me from bearing children; go in to my maid; it may be that I shall obtain children by her.” And Abram listened to the voice of Sar'ai. ^genesis-16-2

3 So, after Abram had dwelt ten years in the land of Canaan, Sar'ai, Abram’s wife, took Hagar the Egyptian, her maid, and gave her to Abram her husband as a wife. ^genesis-16-3

4 And he went in to Hagar, and she conceived; and when she saw that she had conceived, she looked with contempt on her mistress. ^genesis-16-4

5 And Sar'ai said to Abram, “May the wrong done to me be on you! I gave my maid to your embrace, and when she saw that she had conceived, she looked on me with contempt. May the LORD judge between you and me!” ^genesis-16-5

6 But Abram said to Sar'ai, “Behold, your maid is in your power; do to her as you please.” Then Sarai dealt harshly with her, and she fled from her. ^genesis-16-6

7 The angel of the LORD found her by a spring of water in the wilderness, the spring on the way to Shur. ^genesis-16-7

8 And he said, “Hagar, maid of Sar'ai, where have you come from and where are you going?” She said, “I am fleeing from my mistress Sarai.” ^genesis-16-8

9 The angel of the LORD said to her, “Return to your mistress, and submit to her.” ^genesis-16-9

10 The angel of the LORD also said to her, “I will so greatly multiply your descendants that they cannot be numbered for multitude.” ^genesis-16-10

11 And the angel of the LORD said to her, “Behold, you are with child, and shall bear a son; you shall call his name Ish'mael; because the LORD has given heed to your affliction. ^genesis-16-11

12 He shall be a wild donkey of a man, his hand against every man and every man’s hand against him; and he shall dwell over against all his kinsmen.” ^genesis-16-12

13 So she called the name of the LORD who spoke to her, “You are a God of seeing”; for she said, “Have I really seen God and remained alive after seeing him?” ^genesis-16-13

14 Therefore the well was called Be'er-la'hai-roi; it lies between Ka'desh and Be'red. ^genesis-16-14

15 And Hagar bore Abram a son; and Abram called the name of his son, whom Hagar bore, Ish'mael. ^genesis-16-15

16 Abram was eighty-six years old when Hagar bore Ish'mael to Abram. ^genesis-16-16

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (16:1–4) The practice suggested by Sarai, involving as it did polygamy, was in accord with moral standards at that time and is referred to in legal codes of the period. ^genesis-16-dn-1
