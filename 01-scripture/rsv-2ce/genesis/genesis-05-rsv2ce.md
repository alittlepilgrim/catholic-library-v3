# Genesis 5 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-05|NABRE]]

**Adam’s Descendants to Noah**

1 This is the book of the generations of Adam. When God created man, he made him in the likeness of God. ^genesis-05-1

2 Male and female he created them, and he blessed them and named them Man when they were created. ^genesis-05-2

3 When Adam had lived a hundred and thirty years, he became the father of a son in his own likeness, after his image, and named him Seth. ^genesis-05-3

4 The days of Adam after he became the father of Seth were eight hundred years; and he had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-4

5 Thus all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years; and he died. ^genesis-05-5

6 When Seth had lived a hundred and five years, he became the father of E'nosh. ^genesis-05-6

7 Seth lived after the birth of E'nosh eight hundred and seven years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-7

8 Thus all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years; and he died. ^genesis-05-8

9 When E'nosh had lived ninety years, he became the father of Ke'nan. ^genesis-05-9

10 E'nosh lived after the birth of Ke'nan eight hundred and fifteen years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-10

11 Thus all the days of E'nosh were nine hundred and five years; and he died. ^genesis-05-11

12 When Ke'nan had lived seventy years, he became the father of Ma-hal'alel. ^genesis-05-12

13 Ke'nan lived after the birth of Ma-hal'alel eight hundred and forty years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-13

14 Thus all the days of Ke'nan were nine hundred and ten years; and he died. ^genesis-05-14

15 When Ma-hal'alel had lived sixty-five years, he became the father of Jar'ed. ^genesis-05-15

16 Ma-hal'alel lived after the birth of Jar'ed eight hundred and thirty years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-16

17 Thus all the days of Ma-hal'alel were eight hundred and ninety-five years; and he died. ^genesis-05-17

18 When Jar'ed had lived a hundred and sixty-two years he became the father of E'noch. ^genesis-05-18

19 Jared lived after the birth of E'noch eight hundred years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-19

20 Thus all the days of Jar'ed were nine hundred and sixty-two years; and he died. ^genesis-05-20

21 When E'noch had lived sixty-five years, he became the father of Methu'selah. ^genesis-05-21

22 E'noch walked with God after the birth of Methu'selah three hundred years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-22

23 Thus all the days of E'noch were three hundred and sixty-five years. ^genesis-05-23

24 E'noch walked with God; and he was not, for God took him. ^genesis-05-24

25 When Methu'selah had lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, he became the father of La'mech. ^genesis-05-25

26 Methu'selah lived after the birth of La'mech seven hundred and eighty-two years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-26

27 Thus all the days of Methu'selah were nine hundred and sixty-nine years; and he died. ^genesis-05-27

28 When La'mech had lived a hundred and eighty-two years, he became the father of a son, ^genesis-05-28

29 and called his name Noah, saying, “Out of the ground which the LORD has cursed this one shall bring us relief from our work and from the toil of our hands.” ^genesis-05-29

30 La'mech lived after the birth of Noah five hundred and ninety-five years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-30

31 Thus all the days of La'mech were seven hundred and seventy-seven years; and he died. ^genesis-05-31

32 After Noah was five hundred years old, Noah became the father of Shem, Ham, and Ja'pheth. ^genesis-05-32

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (5:1) generations: It should be noted that these genealogies are selective and schematic, and the numbers, as often in the Old Testament, are symbolic. ^genesis-05-dn-1
