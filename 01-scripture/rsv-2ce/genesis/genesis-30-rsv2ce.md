# Genesis 30 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-30|NABRE]]

1 When Rachel saw that she bore Jacob no children, she envied her sister; and she said to Jacob, “Give me children, or I shall die!” ^genesis-30-1

2 Jacob’s anger was kindled against Rachel, and he said, “Am I in the place of God, who has withheld from you the fruit of the womb?” ^genesis-30-2

3 Then she said, “Here is my maid Bilhah; go in to her, that she may bear upon my knees, and even I may have children through her.” ^genesis-30-3

4 So she gave him her maid Bilhah as a wife; and Jacob went in to her. ^genesis-30-4

5 And Bilhah conceived and bore Jacob a son. ^genesis-30-5

6 Then Rachel said, “God has judged me, and has also heard my voice and given me a son”; therefore she called his name Dan. ^genesis-30-6

7 Rachel’s maid Bilhah conceived again and bore Jacob a second son. ^genesis-30-7

8 Then Rachel said, “With mighty wrestlings I have wrestled with my sister, and have prevailed”; so she called his name Naph'tali. ^genesis-30-8

9 When Leah saw that she had ceased bearing children, she took her maid Zilpah and gave her to Jacob as a wife. ^genesis-30-9

10 Then Leah’s maid Zilpah bore Jacob a son. ^genesis-30-10

11 And Leah said, “Good fortune!” so she called his name Gad. ^genesis-30-11

12 Leah’s maid Zilpah bore Jacob a second son. ^genesis-30-12

13 And Leah said, “Blessed am I! For the women will call me blessed”; so she called his name Asher. ^genesis-30-13

14 In the days of wheat harvest Reuben went and found mandrakes in the field, and brought them to his mother Leah. Then Rachel said to Leah, “Give me, I pray, some of your son’s mandrakes.” ^genesis-30-14

15 But she said to her, “Is it a small matter that you have taken away my husband? Would you take away my son’s mandrakes also?” Rachel said, “Then he may lie with you tonight for your son’s mandrakes.” ^genesis-30-15

16 When Jacob came from the field in the evening, Leah went out to meet him, and said, “You must come in to me; for I have hired you with my son’s mandrakes.” So he lay with her that night. ^genesis-30-16

17 And God hearkened to Leah, and she conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son. ^genesis-30-17

18 Leah said, “God has given me my hire because I gave my maid to my husband”; so she called his name Is'sachar. ^genesis-30-18

19 And Leah conceived again, and she bore Jacob a sixth son. ^genesis-30-19

20 Then Leah said, “God has endowed me with a good dowry; now my husband will honor me, because I have borne him six sons”; so she called his name Zeb'ulun. ^genesis-30-20

21 Afterwards she bore a daughter, and called her name Dinah. ^genesis-30-21

22 Then God remembered Rachel, and God hearkened to her and opened her womb. ^genesis-30-22

23 She conceived and bore a son, and said, “God has taken away my reproach”; ^genesis-30-23

24 and she called his name Joseph, saying, “May the LORD add to me another son!” ^genesis-30-24

**Jacob Prospers**

25 When Rachel had borne Joseph, Jacob said to La'ban, “Send me away, that I may go to my own home and country. ^genesis-30-25

26 Give me my wives and my children for whom I have served you, and let me go; for you know the service which I have given you.” ^genesis-30-26

27 But La'ban said to him, “If you will allow me to say so, I have learned by divination that the LORD has blessed me because of you; ^genesis-30-27

28 name your wages, and I will give it.” ^genesis-30-28

29 Jacob said to him, “You yourself know how I have served you, and how your cattle have fared with me. ^genesis-30-29

30 For you had little before I came, and it has increased abundantly; and the LORD has blessed you wherever I turned. But now when shall I provide for my own household also?” ^genesis-30-30

31 He said, “What shall I give you?” Jacob said, “You shall not give me anything; if you will do this for me, I will again feed your flock and keep it: ^genesis-30-31

32 let me pass through all your flock today, removing from it every speckled and spotted sheep and every black lamb, and the spotted and speckled among the goats; and such shall be my wages. ^genesis-30-32

33 So my honesty will answer for me later, when you come to look into my wages with you. Every one that is not speckled and spotted among the goats and black among the lambs, if found with me, shall be counted stolen.” ^genesis-30-33

34 La'ban said, “Good! Let it be as you have said.” ^genesis-30-34

35 But that day La'ban removed the he-goats that were striped and spotted, and all the she-goats that were speckled and spotted, every one that had white on it, and every lamb that was black, and put them in charge of his sons; ^genesis-30-35

36 and he set a distance of three days’ journey between himself and Jacob; and Jacob fed the rest of La'ban’s flock. ^genesis-30-36

37 Then Jacob took fresh rods of poplar and almond and plane, and peeled white streaks in them, exposing the white of the rods. ^genesis-30-37

38 He set the rods which he had peeled in front of the flocks in the runnels, that is, the watering troughs, where the flocks came to drink. And since they bred when they came to drink, ^genesis-30-38

39 the flocks bred in front of the rods and so the flocks brought forth striped, speckled, and spotted. ^genesis-30-39

40 And Jacob separated the lambs, and set the faces of the flocks toward the striped and all the black in the flock of La'ban; and he put his own droves apart, and did not put them with Laban’s flock. ^genesis-30-40

41 Whenever the stronger of the flock were breeding Jacob laid the rods in the runnels before the eyes of the flock, that they might breed among the rods, ^genesis-30-41

42 but for the feebler of the flock he did not lay them there; so the feebler were La'ban’s, and the stronger Jacob’s. ^genesis-30-42

43 Thus the man grew exceedingly rich, and had large flocks, maidservants and menservants, and camels and donkeys. ^genesis-30-43
