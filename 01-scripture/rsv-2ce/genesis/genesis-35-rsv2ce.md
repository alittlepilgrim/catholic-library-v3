# Genesis 35 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-35|NABRE]]

**Jacob Returns to Bethel**

1 God said to Jacob, “Arise, go up to Bethel, and dwell there; and make there an altar to the God who appeared to you when you fled from your brother Esau.” ^genesis-35-1

2 So Jacob said to his household and to all who were with him, “Put away the foreign gods that are among you, and purify yourselves, and change your garments; ^genesis-35-2

3 then let us arise and go up to Bethel, that I may make there an altar to the God who answered me in the day of my distress and has been with me wherever I have gone.” ^genesis-35-3

4 So they gave to Jacob all the foreign gods that they had, and the rings that were in their ears; and Jacob hid them under the oak which was near She'chem. ^genesis-35-4

5 And as they journeyed, a terror from God fell upon the cities that were round about them, so that they did not pursue the sons of Jacob. ^genesis-35-5

6 And Jacob came to Luz (that is, Bethel), which is in the land of Canaan, he and all the people who were with him, ^genesis-35-6

7 and there he built an altar, and called the place El-beth'el, because there God had revealed himself to him when he fled from his brother. ^genesis-35-7

8 And Deborah, Rebekah’s nurse, died, and she was buried under an oak below Bethel; so the name of it was called Al'lon-bac'uth. ^genesis-35-8

9 God appeared to Jacob again, when he came from Pad'dan-ar'am, and blessed him. ^genesis-35-9

10 And God said to him, “Your name is Jacob; no longer shall your name be called Jacob, but Israel shall be your name.” So his name was called Israel. ^genesis-35-10

11 And God said to him, “I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall come from you, and kings shall spring from you. ^genesis-35-11

12 The land which I gave to Abraham and Isaac I will give to you, and I will give the land to your descendants after you.” ^genesis-35-12

13 Then God went up from him in the place where he had spoken with him. ^genesis-35-13

14 And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he had spoken with him, a pillar of stone; and he poured out a drink offering on it, and poured oil on it. ^genesis-35-14

15 So Jacob called the name of the place where God had spoken with him, Bethel. ^genesis-35-15

**The Birth of Benjamin and the Death of Rachel**

16 Then they journeyed from Bethel; and when they were still some distance from Eph'rath, Rachel went into labor, and she had hard labor. ^genesis-35-16

17 And when she was in her hard labor, the midwife said to her, “Fear not; for now you will have another son.” ^genesis-35-17

18 And as her soul was departing (for she died), she called his name Ben-o'ni; but his father called his name Benjamin. ^genesis-35-18

19 So Rachel died, and she was buried on the way to Eph'rath (that is, Bethlehem), ^genesis-35-19

20 and Jacob set up a pillar upon her grave; it is the pillar of Rachel’s tomb, which is there to this day. ^genesis-35-20

21 Israel journeyed on, and pitched his tent beyond the tower of E'der. ^genesis-35-21

22 While Israel dwelt in that land Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father’s concubine; and Israel heard of it. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve. ^genesis-35-22

23 The sons of Leah: Reuben (Jacob’s first-born), Simeon, Levi, Judah, Is'sachar, and Zeb'ulun. ^genesis-35-23

24 The sons of Rachel: Joseph and Benjamin. ^genesis-35-24

25 The sons of Bilhah, Rachel’s maid: Dan and Naph'tali. ^genesis-35-25

26 The sons of Zilpah, Leah’s maid: Gad and Asher. These were the sons of Jacob who were born to him in Pad'dan-ar'am. ^genesis-35-26

**The Death of Isaac**

27 And Jacob came to his father Isaac at Mamre, or Kir'iath-ar'ba (that is, He'bron), where Abraham and Isaac had sojourned. ^genesis-35-27

28 Now the days of Isaac were a hundred and eighty years. ^genesis-35-28

29 And Isaac breathed his last; and he died and was gathered to his people, old and full of days; and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him. ^genesis-35-29
