# Genesis 25 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-25|NABRE]]

**Abraham Marries Keturah**

1 Abraham took another wife, whose name was Ketu'rah. ^genesis-25-1

2 She bore him Zimran, Jokshan, Me'dan, Mid'ian, Ishbak, and Shuah. ^genesis-25-2

3 Jokshan was the father of Sheba and De'dan. The sons of Dedan were Asshu'rim, Letu'shim, and Le-um'mim. ^genesis-25-3

4 The sons of Mid'ian were E'phah, E'pher, Ha'noch, Abi'da, and Elda'ah. All these were the children of Ketu'rah. ^genesis-25-4

5 Abraham gave all he had to Isaac. ^genesis-25-5

6 But to the sons of his concubines Abraham gave gifts, and while he was still living he sent them away from his son Isaac, eastward to the east country. ^genesis-25-6

**The Death of Abraham**

7 These are the days of the years of Abraham’s life, a hundred and seventy-five years. ^genesis-25-7

8 Abraham breathed his last and died in a good old age, an old man and full of years, and was gathered to his people. ^genesis-25-8

9 Isaac and Ish'mael his sons buried him in the cave of Mach-pe'lah, in the field of E'phron the son of Zo'har the Hittite, east of Mamre, ^genesis-25-9

10 the field which Abraham purchased from the Hittites. There Abraham was buried, with Sarah his wife. ^genesis-25-10

11 After the death of Abraham God blessed Isaac his son. And Isaac dwelt at Be'er-la'hai-roi. ^genesis-25-11

**The Descendants of Ishmael**

12 These are the descendants of Ish'mael, Abraham’s son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s maid, bore to Abraham. ^genesis-25-12

13 These are the names of the sons of Ish'mael, named in the order of their birth: Neba'ioth, the first-born of Ishmael; and Ke'dar, Ad'beel, Mibsam, ^genesis-25-13

14 Mishma, Du'mah, Massa, ^genesis-25-14

15 Ha'dad, Te'ma, Je'tur, Na'phish, and Ked'emah. ^genesis-25-15

16 These are the sons of Ish'mael and these are their names, by their villages and by their encampments, twelve princes according to their tribes. ^genesis-25-16

17 (These are the years of the life of Ish'mael, a hundred and thirty-seven years; he breathed his last and died, and was gathered to his kindred.) ^genesis-25-17

18 They dwelt from Hav'ilah to Shur, which is opposite Egypt in the direction of Assyria; he settled over against all his people. ^genesis-25-18

**The Birth of Esau and Jacob**

19 These are the descendants of Isaac, Abraham’s son: Abraham was the father of Isaac, ^genesis-25-19

20 and Isaac was forty years old when he took to wife Rebekah, the daughter of Bethu'el the Arame'an of Pad'dan-ar'am, the sister of La'ban the Aramean. ^genesis-25-20

21 And Isaac prayed to the LORD for his wife, because she was barren; and the LORD granted his prayer, and Rebekah his wife conceived. ^genesis-25-21

22 The children struggled together within her; and she said, “If it is thus, why do I live?” So she went to inquire of the LORD. ^genesis-25-22

23 And the LORD said to her, “Two nations are in your womb, and two peoples, born of you, shall be divided; the one shall be stronger than the other, the elder shall serve the younger.” ^genesis-25-23

24 When her days to be delivered were fulfilled, behold, there were twins in her womb. ^genesis-25-24

25 The first came forth red, all his body like a hairy mantle; so they called his name Esau. ^genesis-25-25

26 Afterward his brother came forth, and his hand had taken hold of Esau’s heel; so his name was called Jacob. Isaac was sixty years old when she bore them. ^genesis-25-26

**Esau Sells His Birthright**

27 When the boys grew up, Esau was a skilful hunter, a man of the field, while Jacob was a quiet man, dwelling in tents. ^genesis-25-27

28 Isaac loved Esau, because he ate of his game; but Rebekah loved Jacob. ^genesis-25-28

29 Once when Jacob was boiling pottage, Esau came in from the field, and he was famished. ^genesis-25-29

30 And Esau said to Jacob, “Let me eat some of that red pottage, for I am famished!” (Therefore his name was called E'dom.) ^genesis-25-30

31 Jacob said, “First sell me your birthright.” ^genesis-25-31

32 Esau said, “I am about to die; of what use is a birthright to me?” ^genesis-25-32

33 Jacob said, “Swear to me first.” So he swore to him, and sold his birthright to Jacob. ^genesis-25-33

34 Then Jacob gave Esau bread and pottage of lentils, and he ate and drank, and rose and went his way. Thus Esau despised his birthright. ^genesis-25-34

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (25:19) With the birth of Esau and Jacob we enter a new narrative cycle. Jacob, renamed Israel, is represented as the ancestor of the twelve tribes and, therefore, of the whole people; cf. Deut 26:5. ^genesis-25-dn-1
