# Genesis 46 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-46|NABRE]]

**Jacob Brings His Whole Family to Egypt**

1 So Israel took his journey with all that he had, and came to Be'er-she'ba, and offered sacrifices to the God of his father Isaac. ^genesis-46-1

2 And God spoke to Israel in visions of the night, and said, “Jacob, Jacob.” And he said, “Here am I.” ^genesis-46-2

3 Then he said, “I am God, the God of your father; do not be afraid to go down to Egypt; for I will there make of you a great nation. ^genesis-46-3

4 I will go down with you to Egypt, and I will also bring you up again; and Joseph’s hand shall close your eyes.” ^genesis-46-4

5 Then Jacob set out from Be'er-she'ba; and the sons of Israel carried Jacob their father, their little ones, and their wives, in the wagons which Pharoah had sent to carry him. ^genesis-46-5

6 They also took their cattle and their goods, which they had gained in the land of Canaan, and came into Egypt, Jacob and all his offspring with him, ^genesis-46-6

7 his sons, and his sons’ sons with him, his daughters, and his sons’ daughters; all his offspring he brought with him into Egypt. ^genesis-46-7

8 Now these are the names of the descendants of Israel, who came into Egypt, Jacob and his sons. Reuben, Jacob’s first-born, ^genesis-46-8

9 and the sons of Reuben: Ha'noch, Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi. ^genesis-46-9

10 The sons of Simeon: Jemu'el, Ja'min, O'had, Ja'chin, Zo'har, and Sha'ul, the son of a Canaanitish woman. ^genesis-46-10

11 The sons of Levi: Gershon, Ko'hath, and Merar'i. ^genesis-46-11

12 The sons of Judah: Er, O'nan, She'lah, Per'ez, and Ze'rah (but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan); and the sons of Perez were Hezron and Ha'mul. ^genesis-46-12

13 The sons of Is'sachar: To'la, Pu'vah, I'ob, and Shimron. ^genesis-46-13

14 The sons of Zeb'ulun: Se'red, E'lon, and Jah'leel ^genesis-46-14

15 (these are the sons of Leah, whom she bore to Jacob in Pad'dan-ar'am, together with his daughter Dinah; altogether his sons and his daughters numbered thirty-three). ^genesis-46-15

16 The sons of Gad: Ziph'ion, Haggi, Shu'ni, Ezbon, E'ri, Aro'di, and Are'li. ^genesis-46-16

17 The sons of Asher: Imnah, Ishvah, Ishvi, Beri'ah, with Se'rah their sister. And the sons of Beriah: He'ber and Mal'chi-el ^genesis-46-17

18 (these are the sons of Zilpah, whom La'ban gave to Leah his daughter; and these she bore to Jacob—sixteen persons). ^genesis-46-18

19 The sons of Rachel, Jacob’s wife: Joseph and Benjamin. ^genesis-46-19

20 And to Joseph in the land of Egypt were born Manas'seh and E'phraim, whom As'enath, the daughter of Poti'phera the priest of On, bore to him. ^genesis-46-20

21 And the sons of Benjamin: Be'la, Be'cher, Ashbel, Ge'ra, Na'aman, E'hi, Rosh, Muppim, Huppim, and Ard ^genesis-46-21

22 (these are the sons of Rachel, who were born to Jacob —fourteen persons in all). ^genesis-46-22

23 The sons of Dan: Hu'shim. ^genesis-46-23

24 The sons of Naph'tali: Jah'zeel, Gu'ni, Je'zer, and Shillem ^genesis-46-24

25 (these are the sons of Bilhah, whom La'ban gave to Rachel his daughter, and these she bore to Jacob—seven persons in all). ^genesis-46-25

26 All the persons belonging to Jacob who came into Egypt, who were his own offspring, not including Jacob’s sons’ wives, were sixty-six persons in all; ^genesis-46-26

27 and the sons of Joseph, who were born to him in Egypt, were two; all the persons of the house of Jacob, that came into Egypt, were seventy. ^genesis-46-27

**Jacob Dwells in the Land of Goshen**

28 He sent Judah before him to Joseph, to appear before him in Go'shen; and they came into the land of Goshen. ^genesis-46-28

29 Then Joseph made ready his chariot and went up to meet Israel his father in Go'shen; and he presented himself to him, and fell on his neck, and wept on his neck a good while. ^genesis-46-29

30 Israel said to Joseph, “Now let me die, since I have seen your face and know that you are still alive.” ^genesis-46-30

31 Joseph said to his brothers and to his father’s household, “I will go up and tell Pharoah, and will say to him, ‘My brothers and my father’s household, who were in the land of Canaan, have come to me; ^genesis-46-31

32 and the men are shepherds, for they have been keepers of cattle; and they have brought their flocks, and their herds, and all that they have.’ ^genesis-46-32

33 When Pharoah calls you, and says, ‘What is your occupation?’ ^genesis-46-33

34 you shall say, ‘Your servants have been keepers of cattle from our youth even until now, both we and our fathers,’ in order that you may dwell in the land of Go'shen; for every shepherd is an abomination to the Egyptians.” ^genesis-46-34
