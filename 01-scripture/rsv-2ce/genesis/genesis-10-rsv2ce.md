# Genesis 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-10|NABRE]]

**Nations Descended from Noah**

1 These are the generations of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Ja'pheth; sons were born to them after the flood. ^genesis-10-1

2 The sons of Ja'pheth: Gomer, Ma'gog, Ma'dai, Ja'van, Tu'bal, Me'shech, and Ti'ras. ^genesis-10-2

3 The sons of Gomer: Ash'kenaz, Ri'phath, and Togar'mah. ^genesis-10-3

4 The sons of Ja'van: Eli'shah, Tar'shish, Kittim, and Do'danim. ^genesis-10-4

5 From these the coastland peoples spread. These are the sons of Ja'pheth in their lands, each with his own language, by their families, in their nations. ^genesis-10-5

6 The sons of Ham: Cush, Egypt, Put, and Canaan. ^genesis-10-6

7 The sons of Cush: Seba, Hav'ilah, Sabtah, Ra'amah, and Sab'teca. The sons of Raamah: Sheba and De'dan. ^genesis-10-7

8 Cush became the father of Nimrod; he was the first on earth to be a mighty man. ^genesis-10-8

9 He was a mighty hunter before the LORD; therefore it is said, “Like Nimrod a mighty hunter before the LORD.” ^genesis-10-9

10 The beginning of his kingdom was Ba'bel, E'rech, and Accad, all of them in the land of Shi'nar. ^genesis-10-10

11 From that land he went into Assyria, and built Nin'eveh, Reho'both-Ir, Ca'lah, and ^genesis-10-11

12 Re'sen between Nin'eveh and Ca'lah; that is the great city. ^genesis-10-12

13 Egypt became the father of Lu'dim, An'amim, Leha'bim, Naph'tuhim, ^genesis-10-13

14 Pathru'sim, Caslu'him (whence came the Philis'tines), and Caph'torim. ^genesis-10-14

15 Canaan became the father of Si'don his first-born, and Heth, ^genesis-10-15

16 and the Jeb'usites, the Am'orites, the Gir'gashites, ^genesis-10-16

17 the Hi'vites, the Arkites, the Si'nites, ^genesis-10-17

18 the Ar'vadites, the Zem'arites, and the Ha'mathites. Afterward the families of the Canaanites spread abroad. ^genesis-10-18

19 And the territory of the Canaanites extended from Si'don, in the direction of Ge'rar, as far as Gaza, and in the direction of Sodom, Gomor'rah, Admah, and Zeboi'im, as far as La'sha. ^genesis-10-19

20 These are the sons of Ham, by their families, their languages, their lands, and their nations. ^genesis-10-20

21 To Shem also, the father of all the children of E'ber, the elder brother of Ja'pheth, children were born. ^genesis-10-21

22 The sons of Shem: E'lam, Asshur, Arpach'shad, Lud, and Ar'am. ^genesis-10-22

23 The sons of Ar'am: Uz, Hul, Ge'ther, and Mash. ^genesis-10-23

24 Arpach'shad became the father of She'lah; and Shelah became the father of E'ber. ^genesis-10-24

25 To E'ber were born two sons: the name of the one was Pe'leg, for in his days the earth was divided, and his brother’s name was Joktan. ^genesis-10-25

26 Joktan became the father of Almo'dad, She'leph, Haz''arma'veth, Je'rah, ^genesis-10-26

27 Hador'am, U'zal, Diklah, ^genesis-10-27

28 O'bal, Abim'a-el, Sheba, ^genesis-10-28

29 O'phir, Hav'ilah, and Jo'bab; all these were the sons of Joktan. ^genesis-10-29

30 The territory in which they lived extended from Me'sha in the direction of Se'phar to the hill country of the east. ^genesis-10-30

31 These are the sons of Shem, by their families, their languages, their lands, and their nations. ^genesis-10-31

32 These are the families of the sons of Noah, according to their genealogies, in their nations; and from these the nations spread abroad on the earth after the flood. ^genesis-10-32

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (10:1) generations: This “table of the nations” makes use of old material to show how all the nations of the world as then known have descended from the generation that survived the Flood. It was from this world that Abraham was called to be the father of the chosen people. ^genesis-10-dn-1
