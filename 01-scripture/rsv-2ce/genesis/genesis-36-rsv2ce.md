# Genesis 36 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-36|NABRE]]

**Esau’s Descendants**

1 These are the descendants of Esau (that is, E'dom). ^genesis-36-1

2 Esau took his wives from the Canaanites: A'dah the daughter of E'lon the Hittite, Oholiba'mah the daughter of An'ah the son of Zib'eon the Hi'vite, ^genesis-36-2

3 and Bas'emath, Ish'mael’s daughter, the sister of Neba'ioth. ^genesis-36-3

4 And A'dah bore to Esau, Eli'phaz; Bas'emath bore Reu'el; ^genesis-36-4

5 and Oholiba'mah bore Je'ush, Ja'lam, and Ko'rah. These are the sons of Esau who were born to him in the land of Canaan. ^genesis-36-5

6 Then Esau took his wives, his sons, his daughters, and all the members of his household, his cattle, all his beasts, and all his property which he had acquired in the land of Canaan; and he went into a land away from his brother Jacob. ^genesis-36-6

7 For their possessions were too great for them to dwell together; the land of their sojournings could not support them because of their cattle. ^genesis-36-7

8 So Esau dwelt in the hill country of Se'ir; Esau is E'dom. ^genesis-36-8

9 These are the descendants of Esau the father of the E'domites in the hill country of Se'ir. ^genesis-36-9

10 These are the names of Esau’s sons: Eli'phaz the son of A'dah the wife of Esau, Reu'el the son of Bas'emath the wife of Esau. ^genesis-36-10

11 The sons of Eli'phaz were Te'man, Omar, Ze'pho, Ga'tam, and Ke'naz. ^genesis-36-11

12 (Timna was a concubine of Eli'phaz, Esau’s son; she bore Am'alek to Eliphaz.) These are the sons of A'dah, Esau’s wife. ^genesis-36-12

13 These are the sons of Reu'el: Na'hath, Ze'rah, Shammah, and Mizzah. These are the sons of Bas'emath, Esau’s wife. ^genesis-36-13

14 These are the sons of Oholiba'mah the daughter of An'ah the son of Zib'eon, Esau’s wife: she bore to Esau Je'ush, Ja'lam, and Ko'rah. ^genesis-36-14

**Chiefs and Kings of Edom**

15 These are the chiefs of the sons of Esau. The sons of Eli'phaz the first-born of Esau: the chiefs Te'man, Omar, Ze'pho, Ke'naz, ^genesis-36-15

16 Ko'rah, Ga'tam, and Am'alek; these are the chiefs of Eli'phaz in the land of E'dom; they are the sons of A'dah. ^genesis-36-16

17 These are the sons of Reu'el, Esau’s son: the chiefs Na'hath, Ze'rah, Shammah, and Mizzah; these are the chiefs of Reu'el in the land of E'dom; they are the sons of Bas'emath, Esau’s wife. ^genesis-36-17

18 These are the sons of Oholiba'mah, Esau’s wife: the chiefs Je'ush, Ja'lam, and Ko'rah; these are the chiefs born of Oholiba'mah the daughter of An'ah, Esau’s wife. ^genesis-36-18

19 These are the sons of Esau (that is, E'dom), and these are their chiefs. ^genesis-36-19

20 These are the sons of Se'ir the Horite, the inhabitants of the land: Lo'tan, Sho'bal, Zib'eon, An'ah, ^genesis-36-20

21 Di'shon, E'zer, and Di'shan; these are the chiefs of the Horites, the sons of Se'ir in the land of E'dom. ^genesis-36-21

22 The sons of Lo'tan were Ho'ri and He'man; and Lo'tan’s sister was Timna. ^genesis-36-22

23 These are the sons of Sho'bal: Alvan, Man'ahath, E'bal, She'pho, and Onam. ^genesis-36-23

24 These are the sons of Zib'eon: A'iah and An'ah; he is the Anah who found the hot springs in the wilderness, as he pastured the donkeys of Zibeon his father. ^genesis-36-24

25 These are the children of An'ah: Di'shon and Oholiba'mah the daughter of Anah. ^genesis-36-25

26 These are the sons of Di'shon: Hemdan, Eshban, Ithran, and Che'ran. ^genesis-36-26

27 These are the sons of E'zer: Bilhan, Za'avan, and A'kan. ^genesis-36-27

28 These are the sons of Di'shan: Uz and Ar'an. ^genesis-36-28

29 These are the chiefs of the Horites: the chiefs Lo'tan, Sho'bal, Zib'eon, An'ah, ^genesis-36-29

30 Di'shon, E'zer, and Di'shan; these are the chiefs of the Horites, according to their clans in the land of Se'ir. ^genesis-36-30

31 These are the kings who reigned in the land of E'dom, before any king reigned over the Israelites. ^genesis-36-31

32 Be'la the son of Beor reigned in E'dom, the name of his city being Din'habah. ^genesis-36-32

33 Be'la died, and Jo'bab the son of Ze'rah of Bozrah reigned in his stead. ^genesis-36-33

34 Jo'bab died, and Hu'sham of the land of the Te'manites reigned in his stead. ^genesis-36-34

35 Hu'sham died, and Ha'dad the son of Be'dad, who defeated Mid'ian in the country of Moab, reigned in his stead, the name of his city being A'vith. ^genesis-36-35

36 Ha'dad died, and Samlah of Masre'kah reigned in his stead. ^genesis-36-36

37 Samlah died, and Sha'ul of Reho'both on the Euphra'tes reigned in his stead. ^genesis-36-37

38 Sha'ul died, and Ba'al-ha'nan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead. ^genesis-36-38

39 Ba'al-ha'nan the son of Achbor died, and Hadar reigned in his stead, the name of his city being Pa'u; his wife’s name was Mehet'abel, the daughter of Ma'tred, daughter of Me'zahab. ^genesis-36-39

40 These are the names of the chiefs of Esau, according to their families and their dwelling places, by their names: the chiefs Timna, Alvah, Je'theth, ^genesis-36-40

41 Oholiba'mah, E'lah, Pi'non, ^genesis-36-41

42 Ke'naz, Te'man, Mibzar, ^genesis-36-42

43 Mag'diel, and I'ram; these are the chiefs of E'dom (that is, Esau, the father of Edom), according to their dwelling places in the land of their possession. ^genesis-36-43
