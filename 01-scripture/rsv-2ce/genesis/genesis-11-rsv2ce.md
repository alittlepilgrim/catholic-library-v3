# Genesis 11 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[genesis-11|NABRE]]

**The Tower of Babel**

1 Now the whole earth had one language and few words. ^genesis-11-1

2 And as men migrated from the east, they found a plain in the land of Shinar and settled there. ^genesis-11-2

3 And they said to one another, “Come, let us make bricks, and burn them thoroughly.” And they had brick for stone, and bitumen for mortar. ^genesis-11-3

4 Then they said, “Come, let us build ourselves a city, and a tower with its top in the heavens, and let us make a name for ourselves, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.” ^genesis-11-4

5 And the LORD came down to see the city and the tower, which the sons of men had built. ^genesis-11-5

6 And the LORD said, “Behold, they are one people, and they have all one language; and this is only the beginning of what they will do; and nothing that they propose to do will now be impossible for them. ^genesis-11-6

7 Come, let us go down, and there confuse their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech.” ^genesis-11-7

8 So the LORD scattered them abroad from there over the face of all the earth, and they left off building the city. ^genesis-11-8

9 Therefore its name was called Ba'bel, because there the LORD confused the language of all the earth; and from there the LORD scattered them abroad over the face of all the earth. ^genesis-11-9

**Descendants of Shem**

10 These are the descendants of Shem. When Shem was a hundred years old, he became the father of Arpach'shad two years after the flood; ^genesis-11-10

11 and Shem lived after the birth of Arpach'shad five hundred years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-11

12 When Arpach'shad had lived thirty-five years, he became the father of She'lah; ^genesis-11-12

13 and Arpach'shad lived after the birth of She'lah four hundred and three years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-13

14 When She'lah had lived thirty years, he became the father of E'ber; ^genesis-11-14

15 and She'lah lived after the birth of E'ber four hundred and three years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-15

16 When E'ber had lived thirty-four years, he became the father of Pe'leg; ^genesis-11-16

17 and E'ber lived after the birth of Pe'leg four hundred and thirty years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-17

18 When Pe'leg had lived thirty years, he became the father of Re'u; ^genesis-11-18

19 and Pe'leg lived after the birth of Re'u two hundred and nine years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-19

20 When Re'u had lived thirty-two years, he became the father of Se'rug; ^genesis-11-20

21 and Re'u lived after the birth of Se'rug two hundred and seven years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-21

22 When Se'rug had lived thirty years, he became the father of Na'hor; ^genesis-11-22

23 and Se'rug lived after the birth of Na'hor two hundred years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-23

24 When Na'hor had lived twenty-nine years, he became the father of Te'rah; ^genesis-11-24

25 and Na'hor lived after the birth of Te'rah a hundred and nineteen years, and had other sons and daughters. ^genesis-11-25

26 When Te'rah had lived seventy years, he became the father of Abram, Na'hor, and Haran. ^genesis-11-26

**Descendants of Terah**

27 Now these are the descendants of Te'rah. Terah was the father of Abram, Na'hor, and Haran; and Haran was the father of Lot. ^genesis-11-27

28 Haran died before his father Te'rah in the land of his birth, in Ur of the Chalde'ans. ^genesis-11-28

29 And Abram and Na'hor took wives; the name of Abram’s wife was Sar'ai, and the name of Nahor’s wife, Milcah, the daughter of Haran the father of Milcah and Is'cah. ^genesis-11-29

30 Now Sar'ai was barren; she had no child. ^genesis-11-30

31 Te'rah took Abram his son and Lot the son of Haran, his grandson, and Sar'ai his daughter-in-law, his son Abram’s wife, and they went forth together from Ur of the Chalde'ans to go into the land of Canaan; but when they came to Haran, they settled there. ^genesis-11-31

32 The days of Te'rah were two hundred and five years; and Terah died in Haran. ^genesis-11-32

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (11:1–9) The tower of Babel ( = Babylon) is taken as a symbol of political power, empire-building, and the civilization that opposes God’s plan. The tower was probably a ziggurat, i.e., Babylonian temple. ^genesis-11-dn-1
