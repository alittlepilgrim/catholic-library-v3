# Amos 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[amos-07|NABRE]]

**Locusts and Fire**

1 Thus the Lord GOD showed me: behold, he was forming locusts in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and behold, it was the latter growth after the king’s mowings. ^amos-07-1

2 When they had finished eating the grass of the land, I said, “O Lord GOD, forgive, I beg you! How can Jacob stand? He is so small!” ^amos-07-2

3 The LORD repented concerning this; “It shall not be,” said the LORD. ^amos-07-3

4 Thus the Lord GOD showed me: behold, the Lord GOD was calling for a judgment by fire, and it devoured the great deep and was eating up the land. ^amos-07-4

5 Then I said, “O Lord GOD, cease, I beg you! How can Jacob stand? He is so small!” ^amos-07-5

6 The LORD repented concerning this; “This also shall not be,” said the Lord GOD. ^amos-07-6

**The Plumb Line**

7 He showed me: behold, the Lord was standing beside a wall built with a plumb line, with a plumb line in his hand. ^amos-07-7

8 And the LORD said to me, “Amos, what do you see?” And I said, “A plumb line.” Then the Lord said, “Behold, I am setting a plumb line in the midst of my people Israel; I will never again pass by them; ^amos-07-8

9 the high places of Isaac shall be made desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste, and I will rise against the house of Jerobo'am with the sword.” ^amos-07-9

**Amaziah’s Complaint**

10 Then Amazi'ah the priest of Bethel sent to Jerobo'am king of Israel, saying, “Amos has conspired against you in the midst of the house of Israel; the land is not able to bear all his words. ^amos-07-10

11 For thus Amos has said, ‘Jerobo'am shall die by the sword, and Israel must go into exile away from his land.’ ” ^amos-07-11

12 And Amazi'ah said to Amos, “O seer, go, flee away to the land of Judah, and eat bread there, and prophesy there; ^amos-07-12

13 but never again prophesy at Bethel, for it is the king’s sanctuary, and it is a temple of the kingdom.” ^amos-07-13

**Amos’ Reply**

14 Then Amos answered Amazi'ah, “I am no prophet, nor a prophet’s son; but I am a herdsman, and a dresser of sycamore trees, ^amos-07-14

15 and the LORD took me from following the flock, and the LORD said to me, ‘Go, prophesy to my people Israel.’ ^amos-07-15

16 “Now therefore hear the word of the LORD. You say, ‘Do not prophesy against Israel, and do not preach against the house of Isaac.’ ^amos-07-16

17 Therefore thus says the LORD: ‘Your wife shall be a harlot in the city, and your sons and your daughters shall fall by the sword, and your land shall be parceled out by line; you yourself shall die in an unclean land, and Israel shall surely go into exile away from its land.’ ” ^amos-07-17
