## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* Amos was a shepherd of Judah called suddenly by God to denounce social corruption and injustice in the northern kingdom during the reign of Jeroboam II (eighth century B.C.). It was a time of great material luxury and worldly splendor, and the pastoral origins of the prophet contrasted strongly with the sophisticated decadence which, together with the people’s infidelity, he denounced. He foretells the “day of the LORD,” a time of punishment for men’s sins, but holds out a hope of God’s mercy to “the remnant of Joseph” (5:15). Some of the prophecies are probably of a later age. ^amos-00-dn-1
