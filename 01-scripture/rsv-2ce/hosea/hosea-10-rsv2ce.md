# Hosea 10 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[hosea-10|NABRE]]

**Israel’s Sin and Captivity**

1 Israel is a luxuriant vine that yields its fruit. The more his fruit increased the more altars he built; as his country improved he improved his pillars. ^hosea-10-1

2 Their heart is false; now they must bear their guilt. The LORD will break down their altars, and destroy their pillars. ^hosea-10-2

3 For now they will say: “We have no king, for we fear not the LORD, and a king, what could he do for us?” ^hosea-10-3

4 They utter mere words; with empty oaths they make covenants; so judgment springs up like poisonous weeds in the furrows of the field. ^hosea-10-4

5 The inhabitants of Samar'ia tremble for the calf of Beth-a'ven. Its people shall mourn for it, and its idolatrous priests shall wail over it, over its glory which has departed from it. ^hosea-10-5

6 Yes, the thing itself shall be carried to Assyria, as tribute to the great king. E'phraim shall be put to shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his idol. ^hosea-10-6

7 Samar'ia’s king shall perish, like a chip on the face of the waters. ^hosea-10-7

8 The high places of A'ven, the sin of Israel, shall be destroyed. Thorn and thistle shall grow up on their altars; and they shall say to the mountains, Cover us, and to the hills, Fall upon us. ^hosea-10-8

9 From the days of Gib'e-ah, you have sinned, O Israel; there they have continued. Shall not war overtake them in Gibe-ah? ^hosea-10-9

10 I will come against the wayward people to chastise them; and nations shall be gathered against them when they are chastised for their double iniquity. ^hosea-10-10

11 E'phraim was a trained heifer that loved to thresh, and I spared her fair neck; but I will put Ephraim to the yoke, Judah must plow, Jacob must harrow for himself. ^hosea-10-11

12 Sow for yourselves righteousness, reap the fruit of mercy; break up your fallow ground, for it is the time to seek the LORD, that he may come and rain salvation upon you. ^hosea-10-12

13 You have plowed iniquity, you have reaped injustice, you have eaten the fruit of lies. Because you have trusted in your chariots and in the multitude of your warriors, ^hosea-10-13

14 therefore the tumult of war shall arise among your people, and all your fortresses shall be destroyed, as Shal'man destroyed Beth-ar'bel on the day of battle; mothers were dashed in pieces with their children. ^hosea-10-14

15 Thus it shall be done to you, O house of Israel, because of your great wickedness. In the storm the king of Israel shall be utterly cut off. ^hosea-10-15
