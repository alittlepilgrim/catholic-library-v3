# Hosea 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[hosea-02|NABRE]]

**Israel’s Infidelity, Punishment, and Redemption**

1 Say to your brother, “My people,” and to your sister, “She has obtained pity.” ^hosea-02-1

2 “Plead with your mother, plead— for she is not my wife, and I am not her husband— that she put away her harlotry from her face, and her adultery from between her breasts; ^hosea-02-2

3 lest I strip her naked and make her as in the day she was born, and make her like a wilderness, and set her like a parched land, and slay her with thirst. ^hosea-02-3

4 Upon her children also I will have no pity, because they are children of harlotry. ^hosea-02-4

5 For their mother has played the harlot; she that conceived them has acted shamefully. For she said, ‘I will go after my lovers, who give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, my oil and my drink.’ ^hosea-02-5

6 Therefore I will hedge up her way with thorns; and I will build a wall against her, so that she cannot find her paths. ^hosea-02-6

7 She shall pursue her lovers, but not overtake them; and she shall seek them, but shall not find them. Then she shall say, ‘I will go and return to my first husband, for it was better with me then than now.’ ^hosea-02-7

8 And she did not know that it was I who gave her the grain, the wine, and the oil, and who lavished upon her silver and gold which they used for Ba'al. ^hosea-02-8

9 Therefore I will take back my grain in its time, and my wine in its season; and I will take away my wool and my flax, which were to cover her nakedness. ^hosea-02-9

10 Now I will uncover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and no one shall rescue her out of my hand. ^hosea-02-10

11 And I will put an end to all her mirth, her feasts, her new moons, her sabbaths, and all her appointed feasts. ^hosea-02-11

12 And I will lay waste her vines and her fig trees, of which she said, ‘These are my hire, which my lovers have given me.’ I will make them a forest, and the beasts of the field shall devour them. ^hosea-02-12

13 And I will punish her for the feast days of the Ba'als when she burned incense to them and decked herself with her ring and jewelry, and went after her lovers, and forgot me, says the LORD. ^hosea-02-13

14 “Therefore, behold, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak tenderly to her. ^hosea-02-14

15 And there I will give her her vineyards, and make the Valley of A'chor a door of hope. And there she shall answer as in the days of her youth, as at the time when she came out of the land of Egypt. ^hosea-02-15

16 “And in that day, says the LORD, you will call me, ‘My husband,’ and no longer will you call me, ‘My Ba'al.’ ^hosea-02-16

17 For I will remove the names of the Ba'als from her mouth, and they shall be mentioned by name no more. ^hosea-02-17

18 And I will make for you a covenant on that day with the beasts of the field, the birds of the air, and the creeping things of the ground; and I will abolish the bow, the sword, and war from the land; and I will make you lie down in safety. ^hosea-02-18

19 And I will espouse you for ever; I will espouse you in righteousness and in justice, in steadfast love, and in mercy. ^hosea-02-19

20 I will espouse you in faithfulness; and you shall know the LORD. ^hosea-02-20

21 “And in that day, says the LORD, I will answer the heavens and they shall answer the earth; ^hosea-02-21

22 and the earth shall answer the grain, the wine, and the oil, and they shall answer Jezre'el; ^hosea-02-22

23 and I will sow him for myself in the land. And I will have pity on Not pitied, and I will say to Not my people, ‘You are my people’; and he shall say ‘You are my God.’ ” ^hosea-02-23
