# Hosea 14 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[hosea-14|NABRE]]

**A Plea for Repentance and a Promise**

1 Return, O Israel, to the LORD your God, for you have stumbled because of your iniquity. ^hosea-14-1

2 Take with you words and return to the LORD; say to him, “Take away all iniquity; accept that which is good and we will render the fruit of our lips. ^hosea-14-2

3 Assyria shall not save us, we will not ride upon horses; and we will say no more, ‘Our God,’ to the work of our hands. In you the orphan finds mercy.” ^hosea-14-3

4 I will heal their faithlessness; I will love them freely, for my anger has turned from them. ^hosea-14-4

5 I will be as the dew to Israel; he shall blossom as the lily, he shall strike root as the poplar; ^hosea-14-5

6 his shoots shall spread out; his beauty shall be like the olive, and his fragrance like Lebanon. ^hosea-14-6

7 They shall return and dwell beneath my shadow, they shall flourish as a garden; they shall blossom as the vine, their fragrance shall be like the wine of Lebanon. ^hosea-14-7

8 O E'phraim, what have I to do with idols? It is I who answer and look after you. I am like an evergreen cypress, from me comes your fruit. ^hosea-14-8

9 Whoever is wise, let him understand these things; whoever is discerning, let him know them; for the ways of the LORD are right, and the upright walk in them, but transgressors stumble in them. ^hosea-14-9
