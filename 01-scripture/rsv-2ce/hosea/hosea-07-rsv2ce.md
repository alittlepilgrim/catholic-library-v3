# Hosea 7 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[hosea-07|NABRE]]

1 when I would heal Israel, the corruption of E'phraim is revealed, and the wicked deeds of Samar'ia; for they deal falsely, the thief breaks in, and the bandits raid without. ^hosea-07-1

2 But they do not consider that I remember all their evil works. Now their deeds encompass them, they are before my face. ^hosea-07-2

3 By their wickedness they make the king glad, and the princes by their treachery. ^hosea-07-3

4 They are all adulterers; they are like a heated oven, whose baker ceases to stir the fire, from the kneading of the dough until it is leavened. ^hosea-07-4

5 On the day of our king the princes became sick with the heat of wine; he stretched out his hand with mockers. ^hosea-07-5

6 For like an oven their hearts burn with intrigue; all night their anger smolders; in the morning it blazes like a flaming fire. ^hosea-07-6

7 All of them are hot as an oven, and they devour their rulers. All their kings have fallen; and none of them calls upon me. ^hosea-07-7

8 E'phraim mixes himself with the peoples; Ephraim is a cake not turned. ^hosea-07-8

9 Aliens devour his strength, and he knows it not; gray hairs are sprinkled upon him, and he knows it not. ^hosea-07-9

10 The pride of Israel witnesses against him; yet they do not return to the LORD their God, nor seek him, for all this. ^hosea-07-10

11 E'phraim is like a dove, silly and without sense, calling to Egypt, going to Assyria. ^hosea-07-11

12 As they go, I will spread over them my net; I will bring them down like birds of the air; I will chastise them for their wicked deeds. ^hosea-07-12

13 Woe to them, for they have strayed from me! Destruction to them, for they have rebelled against me! I would redeem them, but they speak lies against me. ^hosea-07-13

14 They do not cry to me from the heart, but they wail upon their beds; for grain and wine they gash themselves, they rebel against me. ^hosea-07-14

15 Although I trained and strengthened their arms, yet they devise evil against me. ^hosea-07-15

16 They turn to Ba'al; they are like a treacherous bow, their princes shall fall by the sword because of the insolence of their tongue. This shall be their derision in the land of Egypt. ^hosea-07-16
