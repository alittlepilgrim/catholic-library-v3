# Hosea 8 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[hosea-08|NABRE]]

**Israel’s Apostasy**

1 Set the trumpet to your lips, for a vulture is over the house of the LORD, because they have broken my covenant, and transgressed my law. ^hosea-08-1

2 To me they cry, My God, we Israel know you. ^hosea-08-2

3 Israel has spurned the good; the enemy shall pursue him. ^hosea-08-3

4 They made kings, but not through me. They set up princes, but without my knowledge. With their silver and gold they made idols for their own destruction. ^hosea-08-4

5 I have spurned your calf, O Samar'ia. My anger burns against them. How long will it be till they are pure ^hosea-08-5

6 in Israel? A workman made it; it is not God. The calf of Samar'ia shall be broken to pieces. ^hosea-08-6

7 For they sow the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind. The standing grain has no heads, it shall yield no meal; if it were to yield, aliens would devour it. ^hosea-08-7

8 Israel is swallowed up; already they are among the nations as a useless vessel. ^hosea-08-8

9 For they have gone up to Assyria, a wild donkey wandering alone; E'phraim has hired lovers. ^hosea-08-9

10 Though they hire allies among the nations, I will soon gather them up. And they shall cease for a little while from anointing king and princes. ^hosea-08-10

11 Because E'phraim has multiplied altars for sinning, they have become to him altars for sinning. ^hosea-08-11

12 Were I to write for him my laws by ten thousands, they would be regarded as a strange thing. ^hosea-08-12

13 They love sacrifice; they sacrifice flesh and eat it; but the LORD has no delight in them. Now he will remember their iniquity, and punish their sins; they shall return to Egypt. ^hosea-08-13

14 For Israel has forgotten his Maker, and built palaces; and Judah has multiplied fortified cities; but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour his strongholds. ^hosea-08-14
