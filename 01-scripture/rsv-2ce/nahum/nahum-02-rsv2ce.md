# Nahum 2 (RSV-2CE)

*Also:* [[nahum-02|NABRE]]

**The Overthrow of Nineveh**

1 The shatterer has come up against you. Man the ramparts; watch the road; gird your loins; collect all your strength. ^nahum-02-1

2 (For the LORD is restoring the majesty of Jacob as the majesty of Israel, for plunderers have stripped them and ruined their branches.) ^nahum-02-2

3 The shield of his mighty men is red, his soldiers are clothed in scarlet. The chariots flash like flame when mustered in array; the chargers prance. ^nahum-02-3

4 The chariots rage in the streets, they rush back and forth through the squares; they gleam like torches, they dart like lightning. ^nahum-02-4

5 The officers are summoned, they stumble as they go, they hasten to the wall, the mantelet is set up. ^nahum-02-5

6 The river gates are opened, the palace is in dismay; ^nahum-02-6

7 its mistress is stripped, she is carried off, her maidens lamenting, moaning like doves, and beating their breasts. ^nahum-02-7

8 Nin'eveh is like a pool whose waters run away. “Halt! Halt!” they cry; but none turns back. ^nahum-02-8

9 Plunder the silver, plunder the gold! There is no end of treasure, or wealth of every precious thing. ^nahum-02-9

10 Desolate! Desolation and ruin! Hearts faint and knees tremble, anguish is on all loins, all faces grow pale! ^nahum-02-10

11 Where is the lions’ den, the cave of the young lions, where the lion brought his prey, where his cubs were, with none to disturb? ^nahum-02-11

12 The lion tore enough for his whelps and strangled prey for his lionesses; he filled his caves with prey and his dens with torn flesh. ^nahum-02-12

**Utter Ruin of the Evil City**

13 Behold, I am against you, says the LORD of hosts, and I will burn your chariots in smoke, and the sword shall devour your young lions; I will cut off your prey from the earth, and the voice of your messengers shall no more be heard. ^nahum-02-13
