# Mark 13 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Destruction of the Temple Foretold**

1 And as he came out of the temple, one of his disciples said to him, "Look, Teacher, what wonderful stones and what wonderful buildings!" ^mark-13-1

2 And Jesus said to him, "Do you see these great buildings? There will not be left here one stone upon another, that will not be thrown down." ^mark-13-2

3 And as he sat on the Mount of Olives opposite the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately, ^mark-13-3

4 "Tell us, when will this be, and what will be the sign when these things are all to be accomplished?" ^mark-13-4

5 And Jesus began to say to them, "Take heed that no one leads you astray. ^mark-13-5

6 Many will come in my name, saying, 'I am he!' and they will lead many astray. ^mark-13-6

7 And when you hear of wars and rumors of wars, do not be alarmed; this must take place, but the end is not yet. ^mark-13-7

8 For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom; there will be earthquakes in various places, there will be famines; this is but the beginning of the sufferings. ^mark-13-8

**Persecutions Foretold**

9 "But take heed to yourselves; for they will deliver you up to councils; and you will be beaten in synagogues; and you will stand before governors and kings for my sake, to bear testimony before them. ^mark-13-9

10 And the gospel must first be preached to all nations. ^mark-13-10

11 And when they bring you to trial and deliver you up, do not be anxious beforehand about what you are to say; but say whatever is given you in that hour, for it is not you who speak, but the Holy Spirit. ^mark-13-11

12 And brother will deliver up brother to death, and the father his child, and children will rise against parents and have them put to death; ^mark-13-12

13 and you will be hated by all for my name's sake. But he who endures to the end will be saved. ^mark-13-13

**The Desolating Sacrilege**

14 "But when you see the desolating sacrilege set up where it ought not to be (let the reader understand), then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains; ^mark-13-14

15 let him who is on the housetop not go down, nor enter his house, to take anything away; ^mark-13-15

16 and let him who is in the field not turn back to get a coat. ^mark-13-16

17 And alas for those who are with child and for those who are nursing in those days! ^mark-13-17

18 Pray that it may not happen in winter. ^mark-13-18

19 For in those days there will be such tribulation as has not been from the beginning of the creation which God created until now, and never will be. ^mark-13-19

20 And if the Lord had not shortened the days, no human being would be saved; but for the sake of the elect, whom he chose, he shortened the days. ^mark-13-20

21 And then if any one says to you, 'Look, here is the Christ!' or 'Look, there he is!' do not believe it. ^mark-13-21

22 False Christs and false prophets will arise and show signs and wonders, to lead astray, if possible, the elect. ^mark-13-22

23 But take heed; I have told you all things beforehand. ^mark-13-23

**The Coming of the Son of Man**

24 "But in those days, after that tribulation, the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, ^mark-13-24

25 and the stars will be falling from heaven, and the powers in the heavens will be shaken. ^mark-13-25

26 And then they will see the Son of man coming in clouds with great power and glory. ^mark-13-26

27 And then he will send out the angels, and gather his elect from the four winds, from the ends of the earth to the ends of heaven. ^mark-13-27

**The Lesson of the Fig Tree**

28 "From the fig tree learn its lesson: as soon as its branch becomes tender and puts forth its leaves, you know that summer is near. ^mark-13-28

29 So also, when you see these things taking place, you know that he is near, at the very gates. ^mark-13-29

30 Truly, I say to you, this generation will not pass away before all these things take place. ^mark-13-30

31 Heaven and earth will pass away, but my words will not pass away. ^mark-13-31

**The Necessity for Watchfulness**

32 "But of that day or that hour no one knows, not even the angels in heaven, nor the Son, but only the Father. ^mark-13-32

33 Take heed, watch and pray; <sup>a</sup> for you do not know when the time will come. ^mark-13-33

34 It is like a man going on a journey, when he leaves home and puts his servants in charge, each with his work, and commands the doorkeeper to be on the watch. ^mark-13-34

35 Watch therefore—for you do not know when the master of the house will come, in the evening, or at midnight, or at cockcrow, or in the morning— ^mark-13-35

36 lest he come suddenly and find you asleep. ^mark-13-36

37 And what I say to you I say to all: Watch." ^mark-13-37
