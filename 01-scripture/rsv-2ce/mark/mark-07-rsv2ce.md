# Mark 7 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Tradition of the Elders**

1 Now when the Pharisees gathered together to him, with some of the scribes, who had come from Jerusalem, ^mark-07-1

2 they saw that some of his disciples ate with hands defiled, that is, unwashed. ^mark-07-2

3 (For the Pharisees, and all the Jews, do not eat unless they wash their hands, <sup>v</sup> observing the tradition of the elders; ^mark-07-3

4 and when they come from the market place, they do not eat unless they purify <sup>w</sup> themselves; and there are many other traditions which they observe, the washing of cups and pots and vessels of bronze. <sup>x</sup>) ^mark-07-4

5 And the Pharisees and the scribes asked him, "Why do your disciples not live <sup>y</sup> according to the tradition of the elders, but eat with hands defiled?" ^mark-07-5

6 And he said to them, "Well did Isaiah prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written,

'This people honors me with their lips,
but their heart is far from me; ^mark-07-6

7 in vain do they worship me,
  teaching as doctrines the precepts of men.' ^mark-07-7

8 You leave the commandment of God, and hold fast the tradition of men." ^mark-07-8

9 And he said to them, "You have a fine way of rejecting the commandment of God, in order to keep your tradition! ^mark-07-9

10 For Moses said, 'Honor your father and your mother'; and, 'He who speaks evil of father or mother, let him surely die'; ^mark-07-10

11 but you say, 'If a man tells his father or his mother, What you would have gained from me is Corban' (that is, given to God) <sup>z</sup> ^mark-07-11

12 then you no longer permit him to do anything for his father or mother, ^mark-07-12

13 thus making void the word of God through your tradition which you hand on. And many such things you do." ^mark-07-13

14 And he called the people to him again, and said to them, "Hear me, all of you, and understand: ^mark-07-14

15 there is nothing outside a man which by going into him can defile him; but the things which come out of a man are what defile him." <sup>a</sup> ^mark-07-15

17 And when he had entered the house, and left the people, his disciples asked him about the parable. ^mark-07-17

18 And he said to them, "Then are you also without understanding? Do you not see that whatever goes into a man from outside cannot defile him, ^mark-07-18

19 since it enters, not his heart but his stomach, and so passes on?" <sup>b</sup> (Thus he declared all foods clean.) ^mark-07-19

20 And he said, "What comes out of a man is what defiles a man. ^mark-07-20

21 For from within, out of the heart of man, come evil thoughts, fornication, theft, murder, adultery, ^mark-07-21

22 coveting, wickedness, deceit, licentiousness, envy, slander, pride, foolishness. ^mark-07-22

23 All these evil things come from within, and they defile a man." ^mark-07-23

**The Syrophoenician Woman's Faith**

24 And from there he arose and went away to the region of Tyre and Sidon. <sup>c</sup> And he entered a house, and would not have any one know it; yet he could not be hidden. ^mark-07-24

25 But immediately a woman, whose little daughter was possessed by an unclean spirit, heard of him, and came and fell down at his feet. ^mark-07-25

26 Now the woman was a Greek, a Syrophoeni'cian by birth. And she begged him to cast the demon out of her daughter. ^mark-07-26

27 And he said to her, "Let the children first be fed, for it is not right to take the children's bread and throw it to the dogs." ^mark-07-27

28 But she answered him, "Yes, Lord; yet even the dogs under the table eat the children's crumbs." ^mark-07-28

29 And he said to her, "For this saying you may go your way; the demon has left your daughter." ^mark-07-29

30 And she went home, and found the child lying in bed, and the demon gone. ^mark-07-30

**Jesus Cures a Deaf Man**

31 Then he returned from the region of Tyre, and went through Sidon to the Sea of Galilee, through the region of the Decap'olis. ^mark-07-31

32 And they brought to him a man who was deaf and had an impediment in his speech; and they begged him to lay his hand upon him. ^mark-07-32

33 And taking him aside from the multitude privately, he put his fingers into his ears, and he spat and touched his tongue; ^mark-07-33

34 and looking up to heaven, he sighed, and said to him, "Eph'phatha," that is, "Be opened." ^mark-07-34

35 And his ears were opened, his tongue was released, and he spoke plainly. ^mark-07-35

36 And he charged them to tell no one; but the more he charged them, the more zealously they proclaimed it. ^mark-07-36

37 And they were astonished beyond measure, saying, "He has done all things well; he even makes the deaf hear and the mute speak." ^mark-07-37
