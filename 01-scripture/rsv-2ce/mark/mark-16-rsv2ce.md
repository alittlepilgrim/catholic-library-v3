# Mark 16 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Resurrection of Jesus**

1 And when the sabbath was past, Mary Mag'dalene, and Mary the mother of James, and Salo'me, bought spices, so that they might go and anoint him. ^mark-16-1

2 And very early on the first day of the week they went to the tomb when the sun had risen. ^mark-16-2

3 And they were saying to one another, "Who will roll away the stone for us from the door of the tomb?" ^mark-16-3

4 And looking up, they saw that the stone was rolled back; for it was very large. ^mark-16-4

5 And entering the tomb, they saw a young man sitting on the right side, dressed in a white robe; and they were amazed. ^mark-16-5

6 And he said to them, "Do not be amazed; you seek Jesus of Nazareth, who was crucified. He has risen, he is not here; see the place where they laid him. ^mark-16-6

7 But go, tell his disciples and Peter that he is going before you to Galilee; there you will see him, as he told you." ^mark-16-7

8 And they went out and fled from the tomb; for trembling and astonishment had come upon them; and they said nothing to any one, for they were afraid. ^mark-16-8

**Jesus Appears to Mary Magdalene**

9 Now when he rose early on the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, from whom he had cast out seven demons. ^mark-16-9

10 She went and told those who had been with him, as they mourned and wept. ^mark-16-10

11 But when they heard that he was alive and had been seen by her, they would not believe it. ^mark-16-11

**Jesus Appears to Two Disciples**

12 After this he appeared in another form to two of them, as they were walking into the country. ^mark-16-12

13 And they went back and told the rest, but they did not believe them. ^mark-16-13

**Jesus Commissions the Disciples**

14 Afterward he appeared to the Eleven themselves as they sat at table; and he upbraided them for their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they had not believed those who saw him after he had risen. ^mark-16-14

15 And he said to them, "Go into all the world and preach the gospel to the whole creation. ^mark-16-15

16 He who believes and is baptized will be saved; but he who does not believe will be condemned. ^mark-16-16

17 And these signs will accompany those who believe: in my name they will cast out demons; they will speak in new tongues; ^mark-16-17

18 they will pick up serpents, and if they drink any deadly thing, it will not hurt them; they will lay their hands on the sick, and they will recover." ^mark-16-18

**The Ascension of Jesus**

19 So then the Lord Jesus, after he had spoken to them, was taken up into heaven, and sat down at the right hand of God. ^mark-16-19

20 And they went forth and preached everywhere, while the Lord worked with them and confirmed the message by the signs that attended it. Amen. <sup>k</sup> ^mark-16-20

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (16:1) There had been no time on the Friday to anoint him before the sabbath rest. ^mark-16-dn-1

\* (16:9–20) This passage is regarded as inspired and canonical Scripture even if not written by Mark. As it is missing from some important manuscripts, it is possible that Mark did not write it. On the other hand, he would hardly have left his Gospel unfinished at verse 8. Many think that the original ending was lost at a very early date and that this ending was composed at the end of the apostolic period to take its place. ^mark-16-dn-2
