# Mark 15 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Jesus before Pilate**

1 And as soon as it was morning the chief priests, with the elders and scribes, and the whole council held a consultation; and they bound Jesus and led him away and delivered him to Pilate. ^mark-15-1

2 And Pilate asked him, "Are you the King of the Jews?" And he answered him, "You have said so." ^mark-15-2

3 And the chief priests accused him of many things. ^mark-15-3

4 And Pilate again asked him, "Have you no answer to make? See how many charges they bring against you." ^mark-15-4

5 But Jesus made no further answer, so that Pilate wondered. ^mark-15-5

**Pilate Delivers Jesus to Be Crucified**

6 Now at the feast he used to release for them one prisoner for whom they asked. ^mark-15-6

7 And among the rebels in prison, who had committed murder in the insurrection, there was a man called Barab'bas. ^mark-15-7

8 And the crowd came up and began to ask Pilate to do as he always did for them. ^mark-15-8

9 And he answered them, "Do you want me to release for you the King of the Jews?" ^mark-15-9

10 For he perceived that it was out of envy that the chief priests had delivered him up. ^mark-15-10

11 But the chief priests stirred up the crowd to have him release for them Barab'bas instead. ^mark-15-11

12 And Pilate again said to them, "Then what shall I do with the man whom you call the King of the Jews?" ^mark-15-12

13 And they cried out again, "Crucify him." ^mark-15-13

14 And Pilate said to them, "Why, what evil has he done?" But they shouted all the more, "Crucify him." ^mark-15-14

15 So Pilate, wishing to satisfy the crowd, released for them Barab'bas; and having scourged Jesus, he delivered him to be crucified. ^mark-15-15

**The Soldiers Mock Jesus**

16 And the soldiers led him away inside the palace (that is, the praetorium); and they called together the whole battalion. ^mark-15-16

17 And they clothed him in a purple cloak, and plaiting a crown of thorns they put it on him. ^mark-15-17

18 And they began to salute him, "Hail, King of the Jews!" ^mark-15-18

19 And they struck his head with a reed, and spat upon him, and they knelt down in homage to him. ^mark-15-19

20 And when they had mocked him, they stripped him of the purple cloak, and put his own clothes on him. And they led him out to crucify him. ^mark-15-20

**The Crucifixion of Jesus**

21 And they compelled a passer-by, Simon of Cyre'ne, who was coming in from the country, the father of Alexander and Rufus, to carry his cross. ^mark-15-21

22 And they brought him to the place called Gol'gotha (which means the place of a skull). ^mark-15-22

23 And they offered him wine mingled with myrrh; but he did not take it. ^mark-15-23

24 And they crucified him, and divided his garments among them, casting lots for them, to decide what each should take. ^mark-15-24

25 And it was the third hour, when they crucified him. ^mark-15-25

26 And the inscription of the charge against him read, "The King of the Jews." ^mark-15-26

27 And with him they crucified two robbers, one on his right and one on his left. <sup>g</sup> ^mark-15-27

29 And those who passed by derided him, shaking their heads, and saying, "Aha! You who would destroy the temple and build it in three days, ^mark-15-29

30 save yourself, and come down from the cross!" ^mark-15-30

31 So also the chief priests mocked him to one another with the scribes, saying, "He saved others; he cannot save himself. ^mark-15-31

32 Let the Christ, the King of Israel, come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe." Those who were crucified with him also reviled him. ^mark-15-32

**The Death of Jesus**

33 And when the sixth hour had come, there was darkness over the whole land <sup>h</sup> until the ninth hour. ^mark-15-33

34 And at the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, "E'lo-i, Elo-i, la'ma sabach-tha'ni?" which means, "My God, my God, why have you forsaken me?" ^mark-15-34

35 And some of the bystanders hearing it said, "Behold, he is calling Eli'jah." ^mark-15-35

36 And one ran and, filling a sponge full of vinegar, put it on a reed and gave it to him to drink, saying, "Wait, let us see whether Eli'jah will come to take him down." ^mark-15-36

37 And Jesus uttered a loud cry, and breathed his last. ^mark-15-37

38 And the curtain of the temple was torn in two, from top to bottom. ^mark-15-38

39 And when the centurion, who stood facing him, saw that he thus <sup>i</sup> breathed his last, he said, "Truly this man was the Son <sup>x</sup> of God!" ^mark-15-39

40 There were also women looking on from afar, among whom were Mary Mag'dalene, and Mary the mother of James the younger and of Joses, and Salo'me, ^mark-15-40

41 who, when he was in Galilee, followed him, and ministered to him; and also many other women who came up with him to Jerusalem. ^mark-15-41

**The Burial of Jesus**

42 And when evening had come, since it was the day of Preparation, that is, the day before the sabbath, ^mark-15-42

43 Joseph of Arimathe'a, a respected member of the council, who was also himself looking for the kingdom of God, took courage and went to Pilate, and asked for the body of Jesus. ^mark-15-43

44 And Pilate wondered if he were already dead; and summoning the centurion, he asked him whether he was already dead. <sup>j</sup> ^mark-15-44

45 And when he learned from the centurion that he was dead, he granted the body to Joseph. ^mark-15-45

46 And he bought a linen shroud, and taking him down, wrapped him in the linen shroud, and laid him in a tomb which had been hewn out of the rock; and he rolled a stone against the door of the tomb. ^mark-15-46

47 Mary Mag'dalene and Mary the mother of Joses saw where he was laid. ^mark-15-47

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (15:1) The Jews could not execute Jesus without the Roman governor’s permission. ^mark-15-dn-1

\* (15:40) the younger, or “the Less.” ^mark-15-dn-2
