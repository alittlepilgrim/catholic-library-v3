# Mark 9 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

1 And he said to them, "Truly, I say to you, there are some standing here who will not taste death before they see the kingdom of God come with power." ^mark-09-1

**The Transfiguration**

2 And after six days Jesus took with him Peter and James and John, and led them up a high mountain apart by themselves; and he was transfigured before them, ^mark-09-2

3 and his garments became glistening, intensely white, as no fuller on earth could bleach them. ^mark-09-3

4 And there appeared to them Eli'jah with Moses; and they were talking to Jesus. ^mark-09-4

5 And Peter said to Jesus, "Master, <sup>f</sup> it is well that we are here; let us make three booths, one for you and one for Moses and one for Eli'jah." ^mark-09-5

6 For he did not know what to say, for they were exceedingly afraid. ^mark-09-6

7 And a cloud overshadowed them, and a voice came out of the cloud, "This is my beloved Son; <sup>g</sup> listen to him." ^mark-09-7

8 And suddenly looking around they no longer saw any one with them but Jesus only. ^mark-09-8

**The Coming of Elijah**

9 And as they were coming down the mountain, he charged them to tell no one what they had seen, until the Son of man should have risen from the dead. ^mark-09-9

10 So they kept the matter to themselves, questioning what the rising from the dead meant. ^mark-09-10

11 And they asked him, "Why do the scribes say that first Eli'jah must come?" ^mark-09-11

12 And he said to them, "Eli'jah does come first to restore all things; and how is it written of the Son of man, that he should suffer many things and be treated with contempt? ^mark-09-12

13 But I tell you that Eli'jah has come, and they did to him whatever they pleased, as it is written of him." ^mark-09-13

**The Healing of a Boy with a Mute Spirit**

14 And when they came to the disciples, they saw a great crowd about them, and scribes arguing with them. ^mark-09-14

15 And immediately all the crowd, when they saw him, were greatly amazed, and ran up to him and greeted him. ^mark-09-15

16 And he asked them, "What are you discussing with them?" ^mark-09-16

17 And one of the crowd answered him, "Teacher, I brought my son to you, for he has a mute spirit; ^mark-09-17

18 and wherever it seizes him, it dashes him down; and he foams and grinds his teeth and becomes rigid; and I asked your disciples to cast it out, and they were not able." ^mark-09-18

19 And he answered them, "O faithless generation, how long am I to be with you? How long am I to bear with you? Bring him to me." ^mark-09-19

20 And they brought the boy to him; and when the spirit saw him, immediately it convulsed the boy, and he fell on the ground and rolled about, foaming at the mouth. ^mark-09-20

21 And Jesus <sup>h</sup> asked his father, "How long has he had this?" And he said, "From childhood. ^mark-09-21

22 And it has often cast him into the fire and into the water, to destroy him; but if you can do anything, have pity on us and help us." ^mark-09-22

23 And Jesus said to him, "If you can! All things are possible to him who believes." ^mark-09-23

24 Immediately the father of the child cried out <sup>i</sup> and said, "I believe; help my unbelief!" ^mark-09-24

25 And when Jesus saw that a crowd came running together, he rebuked the unclean spirit, saying to it, "You mute and deaf spirit, I command you, come out of him, and never enter him again." ^mark-09-25

26 And after crying out and convulsing him terribly, it came out, and the boy was like a corpse; so that most of them said, "He is dead." ^mark-09-26

27 But Jesus took him by the hand and lifted him up, and he arose. ^mark-09-27

28 And when he had entered the house, his disciples asked him privately, "Why could we not cast it out?" ^mark-09-28

29 And he said to them, "This kind cannot be driven out by anything but prayer and fasting." <sup>j</sup> ^mark-09-29

**Jesus Again Foretells His Death and Resurrection**

30 They went on from there and passed through Galilee. And he would not have any one know it; ^mark-09-30

31 for he was teaching his disciples, saying to them, "The Son of man will be delivered into the hands of men, and they will kill him; and when he is killed, after three days he will rise." ^mark-09-31

32 But they did not understand the saying, and they were afraid to ask him. ^mark-09-32

**True Greatness**

33 And they came to Caper'na-um; and when he was in the house he asked them, "What were you discussing on the way?" ^mark-09-33

34 But they were silent; for on the way they had discussed with one another who was the greatest. ^mark-09-34

35 And he sat down and called the Twelve and he said to them, "If any one would be first, he must be last of all and servant of all." ^mark-09-35

36 And he took a child, and put him in the midst of them; and taking him in his arms, he said to them, ^mark-09-36

37 "Whoever receives one such child in my name receives me; and whoever receives me, receives not me but him who sent me." ^mark-09-37

**Another Exorcist**

38 John said to him, "Teacher, we saw a man casting out demons in your name, <sup>k</sup> and we forbade him, because he was not following us." ^mark-09-38

39 But Jesus said, "Do not forbid him; for no one who does a mighty work in my name will be able soon after to speak evil of me. ^mark-09-39

40 For he that is not against us is for us. ^mark-09-40

41 For truly, I say to you, whoever gives you a cup of water to drink because you bear the name of Christ, will by no means lose his reward. ^mark-09-41

**Temptations to Sin**

42 "Whoever causes one of these little ones who believe in me to sin, <sup>l</sup> it would be better for him if a great millstone were hung round his neck and he were thrown into the sea. ^mark-09-42

43 And if your hand causes you to sin, <sup>l</sup> cut it off; it is better for you to enter life maimed than with two hands to go to hell, <sup>m</sup> to the unquenchable fire. <sup>n</sup> ^mark-09-43

45 And if your foot causes you to sin, <sup>l</sup> cut it off; it is better for you to enter life lame than with two feet to be thrown into hell. <sup>m</sup>, <sup>n</sup> ^mark-09-45

47 And if your eye causes you to sin, <sup>l</sup> pluck it out; it is better for you to enter the kingdom of God with one eye than with two eyes to be thrown into hell, <sup>m</sup> ^mark-09-47

48 where their worm does not die, and the fire is not quenched. ^mark-09-48

49 For every one will be salted with fire. <sup>o</sup> ^mark-09-49

50 Salt is good; but if the salt has lost its saltiness, how will you season it? Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another." ^mark-09-50
