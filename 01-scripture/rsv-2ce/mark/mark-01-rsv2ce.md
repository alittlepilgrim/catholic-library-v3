# Mark 1 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**The Preaching of John the Baptist**

1 The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God. <sup>a</sup> ^mark-01-1

2 As it is written in Isaiah the prophet, <sup>b</sup>

"Behold, I send my messenger before your face,
who shall prepare your way; ^mark-01-2

3 the voice of one crying in the wilderness:
  Prepare the way of the Lord,
  make his paths straight—" ^mark-01-3

4 John the Baptist appeared <sup>c</sup> in the wilderness, preaching a baptism of repentance for the forgiveness of sins. ^mark-01-4

5 And there went out to him all the country of Judea, and all the people of Jerusalem; and they were baptized by him in the river Jordan, confessing their sins. ^mark-01-5

6 Now John was clothed with camel's hair, and had a leather belt around his waist, and ate locusts and wild honey. ^mark-01-6

7 And he preached, saying, "After me comes he who is mightier than I, the thong of whose sandals I am not worthy to stoop down and untie. ^mark-01-7

8 I have baptized you with water; but he will baptize you with the Holy Spirit." ^mark-01-8

**The Baptism of Jesus**

9 In those days Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan. ^mark-01-9

10 And when he came up out of the water, immediately he saw the heavens opened and the Spirit descending upon him like a dove; ^mark-01-10

11 and a voice came from heaven, "You are my beloved Son; <sup>d</sup> with you I am well pleased." ^mark-01-11

**The Temptation of Jesus**

12 The Spirit immediately drove him out into the wilderness. ^mark-01-12

13 And he was in the wilderness forty days, tempted by Satan; and he was with the wild beasts; and the angels ministered to him. ^mark-01-13

**Jesus Preaches the Gospel in Galilee**

14 Now after John was arrested, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the gospel of God, ^mark-01-14

15 and saying, "The time is fulfilled, and the kingdom of God is at hand; repent, and believe in the gospel." ^mark-01-15

**Jesus Calls the First Disciples**

16 And passing along by the Sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew the brother of Simon casting a net in the sea; for they were fishermen. ^mark-01-16

17 And Jesus said to them, "Follow me and I will make you become fishers of men." ^mark-01-17

18 And immediately they left their nets and followed him. ^mark-01-18

19 And going on a little farther, he saw James the son of Zeb'edee and John his brother, who were in their boat mending the nets. ^mark-01-19

20 And immediately he called them; and they left their father Zeb'edee in the boat with the hired servants, and followed him. ^mark-01-20

**The Man with an Unclean Spirit**

21 And they went into Caper'na-um; and immediately on the sabbath he entered the synagogue and taught. ^mark-01-21

22 And they were astonished at his teaching, for he taught them as one who had authority, and not as the scribes. ^mark-01-22

23 And immediately there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; ^mark-01-23

24 and he cried out, "What have you to do with us, Jesus of Nazareth? Have you come to destroy us? I know who you are, the Holy One of God." ^mark-01-24

25 But Jesus rebuked him, saying, "Be silent, and come out of him!" ^mark-01-25

26 And the unclean spirit, convulsing him and crying with a loud voice, came out of him. ^mark-01-26

27 And they were all amazed, so that they questioned among themselves, saying, "What is this? A new teaching! With authority he commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey him." ^mark-01-27

28 And at once his fame spread everywhere throughout all the surrounding region of Galilee. ^mark-01-28

**Healings at Simon's House**

29 And immediately he <sup>e</sup> left the synagogue, and entered the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John. ^mark-01-29

30 Now Simon's mother-in-law lay sick with a fever, and immediately they told him of her. ^mark-01-30

31 And he came and took her by the hand and lifted her up, and the fever left her; and she served them. ^mark-01-31

32 That evening, at sundown, they brought to him all who were sick or possessed with demons. ^mark-01-32

33 And the whole city was gathered together about the door. ^mark-01-33

34 And he healed many who were sick with various diseases, and cast out many demons; and he would not permit the demons to speak, because they knew him. ^mark-01-34

**Jesus Preaches and Heals throughout Galilee**

35 And in the morning, a great while before day, he rose and went out to a lonely place, and there he prayed. ^mark-01-35

36 And Simon and those who were with him followed him, ^mark-01-36

37 and they found him and said to him, "Every one is searching for you." ^mark-01-37

38 And he said to them, "Let us go on to the next towns, that I may preach there also; for that is why I came out." ^mark-01-38

39 And he went throughout all Galilee, preaching in their synagogues and casting out demons. ^mark-01-39

**Jesus Cleanses a Leper**

40 And a leper came to him begging him, and kneeling said to him, "If you will, you can make me clean." ^mark-01-40

41 Moved with pity, he stretched out his hand and touched him, and said to him, "I will; be clean." ^mark-01-41

42 And immediately the leprosy left him, and he was made clean. ^mark-01-42

43 And he sternly charged him, and sent him away at once, ^mark-01-43

44 and said to him, "See that you say nothing to any one; but go, show yourself to the priest, and offer for your cleansing what Moses commanded, for a proof to the people." <sup>f</sup> ^mark-01-44

45 But he went out and began to talk freely about it, and to spread the news, so that Jesus <sup>g</sup> could no longer openly enter a town, but was out in the country; and people came to him from every quarter. ^mark-01-45


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (1:34) Throughout his ministry Jesus forbade the demons and those he healed of their infirmities to reveal his identity as Messiah, because the people, with their ideas of a national leader to come, were only too prone to mistake his true mission. ^mark-01-dn-1
