# 1 Timothy 3 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Qualifications of Bishops**

1 The saying is sure: If any one aspires to the office of bishop, \* he desires a noble task. ^first-timothy-03-1

2 Now a bishop must be above reproach, the husband of one wife, temperate, sensible, dignified, hospitable, an apt teacher, ^first-timothy-03-2

3 no drunkard, not violent but gentle, not quarrelsome, and no lover of money. ^first-timothy-03-3

4 He must manage his own household well, keeping his children submissive and respectful in every way; ^first-timothy-03-4

5 for if a man does not know how to manage his own household, how can he care for God's Church? ^first-timothy-03-5

6 He must not be a recent convert, or he may be puffed up with conceit and fall into the condemnation of the devil;<sup>f</sup> ^first-timothy-03-6

7 moreover he must be well thought of by outsiders, or he may fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.<sup>f</sup> ^first-timothy-03-7

**Qualifications of Deacons**

8 Deacons likewise must be serious, not double-tongued, not addicted to much wine, not greedy for gain; ^first-timothy-03-8

9 they must hold the mystery of the faith with a clear conscience. ^first-timothy-03-9

10 And let them also be tested first; then if they prove themselves blameless let them serve as deacons. ^first-timothy-03-10

11 The women \* likewise must be serious, no slanderers, but temperate, faithful in all things. ^first-timothy-03-11

12 Let deacons be the husband of one wife, and let them manage their children and their households well; ^first-timothy-03-12

13 for those who serve well as deacons gain a good standing for themselves and also great confidence in the faith which is in Christ Jesus. ^first-timothy-03-13

**The Mystery of Our Religion**

14 I hope to come to you soon, but I am writing these instructions to you so that, ^first-timothy-03-14

15 if I am delayed, you may know how one ought to behave in the household of God, which is the Church of the living God, the pillar and bulwark of the truth. ^first-timothy-03-15

16 Great indeed, we confess, is the mystery of our religion:

He<sup>h</sup> was manifested in the flesh,
vindicated<sup>i</sup> in the Spirit,
  seen by angels,
preached among the nations,
believed on in the world,
  taken up in glory. ^first-timothy-03-16


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (3:1) bishop: At this time an office probably not distinct from that of priest. ^first-timothy-03-dn-1

\* (3:11) women: i.e., deaconesses. ^first-timothy-03-dn-2
