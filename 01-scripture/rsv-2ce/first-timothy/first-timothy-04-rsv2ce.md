# 1 Timothy 4 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**False Asceticism**

1 Now the Spirit expressly says that in later times some will depart from the faith by giving heed to deceitful spirits and doctrines of demons, ^first-timothy-04-1

2 through the pretensions of liars whose consciences are seared, ^first-timothy-04-2

3 who forbid marriage \* and enjoin abstinence from foods \* which God created to be received with thanksgiving by those who believe and know the truth. ^first-timothy-04-3

4 For everything created by God is good, and nothing is to be rejected if it is received with thanksgiving; ^first-timothy-04-4

5 for then it is consecrated by the word of God and prayer. ^first-timothy-04-5

**A Good Minister of Jesus Christ**

6 If you put these instructions before the brethren, you will be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished on the words of the faith and of the good doctrine which you have followed. ^first-timothy-04-6

7 Have nothing to do with godless and silly myths. Train yourself in godliness; ^first-timothy-04-7

8 for while bodily training is of some value, godliness is of value in every way, as it holds promise for the present life and also for the life to come. ^first-timothy-04-8

9 The saying is sure and worthy of full acceptance. ^first-timothy-04-9

10 For to this end we toil and strive,<sup>j</sup> because we have our hope set on the living God, who is the Savior of all men, especially of those who believe. ^first-timothy-04-10

11 Command and teach these things. ^first-timothy-04-11

12 Let no one despise your youth, but set the believers an example in speech and conduct, in love, in faith, in purity. ^first-timothy-04-12

13 Till I come, attend to the public reading of Scripture, to preaching, to teaching. ^first-timothy-04-13

14 Do not neglect the gift you have, which was given you by prophetic utterance when the elders laid their hands upon you. ^first-timothy-04-14

15 Practice these duties, devote yourself to them, so that all may see your progress. ^first-timothy-04-15

16 Take heed to yourself and to your teaching; hold to that, for by so doing you will save both yourself and your hearers. ^first-timothy-04-16


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (4:3) forbid marriage: As some Gnostics did. ^first-timothy-04-dn-1

\* (4:3) abstinence from foods: As practiced by Judaizers. ^first-timothy-04-dn-2
