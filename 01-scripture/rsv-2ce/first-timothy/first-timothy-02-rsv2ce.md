# 1 Timothy 2 – Ignatius Catholic Study Bible (RSV-2CE)

**Instructions concerning Prayer**

1 First of all, then, I urge that supplications, prayers, intercessions, and thanksgivings be made for all men, ^first-timothy-02-1

2 for kings and all who are in high positions, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life, godly and respectful in every way. ^first-timothy-02-2

3 This is good, and it is acceptable in the sight of God our Savior, ^first-timothy-02-3

4 who desires all men to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth. ^first-timothy-02-4

5 For there is one God, and there is one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus, ^first-timothy-02-5

6 who gave himself as a ransom for all, \* the testimony to which was given at the proper time. ^first-timothy-02-6

7 For this I was appointed a preacher and apostle (I am telling the truth, I am not lying), a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and truth. ^first-timothy-02-7

8 I desire then that in every place the men should pray, lifting holy hands without anger or quarreling; ^first-timothy-02-8

9 also that women should adorn themselves modestly and sensibly in seemly apparel, not with braided hair or gold or pearls or costly attire ^first-timothy-02-9

10 but by good deeds, as befits women who profess religion. ^first-timothy-02-10

11 Let a woman learn in silence with all submissiveness. ^first-timothy-02-11

12 I permit no woman to teach or to have authority over men; she is to keep silent. ^first-timothy-02-12

13 For Adam was formed first, then Eve; ^first-timothy-02-13

14 and Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived and became a transgressor. ^first-timothy-02-14

15 Yet woman will be saved through bearing children,<sup>c</sup> if she continues<sup>d</sup> in faith and love and holiness, with modesty. ^first-timothy-02-15


---

## Doctrinal Notes (Ignatius Bible)

\* (2:6) ransom for all: This is why Paul wants prayers for all (verse 1). ^first-timothy-02-dn-1
