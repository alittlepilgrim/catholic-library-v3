# John Chapter 7

Christ goes up to the feast of the tabernacles. He teaches in the temple.

1 After these things, Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Judea, because the Jews sought to kill him. ^john-07-1-douay

2 Now the Jews feast of tabernacles was at hand. ^john-07-2-douay

3 And his brethren said to, him: Pass from hence and go into Judea, that thy disciples also may see thy works which thou dost. ^john-07-3-douay

4 For there is no man that doth any thing in secret, and he himself seeketh to be known openly. If thou do these things, manifest thyself to the world. ^john-07-4-douay

5 For neither did his brethren believe in him. ^john-07-5-douay

6 Then Jesus said to them: My time is not yet come; but your time is always ready. ^john-07-6-douay

7 The world cannot hate you: but me it hateth, because I give testimony of it, that the works thereof are evil, ^john-07-7-douay

8 Go you up to this festival day: but I go not up to this festival day, because my time is not accomplished. ^john-07-8-douay

9 When he had said these things, he himself stayed in Galilee. ^john-07-9-douay

10 But after his brethren were gone up, then he also went up to the feast, not openly, but, as it were, in secret. ^john-07-10-douay

11 The Jews therefore sought him on the festival day and said: Where is he? ^john-07-11-douay

12 And there was much murmuring among the multitude concerning him. For some said: He is a good man. And others said: No, but he seduceth the people. ^john-07-12-douay

13 Yet no man spoke openly of him, for fear of the Jews. ^john-07-13-douay

14 Now, about the midst of the feast, Jesus went up into the temple and taught. ^john-07-14-douay

15 And the Jews wondered, saying: How doth this man know letters, having never learned? ^john-07-15-douay

16 Jesus answered them and said: My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me. ^john-07-16-douay

17 If any man will do the will of him, he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself. ^john-07-17-douay

18 He that speaketh of himself seeketh his own glory: but he that seeketh the glory of him that sent him, he is true and there is no injustice in him. ^john-07-18-douay

19 Did not Moses give you the law, and yet none of you keepeth the law? ^john-07-19-douay

20 Why seek you to kill me? The multitude answered and said: Thou hast a devil. Who seeketh to kill thee? ^john-07-20-douay

21 Jesus answered and said to them: One work I have done: and you all wonder. ^john-07-21-douay

22 Therefore, Moses gave you circumcision (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers): and on the sabbath day you circumcise a man. ^john-07-22-douay

23 If a man receive circumcision on the sabbath day, that the law of Moses may not be broken: are you angry at me, because I have healed the whole man on the sabbath day? ^john-07-23-douay

24 Judge not according to the appearance: but judge just judgment. ^john-07-24-douay

25 Some therefore of Jerusalem said: Is not this he whom they seek to kill? ^john-07-25-douay

26 And behold, he speaketh openly: and they say nothing to him. Have the rulers known for a truth that this is the Christ? ^john-07-26-douay

27 But we know this man, whence he is: but when the Christ cometh, no man knoweth, whence he is. ^john-07-27-douay

28 Jesus therefore cried out in the temple, teaching and saying: You both know me, and you know whence I am. And I am not come of myself: but he that sent me is true, whom you know not. ^john-07-28-douay

29 I know him, because I am from him: and he hath sent me. ^john-07-29-douay

30 They sought therefore to apprehend him: and no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come. ^john-07-30-douay

31 But of the people many believed in him and said: When the Christ cometh, shall he do more miracles than this man doth? ^john-07-31-douay

32 The Pharisees heard the people murmuring these things concerning him: and the rulers and Pharisees sent ministers to apprehend him. ^john-07-32-douay

33 Jesus therefore said to them: Yet a little while I am with you: and then I go to him that sent me. ^john-07-33-douay

34 You shall seek me and shall not find me: and where I am, thither you cannot come. ^john-07-34-douay

35 The Jews therefore said among themselves: Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? Will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles and teach the Gentiles? ^john-07-35-douay

36 What is this saying that he hath said: You shall seek me and shall not find me? And: Where I am, you cannot come? ^john-07-36-douay

37 And on the last, and great day of the festivity, Jesus stood and cried, saying: If any man thirst, let him come to me and drink. ^john-07-37-douay

38 He that believeth in me, as the scripture saith: Out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water. ^john-07-38-douay

39 Now this he said of the Spirit which they should receive who believed in him: for as yet the Spirit was not given, because Jesus was not yet glorified. ^john-07-39-douay

40 Of that multitude therefore, when they had heard these words of his, some said: This is the prophet indeed. ^john-07-40-douay

41 Others said: This is the Christ. But some said: Doth the Christ come out of Galilee? ^john-07-41-douay

42 Doth not the scripture say: That Christ cometh of the seed of David and from Bethlehem the town where David was? ^john-07-42-douay

43 So there arose a dissension among the people because of him. ^john-07-43-douay

44 And some of them would have apprehended him: but no man laid hands upon him. ^john-07-44-douay

45 The ministers therefore came to the chief priests and the Pharisees. And they said to them: Why have you not brought him? ^john-07-45-douay

46 The ministers answered: Never did man speak like this man. ^john-07-46-douay

47 The Pharisees therefore answered them: Are you also seduced? ^john-07-47-douay

48 Hath any one of the rulers believed in him, or of the Pharisees? ^john-07-48-douay

49 But this multitude, that knoweth not the law, are accursed. ^john-07-49-douay

50 Nicodemus said to them (he that came to him by night, who was one of them): ^john-07-50-douay

51 Doth our law judge any man, unless it first hear him and know what he doth? ^john-07-51-douay

52 They answered and said to him: Art thou also a Galilean? Search the scriptures, and see that out of Galilee a prophet riseth not. ^john-07-52-douay

53 And every man returned to his own house. ^john-07-53-douay
