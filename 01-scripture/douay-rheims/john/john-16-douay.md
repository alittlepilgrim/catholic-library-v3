# John Chapter 16

The conclusion of Christ's last discourse to his disciples.

1 These things have I spoken to you things have I spoken to you that you may not be scandalized. ^john-16-1-douay

2 They will put you out of the synagogues: yea, the hour cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doth a service to God. ^john-16-2-douay

3 And these things will they do to you; because they have not known the Father nor me. ^john-16-3-douay

4 But these things I have told you, that when the hour shall come, you may remember that I told you of them. ^john-16-4-douay

5 But I told you not these things from the beginning, because I was with you. And now I go to him that sent me, and none of you asketh me: Whither goest thou? ^john-16-5-douay

6 But because I have spoken these things to you, sorrow hath filled your heart. ^john-16-6-douay

7 But I tell you the truth: it is expedient to you that I go. For if I go not, the Paraclete will not come to you: but if I go, I will send him to you. ^john-16-7-douay

8 And when he is come, he will convince the world of sin and of justice and of judgment. **He will convince the world of sin, etc...** The Holy Ghost, by his coming brought over many thousands, first, to a sense of their sin in not believing in Christ. Secondly, to a conviction of the justice of Christ, now sitting at the right hand of his Father. And thirdly, to a right apprehension of the judgment prepared for them that choose to follow Satan, who is already judged and condemned. ^john-16-8-douay

9 Of sin: because they believed not in me. ^john-16-9-douay

10 And of justice: because I go to the Father: and you shall see me no longer. ^john-16-10-douay

11 And of judgment: because the prince of this world is already judged. ^john-16-11-douay

12 I have yet many things to say to you: but you cannot bear them now. ^john-16-12-douay

13 But when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will teach you all truth. For he shall not speak of himself: but what things soever he shall hear, he shall speak. And the things that are to come, he shall show you. **Will teach you all truth...** See the annotation on chap. 14. ver. 26. ^john-16-13-douay

14 He shall glorify me: because he shall receive of mine and shall show it to you. ^john-16-14-douay

15 All things whatsoever the Father hath are mine. Therefore I said that he shall receive of me and show it to you. ^john-16-15-douay

16 A little while, and now you shall not see me: and again a little while, and you shall see me: because I go to the Father. ^john-16-16-douay

17 Then some of his disciples said one to another: What is this that he saith to us: A little while, and you shall not see me: and again a little while, and you shall see me, and, Because I go to the Father? ^john-16-17-douay

18 They said therefore: What is this that he saith, A little while? We know not what he speaketh. ^john-16-18-douay

19 And Jesus knew that they had a mind to ask him. And he said to them: Of this do you inquire among yourselves, because I said: A little while, and you shall not see me; and again a little while, and you shall see me? ^john-16-19-douay

20 Amen, amen, I say to you, that you shall lament and weep, but the world shall rejoice: and you shall be made sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy. ^john-16-20-douay

21 A woman, when she is in labour, hath sorrow, because her hour is come; but when she hath brought forth the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world. ^john-16-21-douay

22 So also you now indeed have sorrow: but I will see you again and your heart shall rejoice. And your joy no man shall take from you. ^john-16-22-douay

23 And in that day you shall not ask me any thing. Amen, amen, I say to you: if you ask the Father any thing in my name, he will give it you. ^john-16-23-douay

24 Hitherto, you have not asked any thing in my name. Ask, and you shall receive; that your joy may be full. ^john-16-24-douay

25 These things I have spoken to you in proverbs. The hour cometh when I will no longer speak to you in proverbs, but will show you plainly of the Father. ^john-16-25-douay

26 In that day, you shall ask in my name: and I say not to you that I will ask the Father for you. ^john-16-26-douay

27 For the Father himself loveth you, because you have loved me and have believed that I came out from God. ^john-16-27-douay

28 I came forth from the Father and am come into the world: again I leave the world and I go to the Father. ^john-16-28-douay

29 His disciples say to him: Behold, now thou speakest plainly and speakest no proverb. ^john-16-29-douay

30 Now we know that thou knowest all things and thou needest not that any man should ask thee. By this we believe that thou camest forth from God. ^john-16-30-douay

31 Jesus answered them: Do you now believe? ^john-16-31-douay

32 Behold, the hour cometh, and it is now come, that you shall be scattered every man to his own and shall leave me alone. And yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me. ^john-16-32-douay

33 These things I have spoken to you, that in me you may have peace. In the world you shall have distress. But have confidence. I have overcome the world. ^john-16-33-douay
