# John Chapter 1

The divinity and incarnation of Christ. John bears witness of him. He begins to call his disciples.

1 In the beginning was the Word: and the Word was with God: and the Word was God. ^john-01-1-douay

2 The same was in the beginning with God. ^john-01-2-douay

3 All things were made by him: and without him was made nothing that was made. ^john-01-3-douay

4 In him was life: and the life was the light of men. ^john-01-4-douay

5 And the light shineth in darkness: and the darkness did not comprehend it. ^john-01-5-douay

6 There was a man sent from God, whose name was John. ^john-01-6-douay

7 This man came for a witness, to give testimony of the light, that all men might believe through him. ^john-01-7-douay

8 He was not the light, but was to give testimony of the light. ^john-01-8-douay

9 That was the true light, which enlighteneth every man that cometh into this world. ^john-01-9-douay

10 He was in the world: and the world was made by him: and the world knew him not. ^john-01-10-douay

11 He came unto his own: and his own received him not. ^john-01-11-douay

12 But as many as received him, he gave them power to be made the sons of God, to them that believe in his name. ^john-01-12-douay

13 Who are born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God. ^john-01-13-douay

14 And the Word was made flesh and dwelt among us (and we saw his glory, the glory as it were of the only begotten of the Father), full of grace and truth. ^john-01-14-douay

15 John beareth witness of him and crieth out, saying: This was he of whom I spoke: He that shall come after me is preferred before me: because he was before me. ^john-01-15-douay

16 And of his fulness we all have received: and grace for grace. ^john-01-16-douay

17 For the law was given by Moses: grace and truth came by Jesus Christ. ^john-01-17-douay

18 No man hath seen God at any time: the only begotten Son who is in the Bosom of the Father, he hath declared him. ^john-01-18-douay

19 And this is the testimony of John, when the Jews sent from Jerusalem priests and Levites to him, to ask him: Who art thou? ^john-01-19-douay

20 And he confessed and did not deny: and he confessed: I am not the Christ. ^john-01-20-douay

21 And they asked him: What then? Art thou Elias? And he said: I am not. Art thou the prophet? And he answered: No. ^john-01-21-douay

22 They said therefore unto him: Who art thou, that we may give an answer to them that sent us? What sayest thou of thyself? ^john-01-22-douay

23 He said: I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, make straight the way of the Lord, as said the prophet Isaias. ^john-01-23-douay

24 And they that were sent were of the Pharisees. ^john-01-24-douay

25 And they asked him and said to him: Why then dost thou baptize, if thou be not Christ, nor Elias, nor the prophet? ^john-01-25-douay

26 John answered them, saying: I baptize with water: but there hath stood one in the midst of you, whom you know not. ^john-01-26-douay

27 The same is he that shall come after me, who is preferred before me: the latchet of whose shoe I am not worthy to loose. ^john-01-27-douay

28 These things were done in Bethania, beyond the Jordan, where John was baptizing. ^john-01-28-douay

29 The next day, John saw Jesus coming to him; and he saith: Behold the Lamb of God. Behold him who taketh away the sin of the world. ^john-01-29-douay

30 This is he of whom I said: After me there cometh a man, who is preferred before me: because he was before me. ^john-01-30-douay

31 And I knew him not: but that he may be made manifest in Israel, therefore am I come baptizing with water. ^john-01-31-douay

32 And John gave testimony, saying: I saw the Spirit coming down, as a dove from heaven; and he remained upon him. ^john-01-32-douay

33 And I knew him not: but he who sent me to baptize with water said to me: He upon whom thou shalt see the Spirit descending and remaining upon him, he it is that baptizeth with the Holy Ghost. ^john-01-33-douay

34 And I saw: and I gave testimony that this is the Son of God. ^john-01-34-douay

35 The next day again John stood and two of his disciples. ^john-01-35-douay

36 And beholding Jesus walking, he saith: Behold the Lamb of God. ^john-01-36-douay

37 And the two disciples heard him speak: and they followed Jesus. ^john-01-37-douay

38 And Jesus turning and seeing them following him, saith to them: What seek you? Who said to him: Rabbi (which is to say, being interpreted, Master), where dwellest thou? ^john-01-38-douay

39 He saith to them: Come and see. They came and saw where he abode: and they stayed with him that day. Now it was about the tenth hour. ^john-01-39-douay

40 And Andrew, the brother of Simon Peter, was one of the two who had heard of John and followed him. ^john-01-40-douay

41 He findeth first his brother Simon and saith to him: We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ. ^john-01-41-douay

42 And he brought him to Jesus. And Jesus looking upon him, said: Thou art Simon the son of Jona. Thou shalt be called Cephas, which is interpreted Peter. ^john-01-42-douay

43 On the following day, he would go forth into Galilee: and he findeth Philip, And Jesus saith to him: follow me. ^john-01-43-douay

44 Now Philip was of Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter. ^john-01-44-douay

45 Philip findeth Nathanael and saith to him: We have found him of whom Moses, in the law and the prophets did write, Jesus the son of Joseph of Nazareth. ^john-01-45-douay

46 And Nathanael said to him: Can any thing of good come from Nazareth? Philip saith to him: Come and see. ^john-01-46-douay

47 Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him and he saith of him: Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom there is no guile. ^john-01-47-douay

48 Nathanael saith to him: Whence knowest thou me? Jesus answered and said to him: Before that Philip called thee, when thou wast under the fig tree, I saw thee. ^john-01-48-douay

49 Nathanael answered him and said: Rabbi: Thou art the Son of God. Thou art the King of Israel. ^john-01-49-douay

50 Jesus answered and said to him: Because I said unto thee, I saw thee under the fig tree, thou believest: greater things than these shalt thou see. ^john-01-50-douay

51 And he saith to him: Amen, amen, I say to you, you shall see the heaven opened and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man. ^john-01-51-douay
