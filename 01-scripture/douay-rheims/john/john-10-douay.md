# John Chapter 10

Christ is the door and the good shepherd. He and his Father are one.

1 Amen, amen, I say to you: He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold but climbeth up another way, the same is a thief and a robber. ^john-10-1-douay

2 But he that entereth in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep. ^john-10-2-douay

3 To him the porter openeth: and the sheep hear his voice. And he calleth his own sheep by name and leadeth them out. ^john-10-3-douay

4 And when he hath let out his own sheep, he goeth before them: and the sheep follow him, because they know his voice. ^john-10-4-douay

5 But a stranger they follow not, but fly from him, because they know not the voice of strangers. ^john-10-5-douay

6 This proverb Jesus spoke to them. But they understood not what he spoke. ^john-10-6-douay

7 Jesus therefore said to them again: Amen, amen, I say to you, I am the door of the sheep. ^john-10-7-douay

8 All others, as many as have come, are thieves and robbers: and the sheep heard them not. ^john-10-8-douay

9 I am the door. By me, if any man enter in, he shall be saved: and he shall go in and go out, and shall find pastures. ^john-10-9-douay

10 The thief cometh not, but for to steal and to kill and to destroy. I am come that they may have life and may have it more abundantly. ^john-10-10-douay

11 I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd giveth his life for his sheep. ^john-10-11-douay

12 But the hireling and he that is not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, seeth the wolf coming and leaveth the sheep and flieth: and the wolf casteth and scattereth the sheep, ^john-10-12-douay

13 And the hireling flieth, because he is a hireling: and he hath no care for the sheep. ^john-10-13-douay

14 I am the good shepherd: and I know mine, and mine know me. ^john-10-14-douay

15 As the Father knoweth me, and I know the Father: and I lay down my life for my sheep. ^john-10-15-douay

16 And other sheep I have that are not of this fold: them also I must bring. And they shall hear my voice: And there shall be one fold and one shepherd. ^john-10-16-douay

17 Therefore doth the Father love me: because I lay down my life, that I may take it again. ^john-10-17-douay

18 No man taketh it away from me: but I lay it down of myself. And I have power to lay it down: and I have power to take it up again. This commandment have I received of my Father. ^john-10-18-douay

19 A dissension rose again among the Jews for these words. ^john-10-19-douay

20 And many of them said: He hath a devil and is mad. Why hear you him? ^john-10-20-douay

21 Others said: These are not the words of one that hath a devil. Can a devil open the eyes of the blind? ^john-10-21-douay

22 And it was the feast of the dedication at Jerusalem: and it was winter. ^john-10-22-douay

23 And Jesus walked in the temple, in Solomon's porch. ^john-10-23-douay

24 The Jews therefore came round about him and said to him: How long dost thou hold our souls in suspense? If thou be the Christ, tell us plainly. ^john-10-24-douay

25 Jesus answered them: I speak to you, and you believe not: the works that I do in the name of my Father, they give testimony of me. ^john-10-25-douay

26 But you do not believe, because you are not of my sheep. ^john-10-26-douay

27 My sheep hear my voice. And I know them: and they follow me. ^john-10-27-douay

28 And I give them life everlasting: and they shall not perish for ever. And no man shall pluck them out of my hand. ^john-10-28-douay

29 That which my Father hath given me is greater than all: and no one can snatch them out of the hand of my Father. ^john-10-29-douay

30 I and the Father are one. **I and the Father are one...** That is, one divine nature, but two distinct persons. ^john-10-30-douay

31 The Jews then took up stones to stone him. ^john-10-31-douay

32 Jesus answered them: Many good works I have showed you from my Father. For which of those works do you stone me? ^john-10-32-douay

33 The Jews answered him: For a good work we stone thee not, but for blasphemy: and because that thou being a man, makest thyself God. ^john-10-33-douay

34 Jesus answered them: Is it not written in your law: I said, you are gods? ^john-10-34-douay

35 If he called them gods to whom the word of God was spoken; and the scripture cannot be broken: ^john-10-35-douay

36 Do you say of him whom the Father hath sanctified and sent into the world: Thou blasphemest; because I said: I am the Son of God? ^john-10-36-douay

37 If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not. ^john-10-37-douay

38 But if I do, though you will not believe me, believe the works: that you may know and believe that the Father is in me and I in the Father. ^john-10-38-douay

39 They sought therefore to take him: and he escaped out of their hands. ^john-10-39-douay

40 And he went again beyond the Jordan, into that place where John was baptizing first. And there he abode. ^john-10-40-douay

41 And many resorted to him: and they said: John indeed did no sign. ^john-10-41-douay

42 But all things whatsoever John said of this man were true. And many believed in him. ^john-10-42-douay
