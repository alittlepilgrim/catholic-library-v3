# John Chapter 8

The woman taken in adultery. Christ justifies his doctrine.

1 And Jesus went unto mount Olivet. ^john-08-1-douay

2 And early in the morning he came again into the temple: and all the people came to him. And sitting down he taught them. ^john-08-2-douay

3 And the scribes and Pharisees bring unto him a woman taken in adultery: and they set her in the midst, ^john-08-3-douay

4 And said to him: Master, this woman was even now taken in adultery. ^john-08-4-douay

5 Now Moses in the law commanded us to stone such a one. But what sayest thou? ^john-08-5-douay

6 And this they said tempting him, that they might accuse him. But Jesus bowing himself down, wrote with his finger on the ground. ^john-08-6-douay

7 When therefore they continued asking him, he lifted up himself and said to them: He that is without sin among you, let him first cast a stone at her. ^john-08-7-douay

8 And again stooping down, he wrote on the ground. ^john-08-8-douay

9 But they hearing this, went out one by one, beginning at the eldest. And Jesus alone remained, and the woman standing in the midst. ^john-08-9-douay

10 Then Jesus lifting up himself, said to her: Woman, where are they that accused thee? Hath no man condemned thee? ^john-08-10-douay

11 Who said: No man, Lord. And Jesus said: Neither will I condemn thee. Go, and now sin no more. ^john-08-11-douay

12 Again therefore, Jesus spoke to: them, saying: I am the light of the world. He that followeth me walketh not in darkness, but shall have the light of life. ^john-08-12-douay

13 The Pharisees therefore said to him: Thou givest testimony of thyself. Thy testimony is not true. ^john-08-13-douay

14 Jesus answered and said to them: Although I give testimony of myself, my testimony is true: for I know whence I came and whither I go. ^john-08-14-douay

15 You judge according to the flesh: I judge not any man. ^john-08-15-douay

16 And if I do judge, my judgment is true: because I am not alone, but I and the Father that sent me. ^john-08-16-douay

17 And in your law it is written that the testimony of two men is true. ^john-08-17-douay

18 I am one that give testimony of myself: and the Father that sent me giveth testimony of me. ^john-08-18-douay

19 They said therefore to him: Where is thy Father? Jesus answered: Neither me do you know, nor my Father. If you did know me, perhaps you would know my Father also. ^john-08-19-douay

20 These words Jesus spoke in the treasury, teaching in the temple: and no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come. ^john-08-20-douay

21 Again therefore Jesus said to them: I go: and you shall seek me. And you shall die in your sin. Whither I go, you cannot come. ^john-08-21-douay

22 The Jews therefore said: Will he kill himself, because he said: Whither I go you cannot come? ^john-08-22-douay

23 And he said to them: You are from beneath: I am from above. You are of this world: I am not of this world. ^john-08-23-douay

24 Therefore I said to you that you shall die in your sins. For if you believe not that I am he, you shall die in your sin. ^john-08-24-douay

25 They said therefore to him: Who art thou? Jesus said to them: The beginning, who also speak unto you. ^john-08-25-douay

26 Many things I have to speak and to judge of you. But he that sent me, is true: and the things I have heard of him, these same I speak in the world. ^john-08-26-douay

27 And they understood not that he called God his Father. ^john-08-27-douay

28 Jesus therefore said to them: When you shall have lifted up, the Son of man, then shall you know that I am he and that I do nothing of myself. But as the Father hath taught me, these things I speak. ^john-08-28-douay

29 And he that sent me is with me: and he hath not left me alone. For I do always the things that please him. ^john-08-29-douay

30 When he spoke these things, many believed in him. ^john-08-30-douay

31 Then Jesus said to those Jews who believed him: If you continue in my word, you shall be my disciples indeed. ^john-08-31-douay

32 And you shall know the truth: and the truth shall make you free. ^john-08-32-douay

33 They answered him: We are the seed of Abraham: and we have never been slaves to any man. How sayest thou: You shall be free? ^john-08-33-douay

34 Jesus answered them: Amen, amen, I say unto you that whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin. ^john-08-34-douay

35 Now the servant abideth not in the house for ever: but the son abideth for ever. ^john-08-35-douay

36 If therefore the son shall make you free, you shall be free indeed. ^john-08-36-douay

37 I know that you are the children of Abraham: but you seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you. ^john-08-37-douay

38 I speak that which I have seen with my Father: and you do the things that you have seen with your father. ^john-08-38-douay

39 They answered and said to him: Abraham is our father. Jesus saith them: If you be the children of Abraham, do the works of Abraham. ^john-08-39-douay

40 But now you seek to kill me, a man who have spoken the truth to you, which I have heard of God. This Abraham did not. ^john-08-40-douay

41 You do the works of your father. They said therefore to him: We are not born of fornication: we have one Father, even God. ^john-08-41-douay

42 Jesus therefore said to them: If God were your Father, you would indeed love me. For from God I proceeded and came. For I came not of myself: but he sent me. ^john-08-42-douay

43 Why do you not know my speech? Because you cannot hear my word. ^john-08-43-douay

44 You are of your father the devil: and the desires of your father you will do. He was a murderer from the beginning: and he stood not in the truth, because truth is not in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father thereof. ^john-08-44-douay

45 But if I say the truth, you believe me not. ^john-08-45-douay

46 Which of you shall convince me of sin? If I say the truth to you, why do you not believe me: ^john-08-46-douay

47 He that is of God heareth the words of God. Therefore you hear them not, because you are not of God. ^john-08-47-douay

48 The Jews therefore answered and said to him: Do not we say well that thou art a Samaritan and hast a devil? ^john-08-48-douay

49 Jesus answered: I have not a devil: but I honour my Father. And you have dishonoured me. ^john-08-49-douay

50 But I seek not my own glory: there is one that seeketh and judgeth. ^john-08-50-douay

51 Amen, amen, I say to you: If any man keep my word, he shall not see death for ever. ^john-08-51-douay

52 The Jews therefore said: Now we know that thou hast a devil. Abraham is dead, and the prophets: and thou sayest: If any man keep my word, he shall not taste death for ever. ^john-08-52-douay

53 Art thou greater than our father Abraham who is dead? And the prophets are dead. Whom dost thou make thyself? ^john-08-53-douay

54 Jesus answered: If I glorify myself, my glory is nothing. It is my Father that glorifieth me, of whom you say that he is your God. ^john-08-54-douay

55 And you have not known him: but I know him. And if I shall say that I know him not, I shall be like to you, a liar. But I do know him and do keep his word. ^john-08-55-douay

56 Abraham your father rejoiced that he might see my day: he saw it and was glad. ^john-08-56-douay

57 The Jews therefore said to him: Thou art not yet fifty years old. And hast thou seen Abraham? ^john-08-57-douay

58 Jesus said to them: Amen, amen, I say to you, before Abraham was made, I AM. ^john-08-58-douay

59 They took up stones therefore to cast at him. But Jesus hid himself and went out of the temple. ^john-08-59-douay
