# John Chapter 3

Christ's discourse with Nicodemus. John's testimony.

1 And there was a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews. ^john-03-1-douay

2 This man came to Jesus by night and said to him: Rabbi, we know that thou art come a teacher from God; for no man can do these signs which thou dost, unless God be with him. ^john-03-2-douay

3 Jesus answered and said to him: Amen, amen, I say to thee, unless a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God. ^john-03-3-douay

4 Nicodemus saith to him: How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother's womb and be born again? ^john-03-4-douay

5 Jesus answered: Amen, amen, I say to thee, unless a man be born again of water and the Holy Ghost, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. **Unless a man be born again, etc...** By these words our Saviour hath declared the necessity of baptism; and by the word *water* it is evident that the application of it is necessary with the words ([[matthew-28-douay|Matthew 28:19]]). ^john-03-5-douay

6 That which is born of the flesh is flesh: and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. ^john-03-6-douay

7 Wonder not that I said to thee: You must be born again. ^john-03-7-douay

8 The Spirit breatheth where he will and thou hearest his voice: but thou knowest not whence he cometh and whither he goeth. So is every one that is born of the Spirit. ^john-03-8-douay

9 Nicodemus answered and said to him: How can these things be done? ^john-03-9-douay

10 Jesus answered and said to him: Art thou a master in Israel, and knowest not these things? ^john-03-10-douay

11 Amen, amen, I say to thee that we speak what we know and we testify what we have seen: and you receive not our testimony. ^john-03-11-douay

12 If I have spoken to you earthly things, and you believe not: how will you believe, if I shall speak to you heavenly things? ^john-03-12-douay

13 And no man hath ascended into heaven, but he that descended from heaven, the Son of man who is in heaven. ^john-03-13-douay

14 And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the desert, so must the Son of man be lifted up: ^john-03-14-douay

15 That whosoever believeth in him may not perish, but may have life everlasting. ^john-03-15-douay

16 For God so loved the world, as to give his only begotten Son: that whosoever believeth in him may not perish, but may have life everlasting. ^john-03-16-douay

17 For God sent not his Son into the world, to judge the world: but that the world may be saved by him. ^john-03-17-douay

18 He that believeth in him is not judged. But he that doth not believe is already judged: because he believeth not in the name of the only begotten Son of God. **Is not judged...** He that believeth, viz., by a faith working through charity, is not judged, that is, is not condemned; but the obstinate unbeliever is judged, that is, condemned already, by retrenching himself from the society of Christ and his church. ^john-03-18-douay

19 And this is the judgment: Because the light is come into the world and men loved darkness rather than the light: for their works were evil. **The judgment...** That is, the cause of his condemnation. ^john-03-19-douay

20 For every one that doth evil hateth the light and cometh not to the light, that his works may not be reproved. ^john-03-20-douay

21 But he that doth truth cometh to the light, that his works may be made manifest: because they are done in God. **He that doth truth...** that is, he that acteth according to truth, which here signifies the Law of God. Thy law is truth. Psa. 118. 142. ^john-03-21-douay

22 After these things, Jesus and his disciples came into the land of Judea: and there he abode with them and baptized. ^john-03-22-douay

23 And John also was baptizing in Ennon near Salim: because there was much water there. And they came and were baptized. ^john-03-23-douay

24 For John was not yet cast into prison. ^john-03-24-douay

25 And there arose a question between some of John's disciples and the Jews, concerning purification. ^john-03-25-douay

26 And they came to John and said to him: Rabbi, he that was with thee beyond the Jordan, to whom thou gavest testimony: behold, he baptizeth and all men come to him. ^john-03-26-douay

27 John answered and said: A man cannot receive any thing, unless it be given him from heaven. ^john-03-27-douay

28 You yourselves do bear me witness that I said that I am not Christ, but that I am sent before him. ^john-03-28-douay

29 He that hath the bride is the bridegroom: but the friend of the bridegroom, who standeth and heareth Him, rejoiceth with joy because of the bridegroom's voice. This my joy therefore is fulfilled. ^john-03-29-douay

30 He must increase: but I must decrease. ^john-03-30-douay

31 He that cometh from above is above all. He that is of the earth, of the earth he is, and of the earth he speaketh. He that cometh from heaven is above all. ^john-03-31-douay

32 And what he hath seen and heard, that he testifieth: and no man receiveth his testimony. ^john-03-32-douay

33 He that hath received his testimony hath set to his seal that God is true. ^john-03-33-douay

34 For he whom God hath sent speaketh the words of God: for God doth not give the Spirit by measure. ^john-03-34-douay

35 The Father loveth the Son: and he hath given all things into his hand. ^john-03-35-douay

36 He that believeth in the Son hath life everlasting: but he that believeth not the Son shall not see life: but the wrath of God abideth on him. ^john-03-36-douay
