# John Chapter 9

He gives sight to the man born blind.

1 And Jesus passing by, saw a man who was blind from his birth. ^john-09-1-douay

2 And his disciples asked him: Rabbi, who hath sinned, this man or his parents, that he should be born blind? ^john-09-2-douay

3 Jesus answered: Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents; but that the works of God should be made manifest in him. ^john-09-3-douay

4 I must work the works of him that sent me, whilst it is day: the night cometh, when no man can work. ^john-09-4-douay

5 As long as I am in the world, I am the light of the world. ^john-09-5-douay

6 When he had said these things, he spat on the ground and made clay of the spittle and spread the clay upon his eyes, ^john-09-6-douay

7 And said to him: Go, wash in the pool of Siloe, which is interpreted, *Sent*. He went therefore and washed: and he came seeing. ^john-09-7-douay

8 The neighbours, therefore, and they who had seen him before that he was a beggar, said: Is not this he that sat and begged? Some said: This is he. ^john-09-8-douay

9 But others said: No, but he is like him. But he said: I am he. ^john-09-9-douay

10 They said therefore to him: How were thy eyes opened? ^john-09-10-douay

11 He answered: That man that is called Jesus made clay and anointed my eyes and said to me: Go to the pool of Siloe and wash. And I went: I washed: and I see. ^john-09-11-douay

12 And they said to him: Where is he? He saith: I know not. ^john-09-12-douay

13 They bring him that had been blind to the Pharisees. ^john-09-13-douay

14 Now it was the sabbath, when Jesus made the clay and opened his eyes. ^john-09-14-douay

15 Again therefore the Pharisees asked him how he had received his sight. But he said to them: He put clay upon my eyes: and I washed: and I see. ^john-09-15-douay

16 Some therefore of the Pharisees said: This man is not of God, who keepeth not the sabbath. But others said: How can a man that is a sinner do such miracles? And there was a division among them. ^john-09-16-douay

17 They say therefore to the blind man again: What sayest thou of him that hath opened thy eyes? And he said: He is a prophet. ^john-09-17-douay

18 The Jews then did not believe concerning him, that he had been blind and had received his sight, until they called the parents of him that had received his sight, ^john-09-18-douay

19 And asked them, saying: Is this your son, who you say was born blind? How then doth he now see? ^john-09-19-douay

20 His parents answered them and said: We know that this is our son and that he was born blind: ^john-09-20-douay

21 But how he now seeth, we know not: or who hath opened his eyes, we know not. Ask himself: he is of age: Let him speak for himself. ^john-09-21-douay

22 These things his parents said, because they feared the Jews: for the Jews had already agreed among themselves that if any man should confess him to be Christ, he should be put out of the synagogue. ^john-09-22-douay

23 Therefore did his parents say: He is of age. Ask himself. ^john-09-23-douay

24 They therefore called the man again that had been blind and said to him: Give glory to God. We know that this man is a sinner. ^john-09-24-douay

25 He said therefore to them: If he be a sinner, I know not. One thing I know, that whereas I was blind now I see. ^john-09-25-douay

26 They said then to him: What did he to thee? How did he open thy eyes? ^john-09-26-douay

27 He answered them: I have told you already, and you have heard. Why would you hear it again? Will you also become his disciples? ^john-09-27-douay

28 They reviled him therefore and said: Be thou his disciple; but we are the disciples of Moses. ^john-09-28-douay

29 We know that God spoke to Moses: but as to this man, we know not from whence he is. ^john-09-29-douay

30 The man answered and said to them: why, herein is a wonderful thing, that you know not from whence he is, and he hath opened my eyes. ^john-09-30-douay

31 Now we know that God doth not hear sinners: but if a man be a server of God and doth his, will, him he heareth. ^john-09-31-douay

32 From the beginning of the world it hath not been heard, that any man hath opened the eyes of one born blind. ^john-09-32-douay

33 Unless this man were of God, he could not do anything. ^john-09-33-douay

34 They answered and said to him: Thou wast wholly born in sins; and dost thou teach us? And they cast him out. ^john-09-34-douay

35 Jesus heard that they had cast him out. And when he had found him, he said to him: Dost thou believe in the Son of God? ^john-09-35-douay

36 He answered, and said: Who is he, Lord, that I may believe in him? ^john-09-36-douay

37 And Jesus said to him: Thou hast both seen him; and it is he that talketh with thee. ^john-09-37-douay

38 And he said: I believe, Lord. And falling down, he adored him. ^john-09-38-douay

39 And Jesus said: For judgment I am come into this world: that they who see not may see; and they who see may become blind. **I am come, etc...** Not that Christ came for that end, that any one should be made blind: but that the Jews, by the abuse of his coming, and by their not receiving him, brought upon themselves this judgment of blindness. ^john-09-39-douay

40 And some of the Pharisees, who were with him, heard: and they said unto him: Are we also blind? ^john-09-40-douay

41 Jesus said to them: If you were blind, you should not have sin: but now you say: We see. Your sin remaineth. **If you were blind, etc...** If you were invincibly ignorant, and had neither read the scriptures, nor seen my miracles, you would not be guilty of the sin of infidelity: but now, as you boast of your knowledge of the scriptures, you are inexcusable. ^john-09-41-douay
