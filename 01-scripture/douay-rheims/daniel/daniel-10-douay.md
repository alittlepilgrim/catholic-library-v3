# Daniel Chapter 10

Daniel having humbled himself by fasting and penance sees a vision, with which he is much terrified; but he is comforted by an angel.

1 In the third year of Cyrus, king of the Persians, a word was revealed to Daniel, surnamed Baltassar, and a true word, and great strength: and he understood the word: for there is need of understanding in a vision. ^daniel-10-1-douay

2 In those days I, Daniel, mourned the days of three weeks. ^daniel-10-2-douay

3 I ate no desirable bread, and neither flesh, nor wine, entered into my mouth, neither was I anointed with ointment: till the days of three weeks were accomplished. ^daniel-10-3-douay

4 And in the four and twentieth day of the first month, I was by the great river, which is the Tigris. ^daniel-10-4-douay

5 And I lifted up my eyes, and I saw: and behold a man clothed in linen, and his loins were girded with the finest gold: ^daniel-10-5-douay

6 And his body was like the chrysolite, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as a burning lamp: and his arms, and all downward even to the feet, like in appearance to glittering brass: and the voice of his word like the voice of a multitude. ^daniel-10-6-douay

7 And I, Daniel alone, saw the vision: for the men that were with me saw it not: but an exceeding great terror fell upon them, and they fled away, and hid themselves. ^daniel-10-7-douay

8 And I, being left alone, saw this great vision: and there remained no strength in me, and the appearance of my countenance was changed in me, and I fainted away, and retained no strength. ^daniel-10-8-douay

9 And I heard the voice of his words: and when I heard I lay in a consternation upon my face, and my face was close to the ground. ^daniel-10-9-douay

10 And behold a hand touched me, and lifted me up upon my knees, and upon the joints of my hands. ^daniel-10-10-douay

11 And he said to me: Daniel, thou man of desires, understand the words that I speak to thee, and stand upright: for I am sent now to thee. And when he had said this word to me, I stood trembling. ^daniel-10-11-douay

12 And he said to me: Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thy heart to understand, to afflict thyself in the sight of thy God, thy words have been heard: and I am come for thy words. ^daniel-10-12-douay

13 But the prince of the kingdom of the Persians resisted me one and twenty days: and behold Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me, and I remained there by the king of the Persians. **The prince, etc...** That is, the angel guardian of Persia: who according to his office, seeking the spiritual good of the Persians was desirous that many of the Jews should remain among them. ^daniel-10-13-douay

14 But I am come to teach thee what things shall befall thy people in the latter days, for as yet the vision is for days. ^daniel-10-14-douay

15 And when he was speaking such words to me, I cast down my countenance to the ground, and held my peace. ^daniel-10-15-douay

16 And behold as it were the likeness of a son of man touched my lips: then I opened my mouth and spoke, and said to him that stood before me: O my lord, at the sight of thee my joints are loosed, and no strength hath remained in me. ^daniel-10-16-douay

17 And how can the servant of my lord speak with my lord? for no strength remaineth in me; moreover, my breath is stopped. ^daniel-10-17-douay

18 Therefore, he that looked like a man, touched me again, and strengthened me. ^daniel-10-18-douay

19 And he said: Fear not, O man of desires, peace be to thee: take courage, and be strong. And when he spoke to me, I grew strong, and I said: Speak, O my lord, for thou hast strengthened me. ^daniel-10-19-douay

20 And he said: Dost thou know wherefore I am come to thee? And now I will return, to fight against the prince of the Persians. When I went forth, there appeared the prince of the Greeks coming. ^daniel-10-20-douay

21 But I will tell thee what is set down in the scripture of truth: and none is my helper in all these things, but Michael your prince. **Michael your prince...** The guardian general of the church of God. ^daniel-10-21-douay
