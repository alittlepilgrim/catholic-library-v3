# Galatians Chapter 1

He blames the Galatians for allowing themselves to be imposed upon by new teachers. The apostle's calling.

1 Paul, an apostle, not of men, neither by man, but by Jesus Christ and God the Father, who raised him from the dead: ^galatians-01-1-douay

2 And all the brethren who are with me: to the churches of Galatia. ^galatians-01-2-douay

3 Grace be to you, and peace from God the Father and from our Lord Jesus Christ, ^galatians-01-3-douay

4 Who gave himself for our sins, that he might deliver us from this present wicked world, according to the will of God and our Father: ^galatians-01-4-douay

5 To whom is glory for ever and ever. Amen. ^galatians-01-5-douay

6 I wonder that you are so soon removed from him that called you into the grace of Christ, unto another gospel. ^galatians-01-6-douay

7 Which is not another: only there are some that trouble you and would pervert the gospel of Christ. ^galatians-01-7-douay

8 But though we, or an angel from heaven, preach a gospel to you besides that which we have preached to you, let him be anathema. ^galatians-01-8-douay

9 As we said before, so now I say again: If any one preach to you a gospel, besides that which you have received, let him be anathema. ^galatians-01-9-douay

10 For do I now persuade men, or God? Or do I seek to please men? If I yet pleased men, I should not be the servant of Christ. ^galatians-01-10-douay

11 For I give you to understand, brethren, that the gospel which was preached by me is not according to man. ^galatians-01-11-douay

12 For neither did I receive it of man: nor did I learn it but by the revelation of Jesus Christ. ^galatians-01-12-douay

13 For you have heard of my conversation in time past in the Jews' religion: how that, beyond measure, I persecuted the church of God and wasted it. ^galatians-01-13-douay

14 And I made progress in the Jew's religion above many of my equals in my own nation, being more abundantly zealous for the traditions of my fathers. ^galatians-01-14-douay

15 But when it pleased him who separated me from my mother's womb and called me by his grace, ^galatians-01-15-douay

16 To reveal his Son in me, that I might preach him among the Gentiles: immediately I condescended not to flesh and blood. ^galatians-01-16-douay

17 Neither went I to Jerusalem, to the apostles who were before me: but I went into Arabia, and again I returned to Damascus. ^galatians-01-17-douay

18 Then, after three years, I went to Jerusalem to see Peter: and I tarried with him fifteen days. ^galatians-01-18-douay

19 But other of the apostles I saw none, saving James the brother of the Lord. ^galatians-01-19-douay

20 Now the things which I write to you, behold, before God, I lie not. ^galatians-01-20-douay

21 Afterwards, I came into the regions of Syria and Cilicia. ^galatians-01-21-douay

22 And I was unknown by face to the churches of Judea, which were in Christ: ^galatians-01-22-douay

23 But they had heard only: He, who persecuted us in times past doth now preach the faith which once he impugned. ^galatians-01-23-douay

24 And they glorified God in me. ^galatians-01-24-douay
