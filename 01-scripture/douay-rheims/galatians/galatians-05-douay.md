# Galatians Chapter 5

He exhorts them to stand to their Christian liberty. Of the fruits of the flesh and of the spirit.

1 Stand fast and be not held again under the yoke of bondage. ^galatians-05-1-douay

2 Behold, I Paul tell you, that if you be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing. ^galatians-05-2-douay

3 And I testify again to every man circumcising himself that he is a debtor to do the whole law. ^galatians-05-3-douay

4 You are made void of Christ, you who are justified in the law: you are fallen from grace. ^galatians-05-4-douay

5 For we in spirit, by faith, wait for the hope of justice. ^galatians-05-5-douay

6 For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing nor uncircumcision: but faith that worketh by Charity. ^galatians-05-6-douay

7 You did run well. What hath hindered you, that you should not obey the truth? ^galatians-05-7-douay

8 This persuasion is not from him that calleth you. ^galatians-05-8-douay

9 A little leaven corrupteth the whole lump. ^galatians-05-9-douay

10 I have confidence in you in the Lord that you will not be of another mind: but he that troubleth you shall bear the judgment, whosoever he be. ^galatians-05-10-douay

11 And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? Then is the scandal of the cross made void. ^galatians-05-11-douay

12 I would they were even cut off, who trouble you. ^galatians-05-12-douay

13 For you, brethren, have been called unto liberty. Only make not liberty an occasion to the flesh: but by charity of the spirit serve one another. ^galatians-05-13-douay

14 For all the law is fulfilled in one word: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. ^galatians-05-14-douay

15 But if you bite and devour one another: take heed you be not consumed one of another. ^galatians-05-15-douay

16 I say then: Walk in the spirit: and you shall not fulfill the lusts of the flesh. ^galatians-05-16-douay

17 For the flesh lusteth against the spirit: and the spirit against the flesh: For these are contrary one to another: so that you do not the things that you would. ^galatians-05-17-douay

18 But if you are led by the spirit, you are not under the law. ^galatians-05-18-douay

19 Now the works of the flesh are manifest: which are fornication, uncleanness, immodesty, luxury, ^galatians-05-19-douay

20 Idolatry, witchcrafts, enmities, contentions, emulations, wraths, quarrels, dissensions, sects, ^galatians-05-20-douay

21 Envies, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like. Of the which I foretell you, as I have foretold to you, that they who do such things shall not obtain the kingdom of God. ^galatians-05-21-douay

22 But the fruit of the Spirit is, charity, joy, peace, patience, benignity, goodness, longanimity, ^galatians-05-22-douay

23 Mildness, faith, modesty, continency, chastity. Against such there is no law. ^galatians-05-23-douay

24 And they that are Christ's have crucified their flesh, with the vices and concupiscences. ^galatians-05-24-douay

25 If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit. ^galatians-05-25-douay

26 Let us not be made desirous of vain glory, provoking one another, envying one another. ^galatians-05-26-douay
