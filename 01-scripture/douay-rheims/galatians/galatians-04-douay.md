# Galatians Chapter 4

Christ has freed us from the servitude of the law. We are the freeborn sons of Abraham.

1 As long as the heir is a child, he differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all, ^galatians-04-1-douay

2 But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed by the father. ^galatians-04-2-douay

3 So we also, when we were children, were serving under the elements of the world. **Under the elements, etc...** That is, under the first rudiments of religion, in which the carnal Jews were trained up; or under those corporeal creatures, used in their manifold rites, sacrifices, and sacraments. ^galatians-04-3-douay

4 But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent his Son, made of a woman, made under the law: ^galatians-04-4-douay

5 That he might redeem them who were under the law: that we might receive the adoption of sons. ^galatians-04-5-douay

6 And because you are sons, God hath sent the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying: Abba, Father. ^galatians-04-6-douay

7 Therefore, now he is not a servant, but a son. And if a son, an heir also through God. ^galatians-04-7-douay

8 But then indeed, not knowing God, you served them who, by nature, are not gods. ^galatians-04-8-douay

9 But now, after that you have known God, or rather are known by God: how turn you again to the weak and needy elements which you desire to serve again? ^galatians-04-9-douay

10 You observe days and months and times, and years. **You observe days, etc...** He speaks not of the observation of the Lord's day, or other Christian festivals; but either of the superstitious observation of days lucky and unlucky; or else of the Jewish festivals, to the observance of which, certain Jewish teachers sought to induce the Galatians. ^galatians-04-10-douay

11 I am afraid of you, lest perhaps I have laboured in vain among you. ^galatians-04-11-douay

12 Be ye as I, because I also am as you brethren, I beseech you. You have not injured me at all. ^galatians-04-12-douay

13 And you know how, through infirmity of the flesh, I preached the gospel to you heretofore: and your temptation in my flesh. ^galatians-04-13-douay

14 You despised not, nor rejected: but received me as an angel of God, even as Christ Jesus. ^galatians-04-14-douay

15 Where is then your blessedness? For I bear you witness that, if it could be done, you would have plucked out your own eyes and would have given them to me. ^galatians-04-15-douay

16 Am I then become your enemy, because I tell you the truth? ^galatians-04-16-douay

17 They are zealous in your regard not well: but they would exclude you, that you might be zealous for them. ^galatians-04-17-douay

18 But be zealous for that which is good in a good thing always: and not only when I am present with you. ^galatians-04-18-douay

19 My little children, of whom I am in labour again, until Christ be formed in you. ^galatians-04-19-douay

20 And I would willingly be present with you now and change my voice: because I am ashamed for you. ^galatians-04-20-douay

21 Tell me, you that desire to be under the law, have you not read the law? ^galatians-04-21-douay

22 For it is written that Abraham had two sons: the one by a bondwoman and the other by a free woman. ^galatians-04-22-douay

23 But he who was of the bondwoman was born according to the flesh: but he of the free woman was by promise. ^galatians-04-23-douay

24 Which things are said by an allegory. For these are the two testaments. The one from Mount Sina, engendering unto bondage, which is Agar. ^galatians-04-24-douay

25 For Sina is a mountain in Arabia, which hath affinity to that Jerusalem which now is: and is in bondage with her children. ^galatians-04-25-douay

26 But that Jerusalem which is above is free: which is our mother. ^galatians-04-26-douay

27 For it is written: Rejoice, thou barren, that bearest not: break forth and cry thou that travailest not: for many are the children of the desolate, more than of her that hath a husband. ^galatians-04-27-douay

28 Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise. ^galatians-04-28-douay

29 But as then he that was born according to the flesh persecuted him that was after the spirit: so also it is now. ^galatians-04-29-douay

30 But what saith the scripture? Cast out the bondwoman and her son: for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the free woman. ^galatians-04-30-douay

31 So then, brethren, we are not the children of the bondwoman but of the free: by the freedom wherewith Christ has made us free. ^galatians-04-31-douay
