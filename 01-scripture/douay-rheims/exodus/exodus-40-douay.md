# Exodus Chapter 40

The tabernacle is commanded to be set up and anointed. God fills it with his majesty.

1 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^exodus-40-1-douay

2 The first month, the first day of the month, thou shalt set up the tabernacle of the testimony, ^exodus-40-2-douay

3 And shalt put the ark in it, and shalt let down the veil before it: ^exodus-40-3-douay

4 And thou shalt bring in the table, and set upon it the things that are commanded according to the rite. The candlestick shall stand with its lamps, ^exodus-40-4-douay

5 And the altar of gold, whereon the incense is burnt before the ark of the testimony. Thou shalt put the hanging in the entry of the tabernacle, ^exodus-40-5-douay

6 And before it the altar of holocaust. ^exodus-40-6-douay

7 The laver between the altar and the tabernacle, and thou shalt fill it with water. ^exodus-40-7-douay

8 And thou shalt encompass the court with hangings, and the entry thereof. ^exodus-40-8-douay

9 And thou shalt take the oil of unction and anoint the tabernacle with its vessels, that they may be sanctified: ^exodus-40-9-douay

10 The altar of holocaust and all its vessels: ^exodus-40-10-douay

11 The laver with its foot: thou shalt consecrate all with the oil of unction, that they may be most holy. ^exodus-40-11-douay

12 And thou shalt bring Aaron and his sons to the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, and having washed them with water, ^exodus-40-12-douay

13 Thou shalt put on them the holy vestments, that they may minister to me, and that the unction of them may prosper to an everlasting priesthood. ^exodus-40-13-douay

14 And Moses did all that the Lord had commanded. ^exodus-40-14-douay

15 So in the first month of the second year, the first day of the month, the tabernacle was set up. ^exodus-40-15-douay

16 And Moses reared it up, and placed the boards and the sockets and the bars, and set up the pillars, ^exodus-40-16-douay

17 And spread the roof over the tabernacle, putting over it a cover, as the Lord had commanded. ^exodus-40-17-douay

18 And he put the testimony in the ark, thrusting bars underneath, and the oracle above. ^exodus-40-18-douay

19 And when he had brought the ark into the tabernacle, he drew the veil before it to fulfil the commandment of the Lord. ^exodus-40-19-douay

20 And he set the table in the tabernacle of the testimony, at the north side, without the veil, ^exodus-40-20-douay

21 Setting there in order the loaves of proposition, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-40-21-douay

22 He set the candlestick also in the tabernacle of the testimony, over against the table on the south side, ^exodus-40-22-douay

23 Placing the lamps in order, according to the precept of the Lord. ^exodus-40-23-douay

24 He set also the altar of gold under the roof of the testimony, over against the veil, ^exodus-40-24-douay

25 And burnt upon it the incense of spices, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-40-25-douay

26 And he put also the hanging in the entry of the tabernacle of the testimony, ^exodus-40-26-douay

27 And the altar of holocaust in the entry of the testimony, offering the holocaust, and the sacrifices upon it, as the Lord had commanded. ^exodus-40-27-douay

28 And he set the laver between the tabernacle of the testimony and the altar, filling it with water. ^exodus-40-28-douay

29 And Moses and Aaron, and his sons, washed their hands and feet, ^exodus-40-29-douay

30 When they went into the tabernacle of the covenant, and went to the altar, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-40-30-douay

31 He set up also the court round about the tabernacle and the altar, drawing the hanging in the entry thereof. After all things were perfected, ^exodus-40-31-douay

32 The cloud covered the tabernacle of the testimony, and the glory of the Lord filled it. ^exodus-40-32-douay

33 Neither could Moses go into the tabernacle of the covenant, the cloud covering all things, and the majesty of the Lord shining, for the cloud had covered all. ^exodus-40-33-douay

34 If at any time the cloud removed from the tabernacle, the children of Israel went forward by their troops: ^exodus-40-34-douay

35 If it hung over, they remained in the same place. ^exodus-40-35-douay

36 For the cloud of the Lord hung over the tabernacle by day, and a fire by night, in the sight of all the children of Israel throughout all their mansions. ^exodus-40-36-douay
