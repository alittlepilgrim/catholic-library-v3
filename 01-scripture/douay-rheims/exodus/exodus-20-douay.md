# Exodus Chapter 20

The ten commandments.

1 And the Lord spoke all these words: ^exodus-20-1-douay

2 I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. ^exodus-20-2-douay

3 Thou shalt not have strange gods before me. ^exodus-20-3-douay

4 Thou shalt not make to thyself a graven thing, nor the likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or in the earth beneath, nor of those things that are in the waters under the earth. **A graven thing, nor the likeness of any thing, etc...** All such images, or likenesses, are forbidden by this commandment, as are made to be adored and served; according to that which immediately follows, thou shalt not adore them, nor serve them. That is, all such as are designed for idols or image-gods, or are worshipped with divine honour. But otherwise images, pictures, or representations, even in the house of God, and in the very sanctuary so far from being forbidden, are expressly authorized by the word of God. See [[exodus-25-douay|Exodus 25:15]], and etc.; [[exodus-38-douay|Exodus 38:7]]; [[numbers-21-douay|Numbers 21:8-9]]; [[1-chronicles-28-douay|1 Chronicles 28:18-19]]; [[2-chronicles-03-douay|2 Chronicles 3:10]]. ^exodus-20-4-douay

5 Thou shalt not adore them, nor serve them: I am the Lord thy God, mighty, jealous, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me: ^exodus-20-5-douay

6 And showing mercy unto thousands to them that love me, and keep my commandments. ^exodus-20-6-douay

7 Thou shalt not take the name of the Lord thy God in vain: for the Lord will not hold him guiltless that shall take the name of the Lord his God in vain. ^exodus-20-7-douay

8 Remember that thou keep holy the sabbath day. ^exodus-20-8-douay

9 Six days shalt thou labour, and shalt do all thy works. ^exodus-20-9-douay

10 But on the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: thou shalt do no work on it, thou nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy beast, nor the stranger that is within thy gates. ^exodus-20-10-douay

11 For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, and the sea, and all things that are in them, and rested on the seventh day: therefore the Lord blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it. ^exodus-20-11-douay

12 Honour thy father and thy mother, that thou mayst be longlived upon the land which the Lord thy God will give thee. ^exodus-20-12-douay

13 Thou shalt not kill. ^exodus-20-13-douay

14 Thou shalt not commit adultery. ^exodus-20-14-douay

15 Thou shalt not steal. ^exodus-20-15-douay

16 Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour. ^exodus-20-16-douay

17 Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour's house; neither shalt thou desire his wife, nor his servant, nor his handmaid, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is his. ^exodus-20-17-douay

18 And all the people saw the voices and the flames, and the sound of the trumpet, and the mount smoking; and being terrified and struck with fear, they stood afar off, ^exodus-20-18-douay

19 Saying to Moses: Speak thou to us, and we will hear: let not the Lord speak to us, lest we die. ^exodus-20-19-douay

20 And Moses said to the people: Fear not; for God is come to prove you, and that the dread of him might be in you, and you should not sin. ^exodus-20-20-douay

21 And the people stood afar off. But Moses went to the dark cloud wherein God was. ^exodus-20-21-douay

22 And the Lord said to Moses: Thus shalt thou say to the children of Israel: You have seen that I have spoken to you from heaven. ^exodus-20-22-douay

23 You shall not make gods of silver, nor shall you make to yourselves gods of gold. ^exodus-20-23-douay

24 You shall make an altar of earth unto me, and you shall offer upon it your holocausts and peace offerings, your sheep and oxen, in every place where the memory of my name shall be: I will come to thee, and will bless thee. ^exodus-20-24-douay

25 And if thou make an altar of stone unto me, thou shalt not build it of hewn stones; for if thou lift up a tool upon it, it shall be defiled. ^exodus-20-25-douay

26 Thou shalt not go up by steps unto my altar, lest thy nakedness be discovered. ^exodus-20-26-douay
