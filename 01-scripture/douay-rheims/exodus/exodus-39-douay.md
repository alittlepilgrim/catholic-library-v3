# Exodus Chapter 39

All the ornaments of Aaron and his sons are made. And the whole work of the tabernacle is finished.

1 And he made, of violet and purple, scarlet and fine linen, the vestments for Aaron to wear when he ministered in the holy places, as the Lord commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-1-douay

2 So he made an ephod of gold, violet, and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine twisted linen, ^exodus-39-2-douay

3 With embroidered work, and he cut thin plates of gold, and drew them small into threads, that they might be twisted with the woof of the foresaid colours, ^exodus-39-3-douay

4 And two borders coupled one to the other in the top on either side, ^exodus-39-4-douay

5 And a girdle of the same colours, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-5-douay

6 He prepared also two onyx stones, fast set and closed in gold, and graven, by the art of a lapidary, with the names of the children of Israel: ^exodus-39-6-douay

7 And he set them in the sides of the ephod, for a memorial of the children of Israel, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-7-douay

8 He made also a rational with embroidered work, according to the work of the ephod, of gold, violet, purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine twisted linen: ^exodus-39-8-douay

9 Foursquare, double, of the measure of a span. ^exodus-39-9-douay

10 And he set four rows of precious stones in it. In the first row was a sardius, a topaz, an emerald. ^exodus-39-10-douay

11 In the second, a carbuncle, a sapphire, and a jasper. ^exodus-39-11-douay

12 In the third, a ligurius, an agate, and an amethyst. ^exodus-39-12-douay

13 In the fourth, a chrysolite, an onyx, and a beryl, set and enclosed in gold by their rows. ^exodus-39-13-douay

14 And the twelve stones, were engraved with the names of the twelve tribes of Israel, each one with its several name. ^exodus-39-14-douay

15 They made also in the rational little chains, linked one to another, of the purest gold, ^exodus-39-15-douay

16 And two hooks, and as many rings of gold. And they set the rings on either side of the rational, ^exodus-39-16-douay

17 On which rings the two golden chains should hang, which they put into the hooks that stood out in the corners of the ephod. ^exodus-39-17-douay

18 These both before and behind so answered one another, that the ephod and the rational were bound together, ^exodus-39-18-douay

19 Being fastened to the girdle, and strongly coupled with rings, which a violet fillet joined, lest they should flag loose, and be moved one from the other, as the Lord commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-19-douay

20 They made also the tunic of the ephod all of violet, ^exodus-39-20-douay

21 And a hole for the head in the upper part at the middle, and a woven border round about the hole: ^exodus-39-21-douay

22 And beneath at the feet pomegranates of violet, purple, scarlet, and fine twisted linen: ^exodus-39-22-douay

23 And little bells of the purest gold, which they put between the pomegranates at the bottom of the tunic round about: ^exodus-39-23-douay

24 To wit, a bell of gold, and a pomegranate, wherewith the high priest went adorned, when he discharged his ministry, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-24-douay

25 They made also fine linen tunics with woven work for Aaron and his sons: ^exodus-39-25-douay

26 And mitres with their little crowns of fine linen: ^exodus-39-26-douay

27 And linen breeches of fine linen: ^exodus-39-27-douay

28 And a girdle of fine twisted linen, violet, purple, and scarlet twice dyed, of embroidery work, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-28-douay

29 They made also the plate of sacred veneration of the purest gold, and they wrote on it with the engraving of a lapidary: The Holy of the Lord: ^exodus-39-29-douay

30 And they fastened it to the mitre with a violet fillet, as the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-30-douay

31 So all the work of the tabernacle and of the roof of the testimony was finished: and the children of Israel did all things which the Lord had commanded Moses. ^exodus-39-31-douay

32 And they offered the tabernacle, and the roof, and the whole furniture, the rings, the boards, the bars, the pillars and their sockets, ^exodus-39-32-douay

33 The cover of rams' skins dyed red, and the other cover of violet skins, ^exodus-39-33-douay

34 The veil, the ark, the bars, the propitiatory, ^exodus-39-34-douay

35 The table, with the vessels thereof, and the loaves of proposition: ^exodus-39-35-douay

36 The candlestick, the lamps, and the furniture of them, with the oil: ^exodus-39-36-douay

37 The altar of gold, and the ointment, and the incense of spices: ^exodus-39-37-douay

38 And the hanging in the entry of the tabernacle: ^exodus-39-38-douay

39 The altar of brass, the grate, the bars, and all the vessels thereof: the laver, with the foot thereof: the hangings of the court, and the pillars, with their sockets: ^exodus-39-39-douay

40 The hanging in the entry of the court, and the little cords, and the pins thereof. Nothing was wanting of the vessels, that were commanded to be made for the ministry of the tabernacle, and for the roof of the covenant. ^exodus-39-40-douay

41 The vestments also, which the priests, to wit, Aaron and his sons, use in the sanctuary, ^exodus-39-41-douay

42 The children of Israel offered, as the Lord had commanded. ^exodus-39-42-douay

43 And when Moses saw all things finished, he blessed them. ^exodus-39-43-douay
