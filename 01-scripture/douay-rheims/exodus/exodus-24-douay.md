# Exodus Chapter 24

Moses writes his law; and after offering sacrifices, sprinkles the blood of the testament upon the people: then goes up the mountain which God covers with a fiery cloud.

1 And he said to Moses: Come up to the Lord, thou, and Aaron, Nadab and Abiu, and seventy of the ancients of Israel, and you shall adore afar off. ^exodus-24-1-douay

2 And Moses alone shall come up to the Lord, but they shall not come nigh; neither shall the people come up with him. ^exodus-24-2-douay

3 So Moses came and told the people all the words of the Lord, and all the judgments: and all the people answered with one voice: We will do all the words of the Lord, which he hath spoken. ^exodus-24-3-douay

4 And Moses wrote all the words of the Lord: and rising in the morning, he built an altar at the foot of the mount, and twelve titles according to the twelve tribes of Israel. **Titles...** That is, pillars. ^exodus-24-4-douay

5 And he sent young men of the children of Israel, and they offered holocausts, and sacrificed pacific victims of calves to the Lord. **Holocausts...** Whole burnt offerings, in which the whole sacrifice was consumed with fire upon the altar. ^exodus-24-5-douay

6 Then Moses took half of the blood, and put it into bowls; and the rest he poured upon the altar. ^exodus-24-6-douay

7 And taking the book of the covenant, he read it in the hearing of the people: and they said: All things that the Lord hath spoken, we will do, we will be obedient. ^exodus-24-7-douay

8 And he took the blood and sprinkled it upon the people, and he said: This is the blood of the covenant, which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words. ^exodus-24-8-douay

9 Then Moses and Aaron, Nadab and Abiu, and seventy of the ancients of Israel went up: ^exodus-24-9-douay

10 And they saw the God of Israel: and under his feet as it were a work of sapphire stone, and as the heaven, when clear. ^exodus-24-10-douay

11 Neither did he lay his hand upon those of the children of Israel, that retired afar off, and they saw God, and they did eat and drink. ^exodus-24-11-douay

12 And the Lord said to Moses: Come up to me into the mount, and be there; and I will give thee tables of stone, and the law, and the commandments which I have written; that thou mayest teach them. ^exodus-24-12-douay

13 Moses rose up, and his minister Joshua: and Moses going up into the mount of God, ^exodus-24-13-douay

14 Said to the ancients: Wait ye here till we return to you. You have Aaron and Hur with you: if any question shall arise, you shall refer it to them. ^exodus-24-14-douay

15 And when Moses was gone up, a cloud covered the mount. ^exodus-24-15-douay

16 And the glory of the Lord dwelt upon Sinai, covering it with a cloud six days: and the seventh day he called him out of the midst of the cloud. ^exodus-24-16-douay

17 And the sight of the glory of the Lord, was like a burning fire upon the top of the mount, in the eyes of the children of Israel. ^exodus-24-17-douay

18 And Moses entering into the midst of the cloud, went up into the mountain: And he was there forty days and forty nights. ^exodus-24-18-douay
