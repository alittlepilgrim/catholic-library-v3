# Exodus Chapter 30

The altar of incense: money to be gathered for the use of the tabernacle: the brazen laver: the holy oil of unction, and the composition of the perfume.

1 Thou shalt make also an altar to burn incense, of setim wood. **An altar to burn incense...** This burning of incense was an emblem of prayer, ascending to God from an inflamed heart. See [[psalm-140-douay|Psalm 140:2]]; [[revelation-05-douay|Apocalypse 5:8]], and [[revelation-08-douay|8:4]]. ^exodus-30-1-douay

2 It shall be a cubit in length, and another in breadth, that is, four square, and two in height. Horns shall go out of the same. ^exodus-30-2-douay

3 And thou shalt overlay it with the purest gold, as well the grate thereof, as the walls round about, and the horns. And thou shalt make to it a crown of gold round about, ^exodus-30-3-douay

4 And two golden rings under the crown on either side, that the bars may be put into them, and the altar be carried. ^exodus-30-4-douay

5 And thou shalt make the bars also of setim wood, and shalt overlay them with gold. ^exodus-30-5-douay

6 And thou shalt set the altar over against the veil, that hangeth before the ark of the testimony before the propitiatory wherewith the testimony is covered, where I will speak to thee. ^exodus-30-6-douay

7 And Aaron shall burn sweet smelling incense upon it in the morning. When he shall dress the lamps, he shall burn it: ^exodus-30-7-douay

8 And when he shall place them in the evening, he shall burn an everlasting incense before the Lord throughout your generations. ^exodus-30-8-douay

9 You shall not offer upon it incense of another composition, nor oblation, and victim, neither shall you offer libations. ^exodus-30-9-douay

10 And Aaron shall pray upon the horns thereof once a year, with the blood of that which was offered for sin; and shall make atonement upon it in your generations. It shall be most holy to the Lord. ^exodus-30-10-douay

11 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^exodus-30-11-douay

12 When thou shalt take the sum of the children of Israel, according to their number, every one of them shall give a price for their souls to the Lord, and there shall be no scourge among them, when they shall be reckoned. ^exodus-30-12-douay

13 And this shall every one give that passeth at the naming, half a sicle according to the standard of the temple. A sicle hath twenty obols. Half a sicle shall be offered to the Lord. **Half a sicle...** A sicle or shekel of silver, (which was also called a stater), according to the standard or weight of the sanctuary, which was the most just and exact, was half an ounce of silver, that is, about half a crown of English money. The obol, or gerah, was about three halfpence. ^exodus-30-13-douay

14 He that is counted in the number from twenty years and upwards, shall give the price. ^exodus-30-14-douay

15 The rich man shall not add to half a sicle, and the poor man shall diminish nothing. ^exodus-30-15-douay

16 And the money received, which was contributed by the children of Israel, thou shalt deliver unto the uses of the tabernacle of the testimony, that it may be a memorial of them before the Lord, and he may be merciful to their souls. ^exodus-30-16-douay

17 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^exodus-30-17-douay

18 Thou shalt make also a brazen laver with its foot to wash in: and thou shalt set it between the tabernacle of the testimony and the altar. And water being put into it: ^exodus-30-18-douay

19 Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and feet in it: ^exodus-30-19-douay

20 When they are going into the tabernacle of the testimony, and when they are to come to the altar, to offer on it incense to the Lord, ^exodus-30-20-douay

21 Lest perhaps they die. It shall be an everlasting law to him, and to his seed by successions. ^exodus-30-21-douay

22 And the Lord spoke to Moses, ^exodus-30-22-douay

23 Saying: Take spices, of principal and chosen myrrh five hundred sicles, and of cinnamon half so much; that is, two hundred and fifty sicles, of calamus in like manner two hundred and fifty, ^exodus-30-23-douay

24 And of cassia five hundred sicles by the weight of the sanctuary, of oil of olives the measure hin: ^exodus-30-24-douay

25 And thou shalt make the holy oil of unction, an ointment compounded after the art of the perfumer, ^exodus-30-25-douay

26 And therewith thou shalt anoint the tabernacle of the testimony, and the ark of the testament, ^exodus-30-26-douay

27 And the table with the vessels thereof, the candlestick and furniture thereof, the altars of incense, ^exodus-30-27-douay

28 And of holocaust, and all the furniture that belongeth to the service of them. ^exodus-30-28-douay

29 And thou shalt sanctify all, and they shall be most holy: he that shall touch them shall be sanctified. ^exodus-30-29-douay

30 Thou shalt anoint Aaron and his sons, and shalt sanctify them, that they may do the office of priesthood unto me. ^exodus-30-30-douay

31 And thou shalt say to the children of Israel: This oil of unction shall be holy unto me throughout your generations. ^exodus-30-31-douay

32 The flesh of man shall not be anointed therewith, and you shall make none other of the same composition, because it is sanctified, and shall be holy unto you. ^exodus-30-32-douay

33 What man soever shall compound such, and shall give thereof to a stranger, he shall be cut off from his people. ^exodus-30-33-douay

34 And the Lord said to Moses: Take unto thee spices, stacte, and onycha, galbanum of sweet savour, and the clearest frankincense, all shall be of equal weight. ^exodus-30-34-douay

35 And thou shalt make incense compounded by the work of the perfumer, well tempered together, and pure, and most worthy of sanctification. ^exodus-30-35-douay

36 And when thou hast beaten all into very small powder, thou shalt set of it before the tabernacle of the testimony, in the place where I will appear to thee. Most holy shall this incense be unto you. ^exodus-30-36-douay

37 You shall not make such a composition for your own uses, because it is holy to the Lord. ^exodus-30-37-douay

38 What man soever shall make the like, to enjoy the smell thereof, he shall perish out of his people. ^exodus-30-38-douay
