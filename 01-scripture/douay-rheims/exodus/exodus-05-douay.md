# Exodus Chapter 5

Pharao refuses to let the people go. They are more oppressed.

1 After these things, Moses and Aaron went in, and said to Pharao: Thus saith the Lord God of Israel: Let my people go, that they may sacrifice to me in the desert. ^exodus-05-1-douay

2 But he answered: Who is the Lord, that I should hear his voice, and let Israel go? I know not the Lord, neither will I let Israel go. ^exodus-05-2-douay

3 And they said: The God of the Hebrews hath called us, to go three days' journey into the wilderness, and to sacrifice to the Lord our God; lest a pestilence or the sword fall upon us. ^exodus-05-3-douay

4 The king of Egypt said to them: Why do you Moses and Aaron draw off the people from their works? Get you gone to your burdens. ^exodus-05-4-douay

5 And Pharao said: The people of the land are numerous; you see that the multitude is increased; how much more if you give them rest from their works? ^exodus-05-5-douay

6 Therefore he commanded the same day the overseers of the works, and the task-masters of the people, saying: ^exodus-05-6-douay

7 You shall give straw no more to the people to make brick, as before; but let them go and gather straw. ^exodus-05-7-douay

8 And you shall lay upon them the task of bricks, which they did before; neither shall you diminish any thing thereof, for they are idle, and therefore they cry, saying: Let us go and sacrifice to our God. ^exodus-05-8-douay

9 Let them be oppressed with works, and let them fulfil them; that they may not regard lying words. ^exodus-05-9-douay

10 And the overseers of the works, and the taskmasters, went out and said to the people: Thus saith Pharao: I allow you no straw; ^exodus-05-10-douay

11 Go, and gather it where you can find it; neither shall any thing of your work be diminished. ^exodus-05-11-douay

12 And the people was scattered through all the land of Egypt to gather straw. ^exodus-05-12-douay

13 And the overseers of the works pressed them, saying: Fulfil your work every day, as before ye were wont to do, when straw was given you. ^exodus-05-13-douay

14 And they that were over the works of the children of Israel, were scourged by Pharao's taskmasters, saying: Why have you not made up the task of bricks, both yesterday and to day, as before? ^exodus-05-14-douay

15 And the officers of the children of Israel came, and cried out to Pharao, saying: Why dealest thou so with thy servants? ^exodus-05-15-douay

16 Straw is not given us, and bricks are required of us as before; behold we, thy servants, are beaten with whips, and thy people is unjustly dealt withal. ^exodus-05-16-douay

17 And he said: You are idle, and therefore you say: Let us go and sacrifice to the Lord. ^exodus-05-17-douay

18 Go therefore and work: straw shall not be given you, and you shall deliver the accustomed number of bricks. ^exodus-05-18-douay

19 And the officers of the children of Israel saw that they were in evil case, because it was said to them: There shall not a whit be diminished of the bricks for every day. ^exodus-05-19-douay

20 And they met Moses and Aaron, who stood over against them as they came out from Pharao: ^exodus-05-20-douay

21 And they said to them: The Lord see and judge, because you have, made our savour to stink before Pharao and his servants, and you have given him a sword, to kill us. ^exodus-05-21-douay

22 And Moses returned to the Lord, and said: Lord, why hast thou afflicted this people? wherefore hast thou sent me? ^exodus-05-22-douay

23 For since the time that I went in to Pharao to speak in thy name, he hath afflicted thy people: and thou hast not delivered them. ^exodus-05-23-douay
