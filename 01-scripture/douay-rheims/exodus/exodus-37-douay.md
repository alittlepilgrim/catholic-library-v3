# Exodus Chapter 37

Beseleel makes the ark: the propitiatory, and cherubims, the table, the candlestick, the lamps, and the altar of incense, and compounds the incense.

1 And Beseleel made also, the ark of setim wood: it was two cubits and a half in length, and a cubit and a half in breadth, and the height was of one cubit and a half: and he overlaid it with the purest gold within and without. ^exodus-37-1-douay

2 And he made to it a crown of gold round about, ^exodus-37-2-douay

3 Casting four rings of gold at the four corners thereof: two rings in one side, and two in the other. ^exodus-37-3-douay

4 And he made bars of setim wood, which he overlaid with gold, ^exodus-37-4-douay

5 And he put them into the rings that were at the sides of the ark to carry it. ^exodus-37-5-douay

6 He made also the propitiatory, that is, the oracle, of the purest gold, two cubits and a half in length, and a cubit and a half in breadth. ^exodus-37-6-douay

7 Two cherubims also of beaten gold, which he set on the two sides of the propitiatory: ^exodus-37-7-douay

8 One cherub in the top of one side, and the other cherub in the top of the other side: two cherubims at the two ends of the propitiatory, ^exodus-37-8-douay

9 Spreading their wings, and covering the propitiatory, and looking one towards the other, and towards it. ^exodus-37-9-douay

10 He made also the table of setim wood, in length two cubits, and in breadth one cubit, and in height it was a cubit and a half. ^exodus-37-10-douay

11 And he overlaid it with the finest gold, and he made to it a golden ledge round about, ^exodus-37-11-douay

12 And to the ledge itself he made a polished crown of gold, of four fingers breadth, and upon the same another golden crown. ^exodus-37-12-douay

13 And he cast four rings of gold, which he put in the four corners at each foot of the table, ^exodus-37-13-douay

14 Over against the crown: and he put the bars into them, that the table might be carried. ^exodus-37-14-douay

15 The bars also themselves he made of setim wood, and overlaid them with gold. ^exodus-37-15-douay

16 And the vessels for the divers uses of the table, dishes, bowls, and cups, and censers of pure gold, wherein the libations are to be offered. ^exodus-37-16-douay

17 He made also the candlestick of beaten work of the finest gold, from the shaft whereof its branches, its cups, and bowls, and lilies came out: ^exodus-37-17-douay

18 Six on the two sides: three branches on one side, and three on the other. ^exodus-37-18-douay

19 Three cups in manner of a nut on each branch, and bowls withal and lilies: and three cups of the fashion of a nut in another branch, and bowls withal and lilies. The work of the six branches, that went out from the shaft of the candlestick was equal. ^exodus-37-19-douay

20 And in the shaft itself were four cups after the manner of a nut, and bowls withal at every one, and lilies: ^exodus-37-20-douay

21 And bowls under two branches in three places, which together made six branches going out from one shaft. ^exodus-37-21-douay

22 So both the bowls, and the branches were of the same, all beaten work of the purest gold. ^exodus-37-22-douay

23 He made also the seven lamps with their snuffers, and the vessels where the snuffings were to be put out, of the purest gold. ^exodus-37-23-douay

24 The candlestick with all the vessels thereof weighed a talent of gold. ^exodus-37-24-douay

25 He made also the altar of incense of setim wood, being a cubit on every side foursquare, and in height two cubits: from the corners of which went out horns. ^exodus-37-25-douay

26 And he overlaid it with the purest gold, with its grate, and the sides, and the horns. ^exodus-37-26-douay

27 And he made to it a crown of gold round about, and two golden rings under the crown at each side, that the bars might be put into them, and the altar be carried. ^exodus-37-27-douay

28 And the bars themselves he made also of setim wood, and overlaid them with plates of gold. ^exodus-37-28-douay

29 He compounded also the oil for the ointment of sanctification, and incense of the purest spices, according to the work of a perfumer. ^exodus-37-29-douay
