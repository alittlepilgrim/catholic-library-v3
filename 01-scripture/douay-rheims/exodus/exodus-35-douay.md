# Exodus Chapter 35

The sabbath. Offerings for making the tabernacle. Beseleel and Ooliab are called to the work.

1 And all the multitude of the children of Israel being gathered together, he said to them: These are the things which the Lord hath commanded to be done: ^exodus-35-1-douay

2 Six days you shall do work; the seventh day shall be holy unto you, the sabbath and the rest of the Lord: he that shall do any work on it, shall be put to death. ^exodus-35-2-douay

3 You shall kindle no fire in any of your habitations on the sabbath day. ^exodus-35-3-douay

4 And Moses said to all the assembly of the children of Israel: This is the word the Lord hath commanded, saying: ^exodus-35-4-douay

5 Set aside with you firstfruits to the Lord. Let every one that is willing and hath a ready heart, offer them to the Lord: gold, and silver, and brass, ^exodus-35-5-douay

6 Violet and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine linen, goats' hair, ^exodus-35-6-douay

7 And rams' skins dyed red, and violet coloured skins, setim wood, ^exodus-35-7-douay

8 And oil to maintain lights, and to make ointment, and most sweet incense, ^exodus-35-8-douay

9 Onyx stones, and precious stones, for the adorning of the ephod and the rational. ^exodus-35-9-douay

10 Whosoever of you is wise, let him come, and make that which the Lord hath commanded: ^exodus-35-10-douay

11 To wit, the tabernacle, and the roof thereof, and the cover, the rings, and the board-work with the bars, the pillars and the sockets: ^exodus-35-11-douay

12 The ark and the staves, the propitiatory, and the veil that is drawn before it: ^exodus-35-12-douay

13 The table with the bars and the vessels, and the loaves of proposition: ^exodus-35-13-douay

14 The candlestick to bear up the lights, the vessels thereof and the lamps, and the oil for the nourishing of fires: ^exodus-35-14-douay

15 The altar of incense, and the bars, and the oil of unction, and the incense of spices: the hanging at the door of the tabernacle: ^exodus-35-15-douay

16 The altar of holocaust, and its grate of brass, with the bars and vessels thereof: the laver and its foot: ^exodus-35-16-douay

17 The curtains of the court, with the pillars and the sockets, the hanging in the doors of the entry. ^exodus-35-17-douay

18 The pins of the tabernacle, and of the court, with their little cords: ^exodus-35-18-douay

19 The vestments that are to be used in the ministry of the sanctuary, the vesture of Aaron the high priest, and of his sons, to do the office of priesthood to me. ^exodus-35-19-douay

20 And all the multitude of the children of Israel going out from the presence of Moses, ^exodus-35-20-douay

21 Offered firstfruits to the Lord with a most ready and devout mind, to make the work of the tabernacle of the testimony. Whatever was necessary to the service and to the holy vestments, ^exodus-35-21-douay

22 Both men and women gave bracelets and earrings, rings and tablets: every vessel of gold was set aside to be offered to the Lord. ^exodus-35-22-douay

23 If any man had violet, and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, fine linen and goats' hair, ramskins dyed red, and violet coloured skins, ^exodus-35-23-douay

24 Metal of silver and brass, they offered it to the Lord, and setim wood for divers uses. ^exodus-35-24-douay

25 The skilful women also gave such things as they had spun, violet, purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, ^exodus-35-25-douay

26 And goats' hair, giving all of their own accord. ^exodus-35-26-douay

27 But the princes offered onyx stones, and precious stones, for the ephod and the rational, ^exodus-35-27-douay

28 And spices and oil for the lights, and for the preparing of ointment, and to make the incense of most sweet savour. ^exodus-35-28-douay

29 All, both men and women, with devout mind offered gifts, that the works might be done which the Lord had commanded by the hand of Moses. All the children of Israel dedicated voluntary offerings to the Lord. ^exodus-35-29-douay

30 And Moses said to the children of Israel: Behold, the Lord hath called by name Beseleel, the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Juda, ^exodus-35-30-douay

31 And hath filled him with the spirit of God, with wisdom and understanding, and knowledge, and all learning, ^exodus-35-31-douay

32 To devise and to work in gold and silver and brass, ^exodus-35-32-douay

33 And in engraving stones, and in carpenters' work. Whatsoever can be devised artificially, ^exodus-35-33-douay

34 He hath given in his heart: Ooliab also, the son of Achisamech, of the tribe of Dan: ^exodus-35-34-douay

35 Both of them hath he instructed with wisdom, to do carpenters' work, and tapestry, and embroidery in blue and purple, and scarlet twice dyed, and fine linen, and to weave all things, and to invent all new things. ^exodus-35-35-douay
