# Proverbs Chapter 14

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 A wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish will pull down with her hands that also which is built. ^proverbs-14-1-douay

2 He that walketh in the right way, and feareth God, is despised by him that goeth by an infamous way. ^proverbs-14-2-douay

3 In the mouth of a fool is the rod of pride: but the lips of the wise preserve them. ^proverbs-14-3-douay

4 Where there are no oxen, the crib is empty: but where there is much corn, there the strength of the ox is manifest. ^proverbs-14-4-douay

5 A faithful witness will not lie: but a deceitful witness uttereth a lie. ^proverbs-14-5-douay

6 A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not: the learning of the wise is easy. ^proverbs-14-6-douay

7 Go against a foolish man, and he knoweth not the lips of prudence. ^proverbs-14-7-douay

8 The wisdom of a discreet man is to understand his way: and the imprudence of fools erreth. ^proverbs-14-8-douay

9 A fool will laugh at sin, but among the just grace shall abide. ^proverbs-14-9-douay

10 The heart that knoweth the bitterness of his own soul, in his joy the stranger shall not intermeddle. ^proverbs-14-10-douay

11 The house of the wicked shall be destroyed: but the tabernacles of the just shall flourish. ^proverbs-14-11-douay

12 There is a way which seemeth just to a man: but the ends thereof lead to death. ^proverbs-14-12-douay

13 Laughter shall be mingled with sorrow, and mourning taketh hold of the ends of joy. ^proverbs-14-13-douay

14 A fool shall be filled with his own ways, and the good man shall be above him. ^proverbs-14-14-douay

15 The innocent believeth every word: the discreet man considereth his steps. No good shall come to the deceitful son: but the wise servant shall prosper in his dealings, and his way shall be made straight. ^proverbs-14-15-douay

16 A wise man feareth, and declineth from evil: the fool leapeth over, and is confident. ^proverbs-14-16-douay

17 The impatient man shall work folly: and the crafty man is hateful. ^proverbs-14-17-douay

18 The childish shall possess folly, and the prudent shall look for knowledge. ^proverbs-14-18-douay

19 The evil shall fall down before the good: and the wicked before the gates of the just. ^proverbs-14-19-douay

20 The poor man shall be hateful even to his own neighbour: but the friends of the rich are many. ^proverbs-14-20-douay

21 He that despiseth his neighbour, sinneth: but he that showeth mercy to the poor, shall be blessed. He that believeth in the Lord, loveth mercy. ^proverbs-14-21-douay

22 They err that work evil: but mercy and truth prepare good things. ^proverbs-14-22-douay

23 In much work there shall be abundance: but where there are many words, there is oftentimes want. ^proverbs-14-23-douay

24 The crown of the wise, is their riches: the folly of fools, imprudence. ^proverbs-14-24-douay

25 A faithful witness delivereth souls: and the double dealer uttereth lies. ^proverbs-14-25-douay

26 In the fear of the Lord is confidence of strength, and there shall be hope for his children. ^proverbs-14-26-douay

27 The fear of the Lord is a fountain of life, to decline from the ruin of death. ^proverbs-14-27-douay

28 In the multitude of people is the dignity of the king: and in the small number of the people the dishonour of the prince. ^proverbs-14-28-douay

29 He that is patient, is governed with much wisdom: but he that is impatient, exalteth his folly. ^proverbs-14-29-douay

30 Soundness of heart is the life of the flesh: but envy is the rottenness of the bones. ^proverbs-14-30-douay

31 He that oppresseth the poor, upbraideth his maker: but he that hath pity on the poor, honoureth him. ^proverbs-14-31-douay

32 The wicked man shall be driven out in his wickedness: but the just hath hope in his death. ^proverbs-14-32-douay

33 In the heart of the prudent resteth wisdom, and it shall instruct all the ignorant. ^proverbs-14-33-douay

34 Justice exalteth a nation: but sin maketh nations miserable. ^proverbs-14-34-douay

35 A wise servant is acceptable to the king: he that is good for nothing shall feel his anger. ^proverbs-14-35-douay
