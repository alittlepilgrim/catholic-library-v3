# Proverbs Chapter 21

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 As the divisions of waters, so the heart of the king is in the hand of the Lord: whithersoever he will, he shall turn it. ^proverbs-21-1-douay

2 Every way of a man seemeth right to himself: but the Lord weigheth the hearts. ^proverbs-21-2-douay

3 To do mercy and judgment, pleaseth the Lord more than victims. ^proverbs-21-3-douay

4 Haughtiness of the eyes is the enlarging of the heart: the lamp of the wicked is sin. ^proverbs-21-4-douay

5 The thoughts of the industrious always bring forth abundance: but every sluggard is always in want. ^proverbs-21-5-douay

6 He that gathereth treasures by a lying tongue, is vain and foolish, and shall stumble upon the snares of death. ^proverbs-21-6-douay

7 The robberies of the wicked shall be their downfall, because they would not do judgment. ^proverbs-21-7-douay

8 The perverse way of a man is strange: but as for him that is pure, his work is right. ^proverbs-21-8-douay

9 It is better to sit in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman, and in a common house. ^proverbs-21-9-douay

10 The soul of the wicked desireth evil, he will not have pity on his neighbour. ^proverbs-21-10-douay

11 When a pestilent man is punished, the little one will be wiser: and if he follow the wise, he will receive knowledge. ^proverbs-21-11-douay

12 The just considereth seriously the house of the wicked, that he may withdraw the wicked from evil. ^proverbs-21-12-douay

13 He that stoppeth his ear against the cry of the poor, shall also cry himself, and shall not be heard. ^proverbs-21-13-douay

14 A secret present quencheth anger: and a gift in the bosom, the greatest wrath. ^proverbs-21-14-douay

15 It is joy to the just to do judgment: and dread to them that work iniquity. ^proverbs-21-15-douay

16 A man that shall wander out of the way of doctrine, shall abide in the company of the giants. ^proverbs-21-16-douay

17 He that loveth good cheer, shall be in want: he that loveth wine, and fat things, shall not be rich. ^proverbs-21-17-douay

18 The wicked is delivered up for the just: and the unjust for the righteous. ^proverbs-21-18-douay

19 It is better to dwell in a wilderness, than with a quarrelsome and passionate woman. ^proverbs-21-19-douay

20 There is a treasure to be desired, and oil in the dwelling of the just: and the foolish man shall spend it. ^proverbs-21-20-douay

21 He that followeth justice and mercy, shall find life, justice, and glory. ^proverbs-21-21-douay

22 The wise man hath scaled the city of the strong, and hath cast down the strength of the confidence thereof. ^proverbs-21-22-douay

23 He that keepeth his mouth and his tongue, keepeth his soul from distress. ^proverbs-21-23-douay

24 The proud and the arrogant is called ignorant, who in anger worketh pride. ^proverbs-21-24-douay

25 Desires kill the slothful: for his hands have refused to work at all. ^proverbs-21-25-douay

26 He longeth and desireth all the day: but he that is just, will give, and will not cease. ^proverbs-21-26-douay

27 The sacrifices of the wicked are abominable, because they are offered of wickedness. ^proverbs-21-27-douay

28 A lying witness shall perish: an obedient man shall speak of victory. ^proverbs-21-28-douay

29 The wicked man impudently hardeneth his face: but he that is righteous, correcteth his way. ^proverbs-21-29-douay

30 There is no wisdom, there is no prudence, there is no counsel against the Lord. ^proverbs-21-30-douay

31 The horse is prepared for the day of battle: but the Lord giveth safety. ^proverbs-21-31-douay
