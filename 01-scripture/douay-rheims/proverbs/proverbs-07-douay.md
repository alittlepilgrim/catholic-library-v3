# Proverbs Chapter 7

The love of wisdom is the best preservative from being led astray by temptation.

1 My son, keep my words, and lay up my precepts with thee. Son, ^proverbs-07-1-douay

2 Keep my commandments, and thou shalt live: and my law as the apple of thy eye: ^proverbs-07-2-douay

3 Bind it upon thy fingers, write it upon the tables of thy heart. ^proverbs-07-3-douay

4 Say to wisdom: Thou art my sister: and call prudence thy friend, ^proverbs-07-4-douay

5 That she may keep thee from the woman that is not thine, and from the stranger who sweeteneth her words. ^proverbs-07-5-douay

6 For I looked out of the window of my house through the lattice, ^proverbs-07-6-douay

7 And I see little ones, I behold a foolish young man, ^proverbs-07-7-douay

8 Who passeth through the street by the corner, and goeth nigh the way of her house, ^proverbs-07-8-douay

9 In the dark when it grows late, in the darkness and obscurity of the night. ^proverbs-07-9-douay

10 And behold a woman meeteth him in harlot's attire, prepared to deceive souls: talkative and wandering, ^proverbs-07-10-douay

11 Not bearing to be quiet, not able to abide still at home, ^proverbs-07-11-douay

12 Now abroad, now in the streets, now lying in wait near the corners. ^proverbs-07-12-douay

13 And catching the young man, she kisseth him, and with an impudent face, flattereth, saying: ^proverbs-07-13-douay

14 I vowed victims for prosperity, this day I have paid my vows. ^proverbs-07-14-douay

15 Therefore I am come out to meet thee, desirous to see thee, and I have found thee. ^proverbs-07-15-douay

16 I have woven my bed with cords, I have covered it with painted tapestry, brought from Egypt. ^proverbs-07-16-douay

17 I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon. ^proverbs-07-17-douay

18 Come, let us be inebriated with the breasts, and let us enjoy the desired embraces, till the day appear. ^proverbs-07-18-douay

19 For my husband is not at home, he is gone a very long journey. ^proverbs-07-19-douay

20 He took with him a bag of money: he will return home the day of the full moon. ^proverbs-07-20-douay

21 She entangled him with many words, and drew him away with the flattery of her lips. ^proverbs-07-21-douay

22 Immediately he followeth her as an ox led to be a victim, and as a lamb playing the wanton, and not knowing that he is drawn like a fool to bonds, ^proverbs-07-22-douay

23 Till the arrow pierce his liver: as if a bird should make haste to the snare, and knoweth not that his life is in danger. ^proverbs-07-23-douay

24 Now, therefore, my son, hear me, and attend to the words of my mouth. ^proverbs-07-24-douay

25 Let not thy mind be drawn away in her ways: neither be thou deceived with her paths. ^proverbs-07-25-douay

26 For she hath cast down many wounded, and the strongest have been slain by her. ^proverbs-07-26-douay

27 Her house is the way to hell, reaching even to the inner chambers of death. ^proverbs-07-27-douay
