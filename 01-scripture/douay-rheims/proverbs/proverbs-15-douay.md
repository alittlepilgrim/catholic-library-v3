# Proverbs Chapter 15

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 A mild answer breaketh wrath: but a harsh word stirreth up fury. ^proverbs-15-1-douay

2 The tongue of the wise adorneth knowledge: but the mouth of fools bubbleth out folly. ^proverbs-15-2-douay

3 The eyes of the Lord in every place behold the good and the evil. ^proverbs-15-3-douay

4 A peaceable tongue is a tree of life: but that which is immoderate, shall crush the spirit. ^proverbs-15-4-douay

5 A fool laugheth at the instruction of his father: but he that regardeth reproofs shall become prudent. In abundant justice there is the greatest strength: but the devices of the wicked shall be rooted out. ^proverbs-15-5-douay

6 The house of the just is very much strength: and in the fruits of the wicked is trouble. ^proverbs-15-6-douay

7 The lips of the wise shall disperse knowledge: the heart of fools shall be unlike. ^proverbs-15-7-douay

8 The victims of the wicked are abominable to the Lord: the vows of the just are acceptable. ^proverbs-15-8-douay

9 The way of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: he that followeth justice is beloved by him. ^proverbs-15-9-douay

10 Instruction is grievous to him that forsaketh the way of life: he that hateth reproof shall die. ^proverbs-15-10-douay

11 Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more the hearts of the children of men? ^proverbs-15-11-douay

12 A corrupt man loveth not one that reproveth him: nor will he go to the wise. ^proverbs-15-12-douay

13 A glad heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by grief of mind the spirit is cast down. ^proverbs-15-13-douay

14 The heart of the wise seeketh instruction: and the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness. ^proverbs-15-14-douay

15 All the days of the poor are evil: a secure mind is like a continual feast. ^proverbs-15-15-douay

16 Better is a little with the fear of the Lord, than great treasures without content. ^proverbs-15-16-douay

17 It is better to be invited to herbs with love, than to a fatted calf with hatred. ^proverbs-15-17-douay

18 A passionate man stirreth up strifes: he that is patient appeaseth those that are stirred up. ^proverbs-15-18-douay

19 The way of the slothful is as a hedge of thorns: the way of the just is without offence. ^proverbs-15-19-douay

20 A wise son maketh a father joyful: but the foolish man despiseth his mother. ^proverbs-15-20-douay

21 Folly is joy to the fool: and the wise man maketh straight his steps. ^proverbs-15-21-douay

22 Designs are brought to nothing where there is no counsel: but where there are many counsellors, they are established. ^proverbs-15-22-douay

23 A man rejoiceth in the sentence of his mouth: and a word in due time is best. ^proverbs-15-23-douay

24 The path of life is above for the wise, that he may decline from the lowest hell. ^proverbs-15-24-douay

25 The Lord will destroy the house of the proud: and will strengthen the borders of the widow. ^proverbs-15-25-douay

26 Evil thoughts are an abomination to the Lord: and pure words most beautiful shall be confirmed by him. ^proverbs-15-26-douay

27 He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house: but he that hateth bribes shall live. By mercy and faith sins are purged away: and by the fear of the Lord every one declineth from evil. ^proverbs-15-27-douay

28 The mind of the just studieth obedience: the mouth of the wicked overfloweth with evils. ^proverbs-15-28-douay

29 The Lord is far from the wicked: and he will hear the prayers of the just. ^proverbs-15-29-douay

30 The light of the eyes rejoiceth the soul: a good name maketh the bones fat. ^proverbs-15-30-douay

31 The ear that heareth the reproofs of life, shall abide in the midst of the wise. ^proverbs-15-31-douay

32 He that rejecteth instruction, despiseth his own soul: but he that yieldeth to reproof, possesseth understanding. ^proverbs-15-32-douay

33 The fear of the Lord is the lesson of wisdom: and humility goeth before glory. ^proverbs-15-33-douay
