# Proverbs Chapter 24

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 Seek not to be like evil men, neither desire to be with them: ^proverbs-24-1-douay

2 Because their mind studieth robberies, and their lips speak deceits. ^proverbs-24-2-douay

3 By wisdom the house shall be built, and by prudence it shall be strengthened. ^proverbs-24-3-douay

4 By instruction the storerooms shall be filled with all precious and most beautiful wealth. ^proverbs-24-4-douay

5 A wise man is strong: and a knowing man, stout and valiant. ^proverbs-24-5-douay

6 Because war is managed by due ordering: and there shall be safety where there are many counsels. ^proverbs-24-6-douay

7 Wisdom is too high for a fool; in the gate he shall not open his mouth. ^proverbs-24-7-douay

8 He that deviseth to do evils, shall be called a fool. ^proverbs-24-8-douay

9 The thought of a fool is sin: and the detractor is the abomination of men. ^proverbs-24-9-douay

10 If thou lose hope, being weary in the day of distress, thy strength shall be diminished. ^proverbs-24-10-douay

11 Deliver them that are led to death: and those that are drawn to death, forbear not to deliver. ^proverbs-24-11-douay

12 If thou say: I have not strength enough: he that seeth into the heart, he understandeth, and nothing deceiveth the keeper of thy soul, and he shall render to a man according to his works. ^proverbs-24-12-douay

13 Eat honey, my son, because it is good, and the honeycomb most sweet to thy throat. ^proverbs-24-13-douay

14 So also is the doctrine of wisdom to thy soul: which when thou hast found, thou shalt have hope in the end, and thy hope shall not perish. ^proverbs-24-14-douay

15 Lie not in wait, nor seek after wickedness in the house of the just, nor spoil his rest. ^proverbs-24-15-douay

16 For a just man shall fall seven times, and shall rise again: but the wicked shall fall down into evil. ^proverbs-24-16-douay

17 When thy enemy shall fall, be not glad, and in his ruin let not thy heart rejoice: ^proverbs-24-17-douay

18 Lest the Lord see, and it displease him, and he turn away his wrath from him. ^proverbs-24-18-douay

19 Contend not with the wicked, nor seek to be like the ungodly. ^proverbs-24-19-douay

20 For evil men have no hope of things to come, and the lamp of the wicked shall be put out. ^proverbs-24-20-douay

21 My son, fear the Lord, and the king: and have nothing to do with detractors. ^proverbs-24-21-douay

22 For their destruction shall rise suddenly: and who knoweth the ruin of both? ^proverbs-24-22-douay

23 These things also to the wise: ^proverbs-24-23-douay

It is not good to have respect to persons in judgment.

24 They that say to the wicked man: Thou art just: shall be cursed by the people, and the tribes shall abhor them. ^proverbs-24-24-douay

25 They that rebuke him shall be praised: and a blessing shall come upon them. ^proverbs-24-25-douay

26 He shall kiss the lips, who answereth right words. ^proverbs-24-26-douay

27 Prepare thy work without, and diligently till thy ground: that afterward thou mayst build thy house. ^proverbs-24-27-douay

28 Be not witness without cause against thy neighbour: and deceive not any man with thy lips. ^proverbs-24-28-douay

29 Say not: I will do to him as he hath done to me: I will render to every one according to his work. ^proverbs-24-29-douay

30 I passed by the field of the slothful man, and by the vineyard of the foolish man: ^proverbs-24-30-douay

31 And behold it was all filled with nettles, and thorns had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall was broken down. ^proverbs-24-31-douay

32 Which when I had seen, I laid it up in my heart, and by the example I received instruction. ^proverbs-24-32-douay

33 Thou wilt sleep a little, said I, thou wilt slumber a little, thou wilt fold thy hands a little to rest. ^proverbs-24-33-douay

34 And poverty shall come to thee as a runner, and beggary as an armed man. ^proverbs-24-34-douay
