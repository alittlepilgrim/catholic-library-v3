# Proverbs Chapter 19

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 Better is the poor man, that walketh in his simplicity, than a rich man that is perverse in his lips and unwise. ^proverbs-19-1-douay

2 Where there is no knowledge of the soul, there is no good: and he that is hasty with his feet shall stumble. ^proverbs-19-2-douay

3 The folly of a man supplanteth his steps: and he fretteth in his mind against God. ^proverbs-19-3-douay

4 Riches make many friends: but from the poor man, even they whom he had, depart. ^proverbs-19-4-douay

5 A false witness shall not be unpunished: and he that speaketh lies, shall not escape. ^proverbs-19-5-douay

6 Many honour the person of him that is mighty, and are friends of him that giveth gifts. ^proverbs-19-6-douay

7 The brethren of the poor man hate him: moreover also his friends have departed far from him. He that followeth after words only, shall have nothing. ^proverbs-19-7-douay

8 But he that possesseth a mind, loveth his own soul, and he that keepeth prudence, shall find good things. ^proverbs-19-8-douay

9 A false witness shall not be unpunished: and he that speaketh lies, shall perish. ^proverbs-19-9-douay

10 Delicacies are not seemly for a fool: nor for a servant to have rule over princes. ^proverbs-19-10-douay

11 The learning of a man is known by patience: and his glory is to pass over wrongs. ^proverbs-19-11-douay

12 As the roaring of a lion, so also is the anger of a king: and his cheerfulness as the dew upon the grass. ^proverbs-19-12-douay

13 A foolish son is the grief of his father: and a wrangling wife is like a roof continually dropping through. ^proverbs-19-13-douay

14 House and riches are given by parents: but a prudent wife is properly from the Lord. ^proverbs-19-14-douay

15 Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep, and an idle soul shall suffer hunger. ^proverbs-19-15-douay

16 He that keepeth the commandment, keepeth his own soul: but he that neglecteth his own way, shall die. ^proverbs-19-16-douay

17 He that hath mercy on the poor, lendeth to the Lord: and he will repay him. ^proverbs-19-17-douay

18 Chastise thy son, despair not: but to the killing of him set not thy soul. ^proverbs-19-18-douay

19 He that is impatient, shall suffer damage: and when he shall take away, he shall add another thing. ^proverbs-19-19-douay

20 Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayst be wise in thy latter end. ^proverbs-19-20-douay

21 There are many thoughts in the heart of a man: but the will of the Lord shall stand firm. ^proverbs-19-21-douay

22 A needy man is merciful: and better is the poor than the lying man. ^proverbs-19-22-douay

23 The fear of the Lord is unto life: and he shall abide in the fulness without being visited with evil. ^proverbs-19-23-douay

24 The slothful hideth his hand under his armpit, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth. ^proverbs-19-24-douay

25 The wicked man being scourged, the fool shall be wiser: but if thou rebuke a wise man, he will understand discipline. ^proverbs-19-25-douay

26 He that afflicteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is infamous and unhappy. ^proverbs-19-26-douay

27 Cease not, O my son, to hear instruction, and be not ignorant of the words of knowledge. ^proverbs-19-27-douay

28 An unjust witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity. ^proverbs-19-28-douay

29 Judgments are prepared for scorners: and striking hammers for the bodies of fools. ^proverbs-19-29-douay
