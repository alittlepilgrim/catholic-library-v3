# Proverbs Chapter 16

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 It is the part of man to prepare the soul: and of the Lord to govern the tongue. **It is the part of man, etc...** That is, a man should prepare in his heart and soul what he is to say: but after all, it must be the Lord that must govern his tongue, to speak to the purpose. Not that we can think any thing of good without God's grace; but that after we have (with God's grace) thought and prepared within our souls what we would speak, if God does not govern our tongue, we shall not succeed in what we speak. ^proverbs-16-1-douay

2 All the ways of a man are open to his eyes: the Lord is the weigher of spirits. ^proverbs-16-2-douay

3 Lay open thy works to the Lord: and thy thoughts shall be directed. ^proverbs-16-3-douay

4 The Lord hath made all things for himself: the wicked also for the evil day. ^proverbs-16-4-douay

5 Every proud man is an abomination to the Lord: though hand should be joined to hand, he is not innocent. The beginning of a good way is to do justice: and this is more acceptable with God, than to offer sacrifices. ^proverbs-16-5-douay

6 By mercy and truth iniquity is redeemed; and by the fear of the Lord men depart from evil. ^proverbs-16-6-douay

7 When the ways of man shall please the Lord, he will convert even his enemies to peace. ^proverbs-16-7-douay

8 Better is a little with justice, than great revenues with iniquity. ^proverbs-16-8-douay

9 The heart of man disposeth his way: but the Lord must direct his steps. ^proverbs-16-9-douay

10 Divination is in the lips of the king, his mouth shall not err in judgment. ^proverbs-16-10-douay

11 Weight and balance are judgments of the Lord: and his work all the weights of the bag. ^proverbs-16-11-douay

12 They that act wickedly are abominable to the king: for the throne is established by justice. ^proverbs-16-12-douay

13 Just lips are the delight of kings: he that speaketh right things shall be loved. ^proverbs-16-13-douay

14 The wrath of a king is as messengers of death: and the wise man will pacify it. ^proverbs-16-14-douay

15 In the cheerfulness of the king's countenance is life: and his clemency is like the latter rain. ^proverbs-16-15-douay

16 Get wisdom, because it is better than gold: and purchase prudence, for it is more precious than silver. ^proverbs-16-16-douay

17 The path of the just departeth from evils: he that keepeth his soul keepeth his way. ^proverbs-16-17-douay

18 Pride goeth before destruction: and the spirit is lifted up before a fall. ^proverbs-16-18-douay

19 It is better to be humbled with the meek, than to divide spoils with the proud. ^proverbs-16-19-douay

20 The learned in word shall find good things: and he that trusteth in the Lord is blessed. ^proverbs-16-20-douay

21 The wise in heart shall be called prudent: and he that is sweet in words, shall attain to greater things. ^proverbs-16-21-douay

22 Knowledge is a fountain of life to him that possesseth it: the instruction of fools is foolishness. ^proverbs-16-22-douay

23 The heart of the wise shall instruct his mouth: and shall add grace to his lips. ^proverbs-16-23-douay

24 Well ordered words are as a honeycomb: sweet to the soul, and health to the bones. ^proverbs-16-24-douay

25 There is a way that seemeth to a man right: and the ends thereof lead to death. ^proverbs-16-25-douay

26 The soul of him that laboureth, laboureth for himself, because his mouth hath obliged him to it. ^proverbs-16-26-douay

27 The wicked man diggeth evil, and in his lips is a burning fire. ^proverbs-16-27-douay

28 A perverse man stirreth up quarrels: and one full of words separateth princes. ^proverbs-16-28-douay

29 An unjust man allureth his friend: and leadeth him into a way that is not good. ^proverbs-16-29-douay

30 He that with fixed eyes deviseth wicked things, biting his lips, bringeth evil to pass. ^proverbs-16-30-douay

31 Old age is a crown of dignity, when it is found in the ways of justice. ^proverbs-16-31-douay

32 The patient man is better than the valiant: and he that ruleth his spirit, than he that taketh cities. ^proverbs-16-32-douay

33 Lots are cast into the lap, but they are disposed of by the Lord. ^proverbs-16-33-douay
