# Proverbs Chapter 6

Documents on several heads.

1 My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, thou hast engaged fast thy hand to a stranger, ^proverbs-06-1-douay

2 Thou art ensnared with the words of thy mouth, and caught with thy own words. ^proverbs-06-2-douay

3 Do, therefore, my son, what I say, and deliver thyself: because thou art fallen into the hand of thy neighbour. Run about, make haste, stir up thy friend: ^proverbs-06-3-douay

4 Give not sleep to thy eyes, neither let thy eyelids slumber. ^proverbs-06-4-douay

5 Deliver thyself as a doe from the hand, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler. ^proverbs-06-5-douay

6 Go to the ant, O sluggard, and consider her ways, and learn wisdom: ^proverbs-06-6-douay

7 Which, although she hath no guide, nor master, nor captain, ^proverbs-06-7-douay

8 Provideth her meat for herself in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest. ^proverbs-06-8-douay

9 How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep? ^proverbs-06-9-douay

10 Thou wilt sleep a little, thou wilt slumber a little, thou wilt fold thy hands a little to sleep: ^proverbs-06-10-douay

11 And want shall come upon thee, as a traveller, and poverty as a man armed. But if thou be diligent, thy harvest shall come as a fountain, and want shall flee far from thee. ^proverbs-06-11-douay

12 A man that is an apostate, an unprofitable man, walketh with a perverse mouth, ^proverbs-06-12-douay

13 He winketh with the eyes, presseth with the foot, speaketh with the finger. ^proverbs-06-13-douay

14 With a wicked heart he deviseth evil, and at all times he soweth discord. ^proverbs-06-14-douay

15 To such a one his destruction shall presently come, and he shall suddenly be destroyed, and shall no longer have any remedy. ^proverbs-06-15-douay

16 Six things there are, which the Lord hateth, and the seventh his soul detesteth: ^proverbs-06-16-douay

17 Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood, ^proverbs-06-17-douay

18 A heart that deviseth wicked plots, feet that are swift to run into mischief, ^proverbs-06-18-douay

19 A deceitful witness that uttereth lies, and him that soweth discord among brethren. ^proverbs-06-19-douay

20 My son, keep the commandments of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother. ^proverbs-06-20-douay

21 Bind them in thy heart continually, and put them about thy neck. ^proverbs-06-21-douay

22 When thou walkest, let them go with thee: when thou sleepest, let them keep thee, and when thou awakest, talk with them. ^proverbs-06-22-douay

23 Because the commandment is a lamp, and the law a light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life: ^proverbs-06-23-douay

24 That they may keep thee from the evil woman, and from the flattering tongue of the stranger. ^proverbs-06-24-douay

25 Let not thy heart covet her beauty, be not caught with her winks: ^proverbs-06-25-douay

26 For the price of a harlot is scarce one loaf: but the woman catcheth the precious soul of a man. ^proverbs-06-26-douay

27 Can a man hide fire in his bosom, and his garments not burn? ^proverbs-06-27-douay

28 Or can he walk upon hot coals, and his feet not be burnt? ^proverbs-06-28-douay

29 So he that goeth in to his neighbour's wife, shall not be clean when he shall touch her. ^proverbs-06-29-douay

30 The fault is not so great when a man hath stolen: for he stealeth to fill his hungry soul: **The fault is not so great, etc...** The sin of theft is not so great, as to be compared with adultery: especially when a person pressed with hunger (which is the case here spoken of) steals to satisfy nature. Moreover the damage done by theft may much more easily be repaired, than the wrong done by adultery. But this does not hinder, but that theft also is a mortal sin, forbidden by one of the ten commandments. ^proverbs-06-30-douay

31 And if he be taken, he shall restore sevenfold, and shall give up all the substance of his house. ^proverbs-06-31-douay

32 But he that is an adulterer, for the folly of his heart shall destroy his own soul: ^proverbs-06-32-douay

33 He gathereth to himself shame and dishonour, and his reproach shall not be blotted out: ^proverbs-06-33-douay

34 Because the jealousy and rage of the husband will not spare in the day of revenge, ^proverbs-06-34-douay

35 Nor will he yield to any man's prayers, nor will he accept for satisfaction ever so many gifts. ^proverbs-06-35-douay
