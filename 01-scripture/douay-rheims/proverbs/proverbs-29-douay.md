# Proverbs Chapter 29

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 The man that with a stiff neck despiseth him that reproveth him, shall suddenly be destroyed: and health shall not follow him. ^proverbs-29-1-douay

2 When just men increase, the people shall rejoice: when the wicked shall bear rule, the people shall mourn. ^proverbs-29-2-douay

3 A man that loveth wisdom, rejoiceth his father: but he that maintaineth harlots, shall squander away his substance. ^proverbs-29-3-douay

4 A just king setteth up the land: a covetous man shall destroy it. ^proverbs-29-4-douay

5 A man that speaketh to his friend with flattering and dissembling words, spreadeth a net for his feet. ^proverbs-29-5-douay

6 A snare shall entangle the wicked man when he sinneth: and the just shall praise and rejoice. ^proverbs-29-6-douay

7 The just taketh notice of the cause of the poor: the wicked is void of knowledge. ^proverbs-29-7-douay

8 Corrupt men bring a city to ruin: but wise men turn away wrath. ^proverbs-29-8-douay

9 If a wise man contend with a fool, whether he be angry, or laugh, he shall find no rest. ^proverbs-29-9-douay

10 Bloodthirsty men hate the upright: but just men seek his soul. ^proverbs-29-10-douay

11 A fool uttereth all his mind: a wise man deferreth, and keepeth it till afterwards. ^proverbs-29-11-douay

12 A prince that gladly heareth lying words, hath all his servants wicked. ^proverbs-29-12-douay

13 The poor man and the creditor have met one another: the Lord is the enlightener of them both. ^proverbs-29-13-douay

14 The king that judgeth the poor in truth, his throne shall be established for ever. ^proverbs-29-14-douay

15 The rod and reproof give wisdom: but the child that is left to his own will, bringeth his mother to shame. ^proverbs-29-15-douay

16 When the wicked are multiplied, crimes shall be multiplied: but the just shall see their downfall. ^proverbs-29-16-douay

17 Instruct thy son and he shall refresh thee, and shall give delight to thy soul. ^proverbs-29-17-douay

18 When prophecy shall fail, the people shall be scattered abroad: but he that keepeth the law, is blessed. ^proverbs-29-18-douay

19 A slave will not be corrected by words: because he understandeth what thou sayest, and will not answer. ^proverbs-29-19-douay

20 Hast thou seen a man hasty to speak? folly is rather to be looked for, than his amendment. ^proverbs-29-20-douay

21 He that nourisheth his servant delicately from his childhood, afterwards shall find him stubborn. ^proverbs-29-21-douay

22 A passionate man provoketh quarrels: and he that is easily stirred up to wrath, shall be more prone to sin. ^proverbs-29-22-douay

23 Humiliation followeth the proud: and glory shall uphold the humble of spirit. ^proverbs-29-23-douay

24 He that is partaker with a thief, hateth his own soul: he heareth one putting him to his oath, and discovereth not. ^proverbs-29-24-douay

25 He that feareth man shall quickly fall: he that trusteth in the Lord, shall be set on high. ^proverbs-29-25-douay

26 Many seek the face of the prince: but the judgment of every one cometh forth from the Lord. ^proverbs-29-26-douay

27 The just abhor a wicked man: and the wicked loathe them that are in the right way. The son that keepeth the word, shall be free from destruction. ^proverbs-29-27-douay
