# Proverbs Chapter 11

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 A deceitful balance is an abomination before the Lord: and a just weight is his will. ^proverbs-11-1-douay

2 Where pride is, there also shall be reproach: but where humility is, there also is wisdom. ^proverbs-11-2-douay

3 The simplicity of the just shall guide them: and the deceitfulness of the wicked shall destroy them. ^proverbs-11-3-douay

4 Riches shall not profit in the day of revenge: but justice shall deliver from death. ^proverbs-11-4-douay

5 The justice of the upright shall make his way prosperous: and the wicked man shall fall by his own wickedness. ^proverbs-11-5-douay

6 The justice of the righteous shall deliver them: and the unjust shall be caught in their own snares. ^proverbs-11-6-douay

7 When the wicked man is dead, there shall be no hope any more: and the expectation of the solicitous shall perish. ^proverbs-11-7-douay

8 The just is delivered out of distress: and the wicked shall be given up for him. ^proverbs-11-8-douay

9 The dissembler with his mouth deceiveth his friend: but the just shall be delivered by knowledge. ^proverbs-11-9-douay

10 When it goeth well with the just, the city shall rejoice: and when the wicked perish, there shall be praise. ^proverbs-11-10-douay

11 By the blessing of the just the city shall be exalted: and by the mouth of the wicked it shall be overthrown. ^proverbs-11-11-douay

12 He that despiseth his friend, is mean of heart: but the wise man will hold his peace. ^proverbs-11-12-douay

13 He that walketh deceitfully, revealeth secrets: but he that is faithful, concealeth the thing committed to him by his friend. ^proverbs-11-13-douay

14 Where there is no governor, the people shall fall: but there is safety where there is much counsel. ^proverbs-11-14-douay

15 He shall be afflicted with evil, that is surety for a stranger: but he that is aware of snares, shall be secure. ^proverbs-11-15-douay

16 A gracious woman shall find glory: and the strong shall have riches. ^proverbs-11-16-douay

17 A merciful man doth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel casteth off even his own kindred. ^proverbs-11-17-douay

18 The wicked maketh an unsteady work: but to him that soweth justice, there is a faithful reward. ^proverbs-11-18-douay

19 Clemency prepareth life: and the pursuing of evil things, death. ^proverbs-11-19-douay

20 A perverse heart is abominable to the Lord: and his will is in them that walk sincerely. ^proverbs-11-20-douay

21 Hand in hand the evil man shall not be innocent: but the seed of the just shall be saved. ^proverbs-11-21-douay

22 A golden ring in a swine's snout, a woman fair and foolish. ^proverbs-11-22-douay

23 The desire of the just is all good, the expectation of the wicked is indignation. ^proverbs-11-23-douay

24 Some distribute their own goods, and grow richer: others take away what is not their own, and are always in want. ^proverbs-11-24-douay

25 The soul that blesseth, shall be made fat: and he that inebriateth, shall be inebriated also himself. ^proverbs-11-25-douay

26 He that hideth up corn, shall be cursed among the people: but a blessing upon the head of them that sell. ^proverbs-11-26-douay

27 Well doth he rise early who seeketh good things; but he that seeketh after evil things, shall be oppressed by them. ^proverbs-11-27-douay

28 He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the just shall spring up as a green leaf. ^proverbs-11-28-douay

29 He that troubleth his own house, shall inherit the winds: and the fool shall serve the wise. ^proverbs-11-29-douay

30 The fruit of the just man is a tree of life: and he that gaineth souls is wise. ^proverbs-11-30-douay

31 If the just man receive in the earth, how much more the wicked and the sinner. ^proverbs-11-31-douay
