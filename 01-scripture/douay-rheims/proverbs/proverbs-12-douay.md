# Proverbs Chapter 12

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 He that loveth correction, loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof, is foolish. ^proverbs-12-1-douay

2 He that is good, shall draw grace from the Lord: but he that trusteth in his own devices, doth wickedly. ^proverbs-12-2-douay

3 Man shall not be strengthened by wickedness: and the root of the just shall not be moved. ^proverbs-12-3-douay

4 A diligent woman is a crown to her husband: and she that doth things worthy of confusion, is as rottenness in his bones. ^proverbs-12-4-douay

5 The thoughts of the just are judgments: and the counsels of the wicked are deceitful. ^proverbs-12-5-douay

6 The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood: the mouth of the just shall deliver them. ^proverbs-12-6-douay

7 Turn the wicked, and they shall not be: but the house of the just shall stand firm. ^proverbs-12-7-douay

8 A man shall be known by his learning: but he that is vain and foolish, shall be exposed to contempt. ^proverbs-12-8-douay

9 Better is the poor man that provideth for himself, than he that is glorious and wanteth bread. ^proverbs-12-9-douay

10 The just regardeth the lives of his beasts: but the bowels of the wicked are cruel. ^proverbs-12-10-douay

11 He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that pursueth idleness is very foolish. He that is delighted in passing his time over wine, leaveth a reproach in his strong holds. ^proverbs-12-11-douay

12 The desire of the wicked is the fortification of evil men: but the root of the just shall prosper. ^proverbs-12-12-douay

13 For the sins of the lips ruin draweth nigh to the evil man: but the just shall escape out of distress. ^proverbs-12-13-douay

14 By the fruit of his own mouth shall a man be filled with good things, and according to the works of his hands it shall be repaid him. ^proverbs-12-14-douay

15 The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that is wise hearkeneth unto counsels. ^proverbs-12-15-douay

16 A fool immediately showeth his anger: but he that dissembleth injuries is wise. ^proverbs-12-16-douay

17 He that speaketh that which he knoweth, showeth forth justice: but he that lieth, is a deceitful witness. ^proverbs-12-17-douay

18 There is that promiseth, and is pricked as it were with a sword of conscience: but the tongue of the wise is health. ^proverbs-12-18-douay

19 The lip of truth shall be steadfast for ever: but he that is a hasty witness, frameth a lying tongue. ^proverbs-12-19-douay

20 Deceit is in the heart of them that think evil things: but joy followeth them that take counsels of peace. ^proverbs-12-20-douay

21 Whatsoever shall befall the just man, shall not make him sad: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief. ^proverbs-12-21-douay

22 Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord: but they that deal faithfully, please him. ^proverbs-12-22-douay

23 A cautious man concealeth knowledge: and the heart of fools publisheth folly. ^proverbs-12-23-douay

24 The hand of the valiant shall bear rule: but that which is slothful shall be under tribute. ^proverbs-12-24-douay

25 Grief in the heart of a man shall bring him low, but with a good word he shall be made glad. ^proverbs-12-25-douay

26 He that neglecteth a loss for the sake of a friend, is just: but the way of the wicked shall deceive them. ^proverbs-12-26-douay

27 The deceitful man shall not find gain: but the substance of a just man shall be precious gold. ^proverbs-12-27-douay

28 In the path of justice is life: but the bye-way leadeth to death. ^proverbs-12-28-douay
