# Proverbs Chapter 2

The advantages of wisdom: and the evils from which it delivers.

1 My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and wilt hide my commandments with thee, ^proverbs-02-1-douay

2 That thy ear may hearken to wisdom: incline thy heart to know prudence. ^proverbs-02-2-douay

3 For if thou shalt call for wisdom, and incline thy heart to prudence: ^proverbs-02-3-douay

4 If thou shalt seek her as money, and shalt dig for her as for a treasure: ^proverbs-02-4-douay

5 Then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and shalt find the knowledge of God: ^proverbs-02-5-douay

6 Because the Lord giveth wisdom: and out of his mouth cometh prudence and knowledge. ^proverbs-02-6-douay

7 He wilt keep the salvation of the righteous, and protect them that walk in simplicity, ^proverbs-02-7-douay

8 Keeping the paths of justice, and guarding the ways of saints. ^proverbs-02-8-douay

9 Then shalt thou understand justice, and judgment, and equity, and every good path. ^proverbs-02-9-douay

10 If wisdom shall enter into thy heart, and knowledge please thy soul: ^proverbs-02-10-douay

11 Counsel shall keep thee, and prudence shall preserve thee, ^proverbs-02-11-douay

12 That thou mayst be delivered from the evil way, and from the man that speaketh perverse things: ^proverbs-02-12-douay

13 Who leave the right way, and walk by dark ways: ^proverbs-02-13-douay

14 Who are glad when they have done evil, and rejoice in the most wicked things: ^proverbs-02-14-douay

15 Whose ways are perverse, and their steps infamous. ^proverbs-02-15-douay

16 That thou mayst be delivered from the strange woman, and from the stranger, who softeneth her words; ^proverbs-02-16-douay

17 And forsaketh the guide of her youth, ^proverbs-02-17-douay

18 And hath forgotten the covenant of her God: for her house inclineth unto death, and her paths to hell. ^proverbs-02-18-douay

19 None that go in unto her, shall return again, neither shall they take hold of the paths of life. ^proverbs-02-19-douay

20 That thou mayst walk in a good way: and mayst keep the paths of the just. ^proverbs-02-20-douay

21 For they that are upright, shall dwell in the earth; and the simple shall continue in it. ^proverbs-02-21-douay

22 But the wicked shall be destroyed from the earth: and they that do unjustly, shall be taken away from it. ^proverbs-02-22-douay
