# Proverbs Chapter 13

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 A wise son heareth the doctrine of his father: but he that is a scorner, heareth not when he is reproved. ^proverbs-13-1-douay

2 Of the fruit of his own month shall a man be filled with good things: but the soul of transgressors is wicked. ^proverbs-13-2-douay

3 He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his soul: but he that hath no guard on his speech shall meet with evils. ^proverbs-13-3-douay

4 The sluggard willeth, and willeth not: but the soul of them that work, shall be made fat. ^proverbs-13-4-douay

5 The just shall hate a lying word: but the wicked confoundeth, and shall be confounded. ^proverbs-13-5-douay

6 Justice keepeth the way of the innocent: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner. ^proverbs-13-6-douay

7 One is as it were rich, when he hath nothing and another is as it were poor, when he hath great riches. ^proverbs-13-7-douay

8 The ransom of a man's life are his riches: but he that is poor, beareth not reprehension. ^proverbs-13-8-douay

9 The light of the just giveth joy: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out. ^proverbs-13-9-douay

10 Among the proud there are always contentions: but they that do all things with counsel, are ruled by wisdom. ^proverbs-13-10-douay

11 Substance got in haste shall be diminished: but that which by little and little is gathered with the hand, shall increase. ^proverbs-13-11-douay

12 Hope that is deferred afflicteth the soul: desire when it cometh, is a tree of life. ^proverbs-13-12-douay

13 Whosoever speaketh ill of any thing, bindeth himself for the time to come: but he that feareth the commandment, shall dwell in peace. Deceitful souls go astray in sins: the just are merciful, and show mercy. ^proverbs-13-13-douay

14 The law of the wise is a fountain of life, that he may decline from the ruin of death. ^proverbs-13-14-douay

15 Good instruction shall give grace: in the way of scorners is a deep pit. ^proverbs-13-15-douay

16 The prudent man doth all things with counsel: but he that is a fool, layeth open his folly. ^proverbs-13-16-douay

17 The messenger of the wicked shall fall into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health. ^proverbs-13-17-douay

18 Poverty and shame to him that refuseth instruction: but he that yieldeth to reproof shall be glorified. ^proverbs-13-18-douay

19 The desire that is accomplished, delighteth the soul: fools hate them that flee from evil things. ^proverbs-13-19-douay

20 He that walketh with the wise, shall be wise: a friend of fools shall become like to them. ^proverbs-13-20-douay

21 Evil pursueth sinners: and to the just good shall be repaid. ^proverbs-13-21-douay

22 The good man leaveth heirs, sons, and grandsons: and the substance of the sinner is kept for the just. ^proverbs-13-22-douay

23 Much food is in the tillage of fathers: but for others it is gathered without judgment. ^proverbs-13-23-douay

24 He that spareth the rod, hateth his son: but he that loveth him, correcteth him betimes. ^proverbs-13-24-douay

25 The just eateth and filleth his soul: but the belly of the wicked is never to be filled. ^proverbs-13-25-douay
