# Proverbs Chapter 8

The preaching of wisdom. Her excellence.

1 Doth not wisdom cry aloud, and prudence put forth her voice? ^proverbs-08-1-douay

2 Standing in the top of the highest places by the way, in the midst of the paths, ^proverbs-08-2-douay

3 Beside the gates of the city, in the very doors she speaketh, saying: ^proverbs-08-3-douay

4 O ye men, to you I call, and my voice is to the sons of men. ^proverbs-08-4-douay

5 O little ones understand subtlety, and ye unwise, take notice. ^proverbs-08-5-douay

6 Hear, for I will speak of great things: and my lips shall be opened to preach right things. ^proverbs-08-6-douay

7 My mouth shall meditate truth, and my lips shall hate wickedness. ^proverbs-08-7-douay

8 All my words are just, there is nothing wicked, nor perverse in them. ^proverbs-08-8-douay

9 They are right to them that understand, and just to them that find knowledge. ^proverbs-08-9-douay

10 Receive my instruction, and not money: choose knowledge rather than gold. ^proverbs-08-10-douay

11 For wisdom is better than all the most precious things: and whatsoever may be desired cannot be compared to it. ^proverbs-08-11-douay

12 I, wisdom, dwell in counsel, and am present in learned thoughts. ^proverbs-08-12-douay

13 The fear of the Lord hateth evil; I hate arrogance, and pride, and every wicked way, and a mouth with a double tongue. ^proverbs-08-13-douay

14 Counsel and equity is mine, prudence is mine, strength is mine. ^proverbs-08-14-douay

15 By me kings reign, and lawgivers decree just things. ^proverbs-08-15-douay

16 By me princes rule, and the mighty decree justice. ^proverbs-08-16-douay

17 I love them that love me: and they that in the morning early watch for me, shall find me. ^proverbs-08-17-douay

18 With me are riches and glory, glorious riches and justice. ^proverbs-08-18-douay

19 For my fruit is better than gold and the precious stone, and my blossoms than choice silver. ^proverbs-08-19-douay

20 I walk in the way of justice, in the midst of the paths of judgment, ^proverbs-08-20-douay

21 That I may enrich them that love me, and may fill their treasures. ^proverbs-08-21-douay

22 The Lord possessed me in the beginning of his ways, before he made any thing from the beginning. ^proverbs-08-22-douay

23 I was set up from eternity, and of old, before the earth was made. ^proverbs-08-23-douay

24 The depths were not as yet, and I was already conceived, neither had the fountains of waters as yet sprung out. ^proverbs-08-24-douay

25 The mountains, with their huge bulk, had not as yet been established: before the hills, I was brought forth: ^proverbs-08-25-douay

26 He had not yet made the earth, nor the rivers, nor the poles of the world. ^proverbs-08-26-douay

27 When he prepared the heavens, I was present: when with a certain law, and compass, he enclosed the depths: ^proverbs-08-27-douay

28 When he established the sky above, and poised the fountains of waters: ^proverbs-08-28-douay

29 When he compassed the sea with its bounds, and set a law to the waters that they should not pass their limits: when he balanced the foundations of the earth; ^proverbs-08-29-douay

30 I was with him forming all things: and was delighted every day, playing before him at all times; ^proverbs-08-30-douay

31 Playing in the world: and my delights were to be with the children of men. ^proverbs-08-31-douay

32 Now, therefore, ye children, hear me: blessed are they that keep my ways. ^proverbs-08-32-douay

33 Hear instruction, and be wise, and refuse it not. ^proverbs-08-33-douay

34 Blessed is the man that heareth me, and that watcheth daily at my gates, and waiteth at the posts of my doors. ^proverbs-08-34-douay

35 He that shall find me, shall find life, and shall have salvation from the Lord. ^proverbs-08-35-douay

36 But he that shall sin against me shall hurt his own soul. All that hate me love death. ^proverbs-08-36-douay
