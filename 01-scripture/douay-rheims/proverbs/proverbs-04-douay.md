# Proverbs Chapter 4

A further exhortation to seek after wisdom.

1 Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend, that you may know prudence. ^proverbs-04-1-douay

2 I will give you a good gift, forsake not my law. ^proverbs-04-2-douay

3 For I also was my father's son, tender, and as an only son in the sight of my mother: ^proverbs-04-3-douay

4 And he taught me, and said: Let thy heart receive my words, keep my commandments, and thou shalt live. ^proverbs-04-4-douay

5 Get wisdom, get prudence: forget not, neither decline from the words of my mouth. ^proverbs-04-5-douay

6 Forsake her not, and she shall keep thee: love her, and she shall preserve thee. ^proverbs-04-6-douay

7 The beginning of wisdom, get wisdom, and with all thy possession purchase prudence. ^proverbs-04-7-douay

8 Take hold on her, and she shall exalt thee: thou shalt be glorified by her, when thou shalt embrace her. ^proverbs-04-8-douay

9 She shall give to thy head increase of graces, and protect thee with a noble crown. ^proverbs-04-9-douay

10 Hear, O my son, and receive my words, that years of life may be multiplied to thee. ^proverbs-04-10-douay

11 I will show thee the way of wisdom, I will lead thee by the paths of equity: ^proverbs-04-11-douay

12 Which when thou shalt have entered, thy steps shall not be straitened, and when thou runnest, thou shalt not meet a stumbling block. ^proverbs-04-12-douay

13 Take hold on instruction, leave it not: keep it, because it is thy life. ^proverbs-04-13-douay

14 Be not delighted in the paths of the wicked, neither let the way of evil men please thee. ^proverbs-04-14-douay

15 Flee from it, pass not by it: go aside, and forsake it. ^proverbs-04-15-douay

16 For they sleep not, except they have done evil: and their sleep is taken away unless they have made some to fall. ^proverbs-04-16-douay

17 They eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of iniquity. ^proverbs-04-17-douay

18 But the path of the just, as a shining light, goeth forwards, and increaseth even to perfect day. ^proverbs-04-18-douay

19 The way of the wicked is darksome: they know not where they fall. ^proverbs-04-19-douay

20 My son, hearken to my words, and incline thy ear to my sayings. ^proverbs-04-20-douay

21 Let them not depart from thy eyes, keep them in the midst of thy heart: ^proverbs-04-21-douay

22 For they are life to those that find them, and health to all flesh. ^proverbs-04-22-douay

23 With all watchfulness keep thy heart, because life issueth out from it. ^proverbs-04-23-douay

24 Remove from thee a froward mouth, and let detracting lips be far from thee. ^proverbs-04-24-douay

25 Let thy eyes look straight on, and let thy eyelids go before thy steps. ^proverbs-04-25-douay

26 Make straight the path for thy feet, and all thy ways shall be established. ^proverbs-04-26-douay

27 Decline not to the right hand, nor to the left: turn away thy foot from evil. For the Lord knoweth the ways that are on the right hand: but those are perverse which are on the left hand. But he will make thy courses straight, he will bring forward thy ways in peace. ^proverbs-04-27-douay
