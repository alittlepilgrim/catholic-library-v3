# Proverbs Chapter 5

An exhortation to fly unlawful lust, and the occasions of it.

1 My son, attend to my wisdom, and incline thy ear to my prudence, ^proverbs-05-1-douay

2 That thou mayst keep thoughts, and thy lips may preserve instruction. Mind not the deceit of a woman. ^proverbs-05-2-douay

3 For the lips of a harlot are like a honeycomb dropping, and her throat is smoother than oil. ^proverbs-05-3-douay

4 But her end is bitter as wormwood, and sharp as a two-edged sword. ^proverbs-05-4-douay

5 Her feet go down into death, and her steps go in as far as hell. ^proverbs-05-5-douay

6 They walk not by the path of life, her steps are wandering, and unaccountable. ^proverbs-05-6-douay

7 Now, therefore, my son, hear me, and depart not from the words of my mouth. ^proverbs-05-7-douay

8 Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the doors of her house. ^proverbs-05-8-douay

9 Give not thy honour to strangers, and thy years to the cruel. ^proverbs-05-9-douay

10 Lest strangers be filled with thy strength, and thy labours be in another man's house, ^proverbs-05-10-douay

11 And thou mourn at the last, when thou shalt have spent thy flesh and thy body, and say; ^proverbs-05-11-douay

12 Why have I hated instruction, and my heart consented not to reproof, ^proverbs-05-12-douay

13 And have not heard the voice of them that taught me, and have not inclined my ear to masters? ^proverbs-05-13-douay

14 I have almost been in all evil, in the midst of the church and of the congregation. ^proverbs-05-14-douay

15 Drink water out of thy own cistern, and the streams of thy own well: ^proverbs-05-15-douay

16 Let thy fountains be conveyed abroad, and in the streets divide thy waters. ^proverbs-05-16-douay

17 Keep them to thyself alone, neither let strangers be partakers with thee. ^proverbs-05-17-douay

18 Let thy vein be blessed, and rejoice with the wife of thy youth: ^proverbs-05-18-douay

19 Let her be thy dearest hind, and most agreeable fawn: let her breasts inebriate thee at all times: be thou delighted continually with her love. ^proverbs-05-19-douay

20 Why art thou seduced, my son, by a strange woman, and art cherished in the bosom of another? ^proverbs-05-20-douay

21 The Lord beholdeth the ways of man, and considereth all his steps. ^proverbs-05-21-douay

22 His own iniquities catch the wicked, and he is fast bound with the ropes of his own sins. ^proverbs-05-22-douay

23 He shall die, because he hath not received instruction, and in the multitude of his folly he shall be deceived. ^proverbs-05-23-douay
