# Proverbs Chapter 3

An exhortation to the practice of virtue.

1 My son, forget not my law, and let thy heart keep my commandments. ^proverbs-03-1-douay

2 For they shall add to thee length of days, and years of life, and peace. ^proverbs-03-2-douay

3 Let not mercy and truth leave thee, put them about thy neck, and write them in the tables of thy heart. ^proverbs-03-3-douay

4 And thou shalt find grace, and good understanding before God and men. ^proverbs-03-4-douay

5 Have confidence in the Lord with all thy heart, and lean not upon thy own prudence. ^proverbs-03-5-douay

6 In all thy ways think on him, and he will direct thy steps. ^proverbs-03-6-douay

7 Be not wise in thy own conceit: fear God, and depart from evil: ^proverbs-03-7-douay

8 For it shall be health to thy navel, and moistening to thy bones. ^proverbs-03-8-douay

9 Honour the Lord with thy substance, and give him of the first of all thy fruits; ^proverbs-03-9-douay

10 And thy barns shall be filled with abundance, and thy presses shall run over with wine. ^proverbs-03-10-douay

11 My son, reject not the correction of the Lord: and do not faint when thou art chastised by him: ^proverbs-03-11-douay

12 For whom the Lord loveth, he chastiseth: and as a father in the son he pleaseth himself. ^proverbs-03-12-douay

13 Blessed is the man that findeth wisdom, and is rich in prudence: ^proverbs-03-13-douay

14 The purchasing thereof is better than the merchandise of silver, and her fruit than the chief and purest gold: ^proverbs-03-14-douay

15 She is more precious than all riches: and all the things that are desired, are not to be compared to her. ^proverbs-03-15-douay

16 Length of days is in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and glory. ^proverbs-03-16-douay

17 Her ways are beautiful ways, and all her paths are peaceable. ^proverbs-03-17-douay

18 She is a tree of life to them that lay hold on her: and he that shall retain her is blessed. ^proverbs-03-18-douay

19 The Lord by wisdom hath founded the earth, hath established the heavens by prudence. ^proverbs-03-19-douay

20 By his wisdom the depths have broken out, and the clouds grow thick with dew. ^proverbs-03-20-douay

21 My son, let not these things depart from thy eyes: keep the law and counsel: ^proverbs-03-21-douay

22 And there shall be life to thy soul, and grace to thy mouth. ^proverbs-03-22-douay

23 Then shalt thou walk confidently in thy way, and thy foot shall not stumble: ^proverbs-03-23-douay

24 If thou sleep, thou shalt not fear: thou shalt rest, and thy sleep shall be sweet. ^proverbs-03-24-douay

25 Be not afraid of sudden fear, nor of the power of the wicked falling upon thee. ^proverbs-03-25-douay

26 For the Lord will be at thy side, and will keep thy foot that thou be not taken. ^proverbs-03-26-douay

27 Do not withhold him from doing good, who is able: if thou art able, do good thyself also. ^proverbs-03-27-douay

28 Say not to thy friend: Go, and come again: and tomorrow I will give to thee: when thou canst give at present. ^proverbs-03-28-douay

29 Practise not evil against thy friend, when he hath confidence in thee. ^proverbs-03-29-douay

30 Strive not against a man without cause, when he hath done thee no evil. ^proverbs-03-30-douay

31 Envy not the unjust man, and do not follow his ways. ^proverbs-03-31-douay

32 For every mocker is an abomination to the Lord, and his communication is with the simple. ^proverbs-03-32-douay

33 Want is from the Lord in the house of the wicked: but the habitations of the just shall be blessed. ^proverbs-03-33-douay

34 He shall scorn the scorners, and to the meek he will give grace. ^proverbs-03-34-douay

35 The wise shall possess glory: the promotion of fools is disgrace. ^proverbs-03-35-douay
