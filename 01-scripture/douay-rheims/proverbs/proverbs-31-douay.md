# Proverbs Chapter 31

An exhortation to chastity, temperance, and works of mercy; with the praise of a wise woman.

1 The words of king Lamuel. The vision wherewith his mother instructed him. **Lamuel...** This name signifies God with him, and is supposed to have been one of the names of Solomon. ^proverbs-31-1-douay

2 What, O my beloved, what, O the beloved of my womb, what, O the beloved of my vows? ^proverbs-31-2-douay

3 Give not thy substance to women, and thy riches to destroy kings. ^proverbs-31-3-douay

4 Give not to kings, O Lamuel, give not wine to kings: because there is no secret where drunkenness reigneth: ^proverbs-31-4-douay

5 And lest they drink and forget judgments, and pervert the cause of the children of the poor. ^proverbs-31-5-douay

6 Give strong drink to them that are sad; and wine to them that are grieved in mind: ^proverbs-31-6-douay

7 Let them drink, and forget their want, and remember their sorrow no more. ^proverbs-31-7-douay

8 Open thy mouth for the dumb, and for the causes of all the children that pass. ^proverbs-31-8-douay

9 Open thy mouth, decree that which is just, and do justice to the needy and poor. ^proverbs-31-9-douay

10 Who shall find a valiant woman? far, and from the uttermost coasts is the price of her. ^proverbs-31-10-douay

11 The heart of her husband trusteth in her, and he shall have no need of spoils. ^proverbs-31-11-douay

12 She will render him good, and not evil all the days of her life. ^proverbs-31-12-douay

13 She hath sought wool and flax, and hath wrought by the counsel of her hands. ^proverbs-31-13-douay

14 She is like the merchant's ship, she bringeth her bread from afar. ^proverbs-31-14-douay

15 And she hath risen in the night, and given a prey to her household, and victuals to her maidens. ^proverbs-31-15-douay

16 She hath considered a field, and bought it: with the fruit of her hands she hath planted a vineyard. ^proverbs-31-16-douay

17 She hath girded her loins with strength, and hath strengthened her arm. ^proverbs-31-17-douay

18 She hath tasted, and seen that her traffic is good: her lamp shall not be put out in the night. ^proverbs-31-18-douay

19 She hath put out her hand to strong things, and her fingers have taken hold of the spindle. ^proverbs-31-19-douay

20 She hath opened her hand to the needy, and stretched out her hands to the poor. ^proverbs-31-20-douay

21 She shall not fear for her house in the cold of snow: for all her domestics are clothed with double garments. ^proverbs-31-21-douay

22 She hath made for herself clothing of tapestry: fine linen, and purple, is her covering. ^proverbs-31-22-douay

23 Her husband is honourable in the gates, when he sitteth among the senators of the land. ^proverbs-31-23-douay

24 She made fine linen, and sold it, and delivered a girdle to the Chanaanite. **The Chanaanite...** The merchant, for Chanaanite, in Hebrew, signifies a merchant. ^proverbs-31-24-douay

25 Strength and beauty are her clothing, and she shall laugh in the latter day. ^proverbs-31-25-douay

26 She hath opened her mouth to wisdom, and the law of clemency is on her tongue. ^proverbs-31-26-douay

27 She hath looked well on the paths of her house, and hath not eaten her bread idle. ^proverbs-31-27-douay

28 Her children rose up, and called her blessed: her husband, and he praised her. ^proverbs-31-28-douay

29 Many daughters have gathered together riches: thou hast surpassed them all. ^proverbs-31-29-douay

30 Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: the woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised. ^proverbs-31-30-douay

31 Give her of the fruit of her hands: and let her works praise her in the gates. ^proverbs-31-31-douay
