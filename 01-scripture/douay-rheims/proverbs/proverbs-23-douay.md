# Proverbs Chapter 23

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 When thou shalt sit to eat with a prince, consider diligently what is set before thy face: ^proverbs-23-1-douay

2 And put a knife to thy throat, if it be so that thou have thy soul in thy own power. ^proverbs-23-2-douay

3 Be not desirous of his meats, in which is the bread of deceit. ^proverbs-23-3-douay

4 Labour not to be rich: but set bounds to thy prudence. ^proverbs-23-4-douay

5 Lift not up thy eyes to riches which thou canst not have: because they shall make themselves wings like those of an eagle, and shall fly towards heaven. ^proverbs-23-5-douay

6 Eat not with an envious man, and desire not his meats: ^proverbs-23-6-douay

7 Because, like a soothsayer, and diviner, he thinketh that which he knoweth not. Eat and drink, will he say to thee: and his mind is not with thee. ^proverbs-23-7-douay

8 The meats which thou hadst eaten, thou shalt vomit up: and shalt lose thy beautiful words. ^proverbs-23-8-douay

9 Speak not in the ears of fools: because they will despise the instruction of thy speech. ^proverbs-23-9-douay

10 Touch not the bounds of little ones: and enter not into the field of the fatherless: ^proverbs-23-10-douay

11 For their near kinsman is strong: and he will judge their cause against thee. ^proverbs-23-11-douay

12 Let thy heart apply itself to instruction and thy ears to words of knowledge. ^proverbs-23-12-douay

13 Withhold not correction from a child: for if thou strike him with the rod, he shall not die. ^proverbs-23-13-douay

14 Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and deliver his soul from hell. ^proverbs-23-14-douay

15 My son, if thy mind be wise, my heart shall rejoice with thee: ^proverbs-23-15-douay

16 And my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips shall speak what is right. ^proverbs-23-16-douay

17 Let not thy heart envy sinners: but be thou in the fear of the Lord all the day long: ^proverbs-23-17-douay

18 Because thou shalt have hope in the latter end, and thy expectation shall not be taken away. ^proverbs-23-18-douay

19 Hear thou, my son, and be wise: and guide thy mind in the way. ^proverbs-23-19-douay

20 Be not in the feasts of great drinkers, nor in their revellings, who contribute flesh to eat: ^proverbs-23-20-douay

21 Because they that give themselves to drinking, and that club together, shall be consumed: and drowsiness shall be clothed with rags. ^proverbs-23-21-douay

22 Hearken to thy father, that begot thee: and despise not thy mother when she is old. ^proverbs-23-22-douay

23 Buy truth, and do not sell wisdom, and instruction, and understanding. ^proverbs-23-23-douay

24 The father of the just rejoiceth greatly: he that hath begotten a wise son, shall have joy in him. ^proverbs-23-24-douay

25 Let thy father and thy mother be joyful, and let her rejoice that bore thee. ^proverbs-23-25-douay

26 My son, give me thy heart: and let thy eyes keep my ways. ^proverbs-23-26-douay

27 For a harlot is a deep ditch: and a strange woman is a narrow pit. ^proverbs-23-27-douay

28 She lieth in wait in the way as a robber, and him whom she shall see unwary, she will kill. ^proverbs-23-28-douay

29 Who hath woe? whose father hath woe? who hath contentions? who falls into pits? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes? ^proverbs-23-29-douay

30 Surely they that pass their time in wine, and study to drink off their cups. ^proverbs-23-30-douay

31 Look not upon the wine when it is yellow, when the colour thereof shineth in the glass: it goeth in pleasantly, ^proverbs-23-31-douay

32 But in the end, it will bite like a snake, and will spread abroad poison like a basilisk. ^proverbs-23-32-douay

33 Thy eyes shall behold strange women, and thy heart shall utter perverse things. ^proverbs-23-33-douay

34 And thou shalt be as one sleeping in the midst of the sea, and as a pilot fast asleep when the stern is lost. ^proverbs-23-34-douay

35 And thou shalt say: They have beaten me, but I was not sensible of pain: they drew me, and I felt not: when shall I awake and find wine again? ^proverbs-23-35-douay
