# Proverbs Chapter 18

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 He that hath a mind to depart from a friend, seeketh occasions: he shall ever be subject to reproach. ^proverbs-18-1-douay

2 A fool receiveth not the words of prudence: unless thou say those things which are in his heart. ^proverbs-18-2-douay

3 The wicked man, when he is come into the depths of sins, contemneth: but ignominy and reproach follow him. ^proverbs-18-3-douay

4 Words from the mouth of a man are as deep water: and the fountain of wisdom is an overflowing stream. ^proverbs-18-4-douay

5 It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to decline from the truth of judgment. ^proverbs-18-5-douay

6 The lips of a fool intermeddle with strife: and his mouth provoketh quarrels. ^proverbs-18-6-douay

7 The mouth of a fool is his destruction: and his lips are the ruin of his soul. ^proverbs-18-7-douay

8 The words of the double tongued are as if they were harmless: and they reach even to the inner parts of the bowels. Fear casteth down the slothful: and the souls of the effeminate shall be hungry. ^proverbs-18-8-douay

9 He that is loose and slack in his work, is the brother of him that wasteth his own works. ^proverbs-18-9-douay

10 The name of the Lord is a strong tower: the just runneth to it, and shall be exalted. ^proverbs-18-10-douay

11 The substance of the rich man is the city of his strength, and as a strong wall compassing him about. ^proverbs-18-11-douay

12 Before destruction, the heart of a man is exalted: and before he be glorified, it is humbled. ^proverbs-18-12-douay

13 He that answereth before he heareth, showeth himself to be a fool, and worthy of confusion. ^proverbs-18-13-douay

14 The spirit of a man upholdeth his infirmity: but a spirit that is easily angered, who can bear? ^proverbs-18-14-douay

15 A wise heart shall acquire knowledge: and the ear of the wise seeketh instruction. ^proverbs-18-15-douay

16 A man's gift enlargeth his way, and maketh him room before princes. ^proverbs-18-16-douay

17 The just is first accuser of himself: his friend cometh, and shall search him. ^proverbs-18-17-douay

18 The lot suppresseth contentions, and determineth even between the mighty. ^proverbs-18-18-douay

19 A brother that is helped by his brother, is like a strong city: and judgments are like the bars of cities. ^proverbs-18-19-douay

20 Of the fruit of a man's mouth shall his belly be satisfied: and the offspring of his lips shall fill him. ^proverbs-18-20-douay

21 Death and life are in the power of the tongue: they that love it, shall eat the fruits thereof. ^proverbs-18-21-douay

22 He that hath found a good wife, hath found a good thing, and shall receive a pleasure from the Lord. He that driveth away a good wife, driveth away a good thing: but he that keepeth an adulteress, is foolish and wicked. ^proverbs-18-22-douay

23 The poor will speak with supplications, and the rich will speak roughly. ^proverbs-18-23-douay

24 A man amiable in society, shall be more friendly than a brother. ^proverbs-18-24-douay
