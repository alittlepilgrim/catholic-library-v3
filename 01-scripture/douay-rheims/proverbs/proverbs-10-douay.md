# Proverbs Chapter 10

In the twenty following chapters are contained many wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 A wise son maketh the father glad: but a foolish son is the sorrow of his mother. ^proverbs-10-1-douay

2 Treasures of wickedness shall profit nothing: but justice shall deliver from death. ^proverbs-10-2-douay

3 The Lord will not afflict the soul of the just with famine, and he will disappoint the deceitful practices of the wicked. ^proverbs-10-3-douay

4 The slothful hand hath wrought poverty: but the hand of the industrious getteth riches. He that trusteth to lies feedeth the winds: and the same runneth after birds, that fly away. ^proverbs-10-4-douay

5 He that gathereth in the harvest, is a wise son: but he that snorteth in the summer, is the son of confusion. ^proverbs-10-5-douay

6 The blessing of the Lord is upon the head of the just: but iniquity covereth the mouth of the wicked. ^proverbs-10-6-douay

7 The memory of the just is with praises: and the name of the wicked shall rot. ^proverbs-10-7-douay

8 The wise of heart receiveth precepts: a fool is beaten with lips. ^proverbs-10-8-douay

9 He that walketh sincerely, walketh confidently: but he that perverteth his ways, shall be manifest. ^proverbs-10-9-douay

10 He that winketh with the eye, shall cause sorrow: and the foolish in lips shall be beaten. ^proverbs-10-10-douay

11 The mouth of the just is a vein of life: and the mouth of the wicked covereth iniquity. ^proverbs-10-11-douay

12 Hatred stirreth up strifes: and charity covereth all sins. ^proverbs-10-12-douay

13 In the lips of the wise is wisdom found: and a rod on the back of him that wanteth sense. ^proverbs-10-13-douay

14 Wise men lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the fool is next to confusion. ^proverbs-10-14-douay

15 The substance of a rich man is the city of his strength: the fear of the poor is their poverty. ^proverbs-10-15-douay

16 The work of the just is unto life: but the fruit of the wicked unto sin. ^proverbs-10-16-douay

17 The way of life, to him that observeth correction: but he that forsaketh reproofs, goeth astray. ^proverbs-10-17-douay

18 Lying lips hide hatred: he that uttereth reproach, is foolish. ^proverbs-10-18-douay

19 In the multitude of words there shall not want sin: but he that refraineth his lips, is most wise. ^proverbs-10-19-douay

20 The tongue of the just is as choice silver: but the heart of the wicked is nothing worth. ^proverbs-10-20-douay

21 The lips of the just teach many: but they that are ignorant, shall die in the want of understanding. ^proverbs-10-21-douay

22 The blessing of the Lord maketh men rich: neither shall affliction be joined to them. ^proverbs-10-22-douay

23 A fool worketh mischief as it were for sport: but wisdom is prudence to a man. ^proverbs-10-23-douay

24 That which the wicked feareth, shall come upon him: to the just their desire shall be given. ^proverbs-10-24-douay

25 As a tempest that passeth, so the wicked shall be no more: but the just is as an everlasting foundation. ^proverbs-10-25-douay

26 As vinegar to the teeth, and smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that sent him. ^proverbs-10-26-douay

27 The fear of the Lord shall prolong days: and the years of the wicked shall be shortened. ^proverbs-10-27-douay

28 The expectation of the just is joy: but the hope of the wicked shall perish. ^proverbs-10-28-douay

29 The strength of the upright is the way of the Lord: and fear to them that work evil. ^proverbs-10-29-douay

30 The just shall never be moved: but the wicked shall not dwell on the earth. ^proverbs-10-30-douay

31 The mouth of the just shall bring forth wisdom: the tongue of the perverse shall perish. ^proverbs-10-31-douay

32 The lips of the just consider what is acceptable: and the mouth of the wicked uttereth perverse things. ^proverbs-10-32-douay
