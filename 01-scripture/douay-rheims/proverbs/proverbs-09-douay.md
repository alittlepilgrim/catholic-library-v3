# Proverbs Chapter 9

Wisdom invites all to her feast. Folly calls another way.

1 Wisdom hath built herself a house, she hath hewn her out seven pillars. ^proverbs-09-1-douay

2 She hath slain her victims, mingled her wine, and set forth her table. ^proverbs-09-2-douay

3 She hath sent her maids to invite to the tower, and to the walls of the city: ^proverbs-09-3-douay

4 Whosoever is a little one, let him come to me. And to the unwise she said: ^proverbs-09-4-douay

5 Come, eat my bread, and drink the wine which I have mingled for you. ^proverbs-09-5-douay

6 Forsake childishness, and live, and walk by the ways of prudence. ^proverbs-09-6-douay

7 He that teacheth a scorner, doth an injury to himself; and he that rebuketh a wicked man, getteth himself a blot. ^proverbs-09-7-douay

8 Rebuke not a scorner, lest he hate thee. Rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee. ^proverbs-09-8-douay

9 Give an occasion to a wise man, and wisdom shall be added to him. Teach a just man, and he shall make haste to receive it. ^proverbs-09-9-douay

10 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is prudence. ^proverbs-09-10-douay

11 For by me shall thy days be multiplied, and years of life shall be added to thee. ^proverbs-09-11-douay

12 If thou be wise, thou shalt be so to thyself: and if a scorner, thou alone shalt bear the evil. ^proverbs-09-12-douay

13 A foolish woman and clamorous, and full of allurements, and knowing nothing at all, ^proverbs-09-13-douay

14 Sat at the door of her house, upon a seat, in a high place of the city, ^proverbs-09-14-douay

15 To call them that pass by the way, and go on their journey: ^proverbs-09-15-douay

16 He that is a little one, let him turn to me. And to the fool she said: ^proverbs-09-16-douay

17 Stolen waters are sweeter, and hidden bread is more pleasant. ^proverbs-09-17-douay

18 And he did not know that giants are there, and that her guests are in the depths of hell. ^proverbs-09-18-douay
