# Proverbs Chapter 1

The use and end of the proverbs. An exhortation to flee the company of the wicked: and to hearken to the voice of wisdom.

1 The parables of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel, ^proverbs-01-1-douay

2 To know wisdom, and instruction: ^proverbs-01-2-douay

3 To understand the words of prudence: and to receive the instruction of doctrine, justice, and judgment, and equity: ^proverbs-01-3-douay

4 To give subtilty to little ones, to the young man knowledge and understanding. ^proverbs-01-4-douay

5 A wise man shall hear, and shall be wiser: and he that understandeth shall possess governments. ^proverbs-01-5-douay

6 He shall understand a parable and the interpretation, the words of the wise, and their mysterious sayings. ^proverbs-01-6-douay

7 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. Fools despise wisdom and instruction. ^proverbs-01-7-douay

8 My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother: ^proverbs-01-8-douay

9 That grace may be added to thy head, and a chain of gold to thy neck. ^proverbs-01-9-douay

10 My son, if sinners shall entice thee, consent not to them. ^proverbs-01-10-douay

11 If they shall say: Come with us, let us lie in wait for blood, let us hide snares for the innocent without cause: ^proverbs-01-11-douay

12 Let us swallow him up alive like hell, and whole as one that goeth down into the pit. ^proverbs-01-12-douay

13 We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoils. ^proverbs-01-13-douay

14 Cast in thy lot with us, let us all have one purse. ^proverbs-01-14-douay

15 My son, walk not thou with them, restrain thy foot from their paths. ^proverbs-01-15-douay

16 For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood. ^proverbs-01-16-douay

17 But a net is spread in vain before the eyes of them that have wings. ^proverbs-01-17-douay

18 And they themselves lie in wait for their own blood, and practise deceits against their own souls. ^proverbs-01-18-douay

19 So the ways of every covetous man destroy the souls of the possessors. ^proverbs-01-19-douay

20 Wisdom preacheth abroad, she uttereth her voice in the streets: ^proverbs-01-20-douay

21 At the head of multitudes she crieth out, in the entrance of the gates of the city she uttereth her words, saying: ^proverbs-01-21-douay

22 O children, how long will you love childishness, and fools covet those things which are hurtful to themselves, and the unwise hate knowledge? ^proverbs-01-22-douay

23 Turn ye at my reproof: behold I will utter my spirit to you, and will show you my words. ^proverbs-01-23-douay

24 Because I called, and you refused: I stretched out my hand, and there was none that regarded. ^proverbs-01-24-douay

25 You have despised all my counsel, and have neglected my reprehensions. ^proverbs-01-25-douay

26 I also will laugh in your destruction, and will mock when that shall come to you which you feared. ^proverbs-01-26-douay

27 When sudden calamity shall fall on you, and destruction, as a tempest, shall be at hand: when tribulation and distress shall come upon you: ^proverbs-01-27-douay

28 Then shall they call upon me, and I will not hear: they shall rise in the morning, and shall not find me: ^proverbs-01-28-douay

29 Because they have hated instruction, and received not the fear of the Lord, ^proverbs-01-29-douay

30 Nor consented to my counsel, but despised all my reproof. ^proverbs-01-30-douay

31 Therefore they shall eat the fruit of their own way, and shall be filled with their own devices. ^proverbs-01-31-douay

32 The turning away of little ones shall kill them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them. ^proverbs-01-32-douay

33 But he that shall hear me, shall rest without terror, and shall enjoy abundance, without fear of evils. ^proverbs-01-33-douay
