# Proverbs Chapter 22

More wise sayings and axioms, relating to wisdom and folly, virtue and vice.

1 A good name is better than great riches: and good favour is above silver and gold. ^proverbs-22-1-douay

2 The rich and poor have met one another: the Lord is the maker of them both. ^proverbs-22-2-douay

3 The prudent man saw the evil, and hid himself: the simple passed on, and suffered loss. ^proverbs-22-3-douay

4 The fruit of humility is the fear of the Lord, riches and glory and life. ^proverbs-22-4-douay

5 Arms and swords are in the way of the perverse: but he that keepeth his own soul, departeth far from them. ^proverbs-22-5-douay

6 It is a proverb: A young man according to his way, even when he is old, he will not depart from it. ^proverbs-22-6-douay

7 The rich ruleth over the poor: and the borrower is servant to him that lendeth. ^proverbs-22-7-douay

8 He that soweth iniquity, shall reap evils, and with the rod of his anger he shall be consumed. ^proverbs-22-8-douay

9 He that is inclined to mercy, shall be blessed: for of his bread he hath given to the poor. He that maketh presents, shall purchase victory and honour: but he carrieth away the souls of the receivers. ^proverbs-22-9-douay

10 Cast out the scoffer, and contention shall go out with him, and quarrels and reproaches shall cease. ^proverbs-22-10-douay

11 He that loveth cleanness of heart, for the grace of his lips shall have the king for his friend. ^proverbs-22-11-douay

12 The eyes of the Lord preserve knowledge: and the words of the unjust are overthrown. ^proverbs-22-12-douay

13 The slothful man saith: There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the midst of the streets. ^proverbs-22-13-douay

14 The mouth of a strange woman is a deep pit: he whom the Lord is angry with, shall fall into it. ^proverbs-22-14-douay

15 Folly is bound up in the heart of a child, and the rod of correction shall drive it away. ^proverbs-22-15-douay

16 He that oppresseth the poor, to increase his own riches, shall himself give to one that is richer, and shall be in need. ^proverbs-22-16-douay

17 Incline thy ear, and hear the words of the wise: and apply thy heart to my doctrine: ^proverbs-22-17-douay

18 Which shall be beautiful for thee, if thou keep it in thy bowels, and it shall flow in thy lips: ^proverbs-22-18-douay

19 That thy trust may be in the Lord, wherefore I have also shown it to thee this day. ^proverbs-22-19-douay

20 Behold I have described it to thee three manner of ways, in thoughts and knowledge: ^proverbs-22-20-douay

21 That I might show thee the certainty, and the words of truth, to answer out of these to them that sent thee. ^proverbs-22-21-douay

22 Do no violence to the poor, because he is poor: and do not oppress the needy in the gate: ^proverbs-22-22-douay

23 Because the Lord will judge his cause: and will afflict them that have afflicted his soul. ^proverbs-22-23-douay

24 Be not a friend to an angry man, and do not walk with a furious man: ^proverbs-22-24-douay

25 Lest perhaps thou learn his ways, and take scandal to thy soul. ^proverbs-22-25-douay

26 Be not with them that fasten down their hands, and that offer themselves sureties for debts: ^proverbs-22-26-douay

27 For if thou have not wherewith to restore, what cause is there that he should take the covering from thy bed? ^proverbs-22-27-douay

28 Pass not beyond the ancient bounds which thy fathers have set. ^proverbs-22-28-douay

29 Hast thou seen a man swift in his work? he shall stand before kings, and shall not be before those that are obscure. ^proverbs-22-29-douay
