# 1 Kings Chapter 4

Solomon's chief officers. His riches and wisdom.

1 And king Solomon reigned over all Israel: ^1-kings-04-1-douay

2 And these were the princes which he had: Azarias, the son of Sadoc, the priest: ^1-kings-04-2-douay

3 Elihoreph, and Ahia, the sons of Sisa, scribes: Josaphat, the son of Ahilud, recorder: ^1-kings-04-3-douay

4 Banaias, the son of Joiada, over the army: and Sadoc, and Abiathar, priests. **Abiathar...** By this it appears that Abiathar was not altogether deposed from the high priesthood; but only banished to his country house, and by that means excluded from the exercise of his functions. ^1-kings-04-4-douay

5 Azarias, the son of Nathan, over them that were about the king: Zabud, the son of Nathan, the priest, the king's friend: ^1-kings-04-5-douay

6 And Ahisar, governor of the house: and Adoniram, the son of Abda, over the tribute. ^1-kings-04-6-douay

7 And Solomon had twelve governors over all Israel, who provided victuals for the king and for his house hold: for every one provided necessaries, each man his month in the year. ^1-kings-04-7-douay

8 And these are their names: Benhur, in mount Ephraim. ^1-kings-04-8-douay

9 Bendecar, in Macces, and in Salebim, and in Bethsames, and in Elon, and in Bethanan. ^1-kings-04-9-douay

10 Benhesed, in Aruboth: his was Socho, and all the land of Epher. ^1-kings-04-10-douay

11 Benabinadab, to whom belonged all Nephath-Dor: he had Tapheth, the daughter of Solomon, to wife. ^1-kings-04-11-douay

12 Bana, the son of Ahilud, who governed Thanac, and Mageddo, and all Bethsan, which is by Sarthana, beneath Jezrael, from Bethsan unto Abelmehula, over against Jecmaan. ^1-kings-04-12-douay

13 Bengaber, in Ramoth Galaad: he had the town of Jair, the son of Manasses, in Galaad: he was chief in all the country of Argob, which is in Basan, threescore great cities with walls, and brazen bolts. ^1-kings-04-13-douay

14 Ahinadab, the son of Addo, was chief in Manaim. ^1-kings-04-14-douay

15 Achimaas, in Nephthali: he also had Basemath, the daughter of Solomon, to wife. ^1-kings-04-15-douay

16 Baana, the son of Husi, in Aser, and in Baloth. ^1-kings-04-16-douay

17 Josaphat, the son of Pharue, in Issachar. ^1-kings-04-17-douay

18 Semei, the son of Ela, in Benjamin. ^1-kings-04-18-douay

19 Gaber, the son of Uri, in the land of Galaad, in the land of Sehon, the king of the Amorrhites, and of Og, the king of Basan, over all that were in that land. ^1-kings-04-19-douay

20 Juda and Israel were innumerable, as the sand of the sea in multitude; eating and drinking, and rejoicing. ^1-kings-04-20-douay

21 And Solomon had under him all the kingdoms, from the river to the land of the Philistines, even to the border of Egypt: and they brought him presents, and served him all the days of his life. **The river...** Euphrates. ^1-kings-04-21-douay

22 And the provision of Solomon, for each day, was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal; ^1-kings-04-22-douay

23 Ten fat oxen, and twenty out of the pastures, and a hundred rams; besides venison of harts, roes, and buffles, and fatted fowls. ^1-kings-04-23-douay

24 For he had all the country which was beyond the river, from Thaphsa to Gazan, and all the kings of those countries: and he had peace on every side round about. ^1-kings-04-24-douay

25 And Juda, and Israel, dwelt without any fear, every one under his vine, and under his fig tree, from Dan to Bersabee, all the days of Solomon. ^1-kings-04-25-douay

26 And Solomon had forty thousand stalls of chariot horses, and twelve thousand for the saddle. ^1-kings-04-26-douay

27 And the foresaid governors of the king fed them; and they furnished the necessaries also for king Solomon's table, with great care, in their time. ^1-kings-04-27-douay

28 They brought barley also, and straw for the horses and beasts, to the place where the king was, according as it was appointed them. ^1-kings-04-28-douay

29 And God gave to Solomon wisdom, and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, as the sand that is on the sea shore. ^1-kings-04-29-douay

30 And the wisdom of Solomon surpassed the wisdom of all the Orientals, and of the Egyptians; ^1-kings-04-30-douay

31 And he was wiser than all men: wiser than Ethan, the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Dorda, the sons of Mahol, and he was renowned in all nations round about. ^1-kings-04-31-douay

32 Solomon also spoke three thousand parables: and his poems were a thousand and five. **Three thousand parables, etc...** These works are all lost, excepting some part of the parables extant in the [[proverbs-00-douay|book of Proverbs]]; and his chief poem called the [[song-of-songs-00-douay|Song of Songs]]. ^1-kings-04-32-douay

33 And he treated about trees, from the cedar that is in Libanus, unto the hyssop that cometh out of the wall: and he discoursed of beasts, and of fowls, and of creeping things, and of fishes. ^1-kings-04-33-douay

34 And they came from all nations to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and from all the kings of the earth, who heard of his wisdom. ^1-kings-04-34-douay
