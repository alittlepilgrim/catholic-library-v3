# Nehemiah Chapter 11

Who were the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and the other cities.

1 And the princes of the people dwelt at Jerusalem: but the rest of the people cast lots, to take one part in ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts in the other cities. ^nehemiah-11-1-douay

2 And the people blessed all the men that willingly offered themselves to dwell in Jerusalem. ^nehemiah-11-2-douay

3 These therefore are the chief men of the province, who dwelt in Jerusalem, and in the cities of Juda. And every one dwelt in his possession, in their cities: Israel, the priests, the Levites, the Nathinites, and the children of the servants of Solomon. ^nehemiah-11-3-douay

4 And in Jerusalem there dwelt some of the children of Juda, and some of the children of Benjamin: of the children of Juda, Athaias the son of Aziam, the son of Zacharias, the son of Amarias, the son of Saphatias, the son of Malaleel: of the sons of Phares, ^nehemiah-11-4-douay

5 Maasia the son of Baruch, the son of Cholhoza, the son of Hazia, the son of Adaia, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zacharias, the son of the Silonite: ^nehemiah-11-5-douay

6 All these the sons of Phares, who dwelt in Jerusalem, were four hundred sixty-eight valiant men. ^nehemiah-11-6-douay

7 And these are the children of Benjamin: Sellum the son of Mosollam, the son of Joed, the son of Phadaia, the son of Colaia, the son of Masia, the son of Etheel, the son of Isaia. ^nehemiah-11-7-douay

8 And after him Gebbai, Sellai, nine hundred twenty-eight. ^nehemiah-11-8-douay

9 And Joel the son of Zechri their ruler, and Judas the son of Senua was second over the city. ^nehemiah-11-9-douay

10 And of the priests Idaia the son of Joarib, Jachin, ^nehemiah-11-10-douay

11 Saraia the son of Helcias, the son of Mosollam, the son of Sadoc, the son of Meraioth, the son of Achitob the prince of the house of God, ^nehemiah-11-11-douay

12 And their brethren that do the works of the temple: eight hundred twenty-two. And Adaia the son of Jeroham, the son of Phelelia, the son of Amsi, the son of Zacharias, the son of Pheshur, the son of Melchias, ^nehemiah-11-12-douay

13 And his brethren the chiefs of the fathers: two hundred forty-two. And Amassai the son of Azreel, the son of Ahazi, the son of Mosollamoth, the son of Emmer, ^nehemiah-11-13-douay

14 And their brethren who were very mighty, a hundred twenty-eight: and their ruler Zabdiel son of the mighty. ^nehemiah-11-14-douay

15 And of the Levites Semeia the son of Hasub, the son of Azaricam, the son of Hasabia, the son of Boni, ^nehemiah-11-15-douay

16 And Sabathai and Jozabed, who were over all the outward business of the house of God, of the princes of the Levites, ^nehemiah-11-16-douay

17 And Mathania the son of Micha, the son of Zebedei, the son of Asaph, was the principal man to praise, and to give glory in prayer, and Becbecia, the second, one of his brethren, and Abda the son of Samua, the son of Galal, the son of Idithun. ^nehemiah-11-17-douay

18 All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred eighty-four. ^nehemiah-11-18-douay

19 And the porters, Accub, Telmon, and their brethren, who kept the doors: a hundred seventy-two. ^nehemiah-11-19-douay

20 And the rest of Israel, the priests and the Levites were in all the cities of Juda, every man in his possession. ^nehemiah-11-20-douay

21 And the Nathinites, that dwelt in Ophel, and Siaha, and Gaspha of the Nathinites. ^nehemiah-11-21-douay

22 And the overseer of the Levites in Jerusalem, was Azzi the son of Bani, the son of Hasabia, the son of Mathania, the son of Micha. Of the sons of Asaph, were the singing men in the ministry of the house of God. ^nehemiah-11-22-douay

23 For the king's commandment was concerning them, and an order among the singing men day by day. ^nehemiah-11-23-douay

24 And Phathahia the son of Mesezebel of the children of Zara the son of Juda was at the hand of the king, in all matters concerning the people, ^nehemiah-11-24-douay

25 And in the houses through all their countries. Of the children of Juda some dwelt at Cariath-Arbe, and in the villages thereof: and at Dibon, and in the villages thereof: and at Cabseel, and in the villages thereof. ^nehemiah-11-25-douay

26 And at Jesue, and at Molada, and at Bethphaleth, ^nehemiah-11-26-douay

27 And at Hasersuel, and at Bersabee, and in the villages thereof, ^nehemiah-11-27-douay

28 And at Siceleg, and at Mochona, and in the villages thereof, ^nehemiah-11-28-douay

29 And at Remmon, and at Saraa, and at Jerimuth, ^nehemiah-11-29-douay

30 Zanoa, Odollam, and in their villages, at Lachis and its dependencies, and at Azeca and the villages thereof. And they dwelt from Bersabee unto the valley of Ennom. ^nehemiah-11-30-douay

31 And the children of Benjamin, from Geba, at Mechmas, and at Hai, and at Bethel, and in the villages thereof, ^nehemiah-11-31-douay

32 At Anathoth, Nob, Anania, ^nehemiah-11-32-douay

33 Asor, Rama, Gethaim, ^nehemiah-11-33-douay

34 Hadid, Seboim, and Neballat, Lod, ^nehemiah-11-34-douay

35 And Ono the valley of craftsmen. ^nehemiah-11-35-douay

36 And of the Levites were portions of Juda and Benjamin. ^nehemiah-11-36-douay
