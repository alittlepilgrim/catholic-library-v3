# Nehemiah Chapter 12

The priests, and Levites that came up with Zorobabel. The succession of high priests: the solemnity of the dedication of the wall.

1 Now these are the priests and the Levites, that went up with Zorobabel the son of Salathiel, and Josue: Saraia, Jeremiah, Esdras, ^nehemiah-12-1-douay

2 Amaria, Melluch, Hattus, ^nehemiah-12-2-douay

3 Sebenias, Rheum, Merimuth, ^nehemiah-12-3-douay

4 Addo, Genthon, Abia, ^nehemiah-12-4-douay

5 Miamin, Madia, Belga, ^nehemiah-12-5-douay

6 Semeia, and Joiarib, Idaia, Sellum Amoc, Helcias, ^nehemiah-12-6-douay

7 Idaia. These were the chief of the priests, and of their brethren in the days of Josue. ^nehemiah-12-7-douay

8 And the Levites, Jesua, Bennui, Cedmihel, Sarebia, Juda, Mathanias, they and their brethren were over the hymns: ^nehemiah-12-8-douay

9 And Becbecia, and Hanni, and their brethren every one in his office. ^nehemiah-12-9-douay

10 And Josue begot Joacim, and Joacim begot Eliasib, and Eliasib begot Joiada, ^nehemiah-12-10-douay

11 And Joiada begot Jonathan and Jonathan begot Jeddoa. ^nehemiah-12-11-douay

12 And in the days of Joacim the priests and heads of the families were: Of Saraia, Maraia: of Jeremiah, Hanania: ^nehemiah-12-12-douay

13 Of Esdras, Mosollam: and of Amaria, Johanan: ^nehemiah-12-13-douay

14 Of Milicho, Jonathan: of Sebenia, Joseph: ^nehemiah-12-14-douay

15 Of Haram, Edna: of Maraioth, Helci: ^nehemiah-12-15-douay

16 Of Adaia, Zacharia: of Genthon, Mosollam: ^nehemiah-12-16-douay

17 Of Abia, Zechri: of Miamin and Moadia, Phelti: ^nehemiah-12-17-douay

18 Of Belga, Sammua of Semaia, Jonathan: ^nehemiah-12-18-douay

19 Of Joiarib, Mathanai: of Jodaia, Azzi: ^nehemiah-12-19-douay

20 Of Sellai, Celai: of Amoc, Heber: ^nehemiah-12-20-douay

21 Of Helcias, Hasebia: of Idaia, Nathanael. ^nehemiah-12-21-douay

22 The Levites the chiefs of the families in the days of Eliasib, and Joiada, and Johanan, and Jeddoa, were recorded, and the priests in the reign of Darius the Persian. ^nehemiah-12-22-douay

23 The sons of Levi, heads of the families were written in the book of Chronicles, even unto the days of Jonathan the son of Eliasib. ^nehemiah-12-23-douay

24 Now the chief of the Levites were Hasebia, Serebia, and Josue the son of Cedmihel: and their brethren by their courses, to praise and to give thanks according to the commandment of David the man of God, and to wait equally in order. ^nehemiah-12-24-douay

25 Mathania, and Becbecia, Obedia, and Mosollam, Telmon, Accub, were keepers of the gates and of the entrances before the gates. ^nehemiah-12-25-douay

26 These were in the days of Joacim the son of Josue, the son of Josedec, and in the days of Nehemias the governor, and of Esdras the priest and scribe. ^nehemiah-12-26-douay

27 And at the dedication of the wall of Jerusalem they sought the Levites out of all their places, to bring them to Jerusalem, and to keep the dedication, and to rejoice with thanksgiving, and with singing, and with cymbals, and psalteries and harps. ^nehemiah-12-27-douay

28 And the sons of the singing men were gathered together out of the plain country about Jerusalem, and out of the villages of Nethuphati, ^nehemiah-12-28-douay

29 And from the house of Galgal, and from the countries of Geba and Azmaveth: for the singing men had built themselves villages round about Jerusalem. ^nehemiah-12-29-douay

30 And the priests and the Levites were purified, and they purified the people, and the gates, and the wall. ^nehemiah-12-30-douay

31 And I made the princes of Juda go up upon the wall, and I appointed two great choirs to give praise. And they went on the right hand upon the wall toward the dung gate. ^nehemiah-12-31-douay

32 And after them went Osaias, and half of the princes of Juda, ^nehemiah-12-32-douay

33 And Azarias, Esdras, and Mosollam, Judas, and Benjamin, and Semeia, and Jeremiah. ^nehemiah-12-33-douay

34 And of the sons of the priests with trumpets, Zacharias the son of Jonathan, the son of Semeia, the son of Mathania, the son of Michaia, the son of Zechur, the son of Asaph, ^nehemiah-12-34-douay

35 And his brethren Semeia, and Azareel, Malalai, Galalai, Maai, Nathanael, and Judas, and Hanani, with the musical instruments of David the man of God: and Esdras the scribe before them at the fountain gate. ^nehemiah-12-35-douay

36 And they went up over against them by the stairs of the city of David, at the going up of the wall of the house of David, and to the water gate eastward: ^nehemiah-12-36-douay

37 And the second choir of them that gave thanks went on the opposite side, and I after them, and the half of the people upon the wall, and upon the tower of the furnaces, even to the broad wall, ^nehemiah-12-37-douay

38 And above the gate of Ephraim, and above the old gate, and above the fish gate and the tower of Hananeel, and the tower of Emath, and even to the flock gate: and they stood still in the watch gate. ^nehemiah-12-38-douay

39 And the two choirs of them that gave praise stood still at the house of God, and I and the half of the magistrates with me. ^nehemiah-12-39-douay

40 And the priests, Eliachim, Maasia, Miamin, Michea, Elioenai, Zacharia, Hanania with trumpets, ^nehemiah-12-40-douay

41 And Maasia, and Semeia, and Eleazar, and Azzi, and Johanan, and Melchia, and Elam, and Ezer. And the singers sung loud, and Jezraia was their overseer: ^nehemiah-12-41-douay

42 And they sacrificed on that day great sacrifices, and they rejoiced: for God had made them joyful with great joy: their wives also and their children rejoiced, and the joy of Jerusalem was heard afar off. ^nehemiah-12-42-douay

43 They appointed also in that day men over the storehouses of the treasure, for the libations, and for the firstfruits, and for the tithes, that the rulers of the city might bring them in by them in honour of thanksgiving, for the priests and Levites: for Juda was joyful in the priests and Levites that assisted. ^nehemiah-12-43-douay

44 And they kept the watch of their God, and the observance of expiation, and the singing men, and the porters, according to the commandment of David, and of Solomon his son. ^nehemiah-12-44-douay

45 For in the days of David and Asaph from the beginning there were chief singers appointed, to praise with canticles, and give thanks to God. ^nehemiah-12-45-douay

46 And all Israel, in the days of Zorobabel, and in the days of Nehemias gave portions to the singing men, and to the porters, day by day, and they sanctified the Levites, and the Levites sanctified the sons of Aaron. **Sanctified...** That is, they gave them that which by the law was set aside, and sanctified for their use. ^nehemiah-12-46-douay
