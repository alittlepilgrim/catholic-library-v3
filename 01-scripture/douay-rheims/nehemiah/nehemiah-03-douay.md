# Nehemiah Chapter 3

They begin to build the walls: the names and order of the builders.

1 Then Eliasib the high priest arose, and his brethren the priests, and they built the flock gate: they sanctified it, and set up the doors thereof, even unto the tower of a hundred cubits they sanctified it unto the tower of Hananeel. ^nehemiah-03-1-douay

2 And next to him the men of Jericho built: and next to them built Zachur the son of Amri. ^nehemiah-03-2-douay

3 But the fish gate the sons of Asnaa built: they covered it, and set up the doors thereof, and the locks, and the bars. And next to them built Marimuth the son of Urias the son of Accus. ^nehemiah-03-3-douay

4 And next to him built Mosollam the son of Barachias, the son of Merezebel, and next to them built Sadoc the son of Baana. ^nehemiah-03-4-douay

5 And next to them the Thecuites built: but their great men did not put their necks to the work of their Lord. ^nehemiah-03-5-douay

6 And Joiada the son of Phasea, and Mosollam the son of Besodia built the old gate: they covered it and set up the doors thereof, and the locks, and the bars. ^nehemiah-03-6-douay

7 And next to them built Meltias the Gabaonite, and Jadon the Meronathite, the men of Gabaon and Maspha, for the governor that was in the country beyond the river. ^nehemiah-03-7-douay

8 And next to him built Eziel the son of Araia the goldsmith: and next to him built Ananias the son of the perfumer: and they left Jerusalem unto the wall of the broad street. ^nehemiah-03-8-douay

9 And next to him built Raphaia the son of Hur, lord of the street of Jerusalem. ^nehemiah-03-9-douay

10 And next to him Jedaia the son of Haromaph over against his own house: and next to him built Hattus the son of Hasebonia. ^nehemiah-03-10-douay

11 Melchias the son of Herem, and Hasub the son of Phahath Moab, built half the street, and the tower of the furnaces. ^nehemiah-03-11-douay

12 And next to him built Sellum the son of Alohes, lord of half the street of Jerusalem, he and his daughters. ^nehemiah-03-12-douay

13 And the gate of the valley Hanun built, and the inhabitants of Zanoe: they built it, and set up the doors thereof, and the locks, and the bars, and a thousand cubits in the wall unto the gate of the dunghill. ^nehemiah-03-13-douay

14 And the gate of the dunghill Melchias the son of Rechab built, lord of the street of Bethacharam: he built it, and set up the doors thereof, and the locks, and the bars. ^nehemiah-03-14-douay

15 And the gate of the fountain, Sellum, the son of Cholhoza, built, lord of the street of Maspha: he built it, and covered it, and set up the doors thereof, and the locks, and the bars, and the walls of the pool of Siloe unto the king's guard, and unto the steps that go down from the city of David. ^nehemiah-03-15-douay

16 After him built Nehemias the son of Azboc, lord of half the street of Bethsur, as far as over against the sepulchre of David, and to the pool, that was built with great labour, and to the house of the mighty. ^nehemiah-03-16-douay

17 After him built the Levites, Rehum the son of Benni. After him built Hasebias, lord of half the street of Ceila in his own street. ^nehemiah-03-17-douay

18 After him built their brethren Bavai the son of Enadad, lord of half Ceila. ^nehemiah-03-18-douay

19 And next to him Aser the son of Josue, lord of Maspha, built another measure, over against the going up of the strong corner. ^nehemiah-03-19-douay

20 After him in the mount Baruch the son of Zachai built another measure, from the corner to the door of the house of Eliasib the high priest. ^nehemiah-03-20-douay

21 After him Merimuth the son of Urias the son of Haccus, built another measure, from the door of the house of Eliasib, to the end of the house of Eliasib. ^nehemiah-03-21-douay

22 And after him built the priests, the men of the plains of the Jordan. ^nehemiah-03-22-douay

23 After him built Benjamin and Hasub, over against their own house: and after him built Azarias the son of Maasias the son of Ananias over against his house. ^nehemiah-03-23-douay

24 After him built Bennui the son of Hanadad another measure, from the house of Azarias unto the bending, and unto the corner. ^nehemiah-03-24-douay

25 Phalel, the son of Ozi, over against the bending and the tower, which lieth out from the king's high house, that is, in the court of the prison: after him Phadaia the son of Pharos. ^nehemiah-03-25-douay

26 And the Nathinites dwelt in Ophel, as far as over against the water gate toward the east, and the tower that stood out. ^nehemiah-03-26-douay

27 After him the Thecuites built another measure over against, from the great tower that standeth out unto the wall of the temple. ^nehemiah-03-27-douay

28 And upward from the horse gate the priests built, every man over against his house. ^nehemiah-03-28-douay

29 After them built Sadoc the son of Emmer over against his house. And after him built Semaia the son of Sechenias, keeper of the east gate. ^nehemiah-03-29-douay

30 After him built Hanania the son of Selemia, and Hanun the sixth son of Seleph, another measure: after him built Mosollam the son of Barachias over against his treasury. After him Melcias the goldsmith's son built unto the house of the Nathinites, and of the sellers of small wares, over against the judgment gate, and unto the chamber of the corner. ^nehemiah-03-30-douay

31 And within the chamber of the corner of the flock gate, the goldsmiths and the merchants built. ^nehemiah-03-31-douay
