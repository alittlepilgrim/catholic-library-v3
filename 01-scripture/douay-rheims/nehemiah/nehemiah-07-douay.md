# Nehemiah Chapter 7

Nehemiah appoints watchmen in Jerusalem. The list of those who came first from Babylon.

1 Now after the wall was built, and I had set up the doors, and numbered the porters and singing men, and Levites: ^nehemiah-07-1-douay

2 I commanded Hanani my brother, and Hananias ruler of the house of Jerusalem, (for he seemed as a sincere man, and one that feared God above the rest), ^nehemiah-07-2-douay

3 And I said to them: Let not the gates of Jerusalem be opened till the sun be hot. And while they were yet standing by the gates were shut, and barred: and I set watchmen of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, every one by their courses, and every man over against his house. ^nehemiah-07-3-douay

4 And the city was very wide and great, and the people few in the midst thereof, and the houses were not built. ^nehemiah-07-4-douay

5 But God had put in my heart, and I assembled the princes and magistrates, and common people, to number them: and I found a book of the number of them who came up at first and therein it was found written: ^nehemiah-07-5-douay

6 These are the children of the province, who came up from the captivity of them that had been carried away, whom Nabuchodonosor the king of Babylon had carried away, and who returned into Judea, every one into his own city. ^nehemiah-07-6-douay

7 Who came with Zorobabel, Josue, Nehemias, Azarias, Raamias, Nahamani, Mardochai, Belsam, Mespharath, Begoia, Nahum, Baana. The number of the men of the people of Israel: ^nehemiah-07-7-douay

8 The children of Pharos, two thousand one hundred seventy-two. ^nehemiah-07-8-douay

9 The children of Sephatia, three hundred seventy-two. ^nehemiah-07-9-douay

10 The children of Area, six hundred fifty-two. ^nehemiah-07-10-douay

11 The children of Phahath Moab of the children of Josue and Joab, two thousand eight hundred eighteen. ^nehemiah-07-11-douay

12 The children of Elam, one thousand two hundred fifty-four. ^nehemiah-07-12-douay

13 The children of Zethua, eight hundred forty-five. ^nehemiah-07-13-douay

14 The children of Zachai, seven hundred sixty. ^nehemiah-07-14-douay

15 The children of Bannui, six hundred forty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-15-douay

16 The children of Bebai, six hundred twenty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-16-douay

17 The children of Azgad, two thousand three hundred twenty-two. ^nehemiah-07-17-douay

18 The children of Adonicam, six hundred sixty-seven. ^nehemiah-07-18-douay

19 The children of Beguai, two thousand sixty-seven. ^nehemiah-07-19-douay

20 The children of Adin, six hundred fifty-five. ^nehemiah-07-20-douay

21 The children of Ater, children of Hezechias, ninety-eight. ^nehemiah-07-21-douay

22 The children of Hasem, three hundred twenty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-22-douay

23 The children of Besai, three hundred twenty-four. ^nehemiah-07-23-douay

24 The children of Hareph, a hundred and twelve. ^nehemiah-07-24-douay

25 The children of Gabaon, ninety-five. ^nehemiah-07-25-douay

26 The children of Bethlehem, and Netupha, a hundred eighty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-26-douay

27 The men of Anathoth, a hundred twenty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-27-douay

28 The men of Bethazmoth, forty-two. ^nehemiah-07-28-douay

29 The men of Cariathiarim, Cephira, and Beroth, seven hundred forty-three. ^nehemiah-07-29-douay

30 The men of Rama and Geba, six hundred twenty-one. ^nehemiah-07-30-douay

31 The men of Machmas, a hundred twenty-two. ^nehemiah-07-31-douay

32 The men of Bethel and Hai, a hundred twenty-three. ^nehemiah-07-32-douay

33 The men of the other Nebo, fifty-two. ^nehemiah-07-33-douay

34 The men of the other Elam, one thousand two hundred fifty-four. ^nehemiah-07-34-douay

35 The children of Harem, three hundred and twenty. ^nehemiah-07-35-douay

36 The children of Jericho, three hundred forty-five. ^nehemiah-07-36-douay

37 The children of Lod, of Hadid and Ono, seven hundred twenty-one. ^nehemiah-07-37-douay

38 The children of Senaa, three thousand nine hundred thirty. ^nehemiah-07-38-douay

39 The priests: the children of Idaia in the house of Josue, nine hundred and seventy-three. ^nehemiah-07-39-douay

40 The children of Emmer, one thousand fifty-two. ^nehemiah-07-40-douay

41 The children of Phashur, one thousand two hundred forty-seven. ^nehemiah-07-41-douay

42 The children of Arem, one thousand and seventeen. The Levites: ^nehemiah-07-42-douay

43 The children of Josue and Cedmihel, the sons ^nehemiah-07-43-douay

44 Of Oduia, seventy-four. The singing men: ^nehemiah-07-44-douay

45 The children of Asaph, a hundred forty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-45-douay

46 The porters: the children of Sellum, the children of Ater, the children of Telmon, the children of Accub, the children of Hatita, the children of Sobai: a hundred thirty-eight. ^nehemiah-07-46-douay

47 The Nathinites: the children of Soha, the children of Hasupha, the children of Tebbaoth, ^nehemiah-07-47-douay

48 The children of Ceros, the children of Siaa, the children of Phadon, the children of Lebana, the children of Hagaba, the children of Selmai, ^nehemiah-07-48-douay

49 The children of Hanan, the children of Geddel, the children of Gaher, ^nehemiah-07-49-douay

50 The children of Raaia, the children of Rasin, the children of Necoda, ^nehemiah-07-50-douay

51 The children of Gezem, the children of Asa, the children of Phasea, ^nehemiah-07-51-douay

52 The children of Besai, the children of Munim, the children of Nephussim, ^nehemiah-07-52-douay

53 The children of Bacbuc, the children of Hacupha, the children of Harhur, ^nehemiah-07-53-douay

54 The children of Besloth, the children of Mahida, the children of Harsa, ^nehemiah-07-54-douay

55 The children of Bercos, the children of Sisara, the children of Thema, ^nehemiah-07-55-douay

56 The children of Nasia, the children of Hatipha, ^nehemiah-07-56-douay

57 The children of the servants of Solomon, the children of Sothai, the children of Sophereth, the children of Pharida, ^nehemiah-07-57-douay

58 The children of Jahala, the children of Darcon, the children of Jeddel, ^nehemiah-07-58-douay

59 The children of Saphatia, the children of Hatil, the children of Phochereth, who was born of Sabaim, the son of Amon. ^nehemiah-07-59-douay

60 All the Nathinites, and the children of the servants of Solomon, three hundred ninety-two. ^nehemiah-07-60-douay

61 And these are they that came up from Telmela, Thelharsa, Cherub, Addon, and Emmer: and could not show the house of their fathers, nor their seed, whether they were of Israel. ^nehemiah-07-61-douay

62 The children of Dalaia, the children of Tobia, the children of Necoda, six hundred forty-two. ^nehemiah-07-62-douay

63 And of the priests, the children of Habia, the children of Accos, the children of Berzellai, who took a wife of the daughters of Berzellai the Galaadite, and he was called by their name. ^nehemiah-07-63-douay

64 These sought their writing in the record, and found it not: and they were cast out of the priesthood. ^nehemiah-07-64-douay

65 And Athersatha said to them, that they should not eat of the holies of holies, until there stood up a priest learned and skilful. ^nehemiah-07-65-douay

66 All the multitude as it were one man, forty-two thousand three hundred sixty, ^nehemiah-07-66-douay

67 Beside their menservants and womenservants, who were seven thousand three hundred thirty-seven: and among them singing men, and singing women, two hundred forty-five. ^nehemiah-07-67-douay

68 Their horses, seven hundred thirty-six: their mules two hundred forty-five. ^nehemiah-07-68-douay

69 Their camels, four hundred thirty-five, their asses, six thousand seven hundred and twenty. ^nehemiah-07-69-douay

*Hitherto is related what was written in the record. From this place forward goeth on the history of Nehemias.*

70 And some of the heads of the families gave unto the work. Athersatha gave into the treasure a thousand drams of gold, fifty bowls, and five hundred and thirty garments for priests. **Athersatha...** That is, Nehemias; as appears from chap. 12. Either that he was so called at the court of the king of Persia, where he was cupbearer: or that, as some think, this name signifies governor; and he was at that time governor of Judea. ^nehemiah-07-70-douay

71 And some of the heads of families gave to the treasure of the work, twenty thousand drams of gold, and two thousand two hundred pounds of silver. ^nehemiah-07-71-douay

72 And that which the rest of the people gave, was twenty thousand drams of gold, and two thousand pounds of silver, and sixty-seven garments for priests. ^nehemiah-07-72-douay

73 And the priests, and the Levites, and the porters, and the singing men, and the rest of the common people, and the Nathinites, and all Israel dwelt in their cities. ^nehemiah-07-73-douay
