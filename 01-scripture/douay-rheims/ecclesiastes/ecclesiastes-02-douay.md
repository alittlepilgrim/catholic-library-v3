# Ecclesiastes Chapter 2

The vanity of pleasures, riches, and worldly labours.

1 I said in my heart: I will go, and abound with delights, and enjoy good things. And I saw that this also was vanity. ^ecclesiastes-02-1-douay

2 Laughter I counted error: and to mirth I said: Why art thou vainly deceived? ^ecclesiastes-02-2-douay

3 I thought in my heart, to withdraw my flesh from wine, that I might turn my mind to wisdom, and might avoid folly, till I might see what was profitable for the children of men: and what they ought to do under the sun, all the days of their life. ^ecclesiastes-02-3-douay

4 I made me great works, I built me houses, and planted vineyards, ^ecclesiastes-02-4-douay

5 I made gardens, and orchards, and set them with trees of all kinds, ^ecclesiastes-02-5-douay

6 And I made me ponds of water, to water therewith the wood of the young trees, ^ecclesiastes-02-6-douay

7 I got me menservants, and maidservants, and had a great family: and herds of oxen, and great flocks of sheep, above all that were before me in Jerusalem: ^ecclesiastes-02-7-douay

8 I heaped together for myself silver and gold, and the wealth of kings, and provinces: I made me singing men, and singing women, and the delights of the sons of men, cups and vessels to serve to pour out wine: ^ecclesiastes-02-8-douay

9 And I surpassed in riches all that were before me in Jerusalem: my wisdom also remained with me. ^ecclesiastes-02-9-douay

10 And whatsoever my eyes desired, I refused them not: and I withheld not my heart from enjoying every pleasure, and delighting itself in the things which I had prepared: and esteemed this my portion, to make use of my own labour. ^ecclesiastes-02-10-douay

11 And when I turned myself to all the works which my hands had wrought, and to the labours wherein I had laboured in vain, I saw in all things vanity, and vexation of mind, and that nothing was lasting under the sun. ^ecclesiastes-02-11-douay

12 I passed further to behold wisdom, and errors and folly, (What is man, said I that he can follow the King his maker?) ^ecclesiastes-02-12-douay

13 And I saw that wisdom excelled folly, as much as light differeth from darkness. ^ecclesiastes-02-13-douay

14 The eyes of a wise man are in his head: the fool walketh in darkness: and I learned that they were to die both alike. ^ecclesiastes-02-14-douay

15 And I said in my heart: If the death of the fool and mine shall be one, what doth it avail me, that I have applied myself more to the study of wisdom? And speaking with my own mind, I perceived that this also was vanity. ^ecclesiastes-02-15-douay

16 For there shall be no remembrance of the wise no more than of the fool forever, and the times to come shall cover all things together with oblivion: the learned dieth in like manner as the unlearned. ^ecclesiastes-02-16-douay

17 And therefore I was weary of my life, when I saw that all things under the sun are evil, and all vanity and vexation of spirit. ^ecclesiastes-02-17-douay

18 Again I hated all my application wherewith I had earnestly laboured under the sun, being like to have an heir after me, ^ecclesiastes-02-18-douay

19 Whom I know not whether he will be a wise man or a fool, and he shall have rule over all my labours with which I have laboured and been solicitous: and is there anything so vain? ^ecclesiastes-02-19-douay

20 Wherefore I left off and my heart renounced labouring anymore under the sun. ^ecclesiastes-02-20-douay

21 For when a man laboureth in wisdom, and knowledge, and carefulness, he leaveth what he hath gotten to an idle man: so this also is vanity, and a great evil. ^ecclesiastes-02-21-douay

22 For what profit shall a man have of all his labour, and vexation of spirit, with which he hath been tormented under the sun? ^ecclesiastes-02-22-douay

23 All his days are full of sorrows and miseries, even in the night he doth not rest in mind: and is not this vanity? ^ecclesiastes-02-23-douay

24 Is it not better to eat and drink, and to show his soul good things of his labours? and this is from the hand of God. ^ecclesiastes-02-24-douay

25 Who shall so feast and abound with delights as I? ^ecclesiastes-02-25-douay

26 God hath given to a man that is good in his sight, wisdom, and knowledge, and joy: but to the sinner he hath given vexation, and superfluous care, to heap up and to gather together, and to give it to him that hath pleased God: but this also is vanity, and a fruitless solicitude of the mind. ^ecclesiastes-02-26-douay
