# Esther

This book takes its name from Queen Esther, whose history is here recorded. The general opinion of almost all commentators on the [[bible-douay|Holy Scriptures]] makes Mardochai the writer of it: which also may be collected below from [[esther-09-douay|9:20]]. (*For more information, see the article* ESTHER *in the Catholic Encyclopedia*.)

[[esther-01-douay|**Chapter 1.**]] King Assuerus maketh a great feast. Queen Vasthi being sent for refuseth to come: for which disobedience she is deposed.

[[esther-02-douay|**Chapter 2.**]] Esther is advanced to be queen. Mardochai detecteth a plot against the king.

[[esther-03-douay|**Chapter 3.**]] Aman, advanced by the king, is offended at Mardochai, and therefore procureth the king's decree to destroy the whole nation of the Jews.

[[esther-04-douay|**Chapter 4.**]] Mardochai desireth Esther to petition the king for the Jews. They join in fasting and prayer.

[[esther-05-douay|**Chapter 5.**]] Esther is graciously received: she inviteth the king and Aman to dinner, Aman prepareth a gibbet for Mardochai.

[[esther-06-douay|**Chapter 6.**]] The king hearing of the good service done him by Mardochai, commandeth Aman to honour him next to the king, which he performeth.

[[esther-07-douay|**Chapter 7.**]] Esther's petition for herself and her people: Aman is hanged upon the gibbet he had prepared for Mardochai.

[[esther-08-douay|**Chapter 8.**]] Mardochai is advanced: Aman's letters are reversed.

[[esther-09-douay|**Chapter 9.**]] The Jews kill their enemies that would have killed them. The days of Phurim are appointed to be kept holy.

[[esther-10-douay|**Chapter 10.**]] Assuerus' greatness. Mardochai's dignity.

[[esther-11-douay|**Chapter 11.**]] The dream of Mardochai, which in the ancient Greek and Latin Bibles was into the beginning of the book, but was detached by St. Jerome, and put in this place.

[[esther-12-douay|**Chapter 12.**]] Mardochai detects the conspiracy of the two eunuchs.

[[esther-13-douay|**Chapter 13.**]] A copy of a letter sent by Aman to destroy the Jews. Mardochai's prayer for the people.

[[esther-14-douay|**Chapter 14.**]] The prayer of Esther for herself and her people.

[[esther-15-douay|**Chapter 15.**]] Esther comes into the king's presence: she is terrified, but God turns his heart.

[[esther-16-douay|**Chapter 16.**]] A copy of the king's letter in favour of the Jews.
