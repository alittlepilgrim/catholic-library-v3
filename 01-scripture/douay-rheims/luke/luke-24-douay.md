# Luke Chapter 24

Christ's resurrection and manifestation of himself to his disciples.

1 And on the first day of the week, very early in the morning, they came to the sepulchre, bringing the spices which they had prepared. ^luke-24-1-douay

2 And they found the stone rolled back from the sepulchre. ^luke-24-2-douay

3 And going in, they found not the body of the Lord Jesus. ^luke-24-3-douay

4 And it came to pass, as they were astonished in their mind at this, behold, two men stood by them, in shining apparel. ^luke-24-4-douay

5 And as they were afraid and bowed down their countenance towards the ground, they said unto them: Why seek you the living with the dead? ^luke-24-5-douay

6 He is not here, but is risen. Remember how he spoke unto you, when he was yet in Galilee, ^luke-24-6-douay

7 Saying: The Son of man must be delivered into the hands of sinful men and be crucified and the third day rise again. ^luke-24-7-douay

8 And they remembered his words. ^luke-24-8-douay

9 And going back from the sepulchre, they told all these things to the eleven and to all the rest. ^luke-24-9-douay

10 And it was Mary Magdalen and Joanna and Mary of James and the other women that were with them, who told these things to the apostles. ^luke-24-10-douay

11 And these words seemed to them as idle tales: and they did not believe them. ^luke-24-11-douay

12 But Peter rising up, ran to the sepulchre and, stooping down, he saw the linen cloths laid by themselves: and went away wondering in himself at that which was come to pass. ^luke-24-12-douay

13 And behold, two of them went, the same day, to a town which was sixty furlongs from Jerusalem, named Emmaus. ^luke-24-13-douay

14 And they talked together of all these things which had happened. ^luke-24-14-douay

15 And it came to pass that while they talked and reasoned with themselves, Jesus himself also, drawing near, went with them. ^luke-24-15-douay

16 But their eyes were held, that they should not know him. ^luke-24-16-douay

17 And he said to them: What are these discourses that you hold one with another as you walk and are sad? ^luke-24-17-douay

18 And the one of them, whose name was Cleophas, answering, said to him: Art thou only a stranger in Jerusalem, and hast not known the things that have been done there in these days? ^luke-24-18-douay

19 To whom he said: What things? And they said: Concerning Jesus of Nazareth, who was a prophet, mighty in work and word before God and all the people. ^luke-24-19-douay

20 And how our chief priests and princes delivered him to be condemned to death and crucified him. ^luke-24-20-douay

21 But we hoped that it was he that should have redeemed Israel. And now besides all this, to-day is the third day since these things were done. ^luke-24-21-douay

22 Yea and certain women also of our company affrighted us who, before it was light, were at the sepulchre, ^luke-24-22-douay

23 And not finding his body, came, saying that they had all seen a vision of angels, who say that he is alive. ^luke-24-23-douay

24 And some of our people went to the sepulchre and found it so as the women had said: but him they found not. ^luke-24-24-douay

25 Then he said to them: O foolish and slow of heart to believe in all things, which the prophets have spoken. ^luke-24-25-douay

26 Ought not Christ to have suffered these things and so, to enter into his glory? ^luke-24-26-douay

27 And beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded to them in all the scriptures the things that were concerning him. ^luke-24-27-douay

28 And they drew nigh to the town whither they were going: and he made as though he would go farther. ^luke-24-28-douay

29 But they constrained him, saying: Stay with us, because it is towards evening and the day is now far spent. And he went in with them. ^luke-24-29-douay

30 And it came to pass, whilst he was at table with them, he took bread and blessed and brake and gave to them. ^luke-24-30-douay

31 And their eyes were opened: and they knew him. And he vanished out of their sight. ^luke-24-31-douay

32 And they said one to the other: Was not our heart burning within us, whilst he spoke in the way and opened to us the scriptures? ^luke-24-32-douay

33 And rising up, the same hour, they went back to Jerusalem: and they found the eleven gathered together, and those that were with them, ^luke-24-33-douay

34 Saying: The Lord is risen indeed and hath appeared to Simon. ^luke-24-34-douay

35 And they told what things were done in the way: and how they knew him in the breaking of bread. ^luke-24-35-douay

36 Now, whilst they were speaking these things, Jesus stood in the midst of them and saith to them: Peace be to you. It is I: Fear not. ^luke-24-36-douay

37 But they being troubled and frightened, supposed that they saw a spirit. ^luke-24-37-douay

38 And he said to them: Why are you troubled, and why do thoughts arise in your hearts? ^luke-24-38-douay

39 See my hands and feet, that it is I myself. Handle, and see: for a spirit hath not flesh and bones, as you see me to have. ^luke-24-39-douay

40 And when he had said this, he showed them his hands and feet. ^luke-24-40-douay

41 But while they yet believed not and wondered for joy, he said: Have you here any thing to eat? ^luke-24-41-douay

42 And they offered him a piece of a broiled fish and a honeycomb. ^luke-24-42-douay

43 And when he had eaten before them, taking the remains, he gave to them. ^luke-24-43-douay

44 And he said to them: These are the words which I spoke to you while I was yet with you, that all things must needs be fulfilled which are written in the law of Moses and in the prophets and in the psalms, concerning me. ^luke-24-44-douay

45 Then he opened their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures. ^luke-24-45-douay

46 And he said to them: Thus it is written, and thus it behoved Christ to suffer and to rise again from the dead, the third day: ^luke-24-46-douay

47 And that penance and remission of sins should be preached in his name, unto all nations, beginning at Jerusalem. ^luke-24-47-douay

48 And you are witnesses of these things. ^luke-24-48-douay

49 And I send the promise of my Father upon you: but stay you in the city till you be endued with power from on high. **The promise of my Father...** that is, the Holy Ghost, whom Christ had promised that his Father and he would send, John 14. 26, and 17. 7. ^luke-24-49-douay

50 And he led them out as far as Bethania: and lifting up his hands, he blessed them. ^luke-24-50-douay

51 And it came to pass, whilst he blessed them, he departed from them and was carried up to heaven. ^luke-24-51-douay

52 And they adoring went back into Jerusalem with great joy. ^luke-24-52-douay

53 And they were always in the temple, praising and blessing God. Amen. ^luke-24-53-douay
