# Luke Chapter 22

The treason of Judas. The last supper. The first part of the history of the passion.

1 Now the feast of unleavened bread, which is called the pasch, was at hand. ^luke-22-1-douay

2 And the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might put Jesus to death: but they feared the people. ^luke-22-2-douay

3 And Satan entered into Judas, who was surnamed Iscariot, one of the twelve. ^luke-22-3-douay

4 And he went and discoursed with the chief priests and the magistrates, how he might betray him to them. ^luke-22-4-douay

5 And they were glad and covenanted to give him money. ^luke-22-5-douay

6 And he promised. And he sought opportunity to betray him in the absence of the multitude. ^luke-22-6-douay

7 And the day of the unleavened bread came, on which it was necessary that the pasch should be killed. ^luke-22-7-douay

8 And he sent Peter and John, saying: Go, and prepare for us the pasch, that we may eat. ^luke-22-8-douay

9 But they said: Where wilt thou that we prepare? ^luke-22-9-douay

10 And he said to them: Behold, as you go into the city, there shall meet you a man carrying a pitcher of water: follow him into the house where he entereth in. ^luke-22-10-douay

11 And you shall say to the goodman of the house: The master saith to thee: Where is the guest chamber, where I may eat the pasch with my disciples? ^luke-22-11-douay

12 And he will show you a large dining room, furnished. And there prepare. ^luke-22-12-douay

13 And they going, found as he had said to them and made ready the pasch. ^luke-22-13-douay

14 And when the hour was come, he sat down: and the twelve apostles with him. ^luke-22-14-douay

15 And he said to them: With desire I have desired to eat this pasch with you, before I suffer. ^luke-22-15-douay

16 For I say to you that from this time I will not eat it, till it be fulfilled in the kingdom of God. ^luke-22-16-douay

17 And having taken the chalice, he gave thanks and said: Take and divide it among you. ^luke-22-17-douay

18 For I say to you that I will not drink of the fruit of the vine, till the kingdom of God come. ^luke-22-18-douay

19 And taking bread, he gave thanks and brake and gave to them, saying: This is my body, which is given for you. Do this for a commemoration of me. **Do this for a commemoration of me...** This sacrifice and sacrament is to be continued in the church, to the end of the world, to show forth the death of Christ, until he cometh. But this commemoration, or remembrance, is by no means inconsistent with the real presence of his body and blood, under these sacramental veils, which represent his death; on the contrary, it is the manner that he himself hath commanded, of commemorating and celebrating his death, by offering in sacrifice, and receiving in the sacrament, that body and blood by which we were redeemed. ^luke-22-19-douay

20 In like manner, the chalice also, after he had supped, saying: This is the chalice, the new testament in my blood, which shall be shed for you. ^luke-22-20-douay

21 But yet behold: the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on the table. ^luke-22-21-douay

22 And the Son of man indeed goeth, according to that which is determined: but yet, woe to that man by whom he shall be betrayed. ^luke-22-22-douay

23 And they began to inquire among themselves, which of them it was that should do this thing. ^luke-22-23-douay

24 And there was also a strife amongst them, which of them should seem to be the greater. ^luke-22-24-douay

25 And he said to them: The kings of the Gentiles lord it over them; and they that have power over them are called beneficent. ^luke-22-25-douay

26 But you not so: but he that is the greater among you, let him become as the younger: and he that is the leader, as he that serveth. ^luke-22-26-douay

27 For which is greater, he that sitteth at table or he that serveth? Is not he that sitteth at table? But I am in the midst of you, as he that serveth. ^luke-22-27-douay

28 And you are they who have continued with me in my temptations: ^luke-22-28-douay

29 And I dispose to you, as my Father hath disposed to me, a kingdom; ^luke-22-29-douay

30 That you may eat and drink at my table, in my kingdom: and may sit upon thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel. ^luke-22-30-douay

31 And the Lord said: Simon, Simon, behold Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat. ^luke-22-31-douay

32 But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and thou, being once converted, confirm thy brethren. ^luke-22-32-douay

33 Who said to him: Lord, I am ready to go with thee, both into prison and to death. ^luke-22-33-douay

34 And he said: I say to thee, Peter, the cock shall not crow this day, till thou thrice deniest that thou knowest me. And he said to them: ^luke-22-34-douay

35 When I sent you without purse and scrip and shoes, did you want anything? ^luke-22-35-douay

36 But they said: Nothing. Then said he unto them: But now he that hath a purse, let him take it, and likewise a scrip: and he that hath not, let him sell his coat and buy a sword. ^luke-22-36-douay

37 For I say to you that this that is written must yet be fulfilled in me. And with the wicked was he reckoned. For the things concerning me have an end. ^luke-22-37-douay

38 But they said: Lord, behold, here are two swords. And he said to them: It is enough. ^luke-22-38-douay

39 And going out, he went, according to his custom, to the Mount of Olives. And his disciples also followed him. ^luke-22-39-douay

40 And when he was come to the place, he said to them: Pray, lest ye enter into temptation. ^luke-22-40-douay

41 And he was withdrawn away from them a stone's cast. And kneeling down, he prayed. ^luke-22-41-douay

42 Saying: Father, if thou wilt, remove this chalice from me: but yet not my will, but thine be done. ^luke-22-42-douay

43 And there appeared to him an angel from heaven, strengthening him. And being in an agony, he prayed the longer. ^luke-22-43-douay

44 And his sweat became as drops of blood, trickling down upon the ground. ^luke-22-44-douay

45 And when he rose up from prayer and was come to the disciples, he found them sleeping for sorrow. ^luke-22-45-douay

46 And he said to them: Why sleep you? Arise: pray: lest you enter into temptation. ^luke-22-46-douay

47 As he was yet speaking, behold a multitude; and he that was called Judas, one of the twelve, went before them and drew near to Jesus, for to kiss him. ^luke-22-47-douay

48 And Jesus said to him: Judas, dost thou betray the Son of man with a kiss? ^luke-22-48-douay

49 And they that were about him, seeing what would follow, said to him: Lord, shall we strike with the sword? ^luke-22-49-douay

50 And one of them struck the servant of the high priest and cut off his right ear. ^luke-22-50-douay

51 But Jesus answering, said: Suffer ye thus far. And when he had touched his ear, he healed him. ^luke-22-51-douay

52 And Jesus said to the chief priests and magistrates of the temple and the ancients, that were come unto him: Are ye come out, as it were against a thief, with swords and clubs? ^luke-22-52-douay

53 When I was daily with you in the temple, you did not stretch forth your hands against me: but this is your hour and the power of darkness. ^luke-22-53-douay

54 And apprehending him, they led him to the high priest's house. But Peter followed afar off. ^luke-22-54-douay

55 And when they had kindled a fire in the midst of the hall and were sitting about it, Peter was in the midst of them. ^luke-22-55-douay

56 Whom when a certain servant maid had seen sitting at the light and had earnestly beheld him, she said: This man also was with him. ^luke-22-56-douay

57 But he denied him, saying: Woman, I know him not. ^luke-22-57-douay

58 And after a little while, another seeing him, said: Thou also art one of them. But Peter said: O man, I am not. **Another, etc...** Observe here, in order to reconcile the four Evangelists, that divers persons concurred in charging Peter with being Christ's disciple; till at length they brought him to deny him thrice. 1. The porteress that let him in, and afterwards seeing him at the fire, first put the question to him; and then positively affirmed that he was with Christ. 2. Another maid accused him to the standers by; and gave occasion to the man here mentioned to renew the charge against him, which caused the second denial. 3. Others of the company took notice of his being a Galilean; and were seconded by the kinsman of Malchus, who affirmed he had seen him in the garden. And this drew on the third denial. ^luke-22-58-douay

59 And after the space, as it were of one hour, another certain man affirmed, saying: Of a truth, this man was also with him: for he is also a Galilean. ^luke-22-59-douay

60 And Peter said: Man, I know not what thou sayest. And immediately, as he was yet speaking, the cock crew. ^luke-22-60-douay

61 And the Lord turning looked on Peter. And Peter remembered the word of the Lord, as he had said: Before the cock crow, thou shalt deny thrice. ^luke-22-61-douay

62 And Peter going out, wept bitterly. ^luke-22-62-douay

63 And the men that held him mocked him and struck him. ^luke-22-63-douay

64 And they blindfolded him and smote his face. And they asked him saying: Prophesy: Who is it that struck thee? ^luke-22-64-douay

65 And blaspheming, many other things they said against him. ^luke-22-65-douay

66 And as soon as it was day, the ancients of the people and the chief priests and scribes came together. And they brought him into their council saying: If thou be the Christ, tell us. ^luke-22-66-douay

67 And he saith to them: If I shall tell you, you will not believe me. ^luke-22-67-douay

68 And if I shall also ask you, you will not answer me, nor let me go. ^luke-22-68-douay

69 But hereafter the Son of man shall be sitting on the right hand of the power of God. ^luke-22-69-douay

70 Then said they all: Art thou then the Son of God? Who said: You say that I am. ^luke-22-70-douay

71 And they said: What need we any further testimony? For we ourselves have heard it from his own mouth. ^luke-22-71-douay
