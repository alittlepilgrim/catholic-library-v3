# Luke Chapter 13

The necessity of penance. The barren fig tree. The cure of the infirm woman. The journey to Jerusalem.

1 And there were present, at that very time, some that told him of the Galileans, whose blood Pilate had mingled with their sacrifices. ^luke-13-1-douay

2 And he answering, said to them: Think you that these Galileans were sinners above all the men of Galilee, because they suffered such things? ^luke-13-2-douay

3 No, I say to you: but unless you shall do penance, you shall all likewise perish. ^luke-13-3-douay

4 Or those eighteen upon whom the tower fell in Siloe and slew them: think you that they also were debtors above all the men that dwelt in Jerusalem? ^luke-13-4-douay

5 No, I say to you: but except you do penance, you shall all likewise perish. ^luke-13-5-douay

6 He spoke also this parable: A certain man had a fig tree planted in his vineyard: and he came seeking fruit on it and found none. ^luke-13-6-douay

7 And he said to the dresser of the vineyard: Behold, for these three years I come seeking fruit on this fig tree and I find none. Cut it down therefore. Why cumbereth it the ground? ^luke-13-7-douay

8 But he answering, said to him: Lord, let it alone this year also, until I dig about it and dung it. ^luke-13-8-douay

9 And if happily it bear fruit: but if not, then after that thou shalt cut it down. ^luke-13-9-douay

10 And he was teaching in their synagogue on their sabbath. ^luke-13-10-douay

11 And behold there was a woman who had a spirit of infirmity eighteen years. And she was bowed together: neither could she look upwards at all. ^luke-13-11-douay

12 Whom when Jesus saw, he called her unto him and said to her: Woman, thou art delivered from thy infirmity. ^luke-13-12-douay

13 And he laid his hands upon her: and immediately she was made straight and glorified God. ^luke-13-13-douay

14 And the ruler of the synagogue being angry that Jesus had healed on the sabbath answering, said to the multitude: Six days there are wherein you ought to work. In them therefore come and be healed: and not on the sabbath day. ^luke-13-14-douay

15 And the Lord answering him, said: Ye hypocrites, doth not every one of you, on the sabbath day, loose his ox or his ass from the manger and lead them to water? ^luke-13-15-douay

16 And ought not this daughter of Abraham, whom Satan hath bound, lo, these eighteen years, be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day? ^luke-13-16-douay

17 And when he said these things, all his adversaries were ashamed: and all the people rejoiced for all the things that were gloriously done by him. ^luke-13-17-douay

18 He said therefore: To what is the kingdom of God like, and whereunto shall I resemble it? ^luke-13-18-douay

19 It is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took and cast into his garden: and it grew and became a great tree, and the birds of the air lodged in the branches thereof. ^luke-13-19-douay

20 And again he said: Whereunto shall I esteem the kingdom of God to be like? ^luke-13-20-douay

21 It is like to leaven, which a woman took and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened. ^luke-13-21-douay

22 And he went through the cities and towns teaching and making his journey to Jerusalem. ^luke-13-22-douay

23 And a certain man said to him: Lord, are they few that are saved? But he said to them: ^luke-13-23-douay

24 Strive to enter by the narrow gate: for many, I say to you, shall seek to enter and shall not be able. **Shall seek, etc...** Shall desire to be saved; but for want of taking sufficient pains, and being thoroughly in earnest, shall not attain to it. ^luke-13-24-douay

25 But when the master of the house shall be gone in and shall shut the door, you shall begin to stand without; and knock at the door, saying: Lord, open to us. And he answering, shall say to you: I know you not, whence you are. ^luke-13-25-douay

26 Then you shall begin to say: We have eaten and drunk in thy presence: and thou hast taught in our streets. ^luke-13-26-douay

27 And he shall say to you: I know you not, whence you are. Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity. ^luke-13-27-douay

28 There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth; when you shall see Abraham and Isaac and Jacob and all the prophets, in the kingdom of God: and you yourselves thrust out. ^luke-13-28-douay

29 And there shall come from the east and the west and the north and the south: and shall sit down in the kingdom of God. ^luke-13-29-douay

30 And behold, they are last that shall be first: and they are first that shall be last. ^luke-13-30-douay

31 The same day, there came some of the Pharisees, saying to him: Depart, and get thee hence, for Herod hath a mind to kill thee. ^luke-13-31-douay

32 And he said to them: Go and tell that fox: Behold, I cast out devils and do cures, to-day and to-morrow, and the third day I am consummated. ^luke-13-32-douay

33 Nevertheless, I must walk to-day and to-morrow and the day following, because it cannot be that a prophet perish, out of Jerusalem. ^luke-13-33-douay

34 Jerusalem, Jerusalem, that killest the prophets; and stonest them that are sent to thee, how often would I have gathered thy children as the bird doth her brood under her wings, and thou wouldest not? ^luke-13-34-douay

35 Behold your house shall be left to you desolate. And I say to you that you shall not see me till the time come when you shall say: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord. ^luke-13-35-douay
