# Luke Chapter 3

John's mission and preaching. Christ is baptized by him.

1 Now in the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar, Pontius Pilate being governor of Judea, and Herod being tetrarch of Galilee, and Philip his brother tetrarch of Iturea and the country of Trachonitis, and Lysanias tetrarch of Abilina: ^luke-03-1-douay

2 Under the high priests Annas and Caiphas: the word of the Lord was made unto John, the son of Zachary, in the desert. ^luke-03-2-douay

3 And he came into all the country about the Jordan, preaching the baptism of penance for the remission of sins. ^luke-03-3-douay

4 As it was written in the book of the sayings of Isaiah the prophet: ^luke-03-4-douay

A voice of one crying in the wilderness: Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight his paths.

5 Every valley shall be filled and every mountain and hill shall be brought low: and the crooked shall be made straight, and the rough ways plain. ^luke-03-5-douay

6 And all flesh shall see the salvation of God. ^luke-03-6-douay

7 He said therefore to the multitudes that went forth to be baptized by him: Ye offspring of vipers, who hath showed you to flee from the wrath to come? ^luke-03-7-douay

8 Bring forth therefore fruits worthy of penance: and do not begin to say, We have Abraham for our father. For I say unto you that God is able of these stones, to raise up children to Abraham. ^luke-03-8-douay

9 For now the axe is laid to the root of the trees. Every tree therefore that bringeth not forth good fruit shall be cut down and cast into the fire. ^luke-03-9-douay

10 And the people asked him, saying: What then shall we do? ^luke-03-10-douay

11 And he answering, said to them: He that hath two coats, let him give to him that hath none; and he that hath meat, let him do in like manner. ^luke-03-11-douay

12 And the publicans also came to be baptized and said to him: Master, what shall we do? ^luke-03-12-douay

13 But he said to them: Do nothing more than that which is appointed you. ^luke-03-13-douay

14 And the soldiers also asked him, saying: And what shall we do? And he said to them: Do violence to no man, neither calumniate any man; and be content with your pay. ^luke-03-14-douay

15 And as the people were of opinion, and all were thinking in their hearts of John, that perhaps he might be the Christ: ^luke-03-15-douay

16 John answered, saying unto all: I indeed baptize you with water: but there shall come one mightier than I, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to loose. He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost and with fire; ^luke-03-16-douay

17 Whose fan is in his hand: and he will purge his floor and will gather the wheat into his barn: but the chaff he will burn with unquenchable fire. ^luke-03-17-douay

18 And many other things exhorting did he preach to the people. ^luke-03-18-douay

19 But Herod the tetrarch, when he was reproved by him for Herodias, his brother's wife, and for all the evils which Herod had done: ^luke-03-19-douay

20 He added this also above all and shut up John in prison. ^luke-03-20-douay

21 Now it came to pass, when all the people were baptized, that Jesus also being baptized and praying, heaven was opened. ^luke-03-21-douay

22 And the Holy Ghost descended in a bodily shape, as a dove, upon him. And a voice came from heaven: Thou art my beloved Son. In thee I am well pleased. ^luke-03-22-douay

23 And Jesus himself was beginning about the age of thirty years: being (as it was supposed) the son of Joseph, who was of Heli, who was of Mathat, **Who was of Heli...** St. Joseph, who by nature was the son of Jacob, ([[matthew-01-douay|Matthew 1:16]]), in the account of the law, was son of Heli. For Heli and Jacob were brothers, by the same mother; and Heli, who was the elder, dying without issue, Jacob, as the law directed, married his widow: in consequence of such marriage, his son Joseph was reputed in the law the son of Heli. ^luke-03-23-douay

24 Who was of Levi, who was of Melchi, who was of Janne, who was of Joseph, ^luke-03-24-douay

25 Who was of Mathathias, who was of Amos, who was of Nahum, who was of Hesli, who was of Nagge, ^luke-03-25-douay

26 Who was of Mahath, who was of Mathathias, who was of Semei, who was of Joseph, who was of Juda, ^luke-03-26-douay

27 Who was of Joanna, who was of Reza, who was of Zorobabel, who was of Salathiel, who was of Neri, ^luke-03-27-douay

28 Who was of Melchi, who was of Addi, who was of Cosan, who was of Helmadan, who was of Her, ^luke-03-28-douay

29 Who was of Jesus, who was of Eliezer, who was of Jorim, who was of Mathat, who was of Levi, ^luke-03-29-douay

30 Who was of Simeon, who was of Judas, who was of Joseph, who was of Jona, who was of Eliakim, ^luke-03-30-douay

31 Who was of Melea, who was of Menna, who was of Mathatha, who was of Nathan, who was of David, ^luke-03-31-douay

32 Who was of Jesse, who was of Obed, who was of Booz, who was of Salmon, who was of Naasson, ^luke-03-32-douay

33 Who was of Aminadab, who was of Aram, who was of Esron, who was of Phares, who was of Judas, ^luke-03-33-douay

34 Who was of Jacob, who was of Isaac, who was of Abraham, who was of Thare, who was of Nachor, ^luke-03-34-douay

35 Who was of Sarug, who was of Ragau, who was of Phaleg, who was of Heber, who was of Sale, ^luke-03-35-douay

36 Who was of Cainan, who was of Arphaxad, who was of Sem, who was of Noe, who was of Lamech, ^luke-03-36-douay

37 Who was of Mathusale, who was of Henoch, who was of Jared, who was of Malaleel, who was of Cainan, ^luke-03-37-douay

38 Who was of Henos, who was of Seth, who was of Adam, who was of God. ^luke-03-38-douay
