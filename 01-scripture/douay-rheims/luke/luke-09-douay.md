# Luke Chapter 9

Christ sends forth his apostles, feeds five thousand with five loaves, is transfigured and casts out a devil.

1 Then calling together the twelve apostles, he gave them power and authority over all devils and to cure diseases. ^luke-09-1-douay

2 And he sent them to preach the kingdom of God and to heal the sick. ^luke-09-2-douay

3 And he said to them: Take nothing for your journey, neither staff, nor scrip, nor bread, nor money; neither have two coats. ^luke-09-3-douay

4 And whatsoever house you shall enter into, abide there and depart not from thence. ^luke-09-4-douay

5 And whosoever will not receive you, when ye go out of that city, shake off even the dust of your feet, for a testimony against them. ^luke-09-5-douay

6 And going out, they went about through the towns, preaching the gospel and healing every where. ^luke-09-6-douay

7 Now Herod, the tetrarch, heard of all things that were done by him. And he was in a doubt, because it was said ^luke-09-7-douay

8 By some that John was risen from the dead: but by other some, that Elias had appeared: and by others, that one of the old prophets was risen again. ^luke-09-8-douay

9 And Herod said: John I have beheaded. But who is this of whom I hear such things? And he sought to see him. ^luke-09-9-douay

10 And the apostles, when they were returned, told him all they had done. And taking them, he went aside into a desert place, apart, which belongeth to Bethsaida. ^luke-09-10-douay

11 Which when the people knew, they followed him: and he received them and spoke to them of the kingdom of God and healed them who had need of healing. ^luke-09-11-douay

12 Now the day began to decline. And the twelve came and said to him: Send away the multitude, that, going into the towns and villages round about, they may lodge and get victuals; for we are here in a desert place. ^luke-09-12-douay

13 But he said to them: Give you them to eat. And they said: We have no more than five loaves and two fishes; unless perhaps, we should go and buy food for all this multitude. ^luke-09-13-douay

14 Now there were about five thousand men. And he said to his disciples: Make them sit down by fifties in a company. ^luke-09-14-douay

15 And they did so and made them all sit down. ^luke-09-15-douay

16 And taking the five loaves and the two fishes, he looked up to heaven and blessed them: and he broke and distributed to his disciples, to set before the multitude. ^luke-09-16-douay

17 And they did all eat and were filled. And there were taken up of fragments that remained to them, twelve baskets. ^luke-09-17-douay

18 And it came to pass, as he was alone praying, his disciples also were with him: and he asked them, saying: Whom do the people say that I am? ^luke-09-18-douay

19 But they answered and said: John the Baptist; but some say Elias: and others say that one of the former prophets is risen again. ^luke-09-19-douay

20 And he said to them: But whom do you say that I am? Simon Peter answering, said: The Christ of God. ^luke-09-20-douay

21 But he strictly charging them, commanded they should tell this to no man. ^luke-09-21-douay

22 Saying: The Son of man must suffer many things and be rejected by the ancients and chief priests and scribes and be killed and the third day rise again. ^luke-09-22-douay

23 And he said to all: If any man will come after me, let him deny himself and take up his cross daily and follow me. ^luke-09-23-douay

24 For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: for he that shall lose his life for my sake shall save it. ^luke-09-24-douay

25 For what is a man advantaged, if he gain the whole world and lose himself and cast away himself? ^luke-09-25-douay

26 For he that shall be ashamed of me and of my words, of him the Son of man shall be ashamed, when he shall come in his majesty and that of his Father and of the holy angels. ^luke-09-26-douay

27 But I tell you of a truth: There are some standing here that shall not taste death till they see the kingdom of God. ^luke-09-27-douay

28 And it came to pass, about eight days after these words, that he took Peter and James and John and went up into a mountain to pray. ^luke-09-28-douay

29 And whilst he prayed, the shape of his countenance was altered and his raiment became white and glittering. ^luke-09-29-douay

30 And behold two men were talking with him. And they were Moses and Elias, ^luke-09-30-douay

31 Appearing in majesty. And they spoke of his decease that he should accomplish in Jerusalem. ^luke-09-31-douay

32 But Peter and they that were with him were heavy with sleep. And waking, they saw his glory and the two men that stood with him. ^luke-09-32-douay

33 And it came to pass that, as they were departing from him, Peter saith to Jesus: Master, it is good for us to be here: and let us make three tabernacles, one for thee, and one for Moses; and one for Elias: not knowing what he said. ^luke-09-33-douay

34 And as he spoke these things, there came a cloud and overshadowed them. And they were afraid when they entered into the cloud. ^luke-09-34-douay

35 And a voice came out of the cloud; saying: This is my beloved son. Hear him. ^luke-09-35-douay

36 And whilst the voice was uttered Jesus was found alone. And they held their peace and told no man in those days any of these things which they had seen. ^luke-09-36-douay

37 And it came to pass the day following, when they came down from the mountain, there met him a great multitude. ^luke-09-37-douay

38 And behold a man among the crowd cried out, saying: Master, I beseech thee, look upon my son, because he is my only one. ^luke-09-38-douay

39 And lo, a spirit seizeth him, and he suddenly crieth out, and he throweth him down and teareth him, so that he foameth; and bruising him, he hardly departeth from him. ^luke-09-39-douay

40 And I desired thy disciples to cast him out: and they could not. ^luke-09-40-douay

41 And Jesus, answering, said: O faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you and suffer you? Bring hither thy son. ^luke-09-41-douay

42 And as he was coming to him, the devil threw him down and tore him. ^luke-09-42-douay

43 And Jesus rebuked the unclean spirit and cured the boy and restored him to his father. ^luke-09-43-douay

44 And all were astonished at the mighty power of God. But while all wondered at all the things he did, he said to his disciples: Lay you up in your hearts these words, for it shall come to pass that the Son of man shall be delivered into the hands of men. ^luke-09-44-douay

45 But they understood not this word: and it was hid from them, so that they perceived it not. And they were afraid to ask him concerning this word. ^luke-09-45-douay

46 And there entered a thought into them, which of them should be greater. ^luke-09-46-douay

47 But Jesus seeing the thoughts of their hearts, took a child and set him by him, ^luke-09-47-douay

48 And said to them: Whosoever shall receive this child in my name receiveth me; and whosoever shall receive me receiveth him that sent me. For he that is the lesser among you all, he is the greater. ^luke-09-48-douay

49 And John, answering, said: Master, we saw a certain man casting out devils in thy name: and we forbade him, because he followeth not with us. ^luke-09-49-douay

50 And Jesus said to him: Forbid him not: for he that is not against you is for you. ^luke-09-50-douay

51 And it came to pass, when the days of his assumption were accomplishing, that he steadfastly set his face to go to Jerusalem. ^luke-09-51-douay

52 And he sent messengers before his face: and going, they entered into a city of the Samaritans, to prepare for him. ^luke-09-52-douay

53 And they received him not, because his face was of one going to Jerusalem. ^luke-09-53-douay

54 And when his disciples, James and John, had seen this, they said: Lord, wilt thou that we command fire to come down from heaven and consume them? ^luke-09-54-douay

55 And turning, he rebuked them, saying: you know not of what spirit you are. ^luke-09-55-douay

56 The Son of man came not to destroy souls, but to save. And they went into another town. ^luke-09-56-douay

57 And it came to pass, as they walked in the way, that a certain man said to him: I will follow thee whithersoever thou goest. ^luke-09-57-douay

58 Jesus said to him: The foxes have holes, and the birds of the air nests: but the Son of man hath not where to lay his head. ^luke-09-58-douay

59 But he said to another: Follow me. And he said: Lord, suffer me first to go and to bury my father. ^luke-09-59-douay

60 And Jesus said to him: Let the dead bury their dead: but go thou and preach the kingdom of God. ^luke-09-60-douay

61 And another said: I will follow thee, Lord; but let me first take my leave of them that are at my house. ^luke-09-61-douay

62 Jesus said to him: No man putting his hand to the plough and looking back is fit for the kingdom of God. ^luke-09-62-douay
