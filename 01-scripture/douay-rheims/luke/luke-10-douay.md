# Luke Chapter 10

Christ sends forth and instructs his seventy-two disciples. The good Samaritan.

1 And after these things, the Lord appointed also other seventy-two. And he sent them two and two before his face into every city and place whither he himself was to come. ^luke-10-1-douay

2 And he said to them: The harvest indeed is great, but the labourers are few. Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest that he send labourers into his harvest. ^luke-10-2-douay

3 Go: Behold I send you as lambs among wolves. ^luke-10-3-douay

4 Carry neither purse, nor scrip, nor shoes: and salute no man by the way. ^luke-10-4-douay

5 Into whatever house you enter, first say: Peace be to this house. ^luke-10-5-douay

6 And if the son of peace be there, your peace shall rest upon him: but if not, it shall return to you. ^luke-10-6-douay

7 And in the same house, remain, eating and drinking such things as they have: for the labourer is worthy of his hire. Remove not from house to house. ^luke-10-7-douay

8 And into what city soever you enter, and they receive you, eat such things as are set before you. ^luke-10-8-douay

9 And heal the sick that are therein and say to them: The kingdom of God is come nigh unto you. ^luke-10-9-douay

10 But into whatsoever city you enter, and they receive you not, going forth into the streets thereof, say: ^luke-10-10-douay

11 Even the very dust of your city that cleaveth to us, we wipe off against you. Yet know this, that the kingdom of God is at hand. ^luke-10-11-douay

12 I say to you, it shall be more tolerable at that day for Sodom than for that city. ^luke-10-12-douay

13 Woe to thee, Corozain! Woe to thee, Bethsaida! For if in Tyre and Sidon had been wrought the mighty works that have been wrought in you, they would have done penance long ago, sitting in sackcloth and ashes. ^luke-10-13-douay

14 But it shall be more tolerable for Tyre and Sidon at the judgment than for you. ^luke-10-14-douay

15 And thou, Capharnaum, which art exalted unto heaven, thou shalt be thrust down to hell. ^luke-10-15-douay

16 He that heareth you heareth me: and he that despiseth you despiseth me: and he that despiseth me despiseth him that sent me. ^luke-10-16-douay

17 And the seventy-two returned with joy, saying: Lord, the devils also are subject to us in thy name. ^luke-10-17-douay

18 And he said to them: I saw Satan like lightning falling from heaven. ^luke-10-18-douay

19 Behold, I have given you power to tread upon serpents and scorpions and upon all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall hurt you. ^luke-10-19-douay

20 But yet rejoice not in this, that spirits are subject unto you: but rejoice in this, that your names are written in heaven. ^luke-10-20-douay

21 In that same hour, he rejoiced in the Holy Ghost and said: I confess to thee, O Father, Lord of heaven and earth, because thou hast hidden these things from the wise and prudent and hast revealed them to little ones. Yea, Father, for so it hath seemed good in thy sight. **He rejoiced in the Holy Ghost...** That is, according to his humanity he rejoiced in the Holy Ghost, and gave thanks to his eternal Father. ^luke-10-21-douay

22 All things are delivered to me by my Father. And no one knoweth who the Son is, but the Father: and who the Father is, but the Son and to whom the Son will reveal him. ^luke-10-22-douay

23 And turning to his disciples, he said: Blessed are the eyes that see the things which you see. ^luke-10-23-douay

24 For I say to you that many prophets and kings have desired to see the things that you see and have not seen them; and to hear the things that you hear and have not heard them. ^luke-10-24-douay

25 And behold a certain lawyer stood up, tempting him and saying, Master, what must I do to possess eternal life? ^luke-10-25-douay

26 But he said to him: What is written in the law? How readest thou? ^luke-10-26-douay

27 He answering, said: Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with thy whole heart and with thy whole soul and with all thy strength and with all thy mind: and thy neighbour as thyself. ^luke-10-27-douay

28 And he said to him: Thou hast answered right. This do: and thou shalt live. ^luke-10-28-douay

29 But he willing to justify himself, said to Jesus: And who is my neighbour? ^luke-10-29-douay

30 And Jesus answering, said: A certain man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho and fell among robbers, who also stripped him and having wounded him went away, leaving him half dead. ^luke-10-30-douay

31 And it chanced, that a certain priest went down the same way: and seeing him, passed by. ^luke-10-31-douay

32 In like manner also a Levite, when he was near the place and saw him, passed by. ^luke-10-32-douay

33 But a certain Samaritan, being on his journey, came near him: and seeing him, was moved with compassion: ^luke-10-33-douay

34 And going up to him, bound up his wounds, pouring in oil and wine: and setting him upon his own beast, brought him to an inn and took care of him. ^luke-10-34-douay

35 And the next day he took out two pence and gave to the host and said: Take care of him; and whatsoever thou shalt spend over and above, I, at my return, will repay thee. ^luke-10-35-douay

36 Which of these three, in thy opinion, was neighbour to him that fell among the robbers? ^luke-10-36-douay

37 But he said: He that showed mercy to him. And Jesus said to him: Go, and do thou in like manner. ^luke-10-37-douay

38 Now it came to pass, as they went, that he entered into a certain town: and a certain woman named Martha received him into her house. ^luke-10-38-douay

39 And she had a sister called Mary, who, sitting also at the Lord's feet, heard his word. ^luke-10-39-douay

40 But Martha was busy about much serving. Who stood and said: Lord, hast thou no care that my sister hath left me alone to serve? Speak to her therefore, that she help me. ^luke-10-40-douay

41 And the Lord answering, said to her: Martha, Martha, thou art careful and art troubled about many things: ^luke-10-41-douay

42 But one thing is necessary. Mary hath chosen the best part, which shall not be taken away from her. ^luke-10-42-douay
