# Luke Chapter 18

We must pray always. The Pharisee and the publican. The danger of riches. The blind man is restored to sight.

1 And he spoke also a parable to them, that we ought always to pray and not to faint, ^luke-18-1-douay

2 Saying: There was a judge in a certain city, who feared not God nor regarded man. ^luke-18-2-douay

3 And there was a certain widow in that city; and she came to him, saying: Avenge me of my adversary. **Avenge...** That is, do me justice. It is a Hebraism. ^luke-18-3-douay

4 And he would not for a long time. But afterwards he said within himself: Although I fear not God nor regard man, ^luke-18-4-douay

5 Yet because this widow is troublesome to me, I will avenge her, lest continually coming she weary me. ^luke-18-5-douay

6 And the Lord said: Hear what the unjust judge saith. ^luke-18-6-douay

7 And will not God revenge his elect who cry to him day and night? And will he have patience in their regard? ^luke-18-7-douay

8 I say to you that he will quickly revenge them. But yet the Son of man, when he cometh, shall he find, think you, faith on earth? ^luke-18-8-douay

9 And to some who trusted in themselves as just and despised others, he spoke also this parable: ^luke-18-9-douay

10 Two men went up into the temple to pray: the one a Pharisee and the other a publican. ^luke-18-10-douay

11 The Pharisee standing, prayed thus with himself: O God, I give thee thanks that I am not as the rest of men, extortioners, unjust, adulterers, as also is this publican. ^luke-18-11-douay

12 I fast twice in a week: I give tithes of all that I possess. ^luke-18-12-douay

13 And the publican, standing afar off, would not so much as lift up his eyes towards heaven; but struck his breast, saying: O God, be merciful to me a sinner. ^luke-18-13-douay

14 I say to you, this man went down into his house justified rather than the other: because every one that exalteth himself shall be humbled: and he that humbleth himself shall be exalted. ^luke-18-14-douay

15 And they brought unto him also infants, that he might touch them. Which when the disciples saw, they rebuked them. ^luke-18-15-douay

16 But Jesus, calling them together, said: Suffer children to come to me and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God. ^luke-18-16-douay

17 Amen, I say to you: Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a child shall not enter into it. ^luke-18-17-douay

18 And a certain ruler asked him, saying: Good master, what shall I do to possess everlasting life? ^luke-18-18-douay

19 And Jesus said to him: Why dost thou call me good? None is good but God alone. ^luke-18-19-douay

20 Thou knowest the commandments: Thou shalt not kill. Thou shalt not commit adultery: Thou shalt not steal: Thou shalt not bear false witness: Honour thy father and mother. ^luke-18-20-douay

21 Who said: All these things have I kept from my youth. ^luke-18-21-douay

22 Which when Jesus had heard, he said to him: Yet one thing is wanting to thee. Sell all whatever thou hast and give to the poor: and thou shalt have treasure in heaven. And come, follow me. ^luke-18-22-douay

23 He having heard these things, became sorrowful: for he was very rich. ^luke-18-23-douay

24 And Jesus seeing him become sorrowful, said: How hardly shall they that have riches enter into the kingdom of God: ^luke-18-24-douay

25 For it is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God. ^luke-18-25-douay

26 And they that heard it said: Who then can be saved? ^luke-18-26-douay

27 He said to them: The things that are impossible with men are possible with God. ^luke-18-27-douay

28 Then Peter said: Behold, we have left all things and have followed thee. ^luke-18-28-douay

29 Who said to them: Amen, I say to you, there is no man that hath left home or parents or brethren or wife or children, for the kingdom of God's sake, ^luke-18-29-douay

30 Who shall not receive much more in this present time, and in the world to come life everlasting. ^luke-18-30-douay

31 Then Jesus took unto him the twelve and said to them: Behold, we go up to Jerusalem; and all things shall be accomplished which were written by the prophets concerning the Son of man. ^luke-18-31-douay

32 For he shall be delivered to the Gentiles and shall be mocked and scourged and spit upon. ^luke-18-32-douay

33 And after they have scourged him, they will put him to death. And the third day he shall rise again. ^luke-18-33-douay

34 And they understood none of these things, and this word was hid from them: and they understood not the things that were said. ^luke-18-34-douay

35 Now it came to pass, when he drew nigh to Jericho, that a certain blind man sat by the way side, begging. ^luke-18-35-douay

36 And when he heard the multitude passing by, he asked what this meant. ^luke-18-36-douay

37 And they told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by. ^luke-18-37-douay

38 And he cried out, saying: Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me. ^luke-18-38-douay

39 And they that went before rebuked him, that he should hold his peace: but he cried out much more: Son of David, have mercy on me. ^luke-18-39-douay

40 And Jesus standing, commanded him to be brought unto him. And when he was come near, he asked him, ^luke-18-40-douay

41 Saying; What wilt thou that I do to thee? But he said: Lord, that I may see. ^luke-18-41-douay

42 And Jesus said to him: Receive thy sight: thy faith hath made thee whole. ^luke-18-42-douay

43 And immediately he saw and followed him, glorifying God. And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God. ^luke-18-43-douay
