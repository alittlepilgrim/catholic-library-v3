# Luke Chapter 21

The widow's mites. The signs that should forerun the destruction of Jerusalem and the end of the world.

1 And looking on, he saw the rich men cast their gifts into the treasury. ^luke-21-1-douay

2 And he saw also a certain poor widow casting in two brass mites. ^luke-21-2-douay

3 And he said: Verily, I say to you that this poor widow hath cast in more than they all. ^luke-21-3-douay

4 For all these have of their abundance cast into the offerings of God: but she of her want hath cast in all the living that she had. ^luke-21-4-douay

5 And some saying of the temple that it was adorned with goodly stones and gifts, he said: ^luke-21-5-douay

6 These things which you see, the days will come in which there shall not be left a stone upon a stone that shall not be thrown down. ^luke-21-6-douay

7 And they asked him, saying: Master, when shall these things be? And what shall be the sign when they shall begin to come to pass? ^luke-21-7-douay

8 Who said: Take heed you be not seduced: for many will come in my name, saying: I am he and the time is at hand. Go ye not therefore after them. ^luke-21-8-douay

9 And when you shall hear of wars and seditions, be not terrified. These things must first come to pass: but the end is not yet presently. ^luke-21-9-douay

10 Then he said to them: Nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. ^luke-21-10-douay

11 And there shall be great earthquakes in divers places and pestilences and famines and terrors from heaven: and there shall be great signs. ^luke-21-11-douay

12 But before all these things, they will lay their hands on you and persecute you, delivering you up to the synagogues and into prisons, dragging you before kings and governors, for my name's sake. ^luke-21-12-douay

13 And it shall happen unto you for a testimony. ^luke-21-13-douay

14 Lay it up therefore in your hearts, not to meditate before how you shall answer: ^luke-21-14-douay

15 For I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to resist and gainsay. ^luke-21-15-douay

16 And you shall be betrayed by your parents and brethren and kinsmen and friends: and some of you they will put to death. ^luke-21-16-douay

17 And you shall be hated by all men for my name's sake. ^luke-21-17-douay

18 But a hair of your head shall not perish. ^luke-21-18-douay

19 In your patience you shall possess your souls. ^luke-21-19-douay

20 And when you shall see Jerusalem compassed about with an army, then know that the desolation thereof is at hand. ^luke-21-20-douay

21 Then let those who are in Judea flee to the mountains: and those who are in the midst thereof depart out: and those who are in the countries not enter into it. ^luke-21-21-douay

22 For these are the days of vengeance, that all things may be fulfilled, that are written. ^luke-21-22-douay

23 But woe to them that are with child and give suck in those days: for there shall be great distress in the land and wrath upon this people. ^luke-21-23-douay

24 And they shall fall by the edge of the sword and shall be led away captives into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down by the Gentiles till the times of the nations be fulfilled. ^luke-21-24-douay

25 And there shall be signs in the sun and in the moon and in the stars; and upon the earth distress of nations, by reason of the confusion of the roaring of the sea, and of the waves: ^luke-21-25-douay

26 Men withering away for fear and expectation of what shall come upon the whole world. For the powers of heaven shall be moved. ^luke-21-26-douay

27 And then they shall see the Son of man coming in a cloud, with great power and majesty. ^luke-21-27-douay

28 But when these things begin to come to pass, look up and lift up your heads, because your redemption is at hand. ^luke-21-28-douay

29 And he spoke to them a similitude. See the fig tree and all the trees: ^luke-21-29-douay

30 When they now shoot forth their fruit, you know that summer is nigh; ^luke-21-30-douay

31 So you also, when you shall see these things come to pass, know that the kingdom of God is at hand. ^luke-21-31-douay

32 Amen, I say to you, this generation shall not pass away till all things be fulfilled. ^luke-21-32-douay

33 Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away. ^luke-21-33-douay

34 And take heed to yourselves, lest perhaps your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness and the cares of this life: and that day come upon you suddenly. ^luke-21-34-douay

35 For as a snare shall it come upon all that sit upon the face of the whole earth. ^luke-21-35-douay

36 Watch ye, therefore, praying at all times, that you may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that are to come and to stand before the Son of man. ^luke-21-36-douay

37 And in the daytime, he was teaching in the temple: but at night going out, he abode in the mount that is called Olivet. ^luke-21-37-douay

38 And all the people came early in the morning to him in the temple, to hear him. ^luke-21-38-douay
