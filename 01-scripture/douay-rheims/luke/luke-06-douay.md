# Luke Chapter 6

Christ excuses his disciples. He cures upon the sabbath day, chooses the twelve and makes a sermon to them.

1 And it came to pass on the second first sabbath that, as he went through the corn fields, his disciples plucked the ears and did eat, rubbing them in their hands. **The second first sabbath...** Some understand this of the sabbath of Pentecost, which was the second in course among the great feasts: others, of a sabbath day that immediately followed any solemn feast. ^luke-06-1-douay

2 And some of the Pharisees said to them: Why do you that which is not lawful on the sabbath days? ^luke-06-2-douay

3 And Jesus answering them, said: Have you not read so much as this, what David did, when himself was hungry and they that were with him: ^luke-06-3-douay

4 How he went into the house of God and took and ate the bread of proposition and gave to them that were with him, which is not lawful to eat but only for the priests? ^luke-06-4-douay

5 And he said to them: The Son of man is Lord also of the sabbath. ^luke-06-5-douay

6 And it came to pass also, on another sabbath, that he entered into the synagogue and taught. And there was a man whose right hand was withered. ^luke-06-6-douay

7 And the scribes and Pharisees watched if he would heal on the sabbath: that they might find an accusation against him. ^luke-06-7-douay

8 But he knew their thoughts and said to the man who had the withered hand: Arise and stand forth in the midst. And rising he stood forth. ^luke-06-8-douay

9 Then Jesus said to them: I ask you, if it be lawful on the sabbath days to do good or to do evil? To save life or to destroy? ^luke-06-9-douay

10 And looking round about on them all, he said to the man: Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth. And his hand was restored. ^luke-06-10-douay

11 And they were filled with madness: and they talked one with another, what they might do to Jesus. ^luke-06-11-douay

12 And it came to pass in those days, that he went out into a mountain to pray: and he passed the whole night in the prayer of God. ^luke-06-12-douay

13 And when day was come, he called unto him his disciples: and he chose twelve of them (whom also he named apostles): ^luke-06-13-douay

14 Simon, whom he surnamed Peter, and Andrew his brother, James and John, Philip and Bartholomew, ^luke-06-14-douay

15 Matthew and Thomas, James the son of Alpheus, and Simon who is called Zelotes, ^luke-06-15-douay

16 And Jude the brother of James, and Judas Iscariot, who was the traitor. ^luke-06-16-douay

17 And coming down with them, he stood in a plain place: and the company of his disciples and a very great multitude of people from all Judea and Jerusalem and the sea coast, both of Tyre and Sidon, ^luke-06-17-douay

18 Who were come to hear him and to be healed of their diseases. And they that were troubled with unclean spirits were cured. ^luke-06-18-douay

19 And all the multitude sought to touch him: for virtue went out from him and healed all. ^luke-06-19-douay

20 And he, lifting up his eyes on his disciples, said: Blessed are ye poor: for yours is the kingdom of God. ^luke-06-20-douay

21 Blessed are ye that hunger now: for you shall be filled. Blessed are ye that weep now: for you shall laugh. ^luke-06-21-douay

22 Blessed shall you be when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you and shall reproach you and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of man's sake. ^luke-06-22-douay

23 Be glad in that day and rejoice: for behold, your reward is great in heaven, For according to these things did their fathers to the prophets. ^luke-06-23-douay

24 But woe to you that are rich: for you have your consolation. ^luke-06-24-douay

25 Woe to you that are filled: for you shall hunger. Woe to you that now laugh: for you shall mourn and weep. ^luke-06-25-douay

26 Woe to you when men shall bless you: for according to these things did their fathers to the false prophets. ^luke-06-26-douay

27 But I say to you that hear: Love your enemies. Do good to them that hate you. ^luke-06-27-douay

28 Bless them that curse you and pray for them that calumniate you. ^luke-06-28-douay

29 And to him that striketh thee on the one cheek, offer also the other. And him that taketh away from thee thy cloak, forbid not to take thy coat also. ^luke-06-29-douay

30 Give to every one that asketh thee: and of him that taketh away thy goods, ask them not again. ^luke-06-30-douay

31 And as you would that men should do to you, do you also to them in like manner. ^luke-06-31-douay

32 And if you love them that love you, what thanks are to you? For sinners also love those that love them. ^luke-06-32-douay

33 And if you do good to them who do good to you, what thanks are to you? For sinners also do this. ^luke-06-33-douay

34 And if you lend to them of whom you hope to receive, what thanks are to you? For sinners also lend to sinners, for to receive as much. ^luke-06-34-douay

35 But love ye your enemies: do good, and lend, hoping for nothing thereby: and your reward shall be great, and you shall be the sons of the Highest. For he is kind to the unthankful and to the evil. ^luke-06-35-douay

36 Be ye therefore merciful, as your Father also is merciful. ^luke-06-36-douay

37 Judge not: and you shall not be judged. Condemn not: and you shall not be condemned. Forgive: and you shall be forgiven. ^luke-06-37-douay

38 Give: and it shall be given to you: good measure and pressed down and shaken together and running over shall they give into your bosom. For with the same measure that you shall mete withal, it shall be measured to you again. ^luke-06-38-douay

39 And he spoke also to them a similitude: Can the blind lead the blind? Do they not both fall into the ditch? ^luke-06-39-douay

40 The disciple is not above his master: but every one shall be perfect, if he be as his master. ^luke-06-40-douay

41 And why seest thou the mote in thy brother's eye: but the beam that is in thy own eye thou considerest not? ^luke-06-41-douay

42 Or how canst thou say to thy brother: Brother, let me pull the mote out of thy eye, when thou thyself seest not the beam in thy own eye? Hypocrite, cast first the beam out of thy own eye: and then shalt thou see clearly to take out the mote from thy brother's eye. ^luke-06-42-douay

43 For there is no good tree that bringeth forth evil fruit: nor an evil tree that bringeth forth good fruit. ^luke-06-43-douay

44 For every tree is known by its fruit. For men do not gather figs from thorns: nor from a bramble bush do they gather the grape. ^luke-06-44-douay

45 A good man out of the good treasure of his heart bringeth forth that which is good: and an evil man out of the evil treasure bringeth forth that which is evil. For out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh. ^luke-06-45-douay

46 And why call you me, Lord, Lord; and do not the things which I say? ^luke-06-46-douay

47 Every one that cometh to me and heareth my words and doth them, I will show you to whom he is like. ^luke-06-47-douay

48 He is like to a man building a house, who digged deep and laid the foundation upon a rock. And when a flood came, the stream beat vehemently upon that house: and it could not shake it: for it was founded on a rock. ^luke-06-48-douay

49 But he that heareth and doth not is like to a man building his house upon the earth without a foundation: against which the stream beat vehemently. And immediately it fell: and the ruin of that house was great. ^luke-06-49-douay
