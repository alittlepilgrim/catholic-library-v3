# Jonah

Jonah prophesied in the reign of Jereboam the Second: as we learn from [[2-samuel-14-douay|2 Samuel 14:25]]. To whom also he foretold his success in restoring all the borders of Israel. He was of Geth Opher in the tribe of Zabulon, and consequently of Galilee: which confutes that assertion of the Pharisees, [[john-07-douay|John 7:52]], that no prophet ever rose out of Galilee. He prophesied and prefigured in his own person the death and resurrection of Christ: and was the only one among the prophets that was sent to preach to the Gentiles. (*For more information, see the article* JONAS *in the Catholic Encyclopedia*.)

[[jonah-01-douay|**Chapter 1.**]] Jonas being sent to preach in Ninive, fleeth away by sea: a tempest riseth: of which he being found, by lot, to be the cause, is cast into the sea, which thereupon is calmed.

[[jonah-02-douay|**Chapter 2.**]] Jonas is swallowed up by a great fish: he prayeth with confidence in God; and the fish casteth him out on the dry land.

[[jonah-03-douay|**Chapter 3.**]] Jonas is sent again to preach in Ninive. Upon their fasting and repentance, God recalleth the sentence by which they were to be destroyed.

[[jonah-04-douay|**Chapter 4.**]] Jonas, repining to see that his prophecy is not fulfilled, is reproved by the type of the ivy.
