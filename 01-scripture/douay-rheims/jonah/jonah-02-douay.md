# Jonah Chapter 2

Jonas is swallowed up by a great fish: he prays with confidence in God; and the fish casts him out on the dry land.

1 Now the Lord prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonas: and Jonas was in the belly of a fish for three days and three nights. ^jonah-02-1-douay

2 And Jonas prayed to the Lord, his God, out of the belly of the fish. ^jonah-02-2-douay

3 And he said: ^jonah-02-3-douay

I cried out of my affliction to the Lord, and he heard me: I cried out of the belly of hell, and thou hast heard my voice.

4 And thou hast cast me forth into the deep, in the heart of the sea, and a flood hast compassed me: all thy billows, and thy waves have passed over me. ^jonah-02-4-douay

5 And I said: I am cast away out of the sight of thy eyes: but yet I shall see thy holy temple again. ^jonah-02-5-douay

6 The waters compassed me about even to the soul: the deep hath closed me round about, the sea hath covered my head. ^jonah-02-6-douay

7 I went down to the lowest parts of the mountains: the bars of the earth have shut me up for ever: and thou wilt bring up my life from corruption, O Lord, my God. ^jonah-02-7-douay

8 When my soul was in distress within me, I remembered the Lord: that my prayer may come to thee, unto thy holy temple. ^jonah-02-8-douay

9 They that in vain observe vanities, forsake their own mercy. ^jonah-02-9-douay

10 But I with the voice of praise will sacrifice to thee: I will pay whatsoever I have vowed for my salvation to the Lord. ^jonah-02-10-douay

11 And the Lord spoke to the fish: and it vomited out Jonas upon the dry land. **Spoke to the fish...** God's speaking to the fish, was nothing else but his will, which all things obey. ^jonah-02-11-douay
