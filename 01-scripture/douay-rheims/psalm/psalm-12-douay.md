# Psalm 12

A prayer in tribulation.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 13**, and the **[[psalm-11-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 12**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David. ^psalm-12-1-douay

How long, O Lord, wilt thou forget me unto the end? how long dost thou turn away thy face from me?

2 How long shall I take counsels in my soul, sorrow in my heart all the day? ^psalm-12-2-douay

3 How long shall my enemy be exalted over Me? ^psalm-12-3-douay

4 Consider, and hear me, O Lord, my God. Enlighten my eyes, that I never sleep in death: ^psalm-12-4-douay

5 Lest at any time my enemy say: I have prevailed against him. They that trouble me, will rejoice when I am moved: ^psalm-12-5-douay

6 But I have trusted in thy mercy. My heart shall rejoice in thy salvation: I will sing to the Lord, who giveth me good things: yea, I will sing to the name of the Lord, the most high. ^psalm-12-6-douay
