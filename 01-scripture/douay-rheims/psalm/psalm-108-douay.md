# Psalm 108

David in the person of Christ, prays against his persecutors; more especially the traitor Judas: foretelling and approving his just punishment for his obstinacy in sin and final impenitence.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 109**, and the **[[psalm-107-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 108**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David. ^psalm-108-1-douay

2 O God, be not thou silent in my praise: for the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of the deceitful man is opened against me. ^psalm-108-2-douay

3 They have spoken against me with deceitful tongues; and they have compassed me about with words of hatred; and have fought against me without cause. ^psalm-108-3-douay

4 Instead of making me a return of love, they detracted me: but I gave myself to prayer. ^psalm-108-4-douay

5 And they repaid me evil for good: and hatred for my love. ^psalm-108-5-douay

6 Set thou the sinner over him: and may the devil stand at his right hand. **Set thou the sinner over him, etc...** Give to the devil, that arch-sinner, power over him: let him enter into him, and possess him. The imprecations, contained in the thirty verses of this psalm, are opposed to the thirty pieces of silver for which Judas betrayed our Lord; and are to be taken as prophetic denunciations of the evils that should befall the traitor and his accomplices the Jews; and not properly as curses. ^psalm-108-6-douay

7 When he is judged, may he go out condemned; and may his prayer be turned to sin. ^psalm-108-7-douay

8 May his days be few: and his bishopric let another take. ^psalm-108-8-douay

9 May his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow. ^psalm-108-9-douay

10 Let his children be carried about vagabonds, and beg; and let them be cast out of their dwellings. ^psalm-108-10-douay

11 May the usurer search all his substance: and let strangers plunder his labours. ^psalm-108-11-douay

12 May there be none to help him: nor none to pity his fatherless offspring. ^psalm-108-12-douay

13 May his posterity be cut off; in one generation may his name be blotted out. ^psalm-108-13-douay

14 May the iniquity of his fathers be remembered in the sight of the Lord: and let not the sin of his mother be blotted out. ^psalm-108-14-douay

15 May they be before the Lord continually, and let the memory of them perish from the earth: ^psalm-108-15-douay

16 Because he remembered not to show mercy, ^psalm-108-16-douay

17 But persecuted the poor man and the beggar; and the broken in heart, to put him to death. ^psalm-108-17-douay

18 And he loved cursing, and it shall come unto him: and he would not have blessing, and it shall be far from him. And he put on cursing, like a garment: and it went in like water into his entrails, and like oil in his bones. ^psalm-108-18-douay

19 May it be unto him like a garment which covereth him; and like a girdle with which he is girded continually. ^psalm-108-19-douay

20 This is the work of them who detract me before the Lord; and who speak evils against my soul. ^psalm-108-20-douay

21 But thou, O Lord, do with me for thy name's sake: because thy mercy is sweet. Do thou deliver me, ^psalm-108-21-douay

22 For I am poor and needy, and my heart is troubled within me. ^psalm-108-22-douay

23 I am taken away like the shadow when it declineth: and I am shaken off as locusts. ^psalm-108-23-douay

24 My knees are weakened through fasting: and my flesh is changed for oil. **For oil...** Propter oleum. The meaning is, my flesh is changed, being perfectly emaciated and dried up, as having lost all its oil or fatness. ^psalm-108-24-douay

25 And I am become a reproach to them: they saw me and they shaked their heads. ^psalm-108-25-douay

26 Help me, O Lord my God; save me; according to thy mercy. ^psalm-108-26-douay

27 And let them know that this is thy hand: and that thou, O Lord, hast done it. ^psalm-108-27-douay

28 They will curse and thou wilt bless: let them that rise up against me be confounded: but thy servant shall rejoice. ^psalm-108-28-douay

29 Let them that detract me be clothed with shame: and let them be covered with their confusion as with a double cloak. ^psalm-108-29-douay

30 I will give great thanks to the Lord with my mouth: and in the midst of many I will praise him. ^psalm-108-30-douay

31 Because he hath stood at the right hand of the poor, to save my soul from persecutors. ^psalm-108-31-douay
