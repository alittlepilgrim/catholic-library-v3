# Psalm 131

A prayer for the fulfilling of the promise made to David.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 132**, and the **[[psalm-130-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 131**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 O Lord, remember David, and all his meekness. ^psalm-131-1-douay

2 How he swore to the Lord, he vowed a vow to the God of Jacob: ^psalm-131-2-douay

3 If I shall enter into the tabernacle of my house: if I shall go up into the bed wherein I lie: ^psalm-131-3-douay

4 If I shall give sleep to my eyes, or slumber to my eyelids, ^psalm-131-4-douay

5 Or rest to my temples: until I find out a place for the Lord, a tabernacle for the God of Jacob. ^psalm-131-5-douay

6 Behold we have heard of it in Ephrata: we have found it in the fields of the wood. **We have heard of it in Ephrata...** When I was young, and lived in Bethlehem, otherwise called Ephrata, I heard of God's tabernacle and ark, and had a devout desire of seeking it; and accordingly I found it at Cariathiarim, the city of the woods: where it was till it was removed to Jerusalem. See 1 Par. 13. ^psalm-131-6-douay

7 We will go into his tabernacle: we will adore in the place where his feet stood. ^psalm-131-7-douay

8 Arise, O Lord, into thy resting place: thou and the ark, which thou hast sanctified. ^psalm-131-8-douay

9 Let thy priests be clothed with justice: and let thy saints rejoice. ^psalm-131-9-douay

10 For thy servant David's sake, turn not away the face of thy anointed. ^psalm-131-10-douay

11 The Lord hath sworn truth to David, and he will not make it void: of the fruit of thy womb I will set upon thy throne. ^psalm-131-11-douay

12 If thy children will keep my covenant, and these my testimonies which I shall teach them: Their children also for evermore shall sit upon thy throne. ^psalm-131-12-douay

13 For the Lord hath chosen Sion: he hath chosen it for his dwelling. ^psalm-131-13-douay

14 This is my rest for ever and ever: here will I dwell, for I have chosen it. ^psalm-131-14-douay

15 Blessing I will bless her widow: I will satisfy her poor with bread. ^psalm-131-15-douay

16 I will clothe her priests with salvation, and her saints shall rejoice with exceeding great joy. ^psalm-131-16-douay

17 There will I bring forth a horn to David: I have prepared a lamp for my anointed. ^psalm-131-17-douay

18 His enemies I will clothe with confusion: but upon him shall my sanctification flourish. ^psalm-131-18-douay
