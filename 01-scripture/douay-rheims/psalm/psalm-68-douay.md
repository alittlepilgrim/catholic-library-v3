# Psalm 68

Christ in his passion declares the greatness of his sufferings, and the malice of his persecutors the Jews; and foretells their reprobation.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 69**, and the **[[psalm-67-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 68**.

1 Unto the end, for them that shall be changed; for David. **For them that shall be changed...** A psalm for Christian converts, to remember the passion of Christ. ^psalm-68-1-douay

2 Save me, O God: for the waters are come in even unto my soul. **The waters...** Of afflictions and sorrows. My soul is sorrowful even unto death. [[matthew-26-douay|Matthew 26:38]]. ^psalm-68-2-douay

3 I stick fast in the mire of the deep and there is no sure standing. I am come into the depth of the sea, and a tempest hath overwhelmed me. ^psalm-68-3-douay

4 I have laboured with crying; my jaws are become hoarse, my eyes have failed, whilst I hope in my God. ^psalm-68-4-douay

5 They are multiplied above the hairs of my head, who hate me without cause. My enemies are grown strong who have wrongfully persecuted me: then did I pay that which I took not away. **I pay that which I took not away...** Christ in his passion made restitution of what he had not taken away, by suffering the punishment due to our sins, and so repairing the injury we had done to God. ^psalm-68-5-douay

6 O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my offences are not hidden from thee: **My foolishness and my offences...** which my enemies impute to me: or the follies and sins of men, which I have taken upon myself. ^psalm-68-6-douay

7 Let not them be ashamed for me, who look for thee, O Lord, the Lord of hosts. Let them not be confounded on my account, who seek thee, O God of Israel. ^psalm-68-7-douay

8 Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face. ^psalm-68-8-douay

9 I am become a stranger to my brethren, and an alien to the sons of my mother. ^psalm-68-9-douay

10 For the zeal of thy house hath eaten me up: and the reproaches of them that reproached thee are fallen upon me. ^psalm-68-10-douay

11 And I covered my soul in fasting: and it was made a reproach to me. ^psalm-68-11-douay

12 And I made haircloth my garment: and I became a byword to them. ^psalm-68-12-douay

13 They that sat in the gate spoke against me: and they that drank wine made me their song. ^psalm-68-13-douay

14 But as for me, my prayer is to thee, O Lord; for the time of thy good pleasure, O God. In the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation. ^psalm-68-14-douay

15 Draw me out of the mire, that I may not stick fast: deliver me from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters. ^psalm-68-15-douay

16 Let not the tempest of water drown me, nor the deep water swallow me up: and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me. ^psalm-68-16-douay

17 Hear me, O Lord, for thy mercy is kind; look upon me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies. ^psalm-68-17-douay

18 And turn not away thy face from thy servant: for I am in trouble, hear me speedily. ^psalm-68-18-douay

19 Attend to my soul, and deliver it: save me because of my enemies. ^psalm-68-19-douay

20 Thou knowest my reproach, and my confusion, and my shame. ^psalm-68-20-douay

21 In thy sight are all they that afflict me; my heart hath expected reproach and misery. And I looked for one that would grieve together with me, but there was none: and for one that would comfort me, and I found none. ^psalm-68-21-douay

22 And they gave me gall for my food, and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. ^psalm-68-22-douay

23 Let their table become as a snare before them, and a recompense, and a stumbling block. **Let their table, etc...** What here follows in the style of an imprecation, is a prophecy of the wretched state to which the Jews should be reduced in punishment of their wilful obstinacy. ^psalm-68-23-douay

24 Let their eyes be darkened that they see not; and their back bend thou down always. ^psalm-68-24-douay

25 Pour out thy indignation upon them: and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them. ^psalm-68-25-douay

26 Let their habitation be made desolate: and let there be none to dwell in their tabernacles. ^psalm-68-26-douay

27 Because they have persecuted him whom thou hast smitten; and they have added to the grief of my wounds. ^psalm-68-27-douay

28 Add thou iniquity upon their iniquity: and let them not come into thy justice. ^psalm-68-28-douay

29 Let them be blotted out of the book of the living; and with the just let them not be written. ^psalm-68-29-douay

30 But I am poor and sorrowful: thy salvation, O God, hath set me up. ^psalm-68-30-douay

31 I will praise the name of God with a canticle: and I will magnify him with praise. ^psalm-68-31-douay

32 And it shall please God better than a young calf, that bringeth forth horns and hoofs. ^psalm-68-32-douay

33 Let the poor see and rejoice: seek ye God, and your soul shall live. ^psalm-68-33-douay

34 For the Lord hath heard the poor: and hath not despised his prisoners. ^psalm-68-34-douay

35 Let the heavens and the earth praise him; the sea, and every thing that creepeth therein. ^psalm-68-35-douay

36 For God will save Sion, and the cities of Juda shall be built up. And they shall dwell there, and acquire it by inheritance. **Sion...** The catholic church. The cities of Juda, etc., her places of worship, which shall be established throughout the world. And there, viz., in this church of Christ, shall his servants dwell, etc. ^psalm-68-36-douay

37 And the seed of his servants shall possess it; and they that love his name shall dwell therein. ^psalm-68-37-douay
