# Psalm 65

An invitation to praise God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 66**, and the **[[psalm-64-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 65**.

*Unto the end, a canticle of a psalm of the resurrection.*

1 Shout with joy to God, all the earth, ^psalm-65-1-douay

2 Sing ye a psalm to his name; give glory to his praise. ^psalm-65-2-douay

3 Say unto God, How terrible are thy works, O Lord! in the multitude of thy strength thy enemies shall lie to thee. ^psalm-65-3-douay

4 Let all the earth adore thee, and sing to thee: let it sing a psalm to thy name. ^psalm-65-4-douay

5 Come and see the works of God; who is terrible in his counsels over the sons of men. ^psalm-65-5-douay

6 Who turneth the sea into dry land, in the river they shall pass on foot: there shall we rejoice in him. ^psalm-65-6-douay

7 Who by his power ruleth for ever: his eyes behold the nations; let not them that provoke him be exalted in themselves. ^psalm-65-7-douay

8 O bless our God, ye Gentiles: and make the voice of his praise to be heard. ^psalm-65-8-douay

9 Who hath set my soul to live: and hath not suffered my feet to be moved: ^psalm-65-9-douay

10 For thou, O God, hast proved us: thou hast tried us by fire, as silver is tried. ^psalm-65-10-douay

11 Thou hast brought us into a net, thou hast laid afflictions on our back: ^psalm-65-11-douay

12 Thou hast set men over our heads. We have passed through fire and water, and thou hast brought us out into a refreshment. ^psalm-65-12-douay

13 I will go into thy house with burnt offerings: I will pay thee my vows, ^psalm-65-13-douay

14 Which my lips have uttered, And my mouth hath spoken, when I was in trouble. ^psalm-65-14-douay

15 I will offer up to thee holocausts full of marrow, with burnt offerings of rams: I will offer to thee bullocks with goats. ^psalm-65-15-douay

16 Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will tell you what great things he hath done for my soul. ^psalm-65-16-douay

17 I cried to him with my mouth: and I extolled him with my tongue. ^psalm-65-17-douay

18 If I have looked at iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me. ^psalm-65-18-douay

19 Therefore hath God heard me, and hath attended to the voice of my supplication. ^psalm-65-19-douay

20 Blessed be God, who hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me. ^psalm-65-20-douay
