# Psalm 128

The Church of God is invincible: her persecutors come to nothing.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 129**, and the **[[psalm-127-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 128**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 Often have they fought against me from my youth, let Israel now say. ^psalm-128-1-douay

2 Often have they fought against me from my youth: but they could not prevail over me. ^psalm-128-2-douay

3 The wicked have wrought upon my back: they have lengthened their iniquity. ^psalm-128-3-douay

4 The Lord who is just will cut the necks of sinners: ^psalm-128-4-douay

5 Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Sion. ^psalm-128-5-douay

6 Let them be as grass upon the tops of houses: which withereth before it be plucked up: ^psalm-128-6-douay

7 Who with the mower filleth not his hand: nor he that gathereth sheaves his bosom. ^psalm-128-7-douay

8 And they that passed by have not said: The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we have blessed you in the name of the Lord. ^psalm-128-8-douay
