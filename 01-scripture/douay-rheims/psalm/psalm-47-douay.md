# Psalm 47

God is greatly to be praised for the establishment of his Church.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 48**, and the **[[psalm-46-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 47**.

1 A psalm of a canticle, for the sons of Core, on the second day of the week. ^psalm-47-1-douay

2 Great is the Lord, and exceedingly to be praised in the city of our God, in his holy mountain. ^psalm-47-2-douay

3 With the joy of the whole earth is mount Sion founded, on the sides of the north, the city of the great king. ^psalm-47-3-douay

4 In her houses shall God be known, when he shall protect her. ^psalm-47-4-douay

5 For behold the kings of the earth assembled themselves: they gathered together. ^psalm-47-5-douay

6 So they saw, and they wondered, they were troubled, they were moved: ^psalm-47-6-douay

7 Trembling took hold of them. There were pains as of a woman in labour. ^psalm-47-7-douay

8 With a vehement wind thou shalt break in pieces the ships of Tharsis. ^psalm-47-8-douay

9 As we have heard, so have we seen, in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God: God hath founded it for ever. ^psalm-47-9-douay

10 We have received thy mercy, O God, in the midst of thy temple. ^psalm-47-10-douay

11 According to thy name, O God, so also is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of justice. ^psalm-47-11-douay

12 Let mount Sion rejoice, and the daughters of Juda be glad; because of thy judgments, O Lord. ^psalm-47-12-douay

13 Surround Sion, and encompass her: tell lye in her towers. ^psalm-47-13-douay

14 Set your hearts on her strength; and distribute her houses, that ye may relate it in another generation. ^psalm-47-14-douay

15 For this is God, our God unto eternity, and for ever and ever: he shall rule us for evermore. ^psalm-47-15-douay
