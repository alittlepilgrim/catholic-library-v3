# Psalm 62

The prophet aspires after God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 63**, and the **[[psalm-61-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 62**.

1 A psalm of David while he was in the desert of Edom. ^psalm-62-1-douay

2 O God, my God, to thee do I watch at break of day. For thee my soul hath thirsted; for thee my flesh, O how many ways! ^psalm-62-2-douay

3 In a desert land, and where there is no way, and no water: so in the sanctuary have I come before thee, to see thy power and thy glory. ^psalm-62-3-douay

4 For thy mercy is better than lives: thee my lips will praise. ^psalm-62-4-douay

5 Thus will I bless thee all my life long: and in thy name I will lift up my hands. ^psalm-62-5-douay

6 Let my soul be filled as with marrow and fatness: and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips. ^psalm-62-6-douay

7 If I have remembered thee upon my bed, I will meditate on thee in the morning: ^psalm-62-7-douay

8 Because thou hast been my helper. And I will rejoice under the covert of thy wings: ^psalm-62-8-douay

9 My soul hath stuck close to thee: thy right hand hath received me. ^psalm-62-9-douay

10 But they have fought my soul in vain, they shall go into the lower parts of the earth: ^psalm-62-10-douay

11 They shall be delivered into the hands of the sword, they shall be the portions of foxes. ^psalm-62-11-douay

12 But the king shall rejoice in God, all they shall be praised that swear by him: because the mouth is stopped of them that speak wicked things. ^psalm-62-12-douay
