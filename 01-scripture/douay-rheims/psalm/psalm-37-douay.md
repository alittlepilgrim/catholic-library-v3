# Psalm 37

A prayer of a penitent for the remission of his sins. The third penitential psalm.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 38**, and the **[[psalm-36-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 37**.

1 A psalm for David, for a remembrance of the sabbath. **For a remembrance...** Viz., of our miseries and sins: and to be sung on the sabbath day. ^psalm-37-1-douay

2 Rebuke me not, O Lord, in thy indignation; nor chastise me in thy wrath. ^psalm-37-2-douay

3 For thy arrows are fastened in me: and thy hand hath been strong upon me. ^psalm-37-3-douay

4 There is no health in my flesh, because of thy wrath: there is no peace for my bones, because of my sins. ^psalm-37-4-douay

5 For my iniquities are gone over my head: and as a heavy burden are become heavy upon me. ^psalm-37-5-douay

6 My sores are putrefied and corrupted, because of my foolishness. ^psalm-37-6-douay

7 I am become miserable, and am bowed down even to the end: I walked sorrowful all the day long. ^psalm-37-7-douay

8 For my loins are filled with illusions; and there is no health in my flesh. ^psalm-37-8-douay

9 I am afflicted and humbled exceedingly: I roared with the groaning of my heart. ^psalm-37-9-douay

10 Lord, all my desire is before thee, and my groaning is not hidden from thee. ^psalm-37-10-douay

11 My heart is troubled, my strength hath left me, and the light of my eyes itself is not with me. ^psalm-37-11-douay

12 My friends and my neighbours have drawn near, and stood against me. And they that were near me stood afar off: ^psalm-37-12-douay

13 And they that sought my soul used violence. And they that sought evils to me spoke vain things, and studied deceits all the day long. ^psalm-37-13-douay

14 But I, as a deaf man, heard not: and as a dumb man not opening his mouth. ^psalm-37-14-douay

15 And I became as a man that heareth not: and that hath no reproofs in his mouth. ^psalm-37-15-douay

16 For in thee, O Lord, have I hoped: thou wilt hear me, O Lord my God. ^psalm-37-16-douay

17 For I said: Lest at any time my enemies rejoice over me: and whilst my feet are moved, they speak great things against me. ^psalm-37-17-douay

18 For I am ready for scourges: and my sorrow is continually before me. ^psalm-37-18-douay

19 For I will declare my iniquity: and I will think for my sin. ^psalm-37-19-douay

20 But my enemies live, and are stronger than I: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied. ^psalm-37-20-douay

21 They that render evil for good, have detracted me, because I followed goodness. ^psalm-37-21-douay

22 For sake me not, O Lord my God: do not thou depart from me. ^psalm-37-22-douay

23 Attend unto my help, O Lord, the God of my salvation. ^psalm-37-23-douay
