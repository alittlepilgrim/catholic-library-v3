# Psalm 50

The repentance and confession of David after his sin. The fourth penitential psalm.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 51**, and the **[[psalm-49-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 50**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm of David, ^psalm-50-1-douay

2 When Nathan the prophet came to him, after he had sinned with Bethsabee. [2 Samuel 12.] ^psalm-50-2-douay

3 Have mercy on me, O God, according to thy great mercy. And according to the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my iniquity. ^psalm-50-3-douay

4 Wash me yet more from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. ^psalm-50-4-douay

5 For I know my iniquity, and my sin is always before me. ^psalm-50-5-douay

6 To thee only have I sinned, and have done evil before thee: that thou mayst be justified in thy words, and mayst overcome when thou art judged. ^psalm-50-6-douay

7 For behold I was conceived in iniquities; and in sins did my mother conceive me. ^psalm-50-7-douay

8 For behold thou hast loved truth: the uncertain and hidden things of thy wisdom thou hast made manifest to me. ^psalm-50-8-douay

9 Thou shalt sprinkle me with hyssop, and I shall be cleansed: thou shalt wash me, and I shall be made whiter than snow. ^psalm-50-9-douay

10 To my hearing thou shalt give joy and gladness: and the bones that have been humbled shall rejoice. ^psalm-50-10-douay

11 Turn away thy face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities. ^psalm-50-11-douay

12 Create a clean heart in me, O God: and renew a right spirit within my bowels. ^psalm-50-12-douay

13 Cast me not away from thy face; and take not thy holy spirit from me. ^psalm-50-13-douay

14 Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation, and strengthen me with a perfect spirit. ^psalm-50-14-douay

15 I will teach the unjust thy ways: and the wicked shall be converted to thee. ^psalm-50-15-douay

16 Deliver me from blood, O God, thou God of my salvation: and my tongue shall extol thy justice. ^psalm-50-16-douay

17 O Lord, thou wilt open my lips: and my mouth shall declare thy praise. ^psalm-50-17-douay

18 For if thou hadst desired sacrifice, I would indeed have given it: with burnt offerings thou wilt not be delighted. ^psalm-50-18-douay

19 A sacrifice to God is an afflicted spirit: a contrite and humbled heart, O God, thou wilt not despise. ^psalm-50-19-douay

20 Deal favourably, O Lord, in thy good will with Sion; that the walls of Jerusalem may be built up. ^psalm-50-20-douay

21 Then shalt thou accept the sacrifice of justice, oblations and whole burnt offerings: then shall they lay calves upon thy altar. ^psalm-50-21-douay
