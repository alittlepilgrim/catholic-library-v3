# Psalm 80

An invitation to a solemn praising of God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 81**, and the **[[psalm-79-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 80**.

1 Unto the end, for the winepresses, a psalm for Asaph himself. **For the winepresses, etc...** Torcularibus. It either signifies a musical instrument, or that this psalm was to be sung at the feast of the tabernacles after the gathering in of the vintage. ^psalm-80-1-douay

2 Rejoice to God our helper: sing aloud to the God of Jacob. ^psalm-80-2-douay

3 Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel: the pleasant psaltery with the harp. ^psalm-80-3-douay

4 Blow up the trumpet on the new moon, on the noted day of your solemnity. ^psalm-80-4-douay

5 For it is a commandment in Israel, and a judgment to the God of Jacob. ^psalm-80-5-douay

6 He ordained it for a testimony in Joseph, when he came out of the land of Egypt: he heard a tongue which he knew not. ^psalm-80-6-douay

7 He removed his back from the burdens: his hands had served in baskets. ^psalm-80-7-douay

8 Thou calledst upon me in affliction, and I delivered thee: I heard thee in the secret place of tempest: I proved thee at the waters of contradiction. **In the secret place of tempest...** Hebrew, *of thunder*. When thou soughtest to hide thyself from the tempest: or, when I came down to mount Sina, hidden from thy eyes in a storm of thunder. ^psalm-80-8-douay

9 Hear, O my people, and I will testify to thee: O Israel, if thou wilt hearken to me, ^psalm-80-9-douay

10 There shall be no new god in thee: neither shalt thou adore a strange god. ^psalm-80-10-douay

11 For I am the Lord thy God, who brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it. ^psalm-80-11-douay

12 But my people heard not my voice: and Israel hearkened not to me. ^psalm-80-12-douay

13 So I let them go according to the desires of their heart: they shall walk in their own inventions. ^psalm-80-13-douay

14 If my people had heard me: if Israel had walked in my ways: ^psalm-80-14-douay

15 I should soon have humbled their enemies, and laid my hand on them that troubled them. ^psalm-80-15-douay

16 The enemies of the Lord have lied to him: and their time shall be for ever. **Their time shall be forever...** Impenitent sinners shall suffer for ever. ^psalm-80-16-douay

17 And he fed them with the fat of wheat, and filled them with honey out of the rock. ^psalm-80-17-douay
