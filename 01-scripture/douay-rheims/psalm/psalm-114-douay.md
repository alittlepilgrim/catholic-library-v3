# Psalm 114

The prayer of a just man in affliction, with a lively confidence in God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in many popular Bible translations, the psalm below is combined with the **[[psalm-115-douay|next psalm]]** to form **Psalm 116**.

*Alleluia.*

1 I have loved, because the Lord will hear the voice of my prayer. ^psalm-114-1-douay

2 Because he hath inclined his ear unto me: and in my days I will call upon him. ^psalm-114-2-douay

3 The sorrows of death have compassed me: and the perils of hell have found me. I met with trouble and sorrow: ^psalm-114-3-douay

4 And I called upon the name of the Lord. O Lord, deliver my soul. ^psalm-114-4-douay

5 The Lord is merciful and just, and our God showeth mercy. ^psalm-114-5-douay

6 The Lord is the keeper of little ones: I was humbled, and he delivered me. ^psalm-114-6-douay

7 Turn, O my soul, into thy rest: for the Lord hath been bountiful to thee. ^psalm-114-7-douay

8 For he hath delivered my soul from death: my eyes from tears, my feet from falling. ^psalm-114-8-douay

9 I will please the Lord in the land of the living. ^psalm-114-9-douay
