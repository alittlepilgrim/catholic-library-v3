# Psalm 2

1 Why have the Gentiles raged, and the people devised vain things? ^psalm-02-1-douay

2 The kings of the earth stood up, and the princes met together, against the Lord, and against his Christ. ^psalm-02-2-douay

3 Let us break their bonds asunder: and let us cast away their yoke from us. ^psalm-02-3-douay

4 He that dwelleth in heaven shall laugh at them: and the Lord shall deride them. ^psalm-02-4-douay

5 Then shall he speak to them in his anger, and trouble them in his rage. ^psalm-02-5-douay

6 But I am appointed king by him over Sion, his holy mountain, preaching his commandment. ^psalm-02-6-douay

7 The Lord hath said to me: Thou art my son, this day have I begotten thee. ^psalm-02-7-douay

8 Ask of me, and I will give thee the Gentiles for thy inheritance, and the utmost parts of the earth for thy possession. ^psalm-02-8-douay

9 Thou shalt rule them with a rod of iron, and shalt break them in pieces like a potter's vessel. ^psalm-02-9-douay

10 And now, O ye kings, understand: receive instruction, you that judge the earth. ^psalm-02-10-douay

11 Serve ye the Lord with fear: and rejoice unto him with trembling. ^psalm-02-11-douay

12 Embrace discipline, lest at any time the Lord be angry, and you perish from the just way. ^psalm-02-12-douay

13 When his wrath shall be kindled in a short time, blessed are all they that trust in him. ^psalm-02-13-douay
