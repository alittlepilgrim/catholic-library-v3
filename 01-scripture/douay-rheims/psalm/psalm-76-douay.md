# Psalm 76

The faithful have recourse to God in trouble of mind, with confidence in his mercy and power.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 77**, and the **[[psalm-75-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 76**.

1 Unto the end, for Idithun, a psalm of Asaph. ^psalm-76-1-douay

2 I cried to the Lord with my voice; to God with my voice, and he gave ear to me. ^psalm-76-2-douay

3 In the days of my trouble I sought God, with my hands lifted up to him in the night, and I was not deceived. My soul refused to be comforted: ^psalm-76-3-douay

4 I remembered God, and was delighted, and was exercised, and my spirit swooned away. ^psalm-76-4-douay

5 My eyes prevented the watches: I was troubled, and I spoke not. ^psalm-76-5-douay

6 I thought upon the days of old: and I had in my mind the eternal years. ^psalm-76-6-douay

7 And I meditated in the night with my own heart: and I was exercised and I swept my spirit. ^psalm-76-7-douay

8 Will God then cast off for ever? or will he never be more favourable again? ^psalm-76-8-douay

9 Or will he cut off his mercy for ever, from generation to generation? ^psalm-76-9-douay

10 Or will God forget to show mercy? or will he in his anger shut up his mercies? ^psalm-76-10-douay

11 And I said, Now have I begun: this is the change of the right hand of the most High. ^psalm-76-11-douay

12 I remembered the works of the Lord: for I will be mindful of thy wonders from the beginning. ^psalm-76-12-douay

13 And I will meditate on all thy works: and will be employed in thy inventions. ^psalm-76-13-douay

14 Thy way, O God, is in the holy place: who is the great God like our God? ^psalm-76-14-douay

15 Thou art the God that dost wonders. Thou hast made thy power known among the nations: ^psalm-76-15-douay

16 With thy arm thou hast redeemed thy people the children of Jacob and of Joseph. ^psalm-76-16-douay

17 The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee: and they were afraid, and the depths were troubled. ^psalm-76-17-douay

18 Great was the noise of the waters: the clouds sent out a sound. For thy arrows pass: ^psalm-76-18-douay

19 The voice of thy thunder in a wheel. Thy lightnings enlightened the world: the earth shook and trembled. ^psalm-76-19-douay

20 Thy way is in the sea, and thy paths in many waters: and thy footsteps shall not be known. ^psalm-76-20-douay

21 Thou hast conducted thy people like sheep, by the hand of Moses and Aaron. ^psalm-76-21-douay
