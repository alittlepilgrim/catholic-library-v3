# Psalm 118

Of the excellence of virtue consisting in the love and observance of the commandments of God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 119**, and the **[[psalm-117-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 118**.

*Alleluia.*

**ALEPH**

1 Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord. **Aleph...** The first eight verses of this psalm in the original begin with Aleph, which is the name of the first letter of the Hebrew alphabet. The second eight verses begin with Beth, the name of the second letter of the Hebrew alphabet; and so to the end of the whole alphabet, in all twenty-two letters, each letter having eight verses. This order is variously expounded by the holy fathers; which shows the difficulty of understanding the holy scriptures, and consequently with what humility, and submission to the Church they are to be read. ^psalm-118-1-douay

2 Blessed are they that search his testimonies: that seek him with their whole heart. **His testimonies...** The commandments of God are called his testimonies, because they testify his holy will unto us. Note here, that in almost every verse of this psalm (which in number are 176) the word and law of God, and the love and observance of it, is perpetually inculcated, under a variety of denominations, all signifying the same thing. ^psalm-118-2-douay

3 For they that work iniquity, have not walked in his ways. ^psalm-118-3-douay

4 Thou hast commanded thy commandments to be kept most diligently. ^psalm-118-4-douay

5 O! that my ways may be directed to keep thy justifications. ^psalm-118-5-douay

6 Then shall I not be confounded, when I shall look into all thy commandments. ^psalm-118-6-douay

7 I will praise thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned the judgments of thy justice. ^psalm-118-7-douay

8 I will keep thy justifications: O! do not thou utterly forsake me. ^psalm-118-8-douay

**BETH**


9 By what doth a young man correct his way? by observing thy words. ^psalm-118-9-douay

10 With my whole heart have I sought after thee: let me not stray from thy commandments. ^psalm-118-10-douay

11 Thy words have I hidden in my heart, that I may not sin against thee. ^psalm-118-11-douay

12 Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-12-douay

13 With my lips I have pronounced all the judgments of thy mouth. ^psalm-118-13-douay

14 I have been delighted in the way of thy testimonies, as in all riches. ^psalm-118-14-douay

15 I will meditate on thy commandments: and I will consider thy ways. ^psalm-118-15-douay

16 I will think of thy justifications: I will not forget thy words. ^psalm-118-16-douay

**GIMEL**


17 Give bountifully to thy servant, enliven me: and I shall keep thy words. ^psalm-118-17-douay

18 Open thou my eyes: and I will consider the wondrous things of thy law. ^psalm-118-18-douay

19 I am a sojourner on the earth: hide not thy commandments from me. ^psalm-118-19-douay

20 My soul hath coveted to long for thy justifications, at all times. ^psalm-118-20-douay

21 Thou hast rebuked the proud: they are cursed who decline from thy commandments. ^psalm-118-21-douay

22 Remove from me reproach and contempt: because I have sought after thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-22-douay

23 For princes sat, and spoke against me: but thy servant was employed in thy justifications. ^psalm-118-23-douay

24 For thy testimonies are my meditation: and thy justifications my counsel. ^psalm-118-24-douay

**DALETH**


25 My soul hath cleaved to the pavement: quicken thou me according to thy word. ^psalm-118-25-douay

26 I have declared my ways, and thou hast heard me: teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-26-douay

27 Make me to understand the way of thy justifications: and I shall be exercised in thy wondrous works. ^psalm-118-27-douay

28 My soul hath slumbered through heaviness: strengthen thou me in thy words. ^psalm-118-28-douay

29 Remove from me the way of iniquity: and out of thy law have mercy on me. ^psalm-118-29-douay

30 I have chosen the way of truth: thy judgments I have not forgotten. ^psalm-118-30-douay

31 I have stuck to thy testimonies, O Lord: put me not to shame. ^psalm-118-31-douay

32 I have run the way of thy commandments, when thou didst enlarge my heart. ^psalm-118-32-douay

**HE**


33 Set before me for a law the way of thy justifications, O Lord: and I will always seek after it. ^psalm-118-33-douay

34 Give me understanding, and I will search thy law; and I will keep it with my whole heart. ^psalm-118-34-douay

35 Lead me into the path of thy commandments; for this same I have desired. ^psalm-118-35-douay

36 Incline my heart into thy testimonies and not to covetousness. ^psalm-118-36-douay

37 Turn away my eyes that they may not behold vanity: quicken me in thy way. ^psalm-118-37-douay

38 Establish thy word to thy servant, in thy fear. ^psalm-118-38-douay

39 Turn away my reproach, which I have apprehended: for thy judgments are delightful. ^psalm-118-39-douay

40 Behold I have longed after thy precepts: quicken me in thy justice. ^psalm-118-40-douay

**VAU**


41 Let thy mercy also come upon me, O Lord: thy salvation according to thy word. ^psalm-118-41-douay

42 So shall I answer them that reproach me in any thing; that I have trusted in thy words. ^psalm-118-42-douay

43 And take not thou the word of truth utterly out of my mouth: for in thy words, I have hoped exceedingly. ^psalm-118-43-douay

44 So shall I always keep thy law, for ever and ever. ^psalm-118-44-douay

45 And I walked at large: because I have sought after thy commandments. ^psalm-118-45-douay

46 And I spoke of thy testimonies before kings: and I was not ashamed. ^psalm-118-46-douay

47 I meditated also on thy commandments, which I loved. ^psalm-118-47-douay

48 And I lifted up my hands to thy commandments, which I loved: and I was exercised in thy justifications. ^psalm-118-48-douay

**ZAIN**


49 Be thou mindful of thy word to thy servant, in which thou hast given me hope. ^psalm-118-49-douay

50 This hath comforted me in my humiliation: because thy word hath enlivened me. ^psalm-118-50-douay

51 The proud did iniquitously altogether: but I declined not from thy law. ^psalm-118-51-douay

52 I remembered, O Lord, thy judgments of old: and I was comforted. ^psalm-118-52-douay

53 A fainting hath taken hold of me, because of the wicked that forsake thy law. ^psalm-118-53-douay

54 Thy justifications were the subject of my song, in the place of my pilgrimage. ^psalm-118-54-douay

55 In the night I have remembered thy name, O Lord: and have kept thy law. ^psalm-118-55-douay

56 This happened to me: because I sought after thy justifications. ^psalm-118-56-douay

**HETH**


57 O Lord, my portion, I have said, I would keep thy law. ^psalm-118-57-douay

58 I entreated thy face with all my heart: have mercy on me according to thy word. ^psalm-118-58-douay

59 I have thought on my ways: and turned my feet unto thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-59-douay

60 I am ready, and am not troubled: that I may keep thy commandments. ^psalm-118-60-douay

61 The cords of the wicked have encompassed me: but I have not forgotten thy law. ^psalm-118-61-douay

62 I rose at midnight to give praise to thee; for the judgments of thy justification. ^psalm-118-62-douay

63 I am a partaker with all them that fear thee, and that keep thy commandments. ^psalm-118-63-douay

64 The earth, O Lord, is full of thy mercy: teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-64-douay

**TETH**


65 Thou hast done well with thy servant, O Lord, according to thy word. ^psalm-118-65-douay

66 Teach me goodness and discipline and knowledge; for I have believed thy commandments. ^psalm-118-66-douay

67 Before I was humbled I offended; therefore have I kept thy word. ^psalm-118-67-douay

68 Thou art good; and in thy goodness teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-68-douay

69 The iniquity of the proud hath been multiplied over me: but I will seek thy commandments with my whole heart. ^psalm-118-69-douay

70 Their heart is curdled like milk: but I have meditated on thy law. ^psalm-118-70-douay

71 It is good for me that thou hast humbled me, that I may learn thy justifications. ^psalm-118-71-douay

72 The law of thy mouth is good to me, above thousands of gold and silver. ^psalm-118-72-douay

**JOD**


73 Thy hands have made me and formed me: give me understanding, and I will learn thy commandments. ^psalm-118-73-douay

74 They that fear thee shall see me, and shall be glad: because I have greatly hoped in thy words. ^psalm-118-74-douay

75 I know, O Lord, that thy judgments are equity: and in thy truth thou hast humbled me. ^psalm-118-75-douay

76 O! let thy mercy be for my comfort, according to thy word unto thy servant. ^psalm-118-76-douay

77 Let thy tender mercies come unto me, and I shall live: for thy law is my meditation. ^psalm-118-77-douay

78 Let the proud be ashamed, because they have done unjustly towards me: but I will be employed in thy commandments. ^psalm-118-78-douay

79 Let them that fear thee turn to me: and they that know thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-79-douay

80 Let my heart be undefiled in thy justifications, that I may not be confounded. ^psalm-118-80-douay

**CAPH**


81 My soul hath fainted after thy salvation: and in thy word I have very much hoped. ^psalm-118-81-douay

82 My eyes have failed for thy word, saying: When wilt thou comfort me? ^psalm-118-82-douay

83 For I am become like a bottle in the frost: I have not forgotten thy justifications. ^psalm-118-83-douay

84 How many are the days of thy servant: when wilt thou execute judgment on them that persecute me? ^psalm-118-84-douay

85 The wicked have told me fables: but not as thy law. ^psalm-118-85-douay

86 All thy statutes are truth: they have persecuted me unjustly, do thou help me. ^psalm-118-86-douay

87 They had almost made an end of me upon earth: but I have not forsaken thy commandments. ^psalm-118-87-douay

88 Quicken thou me according to thy mercy: and I shall keep the testimonies of thy mouth. ^psalm-118-88-douay

**LAMED**


89 For ever, O Lord, thy word standeth firm in heaven. ^psalm-118-89-douay

90 Thy truth unto all generations: thou hast founded the earth, and it continueth. ^psalm-118-90-douay

91 By thy ordinance the day goeth on: for all things serve thee. ^psalm-118-91-douay

92 Unless thy law had been my meditation, I had then perhaps perished in my abjection. ^psalm-118-92-douay

93 Thy justifications I will never forget: for by them thou hast given me life. ^psalm-118-93-douay

94 I am thine, save thou me: for I have sought thy justifications. ^psalm-118-94-douay

95 The wicked have waited for me to destroy me: but I have understood thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-95-douay

96 I have seen an end of all perfection: thy commandment is exceeding broad. ^psalm-118-96-douay

**MEM**


97 O how have I loved thy law, O Lord! it is my meditation all the day. ^psalm-118-97-douay

98 Through thy commandment, thou hast made me wiser than my enemies: for it is ever with me. ^psalm-118-98-douay

99 I have understood more than all my teachers: because thy testimonies are my meditation. ^psalm-118-99-douay

100 I have had understanding above ancients: because I have sought thy commandments. ^psalm-118-100-douay

101 I have restrained my feet from every evil way: that I may keep thy words. ^psalm-118-101-douay

102 I have not declined from thy judgments, because thou hast set me a law. ^psalm-118-102-douay

103 How sweet are thy words to my palate! more than honey to my mouth. ^psalm-118-103-douay

104 By thy commandments I have had understanding: therefore have I hated every way of iniquity. ^psalm-118-104-douay

**NUN**


105 Thy word is a lamp to my feet, and a light to my paths. ^psalm-118-105-douay

106 I have sworn and am determined to keep the judgments of thy justice. ^psalm-118-106-douay

107 I have been humbled, O Lord, exceedingly: quicken thou me according to thy word. ^psalm-118-107-douay

108 The free offerings of my mouth make acceptable, O Lord: and teach me thy judgments. ^psalm-118-108-douay

109 My soul is continually in my hands: and I have not forgotten thy law. ^psalm-118-109-douay

110 Sinners have laid a snare for me: but I have not erred from thy precepts. ^psalm-118-110-douay

111 I have purchased thy testimonies for an inheritance for ever: because they are the joy of my heart. ^psalm-118-111-douay

112 I have inclined my heart to do thy justifications for ever, for the reward. ^psalm-118-112-douay

**SAMECH**


113 I have hated the unjust: and have loved thy law. ^psalm-118-113-douay

114 Thou art my helper and my protector: and in thy word I have greatly hoped. ^psalm-118-114-douay

115 Depart from me, ye malignant: and I will search the commandments of my God. ^psalm-118-115-douay

116 Uphold me according to thy word, and I shall live: and let me not be confounded in my expectation. ^psalm-118-116-douay

117 Help me, and I shall be saved: and I will meditate always on thy justifications. ^psalm-118-117-douay

118 Thou hast despised all them that fall off from thy judgments; for their thought is unjust. ^psalm-118-118-douay

119 I have accounted all the sinners of the earth prevaricators: therefore have I loved thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-119-douay

120 Pierce thou my flesh with thy fear: for I am afraid of thy judgments. ^psalm-118-120-douay

**AIN**


121 I have done judgment and justice: give me not up to them that slander me. ^psalm-118-121-douay

122 Uphold thy servant unto good: let not the proud calumniate me. ^psalm-118-122-douay

123 My eyes have fainted after thy salvation: and for the word of thy justice. ^psalm-118-123-douay

124 Deal with thy servant according to thy mercy: and teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-124-douay

125 I am thy servant: give me understanding that I may know thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-125-douay

126 It is time, O Lord, to do: they have dissipated thy law. ^psalm-118-126-douay

127 Therefore have I loved thy commandments above gold and the topaz. ^psalm-118-127-douay

128 Therefore was I directed to all thy commandments: I have hated all wicked ways. ^psalm-118-128-douay

**PHE**


129 Thy testimonies are wonderful: therefore my soul hath sought them. ^psalm-118-129-douay

130 The declaration of thy words giveth light: and giveth understanding to little ones. ^psalm-118-130-douay

131 I opened my mouth, and panted: because I longed for thy commandments. ^psalm-118-131-douay

132 Look thou upon me, and have mercy on me according to the judgment of them that love thy name. ^psalm-118-132-douay

133 Direct my steps according to thy word: and let no iniquity have dominion over me. ^psalm-118-133-douay

134 Redeem me from the calumnies of men: that I may keep thy commandments. ^psalm-118-134-douay

135 Make thy face to shine upon thy servant: and teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-135-douay

136 My eyes have sent forth springs of water: because they have not kept thy law. ^psalm-118-136-douay

**SADE**


137 Thou art just, O Lord: and thy judgment is right. ^psalm-118-137-douay

138 Thou hast commanded justice thy testimonies: and thy truth exceedingly. ^psalm-118-138-douay

139 My zeal hath made me pine away: because my enemies forgot thy words. ^psalm-118-139-douay

140 Thy word is exceedingly refined: and thy servant hath loved it. ^psalm-118-140-douay

141 I am very young and despised; but I forget not thy justifications. ^psalm-118-141-douay

142 Thy justice is justice for ever: and thy law is the truth. ^psalm-118-142-douay

143 Trouble and anguish have found me: thy commandments are my meditation. ^psalm-118-143-douay

144 Thy testimonies are justice for ever: give me understanding, and I shall live. ^psalm-118-144-douay

**COPH**


145 I cried with my whole heart, hear me, O Lord: I will seek thy justifications. ^psalm-118-145-douay

146 I cried unto thee, save me: that I may keep thy commandments. ^psalm-118-146-douay

147 I prevented the dawning of the day, and cried: because in thy words I very much hoped. ^psalm-118-147-douay

148 My eyes to thee have prevented the morning: that I might meditate on thy words. ^psalm-118-148-douay

149 Hear thou my voice, O Lord, according to thy mercy: and quicken me according to thy judgment. ^psalm-118-149-douay

150 They that persecute me have drawn nigh to iniquity; but they are gone far off from thy law. ^psalm-118-150-douay

151 Thou art near, O Lord: and all thy ways are truth. ^psalm-118-151-douay

152 I have known from the beginning concerning thy testimonies: that thou hast founded them for ever. ^psalm-118-152-douay

**RES**


153 See my humiliation and deliver me for I have not forgotten thy law. ^psalm-118-153-douay

154 Judge my judgment and redeem me: quicken thou me for thy word's sake. ^psalm-118-154-douay

155 Salvation is far from sinners; because they have not sought thy justifications. ^psalm-118-155-douay

156 Many, O Lord, are thy mercies: quicken me according to thy judgment. ^psalm-118-156-douay

157 Many are they that persecute me and afflict me; but I have not declined from thy testimonies. ^psalm-118-157-douay

158 I beheld the transgressors, and pined away; because they kept not thy word. ^psalm-118-158-douay

159 Behold I have loved thy commandments, O Lord; quicken me thou in thy mercy. ^psalm-118-159-douay

160 The beginning of thy words is truth: all the judgments of thy justice are for ever. ^psalm-118-160-douay

**SIN**


161 Princes have persecuted me without cause: and my heart hath been in awe of thy words. ^psalm-118-161-douay

162 I will rejoice at thy words, as one that hath found great spoil. ^psalm-118-162-douay

163 I have hated and abhorred iniquity; but I have loved thy law. ^psalm-118-163-douay

164 Seven times a day I have given praise to thee, for the judgments of thy justice. ^psalm-118-164-douay

165 Much peace have they that love thy law, and to them there is no stumbling block. ^psalm-118-165-douay

166 I looked for thy salvation, O Lord: and I loved thy commandments. ^psalm-118-166-douay

167 My soul hath kept thy testimonies and hath loved them exceedingly. ^psalm-118-167-douay

168 I have kept thy commandments and thy testimonies: because all my ways are in thy sight. ^psalm-118-168-douay

**TAU**


169 Let my supplication, O Lord, come near in thy sight: give me understanding according to thy word. ^psalm-118-169-douay

170 Let my request come in before thee; deliver thou me according to thy word. ^psalm-118-170-douay

171 My lips shall utter a hymn, when thou shalt teach me thy justifications. ^psalm-118-171-douay

172 My tongue shall pronounce thy word: because all thy commandments are justice. ^psalm-118-172-douay

173 Let thy hand be with me to save me; for I have chosen thy precepts. ^psalm-118-173-douay

174 I have longed for thy salvation, O Lord; and thy law is my meditation. ^psalm-118-174-douay

175 My soul shall live and shall praise thee: and thy judgments shall help me. ^psalm-118-175-douay

176 I have gone astray like a sheep that is lost: seek thy servant, because I have not forgotten thy commandments. ^psalm-118-176-douay
