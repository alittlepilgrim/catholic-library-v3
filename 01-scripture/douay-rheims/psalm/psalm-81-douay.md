# Psalm 81

An exhortation to judges and men in power.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 82**, and the **[[psalm-80-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 81**.

1 A psalm for Asaph. ^psalm-81-1-douay

God hath stood in the congregation of gods: and being in the midst of them he judgeth gods.

2 How long will you judge unjustly: and accept the persons of the wicked? ^psalm-81-2-douay

3 Judge for the needy and fatherless: do justice to the humble and the poor. ^psalm-81-3-douay

4 Rescue the poor; and deliver the needy out of the hand of the sinner. ^psalm-81-4-douay

5 They have not known nor understood: they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth shall be moved. ^psalm-81-5-douay

6 I have said: You are gods and all of you the sons of the most High. ^psalm-81-6-douay

7 But you like men shall die: and shall fall like one of the princes. ^psalm-81-7-douay

8 Arise, O God, judge thou the earth: for thou shalt inherit among all the nations. ^psalm-81-8-douay
