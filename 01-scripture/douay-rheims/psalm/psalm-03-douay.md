# Psalm 3

1 The psalm of David when he fled from the face of his son Absalom. ^psalm-03-1-douay

2 Why, O Lord, are they multiplied that afflict me? many are they who rise up against me. ^psalm-03-2-douay

3 Many say to my soul: There is no salvation for him in his God. ^psalm-03-3-douay

4 But thou, O Lord, art my protector, my glory, and the lifter up of my head. ^psalm-03-4-douay

5 I have cried to the Lord with my voice: and he hath heard me from his holy hill. ^psalm-03-5-douay

6 I have slept and have taken my rest: and I have risen up, because the Lord hath protected me. ^psalm-03-6-douay

7 I will not fear thousands of the people surrounding me: arise, O Lord; save me, O my God. ^psalm-03-7-douay

8 For thou hast struck all them who are my adversaries without cause: thou hast broken the teeth of sinners. ^psalm-03-8-douay

9 Salvation is of the Lord: and thy blessing is upon thy people. ^psalm-03-9-douay
