# Psalm 33

An exhortation to the praise, and service of God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 34**, and the **[[psalm-32-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 33**.

1 For David, when he changed his countenance before Achimelech, who dismissed him, and he went his way. [1 Samuel 21.] ^psalm-33-1-douay

2 I will bless the Lord at all times, his praise shall be always in my mouth. ^psalm-33-2-douay

3 In the Lord shall my soul be praised: let the meek hear and rejoice. ^psalm-33-3-douay

4 O magnify the Lord with me; and let us extol his name together. ^psalm-33-4-douay

5 I sought the Lord, and he heard me; and he delivered me from all my troubles. ^psalm-33-5-douay

6 Come ye to him and be enlightened: and your faces shall not be confounded. ^psalm-33-6-douay

7 This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him: and saved him out of all his troubles. ^psalm-33-7-douay

8 The angel of the Lord shall encamp round about them that fear him: and shall deliver them. ^psalm-33-8-douay

9 O taste, and see that the Lord is sweet: blessed is the man that hopeth in him. ^psalm-33-9-douay

10 Fear the Lord, all ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him. ^psalm-33-10-douay

11 The rich have wanted, and have suffered hunger: but they that seek the Lord shall not be deprived of any good. ^psalm-33-11-douay

12 Come, children, hearken to me: I will teach you the fear of the Lord. ^psalm-33-12-douay

13 Who is the man that desireth life: who liveth to see good days? ^psalm-33-13-douay

14 Keep thy tongue form evil, and thy lips from speaking guile. ^psalm-33-14-douay

15 Turn away from evil and do good: seek after peace and pursue it. ^psalm-33-15-douay

16 The eyes of the Lord are upon the just: and his ears unto their prayers. ^psalm-33-16-douay

17 But the countenance of the Lord is against them that do evil things: to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth. ^psalm-33-17-douay

18 The just cried, and the Lord heard them: and delivered them out of all their troubles. ^psalm-33-18-douay

19 The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a contrite heart: and he will save the humble of spirit. ^psalm-33-19-douay

20 Many are the afflictions of the just; but out of them all will the Lord deliver them. ^psalm-33-20-douay

21 The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a contrite heart: and he will save the humble of spirit. ^psalm-33-21-douay

22 The death of the wicked is very evil: and they that hate the just shall be guilty. ^psalm-33-22-douay

23 The Lord will redeem the souls of his servants: and none of them that trust in him shall offend. ^psalm-33-23-douay
