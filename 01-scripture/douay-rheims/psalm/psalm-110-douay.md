# Psalm 110

God is to be praised for his graces, and benefits to his Church.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 111**, and the **[[psalm-109-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 110**.

*Alleluia.*

1 I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; in the council of the just, and in the congregation. ^psalm-110-1-douay

2 Great are the works of the Lord: sought out according to all his wills. ^psalm-110-2-douay

3 His work is praise and magnificence: and his justice continueth for ever and ever. ^psalm-110-3-douay

4 He hath made a remembrance of his wonderful works, being a merciful and gracious Lord: ^psalm-110-4-douay

5 He hath given food to them that fear him. He will be mindful for ever of his covenant: ^psalm-110-5-douay

6 He will show forth to his people the power of his works. ^psalm-110-6-douay

7 That he may give them the inheritance of the Gentiles: the works of his hands are truth and judgment. ^psalm-110-7-douay

8 All his commandments are faithful: confirmed for ever and ever, made in truth and equity. ^psalm-110-8-douay

9 He hath sent redemption to his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever. Holy and terrible is his name: ^psalm-110-9-douay

10 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom. A good understanding to all that do it: his praise continueth for ever and ever. ^psalm-110-10-douay
