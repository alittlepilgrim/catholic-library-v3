# Psalm 117

The psalmist praises God for his delivery from evils: puts his whole trust in him; and foretells the coming of Christ.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 118**, and the **[[psalm-116-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 117**.

*Alleluia.*

1 Give praise to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-117-1-douay

2 Let Israel now say, that he is good: that his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-117-2-douay

3 Let the house of Aaron now say, that his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-117-3-douay

4 Let them that fear the Lord now say, that his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-117-4-douay

5 In my trouble I called upon the Lord: and the Lord heard me, and enlarged me. ^psalm-117-5-douay

6 The Lord is my helper: I will not fear what man can do unto me. ^psalm-117-6-douay

7 The Lord is my helper: and I will look over my enemies. ^psalm-117-7-douay

8 It is good to confide in the Lord, rather than to have confidence in man. ^psalm-117-8-douay

9 It is good to trust in the Lord, rather than to trust in princes. ^psalm-117-9-douay

10 All nations compassed me about; and, in the name of the Lord I have been revenged on them. ^psalm-117-10-douay

11 Surrounding me they compassed me about: and in the name of the Lord I have been revenged on them. ^psalm-117-11-douay

12 They surrounded me like bees, and they burned like fire among thorns: and in the name of the Lord I was revenged on them. ^psalm-117-12-douay

13 Being pushed I was overturned that I might fall: but the Lord supported me. ^psalm-117-13-douay

14 The Lord is my strength and my praise: and he is become my salvation. ^psalm-117-14-douay

15 The voice of rejoicing and of salvation is in the tabernacles of the just. ^psalm-117-15-douay

16 The right hand of the Lord hath wrought strength: the right hand of the Lord hath exalted me: the right hand of the Lord hath wrought strength. ^psalm-117-16-douay

17 I shall not die, but live: and shall declare the works of the Lord. ^psalm-117-17-douay

18 The Lord chastising hath chastised me: but he hath not delivered me over to death. ^psalm-117-18-douay

19 Open ye to me the gates of justice: I will go in to them, and give praise to the Lord. ^psalm-117-19-douay

20 This is the gate of the Lord, the just shall enter into it. ^psalm-117-20-douay

21 I will give glory to thee because thou hast heard me: and art become my salvation. ^psalm-117-21-douay

22 The stone which the builders rejected; the same is become the head of the corner. ^psalm-117-22-douay

23 This is the Lord's doing, and it is wonderful in our eyes. ^psalm-117-23-douay

24 This is the day which the Lord hath made: let us be glad and rejoice therein. ^psalm-117-24-douay

25 O Lord, save me: O Lord, give good success. ^psalm-117-25-douay

26 Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord. We have blessed you out of the house of the Lord. ^psalm-117-26-douay

27 The Lord is God, and he hath shone upon us. Appoint a solemn day, with shady boughs, even to the horn of the altar. ^psalm-117-27-douay

28 Thou art my God, and I will praise thee: thou art my God, and I will exalt thee. I will praise thee, because thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation. ^psalm-117-28-douay

29 O praise ye the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-117-29-douay
