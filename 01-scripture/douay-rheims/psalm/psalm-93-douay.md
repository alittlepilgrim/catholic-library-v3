# Psalm 93

God shall judge and punish the oppressors of his people.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 94**, and the **[[psalm-92-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 93**.

*A psalm for David himself on the fourth day of the week.*

1 The Lord is the God to whom revenge belongeth: the God of revenge hath acted freely. ^psalm-93-1-douay

2 Lift up thyself, thou that judgest the earth: render a reward to the proud. ^psalm-93-2-douay

3 How long shall sinners, O Lord: how long shall sinners glory? ^psalm-93-3-douay

4 Shall they utter, and speak iniquity: shall all speak who work injustice? ^psalm-93-4-douay

5 Thy people, O Lord, they have brought low: and they have afflicted thy inheritance. ^psalm-93-5-douay

6 They have slain the widow and the stranger: and they have murdered the fatherless. ^psalm-93-6-douay

7 And they have said: The Lord shall not see: neither shall the God of Jacob understand. ^psalm-93-7-douay

8 Understand, ye senseless among the people: and, you fools, be wise at last. ^psalm-93-8-douay

9 He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? or he that formed the eye, doth he not consider? ^psalm-93-9-douay

10 He that chastiseth nations, shall he not rebuke: he that teacheth man knowledge? ^psalm-93-10-douay

11 The Lord knoweth the thoughts of men, that they are vain. ^psalm-93-11-douay

12 Blessed is the man whom thou shalt instruct, O Lord: and shalt teach him out of thy law. ^psalm-93-12-douay

13 That thou mayst give him rest from the evil days: till a pit be dug for the wicked. **Rest from the evil days...** That thou mayst mitigate the sorrows, to which he is exposed, during the short and evil days of his mortality. ^psalm-93-13-douay

14 For the Lord will not cast off his people: neither will he forsake his own inheritance. ^psalm-93-14-douay

15 Until justice be turned into judgment: and they that are near it are all the upright in heart. **Until justice be turned into judgment, etc...** By being put in execution; which will be agreeable to all the upright in heart. ^psalm-93-15-douay

16 Who shall rise up for me against the evildoers? or who shall stand with me against the workers of iniquity? ^psalm-93-16-douay

17 Unless the Lord had been my helper, my soul had almost dwelt in hell. ^psalm-93-17-douay

18 If I said: My foot is moved: thy mercy, O Lord, assisted me. ^psalm-93-18-douay

19 According to the multitude of my sorrows in my heart, thy comforts have given joy to my soul. ^psalm-93-19-douay

20 Doth the seat of iniquity stick to thee, who framest labour in commandment? **Doth the seat of iniquity stick to thee, etc...** That is, wilt thou, O God, who art always just, admit of the seat of iniquity: that is, of injustice, or unjust judges, to have any partnership with thee? Thou who framest, or makest, labour in commandment, that is, thou who obligest us to labour with all diligence to keep thy commandments. ^psalm-93-20-douay

21 They will hunt after the soul of the just, and will condemn innocent blood. ^psalm-93-21-douay

22 But the Lord is my refuge: and my God the help of my hope. ^psalm-93-22-douay

23 And he will render them their iniquity: and in their malice he will destroy them: the Lord our God will destroy them. ^psalm-93-23-douay
