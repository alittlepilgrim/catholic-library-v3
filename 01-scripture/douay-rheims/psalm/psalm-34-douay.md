# Psalm 34

David, in the person of Christ, prays against his persecutors: prophetically foreshowing the punishments that shall fall upon them.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 35**, and the **[[psalm-33-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 34**.

1 For David himself. ^psalm-34-1-douay

Judge thou, O Lord, them that wrong me: overthrow them that fight against me.

2 Take hold of arms and shield: and rise up to help me. ^psalm-34-2-douay

3 Bring out the sword, and shut up the way against them that persecute me: say to my soul: I am thy salvation. ^psalm-34-3-douay

4 Let them be confounded and ashamed that seek after my soul. Let them be turned back and be confounded that devise evil against me. ^psalm-34-4-douay

5 Let them become as dust before the wind: and let the angel of the Lord straiten them. ^psalm-34-5-douay

6 Let their way become dark and slippery; and let the angel of the Lord pursue them. ^psalm-34-6-douay

7 For without cause they have hidden their net for me unto destruction: without cause they have upbraided my soul. ^psalm-34-7-douay

8 Let the snare which he knoweth not come upon him: and let the net which he hath hidden catch him: and into that very snare let them fall. ^psalm-34-8-douay

9 But my soul shall rejoice in the Lord; and shall be delighted in his salvation. ^psalm-34-9-douay

10 All my bones shall say: Lord, who is like to thee? Who deliverest the poor from the hand of them that are stronger than he; the needy and the poor from them that strip him. ^psalm-34-10-douay

11 Unjust witnesses rising up have asked me things I knew not. ^psalm-34-11-douay

12 They repaid me evil for good: to the depriving me of my soul. ^psalm-34-12-douay

13 But as for me, when they were troublesome to me, I was clothed with haircloth. I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer shall be turned into my bosom. ^psalm-34-13-douay

14 As a neighbour and as an own brother, so did I please: as one mourning and sorrowful so was I humbled. ^psalm-34-14-douay

15 But they rejoiced against me, and came together: scourges were gathered together upon me, and I knew not. ^psalm-34-15-douay

16 They were separated, and repented not: they tempted me, they scoffed at me with scorn: they gnashed upon me with their teeth. ^psalm-34-16-douay

17 Lord, when wilt thou look upon me? rescue thou my soul from their malice: my only one from the lions. ^psalm-34-17-douay

18 I will give thanks to thee in a great church; I will praise thee in a strong people. ^psalm-34-18-douay

19 Let not them that are my enemies wrongfully rejoice over me: who have hated me without cause, and wink with the eyes. ^psalm-34-19-douay

20 For they spoke indeed peaceably to me; and speaking in the anger of the earth they devised guile. ^psalm-34-20-douay

21 And they opened their mouth wide against me; they said: Well done, well done, our eyes have seen it. ^psalm-34-21-douay

22 Thou hast seen, O Lord, be not thou silent: O Lord, depart not from me. ^psalm-34-22-douay

23 Arise, and be attentive to my judgment: to my cause, my God, and my Lord. ^psalm-34-23-douay

24 Judge me, O Lord my God according to thy justice, and let them not rejoice over me. ^psalm-34-24-douay

25 Let them not say in their hearts: It is well, it is well, to our mind: neither let them say: We have swallowed him up. ^psalm-34-25-douay

26 Let them blush: and be ashamed together, who rejoice at my evils. Let them be clothed with confusion and shame, who speak great things against me. ^psalm-34-26-douay

27 Let them rejoice and be glad, who are well pleased with my justice, and let them say always: The Lord be magnified, who delights in the peace of his servant. ^psalm-34-27-douay

28 And my tongue shall meditate thy justice, thy praise all the day long. ^psalm-34-28-douay
