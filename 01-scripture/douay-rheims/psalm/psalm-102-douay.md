# Psalm 102

Thanksgiving to God for his mercies.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 103**, and the **[[psalm-101-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 102**.

1 For David himself. ^psalm-102-1-douay

Bless the Lord, O my soul: and let all that is within me bless his holy name.

2 Bless the Lord, O my soul, and never forget all he hath done for thee. ^psalm-102-2-douay

3 Who forgiveth all thy iniquities: who healeth all thy diseases. ^psalm-102-3-douay

4 Who redeemeth thy life from destruction: who crowneth thee with mercy and compassion. ^psalm-102-4-douay

5 Who satisfieth thy desire with good things: thy youth shall be renewed like the eagle's. ^psalm-102-5-douay

6 The Lord doth mercies, and judgment for all that suffer wrong. ^psalm-102-6-douay

7 He hath made his ways known to Moses: his wills to the children of Israel. ^psalm-102-7-douay

8 The Lord is compassionate and merciful: longsuffering and plenteous in mercy. ^psalm-102-8-douay

9 He will not always be angry: nor will he threaten for ever. ^psalm-102-9-douay

10 He hath not dealt with us according to our sins: nor rewarded us according to our iniquities. ^psalm-102-10-douay

11 For according to the height of the heaven above the earth: he hath strengthened his mercy towards them that fear him. ^psalm-102-11-douay

12 As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our iniquities from us. ^psalm-102-12-douay

13 As a father hath compassion on his children, so hath the Lord compassion on them that fear him: ^psalm-102-13-douay

14 For he knoweth our frame. He remembereth that we are dust: ^psalm-102-14-douay

15 Man's days are as grass, as the flower of the field so shall he flourish. ^psalm-102-15-douay

16 For the spirit shall pass in him, and he shall not be: and he shall know his place no more. ^psalm-102-16-douay

17 But the mercy of the Lord is from eternity and unto eternity upon them that fear him: And his justice unto children's children, ^psalm-102-17-douay

18 To such as keep his covenant, And are mindful of his commandments to do them. ^psalm-102-18-douay

19 The lord hath prepared his throne in heaven: and his kingdom shall rule over all. ^psalm-102-19-douay

20 Bless the Lord, all ye his angels: you that are mighty in strength, and execute his word, hearkening to the voice of his orders. ^psalm-102-20-douay

21 Bless the Lord, all ye his hosts: you ministers of his that do his will. ^psalm-102-21-douay

22 Bless the Lord, all his works: in every place of his dominion, O my soul, bless thou the Lord. ^psalm-102-22-douay
