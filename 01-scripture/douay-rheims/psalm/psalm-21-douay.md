# Psalm 21

Christ's passion: and the conversion of the Gentiles.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 22**, and the **[[psalm-20-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 21**.

1 Unto the end, for the morning protection, a psalm for David. ^psalm-21-1-douay

2 O God my God, look upon me: why hast thou forsaken me? Far from my salvation are the words of my sins. **The words of my sins...** That is, the sins of the world, which I have taken upon myself, cry out against me, and are the cause of all my sufferings. ^psalm-21-2-douay

3 O my God, I shall cry by day, and thou wilt not hear: and by night, and it shall not be reputed as folly in me. ^psalm-21-3-douay

4 But thou dwellest in the holy place, the praise of Israel. ^psalm-21-4-douay

5 In thee have our fathers hoped: they have hoped, and thou hast delivered them. ^psalm-21-5-douay

6 They cried to thee, and they were saved: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded. ^psalm-21-6-douay

7 But I am a worm, and no man: the reproach of men, and the outcast of the people. ^psalm-21-7-douay

8 All they that saw me have laughed me to scorn: they have spoken with the lips, and wagged the head. ^psalm-21-8-douay

9 He hoped in the Lord, let him deliver him: let him save him, seeing he delighteth in him. ^psalm-21-9-douay

10 For thou art he that hast drawn me out of the womb: my hope from the breasts of my mother. ^psalm-21-10-douay

11 I was cast upon thee from the womb. From my mother's womb thou art my God, ^psalm-21-11-douay

12 Depart not from me. For tribulation is very near: for there is none to help me. ^psalm-21-12-douay

13 Many calves have surrounded me: fat bulls have besieged me. ^psalm-21-13-douay

14 They have opened their mouths against me, as a lion ravening and roaring. ^psalm-21-14-douay

15 I am poured out like water; and all my bones are scattered. My heart is become like wax melting in the midst of my bowels. ^psalm-21-15-douay

16 My strength is dried up like a potsherd, and my tongue hath cleaved to my jaws: and thou hast brought me down into the dust of death. ^psalm-21-16-douay

17 For many dogs have encompassed me: the council of the malignant hath besieged me. They have dug my hands and feet. ^psalm-21-17-douay

18 They have numbered all my bones. And they have looked and stared upon me. ^psalm-21-18-douay

19 They parted my garments amongst them; and upon my vesture they cast lots. ^psalm-21-19-douay

20 But thou, O Lord, remove not thy help to a distance from me; look towards my defence. ^psalm-21-20-douay

21 Deliver, O God, my soul from the sword: my only one from the hand of the dog. ^psalm-21-21-douay

22 Save me from the lion's mouth; and my lowness from the horns of the unicorns. ^psalm-21-22-douay

23 I will declare thy name to my brethren: in the midst of the church will I praise thee. ^psalm-21-23-douay

24 Ye that fear the Lord, praise him: all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him. ^psalm-21-24-douay

25 Let all the seed of Israel fear him: because he hath not slighted nor despised the supplication of the poor man. Neither hath he turned away his face form me: and when I cried to him he heard me. ^psalm-21-25-douay

26 With thee is my praise in a great church: I will pay my vows in the sight of them that fear him. ^psalm-21-26-douay

27 The poor shall eat and shall be filled: and they shall praise the Lord that seek him: their hearts shall live for ever and ever. ^psalm-21-27-douay

28 All the ends of the earth shall remember, and shall be converted to the Lord: And all the kindreds of the Gentiles shall adore in his sight. ^psalm-21-28-douay

29 For the kingdom is the Lord's; and he shall have dominion over the nations. ^psalm-21-29-douay

30 All the fat ones of the earth have eaten and have adored: all they that go down to the earth shall fall before him. ^psalm-21-30-douay

31 And to him my soul shall live: and my seed shall serve him. ^psalm-21-31-douay

32 There shall be declared to the Lord a generation to come: and the heavens shall show forth his justice to a people that shall be born, which the Lord hath made. ^psalm-21-32-douay
