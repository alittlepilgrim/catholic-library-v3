# Psalm 130

The prophet's humility.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 131**, and the **[[psalm-129-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 130**.

*A gradual canticle of David.*

1 Lord, my heart is not exalted: nor are my eyes lofty. Neither have I walked in great matters, nor in wonderful things above me. ^psalm-130-1-douay

2 If I was not humbly minded, but exalted my soul: As a child that is weaned is towards his mother, so reward in my soul. ^psalm-130-2-douay

3 Let Israel hope in the Lord, from henceforth now and for ever. ^psalm-130-3-douay
