# Psalm 72

The temptation of the weak, upon seeing the prosperity of the wicked, is overcome by the consideration of the justice of God, who will quickly render to every one according to his works.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 73**, and the **[[psalm-71-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 72**.

1 A psalm for Asaph. ^psalm-72-1-douay

How good is God to Israel, to them that are of a right heart!

2 But my feet were almost moved; my steps had well nigh slipped. ^psalm-72-2-douay

3 Because I had a zeal on occasion of the wicked, seeing the prosperity of sinners. ^psalm-72-3-douay

4 For there is no regard to their death, nor is there strength in their stripes. ^psalm-72-4-douay

5 They are not in the labour of men: neither shall they be scourged like other men. ^psalm-72-5-douay

6 Therefore pride hath held them fast: they are covered with their iniquity and their wickedness. ^psalm-72-6-douay

7 Their iniquity hath come forth, as it were from fatness: they have passed into the affection of the heart. **Fatness...** Abundance and temporal prosperity, which hath encouraged them in their iniquity: and made them give themselves up to their irregular affections. ^psalm-72-7-douay

8 They have thought and spoken wickedness: they have spoken iniquity on high. ^psalm-72-8-douay

9 They have set their mouth against heaven: and their tongue hath passed through the earth. ^psalm-72-9-douay

10 Therefore will my people return here and full days shall be found in them. **Return here...** or hither. The weak among the servants of God, will be apt often to return to this thought, and will be shocked when they consider the full days, that is, the long and prosperous life of the wicked; and will be tempted to make the reflections against providence which are set down in the following verses. ^psalm-72-10-douay

11 And they said: How doth God know? and is there knowledge in the most High? ^psalm-72-11-douay

12 Behold these are sinners; and yet, abounding in the world they have obtained riches. ^psalm-72-12-douay

13 And I said: Then have I in vain justified my heart, and washed my hands among the innocent. ^psalm-72-13-douay

14 And I have been scourged all the day; and my chastisement hath been in the mornings. ^psalm-72-14-douay

15 If I said: I will speak thus; behold I should condemn the generation of thy children. **If I said, etc...** That is, if I should indulge such thoughts as these. ^psalm-72-15-douay

16 I studied that I might know this thing, it is a labour in my sight: ^psalm-72-16-douay

17 Until I go into the sanctuary of God, and understand concerning their last ends. ^psalm-72-17-douay

18 But indeed for deceits thou hast put it to them: when they were lifted up thou hast cast them down. **Thou hast put it to them...** In punishment of their deceits, or for deceiving them, thou hast brought evils upon them in their last end, which, in their prosperity they never apprehended. ^psalm-72-18-douay

19 How are they brought to desolation? they have suddenly ceased to be: they have perished by reason of their iniquity. ^psalm-72-19-douay

20 As the dream of them that awake, O Lord; so in thy city thou shalt bring their image to nothing. ^psalm-72-20-douay

21 For my heart hath been inflamed, and my reins have been changed: ^psalm-72-21-douay

22 And I am brought to nothing, and I knew not. ^psalm-72-22-douay

23 I am become as a beast before thee: and I am always with thee. ^psalm-72-23-douay

24 Thou hast held me by my right hand; and by thy will thou hast conducted me, and with thy glory thou hast received me. ^psalm-72-24-douay

25 For what have I in heaven? and besides thee what do I desire upon earth? ^psalm-72-25-douay

26 For thee my flesh and my heart hath fainted away: thou art the God of my heart, and the God that is my portion for ever. ^psalm-72-26-douay

27 For behold they that go far from thee shall perish: thou hast destroyed all them that are disloyal to thee. ^psalm-72-27-douay

28 But it is good for me to adhere to my God, to put my hope in the Lord God: That I may declare all thy praises, in the gates of the daughter of Sion. ^psalm-72-28-douay
