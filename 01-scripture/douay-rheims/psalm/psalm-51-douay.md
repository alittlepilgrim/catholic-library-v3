# Psalm 51

David condemns the wickedness of Doeg, and foretells his destruction.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 52**, and the **[[psalm-50-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 51**.

1 Unto the end, understanding for David, ^psalm-51-1-douay

2 When Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul: David went to the house of Achimelech. ^psalm-51-2-douay

3 Why dost thou glory in malice, thou that art mighty in iniquity? ^psalm-51-3-douay

4 All the day long thy tongue hath devised injustice: as a sharp razor, thou hast wrought deceit. ^psalm-51-4-douay

5 Thou hast loved malice more than goodness: and iniquity rather than to speak righteousness. ^psalm-51-5-douay

6 Thou hast loved all the words of ruin, O deceitful tongue. ^psalm-51-6-douay

7 Therefore will God destroy thee for ever: he will pluck thee out, and remove thee from thy dwelling place: and thy root out of the land of the living. ^psalm-51-7-douay

8 The just shall see and fear, and shall laugh at him, and say: ^psalm-51-8-douay

9 Behold the man that made not God his helper: But trusted in the abundance of his riches: and prevailed in his vanity. ^psalm-51-9-douay

10 But I, as a fruitful olive tree in the house of God, have hoped in the mercy of God for ever, yea for ever and ever. ^psalm-51-10-douay

11 I will praise thee for ever, because thou hast done it: and I will wait on thy name, for it is good in the sight of thy saints. ^psalm-51-11-douay
