# Psalm 88

The perpetuity of the Church of Christ, in consequence of the promise of God: which, notwithstanding, God permits her to suffer sometimes most grievous afflictions.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 89**, and the **[[psalm-87-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 88**.

1 Of understanding, for Ethan the Ezrahite. ^psalm-88-1-douay

2 The mercies of the Lord I will sing for ever. I will show forth thy truth with my mouth to generation and generation. ^psalm-88-2-douay

3 For thou hast said: Mercy shall be built up for ever in the heavens: thy truth shall be prepared in them. ^psalm-88-3-douay

4 I have made a covenant with my elect: I have sworn to David my servant: ^psalm-88-4-douay

5 Thy seed will I settle for ever. And I will build up thy throne unto generation and generation. ^psalm-88-5-douay

6 The heavens shall confess thy wonders, O Lord: and thy truth in the church of the saints. ^psalm-88-6-douay

7 For who in the clouds can be compared to the Lord: or who among the sons of God shall be like to God? ^psalm-88-7-douay

8 God, who is glorified in the assembly of the saints: great and terrible above all them that are about him. ^psalm-88-8-douay

9 O Lord God of hosts, who is like to thee? thou art mighty, O Lord, and thy truth is round about thee. ^psalm-88-9-douay

10 Thou rulest the power of the sea: and appeasest the motion of the waves thereof. ^psalm-88-10-douay

11 Thou hast humbled the proud one, as one that is slain: with the arm of thy strength thou hast scattered thy enemies. ^psalm-88-11-douay

12 Thine are the heavens, and thine is the earth: the world and the fulness thereof thou hast founded: ^psalm-88-12-douay

13 The north and the sea thou hast created. Thabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name: ^psalm-88-13-douay

14 Thy arm is with might. Let thy hand be strengthened, and thy right hand exalted: ^psalm-88-14-douay

15 Justice and judgment are the preparation of thy throne. Mercy and truth shall go before thy face: ^psalm-88-15-douay

16 Blessed is the people that knoweth jubilation. They shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance: ^psalm-88-16-douay

17 And in thy name they shall rejoice all the day, and in thy justice they shall be exalted. ^psalm-88-17-douay

18 For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy good pleasure shall our horn be exalted. ^psalm-88-18-douay

19 For our protection is of the Lord, and of our king the holy one of Israel. ^psalm-88-19-douay

20 Then thou spokest in a vision to thy saints, and saidst: I have laid help upon one that is mighty, and have exalted one chosen out of my people. ^psalm-88-20-douay

21 I have found David my servant: with my holy oil I have anointed him. ^psalm-88-21-douay

22 For my hand shall help him: and my arm shall strengthen him. ^psalm-88-22-douay

23 The enemy shall have no advantage over him: nor the son of iniquity have power to hurt him. ^psalm-88-23-douay

24 And I will cut down his enemies before his face; and them that hate him I will put to flight. ^psalm-88-24-douay

25 And my truth and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted. ^psalm-88-25-douay

26 And I will set his hand in the sea; and his right hand in the rivers. ^psalm-88-26-douay

27 He shall cry out to me: Thou art my father: my God, and the support of my salvation. ^psalm-88-27-douay

28 And I will make him my firstborn, high above the kings of the earth. ^psalm-88-28-douay

29 I will keep my mercy for him for ever: and my covenant faithful to him. ^psalm-88-29-douay

30 And I will make his seed to endure for evermore: and his throne as the days of heaven. ^psalm-88-30-douay

31 And if his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments: ^psalm-88-31-douay

32 If they profane my justices: and keep not my commandments: ^psalm-88-32-douay

33 I will visit their iniquities with a rod and their sins with stripes. ^psalm-88-33-douay

34 But my mercy I will not take away from him: nor will I suffer my truth to fail. ^psalm-88-34-douay

35 Neither will I profane my covenant: and the words that proceed from my mouth I will not make void. ^psalm-88-35-douay

36 Once have I sworn by my holiness: I will not lie unto David: ^psalm-88-36-douay

37 His seed shall endure for ever. ^psalm-88-37-douay

38 And his throne as the sun before me: and as the moon perfect for ever, and a faithful witness in heaven. ^psalm-88-38-douay

39 But thou hast rejected and despised: thou hast been angry with my anointed. ^psalm-88-39-douay

40 Thou hast overthrown the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his sanctuary on the earth. **Overthrown the covenant, etc...** All this seems to relate to the time of the captivity of Babylon, in which, for the sins of the people and their princes, God seemed to have set aside for a while the covenant he made with David. ^psalm-88-40-douay

41 Thou hast broken down all his hedges: thou hast made his strength fear. ^psalm-88-41-douay

42 All that pass by the way have robbed him: he is become a reproach to his neighbours. ^psalm-88-42-douay

43 Thou hast set up the right hand of them that oppress him: thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice. ^psalm-88-43-douay

44 Thou hast turned away the help of his sword; and hast not assisted him in battle. ^psalm-88-44-douay

45 Thou hast made his purification to cease: and thou hast cast his throne down to the ground. ^psalm-88-45-douay

46 Thou hast shortened the days of his time: thou hast covered him with confusion. ^psalm-88-46-douay

47 How long, O Lord, turnest thou away unto the end? shall thy anger burn like fire? ^psalm-88-47-douay

48 Remember what my substance is: for hast thou made all the children of men in vain? ^psalm-88-48-douay

49 Who is the man that shall live, and not see death: that shall deliver his soul from the hand of hell? ^psalm-88-49-douay

50 Lord, where are thy ancient mercies, according to what thou didst swear to David in thy truth? ^psalm-88-50-douay

51 Be mindful, O Lord, of the reproach of thy servants (which I have held in my bosom) of many nations: ^psalm-88-51-douay

52 Wherewith thy enemies have reproached, O Lord; wherewith they have reproached the change of thy anointed. ^psalm-88-52-douay

53 Blessed be the Lord for evermore. So be it. So be it. ^psalm-88-53-douay
