# Psalm 127

The fear of God is the way to happiness.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 128**, and the **[[psalm-126-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 127**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 Blessed are all they that fear the Lord: that walk in his ways. ^psalm-127-1-douay

2 For thou shalt eat the labours of thy hands: blessed art thou, and it shall be well with thee. ^psalm-127-2-douay

3 Thy wife as a fruitful vine, on the sides of thy house. Thy children as olive plants, round about thy table. ^psalm-127-3-douay

4 Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord. ^psalm-127-4-douay

5 May the Lord bless thee out of Sion: and mayst thou see the good things of Jerusalem all the days of thy life. ^psalm-127-5-douay

6 And mayst thou see thy children's children, peace upon Israel. ^psalm-127-6-douay
