# Psalm 22

God's spiritual benefits to faithful souls.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 23**, and the **[[psalm-21-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 22**.

1 A psalm for David. ^psalm-22-1-douay

The Lord ruleth me: and I shall want nothing. **Ruleth me...** In Hebrew, *Is my shepherd*, viz., to feed, guide, and govern me.

2 He hath set me in a place of pasture. He hath brought me up, on the water of refreshment: ^psalm-22-2-douay

3 He hath converted my soul. He hath led me on the paths of justice, for his own name's sake. ^psalm-22-3-douay

4 For though I should walk in the midst of the shadow of death, I will fear no evils, for thou art with me. Thy rod and thy staff, they have comforted me. ^psalm-22-4-douay

5 Thou hast prepared a table before me against them that afflict me. Thou hast anointed my head with oil; and my chalice which inebriateth me, how goodly is it! ^psalm-22-5-douay

6 And thy mercy will follow me all the days of my life. And that I may dwell in the house of the Lord unto length of days. ^psalm-22-6-douay
