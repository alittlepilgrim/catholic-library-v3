# Psalm 19

A prayer for the king.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 20**, and the **[[psalm-18-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 19**.

1 Unto the end. A psalm for David. ^psalm-19-1-douay

2 May the Lord hear thee in the day of tribulation: may the name of the God of Jacob protect thee. ^psalm-19-2-douay

3 May he send thee help from the sanctuary: and defend thee out of Sion. ^psalm-19-3-douay

4 May he be mindful of all thy sacrifices: and may thy whole burnt offering be made fat. ^psalm-19-4-douay

5 May he give thee according to thy own heart; and confirm all thy counsels. ^psalm-19-5-douay

6 We will rejoice in thy salvation; and in the name of our God we shall be exalted. ^psalm-19-6-douay

7 The Lord fulfil all thy petitions: now have I known that the Lord hath saved his anointed. He will hear him from his holy heaven: the salvation of his right hand is in powers. **The salvation of his right hand is in powers...** That is, in strength. His right hand is strong and mighty to save them that trust in him. ^psalm-19-7-douay

8 Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will call upon the name of the Lord, our God. ^psalm-19-8-douay

9 They are bound, and have fallen: but we are risen, and are set upright. O Lord, save the king: and hear us in the day that we shall call upon thee. ^psalm-19-9-douay
