# Psalm 32

An exhortation to praise God, and to trust in him.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 33**, and the **[[psalm-31-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 32**.

1 A psalm for David. ^psalm-32-1-douay

Rejoice in the Lord, O ye just: praise becometh the upright.

2 Give praise to the Lord on the harp; sing to him with the psaltery, the instrument of ten strings. ^psalm-32-2-douay

3 Sing to him a new canticle, sing well unto him with a loud noise. ^psalm-32-3-douay

4 For the word of the Lord is right, and all his works are done with faithfulness. ^psalm-32-4-douay

5 He loveth mercy and judgment; the earth is full of the mercy of the Lord. ^psalm-32-5-douay

6 By the word of the Lord the heavens were established; and all the power of them by the spirit of his mouth: ^psalm-32-6-douay

7 Gathering together the waters of the sea, as in a vessel; laying up the depths in storehouses. ^psalm-32-7-douay

8 Let all the earth fear the Lord, and let all the inhabitants of the world be in awe of him. ^psalm-32-8-douay

9 For he spoke and they were made: he commanded and they were created. ^psalm-32-9-douay

10 The Lord bringeth to nought the counsels of nations; and he rejecteth the devices of people, and casteth away the counsels of princes. ^psalm-32-10-douay

11 But the counsel of the Lord standeth for ever: the thoughts of his heart to all generations. ^psalm-32-11-douay

12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord: the people whom he hath chosen for his inheritance. ^psalm-32-12-douay

13 The Lord hath looked from heaven: he hath beheld all the sons of men. ^psalm-32-13-douay

14 From his habitation which he hath prepared, he hath looked upon all that dwell on the earth. ^psalm-32-14-douay

15 He who hath made the hearts of every one of them: who understandeth all their works. ^psalm-32-15-douay

16 The king is not saved by a great army: nor shall the giant be saved by his own great strength. ^psalm-32-16-douay

17 Vain is the horse for safety: neither shall he be saved by the abundance of his strength. ^psalm-32-17-douay

18 Behold the eyes of the Lord are on them that fear him: and on them that hope in his mercy. ^psalm-32-18-douay

19 To deliver their souls from death; and feed them in famine. ^psalm-32-19-douay

20 Our soul waiteth for the Lord: for he is our helper and protector. ^psalm-32-20-douay

21 For in him our heart shall rejoice: and in his holy name we have trusted. ^psalm-32-21-douay

22 Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, as we have hoped in thee. ^psalm-32-22-douay
