# Psalm 35

The malice of sinners, and the goodness of God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 36**, and the **[[psalm-34-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 35**.

1 Unto the end, for the servant of God, David himself. ^psalm-35-1-douay

2 The unjust hath said within himself, that he would sin: there is no fear of God before his eyes. ^psalm-35-2-douay

3 For in his sight he hath done deceitfully, that his iniquity may be found unto hatred. **Unto hatred...** That is, hateful to God. ^psalm-35-3-douay

4 The words of his mouth are iniquity and guile: he would not understand that he might do well. ^psalm-35-4-douay

5 He hath devised iniquity on his bed, he hath set himself on every way that is not good: but evil he hath not hated. ^psalm-35-5-douay

6 O Lord, thy mercy is in heaven, and thy truth reacheth even to the clouds. ^psalm-35-6-douay

7 Thy justice is as the mountains of God, thy judgments are a great deep. Men and beasts thou wilt preserve, O Lord: ^psalm-35-7-douay

8 O how hast thou multiplied thy mercy, O God! But the children of men shall put their trust under the covert of thy wings. ^psalm-35-8-douay

9 They shall be inebriated with the plenty of thy house; and thou shalt make them drink of the torrent of thy pleasure. ^psalm-35-9-douay

10 For with thee is the fountain of life; and in thy light we shall see light. ^psalm-35-10-douay

11 Extend thy mercy to them that know thee, and thy justice to them that are right in heart. ^psalm-35-11-douay

12 Let not the foot of pride come to me, and let not the hand of the sinner move me. ^psalm-35-12-douay

13 There the workers of iniquity are fallen, they are cast out, and could not stand. ^psalm-35-13-douay
