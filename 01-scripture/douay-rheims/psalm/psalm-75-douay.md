# Psalm 75

God is known in his Church: and exerts his power in protecting it. It alludes to the slaughter of the Assyrians, in the days of King Hezekiah.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 76**, and the **[[psalm-74-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 75**.

1 Unto the end, in praises, a psalm for Asaph: a canticle to the Assyrians. ^psalm-75-1-douay

2 In Judea God is known: his name is great in Israel. ^psalm-75-2-douay

3 And his place is in peace: and his abode in Sion: ^psalm-75-3-douay

4 There hath he broken the powers of bows, the shield, the sword, and the battle. ^psalm-75-4-douay

5 Thou enlightenest wonderfully from the everlasting hills. ^psalm-75-5-douay

6 All the foolish of heart were troubled. They have slept their sleep; and all the men of riches have found nothing in their hands. ^psalm-75-6-douay

7 At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, they have all slumbered that mounted on horseback. ^psalm-75-7-douay

8 Thou art terrible, and who shall resist thee? from that time thy wrath. **From that time, etc...** From the time that thy wrath shall break out. ^psalm-75-8-douay

9 Thou hast caused judgment to be heard from heaven: the earth trembled and was still, ^psalm-75-9-douay

10 When God arose in judgment, to save all the meek of the earth. ^psalm-75-10-douay

11 For the thought of man shall give praise to thee: and the remainders of the thought shall keep holiday to thee. ^psalm-75-11-douay

12 Vow ye, and pay to the Lord your God: all you that are round about him bring presents. To him that is terrible, ^psalm-75-12-douay

13 Even to him who taketh away the spirit of princes: to the terrible with the kings of the earth. ^psalm-75-13-douay
