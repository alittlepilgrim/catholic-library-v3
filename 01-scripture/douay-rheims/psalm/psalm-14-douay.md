# Psalm 14

What kind of men shall dwell in the heavenly Zion.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 15**, and the **[[psalm-13-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 14**.

1 A psalm for David. ^psalm-14-1-douay

Lord, who shall dwell in thy tabernacle? or who shall rest in thy holy hill?

2 He that walketh without blemish, and worketh justice: ^psalm-14-2-douay

3 He that speaketh truth in his heart, who hath not used deceit in his tongue: Nor hath done evil to his neighbour: nor taken up a reproach against his neighbours. ^psalm-14-3-douay

4 In his sight the malignant is brought to nothing: but he glorifieth them that fear the Lord. He that sweareth to his neighbour, and deceiveth not; ^psalm-14-4-douay

5 He that hath not put out his money to usury, nor taken bribes against the innocent: He that doth these things, shall not be moved for ever. ^psalm-14-5-douay
