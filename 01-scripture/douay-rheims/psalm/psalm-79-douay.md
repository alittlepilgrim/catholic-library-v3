# Psalm 79

A prayer for the Church in tribulation, commemorating God's former favours.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 80**, and the **[[psalm-78-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 79**.

1 Unto the end, for them that shall be changed, a testimony for Asaph, a psalm. ^psalm-79-1-douay

2 Give ear, O thou that rulest Israel: thou that leadest Joseph like a sheep. Thou that sittest upon the cherubims, shine forth ^psalm-79-2-douay

3 Before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasses. Stir up thy might, and come to save us. ^psalm-79-3-douay

4 Convert us, O God: and show us thy face, and we shall be saved. ^psalm-79-4-douay

5 O Lord God of hosts, how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy servant? ^psalm-79-5-douay

6 How long wilt thou feed us with the bread of tears: and give us for our drink tears in measure? ^psalm-79-6-douay

7 Thou hast made us to be a contradiction to our neighbours: and our enemies have scoffed at us. ^psalm-79-7-douay

8 O God of hosts, convert us: and show thy face, and we shall be saved. ^psalm-79-8-douay

9 Thou hast brought a vineyard out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the Gentiles and planted it. ^psalm-79-9-douay

10 Thou wast the guide of its journey in its sight: thou plantedst the roots thereof, and it filled the land. ^psalm-79-10-douay

11 The shadow of it covered the hills: and the branches thereof the cedars of God. ^psalm-79-11-douay

12 It stretched forth its branches unto the sea, and its boughs unto the river. ^psalm-79-12-douay

13 Why hast thou broken down the hedge thereof, so that all they who pass by the way do pluck it? ^psalm-79-13-douay

14 The boar out of the wood hath laid it waste: and a singular wild beast hath devoured it. ^psalm-79-14-douay

15 Turn again, O God of hosts, look down from heaven, and see, and visit this vineyard: ^psalm-79-15-douay

16 And perfect the same which thy right hand hath planted: and upon the son of man whom thou hast confirmed for thyself. ^psalm-79-16-douay

17 Things set on fire and dug down shall perish at the rebuke of thy countenance. **Things set on fire, etc...** So this vineyard of thine, almost consumed already, must perish, if thou continue thy rebukes. ^psalm-79-17-douay

18 Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand: and upon the son of man whom thou hast confirmed for thyself. **The man of thy right hand...** Christ. ^psalm-79-18-douay

19 And we depart not from thee, thou shalt quicken us: and we will call upon thy name. ^psalm-79-19-douay

20 O Lord God of hosts, convert us and show thy face, and we shall be saved. ^psalm-79-20-douay
