# Psalm 15

Christ's future victory and triumph over the world and death.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 16**, and the **[[psalm-14-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 15**.

1 The inscription of a title to David himself. ^psalm-15-1-douay

Preserve me, O Lord, for I have put my trust in thee. **The inscription of a title...** That is, of a pillar or monument, *steylographia*: which is as much as to say, that this psalm is most worthy to be engraved on an everlasting monument.

2 I have said to the Lord, thou art my God, for thou hast no need of my goods. ^psalm-15-2-douay

3 To the saints, who are in his land, he hath made wonderful all my desires in them. ^psalm-15-3-douay

4 Their infirmities were multiplied: afterwards they made haste. I will not gather together their meetings for blood offerings: nor will I be mindful of their names by my lips. ^psalm-15-4-douay

5 The Lord is the portion of my inheritance and of my cup: it is thou that wilt restore my inheritance to me. ^psalm-15-5-douay

6 The lines are fallen unto me in goodly places: for my inheritance is goodly to me. ^psalm-15-6-douay

7 I will bless the Lord, who hath given me understanding: moreover, my reins also have corrected me even till night. ^psalm-15-7-douay

8 I set the Lord always in my sight: for he is at my right hand, that I be not moved. ^psalm-15-8-douay

9 Therefore my heart hath been glad, and my tongue hath rejoiced: moreover, my flesh also shall rest in hope. ^psalm-15-9-douay

10 Because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; nor wilt thou give thy holy one to see corruption. ^psalm-15-10-douay


11 Thou hast made known to me the ways of life, thou shalt fill me with joy with thy countenance: at thy right hand are delights even to the end. ^psalm-15-11-douay
