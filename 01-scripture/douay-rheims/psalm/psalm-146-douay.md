# Psalm 146

An exhortation to praise God for his benefits.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in many popular Bible translations, the psalm below is combined with the **[[psalm-147-douay|next psalm]]** to form **Psalm 147**.

1 Alleluia. ^psalm-146-1-douay

Praise ye the Lord, because psalm is good: to our God be joyful and comely praise.

2 The Lord buildeth up Jerusalem: he will gather together the dispersed of Israel. ^psalm-146-2-douay

3 Who healeth the broken of heart, and bindeth up their bruises. ^psalm-146-3-douay

4 Who telleth the number of the stars: and calleth them all by their names. ^psalm-146-4-douay

5 Great is our Lord, and great is his power: and of his wisdom there is no number. ^psalm-146-5-douay

6 The Lord lifteth up the meek, and bringeth the wicked down even to the ground. ^psalm-146-6-douay

7 Sing ye to the Lord with praise: sing to our God upon the harp. ^psalm-146-7-douay

8 Who covereth the heaven with clouds, and prepareth rain for the earth. Who maketh grass to grow on the mountains, and herbs for the service of men. ^psalm-146-8-douay

9 Who giveth to beasts their food: and to the young ravens that call upon him. ^psalm-146-9-douay

10 He shall not delight in the strength of the horse: nor take pleasure in the legs of a man. ^psalm-146-10-douay

11 The Lord taketh pleasure in them that fear him: and in them that hope in his mercy. ^psalm-146-11-douay
