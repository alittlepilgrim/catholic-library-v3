# Psalm 112

God is to be praised for his regard to the poor and humble.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 113**, and the **[[psalm-111-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 112**.

*Alleluia.*

1 Praise the Lord, ye children: praise ye the name of the Lord. ^psalm-112-1-douay

2 Blessed be the name of the Lord, from henceforth now and for ever. ^psalm-112-2-douay

3 From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same, the name of the Lord is worthy of praise. ^psalm-112-3-douay

4 The Lord is high above all nations; and his glory above the heavens. ^psalm-112-4-douay

5 Who is as the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high: ^psalm-112-5-douay

6 And looketh down on the low things in heaven and in earth? ^psalm-112-6-douay

7 Raising up the needy from the earth, and lifting up the poor out of the dunghill: ^psalm-112-7-douay

8 That he may place him with princes, with the princes of his people. ^psalm-112-8-douay

9 Who maketh a barren woman to dwell in a house, the joyful mother of children. ^psalm-112-9-douay
