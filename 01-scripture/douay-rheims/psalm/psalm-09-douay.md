# Psalm 9

The Church praises God for his protection against her enemies.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used by many popular Bible translations, this psalm is split into two parts, called **Psalm 9** and **Psalm 10**.

1 Unto the end, for the hidden things of the Son. A psalm for David. **The hidden things of the Son...** The humility and sufferings of Christ, the Son of God; and of good Christians, who are his sons by adoption; are called hidden things, with regard to the children of this world, who know not the value and merit of them. ^psalm-09-1-douay

2 I will give praise to thee, O Lord, with my whole heart: I will relate all thy wonders. ^psalm-09-2-douay

3 I will be glad, and rejoice in thee: I will sing to thy name, O thou most high. ^psalm-09-3-douay

4 When my enemy shall be turned back: they shall be weakened, and perish before thy face. ^psalm-09-4-douay

5 For thou hast maintained my judgment and my cause: thou hast sat on the throne, who judgest justice. ^psalm-09-5-douay

6 Thou hast rebuked the Gentiles, and the wicked one hath perished; thou hast blotted out their name for ever and ever. ^psalm-09-6-douay

7 The swords of the enemy have failed unto the end: and their cities thou hast destroyed. Their memory hath perished with a noise: ^psalm-09-7-douay

8 But the Lord remaineth for ever. He hath prepared his throne in judgment: ^psalm-09-8-douay

9 And he shall judge the world in equity, he shall judge the people in justice. ^psalm-09-9-douay

10 And the Lord is become a refuge for the poor: a helper in due time in tribulation. ^psalm-09-10-douay

11 And let them trust in thee who know thy name: for thou hast not forsaken them that seek thee, O Lord. ^psalm-09-11-douay

12 Sing ye to the Lord, who dwelleth in Sion: declare his ways among the Gentiles: ^psalm-09-12-douay

13 For requiring their blood, he hath remembered them: he hath not forgotten the cry of the poor. ^psalm-09-13-douay

14 Have mercy on me, O Lord: see my humiliation which I suffer from my enemies. ^psalm-09-14-douay

15 Thou that liftest me up from the gates of death, that I may declare all thy praises in the gates of the daughter of Sion. ^psalm-09-15-douay

16 I will rejoice in thy salvation: the Gentiles have stuck fast in the destruction which they prepared. Their foot hath been taken in the very snare which they hid. ^psalm-09-16-douay

17 The Lord shall be known when he executeth judgments: the sinner hath been caught in the works of his own hands. ^psalm-09-17-douay

18 The wicked shall be turned into hell, all the nations that forget God. ^psalm-09-18-douay

19 For the poor man shall not be forgotten to the end: the patience of the poor shall not perish for ever. ^psalm-09-19-douay

20 Arise, O Lord, let not man be strengthened: let the Gentiles be judged in thy sight. ^psalm-09-20-douay

21 Appoint, O Lord, a lawgiver over them: that the Gentiles may know themselves to be but men. ^psalm-09-21-douay

The Masoretic (Hebrew) version of **Psalm 10** begins here.

22 Why, O Lord, hast thou retired afar off? why dost thou slight us in our wants, in the time of trouble? ^psalm-09-22-douay

23 Whilst the wicked man is proud, the poor is set on fire: they are caught in the counsels which they devise. ^psalm-09-23-douay

24 For the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul: and the unjust man is blessed. ^psalm-09-24-douay

25 The sinner hath provoked the Lord, according to the multitude of his wrath, he will not seek him: ^psalm-09-25-douay

26 God is not before his eyes: his ways are filthy at all times. Thy judgments are removed form his sight: he shall rule over all his enemies. ^psalm-09-26-douay

27 For he hath said in his heart: I shall not be moved from generation to generation, and shall be without evil. ^psalm-09-27-douay

28 His mouth is full of cursing, and of bitterness, and of deceit: under his tongue are labour and sorrow. ^psalm-09-28-douay

29 He sitteth in ambush with the rich, in private places, that he may kill the innocent. ^psalm-09-29-douay

30 His eyes are upon the poor man: he lieth in wait, in secret, like a lion in his den. He lieth in ambush, that he may catch the poor man: so catch the poor, whilst he draweth him to him. ^psalm-09-30-douay

31 In his net he will bring him down, he will crouch and fall, when he shall have power over the poor. ^psalm-09-31-douay

32 For he hath said in his heart: God hath forgotten, he hath turned away his face, not to see to the end. ^psalm-09-32-douay

33 Arise, O Lord God, let thy hand be exalted: forget not the poor. ^psalm-09-33-douay

34 Wherefore hath the wicked provoked God? for he hath said in his heart: He will not require it. ^psalm-09-34-douay

35 Thou seest it, for thou considerest labour and sorrow: that thou mayst deliver them into thy hands. To thee is the poor man left: thou wilt be a helper to the orphan. ^psalm-09-35-douay

36 Break thou the arm of the sinner and of the malignant: his sin shall be sought, and shall not be found. ^psalm-09-36-douay

37 The Lord shall reign to eternity, yea, for ever and ever: ye Gentiles shall perish from his land. ^psalm-09-37-douay

38 The Lord hath heard the desire of the poor: thy ear hath heard the preparation of their heart. ^psalm-09-38-douay

39 To judge for the fatherless and for the humble, that man may no more presume to magnify himself upon earth. ^psalm-09-39-douay
