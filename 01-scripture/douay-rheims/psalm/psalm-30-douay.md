# Psalm 30

A prayer of a just man under affliction.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 31**, and the **[[psalm-29-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 30**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David, in an ecstasy. ^psalm-30-1-douay

2 In thee, O Lord, have I hoped, let me never be confounded: deliver me in thy justice. ^psalm-30-2-douay

3 Bow down thy ear to me: make haste to deliver me. Be thou unto me a God, a protector, and a house of refuge, to save me. ^psalm-30-3-douay

4 For thou art my strength and my refuge; and for thy name's sake thou wilt lead me, and nourish me. ^psalm-30-4-douay

5 Thou wilt bring me out of this snare, which they have hidden for me: for thou art my protector. ^psalm-30-5-douay

6 Into thy hands I commend my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O Lord, the God of truth. ^psalm-30-6-douay

7 Thou hast hated them that regard vanities, to no purpose. But I have hoped in the Lord: ^psalm-30-7-douay

8 I will be glad and rejoice in thy mercy. For thou hast regarded my humility, thou hast saved my soul out of distresses. ^psalm-30-8-douay

9 And thou hast not shut me up in the hands of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in a spacious place. ^psalm-30-9-douay

10 Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am afflicted: my eye is troubled with wrath, my soul, and my belly: ^psalm-30-10-douay

11 For my life is wasted with grief: and my years in sighs. My strength is weakened through poverty and my bones are disturbed. ^psalm-30-11-douay

12 I am become a reproach among all my enemies, and very much to my neighbours; and a fear to my acquaintance. They that saw me without fled from me. ^psalm-30-12-douay

13 I am forgotten as one dead from the heart. I am become as a vessel that is destroyed. ^psalm-30-13-douay

14 For I have heard the blame of many that dwell round about. While they assembled together against me, they consulted to take away my life. ^psalm-30-14-douay

15 But I have put my trust in thee, O Lord: I said: Thou art my God. ^psalm-30-15-douay

16 My lots are in thy hands. Deliver me out of the hands of my enemies; and from them that persecute me. ^psalm-30-16-douay

17 Make thy face to shine upon thy servant; save me in thy mercy. ^psalm-30-17-douay

18 Let me not be confounded, O Lord, for I have called upon thee. Let the wicked be ashamed, and be brought down to hell. ^psalm-30-18-douay

19 Let deceitful lips be made dumb. Which speak iniquity against the just, with pride and abuse. ^psalm-30-19-douay

20 O how great is the multitude of thy sweetness, O Lord, which thou hast hidden for them that fear thee! Which thou hast wrought for them that hope in thee, in the sight of the sons of men. ^psalm-30-20-douay

21 Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy face, from the disturbance of men. Thou shalt protect them in thy tabernacle from the contradiction of tongues. ^psalm-30-21-douay

22 Blessed be the Lord, for he hath shown his wonderful mercy to me in a fortified city. ^psalm-30-22-douay

23 But I said in the excess of my mind: I am cast away from before thy eyes. Therefore thou hast heard the voice of my prayer, when I cried to thee. ^psalm-30-23-douay

24 O love the Lord, all ye his saints: for the Lord will require truth, and will repay them abundantly that act proudly. ^psalm-30-24-douay

25 Do ye manfully, and let your heart be strengthened, all ye that hope in the Lord. ^psalm-30-25-douay
