# Psalm 106

All are invited to give thanks to God for his perpetual providence over men.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 107**, and the **[[psalm-105-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 106**.

*Alleluia.*

1 Give glory to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-106-1-douay

2 Let them say so that have been redeemed by the Lord, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy: and gathered out of the countries. ^psalm-106-2-douay

3 From the rising and from the setting of the sun, from the north and from the sea. ^psalm-106-3-douay

4 They wandered in a wilderness, in a place without water: they found not the way of a city for their habitation. ^psalm-106-4-douay

5 They were hungry and thirsty: their soul fainted in them. ^psalm-106-5-douay

6 And they cried to the Lord in their tribulation: and he delivered them out of their distresses. ^psalm-106-6-douay

7 And he led them into the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation. ^psalm-106-7-douay

8 Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him: and his wonderful works to the children of men. ^psalm-106-8-douay

9 For he hath satisfied the empty soul, and hath filled the hungry soul with good things. ^psalm-106-9-douay

10 Such as sat in darkness and in the shadow of death: bound in want and in iron. ^psalm-106-10-douay

11 Because they had exasperated the words of God: and provoked the counsel of the most High: ^psalm-106-11-douay

12 And their heart was humbled with labours: they were weakened, and there was none to help them. ^psalm-106-12-douay

13 Then they cried to the Lord in their affliction: and he delivered them out of their distresses. ^psalm-106-13-douay

14 And he brought them out of darkness, and the shadow of death; and broke their bonds in sunder. ^psalm-106-14-douay

15 Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him, and his wonderful works to the children of men. ^psalm-106-15-douay

16 Because he hath broken gates of brass, and burst iron bars. ^psalm-106-16-douay

17 He took them out of the way of their iniquity: for they were brought low for their injustices. ^psalm-106-17-douay

18 Their soul abhorred all manner of meat: and they drew nigh even to the gates of death. ^psalm-106-18-douay

19 And they cried to the Lord in their affliction: and he delivered them out of their distresses. ^psalm-106-19-douay

20 He sent his word, and healed them: and delivered them from their destructions. ^psalm-106-20-douay

21 Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him: and his wonderful works to the children of men. ^psalm-106-21-douay

22 And let them sacrifice the sacrifice of praise: and declare his works with joy. ^psalm-106-22-douay

23 They that go down to the sea in ships, doing business in the great waters: ^psalm-106-23-douay

24 These have seen the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep. ^psalm-106-24-douay

25 He said the word, and there arose a storm of wind: and the waves thereof were lifted up. ^psalm-106-25-douay

26 They mount up to the heavens, and they go down to the depths: their soul pined away with evils. ^psalm-106-26-douay

27 They were troubled, and reeled like a drunken man; and all their wisdom was swallowed up. ^psalm-106-27-douay

28 And they cried to the Lord in their affliction: and he brought them out of their distresses. ^psalm-106-28-douay

29 And he turned the storm into a breeze: and its waves were still. ^psalm-106-29-douay

30 And they rejoiced because they were still: and he brought them to the haven which they wished for. ^psalm-106-30-douay

31 Let the mercies of the Lord give glory to him, and his wonderful works to the children of men. ^psalm-106-31-douay

32 And let them exalt him in the church of the people: and praise him in the chair of the ancients. ^psalm-106-32-douay

33 He hath turned rivers into a wilderness: and the sources of waters into dry ground: ^psalm-106-33-douay

34 A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein. ^psalm-106-34-douay

35 He hath turned a wilderness into pools of waters, and a dry land into water springs. ^psalm-106-35-douay

36 And hath placed there the hungry; and they made a city for their habitation. ^psalm-106-36-douay

37 Anti they sowed fields, and planted vineyards: and they yielded fruit of birth. ^psalm-106-37-douay

38 And he blessed them, and they were multiplied exceedingly: and their cattle he suffered not to decrease. ^psalm-106-38-douay

39 Then they were brought to be few: and they were afflicted through the trouble of evils and sorrow. ^psalm-106-39-douay

40 Contempt was poured forth upon their princes: and he caused them to wander where there was no passing, and out of the way. ^psalm-106-40-douay

41 And he helped the poor out of poverty: and made him families like a flock of sheep. ^psalm-106-41-douay

42 The just shall see, and shall rejoice, and all iniquity shall stop her mouth. ^psalm-106-42-douay

43 Who is wise, and will keep these things; and will understand the mercies of the Lord? ^psalm-106-43-douay
