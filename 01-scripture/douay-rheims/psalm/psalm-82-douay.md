# Psalm 82

A prayer against the enemies of God's Church.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 83**, and the **[[psalm-81-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 82**.

1 A canticle of a psalm for Asaph. ^psalm-82-1-douay

2 O God, who shall be like to thee? hold not thy peace, neither be thou still, O God. ^psalm-82-2-douay

3 For lo, thy enemies have made a noise: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head. ^psalm-82-3-douay

4 They have taken a malicious counsel against thy people, and have consulted against thy saints. ^psalm-82-4-douay

5 They have said: Come and let us destroy them, so that they be not a nation: and let the name of Israel be remembered no more. ^psalm-82-5-douay

6 For they have contrived with one consent: they have made a covenant together against thee, ^psalm-82-6-douay

7 The tabernacle of the Edomites, and the Ishmahelites: Moab, and the Agarens, ^psalm-82-7-douay

8 Gebal, and Ammon and Amalec: the Philistines, with the inhabitants of Tyre. ^psalm-82-8-douay

9 Yea, and the Assyrian also is joined with them: they are come to the aid of the sons of Lot. ^psalm-82-9-douay

10 Do to them as thou didst to Madian and to Sisara: as to Jabin at the brook of Cisson. ^psalm-82-10-douay

11 Who perished at Endor: and became as dung for the earth. ^psalm-82-11-douay

12 Make their princes like Oreb, and Zeb, and Zebee, and Salmana. All their princes, ^psalm-82-12-douay

13 Who have said: Let us possess the sanctuary of God for an inheritance. ^psalm-82-13-douay

14 O my God, make them like a wheel; and as stubble before the wind. ^psalm-82-14-douay

15 As fire which burneth the wood: and as a flame burning mountains: ^psalm-82-15-douay

16 So shalt thou pursue them with thy tempest: and shalt trouble them in thy wrath. ^psalm-82-16-douay

17 Fill their faces with shame; and they shall seek thy name, O Lord. ^psalm-82-17-douay

18 Let them be ashamed and troubled for ever and ever: and let them be confounded and perish. ^psalm-82-18-douay

19 And let them know that the Lord is thy name: thou alone art the most High over all the earth. ^psalm-82-19-douay
