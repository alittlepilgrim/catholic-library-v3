# Psalm 101

A prayer for one in affliction: the fifth penitential psalm.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 102**, and the **[[psalm-100-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 101**.

1 The prayer of the poor man, when he was anxious, and poured out his supplication before the Lord. ^psalm-101-1-douay

2 Hear, O Lord, my prayer: and let my cry come to thee. ^psalm-101-2-douay

3 Turn not away thy face from me: in the day when I am in trouble, incline thy ear to me. In what day soever I shall call upon thee, hear me speedily. ^psalm-101-3-douay

4 For my days are vanished like smoke, and my bones are grown dry like fuel for the fire. ^psalm-101-4-douay

5 I am smitten as grass, and my heart is withered: because I forgot to eat my bread. ^psalm-101-5-douay

6 Through the voice of my groaning, my bone hath cleaved to my flesh. ^psalm-101-6-douay

7 I am become like to a pelican of the wilderness: I am like a night raven in the house. **A pelican, etc...** I am become through grief, like birds that affect solitude and darkness. ^psalm-101-7-douay

8 I have watched, and am become as a sparrow all alone on the housetop. ^psalm-101-8-douay

9 All the day long my enemies reproached me: and they that praised me did swear against me. ^psalm-101-9-douay

10 For I did eat ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping. ^psalm-101-10-douay

11 Because of thy anger and indignation: for having lifted me up thou hast thrown me down. ^psalm-101-11-douay

12 My days have declined like a shadow, and I am withered like grass. ^psalm-101-12-douay

13 But thou, O Lord, endurest for ever: and thy memorial to all generations. ^psalm-101-13-douay

14 Thou shalt arise and have mercy on Sion: for it is time to have mercy on it, for the time is come. ^psalm-101-14-douay

15 For the stones thereof have pleased thy servants: and they shall have pity on the earth thereof. ^psalm-101-15-douay

16 All the Gentiles shall fear thy name, O Lord, and all the kings of the earth thy glory. ^psalm-101-16-douay

17 For the Lord hath built up Sion: and he shall be seen in his glory. ^psalm-101-17-douay

18 He hath had regard to the prayer of the humble: and he hath not despised their petition. ^psalm-101-18-douay

19 Let these things be written unto another generation: and the people that shall be created shall praise the Lord: ^psalm-101-19-douay

20 Because he hath looked forth from his high sanctuary: from heaven the Lord hath looked upon the earth. ^psalm-101-20-douay

21 That he might hear the groans of them that are in fetters: that he might release the children of the slain: ^psalm-101-21-douay

22 That they may declare the name of the Lord in Sion: and his praise in Jerusalem; ^psalm-101-22-douay

23 When the people assemble together, and kings, to serve the Lord. ^psalm-101-23-douay

24 He answered him in the way of his strength: Declare unto me the fewness of my days. **He answered him in the way of his strength...** That is, the people, mentioned in the foregoing verse, or the penitent, in whose person this psalm is delivered, answered the Lord in the way of his strength: that is, according to the best of his power and strength: or when he was in the flower of his age and strength: inquiring after the fewness of his days: to know if he should live long enough to see the happy restoration of Sion, etc. ^psalm-101-24-douay

25 Call me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are unto generation and generation. ^psalm-101-25-douay

26 In the beginning, O Lord, thou foundedst the earth: and the heavens are the works of thy hands. ^psalm-101-26-douay

27 They shall perish but thou remainest: and all of them shall grow old like a garment: And as a vesture thou shalt change them, and they shall be changed. ^psalm-101-27-douay

28 But thou art always the selfsame, and thy years shall not fail. ^psalm-101-28-douay

29 The children of thy servants shall continue and their seed shall be directed for ever. ^psalm-101-29-douay
