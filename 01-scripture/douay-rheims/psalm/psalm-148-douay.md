# Psalm 148

*Alleluia.*

1 Praise ye the Lord from the heavens: praise ye him in the high places. ^psalm-148-1-douay

2 Praise ye him, all his angels, praise ye him, all his hosts. ^psalm-148-2-douay

3 Praise ye him, O sun and moon: praise him, all ye stars and light. ^psalm-148-3-douay

4 Praise him, ye heavens of heavens: and let all the waters that are above the heavens ^psalm-148-4-douay

5 Praise the name of the Lord. For he spoke, and they were made: he commanded, and they were created. ^psalm-148-5-douay

6 He hath established them for ever, and for ages of ages: he hath made a decree, and it shall not pass away. ^psalm-148-6-douay

7 Praise the Lord from the earth, ye dragons, and all ye deeps: ^psalm-148-7-douay

8 Fire, hail, snow, ice, stormy winds, which fulfil his word: ^psalm-148-8-douay

9 Mountains and all hills, fruitful trees and all cedars: ^psalm-148-9-douay

10 Beasts and all cattle: serpents and feathered fowls: ^psalm-148-10-douay

11 Kings of the earth and all people: princes and all judges of the earth: ^psalm-148-11-douay

12 Young men and maidens: let the old with the younger, praise the name of the Lord: ^psalm-148-12-douay

13 For his name alone is exalted. ^psalm-148-13-douay

14 The praise of him is above heaven and earth: and he hath exalted the horn of his people. A hymn to all his saints to the children of Israel, a people approaching to him. Alleluia. ^psalm-148-14-douay
