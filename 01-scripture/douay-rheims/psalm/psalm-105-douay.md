# Psalm 105

A confession of the manifold sins and ingratitudes of the Israelites.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 106**, and the **[[psalm-104-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 105**.

*Alleluia.*

1 Give glory to the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-105-1-douay

2 Who shall declare the powers of the Lord? who shall set forth all his praises? ^psalm-105-2-douay

3 Blessed are they that keep judgment, and do justice at all times. ^psalm-105-3-douay

4 Remember us, O Lord, in the favour of thy people: visit us with thy salvation. ^psalm-105-4-douay

5 That we may see the good of thy chosen, that we may rejoice in the joy of thy nation: that thou mayst be praised with thy inheritance. ^psalm-105-5-douay

6 We have sinned with our fathers: we have acted unjustly, we have wrought iniquity. ^psalm-105-6-douay

7 Our fathers understood not thy wonders in Egypt: they remembered not the multitude of thy mercies: And they provoked to wrath going up to the sea, even the Red Sea. ^psalm-105-7-douay

8 And he saved them for his own name's sake: that he might make his power known. ^psalm-105-8-douay

9 And he rebuked the Red Sea and it was dried up: and he led them through the depths, as in a wilderness. ^psalm-105-9-douay

10 And he saved them from the hand of them that hated them: and he redeemed them from the hand of the enemy. ^psalm-105-10-douay

11 And the water covered them that afflicted them: there was not one of them left. ^psalm-105-11-douay

12 And they believed his words: and they sang his praises. ^psalm-105-12-douay

13 They had quickly done, they forgot his works: and they waited not for his counsel. ^psalm-105-13-douay

14 And they coveted their desire in the desert: and they tempted God in the place without water. ^psalm-105-14-douay

15 And he gave them their request: and sent fulness into their souls. ^psalm-105-15-douay

16 And they provoked Moses in the camp, Aaron the holy one of the Lord. ^psalm-105-16-douay

17 The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan: and covered the congregation of Abiron. ^psalm-105-17-douay

18 And a fire was kindled in their congregation: the flame burned the wicked. ^psalm-105-18-douay

19 They made also a calf in Horeb: and they adored the graven thing. ^psalm-105-19-douay

20 And they changed their glory into the likeness of a calf that eateth grass. ^psalm-105-20-douay

21 They forgot God, who saved them, who had done great things in Egypt, ^psalm-105-21-douay

22 Wondrous works in the land of Cham: terrible things in the Red Sea. ^psalm-105-22-douay

23 And he said that he would destroy them: had not Moses his chosen stood before him in the breach: To turn away his wrath, lest he should destroy them. ^psalm-105-23-douay

24 And they set at nought the desirable land. They believed not his word, ^psalm-105-24-douay

25 And they murmured in their tents: they hearkened not to the voice of the Lord. ^psalm-105-25-douay

26 And he lifted up his hand over them: to overthrow them in the desert; ^psalm-105-26-douay

27 And to cast down their seed among the nations, and to scatter them in the countries. ^psalm-105-27-douay

28 They also were initiated to Beelphegor: and ate the sacrifices of the dead. **Initiated...** That is, they dedicated, or consecrated themselves to the idol of the Moabites and Madianites, called Beelphegor, or Baal-Peor ([[numbers-25-douay|Numbers 25:3]]). -- Ibid. **The dead...** Viz., idols without life. ^psalm-105-28-douay

29 And they provoked him with their inventions: and destruction was multiplied among them. ^psalm-105-29-douay

30 Then Phinees stood up, and pacified him: and the slaughter ceased. ^psalm-105-30-douay

31 And it was reputed to him unto justice, to generation and generation for evermore. ^psalm-105-31-douay

32 They provoked him also at the waters of contradiction: and Moses was afflicted for their sakes: ^psalm-105-32-douay

33 Because they exasperated his spirit. And he distinguished with his lips. **He distinguished with his lips...** Moses, by occasion of the people's rebellion and incredulity, was guilty of distinguishing with his lips; when, instead of speaking to the rock, as God had commanded, he said to the people, with a certain hesitation in his faith, Hear ye, rebellious and incredulous: Can we from this rock bring out water for you? ([[numbers-20-douay|Numbers 20:10]]) ^psalm-105-33-douay

34 They did not destroy the nations of which the Lord spoke unto them. ^psalm-105-34-douay

35 And they were mingled among the heathens, and learned their works: ^psalm-105-35-douay

36 And served their idols, and it became a stumbling block to them. ^psalm-105-36-douay

37 And they sacrificed their sons, and their daughters to devils. ^psalm-105-37-douay

38 And they shed innocent blood: the blood of their sons and of their daughters which they sacrificed to the idols of Chanaan. And the land was polluted with blood, ^psalm-105-38-douay

39 And was defiled with their works: and they went aside after their own inventions. ^psalm-105-39-douay

40 And the Lord was exceedingly angry with his people: and he abhorred his inheritance. ^psalm-105-40-douay

41 And he delivered them into the hands of the nations: and they that hated them had dominion over them. ^psalm-105-41-douay

42 And their enemies afflicted them: and they were humbled under their hands: ^psalm-105-42-douay

43 Many times did he deliver them. But they provoked him with their counsel: and they were brought low by their iniquities. ^psalm-105-43-douay

44 And he saw when they were in tribulation: and he heard their prayer. ^psalm-105-44-douay

45 And he was mindful of his covenant: and repented according to the multitude of his mercies. ^psalm-105-45-douay

46 And he gave them unto mercies, in the sight of all those that had made them captives. ^psalm-105-46-douay

47 Save us, O Lord, our God: and gather us from among the nations: That we may give thanks to thy holy name, and may glory in thy praise. ^psalm-105-47-douay

48 Blessed be the Lord the God of Israel, from everlasting to everlasting: and let all the people say: So be it, so be it. ^psalm-105-48-douay
