# Psalm 107

The prophet praises God for benefits received.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 108**, and the **[[psalm-106-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 107**.

1 A canticle of a psalm for David himself. ^psalm-107-1-douay

2 My heart is ready, O God, my heart is ready: I will sing, and will give praise, with my glory. ^psalm-107-2-douay

3 Arise, my glory; arise, psaltery and harp: I will arise in the morning early. ^psalm-107-3-douay

4 I will praise thee, O Lord, among the people: and I will sing unto thee among the nations. ^psalm-107-4-douay

5 For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth even unto the clouds. ^psalm-107-5-douay

6 Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory over all the earth: ^psalm-107-6-douay

7 That thy beloved may be delivered. Save with thy right hand and hear me. ^psalm-107-7-douay

8 God hath spoken in his holiness. I will rejoice, and I will divide Sichem and I will mete out the vale of tabernacles. ^psalm-107-8-douay

9 Galaad is mine: and Manasses is mine and Ephraim the protection of my head. Juda is my king: ^psalm-107-9-douay

10 Moab the pot of my hope. Over Edom I will stretch out my shoe: the aliens are become my friends. ^psalm-107-10-douay

11 Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom? ^psalm-107-11-douay

12 Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our armies? ^psalm-107-12-douay

13 O grant us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man. ^psalm-107-13-douay

14 Through God we shall do mightily: and he will bring our enemies to nothing. ^psalm-107-14-douay
