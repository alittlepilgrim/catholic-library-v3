# Psalm 69

A prayer in persecution.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 70**, and the **[[psalm-68-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 69**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David, to bring to remembrance that the Lord saved him. ^psalm-69-1-douay

2 O God, come to my assistance; O Lord, make haste to help me. ^psalm-69-2-douay

3 Let them be confounded and ashamed that seek my soul: ^psalm-69-3-douay

4 Let them be turned backward, and blush for shame that desire evils to me: Let them be presently turned away blushing for shame that say to me: 'Tis well, 'tis well. **'T is well, 't is well...** *Euge, euge*. St. Jerome renders it, *vah, vah*! which is the voice of one insulting and deriding. Some understand it as a detestation of deceitful flatterers. ^psalm-69-4-douay

5 Let all that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee; and let such as love thy salvation say always: The Lord be magnified. ^psalm-69-5-douay

6 But I am needy and poor; O God, help me. Thou art my helper and my deliverer: O lord, make no delay. ^psalm-69-6-douay
