# Psalm 54

A prayer of a just man under persecution from the wicked. It agrees to Christ persecuted by the Jews, and betrayed by Judas.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 55**, and the **[[psalm-53-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 54**.

1 Unto the end, in verses, understanding for David. ^psalm-54-1-douay

2 Hear, O God, my prayer, and despise not my supplication: ^psalm-54-2-douay

3 Be attentive to me and hear me. I am grieved in my exercise; and am troubled, ^psalm-54-3-douay

4 At the voice of the enemy, and at the tribulation of the sinner. For they have cast iniquities upon me: and in wrath they were troublesome to me. ^psalm-54-4-douay

5 My heart is troubled within me: and the fear of death is fallen upon me. ^psalm-54-5-douay

6 Fear and trembling are come upon me: and darkness hath covered me. ^psalm-54-6-douay

7 And I said: Who will give me wings like a dove, and I will fly and be at rest? ^psalm-54-7-douay

8 Lo, I have gone far off flying away; and I abode in the wilderness. ^psalm-54-8-douay

9 I waited for him that hath saved me from pusillanimity of spirit, and a storm. ^psalm-54-9-douay

10 Cast down, O Lord, and divide their tongues; for I have seen iniquity and contradiction in the city. ^psalm-54-10-douay

11 Day and night shall iniquity surround it upon its walls: and in the midst thereof are labour, ^psalm-54-11-douay

12 And injustice. And usury and deceit have not departed from its streets. ^psalm-54-12-douay

13 For if my enemy had reviled me, I would verily have borne with it. And if he that hated me had spoken great things against me, I would perhaps have hidden my self from him. ^psalm-54-13-douay

14 But thou a man of one mind, my guide, and my familiar, ^psalm-54-14-douay

15 Who didst take sweetmeats together with me: in the house of God we walked with consent. ^psalm-54-15-douay

16 Let death come upon them, and let them go down alive into hell. For there is wickedness in their dwellings: in the midst of them. **Let death, etc...** This, and such like imprecations which occur in the psalms, are delivered prophetically; that is, by way of foretelling the punishments which shall fall upon the wicked from divine justice, and approving the righteous ways of God: but not by way of ill will, or uncharitable curses, which the law of God disallows. ^psalm-54-16-douay

17 But I have cried to God: and the Lord will save me. ^psalm-54-17-douay

18 Evening and morning, and at noon I will speak and declare: and he shall hear my voice. ^psalm-54-18-douay

19 He shall redeem my soul in peace from them that draw near to me: for among many they were with me. **Among many, etc...** That is, they that drew near to attack me were many in company all combined to fight against me. ^psalm-54-19-douay

20 God shall hear, and the Eternal shall humble them. For there is no change with them, and they have not feared God: ^psalm-54-20-douay

21 He hath stretched forth his hand to repay. They have defiled his covenant, ^psalm-54-21-douay

22 They are divided by the wrath of his countenance, and his heart hath drawn near. His words are smoother than oil, and the same are darts. **They are divided, etc...** Dispersed, scattered, and brought to nothing, by the wrath of God; who looks with indignation on their wicked and deceitful ways. ^psalm-54-22-douay

23 Cast thy care upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall not suffer the just to waver for ever. ^psalm-54-23-douay

24 But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction. Bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee, O Lord. ^psalm-54-24-douay
