# Psalm 134

An exhortation to praise God: the vanity of idols.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 135**, and the **[[psalm-133-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 134**.

1 Alleluia. ^psalm-134-1-douay

Praise ye the name of the Lord: O you his servants, praise the Lord:

2 You that stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God. ^psalm-134-2-douay

3 Praise ye the Lord, for the Lord is good: sing ye to his name, for it is sweet. ^psalm-134-3-douay

4 For the Lord hath chosen Jacob unto himself: Israel for his own possession. ^psalm-134-4-douay

5 For I have known that the Lord is great, and our God is above all gods. ^psalm-134-5-douay

6 Whatsoever the Lord pleased he hath done, in heaven, in earth, in the sea, and in all the deeps. ^psalm-134-6-douay

7 He bringeth up clouds from the end of the earth: he hath made lightnings for the rain. He bringeth forth winds out of his stores: ^psalm-134-7-douay

8 He slew the firstborn of Egypt from man even unto beast. ^psalm-134-8-douay

9 He sent forth signs and wonders in the midst of thee, O Egypt: upon Pharao, and upon all his servants. ^psalm-134-9-douay

10 He smote many nations, and slew mighty kings: ^psalm-134-10-douay

11 Sehon king of the Amorrhites, and Og king of Basan, and all the kingdoms of Chanaan. ^psalm-134-11-douay

12 And gave their land for an inheritance, for an inheritance to his people Israel. ^psalm-134-12-douay

13 Thy name, O Lord, is for ever: thy memorial, O Lord, unto all generations. ^psalm-134-13-douay

14 For the Lord will judge his people, and will be entreated in favour of his servants. ^psalm-134-14-douay

15 The idols of the Gentiles are silver and gold, the works of men's hands. ^psalm-134-15-douay

16 They have a mouth, but they speak not: they have eyes, but they see not. ^psalm-134-16-douay

17 They have ears, but they hear not: neither is there any breath in their mouths. ^psalm-134-17-douay

18 Let them that make them be like to them: and every one that trusteth in them. ^psalm-134-18-douay

19 Bless the Lord, O house of Israel: bless the Lord, O house of Aaron. ^psalm-134-19-douay

20 Bless the Lord, O house of Levi: you that fear the Lord, bless the Lord. ^psalm-134-20-douay

21 Blessed be the Lord out of Sion, who dwelleth in Jerusalem. ^psalm-134-21-douay
