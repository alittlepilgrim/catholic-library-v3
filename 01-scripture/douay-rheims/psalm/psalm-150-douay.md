# Psalm 150

*Alleluia.*

1 Praise ye the Lord in his holy places: praise ye him in the firmament of his power. ^psalm-150-1-douay

2 Praise ye him for his mighty acts: praise ye him according to the multitude of his greatness. ^psalm-150-2-douay

3 Praise him with the sound of trumpet: praise him with psaltery and harp. ^psalm-150-3-douay

4 Praise him with timbrel and choir: praise him with strings and organs. ^psalm-150-4-douay

5 Praise him on high sounding cymbals: praise him on cymbals of joy: let every spirit praise the Lord. Alleluia. ^psalm-150-5-douay
