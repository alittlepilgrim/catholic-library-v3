# Psalm 39

Christ's coming, and redeeming mankind.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 40**, and the **[[psalm-38-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 39**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David himself. ^psalm-39-1-douay

2 With expectation I have waited for the Lord, and he was attentive to me. ^psalm-39-2-douay

3 And he heard my prayers, and brought me out of the pit of misery and the mire of dregs. And he set my feet upon a rock, and directed my steps. ^psalm-39-3-douay

4 And he put a new canticle into my mouth, a song to our God. Many shall see, and shall fear: and they shall hope in the Lord. ^psalm-39-4-douay

5 Blessed is the man whose trust is in the name of the Lord; and who hath not had regard to vanities, and lying follies. ^psalm-39-5-douay

6 Thou hast multiplied thy wonderful works, O Lord my God: and in thy thoughts there is no one like to thee. I have declared and I have spoken they are multiplied above number. ^psalm-39-6-douay

7 Sacrifice and oblation thou didst not desire; but thou hast pierced ears for me. Burnt offering and sin offering thou didst not require: ^psalm-39-7-douay

8 Then said I, Behold I come. In the head of the book it is written of me ^psalm-39-8-douay

9 That I should do thy will: O my God, I have desired it, and thy law in the midst of my heart. ^psalm-39-9-douay

10 I have declared thy justice in a great church, lo, I will not restrain my lips: O Lord, thou knowest it. ^psalm-39-10-douay

11 I have not hid thy justice within my heart: I have declared thy truth and thy salvation. I have not concealed thy mercy and thy truth from a great council. ^psalm-39-11-douay

12 Withhold not thou, O Lord, thy tender mercies from me: thy mercy and thy truth have always upheld me. ^psalm-39-12-douay

13 For evils without number have surrounded me; my iniquities have overtaken me, and I was not able to see. They are multiplied above the hairs of my head: and my heart hath forsaken me. **My iniquities...** That is, the sins of all mankind, which I have taken upon me. ^psalm-39-13-douay

14 Be pleased, O Lord, to deliver me. Look down, O Lord, to help me. ^psalm-39-14-douay

15 Let them be confounded and ashamed together, that seek after my soul to take it away. Let them be turned backward and be ashamed that desire evils to me. ^psalm-39-15-douay

16 Let them immediately bear their confusion, that say to me: 'T is well, 't is well. **'T is well...** The Hebrew here is an interjection of insult and derision, like the *Vah*. [[matthew-27-douay|Matthew 27:49]]. ^psalm-39-16-douay

17 Let all that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say always: The Lord be magnified. ^psalm-39-17-douay

18 But I am a beggar and poor: the Lord is careful for me. Thou art my helper and my protector: O my God, be not slack. ^psalm-39-18-douay
