# Psalm 85

A prayer for God's grace to assist us to the end.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 86**, and the **[[psalm-84-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 85**.

1 A prayer for David himself. ^psalm-85-1-douay

Incline thy ear, O Lord, and hear me: for I am needy and poor.

2 Preserve my soul, for I am holy: save thy servant, O my God, that trusteth in thee. **I am holy...** I am by my office and profession dedicated to thy service. ^psalm-85-2-douay

3 Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I have cried to thee all the day. ^psalm-85-3-douay

4 Give joy to the soul of thy servant, for to thee, O Lord, I have lifted up my soul. ^psalm-85-4-douay

5 For thou, O Lord, art sweet and mild: and plenteous in mercy to all that call upon thee. ^psalm-85-5-douay

6 Give ear, O Lord, to my prayer: and attend to the voice of my petition. ^psalm-85-6-douay

7 I have called upon thee in the day of my trouble: because thou hast heard me. ^psalm-85-7-douay

8 There is none among the gods like unto thee, O Lord: and there is none according to thy works. ^psalm-85-8-douay

9 All the nations thou hast made shall come and adore before thee, O Lord: and they shall glorify thy name. ^psalm-85-9-douay

10 For thou art great and dost wonderful things: thou art God alone. ^psalm-85-10-douay

11 Conduct me, O Lord, in thy way, and I will walk in thy truth: let my heart rejoice that it may fear thy name. ^psalm-85-11-douay

12 I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with my whole heart, and I will glorify thy name for ever: ^psalm-85-12-douay

13 For thy mercy is great towards me: and thou hast delivered my soul out of the lower hell. ^psalm-85-13-douay

14 O God, the wicked are risen up against me, and the assembly of the mighty have sought my soul: and they have not set thee before their eyes. ^psalm-85-14-douay

15 And thou, O Lord, art a God of compassion, and merciful, patient, and of much mercy, and true. ^psalm-85-15-douay

16 O look upon me, and have mercy on me: give thy command to thy servant, and save the son of thy handmaid. ^psalm-85-16-douay

17 Show me a token for good: that they who hate me may see, and be confounded, because thou, O Lord, hast helped me and hast comforted me. ^psalm-85-17-douay
