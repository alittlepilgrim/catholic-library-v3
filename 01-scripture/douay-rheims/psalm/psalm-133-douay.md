# Psalm 133

An exhortation to praise God continually.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 134**, and the **[[psalm-132-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 133**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 Behold now bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord: Who stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God. ^psalm-133-1-douay

2 In the nights lift up your hands to the holy places, and bless ye the Lord. ^psalm-133-2-douay

3 May the Lord out of Sion bless thee, he that made heaven and earth. ^psalm-133-3-douay
