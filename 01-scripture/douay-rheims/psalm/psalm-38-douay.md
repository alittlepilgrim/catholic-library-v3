# Psalm 38

A just man's peace and patience in his sufferings; considering the vanity of the world, and the providence of God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 39**, and the **[[psalm-37-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 38**.

1 Unto the end, for Idithun himself, a canticle of David. ^psalm-38-1-douay

2 I said: I will take heed to my ways: that I sin not with my tongue. I have set a guard to my mouth, when the sinner stood against me. ^psalm-38-2-douay

3 I was dumb, and was humbled, and kept silence from good things: and my sorrow was renewed. ^psalm-38-3-douay

4 My heart grew hot within me: and in my meditation a fire shall flame out. ^psalm-38-4-douay

5 I spoke with my tongue: O Lord, make me know my end. And what is the number of my days: that I may know what is wanting to me. ^psalm-38-5-douay

6 Behold thou hast made my days measurable and my substance is as nothing before thee. And indeed all things are vanity: every man living. ^psalm-38-6-douay

7 Surely man passeth as an image: yea, and he is disquieted in vain. He storeth up: and he knoweth not for whom he shall gather these things. ^psalm-38-7-douay

8 And now what is my hope? is it not the Lord? and my substance is with thee. ^psalm-38-8-douay

9 Deliver thou me from all my iniquities: thou hast made me a reproach to the fool. ^psalm-38-9-douay

10 I was dumb, and I opened not my mouth, because thou hast done it. ^psalm-38-10-douay

11 Remove thy scourges from me. The strength of thy hand hath made me faint in rebukes: ^psalm-38-11-douay

12 Thou hast corrected man for iniquity. And thou hast made his soul to waste away like a spider: surely in vain is any man disquieted. ^psalm-38-12-douay

13 Hear my prayer, O Lord, and my supplication: give ear to my tears. Be no silent: for I am a stranger with thee, and a sojourner as all my fathers were. ^psalm-38-13-douay

14 O forgive me, that I may be refreshed, before I go hence, and be no more. ^psalm-38-14-douay
