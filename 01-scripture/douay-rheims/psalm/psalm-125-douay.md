# Psalm 125

The people of God rejoice at their delivery from captivity.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 126**, and the **[[psalm-124-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 125**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 When the Lord brought back the captivity of Sion, we became like men comforted. ^psalm-125-1-douay

2 Then was our mouth filled with gladness; and our tongue with joy. Then shall they say among the Gentiles: The Lord hath done great things for them. ^psalm-125-2-douay

3 The Lord hath done great things for us: we are become joyful. ^psalm-125-3-douay

4 Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as a stream in the south. ^psalm-125-4-douay

5 They that sow in tears shall reap in joy. ^psalm-125-5-douay

6 Going they went and wept, casting their seeds. ^psalm-125-6-douay


7 But coming they shall come with joyfulness, carrying their sheaves. ^psalm-125-7-douay
