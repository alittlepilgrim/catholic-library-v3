# Psalm 149

*Alleluia.*

1 Sing ye to the Lord a new canticle: let his praise be in the church of the saints. ^psalm-149-1-douay

2 Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: and let the children of Sion be joyful in their king. ^psalm-149-2-douay

3 Let them praise his name in choir: let them sing to him with the timbrel and the psaltery. ^psalm-149-3-douay

4 For the Lord is well pleased with his people: and he will exalt the meek unto salvation. ^psalm-149-4-douay

5 The saints shall rejoice in glory: they shall be joyful in their beds. ^psalm-149-5-douay

6 The high praises of God shall be in their mouth: and two-edged swords in their hands: ^psalm-149-6-douay

7 To execute vengeance upon the nations, chastisements among the people: ^psalm-149-7-douay

8 To bind their kings with fetters, and their nobles with manacles of iron. ^psalm-149-8-douay

9 To execute upon them the judgment that is written: this glory is to all his saints. Alleluia. ^psalm-149-9-douay
