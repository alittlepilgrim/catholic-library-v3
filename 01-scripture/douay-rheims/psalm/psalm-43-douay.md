# Psalm 43

The Church commemorates former favours, and present afflictions; under which she prays for succour.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 44**, and the **[[psalm-42-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 43**.

1 Unto the end, for the sons of Core, to give understanding. ^psalm-43-1-douay

2 We have heard, O God, with our ears: our fathers have declared to us, The work thou hast wrought in their days, and in the days of old. ^psalm-43-2-douay

3 Thy hand destroyed the Gentiles, and thou plantedst them: thou didst afflict the people and cast them out. ^psalm-43-3-douay

4 For they got not the possession of the land by their own sword: neither did their own arm save them. But thy right hand and thy arm, and the light of thy countenance: because thou wast pleased with them. ^psalm-43-4-douay

5 Thou art thyself my king and my God, who commandest the saving of Jacob. ^psalm-43-5-douay

6 Through thee we will push down our enemies with the horn: and through thy name we will despise them that rise up against us. ^psalm-43-6-douay

7 For I will not trust in my bow: neither shall my sword save me. ^psalm-43-7-douay

8 But thou hast saved us from them that afflict us: and hast put them to shame that hate us. ^psalm-43-8-douay

9 In God shall we glory all the day long: and in thy name we will give praise for ever. ^psalm-43-9-douay

10 But now thou hast cast us off, and put us to shame: and thou, O God, wilt not go out with our armies. ^psalm-43-10-douay

11 Thou hast made us turn our back to our enemies: and they that hated us plundered for themselves. ^psalm-43-11-douay

12 Thou hast given us up like sheep to be eaten: thou hast scattered us among the nations. ^psalm-43-12-douay

13 Thou hast sold thy people for no price: and there was no reckoning in the exchange of them. ^psalm-43-13-douay

14 Thou hast made us a reproach to our neighbours, a scoff and derision to them that are round about us. ^psalm-43-14-douay

15 Thou hast made us a byword among the Gentiles: a shaking of the head among the people. ^psalm-43-15-douay

16 All the day long my shame is before me: and the confusion of my face hath covered me, ^psalm-43-16-douay

17 At the voice of him that reproacheth and detracteth me: at the face of the enemy and persecutor. ^psalm-43-17-douay

18 All these things have come upon us, yet we have not forgotten thee: and we have not done wickedly in thy covenant. ^psalm-43-18-douay

19 And our heart hath not turned back: neither hast thou turned aside our steps from thy way. ^psalm-43-19-douay

20 For thou hast humbled us in the place of affliction: and the shadow of death hath covered us. ^psalm-43-20-douay

21 If we have forgotten the name of our God, and if we have spread forth our hands to a strange god: ^psalm-43-21-douay

22 Shall not God search out these things: for he knoweth the secrets of the heart. Because for thy sake we are killed all the day long: we are counted as sheep for the slaughter. ^psalm-43-22-douay

23 Arise, why sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, and cast us not off to the end. ^psalm-43-23-douay

24 Why turnest thou thy face away? and forgettest our want and our trouble? ^psalm-43-24-douay

25 For our soul is humbled down to the dust: our belly cleaveth to the earth. ^psalm-43-25-douay

26 Arise, O Lord, help us and redeem us for thy name's sake. ^psalm-43-26-douay
