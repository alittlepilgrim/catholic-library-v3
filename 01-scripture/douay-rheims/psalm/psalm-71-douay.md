# Psalm 71

A prophecy of the coming of Christ, and of his kingdom: prefigured by Solomon and his happy reign.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 72**, and the **[[psalm-70-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 71**.

1 A psalm on Solomon. ^psalm-71-1-douay

2 Give to the king thy judgment, O God, and to the king's son thy justice: To judge thy people with justice, and thy poor with judgment. ^psalm-71-2-douay

3 Let the mountains receive peace for the people: and the hills justice. ^psalm-71-3-douay

4 He shall judge the poor of the people, and he shall save the children of the poor: and he shall humble the oppressor. ^psalm-71-4-douay

5 And he shall continue with the sun and before the moon, throughout all generations. ^psalm-71-5-douay

6 He shall come down like rain upon the fleece; and as showers falling gently upon the earth. ^psalm-71-6-douay

7 In his days shall justice spring up, and abundance of peace, till the moon be taken away. ^psalm-71-7-douay

8 And he shall rule from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth. ^psalm-71-8-douay

9 Before him the Ethiopians shall fall down: and his enemies shall lick the ground. ^psalm-71-9-douay

10 The kings of Tharsis and the islands shall offer presents: the kings of the Arabians and of Saba shall bring gifts: ^psalm-71-10-douay

11 And all kings of the earth shall adore him: all nations shall serve him. ^psalm-71-11-douay

12 For he shall deliver the poor from the mighty: and the needy that had no helper. ^psalm-71-12-douay

13 He shall spare the poor and needy: and he shall save the souls of the poor. ^psalm-71-13-douay

14 He shall redeem their souls from usuries and iniquity: and their names shall be honourable in his sight. ^psalm-71-14-douay

15 And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Arabia, for him they shall always adore: they shall bless him all the day. ^psalm-71-15-douay

16 And there shall be a firmament on the earth on the tops of mountains, above Libanus shall the fruit thereof be exalted: and they of the city shall flourish like the grass of the earth. **A firmament on the earth, etc...** This may be understood of the church of Christ, ever firm and visible: and of the flourishing condition of its congregation. ^psalm-71-16-douay

17 Let his name be blessed for evermore: his name continueth before the sun. And in him shall all the tribes of the earth be blessed: all nations shall magnify him. ^psalm-71-17-douay

18 Blessed be the Lord, the God of Israel, who alone doth wonderful things. ^psalm-71-18-douay

19 And blessed be the name of his majesty for ever: and the whole earth shall be filled with his majesty. So be it. So be it. ^psalm-71-19-douay

20 The praises of David, the son of Jesse, are ended. **Are ended...** By this it appears that this psalm, though placed here, was in order of time the last of those which David composed. ^psalm-71-20-douay
