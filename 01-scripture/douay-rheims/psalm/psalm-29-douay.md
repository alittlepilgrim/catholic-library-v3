# Psalm 29

David praises God for his deliverance, and his merciful dealings with him.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 30**, and the **[[psalm-28-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 29**.

1 A psalm of a canticle, at the dedication of David's house. ^psalm-29-1-douay

2 I will extol thee, O Lord, for thou hast upheld me: and hast not made my enemies to rejoice over me. ^psalm-29-2-douay

3 O Lord my God, I have cried to thee, and thou hast healed me. ^psalm-29-3-douay

4 Thou hast brought forth, O Lord, my soul from hell: thou hast saved me from them that go down into the pit. ^psalm-29-4-douay

5 Sing to the Lord, O ye his saints: and give praise to the memory of his holiness. ^psalm-29-5-douay

6 For wrath is in his indignation; and life in his good will. In the evening weeping shall have place, and in the morning gladness. ^psalm-29-6-douay

7 And in my abundance I said: I shall never be moved. ^psalm-29-7-douay

8 O Lord, in thy favour, thou gavest strength to my beauty. Thou turnedst away thy face from me, and I became troubled. ^psalm-29-8-douay

9 To thee, O Lord, will I cry: and I will make supplication to my God. ^psalm-29-9-douay

10 What profit is there in my blood, whilst I go down to corruption? Shall dust confess to thee, or declare thy truth? ^psalm-29-10-douay

11 The Lord hath heard, and hath had mercy on me: the Lord became my helper. ^psalm-29-11-douay

12 Thou hast turned for me my mourning into joy: thou hast cut my sackcloth, and hast compassed me with gladness: ^psalm-29-12-douay

13 To the end that my glory may sing to thee, and I may not regret: O Lord my God, I will give praise to thee for ever. ^psalm-29-13-douay
