# Psalm 8

1 Unto the end, for the presses: a psalm for David. **The presses...** In Hebrew, *Gittith*, supposed to be a musical instrument. ^psalm-08-1-douay

2 O Lord, our Lord, how admirable is thy name in the whole earth! For thy magnificence is elevated above the heavens. ^psalm-08-2-douay

3 Out of the mouth of infants and of sucklings thou hast perfected praise, because of thy enemies, that thou mayst destroy the enemy and the avenger. ^psalm-08-3-douay

4 For I will behold thy heavens, the works of thy fingers: the moon and the stars which thou hast founded. ^psalm-08-4-douay

5 What is man, that thou art mindful of him? or the son of man, that thou visitest him? ^psalm-08-5-douay

6 Thou hast made him a little less than the angels, thou hast crowned him with glory and honour: ^psalm-08-6-douay

7 And hast set him over the works of thy hands. ^psalm-08-7-douay

8 Thou hast subjected all things under his feet, all sheep and oxen: moreover, the beasts also of the fields. ^psalm-08-8-douay

9 The birds of the air, and the fishes of the sea, that pass through the paths of the sea. ^psalm-08-9-douay

10 O Lord, our Lord, how admirable is thy name in the whole earth! ^psalm-08-10-douay
