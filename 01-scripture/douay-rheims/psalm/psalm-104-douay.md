# Psalm 104

A thanksgiving to God for his benefits to his people Israel.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 105**, and the **[[psalm-103-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 104**.

*Alleluia.*

1 Give glory to the Lord, and call upon his name: declare his deeds among the Gentiles. ^psalm-104-1-douay

2 Sing to him, yea sing praises to him: relate all his wondrous works. ^psalm-104-2-douay

3 Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord. ^psalm-104-3-douay

4 Seek ye the lord, and be strengthened: seek his face evermore. ^psalm-104-4-douay

5 Remember his marvellous works which he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth. ^psalm-104-5-douay

6 O ye seed of Abraham his servant; ye sons of Jacob his chosen. ^psalm-104-6-douay

7 He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth. ^psalm-104-7-douay

8 He hath remembered his covenant for ever: the word which he commanded to a thousand generations. ^psalm-104-8-douay

9 Which he made to Abraham; and his oath to Isaac: ^psalm-104-9-douay

10 And he appointed the same to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting testament: ^psalm-104-10-douay

11 Saying: To thee will I give the land of Chanaan, the lot of your inheritance. ^psalm-104-11-douay

12 When they were but a small number: yea very few, and sojourners therein: ^psalm-104-12-douay

13 And they passed from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people. ^psalm-104-13-douay

14 He suffered no man to hurt them: and he reproved kings for their sakes. ^psalm-104-14-douay

15 Touch ye not my anointed: and do no evil to my prophets. ^psalm-104-15-douay

16 And he called a famine upon the land: and he broke in pieces all the support of bread. ^psalm-104-16-douay

17 He sent a man before them: Joseph, who was sold for a slave. ^psalm-104-17-douay

18 They humbled his feet in fetters: the iron pierced his soul, ^psalm-104-18-douay

19 Until his word came. The word of the Lord inflamed him. ^psalm-104-19-douay

20 The king sent, and he released him: the ruler of the people, and he set him at liberty. ^psalm-104-20-douay

21 He made him master of his house, and ruler of all his possession. ^psalm-104-21-douay

22 That he might instruct his princes as himself, and teach his ancients wisdom. ^psalm-104-22-douay

23 And Israel went into Egypt: and Jacob was a sojourner in the land of Cham. ^psalm-104-23-douay

24 And he increased his people exceedingly: and strengthened them over their enemies. ^psalm-104-24-douay

25 He turned their heart to hate his people: and to deal deceitfully with his servants. **He turned their heart, etc...** Not that God (who is never the author of sin) moved the Egyptians to hate and persecute his people; but that the Egyptians took occasion of hating and envying them, from the sight of the benefits which God bestowed upon them. ^psalm-104-25-douay

26 He sent Moses his servant: Aaron the man whom he had chosen. ^psalm-104-26-douay

27 He gave them power to show them signs, and his wonders in the land of Cham. ^psalm-104-27-douay

28 He sent darkness, and made it obscure: and grieved not his words. **Grieved not his words...** That is, he was not wanting to fulfil his words: or he did not grieve Moses and Aaron, the carriers of his words: or he did not grieve his words, that is, his sons, the children of Israel, who enjoyed light whilst the Egyptians were oppressed with darkness. ^psalm-104-28-douay

29 He turned their waters into blood, and destroyed their fish. ^psalm-104-29-douay

30 Their land brought forth frogs, in the inner chambers of their kings. ^psalm-104-30-douay

31 He spoke, and there came divers sorts of flies and sciniphs in all their coasts. **Sciniphs...** See the annotation, [[exodus-08-douay|Exodus 8:16]]. ^psalm-104-31-douay

32 He gave them hail for rain, a burning fire in the land. ^psalm-104-32-douay

33 And he destroyed their vineyards and their fig trees: and he broke in pieces the trees of their coasts. ^psalm-104-33-douay

34 He spoke, and the locust came, and the bruchus, of which there was no number. **Bruchus...** An insect of the locust kind. ^psalm-104-34-douay

35 And they devoured all the grass in their land, and consumed all the fruit of their ground. ^psalm-104-35-douay

36 And he slew all the firstborn in their land: the firstfruits of all their labour. ^psalm-104-36-douay

37 And he brought them out with silver and gold: and there was not among their tribes one that was feeble. ^psalm-104-37-douay

38 Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them lay upon them. ^psalm-104-38-douay

39 He spread a cloud for their protection, and fire to give them light in the night. ^psalm-104-39-douay

40 They asked, and the quail came: and he filled them with the bread of heaven. ^psalm-104-40-douay

41 He opened the rock, and waters flowed: rivers ran down in the dry land. ^psalm-104-41-douay

42 Because he remembered his holy word, which he had spoken to his servant Abraham. ^psalm-104-42-douay

43 And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness. ^psalm-104-43-douay

44 And he gave them the lands of the Gentiles: and they possessed the labours of the people: ^psalm-104-44-douay

45 That they might observe his justifications, and seek after his law. **His justifications...** That is, his commandments; which here, and in many other places of the scripture, are called justifications, because the keeping of them makes man just. The Protestants render it by the word *statutes*, in favour of their doctrine, which does not allow good works to justify. ^psalm-104-45-douay
