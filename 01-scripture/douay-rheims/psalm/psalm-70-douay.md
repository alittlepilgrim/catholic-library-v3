# Psalm 70

A prayer for perseverance.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 71**, and the **[[psalm-69-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 70**.

1 A psalm for David. ^psalm-70-1-douay

Of the sons of Jonadab, and the former captives. In thee, O Lord, I have hoped, let me never be put to confusion: **Of the sons of Jonadab...** The Rechabites, of whom see [[jeremiah-35-douay|Jeremiah 35]]. By this addition of the seventy-two interpreters, we gather that this psalm was usually sung in the synagogue, in the person of the Rechabites, and of those who were first carried away into captivity.

2 Deliver me in thy justice, and rescue me. Incline thy ear unto me, and save me. ^psalm-70-2-douay

3 Be thou unto me a God, a protector, and a place of strength: that thou mayst make me safe. For thou art my firmament and my refuge. ^psalm-70-3-douay

4 Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the sinner, and out of the hand of the transgressor of the law and of the unjust. ^psalm-70-4-douay

5 For thou art my patience, O Lord: my hope, O Lord, from my youth. ^psalm-70-5-douay

6 By thee have I been confirmed from the womb: from my mother's womb thou art my protector. Of thee I shall continually sing: ^psalm-70-6-douay

7 I am become unto many as a wonder, but thou art a strong helper. ^psalm-70-7-douay

8 Let my mouth be filled with praise, that I may sing thy glory; thy greatness all the day long. ^psalm-70-8-douay

9 Cast me not off in the time of old age: when my strength shall fail, do not thou forsake me. ^psalm-70-9-douay

10 For my enemies have spoken against me; and they that watched my soul have consulted together, ^psalm-70-10-douay

11 Saying: God hath forsaken him: pursue and take him, for there is none to deliver him. ^psalm-70-11-douay

12 O God, be not thou far from me: O my God, make haste to my help. ^psalm-70-12-douay

13 Let them be confounded and come to nothing that detract my soul; let them be covered with confusion and blame that seek my hurt. ^psalm-70-13-douay

14 But I will always hope; and will add to all thy praise. ^psalm-70-14-douay

15 My mouth shall show forth thy justice; thy salvation all the day long. Because I have not known learning, **Learning...** As much as to say, I build not upon human learning, but only on the power and justice of God. ^psalm-70-15-douay

16 I will enter into the powers of the Lord: O Lord, I will be mindful of thy justice alone. ^psalm-70-16-douay

17 Thou hast taught me, O God, from my youth: and till now I will declare thy wonderful works. ^psalm-70-17-douay

18 And unto old age and grey hairs: O God, forsake me not, Until I show forth thy arm to all the generation that is to come: Thy power, ^psalm-70-18-douay

19 And thy justice, O God, even to the highest great things thou hast done: O God, who is like to thee? ^psalm-70-19-douay

20 How great troubles hast thou shown me, many and grievous: and turning thou hast brought me to life, and hast brought me back again from the depths of the earth: ^psalm-70-20-douay

21 Thou hast multiplied thy magnificence; and turning to me thou hast comforted me. ^psalm-70-21-douay

22 For I will also confess to thee thy truth with the instruments of psaltery: O God, I will sing to thee with the harp, thou holy one of Israel. ^psalm-70-22-douay

23 My lips shall greatly rejoice, when I shall sing to thee; and my soul which thou hast redeemed. ^psalm-70-23-douay

24 Yea and my tongue shall meditate on thy justice all the day; when they shall be confounded and put to shame that seek evils to me. ^psalm-70-24-douay
