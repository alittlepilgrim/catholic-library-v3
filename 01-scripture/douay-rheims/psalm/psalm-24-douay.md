# Psalm 24

A prayer for grace, mercy, and protection against our enemies.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 25**, and the **[[psalm-23-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 24**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David. ^psalm-24-1-douay

To thee, O Lord, have I lifted up my soul.

2 In thee, O my God, I put my trust; let me not be ashamed. ^psalm-24-2-douay

3 Neither let my enemies laugh at me: for none of them that wait on thee shall be confounded. ^psalm-24-3-douay

4 Let all them be confounded that act unjust things without cause. Show, O Lord, thy ways to me, and teach me thy paths. ^psalm-24-4-douay

5 Direct me in thy truth, and teach me; for thou art God my Saviour; and on thee have I waited all the day long. ^psalm-24-5-douay

6 Remember, O Lord, thy bowels of compassion; and thy mercies that are from the beginning of the world. ^psalm-24-6-douay

7 The sins of my youth and my ignorances do not remember. According to thy mercy remember thou me: for thy goodness' sake, O Lord. ^psalm-24-7-douay

8 The Lord is sweet and righteous: therefore he will give a law to sinners in the way. ^psalm-24-8-douay

9 He will guide the mild in judgment: he will teach the meek his ways. ^psalm-24-9-douay

10 All the ways of the Lord are mercy and truth, to them that seek after his covenant and his testimonies. ^psalm-24-10-douay

11 For thy name's sake, O Lord, thou wilt pardon my sin: for it is great. ^psalm-24-11-douay

12 Who is the man that feareth the Lord? He hath appointed him a law in the way he hath chosen. ^psalm-24-12-douay

13 His soul shall dwell in good things: and his seed shall inherit the land. ^psalm-24-13-douay

14 The Lord is a firmament to them that fear him: and his covenant shall be made manifest to them. ^psalm-24-14-douay

15 My eyes are ever towards the Lord: for he shall pluck my feet out of the snare. ^psalm-24-15-douay

16 Look thou upon me, and have mercy on me; for I am alone and poor. ^psalm-24-16-douay

17 The troubles of my heart are multiplied: deliver me from my necessities. ^psalm-24-17-douay

18 See my abjection and my labour; and forgive me all my sins. ^psalm-24-18-douay

19 Consider my enemies for they are multiplied, and have hated me with an unjust hatred. ^psalm-24-19-douay

20 Deep thou my soul, and deliver me: I shall not be ashamed, for I have hoped in thee. ^psalm-24-20-douay

21 The innocent and the upright have adhered to me: because I have waited on thee. ^psalm-24-21-douay

22 Deliver Israel, O God, from all his tribulations. ^psalm-24-22-douay
