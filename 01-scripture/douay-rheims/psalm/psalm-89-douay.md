# Psalm 89

A prayer for the mercy of God: recounting the shortness and miseries of the days of man.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 90**, and the **[[psalm-88-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 89**.

1 A prayer of Moses the man of God. ^psalm-89-1-douay

Lord, thou hast been our refuge from generation to generation.

2 Before the mountains were made, or the earth and the world was formed; from eternity and to eternity thou art God. ^psalm-89-2-douay

3 Turn not man away to be brought low: and thou hast said: Be converted, O ye sons of men. **Turn not man away, etc...** Suffer him not quite to perish from thee, since thou art pleased to call upon him to be converted to thee. ^psalm-89-3-douay

4 For a thousand years in thy sight are as yesterday, which is past. And as a watch in the night, ^psalm-89-4-douay

5 Things that are counted nothing, shall their years be. ^psalm-89-5-douay

6 In the morning man shall grow up like grass; in the morning he shall flourish and pass away: in the evening he shall fall, grow dry, and wither. ^psalm-89-6-douay

7 For in thy wrath we have fainted away: and are troubled in thy indignation. ^psalm-89-7-douay

8 Thou hast set our iniquities before thy eyes: our life in the light of thy countenance. ^psalm-89-8-douay

9 For all our days are spent; and in thy wrath we have fainted away. Our years shall be considered as a spider: **As a spider...** As frail and weak as a spider's web; and miserable withal, whilst like a spider we spend our bowels in weaving webs to catch flies. ^psalm-89-9-douay

10 The days of our years in them are threescore and ten years. But if in the strong they be fourscore years: and what is more of them is labour and sorrow. For mildness is come upon us: and we shall be corrected. **Mildness is come upon us, etc...** God's mildness corrects us; inasmuch as he deals kindly with us, in shortening the days of this miserable life; and so weaning our affections from all its transitory enjoyments, and teaching us true wisdom. ^psalm-89-10-douay

11 Who knoweth the power of thy anger, and for thy fear ^psalm-89-11-douay

12 Can number thy wrath? So make thy right hand known: and men learned in heart, in wisdom. ^psalm-89-12-douay

13 Return, O Lord, how long? and be entreated in favour of thy servants. ^psalm-89-13-douay

14 We are filled in the morning with thy mercy: and we have rejoiced, and are delighted all our days. ^psalm-89-14-douay

15 We have rejoiced for the days in which thou hast humbled us: for the years in which we have seen evils. ^psalm-89-15-douay

16 Look upon thy servants and upon their works: and direct their children. ^psalm-89-16-douay

17 And let the brightness of the Lord our God be upon us: and direct thou the works of our hands over us; yea, the work of our hands do thou direct. ^psalm-89-17-douay
