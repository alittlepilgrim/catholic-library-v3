# Psalm 25

David's prayer to God in his distress, to be delivered, that he may come to worship him in his tabernacle.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 26**, and the **[[psalm-24-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 25**.

1 Unto the end, a psalm for David. ^psalm-25-1-douay

Judge me, O Lord, for I have walked in my innocence: and I have put my trust in the Lord, and shall not be weakened.

2 Prove me, O Lord, and try me; burn my reins and my heart. ^psalm-25-2-douay

3 For thy mercy is before my eyes; and I am well pleased with thy truth. ^psalm-25-3-douay

4 I have not sat with the council of vanity: neither will I go in with the doers of unjust things. ^psalm-25-4-douay

5 I have hated the assembly of the malignant; and with the wicked I will not sit. ^psalm-25-5-douay

6 I will wash my hands among the innocent; and will compass thy altar, O Lord: ^psalm-25-6-douay

7 That I may hear the voice of thy praise: and tell of all thy wondrous works. ^psalm-25-7-douay

8 I have loved, O Lord, the beauty of thy house; and the place where thy glory dwelleth. ^psalm-25-8-douay

9 Take not away my soul, O God, with the wicked: nor my life with bloody men: ^psalm-25-9-douay

10 In whose hands are iniquities: their right hand is filled with gifts. ^psalm-25-10-douay

11 But as for me, I have walked in my innocence: redeem me, and have mercy on me. ^psalm-25-11-douay

12 My foot hath stood in the direct way: in the churches I will bless thee, O Lord. ^psalm-25-12-douay
