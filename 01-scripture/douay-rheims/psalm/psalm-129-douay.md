# Psalm 129

A prayer of a sinner, trusting in the mercies of God. The sixth penitential psalm.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 130**, and the **[[psalm-128-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 129**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 Out of the depths I have cried to thee, O Lord: ^psalm-129-1-douay

2 Lord, hear my voice. Let thy ears be attentive to the voice of my supplication. ^psalm-129-2-douay

3 If thou, O Lord, wilt mark iniquities: Lord, who shall stand it. ^psalm-129-3-douay

4 For with thee there is merciful forgiveness: and by reason of thy law, I have waited for thee, O Lord. My soul hath relied on his word: ^psalm-129-4-douay

5 My soul hath hoped in the Lord. ^psalm-129-5-douay

6 From the morning watch even until night, let Israel hope in the Lord. ^psalm-129-6-douay

7 Because with the Lord there is mercy: and with him plentiful redemption. ^psalm-129-7-douay

8 And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities. ^psalm-129-8-douay
