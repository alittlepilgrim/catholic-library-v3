# Psalm 141

A prayer of David in extremity of danger.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 142**, and the **[[psalm-140-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 141**.

1 Of understanding for David, A prayer when he was in the cave. [1 Samuel 24.] ^psalm-141-1-douay

2 I cried to the Lord with my voice: with my voice I made supplication to the Lord. ^psalm-141-2-douay

3 In his sight I pour out my prayer, and before him I declare my trouble: ^psalm-141-3-douay

4 When my spirit failed me, then thou knewest my paths. In this way wherein I walked, they have hidden a snare for me. ^psalm-141-4-douay

5 I looked on my right hand, and beheld, and there was no one that would know me. Flight hath failed me: and there is no one that hath regard to my soul. ^psalm-141-5-douay

6 I cried to thee, O Lord: I said: Thou art my hope, my portion in the land of the living. ^psalm-141-6-douay

7 Attend to my supplication: for I am brought very low. Deliver me from my persecutors; for they are stronger than I. ^psalm-141-7-douay

8 Bring my soul out of prison, that I may praise thy name: the just wait for me, until thou reward me. ^psalm-141-8-douay
