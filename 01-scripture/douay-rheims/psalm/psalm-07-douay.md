# Psalm 7

1 The psalm of David, which he sung to the Lord, for the words of Chusi, the son of Jemini. ^psalm-07-1-douay

2 O Lord, my God, in thee have I put my trust; same me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me. ^psalm-07-2-douay

3 Lest at any time he seize upon my soul like a lion, while there is no one to redeem me, nor to save. ^psalm-07-3-douay

4 O Lord, my God, if I have done this thing, if there be iniquity in my hands: ^psalm-07-4-douay

5 If I have rendered to them that repaid me evils, let me deservedly fall empty before my enemies. ^psalm-07-5-douay

6 Let the enemy pursue my soul, and take it, and tread down my life, on the earth, and bring down my glory to the dust. ^psalm-07-6-douay

7 Rise up, O Lord, in thy anger: and be thou exalted in the borders of my enemies. And arise, O Lord, my God, in the precept which thou hast commanded: ^psalm-07-7-douay

8 And a congregation of people shall surround thee. And for their sakes return thou on high. ^psalm-07-8-douay

9 The Lord judgeth the people. Judge me, O Lord, according to my justice, and according to my innocence in me. ^psalm-07-9-douay

10 The wickedness of sinners shall be brought to nought; and thou shalt direct the just: the searcher of hearts and reins is God. ^psalm-07-10-douay

11 Just is my help from the Lord; who saveth the upright of heart. ^psalm-07-11-douay

12 God is a just judge, strong and patient: is he angry every day? ^psalm-07-12-douay

13 Except you will be converted, he will brandish his sword; he hath bent his bow, and made it ready. ^psalm-07-13-douay

14 And in it he hath prepared to instruments of death, he hath made ready his arrows for them that burn. **For them that burn...** That is, against the persecutors of his saints. ^psalm-07-14-douay

15 Behold he hath been in labour with injustice: he hath conceived sorrow, and brought forth iniquity. ^psalm-07-15-douay

16 He hath opened a pit and dug it: and he is fallen into the hole he made. ^psalm-07-16-douay

17 His sorrow shall be turned on his own head: and his iniquity shall come down upon his crown. ^psalm-07-17-douay

18 I will give glory to the Lord according to his justice: and will sing to the name of the Lord the most high. ^psalm-07-18-douay
