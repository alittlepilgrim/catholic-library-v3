# Psalm 23

Who are they that shall ascend to heaven: Christ's triumphant ascension thither.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 24**, and the **[[psalm-22-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 23**.

1 On the first day of the week, a psalm for David. ^psalm-23-1-douay

The earth is the Lord's and the fulness thereof: the world, and all they that dwell therein.

2 For he hath founded it upon the seas; and hath prepared it upon the rivers. ^psalm-23-2-douay

3 Who shall ascend into the mountain of the Lord: or who shall stand in his holy place? ^psalm-23-3-douay

4 The innocent in hands, and clean of heart, who hath not taken his soul in vain, nor sworn deceitfully to his neighbour. ^psalm-23-4-douay

5 He shall receive a blessing from the Lord, and mercy from God his Saviour. ^psalm-23-5-douay

6 This is the generation of them that seek him, of them that seek the face of the God of Jacob. ^psalm-23-6-douay

7 Lift up your gates, O ye princes, and be ye lifted up, O eternal gates: and the King of Glory shall enter in. ^psalm-23-7-douay

8 Who is this King of Glory? the Lord who is strong and mighty: the Lord mighty in battle. ^psalm-23-8-douay

9 Lift up your gates, O ye princes, and be ye lifted up, O eternal gates: and the King of Glory shall enter in. ^psalm-23-9-douay

10 Who is this King of Glory? the Lord of hosts, he is the King of Glory. ^psalm-23-10-douay
