# Psalm 17

David's thanks to God for his delivery from all his enemies.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 18**, and the **[[psalm-16-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 17**.

1 Unto the end, for David, the servant of the Lord, who spoke to the Lord the words of this canticle, in the day that the Lord delivered him from the hand of all his enemies, and from the hand of Saul: and he said: ^psalm-17-1-douay

2 I will love thee, O Lord, my strength: ^psalm-17-2-douay

3 The Lord is my firmament, my refuge, and my deliverer. My God is my helper, and in him will I put my trust. My protector, and the horn of my salvation, and my support. ^psalm-17-3-douay

4 Praising, I will call upon the Lord: and I shall be saved from my enemies. ^psalm-17-4-douay

5 The sorrows of death surrounded me: and the torrents of iniquity troubled me. ^psalm-17-5-douay

6 The sorrows of hell encompassed me: and the snares of death prevented me. ^psalm-17-6-douay

7 In my affliction I called upon the Lord, and I cried to my God: And he heard my voice from his holy temple: and my cry before him came into his ears. ^psalm-17-7-douay

8 The earth shook and trembled: the foundations of the mountains were troubled and were moved, because he was angry with them. ^psalm-17-8-douay

9 There went up a smoke in his wrath: and a fire flamed from his face: coals were kindled by it. ^psalm-17-9-douay

10 He bowed the heavens, and came down, and darkness was under his feet. ^psalm-17-10-douay

11 And he ascended upon the cherubim, and he flew; he flew upon the wings of the winds. ^psalm-17-11-douay

12 And he made darkness his covert, his pavilion round about him: dark waters in the clouds of the air. ^psalm-17-12-douay

13 At the brightness that was before him the clouds passed, hail and coals of fire. ^psalm-17-13-douay

14 And the Lord thundered from heaven, and the Highest gave his voice: hail and coals of fire. ^psalm-17-14-douay

15 And he sent forth his arrows, and he scattered them: he multiplied lightnings, and troubled them. ^psalm-17-15-douay

16 Then the fountains of waters appeared, and the foundations of the world were discovered: At thy rebuke, O Lord, at the blast of the spirit of thy wrath. ^psalm-17-16-douay

17 He sent from on high, and took me: and received me out of many waters. ^psalm-17-17-douay

18 He delivered me from my strongest enemies, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me. ^psalm-17-18-douay

19 They prevented me in the day of my affliction: and the Lord became my protector. ^psalm-17-19-douay

20 And he brought me forth into a large place: he saved me, because he was well pleased with me. ^psalm-17-20-douay

21 And the Lord will reward me according to my justice; and will repay me according to the cleanness of my hands: ^psalm-17-21-douay

22 Because I have kept the ways of the Lord; and have not done wickedly against my God. ^psalm-17-22-douay

23 For all his judgments are in my sight: and his justices I have not put away from me. ^psalm-17-23-douay

24 And I shall be spotless with him: and shall keep myself from my iniquity. ^psalm-17-24-douay

25 And the Lord will reward me according to my justice: and according to the cleanness of my hands before his eyes. ^psalm-17-25-douay

26 With the holy thou wilt be holy; and with the innocent man thou wilt be innocent: ^psalm-17-26-douay

27 And with the elect thou wilt be elect: and with the perverse thou wilt be perverted. ^psalm-17-27-douay

28 For thou wilt save the humble people; but wilt bring down the eyes of the proud. ^psalm-17-28-douay

29 For thou lightest my lamp, O Lord: O my God, enlighten my darkness. ^psalm-17-29-douay

30 For by thee I shall be delivered from temptation; and through my God I shall go over a wall. ^psalm-17-30-douay

31 As for my God, his way is undefiled: the words of the Lord are fire-tried: he is the protector of all that trust in him. ^psalm-17-31-douay

32 For who is God but the Lord? or who is God but our God? ^psalm-17-32-douay

33 God, who hath girt me with strength; and made my way blameless. ^psalm-17-33-douay

34 Who hath made my feet like the feet of harts: and who setteth me upon high places. ^psalm-17-34-douay

35 Who teacheth my hands to war: and thou hast made my arms like a brazen bow. ^psalm-17-35-douay

36 And thou hast given me the protection of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath held me up: And thy discipline hath corrected me unto the end: and thy discipline, the same shall teach me. ^psalm-17-36-douay

37 Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; and my feet are not weakened. ^psalm-17-37-douay

38 I will pursue after my enemies, and overtake them: and I will not turn again till they are consumed. ^psalm-17-38-douay

39 I will break them, and they shall not be able to stand: they shall fall under my feet. ^psalm-17-39-douay

40 And thou hast girded me with strength unto battle; and hast subdued under me them that rose up against me. ^psalm-17-40-douay

41 And thou hast made my enemies turn their back upon me, and hast destroyed them that hated me. ^psalm-17-41-douay

42 They cried, but there was none to save them, to the Lord: but he heard them not. ^psalm-17-42-douay

43 And I shall beat them as small as the dust before the wind; I shall bring them to nought, like the dirt in the streets. ^psalm-17-43-douay

44 Thou wilt deliver me from the contradictions of the people; thou wilt make me head of the Gentiles. ^psalm-17-44-douay

45 A people which I knew not, hath served me: at the hearing of the ear they have obeyed me. ^psalm-17-45-douay

46 The children that are strangers have lied to me, strange children have faded away, and have halted from their paths. ^psalm-17-46-douay

47 The Lord liveth, and blessed by my God, and let the God of my salvation be exalted. ^psalm-17-47-douay

48 O God, who avengest me, and subduest the people under me, my deliverer from my enraged enemies. ^psalm-17-48-douay

49 And thou wilt lift me up above them that rise up against me: from the unjust man thou wilt deliver me. ^psalm-17-49-douay

50 Therefore will I give glory to thee, O Lord, among the nations, and I will sing a psalm to thy name. ^psalm-17-50-douay

51 Giving great deliverance to his king, and showing mercy to David, his anointed: and to his seed for ever. ^psalm-17-51-douay
