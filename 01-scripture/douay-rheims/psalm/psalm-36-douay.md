# Psalm 36

An exhortation to despise this world; and the short prosperity of the wicked; and to trust in Providence.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 37**, and the **[[psalm-35-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 36**.

1 Be not emulous of evildoers; nor envy them that work iniquity. ^psalm-36-1-douay

2 For they shall shortly wither away as grass, and as the green herbs shall quickly fall. ^psalm-36-2-douay

3 Trust in the Lord, and do good, and dwell in the land, and thou shalt be fed with its riches. ^psalm-36-3-douay

4 Delight in the Lord, and he will give thee the requests of thy heart. ^psalm-36-4-douay

5 Commit thy way to the Lord, and trust in him, and he will do it. ^psalm-36-5-douay

6 And he will bring forth thy justice as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday. ^psalm-36-6-douay

7 Be subject to the Lord and pray to him. Envy not the man who prospereth in his way; the man who doth unjust things. ^psalm-36-7-douay

8 Cease from anger, and leave rage; have no emulation to do evil. ^psalm-36-8-douay

9 For evildoers shall be cut off: but they that wait upon the Lord, they shall inherit the land. ^psalm-36-9-douay

10 For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: and thou shalt seek his place, and shalt not find it. ^psalm-36-10-douay

11 But the meek shall inherit the land, and shall delight in abundance of peace. ^psalm-36-11-douay

12 The sinner shall watch the just man: and shall gnash upon him with his teeth. ^psalm-36-12-douay

13 But the Lord shall laugh at him: for he foreseeth that his day shall come. ^psalm-36-13-douay

14 The wicked have drawn out the sword: they have bent their bow. To cast down the poor and needy, to kill the upright of heart. ^psalm-36-14-douay

15 Let their sword enter into their own hearts, and let their bow be broken. ^psalm-36-15-douay

16 Better is a little to the just, than the great riches of the wicked. ^psalm-36-16-douay

17 For the arms of the wicked shall be broken in pieces; but the Lord strengtheneth the just. ^psalm-36-17-douay

18 The Lord knoweth the days of the undefiled; and their inheritance shall be for ever. ^psalm-36-18-douay

19 They shall not be confounded in the evil time; and in the days of famine they shall be filled: ^psalm-36-19-douay

20 Because the wicked shall perish. And the enemies of the Lord, presently after they shall be honoured and exalted, shall come to nothing and vanish like smoke. ^psalm-36-20-douay

21 The sinner shall borrow, and not pay again; but the just showeth mercy and shall give. ^psalm-36-21-douay

22 For such as bless him shall inherit the land: but such as curse him shall perish. ^psalm-36-22-douay

23 With the Lord shall the steps of a man be directed, and he shall like well his way. ^psalm-36-23-douay

24 When he shall fall he shall not be bruised, for the Lord putteth his hand under him. ^psalm-36-24-douay

25 I have been young and now am old; and I have not seen the just forsaken, nor his seed seeking bread. ^psalm-36-25-douay

26 He showeth mercy, and lendeth all the day long; and his seed shall be in blessing. ^psalm-36-26-douay

27 Decline from evil and do good, and dwell for ever and ever. ^psalm-36-27-douay

28 For the Lord loveth judgment, and will not forsake his saints: they shall be preserved for ever. The unjust shall be punished, and the seed of the wicked shall perish. ^psalm-36-28-douay

29 But the just shall inherit the land, and shall dwell therein for evermore. ^psalm-36-29-douay

30 The mouth of the just shall meditate wisdom: and his tongue shall speak judgment. ^psalm-36-30-douay

31 The law of his God is in his heart, and his steps shall not be supplanted. ^psalm-36-31-douay

32 The wicked watcheth the just man, and seeketh to put him to death, ^psalm-36-32-douay

33 But the Lord will not leave him in his hands; nor condemn him when he shall be judged. ^psalm-36-33-douay

34 Expect the Lord and keep his way: and he will exalt thee to inherit the land: when the sinners shall perish thou shalt see. ^psalm-36-34-douay

35 I have seen the wicked highly exalted, and lifted up like the cedars of Libanus. ^psalm-36-35-douay

36 And I passed by, and lo, he was not: and I sought him and his place was not found. ^psalm-36-36-douay

37 Keep innocence, and behold justice: for there are remnants for the peaceable man. ^psalm-36-37-douay

38 But the unjust shall be destroyed together: the remnants of the wicked shall perish. ^psalm-36-38-douay

39 But the salvation of the just is from the Lord, and he is their protector in the time of trouble. ^psalm-36-39-douay

40 And the Lord will help them and deliver them: and he will rescue them from the wicked, and save them because they have hoped in him. ^psalm-36-40-douay
