# Psalm 124

The just are always under God's protection.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 125**, and the **[[psalm-123-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 124**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 They that trust in the Lord shall be as mount Sion: he shall not be moved for ever that dwelleth ^psalm-124-1-douay

2 In Jerusalem. Mountains are round about it: so the Lord is round about his people from henceforth now and for ever. ^psalm-124-2-douay

3 For the Lord will not leave the rod of sinners upon the lot of the just: that the just may not stretch forth their hands to iniquity. ^psalm-124-3-douay

4 Do good, O Lord, to those that are good, and to the upright of heart. ^psalm-124-4-douay

5 But such as turn aside into bonds, the Lord shall lead out with the workers of iniquity: peace upon Israel. ^psalm-124-5-douay
