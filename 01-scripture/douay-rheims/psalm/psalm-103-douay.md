# Psalm 103

God is to be praised for his mighty works, and wonderful providence.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 104**, and the **[[psalm-102-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 103**.

1 For David himself. ^psalm-103-1-douay

Bless the Lord, O my soul: O Lord my God, thou art exceedingly great. Thou hast put on praise and beauty:

2 And art clothed with light as with a garment. Who stretchest out the heaven like a pavilion: ^psalm-103-2-douay

3 Who coverest the higher rooms thereof with water. Who makest the clouds thy chariot: who walkest upon the wings of the winds. ^psalm-103-3-douay

4 Who makest thy angels spirits: and thy ministers a burning fire. ^psalm-103-4-douay

5 Who hast founded the earth upon its own bases: it shall not be moved for ever and ever. ^psalm-103-5-douay

6 The deep like a garment is its clothing: above the mountains shall the waters stand. ^psalm-103-6-douay

7 At thy rebuke they shall flee: at the voice of thy thunder they shall fear. ^psalm-103-7-douay

8 The mountains ascend, and the plains descend into the place which thou hast founded for them. ^psalm-103-8-douay

9 Thou hast set a bound which they shall not pass over; neither shall they return to cover the earth. ^psalm-103-9-douay

10 Thou sendest forth springs in the vales: between the midst of the hills the waters shall pass. ^psalm-103-10-douay

11 All the beasts of the field shall drink: the wild asses shall expect in their thirst. ^psalm-103-11-douay

12 Over them the birds of the air shall dwell: from the midst of the rocks they shall give forth their voices. ^psalm-103-12-douay

13 Thou waterest the hills from thy upper rooms: the earth shall be filled with the fruit of thy works: ^psalm-103-13-douay

14 Bringing forth grass for cattle, and herb for the service of men. That thou mayst bring bread out of the earth: ^psalm-103-14-douay

15 And that wine may cheer the heart of man. That he may make the face cheerful with oil: and that bread may strengthen man's heart. ^psalm-103-15-douay

16 The trees of the field shall be filled, and the cedars of Libanus which he hath planted: ^psalm-103-16-douay

17 There the sparrows shall make their nests. The highest of them is the house of the heron. ^psalm-103-17-douay

18 The high hills are a refuge for the harts, the rock for the irchins. ^psalm-103-18-douay

19 He hath made the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down. ^psalm-103-19-douay

20 Thou hast appointed darkness, and it is night: in it shall all the beasts of the woods go about: ^psalm-103-20-douay

21 The young lions roaring after their prey, and seeking their meat from God. ^psalm-103-21-douay

22 The sun ariseth, and they are gathered together: and they shall lie down in their dens. ^psalm-103-22-douay

23 Man shall go forth to his work, and to his labour until the evening. ^psalm-103-23-douay

24 How great are thy works, O Lord? thou hast made all things in wisdom: the earth is filled with thy riches. ^psalm-103-24-douay

25 So is this great sea, which stretcheth wide its arms: there are creeping things without number: Creatures little and great. ^psalm-103-25-douay

26 There the ships shall go. This sea dragon which thou hast formed to play therein. ^psalm-103-26-douay

27 All expect of thee that thou give them food in season. ^psalm-103-27-douay

28 What thou givest to them they shall gather up: when thou openest thy hand, they shall all be filled with good. ^psalm-103-28-douay

29 But if thou turnest away thy face, they shall be troubled: thou shalt take away their breath, and they shall fail, and shall return to their dust. ^psalm-103-29-douay

30 Thou shalt send forth thy spirit, and they shall be created: and thou shalt renew the face of the earth. ^psalm-103-30-douay

31 May the glory of the Lord endure for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works. ^psalm-103-31-douay

32 He looketh upon the earth, and maketh it tremble: he troubleth the mountains, and they smoke. ^psalm-103-32-douay

33 I will sing to the Lord as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being. ^psalm-103-33-douay

34 Let my speech be acceptable to him: but I will take delight in the Lord. ^psalm-103-34-douay

35 Let sinners be consumed out of the earth, and the unjust, so that they be no more: O my soul, bless thou the Lord. ^psalm-103-35-douay
