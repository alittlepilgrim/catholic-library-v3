# Psalm 122

A prayer in affliction, with confidence in God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 123**, and the **[[psalm-121-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 122**.

*A gradual canticle.*

1 To thee have I lifted up my eyes, who dwellest in heaven. ^psalm-122-1-douay

2 Behold as the eyes of servants are on the hands of their masters, As the eyes of the handmaid are on the hands of her mistress: so are our eyes unto the Lord our God, until he have mercy on us. ^psalm-122-2-douay

3 Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us: for we are greatly filled with contempt. ^psalm-122-3-douay

4 For our soul is greatly filled: we are a reproach to the rich, and contempt to the proud. ^psalm-122-4-douay
