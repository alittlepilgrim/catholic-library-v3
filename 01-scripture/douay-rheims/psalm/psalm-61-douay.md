# Psalm 61

The prophet encourages himself and all others to trust in God, and serve him.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 62**, and the **[[psalm-60-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 61**.

1 Unto the end, for Idithun, a psalm of David. ^psalm-61-1-douay

2 Shall not my soul be subject to God? for from him is my salvation. ^psalm-61-2-douay

3 For he is my God and my saviour: he is my protector, I shall be moved no more. ^psalm-61-3-douay

4 How long do you rush in upon a man? you all kill, as if you were thrusting down a leaning wall, and a tottering fence. ^psalm-61-4-douay

5 But they have thought to cast away my price; I ran in thirst: they blessed with their mouth, but cursed with their heart. ^psalm-61-5-douay

6 But be thou, O my soul, subject to God: for from him is my patience. ^psalm-61-6-douay

7 For he is my God and my saviour: he is my helper, I shall not be moved. ^psalm-61-7-douay

8 In God is my salvation and my glory: he is the God of my help, and my hope is in God. ^psalm-61-8-douay

9 Trust in him, all ye congregation of people: pour out your hearts before him. God is our helper for ever. ^psalm-61-9-douay

10 But vain are the sons of men, the sons of men are liars in the balances: that by vanity they may together deceive. **Are liars in the balances, etc...** They are so vain and light, that if they are put into the scales, they will be found to be of no weight; and to be mere lies, deceit, and vanity. Or, They are liars in their balances, by weighing things by false weights, and preferring the temporal before the eternal. ^psalm-61-10-douay

11 Trust not in iniquity, and cover not robberies: if riches abound, set not your heart upon them. ^psalm-61-11-douay

12 God hath spoken once, these two things have I heard, that power belongeth to God, ^psalm-61-12-douay

13 And mercy to thee, O Lord; for thou wilt render to every man according to his works. ^psalm-61-13-douay
