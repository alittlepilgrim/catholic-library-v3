# Psalm 135

God is to be praised for his wonderful works.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 136**, and the **[[psalm-134-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 135**.

1 Alleluia. Praise the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. **Praise the Lord...** By this invitation to praise the Lord, thrice repeated, we profess the Blessed Trinity, One God in three distinct Persons, the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Ghost. ^psalm-135-1-douay

2 Praise ye the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-2-douay

3 Praise ye the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-3-douay

4 Who alone doth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-4-douay

5 Who made the heavens in understanding: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-5-douay

6 Who established the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-6-douay

7 Who made the great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-7-douay

8 The sun to rule the day: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-8-douay

9 The moon and the stars to rule the night: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-9-douay

10 Who smote Egypt with their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-10-douay

11 Who brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-11-douay

12 With a mighty hand and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-12-douay

13 Who divided the Red Sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-13-douay

14 And brought out Israel through the midst thereof: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-14-douay

15 And overthrew Pharao and his host in the Red Sea: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-15-douay

16 Who led his people through the desert: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-16-douay

17 Who smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-17-douay

18 And slew strong kings: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-18-douay

19 Sehon king of the Amorrhites: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-19-douay

20 And Og king of Basan: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-20-douay

21 And he gave their land for an inheritance: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-21-douay

22 For an inheritance to his servant Israel: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-22-douay

23 For he was mindful of us in our affliction: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-23-douay

24 And he redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-24-douay

25 Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-25-douay

26 Give glory to the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-26-douay


27 Give glory to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^psalm-135-27-douay
