# Psalm 113

God has shown his power in delivering his people: idols are vain. The Hebrews divide this into two psalms.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in many popular Bible translations, this psalm is split into two parts, called **Psalm 114** and **Psalm 115**.

*Alleluia.*

1 When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a barbarous people: ^psalm-113-1-douay

2 Judea was made his sanctuary, Israel his dominion. ^psalm-113-2-douay

3 The sea saw and fled: Jordan was turned back. ^psalm-113-3-douay

4 The mountains skipped like rams, and the hills like the lambs of the flock. ^psalm-113-4-douay

5 What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou didst flee: and thou, O Jordan, that thou wast turned back? ^psalm-113-5-douay

6 Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams, and ye hills, like lambs of the flock? ^psalm-113-6-douay

7 At the presence of the Lord the earth was moved, at the presence of the God of Jacob: ^psalm-113-7-douay

8 Who turned the rock into pools of water, and the stony hill into fountains of waters. ^psalm-113-8-douay

9 Not to us, O Lord, not to us; but to thy name give glory. ^psalm-113-9-douay

10 For thy mercy, and for thy truth's sake: lest the Gentiles should say: Where is their God? ^psalm-113-10-douay

11 But our God is in heaven: he hath done all things whatsoever he would. ^psalm-113-11-douay

12 The idols of the Gentiles are silver and gold, the works of the hands of men. ^psalm-113-12-douay

13 They have mouths and speak not: they have eyes and see not. ^psalm-113-13-douay

14 They have ears and hear not: they have noses and smell not. ^psalm-113-14-douay

15 They have hands and feel not: they have feet and walk not: neither shall they cry out through their throat. ^psalm-113-15-douay

16 Let them that make them become like unto them: and all such as trust in them. ^psalm-113-16-douay

17 The house of Israel hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector. ^psalm-113-17-douay

18 The house of Aaron hath hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector. ^psalm-113-18-douay

19 They that fear the Lord have hoped in the Lord: he is their helper and their protector. ^psalm-113-19-douay

20 The Lord hath been mindful of us, and hath blessed us. He hath blessed the house of Israel: he hath blessed the house of Aaron. ^psalm-113-20-douay

21 He hath blessed all that fear the Lord, both little and great. ^psalm-113-21-douay

22 May the Lord add blessings upon you: upon you, and upon your children. ^psalm-113-22-douay

23 Blessed be you of the Lord, who made heaven and earth. ^psalm-113-23-douay

24 The heaven of heaven is the Lord's: but the earth he has given to the children of men. ^psalm-113-24-douay

25 The dead shall not praise thee, O Lord: nor any of them that go down to hell. ^psalm-113-25-douay

26 But we that live bless the Lord: from this time now and for ever. ^psalm-113-26-douay
