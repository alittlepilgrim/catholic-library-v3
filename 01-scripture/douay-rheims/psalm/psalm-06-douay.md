# Psalm 6

1 Unto the end, in verses, a psalm for David, for the octave. **For the octave...** That is, to be sung on an instrument of eight strings. St. Augustine understands it mystically, of the last resurrection, and the world to come; which is, as it were, the octave, or eighth day, after the seven days of this mortal life: and for this octave, sinners must dispose themselves, like David, by bewailing their sins, whilst they are here upon earth. ^psalm-06-1-douay

2 O Lord, rebuke me not in thy indignation, nor chastise me in thy wrath. ^psalm-06-2-douay

3 Have mercy on me, O Lord, for I am weak: heal me, O Lord, for my bones are troubled. ^psalm-06-3-douay

4 And my soul is troubled exceedingly: but thou, O Lord, how long? ^psalm-06-4-douay

5 Turn to me, O Lord, and deliver my soul: O save me for thy mercy's sake. ^psalm-06-5-douay

6 For there is no one in death, that is mindful of thee: and who shall confess to thee in hell? ^psalm-06-6-douay

7 I have laboured in my groanings, every night I will wash my bed: I will water my couch with my tears. ^psalm-06-7-douay

8 My eye is troubled through indignation: I have grown old amongst all my enemies. ^psalm-06-8-douay

9 Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity: for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping. ^psalm-06-9-douay

10 The Lord hath heard my supplication: the Lord hath received my prayer. ^psalm-06-10-douay

11 Let all my enemies be ashamed, and be very much troubled: let them be turned back, and be ashamed very speedily. ^psalm-06-11-douay
