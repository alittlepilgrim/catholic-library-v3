# Psalm 144

A psalm of praise, to the infinite majesty of God.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 145**, and the **[[psalm-143-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 144**.

1 Praise, for David himself. ^psalm-144-1-douay

I will extol thee, O God my king: and I will bless thy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever.

2 Every day will I bless thee: and I will praise thy name for ever; yea, for ever and ever. ^psalm-144-2-douay

3 Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised: and of his greatness there is no end. ^psalm-144-3-douay

4 Generation and generation shall praise thy works: and they shall declare thy power. ^psalm-144-4-douay

5 They shall speak of the magnificence of the glory of thy holiness: and shall tell thy wondrous works. ^psalm-144-5-douay

6 And they shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and shall declare thy greatness. ^psalm-144-6-douay

7 They shall publish the memory of the abundance of thy sweetness: and shall rejoice in thy justice. ^psalm-144-7-douay

8 The Lord is gracious and merciful: patient and plenteous in mercy. ^psalm-144-8-douay

9 The Lord is sweet to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works. ^psalm-144-9-douay

10 Let all thy works, O lord, praise thee: and let thy saints bless thee. ^psalm-144-10-douay

11 They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom: and shall tell of thy power: ^psalm-144-11-douay

12 To make thy might known to the sons of men: and the glory of the magnificence of thy kingdom. ^psalm-144-12-douay

13 Thy kingdom is a kingdom of all ages: and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations. The Lord is faithful in all his words: and holy in all his works. ^psalm-144-13-douay

14 The Lord lifteth up all that fall: and setteth up all that are cast down. ^psalm-144-14-douay

15 The eyes of all hope in thee, O Lord: and thou givest them meat in due season. ^psalm-144-15-douay

16 Thou openest thy hand, and fillest with blessing every living creature. ^psalm-144-16-douay

17 The Lord is just in all his ways: and holy in all his works. ^psalm-144-17-douay

18 The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him: to all that call upon him in truth. ^psalm-144-18-douay

19 He will do the will of them that fear him: and he will hear their prayer, and save them. ^psalm-144-19-douay

20 The Lord keepeth all them that love him; but all the wicked he will destroy. ^psalm-144-20-douay

21 My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name forever; yea, for ever and ever. ^psalm-144-21-douay
