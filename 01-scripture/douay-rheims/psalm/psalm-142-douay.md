# Psalm 142

The psalmist in tribulation calls upon God for his delivery. The seventh penitential psalm.

Does this psalm differ from what you were expecting? Don't be confused. There are two systems for arranging the psalms: the **Septuagint** and the **Masoretic**. We use the Septuagint system here. In the Masoretic system, which is used in most modern Bible translations, this psalm is called **Psalm 143**, and the **[[psalm-141-douay|previous psalm]]** is known as **Psalm 142**.

1 A psalm of David, when his son Absalom pursued him. [[2-samuel-17-douay|2 Samuel 17]]] ^psalm-142-1-douay

Hear, O Lord, my prayer: give ear to my supplication in thy truth: hear me in thy justice.

2 And enter not into judgment with thy servant: for in thy sight no man living shall be justified. ^psalm-142-2-douay

3 For the enemy hath persecuted my soul: he hath brought down my life to the earth. He hath made me to dwell in darkness as those that have been dead of old: ^psalm-142-3-douay

4 And my spirit is in anguish within me: my heart within me is troubled. ^psalm-142-4-douay

5 I remembered the days of old, I meditated on all thy works: I meditated upon the works of thy hands. ^psalm-142-5-douay

6 I stretched forth my hands to thee: my soul is as earth without water unto thee. ^psalm-142-6-douay

7 Hear me speedily, O Lord: my spirit hath fainted away. Turn not away thy face from me, lest I be like unto them that go down into the pit. ^psalm-142-7-douay

8 Cause me to hear thy mercy in the morning; for in thee have I hoped. Make the way known to me, wherein I should walk: for I have lifted up my soul to thee. ^psalm-142-8-douay

9 Deliver me from my enemies, O Lord, to thee have I fled: ^psalm-142-9-douay

10 Teach me to do thy will, for thou art my God. Thy good spirit shall lead me into the right land: ^psalm-142-10-douay

11 For thy name's sake, O Lord, thou wilt quicken me in thy justice. Thou wilt bring my soul out of trouble: ^psalm-142-11-douay

12 And in thy mercy thou wilt destroy my enemies. And thou wilt cut off all them that afflict my soul: for I am thy servant. ^psalm-142-12-douay
