# 2 Timothy Chapter 2

He exhorts him to diligence in his office and patience in suffering. The danger of the delusions of heretics.

1 Thou therefore, my son, be strong in Christ Jesus: ^2-timothy-02-1-douay

2 And the things which thou hast heard of me by many witnesses, the same commend to faithful men who shall be fit to teach others also. ^2-timothy-02-2-douay

3 Labour as a good soldier of Christ Jesus. ^2-timothy-02-3-douay

4 No man, being a soldier to God, entangleth himself with secular businesses: that he may please him to whom he hath engaged himself. ^2-timothy-02-4-douay

5 For he also that striveth for the mastery is not crowned, except he strive lawfully. ^2-timothy-02-5-douay

6 The husbandman that laboureth must first partake of the fruits. ^2-timothy-02-6-douay

7 Understand what I say: for the Lord will give thee in all things understanding. ^2-timothy-02-7-douay

8 Be mindful that the Lord Jesus Christ is risen again from the dead, of the seed of David, according to my gospel: ^2-timothy-02-8-douay

9 Wherein I labour even unto bands, as an evildoer. But the word of God is not bound. ^2-timothy-02-9-douay

10 Therefore I endure all things for the sake of the elect, that they also may obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus, with heavenly glory. ^2-timothy-02-10-douay

11 A faithful saying: for if we be dead with him, we shall live also with him. ^2-timothy-02-11-douay

12 If we suffer, we shall also reign with him. If we deny him, he will also deny us. ^2-timothy-02-12-douay

13 If we believe not, he continueth faithful, he cannot deny himself. ^2-timothy-02-13-douay

14 Of these things put them in mind, charging them before the Lord. Contend not in words: for it is to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers. ^2-timothy-02-14-douay

15 Carefully study to present thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly handling the word of truth. ^2-timothy-02-15-douay

16 But shun profane and vain babblings: for they grow much towards ungodliness. ^2-timothy-02-16-douay

17 And their speech spreadeth like a canker: of whom are Hymeneus and Philetus: ^2-timothy-02-17-douay

18 Who have erred from the truth, saying that the resurrection is past already, and have subverted the faith of some. ^2-timothy-02-18-douay

19 But the sure foundation of God standeth firm, having this seal: the Lord knoweth who are his; and let every one depart from iniquity who nameth the name of the Lord. ^2-timothy-02-19-douay

20 But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth: and some indeed unto honour, but some unto dishonour. ^2-timothy-02-20-douay

21 If any man therefore shall cleanse himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified and profitable to the Lord, prepared unto every good work. ^2-timothy-02-21-douay

22 But flee thou youthful desires, and pursue justice, faith, charity and peace with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart. ^2-timothy-02-22-douay

23 And avoid foolish and unlearned questions, knowing that they beget strifes. ^2-timothy-02-23-douay

24 But the servant of the Lord must not wrangle: but be mild toward all men, apt to teach, patient, ^2-timothy-02-24-douay

25 With modesty admonishing them that resist the truth: if peradventure God may give them repentance to know the truth; ^2-timothy-02-25-douay

26 And they may recover themselves from the snares of the devil by whom they are held captive at his will. ^2-timothy-02-26-douay
