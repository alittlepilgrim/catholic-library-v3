# 2 Corinthians Chapter 6

He exhorts them to a correspondence with God's grace and not to associate with unbelievers.

1 And we helping do exhort you that you receive not the grace of God in vain. ^2-corinthians-06-1-douay

2 For he saith: In an accepted time have I heard thee and in the day of salvation have I helped thee. Behold, now is the acceptable time: behold, now is the day of salvation. ^2-corinthians-06-2-douay

3 Giving no offence to any man, that our ministry be not blamed. ^2-corinthians-06-3-douay

4 But in all things let us exhibit ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in tribulation, in necessities, in distresses, ^2-corinthians-06-4-douay

5 In stripes, in prisons, in seditions, in labours, in watchings, in fastings, ^2-corinthians-06-5-douay

6 In chastity, in knowledge, in longsuffering, in sweetness, in the Holy Ghost, in charity unfeigned, ^2-corinthians-06-6-douay

7 In the word of truth, in the power of God: by the armour of justice on the right hand and on the left: ^2-corinthians-06-7-douay

8 By honour and dishonour: by evil report and good report: as deceivers and yet true: as unknown and yet known: ^2-corinthians-06-8-douay

9 As dying and behold we live: as chastised and not killed: ^2-corinthians-06-9-douay

10 As sorrowful, yet always rejoicing: as needy, yet enriching many: as having nothing and possessing all things. ^2-corinthians-06-10-douay

11 Our mouth is open to you, O ye Corinthians: our heart is enlarged. ^2-corinthians-06-11-douay

12 You are not straitened in us: but in your own bowels you are straitened. ^2-corinthians-06-12-douay

13 But having the same recompense (I speak as to my children): be you also enlarged. ^2-corinthians-06-13-douay

14 Bear not the yoke with unbelievers. For what participation hath justice with injustice? Or what fellowship hath light with darkness? ^2-corinthians-06-14-douay

15 And what concord hath Christ with Belial? Or what part hath the faithful with the unbeliever? ^2-corinthians-06-15-douay

16 And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? For you are the temple of the living God: as God saith: ^2-corinthians-06-16-douay

I will dwell in them and walk among them. And I will be their God: and they shall be my people.

17 Wherefore: Go out from among them and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing: ^2-corinthians-06-17-douay

18 And I will receive you. And will be a Father to you: and you shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty. ^2-corinthians-06-18-douay
