# 2 Corinthians Chapter 10

To stop the calumny and boasting of false apostles, he set forth the power of his apostleship.

1 Now I Paul, myself beseech you, by the mildness and modesty of Christ: who in presence indeed am lowly among you, but being absent am bold toward you. ^2-corinthians-10-1-douay

2 But I beseech you, that I may not be bold when I am present with that confidence wherewith I am thought to be bold, against some who reckon us as if we walked according to the flesh. ^2-corinthians-10-2-douay

3 For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war according to the flesh. ^2-corinthians-10-3-douay

4 For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal but mighty to God, unto the pulling down of fortifications, destroying counsels, ^2-corinthians-10-4-douay

5 And every height that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God: and bringing into captivity every understanding unto the obedience of Christ: ^2-corinthians-10-5-douay

6 And having in readiness to revenge all disobedience, when your obedience shall be fulfilled. ^2-corinthians-10-6-douay

7 See the things that are according to outward appearance. If any man trust to himself, that he is Christ's let him think this again with himself, that as he is Christ's, so are we also. ^2-corinthians-10-7-douay

8 For if also I should boast somewhat more of our power, which the Lord hath given us unto edification and not for your destruction, I should not be ashamed. ^2-corinthians-10-8-douay

9 But that I may not be thought as it were to terrify you by epistles, ^2-corinthians-10-9-douay

10 (For his epistles indeed, say they, are weighty and strong; but his bodily presence is weak and his speech contemptible): ^2-corinthians-10-10-douay

11 Let such a one think this, that such as we are in word by epistles when absent, such also we will be indeed when present. ^2-corinthians-10-11-douay

12 For we dare not match or compare ourselves with some that commend themselves: but we measure ourselves by ourselves and compare ourselves with ourselves. ^2-corinthians-10-12-douay

13 But we will not glory beyond our measure: but according to the measure of the rule which God hath measured to us, a measure to reach even unto you. ^2-corinthians-10-13-douay

14 For we stretch not ourselves beyond our measure, as if we reached not unto you. For we are come as far as to you in the Gospel of Christ. ^2-corinthians-10-14-douay

15 Not glorying beyond measure in other men's labours: but having hope of your increasing faith, to be magnified in you according to our rule abundantly. ^2-corinthians-10-15-douay

16 Yea, unto those places that are beyond you to preach the gospel: not to glory in another man's rule, in those things that are made ready to our hand. ^2-corinthians-10-16-douay

17 But he that glorieth, let him glory in the Lord. ^2-corinthians-10-17-douay

18 For not he who commendeth himself is approved: but he, whom God commendeth. ^2-corinthians-10-18-douay
