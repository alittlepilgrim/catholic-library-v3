# Micah Chapter 6

God expostulates with the Jews for their ingratitude and sins: for which they shall be punished.

1 Hear ye what the Lord saith: Arise, contend thou in judgment against the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice. **The mountains, etc...** That is, the great ones, the princes of the people. ^micah-06-1-douay

2 Let the mountains hear the judgment of the Lord, and the strong foundations of the earth: for the Lord will enter into judgment with his people, and he will plead against Israel. ^micah-06-2-douay

3 O my people, what have I done to thee, or in what have I molested thee? answer thou me. ^micah-06-3-douay

4 For I brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and delivered thee out of the house of slaves: and I sent before thy face Moses, and Aaron, and Mary. ^micah-06-4-douay

5 O my people, remember, I pray thee, what Balach, the king of Moab, purposed: and what Balaam, the son of Beor, answered him, from Setim to Galgal, that thou mightest know the justice of the Lord. **From Setim to Galgal...** He puts them in mind of the favour he did them, in not suffering them to be quite destroyed by the evil purpose of Balach, and the wicked counsel of Balaam: and then gives them a hint of the wonders he wrought, in order to bring them into the land of Promise, by stopping the course of the Jordan, in their march from Setim to Galgal. ^micah-06-5-douay

6 What shall I offer to the Lord that is worthy? wherewith shall I kneel before the high God? shall I offer holocausts unto him, and calves of a year old? **What shall I offer, etc...** This is spoken in the person of the people, desiring to be informed what they are to do to please God. ^micah-06-6-douay

7 May the Lord be appeased with thousands of rams, or with many thousands of fat he goats? shall I give my firstborn for my wickedness, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul? ^micah-06-7-douay

8 I will show thee, O man, what is good, and what the Lord requireth of thee: Verily to do judgment, and to love mercy, and to walk solicitous with thy God. ^micah-06-8-douay

9 The voice of the Lord crieth to the city, and salvation shall be to them that fear thy name: hear O ye tribes, and who shall approve it? ^micah-06-9-douay

10 As yet there is a fire in the house of the wicked, the treasures of iniquity, and a scant measure full of wrath. **Full of wrath, etc...** That is, highly provoking in the sight of God. ^micah-06-10-douay

11 Shall I justify wicked balances, and the deceitful weights of the bag? ^micah-06-11-douay

12 By which her rich men were filled with iniquity, and the inhabitants thereof have spoken lies, and their tongue was deceitful in their mouth. ^micah-06-12-douay

13 And I therefore began to strike thee with desolation for thy sins. ^micah-06-13-douay

14 Thou shalt eat, but shalt not be filled: and thy humiliation shall be in the midst of thee: and thou shalt take hold, but shalt not save: and those whom thou shalt save, I will give up to the sword. ^micah-06-14-douay

15 Thou shalt sow, but shalt not reap: thou shalt tread the olives, but shalt not be anointed with the oil: and the new wine, but shalt not drink the wine. ^micah-06-15-douay

16 For thou hast kept the statutes of Amri, and all the works of the house of Achab: and thou hast walked according to their wills, that I should make thee a desolation, and the inhabitants thereof a hissing, and you shall bear the reproach of my people. **The statutes of Amri, etc...** The wicked ways of Amri and Achab, idolatrous kings. ^micah-06-16-douay
