# Wisdom Chapter 12

God's wisdom and mercy in his proceedings with the Canaanites.

1 O how good and sweet is thy Spirit, O Lord, in all things! ^wisdom-12-1-douay

2 And therefore thou chastisest them that err, by little and little: and admonishest them, and speakest to them, concerning the things wherein they offend: that leaving their wickedness, they may believe in thee, O Lord. ^wisdom-12-2-douay

3 For those ancient inhabitants of thy holy land, whom thou didst abhor, ^wisdom-12-3-douay

4 Because they did works hateful to thee by their sorceries, and wicked sacrifices, ^wisdom-12-4-douay

5 And those merciless murderers of their own children, and eaters of men's bowels, and devourers of blood from the midst of thy consecration, **From the midst of thy consecration...** Literally, sacrament. That is, the land sacred to thee, in which thy temple was to be established, and man's redemption to be wrought. ^wisdom-12-5-douay

6 And those parents sacrificing with their own hands helpless souls, it was thy will to destroy by the hands of our parents, ^wisdom-12-6-douay

7 That the land which of all is most dear to thee, might receive a worthy colony of the children of God. ^wisdom-12-7-douay

8 Yet even those thou sparedst as men, and didst send wasps forerunners of thy host, to destroy them by little and little. ^wisdom-12-8-douay

9 Not that thou wast unable to bring the wicked under the just by war, or by cruel beasts, or with one rough word to destroy them at once: ^wisdom-12-9-douay

10 But executing thy judgments by degrees, thou gavest them place of repentance, not being ignorant that they were a wicked generation, and their malice natural, and that their thought could never be changed. ^wisdom-12-10-douay

11 For it was a cursed seed from the beginning: neither didst thou for fear of any one give pardon to their sins. ^wisdom-12-11-douay

12 For who shall say to thee: What hast thou done? or who shall withstand thy judgment? or who shall come before thee to be a revenger of wicked men? or who shall accuse thee, if the nations perish, which thou hast made? ^wisdom-12-12-douay

13 For there is no other God but thou, who hast care of all, that thou shouldst show that thou dost not give judgment unjustly. ^wisdom-12-13-douay

14 Neither shall king, nor tyrant, in thy sight inquire about them whom thou hast destroyed. ^wisdom-12-14-douay

15 For so much then, as thou art just, thou orderest all things justly: thinking it not agreeable to the power, to condemn him who deserveth not to be punished. ^wisdom-12-15-douay

16 For thy power is the beginning of justice: and because thou art Lord of all, thou makest thyself gracious to all. ^wisdom-12-16-douay

17 For thou showest thy power, when men will not believe thee to be absolute in power, and thou convincest the boldness of them that know thee not. ^wisdom-12-17-douay

18 But thou being master of power, judgest with tranquillity, and with great favour disposest of us: for thy power is at hand when thou wilt. ^wisdom-12-18-douay

19 But thou hast taught thy people by such works, that they must be just and humane, and hast made thy children to be of a good hope: because in judging, thou givest place for repentance for sins. ^wisdom-12-19-douay

20 For if thou didst punish the enemies of thy servants, and that deserved to die, with so great deliberation, giving them time and place whereby they might be changed from their wickedness: ^wisdom-12-20-douay

21 With what circumspection hast thou judged thy own children, to whose parents thou hast sworn, and made covenants of good promises? ^wisdom-12-21-douay

22 Therefore whereas thou chastisest us, thou scourgest our enemies very many ways, to the end that when we judge we may think on thy goodness: and when we are judged, we may hope for thy mercy. ^wisdom-12-22-douay

23 Wherefore thou hast also greatly tormented them, who, in their life, have lived foolishly and unjustly, by the same things which they worshipped. ^wisdom-12-23-douay

24 For they went astray for a long time in the ways of error, holding those things for gods which are the most worthless among beasts, living after the manner of children without understanding. ^wisdom-12-24-douay

25 Therefore thou hast sent a judgment upon them, as senseless children, to mock them. ^wisdom-12-25-douay

26 But they that were not amended by mockeries and reprehensions, experienced the worthy judgment of God. ^wisdom-12-26-douay

27 For seeing, with indignation, that they suffered by those very things which they took for gods, when they were destroyed by the same, they acknowledged him the true God, whom in time past they denied that they knew: for which cause the end also of their condemnation came upon them. ^wisdom-12-27-douay
