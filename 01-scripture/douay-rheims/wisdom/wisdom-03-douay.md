# Wisdom Chapter 3

The happiness of the just: and the unhappiness of the wicked.

1 But the souls of the just are in the hand of God, and the torment of death shall not touch them. ^wisdom-03-1-douay

2 In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure was taken for misery: ^wisdom-03-2-douay

3 And their going away from us, for utter destruction: but they are in peace. ^wisdom-03-3-douay

4 And though in the sight of men they suffered torments, their hope is full of immortality. ^wisdom-03-4-douay

5 Afflicted in few things, in many they shall be well rewarded: because God hath tried them, and found them worthy of himself. ^wisdom-03-5-douay

6 As gold in the furnace, he hath proved them, and as a victim of a holocaust, he hath received them, and in time there shall be respect had to them. ^wisdom-03-6-douay

7 The just shall shine, and shall run to and fro like sparks among the reeds. ^wisdom-03-7-douay

8 They shall judge nations, and rule over people, and their Lord shall reign for ever. ^wisdom-03-8-douay

9 They that trust in him shall understand the truth: and they that are faithful in love, shall rest in him: for grace and peace are to his elect. ^wisdom-03-9-douay

10 But the wicked shall be punished according to their own devices: who have neglected the just, and have revolted from the Lord. ^wisdom-03-10-douay

11 For he that rejecteth wisdom, and discipline, is unhappy: and their hope is vain, and their labours without fruit, and their works unprofitable. ^wisdom-03-11-douay

12 Their wives are foolish, and their children wicked. ^wisdom-03-12-douay

13 Their offspring is cursed, for happy is the barren: and the undefiled, that hath not known bed in sin, she shall have fruit in the visitation of holy souls. ^wisdom-03-13-douay

14 And the eunuch, that hath not wrought iniquity with his hands, nor thought wicked things against God for the precious gift of faith shall be given to him, and a most acceptable lot in the temple of God. ^wisdom-03-14-douay

15 For the fruit of good labours is glorious, and the root of wisdom never faileth. ^wisdom-03-15-douay

16 But the children of adulterers shall not come to perfection, and the seed of the unlawful bed shall be rooted out. ^wisdom-03-16-douay

17 And if they live long, they shall be nothing regarded, and their last old age shall be without honour. ^wisdom-03-17-douay

18 And if they die quickly, they shall have no hope, nor speech of comfort in the day of trial. ^wisdom-03-18-douay

19 For dreadful are the ends of a wicked race. ^wisdom-03-19-douay
