# Wisdom Chapter 7

The excellence of wisdom: how she is to be found.

1 I myself am a mortal man, like all others, and of the race of him, that was first made of the earth, and in the womb of my mother I was fashioned to be flesh. ^wisdom-07-1-douay

2 In the time of ten months I was compacted in blood, of the seed of man, and the pleasure of sleep concurring. ^wisdom-07-2-douay

3 And being born, I drew in the common air, and fell upon the earth, that is made alike, and the first voice which I uttered was crying, as all others do. ^wisdom-07-3-douay

4 I was nursed in swaddling clothes, and with great cares. ^wisdom-07-4-douay

5 For none of the kings had any other beginning of birth. ^wisdom-07-5-douay

6 For all men have one entrance into life, and the like going out. ^wisdom-07-6-douay

7 Wherefore I wished, and understanding was given me: and I called upon God, and the spirit of wisdom came upon me: ^wisdom-07-7-douay

8 And I preferred her before kingdoms and thrones, and esteemed riches nothing in comparison of her. ^wisdom-07-8-douay

9 Neither did I compare unto her any precious stone: for all gold, in comparison of her, is as a little sand; and silver, in respect to her, shall be counted as clay. ^wisdom-07-9-douay

10 I loved her above health and beauty, and chose to have her instead of light: for her light cannot be put out. ^wisdom-07-10-douay

11 Now all good things came to me together with her, and innumerable riches through her hands, ^wisdom-07-11-douay

12 And I rejoiced in all these: for this wisdom went before me, and I knew not that she was the mother of them all. ^wisdom-07-12-douay

13 Which I have learned without guile, and communicate without envy, and her riches I hide not. ^wisdom-07-13-douay

14 For she is an infinite treasure to men: which they that use, become the friends of God, being commended for the gifts of discipline. ^wisdom-07-14-douay

15 And God hath given to me to speak as I would, and to conceive thoughts worthy of those things that are given me: because he is the guide of wisdom, and the director of the wise: ^wisdom-07-15-douay

16 For in his hand are both we, and our words, and all wisdom, and the knowledge and skill of works. ^wisdom-07-16-douay

17 For he hath given me the true knowledge of the things that are: to know the disposition of the whole world, and the virtues of the elements, ^wisdom-07-17-douay

18 The beginning, and ending, and midst of the times, the alterations of their courses, and the changes of seasons, ^wisdom-07-18-douay

19 The revolutions of the year, and the dispositions of the stars, ^wisdom-07-19-douay

20 The natures of living creatures, and rage of wild beasts, the force of winds, and reasonings of men, the diversities of plants, and the virtues of roots, ^wisdom-07-20-douay

21 And all such things as are hid, and not foreseen, I have learned: for wisdom, which is the worker of all things, taught me. ^wisdom-07-21-douay

22 For in her is the spirit of understanding; holy, one, manifold, subtile, eloquent, active, undefiled, sure, sweet, loving that which is good, quick, which nothing hindereth, beneficent, ^wisdom-07-22-douay

23 Gentle, kind, steadfast, assured, secure, having all power, overseeing all things, and containing all spirits: intelligible, pure, subtile: ^wisdom-07-23-douay

24 For wisdom is more active than all active things; and reacheth everywhere, by reason of her purity. ^wisdom-07-24-douay

25 For she is a vapour of the power of God, and a certain pure emanation of the glory of the Almighty God: and therefore no defiled thing cometh into her. ^wisdom-07-25-douay

26 For she is the brightness of eternal light, and the unspotted mirror of God's majesty, and the image of his goodness. ^wisdom-07-26-douay

27 And being but one, she can do all things: and remaining in herself the same, she reneweth all things, and through nations conveyeth herself into holy souls, she maketh the friends of God and prophets. ^wisdom-07-27-douay

28 For God loveth none but him that dwelleth with wisdom. ^wisdom-07-28-douay

29 For she is more beautiful than the sun, and above all the order of the stars: being compared with the light, she is found before it. ^wisdom-07-29-douay

30 For after this cometh night, but no evil can overcome wisdom. ^wisdom-07-30-douay
