# Hebrews Chapter 3

Christ is more excellent than Moses. Wherefore we must adhere to him by faith and obedience.

1 Wherefore, holy brethren, partakers of the heavenly vocation consider the apostle and high priest of our confession, Jesus: ^hebrews-03-1-douay

2 Who is faithful to him that made him, as was also Moses in all his house. ^hebrews-03-2-douay

3 For this man was counted worthy of greater glory than Moses, by so much as he that hath built the house hath greater honour than the house. ^hebrews-03-3-douay

4 For every house is built by some man: but he that created all things is God. ^hebrews-03-4-douay

5 And Moses indeed was faithful in all his house as a servant, for a testimony of those things which were to be said: ^hebrews-03-5-douay

6 But Christ, as the Son in his own house: which house are we, if we hold fast the confidence and glory of hope unto the end. ^hebrews-03-6-douay

7 Wherefore, as the Holy Ghost saith: To-day if you shall hear his voice, ^hebrews-03-7-douay

8 Harden not your hearts, as in the provocation, in the day of temptation in the desert, ^hebrews-03-8-douay

9 Where your fathers tempted me, proved and saw my works, ^hebrews-03-9-douay

10 Forty years: for which cause I was offended with this generation, and I said: They always err in heart. And they have not known my ways. ^hebrews-03-10-douay

11 As I have sworn in my wrath: If they shall enter into my rest. ^hebrews-03-11-douay

12 Take heed, brethren, lest perhaps there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, to depart from the living God. ^hebrews-03-12-douay

13 But exhort one another every day, whilst it is called to day, that none of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin. ^hebrews-03-13-douay

14 For we are made partakers of Christ: yet so, if we hold the beginning of his substance firm unto the end. ^hebrews-03-14-douay

15 While it is said: To day, if you shall hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in that provocation. ^hebrews-03-15-douay

16 For some who heard did provoke: but not all that came out of Egypt by Moses. ^hebrews-03-16-douay

17 And with whom was he offended forty years? Was it not with them that sinned, whose carcasses were overthrown in the desert? ^hebrews-03-17-douay

18 And to whom did he swear, that they should not enter into his rest: but to them that were incredulous? ^hebrews-03-18-douay

19 And we see that they could not enter in, because of unbelief. ^hebrews-03-19-douay
