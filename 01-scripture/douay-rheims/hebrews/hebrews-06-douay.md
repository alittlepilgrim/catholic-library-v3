# Hebrews Chapter 6

He warns them of the danger of falling by apostasy and exhorts them to patience and perseverance.

1 Wherefore, leaving the word of the beginning of Christ, let us go on to things more perfect: not laying again the foundation of penance from dead works and of faith towards God, **The word of the beginning...** The first rudiments of the Christian doctrine. ^hebrews-06-1-douay

2 Of the doctrine of baptisms and imposition of hands, and of the resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment. ^hebrews-06-2-douay

3 And this will we do, if God permit. ^hebrews-06-3-douay

4 For it is impossible for those who were once illuminated, have tasted also the heavenly gift and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost, **It is impossible, etc...** The meaning is, that it is impossible for such as have fallen after baptism, to be again baptized; and very hard for such as have apostatized from the faith, after having received many graces, to return again to the happy state from which they fell. ^hebrews-06-4-douay

5 Have moreover tasted the good word of God and the powers of the world to come, ^hebrews-06-5-douay

6 And are fallen away: to be renewed again to penance, crucifying again to themselves the Son of God and making him a mockery. ^hebrews-06-6-douay

7 For the earth, that drinketh in the rain which cometh often upon it and bringeth forth herbs meet for them by whom it is tilled, receiveth blessing from God. ^hebrews-06-7-douay

8 But that which bringeth forth thorns and briers is reprobate and very near unto a curse: whose end is to be burnt. ^hebrews-06-8-douay

9 But, my dearly beloved, we trust better things of you, and nearer to salvation; though we speak thus. ^hebrews-06-9-douay

10 For God is not unjust, that he should forget your work and the love which you have shown in his name, you who have ministered and do minister to the saints. ^hebrews-06-10-douay

11 And we desire that every one of you show forth the same carefulness to the accomplishing of hope unto the end: ^hebrews-06-11-douay

12 That you become not slothful, but followers of them who through faith and patience shall inherit the promises. ^hebrews-06-12-douay

13 For God making promises to Abraham, because he had no one greater by whom he might swear, swore by himself, ^hebrews-06-13-douay

14 Saying: Unless blessing I shall bless thee and multiplying I shall multiply thee. ^hebrews-06-14-douay

15 And so patiently enduring he obtained the promise. ^hebrews-06-15-douay

16 For men swear by one greater than themselves: and an oath for confirmation is the end of all their controversy. ^hebrews-06-16-douay

17 Wherein God, meaning more abundantly to show to the heirs of the promise the immutability of his counsel, interposed an oath: ^hebrews-06-17-douay

18 That by two immutable things in which it is impossible for God to lie, we may have the strongest comfort, we who have fled for refuge to hold fast the hope set before us. ^hebrews-06-18-douay

19 Which we have as an anchor of the soul, sure and firm, and which entereth in even within the veil: ^hebrews-06-19-douay

20 Where the forerunner Jesus is entered for us, made a high priest for ever according to the order of Melchisedech. ^hebrews-06-20-douay
