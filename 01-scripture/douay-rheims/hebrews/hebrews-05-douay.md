# Hebrews Chapter 5

The office of a high priest. Christ is our high priest.

1 For every high priest taken from among men is ordained for men in the things that appertain to God, that he may offer up gifts and sacrifices for sins: ^hebrews-05-1-douay

2 Who can have compassion on them that are ignorant and that err: because he himself also is compassed with infirmity. ^hebrews-05-2-douay

3 And therefore he ought, as for the people, so also for himself, to offer for sins. ^hebrews-05-3-douay

4 Neither doth any man take the honour to himself, but he that is called by God, as Aaron was. ^hebrews-05-4-douay

5 So Christ also did not glorify himself, that he might be made a high priest: but he that said unto him: Thou art my Son: this day have I begotten thee. ^hebrews-05-5-douay

6 As he saith also in another place: Thou art a priest for ever, according to the order of Melchisedech. ^hebrews-05-6-douay

7 Who in the days of his flesh, with a strong cry and tears, offering up prayers and supplications to him that was able to save him from death, was heard for his reverence. ^hebrews-05-7-douay

8 And whereas indeed he was the Son of God, he learned obedience by the things which he suffered. ^hebrews-05-8-douay

9 And being consummated, he became, to all that obey him, the cause of eternal salvation: ^hebrews-05-9-douay

10 Called by God a high priest, according to the order of Melchisedech. ^hebrews-05-10-douay

11 Of whom we have much to say and hard to be intelligibly uttered: because you are become weak to hear. ^hebrews-05-11-douay

12 For whereas for the time you ought to be masters, you have need to be taught again what are the first elements of the words of God: and you are become such as have need of milk and not of strong meat. ^hebrews-05-12-douay

13 For every one that is a partaker of milk is unskilful in the word of justice: for he is a little child. ^hebrews-05-13-douay

14 But strong meat is for the perfect: for them who by custom have their senses exercised to the discerning of good and evil. ^hebrews-05-14-douay
