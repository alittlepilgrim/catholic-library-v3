# Philippians Chapter 1

The apostle's affection for the Philippians.

1 Paul and Timothy, the servants of Jesus Christ: to all the saints in Christ Jesus who are at Philippi, with the bishops and deacons. ^philippians-01-1-douay

2 Grace be unto you and peace, from God our Father and from the Lord Jesus Christ. ^philippians-01-2-douay

3 I give thanks to my God in every remembrance of you: ^philippians-01-3-douay

4 Always in all my prayers making supplication for you all with joy: ^philippians-01-4-douay

5 For your communication in the gospel of Christ, from the first day unto now. ^philippians-01-5-douay

6 Being confident of this very thing: that he who hath begun a good work in you will perfect it unto the day of Christ Jesus. ^philippians-01-6-douay

7 As it is meet for me to think this for you all, for that I have you in my heart; and that, in my bands and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, you all are partakers of my joy. ^philippians-01-7-douay

8 For God is my witness how I long after you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ. ^philippians-01-8-douay

9 And this I pray: That your charity may more and more abound in knowledge and in all understanding: ^philippians-01-9-douay

10 That you may approve the better things: that you may be sincere and without offence unto the day of Christ: ^philippians-01-10-douay

11 Filled with the fruit of justice, through Jesus Christ, unto the glory and praise of God. ^philippians-01-11-douay

12 Now, brethren, I desire you should know that the things which have happened to me have fallen out rather to the furtherance of the gospel: ^philippians-01-12-douay

13 So that my bands are made manifest in Christ, in all the court and in all other places. ^philippians-01-13-douay

14 And many of the brethren in the Lord, growing confident by my bands, are much more bold to speak the word of God without fear. ^philippians-01-14-douay

15 Some indeed, even out of envy and contention: but some also for good will preach Christ. ^philippians-01-15-douay

16 Some out of charity, knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel. ^philippians-01-16-douay

17 And some out of contention preach Christ not sincerely: supposing that they raise affliction to my bands. ^philippians-01-17-douay

18 But what then? So that by all means, whether by occasion or by truth, Christ be preached: in this also I rejoice, yea, and will rejoice. ^philippians-01-18-douay

19 For I know that this shall fall out to me unto salvation, through your prayer and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ, ^philippians-01-19-douay

20 According to my expectation and hope; that in nothing I shall be confounded: but with all confidence, as always, so now also, shall Christ be magnified in my body, whether it be by life or by death. ^philippians-01-20-douay

21 For to me, to live is Christ: and to die is gain. ^philippians-01-21-douay

22 And if to live in the flesh: this is to me the fruit of labour. And what I shall choose I know not. **This is to me, etc...** His meaning is, that although his dying immediately for Christ would be his gain, by putting him presently in possession of heaven; yet he is doubtful what he should choose, because by staying longer in the flesh, he should be more beneficial to the souls of his neighbours. ^philippians-01-22-douay

23 But I am straitened between two: having a desire to be dissolved and to be with Christ, a thing by far the better. ^philippians-01-23-douay

24 But to abide still in the flesh is needful for you. ^philippians-01-24-douay

25 And having this confidence, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all, for your furtherance and joy of faith: ^philippians-01-25-douay

26 That your rejoicing may abound in Christ Jesus for me, by my coming to you again. ^philippians-01-26-douay

27 Only let your conversation be worthy of the gospel of Christ: that, whether I come and see you, or, being absent, may hear of you, that you stand fast in one spirit, with one mind labouring together for the faith of the gospel. ^philippians-01-27-douay

28 And in nothing be ye terrified by the adversaries: which to them is a cause of perdition, but to you of salvation, and this from God. ^philippians-01-28-douay

29 For unto you it is given for Christ, not only to believe in him, but also to suffer for him: ^philippians-01-29-douay

30 Having the same conflict as that which you have seen in me and now have heard of me. ^philippians-01-30-douay
