# Philippians Chapter 4

He exhorts them to perseverance in all good and acknowledges their charitable contributions to him.

1 Therefore my dearly beloved brethren and most desired, my joy and my crown: so stand fast in the Lord, my dearly beloved. ^philippians-04-1-douay

2 I beg of Evodia and I beseech Syntyche to be of one mind in the Lord. ^philippians-04-2-douay

3 And I entreat thee also, my sincere companion, help those women who have laboured with me in the gospel, with Clement and the rest of my fellow labourers, whose names are in the book of life. ^philippians-04-3-douay

4 Rejoice in the Lord always: again, I say, rejoice. ^philippians-04-4-douay

5 Let your modesty be known to all men. The Lord is nigh. ^philippians-04-5-douay

6 Be nothing solicitous: but in every thing, by prayer and supplication, with thanksgiving, let your petitions be made known to God. ^philippians-04-6-douay

7 And the peace of God, which surpasseth all understanding, keep your hearts and minds in Christ Jesus. ^philippians-04-7-douay

8 For the rest, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever modest, whatsoever just, whatsoever holy, whatsoever lovely, whatsoever of good fame, if there be any virtue, if any praise of discipline: think on these things. **For the rest, brethren, whatsoever things are true, etc...** Here the apostle enumerates general precepts of morality, which they ought to practise. **Whatsoever things are true...** in words, in promises, in lawful oaths, etc., he commands rectitude of mind, and sincerity of heart. **Whatsoever modest...** by these words he prescribes gravity in manners, modesty in dress, and decency in conversation. **Whatsoever just...** That is, in dealing with others, in buying or selling, in trade or business, to be fair and honest. **Whatsoever holy...** by these words may be understood, that those who are in a religious state professed, or in holy orders, should lead a life of sanctity and chastity, according to the vows they make; but these words being also applied to those in the world, indicate the virtuous life they are bound by the divine commandments to follow. **Whatsoever lovely...** that is, to practise those good offices in society, that procure us the esteem and good will of our neighbours. **Whatsoever of good fame...** That is, that by our conduct and behaviour we should edify our neighbours, and give them good example by our actions. **If there be any virtue, if any praise of discipline...** that those in error, by seeing the morality and good discipline of the true religion, may be converted. **And finally, the apostle commands, not only the Philippians, but all Christians, to think on these things...** that is, to make it their study and concern that the peace of God might be with them. ^philippians-04-8-douay

9 The things which you have both learned and received and heard and seen in me, these do ye: and the God of peace shall be with you. ^philippians-04-9-douay

10 Now I rejoice in the Lord exceedingly that now at length your thought for me hath flourished again, as you did also think; but you were busied. ^philippians-04-10-douay

11 I speak not as it were for want. For I have learned, in whatsoever state I am, to be content therewith. ^philippians-04-11-douay

12 I know both how to be brought low, and I know how to abound (every where and in all things I am instructed): both to be full and to be hungry: both to abound and to suffer need. ^philippians-04-12-douay

13 I can do all things in him who strengtheneth me. ^philippians-04-13-douay

14 Nevertheless, you have done well in communicating to my tribulation. ^philippians-04-14-douay

15 And you also know, O Philippians, that in the beginning of the gospel, when I departed from Macedonia, no church communicated with me as concerning giving and receiving, but you only. ^philippians-04-15-douay

16 For unto Thessalonica also you sent once and again for my use. ^philippians-04-16-douay

17 Not that I seek the gift: but I seek the fruit that may abound to your account. ^philippians-04-17-douay

18 But I have all and abound: I am filled, having received from Epaphroditus the things you sent, an odour of sweetness, an acceptable sacrifice, pleasing to God. ^philippians-04-18-douay

19 And may my God supply all your want, according to his riches in glory in Christ Jesus. ^philippians-04-19-douay

20 Now to God and our Father be glory, world without end. Amen. ^philippians-04-20-douay

21 Salute ye every saint in Christ Jesus. ^philippians-04-21-douay

22 The brethren who are with me salute you. All the saints salute you: especially they that are of Caesar's household. ^philippians-04-22-douay

23 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. ^philippians-04-23-douay
