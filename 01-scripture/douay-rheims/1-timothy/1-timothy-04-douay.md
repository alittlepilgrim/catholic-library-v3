# 1 Timothy Chapter 4

He warns him against heretics, and exhorts him to the exercise of piety.

1 Now the Spirit manifestly saith that in the last times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to spirits of error and doctrines of devils, ^1-timothy-04-1-douay

2 Speaking lies in hypocrisy and having their conscience seared, ^1-timothy-04-2-douay

3 Forbidding to marry, to abstain from meats, which God hath created to be received with thanksgiving by the faithful and by them that have known the truth. **Forbidding to marry, to abstain from meats...**. He speaks of the Gnostics, the Marcionites, the Encratites, the Manicheans, and other ancient heretics, who absolutely condemned marriage, and the use of all kind of meat; because they pretended that all flesh was from an evil principle. Whereas the church of God, so far from condemning marriage, holds it a holy sacrament; and forbids it to none but such as by vow have chosen the better part: and prohibits not the use of any meats whatsoever in proper times and seasons; though she does not judge all kind of diet proper for days of fasting and penance. ^1-timothy-04-3-douay

4 For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be rejected that is received with thanksgiving: ^1-timothy-04-4-douay

5 For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer. ^1-timothy-04-5-douay

6 These things proposing to the brethren, thou shalt be a good minister of Christ Jesus, nourished up in the words of faith and of the good doctrine which thou hast attained unto. ^1-timothy-04-6-douay

7 But avoid foolish and old wives fables: and exercise thyself unto godliness. ^1-timothy-04-7-douay

8 For bodily exercise is profitable to little: but godliness is profitable to all things, having promise of the life that now is and of that which is to come. ^1-timothy-04-8-douay

9 A faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation. ^1-timothy-04-9-douay

10 For therefore we labour and are reviled, because we hope in the living God, who is the Saviour of all men, especially of the faithful. ^1-timothy-04-10-douay

11 These things command and teach: ^1-timothy-04-11-douay

12 Let no man despise thy youth: but be thou an example of the faithful, in word, in conversation, in charity, in faith, in chastity. ^1-timothy-04-12-douay

13 Till I come, attend unto reading, to exhortation and to doctrine. ^1-timothy-04-13-douay

14 Neglect not the grace that is in thee, which was given thee by prophecy, with imposition of the hands of the priesthood. ^1-timothy-04-14-douay

15 Meditate upon these things, be wholly in these things: that thy profiting may be manifest to all. ^1-timothy-04-15-douay

16 Take heed to thyself and to doctrine: be earnest in them. For in doing this thou shalt both save thyself and them that hear thee. ^1-timothy-04-16-douay
