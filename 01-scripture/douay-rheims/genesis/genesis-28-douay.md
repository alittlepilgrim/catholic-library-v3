# Genesis Chapter 28

Jacob's journey to Mesopotamia: his vision and vow.

1 And Isaac called Jacob, and blessed him, and charged him, saying: Take not a wife of the stock of Chanaan: ^genesis-28-1-douay

2 But go, and take a journey to Mesopotamia of Syria, to the house of Bathuel, thy mother's father, and take thee a wife thence of the daughters of Laban, thy uncle. ^genesis-28-2-douay

3 And God almighty bless thee, and make thee to increase and multiply thee: that thou mayst be a multitude of people. ^genesis-28-3-douay

4 And give the blessings of Abraham to thee, and to thy seed after thee: that thou mayst possess the land of thy sojournment, which he promised to thy grandfather. ^genesis-28-4-douay

5 And when Isaac had sent him away, he took his journey and went to Mesopotamia of Syria, to Laban, the son of Bathuel, the Syrian, brother to Rebecca, his mother. ^genesis-28-5-douay

6 And Esau seeing that his father had blessed Jacob, and had sent him into Mesopotamia of Syria, to marry a wife thence; and that after the blessing he had charged him, saying: Thou shalt not take a wife of the daughters of Chanaan: ^genesis-28-6-douay

7 And that Jacob obeying his parents, was gone into Syria: ^genesis-28-7-douay

8 Experiencing also, that his father was not well pleased with the daughters of Chanaan: ^genesis-28-8-douay

9 He went to Ismael, and took to wife, besides them he had before, Maheleth, the daughter of Ismael, Abraham's son, the sister of Nabajoth. ^genesis-28-9-douay

10 But Jacob being departed from Bersabee, went on to Haran. ^genesis-28-10-douay

11 And when he was come to a certain place, and would rest in it after sunset, he took of the stones that lay there, and putting under his head, slept in the same place. ^genesis-28-11-douay

12 And he saw in his sleep a ladder standing upon the earth, and the top thereof touching heaven: the angels also of God ascending and descending by it. ^genesis-28-12-douay

13 And the Lord leaning upon the ladder saying to him: I am the Lord God of Abraham thy father, and the God of Isaac: The land, wherein thou sleepest, I will give to thee and to thy seed. ^genesis-28-13-douay

14 And thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth: thou shalt spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south: and in thee and thy seed, all the tribes of the earth shall be blessed. ^genesis-28-14-douay

15 And I will be thy keeper whithersoever thou goest, and will bring thee back into this land: neither will I leave thee, till I shall have accomplished all that I have said. ^genesis-28-15-douay

16 And when Jacob awaked out of sleep, he said: Indeed the Lord is in this place, and I knew it not. ^genesis-28-16-douay

17 And trembling, he said: How terrible is this place? this is no other but the house of God, and the gate of heaven. ^genesis-28-17-douay

18 And Jacob arising in the morning, took the stone which he had laid under his head, and set it up for a title, pouring oil upon the top of it. ^genesis-28-18-douay

19 And he called the name of the city Bethel, which before was called Luza. **Bethel...** This name signifies the *house of God*. ^genesis-28-19-douay

20 And he made a vow, saying: If God shall be with me, and shall keep me in the way, by which I walk, and shall give me bread to eat, and raiment to put on, ^genesis-28-20-douay

21 And I shall return prosperously to my father's house: the Lord shall be my God: ^genesis-28-21-douay

22 And this stone, which I have set up for a title, shall be called the *house of God*: and of all things that thou shalt give to me, I will offer tithes to thee. ^genesis-28-22-douay
