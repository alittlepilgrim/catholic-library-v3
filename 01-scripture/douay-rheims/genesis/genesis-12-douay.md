# Genesis Chapter 12

The call of Abram, and the promise made to him. He sojourns in Canaan, and then goes down to Egypt during a famine.

1 And the Lord said to Abram: Go forth out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and out of thy father's house, and come into the land which I shall show thee. ^genesis-12-1-douay

2 And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and magnify thy name, and thou shalt be blessed. ^genesis-12-2-douay

3 I will bless them that bless thee, and curse them that curse thee, and IN THEE shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed. ^genesis-12-3-douay

4 So Abram went out as the Lord had commanded him, and Lot went with him: Abram was seventy-five years old when he went forth from Haran. ^genesis-12-4-douay

5 And he took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother's son, and all the substance which they had gathered, and the souls which they had gotten in Haran: and they went out to go into the land of Chanaan. And when they were come into it, ^genesis-12-5-douay

6 Abram passed through the country unto the place of Sichem, as far as the noble vale: now the Chanaanite was at that time in the land. ^genesis-12-6-douay

7 And the Lord appeared to Abram, and said to him: To thy seed will I give this land. And he built there an altar to the Lord, who had appeared to him. ^genesis-12-7-douay

8 And passing on from thence to a mountain, that was on the east side of Bethel, he there pitched his tent, having Bethel on the west, and Hai on the east: he built there also an altar to the Lord, and called upon his name. ^genesis-12-8-douay

9 And Abram went forward, going and proceeding on to the south. ^genesis-12-9-douay

10 And there came a famine in the country: and Abram went down into Egypt, to sojourn there: for the famine was very grievous in the land. ^genesis-12-10-douay

11 And when he was near to enter into Egypt, he said to Sarai his wife: I know that thou art a beautiful woman: ^genesis-12-11-douay

12 And that when the Egyptians shall see thee, they will say: She is his wife: and they will kill me, and keep thee. ^genesis-12-12-douay

13 Say, therefore, I pray thee, that thou art my sister: that I may be well used for thee, and that my soul may live for thy sake. **My sister...** This was no lie; because she was his niece, being daughter to his brother Aran, and therefore, in the style of the Hebrews, she might truly be called his sister, as Lot is called Abram's brother, [[genesis-14-douay|Genesis 14:14]]. See [[genesis-20-douay|Genesis 20:12]]. ^genesis-12-13-douay

14 And when Abram was come into Egypt, the Egyptians saw the woman that she was very beautiful. ^genesis-12-14-douay

15 And the princes told Pharao, and praised her before him: and the woman was taken into the house of Pharao. ^genesis-12-15-douay

16 And they used Abram well for her sake. And he had sheep and oxen and he asses, and men servants, and maid servants, and she asses, and camels. ^genesis-12-16-douay

17 But the Lord scourged Pharao and his house with most grievous stripes for Sarai, Abram's wife. ^genesis-12-17-douay

18 And Pharao called Abram, and said to him: What is this that thou hast done to me? Why didst thou not tell me that she was thy wife? ^genesis-12-18-douay

19 For what cause didst thou say, she was thy sister, that I might take her to my wife? Now therefore there is thy wife, take her, and go thy way. ^genesis-12-19-douay

20 And Pharao gave his men orders concerning Abram: and they led him away and his wife, and all that he had. ^genesis-12-20-douay
