# Genesis Chapter 25

Abraham's children by Cetura; his death and that of Ishmael. Isaac has Esau and Jacob twins. Esau sells his first birthright to Jacob.

1 And Abraham married another wife named Cetura: ^genesis-25-1-douay

2 Who bore him Zamram, and Jecsan, and Madan, and Madian, and Jesboc, and Sue. ^genesis-25-2-douay

3 Jecsan also begot Saba, and Dadan. The children of Dadan were Assurim, and Latusim, and Loomim. ^genesis-25-3-douay

4 But of Madian was born Epha, and Opher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaa: all these were the children of Cetura. ^genesis-25-4-douay

5 And Abraham gave all his possessions to Isaac: ^genesis-25-5-douay

6 And to the children of the concubines he gave gifts, and separated them from Isaac his son, while he yet lived, to the east country. **Concubines...** Agar and Cetura are here called concubines, (though they were lawful wives, and in other places are so called), because they were of an inferior degree, and such in scripture are usually called concubines. ^genesis-25-6-douay

7 And the days of Abraham's life were a hundred and seventy-five years. ^genesis-25-7-douay

8 And decaying he died in a good old age, and having lived a long time, and being full of days: and was gathered to his people. ^genesis-25-8-douay

9 And Isaac and Ismael his sons buried him in the double cave, which was situated in the field of Ephron the son of Seor the Hethite, over against Mambre, ^genesis-25-9-douay

10 Which he had bought of the children of Heth: there was he buried, and Sara his wife. ^genesis-25-10-douay

11 And after his death, God blessed Isaac his son, who dwelt by the well named *Of the living and seeing*. ^genesis-25-11-douay

12 These are the generations of Ismael the son of Abraham, whom Agar the Egyptian, Sara's servant, bore unto him: ^genesis-25-12-douay

13 And these are the names of his children according to their calling and generations. The firstborn of Ismael was Nabajoth, then Cedar, and Adbeel, and Mabsam, ^genesis-25-13-douay

14 And Masma, and Duma, and Massa, ^genesis-25-14-douay

15 Hadar, and Thema, and Jethur, and Naphis, and Cedma. ^genesis-25-15-douay

16 These are the sons of Ismael: and these are their names by their castles and towns, twelve princes of their tribes. ^genesis-25-16-douay

17 And the years of Ismael's life were a hundred and thirty-seven, and decaying he died, and was gathered unto his people. ^genesis-25-17-douay

18 And he dwelt from Hevila as far as Sur, which looketh towards Egypt, to them that go towards the Assyrians. He died in the presence of all his brethren. ^genesis-25-18-douay

19 These also are the generations of Isaac the son of Abraham: Abraham begot Isaac: ^genesis-25-19-douay

20 Who when he was forty years old, took to wife Rebecca the daughter of Bathuel the Syrian of Mesopotamia, sister to Laban. ^genesis-25-20-douay

21 And Isaac besought the Lord for his wife, because she was barren: and he heard him, and made Rebecca to conceive. ^genesis-25-21-douay

22 But the children struggled in her womb, and she said: If it were to be so with me, what need was there to conceive? And she went to consult the Lord. ^genesis-25-22-douay

23 And he answering, said: Two nations are in thy womb, and two peoples shall be divided out of thy womb, and one people shall overcome the other, and the elder shall serve the younger. ^genesis-25-23-douay

24 And when her time was come to be delivered, behold twins were found in her womb. ^genesis-25-24-douay

25 He that came forth first was red, and hairy like a skin: and his name was called Esau. Immediately the other coming forth, held his brother's foot in his hand: and therefore he was called Jacob. ^genesis-25-25-douay

26 Isaac was threescore years old when the children were born unto him. ^genesis-25-26-douay

27 And when they were grown up, Esau became a skilful hunter, and a husbandman: but Jacob, a plain man, dwelt in tents. ^genesis-25-27-douay

28 Isaac loved Esau, because he ate of his hunting: and Rebecca loved Jacob. ^genesis-25-28-douay

29 And Jacob boiled pottage: to whom Esau, coming faint out of the field, ^genesis-25-29-douay

30 Said: Give me of this red pottage, for I am exceeding faint. For which reason his name was called Edom. ^genesis-25-30-douay

31 And Jacob said to him: Sell me thy first birthright. ^genesis-25-31-douay

32 He answered: Lo I die, what will the first birthright avail me? ^genesis-25-32-douay

33 Jacob said: Swear therefore to me. Esau swore to him, and sold his first birthright. ^genesis-25-33-douay

34 And so taking bread and the pottage of lentils, he ate, and drank, and went on his way; making little account of having sold his first birthright. ^genesis-25-34-douay
