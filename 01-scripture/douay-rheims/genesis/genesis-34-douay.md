# Genesis Chapter 34

Dina is ravished, for which the Sichemites are destroyed.

1 And Dina the daughter of Lia went out to see the women of that country. ^genesis-34-1-douay

2 And when Sichem the son of Hemor the Hevite, the prince of that land, saw her, he was in love with her: and took her away, and lay with her, ravishing the virgin. ^genesis-34-2-douay

3 And his soul was fast knit unto her; and whereas she was sad, he comforted her with sweet words. ^genesis-34-3-douay

4 And going to Hemor his father, he said: Get me this damsel to wife. ^genesis-34-4-douay

5 But when Jacob had heard this, his sons being absent, and employed in feeding the cattle, he held his peace till they came back. ^genesis-34-5-douay

6 And when Hemor the father of Sichem was come out to speak to Jacob, ^genesis-34-6-douay

7 Behold his sons came from the field: and hearing what had passed, they were exceeding angry, because he had done a foul thing in Israel, and committed an unlawful act, in ravishing Jacob's daughter. ^genesis-34-7-douay

8 And Hemor spoke to them: The soul of my son Sichem has a longing for your daughter: give her him to wife: ^genesis-34-8-douay

9 And let us contract marriages one with another: give us your daughters, and take you our daughters. ^genesis-34-9-douay

10 And dwell with us: the land is at your command, till, trade, and possess it. ^genesis-34-10-douay

11 Sichem also said to her father and to her brethren: Let me find favour in your sight, and whatsoever you shall appoint I will give: ^genesis-34-11-douay

12 Raise the dowry, and ask gifts, and I will gladly give what you shall demand: only give me this damsel to wife. ^genesis-34-12-douay

13 The sons of Jacob answered Sichem and his father deceitfully, being enraged at the deflowering of their sister: **Deceitfully...** The sons of Jacob, on this occasion, were guilty of a grievous sin, as well by falsely pretending religion, as by excess of revenge: though otherwise their zeal against so foul a crime was commendable. ^genesis-34-13-douay

14 We cannot do what you demand, nor give our sister to one that is uncircumcised; which with us is unlawful and abominable. ^genesis-34-14-douay

15 But in this we may be allied with you, if you will be like us, and all the male sex among you be circumcised: ^genesis-34-15-douay

16 Then will we mutually give and take your daughters, and ours; and we will dwell with you, and will be one people: ^genesis-34-16-douay

17 But if you will not be circumcised, we will take our daughter and depart. ^genesis-34-17-douay

18 Their offer pleased Hemor, and Sichem, his son: ^genesis-34-18-douay

19 And the young man made no delay, but forthwith fulfilled what was required: for he loved the damsel exceedingly, and he was the greatest man in all his father's house. ^genesis-34-19-douay

20 And going into the gate of the city, they spoke to the people: ^genesis-34-20-douay

21 These men are peaceable, and are willing to dwell with us: let them trade in the land, and till it, which being large and wide wanteth men to till it: we shall take their daughters for wives, and we will give them ours. ^genesis-34-21-douay

22 One thing there is for which so great a good is deferred: We must circumcise every male among us, following the manner of the nation. ^genesis-34-22-douay

23 And their substance, and cattle, and all that they possess, shall be ours; only in this let us condescend, and by dwelling together, we shall make one people. ^genesis-34-23-douay

24 And they all agreed, and circumcised all the males. ^genesis-34-24-douay

25 And behold the third day, when the pain of the wound was greatest: two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, the brothers of Dina, taking their swords, entered boldly into the city and slew all the men. ^genesis-34-25-douay

26 And they killed also Hemor and Sichem, and took away their sister Dina out of Sichem's house. ^genesis-34-26-douay

27 And when they were gone out, the other sons of Jacob came upon the slain; and plundered the city in revenge of the rape. ^genesis-34-27-douay

28 And they took their sheep, and their herds, and their asses, wasting all they had in their houses and in their fields. ^genesis-34-28-douay

29 And their children and wives they took captive. ^genesis-34-29-douay

30 And when they had boldly perpetrated these things, Jacob said to Simeon and Levi: You have troubled me, and made me hateful to the Chanaanites and Pherezites, the inhabitants of this land. We are few: they will gather themselves together and kill me; and both I, and my house shall be destroyed. ^genesis-34-30-douay

31 They answered: Should they abuse our sister as a strumpet? ^genesis-34-31-douay
