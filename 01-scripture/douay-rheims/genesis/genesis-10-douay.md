# Genesis Chapter 10

The genealogy of the children of Noah, by whom the world was peopled after the flood.

1 These are the generations of the sons of Noe: Sem, Cham, and Japheth: and unto them sons were born after the flood. ^genesis-10-1-douay

2 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Thubal, and Mosoch, and Thiras. ^genesis-10-2-douay

3 And the sons of Gomer: Ascenez and Riphath and Thogorma. ^genesis-10-3-douay

4 And the sons of Javan: Elisa and Tharsis, Cetthim and Dodanim. ^genesis-10-4-douay

5 By these were divided the islands of the Gentiles in their lands, every one according to his tongue and their families in their nations. **The islands...** So the Hebrews called all the remote countries, to which they went by ships from Judea, to Greece, Italy, Spain, etc. ^genesis-10-5-douay

6 And the Sons of Cham: Chus, and Mesram, and Phuth, and Chanaan. ^genesis-10-6-douay

7 And the sons of Chus: Saba, and Hevila, and Sabatha, and Regma, and Sabatacha. The sons of Regma: Saba, and Dadan. ^genesis-10-7-douay

8 Now Chus begot Nemrod: he began to be mighty on the earth. ^genesis-10-8-douay

9 And he was a stout hunter before the Lord. Hence came a proverb: Even as Nemrod the stout hunter before the Lord. **A stout hunter...** Not of beasts but of men: whom by violence and tyranny he brought under his dominion. And such he was, not only in the opinion of men, but before the Lord, that is, in his sight who cannot be deceived. ^genesis-10-9-douay

10 And the beginning of his kingdom was Babylon, and Arach, and Achad, and Chalanne in the land of Sennaar. ^genesis-10-10-douay

11 Out of that land came forth Assur, and built Ninive, and the streets of the city, and Chale. ^genesis-10-11-douay

12 Resen also between Ninive and Chale: this is the great city. ^genesis-10-12-douay

13 And Mesraim begot Ludim, and Anamim and Laabim, Nephthuim. ^genesis-10-13-douay

14 And Phetrusim, and Chasluim; of whom came forth the Philistines, and the Capthorim. ^genesis-10-14-douay

15 And Chanaan begot Sidon his firstborn, the Hethite, ^genesis-10-15-douay

16 And the Jebusite, and the Amorrhite, and the Gergesite. ^genesis-10-16-douay

17 The Hevite and Aracite: the Sinite, ^genesis-10-17-douay

18 And the Aradian, the Samarite, and the Hamathite: and afterwards the families of the Chanaanites were spread abroad. ^genesis-10-18-douay

19 And the limits of Chanaan were from Sidon as one comes to Gerara even to Gaza, until thou enter Sodom and Gomorrha, and Adama, and Seboim even to Lesa. ^genesis-10-19-douay

20 These are the children of Cham in their kindreds and tongues, and generations, and lands, and nations. ^genesis-10-20-douay

21 Of Sem also the father of all the children of Heber, the elder brother of Japheth, sons were born. ^genesis-10-21-douay

22 The sons of Sem: Elam and Assur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram. ^genesis-10-22-douay

23 The sons of Aram: Us, and Hull, and Gether; and Mes. ^genesis-10-23-douay

24 But Arphaxad begot Sale, of whom was born Heber. ^genesis-10-24-douay

25 And to Heber were born two sons: the name of the one was Phaleg, because in his days was the earth divided: and his brother's name Jectan. ^genesis-10-25-douay

26 Which Jectan begot Elmodad, and Saleph, and Asarmoth, Jare, ^genesis-10-26-douay

27 And Aduram, and Uzal, and Decla, ^genesis-10-27-douay

28 And Ebal, and Abimael, Saba, ^genesis-10-28-douay

29 And Ophir, and Hevila, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Jectan. ^genesis-10-29-douay

30 And their dwelling was from Messa as we go on as far as Sephar, a mountain in the east. ^genesis-10-30-douay

31 These are the children of Sem according to their kindreds and tongues, and countries in their nations. ^genesis-10-31-douay

32 These are the families of Noe, according to their people and nations. By these were the nations divided on the earth after the flood. ^genesis-10-32-douay
