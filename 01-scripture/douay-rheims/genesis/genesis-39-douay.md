# Genesis Chapter 39

Joseph has charge of his master's house: rejects his mistress' solicitations: is falsely accused by her, and cast into prison, where he has the charge of all the prisoners.

1 And Joseph was brought into Egypt, and Putiphar, an eunuch of Pharao, chief captain of the army, an Egyptian, bought him of the Ismaelites, by whom he was brought. ^genesis-39-1-douay

2 And the Lord was with him, and he was a prosperous man in all things: and he dwelt in his master's house: ^genesis-39-2-douay

3 Who knew very well that the Lord was with him, and made all that he did to prosper in his hand. ^genesis-39-3-douay

4 And Joseph found favour in the sight of his master, and ministered to him: and being set over all by him, he governed the house committed to him, and all things that were delivered to him: ^genesis-39-4-douay

5 And the Lord blessed the house of the Egyptian for Joseph's sake, and multiplied all his substance, both at home and in the fields. ^genesis-39-5-douay

6 Neither knew he any other thing, but the bread which he ate. And Joseph was of a beautiful countenance, and comely to behold. ^genesis-39-6-douay

7 And after many days, his mistress cast her eyes on Joseph, and said: Lie with me. ^genesis-39-7-douay

8 But he in no wise consenting to that wicked act said to her: Behold, my master hath delivered all things to me, and knoweth not what he hath in his own house: ^genesis-39-8-douay

9 Neither is there any thing which is not in my power, or that he hath not delivered to me, but thee, who art his wife; how then can I do this wicked thing, and sin against my God? ^genesis-39-9-douay

10 With such words as these day by day, both the woman was importunate with the young man, and he refused the adultery. ^genesis-39-10-douay

11 Now it happened on a certain day, that Joseph went into the house, and was doing some business, without any man with him: ^genesis-39-11-douay

12 And she catching the skirt of his garment, said: Lie with me. But he leaving the garment in her hand, fled, and went out. ^genesis-39-12-douay

13 And when the woman saw the garment in her hands, and herself disregarded, ^genesis-39-13-douay

14 She called to her the men of her house, and said to them: See, he hath brought in a Hebrew, to abuse us: he came in to me, to lie with me; and when I cried out, ^genesis-39-14-douay

15 And he heard my voice, he left the garment that I held, and got him out. ^genesis-39-15-douay

16 For a proof therefore of her fidelity, she kept the garment, and showed it to her husband when he returned home: **A proof of her fidelity...** or an argument to gain credit, *argumentum fidei*. ^genesis-39-16-douay

17 And said: The Hebrew servant, whom thou hast brought, came to me to abuse me. ^genesis-39-17-douay

18 And when he heard me cry, he left the garment which I held, and fled out. ^genesis-39-18-douay

19 His master hearing these things, and giving too much credit to his wife's words, was very angry, ^genesis-39-19-douay

20 And cast Joseph into the prison, where the king's prisoners were kept, and he was there shut up. ^genesis-39-20-douay

21 But the Lord was with Joseph, and having mercy upon him gave him favour in the sight of the chief keeper of the prison: ^genesis-39-21-douay

22 Who delivered into his hand all the prisoners that were kept in custody: and whatsoever was done, was under him. ^genesis-39-22-douay

23 Neither did he himself know any thing, having committed all things to him: for the Lord was with him, and made all that he did to prosper. ^genesis-39-23-douay
