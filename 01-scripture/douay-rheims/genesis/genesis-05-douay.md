# Genesis Chapter 5

The genealogy, age, and death of the Patriarchs, from Adam to Noah. The translation of Enoch.

1 This is the book of the generation of Adam. In the day that God created man, he made him to the likeness of God. ^genesis-05-1-douay

2 He created them male and female; and blessed them: and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created. ^genesis-05-2-douay

3 And Adam lived a hundred and thirty years, and begot a son to his own image and likeness, and called his name Seth. ^genesis-05-3-douay

4 And the days of Adam, after he begot Seth, were eight hundred years: and he begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-4-douay

5 And all the time that Adam lived, came to nine hundred and thirty years, and he died. ^genesis-05-5-douay

6 Seth also lived a hundred and five years, and begot Enos. ^genesis-05-6-douay

7 And Seth lived after he begot Enos, eight hundred and seven years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-7-douay

8 And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years, and he died. ^genesis-05-8-douay

9 And Enos lived ninety years, and begot Cainan. ^genesis-05-9-douay

10 After whose birth he lived eight hundred and fifteen years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-10-douay

11 And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years, and he died. ^genesis-05-11-douay

12 And Cainan lived seventy years, and begot Malaleel. ^genesis-05-12-douay

13 And Cainan lived after he begot Malaleel, eight hundred and forty years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-13-douay

14 And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years, and he died. ^genesis-05-14-douay

15 And Malaleel lived sixty-five years and begot Jared. ^genesis-05-15-douay

16 And Malaleel lived after he begot Jared, eight hundred and thirty years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-16-douay

17 And all the days of Malaleel were eight hundred and ninety-five years, and he died. ^genesis-05-17-douay

18 And Jared lived a hundred and sixty-two years, and begot Henoch. ^genesis-05-18-douay

19 And Jared lived after he begot Henoch, eight hundred years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-19-douay

20 And all the days of Jared were nine hundred and sixty-two years, and he died. ^genesis-05-20-douay

21 And Henoch lived sixty-five years, and begot Mathusala. ^genesis-05-21-douay

22 And Henoch walked with God: and lived after he begot Mathusala, three hundred years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-22-douay

23 And all the days of Henoch were three hundred and sixty-five years. ^genesis-05-23-douay

24 And he walked with God, and was seen no more: because God took him. ^genesis-05-24-douay

25 And Mathusala lived a hundred and eighty-seven years, and begot Lamech. ^genesis-05-25-douay

26 And Mathusala lived after he begot Lamech, seven hundred and eighty-two years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-26-douay

27 And all the days of Mathusala were nine hundred and sixty-nine years, and he died. ^genesis-05-27-douay

28 And Lamech lived a hundred and eighty-two years, and begot a son. ^genesis-05-28-douay

29 And he called his name Noe, saying: This same shall comfort us from the works and labours of our hands on the earth, which the Lord hath cursed. ^genesis-05-29-douay

30 And Lamech lived after he begot Noe, five hundred and ninety-five years, and begot sons and daughters. ^genesis-05-30-douay

31 And all the days of Lamech came to seven hundred and seventy-seven years, and he died. And Noe, when he was five hundred years old, begot Sem, Cham, and Japheth. ^genesis-05-31-douay
