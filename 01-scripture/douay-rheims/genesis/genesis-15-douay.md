# Genesis Chapter 15

God promises seed to Abram. His faith, sacrifice and vision.

1 Now when these things were done, the word of the Lord came to Abram by a vision, saying: Fear not, Abram, I am thy protector, and thy reward exceeding great. ^genesis-15-1-douay

2 And Abram said: Lord God, what wilt thou give me? I shall go without children: and the son of the steward of my house is this Damascus Eliezer. ^genesis-15-2-douay

3 And Abram added: But to me thou hast not given seed: and lo my servant born in my house, shall be my heir. ^genesis-15-3-douay

4 And immediately the word of the Lord came to him, saying: He shall not be thy heir: but he that shall come out of thy bowels, him shalt thou have for thy heir. ^genesis-15-4-douay

5 And he brought him forth abroad, and said to him: Look up to heaven and number the stars if thou canst. And he said to him: So shall thy seed be. ^genesis-15-5-douay

6 Abram believed God, and it was reputed to him unto justice. ^genesis-15-6-douay

7 And he said to him: I am the Lord who brought thee out from Ur of the Chaldees, to give thee this land, and that thou mightest possess it. ^genesis-15-7-douay

8 But he said: Lord God, whereby may I know that I shall possess it? ^genesis-15-8-douay

9 And the Lord answered, and said: Take me a cow of three years old, and a she goat of three years and a ram of three years, a turtle also, and a pigeon. ^genesis-15-9-douay

10 And he took all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid the two pieces of each one against the other: but the birds he divided not. ^genesis-15-10-douay

11 And the fowls came down upon the carcasses, and Abram drove them away. ^genesis-15-11-douay

12 And when the sun was setting, a deep sleep fell upon Abram, and a great and darksome horror seized upon him. ^genesis-15-12-douay

13 And it was said unto him: Know thou beforehand that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land not their own, and they shall bring them under bondage, and afflict them four hundred years. ^genesis-15-13-douay

14 But I will judge the nation which they shall serve, and after this they shall come out with great substance. ^genesis-15-14-douay

15 And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace, and be buried in a good old age. ^genesis-15-15-douay

16 But in the fourth generation they shall return hither: for as yet the iniquities of the Amorrhites are not at the full until this present time. ^genesis-15-16-douay

17 And when the sun was set, there arose a dark mist, and there appeared a smoking furnace, and a lamp of fire passing between those divisions. ^genesis-15-17-douay

18 That day God made a covenant with Abram, saying: To thy seed will I give this land, from the river of Egypt even to the great river Euphrates. ^genesis-15-18-douay

19 The Cineans, and Cenezites, the Cedmonites, ^genesis-15-19-douay

20 And the Hethites, and the Pherezites, the Raphaim also, ^genesis-15-20-douay

21 And the Amorrhites, and the Chanaanites, and the Gergesites, and the Jebusites. ^genesis-15-21-douay
