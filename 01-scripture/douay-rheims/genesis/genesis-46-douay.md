# Genesis Chapter 46

Israel, warranted by a vision from God, goes down into Egypt with all his family.

1 And Israel taking his journey, with all that he had, came to the well of the oath, and killing victims there to the God of his father Isaac, **The well of the oath...** Bersabee. ^genesis-46-1-douay

2 He heard him, by a vision in the night, calling him, and saying to him: Jacob, Jacob. And he answered him: Lo, here I am. ^genesis-46-2-douay

3 God said to him: I am the most mighty God of thy father; fear not, go down into Egypt, for I will make a great nation of thee there. ^genesis-46-3-douay

4 I will go down with thee thither, and will bring thee back again from thence: Joseph also shall put his hands upon thy eyes. ^genesis-46-4-douay

5 And Jacob rose up from the well of the oath: and his sons took him up, with their children and wives in the wagons, which Pharao had sent to carry the old man, ^genesis-46-5-douay

6 And all that he had in the land of Chanaan: and he came into Egypt with all his seed; ^genesis-46-6-douay

7 His sons, and grandsons, daughters, and all his offspring together. ^genesis-46-7-douay

8 And these are the names of the children of Israel, that entered into Egypt, he and his children. His firstborn Ruben, ^genesis-46-8-douay

9 The sons of Ruben: Henoch and Phallu, and Hesron and Charmi. ^genesis-46-9-douay

10 The sons of Simeon: Jamuel and Jamin and Ahod, and Jachin and Sohar, and Saul, the son of a woman of Chanaan. ^genesis-46-10-douay

11 The sons of Levi: Gerson and Caath, and Merari. ^genesis-46-11-douay

12 The sons of Juda: Her and Onan, and Sela, and Phares and Zara. And Her and Onan died in the land of Chanaan. And sons were born to Phares: Hesron and Hamul. ^genesis-46-12-douay

13 The sons of Issachar: Thola and Phua, and Job and Semron. ^genesis-46-13-douay

14 The sons of Zabulon: Sared, and Elon, and Jahelel. ^genesis-46-14-douay

15 These are the sons of Lia, whom she bore in Mesopotamia of Syria, with Dina, his daughter. All the souls of her sons and daughters, thirty-three. ^genesis-46-15-douay

16 The sons of Gad: Sephion and Haggi, and Suni and Esebon, and Heri and Arodi, and Areli. ^genesis-46-16-douay

17 The sons of Aser: Jamne and Jesua, and Jessuri and Beria, and Sara their sister. The sons of Beria: Heber and Melchiel. ^genesis-46-17-douay

18 These are the sons of Zelpha, whom Laban gave to Lia, his daughter. And these she bore to Jacob, sixteen souls. ^genesis-46-18-douay

19 The sons of Rachel, Jacob's wife: Joseph and Benjamin. ^genesis-46-19-douay

20 And sons were born to Joseph, in the land of Egypt, whom Aseneth, the daughter of Putiphare, priest of Heliopolis, bore him: Manasses and Ephraim. ^genesis-46-20-douay

21 The sons of Benjamin: Bela and Bechor, and Asbel and Gera, and Naaman and Echi, and Ross and Mophim, and Ophim and Ared. ^genesis-46-21-douay

22 These are the sons of Rachel, whom she bore to Jacob: all the souls, fourteen. ^genesis-46-22-douay

23 The sons of Dan: Husim. ^genesis-46-23-douay

24 The sons of Nephthali: Jaziel and Guni, and Jeser and Sallem. ^genesis-46-24-douay

25 These are the sons of Bala, whom Laban gave to Rachel, his daughter: and these she bore to Jacob: all the souls, seven. ^genesis-46-25-douay

26 All the souls that went with Jacob into Egypt, and that came out of his thigh, besides his sons' wives, sixty-six. ^genesis-46-26-douay

27 And the sons of Joseph, that were born to him in the land of Egypt, two souls. All the souls of the house of Jacob, that entered into Egypt, were seventy. ^genesis-46-27-douay

28 And he sent Juda before him to Joseph, to tell him; and that he should meet him in Gessen. ^genesis-46-28-douay

29 And when he was come thither, Joseph made ready his chariot, and went up to meet his father in the same place: and seeing him, he fell upon his neck, and embracing him, wept. ^genesis-46-29-douay

30 And the father said to Joseph: Now shall I die with joy, because I have seen thy face, and leave thee alive. ^genesis-46-30-douay

31 And Joseph said to his brethren, and to all his father's house: I will go up, and will tell Pharao, and will say to him: My brethren, and my father's house, that were in the land of Chanaan, are come to me: ^genesis-46-31-douay

32 And the men are shepherds, and their occupation is to feed cattle; their flocks, and herds, and all they have, they have brought with them. ^genesis-46-32-douay

33 And when he shall call you, and shall say: What is your occupation? ^genesis-46-33-douay

34 You shall answer: We, thy servants, are shepherds, from our infancy until now, both we and our fathers. And this you shall say, that you may dwell in the land of Gessen, because the Egyptians have all shepherds in abomination. ^genesis-46-34-douay
