# Colossians Chapter 3

He exhorts them to put off the old man, and to put on the new. The duties of wives and husbands, children and servants.

1 Therefore if you be risen with Christ, seek the things that are above, where Christ is sitting at the right hand of God. ^colossians-03-1-douay

2 Mind the things that are above, not the things that are upon the earth. ^colossians-03-2-douay

3 For you are dead: and your life is hid with Christ in God. ^colossians-03-3-douay

4 When Christ shall appear, who is your life, then you also shall appear with him in glory. ^colossians-03-4-douay

5 Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth: fornication, uncleanness, lust, evil concupiscence and covetousness, which is the service of idols. ^colossians-03-5-douay

6 For which things the wrath of God cometh upon the children of unbelief. ^colossians-03-6-douay

7 In which you also walked some time, when you lived in them. ^colossians-03-7-douay

8 But now put you also all away: anger, indignation, malice, blasphemy, filthy speech out of your mouth. ^colossians-03-8-douay

9 Lie not one to another: stripping yourselves of the old man with his deeds, ^colossians-03-9-douay

10 And putting on the new, him who is renewed unto knowledge, according to the image of him that created him. ^colossians-03-10-douay

11 Where there is neither Gentile nor Jew, circumcision nor uncircumcision, Barbarian nor Scythian, bond nor free. But Christ is all and in all. ^colossians-03-11-douay

12 Put ye on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, the bowels of mercy, benignity, humility, modesty, patience: ^colossians-03-12-douay

13 Bearing with one another and forgiving one another, if any have a complaint against another. Even as the Lord hath forgiven you, so do you also. ^colossians-03-13-douay

14 But above all these things have charity, which is the bond of perfection. ^colossians-03-14-douay

15 And let the peace of Christ rejoice in your hearts, wherein also you are called in one body: and be ye thankful. ^colossians-03-15-douay

16 Let the word of Christ dwell in you abundantly: in all wisdom, teaching and admonishing one another in psalms, hymns and spiritual canticles, singing in grace in your hearts to God. ^colossians-03-16-douay

17 All whatsoever you do in word or in work, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus Christ, giving thanks to God and the Father by him. ^colossians-03-17-douay

18 Wives, be subject to your husbands, as it behoveth in the Lord. ^colossians-03-18-douay

19 Husbands, love your wives and be not bitter towards them. ^colossians-03-19-douay

20 Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing to the Lord. ^colossians-03-20-douay

21 Fathers, provoke not your children to indignation, lest they be discouraged. ^colossians-03-21-douay

22 Servants, obey in all things your masters according to the flesh: not serving to the eye, as pleasing men: but in simplicity of heart, fearing God. ^colossians-03-22-douay

23 Whatsoever you do, do it from the heart, as to the Lord, and not to men: ^colossians-03-23-douay

24 Knowing that you shall receive of the Lord the reward of inheritance. Serve ye the Lord Christ. ^colossians-03-24-douay

25 For he that doth wrong shall receive for that which he hath done wrongfully. And there is no respect of persons with God. ^colossians-03-25-douay
