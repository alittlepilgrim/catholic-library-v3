# Hosea Chapter 8

The Israelites are threatened with destruction for their impiety and idolatry.

1 Let there be a trumpet in thy throat: like an eagle upon the house of the Lord: because they have transgressed my covenant: and have violated my law. ^hosea-08-1-douay

2 They shall call upon me: O my God, we, Israel, know thee. ^hosea-08-2-douay

3 Israel hath cast off the thing that is good, the enemy shall pursue him. ^hosea-08-3-douay

4 They have reigned, but not by me: they have been princes, and I knew not: of their silver and their gold they have made idols to themselves, that they might perish. ^hosea-08-4-douay

5 Thy calf, O Samaria, is cast off, my wrath is kindled against them. How long will they be incapable of being cleansed? ^hosea-08-5-douay

6 For itself also is the invention of Israel: a workman made it, and it is no god: for the calf of Samaria shall be turned to spiders' webs. ^hosea-08-6-douay

7 For they shall sow wind, and reap a whirlwind, there is no standing stalk in it, the bud shall yield no meal; and if it should yield, strangers shall eat it. ^hosea-08-7-douay

8 Israel is swallowed up: now is he become among the nations like an unclean vessel. ^hosea-08-8-douay

9 For they are gone up to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself: Ephraim hath given gifts to his lovers. ^hosea-08-9-douay

10 But even though they shall have hired the nations, now will I gather them together: and they shall rest a while from the burden of the king, and the princes. ^hosea-08-10-douay

11 Because Ephraim hath made many altars to sin: altars are become to him unto sin. ^hosea-08-11-douay

12 I shall write to him my manifold laws, which have been accounted as foreign. ^hosea-08-12-douay

13 They shall offer victims, they shall sacrifice flesh, and shall eat it, and the Lord will not receive them: now will he remember their iniquity, and will visit their sins: they shall return to Egypt. ^hosea-08-13-douay

14 And Israel hath forgotten his Maker, and hath built temples: and Juda hath built many fenced cities: and I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour the houses thereof. ^hosea-08-14-douay
