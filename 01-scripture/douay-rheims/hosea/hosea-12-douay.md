# Hosea Chapter 12

Israel is reproved for sin. God's favours to them.

1 Ephraim feedeth on the wind, and followeth the burning heat: all the day long he multiplied lies and desolation: and he hath made a covenant with the Assyrians, and carried oil into Egypt. ^hosea-12-1-douay

2 Therefore there is a judgment of the Lord with Juda, and a visitation for Jacob: he will render to him according to his ways, and according to his devices. ^hosea-12-2-douay

3 In the womb he supplanted his brother: and by his strength he had success with an angel. ^hosea-12-3-douay

4 And he prevailed over the angel, and was strengthened: he wept, and made supplication to him: he found him in Bethel, and there he spoke with us. ^hosea-12-4-douay

5 Even the Lord God of hosts, the Lord is his memorial. ^hosea-12-5-douay

6 Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and hope in thy God always. ^hosea-12-6-douay

7 He is like Chanaan, there is a deceitful balance in his hand, he hath loved oppression. ^hosea-12-7-douay

8 And Ephraim said: But yet I am become rich, I have found me an idol: all my labours shall not find me the iniquity that I have committed. ^hosea-12-8-douay

9 And I that am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt, will yet cause thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the feast. ^hosea-12-9-douay

10 And I have spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and I have used similitudes by the ministry of the prophets. ^hosea-12-10-douay

11 If Galaad be an idol, then in vain were they in Galgal offering sacrifices with bullocks: for their altars also are as heaps in the furrows of the field. **If Galaad be an idol, etc...** That is, if Galaad with all its idols and sacrifices be like a mere idol itself, being brought to nothing by Theglathphalasar: how vain is it to expect, that the idols worshipped in Galgal shall be of any service to the tribes that remain. ^hosea-12-11-douay

12 Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and was a keeper for a wife. ^hosea-12-12-douay

13 But the Lord by a prophet brought Israel out of Egypt: and he was preserved by a prophet. ^hosea-12-13-douay

14 Ephraim hath provoked me to wrath with his bitterness, and his blood shall come upon him, and his Lord will render his reproach unto him. ^hosea-12-14-douay
