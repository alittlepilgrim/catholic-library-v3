# Hosea Chapter 3

The prophet is commanded again to love an adulteress; to signify God's love to the synagogue. The wretched state of the Jews for a long time, till at last they shall be converted.

1 And the Lord said to me: Go yet again, and love a woman beloved of her friend, and an adulteress: as the Lord loveth the children of Israel, and they look to strange gods, and love the husks of the grapes. ^hosea-03-1-douay

2 And I bought her to me for fifteen pieces of silver, and for a core of barley, and for half a core of barley. ^hosea-03-2-douay

3 And I said to her: Thou shalt wait for me many days: thou shalt not play the harlot, and thou shalt be no man's, and I also will wait for thee. ^hosea-03-3-douay

4 For the children of Israel shall sit many days without king, and without prince, and without sacrifice, and without altar, and without ephod, and without theraphim. **Theraphim...** Images or representations. ^hosea-03-4-douay

5 And after this the children of Israel shall return and shall seek the Lord, their God, and David, their king: and they shall fear the Lord, and his goodness, in the last days. **David their king...** That is, Christ, who is of the house of David. ^hosea-03-5-douay
