# Ezra

## The Book of Ezra, Also Called the First Book of Esdras

This book takes its name from the writer: who was a holy priest, and doctor of the law. He is called by the Hebrews, *Ezra*. (*For more information, see the article* ESDRAS *in the Catholic Encyclopedia*.)

[[ezra-01-douay|**Chapter 1.**]] Cyrus king of Persia releaseth God's people from their captivity, with license to return and build the temple in Jerusalem: and restoreth the holy vessels which Nabuchodonosor had taken from thence.

[[ezra-02-douay|**Chapter 2.**]] The number of them that returned to Judea: their oblations.

[[ezra-03-douay|**Chapter 3.**]] An altar is built for sacrifice, the feast of tabernacles is solemnly celebrated, and the foundations of the temple are laid.

[[ezra-04-douay|**Chapter 4.**]] The Samaritans by their letter to the king hinder the building.

[[ezra-05-douay|**Chapter 5.**]] By the exhortation of Aggeus, and Zacharias, the people proceed in building the temple. Which their enemies strive in vain to hinder.

[[ezra-06-douay|**Chapter 6.**]] King Darius favoureth the building and contributeth to it.

[[ezra-07-douay|**Chapter 7.**]] Esdras goeth up to Jerusalem to teach, and assist the people, with a gracious decree of Artaxerxes.

[[ezra-08-douay|**Chapter 8.**]] The companions of Esdras. The fast which he appointed. They bring the holy vessels into the temple.

[[ezra-09-douay|**Chapter 9.**]] Esdras mourneth for the transgression of the people: his confession and prayer.

[[ezra-10-douay|**Chapter 10.**]] Order is given for discharging strange women: the names of the guilty.
