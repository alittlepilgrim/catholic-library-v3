# Ezra Chapter 8

The companions of Esdras. The fast which he appointed. They bring the holy vessels into the temple.

1 Now these are the chief of families, and the genealogy of them, who came up with me from Babylon in the reign of Artaxerxes the king. ^ezra-08-1-douay

2 Of the sons of Phinees, Gersom. Of the sons of Ithamar, Daniel. Of the sons of David, Hattus. ^ezra-08-2-douay

3 Of the sons of Sechenias, the son of Pharos, Zacharias, and with him were numbered a hundred and fifty men. ^ezra-08-3-douay

4 Of the sons of Phahath Moab, Eleoenai the son of Zareha, and with him two hundred men. ^ezra-08-4-douay

5 Of the sons of Sechenias, the son of Ezechiel, and with him three hundred men. ^ezra-08-5-douay

6 Of the sons of Adan, Abed the son of Jonathan, and with him fifty men. ^ezra-08-6-douay

7 Of the sons of Alam, Isaias the son of Athalias, and with him seventy men. ^ezra-08-7-douay

8 Of the sons of Saphatia: Zebodia the son of Michael, and with him eighty men. ^ezra-08-8-douay

9 Of the sons of Joab, Obedia the son of Jahiel, and with him two hundred and eighteen men. ^ezra-08-9-douay

10 Of the sons of Selomith, the son of Josphia, and with him a hundred and sixty men. ^ezra-08-10-douay

11 Of the sons of Bebai, Zacharias the son of Bebai: and with him eight and twenty men. ^ezra-08-11-douay

12 Of the sons of Azgad, Joanan the son of Eccetan, and with him a hundred and ten men. ^ezra-08-12-douay

13 Of the sons of Adonicam, who were the last: and these are their names: Eliphelet, and Jehiel, and Samaias, and with them sixty men. ^ezra-08-13-douay

14 Of the sons of Begui, Uthai and Zachur, and with them seventy men. ^ezra-08-14-douay

15 And I gathered them together to the river, which runneth down to Ahava, and we stayed there three days: and I sought among the people and among the priests for the sons of Levi, and found none there. ^ezra-08-15-douay

16 So I sent Eliezer, and Ariel, and Semeias, and Elnathan, and Jarib, and another Elnathan, and Nathan, and Zacharias, and Mosollam, chief men: and Joiarib, and Elnathan, wise men. ^ezra-08-16-douay

17 And I sent them to Eddo, who is chief in the place of Chasphia, and I put in their mouth the words that they should speak to Eddo, and his brethren the Nathinites in the place of Chasphia, that they should bring us ministers of the house of our God. ^ezra-08-17-douay

18 And by the good hand of our God upon us, they brought us a most learned man of the sons of Moholi the son of Levi the son of Israel, and Sarabias and his sons, and his brethren eighteen, ^ezra-08-18-douay

19 And Hasabias, and with him Isaias of the sons of Merari, and his brethren, and his sons twenty. ^ezra-08-19-douay

20 And of the Nathinites, whom David, and the princes gave for the service of the Levites, Nathinites two hundred and twenty: all these were called by their names. ^ezra-08-20-douay

21 And I proclaimed there a fast by the river Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before the Lord our God, and might ask of him a right way for us and for our children, and for all our substance. **And I proclaimed a fast...** It is not enough to part from Babylon, that is, figuratively from sin, but we must also do works of penance; and therefore Esdras here proclaimed an extraordinary fast to those that were come from captivity. This shows that fasting was commanded and practised from the earliest times. ^ezra-08-21-douay

22 For I was ashamed to ask the king for aid and for horsemen, to defend us from the enemy in the way: because we had said to the king: The hand of our God is upon all them that seek him in goodness: and his power and strength, and wrath upon all them that forsake him. ^ezra-08-22-douay

23 And we fasted, and besought our God for this: and it fell out prosperously unto us. ^ezra-08-23-douay

24 And I separated twelve of the chief of the priests, Sarabias, and Hasabias, and with them ten of their brethren, ^ezra-08-24-douay

25 And I weighed unto them the silver and gold, and the vessels consecrated for the house of our God, which the king and his counsellors, and his princes, and all Israel, that were found had offered. ^ezra-08-25-douay

26 And I weighed to their hands six hundred and fifty talents of silver, and a hundred vessels of silver, and a hundred talents of gold, ^ezra-08-26-douay

27 And twenty cups of gold, of a thousand solids, and two vessels of the best shining brass, beautiful as gold. ^ezra-08-27-douay

28 And I said to them: You are the holy ones of the Lord, and the vessels are holy, and the silver and gold, that is freely offered to the Lord the God of our fathers. ^ezra-08-28-douay

29 Watch ye and keep them, till you deliver them by weight before the chief of the priests, and of the Levites, and the heads of the families of Israel in Jerusalem, into the treasure of the house of the Lord. ^ezra-08-29-douay

30 And the priests and the Levites received the weight of the silver and gold, and the vessels, to carry them to Jerusalem to the house of our God. ^ezra-08-30-douay

31 Then we set forward from the river Ahava on the twelfth day of the first month to go to Jerusalem: and the hand of our God was upon us, and delivered us from the hand of the enemy, and of such as lay in wait by the way. ^ezra-08-31-douay

32 And we came to Jerusalem, and we stayed there three days. ^ezra-08-32-douay

33 And on the fourth day the silver and the gold, and the vessels were weighed in the house of our God by the hand of Meremoth the son of Urias the priest, and with him was Eleazar the son of Phinees, and with them Jozabad the son of Josue, and Noadaia the son of Benoi, Levites. ^ezra-08-33-douay

34 According to the number and weight of everything: and all the weight was written at that time. ^ezra-08-34-douay

35 Moreover the children of them that had been carried away that were come out of the captivity, offered holocausts to the God of Israel, twelve calves for all the people of Israel, ninety-six rams, seventy-seven lambs, and twelve he goats for sin: all for a holocaust to the Lord. ^ezra-08-35-douay

36 And they gave the king's edicts to the lords that were from the king's court, and the governors beyond the river, and they furthered the people and the house of God. ^ezra-08-36-douay
