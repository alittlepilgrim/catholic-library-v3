# Ezra Chapter 2

The number of them that returned to Judea: their oblations.

1 Now these are the children of the province, that went out of the captivity, which Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon had carried away to Babylon, and who returned to Jerusalem and Juda, every man to his city. ^ezra-02-1-douay

2 Who came with Zorobabel, Josue, Nehemia, Saraia, Rahelaia, Mardochai, Belsan, Mesphar, Beguai, Rehum, Baana. The number of the men of the people of Israel: ^ezra-02-2-douay

3 The children of Pharos two thousand one hundred seventy-two. ^ezra-02-3-douay

4 The children of Sephatia, three hundred seventy-two. ^ezra-02-4-douay

5 The children of Area, seven hundred seventy-five. ^ezra-02-5-douay

6 The children of Phahath Moab, of the children of Josue: Joab, Two thousand eight hundred twelve. ^ezra-02-6-douay

7 The children of Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty-four. ^ezra-02-7-douay

8 The children of Zethua, nine hundred forty-five. ^ezra-02-8-douay

9 The children of Zachai, seven hundred sixty. ^ezra-02-9-douay

10 The children of Bani, six hundred forty-two. ^ezra-02-10-douay

11 The children of Bebai, six hundred twenty-three. ^ezra-02-11-douay

12 The children of Azgad, a thousand two hundred twenty-two. ^ezra-02-12-douay

13 The children of Adonicam, six hundred sixty-six. ^ezra-02-13-douay

14 The children of Beguai, two thousand fifty-six. ^ezra-02-14-douay

15 The children of Adin, four hundred fifty-four. ^ezra-02-15-douay

16 The children of Ather, who were of Ezechias, ninety-eight. ^ezra-02-16-douay

17 The children of Besai, three hundred and twenty-three. ^ezra-02-17-douay

18 The children of Jora, a hundred and twelve. ^ezra-02-18-douay

19 The children of Hasum, two hundred twenty-three. ^ezra-02-19-douay

20 The children of Gebbar, ninety-five. ^ezra-02-20-douay

21 The children of Bethlehem, a hundred twenty-three. ^ezra-02-21-douay

22 The men of Netupha, fifty-six. ^ezra-02-22-douay

23 The men of Anathoth, a hundred twenty-eight. ^ezra-02-23-douay

24 The children of Azmaveth, forty-two. ^ezra-02-24-douay

25 The children of Cariathiarim, Cephira, and Beroth, seven hundred forty-three. ^ezra-02-25-douay

26 The children of Rama and Gabaa, six hundred twenty-one. ^ezra-02-26-douay

27 The men of Machmas, a hundred twenty-two. ^ezra-02-27-douay

28 The men of Bethel and Hai, two hundred twenty-three. ^ezra-02-28-douay

29 The children of Nebo, fifty-two. ^ezra-02-29-douay

30 The children of Megbis, a hundred fifty-six. ^ezra-02-30-douay

31 The children of the other Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty-five. ^ezra-02-31-douay

32 The children of Harim, three hundred and twenty. ^ezra-02-32-douay

33 The children of Lod, Hadid and Ono, seven hundred twenty-five. ^ezra-02-33-douay

34 The children of Jericho, three hundred forty-five. ^ezra-02-34-douay

35 The children of Senaa, three thousand six hundred thirty. ^ezra-02-35-douay

36 The priests: the children of Jadaia of the house of Josue, nine hundred seventy-three. ^ezra-02-36-douay

37 The children of Emmer, a thousand fifty-two. ^ezra-02-37-douay

38 The children of Pheshur, a thousand two hundred forty-seven. ^ezra-02-38-douay

39 The children of Harim, a thousand and seventeen. ^ezra-02-39-douay

40 The Levites: the children of Josue and of Cedmihel, the children of Odovia, seventy-four. ^ezra-02-40-douay

41 The singing men: the children of Asaph, a hundred twenty-eight. ^ezra-02-41-douay

42 The children of the porters: the children of Sellum, the children of Ater, the children of Telmon, the children of Accub, the children of Hatita, the children of Sobai: in all a hundred thirty-nine. ^ezra-02-42-douay

43 The Nathinites: the children of Siha, the children of Hasupha, the children of Tabbaoth, ^ezra-02-43-douay

44 The children of Ceros, the children of Sia, the children of Phadon, ^ezra-02-44-douay

45 The children of Lebana, the children of Hegaba, the children of Accub, ^ezra-02-45-douay

46 The children of Hagab, the children of Semlai, the children of Hanan, ^ezra-02-46-douay

47 The children of Gaddel, the children of Gaher, the children of Raaia, ^ezra-02-47-douay

48 The children of Rasin, the children of Necoda, the children of Gazam, ^ezra-02-48-douay

49 The children of Asa, the children of Phasea, the children of Besee, ^ezra-02-49-douay

50 The children of Asena, the children of Munim, the children of Nephusim, ^ezra-02-50-douay

51 The children of Bacbuc, the children of Hacupha, the children of Harhur, ^ezra-02-51-douay

52 The children of Besluth, the children of Mahida, the children of Harsa, ^ezra-02-52-douay

53 The children of Bercos, the children of Sisara, the children of Thema, ^ezra-02-53-douay

54 The children of Nasia, the children of Hatipha, ^ezra-02-54-douay

55 The children of the servants of Solomon, the children of Sotai, the children of Sopheret, the children of Pharuda, ^ezra-02-55-douay

56 The children of Jala, the children of Dercon, the children of Geddel, ^ezra-02-56-douay

57 The children of Saphatia, the children of Hatil, the children of Phochereth, which were of Asebaim, the children of Ami, ^ezra-02-57-douay

58 All the Nathinites, and the children of the servants of Solomon, three hundred ninety-two. ^ezra-02-58-douay

59 And these are they that came up from Thelmela, Thelharsa, Cherub, and Adon, and Emer. And they could not show the house of their fathers and their seed, whether they were of Israel. ^ezra-02-59-douay

60 The children of Dalaia, the children of Tobia, the children of Necoda, six hundred fifty-two. ^ezra-02-60-douay

61 And of the children of the priests: the children of Hobia, the children of Accos, the children of Berzellai, who took a wife of the daughters of Berzellai, the Galaadite, and was called by their name: ^ezra-02-61-douay

62 These sought the writing of their genealogy, and found it not, and they were cast out of the priesthood. ^ezra-02-62-douay

63 And Athersatha said to them, that they should not eat of the holy of holies, till there arose a priest learned and perfect. ^ezra-02-63-douay

64 All the multitudes as one man, were forty-two thousand three hundred and sixty: **Forty-two thousand, etc...** Those who are reckoned up above of the tribes of Juda, Benjamin, and Levi, fall short of this number. The rest, who must be taken in to make up the whole sum, were of the other tribes. ^ezra-02-64-douay

65 Besides their menservants, and womenservants, of whom there were seven thousand three hundred and thirty-seven: and among them singing men, and singing women two hundred. ^ezra-02-65-douay

66 Their horses seven hundred thirty-six, their mules two hundred forty-five, ^ezra-02-66-douay

67 Their camels four hundred thirty-five, their asses six thousand seven hundred and twenty. ^ezra-02-67-douay

68 And some of the chief of the fathers, when they came to the temple of the Lord, which is in Jerusalem, offered freely to the house of the Lord to build it in its place. ^ezra-02-68-douay

69 According to their ability, they gave towards the expenses of the work, sixty-one thousand solids of gold, five thousand pounds of silver, and a hundred garments for the priests. ^ezra-02-69-douay

70 So the priests and the Levites, and some of the people, and the singing men, and the porters, and the Nathinites dwelt in their cities, and all Israel in their cities. ^ezra-02-70-douay
