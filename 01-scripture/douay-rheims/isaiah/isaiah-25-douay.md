# Isaiah Chapter 25

A canticle of thanksgiving for God's judgments and benefits.

1 O Lord, thou art my God, I will exalt O thee, and give glory to thy name: for thou hast done wonderful things, thy designs of old faithful, amen. ^isaiah-25-1-douay

2 For thou hast reduced the city to a heap, the strong city to ruin, the house of strangers, to be no city, and to be no more built up for ever. ^isaiah-25-2-douay

3 Therefore shall a strong people praise thee, the city of mighty nations shall fear thee. ^isaiah-25-3-douay

4 Because thou hast been a strength to the poor, a strength to the needy in his distress: a refuge from the whirlwind, a shadow from the heat. For the blast of the mighty is like a whirlwind beating against a wall. ^isaiah-25-4-douay

5 Thou shalt bring down the tumult of strangers, as heat in thirst: and as with heat under a burning cloud, thou shalt make the branch of the mighty to wither away. ^isaiah-25-5-douay

6 And the Lord of hosts shall make unto all people in this mountain, a feast of fat things, a feast of wine, of fat things full of marrow, of wine purified from the lees. ^isaiah-25-6-douay

7 And he shall destroy in this mountain the face of the bond with which all people were tied, and the web that he began over all nations. ^isaiah-25-7-douay

8 He shall cast death down headlong for ever: and the Lord God shall wipe away tears from every face, and the reproach of his people he shall take away from off the whole earth: for the Lord hath spoken it. ^isaiah-25-8-douay

9 And they shall say in that day: Lo, this is our God, we have waited for him, and he will save us: this is the Lord, we have patiently waited for him, we shall rejoice and be joyful in his salvation. ^isaiah-25-9-douay

10 For the hand of the Lord shall rest in this mountain: and Moab shall be trodden down under him, as straw is broken in pieces with the wain. **Moab...** That is, the reprobate, whose eternal punishment, from which they can no way escape, is described under these figures. ^isaiah-25-10-douay

11 And he shall stretch forth his hands under him, as he that swimmeth stretcheth forth his hands to swim: and he shall bring down his glory with the dashing of his hands. ^isaiah-25-11-douay

12 And the bulwarks of thy high walls shall fall, and be brought low, and shall be pulled down to the ground, even to the dust. ^isaiah-25-12-douay
