# Isaiah Chapter 54

The Gentiles, who were barren before, shall multiply in the Church of Christ: from which God's mercy shall never depart.

1 Give praise, O thou barren, that bearest not: sing forth praise, and make a joyful noise, thou that didst not travail with child: for many are the children of the desolate, more than of her that hath a husband, saith the Lord. ^isaiah-54-1-douay

2 Enlarge the place of thy tent, and stretch out the skins of thy tabernacles, spare not: lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes. ^isaiah-54-2-douay

3 For thou shalt pass on to the right hand, and to the left: and thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles, and shall inhabit the desolate cities. ^isaiah-54-3-douay

4 Fear not, for thou shalt not be confounded, nor blush: for thou shalt not be put to shame, because thou shalt forget the shame of thy youth, and shalt remember no more the reproach of thy widowhood. ^isaiah-54-4-douay

5 For he that made thee shall rule over thee, the Lord of hosts is his name: and thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, shall be called the God of all the earth. ^isaiah-54-5-douay

6 For the Lord hath called thee as a woman forsaken and mourning in spirit, and as a wife cast off from her youth, said thy God. ^isaiah-54-6-douay

7 For a small moment have I forsaken thee, but with great mercies will I gather thee. ^isaiah-54-7-douay

8 In a moment of indignation have I hid my face a little while from thee, but with everlasting kindness have I had mercy on thee, said the Lord thy Redeemer. ^isaiah-54-8-douay

9 This thing is to me as in the days of Noe, to whom I swore, that I would no more bring in the waters of Noe upon the earth: so have I sworn not to be angry with thee, and not to rebuke thee. ^isaiah-54-9-douay

10 For the mountains shall be moved, and the hills shall tremble; but my mercy shall not depart from thee, and the covenant of my peace shall not be moved: said the Lord that hath mercy on thee. ^isaiah-54-10-douay

11 O poor little one, tossed with tempest, without all comfort, behold I will lay thy stones in order, and will lay thy foundations with sapphires, ^isaiah-54-11-douay

12 And I will make thy bulwarks of jasper: and thy gates of graven stones, and all thy borders of desirable stones. ^isaiah-54-12-douay

13 All thy children shall be taught of the Lord: and great shall be the peace of thy children. ^isaiah-54-13-douay

14 And thou shalt be founded in justice: depart far from oppression, for thou shalt not fear; and from terror, for it shall not come near thee. ^isaiah-54-14-douay

15 Behold, an inhabitant shall come, who was not with me, he that was a stranger to thee before, shall be joined to thee. ^isaiah-54-15-douay

16 Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and bringeth forth an instrument for his work, and I have created the killer to destroy. ^isaiah-54-16-douay

17 No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper: and every tongue that resisteth thee in judgment, thou shalt condemn. This is the inheritance of the servants of the Lord, and their justice with me, saith the Lord. ^isaiah-54-17-douay
