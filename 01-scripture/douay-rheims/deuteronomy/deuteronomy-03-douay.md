# Deuteronomy Chapter 3

The victory over Og king of Basan. Ruben, Gad, and half the tribe of Manasseh receive their possession on the other side of Jordan.

1 Then we turned and went by the way of Basan: and Og the king of Basan came out to meet us with his people to fight in Edrai. ^deuteronomy-03-1-douay

2 And the Lord said to me: Fear him not: because he is delivered into thy hand, with all his people and his land: and thou shalt do to him as thou hast done to Sehon king of the Amorrhites, that dwelt in Hesebon. ^deuteronomy-03-2-douay

3 So the Lord our God delivered into our hands, Og also, the king of Basan, and all his people: and we utterly destroyed them, ^deuteronomy-03-3-douay

4 Wasting all his cities at one time, there was not a town that escaped us: sixty cities, all the country of Argob the kingdom of Og in Basan. ^deuteronomy-03-4-douay

5 All the cities were fenced with very high walls, and with gates and bars, besides innumerable towns that had no walls. ^deuteronomy-03-5-douay

6 And we utterly destroyed them, as we had done to Sehon the king of Hesebon, destroying every city, men and women and children: ^deuteronomy-03-6-douay

7 But the cattle and the spoils of the cities we took for our prey. ^deuteronomy-03-7-douay

8 And we took at that time the land out of the hand of the two kings of the Amorrhites, that were beyond the Jordan: from the torrent Arnon unto the mount Hermon, ^deuteronomy-03-8-douay

9 Which the Sidonians call Sarion, and the Amorrhites Sanir: ^deuteronomy-03-9-douay

10 All the cities that are situate in the plain, and all the land of Galaad and Basan as far as Selcha and Edrai, cities of the kingdom of Og in Basan. ^deuteronomy-03-10-douay

11 For only Og king of Basan remained of the race of the giants. His bed of iron is shown, which is in Rabbath of the children of Ammon, being nine cubits long, and four broad after the measure of the cubit of a man's hand. ^deuteronomy-03-11-douay

12 And we possessed the land at that time from Aroer, which is upon the bank of the torrent Arnon, unto the half of mount Galaad: and I gave the cities thereof to Ruben and Gad. ^deuteronomy-03-12-douay

13 And I delivered the other part of Galaad, and all Basan the kingdom of Og to the half tribe of Manasses, all the country of Argob: and all Basan is called the *Land of giants*. ^deuteronomy-03-13-douay

14 Jair the son of Manasses possessed all the country of Argob unto the borders of Gessuri, and Machati. And he called Basan by his own name, Havoth Jair, that is to say, the towns of Jair, until this present day. ^deuteronomy-03-14-douay

15 To Machir also I gave Galaad. ^deuteronomy-03-15-douay

16 And to the tribes of Ruben and Gad I gave of the land of Galaad as far as the torrent Arnon, half the torrent, and the confines even unto the torrent Jeboc, which is the border of the children of Ammon: ^deuteronomy-03-16-douay

17 And the plain of the wilderness, and the Jordan, and the borders of Cenereth unto the sea of the desert, which is the most salt sea, to the foot of mount Phasga eastward. ^deuteronomy-03-17-douay

18 And I commanded you at that time, saying: The Lord your God giveth you this land for an inheritance, go ye well appointed before your brethren the children of Israel, all the strong men of you. ^deuteronomy-03-18-douay

19 Leaving your wives and children and cattle. For I know you have much cattle, and they must remain in the cities, which I have delivered to you. ^deuteronomy-03-19-douay

20 Until the Lord give rest to your brethren, as he hath given to you: and they also possess the land, which he will give them beyond the Jordan: then shall every man return to his possession, which I have given you. ^deuteronomy-03-20-douay

21 I commanded Joshua also at that time, saying: Thy eyes have seen what the Lord your God hath done to these two kings: so will he do to all the kingdoms to which thou shalt pass. ^deuteronomy-03-21-douay

22 Fear them not: for the Lord your God will fight for you. ^deuteronomy-03-22-douay

23 And I besought the Lord at that time, saying: ^deuteronomy-03-23-douay

24 Lord God, thou hast begun to show unto thy servant thy greatness, and most mighty hand, for there is no other God either in heaven or earth, that is able to do thy works, or to be compared to thy strength. ^deuteronomy-03-24-douay

25 I will pass over therefore, and will see this excellent land beyond the Jordan, and this goodly mountain, and Libanus. ^deuteronomy-03-25-douay

26 And the Lord was angry with me on your account and heard me not, but said to me: It is enough: speak no more to me of this matter. ^deuteronomy-03-26-douay

27 Go up to the top of Phasga, and cast thy eyes round about to the west, and to the north, and to the south, and to the east, and behold it, for thou shalt not pass this Jordan. ^deuteronomy-03-27-douay

28 Command Joshua, and encourage and strengthen him: for he shall go before this people, and shall divide unto them the land which thou shalt see. ^deuteronomy-03-28-douay

29 And we abode in the valley over against the temple of Phogor. ^deuteronomy-03-29-douay
