# 2 Chronicles Chapter 10

Roboam answers the people roughly: upon which ten tribes revolt.

1 And Roboam went to Sichem: for thither all Israel were assembled, to make him king. ^2-chronicles-10-1-douay

2 And when Jeroboam the son of Nabat, who was in Egypt, (for he was fled thither from Solomon), heard it, forthwith he returned. ^2-chronicles-10-2-douay

3 And they sent for him, and he came with all Israel, and they spoke to Roboam, saying: ^2-chronicles-10-3-douay

4 Thy father oppressed with a most grievous yoke, do thou govern us with a lighter hand than thy father, who laid upon us a heavy servitude, and ease some thing of the burden, that we may serve thee. ^2-chronicles-10-4-douay

5 And he said to them: Come to me again after three days. And when the people were gone, ^2-chronicles-10-5-douay

6 He took counsel with the ancients, who had stood before his father Solomon, while he yet lived, saying: What counsel give you to me, that I may answer the people? ^2-chronicles-10-6-douay

7 And they said to him: If thou please this people, and soothe them with kind words, they will be thy servants for ever. ^2-chronicles-10-7-douay

8 But he forsook the counsel of the ancients, and began to treat with the young men, that had been brought up with him, and were in his train. ^2-chronicles-10-8-douay

9 And he said to them: What seemeth good to you? or what shall I answer this people, who have said to me: Ease the yoke which thy father laid upon us? ^2-chronicles-10-9-douay

10 But they answered as young men, and brought up with him in pleasures, and said: Thus shalt thou speak to the people, that said to thee: Thy father made our yoke heavy, do thou ease it: thus shalt thou answer them: My little finger is thicker than the loins of my father. ^2-chronicles-10-10-douay

11 My father laid upon you a heavy yoke, and I will add more weight to it: my father beat you with scourges, but I will beat you with scorpions. ^2-chronicles-10-11-douay

12 So Jeroboam, and all the people came to Roboam the third day, as he commanded them. ^2-chronicles-10-12-douay

13 And the king answered roughly, leaving the counsel of the ancients. ^2-chronicles-10-13-douay

14 And he spoke according to the advice of the young men: My father laid upon you a heavy yoke, which I will make heavier: my father beat you with scourges, but I will beat you with scorpions. ^2-chronicles-10-14-douay

15 And he condescended not to the people's requests: for it was the will of God, that his word might be fulfilled which he had spoken by the hand of Ahias the Silonite to Jeroboam the son of Nabat. ^2-chronicles-10-15-douay

16 And all the people upon the king's speaking roughly, said thus unto him: We have no part in David, nor inheritance in the son of Isai. Return to thy dwellings, O Israel, and do thou, O David feed thy own house. And Israel went away to their dwellings. ^2-chronicles-10-16-douay

17 But Roboam reigned over the children of Israel that dwelt in the cities of Juda. ^2-chronicles-10-17-douay

18 And king Roboam sent Aduram, who was over the tributes, and the children of Israel stoned him, and he died: and king Roboam made haste to get up into his chariot, and fled into Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-10-18-douay

19 And Israel revolted from the house of David unto this day. ^2-chronicles-10-19-douay
