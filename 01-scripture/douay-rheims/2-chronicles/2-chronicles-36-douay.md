# 2 Chronicles Chapter 36

The reigns of Joachaz, Joakim, Joachin, and Sedecias: the captivity of Babylon released at length by Cyrus.

1 Then the people of the land took Joachaz the son of Josias, and made him king instead of his father in Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-36-1-douay

2 Joachaz was three and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-36-2-douay

3 And the king of Egypt came to Jerusalem, and deposed him, and condemned the land in a hundred talents of silver, and a talent of gold. ^2-chronicles-36-3-douay

4 And he made Eliakim his brother king in his stead, over Juda and Jerusalem: and he turned his name to Joakim: but he took Joachaz with him and carried him away into Egypt. ^2-chronicles-36-4-douay

5 Joakim was five and twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and he did evil before the Lord his God. ^2-chronicles-36-5-douay

6 Against him came up Nabuchodonosor king of the Chaldeans, and led him bound in chains into Babylon. ^2-chronicles-36-6-douay

7 And he carried also thither the vessels of the Lord, and put them in his temple. ^2-chronicles-36-7-douay

8 But the rest of the acts of Joakim, and his abominations, which he wrought, and the things that were found in him, are contained in the book of the kings of Juda and Israel. And Joachin his son reigned in his stead. ^2-chronicles-36-8-douay

9 Joachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem, and he did evil in the sight of the Lord. **Eight years old...** He was associated by his father to the kingdom, when he was but eight years old; but after his father's death, when he reigned alone, he was eighteen years old. [[2-kings-24-douay|2 Kings 24:8]]. ^2-chronicles-36-9-douay

10 And at the return of the year, king Nabuchodonosor sent, and brought him to Babylon, carrying away at the same time the most precious vessels of the house of the Lord: and he made Sedecias his uncle king over Juda and Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-36-10-douay

11 Sedecias was one and twenty years old when he began to reign: and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-36-11-douay

12 And he did evil in the eyes of the Lord his God, and did not reverence the face of Jeremiah the prophet speaking to him from the mouth of the Lord. ^2-chronicles-36-12-douay

13 He also revolted from king Nabuchodonosor, who had made him swear by God: and he hardened his neck and his heart, from returning to the Lord the God of Israel. ^2-chronicles-36-13-douay

14 Moreover all the chief of the priests, and the people wickedly transgressed according to all the abominations of the Gentiles: and they defiled the house of the Lord, which he had sanctified to himself in Jerusalem. ^2-chronicles-36-14-douay

15 And the Lord the God of their fathers sent to them, by the hand of his messengers, rising early, and daily admonishing them: because he spared his people and his dwelling place. ^2-chronicles-36-15-douay

16 But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused the prophets, until the wrath of the Lord arose against his people, and there was no remedy. ^2-chronicles-36-16-douay

17 For he brought upon them the king of the Chaldeans, and he slew their young men with the sword in the house of his sanctuary, he had no compassion on young man, or maiden, old man or even him that stooped for age, but he delivered them all into his hands. ^2-chronicles-36-17-douay

18 And all the vessels of the house of Lord, great and small, and the treasures of the temple and of the king, and of the princes he carried away to Babylon. ^2-chronicles-36-18-douay

19 And the enemies set fire to the house of God, and broke down the wall of Jerusalem, burnt all the towers, and what soever was precious they destroyed. ^2-chronicles-36-19-douay

20 Whosoever escaped the sword, was led into Babylon, and there served the king and his sons, till the reign of the king of Persia, ^2-chronicles-36-20-douay

21 That the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremiah might be fulfilled, and the land might keep her sabbaths: for all the days of the desolation she kept a sabbath, till the seventy years were expired. ^2-chronicles-36-21-douay

22 But in the first year of Cyrus king of the Persians, to fulfil the word of the Lord, which he had spoken by the mouth of Jeremiah, the Lord stirred up the heart of Cyrus, king of the Persians: who commanded it to be proclaimed through all his kingdom, and by writing also, saying: ^2-chronicles-36-22-douay

23 Thus saith Cyrus king of the Persians: All the kingdoms of the earth hath the Lord the God of heaven given to me, and he hath charged me to build him a house in Jerusalem, which is in Judea: who is there among you of all his people? The Lord his God be with him, and let him go up. ^2-chronicles-36-23-douay
