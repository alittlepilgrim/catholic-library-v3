# 2 Maccabees Chapter 7

The glorious martyrdom of the seven brethren and their mother.

1 It came to pass also, that seven brethren, together with their mother, were apprehended, and compelled by the king to eat swine's flesh against the law, for which end they were tormented with whips and scourges. ^2-maccabees-07-1-douay

2 But one of them, who was the eldest, said thus: What wouldst thou ask, or learn of us? we are ready to die, rather than to transgress the laws of God, received from our fathers. ^2-maccabees-07-2-douay

3 Then the king being angry, commanded frying pans and brazen caldrons to be made hot: which forthwith being heated, ^2-maccabees-07-3-douay

4 He commanded to cut out the tongue of him that had spoken first: and the skin of his head being drawn off, to chop off also the extremities of his hands and feet, the rest of his brethren and his mother looking on. ^2-maccabees-07-4-douay

5 And when he was now maimed in all parts, he commanded him, being yet alive, to be brought to the fire, and to be fried in the frying pan: and while he was suffering therein long torments, the rest, together with the mother, exhorted one another to die manfully, ^2-maccabees-07-5-douay

6 Saying: The Lord God will look upon the truth, and will take pleasure in us, as Moses declared in the profession of the canticle; And in his servants he will take pleasure. ^2-maccabees-07-6-douay

7 So when the first was dead after this manner, they brought the next to make him a mocking stock: and when they had pulled off the skin of his head with the hair, they asked him if he would eat, before he were punished throughout the whole body in every limb. ^2-maccabees-07-7-douay

8 But he answered in his own language, and said: I will not do it. Wherefore he also, in the next place, received the torments of the first: ^2-maccabees-07-8-douay

9 And when he was at the last gasp, he said thus: Thou indeed, O most wicked man, destroyest us out of this present life: but the King of the world will raise us up, who die for his laws, in the resurrection of eternal life. ^2-maccabees-07-9-douay

10 After him the third was made a mocking stock, and when he was required, he quickly put forth his tongue, and courageously stretched out his hands: ^2-maccabees-07-10-douay

11 And said with confidence: These I have from heaven, but for the laws of God I now despise them, because I hope to receive them again from him. ^2-maccabees-07-11-douay

12 So that the king, and they that were with him, wondered at the young man's courage, because he esteemed the torments as nothing. ^2-maccabees-07-12-douay

13 And after he was thus dead, they tormented the fourth in the like manner. ^2-maccabees-07-13-douay

14 And when he was now ready to die, he spoke thus: It is better, being put to death by men, to look for hope from God, to be raised up again by him; for, as to thee, thou shalt have no resurrection unto life. ^2-maccabees-07-14-douay

15 And when they had brought the fifth, they tormented him. But he, looking upon the king, ^2-maccabees-07-15-douay

16 Said: Whereas thou hast power among men though thou art corruptible, thou dost what thou wilt but think not that our nation is forsaken by God. ^2-maccabees-07-16-douay

17 But stay patiently a while, and thou shalt see his great power, in what manner he will torment thee and thy seed. ^2-maccabees-07-17-douay

18 After him they brought the sixth, and he being ready to die, spoke thus: Be not deceived without cause: for we suffer these things for ourselves, having sinned against our God, and things worthy of admiration are done to us: ^2-maccabees-07-18-douay

19 But do not think that thou shalt escape unpunished, for that thou hast attempted to fight against God. ^2-maccabees-07-19-douay

20 Now the mother was to be admired above measure, and worthy to be remembered by good men, who beheld her seven sons slain in the space of one day, and bore it with a good courage, for the hope that she had in God: ^2-maccabees-07-20-douay

21 And she bravely exhorted every one of them in her own language, being filled with wisdom; and joining a man's heart to a woman's thought, ^2-maccabees-07-21-douay

22 She said to them: I know not how you were formed in my womb; for I neither gave you breath, nor soul, nor life, neither did I frame the limbs of every one of you. ^2-maccabees-07-22-douay

23 But the Creator of the world, that formed the nativity of man, and that found out the origin of all, he will restore to you again, in his mercy, both breath and life, as now you despise yourselves for the sake of his laws. ^2-maccabees-07-23-douay

24 Now Antiochus, thinking himself despised, and withal despising the voice of the upbraider, when the youngest was yet alive, did not only exhort him by words, but also assured him with an oath, that he would make him a rich and a happy man, and, if he would turn from the laws of his fathers, would take him for a friend, and furnish him with things necessary. ^2-maccabees-07-24-douay

25 But when the young man was not moved with these things, the king called the mother, and counselled her to deal with the young man to save his life. ^2-maccabees-07-25-douay

26 And when he had exhorted her with many words she promised that she would counsel her son. ^2-maccabees-07-26-douay

27 So bending herself towards him, mocking the cruel tyrant, she said in her own language: My son have pity upon me, that bore thee nine months in my womb, and gave thee suck three years, and nourished thee, and brought thee up unto this age. ^2-maccabees-07-27-douay

28 I beseech thee, my son, look upon heaven and earth, and all that is in them, and consider that God made them out of nothing, and mankind also: ^2-maccabees-07-28-douay

29 So thou shalt not fear this tormentor, but being made a worthy partner with thy brethren, receive death, that in that mercy I may receive thee again with thy brethren. ^2-maccabees-07-29-douay

30 While she was yet speaking these words, the young man said: For whom do you stay? I will not obey the commandment of the king, but the commandment of the law which was given us by Moses. ^2-maccabees-07-30-douay

31 But thou that hast been the author of all mischief against the Hebrews, shalt not escape the hand of God. ^2-maccabees-07-31-douay

32 For we suffer thus for our sins. ^2-maccabees-07-32-douay

33 And though the Lord, our God, is angry with us a little while, for our chastisement and correction, yet he will be reconciled again to his servants. ^2-maccabees-07-33-douay

34 But thou, O wicked, and of all men most flagitious, be not lifted up without cause with vain hopes, whilst thou art raging against his servants. ^2-maccabees-07-34-douay

35 For thou hast not yet escaped the judgment of the Almighty God, who beholdeth all things. ^2-maccabees-07-35-douay

36 For my brethren having now undergone a short pain, are under the covenant of eternal life: but thou, by the judgment of God, shalt receive just punishment for thy pride. ^2-maccabees-07-36-douay

37 But I, like my brethren, offer up my life and my body for the laws of our fathers: calling upon God to be speedily merciful to our nation, and that thou by torments and stripes mayst confess that he alone is God. ^2-maccabees-07-37-douay

38 But in me, and in my brethren, the wrath of the Almighty, which hath justly been brought upon all our nation, shall cease. ^2-maccabees-07-38-douay

39 Then the king being incensed with anger, raged against him more cruelly than all the rest, taking it grievously that he was mocked. ^2-maccabees-07-39-douay

40 So this man also died undefiled, wholly trusting in the Lord. ^2-maccabees-07-40-douay

41 And last of all, after the sons, the mother also was consumed. ^2-maccabees-07-41-douay

42 But now there is enough said of the sacrifices and of the excessive cruelties. ^2-maccabees-07-42-douay
