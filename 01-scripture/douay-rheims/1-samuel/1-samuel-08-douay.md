# 1 Samuel Chapter 8

Samuel growing old, and his sons not walking in his ways, the people desire a king.

1 And it came to pass, when Samuel was old, that he appointed his sons to be judges over Israel. ^1-samuel-08-1-douay

2 Now the name of his firstborn son was Joel: and the name of the second was Abia, judges in Bersabee. ^1-samuel-08-2-douay

3 And his sons walked not in his ways: but they turned aside after lucre, and took bribes, and perverted judgment. ^1-samuel-08-3-douay

4 Then all the ancients of Israel being assembled came to Samuel to Ramatha. ^1-samuel-08-4-douay

5 And they said to him: Behold thou art old, and thy sons walk not in thy ways: make us a king, to judge us, as all nations have. ^1-samuel-08-5-douay

6 And the word was displeasing in the eyes of Samuel, that they should say: Give us a king to judge us. And Samuel prayed to the Lord. ^1-samuel-08-6-douay

7 And the Lord said to Samuel: Hearken to the voice of the people in all that they say to thee. For they have not rejected thee, but me, that I should not reign over them. **Rejected, etc...** The government of Israel hitherto had been a theocracy, in which God himself immediately ruled, by laws which he had enacted, and by judges extraordinarily raised up by himself; and therefore he complains that his people rejected him, in desiring a change of government. ^1-samuel-08-7-douay

8 According to all their works, they have done from the day that I brought them out of Egypt until this day: as they have forsaken me, and served strange gods, so do they also unto thee. ^1-samuel-08-8-douay

9 Now, therefore, hearken to their voice: but yet testify to them, and foretell them the right of the king, that shall reign over them. **The right...** That is, the manner (*misphat*) after which he shall proceed, having no one to control him, when he has the power in his hand. ^1-samuel-08-9-douay

10 Then Samuel told all the words of the Lord to the people that had desired a king of him, ^1-samuel-08-10-douay

11 And said: This will be the right of the king that shall reign over you: He will take your sons, and put them in his chariots, and will make them his horsemen, and his running footmen, to run before his chariots, ^1-samuel-08-11-douay

12 And he will appoint of them to be his tribunes, and his centurions, and to plough his fields, and to reap his corn, and to make him arms and chariots. ^1-samuel-08-12-douay

13 Your daughters also he will take to make him ointments, and to be his cooks, and bakers. ^1-samuel-08-13-douay

14 And he will take your fields, and your vineyards, and your best oliveyards, and give them to his servants. ^1-samuel-08-14-douay

15 Moreover he will take the tenth of your corn, and of the revenues of your vineyards, to give to his eunuchs and servants. ^1-samuel-08-15-douay

16 Your servants also, and handmaids, and your goodliest young men, and your asses, he will take away, and put them to his work. ^1-samuel-08-16-douay

17 Your flocks also he will tithe, and you shall be his servants. ^1-samuel-08-17-douay

18 And you shall cry out in that day from the face of the king, whom you have chosen to yourselves: and the Lord will not hear you in that day, because you desired unto yourselves a king. ^1-samuel-08-18-douay

19 But the people would not hear the voice of Samuel, and they said, Nay: but there shall be a king over us, ^1-samuel-08-19-douay

20 And we also will be like all nations: and our king shall judge us, and go out before us, and fight our battles for us. ^1-samuel-08-20-douay

21 And Samuel heard all the words of the people, and rehearsed them in the ears of the Lord. ^1-samuel-08-21-douay

22 And the Lord said to Samuel: Hearken to their voice, and make them a king. And Samuel said to the men of Israel: Let every man go to his city. ^1-samuel-08-22-douay
