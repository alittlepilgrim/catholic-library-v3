# 1 Samuel Chapter 13

The war between Saul and the Philistines. The distress of the Israelites. Saul offers sacrifice before the coming of Samuel: for which he is reproved.

1 Saul was a child of one year when he began to reign, and he reigned two years over Israel. **Of one year...** That is, he was good and like an innocent child, and for two years continued in that innocency. ^1-samuel-13-1-douay

2 And Saul chose him three thousand men of Israel: and two thousand were with Saul in Machmas, and in mount Bethel: and a thousand with Jonathan in Gabaa of Benjamin: and the rest of the people he sent back every man to their dwellings. ^1-samuel-13-2-douay

3 And Jonathan smote the garrison of the Philistines which was in Gabaa. And when the Philistines had heard of it, Saul sounded the trumpet over all the land, saying: Let the Hebrews hear. ^1-samuel-13-3-douay

4 And all Israel heard this report: Saul hath smitten the garrison of the Philistines: and Israel took courage against the Philistines. And the people were called together after Saul to Galgal. ^1-samuel-13-4-douay

5 The Philistines also were assembled to fight against Israel, thirty thousand chariots, and six thousand horsemen, and a multitude of people besides, like the sand on the seashore for number. And going up they camped in Machmas, at the east of Bethaven. ^1-samuel-13-5-douay

6 And when the men of Israel saw that they were straitened (for the people were distressed), they hid themselves in caves, and in thickets, and in rocks, and in dens, and in pits. ^1-samuel-13-6-douay

7 And some of the Hebrews passed over the Jordan into the land of Gad and Galaad. And when Saul was yet in Galgal, all the people that followed him were greatly afraid. ^1-samuel-13-7-douay

8 And he waited seven days, according to the appointment of Samuel, and Samuel came not to Galgal, and the people slipt away from him. ^1-samuel-13-8-douay

9 Then Saul said: Bring me the holocaust, and the peace offerings. And he offered the holocaust. ^1-samuel-13-9-douay

10 And when he had made an end of offering the holocaust, behold Samuel came: and Saul went forth to meet him and salute him. ^1-samuel-13-10-douay

11 And Samuel said to him: What hast thou done? Saul answered: Because I saw that the people slipt from me, and thou wast not come according to the days appointed, and the Philistines were gathered together in Machmas, ^1-samuel-13-11-douay

12 I said: Now will the Philistines come down upon me to Galgal, and I have not appeased the face of the Lord. Forced by necessity, I offered the holocaust. ^1-samuel-13-12-douay

13 And Samuel said to Saul: Thou hast done foolishly, and hast not kept the commandments of the Lord thy God, which he commanded thee. And if thou hadst not done thus, the Lord would now have established thy kingdom over Israel for ever: ^1-samuel-13-13-douay

14 But thy kingdom shall not continue. The Lord hath sought him a man according to his own heart: and him hath the Lord commanded to be prince over his people, because thou hast not observed that which the Lord commanded. ^1-samuel-13-14-douay

15 And Samuel arose and went up from Galgal to Gabaa of Benjamin. And the rest of the people went up after Saul, to meet the people who fought against them, going from Galgal to Gabaa, in the hill of Benjamin. And Saul numbered the people, that were found with him, about six hundred men. ^1-samuel-13-15-douay

16 And Saul, and Jonathan his son, and the people that were present with them, were in Gabaa of Benjamin: But the Philistines encamped in Machmas. ^1-samuel-13-16-douay

17 And there went out of the camp of the Philistines three companies to plunder. One company went towards the way of Ephra to the land of Sual; ^1-samuel-13-17-douay

18 And another went by the way of Bethoron, and the third turned to the way of the border, above the valley of Seboim towards the desert. ^1-samuel-13-18-douay

19 Now there was no smith to be found in all the land of Israel, for the Philistines had taken this precaution, lest the Hebrews should make them swords or spears. ^1-samuel-13-19-douay

20 So all Israel went down to the Philistines, to sharpen every man his ploughshare, and his spade, and his axe, and his rake. ^1-samuel-13-20-douay

21 So that their shares, and their spades, and their forks, and their axes, were blunt, even to the goad, which was to be mended. ^1-samuel-13-21-douay

22 And when the day of battle was come, there was neither sword nor spear found in the hand of any of the people that were with Saul and Jonathan, except Saul and Jonathan his son. ^1-samuel-13-22-douay

23 And the army of the Philistines went out in order to advance further in Machmas. ^1-samuel-13-23-douay
