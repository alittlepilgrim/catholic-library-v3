# Leviticus Chapter 15

Other legal uncleannesses.

1 And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ^leviticus-15-1-douay

2 Speak to the children of Israel, and say to them: The man that hath an issue of seed, shall be unclean. **Issue of seed shall be unclean...** These legal uncleannesses were instituted in order to give the people a horror of carnal impurities. ^leviticus-15-2-douay

3 And then shall he be judged subject to this evil, when a filthy humour, at every moment, cleaveth to his flesh, and gathereth there. ^leviticus-15-3-douay

4 Every bed on which he sleepeth, shall be unclean, and every place on which he sitteth. ^leviticus-15-4-douay

5 If any man touch his bed, he shall wash his clothes and being washed with water, he shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-5-douay

6 If a man sit where that man hath sitten, he also shall wash his clothes: and being washed with water, shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-6-douay

7 He that toucheth his flesh, shall wash his clothes: and being himself washed with water shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-7-douay

8 If such a man cast his spittle upon him that is clean, he shall wash his clothes: and being washed with water, he shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-8-douay

9 The saddle on which he hath sitten shall be unclean. ^leviticus-15-9-douay

10 And whatsoever has been under him that hath the issue of seed, shall be unclean until the evening. He that carrieth any of these things, shall wash his clothes: and being washed with water, he shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-10-douay

11 Every person whom such a one shall touch, not having washed his hands before, shall wash his clothes: and being washed with water, shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-11-douay

12 If he touch a vessel of earth, it shall be broken: but if a vessel of wood, it shall be washed with water. ^leviticus-15-12-douay

13 If he who suffereth this disease be healed, he shall number seven days after his cleansing: and having washed his clothes, and all his body in living water, he shall be clean. ^leviticus-15-13-douay

14 And on the eighth day he shall take two turtles, or two young pigeons, and he shall come before the Lord, to the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, and shall give them to the priest. ^leviticus-15-14-douay

15 Who shall offer one for sin, and the other for a holocaust: and he shall pray for him before the Lord, that he may be cleansed of the issue of his seed. ^leviticus-15-15-douay

16 The man from whom the seed of copulation goeth out, shall wash all his body with water: and he shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-16-douay

17 The garment or skin that he weareth, he shall wash with water: and it shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-17-douay

18 The woman, with whom he copulateth, shall be washed with water: and shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-18-douay

19 The woman, who at the return of the month, hath her issue of blood, shall be separated seven days. ^leviticus-15-19-douay

20 Every one that toucheth her, shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-20-douay

21 And every thing that she sleepeth on, or that she sitteth on in the days of her separation, shall be defiled. ^leviticus-15-21-douay

22 He that toucheth her bed shall wash his clothes: and being himself washed with water, shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-22-douay

23 Whosoever shall touch any vessel on which she sitteth, shall wash his clothes: and himself being washed with water, shall be defiled until the evening. ^leviticus-15-23-douay

24 If a man copulateth with her in the time of her flowers, he shall be unclean seven days: and every bed on which he shall sleep, shall be defiled. ^leviticus-15-24-douay

25 The woman that hath still issue of blood many days out of her ordinary time, or that ceaseth not to flow after the monthly courses, as long as she is subject to this disease, shall be unclean, in the same manner as if she were in her flowers. ^leviticus-15-25-douay

26 Every bed on which she sleepeth, and every vessel on which she sitteth, shall be defiled. ^leviticus-15-26-douay

27 Whosoever toucheth them shall wash his clothes: and himself being washed with water, shall be unclean until the evening. ^leviticus-15-27-douay

28 If the blood stop and cease to run, she shall count seven days of her purification: ^leviticus-15-28-douay

29 And on the eighth day she shall offer for herself to the priest, two turtles, or two young pigeons, at the door of the tabernacle of the testimony: ^leviticus-15-29-douay

30 And he shall offer one for sin, and the other for a holocaust, and he shall pray for her before the Lord, and for the issue of her uncleanness. ^leviticus-15-30-douay

31 You shall teach therefore the children of Israel to take heed of uncleanness, that they may not die in their filth, when they shall have defiled my tabernacle that is among them. ^leviticus-15-31-douay

32 This is the law of him that hath the issue of seed, and that is defiled by copulation. ^leviticus-15-32-douay

33 And of the woman that is separated in her monthly times, or that hath a continual issue of blood, and of the man that sleepeth with her. ^leviticus-15-33-douay
