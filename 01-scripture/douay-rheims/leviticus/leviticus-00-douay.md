# Leviticus

This book is called *Leviticus*, because it treats of the offices, ministries, rites and ceremonies of the priests and Levites. The Hebrews call it *Vaicra*, from the word with which it begins. (*For more information, see the articles* LEVITICUS *and* PENTATEUCH *in the Catholic Encyclopedia*.)

[[leviticus-01-douay|**Chapter 1.**]] Of holocausts or burnt offerings.

[[leviticus-02-douay|**Chapter 2.**]] Of offerings of flour, and firstfruits.

[[leviticus-03-douay|**Chapter 3.**]] Of peace offerings.

[[leviticus-04-douay|**Chapter 4.**]] Of offerings for sins of ignorance.

[[leviticus-05-douay|**Chapter 5.**]] Of other sacrifices for sins.

[[leviticus-06-douay|**Chapter 6.**]] Oblation for sins of injustice: ordinances concerning the holocausts and the perpetual fire: the sacrifices of the priests, and the sin offerings.

[[leviticus-07-douay|**Chapter 7.**]] Of sacrifices for trespasses and thanks offerings. No fat nor blood is to be eaten.

[[leviticus-08-douay|**Chapter 8.**]] Moses consecrateth Aaron and his sons.

[[leviticus-09-douay|**Chapter 9.**]] Aaron offereth sacrifice for himself and the people. Fire cometh from the Lord upon the altar.

[[leviticus-10-douay|**Chapter 10.**]] Nadab and Abiu for offering strange fire, are burnt by fire. Priests are forbidden to drink wine, when they enter into the tabernacle. The law of eating the holy things.

[[leviticus-11-douay|**Chapter 11.**]] The distinction of clean and unclean animals.

[[leviticus-12-douay|**Chapter 12.**]] The purification of women after childbirth.

[[leviticus-13-douay|**Chapter 13.**]] The law concerning leprosy in men, and in garments.

[[leviticus-14-douay|**Chapter 14.**]] The rites of sacrifices in cleansing the leprosy. Leprosy in houses.

[[leviticus-15-douay|**Chapter 15.**]] Other legal uncleannesses.

[[leviticus-16-douay|**Chapter 16.**]] When and how the high priest must enter into the sanctuary. The feast of expiation.

[[leviticus-17-douay|**Chapter 17.**]] No sacrifices to be offered but at the door of the tabernacle: a prohibition of blood.

[[leviticus-18-douay|**Chapter 18.**]] Marriage is prohibited in certain degrees of kindred: and all unnatural lusts.

[[leviticus-19-douay|**Chapter 19.**]] Divers ordinances, partly moral, partly ceremonial or judicial.

[[leviticus-20-douay|**Chapter 20.**]] Divers crimes to be punished with death.

[[leviticus-21-douay|**Chapter 21.**]] Ordinances relating to the priests.

[[leviticus-22-douay|**Chapter 22.**]] Who may eat the holy things: and what things may be offered.

[[leviticus-23-douay|**Chapter 23.**]] Holy days to be kept.

[[leviticus-24-douay|**Chapter 24.**]] The oil for the lamps. The loaves of proposition. The punishment of blasphemy.

[[leviticus-25-douay|**Chapter 25.**]] The law of the seventh and of the fiftieth year of jubilee.

[[leviticus-26-douay|**Chapter 26.**]] God's promises to them that keep his commandments. And the many punishments with which he threatens transgressors.

[[leviticus-27-douay|**Chapter 27.**]] Of vows and tithes.
