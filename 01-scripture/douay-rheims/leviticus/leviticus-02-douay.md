# Leviticus Chapter 2

Of offerings of flour, and firstfruits.

1 When any one shall offer an oblation of sacrifice to the Lord, his offering shall be of fine flour: and he shall pour oil upon it, and put frankincense, ^leviticus-02-1-douay

2 And shall bring it to the sons of Aaron the priests. And one of them shall take a handful of the flour and oil, and all the frankincense; and shall put it a memorial upon the altar for a most sweet savour to the Lord. ^leviticus-02-2-douay

3 And the remnant of the sacrifice shall be Aaron's, and his sons', holy of holies of the offerings of the Lord. **Holy of holies...** That is, most holy, as being dedicated to God, and set aside by his ordinance for the use of his priests. ^leviticus-02-3-douay

4 But when thou offerest a sacrifice baked in the oven of flour, to wit, loaves without leaven, tempered with oil, and unleavened wafers, anointed with oil: ^leviticus-02-4-douay

5 If thy oblation be from the frying pan, of flour tempered with oil, and without leaven: ^leviticus-02-5-douay

6 Thou shalt divide it into little pieces, and shalt pour oil upon it. ^leviticus-02-6-douay

7 And if the sacrifice be from the gridiron, in like manner the flour shall be tempered with oil. ^leviticus-02-7-douay

8 And when thou offerest it to the Lord, thou shalt deliver it to the hands of the priest. ^leviticus-02-8-douay

9 And when he hath offered it, he shall take a memorial out of the sacrifice, and burn it upon the altar for a sweet savour to the Lord. ^leviticus-02-9-douay

10 And whatsoever is left, shall be Aaron's, and his sons': holy of holies of the offerings of the Lord. ^leviticus-02-10-douay

11 Every oblation that is offered to the Lord shall be made without leaven: neither shall any leaven or honey be burnt in the sacrifice to the Lord. **Without leaven or honey...** No leaven nor honey was to be used in the sacrifice offered to God; to signify that we are to exclude from the pure worship of the gospel, all double dealing and affection to carnal pleasures. ^leviticus-02-11-douay

12 You shall offer only the firstfruits of them and gifts: but they shall not be put upon the altar, for a savour of sweetness. ^leviticus-02-12-douay

13 Whatsoever sacrifice thou offerest, thou shalt season it with salt: neither shalt thou take away the salt of the covenant of thy God from thy sacrifice. In all thy oblations thou shalt offer salt. **Salt...** In every sacrifice salt was to be used, which is an emblem of wisdom and discretion, without which none of our performances are agreeable to God. ^leviticus-02-13-douay

14 But if thou offer a gift of the firstfruits of thy corn to the Lord, of the ears yet green, thou shalt dry it at the fire, and break it small like meal; and so shalt thou offer thy firstfruits to the Lord: ^leviticus-02-14-douay

15 Pouring oil upon it and putting on frankincense, because it is the oblation of the Lord. ^leviticus-02-15-douay

16 Whereof the priest shall burn for a memorial of the gift, part of the corn broken small and of the oil, and all the frankincense. ^leviticus-02-16-douay
