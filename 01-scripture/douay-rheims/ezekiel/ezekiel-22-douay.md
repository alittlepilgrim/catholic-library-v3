# Ezekiel Chapter 22

The general corruption of the inhabitants of Jerusalem: for which God will consume them as dross in his furnace.

1 And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: ^ezekiel-22-1-douay

2 And thou son of man, dost thou not judge, dost thou not judge the city of blood? ^ezekiel-22-2-douay

3 And thou shalt show her all her abominations, and shalt say: Thus saith the Lord God: This is the city that sheddeth blood in the midst of her, that her time may come: and that hath made idols against herself, to defile herself. ^ezekiel-22-3-douay

4 Thou art become guilty in thy blood which thou hast shed: and thou art defiled in thy idols which thou hast made: and thou hast made thy days to draw near, and hast brought on the time of thy years: therefore have I made thee a reproach to the Gentiles, and a mockery to all countries. ^ezekiel-22-4-douay

5 Those that are near, and those that are far from thee, shall triumph over thee: thou filthy one, infamous, great in destruction. ^ezekiel-22-5-douay

6 Behold the princes of Israel, every one hath employed his arm in thee to shed blood. ^ezekiel-22-6-douay

7 They have abused father and mother in thee, they have oppressed the stranger in the midst of thee, they have grieved the fatherless and widow in thee. ^ezekiel-22-7-douay

8 Thou hast despised my sanctuaries, and profaned my sabbaths. ^ezekiel-22-8-douay

9 Slanderers have been in thee to shed blood, and they have eaten upon the mountains in thee, they have committed wickedness in the midst of thee. ^ezekiel-22-9-douay

10 They have discovered the nakedness of their father in thee, they have humbled the uncleanness of the menstruous woman in thee. ^ezekiel-22-10-douay

11 And every one hath committed abomination with his neighbour's wife, and the father in law hath wickedly defiled his daughter in law, the brother hath oppressed his sister the daughter of his father in thee. ^ezekiel-22-11-douay

12 They have taken gifts in thee to shed blood: thou hast taken usury and increase, and hast covetously oppressed thy neighbours: and thou hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God. ^ezekiel-22-12-douay

13 Behold, I have clapped my hands at thy covetousness, which thou hast exercised: and at the blood that hath been shed in the midst of thee. ^ezekiel-22-13-douay

14 Shall thy heart endure, or shall thy hands prevail in the days which I will bring upon thee: I the Lord have spoken, and will do it. ^ezekiel-22-14-douay

15 And I will disperse thee in the nations, and will scatter thee among the countries, and I will put an end to thy uncleanness in thee. ^ezekiel-22-15-douay

16 And I will possess thee in the sight of the Gentiles, and thou shalt know that I am the Lord. ^ezekiel-22-16-douay

17 And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: ^ezekiel-22-17-douay

18 Son of man, the house of Israel is become dross to me: all these are brass, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of the furnace: they are become the dross of silver. ^ezekiel-22-18-douay

19 Therefore thus saith the Lord God: Because you are all turned into dross, therefore behold I will gather you together in the midst of Jerusalem. ^ezekiel-22-19-douay

20 As they gather silver, and brass, and tin, and iron, and lead in the midst of the furnace: that I may kindle a fire in it to melt it: so will I gather you together in my fury and in my wrath, and will take my rest, and I will melt you down. ^ezekiel-22-20-douay

21 And will gather you together, and will burn you in the fire of my wrath, and you shall be melted in the midst thereof. ^ezekiel-22-21-douay

22 As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall you be in the midst thereof: and you shall know that I am the Lord, when I have poured out my indignation upon you. ^ezekiel-22-22-douay

23 And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: ^ezekiel-22-23-douay

24 Son of man, say to her: Thou art a land that is unclean, and not rained upon in the day of wrath. ^ezekiel-22-24-douay

25 There is a conspiracy of prophets in the midst thereof: like a lion that roareth and catcheth the prey, they have devoured souls, they have taken riches and hire, they have made many widows in the midst thereof. ^ezekiel-22-25-douay

26 Her priests have despised my law, and have defiled my sanctuaries: they have put no difference between holy and profane: nor have distinguished between the polluted and the clean: and they have turned away their eyes from my sabbaths, and I was profaned in the midst of them. ^ezekiel-22-26-douay

27 Her princes in the midst of her, are like wolves ravening the prey to shed blood, and to destroy souls, and to run after gains through covetousness. ^ezekiel-22-27-douay

28 And her prophets have daubed them without tempering the mortar, seeing vain things, and divining lies unto them, saying: Thus saith the Lord God: when the Lord hath not spoken. ^ezekiel-22-28-douay

29 The people of the land have used oppression, and committed robbery: they afflicted the needy and poor, and they oppressed the stranger by calumny without judgment. ^ezekiel-22-29-douay

30 And I sought among them for a man that might set up a hedge, and stand in the gap before me in favour of the land, that I might not destroy it: and I found none. ^ezekiel-22-30-douay

31 And I poured out my indignation upon them, in the fire of my wrath I consumed them: I have rendered their way upon their own head, saith the Lord God. ^ezekiel-22-31-douay
