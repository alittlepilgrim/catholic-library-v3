# Ezekiel Chapter 19

The parable of the young lions, and of the vineyard that is wasted.

1 Moreover take thou up a lamentation for the princes of Israel, ^ezekiel-19-1-douay

2 And say: ^ezekiel-19-2-douay

Why did thy mother the lioness lie down among the lions, and bring up her whelps in the midst of young lions? **Thy mother the lioness...** Jerusalem.

3 And she brought out one of her whelps, and he became a lion: and he learned to catch the prey, and to devour men. **One of her whelps...** Viz., Joachaz, alias Sellum. ^ezekiel-19-3-douay

4 And the nations heard of him, and took him, but not without receiving wounds: and they brought him in chains into the land of Egypt. ^ezekiel-19-4-douay

5 But she seeing herself weakened, and that her hope was lost, took one of her young lions, and set him up for a lion. **One of her young lions...** Joakim. ^ezekiel-19-5-douay

6 And he went up and down among the lions, and became a lion: and he learned to catch the prey, and to devour men. ^ezekiel-19-6-douay

7 He learned to make widows, and to lay waste their cities: and the land became desolate, and the fulness thereof by the noise of his roaring. ^ezekiel-19-7-douay

8 And the nations came together against him on every side out of the provinces, and they spread their net over him, in their wounds he was taken. ^ezekiel-19-8-douay

9 And they put him into a cage, they brought him in chains to the king of Babylon: and they cast him into prison, that his voice should no more be heard upon the mountains of Israel. ^ezekiel-19-9-douay

10 Thy mother is like a vine in thy blood planted by the water: her fruit and her branches have grown out of many waters. ^ezekiel-19-10-douay

11 And she hath strong rods to make sceptres for them that bear rule, and her stature was exalted among the branches: and she saw her height in the multitude of her branches. ^ezekiel-19-11-douay

12 But she was plucked up in wrath, and cast on the ground, and the burning wind dried up her fruit: her strong rods are withered, and dried up: the fire hath devoured her. ^ezekiel-19-12-douay

13 And now she is transplanted into the desert, in a land not passable, and dry. ^ezekiel-19-13-douay

14 And a fire is gone out from a rod of her branches, which hath devoured her fruit: so that she now hath no strong rod, to be a sceptre of rulers. This is a lamentation, and it shall be for a lamentation. ^ezekiel-19-14-douay
