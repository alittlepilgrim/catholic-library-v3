# Ezekiel Chapter 27

A description of the glory and riches of Tyre: and of her irrecoverable fall.

1 And the word of the Lord came to me, saying: ^ezekiel-27-1-douay

2 Thou therefore, O son of man, take up a lamentation for Tyre: ^ezekiel-27-2-douay

3 And say to Tyre that dwelleth at the entry of the sea, being the mart of the people for many islands: Thus saith the Lord God: O Tyre, thou hast said: I am of perfect beauty, ^ezekiel-27-3-douay

4 And situate in the heart of the sea. Thy neighbours, that built thee, have perfected thy beauty: ^ezekiel-27-4-douay

5 With fir trees of Sanir they have built thee with all sea planks: they have taken cedars from Libanus to make thee masts. **Sea planks...** That is, timber brought by sea to build the city. ^ezekiel-27-5-douay

6 They have cut thy oars out of the oaks of Basan: and they have made thee benches of Indian ivory and cabins with things brought from the islands of Italy. ^ezekiel-27-6-douay

7 Fine broidered linen from Egypt was woven for thy sail, to be spread on thy mast: blue and purple from the islands of Elisa, were made thy covering. ^ezekiel-27-7-douay

8 The inhabitants of Sidon, and the Arabians were thy rowers: thy wise men, O Tyre, were thy pilots. ^ezekiel-27-8-douay

9 The ancients of Gebal, and the wise men thereof furnished mariners for the service of thy various furniture: all the ships of the sea, and their mariners were thy factors. ^ezekiel-27-9-douay

10 The Persians, and Lydians, and the Libyans were thy soldiers in thy army: they hung up the buckler and the helmet in thee for thy ornament. ^ezekiel-27-10-douay

11 The men of Arad were with thy army upon thy walls round about: the Pygmeans also that were in thy towers, hung up their quivers on thy walls round about: they perfected thy beauty. **Pygmeans...** That is, strong and valiant men. In Hebrew, Gammadim. ^ezekiel-27-11-douay

12 The Carthaginians thy merchants supplied thy fairs with a multitude of all kinds of riches, with silver, iron, tin, and lead, ^ezekiel-27-12-douay

13 Greece, Thubal, and Mosoch, they were thy merchants, they brought to thy people slaves and vessels of brass. ^ezekiel-27-13-douay

14 From the house of Thogorma they brought horses, and horsemen, and mules to thy market. ^ezekiel-27-14-douay

15 The men of Dedan were thy merchants: many islands were the traffic of thy hand, they exchanged for thy price teeth of ivory and ebony. ^ezekiel-27-15-douay

16 The Syrian was thy merchant: by reason of the multitude of thy works, they set forth precious stones, and purple, and broidered works, and fine linen, and silk, and chodchod in thy market. **Chodchod...** It is the Hebrew name for some precious stone; but of what kind in particular interpreters are not agreed. ^ezekiel-27-16-douay

17 Juda and the land of Israel, they were thy merchants with the best corn: they set forth balm, and honey, and oil and rosin in thy fairs. ^ezekiel-27-17-douay

18 The men of Damascus were thy merchants in the multitude of thy works, in the multitude of divers riches, in rich wine, in wool of the best colour. ^ezekiel-27-18-douay

19 Dan, and Greece, and Mosel have set forth in thy marts wrought iron: stacte, and calamus were in thy market. ^ezekiel-27-19-douay

20 The men of Dedan were thy merchants in tapestry for seats. ^ezekiel-27-20-douay

21 Arabia, and all the princes of Cedar, they were the merchants of thy hand: thy merchants came to thee with lambs, and rams, and kids. ^ezekiel-27-21-douay

22 The sellers of Saba, and Reema, they were thy merchants: with all the best spices, and precious stones, and gold, which they set forth in thy market. ^ezekiel-27-22-douay

23 Haran, and Chene, and Eden were thy merchants; Saba, Assur, and Chelmad sold to thee. ^ezekiel-27-23-douay

24 They were thy merchants in divers manners, with bales of blue cloth, and of embroidered work, and of precious riches, which were wrapped up and bound with cords: they had cedars also in thy merchandise. ^ezekiel-27-24-douay

25 The ships of the sea, were thy chief in thy merchandise: and thou wast replenished, and glorified exceedingly in the heart of the sea. ^ezekiel-27-25-douay

26 Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the south wind hath broken thee in the heart of the sea. ^ezekiel-27-26-douay

27 Thy riches, and thy treasures, and thy manifold furniture, thy mariners, and thy pilots, who kept thy goods, and were chief over thy people: thy men of war also, that were in thee, with all thy multitude that is in the midst of thee: shall fall in the heart of the sea in the day of thy ruin. ^ezekiel-27-27-douay

28 Thy fleets shall be troubled at the sound of the cry of thy pilots. ^ezekiel-27-28-douay

29 And all that handled the oar shall come down from their ships: the mariners, and all the pilots of the sea shall stand upon the land: ^ezekiel-27-29-douay

30 And they shall mourn over thee with a loud voice and shall cry bitterly: and they shall cast up dust upon their heads and shall be sprinkled with ashes. ^ezekiel-27-30-douay

31 And they shall shave themselves bald for thee, and shall be girded with haircloth: and they shall weep for thee with bitterness of soul, with most bitter weeping. ^ezekiel-27-31-douay

32 And they shall take up a mournful song for thee, and shall lament thee: What city is like Tyre, which is become silent in the midst of the sea? ^ezekiel-27-32-douay

33 Which by thy merchandise that went from thee by sea didst fill many people: which by the multitude of thy riches, and of thy people didst enrich the kings of the earth. ^ezekiel-27-33-douay

34 Now thou art destroyed by the sea, thy riches are in the bottom of the waters, and all the multitude that was in the midst of thee is fallen. ^ezekiel-27-34-douay

35 All the inhabitants of the islands are astonished at thee: and all their kings being struck with the storm have changed their countenance. ^ezekiel-27-35-douay

36 The merchants of people have hissed at thee: thou art brought to nothing, and thou shalt never be any more. ^ezekiel-27-36-douay
