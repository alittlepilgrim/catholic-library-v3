# Tobit Chapter 9

The angel Raphael goes to Gabelus, receives the money, and brings him to the marriage.

1 Then Tobias called the angel to him, whom he took to be a man, and said to him: Brother Azarias, I pray thee hearken to my words: ^tobit-09-1-douay

2 If I should give myself to be thy servant I should not make a worthy return for thy care. ^tobit-09-2-douay

3 However, I beseech thee, to take with thee beasts and servants, and to go to Gabelus to Rages the city of the Medes: and to restore to him his note of hand, and receive of him the money, and desire him to come to my wedding. ^tobit-09-3-douay

4 For thou knowest that my father numbereth the days: and if I stay one day more, his soul will be afflicted. ^tobit-09-4-douay

5 And indeed thou seest how Raguel hath adjured me, whose adjuring I cannot despise. ^tobit-09-5-douay

6 Then Raphael took four of Raguel's servants, and two camels, and went to Rages the city of the Medes: and finding Gabelus, gave him his note of hand, and received of him all the money. ^tobit-09-6-douay

7 And he told him concerning Tobias the son of Tobias, all that had been done: and made him come with him to the wedding. ^tobit-09-7-douay

8 And when he was come into Raguel's house he found Tobias sitting at the table: and he leaped up, and they kissed each other: and Gabelus wept, and blessed God, ^tobit-09-8-douay

9 And said: The God of Israel bless thee, because thou art the son of a very good and just man, and that feareth God, and doth almsdeeds: ^tobit-09-9-douay

10 And may a blessing come upon thy wife and upon your parents. ^tobit-09-10-douay

11 And may you see your children, and your children's children, unto the third and fourth generation: and may your seed be blessed by the God of Israel, who reigneth for ever and ever. ^tobit-09-11-douay

12 And when all had said, Amen, they went to the feast: but the marriage feast they celebrated also with the fear of the Lord. ^tobit-09-12-douay
