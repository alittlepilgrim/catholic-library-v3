# Tobit Chapter 4

Tobias thinking he shall die, gives his son godly admonitions: and tells him of money he had lent to a friend.

1 Therefore when Tobias thought that his prayer was heard that he might die, he called to him Tobias his son, ^tobit-04-1-douay

2 And said to him: Hear, my son, the words of my mouth, and lay them as a foundation in thy heart. ^tobit-04-2-douay

3 When God shall take my soul, thou shalt bury my body: and thou shalt honour thy mother all the days of her life: ^tobit-04-3-douay

4 For thou must be mindful what and how great perils she suffered for thee in her womb. ^tobit-04-4-douay

5 And when she also shall have ended the time of her life, bury her by me. ^tobit-04-5-douay

6 And all the days of thy life have God in thy mind: and take heed thou never consent to sin, nor transgress the commandments of the Lord our God. ^tobit-04-6-douay

7 Give alms out of thy substance, and turn not away thy face from any poor person: for so it shall come to pass that the face of the Lord shall not be turned from thee. ^tobit-04-7-douay

8 According to thy ability be merciful. ^tobit-04-8-douay

9 If thou have much give abundantly: if thou have little, take care even so to bestow willingly a little. ^tobit-04-9-douay

10 For thus thou storest up to thyself a good reward for the day of necessity. ^tobit-04-10-douay

11 For alms deliver from all sin, and from death, and will not suffer the soul to go into darkness. ^tobit-04-11-douay

12 Alms shall be a great confidence before the most high God, to all them that give it. ^tobit-04-12-douay

13 Take heed to keep thyself, my son, from all fornication, and beside thy wife never endure to know a crime. ^tobit-04-13-douay

14 Never suffer pride to reign in thy mind, or in thy words: for from it all perdition took its beginning. ^tobit-04-14-douay

15 If any man hath done any work for thee, immediately pay him his hire, and let not the wages of thy hired servant stay with thee at all. ^tobit-04-15-douay

16 See thou never do to another what thou wouldst hate to have done to thee by another. ^tobit-04-16-douay

17 Eat thy bread with the hungry and the needy, and with thy garments cover the naked, ^tobit-04-17-douay

18 Lay out thy bread, and thy wine upon the burial of a just man, and do not eat and drink thereof with the wicked. ^tobit-04-18-douay

19 Seek counsel always of a wise man. ^tobit-04-19-douay

20 Bless God at all times: and desire of him to direct thy ways, and that all thy counsels may abide in him. ^tobit-04-20-douay

21 I tell thee also, my son, that I lent ten talents of silver, while thou wast yet a child, to Gabelus, in Rages a city of the Medes, and I have a note of his hand with me: ^tobit-04-21-douay

22 Now therefore inquire how thou mayst go to him, and receive of him the foresaid sum of money, and restore to him the note of his hand. ^tobit-04-22-douay

23 Fear not, my son: we lead indeed a poor life, but we shall have many good things if we fear God, and depart from all sin, and do that which is good. ^tobit-04-23-douay
