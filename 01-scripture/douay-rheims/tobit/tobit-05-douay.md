# Tobit Chapter 5

Young Tobias seeking a guide for his journey, the angel Raphael, in shape of a man, undertakes this office.

1 Then Tobias answered his father, and said: I will do all things, father, which thou hast commanded me. ^tobit-05-1-douay

2 But how I shall get this money, I cannot tell; he knoweth not me, and I know not him: what token shall I give him? nor did I ever know the way which leadeth thither. ^tobit-05-2-douay

3 Then his father answered him, and said: I have a note of his hand with me, which when thou shalt show him, he will presently pay it. ^tobit-05-3-douay

4 But go now, and seek thee out some faithful man, to go with thee for his hire: that thou mayst receive it, while I yet live. ^tobit-05-4-douay

5 Then Tobias going forth, found a beautiful young man, standing girded, and as it were ready to walk. ^tobit-05-5-douay

6 And not knowing that he was an angel of God, he saluted him, and said: From whence art thou, good young man? ^tobit-05-6-douay

7 But he answered: Of the children of Israel. And Tobias said to him: Knowest thou the way that leadeth to the country of the Medes? ^tobit-05-7-douay

8 And he answered: I know it: and I have often walked through all the ways thereof, and I have abode with Gabelus our brother, who dwelleth at Rages a city of the Medes, which is situate in the mount of Ecbatana. ^tobit-05-8-douay

9 And Tobias said to him: Stay for me, I beseech thee, till I tell these same things to my father. ^tobit-05-9-douay

10 Then Tobias going in told all these things to his father. Upon which his father being in admiration, desired that he would come in unto him. ^tobit-05-10-douay

11 So going in he saluted him, and said: Joy be to thee always. ^tobit-05-11-douay

12 And Tobias said: What manner of joy shall be to me, who sit in darkness and see not the light of heaven? ^tobit-05-12-douay

13 And the young man said to him: Be of good courage, thy cure from God is at hand. ^tobit-05-13-douay

14 And Tobias said to him: Canst thou conduct my son to Gabelus at Rages, a city of the Medes? and when thou shalt return, I will pay thee thy hire. ^tobit-05-14-douay

15 And the angel said to him: I will conduct him thither, and bring him back to thee. ^tobit-05-15-douay

16 And Tobias said to him: I pray thee, tell me, of what family, or what tribe art thou? ^tobit-05-16-douay

17 And Raphael the angel answered: Dost thou seek the family of him thou hirest, or the hired servant himself to go with thy son? ^tobit-05-17-douay

18 But lest I should make thee uneasy, I am Azarias the son of the great Ananias. **Azarias...** The angel took the form of Azarias: and therefore might call himself by the name of the man whom he personated. Azarias, in Hebrew, signifies the help of God, and Ananias the grace of God. ^tobit-05-18-douay

19 And Tobias answered: Thou art of a great family. But I pray thee be not angry that I desired to know thy family. ^tobit-05-19-douay

20 And the angel said to him: I will lead thy son safe, and bring him to thee again safe. ^tobit-05-20-douay

21 And Tobias answering, said: May you have a good journey, and God be with you in your way, and his angel accompany you. ^tobit-05-21-douay

22 Then all things being ready, that were to be carried in their journey, Tobias bade his father and his mother farewell, and they set out both together. ^tobit-05-22-douay

23 And when they were departed, his mother began to weep, and to say: Thou hast taken the staff of our old age, and sent him away from us. ^tobit-05-23-douay

24 I wish the money for which thou hast sent him, had never been. ^tobit-05-24-douay

25 For our poverty was sufficient for us, that we might account it as riches, that we saw our son. ^tobit-05-25-douay

26 And Tobias said to her: Weep not, our son will arrive thither safe, and will return safe to us, and thy eyes shall see him. ^tobit-05-26-douay

27 For I believe that the good angel of God doth accompany him, and doth order all things well that are done about him, so that he shall return to us with joy. ^tobit-05-27-douay

28 At these words his mother ceased weeping, and held her peace. ^tobit-05-28-douay
