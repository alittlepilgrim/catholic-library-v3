# Tobit Chapter 8

Tobias burns part of the fish's liver, and Raphael binds the devil. Tobias and Sarah pray.

1 And after they had supped, they brought in the young man to her. ^tobit-08-1-douay

2 And Tobias remembering the angel's word, took out of his bag part of the liver, and laid it upon burning coals. ^tobit-08-2-douay

3 Then the angel Raphael took the devil, and bound him in the desert of upper Egypt. ^tobit-08-3-douay

4 Then Tobias exhorted the virgin, and said to her: Sara, arise, and let us pray to God to day, and tomorrow, and the next day: because for these three nights we are joined to God: and when the third night is over, we will be in our own wedlock. ^tobit-08-4-douay

5 For we are the children of saints, and we must not be joined together like heathens that know not God. ^tobit-08-5-douay

6 So they both arose, and prayed earnestly both together that health might be given them, ^tobit-08-6-douay

7 And Tobias said: Lord God of our fathers, may the heavens and the earth, and the sea, and the fountains, and the rivers, and all thy creatures that are in them, bless thee. ^tobit-08-7-douay

8 Thou madest Adam of the slime of the earth, and gavest him Eve for a helper. ^tobit-08-8-douay

9 And now, Lord, thou knowest, that not for fleshly lust do I take my sister to wife, but only for the love of posterity, in which thy name may be blessed for ever and ever. ^tobit-08-9-douay

10 Sara also said: Have mercy on us, O Lord, have mercy on us, and let us grow old both together in health. ^tobit-08-10-douay

11 And it came to pass about the cockcrowing, Raguel ordered his servants to be called for, and they went with him together to dig a grave. ^tobit-08-11-douay

12 For he said: Lest perhaps it may have happened to him, in like manner as it did to the other seven husbands, that went in unto her. ^tobit-08-12-douay

13 And when they had prepared the pit, Raguel went back to his wife, and said to her: ^tobit-08-13-douay

14 Send one of thy maids, and let her see if he be dead, that I may bury him before it be day. ^tobit-08-14-douay

15 So she sent one of her maidservants, who went into the chamber, and found them safe and sound, sleeping both together. ^tobit-08-15-douay

16 And returning she brought the good news: and Raguel and Anna his wife blessed the Lord, ^tobit-08-16-douay

17 And said: We bless thee, O Lord God of Israel, because it hath not happened as we suspected. ^tobit-08-17-douay

18 For thou hast shown thy mercy to us, and hast shut out from us the enemy that persecuted us. ^tobit-08-18-douay

19 And thou hast taken pity upon two only children. Make them, O Lord, bless thee more fully: and to offer up to thee a sacrifice of thy praise, and of their health, that all nations may know, that thou alone art God in all the earth. ^tobit-08-19-douay

20 And immediately Raguel commanded his servants, to fill up the pit they had made, before it was day. ^tobit-08-20-douay

21 And he spoke to his wife to make ready a feast, and prepare all kind of provisions that are necessary for such as go a journey. ^tobit-08-21-douay

22 He caused also two fat kine, and four wethers to be killed, and a banquet to be prepared for all his neighbours, and all his friends, ^tobit-08-22-douay

23 And Raguel adjured Tobias, to abide with him two weeks. ^tobit-08-23-douay

24 And of all things which Raguel possessed, he gave one half to Tobias, and made a writing, that the half that remained should after their decease come also to Tobias. ^tobit-08-24-douay
