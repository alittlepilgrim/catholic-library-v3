# 1 Chronicles Chapter 6

The genealogies of Levi, and of Aaron. The cities of the Levites.

1 The sons of Levi were Gerson, Caath, and Merari. ^1-chronicles-06-1-douay

2 The Sons of Caath: Amram, Isaar, Hebron, and Oziel. ^1-chronicles-06-2-douay

3 The children of Amram: Aaron, Moses, and Mary. The Sons of Aaron: Nadab and Abiu, Eleazar and Ithamar. ^1-chronicles-06-3-douay

4 Eleazar begot Phinees, and Phinees begot Abisue, ^1-chronicles-06-4-douay

5 And Abisue begot Bocci, and Bocci begot Ozi. ^1-chronicles-06-5-douay

6 Ozi begot Zaraias, and Zaraias begot Maraioth. ^1-chronicles-06-6-douay

7 And Maraioth begot Amarias, and Amarias begot Achitob. ^1-chronicles-06-7-douay

8 Achitob begot Sadoc, and Sadoc begot Achimaas. ^1-chronicles-06-8-douay

9 Achimaas begot Azarias, Azarias begot Johanan, ^1-chronicles-06-9-douay

10 Johanan begot Azarias. This is he that executed the priestly office in the house which Solomon built in Jerusalem. ^1-chronicles-06-10-douay

11 And Azarias begot Amarias, and Amarias begot Achitob. ^1-chronicles-06-11-douay

12 And Achitob begot Sadoc, and Sadoc begot Sellum, ^1-chronicles-06-12-douay

13 Sellum begot Helcias, and Helcias begot Azarias, ^1-chronicles-06-13-douay

14 Azarias begot Saraias, and Saraias begot Josedec. ^1-chronicles-06-14-douay

15 Now Josedec went out, when the Lord carried awayJuda, and Jerusalem, by the hands of Nabuchodonosor. ^1-chronicles-06-15-douay

16 So the sons of Levi were Gerson, Caath, and Merari. ^1-chronicles-06-16-douay

17 And these are the names of the sons of Gerson: Lobni and Semei. ^1-chronicles-06-17-douay

18 The sons of Caath: Amram, and Isaar, and Hebron, and Oziel. ^1-chronicles-06-18-douay

19 The sons of Merari: Moholi and Musi. And these are the kindreds of Levi according to their families. ^1-chronicles-06-19-douay

20 Of Gerson: Lobni his son, Jahath his son, Zamma his son, ^1-chronicles-06-20-douay

21 Joah his son, Addo his son, Zara his son, Jethrai his son. ^1-chronicles-06-21-douay

22 The sons of Caath, Aminadab his son, Core his son, Asir his son, ^1-chronicles-06-22-douay

23 Elcana his son, Abiasaph his son, Asir his son, ^1-chronicles-06-23-douay

24 Thahath his son, Uriel his son, Ozias his son, Saul his son. ^1-chronicles-06-24-douay

25 The sons of Elcana: Amasai, and Achimoth. ^1-chronicles-06-25-douay

26 And Elcana. The sons of Elcana: Sophai his son, Nahath his son, ^1-chronicles-06-26-douay

27 Eliab his son, Jeroham his son, Elcana his son. ^1-chronicles-06-27-douay

28 The sons of Samuel: the firstborn Vasseni, and Abia. ^1-chronicles-06-28-douay

29 And the sons of Merari, Moholi: Lobni his son, Semei his son, Oza his son, ^1-chronicles-06-29-douay

30 Sammaa his son, Haggia his son, Asaia his son. ^1-chronicles-06-30-douay

31 These are they, whom David set over the singing men of the house of the Lord, after that the ark was placed. ^1-chronicles-06-31-douay

32 And they ministered before the tabernacle of the testimony, with singing, until Solomon built the house of the Lord in Jerusalem, and they stood according to their order in the ministry. ^1-chronicles-06-32-douay

33 And these are they that stood with their sons, of the sons of Caath, Hemam a singer, the son of Joel, the son of Sammuel, ^1-chronicles-06-33-douay

34 The son of Elcana, the son of Jeroham, the son of Eliel, the son of Thohu, ^1-chronicles-06-34-douay

35 The son of Suph, the son of Elcana, the son of Mahath, the son of Amasai, ^1-chronicles-06-35-douay

36 The son of Elcana, the son of Johel, the son of Azarias, the son of Sophonias, ^1-chronicles-06-36-douay

37 The son of Thahath, the son of Asir, the son of Abiasaph, the son of Core, ^1-chronicles-06-37-douay

38 The son of Isaar, the son of Caath, the son of Levi, the son of Israel. ^1-chronicles-06-38-douay

39 And his brother Asaph, who stood on his right hand, Asaph the son of Barachias, the son of Samaa. ^1-chronicles-06-39-douay

40 The son of Michael, the son of Basaia, the, son of Melchia. ^1-chronicles-06-40-douay

41 The son of Athanai, the son of Zara, the son of Adaia. ^1-chronicles-06-41-douay

42 The son of Ethan, the son of Zamma, the son of Semei. ^1-chronicles-06-42-douay

43 The son of Jeth, the son of Gerson, the son of Levi. ^1-chronicles-06-43-douay

44 And the sons of Merari their brethren, on the left hand, Ethan the son of Cusi, the son of Abdi, the son of Meloch, ^1-chronicles-06-44-douay

45 The son of Hasabia, the son of Amasai, the son of Helcias, ^1-chronicles-06-45-douay

46 The son of Amasai, the son of Boni, the son of Somer, ^1-chronicles-06-46-douay

47 The son of Moholi, the son of Musi, the son of Merari, the son of Levi. ^1-chronicles-06-47-douay

48 Their brethren also the Levites, who were appointed for all the ministry of the tabernacle of the house of the Lord. ^1-chronicles-06-48-douay

49 But Aaron and his sons offered burnt offerings upon the altar of holocausts, and upon the altar of incense, for every work of the holy of holies: and to pray for Israel according to all that Moses the servant of God had commanded. ^1-chronicles-06-49-douay

50 And these are the sons of Aaron: Eleazar his son, Phinees his son, Abisue his son, ^1-chronicles-06-50-douay

51 Bocci his son, Ozi his son, Zarahia his son, ^1-chronicles-06-51-douay

52 Meraioth his son, Amarias his son, Achitob his son, ^1-chronicles-06-52-douay

53 Sadoc his son, Achimaas his son. ^1-chronicles-06-53-douay

54 And these are their dwelling places by the towns and confines, to wit, of the sons of Aaron, of the families of the Caathites: for they fell to them by lot. ^1-chronicles-06-54-douay

55 And they gave them Hebron in the land of Juda, and the suburbs thereof round about: ^1-chronicles-06-55-douay

56 But the fields of the city, and the villages to Caleb son of Jephone. ^1-chronicles-06-56-douay

57 And to the sons of Aaron they gave the cities for refuge Hebron, and Lobna, and the suburbs thereof, ^1-chronicles-06-57-douay

58 And Jether and Esthemo, with their suburbs, and Helon, and Dabir with their suburbs: ^1-chronicles-06-58-douay

59 Asan also, and Bethsames, with their suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-59-douay

60 And out of the tribe of Benjamin: Gabee and its suburbs, Almath with its suburbs, Anathoth also with its suburbs: all their cities throughout their families were thirteen. ^1-chronicles-06-60-douay

61 And to the sons of Caath that remained of their kindred they gave out of the half tribe of Manasses ten cities in possession. ^1-chronicles-06-61-douay

62 And to the sons of Gerson by their families out of the tribe of Issachar, and out of the tribe of Aser, and out of the tribe of Nephtali, and out of the tribe Manasses in Basan, thirteen cities. ^1-chronicles-06-62-douay

63 And to the sons of Merari by their families out of the tribe of Ruben, and out of the tribe of Gad, and out of the tribe of Zabulon, they gave by lot twelve cities. ^1-chronicles-06-63-douay

64 And the children of Israel gave to the Levites the cities, and their suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-64-douay

65 And they gave them by lot, out of the tribe of the sons of Juda, and out of the tribe of the sons of Simeon, and out of the tribe of the sons of Benjamin, these cities which they called by their names. ^1-chronicles-06-65-douay

66 And to them that were of the kindred of the sons of Caath, and the cities in their borders were of the tribe of Ephraim. ^1-chronicles-06-66-douay

67 And they gave the cities of refuge Sichem with its suburbs in mount Ephraim, and Gazer with its suburbs, ^1-chronicles-06-67-douay

68 Jecmaan also with its suburbs, and Beth-horon in like manner, ^1-chronicles-06-68-douay

69 Helon also with its suburbs, and Gethremmon in like manner, ^1-chronicles-06-69-douay

70 And out of the half tribe of Manasses, Aner and its suburbs, Baalam and its suburbs, to wit, to them that were left of the family of the sons of Caath. ^1-chronicles-06-70-douay

71 And to the sons of Gersom, out the kindred of the half tribe of Manasses, Gaulon, in Basan, and its suburbs, and Astharoth with its suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-71-douay

72 Out of the tribe of Issachar, Cedes and its suburbs, and Dabereth with its suburbs; ^1-chronicles-06-72-douay

73 Ramoth also and its suburbs, and Anem with its suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-73-douay

74 And out of the tribe of Aser: Masal with its suburbs, and Abdon in like manner; ^1-chronicles-06-74-douay

75 Hucac also and its suburbs, and Rohol with its suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-75-douay

76 And out of the tribe of Nephtali, Cedes in Galilee and its suburbs, Hamon with its suburbs, and Cariathaim, and its suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-76-douay

77 And to the sons of Merari that remained: out of the tribe of Zabulon, Remmono and its suburbs, and Thabor with its suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-77-douay

78 Beyond the Jordan also over against Jericho, on the east side of the Jordan and out of the tribe of Ruben, Bosor in the wilderness with its suburbs, and Jassa with its suburbs; ^1-chronicles-06-78-douay

79 Cademoth also and its suburbs, and Mephaath with its suburbs; ^1-chronicles-06-79-douay

80 Moreover also out of the tribe of Gad, Ramoth in Galaad and its suburbs, and Manaim with its suburbs; ^1-chronicles-06-80-douay

81 Hesebon also with its suburbs, and Jazer with its suburbs. ^1-chronicles-06-81-douay
