# 1 Chronicles Chapter 26

The divisions of the porters. Offices of other Levites.

1 And the divisions of the porters: of the Corites Meselemia, the son of Core, of the sons of Asaph. ^1-chronicles-26-1-douay

2 The sons of Meselemia: Zacharias the firstborn, Jadihel the second, Zabadias the third, Jathanael the fourth, ^1-chronicles-26-2-douay

3 Elam the fifth, Johanan the sixth, Elioenai the seventh. ^1-chronicles-26-3-douay

4 And the sons of Obededom, Semeias the firstborn, Jozabad the second, Joaha the third, Sachar the fourth, Nathanael the fifth, ^1-chronicles-26-4-douay

5 Ammiel the sixth, Issachar the seventh, Phollathi the eighth: for the Lord had blessed him. ^1-chronicles-26-5-douay

6 And to Semei his son were born sons, heads of their families: for they were men of great valour. ^1-chronicles-26-6-douay

7 The sons then of Semeias were Othni, and Raphael, and Obed, Elizabad, and his brethren most valiant men: and Eliu, and Samachias. ^1-chronicles-26-7-douay

8 All these of the sons of Obededom: they, and their sons, and their brethren most able men for service, sixty-two of Obededom. ^1-chronicles-26-8-douay

9 And the sons of Meselemia, and their brethren strong men, were eighteen. ^1-chronicles-26-9-douay

10 And of Hosa, that is, of the sons of Merari: Semri the chief, (for he had not a firstborn, and therefore his father made him chief.) **He had not a firstborn...** That is, his firstborn was either dead or not fit to be chief; and therefore he made Semri the chief. ^1-chronicles-26-10-douay

11 Helcias the second, Tabelias the third, Zacharias the fourth: all these the sons, and the brethren of Hosa, were thirteen. ^1-chronicles-26-11-douay

12 Among these were the divisions of the porters, so that the chiefs of the wards, as well as their brethren, always ministered in the house of the Lord. ^1-chronicles-26-12-douay

13 And they cast lots equally, both little and great, by their families for every one of the gates. ^1-chronicles-26-13-douay

14 And the lot of the east fell to Selemias. But to his son Zacharias, a very wise and learned man, the north gate fell by lot. ^1-chronicles-26-14-douay

15 And to Obededom and his sons that towards the south: in which part of the house was the council of the ancients. ^1-chronicles-26-15-douay

16 To Sephim, and Hosa towards the west, by the gate which leadeth to the way of the ascent: ward against ward. ^1-chronicles-26-16-douay

17 Now towards the east were six Levites: and towards the north four a day: and towards the south likewise four a day: and where the council was, two and two. ^1-chronicles-26-17-douay

18 In the cells also of the porters toward the west four in the way: and two at every cell. ^1-chronicles-26-18-douay

19 These are the divisions of the porters of the sons of Core, and of Merari. ^1-chronicles-26-19-douay

20 Now Achias was over the treasures of the house of God, and the holy vessels. **Holy vessels...** Or vessels of the holy places, or of things holy. *Vasa sanctorum*. ^1-chronicles-26-20-douay

21 The sons of Ledan, the sons of Gersonni: of Ledan were heads of the families, of Ledan, and Gersonni, Jehieli. ^1-chronicles-26-21-douay

22 The sons of Jehieli: Zathan and Joel, his brethren over the treasures of the house of the Lord, ^1-chronicles-26-22-douay

23 With the Amramites, and Isaarites, and Hebronites, and Ozielites. ^1-chronicles-26-23-douay

24 And Subael the son of Gersom, the son of Moses, was chief over the treasures. ^1-chronicles-26-24-douay

25 His brethren also, Eliezer, whose son Rohobia, and his son Isaias, and his son Joram, and his son Zechri, and his son Selemith. ^1-chronicles-26-25-douay

26 Which Selemith and his brethren were over the treasures of the holy things, which king David, and the heads of families, and the captains over thousands and over hundreds, and the captains of the host had dedicated, ^1-chronicles-26-26-douay

27 Out of the wars, and the spoils won in battles, which they had consecrated to the building and furniture of the temple of the Lord. ^1-chronicles-26-27-douay

28 And all these things that Samuel the seer and Saul the son of Cis, and Abner the son of Ner, and Joab the son of Sarvia had sanctified: and whosoever had sanctified those things, they were under the hand of Selemith and his brethren. ^1-chronicles-26-28-douay

29 But Chonenias and his sons were over the Isaarites, for the business abroad over Israel to teach them and judge them. ^1-chronicles-26-29-douay

30 And of the Hebronites Hasabias, and his brethren most able men, a thousand seven hundred had the charge over Israel beyond the Jordan westward, in all the works of the Lord, and for the service of the king. ^1-chronicles-26-30-douay

31 And the chief of the Hebronites was Jeria according to their families and kindreds. In the fortieth year of the reign of David they were numbered, and there were found most valiant men in Jazer Galaad, ^1-chronicles-26-31-douay

32 And his brethren of stronger age, two thousand seven hundred chiefs of families. And king David made them rulers over the Rubenites and the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasses, for all the service of God, and the king. ^1-chronicles-26-32-douay
