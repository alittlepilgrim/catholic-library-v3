# 1 Chronicles Chapter 24

The divisions of the priests into 24 courses, to serve in the temple. The chiefs of the Levites.

1 Now these were the divisions of the sons of Aaron: The sons of Aaron: Nadab, and Abiu, and Eleazar, and Ithamar. ^1-chronicles-24-1-douay

2 But Nadab and Abiu died before their father, and had no children: so Eleazar, and Ithamar did the office of the priesthood. ^1-chronicles-24-2-douay

3 And David distributed them, that is, Sadoc of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their courses and ministry. ^1-chronicles-24-3-douay

4 And there were found many more of the sons of Eleazar among the principal men, than of the sons of Ithamar. And he divided them so, that there were of the sons of Eleazar, sixteen chief men by their families: and of the sons of Ithamar eight by their families and houses. ^1-chronicles-24-4-douay

5 And he divided both the families one with the other by lot: for there were princes of the sanctuary, and princes of God, both of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar. ^1-chronicles-24-5-douay

6 And Semeias the son of Nathanael the scribe a Levite, wrote them down before the king and the princes, and Sadoc the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and the princes also of the priestly and Levitical families: one house, which was over the rest, of Eleazar: and another house, which had the rest under it, of Ithamar. ^1-chronicles-24-6-douay

7 Now the first lot came forth to Joiarib, the second to Jedei, ^1-chronicles-24-7-douay

8 The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim, ^1-chronicles-24-8-douay

9 The fifth to Melchia, the sixth to Maiman, ^1-chronicles-24-9-douay

10 The seventh to Accos, the eighth to Abia, ^1-chronicles-24-10-douay

11 The ninth to Jesua, the tenth to Sechenia, ^1-chronicles-24-11-douay

12 The eleventh to Eliasib, the twelfth to Jacim, ^1-chronicles-24-12-douay

13 The thirteenth to Hoppha, the fourteenth to Isbaab, ^1-chronicles-24-13-douay

14 The fifteenth to Belga, the sixteenth to Emmer, ^1-chronicles-24-14-douay

15 The seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses, ^1-chronicles-24-15-douay

16 The nineteenth to Pheteia, the twentieth to Hezechiel, ^1-chronicles-24-16-douay

17 The one and twentieth to Jachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul, ^1-chronicles-24-17-douay

18 The three and twentieth to Dalaiau, the four and twentieth to Maaziau. ^1-chronicles-24-18-douay

19 These are their courses according to their ministries, to come into the house of the Lord, and according to their manner under the hand of Aaron their father: as the Lord the God of Israel had commanded. ^1-chronicles-24-19-douay

20 Now of the rest of the sons of Levi, there was of the sons of Amram, Subael: and of the sons of Subael, Jehedeia. ^1-chronicles-24-20-douay

21 Also of the sons of Rohobia the chief Jesias. ^1-chronicles-24-21-douay

22 And the son of Isaar Salemoth, and the son of Salemoth Jahath: ^1-chronicles-24-22-douay

23 And his son Jeriau the first, Amarias the second, Jahaziel the third, Jecmaan the fourth. ^1-chronicles-24-23-douay

24 The son of Oziel, Micha: the son of Micha, Samir. ^1-chronicles-24-24-douay

25 The brother of Micha, Jesia: and the son of Jesia, Zacharias. ^1-chronicles-24-25-douay

26 The sons of Merari: Moholi and Musi: the son of Oziau: Benno. ^1-chronicles-24-26-douay

27 The son also of Merari Oziau, and Soam, and Zacchur, and Hebri. ^1-chronicles-24-27-douay

28 And the son of Moholi: Eleazar, who had no sons. ^1-chronicles-24-28-douay

29 And the son of Cis, Jeramael. ^1-chronicles-24-29-douay

30 The sons of Musi: Moholi, Eder, and Jerimoth. These are the sons of Levi according to the houses of their families. ^1-chronicles-24-30-douay

31 And they also cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron before David the king, and Sadoc, and Ahimelech, and the princes of the priestly and Levitical families, both the elder and the younger. The lot divided all equally. ^1-chronicles-24-31-douay
