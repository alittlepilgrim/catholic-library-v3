# 1 Chronicles Chapter 3

The genealogy of the house of David.

1 Now these were the sons of David that were born to him in Hebron: the firstborn Amnon of Achinoam the Jezrahelitess, the second Daniel of Abigail the Carmelitess. ^1-chronicles-03-1-douay

2 The third Absalom the son of Maacha the daughter of Tolmai king of Gessur, the fourth Adonias the son of Aggith, ^1-chronicles-03-2-douay

3 The fifth Saphatias of Abital, the sixth Jethrahem of Egla his wife. ^1-chronicles-03-3-douay

4 So six sons were born to him in Hebron, where he reigned seven years and six months. And in Jerusalem he reigned three and thirty years. ^1-chronicles-03-4-douay

5 And these sons were born to him in Jerusalem: Simmaa, and Sobab, and Nathan, and Solomon, four of Bethsabee the daughter of Ammiel. ^1-chronicles-03-5-douay

6 Jebaar also and Elisama, ^1-chronicles-03-6-douay

7 And Eliphaleth, and Noge, and Nepheg, and Japhia, ^1-chronicles-03-7-douay

8 And Elisama, and Eliada, and Elipheleth, nine: ^1-chronicles-03-8-douay

9 All these the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines: and they had a sister Thamar. **The concubines...** The inferior wives. ^1-chronicles-03-9-douay

10 And Solomon's son was Roboam: whose son Abia begot Asa. And his son was Josaphat, ^1-chronicles-03-10-douay

11 The father of Joram: and Joram begot Ochozias, of whom was born Joas: ^1-chronicles-03-11-douay

12 And his son Amasias begot Azarias. And Joathan the son of Azarias ^1-chronicles-03-12-douay

13 Begot Achaz, the father of Ezechias, of whom was born Manasses. ^1-chronicles-03-13-douay

14 And Manasses begot Amon the father of Josias. ^1-chronicles-03-14-douay

15 And the sons of Josias were, the firstborn Johanan, the second Joakim, the third Sedecias, the fourth Sellum. ^1-chronicles-03-15-douay

16 Of Joakim was born Jechonias, and Sedecias. ^1-chronicles-03-16-douay

17 The sons of Jechonias were Asir, Salathiel, ^1-chronicles-03-17-douay

18 Melchiram, Phadaia, Senneser and Jecemia, Sama, and Nadabia. ^1-chronicles-03-18-douay

19 Of Phadaia were born Zorobabel and Semei. Zorobabel begot Mosollam, Hananias, and Salomith their sister: ^1-chronicles-03-19-douay

20 Hasaba also, and Ohol, and Barachias, and Hasadias, Josabhesed, five. ^1-chronicles-03-20-douay

21 And the son of Hananias was Phaltias the father of Jeseias, whose son was Raphaia. And his son was Arnan, of whom was born Obdia, whose son was Sechenias. ^1-chronicles-03-21-douay

22 The son of Sechenias was Semeia, whose sons were Hattus, and Jegaal, and Baria, and Naaria, and Saphat, six in number. **Six...** Counting the father in the number. ^1-chronicles-03-22-douay

23 The sons of Naaria, Elioenai, and Ezechias, and Ezricam, three. ^1-chronicles-03-23-douay

24 The sons of Elioenai, Oduia, and Eliasub, and Pheleia, and Accub, and Johanan, and Dalaia, and Anani, seven. ^1-chronicles-03-24-douay
