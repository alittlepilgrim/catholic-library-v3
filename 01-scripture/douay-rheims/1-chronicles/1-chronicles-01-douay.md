# 1 Chronicles Chapter 1

The genealogy of the patriarchs down to Abraham. The posterity of Abraham and of Esau.

1 Adam, Seth, Enos, ^1-chronicles-01-1-douay

2 Cainan, Malaleel, Jared, ^1-chronicles-01-2-douay

3 Henoc, Mathusale, Lamech, ^1-chronicles-01-3-douay

4 Noe, Sem, Cham, and Japheth. ^1-chronicles-01-4-douay

5 The sons of Japheth: Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, Thubal, Mosoch, Thiras. ^1-chronicles-01-5-douay

6 And the sons of Gomer: Ascenez, and Riphath, and Thogorma. ^1-chronicles-01-6-douay

7 And the sons of Javan: Elisa and Tharsis, Cethim and Dodanim. ^1-chronicles-01-7-douay

8 The sons of Cham: Chus, and Mesrai, and Phut, and Chanaan. ^1-chronicles-01-8-douay

9 And the sons of Chus: Saba, and Hevila, Sabatha, and Regma, and Sabathaca. And the sons of Regma: Saba, and Dadan. ^1-chronicles-01-9-douay

10 Now Chus begot Nemrod: he began to be mighty upon earth. ^1-chronicles-01-10-douay

11 But Mesraim begot Ludim, and Anamim, and Laabim, and Nephtuim, ^1-chronicles-01-11-douay

12 Phetrusim also, and Casluim: from whom came the Philistines, and Caphtorim. ^1-chronicles-01-12-douay

13 And Chanaan begot Sidon his firstborn, and the Hethite, ^1-chronicles-01-13-douay

14 And the Jebusite, and the Amorrhite, and the Gergesite, ^1-chronicles-01-14-douay

15 And the Hevite, and the Aracite, and the Sinite, ^1-chronicles-01-15-douay

16 And the Aradian, and the Samarite, and the Hamathite. ^1-chronicles-01-16-douay

17 The sons of Sem: Elam and Asur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Hus, and Hul, and Gether, and Mosoch. ^1-chronicles-01-17-douay

18 And Arphaxad begot Sale, and Sale begot Heber. ^1-chronicles-01-18-douay

19 And to Heber were born two sons, the name of the one was Phaleg, because in his days the earth was divided; and the name of his brother was Jectan. ^1-chronicles-01-19-douay

20 And Jectan begot Elmodad, and Saleph, and Asarmoth, and Jare, ^1-chronicles-01-20-douay

21 And Adoram, and Usal, and Decla, ^1-chronicles-01-21-douay

22 And Hebal, and Abimael, and Saba, ^1-chronicles-01-22-douay

23 And Ophir, and Hevila, and Jobab. All these are the sons of Jectan. ^1-chronicles-01-23-douay

24 Sem, Arphaxad, Sale, ^1-chronicles-01-24-douay

25 Heber, Phaleg, Ragau, ^1-chronicles-01-25-douay

26 Serug, Nachor, Thare, ^1-chronicles-01-26-douay

27 Abram, this is Abraham. ^1-chronicles-01-27-douay

28 And the sons of Abraham, Isaac and Ismahel. ^1-chronicles-01-28-douay

29 And these are the generations of them. The firstborn of Ismahel, Nabajoth, then Cedar, and Adbeel, and Mabsam, ^1-chronicles-01-29-douay

30 And Masma, and Duma, Massa, Hadad, and Thema, ^1-chronicles-01-30-douay

31 Jetur, Naphis, Cedma: these are the sons of Ismahel. ^1-chronicles-01-31-douay

32 And the sons of Cetura, Abraham's concubine, whom she bore: Zamran, Jecsan, Madan, Madian, Jesboc, and Sue. And the sons of Jecsan, Saba, and Dadan. And the sons of Dadan: Assurim, and Latussim, and Laomin. **Concubine...** She was his lawful wife, but of an inferior degree. ^1-chronicles-01-32-douay

33 And the sons of Madian: Epha, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaa. All these are the sons of Cetura. ^1-chronicles-01-33-douay

34 And Abraham begot Isaac: and his sons were Esau and Israel. ^1-chronicles-01-34-douay

35 The sons of Esau: Eliphaz, Rahuel, Jehus, Ihelom, and Core. ^1-chronicles-01-35-douay

36 The sons of Eliphaz: Theman, Omar, Sephi, Gathan, Cenez, and by Thamna, Amalec. ^1-chronicles-01-36-douay

37 The sons of Rahuel: Nahath, Zara, Samma, Meza. ^1-chronicles-01-37-douay

38 The sons of Seir: Lotan, Sobal, Sebeon, Ana, Dison, Eser, Disan. ^1-chronicles-01-38-douay

39 The sons of Lotan: Hori, Homam. And the sister of Lotan was Thamna. ^1-chronicles-01-39-douay

40 The sons of Sobal: Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Sephi, and Onam. The sons of Sebeon: Aia, and Ana. The son of Ana: Dison. ^1-chronicles-01-40-douay

41 The sons of Dison: Hamram, and Eseban, and Jethran, and Charan. ^1-chronicles-01-41-douay

42 The sons of Eser: Balaan, and Zavan, and Jacan. The sons of Disan: Hus and Aran. ^1-chronicles-01-42-douay

43 Now these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom, before there was a king over the children of Israel: Bale the son of Beor: and the name of his city was Denaba. ^1-chronicles-01-43-douay

44 And Bale died, and Jobab the son of Zare of Bosra, reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-44-douay

45 And when Jobab also was dead, Husam of the land of the Themanites reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-45-douay

46 And Husam also died, and Adad the son of Badad reigned in his stead, and he defeated the Madianites in the land of Moab: the name of his city was Avith. ^1-chronicles-01-46-douay

47 And when Adad also was dead, Semla of Masreca reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-47-douay

48 Semla also died, and Saul of Rohoboth, which is near the river, reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-48-douay

49 And when Saul was dead, Balanan the son of Achobor reigned in his stead. ^1-chronicles-01-49-douay

50 He also died, and Adad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Phau, and his wife was called Meetabel the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezaab. ^1-chronicles-01-50-douay

51 And after the death of Adad, there began to be dukes in Edom instead of kings: duke Thamna, duke Alva, duke Jetheth, ^1-chronicles-01-51-douay

52 Duke Oolibama, duke Ela, duke Phinon, ^1-chronicles-01-52-douay

53 Duke Cenez, duke Theman, duke Mabsar, ^1-chronicles-01-53-douay

54 Duke Magdiel, duke Hiram. These are the dukes of Edom. ^1-chronicles-01-54-douay
