# 1 Chronicles Chapter 8

The posterity of Benjamin is further declared down to Saul. His issue.

1 Now Benjamin begot Bale his firstborn, Asbel the second, Ahara the third, ^1-chronicles-08-1-douay

2 Nohaa the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^1-chronicles-08-2-douay

3 And the sons of Bale were Addar, and Gera, and Abiud, ^1-chronicles-08-3-douay

4 And Abisue, and Naaman, and Ahoe, ^1-chronicles-08-4-douay

5 And Gera, and Sephuphan, and Huram. ^1-chronicles-08-5-douay

6 These are the sons of Abed, heads of families that dwelt in Gabaa, who were removed into Manahath. ^1-chronicles-08-6-douay

7 And Naaman, and Achia, and Gera he removed them, and begot Oza, and Ahiud. ^1-chronicles-08-7-douay

8 And Saharim begot in the land of Moab, after he sent away Husim and Bara his wives. ^1-chronicles-08-8-douay

9 And he begot of Hodes his wife Jobab, and Sebia, and Mosa, and Molchom, ^1-chronicles-08-9-douay

10 And Jehus and Sechia, and Marma. These were his sons heads of their families. ^1-chronicles-08-10-douay

11 And Mehusim begot Abitob, and Elphaal. ^1-chronicles-08-11-douay

12 And the sons of Elphaal were Heber, and Misaam, and Samad: who built Ono, and Lod, and its daughters. ^1-chronicles-08-12-douay

13 And Baria, and Sama were heads of their kindreds that dwelt in Aialon: these drove away the inhabitants of Geth. ^1-chronicles-08-13-douay

14 And Ahio, and Sesac, and Jerimoth, ^1-chronicles-08-14-douay

15 And Zabadia, and Arod, and Heder, ^1-chronicles-08-15-douay

16 And Michael, and Jespha, and Joha, the sons of Baria. ^1-chronicles-08-16-douay

17 And Zabadia, and Mosollam, Hezeci, and Heber, ^1-chronicles-08-17-douay

18 And Jesamari, and Jezlia, and Jobab, sons of Elphaal, ^1-chronicles-08-18-douay

19 And Jacim, and Zechri, and Zabdi, ^1-chronicles-08-19-douay

20 And Elioenai, and Selethai, and Elial, ^1-chronicles-08-20-douay

21 And Adaia, and Baraia, and Samareth, the sons of Semei. ^1-chronicles-08-21-douay

22 And Jespham, and Heber, and Eliel, ^1-chronicles-08-22-douay

23 And Abdon, and Zechri, and Hanan, ^1-chronicles-08-23-douay

24 And Hanania, and Elam, and Anathothia. ^1-chronicles-08-24-douay

25 And Jephdaia, and Phanuel the sons of Sesac. ^1-chronicles-08-25-douay

26 And Samsari, and Sohoria and Otholia, ^1-chronicles-08-26-douay

27 And Jersia, and Elia, and Zechri, the sons of Jeroham. ^1-chronicles-08-27-douay

28 These were the chief fathers, and heads of their families who dwelt in Jerusalem. ^1-chronicles-08-28-douay

29 And at Gabaon dwelt Abigabaon, and the name of his wife was Maacha: ^1-chronicles-08-29-douay

30 And his firstborn son Abdon, and Sur, and Cis, and Baal, and Nadab, ^1-chronicles-08-30-douay

31 And Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher, and Macelloth: ^1-chronicles-08-31-douay

32 And Macelloth begot Samaa: and they dwelt over against their brethren in Jerusalem with their brethren. ^1-chronicles-08-32-douay

33 And Ner begot Cis and Cis begot Saul. And Saul begot Jonathan and Melchisua, and Abinadab, and Esbaal. **Esbaal...** Alias Isboseth. ^1-chronicles-08-33-douay

34 And the son of Jonathan was Meribbaal: and Meribbaal begot Micha. **Meribbaal...** Alias Miphiboseth. [[2-samuel-04-douay|2 Samuel 4:4]]. ^1-chronicles-08-34-douay

35 And the sons of Micha were Phithon, and Melech, and Tharaa, and Ahaz. ^1-chronicles-08-35-douay

36 And Ahaz begot Joada: and Joada begot Alamath, and Azmoth, and Zamri: and Zamri begot Mosa, ^1-chronicles-08-36-douay

37 And Mosa begot Banaa, whose son was Rapha, of whom was born Elasa, who begot Asel. ^1-chronicles-08-37-douay

38 And Asel had six sons whose names were Ezricam, Bochru, Ismahel, Saris, Obdia, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Asel. ^1-chronicles-08-38-douay

39 And the sons of Esec, his brother, were Ulam the firstborn, and Jehus the second, and Eliphalet the third. ^1-chronicles-08-39-douay

40 And the sons of Ulam were most valiant men, and archers of great strength: and they had many sons and grandsons, even to a hundred and fifty. All these were children of Benjamin. ^1-chronicles-08-40-douay
