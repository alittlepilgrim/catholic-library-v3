# 1 Chronicles Chapter 2

The twelve tribes of Israel. The genealogy of Judah down to David. Other genealogies of the tribe of Judah.

1 And these are the sons of Israel: Ruben, Simeon, Levi, Juda, Issachar, and Zabulon, ^1-chronicles-02-1-douay

2 Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Nephtali, Gad, and Aser. ^1-chronicles-02-2-douay

3 The sons of Juda: Her, Onan and Sela. These three were born to him of the Chanaanitess the daughter of Sue. And Her the firstborn of Juda, was wicked in the sight of the Lord, and he slew him. ^1-chronicles-02-3-douay

4 And Thamar his daughter in law bore him Phares and Zara. So all the sons of Juda were five. ^1-chronicles-02-4-douay

5 And the sons of Phares, were Hesron and Hamul. ^1-chronicles-02-5-douay

6 And the sons also of Zare: Zamri, and Ethan, and Eman, and Chalchal, and Dara, five in all. ^1-chronicles-02-6-douay

7 And the sons of Charmi: Achar, who troubled Israel, and sinned by the theft of the anathema. **Achar...** Alias *Achan* ([[joshua-07-douay|Joshua 7]]) -- Ibid. **The anathema...** The thing devoted or accursed, viz., the spoils of Jericho. ^1-chronicles-02-7-douay

8 The sons of Ethan: Azarias, ^1-chronicles-02-8-douay

9 And the sons of Hesron that were born to him: Jerameel, and Ram, and Calubi. ^1-chronicles-02-9-douay

10 And Ram begot Aminadab, and Aminadab begot Nahasson, prince of the children of Juda. **Ram...** He is commonly called Aram. But it is to be observed here, once for all, that it was a common thing among the Hebrews for the same persons to have different names: and that it is not impossible among so many proper names, as here occur in the first nine chapters of this book, that the transcribers of the ancient Hebrew copies may have made some slips in the orthography. ^1-chronicles-02-10-douay

11 And Nahasson begot Salma, the father of Booz. ^1-chronicles-02-11-douay

12 And Booz begot Obed, and Obed begot Isai. ^1-chronicles-02-12-douay

13 And Isai begot Eliab his firstborn, the second Abinadab, the third Simmaa, ^1-chronicles-02-13-douay

14 The fourth, Nathanael, the fifth Raddai, ^1-chronicles-02-14-douay

15 The sixth Asom, the seventh David. ^1-chronicles-02-15-douay

16 And their sisters were Sarvia, and Abigail. The sons of Sarvia: Abisai, Joab, and Asael, three. ^1-chronicles-02-16-douay

17 And Abigail bore Amasa, whose father was Jether the Ismahelite. ^1-chronicles-02-17-douay

18 And Caleb the son of Hesron took a wife named Azuba, of whom he had Jerioth: and her sons were Jaser, and Sobab, and Ardon. **Caleb...** Alias Calubi, [[1-chronicles-02-douay|verse 9]]. ^1-chronicles-02-18-douay

19 And when Azuba was dead, Caleb took to wife Ephrata: who bore him Hur. ^1-chronicles-02-19-douay

20 And Hur begot Uri: and Uri begot Bezeleel. ^1-chronicles-02-20-douay

21 And afterwards Hesron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Galaad, and took her to wife when he was threescore years old: and she bore him Segub. ^1-chronicles-02-21-douay

22 And Segub begot Jair, and he had three and twenty cities in the land of Galaad. ^1-chronicles-02-22-douay

23 And he took Gessur, and Aram the towns of Jair, and Canath, and the villages thereof, threescore cities. All these, the sons of Machir father of Galaad. ^1-chronicles-02-23-douay

24 And when Hesron was dead, Caleb went in to Ephrata. Hesron also had to wife Abia who bore him Ashur the father of Thecua. ^1-chronicles-02-24-douay

25 And the sons of Jerameel the firstborn of Hesron, were Ram his firstborn, and Buna, and Aram, and Asom, and Achia. ^1-chronicles-02-25-douay

26 And Jerameel married another wife, named Atara, who was the mother of Onam. ^1-chronicles-02-26-douay

27 And the sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerameel, were Moos, Jamin, and Achar. ^1-chronicles-02-27-douay

28 And Onam had sons Semei, and Jada. And the sons of Semei: Nadab, and Abisur. ^1-chronicles-02-28-douay

29 And the name of Abisur's wife was Abihail, who bore him Ahobban, and Molid. ^1-chronicles-02-29-douay

30 And the sons of Nadab were Saled and Apphaim. And Saled died without children. ^1-chronicles-02-30-douay

31 But the son of Apphaim was Jesi: and Jesi begot Sesan. And Sesan begot Oholai. ^1-chronicles-02-31-douay

32 And the sons of Jada the brother of Semei: Jether and Jonathan. And Jether also died without children. ^1-chronicles-02-32-douay

33 But Jonathan begot Phaleth, and Ziza. These were the sons of Jerameel. ^1-chronicles-02-33-douay

34 And Sesan had no sons, but daughters and a servant an Egyptian, named Jeraa. ^1-chronicles-02-34-douay

35 And he gave him his daughter to wife: and she bore him Ethei. ^1-chronicles-02-35-douay

36 And Ethei begot Nathan, and Nathan begot Zabad. ^1-chronicles-02-36-douay

37 And Zabad begot Ophlal, and Ophlal begot Obed. ^1-chronicles-02-37-douay

38 Obed begot Jehu, Jehu begot Azarias. ^1-chronicles-02-38-douay

39 Azarias begot Helles, and Helles begot Elasa. ^1-chronicles-02-39-douay

40 Elasa begot Sisamoi, Sisamoi begot Sellum, ^1-chronicles-02-40-douay

41 Sellum begot Icamia, and Icamia begot Elisama. ^1-chronicles-02-41-douay

42 Now the sons of Caleb the brother of Jerameel were Mesa his firstborn, who was the father of Siph: and the sons of Maresa father of Hebron. ^1-chronicles-02-42-douay

43 And the sons of Hebron, Core, and Thaphua, and Recem, and Samma. ^1-chronicles-02-43-douay

44 And Samma begot Raham, the father of Jercaam, and Recem begot Sammai. ^1-chronicles-02-44-douay

45 The son of Sammai, Maon: and Maon the father of Bethsur. ^1-chronicles-02-45-douay

46 And Epha the concubine of Caleb bore Haran, and Mosa, and Gezez. And Haran begot Gezez. ^1-chronicles-02-46-douay

47 And the sons of Jahaddai, Rogom, and Joathan, and Gesan, and Phalet, and Epha, and Saaph. ^1-chronicles-02-47-douay

48 And Maacha the concubine of Caleb bore Saber, and Tharana. ^1-chronicles-02-48-douay

49 And Saaph the father of Madmena begot Sue the father of Machbena, and the father of Gabaa. And the daughter of Caleb was Achsa. ^1-chronicles-02-49-douay

50 These were the sons of Caleb, the son of Hur the firstborn of Ephrata, Sobal the father of Cariathiarim. ^1-chronicles-02-50-douay

51 Salma the father of Bethlehem, Hariph the father of Bethgader. ^1-chronicles-02-51-douay

52 And Sobal the father of Cariathiarim had sons: he that saw half of the places of rest. **He that saw, etc...** The Latin interpreter seems to have given us here, instead of the proper names, the meaning of those names in the Hebrew. He has done in like manner, [[1-chronicles-02-douay|verse 55]]. ^1-chronicles-02-52-douay

53 And of the kindred of Cariathiarim, the Jethrites, and Aphuthites, and Semathites, and Maserites. Of them came the Saraites, and Esthaolites. ^1-chronicles-02-53-douay

54 The sons of Salma, Bethlehem, and Netophathi, the crowns of the house of Joab, and half of the place of rest of Sarai. ^1-chronicles-02-54-douay

55 And the families of the scribes that dwell in Jabes, singing and making melody, and abiding in tents. These are the Cinites, who came of Calor (Chamath) father of the house of Rechab. ^1-chronicles-02-55-douay
