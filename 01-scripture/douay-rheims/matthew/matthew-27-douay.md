# Matthew Chapter 27

The continuation of the history of the passion of Christ. His death and burial.

1 And when morning was come, all the chief priests and ancients of the people took counsel against Jesus, that they might put him to death. ^matthew-27-1-douay

2 And they brought him bound and delivered him to Pontius Pilate the governor. ^matthew-27-2-douay

3 Then Judas, who betrayed him, seeing that he was condemned, repenting himself, brought back the thirty pieces of silver to the chief priests and ancients, ^matthew-27-3-douay

4 Saying: I have sinned in betraying innocent blood. But they said: What is that to us? Look thou to it. ^matthew-27-4-douay

5 And casting down the pieces of silver in the temple, he departed and went and hanged himself with an halter. ^matthew-27-5-douay

6 But the chief priests having taken the pieces of silver, said: It is not lawful to put them into the *corbona*, because it is the price of blood. **Corbona...** A place in the temple where the people put in their gifts or offerings. ^matthew-27-6-douay

7 And after they had consulted together, they bought with them the potter's field, to be a burying place for strangers. ^matthew-27-7-douay

8 For this cause that field was called *Haceldama*, that is, *the field of blood*, even to this day. ^matthew-27-8-douay

9 Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremiah the prophet, saying: And they took the thirty pieces of silver, the price of him that was prized, whom they prized of the children of Israel. ^matthew-27-9-douay

10 And they gave them unto the potter's field, as the Lord appointed to me. ^matthew-27-10-douay

11 And Jesus stood before the governor, and the governor asked him, saying: Art thou the king of the Jews? Jesus saith to him: Thou sayest it. ^matthew-27-11-douay

12 And when he was accused by the chief priests and ancients, he answered nothing. ^matthew-27-12-douay

13 Then Pilate saith to him: Dost not thou hear how great testimonies they allege against thee? ^matthew-27-13-douay

14 And he answered him to never a word, so that the governor wondered exceedingly. ^matthew-27-14-douay

15 Now upon the solemn day the governor was accustomed to release to the people one prisoner, whom they would. ^matthew-27-15-douay

16 And he had then a notorious prisoner that was called Barabbas. ^matthew-27-16-douay

17 They therefore being gathered together, Pilate said: Whom will you that I release to you: Barabbas, or Jesus that is called Christ? ^matthew-27-17-douay

18 For he knew that for envy they had delivered him. ^matthew-27-18-douay

19 And as he was sitting in the place of judgment, his wife sent to him, saying: Have thou nothing to do with that just man; for I have suffered many things this day in a dream because of him. ^matthew-27-19-douay

20 But the chief priests and ancients persuaded the people that they should ask Barabbas and make Jesus away. ^matthew-27-20-douay

21 And the governor answering, said to them: Whether will you of the two to be released unto you? But they said: Barabbas. ^matthew-27-21-douay

22 Pilate saith to them: What shall I do then with Jesus that is called Christ? They say all: Let him be crucified. ^matthew-27-22-douay

23 The governor said to them: Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more, saying: Let him be crucified. ^matthew-27-23-douay

24 And Pilate seeing that he prevailed nothing, but that rather a tumult was made, taking water washed his hands before the people, saying: I am innocent of the blood of this just man. Look you to it. ^matthew-27-24-douay

25 And the whole people answering, said: His blood be upon us and upon our children. ^matthew-27-25-douay

26 Then he released to them Barabbas: and having scourged Jesus, delivered him unto them to be crucified. ^matthew-27-26-douay

27 Then the soldiers of the governor, taking Jesus into the hall, gathered together unto him the whole band. ^matthew-27-27-douay

28 And stripping him, they put a scarlet cloak about him. ^matthew-27-28-douay

29 And platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand. And bowing the knee before him, they mocked him, saying: Hail, King of the Jews. ^matthew-27-29-douay

30 And spitting upon him, they took the reed and struck his head. ^matthew-27-30-douay

31 And after they had mocked him, they took off the cloak from him and put on him his own garments and led him away to crucify him. ^matthew-27-31-douay

32 And going out, they found a man of Cyrene, named Simon: him they forced to take up his cross. ^matthew-27-32-douay

33 And they came to the place that is called Golgotha, which is the place of Calvary. ^matthew-27-33-douay

34 And they gave him wine to drink mingled with gall. And when he had tasted, he would not drink. ^matthew-27-34-douay

35 And after they had crucified him, they divided his garments, casting lots; that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying: They divided my garments among them; and upon my vesture they cast lots. ^matthew-27-35-douay

36 And they sat and watched him. ^matthew-27-36-douay

37 And they put over his head his cause written: THIS IS JESUS THE KING OF THE JEWS. ^matthew-27-37-douay

38 Then were crucified with him two thieves: one on the right hand and one on the left. ^matthew-27-38-douay

39 And they that passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads, ^matthew-27-39-douay

40 And saying: Vah, thou that destroyest the temple of God and in three days dost rebuild it: save thy own self. If thou be the Son of God, come down from the cross. ^matthew-27-40-douay

41 In like manner also the chief priests, with the scribes and ancients, mocking said: ^matthew-27-41-douay

42 He saved others: himself he cannot save. If he be the king of Israel, let him now come down from the cross: and we will believe him. ^matthew-27-42-douay

43 He trusted in God: let him now deliver him if he will have him. For he said: I am the Son of God. ^matthew-27-43-douay

44 And the selfsame thing the thieves also that were crucified with him reproached him with. ^matthew-27-44-douay

45 Now from the sixth hour, there was darkness over the whole earth, until the ninth hour. ^matthew-27-45-douay

46 And about the ninth hour, Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying: Eli, Eli, lamma sabacthani? That is, My God, My God, why hast thou forsaken me? ^matthew-27-46-douay

47 And some that stood there and heard said: This man calleth Elias. ^matthew-27-47-douay

48 And immediately one of them running took a sponge and filled it with vinegar and put it on a reed and gave him to drink. ^matthew-27-48-douay

49 And the others said: Let be. Let us see whether Elias will come to deliver him. ^matthew-27-49-douay

50 And Jesus again crying with a loud voice, yielded up the ghost. ^matthew-27-50-douay

51 And behold the veil of the temple was rent in two from the top even to the bottom: and the earth quaked and the rocks were rent. ^matthew-27-51-douay

52 And the graves were opened: and many bodies of the saints that had slept arose, ^matthew-27-52-douay

53 And coming out of the tombs after his resurrection, came into the holy city and appeared to many. ^matthew-27-53-douay

54 Now the centurion and they that were with him watching Jesus, having seen the earthquake and the things that were done, were sore afraid, saying: Indeed this was the Son of God. ^matthew-27-54-douay

55 And there were there many women afar off, who had followed Jesus from Galilee, ministering unto him: ^matthew-27-55-douay

56 Among whom was Mary Magdalen and Mary the mother of James and Joseph and the mother of the sons of Zebedee. ^matthew-27-56-douay

57 And when it was evening, there came a certain rich man of Arimathea, named Joseph, who also himself was a disciple of Jesus. ^matthew-27-57-douay

58 He went to Pilate and asked the body of Jesus. Then Pilate commanded that the body should be delivered. ^matthew-27-58-douay

59 And Joseph taking the body wrapped it up in a clean linen cloth: ^matthew-27-59-douay

60 And laid it in his own new monument, which he had hewed out in a rock. And he rolled a great stone to the door of the monument and went his way. ^matthew-27-60-douay

61 And there was there Mary Magdalen and the other Mary, sitting over against the sepulchre. ^matthew-27-61-douay

62 And the next day, which followed the day of preparation, the chief priests and the Pharisees came together to Pilate, **The day of preparation...** The eve of the sabbath; so called, because on that day they prepared all things necessary; not being allowed so much as to dress their meat on the sabbath day. ^matthew-27-62-douay

63 Saying: Sir, we have remembered, that that seducer said, while he was yet alive: After three days I will rise again. ^matthew-27-63-douay

64 Command therefore the sepulchre to be guarded until the third day: lest perhaps his disciples come and steal him away and say to the people: He is risen from the dead. And the last error shall be worse than the first. ^matthew-27-64-douay

65 Pilate saith to them: You have a guard. Go, guard it as you know. ^matthew-27-65-douay

66 And they departing, made the sepulchre sure, sealing the stone and setting guards. ^matthew-27-66-douay
