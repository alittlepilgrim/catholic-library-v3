# Matthew Chapter 20

The parable of the labourers in the vineyard. The ambition of the two sons of Zebedee. Christ gives sight to two blind men.

1 The kingdom of heaven is like to an householder, who went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard. ^matthew-20-1-douay

2 And having agreed with the labourers for a penny a day, he sent them into his vineyard. ^matthew-20-2-douay

3 And going out about the third hour, he saw others standing in the marketplace idle. ^matthew-20-3-douay

4 And he said to them: Go you also into my vineyard, and I will give you what shall be just. ^matthew-20-4-douay

5 And they went their way. And again he went out about the sixth and the ninth hour, and did in like manner. ^matthew-20-5-douay

6 But about the eleventh hour he went out and found others standing, and he saith to them: Why stand you here all the day idle? ^matthew-20-6-douay

7 They say to him: Because no man hath hired us. He saith to them: Go ye also into my vineyard. ^matthew-20-7-douay

8 And when evening was come, the lord of the vineyard saith to his steward: Call the labourers and pay them their hire, beginning from the last even to the first. ^matthew-20-8-douay

9 When therefore they were come that came about the eleventh hour, they received every man a penny. ^matthew-20-9-douay

10 But when the first also came, they thought that they should receive more: And they also received every man a penny. ^matthew-20-10-douay

11 And receiving it they murmured against the master of the house, ^matthew-20-11-douay

12 Saying: These last have worked but one hour and thou hast made them equal to us, that have borne the burden of the day and the heats. ^matthew-20-12-douay

13 But he answering said to one of them: friend, I do thee no wrong: didst thou not agree with me for a penny? ^matthew-20-13-douay

14 Take what is thine, and go thy way: I will also give to this last even as to thee. ^matthew-20-14-douay

15 Or, is it not lawful for me to do what I will? Is thy eye evil, because I am good? **What I will...** Viz., with my own, and in matters that depend on my own bounty. ^matthew-20-15-douay

16 So shall the last be first and the first last. For many are called but few chosen. ^matthew-20-16-douay

17 And Jesus going up to Jerusalem, took the twelve disciples apart and said to them: ^matthew-20-17-douay

18 Behold we go up to Jerusalem, and the Son of man shall be betrayed to the chief priests and the scribes: and they shall condemn him to death. ^matthew-20-18-douay

19 And shall deliver him to the Gentiles to be mocked and scourged and crucified: and the third day he shall rise again. ^matthew-20-19-douay

20 Then came to him the mother of the sons of Zebedee with her sons, adoring and asking something of him. ^matthew-20-20-douay

21 Who said to her: What wilt thou? She saith to him: say that these my two sons may sit, the one on thy right hand, and the other on thy left, in thy kingdom. ^matthew-20-21-douay

22 And Jesus answering, said: You know not what you ask. Can you drink the chalice that I shall drink? They say to him: We can. ^matthew-20-22-douay

23 He saith to them: My chalice indeed you shall drink; but to sit on my right or left hand is not mine to give to you, but to them for whom it is prepared by my Father. ^matthew-20-23-douay

24 And the ten, hearing it, were moved with indignation against the two brethren. ^matthew-20-24-douay

25 But Jesus called them to him and said: You know that the princes of the Gentiles lord it over them; and that they that are the greater, exercise power upon them. ^matthew-20-25-douay

26 It shall not be so among you: but whosoever is the greater among you, let him be your minister. ^matthew-20-26-douay

27 And he that will be first among you shall be your servant. ^matthew-20-27-douay

28 Even as the Son of man is not come to be ministered unto, but to minister and to give his life a redemption for many. ^matthew-20-28-douay

29 And when they went out from Jericho, a great multitude followed him. ^matthew-20-29-douay

30 And behold two blind men sitting by the way side heard that Jesus passed by. And they cried out, saying: O Lord, thou son of David, have mercy on us. ^matthew-20-30-douay

31 And the multitude rebuked them that they should hold their peace. But they cried out the more, saying: O Lord, thou son of David, have mercy on us. ^matthew-20-31-douay

32 And Jesus stood and called them and said: What will ye that I do to you? ^matthew-20-32-douay

33 They say to him: Lord, that our eyes be opened. ^matthew-20-33-douay

34 And Jesus having compassion on them, touched their eyes. And immediately they saw and followed him. ^matthew-20-34-douay
