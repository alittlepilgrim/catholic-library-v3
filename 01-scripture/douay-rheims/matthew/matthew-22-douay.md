# Matthew Chapter 22

The parable of the marriage feast. Christ orders tribute to be paid to Caesar. He confutes the Sadducees, shows which is the first commandment in the law and puzzles the Pharisees.

1 And Jesus answering, spoke again in parables to them, saying: ^matthew-22-1-douay

2 The kingdom of heaven is likened to a king who made a marriage for his son. ^matthew-22-2-douay

3 And he sent his servants to call them that were invited to the marriage: and they would not come. ^matthew-22-3-douay

4 Again he sent other servants, saying: Tell them that were invited, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: my beeves and fatlings are killed, and all things are ready. Come ye to the marriage. ^matthew-22-4-douay

5 But they neglected and went their ways, one to his farm and another to his merchandise. ^matthew-22-5-douay

6 And the rest laid hands on his servants and, having treated them contumeliously, put them to death. ^matthew-22-6-douay

7 But when the king had heard of it, he was angry: and sending his armies, he destroyed those murderers and burnt their city. ^matthew-22-7-douay

8 Then he saith to his servants: The marriage indeed is ready; but they that were invited were not worthy. ^matthew-22-8-douay

9 Go ye therefore into the highways; and as many as you shall find, call to the marriage. ^matthew-22-9-douay

10 And his servants going forth into the ways, gathered together all that they found, both bad and good: and the marriage was filled with guests. ^matthew-22-10-douay

11 And the king went in to see the guests: and he saw there a man who had not on a wedding garment. ^matthew-22-11-douay

12 And he saith to him: Friend, how camest thou in hither not having on a wedding garment? But he was silent. ^matthew-22-12-douay

13 Then the king said to the waiters: Bind his hands and feet, and cast him into the exterior darkness. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth. ^matthew-22-13-douay

14 For many are called, but few are chosen. ^matthew-22-14-douay

15 Then the Pharisees going, consulted among themselves how to insnare him in his speech. ^matthew-22-15-douay

16 And they sent to him their disciples with the Herodians, saying: Master, we know that thou art a true speaker and teachest the way of God in truth. Neither carest thou for any man: for thou dost not regard the person of men. **The Herodians...** That is, some that belonged to Herod, and that joined with him in standing up for the necessity of paying tribute to Caesar, that is, to the Roman emperor. Some are of opinion that there was a sect among the Jews called Herodians, from their maintaining that Herod was the Messias. ^matthew-22-16-douay

17 Tell us therefore what dost thou think? Is it lawful to give tribute to Caesar, or not? ^matthew-22-17-douay

18 But Jesus knowing their wickedness, said: Why do you tempt me, ye hypocrites? ^matthew-22-18-douay

19 Show me the coin of the tribute. And they offered him a penny. ^matthew-22-19-douay

20 And Jesus saith to them: Whose image and inscription is this? ^matthew-22-20-douay

21 They say to him: Caesar's. Then he saith to them: Render therefore to Caesar the things that are Caesar's; and to God, the things that are God's. ^matthew-22-21-douay

22 And hearing this, they wondered and, leaving him, went their ways. ^matthew-22-22-douay

23 That day there came to him the Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection; and asked him, ^matthew-22-23-douay

24 Saying: Master, Moses said: If a man die having no son, his brother shall marry his wife and raise up issue to his brother. ^matthew-22-24-douay

25 Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first having married a wife, died; and not having issue, left his wife to his brother. ^matthew-22-25-douay

26 In like manner the second and the third and so on, to the seventh. ^matthew-22-26-douay

27 And last of all the woman died also. ^matthew-22-27-douay

28 At the resurrection therefore, whose wife of the seven shall she be? For they all had her. ^matthew-22-28-douay

29 And Jesus answering, said to them: You err, not knowing the Scriptures nor the power of God. ^matthew-22-29-douay

30 For in the resurrection they shall neither marry nor be married, but shall be as the angels of God in heaven. ^matthew-22-30-douay

31 And concerning the resurrection of the dead, have you not read that which was spoken by God, saying to you: ^matthew-22-31-douay

32 I am the God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob? He is not the God of the dead but of the living. ^matthew-22-32-douay

33 And the multitudes hearing it were in admiration at his doctrine. ^matthew-22-33-douay

34 But the Pharisees, hearing that he had silenced the Sadducees, came together. ^matthew-22-34-douay

35 And one of them, a doctor of the law, asked him, tempting him: ^matthew-22-35-douay

36 Master, which is the great commandment in the law? ^matthew-22-36-douay

37 Jesus said to him: Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with thy whole heart and with thy whole soul and with thy whole mind. ^matthew-22-37-douay

38 This is the greatest and the first commandment. ^matthew-22-38-douay

39 And the second is like to this: Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. ^matthew-22-39-douay

40 On these two commandments dependeth the whole law and the prophets. ^matthew-22-40-douay

41 And the Pharisees being gathered together, Jesus asked them, ^matthew-22-41-douay

42 Saying: What think you of Christ? Whose son is he? They say to him: David's. ^matthew-22-42-douay

43 He saith to them: How then doth David in spirit call him Lord, saying: ^matthew-22-43-douay

44 The Lord said to my Lord: Sit on my right hand, until I make thy enemies thy footstool? ^matthew-22-44-douay

45 If David then call him Lord, how is he his son? ^matthew-22-45-douay

46 And no man was able to answer him a word: neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions. ^matthew-22-46-douay
