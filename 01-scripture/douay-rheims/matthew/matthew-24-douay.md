# Matthew Chapter 24

Christ foretells the destruction of the temple, with the signs that shall come before it and before the last judgment. We must always watch.

1 And Jesus being come out of the temple, went away. And his disciples came to show him the buildings of the temple. ^matthew-24-1-douay

2 And he answering, said to them: Do you see all these things? Amen I say to you, there shall not be left here a stone upon a stone that shall not be destroyed. ^matthew-24-2-douay

3 And when he was sitting on mount Olivet, the disciples came to him privately, saying: Tell us when shall these things be? And what shall be the sign of thy coming and of the consummation of the world? ^matthew-24-3-douay

4 And Jesus answering, said to them: Take heed that no man seduce you. ^matthew-24-4-douay

5 For many will come in my name saying, I am Christ. And they will seduce many. ^matthew-24-5-douay

6 And you shall hear of wars and rumours of wars. See that ye be not troubled. For these things must come to pass: but the end is not yet. ^matthew-24-6-douay

7 For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be pestilences and famines and earthquakes in places. ^matthew-24-7-douay

8 Now all these are the beginnings of sorrows. ^matthew-24-8-douay

9 Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted and shall put you to death: and you shall be hated by all nations for my name's sake. ^matthew-24-9-douay

10 And then shall many be scandalized and shall betray one another and shall hate one another. ^matthew-24-10-douay

11 And many false prophets shall rise and shall seduce many. ^matthew-24-11-douay

12 And because iniquity hath abounded, the charity of many shall grow cold. ^matthew-24-12-douay

13 But he that shall persevere to the end, he shall be saved. ^matthew-24-13-douay

14 And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in the whole world, for a testimony to all nations: and then shall the consummation come. ^matthew-24-14-douay

15 When therefore you shall see the abomination of desolation, which was spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place: he that readeth let him understand. ^matthew-24-15-douay

16 Then they that are in Judea, let them flee to the mountains: ^matthew-24-16-douay

17 And he that is on the housetop, let him not come down to take any thing out of his house: ^matthew-24-17-douay

18 And he that is in the field, let him not go back to take his coat. ^matthew-24-18-douay

19 And woe to them that are with child and that give suck in those days. ^matthew-24-19-douay

20 But pray that your flight be not in the winter or on the sabbath. ^matthew-24-20-douay

21 For there shall be then great tribulation, such as hath not been from the beginning of the world until now, neither shall be. ^matthew-24-21-douay

22 And unless those days had been shortened, no flesh should be saved: but for the sake of the elect those days shall be shortened. ^matthew-24-22-douay

23 Then if any man shall say to you, Lo here is Christ, or there: do not believe him. ^matthew-24-23-douay

24 For there shall arise false Christs and false prophets and shall show great signs and wonders, insomuch as to deceive (if possible) even the elect. ^matthew-24-24-douay

25 Behold I have told it to you, beforehand. ^matthew-24-25-douay

26 If therefore they shall say to you, Behold he is in the desert: go ye not out. Behold he is in the closets: believe it not. ^matthew-24-26-douay

27 For as lightning cometh out of the east and appeareth even into the west: so shall also the coming of the Son of man be. ^matthew-24-27-douay

28 Wheresoever the body shall be, there shall the eagles also be gathered together. **Wheresoever, etc...** The coming of Christ shall be sudden, and manifest to all the world, like lightning: and wheresoever he shall come, thither shall all mankind be gathered to him, as eagles are gathered about a dead body. ^matthew-24-28-douay

29 And immediately after the tribulation of those days, the sun shall be darkened and the moon shall not give her light and the stars shall fall from heaven and the powers of heaven shall be moved. **The stars...** Or flaming meteors resembling stars. ^matthew-24-29-douay

30 And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven. And then shall all tribes of the earth mourn: and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with much power and majesty. **The sign, etc...** The cross of Christ. ^matthew-24-30-douay

31 And he shall send his angels with a trumpet and a great voice: and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from the farthest parts of the heavens to the utmost bounds of them. ^matthew-24-31-douay

32 And from the fig tree learn a parable: When the branch thereof is now tender and the leaves come forth, you know that summer is nigh. ^matthew-24-32-douay

33 So you also, when you shall see all these things, know ye that it is nigh, even at the doors. ^matthew-24-33-douay

34 Amen I say to you that this generation shall not pass till all these things be done. ^matthew-24-34-douay

35 Heaven and earth shall pass: but my words shall not pass. **Shall pass...** Because they shall be changed at the end of the world into a new heaven and new earth. ^matthew-24-35-douay

36 But of that day and hour no one knoweth: no, not the angels of heaven, but the Father alone. ^matthew-24-36-douay

37 And as in the days of Noe, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be. ^matthew-24-37-douay

38 For, as in the days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, even till that day in which Noe entered into the ark: ^matthew-24-38-douay

39 And they knew not till the flood came and took them all away: so also shall the coming of the Son of man be. ^matthew-24-39-douay

40 Then two shall be in the field. One shall be taken and one shall be left. ^matthew-24-40-douay

41 Two women shall be grinding at the mill. One shall be taken and one shall be left. ^matthew-24-41-douay

42 Watch ye therefore, because you know not what hour your Lord will come. ^matthew-24-42-douay

43 But this know ye, that, if the goodman of the house knew at what hour the thief would come, he would certainly watch and would not suffer his house to be broken open. ^matthew-24-43-douay

44 Wherefore be you also ready, because at what hour you know not the Son of man will come. ^matthew-24-44-douay

45 Who, thinkest thou, is a faithful and wise servant, whom his lord hath appointed over his family, to give them meat in season? ^matthew-24-45-douay

46 Blessed is that servant, whom when his lord shall come he shall find so doing. ^matthew-24-46-douay

47 Amen I say to you: he shall place him over all his goods. ^matthew-24-47-douay

48 But if that evil servant shall say in his heart: My lord is long a coming: ^matthew-24-48-douay

49 And shall begin to strike his fellow servants and shall eat and drink with drunkards: ^matthew-24-49-douay

50 The lord of that servant shall come in a day that he hopeth not and at an hour that he knoweth not: ^matthew-24-50-douay

51 And shall separate him and appoint his portion with the hypocrites. There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth. ^matthew-24-51-douay
