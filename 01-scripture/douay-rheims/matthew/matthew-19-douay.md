# Matthew Chapter 19

Christ declares matrimony to be indissoluble: he recommends the making one's self an eunuch for the kingdom of heaven; and parting with all things for him. He shows the danger of riches, and the reward of leaving all to follow him.

1 And it came to pass when Jesus had ended these words, he departed from Galilee and came into the coasts of Judea, beyond Jordan. ^matthew-19-1-douay

2 And great multitudes followed him: and he healed them there. ^matthew-19-2-douay

3 And there came to him the Pharisees tempting him, saying: Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause? ^matthew-19-3-douay

4 Who answering, said to them: Have ye not read, that he who made man from the beginning, made them male and female? And he said: ^matthew-19-4-douay

5 For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife, and they two shall be in one flesh. ^matthew-19-5-douay

6 Therefore now they are not two, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let no man put asunder. ^matthew-19-6-douay

7 They say to him: Why then did Moses command to give a bill of divorce, and to put away? ^matthew-19-7-douay

8 He saith to them: Because Moses by reason of the hardness of your heart permitted you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so. ^matthew-19-8-douay

9 And I say to you, that whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery: and he that shall marry her that is put away, committeth adultery. **Except it be, etc...** In the case of fornication, that is, of adultery, the wife may be put away: but even then the husband cannot marry another as long as the wife is living. ^matthew-19-9-douay

10 His disciples say unto him: If the case of a man with his wife be so, it is not expedient to marry. ^matthew-19-10-douay

11 Who said to them: All men take not this word, but they to whom it is given. **All men take not this word...** That is, all receive not the gift of living singly and chastely, unless they pray for the grace of God to enable them to live so, and for some it may be necessary to that end to fast as well as pray: and to those it is given from above. ^matthew-19-11-douay

12 For there are eunuchs, who were born so from their mothers womb: and there are eunuchs, who were made so by men: and there are eunuchs, who have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven. He that can take, let him take it. **There are eunuchs, who have made themselves eunuchs, for the kingdom of heaven...** This text is not to be taken in the literal sense; but means, that there are such, who have taken a firm and commendable resolution of leading a single and chaste life, in order to serve God in a more perfect state than those who marry: as St. Paul clearly shows. [[1-corinthians-07-douay|1 Corinthians 7:37-38]]. ^matthew-19-12-douay

13 Then were little children presented to him, that he should impose hands upon them and pray. And the disciples rebuked them. ^matthew-19-13-douay

14 But Jesus said to them: Suffer the little children, and forbid them not to come to me: for the kingdom of heaven is for such. ^matthew-19-14-douay

15 And when he had imposed hands upon them, he departed from thence. ^matthew-19-15-douay

16 And behold one came and said to him: Good master, what good shall I do that I may have life everlasting? ^matthew-19-16-douay

17 Who said to him: Why askest thou me concerning good? One is good, God. But if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments. ^matthew-19-17-douay

18 He said to him: Which? And Jesus said: Thou shalt do no murder, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness. ^matthew-19-18-douay

19 Honour thy father and thy mother: and, Thou shalt love thy neighbor as thyself. ^matthew-19-19-douay

20 The young man saith to him: All these have I kept from my youth, what is yet wanting to me? ^matthew-19-20-douay

21 Jesus saith to him: If thou wilt be perfect, go sell what thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven: and come, follow me. ^matthew-19-21-douay

22 And when the young man had heard this word, he went away sad: for he had great possessions. ^matthew-19-22-douay

23 Then Jesus said to his disciples: Amen, I say to you, that a rich man shall hardly enter into the kingdom of heaven. ^matthew-19-23-douay

24 And again I say to you: It is easier for a camel to pass through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of heaven. ^matthew-19-24-douay

25 And when they had heard this, the disciples wondered much, saying: Who then can be saved? ^matthew-19-25-douay

26 And Jesus beholding, said to them: With men this is impossible: but with God all things are possible. ^matthew-19-26-douay

27 Then Peter answering, said to him: Behold we have left all things, and have followed thee: what therefore shall we have? ^matthew-19-27-douay

28 And Jesus said to them: Amen I say to you, that you who have followed me, in the regeneration, when the Son of man shall sit on the seat of his majesty, you also shall sit on twelve seats judging the twelve tribes of Israel. ^matthew-19-28-douay

29 And every one that hath left house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands for my name's sake, shall receive an hundredfold, and shall possess life everlasting. ^matthew-19-29-douay

30 And many that are first, shall be last: and the last shall be first. ^matthew-19-30-douay
