# Matthew Chapter 14

Herod puts John to death. Christ feeds five thousand in the desert. He walks upon the sea, and heals all the diseased with the touch of his garment.

1 At that time Herod the Tetrarch heard the fame of Jesus. **Tetrarch...** This word, derived from the Greek, signifies one that rules over the fourth part of a kingdom: as Herod then ruled over Galilee, which was but the fourth part of the kingdom of his father. ^matthew-14-1-douay

2 And he said to his servants: This is John the Baptist: he is risen from the dead, and therefore mighty works show forth themselves in him. ^matthew-14-2-douay

3 For Herod had apprehended John and bound him, and put him into prison, because of Herodias, his brother's wife. ^matthew-14-3-douay

4 For John said to him: It is not lawful for thee to have her. ^matthew-14-4-douay

5 And having a mind to put him to death, he feared the people: because they esteemed him as a prophet. ^matthew-14-5-douay

6 But on Herod's birthday, the daughter of Herodias danced before them: and pleased Herod. ^matthew-14-6-douay

7 Whereupon he promised with an oath, to give her whatsoever she would ask of him. ^matthew-14-7-douay

8 But she being instructed before by her mother, said: Give me here in a dish the head of John the Baptist. ^matthew-14-8-douay

9 And the king was struck sad: yet because of his oath, and for them that sat with him at table, he commanded it to be given. ^matthew-14-9-douay

10 And he sent, and beheaded John in the prison. ^matthew-14-10-douay

11 And his head was brought in a dish: and it was given to the damsel, and she brought it to her mother. ^matthew-14-11-douay

12 And his disciples came and took the body, and buried it, and came and told Jesus. ^matthew-14-12-douay

13 Which when Jesus had heard, he retired from thence by a boat, into a desert place apart, and the multitudes having heard of it, followed him on foot out of the cities. ^matthew-14-13-douay

14 And he coming forth saw a great multitude, and had compassion on them, and healed their sick. ^matthew-14-14-douay

15 And when it was evening, his disciples came to him, saying: This is a desert place, and the hour is now past: send away the multitudes, that going into the towns, they may buy themselves victuals. ^matthew-14-15-douay

16 But Jesus said to them, They have no need to go: give you them to eat. ^matthew-14-16-douay

17 They answered him: We have not here, but five loaves, and two fishes. ^matthew-14-17-douay

18 Who said to them: Bring them hither to me. ^matthew-14-18-douay

19 And when he had commanded the multitude to sit down upon the grass, he took the five loaves and the two fishes, and looking up to heaven, he blessed, and brake, and gave the loaves to his disciples, and the disciples to the multitudes. ^matthew-14-19-douay

20 And they did all eat, and were filled. And they took up what remained, twelve full baskets of fragments. ^matthew-14-20-douay

21 And the number of them that did eat, was five thousand men, besides women and children. ^matthew-14-21-douay

22 And forthwith Jesus obliged his disciples to go up into the boat, and to go before him over the water, till he dismissed the people. ^matthew-14-22-douay

23 And having dismissed the multitude, he went into a mountain alone to pray. And when it was evening, he was there alone. ^matthew-14-23-douay

24 But the boat in the midst of the sea was tossed with the waves: for the wind was contrary. ^matthew-14-24-douay

25 And in the fourth watch of the night, he came to them walking upon the sea. ^matthew-14-25-douay

26 And they seeing him walking upon the sea, were troubled, saying: It is an apparition. And they cried out for fear. ^matthew-14-26-douay

27 And immediately Jesus spoke to them, saying: Be of good heart: it is I, fear ye not. ^matthew-14-27-douay

28 And Peter making answer, said: Lord, if it be thou, bid me come to thee upon the waters. ^matthew-14-28-douay

29 And he said: Come. And Peter going down out of the boat walked upon the water to come to Jesus. ^matthew-14-29-douay

30 But seeing the wind strong, he was afraid: and when he began to sink, he cried out, saying: Lord, save me. ^matthew-14-30-douay

31 And immediately Jesus stretching forth his hand took hold of him, and said to him: O thou of little faith, why didst thou doubt? ^matthew-14-31-douay

32 And when they were come up into the boat, the wind ceased. ^matthew-14-32-douay

33 And they that were in the boat came and adored him, saying: Indeed thou art the Son of God. ^matthew-14-33-douay

34 And having passed the water, they came into the country of Genesar. ^matthew-14-34-douay

35 And when the men of that place had knowledge of him, they sent into all that country, and brought to him all that were diseased. ^matthew-14-35-douay

36 And they besought him that they might touch but the hem of his garment. And as many as touched, were made whole. ^matthew-14-36-douay
