# Lamentations Chapter 5

The prayer of Jeremiah the Prophet.

1 Remember, O Lord, what is come upon us: consider and behold our reproach. ^lamentations-05-1-douay

2 Our inheritance is turned to aliens: our houses to strangers. ^lamentations-05-2-douay

3 We are become orphans without a father: our mothers are as widows. ^lamentations-05-3-douay

4 We have drunk our water for money: we have bought our wood. ^lamentations-05-4-douay

5 We were dragged by the necks, we were weary and no rest was given us. ^lamentations-05-5-douay

6 We have given our hand to Egypt, and to the Assyrians, that we might be satisfied with bread. ^lamentations-05-6-douay

7 Our fathers have sinned, and are not: and we have borne their iniquities. ^lamentations-05-7-douay

8 Servants have ruled over us: there was none to redeem us out of their hand. ^lamentations-05-8-douay

9 We fetched our bread at the peril of our lives, because of the sword in the desert. ^lamentations-05-9-douay

10 Our skin was burnt as an oven, by reason of the violence of the famine. ^lamentations-05-10-douay

11 They oppressed the women in Sion, and the virgins in the cities of Juda. ^lamentations-05-11-douay

12 The princes were hanged up by their hand: they did not respect the persons of the ancients. ^lamentations-05-12-douay

13 They abused the young men indecently: and the children fell under the wood. ^lamentations-05-13-douay

14 The ancients have ceased from the gates: the young men from the choir of the singers. ^lamentations-05-14-douay

15 The joy of our heart is ceased, our dancing is turned into mourning. ^lamentations-05-15-douay

16 The crown is fallen from our head: woe to us, because we have sinned. ^lamentations-05-16-douay

17 Therefore is our heart sorrowful, therefore are our eyes become dim. ^lamentations-05-17-douay

18 For mount Sion, because it is destroyed, foxes have walked upon it. ^lamentations-05-18-douay

19 But thou, O Lord, shalt remain for ever, thy throne from generation to generation. ^lamentations-05-19-douay

20 Why wilt thou forget us for ever? why wilt thou forsake us for a long time? ^lamentations-05-20-douay

21 Convert us, O Lord, to thee, and we shall be converted: renew our days, as from the beginning. ^lamentations-05-21-douay

22 But thou hast utterly rejected us, thou art exceedingly angry against us. ^lamentations-05-22-douay
