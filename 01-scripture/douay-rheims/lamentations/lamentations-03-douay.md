# Lamentations Chapter 3

Third alphabetical cycle.

1 *Aleph.* I am the man that see my poverty by the rod of his indignation. ^lamentations-03-1-douay

2 *Aleph.* He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, and not into light. ^lamentations-03-2-douay

3 *Aleph.* Only against me he hath turned, and turned again his hand all the day. ^lamentations-03-3-douay

4 *Beth.* My skin and my flesh he hath made old, he hath broken my bones. ^lamentations-03-4-douay

5 *Beth.* He hath built round about me, and he hath compassed me with gall, and labour. ^lamentations-03-5-douay

6 *Beth.* He hath set me in dark places as those that are dead for ever. ^lamentations-03-6-douay

7 *Ghimel.* He hath built against me round about, that I may not get out: he hath made my fetters heavy. ^lamentations-03-7-douay

8 *Ghimel.* Yea, and when I cry, and entreat, he hath shut out my prayer. ^lamentations-03-8-douay

9 *Ghimel.* He hath shut up my ways with square stones, he hath turned my paths upside down. ^lamentations-03-9-douay

10 *Daleth.* He is become to me as a bear lying in wait: as a lion in secret places. ^lamentations-03-10-douay

11 *Daleth.* He hath turned aside my paths, and hath broken me in pieces, he hath made me desolate. ^lamentations-03-11-douay

12 *Daleth.* He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for his arrows. ^lamentations-03-12-douay

13 *He.* He hath shot into my reins the daughters of his quiver. ^lamentations-03-13-douay

14 *He.* I am made a derision to all my people, their song all the day long. ^lamentations-03-14-douay

15 *He.* He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath inebriated me with wormwood. ^lamentations-03-15-douay

16 *Vau.* And he hath broken my teeth one by one, he hath fed me with ashes. ^lamentations-03-16-douay

17 *Vau.* And my soul is removed far off from peace, I have forgotten good things. ^lamentations-03-17-douay

18 *Vau.* And I said: My end and my hope is perished from the Lord. ^lamentations-03-18-douay

19 *Zain.* Remember my poverty, and transgression, the wormwood and the gall. ^lamentations-03-19-douay

20 *Zain.* I will be mindful and remember, and my soul shall languish within me. ^lamentations-03-20-douay

21 *Zain.* These things I shall think over in my heart, therefore will I hope. ^lamentations-03-21-douay

22 *Heth.* The mercies of the Lord that we are not consumed: because his commiserations have not failed. ^lamentations-03-22-douay

23 *Heth.* They are new every morning, great is thy faithfulness. ^lamentations-03-23-douay

24 *Heth.* The Lord is my portion, said my soul: therefore will I wait for him. ^lamentations-03-24-douay

25 *Teth.* The Lord is good to them that hope in him, to the soul that seeketh him. ^lamentations-03-25-douay

26 *Teth.* It is good to wait with silence for the salvation of God. ^lamentations-03-26-douay

27 *Teth.* It is good for a man, when he hath borne the yoke from his youth. ^lamentations-03-27-douay

28 *Jod.* He shall sit solitary, and hold his peace: because he hath taken it up upon himself. ^lamentations-03-28-douay

29 *Jod.* He shall put his mouth in the dust, if so be there may be hope. ^lamentations-03-29-douay

30 *Jod.* He shall give his cheek to him that striketh him, he shall be filled with reproaches. ^lamentations-03-30-douay

31 *Caph.* For the Lord will not cast off for ever. ^lamentations-03-31-douay

32 *Caph.* For if he hath cast off, he will also have mercy, according to the multitude of his mercies. ^lamentations-03-32-douay

33 *Caph.* For he hath not willingly afflicted, nor cast off the children of men. ^lamentations-03-33-douay

34 *Lamed.* To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the land, ^lamentations-03-34-douay

35 *Lamed.* To turn aside the judgment of a man before the face of the most High, ^lamentations-03-35-douay

36 *Lamed.* To destroy a man wrongfully in his judgment, the Lord hath not approved. ^lamentations-03-36-douay

37 *Mem.* Who is he that hath commanded a thing to be done, when the Lord commandeth it not? ^lamentations-03-37-douay

38 *Mem.* Shall not both evil and good proceed out of the mouth of the Highest? ^lamentations-03-38-douay

39 *Mem.* Why hath a living man murmured, man suffering for his sins? ^lamentations-03-39-douay

40 *Nun.* Let us search our ways, and seek, and return to the Lord. ^lamentations-03-40-douay

41 *Nun.* Let us lift up our hearts with our hands to the Lord in the heavens. ^lamentations-03-41-douay

42 *Nun.* We have done wickedly, and provoked thee to wrath: therefore thou art inexorable. ^lamentations-03-42-douay

43 *Samech.* Thou hast covered in thy wrath, and hast struck us: thou hast killed and hast not spared. ^lamentations-03-43-douay

44 *Samech.* Thou hast set a cloud before thee, that our prayer may not pass through. ^lamentations-03-44-douay

45 *Samech.* Thou hast made me as an outcast, and refuse in the midst of the people. ^lamentations-03-45-douay

46 *Phe.* All our enemies have opened their mouths against us. ^lamentations-03-46-douay

47 *Phe.* Prophecy is become to us a fear, and a snare, and destruction. ^lamentations-03-47-douay

48 *Phe.* My eye hath run down with streams of water, for the destruction of the daughter of my people. ^lamentations-03-48-douay

49 *Ain.* My eye is afflicted, and hath not been quiet, because there was no rest: ^lamentations-03-49-douay

50 *Ain.* Till the Lord regarded and looked down from the heavens. ^lamentations-03-50-douay

51 *Ain.* My eye hath wasted my soul because of all the daughters of my city. ^lamentations-03-51-douay

52 *Sade.* My enemies have chased me and caught me like a bird, without cause. ^lamentations-03-52-douay

53 *Sade.* My life is fallen into the pit, and they have laid a stone over me. ^lamentations-03-53-douay

54 *Sade.* Waters have flowed over my head: I said: I am cut off. ^lamentations-03-54-douay

55 *Coph.* I have called upon thy name, O Lord, from the lowest pit. ^lamentations-03-55-douay

56 *Coph.* Thou hast heard my voice: turn not away thy ear from my sighs, and cries. ^lamentations-03-56-douay

57 *Coph.* Thou drewest near in the day, when I called upon thee, thou saidst: Fear not. ^lamentations-03-57-douay

58 *Res.* Thou hast judged, O Lord, the cause of my soul, thou the Redeemer of my life. ^lamentations-03-58-douay

59 *Res.* Thou hast seen, O Lord, their iniquity against me: judge thou my judgment. ^lamentations-03-59-douay

60 *Res.* Thou hast seen all their fury, and all their thoughts against me. ^lamentations-03-60-douay

61 *Sin.* Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, all their imaginations against me. ^lamentations-03-61-douay

62 *Sin.* The lips of them that rise up against me: and their devices against me all the day. ^lamentations-03-62-douay

63 *Sin.* Behold their sitting down, and their rising up, I am their song. ^lamentations-03-63-douay

64 *Thau.* Thou shalt render them a recompense, O Lord, according to the works of their hands. ^lamentations-03-64-douay

65 *Thau.* Thou shalt give them a buckler of heart, thy labour. ^lamentations-03-65-douay

66 *Thau.* Thou shalt persecute them in anger, and shalt destroy them from under the heavens, O Lord. ^lamentations-03-66-douay
