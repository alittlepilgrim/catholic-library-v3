# 1 Peter Chapter 5

He exhorts both priests and laity to their respective duties and recommends to all humility and watchfulness.

1 The ancients therefore that are among you, I beseech who am myself also an ancient and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, as also a partaker of that glory which is to be revealed in time to come: ^1-peter-05-1-douay

2 Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking care of it, not by constraint but willingly, according to God: not for filthy lucre's sake but voluntarily: ^1-peter-05-2-douay

3 Neither as lording it over the clergy but being made a pattern of the flock from the heart. ^1-peter-05-3-douay

4 And when the prince of pastors shall appear, you shall receive a never fading crown of glory. ^1-peter-05-4-douay

5 In like manner, ye young men, be subject to the ancients. And do you all insinuate humility one to another: for God resisteth the proud, but to the humble he giveth grace. ^1-peter-05-5-douay

6 Be you humbled therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in the time of visitation: ^1-peter-05-6-douay

7 Casting all your care upon him, for he hath care of you. ^1-peter-05-7-douay

8 Be sober and watch: because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, goeth about seeking whom he may devour. ^1-peter-05-8-douay

9 Whom resist ye, strong in faith: knowing that the same affliction befalls your brethren who are in the world. ^1-peter-05-9-douay

10 But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory in Christ Jesus, after you have suffered a little, will himself perfect you and confirm you and establish you. ^1-peter-05-10-douay

11 To him be glory and empire, for ever and ever. Amen. ^1-peter-05-11-douay

12 By Sylvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I think, I have written briefly: beseeching and testifying that this is the true grace of God, wherein you stand. ^1-peter-05-12-douay

13 The church that is in Babylon, elected together with you, saluteth you. And so doth my son, Mark. ^1-peter-05-13-douay

14 Salute one another with a holy kiss. Grace be to all you who are in Christ Jesus. Amen. ^1-peter-05-14-douay
