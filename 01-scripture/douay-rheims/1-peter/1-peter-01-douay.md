# 1 Peter Chapter 1

He gives thanks to God for the benefit of our being called to the true faith and to eternal life, into which we are to enter by many tribulations. He exhorts to holiness of life, considering the holiness of God and our redemption by the blood of Christ.

1 Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers dispersed through Pontus, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia, elect, ^1-peter-01-1-douay

2 According to the foreknowledge of God the Father, unto the sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ. Grace unto you and peace be multiplied. ^1-peter-01-2-douay

3 Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who according to his great mercy hath regenerated us unto a lively hope, by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead: ^1-peter-01-3-douay

4 Unto an inheritance, incorruptible, and undefiled and that cannot fade, reserved in heaven for you, ^1-peter-01-4-douay

5 Who, by the power of God, are kept by faith unto salvation, ready to be revealed in the last time. ^1-peter-01-5-douay

6 Wherein you shalt greatly rejoice, if now you must be for a little time made sorrowful in divers temptations: ^1-peter-01-6-douay

7 That the trial of your faith (much more precious than gold which is tried by the fire) may be found unto praise and glory and honour at the appearing of Jesus Christ. ^1-peter-01-7-douay

8 Whom having not seen, you love: in whom also now though you see him not, you believe and, believing, shall rejoice with joy unspeakable and glorified; ^1-peter-01-8-douay

9 Receiving the end of your faith, even the salvation of your souls. ^1-peter-01-9-douay

10 Of which salvation the prophets have inquired and diligently searched, who prophesied of the grace to come in you. ^1-peter-01-10-douay

11 Searching what or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ in them did signify, when it foretold those sufferings that are in Christ and the glories that should follow. ^1-peter-01-11-douay

12 To whom it was revealed that, not to themselves but to you, they ministered those things which are now declared to you by them that have preached the gospel to you: the Holy Ghost being sent down from heaven, on whom the angels desire to look. ^1-peter-01-12-douay

13 Wherefore, having the loins of your mind girt up, being sober, trust perfectly in the grace which is offered you in the revelation of Jesus Christ. ^1-peter-01-13-douay

14 As children of obedience, not fashioned according to the former desires of your ignorance, ^1-peter-01-14-douay

15 But according to him that hath called you, who is holy, be you also in all manner of conversation holy: ^1-peter-01-15-douay

16 Because it is written: You shall be holy, for I am holy. ^1-peter-01-16-douay

17 And if you invoke as Father him who, without respect of persons, judgeth according to every one's work: converse in fear during the time of your sojourning here. ^1-peter-01-17-douay

18 Knowing that you were not redeemed with corruptible things, as gold or silver, from your vain conversation of the tradition of your fathers: ^1-peter-01-18-douay

19 But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb unspotted and undefiled. ^1-peter-01-19-douay

20 Foreknown indeed before the foundation of the world, but manifested in the last times for you: ^1-peter-01-20-douay

21 Who through him are faithful in God who raised him up from the dead and hath given him glory, that your faith and hope might be in God. ^1-peter-01-21-douay

22 Purifying your souls in the obedience of charity, with a brotherly love, from a sincere heart love one another earnestly: ^1-peter-01-22-douay

23 Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but incorruptible, by the word of God who liveth and remaineth for ever. ^1-peter-01-23-douay

24 For all flesh is as grass and all the glory thereof as the flower of grass. The grass is withered and the flower thereof is fallen away. ^1-peter-01-24-douay

25 But the word of the Lord endureth for ever. And this is the word which by the gospel hath been preached unto you. ^1-peter-01-25-douay
