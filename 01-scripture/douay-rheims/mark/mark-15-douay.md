# Mark Chapter 15

The continuation of the history of the passion.

1 And straightway in the morning, the chief priests holding a consultation with the ancients and the scribes and the whole council, binding Jesus, led him away and delivered him to Pilate. ^mark-15-1-douay

2 And Pilate asked him: Art thou the king of the Jews? But he answering, saith to him: Thou sayest it. ^mark-15-2-douay

3 And the chief priests accused him in many things. ^mark-15-3-douay

4 And Pilate again asked him, saying: Answerest thou nothing? Behold in how many things they accuse thee. ^mark-15-4-douay

5 But Jesus still answered nothing: so that Pilate wondered. ^mark-15-5-douay

6 Now on the festival day he was wont to release unto them one of the prisoners, whomsoever they demanded. ^mark-15-6-douay

7 And there was one called Barabbas, who was put in prison with some seditious men, who in the sedition had committed murder. ^mark-15-7-douay

8 And when the multitude was come up, they began to desire that he would do as he had ever done unto them. ^mark-15-8-douay

9 And Pilate answered them and said: Will you that I release to you the king of the Jews? ^mark-15-9-douay

10 For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him up out of envy. ^mark-15-10-douay

11 But the chief priests moved the people, that he should rather release Barabbas to them. ^mark-15-11-douay

12 And Pilate again answering, saith to them: What will you then that I do to the king of the Jews? ^mark-15-12-douay

13 But they again cried out: Crucify him. ^mark-15-13-douay

14 And Pilate saith to them: Why, what evil hath he done? But they cried out the more: Crucify him. ^mark-15-14-douay

15 And so Pilate being willing to satisfy the people, released to them Barabbas: and delivered up Jesus, when he had scourged him, to be crucified. ^mark-15-15-douay

16 And the soldiers led him away into the court of the palace: and they called together the whole band. ^mark-15-16-douay

17 And they clothed him with purple: and, platting a crown of thorns, they put it upon him. ^mark-15-17-douay

18 And they began to salute him: Hail, king of the Jews. ^mark-15-18-douay

19 And they struck his head with a reed: and they did spit on him. And bowing their knees, they adored him. ^mark-15-19-douay

20 And after they had mocked him, they took off the purple from him and put his own garments on him: and they led him out to crucify him. ^mark-15-20-douay

21 And they forced one Simon a Cyrenian, who passed by coming out of the country, the father of Alexander and of Rufus, to take up his cross. ^mark-15-21-douay

22 And they bring him into the place called Golgotha, which being interpreted is, *The place of Calvary*. ^mark-15-22-douay

23 And they gave him to drink wine mingled with myrrh. But he took it not. ^mark-15-23-douay

24 And crucifying him, they divided his garments, casting lots upon them, what every man should take. ^mark-15-24-douay

25 And it was the third hour: and they crucified him. **The third hour...** The ancient account divided the day into four parts, which were named from the hour from which they began; the first, third, sixth, and ninth hour. Our Lord was crucified a little before noon; before the third hour had quite expired; but when the sixth hour was near at hand. ^mark-15-25-douay

26 And the inscription of his cause was written over: THE KING OF THE JEWS. ^mark-15-26-douay

27 And with him they crucify two thieves: the one on his right hand, and the other on his left. ^mark-15-27-douay

28 And the scripture was fulfilled, which saith: And with the wicked he was reputed. ^mark-15-28-douay

29 And they that passed by blasphemed him, wagging their heads and saying: Vah, thou that destroyest the temple of God and in three days buildest it up again: ^mark-15-29-douay

30 Save thyself, coming down from the cross. ^mark-15-30-douay

31 In like manner also the chief priests, mocking, said with the scribes one to another: He saved others; himself he cannot save. ^mark-15-31-douay

32 Let Christ the king of Israel come down now from the cross, that we may see and believe. And they that were crucified with him, reviled him. ^mark-15-32-douay

33 And when the sixth hour was come, there was darkness over the whole earth until the ninth hour. ^mark-15-33-douay

34 And at the ninth hour, Jesus cried out with a loud voice, saying: Eloi, Eloi, lamma sabacthani? Which is, being interpreted: My God, My God, Why hast thou forsaken me? ^mark-15-34-douay

35 And some of the standers by hearing, said: Behold he calleth Elias. ^mark-15-35-douay

36 And one running and filling a sponge with vinegar and putting it upon a reed, gave him to drink, saying: Stay, let us see if Elias come to take him down. ^mark-15-36-douay

37 And Jesus, having cried out with a loud voice, gave up the ghost. ^mark-15-37-douay

38 And the veil of the temple was rent in two, from the top to the bottom. ^mark-15-38-douay

39 And the centurion who stood over against him, seeing that crying out in this manner he had given up the ghost said: Indeed this man was the son of God. ^mark-15-39-douay

40 And there were also women looking on afar off: among whom was Mary Magdalen and Mary the mother of James the Less and of Joseph and Salome, ^mark-15-40-douay

41 Who also when he was in Galilee followed him and ministered to him, and many other women that came up with him to Jerusalem. ^mark-15-41-douay

42 And when evening was now come (because it was the Parasceve, that is, the day before the sabbath), ^mark-15-42-douay

43 Joseph of Arimathea, a noble counsellor, who was also himself looking for the kingdom of God, came and went in boldly to Pilate and begged the body of Jesus. ^mark-15-43-douay

44 But Pilate wondered that he should be already dead. And sending for the centurion, he asked him if he were already dead. ^mark-15-44-douay

45 And when he had understood it by the centurion, he gave the body to Joseph. ^mark-15-45-douay

46 And Joseph, buying fine linen and taking him down, wrapped him up in the fine linen and laid him in a sepulchre which was hewed out of a rock. And he rolled a stone to the door of the sepulchre. ^mark-15-46-douay

47 And Mary Magdalen and Mary the mother of Joseph, beheld where he was laid. ^mark-15-47-douay
