# Mark Chapter 3

Christ heals the withered hand. He chooses the twelve. He confutes the blasphemy of the Pharisees.

1 And he entered again into the synagogue: and there was a man there who had a withered hand. ^mark-03-1-douay

2 And they watched him whether he would heal on the sabbath days, that they might accuse him. ^mark-03-2-douay

3 And he said to the man who had the withered hand: Stand up in the midst. ^mark-03-3-douay

4 And he saith to them: Is it lawful to do good on the sabbath days, or to do evil? To save life, or to destroy? But they held their peace. ^mark-03-4-douay

5 And looking round about on them with anger, being grieved for the blindness of their hearts, he saith to the man: Stretch forth thy hand. And he stretched it forth: and his hand was restored unto him. ^mark-03-5-douay

6 And the Pharisees going out, immediately made a consultation with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him. ^mark-03-6-douay

7 But Jesus retired with his disciples to the sea; and a great multitude followed him from Galilee and Judea, ^mark-03-7-douay

8 And from Jerusalem, and from Idumea and from beyond the Jordan. And they about Tyre and Sidon, a great multitude, hearing the things which he did, came to him. ^mark-03-8-douay

9 And he spoke to his disciples that a small ship should wait on him, because of the multitude, lest they should throng him. ^mark-03-9-douay

10 For he healed many, so that they pressed upon him for to touch him, as many as had evils. ^mark-03-10-douay

11 And the unclean spirits, when they saw him, fell down before him: and they cried, saying: ^mark-03-11-douay

12 Thou art the Son of God. And he strictly charged them that they should not make him known. ^mark-03-12-douay

13 And going up into a mountain, he called unto him whom he would himself: and they came to him. ^mark-03-13-douay

14 And he made that twelve should be with him, and that he might send them to preach. ^mark-03-14-douay

15 And he gave them power to heal sicknesses, and to cast out devils. ^mark-03-15-douay

16 And to Simon he gave the name *Peter*: ^mark-03-16-douay

17 And James the son of Zebedee, and John the brother of James; and he named them Boanerges, which is, *The sons of thunder*. ^mark-03-17-douay

18 And Andrew and Philip, and Bartholomew and Matthew, and Thomas and James of Alpheus, and Thaddeus and Simon the Cananean: ^mark-03-18-douay

19 And Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him. ^mark-03-19-douay

20 And they come to a house, and the multitude cometh together again, so that they could not so much as eat bread. ^mark-03-20-douay

21 And when his friends had heard of it, they went out to lay hold on him. For they said: He is become mad. ^mark-03-21-douay

22 And the scribes who were come down from Jerusalem, said: He hath Beelzebub, and by the prince of devils he casteth out devils. ^mark-03-22-douay

23 And after he had called them together, he said to them in parables: How can Satan cast out Satan? ^mark-03-23-douay

24 And if a kingdom be divided against itself, that kingdom cannot stand. ^mark-03-24-douay

25 And if a house be divided against itself, that house cannot stand. ^mark-03-25-douay

26 And if Satan be risen up against himself, he is divided, and cannot stand, but hath an end. ^mark-03-26-douay

27 No man can enter into the house of a strong man and rob him of his goods, unless he first bind the strong man, and then shall he plunder his house. ^mark-03-27-douay

28 Amen I say to you that all sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and the blasphemies wherewith they shall blaspheme: ^mark-03-28-douay

29 But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost, shall never have forgiveness, but shall be guilty of an everlasting sin. ^mark-03-29-douay

30 Because they said: He hath an unclean spirit. ^mark-03-30-douay

31 And his mother and his brethren came; and standing without, sent unto him, calling him. ^mark-03-31-douay

32 And the multitude sat about him; and they say to him: Behold thy mother and thy brethren without seek for thee. ^mark-03-32-douay

33 And answering them, he said: Who is my mother and my brethren? ^mark-03-33-douay

34 And looking round about on them who sat about him, he saith: Behold my mother and my brethren. ^mark-03-34-douay

35 For whosoever shall do the will of God, he is my brother, and my sister, and mother. ^mark-03-35-douay
