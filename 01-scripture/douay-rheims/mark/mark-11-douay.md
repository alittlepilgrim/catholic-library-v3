# Mark Chapter 11

Christ enters into Jerusalem upon an ass. He curses the barren fig tree and drives the buyers and sellers out of the temple.

1 And when they were drawing near to Jerusalem and to Bethania, at the mount of Olives, he sendeth two of his disciples, ^mark-11-1-douay

2 And saith to them: Go into the village that is over against you, and immediately at your coming in thither, you shall find a colt tied, upon which no man yet hath sat. Loose him and bring him. ^mark-11-2-douay

3 And if any man shall say to you: What are you doing? Say ye that the Lord hath need of him. And immediately he will let him come hither. ^mark-11-3-douay

4 And going their way, they found the colt tied before the gate without, in the meeting of two ways. And they loose him. ^mark-11-4-douay

5 And some of them that stood there said to them: What do you loosing the colt? ^mark-11-5-douay

6 Who said to them as Jesus had commanded them. And they let him go with them. ^mark-11-6-douay

7 And they brought the colt to Jesus. And they lay their garments on him: and he sat upon him. ^mark-11-7-douay

8 And many spread their garments in the way: and others cut down boughs from the trees and strewed them in the way. ^mark-11-8-douay

9 And they that went before and they that followed cried, saying: Hosanna: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord. ^mark-11-9-douay

10 Blessed be the kingdom of our father David that cometh: Hosanna in the highest. ^mark-11-10-douay

11 And he entered into Jerusalem, into the temple: and having viewed all things round about, when now the eventide was come, he went out to Bethania with the twelve. ^mark-11-11-douay

12 And the next day when they came out from Bethania, he was hungry. ^mark-11-12-douay

13 And when he had seen afar off a fig tree having leaves, he came, if perhaps he might find any thing on it. And when he was come to it, he found nothing but leaves. For it was not the time for figs. ^mark-11-13-douay

14 And answering he said to it: May no man hereafter eat fruit of thee any more for ever! And his disciples heard it. ^mark-11-14-douay

15 And they came to Jerusalem. And when he was entered into the temple, he began to cast out them that sold and bought in the temple: and over threw the tables of the moneychangers and the chairs of them that sold doves. ^mark-11-15-douay

16 And he suffered not that any man should carry a vessel through the temple. ^mark-11-16-douay

17 And he taught, saying to them: Is it not written: My house shall be called the house of prayer to all nations, but you have made it a den of thieves. ^mark-11-17-douay

18 Which when the chief priests and the scribes had heard, they sought how they might destroy him. For they feared him, because the whole multitude was in admiration at his doctrine. ^mark-11-18-douay

19 And when evening was come, he went forth out of the city. ^mark-11-19-douay

20 And when they passed by in the morning they saw the fig tree dried up from the roots. ^mark-11-20-douay

21 And Peter remembering, said to him: Rabbi, behold the fig tree which thou didst curse is withered away. ^mark-11-21-douay

22 And Jesus answering, saith to them: Have the faith of God. ^mark-11-22-douay

23 Amen I say to you that whosoever shall say to this mountain, Be thou removed and be cast into the sea, and shall not stagger in his heart, but be believe that whatsoever he saith shall be done; it shall be done unto him. ^mark-11-23-douay

24 Therefore I say unto you, all things, whatsoever you ask when ye pray, believe that you shall receive: and they shall come unto you. ^mark-11-24-douay

25 And when you shall stand to pray, forgive, if you have aught against any man: that your Father also, who is in heaven, may forgive you your sins. ^mark-11-25-douay

26 But if you will not forgive, neither will your father that is in heaven forgive you your sins. ^mark-11-26-douay

27 And they come again to Jerusalem. And when he was walking in the temple, there come to him the chief priests and the scribes and the ancients. ^mark-11-27-douay

28 And they say to him: By what authority dost thou these things? And who hath given thee this authority that thou shouldst do these things? ^mark-11-28-douay

29 And Jesus answering, said to them: I will also ask you one word. And answer you me: and I will tell you by what authority I do these things. ^mark-11-29-douay

30 The baptism of John, was it from heaven or from men? Answer me. ^mark-11-30-douay

31 But they thought with themselves, saying: If we say, From heaven; he will say, Why then did you not believe him? ^mark-11-31-douay

32 If we say, From men, we fear the people. For all men counted John that he was a prophet indeed. ^mark-11-32-douay

33 And they answering, say to Jesus: We know not. And Jesus answering, saith to them: Neither do I tell you by what authority I do these things. ^mark-11-33-douay
