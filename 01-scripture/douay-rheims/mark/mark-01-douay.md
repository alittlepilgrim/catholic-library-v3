# Mark Chapter 1

The preaching of John the Baptist. Christ is baptized by him. He calls his disciples and works many miracles.

1 The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God. ^mark-01-1-douay

2 As it is written in Isaiah the prophet: ^mark-01-2-douay

Behold I send my angel before thy face, who shall prepare the way before thee.

3 A voice of one crying in the desert: Prepare ye the way of the Lord; make straight his paths. ^mark-01-3-douay

4 John was in the desert, baptizing and preaching the baptism of penance, unto remission of sins. ^mark-01-4-douay

5 And there went out to him all the country of Judea and all they of Jerusalem and were baptized by him in the river of Jordan, confessing their sins. ^mark-01-5-douay

6 And John was clothed with camel's hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and he ate locusts and wild honey. ^mark-01-6-douay

7 And he preached, saying: There cometh after me one mightier than I, the latchet of whose shoes I am not worthy to stoop down and loose. ^mark-01-7-douay

8 I have baptized you with water: but he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost. ^mark-01-8-douay

9 And it came to pass, in those days, Jesus came from Nazareth of Galilee and was baptized by John in the Jordan. ^mark-01-9-douay

10 And forthwith coming up out of the water, he saw the heavens open and the Spirit as a dove descending and remaining on him. ^mark-01-10-douay

11 And there came a voice from heaven: Thou art my beloved Son; in thee I am well pleased. ^mark-01-11-douay

12 And immediately the Spirit drove him out into the desert. ^mark-01-12-douay

13 And he was in the desert forty days and forty nights, and was tempted by Satan. And he was with beasts: and the angels ministered to him. ^mark-01-13-douay

14 And after that John was delivered up, Jesus came into Galilee, preaching the gospel of the kingdom of God, ^mark-01-14-douay

15 And saying: The time is accomplished and the kingdom of God is at hand. Repent and believe the gospel: ^mark-01-15-douay

16 And passing by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother, casting nets into the sea for they were fishermen. ^mark-01-16-douay

17 And Jesus said to them: Come after me; and I will make you to become fishers of men. ^mark-01-17-douay

18 And immediately leaving their nets, they followed him. ^mark-01-18-douay

19 And going on from thence a little farther, he saw James the son of Zebedee and John his brother, who also were mending their nets in the ship: ^mark-01-19-douay

20 And forthwith he called them. And leaving their father Zebedee in the ship with his hired men, they followed him. ^mark-01-20-douay

21 And they entered into Capharnaum: and forthwith upon the sabbath days going into the synagogue, he taught them. ^mark-01-21-douay

22 And they were astonished at his doctrine. For he was teaching them as one having power, and not as the scribes. ^mark-01-22-douay

23 And there was in their synagogue a man with an unclean spirit; and he cried out, ^mark-01-23-douay

24 Saying: What have we to do with thee, Jesus of Nazareth? Art thou come to destroy us? I know who thou art, the Holy One of God. ^mark-01-24-douay

25 And Jesus threatened him, saying: Speak no more, and go out of the man. ^mark-01-25-douay

26 And the unclean spirit, tearing him and crying out with a loud voice, went out of him. ^mark-01-26-douay

27 And they were all amazed insomuch that they questioned among themselves, saying: What thing is this? What is this new doctrine? For with power he commandeth even the unclean spirits: and they obey him. ^mark-01-27-douay

28 And the fame of him was spread forthwith into all the country of Galilee. ^mark-01-28-douay

29 And immediately going out of the synagogue they came into the house of Simon and Andrew, with James and John. ^mark-01-29-douay

30 And Simon's wife's mother lay in a fit of a fever: and forthwith they tell him of her. ^mark-01-30-douay

31 And coming to her, he lifted her up, taking her by the hand; and immediately the fever left her, and she ministered unto them. ^mark-01-31-douay

32 And when it was evening, after sunset, they brought to him all that were ill and that were possessed with devils. ^mark-01-32-douay

33 And all the city was gathered together at the door. ^mark-01-33-douay

34 And he healed many that were troubled with divers diseases. And he cast out many devils: and he suffered them not to speak, because they knew him. ^mark-01-34-douay

35 And rising very early, going out, he went into a desert place: and there he prayed. ^mark-01-35-douay

36 And Simon and they that were with him followed after him. ^mark-01-36-douay

37 And when they had found him, they said to him: All seek for thee. ^mark-01-37-douay

38 And he saith to them: Let us go into the neighbouring towns and cities, that I may preach there also; for to this purpose am I come. ^mark-01-38-douay

39 And he was preaching in their synagogues and in all Galilee and casting out devils. ^mark-01-39-douay

40 And there came a leper to him, beseeching him and kneeling down, said to him: If thou wilt thou canst make me clean. ^mark-01-40-douay

41 And Jesus, having compassion on him, stretched forth his hand and touching him saith to him: I will. Be thou made clean. ^mark-01-41-douay

42 And when he had spoken, immediately the leprosy departed from him: and he was made clean. ^mark-01-42-douay

43 And he strictly charged him and forthwith sent him away. ^mark-01-43-douay

44 And he saith to him: See thou tell no one; but go, show thyself to the high priest and offer for thy cleansing the things that Moses commanded, for a testimony to them. ^mark-01-44-douay

45 But he being gone out, began to publish and to blaze abroad the word: so that he could not openly go into the city, but was without in desert places. And they flocked to him from all sides. ^mark-01-45-douay
