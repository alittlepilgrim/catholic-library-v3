# Mark Chapter 14

The first part of the history of the passion of Christ.

1 Now the feast of the pasch and of the Azymes was after two days: and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might by some wile lay hold on him and kill him. **Azymes...** That is, the feast of the unleavened bread. ^mark-14-1-douay

2 But they said: Not on the festival day, lest there should be a tumult among the people. ^mark-14-2-douay

3 And when he was in Bethania, in the house of Simon the leper, and was at meat, there came a woman having an alabaster box of ointment of precious spikenard. And breaking the alabaster box, she poured it out upon his head. ^mark-14-3-douay

4 Now there were some that had indignation within themselves and said: Why was this waste of the ointment made? ^mark-14-4-douay

5 For this ointment might have been sold for more than three hundred pence and given to the poor. And they murmured against her. ^mark-14-5-douay

6 But Jesus said: Let her alone. Why do You molest her? She hath wrought a good work upon me. ^mark-14-6-douay

7 For the poor you have always with you: and whensoever you will, you may do them good: but me you have not always. ^mark-14-7-douay

8 She hath done what she could: she is come beforehand to anoint my body for the burial. ^mark-14-8-douay

9 Amen, I say to you, wheresoever this gospel shall be preached in the whole world, that also which she hath done shall be told for a memorial of her. ^mark-14-9-douay

10 And Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went to the chief priests, to betray him to them. ^mark-14-10-douay

11 Who hearing it were glad: and they promised him they would give him money. And he sought how he might conveniently betray him. ^mark-14-11-douay

12 Now on the first day of the unleavened bread, when they sacrificed the pasch, the disciples say to him: Whither wilt thou that we go and prepare for thee to eat the pasch? ^mark-14-12-douay

13 And he sendeth two of his disciples and saith to them: Go ye into the city; and there shall meet you a man carrying a pitcher of water. Follow him. ^mark-14-13-douay

14 And whithersoever he shall go in, say to the master of the house, The master saith, Where is my refectory, where I may eat the pasch with my disciples? ^mark-14-14-douay

15 And he will show you a large dining room furnished. And there prepare ye for us. ^mark-14-15-douay

16 And his disciples went their way and came into the city. And they found as he had told them: and they prepared the pasch. ^mark-14-16-douay

17 And when evening was come, he cometh with the twelve. ^mark-14-17-douay

18 And when they were at table and eating, Jesus saith: Amen I say to you, one of you that eateth with me shall betray me. ^mark-14-18-douay

19 But they began to be sorrowful and to say to him, one by one: Is it I? ^mark-14-19-douay

20 Who saith to them: One of the twelve, who dippeth with me his hand in the dish. ^mark-14-20-douay

21 And the Son of man indeed goeth, as it is written of him: but woe to that man by whom the Son of man shall be betrayed. It were better for him, if that man had not been born. ^mark-14-21-douay

22 And whilst they were eating, Jesus took bread; and blessing, broke and gave to them and said: Take ye. This is my body. ^mark-14-22-douay

23 And having taken the chalice, giving thanks, he gave it to them. And they all drank of it. ^mark-14-23-douay

24 And he said to them: This is my blood of the new testament, which shall be shed for many. ^mark-14-24-douay

25 Amen I say to you that I will drink no more of the fruit of the vine until that day when I shall drink it new in the kingdom of God. ^mark-14-25-douay

26 And when they had sung an hymn, they went forth to the mount of Olives. ^mark-14-26-douay

27 And Jesus saith to them: You will all be scandalized in my regard this night. For it is written: I will strike the shepherd, and the sheep shall be dispersed. ^mark-14-27-douay

28 But after I shall be risen again, I will go before you into Galilee. ^mark-14-28-douay

29 But Peter saith to him: Although all shall be scandalized in thee, yet not I. ^mark-14-29-douay

30 And Jesus saith to him: Amen I say to thee, to-day, even in this night, before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice. **Crow twice...** The cocks crow at two different times of the night; viz., about midnight for the first time; and then about the time commonly called the cock crowing; and this was the cock crowing our Saviour spoke of; and therefore the other Evangelists take no notice of the first crowing. ^mark-14-30-douay

31 But he spoke the more vehemently: Although I should die together with thee, I will not deny thee. And in like manner also said they all. ^mark-14-31-douay

32 And they came to a farm called Gethsemani. And he saith to his disciples: Sit you here, while I pray. ^mark-14-32-douay

33 And he taketh Peter and James and John with him: and he began to fear and to be heavy. ^mark-14-33-douay

34 And he saith to them: My soul is sorrowful even unto death. Stay you here and watch. ^mark-14-34-douay

35 And when he was gone forward a little, he fell flat on the ground: and he prayed that, if it might be, the hour might pass from him. ^mark-14-35-douay

36 And he saith: Abba, Father, all things are possible to thee: remove this chalice from me; but not what I will, but what thou wilt. ^mark-14-36-douay

37 And he cometh and findeth them sleeping. And he saith to Peter: Simon, sleepest thou? Couldst thou not watch one hour? ^mark-14-37-douay

38 Watch ye: and pray that you enter not into temptation. The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak. ^mark-14-38-douay

39 And going away again, he prayed, saying the same words. ^mark-14-39-douay

40 And when he returned, he found them again asleep (for their eyes were heavy): and they knew not what to answer him. ^mark-14-40-douay

41 And he cometh the third time and saith to them: Sleep ye now and take your rest. It is enough. The hour is come: behold the Son of man shall be betrayed into the hands of sinners. ^mark-14-41-douay

42 Rise up: let us go. Behold, he that will betray me is at hand. ^mark-14-42-douay

43 And while he was yet speaking, cometh Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve: and with him a great multitude with swords and staves, from the chief priests and the scribes and the ancients. ^mark-14-43-douay

44 And he that betrayed him had given them a sign, saying: Whomsoever I shall kiss, that is he. Lay hold on him: and lead him away carefully. ^mark-14-44-douay

45 And when he was come, immediately going up to him he saith: Hail, Rabbi! And he kissed him. ^mark-14-45-douay

46 But they laid hands on him and held him. ^mark-14-46-douay

47 And one of them that stood by, drawing a sword, struck a servant of the chief priest and cut off his ear. ^mark-14-47-douay

48 And Jesus answering, said to them: Are you come out as to a robber, with swords and staves to apprehend me? ^mark-14-48-douay

49 I was daily with you in the temple teaching: and you did not lay hands on me. But that the scriptures may be fulfilled. ^mark-14-49-douay

50 Then his disciples, leaving him, all fled away. ^mark-14-50-douay

51 And a certain young man followed him, having a linen cloth cast about his naked body. And they laid hold on him. ^mark-14-51-douay

52 But he, casting off the linen cloth, fled from them naked. ^mark-14-52-douay

53 And they brought Jesus to the high priest. And all the priests and the scribes and the ancients assembled together. ^mark-14-53-douay

54 And Peter followed him afar off, even into the court of the high priest. And he sat with the servants at the fire and warmed himself. ^mark-14-54-douay

55 And the chief priests and all the council sought for evidence against Jesus, that they might put him to death: and found none. ^mark-14-55-douay

56 For many bore false witness against him: and their evidences were not agreeing. ^mark-14-56-douay

57 And some rising up, bore false witness against him, saying: ^mark-14-57-douay

58 We heard him say, I will destroy this temple made with hands and within three days I will build another not made with hands. ^mark-14-58-douay

59 And their witness did not agree. ^mark-14-59-douay

60 And the high priest rising up in the midst, asked Jesus, saying: Answerest thou nothing to the things that are laid to thy charge by these men? ^mark-14-60-douay

61 But he held his peace and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked him and said to him: Art thou the Christ, the Son of the Blessed God? ^mark-14-61-douay

62 And Jesus said to him: I am. And you shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of the power of God and coming with the clouds of heaven. ^mark-14-62-douay

63 Then the high priest rending his garments, saith: What need we any further witnesses? ^mark-14-63-douay

64 You have heard the blasphemy. What think you? Who all condemned him to be guilty of death. ^mark-14-64-douay

65 And some began to spit on him and to cover his face and to buffet him and to say unto him: Prophesy. And the servants struck him with the palms their hands. ^mark-14-65-douay

66 Now when Peter was in the court below, there cometh one of the maidservants of the high priest. ^mark-14-66-douay

67 And when she had seen Peter warming himself looking on him, she saith: Thou also wast with Jesus of Nazareth. ^mark-14-67-douay

68 But he denied, saying: I neither know nor understand what thou sayest. And he went forth before the court; and the cock crew. ^mark-14-68-douay

69 And again a maidservant seeing him, began to say to the standers by: This is one of them. ^mark-14-69-douay

70 But he denied again. And after a while they that stood by said again to Peter: Surely thou art one of them; for thou art also a Galilean. ^mark-14-70-douay

71 But he began o curse and to swear, saying: I know not this man of whom you speak. ^mark-14-71-douay

72 And immediately the cock crew again. And Peter remembered the word that Jesus had said unto him: Before the cock crow twice, thou shalt thrice deny me. And he began to weep, ^mark-14-72-douay
