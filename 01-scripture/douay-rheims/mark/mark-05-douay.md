# Mark Chapter 5

Christ casts out a legion of devils: he heals the issue of blood, and raises the daughter of Jairus to life.

1 And they came over the strait of the sea, into the country of the Gerasens. ^mark-05-1-douay

2 And as he went out of the ship, immediately there met him out of the monuments a man with an unclean spirit, ^mark-05-2-douay

3 Who had his dwelling in the tombs, and no man now could bind him, not even with chains. ^mark-05-3-douay

4 For having been often bound with fetters and chains, he had burst the chains, and broken the fetters in pieces, and no one could tame him. ^mark-05-4-douay

5 And he was always day and night in the monuments and in the mountains, crying and cutting himself with stones. ^mark-05-5-douay

6 And seeing Jesus afar off, he ran and adored him. ^mark-05-6-douay

7 And crying with a loud voice, he said: What have I to do with thee, Jesus the Son of the most high God? I adjure thee by God that thou torment me not. ^mark-05-7-douay

8 For he said unto him: Go out of the man, thou unclean spirit. ^mark-05-8-douay

9 And he asked him: What is thy name? And he saith to him: My name is Legion, for we are many. ^mark-05-9-douay

10 And he besought him much, that he would not drive him away out of the country. ^mark-05-10-douay

11 And there was there near the mountain a great herd of swine, feeding. ^mark-05-11-douay

12 And the spirits besought him, saying: Send us into the swine, that we may enter into them. ^mark-05-12-douay

13 And Jesus immediately gave them leave. And the unclean spirits going out, entered into the swine: and the herd with great violence was carried headlong into the sea, being about two thousand, were stifled in the sea. ^mark-05-13-douay

14 And they that fed them fled, and told it in the city and in the fields. And they went out to see what was done: ^mark-05-14-douay

15 And they came to Jesus, and they see him that was troubled with the devil, sitting, clothed, and well in his wits, and they were afraid. ^mark-05-15-douay

16 And they that had seen it, told them, in what manner he had been dealt with who had the devil; and concerning the swine. ^mark-05-16-douay

17 And they began to pray him that he would depart from their coasts. ^mark-05-17-douay

18 And when he went up into the ship, he that had been troubled with the devil, began to beseech him that he might be with him. ^mark-05-18-douay

19 And he admitted him not, but saith to him: Go into thy house to thy friends, and tell them how great things the Lord hath done for thee, and hath had mercy on thee. ^mark-05-19-douay

20 And he went his way, and began to publish in Decapolis how great things Jesus had done for him: and all men wondered. ^mark-05-20-douay

21 And when Jesus had passed again in the ship over the strait, a great multitude assembled together unto him, and he was nigh unto the sea. ^mark-05-21-douay

22 And there cometh one of the rulers of the synagogue named Jairus: and seeing him, falleth down at his feet. ^mark-05-22-douay

23 And he besought him much, saying: My daughter is at the point of death, come, lay thy hand upon her, that she may be safe, and may live. ^mark-05-23-douay

24 And he went with him, and a great multitude followed him, and they thronged him. ^mark-05-24-douay

25 And a woman who was under an issue of blood twelve years, ^mark-05-25-douay

26 And had suffered many things from many physicians; and had spent all that she had, and was nothing the better, but rather worse, ^mark-05-26-douay

27 When she had heard of Jesus, came in the crowd behind him, and touched his garment. ^mark-05-27-douay

28 For she said: If I shall touch but his garment, I shall be whole. ^mark-05-28-douay

29 And forthwith the fountain of her blood was dried up, and she felt in her body that she was healed of the evil. ^mark-05-29-douay

30 And immediately Jesus knowing in himself the virtue that had proceeded from him, turning to the multitude, said: Who hath touched my garments? ^mark-05-30-douay

31 And his disciples said to him: Thou seest the multitude thronging thee, and sayest thou who hath touched me? ^mark-05-31-douay

32 And he looked about to see her who had done this. ^mark-05-32-douay

33 But the woman fearing and trembling, knowing what was done in her, came and fell down before him, and told him all the truth. ^mark-05-33-douay

34 And he said to her: Daughter, thy faith hath made thee whole: go in peace, and be thou whole of thy disease. ^mark-05-34-douay

35 While he was yet speaking, some come from the ruler of the synagogue's house, saying: Thy daughter is dead: why dost thou trouble the master any further? ^mark-05-35-douay

36 But Jesus having heard the word that was spoken, saith to the ruler of the synagogue: Fear not, only believe. ^mark-05-36-douay

37 And he admitted not any man to follow him, but Peter, and James, and John the brother of James. ^mark-05-37-douay

38 And they come to the house of the ruler of the synagogue; and he seeth a tumult, and people weeping and wailing much. ^mark-05-38-douay

39 And going in, he saith to them Why make you this ado, and weep? the damsel is not dead, but sleepeth. ^mark-05-39-douay

40 And they laughed him to scorn. But he having put them all out, taketh the father and the mother of the damsel, and them that were with him, and entereth in where the damsel was lying. ^mark-05-40-douay

41 And taking the damsel by the hand, he saith to her: *Talitha cumi*, which is, being interpreted: Damsel (I say to thee) arise. ^mark-05-41-douay

42 And immediately the damsel rose up, and walked: and she was twelve years old: and they were astonished with a great astonishment. ^mark-05-42-douay

43 And he charged them strictly that no man should know it: and commanded that something should be given her to eat. ^mark-05-43-douay
