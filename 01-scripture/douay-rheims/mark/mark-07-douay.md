# Mark Chapter 7

Christ rebukes the Pharisees. He heals the daughter of the woman of Canaan; and the man that was deaf and dumb.

1 And there assembled together unto him the Pharisees and some of the scribes, coming from Jerusalem. ^mark-07-1-douay

2 And when they had seen some of his disciples eat bread with common, that is, with unwashed hands, they found fault. ^mark-07-2-douay

3 For the Pharisees and all the Jews eat not without often washing their hands, holding the tradition of the ancients. ^mark-07-3-douay

4 And when they come from the market, unless they be washed, they eat not: and many other things there are that have been delivered to them to observe, the washings of cups and of pots and of brazen vessels and of beds. ^mark-07-4-douay

5 And the Pharisees and scribes asked him: Why do not thy disciples walk according to the tradition of the ancients, but they eat bread with common hands? ^mark-07-5-douay

6 But he answering, said to them: Well did Isaiah prophesy of you hypocrites, as it is written: ^mark-07-6-douay

This people honoureth me with their lips, but their heart is far from me.

7 And in vain do they worship me, teaching doctrines and precepts of men. **Doctrines and precepts of men...** See the annotations, Matt. 15. 9, 11. ^mark-07-7-douay

8 For leaving the commandment of God, you hold the tradition of men, the washing of pots and of cups: and many other things you do like to these. ^mark-07-8-douay

9 And he said to them: Well do you make void the commandment of God, that you may keep your own tradition. ^mark-07-9-douay

10 For Moses said: Honour thy father and thy mother. And He that shall curse father or mother, dying let him die. ^mark-07-10-douay

11 But you say: If a man shall say to his father or mother, Corban (which is a gift) whatsoever is from me shall profit thee. ^mark-07-11-douay

12 And further you suffer him not to do any thing for his father or mother, ^mark-07-12-douay

13 Making void the word of God by your own tradition, which you have given forth. And many other such like things you do. ^mark-07-13-douay

14 And calling again the multitude unto him, he said to them: Hear ye me all and understand. ^mark-07-14-douay

15 There is nothing from without a man that entering into him can defile him. But the things which come from a man, those are they that defile a man. ^mark-07-15-douay

16 If any man have ears to hear, let him hear. ^mark-07-16-douay

17 And when he was come into the house from the multitude, his disciples asked him the parable. ^mark-07-17-douay

18 And he saith to them: So are you also without knowledge? Understand you not that every thing from without entering into a man cannot defile him: ^mark-07-18-douay

19 Because it entereth not into his heart but goeth into his belly and goeth out into the privy, purging all meats? ^mark-07-19-douay

20 But he said that the things which come out from a man, they defile a man. ^mark-07-20-douay

21 For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders, ^mark-07-21-douay

22 Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness. ^mark-07-22-douay

23 All these evil things come from within and defile a man. ^mark-07-23-douay

24 And rising from thence he went into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon: and entering into a house, he would that no man should know it. And he could not be hid. ^mark-07-24-douay

25 For a woman as soon as she heard of him, whose daughter had an unclean spirit, came in and fell down at his feet. ^mark-07-25-douay

26 For the woman was a Gentile, a Syrophenician born. And she besought him that he would cast forth the devil out of her daughter. ^mark-07-26-douay

27 Who said to her: Suffer first the children to be filled: for it is not good to take the bread of the children and cast it to the dogs. ^mark-07-27-douay

28 But she answered and said to him: Yea, Lord; for the whelps also eat under the table of the crumbs of the children. ^mark-07-28-douay

29 And he said to her: For this saying, go thy way. The devil is gone out of thy daughter. ^mark-07-29-douay

30 And when she was come into her house, she found the girl lying upon the bed and that the devil was gone out. ^mark-07-30-douay

31 And again going out of the coasts of Tyre, he came by Sidon to the sea of Galilee, through the midst of the coasts of Decapolis. ^mark-07-31-douay

32 And they bring to him one deaf and dumb: and they besought him that he would lay his hand upon him. ^mark-07-32-douay

33 And taking him from the multitude apart, he put his fingers into his ears: and spitting, he touched his tongue. ^mark-07-33-douay

34 And looking up to heaven, he groaned and said to him: Ephpheta, which is, Be thou opened. ^mark-07-34-douay

35 And immediately his ears were opened and the string of his tongue was loosed and he spoke right. ^mark-07-35-douay

36 And he charged them that they should tell no man. But the more he charged them, so much the more a great deal did they publish it. ^mark-07-36-douay

37 And so much the more did they wonder, saying: He hath done all things well. He hath made both the deaf to hear and the dumb to speak. ^mark-07-37-douay
