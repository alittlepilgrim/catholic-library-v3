# 2 Samuel Chapter 22

King David's psalm of thanksgiving for his deliverance from all his enemies.

1 And David spoke to the Lord the words of this canticle, in the day that the Lord delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul, ^2-samuel-22-1-douay

2 And he said: ^2-samuel-22-2-douay

The Lord is my rock, and my strength, and my saviour.

3 God is my strong one, in him will I trust: my shield, and the horn of my salvation: he lifteth me up, and is my refuge: my saviour, thou wilt deliver me from iniquity. ^2-samuel-22-3-douay

4 I will call on the Lord who is worthy to be praised: and I shall be saved from my enemies. ^2-samuel-22-4-douay

5 For the pangs of death have surrounded me: the floods of Belial have made me afraid. ^2-samuel-22-5-douay

6 The cords of hell compassed me: the snares of death prevented me. ^2-samuel-22-6-douay

7 In my distress I will call upon the Lord, and I will cry to my God: and he will hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry shall come to his ears. ^2-samuel-22-7-douay

8 The earth shook and trembled, the foundations of the mountains were moved, and shaken, because he was angry with them. ^2-samuel-22-8-douay

9 A smoke went up from his nostrils, and a devouring fire out of his mouth: coals were kindled by it. ^2-samuel-22-9-douay

10 He bowed the heavens, and came down: and darkness was under his feet. ^2-samuel-22-10-douay

11 And he rode upon the cherubims, and flew: and slid upon the wings of the wind. ^2-samuel-22-11-douay

12 He made darkness a covering round about him: dropping waters out of the clouds of the heavens. ^2-samuel-22-12-douay

13 By the brightness before him, the coals of fire were kindled. ^2-samuel-22-13-douay

14 The Lord shall thunder from heaven: and the most high shall give forth his voice. ^2-samuel-22-14-douay

15 He shot arrows and scattered them: lightning, and consumed them. ^2-samuel-22-15-douay

16 And the overflowings of the sea appeared, and the foundations of the world were laid open at the rebuke of the Lord, at the blast of the spirit of his wrath. ^2-samuel-22-16-douay

17 He sent from on high, and took me, and drew me out of many waters. ^2-samuel-22-17-douay

18 He delivered me from my most mighty enemy, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me. ^2-samuel-22-18-douay

19 He prevented me in the day of my affliction, and the Lord became my stay. ^2-samuel-22-19-douay

20 And he brought me forth into a large place, he delivered me, because I pleased him. ^2-samuel-22-20-douay

21 The Lord will reward me according to my justice: and according to the cleanness of my hands he will render to me. ^2-samuel-22-21-douay

22 Because I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God. ^2-samuel-22-22-douay

23 For all his judgments are in my sight: and his precepts I have not removed from me. ^2-samuel-22-23-douay

24 And I shall be perfect with him: and shall keep myself from my iniquity. ^2-samuel-22-24-douay

25 And the Lord will recompense me according to my justice: and according to the cleanness of my hands in the sight of his eyes. ^2-samuel-22-25-douay

26 With the holy one thou wilt be holy: and with the valiant perfect. ^2-samuel-22-26-douay

27 With the elect thou wilt be elect: and with the perverse thou wilt be perverted. ^2-samuel-22-27-douay

28 And the poor people thou wilt save: and with thy eyes thou shalt humble the haughty. ^2-samuel-22-28-douay

29 For thou art my lamp O Lord: and thou, O Lord, wilt enlighten my darkness. ^2-samuel-22-29-douay

30 For in thee I will run girded: in my God I will leap over the wall. ^2-samuel-22-30-douay

31 God, his way is immaculate, the word of the Lord is tried by fire: he is the shield of all that trust in him. ^2-samuel-22-31-douay

32 Who is God but the Lord: and who is strong but our God? ^2-samuel-22-32-douay

33 God who hath girded me with strength, and made my way perfect. ^2-samuel-22-33-douay

34 Making my feet like the feet of harts, and setting me upon my high places. ^2-samuel-22-34-douay

35 He teacheth my hands to war: and maketh my arms like a bow of brass. ^2-samuel-22-35-douay

36 Thou hast given me the shield of my salvation: and thy mildness hath multiplied me. ^2-samuel-22-36-douay

37 Thou shalt enlarge my steps under me: and my ankles shall not fail. ^2-samuel-22-37-douay

38 I will pursue after my enemies, and crush them: and will not return again till I consume them. ^2-samuel-22-38-douay

39 I will consume them and break them in pieces, so that they shall not rise: they shall fall under my feet. ^2-samuel-22-39-douay

40 Thou hast girded me with strength to battle: thou hast made them that resisted me to bow under me. ^2-samuel-22-40-douay

41 My enemies thou hast made to turn their back to me: them that hated me, and I shall destroy them. ^2-samuel-22-41-douay

42 They shall cry, and there shall be none to save: to the Lord, and he shall not hear them. ^2-samuel-22-42-douay

43 I shall beat them as small as the dust of the earth: I shall crush them and spread them abroad like the mire of the streets. ^2-samuel-22-43-douay

44 Thou wilt save me from the contradictions of my people: thou wilt keep me to be the head of the Gentiles: the people which I know not, shall serve me, ^2-samuel-22-44-douay

45 The sons of the stranger will resist me, at the hearing of the ear they will obey me. ^2-samuel-22-45-douay

46 The strangers are melted away, and shall be straitened in their distresses. ^2-samuel-22-46-douay

47 The Lord liveth, and my God is blessed: and the strong God of my salvation shall be exalted: ^2-samuel-22-47-douay

48 God who giveth me revenge, and bringest down people under me, ^2-samuel-22-48-douay

49 Who bringest me forth from my enemies, and liftest me up from them that resist me: from the wicked man thou shalt deliver me. ^2-samuel-22-49-douay

50 Therefore will I give thanks to thee, O Lord, among the Gentiles, and will sing to thy name. ^2-samuel-22-50-douay

51 Giving great salvation to his king, and showing mercy to David his anointed, and to his seed for ever. ^2-samuel-22-51-douay
