# 1 Corinthians Chapter 12

Of the diversity of spiritual gifts. The members of the mystical body, like those of the natural body, must mutually cherish one another.

1 Now concerning spiritual things, my brethren, I would not have you ignorant. ^1-corinthians-12-1-douay

2 You know that when you were heathens, you went to dumb idols, according as you were led. ^1-corinthians-12-2-douay

3 Wherefore, I give you to understand that no man, speaking by the Spirit of God, saith Anathema to Jesus. And no man can say *The Lord Jesus*, but by the Holy Ghost. ^1-corinthians-12-3-douay

4 Now there are diversities of graces, but the same Spirit. ^1-corinthians-12-4-douay

5 And there are diversities of ministries but the same Lord. ^1-corinthians-12-5-douay

6 And there are diversities of operations, but the same God, who worketh all in all. ^1-corinthians-12-6-douay

7 And the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man unto profit. ^1-corinthians-12-7-douay

8 To one indeed, by the Spirit, is given the word of wisdom: and to another, the word of knowledge, according to the same Spirit: ^1-corinthians-12-8-douay

9 To another, faith in the same spirit: to another, the grace of healing in one Spirit: ^1-corinthians-12-9-douay

10 To another the working of miracles: to another, prophecy: to another, the discerning of spirits: to another, diverse kinds of tongues: to another, interpretation of speeches. ^1-corinthians-12-10-douay

11 But all these things, one and the same Spirit worketh, dividing to every one according as he will. ^1-corinthians-12-11-douay

12 For as the body is one and hath many members; and all the members of the body, whereas they are many, yet are one body: So also is Christ. ^1-corinthians-12-12-douay

13 For in one Spirit were we all baptized into one body, whether Jews or Gentiles, whether bond or free: and in one Spirit we have all been made to drink. ^1-corinthians-12-13-douay

14 For the body also is not one member, but many. ^1-corinthians-12-14-douay

15 If the foot should say: Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body: Is it therefore not of the Body? ^1-corinthians-12-15-douay

16 And if the ear should say: Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body: Is it therefore not of the body? ^1-corinthians-12-16-douay

17 If the whole body were the eye, where would be the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where would be the smelling? ^1-corinthians-12-17-douay

18 But now God hath set the members, every one of them, in the body as it hath pleased him. ^1-corinthians-12-18-douay

19 And if they all were one member, where would be the body? ^1-corinthians-12-19-douay

20 But now there are many members indeed, yet one body. ^1-corinthians-12-20-douay

21 And the eye cannot say to the hand: I need not thy help. Nor again the head to the feet: I have no need of you. ^1-corinthians-12-21-douay

22 Yea, much, more those that seem to be the more feeble members of the body are more necessary. ^1-corinthians-12-22-douay

23 And such as we think to be the less honourable members of the body, about these we put more abundant honour: and those that are our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness. ^1-corinthians-12-23-douay

24 But our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, giving to that which wanted the more abundant honour. ^1-corinthians-12-24-douay

25 That there might be no schism in the body: but the members might be mutually careful one for another. ^1-corinthians-12-25-douay

26 And if one member suffer any thing, all the members suffer with it: or if one member glory, all the members rejoice with it. ^1-corinthians-12-26-douay

27 Now you are the body of Christ and members of member. ^1-corinthians-12-27-douay

28 And God indeed hath set some in the church; first apostles, secondly prophets, thirdly doctors: after that miracles: then the graces of healings, helps, governments, kinds of tongues, interpretations of speeches. ^1-corinthians-12-28-douay

29 Are all apostles? Are all prophets? Are all doctors? ^1-corinthians-12-29-douay

30 Are all workers of miracles? Have all the grace of healing? Do all speak with tongues? Do all interpret? ^1-corinthians-12-30-douay

31 But be zealous for the better gifts. And I show unto you yet a more excellent way. ^1-corinthians-12-31-douay
