# 1 Corinthians Chapter 10

By the example of the Israelites, he shows that we are not to build too much upon favours received but to avoid their sins and fly from the service of idols and from things offered to idols.

1 For I would not have you ignorant, brethren, that our fathers were all under the cloud: and all passed through the sea. ^1-corinthians-10-1-douay

2 And all in Moses were baptized, in the cloud and in the sea: **In Moses...** Under the conduct of Moses, they received baptism in figure, by passing under the cloud, and through the sea; and they partook of the body and blood of Christ in figure, by eating of the manna, (called here a spiritual food because it was a figure of the true bread which comes down from heaven), and drinking the water, miraculously brought out of the rock, called here a spiritual rock, because it was also a figure of Christ. ^1-corinthians-10-2-douay

3 And did all eat the same spiritual food: ^1-corinthians-10-3-douay

4 And all drank the same spiritual drink: (And they drank of the spiritual rock that followed them: and the rock was Christ.) ^1-corinthians-10-4-douay

5 But with most of them God was not well pleased: for they were overthrown in the desert. ^1-corinthians-10-5-douay

6 Now these things were done in a figure of us, that we should not covet evil things, as they also coveted. ^1-corinthians-10-6-douay

7 Neither become ye idolaters, as some of them, as it is written: The people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to play. ^1-corinthians-10-7-douay

8 Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them that committed fornication: and there fell in one day three and twenty thousand. ^1-corinthians-10-8-douay

9 Neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them tempted and perished by the serpent. ^1-corinthians-10-9-douay

10 Neither do you murmur, as some of them murmured and were destroyed by the destroyer. ^1-corinthians-10-10-douay

11 Now all these things happened to them in figure: and they are written for our correction, upon whom the ends of the world are come. **The ends of the world...** That is, the last ages. ^1-corinthians-10-11-douay

12 Wherefore, he that thinketh himself to stand, let him take heed lest he fall. ^1-corinthians-10-12-douay

13 Let no temptation take hold on you, but such as is human. And God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that which you are able: but will make also with temptation issue, that you may be able to bear it. **Let no temptation take hold on you...** Or, *no temptation hath taken hold of you* or come upon you as yet, but what is human, or incident to man. **Issue...** or a way to escape. ^1-corinthians-10-13-douay

14 Wherefore, my dearly beloved, fly from the service of idols. ^1-corinthians-10-14-douay

15 I speak as to wise men: judge ye yourselves what I say. ^1-corinthians-10-15-douay

16 The chalice of benediction which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? And the bread which we break, is it not the partaking of the body of the Lord? **Which we bless...** Here the apostle puts them in mind of their partaking of the body and blood of Christ in the sacred mysteries, and becoming thereby one mystical body with Christ. From whence he infers, [[1-corinthians-10-douay|1 Corinthians 10:21]], that they who are made partakers with Christ, by the eucharistic sacrifice and sacrament, must not be made partakers with devils by eating of the meats sacrificed to them. ^1-corinthians-10-16-douay

17 For we, being many, are one bread, one body: all that partake of one bread. **One bread...** or, as it may be rendered, agreeably both to the Latin and Greek, because the bread is one, all we, being many, are one body, who partake of that one bread. For it is by our communicating with Christ, and with one another, in this blessed sacrament, that we are formed into one mystical body; and made, as it were, one bread, compounded of many grains of corn, closely united together. ^1-corinthians-10-17-douay

18 Behold Israel according to the flesh. Are not they that eat of the sacrifices partakers of the altar? ^1-corinthians-10-18-douay

19 What then? Do I say that what is offered in sacrifice to idols is any thing? Or that the idol is any thing? ^1-corinthians-10-19-douay

20 But the things which the heathens sacrifice, they sacrifice to devils and not to God. And I would not that you should be made partakers with devils. ^1-corinthians-10-20-douay

21 You cannot drink the chalice of the Lord and the chalice of devils: you cannot be partakers of the table of the Lord and of the table of devils. ^1-corinthians-10-21-douay

22 Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? Are we stronger than he? All things are lawful for me: but all things are not expedient. ^1-corinthians-10-22-douay

23 All things are lawful for me: but all things do not edify. ^1-corinthians-10-23-douay

24 Let no man seek his own, but that which is another's. ^1-corinthians-10-24-douay

25 Whatsoever is sold in the shambles, eat: asking no question for conscience' sake. ^1-corinthians-10-25-douay

26 The earth is the Lord's and the fulness thereof. ^1-corinthians-10-26-douay

27 If any of them that believe not, invite you, and you be willing to go: eat of any thing that is set before you, asking no question for conscience' sake. ^1-corinthians-10-27-douay

28 But if any man say: This has been sacrificed to idols: do not eat of it, for his sake that told it and for conscience' sake. ^1-corinthians-10-28-douay

29 Conscience I say, not thy own, but the other's. For why is my liberty judged by another man's conscience? ^1-corinthians-10-29-douay

30 If I partake with thanksgiving, why am I evil spoken of for that for which I give thanks? ^1-corinthians-10-30-douay

31 Therefore, whether you eat or drink, or whatsoever else you do, do all to the glory of God. ^1-corinthians-10-31-douay

32 Be without offence to the Jew, and to the Gentiles and to the church of God: ^1-corinthians-10-32-douay

33 As I also in all things please all men, not seeking that which is profitable to myself but to many: that they may be saved. ^1-corinthians-10-33-douay
