# Job Chapter 18

Baldad again reproves Job and describes the miseries of the wicked.

1 Then Baldad the Suhite answered, and said: ^job-18-1-douay

2 How long will you throw out words? understand first, and so let us speak. ^job-18-2-douay

3 Why are we reputed as beasts, and counted vile before you? ^job-18-3-douay

4 Thou that destroyest thy soul in thy fury, shall the earth be forsaken for thee, and shall rocks be removed out of their place? ^job-18-4-douay

5 Shall not the light of the wicked be extinguished, and the flame of his fire not shine? ^job-18-5-douay

6 The light shall be dark in his tabernacle, and the lamp that is over him, shall be put out. ^job-18-6-douay

7 The step of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down headlong. ^job-18-7-douay

8 For he hath thrust his feet into a net, and walketh in its meshes. ^job-18-8-douay

9 The sole of his foot shall be held in a snare, and thirst shall burn against him. ^job-18-9-douay

10 A gin is hidden for him in the earth, and his trap upon the path. ^job-18-10-douay

11 Fears shall terrify him on every side, and shall entangle his feet. ^job-18-11-douay

12 Let his strength be wasted with famine, and let hunger invade his ribs. ^job-18-12-douay

13 Let it devour the beauty of his skin, let the firstborn death consume his arms. ^job-18-13-douay

14 Let his confidence be rooted out of his tabernacle, and let destruction tread upon him like a king. ^job-18-14-douay

15 Let the companions of him that is not, dwell in his tabernacle, let brimstone be sprinkled in his tent. ^job-18-15-douay

16 Let his roots be dried up beneath, and his harvest destroyed above. ^job-18-16-douay

17 Let the memory of him perish from the earth, and let not his name be renowned in the streets. ^job-18-17-douay

18 He shall drive him out of light into darkness, and shall remove him out of the world. ^job-18-18-douay

19 His seed shall not subsist, nor his offspring among his people, nor any remnants in his country. ^job-18-19-douay

20 They that come after him shall be astonished at his day, and horror shall fall upon them that went before. ^job-18-20-douay

21 These then are the tabernacles of the wicked, and this the place of him that knoweth not God. ^job-18-21-douay
