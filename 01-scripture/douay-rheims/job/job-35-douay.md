# Job Chapter 35

Eliu declares that the good or evil done by man cannot reach God.

1 Moreover Eliu spoke these words: ^job-35-1-douay

2 Doth thy thought seem right to thee, that thou shouldst say: I am more just than God? ^job-35-2-douay

3 For thou saidst: That which is right doth not please thee: or what will it profit thee if I sin? ^job-35-3-douay

4 Therefore I will answer thy words, and thy friends with thee. ^job-35-4-douay

5 Look up to heaven and see, and behold the sky, that it is higher than thee. ^job-35-5-douay

6 If thou sin, what shalt thou hurt him? and if thy iniquities be multiplied, what shalt thou do against him? ^job-35-6-douay

7 And if thou do justly, what shalt thou give him, or what shall he receive of thy hand? ^job-35-7-douay

8 Thy wickedness may hurt a man that is like thee: and thy justice may help the son of man. ^job-35-8-douay

9 By reason of the multitude of oppressors they shall cry out: and shall wail for the violence of the arm of tyrants. ^job-35-9-douay

10 And he hath not said: Where is God, who made me, who hath given songs in the night? ^job-35-10-douay

11 Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and instructeth us more than the fowls of the air. ^job-35-11-douay

12 There shall they cry, and he will not hear, because of the pride of evil men. ^job-35-12-douay

13 God therefore will not hear in vain, and the Almighty will look into the causes of every one. ^job-35-13-douay

14 Yea, when thou shalt say: He considereth not: be judged before him, and expect him. ^job-35-14-douay

15 For he doth not now bring on his fury, neither doth he revenge wickedness exceedingly. ^job-35-15-douay

16 Therefore Job openeth his mouth in vain, and multiplieth words without knowledge. ^job-35-16-douay
