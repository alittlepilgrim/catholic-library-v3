# Job Chapter 12

Job's reply to Sophar. He extols God's power and wisdom.

1 Then Job answered, and said: ^job-12-1-douay

2 Are you then men alone, and shall wisdom die with you? ^job-12-2-douay

3 I also have a heart as well as you: for who is ignorant of these things, which you know? ^job-12-3-douay

4 He that is mocked by his friends as I, shall call upon God and he will hear him: for the simplicity of the just man is laughed to scorn. ^job-12-4-douay

5 The lamp despised in the thoughts of the rich, is ready for the time appointed. ^job-12-5-douay

6 The tabernacles of robbers abound, and they provoke God boldly; whereas it is he that hath given all into their hands: ^job-12-6-douay

7 But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee: and the birds of the air, and they shall tell thee. ^job-12-7-douay

8 Speak to the earth, and it shall answer thee: and the fishes of the sea shall tell. ^job-12-8-douay

9 Who is ignorant that the hand of the Lord hath made all these things? ^job-12-9-douay

10 In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the spirit of all flesh of man. ^job-12-10-douay

11 Doth not the ear discern words, and the palate of him that eateth, the taste? ^job-12-11-douay

12 In the ancient is wisdom, and in length of days prudence. ^job-12-12-douay

13 With him is wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding. ^job-12-13-douay

14 If he pull down, there is no man that can build up: if he shut up a man, there is none that can open. ^job-12-14-douay

15 If he withhold the waters, all things shall be dried up: and if he send them out, they shall overturn the earth. ^job-12-15-douay

16 With him is strength and wisdom: he knoweth both the deceivers, and him that is deceived. ^job-12-16-douay

17 He bringeth counsellors to a foolish end, and judges to insensibility. ^job-12-17-douay

18 He looseth the belt of kings, and girdeth their loins with a cord. ^job-12-18-douay

19 He leadeth away priests without glory, and overthroweth nobles. ^job-12-19-douay

20 He changeth the speech of the true speakers, and taketh away the doctrine of the aged. ^job-12-20-douay

21 He poureth contempt upon princes, and relieveth them that were oppressed. ^job-12-21-douay

22 He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth up to light the shadow of death. ^job-12-22-douay

23 He multiplieth nations, and destroyeth them, and restoreth them again after they were overthrown. ^job-12-23-douay

24 He changeth the heart of the princes of the people of the earth, and deceiveth them that they walk in vain where there is no way. ^job-12-24-douay

25 They shall grope as in the dark, and not in the light, and he shall make them stagger like men that are drunk. ^job-12-25-douay
