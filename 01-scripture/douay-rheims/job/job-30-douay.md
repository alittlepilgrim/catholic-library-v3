# Job Chapter 30

Job shows the wonderful change of his temporal estate, from welfare to great calamity.

1 But now the younger in time scorn me, whose fathers I would not have set with the dogs of my flock: **But now the younger in time...** That is, younger than I am, and as it were obscure, when I was conspicuous and in magnificence; they now look down on me. ^job-30-1-douay

2 The strength of whose hands was to me as nothing, and they were thought unworthy of life itself. ^job-30-2-douay

3 Barren with want and hunger, who gnawed in the wilderness, disfigured with calamity and misery. ^job-30-3-douay

4 And they ate grass, and barks of trees, and the root of junipers was their food. ^job-30-4-douay

5 Who snatched up these things out of the valleys, and when they had found any of them, they ran to them with a cry. ^job-30-5-douay

6 They dwelt in the desert places of torrents, and in caves of earth, or upon the gravel. ^job-30-6-douay

7 They pleased themselves among these kind of things, and counted it delightful to be under the briers. ^job-30-7-douay

8 The children of foolish and base men, and not appearing at all upon the earth. ^job-30-8-douay

9 Now I am turned into their song, and am become their byword. ^job-30-9-douay

10 They abhor me, and flee far from me, and are not afraid to spit in my face. ^job-30-10-douay

11 For he hath opened his quiver, and hath afflicted me, and hath put a bridle into my mouth. ^job-30-11-douay

12 At the right hand of my rising, my calamities forthwith arose: they have overthrown my feet, and have overwhelmed me with their paths as with waves. ^job-30-12-douay

13 They have destroyed my ways, they have lain in wait against me, and they have prevailed, and there was none to help. ^job-30-13-douay

14 They have rushed in upon me, as when a wall is broken, and a gate opened, and have rolled themselves down to my miseries. ^job-30-14-douay

15 I am brought to nothing: as a wind thou hast taken away my desire: and my prosperity hath passed away like a cloud. ^job-30-15-douay

16 And now my soul fadeth within myself, and the days of affliction possess me. ^job-30-16-douay

17 In the night my bone is pierced with sorrows: and they that feed upon me, do not sleep. ^job-30-17-douay

18 With the multitude of them my garment is consumed, and they have girded me about, as with the collar of my coat. ^job-30-18-douay

19 I am compared to dirt, and am likened to embers and ashes. ^job-30-19-douay

20 I cry to thee, and thou hearest me not: I stand up, and thou dost not regard me. ^job-30-20-douay

21 Thou art changed to be cruel toward me, and in the hardness of thy hand thou art against me. ^job-30-21-douay

22 Thou hast lifted me up, and set me as it were upon the wind, and thou hast mightily dashed me. ^job-30-22-douay

23 I know that thou wilt deliver me to death, where a house is appointed for every one that liveth. ^job-30-23-douay

24 But yet thou stretchest not forth thy hand to their consumption: and if they shall fall down thou wilt save. ^job-30-24-douay

25 I wept heretofore for him that was afflicted, and my soul had compassion on the poor. ^job-30-25-douay

26 I expected good things, and evils are come upon me: I waited for light, and darkness broke out. ^job-30-26-douay

27 My inner parts have boiled without any rest, the days of affliction have prevented me. ^job-30-27-douay

28 I went mourning without indignation; I rose up, and cried in the crowd. ^job-30-28-douay

29 I was the brother of dragons, and companion of ostriches. **Brother of dragons, etc...** Imitating these creatures in their lamentable noise. ^job-30-29-douay

30 My skin is become black upon me, and my bones are dried up with heat. ^job-30-30-douay

31 My harp is turned to mourning, and my organ into the voice of those that weep. ^job-30-31-douay
