# Job Chapter 22

Eliphaz falsely imputes many crimes to Job, but promises him prosperity if he will repent.

1 Then Eliphaz the Themanite answered, and said: ^job-22-1-douay

2 Can man be compared with God, even though he were of perfect knowledge? ^job-22-2-douay

3 What doth it profit God if thou be just? or what dost thou give him if thy way be unspotted? ^job-22-3-douay

4 Shall he reprove thee for fear, and come with thee into judgment: ^job-22-4-douay

5 And not for thy manifold wickedness and thy infinite iniquities? ^job-22-5-douay

6 For thou hast taken away the pledge of thy brethren without cause, and stripped the naked of their clothing. ^job-22-6-douay

7 Thou hast not given water to the weary, thou hast withdrawn bread from the hungry. ^job-22-7-douay

8 In the strength of thy arm thou didst possess the land, and being the most mighty thou holdest it. ^job-22-8-douay

9 Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless thou hast broken in pieces. ^job-22-9-douay

10 Therefore art thou surrounded with shares, and sudden fear troubleth thee. ^job-22-10-douay

11 And didst thou think that thou shouldst not see darkness, and that thou shouldst not be covered with the violence of overflowing waters? ^job-22-11-douay

12 Dost not thou think that God is higher than heaven, and is elevated above the height of the stars? ^job-22-12-douay

13 And thou sayst: What doth God know? and he judgeth as it were through a mist. ^job-22-13-douay

14 The clouds are his covert, and he doth not consider our things, and he walketh about the poles of heaven. ^job-22-14-douay

15 Dost thou desire to keep the path of ages, which wicked men have trodden? ^job-22-15-douay

16 Who were taken away before their time, and a flood hath overthrown their foundation. ^job-22-16-douay

17 Who said to God: Depart from us: and looked upon the Almighty as if he could do nothing: ^job-22-17-douay

18 Whereas he had filled their houses with good things: whose way of thinking be far from me. ^job-22-18-douay

19 The just shall see, and shall rejoice, and the innocent shall laugh them to scorn. ^job-22-19-douay

20 Is not their exaltation cut down, and hath not fire devoured the remnants of them? ^job-22-20-douay

21 Submit thyself then to him, and be at peace: and thereby thou shalt have the best fruits. ^job-22-21-douay

22 Receive the law of his mouth, and lay up his words in thy heart. ^job-22-22-douay

23 If thou wilt return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, and shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacle. ^job-22-23-douay

24 He shall give for earth flint, and for flint torrents of gold. ^job-22-24-douay

25 And the Almighty shall be against thy enemies, and silver shall be heaped together for thee. ^job-22-25-douay

26 Then shalt thou abound in delights in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face to God. ^job-22-26-douay

27 Thou shalt pray to him, and he will hear thee, and thou shalt pay vows. ^job-22-27-douay

28 Thou shalt decree a thing, and it shall come to thee, and light shall shine in thy ways. ^job-22-28-douay

29 For he that hath been humbled, shall be in glory: and he that shall bow down his eyes, he shall be saved. ^job-22-29-douay

30 The innocent shall be saved, and he shall be saved by the cleanness of his hands. ^job-22-30-douay
