# Job Chapter 29

Job relates his former happiness, and the respect that all men showed him.

1 Job also added, taking up his parable, and said: ^job-29-1-douay

2 Who will grant me, that I might be according to the months past, according to the days in which God kept me? ^job-29-2-douay

3 When his lamp shined over my head, and I walked by his light in darkness? ^job-29-3-douay

4 As I was in the days of my youth, when God was secretly in my tabernacle? ^job-29-4-douay

5 When the Almighty was with me: and my servants round about me? ^job-29-5-douay

6 When I washed my feet with butter, and the rock poured me out rivers of oil? ^job-29-6-douay

7 When I went out to the gate of the city, and in the street they prepared me a chair? ^job-29-7-douay

8 The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and the old men rose up and stood. ^job-29-8-douay

9 The princes ceased to speak, and laid the finger on their mouth. ^job-29-9-douay

10 The rulers held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to their throat. ^job-29-10-douay

11 The ear that heard me blessed me, and the eye that saw me gave witness to me: ^job-29-11-douay

12 Because I had delivered the poor man that cried out; and the fatherless, that had no helper. ^job-29-12-douay

13 The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me, and I comforted the heart of the widow. ^job-29-13-douay

14 I was clad with justice: and I clothed myself with my judgment, as with a robe and a diadem. ^job-29-14-douay

15 I was an eye to the blind, and a foot to the lame. ^job-29-15-douay

16 I was the father of the poor: and the cause which I knew not, I searched out most diligently. ^job-29-16-douay

17 I broke the jaws of the wicked man, and out of his teeth I took away the prey. ^job-29-17-douay

18 And I said: I shall die in my nest, and as a palm tree shall multiply my days. ^job-29-18-douay

19 My root is opened beside the waters, and dew shall continue in my harvest. ^job-29-19-douay

20 My glory shall always be renewed, and my bow in my hand shall be repaired. ^job-29-20-douay

21 They that heard me, waited for my sentence, and being attentive held their peace at my counsel. ^job-29-21-douay

22 To my words they durst add nothing, and my speech dropped upon them. ^job-29-22-douay

23 They waited for me as for rain, and they opened their mouth as for a latter shower. ^job-29-23-douay

24 If at any time I laughed on them, they believed not, and the light of my countenance fell not on earth. ^job-29-24-douay

25 If I had a mind to go to them, I sat first, and when I sat as a king, with his army standing about him, yet I was a comforter of them that mourned. ^job-29-25-douay
