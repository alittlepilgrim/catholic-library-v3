# Job Chapter 10

Job laments his afflictions and begs to be delivered.

1 My soul is weary of my life, I will let go my speech against myself, I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^job-10-1-douay

2 I will say to God: Do not condemn me: tell me why thou judgest me so? ^job-10-2-douay

3 Doth it seem good to thee that thou shouldst calumniate me, and oppress me, the work of thy own hands, and help the counsel of the wicked? ^job-10-3-douay

4 Hast thou eyes of flesh: or, shalt thou see as man seeth? ^job-10-4-douay

5 Are thy days as the days of man, and are thy years as the times of men: ^job-10-5-douay

6 That thou shouldst inquire after my iniquity, and search after my sin? ^job-10-6-douay

7 And shouldst know that I have done no wicked thing, whereas there is no man that can deliver out of thy hand? ^job-10-7-douay

8 Thy hands have made me, and fashioned me wholly round about, and dost thou thus cast me down headlong on a sudden? ^job-10-8-douay

9 Remember, I beseech thee, that thou hast made me as the clay, and thou wilt bring me into dust. ^job-10-9-douay

10 Hast thou not milked me as milk, and curdled me like cheese? ^job-10-10-douay

11 Thou hast clothed me with skin and flesh: thou hast put me together with bones and sinews: ^job-10-11-douay

12 Thou hast granted me life and mercy, and thy visitation hath preserved my spirit. ^job-10-12-douay

13 Although thou conceal these things in thy heart, yet I know that thou rememberest all things. ^job-10-13-douay

14 If I have sinned, and thou hast spared me for an hour: why dost thou not suffer me to be clean from my iniquity? ^job-10-14-douay

15 And if I be wicked, woe unto me: and if just, I shall not lift up my head, being filled with affliction and misery. ^job-10-15-douay

16 And for pride thou wilt take me as a lioness, and returning, thou tormentest me wonderfully. ^job-10-16-douay

17 Thou renewest thy witnesses against me, and multipliest thy wrath upon me, and pains war against me. ^job-10-17-douay

18 Why didst thou bring me forth out of the womb? O that I had been consumed, that eye might not see me! ^job-10-18-douay

19 I should have been as if I had not been, carried from the womb to the grave. ^job-10-19-douay

20 Shall not the fewness of my days be ended shortly? Suffer me, therefore, that I may lament my sorrow a little: ^job-10-20-douay

21 Before I go and return no more, to a land that is dark and covered with the mist of death: ^job-10-21-douay

22 A land of misery and darkness, where the shadow of death, and no order, but everlasting horror dwelleth. ^job-10-22-douay
