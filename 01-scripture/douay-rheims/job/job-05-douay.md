# Job Chapter 5

Eliphaz proceeds in his charge, and exhorts Job to acknowledge his sins.

1 Call now, if there be any that will answer thee, and turn to some of the saints. ^job-05-1-douay

2 Anger indeed killeth the foolish, and envy slayeth the little one. ^job-05-2-douay

3 I have seen a fool with a strong root, and I cursed his beauty immediately. ^job-05-3-douay

4 His children shall be far from safety, and shall be destroyed in the gate, and there shall be none to deliver them. ^job-05-4-douay

5 Whose harvest the hungry shall eat, and the armed man shall take him by violence, and the thirsty shall drink up his riches. ^job-05-5-douay

6 Nothing upon earth is done without a cause, and sorrow doth not spring out of the ground. ^job-05-6-douay

7 Man is born to labour, and the bird to fly. ^job-05-7-douay

8 Wherefore I will pray to the Lord, and address my speech to God: ^job-05-8-douay

9 Who doth great things, and unsearchable and wonderful things without number: ^job-05-9-douay

10 Who giveth rain upon the face of the earth, and watereth all things with waters: ^job-05-10-douay

11 Who setteth up the humble on high, and comforteth with health those that mourn. ^job-05-11-douay

12 Who bringeth to nought the designs of the malignant, so that their hands cannot accomplish what they had begun: ^job-05-12-douay

13 Who catcheth the wise in their craftiness, and disappointeth the counsel of the wicked: ^job-05-13-douay

14 They shall meet with darkness in the day, and grope at noonday as in the night. ^job-05-14-douay

15 But he shall save the needy from the sword of their mouth, and the poor from the hand of the violent. ^job-05-15-douay

16 And to the needy there shall be hope, but iniquity shall draw in her mouth. ^job-05-16-douay

17 Blessed is the man whom God correcteth: refuse not, therefore, the chastising of the Lord. ^job-05-17-douay

18 For he woundeth, and cureth: he striketh, and his hands shall heal. ^job-05-18-douay

19 In six troubles he shall deliver thee, and in the seventh, evil shall not touch thee. ^job-05-19-douay

20 In famine he shall deliver thee from death; and in battle, from the hand of the sword. ^job-05-20-douay

21 Thou shalt be hidden from the scourge of the tongue: and thou shalt not fear calamity when it cometh. ^job-05-21-douay

22 In destruction and famine thou shalt laugh: and thou shalt not be afraid of the beasts of the earth. ^job-05-22-douay

23 But thou shalt have a covenant with the stones of the lands, and the beasts of the earth shall be at peace with thee. ^job-05-23-douay

24 And thou shalt know that thy tabernacle is in peace, and visiting thy beauty, thou shalt not sin. ^job-05-24-douay

25 Thou shalt know also that thy seed shall be multiplied, and thy offspring like the grass of the earth. ^job-05-25-douay

26 Thou shalt enter into the grave in abundance, as a heap of wheat is brought in its season. ^job-05-26-douay

27 Behold, this is even so, as we have searched out: which thou having heard, consider it thoroughly in thy mind. ^job-05-27-douay
