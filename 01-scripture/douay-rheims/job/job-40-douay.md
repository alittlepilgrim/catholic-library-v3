# Job Chapter 40

Of the power of God in the behemoth and the leviathan.

1 And the Lord answering Job out of the whirlwind, said: ^job-40-1-douay

2 Gird up thy loins like a man: I will ask thee, and do thou tell me. ^job-40-2-douay

3 Wilt thou make void my judgment: and condemn me, that thou mayst be justified? ^job-40-3-douay

4 And hast thou an arm like God, and canst thou thunder with a voice like him? ^job-40-4-douay

5 Clothe thyself with beauty, and set thyself up on high, and be glorious, and put on goodly garments. ^job-40-5-douay

6 Scatter the proud in thy indignation, and behold every arrogant man, and humble him. ^job-40-6-douay

7 Look on all that are proud, and confound them, and crush the wicked in their place, ^job-40-7-douay

8 Hide them in the dust together, and plunge their faces into the pit. ^job-40-8-douay

9 Then I will confess that thy right hand is able to save thee. ^job-40-9-douay

10 Behold behemoth whom I made with thee, he eateth grass like an ox. **Behemoth...** In Hebrew, *behema*, which signifies in general an animal; but many authors explain, that here it is put for the elephant. ^job-40-10-douay

11 His strength is in his loins, and his force in the navel of his belly. ^job-40-11-douay

12 He setteth up his tail like a cedar, the sinews of his testicles are wrapped together. ^job-40-12-douay

13 His bones are like pipes of brass, his gristle like plates of iron. ^job-40-13-douay

14 He is the beginning of the ways of God, who made him, he will apply his sword. **He will apply his sword...** This text is variously explained: some explain the sword, the horn given to the animal for his defence: others, the power that God hath given to the animal for his defence: others, the power that God hath given to man to slay him, notwithstanding his great size and strength. ^job-40-14-douay

15 To him the mountains bring forth grass: there all the beasts of the field shall play. ^job-40-15-douay

16 He sleepeth under the shadow, in the covert of the reed, and in moist places. ^job-40-16-douay

17 The shades cover his shadow, the willows of the brook shall compass him about. ^job-40-17-douay

18 Behold, he will drink up a river, and not wonder: and he trusteth that the Jordan may run into his mouth. ^job-40-18-douay

19 In his eyes as with a hook he shall take him, and bore through his nostrils with stakes. ^job-40-19-douay

20 Canst thou draw out the leviathan with a hook, or canst thou tie his tongue with a cord? **Leviathan...** The whale or some sea monster. ^job-40-20-douay

21 Canst thou put a ring in his nose, or bore through his jaw with a buckle? ^job-40-21-douay

22 Will he make many supplications to thee, or speak soft words to thee? ^job-40-22-douay

23 Will he make a covenant with thee, and wilt thou take him to be a servant for ever, ^job-40-23-douay

24 Shalt thou play with him as with a bird, or tie him up for thy handmaids? ^job-40-24-douay

25 Shall friends cut him in pieces, shall merchants divide him? ^job-40-25-douay

26 Wilt thou fill nets with his skin, and the cabins of fishes with his head? ^job-40-26-douay

27 Lay thy hand upon him: remember the battle, and speak no more. ^job-40-27-douay

28 Behold his hope shall fail him, and in the sight of all he shall be cast down. ^job-40-28-douay
