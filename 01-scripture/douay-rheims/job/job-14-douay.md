# Job Chapter 14

Job declares the shortness of man's days: and professes his belief of a resurrection.

1 Man born of a woman, living for a short time, is filled with many miseries. ^job-14-1-douay

2 Who cometh forth like a flower, and is destroyed, and fleeth as a shadow, and never continueth in the same state. ^job-14-2-douay

3 And dost thou think it meet to open thy eyes upon such an one, and to bring him into judgment with thee? ^job-14-3-douay

4 Who can make him clean that is conceived of unclean seed? is it not thou who only art? ^job-14-4-douay

5 The days of man are short, and the number of his months is with thee: thou hast appointed his bounds which cannot be passed. ^job-14-5-douay

6 Depart a little from him, that he may rest until his wished for day come, as that of the hireling. ^job-14-6-douay

7 A tree hath hope: if it be cut, it growth green again, and the boughs thereof sprout. ^job-14-7-douay

8 If its roots be old in the earth, and its stock be dead in the dust: ^job-14-8-douay

9 At the scent of water, it shall spring, and bring forth leaves, as when it was first planted. ^job-14-9-douay

10 But man when he shall be dead, and stripped and consumed, I pray you where is he? ^job-14-10-douay

11 As if the waters should depart out of the sea, and an emptied river should be dried up; ^job-14-11-douay

12 So man when he is fallen asleep shall not rise again; till the heavens be broken, he shall not awake, nor rise up out of his sleep. ^job-14-12-douay

13 Who will grant me this, that thou mayst protect me in hell, and hide me till thy wrath pass, and appoint me a time when thou wilt remember me? **That thou mayst protect me in hell...** That is, in the state of the dead; and in the place where the souls are kept waiting for their Redeemer. ^job-14-13-douay

14 Shall man that is dead, thinkest thou, live again? all the days in which I am now in warfare, I expect until my change come. ^job-14-14-douay

15 Thou shalt call me, and I will answer thee: to the work of thy hands thou shalt reach out thy right hand. ^job-14-15-douay

16 Thou indeed hast numbered my steps, but spare my sins. ^job-14-16-douay

17 Thou hast sealed up my offences as it were in a bag, but hast cured my iniquity. ^job-14-17-douay

18 A mountain falling cometh to nought, and a rock is removed out of its place. ^job-14-18-douay

19 Waters wear away the stones, and with inundation the ground by little and little is washed away: so in like manner thou shalt destroy man. ^job-14-19-douay

20 Thou hast strengthened him for a little while, that he may pass away for ever: thou shalt change his face, and shalt send him away. ^job-14-20-douay

21 Whether his children come to honour or dishonour, he shall not understand. ^job-14-21-douay

22 But yet his flesh, while he shall live, shall have pain, and his soul shall mourn over him. ^job-14-22-douay
