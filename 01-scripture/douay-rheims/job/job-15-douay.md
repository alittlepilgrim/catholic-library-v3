# Job Chapter 15

Eliphaz returns to the charge against Job, and describes the wretched state of the wicked.

1 And Eliphaz the Themanite, answered, and said: ^job-15-1-douay

2 Will a wise man answer as if he were speaking in the wind, and fill his stomach with burning heat? ^job-15-2-douay

3 Thou reprovest him by words, who is not equal to thee, and thou speakest that which is not good for thee. ^job-15-3-douay

4 As much as is in thee, thou hast made void fear, and hast taken away prayers from before God. **Thou hast made void fear...** That is, cast off the fear of offending God. ^job-15-4-douay

5 For thy iniquity hath taught thy mouth, and thou imitatest the tongue of blasphemers. ^job-15-5-douay

6 Thy own mouth shall condemn thee, and not I: and thy own lips shall answer thee. ^job-15-6-douay

7 Art thou the first man that was born, or wast thou made before the hills? ^job-15-7-douay

8 Hast thou heard God's counsel, and shall his wisdom be inferior to thee? ^job-15-8-douay

9 What knowest thou that we are ignorant of? what dost thou understand that we know not? ^job-15-9-douay

10 There are with us also aged and ancient men, much elder than thy fathers. ^job-15-10-douay

11 Is it a great matter that God should comfort thee? but thy wicked words hinder this. ^job-15-11-douay

12 Why doth thy heart elevate thee, and why dost thou stare with thy eyes, as if they were thinking great things? ^job-15-12-douay

13 Why doth thy spirit swell against God, to utter such words out of thy mouth? ^job-15-13-douay

14 What is man that he should be without spot, and he that is born of a woman that he should appear just? ^job-15-14-douay

15 Behold among his saints none is unchangeable, and the heavens are not pure in his sight. ^job-15-15-douay

16 How much more is man abominable, and unprofitable, who drinketh iniquity like water? ^job-15-16-douay

17 I will show thee, hear me: and I will tell thee what I have seen. ^job-15-17-douay

18 Wise men confess and hide not their fathers. **Wise men confess and hide not their fathers...** That is, the knowledge and documents they have received from their fathers they are not ashamed to own. ^job-15-18-douay

19 To whom alone the earth was given, and no stranger hath passed among them. ^job-15-19-douay

20 The wicked man is proud all his days, and the number of the years of his tyranny is uncertain. ^job-15-20-douay

21 The sound of dread is always in his ears: and when there is peace, he always suspecteth treason. ^job-15-21-douay

22 He believeth not that he may return from darkness to light, looking round about for the sword on every side. ^job-15-22-douay

23 When he moveth himself to seek bread, he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand. ^job-15-23-douay

24 Tribulation shall terrify him, and distress shall surround him, as a king that is prepared for the battle. ^job-15-24-douay

25 For he hath stretched out his hand against God, and hath strengthened himself against the Almighty. ^job-15-25-douay

26 He hath run against him with his neck raised up, and is armed with a fat neck. ^job-15-26-douay

27 Fatness hath covered his face, and the fat hangeth down on his sides. ^job-15-27-douay

28 He hath dwelt in desolate cities, and in desert houses that are reduced into heaps. ^job-15-28-douay

29 He shall not be enriched, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he push his root in the earth. ^job-15-29-douay

30 He shall not depart out of darkness: the flame shall dry up his branches, and he shall be taken away by the breath of his own mouth. ^job-15-30-douay

31 He shall not believe, being vainly deceived by error, that he may be redeemed with any price. ^job-15-31-douay

32 Before his days be full he shall perish: and his hands shall wither away. ^job-15-32-douay

33 He shall be blasted as a vine when its grapes are in the first flower, and as an olive tree that casteth its flower. ^job-15-33-douay

34 For the congregation of the hypocrite is barren, and fire shall devour their tabernacles, who love to take bribes. ^job-15-34-douay

35 He hath conceived sorrow, and hath brought forth iniquity, and his womb prepareth deceits. ^job-15-35-douay
