# Job Chapter 27

Job persists in asserting his own innocence, and that hypocrites will be punished in the end.

1 Job also added, taking up his parable, and said: ^job-27-1-douay

2 As God liveth, who hath taken away my judgment, and the Almighty, who hath brought my soul to bitterness, ^job-27-2-douay

3 As long as breath remaineth in me, and the spirit of God in my nostrils, ^job-27-3-douay

4 My lips shall not speak iniquity, neither shall my tongue contrive lying. ^job-27-4-douay

5 God forbid that I should judge you to be just: till I die I will not depart from my innocence. ^job-27-5-douay

6 My justification, which I have begun to hold, I will not forsake: for my heart doth not reprehend me in all my life. ^job-27-6-douay

7 Let my enemy be as the ungodly, and my adversary as the wicked one. ^job-27-7-douay

8 For what is the hope of the hypocrite if through covetousness he take by violence, and God deliver not his soul? ^job-27-8-douay

9 Will God hear his cry, when distress shall come upon him? ^job-27-9-douay

10 Or can he delight himself in the Almighty, and call upon God at all times? ^job-27-10-douay

11 I will teach you by the hand of God, what the Almighty hath, and I will not conceal it. ^job-27-11-douay

12 Behold you all know it, and why do you speak vain things without cause? ^job-27-12-douay

13 This is the portion of a wicked man with God, and the inheritance of the violent, which they shall receive of the Almighty. ^job-27-13-douay

14 If his sons be multiplied, they shall be for the sword, and his grandsons shall not be filled with bread. ^job-27-14-douay

15 They that shall remain of him, shall be buried in death, and his widows shall not weep. ^job-27-15-douay

16 If he shall heap together silver as earth, and prepare raiment as clay, ^job-27-16-douay

17 He shall prepare indeed, but the just man shall be clothed with it: and the innocent shall divide the silver. ^job-27-17-douay

18 He hath built his house as a moth, and as a keeper he hath made a booth. ^job-27-18-douay

19 The rich man when he shall sleep shall take away nothing with him: he shall open his eyes and find nothing. ^job-27-19-douay

20 Poverty like water shall take hold on him, a tempest shall oppress him in the night: ^job-27-20-douay

21 A burning wind shall take him up, and carry him away, and as a whirlwind shall snatch him from his place. ^job-27-21-douay

22 And he shall cast upon him, and shall not spare: out of his hand he would willingly flee. ^job-27-22-douay

23 He shall clasp his hands upon him, and shall hiss at him, beholding his place. ^job-27-23-douay
