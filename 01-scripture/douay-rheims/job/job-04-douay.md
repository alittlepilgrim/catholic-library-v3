# Job Chapter 4

Eliphas charges Job with impatience, and pretends that God never afflicts the innocent.

1 Then Eliphaz, the Themanite, answered, and said: ^job-04-1-douay

2 If we begin to speak to thee, perhaps thou wilt take it ill; but who can withhold the words he hath conceived? ^job-04-2-douay

3 Behold thou hast taught many, and thou hast strengthened the weary hands: ^job-04-3-douay

4 Thy words have confirmed them that were staggering, and thou hast strengthened the trembling knees: ^job-04-4-douay

5 But now the scourge is come upon thee, and thou faintest: It hath touched thee, and thou art troubled. ^job-04-5-douay

6 Where is thy fear, thy fortitude, thy patience, and the perfection of thy ways? ^job-04-6-douay

7 Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished being innocent? or when were the just destroyed? ^job-04-7-douay

8 On the contrary, I have seen those who work iniquity, and sow sorrows, and reap them, ^job-04-8-douay

9 Perishing by the blast of God, and consumed by the spirit of his wrath. ^job-04-9-douay

10 The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the lioness, and the teeth of the whelps of lions, are broken: ^job-04-10-douay

11 The tiger hath perished for want of prey, and the young lions are scattered abroad. ^job-04-11-douay

12 Now there was a word spoken to me in private, and my ears by stealth, as it were, received the veins of its whisper. ^job-04-12-douay

13 In the horror of a vision by night, when deep sleep is wont to hold men, ^job-04-13-douay

14 Fear seized upon me, and trembling, and all my bones were affrighted: ^job-04-14-douay

15 And when a spirit passed before me, the hair of my flesh stood up. ^job-04-15-douay

16 There stood one whose countenance I knew not, an image before my eyes, and I heard the voice, as it were, of a gentle wind. ^job-04-16-douay

17 Shall man be justified in comparison of God, or shall a man be more pure than his maker? **Shall man be justified in comparison of God, etc...** These are the words which Eliphaz had heard from an angel, which ([[job-04-douay|verse 15]]) he calls a spirit. ^job-04-17-douay

18 Behold, they that serve him are not steadfast, and in his angels he found wickedness: ^job-04-18-douay

19 How much more shall they that dwell in houses of clay, who have an earthly foundation, be consumed as with the moth? ^job-04-19-douay

20 From morning till evening they shall be cut down: and because no one understandeth, they shall perish for ever. ^job-04-20-douay

21 And they that shall be left, shall be taken away from them: they shall die, and not in wisdom. ^job-04-21-douay
