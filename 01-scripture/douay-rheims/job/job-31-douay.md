# Job Chapter 31

Job, to defend himself from the unjust judgments of his friends, gives a sincere account of his own virtues.

1 I made a covenant with my eyes, that I would not so much as think upon a virgin. ^job-31-1-douay

2 For what part should God from above have in me, and what inheritance the Almighty from on high? ^job-31-2-douay

3 Is not destruction to the wicked, and aversion to them that work iniquity? ^job-31-3-douay

4 Doth not he consider my ways, and number all my steps? ^job-31-4-douay

5 If I have walked in vanity, and my foot hath made haste to deceit: ^job-31-5-douay

6 Let him weigh me in a just balance, and let God know my simplicity. ^job-31-6-douay

7 If my step hath turned out of the way, and if my heart hath followed my eyes, and if a spot hath cleaved to my hands: ^job-31-7-douay

8 Then let me sow and let another reap: and let my offspring be rooted out. ^job-31-8-douay

9 If my heart hath been deceived upon a woman, and if I have laid wait at my friend's door: ^job-31-9-douay

10 Let my wife be the harlot of another, and let other men lie with her. ^job-31-10-douay

11 For this is a heinous crime, and a most grievous iniquity. ^job-31-11-douay

12 It is a fire that devoureth even to destruction, and rooteth up all things that spring. ^job-31-12-douay

13 If I have despised to abide judgment with my manservant, or my maidservant, when they had any controversy against me: ^job-31-13-douay

14 For what shall I do when God shall rise to judge? and when he shall examine, what shall I answer him? ^job-31-14-douay

15 Did not he that made me in the womb make him also: and did not one and the same form me in the womb? ^job-31-15-douay

16 If I have denied to the poor what they desired, and have made the eyes of the widow wait: ^job-31-16-douay

17 If I have eaten my morsel alone, and the fatherless hath not eaten thereof: ^job-31-17-douay

18 (For from my infancy mercy grew up with me: and it came out with me from my mother's womb:) ^job-31-18-douay

19 If I have despised him that was perishing for want of clothing, and the poor man that had no covering: ^job-31-19-douay

20 If his sides have not blessed me, and if he were not warmed with the fleece of my sheep: ^job-31-20-douay

21 If I have lifted up my hand against the fatherless, even when I saw myself superior in the gate: ^job-31-21-douay

22 Let my shoulder fall from its joint, and let my arm with its bones be broken. ^job-31-22-douay

23 For I have always feared God as waves swelling over me, and his weight I was unable to bear. ^job-31-23-douay

24 If I have thought gold my strength, and have said to fine gold: My confidence: ^job-31-24-douay

25 If I have rejoiced over my great riches, and because my hand had gotten much. ^job-31-25-douay

26 If I beheld the sun when it shined and the moon going in brightness: **If I beheld the sun, etc...** If I behold the sun and moon with admiration, knowing them to be created and governed by the power of God, I call on my adversaries to produce any thing against me, whereby I could be charged with worshipping the sun or moon. ^job-31-26-douay

27 And my heart in secret hath rejoiced, and I have kissed my hand with, my mouth: ^job-31-27-douay

28 Which is a very great iniquity, and a denial against the most high God. ^job-31-28-douay

29 If I have been glad at the downfall of him that hated me, and have rejoiced that evil had found him. ^job-31-29-douay

30 For I have not given my mouth to sin, by wishing a curse to his soul. ^job-31-30-douay

31 If the men of my tabernacle have not said: Who will give us of his flesh that we may be filled? ^job-31-31-douay

32 The stranger did not stay without, my door was open to the traveller. ^job-31-32-douay

33 If as a man I have hid my sin, and have concealed my iniquity in my bosom. ^job-31-33-douay

34 If I have been afraid at a very great multitude, and the contempt of kinsmen hath terrified me: and have not rather held my peace, and not gone out of the door. ^job-31-34-douay

35 Who would grant me a hearing, that the Almighty may hear my desire: and that he himself that judgeth would write a book, ^job-31-35-douay

36 That I may carry it on my shoulder, and put it about me as a crown? ^job-31-36-douay

37 At every step of mine I would pronounce it, and offer it as to a prince. ^job-31-37-douay

38 If my land cry against me, and with it the furrows thereof mourn: ^job-31-38-douay

39 If I have eaten the fruits thereof without money, and have afflicted the son of the tillers thereof: ^job-31-39-douay

40 Let thistles grow up to me instead of wheat, and thorns instead of barley. ^job-31-40-douay

*The words of Job are ended.*
