# Job Chapter 33

Eliu blames Job for asserting his own innocence.

1 Hear therefore, O Job, my speeches, and hearken to all my words. ^job-33-1-douay

2 Behold now I have opened my mouth, let my tongue speak within my jaws. ^job-33-2-douay

3 My words are from my upright heart, and my lips shall speak a pure sentence. ^job-33-3-douay

4 The spirit of God made me, and the breath of the Almighty gave me life. ^job-33-4-douay

5 If thou canst, answer me, and stand up against my face. ^job-33-5-douay

6 Behold God hath made me as well as thee, and of the same clay I also was formed. ^job-33-6-douay

7 But yet let not my wonder terrify thee, and let not my eloquence be burdensome to thee. ^job-33-7-douay

8 Now thou hast said in my hearing, and I have heard the voice of thy words: ^job-33-8-douay

9 I am clean, and without sin: I am unspotted, and there is no iniquity in me. ^job-33-9-douay

10 Because he hath found complaints against me, therefore he hath counted me for his enemy. ^job-33-10-douay

11 He hath put my feet in the stocks, he hath observed all my paths. ^job-33-11-douay

12 Now this is the thing in which thou art not justified: I will answer thee, that God is greater than man. ^job-33-12-douay

13 Dost thou strive against him, because he hath not answered thee to all words? ^job-33-13-douay

14 God speaketh once, and repeateth not the selfsame thing the second time. ^job-33-14-douay

15 By a dream in a vision by night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, and they are sleeping in their beds: ^job-33-15-douay

16 Then he openeth the ears of men, and teaching instructeth them in what they are to learn. ^job-33-16-douay

17 That he may withdraw a man from the things he is doing, and may deliver him from pride. ^job-33-17-douay

18 Rescuing his soul from corruption: and his life from passing to the sword. ^job-33-18-douay

19 He rebuketh also by sorrow in the bed, and he maketh all his bones to wither. ^job-33-19-douay

20 Bread becometh abominable to him in his life, and to his soul the meat which before he desired. ^job-33-20-douay

21 His flesh shall be consumed away, and his bones that were covered shall be made bare. ^job-33-21-douay

22 His soul hath drawn near to corruption, and his life to the destroyers. ^job-33-22-douay

23 If there shall be an angel speaking for him, one among thousands, to declare man's uprightness, ^job-33-23-douay

24 He shall have mercy on him, and shall say: Deliver him, that he may not go down to corruption: I have found wherein I may be merciful to him. ^job-33-24-douay

25 His flesh is consumed with punishments, let him return to the days of his youth. ^job-33-25-douay

26 He shall pray to God, and he will be gracious to him: and he shall see his face with joy, and he will render to man his justice. ^job-33-26-douay

27 He shall look upon men, and shall say: I have sinned, and indeed I have offended, and I have not received what I have deserved. ^job-33-27-douay

28 He hath delivered his soul from going into destruction, that it may live and see the light. ^job-33-28-douay

29 Behold, all these things God worketh three times within every one. ^job-33-29-douay

30 That he may withdraw their souls from corruption, and enlighten them with the light of the living. ^job-33-30-douay

31 Attend, Job, and hearken to me, and hold thy peace, whilst I speak. ^job-33-31-douay

32 But if thou hast any thing to say, answer me, speak: for I would have thee to appear just. ^job-33-32-douay

33 And if thou have not, hear me: hold thy peace, and I will teach thee wisdom. ^job-33-33-douay
