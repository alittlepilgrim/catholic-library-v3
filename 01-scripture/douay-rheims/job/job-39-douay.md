# Job Chapter 39

The wonders of the power and providence of God in many of his creatures.

1 Knowest thou the time when the wild goats bring forth among the rocks, or hast thou observed the hinds when they fawn? ^job-39-1-douay

2 Hast thou numbered the months of their conceiving, or knowest thou the time when they bring forth? ^job-39-2-douay

3 They bow themselves to bring forth young, and they cast them, and send forth roarings. ^job-39-3-douay

4 Their young are weaned and go to feed: they go forth, and return not to them. ^job-39-4-douay

5 Who hath sent out the wild ass free, and who hath loosed his bonds? ^job-39-5-douay

6 To whom I have given a house in the wilderness, and his dwellings in the barren land. ^job-39-6-douay

7 He scorneth the multitude of the city, he heareth not the cry of the driver. ^job-39-7-douay

8 He looketh round about the mountains of his pasture, and seeketh for every green thing, ^job-39-8-douay

9 Shall the rhinoceros be willing to serve thee, or will he stay at thy crib? ^job-39-9-douay

10 Canst thou bind the rhinoceros with thy thong to plough, or will he break the clods of the valleys after thee? ^job-39-10-douay

11 Wilt thou have confidence in his great strength, and leave thy labours to him? ^job-39-11-douay

12 Wilt thou trust him that he will render thee the seed, and gather it into thy barnfloor? ^job-39-12-douay

13 The wing of the ostrich is like the wings of the heron, and of the hawk. ^job-39-13-douay

14 When she leaveth her eggs on the earth, thou perhaps wilt warm them in the dust. ^job-39-14-douay

15 She forgetteth that the foot may tread upon them, or that the beasts of the field may break them. ^job-39-15-douay

16 She is hardened against her young ones, as though they were not hers, she hath laboured in vain, no fear constraining her. ^job-39-16-douay

17 For God hath deprived her of wisdom, neither hath he given her understanding. ^job-39-17-douay

18 When time shall be, she setteth up her wings on high: she scorneth the horse and his rider. ^job-39-18-douay

19 Wilt thou give strength to the horse or clothe his neck with neighing? ^job-39-19-douay

20 Wilt thou lift him up like the locusts? the glory of his nostrils is terror. ^job-39-20-douay

21 He breaketh up the earth with his hoof, he pranceth boldly, he goeth forward to meet armed men. ^job-39-21-douay

22 He despiseth fear, he turneth not his back to the sword. ^job-39-22-douay

23 Above him shall the quiver rattle, the spear and shield shall glitter. ^job-39-23-douay

24 Chasing and raging he swalloweth the ground, neither doth he make account when the noise of the trumpet soundeth. ^job-39-24-douay

25 When he heareth the trumpet he saith: Ha, ha: he smelleth the battle afar off, the encouraging of the captains, and the shouting of the army. ^job-39-25-douay

26 Doth the hawk wax feathered by thy wisdom, spreading her wings to the south? ^job-39-26-douay

27 Will the eagle mount up at thy command, and make her nest in high places? ^job-39-27-douay

28 She abideth among the rocks, and dwelleth among cragged flints, and stony hills, where there is no access. ^job-39-28-douay

29 From thence she looketh for the prey, and her eyes behold afar off. ^job-39-29-douay

30 Her young ones shall suck up blood: and wheresoever the carcass shall be, she is immediately there. ^job-39-30-douay

31 And the Lord went on, and said to Job: ^job-39-31-douay

32 Shall he that contendeth with God be so easily silenced? surely he that reproveth God, ought to answer him. ^job-39-32-douay

33 Then Job answered the Lord, and said: ^job-39-33-douay

34 What can I answer, who hath spoken inconsiderately? I will lay my hand upon my mouth. **Spoken inconsiderately...** If we discuss all Job's words (saith St. Gregory), we shall find nothing impiously spoken; as may be gathered from the words of the Lord himself ([[job-42-douay|Job 42:7-8]]); but what was reprehensible in him, was the manner of expressing himself at times, speaking too much of his own affliction, and too little of God's goodness towards him, which here he acknowledges as inconsiderate. ^job-39-34-douay

35 One thing I have spoken, which I wish I had not said: and another, to which I will add no more. ^job-39-35-douay
