# Job Chapter 25

God's providence often allows the wicked to go on a long time in their sins: but punishes them in another life.

1 Then Baldad the Suhite answered, and said: ^job-25-1-douay

2 Power and terror are with him, who maketh peace in his high places. ^job-25-2-douay

3 Is there any numbering of his soldiers? and upon whom shall not his light arise? ^job-25-3-douay

4 Can man be justified compared with God, or he that is born of a woman appear clean? ^job-25-4-douay

5 Behold even the moon doth not shine, and the stars are not pure in his sight. ^job-25-5-douay

6 How much less man that is rottenness and the son of man who is a worm? ^job-25-6-douay
