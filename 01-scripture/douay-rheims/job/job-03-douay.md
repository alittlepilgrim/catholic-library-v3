# Job Chapter 3

Job expresses his sense of the miseries of man's life, by cursing the day of his birth.

1 After this, Job opened his mouth, and cursed his day, **Cursed his day...** Job cursed the day of his birth, not by way of wishing evil to any thing of God's creation; but only to express in a stronger manner his sense of human miseries in general, and of his own calamities in particular. ^job-03-1-douay

2 And he said: ^job-03-2-douay

3 Let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night in which it was said: A man child is conceived. ^job-03-3-douay

4 Let that day be turned into darkness, let not God regard it from above, and let not the light shine upon it. ^job-03-4-douay

5 Let darkness, and the shadow of death, cover it, let a mist overspread it, and let it be wrapped up in bitterness. ^job-03-5-douay

6 Let a darksome whirlwind seize upon that night, let it not be counted in the days of the year, nor numbered in the months. ^job-03-6-douay

7 Let that night be solitary, and not worthy of praise. ^job-03-7-douay

8 Let them curse it who curse the day, who are ready to raise up a leviathan: ^job-03-8-douay

9 Let the stars be darkened with the mist thereof: let it expect light, and not see it, nor the rising of the dawning of the day: ^job-03-9-douay

10 Because it shut not up the doors of the womb that bore me, nor took away evils from my eyes. ^job-03-10-douay

11 Why did I not die in the womb? why did I not perish when I came out of the belly? ^job-03-11-douay

12 Why received upon the knees? why suckled at the breasts? ^job-03-12-douay

13 For now I should have been asleep and still, and should have rest in my sleep: ^job-03-13-douay

14 With kings and consuls of the earth, who build themselves solitudes: ^job-03-14-douay

15 Or with princes, that possess gold, and fill their houses with silver: ^job-03-15-douay

16 Or as a hidden untimely birth, I should not be; or as they that, being conceived, have not seen the light. ^job-03-16-douay

17 There the wicked cease from tumult, and there the wearied in strength are at rest. ^job-03-17-douay

18 And they sometime bound together without disquiet, have not heard the voice of the oppressor. ^job-03-18-douay

19 The small and great are there, and the servant is free from his master. ^job-03-19-douay

20 Why is light given to him that is in misery, and life to them that are in bitterness of soul? ^job-03-20-douay

21 That look for death, and it cometh not, as they that dig for a treasure: ^job-03-21-douay

22 And they rejoice exceedingly when they have found the grave? ^job-03-22-douay

23 To a man whose way is hidden, and God hath surrounded him with darkness? ^job-03-23-douay

24 Before I eat I sigh: and as overflowing waters, so is my roaring: ^job-03-24-douay

25 For the fear which I feared, hath come upon me: and that which I was afraid of, hath befallen me. ^job-03-25-douay

26 Have I not dissembled? have I not kept silence? have I not been quiet? and indignation is come upon me. ^job-03-26-douay
