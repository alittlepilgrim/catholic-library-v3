# Job Chapter 11

Sophar reproves Job, for justifying himself, and invites him to repentance.

1 Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said: ^job-11-1-douay

2 Shall not he that speaketh much, hear also? or shall a man full of talk be justified? ^job-11-2-douay

3 Shall men hold their peace to thee only? and when thou hast mocked others, shall no man confute thee? ^job-11-3-douay

4 For thou hast said: My word is pure, and I am clean in thy sight. ^job-11-4-douay

5 And I wish that God would speak with thee, and would open his lips to thee, ^job-11-5-douay

6 That he might show thee the secrets of wisdom, and that his law is manifold, and thou mightest understand that he exacteth much less of thee, than thy iniquity deserveth. ^job-11-6-douay

7 Peradventure thou wilt comprehend the steps of God, and wilt find out the Almighty perfectly? ^job-11-7-douay

8 He is higher than heaven, and what wilt thou do? he is deeper than hell, and how wilt thou know? ^job-11-8-douay

9 The measure of him is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea. ^job-11-9-douay

10 If he shall overturn all things, or shall press them together, who shall contradict him? ^job-11-10-douay

11 For he knoweth the vanity of men, and when he seeth iniquity, doth he not consider it? ^job-11-11-douay

12 A vain man is lifted up into pride, and thinketh himself born free like a wild ass' colt. ^job-11-12-douay

13 But thou hast hardened thy heart, and hast spread thy hands to him. ^job-11-13-douay

14 If thou wilt put away from thee the iniquity that is in thy hand, and let not injustice remain in thy tabernacle: ^job-11-14-douay

15 Then mayst thou lift up thy face without spot, and thou shalt be steadfast, and shalt not fear. ^job-11-15-douay

16 Thou shalt also forget misery, and remember it only as waters that are passed away. ^job-11-16-douay

17 And brightness like that of the noonday, shall arise to thee at evening: and when thou shalt think thyself consumed, thou shalt rise as the day star. ^job-11-17-douay

18 And thou shalt have confidence, hope being set before thee, and being buried thou shalt sleep secure. ^job-11-18-douay

19 Thou shalt rest, and there shall be none to make thee afraid: and many shall entreat thy face. ^job-11-19-douay

20 But the eyes of the wicked shall decay, and the way to escape shall fail them, and their hope the abomination of the soul. ^job-11-20-douay
