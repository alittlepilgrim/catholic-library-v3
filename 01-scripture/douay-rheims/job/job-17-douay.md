# Job Chapter 17

Job's hope in God: he expects rest in death.

1 My spirit shall be wasted, my days shall be shortened and only the grave remaineth for me. ^job-17-1-douay

2 I have not sinned, and my eye abideth in bitterness. **Not sinned...** That is, I am not guilty of such sins as they charge me with. ^job-17-2-douay

3 Deliver me, O Lord, and set me beside thee, and let any man's hand fight against me. ^job-17-3-douay

4 Thou hast set their heart far from understanding, therefore they shall not be exalted. ^job-17-4-douay

5 He promiseth a prey to his companions, and the eyes of his children shall fail. ^job-17-5-douay

6 He hath made me as it were a byword of the people, and I am an example before them. ^job-17-6-douay

7 My eye is dim through indignation, and my limbs are brought as it were to nothing. ^job-17-7-douay

8 The just shall be astonished at this, and the innocent shall be raised up against the hypocrite. ^job-17-8-douay

9 And the just man shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger. ^job-17-9-douay

10 Wherefore be you all converted, and come, and I shall not find among you any wise man. ^job-17-10-douay

11 My days have passed away, my thoughts are dissipated, tormenting my heart. ^job-17-11-douay

12 They have turned night into day, and after darkness I hope for light again. ^job-17-12-douay

13 If I wait hell is my house, and I have made my bed in darkness. **Hell...** Sheol. The region of the dead. ^job-17-13-douay

14 I have said to rottenness: Thou art my father; to worms, my mother and my sister. ^job-17-14-douay

15 Where is now then my expectation, and who considereth my patience? ^job-17-15-douay

16 All that I have shall go down into the deepest pit: thinkest thou that there at least I shall have rest? **Deepest pit...** Literally, hell. ^job-17-16-douay
