# Job Chapter 20

Sophar declares the shortness of the prosperity of the wicked: and their sudden downfall.

1 Then Sophar the Naamathite answered, and said: ^job-20-1-douay

2 Therefore various thoughts succeed one another in me, and my mind is hurried away to different things. ^job-20-2-douay

3 The doctrine with which thou reprovest me, I will hear, and the spirit of my understanding shall answer for me. ^job-20-3-douay

4 This I know from the beginning, since man was placed upon the earth, ^job-20-4-douay

5 That the praise of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment. ^job-20-5-douay

6 If his pride mount up even to heaven, and his head touch the clouds: ^job-20-6-douay

7 In the end he shall be destroyed like a dunghill, and they that had seen him, shall say: Where is he? ^job-20-7-douay

8 As a dream that fleeth away he shall not be found, he shall pass as a vision of the night: ^job-20-8-douay

9 The eyes that had seen him, shall see him no more, neither shall his place any more behold him. ^job-20-9-douay

10 His children shall be oppressed with want, and his hands shall render to him his sorrow. ^job-20-10-douay

11 His bones shall be filled with the vices of his youth, and they shall sleep with him in the dust. ^job-20-11-douay

12 For when evil shall be sweet in his mouth, he will hide it under his tongue. ^job-20-12-douay

13 He will spare it, and not leave it, and will hide it in his throat. ^job-20-13-douay

14 His bread in his belly shall be turned into the gall of asps within him, ^job-20-14-douay

15 The riches which he hath swallowed, he shall vomit up, and God shall draw them out of his belly. ^job-20-15-douay

16 He shall suck the head of asps, and the viper's tongue shall kill him. ^job-20-16-douay

17 Let him not see the streams of the river, the brooks of honey and of butter. ^job-20-17-douay

18 He shall be punished for all that he did, and yet shall not be consumed: according to the multitude of his devices so also shall he suffer. **According to the multitude of his devices...** That is, his stratagems to gratify his passions and to oppress and destroy the poor. ^job-20-18-douay

19 Because he broke in and stripped the poor: he hath violently taken away a house which he did not build. ^job-20-19-douay

20 And yet his belly was not filled: and when he hath the things he coveted, he shall not be able to possess them. ^job-20-20-douay

21 There was nothing left of his meat, and therefore nothing shall continue of his goods: ^job-20-21-douay

22 When he shall be filled, he shall be straitened, he shall burn, and every sorrow shall fall upon him. ^job-20-22-douay

23 May his belly be filled, that God may send forth the wrath of his indignation upon him, and rain down his war upon him. ^job-20-23-douay

24 He shall flee from weapons of iron, and shall fall upon a bow of brass. ^job-20-24-douay

25 The sword is drawn out, and cometh forth from its scabbard, and glittereth in his bitterness: the terrible ones shall go and come upon him. ^job-20-25-douay

26 All darkness is hid in his secret places: a fire that is not kindled shall devour him, he shall be afflicted when left in his tabernacle. ^job-20-26-douay

27 The heavens shall reveal his iniquity, and the earth shall rise up against him. ^job-20-27-douay

28 The offspring of his house shall be exposed, he shall be pulled down in the day of God's wrath. ^job-20-28-douay

29 This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the inheritance of his doings from the Lord. ^job-20-29-douay
