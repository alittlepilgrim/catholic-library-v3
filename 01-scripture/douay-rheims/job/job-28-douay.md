# Job Chapter 28

Man's industry searches out many things: true wisdom is taught by God alone.

1 Silver hath beginnings of its veins, and gold hath a place wherein it is melted. ^job-28-1-douay

2 Iron is taken out of the earth, and stone melted with heat is turned into brass. ^job-28-2-douay

3 He hath set a time for darkness, and the end of all things he considereth, the stone also that is in the dark and the shadow of death. ^job-28-3-douay

4 The flood divideth from the people that are on their journey, those whom the food of the needy man hath forgotten, and who cannot be come at. ^job-28-4-douay

5 The land, out of which bread grew in its place, hath been overturned with fire. ^job-28-5-douay

6 The stones of it are the place of sapphires, and the clods of it are gold. ^job-28-6-douay

7 The bird hath not known the path, neither hath the eye of the vulture beheld it. ^job-28-7-douay

8 The children of the merchants have not trodden it, neither hath the lioness passed by it. ^job-28-8-douay

9 He hath stretched forth his hand to the flint, he hath overturned mountains from the roots. ^job-28-9-douay

10 In the rocks he hath cut out rivers, and his eye hath seen every precious thing. ^job-28-10-douay

11 The depths also of rivers he hath searched, and hidden things he hath brought forth to light. ^job-28-11-douay

12 But where is wisdom to be found, and where is the place of understanding? ^job-28-12-douay

13 Man knoweth not the price thereof, neither is it found in the land of them that live in delights. ^job-28-13-douay

14 The depth saith: It is not in me: and the sea saith: It is not with me. ^job-28-14-douay

15 The finest gold shall not purchase it, neither shall silver be weighed in exchange for it. ^job-28-15-douay

16 It shall not be compared with the dyed colours of India, or with the most precious stone sardonyx, or the sapphire. ^job-28-16-douay

17 Gold or crystal cannot equal it, neither shall any vessels of gold be changed for it. ^job-28-17-douay

18 High and eminent things shall not be mentioned in comparison of it: but wisdom is drawn out of secret places. ^job-28-18-douay

19 The topaz of Ethiopia shall not be equal to it, neither shall it be compared to the cleanest dyeing. ^job-28-19-douay

20 Whence then cometh wisdom? and where is the place of understanding? ^job-28-20-douay

21 It is hid from the eyes of all living, and the fowls of the air know it not. ^job-28-21-douay

22 Destruction and death have said: With our ears we have heard the fame thereof. ^job-28-22-douay

23 God understandeth the way of it, and he knoweth the place thereof. ^job-28-23-douay

24 For he beholdeth the ends of the world: and looketh on all things that are under heaven. ^job-28-24-douay

25 Who made a weight for the winds, and weighed the waters by measure. ^job-28-25-douay

26 When he gave a law for the rain, and a way for the sounding storms. ^job-28-26-douay

27 Then he saw it, and declared, and prepared, and searched it. ^job-28-27-douay

28 And he said to man: Behold the fear of the Lord, that is wisdom: and to depart from evil, is understanding. ^job-28-28-douay
