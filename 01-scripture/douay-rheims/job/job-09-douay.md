# Job Chapter 9

Job acknowledges God's justice: although he often afflicts the innocent.

1 And Job answered, and said: ^job-09-1-douay

2 Indeed I know it is so, and that man cannot be justified, compared with God. ^job-09-2-douay

3 If he will contend with him, he cannot answer him one for a thousand. ^job-09-3-douay

4 He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength: who hath resisted him, and hath had peace? ^job-09-4-douay

5 Who hath removed mountains, and they whom he overthrew in his wrath, knew it not. ^job-09-5-douay

6 Who shaketh the earth out of her place, and the pillars thereof tremble. ^job-09-6-douay

7 Who commandeth the sun, and it riseth not: and shutteth up the stars, as it were, under a seal: ^job-09-7-douay

8 Who alone spreadeth out the heavens, and walketh upon the waves of the sea, ^job-09-8-douay

9 Who maketh Arcturus, and Orion, and Hyades, and the inner parts of the south. **Arcturus, etc...** These are names of stars or constellations. In Hebrew, Ash, Cesil, and Cimah. See note on [[job-38-douay|Job 38:31]]. ^job-09-9-douay

10 Who doth things great and incomprehensible, and wonderful, of which there is no number. ^job-09-10-douay

11 If he come to me, I shall not see him: if he depart, I shall not understand. ^job-09-11-douay

12 If he examine on a sudden, who shall answer him? or who can say: Why dost thou so? ^job-09-12-douay

13 God, whose wrath no man can resist, and under whom they stoop that bear up the world. ^job-09-13-douay

14 What am I then, that I should answer him, and have words with him? ^job-09-14-douay

15 I, who although I should have any just thing, would not answer, but would make supplication to my judge. ^job-09-15-douay

16 And if he should hear me when I call, I should not believe that he had heard my voice. ^job-09-16-douay

17 For he shall crush me in a whirlwind, and multiply my wounds even without cause. **Without cause...** That is, without my knowing the cause: or without any crime of mine. ^job-09-17-douay

18 He alloweth not my spirit to rest, and he filleth me with bitterness. ^job-09-18-douay

19 If strength be demanded, he is most strong: if equity of judgment, no man dare bear witness for me. ^job-09-19-douay

20 If I would justify myself, my own mouth shall condemn me: if I would show myself innocent, he shall prove me wicked. ^job-09-20-douay

21 Although I should be simple, even this my soul shall be ignorant of, and I shall be weary of my life. ^job-09-21-douay

22 One thing there is that I have spoken, both the innocent and the wicked he consumeth. ^job-09-22-douay

23 If he scourge, let him kill at once, and not laugh at the pains of the innocent. ^job-09-23-douay

24 The earth is given into the hand of the wicked, he covereth the face of the judges thereof: and if it be not he, who is it then? ^job-09-24-douay

25 My days have been swifter than a post: they have fled away and have not seen good. ^job-09-25-douay

26 They have passed by as ships carrying fruits, as an eagle flying to the prey. ^job-09-26-douay

27 If I say: I will not speak so: I change my face, and am tormented with sorrow. ^job-09-27-douay

28 I feared all my works, knowing that thou didst not spare the offender. ^job-09-28-douay

29 But if so also I am wicked, why have I laboured in vain? ^job-09-29-douay

30 If I be washed, as it were, with snow waters, and my hands shall shine ever clean: ^job-09-30-douay

31 Yet thou shalt plunge me in filth, and my garments shall abhor me. ^job-09-31-douay

32 For I shall not answer a man that is like myself: nor one that may be heard with me equally in judgment. ^job-09-32-douay

33 There is none that may be able to reprove both, and to put his hand between both. ^job-09-33-douay

34 Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his fear terrify me. ^job-09-34-douay

35 I will speak, and will not fear him: for I cannot answer while I am in fear. ^job-09-35-douay
