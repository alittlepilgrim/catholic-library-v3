# Job Chapter 41

A further description of the leviathan.

1 I will not stir him up, like one that is cruel, for who can resist my countenance? ^job-41-1-douay

2 Who hath given me before that I should repay him? All things that are under heaven are mine. ^job-41-2-douay

3 I will not spare him, nor his mighty words, and framed to make supplication. ^job-41-3-douay

4 Who can discover the face of his garment? or who can go into the midst of his mouth? ^job-41-4-douay

5 Who can open the doors of his face? his teeth are terrible round about. ^job-41-5-douay

6 His body is like molten shields, shut close up with scales pressing upon one another. ^job-41-6-douay

7 One is joined to another, and not so much as any air can come between them: ^job-41-7-douay

8 They stick one to another and they hold one another fast, and shall not be separated. ^job-41-8-douay

9 His sneezing is like the shining of fire, and his eyes like the eyelids of the morning. ^job-41-9-douay

10 Out of his mouth go forth lamps, like torches of lighted fire. ^job-41-10-douay

11 Out of his nostrils goeth smoke, like that of a pot heated and boiling. ^job-41-11-douay

12 His breath kindleth coals, and a flame cometh forth out of his mouth. ^job-41-12-douay

13 In his neck strength shall dwell, and want goeth before his face. ^job-41-13-douay

14 The members of his flesh cleave one to another: he shall send lightnings against him, and they shall not be carried to another place. ^job-41-14-douay

15 His heart shall be as hard as a stone, and as firm as a smith's anvil, ^job-41-15-douay

16 When he shall raise him up, the angels shall fear, and being affrighted shall purify themselves. **Angels...** Elim, Hebrew: which signifies here, the mighty, the most valiant, shall fear this monstrous fish, and in their fear shall seek to be purified. ^job-41-16-douay

17 When a sword shall lay at him, it shall not be able to hold, nor a spear, nor a breastplate. ^job-41-17-douay

18 For he shall esteem iron as straw, and brass as rotten wood. ^job-41-18-douay

19 The archer shall not put him to flight, the stones of the sling are to him like stubble. ^job-41-19-douay

20 As stubble will he esteem the hammer, and he will laugh him to scorn who shaketh the spear. ^job-41-20-douay

21 The beams of the sun shall be under him, and he shall strew gold under him like mire. **Under him...** He shall not value the beams of the sun; and gold to him shall be like mire. ^job-41-21-douay

22 He shall make the deep sea to boil like a pot, and shall make it as when ointments boil. ^job-41-22-douay

23 A path shall shine after him, he shall esteem the deep as growing old. **The deep as growing old...** Growing hoary, as it were with the froth which he leaves behind him. ^job-41-23-douay

24 There is no power upon earth that can be compared with him who was made to fear no one, ^job-41-24-douay

25 He beholdeth every high thing, he is king over all the children of pride. **He is king, etc...** He is superior in strength to all that are great and strong amongst living creatures: mystically it is understood of the devil, who is king over all the proud. ^job-41-25-douay
