# Job Chapter 38

God interposes and shows from the things he has made, that man cannot comprehend his power and wisdom.

1 Then the Lord answered Job out of a whirlwind, and said: **The Lord...** That is, an angel speaking in the name of the Lord. ^job-38-1-douay

2 Who is this that wrappeth up sentences in unskilful words? ^job-38-2-douay

3 Gird up thy loins like a man: I will ask thee, and answer thou me. ^job-38-3-douay

4 Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? tell me if thou hast understanding. ^job-38-4-douay

5 Who hath laid the measures thereof, if thou knowest or who hath stretched the line upon it? ^job-38-5-douay

6 Upon what are its bases grounded? or who laid the corner stone thereof, ^job-38-6-douay

7 When the morning stars praised me together, and all the sons of God made a joyful melody? ^job-38-7-douay

8 Who shut up the sea with doors, when it broke forth as issuing out of the womb: ^job-38-8-douay

9 When I made a cloud the garment thereof, and wrapped it in a mist as in swaddling bands? ^job-38-9-douay

10 I set my bounds around it, and made it bars and doors: ^job-38-10-douay

11 And I said: Hitherto thou shalt come, and shalt go no further, and here thou shalt break thy swelling waves. ^job-38-11-douay

12 Didst thou since thy birth command the morning, and show the dawning of the day its place? ^job-38-12-douay

13 And didst thou hold the extremities of the earth shaking them, and hast thou shaken the ungodly out of it? ^job-38-13-douay

14 The seal shall be restored as clay, and shall stand as a garment. ^job-38-14-douay

15 From the wicked their light shall be taken away, and the high arm shall be broken. ^job-38-15-douay

16 Hast thou entered into the depths of the sea, and walked in the lowest parts of the deep? ^job-38-16-douay

17 Have the gates of death been opened to thee, and hast thou seen the darksome doors? ^job-38-17-douay

18 Hast thou considered the breadth of the earth? tell me, if thou knowest all things? ^job-38-18-douay

19 Where is the way where light dwelleth, and where is the place of darkness? ^job-38-19-douay

20 That thou mayst bring every thing to its own bounds, and understand the paths of the house thereof. ^job-38-20-douay

21 Didst thou know then that thou shouldst be born? and didst thou know the number of thy days? ^job-38-21-douay

22 Hast thou entered into the storehouses of the snow, or hast thou beheld the treasures of the hail: ^job-38-22-douay

23 Which I have prepared for the time of the enemy, against the day of battle and war? ^job-38-23-douay

24 By what way is the light spread, and heat divided upon the earth? ^job-38-24-douay

25 Who gave a course to violent showers, or a way for noisy thunder: ^job-38-25-douay

26 That it should rain on the earth without man in the wilderness, where no mortal dwelleth: ^job-38-26-douay

27 That it should fill the desert and desolate land, and should bring forth green grass? ^job-38-27-douay

28 Who is the father of rain? or who begot the drops of dew? ^job-38-28-douay

29 Out of whose womb came the ice? and the frost from heaven who hath gendered it? ^job-38-29-douay

30 The waters are hardened like a stone, and the surface of the deep is congealed. ^job-38-30-douay

31 Shalt thou be able to join together the shining stars the Pleiades, or canst thou stop the turning about of Arcturus? **Pleiades...** Hebrew, *Cimah*. A cluster of seven stars in the constellation Taurus or the Bull. Arcturus, a bright star in the constellation Bootes. The Hebrew name *Cesil*, is variously interpreted; by some, Orion; by others, the Great Bear is understood. ^job-38-31-douay

32 Canst thou bring forth the day star in its time, and make the evening star to rise upon the children of the earth? ^job-38-32-douay

33 Dost thou know the order of heaven, and canst thou set down the reason thereof on the earth? ^job-38-33-douay

34 Canst thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, that an abundance of waters may cover thee? ^job-38-34-douay

35 Canst thou send lightnings, and will they go, and will they return and say to thee: Here we are? ^job-38-35-douay

36 Who hath put wisdom in the heart of man? or who gave the cock understanding? **Understanding...** That instinct by which he distinguishes the times of crowing in the night. ^job-38-36-douay

37 Who can declare the order of the heavens, or who can make the harmony of heaven to sleep? ^job-38-37-douay

38 When was the dust poured on the earth, and the clods fastened together? ^job-38-38-douay

39 Wilt thou take the prey for the lioness, and satisfy the appetite of her whelps, ^job-38-39-douay

40 When they couch in the dens and lie in wait in holes? ^job-38-40-douay

41 Who provideth food for the raven, when her young ones cry to God, wandering about, because they have no meat? ^job-38-41-douay
