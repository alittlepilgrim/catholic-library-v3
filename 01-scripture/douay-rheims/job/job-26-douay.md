# Job Chapter 26

Job declares his sentiments of the wisdom and power of God.

1 Then Job answered, and said: ^job-26-1-douay

2 Whose helper art thou? is it of him that is weak? and dost thou hold up the arm of him that has no strength? ^job-26-2-douay

3 To whom hast thou given counsel? perhaps to him that hath no wisdom, and thou hast shown thy very great prudence. ^job-26-3-douay

4 Whom hast thou desired to teach? was it not him that made life? ^job-26-4-douay

5 Behold the giants groan under the waters, and they that dwell with them. ^job-26-5-douay

6 Hell is naked before him, and there is no covering for destruction. ^job-26-6-douay

7 He stretched out the north over the empty space, and hangeth the earth upon nothing. ^job-26-7-douay

8 He bindeth up the waters in his clouds, so that they break not out and fall down together. ^job-26-8-douay

9 He withholdeth the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud over it. ^job-26-9-douay

10 He hath set bounds about the waters, till light and darkness come to an end. ^job-26-10-douay

11 The pillars of heaven tremble, and dread at his beck. ^job-26-11-douay

12 By his power the seas are suddenly gathered together, and his wisdom has struck the proud one. ^job-26-12-douay

13 His spirit hath adorned the heavens, and his obstetric hand brought forth the winding serpent. **His obstetric hand brought forth the winding serpent...** That is, the omnipotent power of God: which brought forth all things created in time, but conceived in the Divine mind from all eternity. **The winding serpent,** a constellation of fixed stars winding round the north pole, called Draco. This appears from the foregoing part of the same verse, *His spirit hath adorned the heavens*. ^job-26-13-douay

14 Lo, these things are said in part of his ways: and seeing we have heard scarce a little drop of his word, who shall be able to behold the thunder of his greatness? ^job-26-14-douay
