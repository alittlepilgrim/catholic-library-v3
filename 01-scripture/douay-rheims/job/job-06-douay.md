# Job Chapter 6

Job maintains his innocence, and complains of his friends.

1 But Job answered, and said: ^job-06-1-douay

2 O that my sins, whereby I have deserved wrath, and the calamity that I suffer, were weighed in a balance. **My sins, etc...** He does not mean to compare his sufferings with his real sins: but with the imaginary crimes which his friends imputed to him: and especially with his wrath, or grief, expressed in the third chapter, which they so much accused. Though, as he tells them here, it bore no proportion with the greatness of his calamity. ^job-06-2-douay

3 As the sand of the sea, this would appear heavier: therefore, my words are full of sorrow: ^job-06-3-douay

4 For the arrows of the Lord are in me, the rage whereof drinketh up my spirit, and the terrors of the Lord war against me. ^job-06-4-douay

5 Will the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or will the ox low when he standeth before a full manger? ^job-06-5-douay

6 Or can an unsavoury thing be eaten, that is not seasoned with salt? or can a man taste that which, when tasted, bringeth death? ^job-06-6-douay

7 The things which before my soul would not touch, now, through anguish, are my meats. ^job-06-7-douay

8 Who will grant that my request may come: and that God may give me what I look for? ^job-06-8-douay

9 And that he that hath begun may destroy me, that he may let loose his hand, and cut me off? ^job-06-9-douay

10 And that this may be my comfort, that afflicting me with sorrow, he spare not, nor I contradict the words of the Holy one. ^job-06-10-douay

11 For what is my strength, that I can hold out? or what is my end, that I should keep patience? ^job-06-11-douay

12 My strength is not the strength of stones, nor is my flesh of brass. ^job-06-12-douay

13 Behold there is no help for me in myself, and my familiar friends also are departed from me. ^job-06-13-douay

14 He that taketh away mercy from his friend, forsaketh the fear of the Lord. ^job-06-14-douay

15 My brethren have passed by me, as the torrent that passeth swiftly in the valleys. ^job-06-15-douay

16 They that fear the hoary frost, the snow shall fall upon them. ^job-06-16-douay

17 At the time when they shall be scattered they shall perish: and after it groweth hot, they shall be melted out of their place. ^job-06-17-douay

18 The paths of their steps are entangled: they shall walk in vain, and shall perish. ^job-06-18-douay

19 Consider the paths of Thema, the ways of Saba, and wait a little while. ^job-06-19-douay

20 They arc confounded, because I have hoped: they are come also even unto me, and are covered with shame. ^job-06-20-douay

21 Now you are come: and now, seeing my affliction, you are afraid. ^job-06-21-douay

22 Did I say: Bring to me, and give me of your substance? ^job-06-22-douay

23 Or deliver me from the hand of the enemy, and rescue me out of the hand of the mighty? ^job-06-23-douay

24 Teach me, and I will hold my peace: and if I have been ignorant of any thing, instruct me. ^job-06-24-douay

25 Why have you detracted the words of truth, whereas there is none of you that can reprove me? ^job-06-25-douay

26 You dress up speeches only to rebuke, and you utter words to the wind. ^job-06-26-douay

27 You rush in upon the fatherless, and you endeavour to overthrow your friend. ^job-06-27-douay

28 However, finish what you have begun: give ear and see whether I lie. ^job-06-28-douay

29 Answer, I beseech you, without contention: and speaking that which is just, judge ye. ^job-06-29-douay

30 And you shall not find iniquity in my tongue, neither shall folly sound in my mouth. ^job-06-30-douay
