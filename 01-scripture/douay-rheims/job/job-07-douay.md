# Job Chapter 7

Job declares the miseries of man's life: and addresses himself to God.

1 The life of man upon earth is a warfare, and his days are like the days of a hireling. ^job-07-1-douay

2 As a servant longeth for the shade, as the hireling looketh for the end of his work; ^job-07-2-douay

3 So I also have had empty months, and have numbered to myself wearisome nights. ^job-07-3-douay

4 If I lie down to sleep, I shall say: When shall I rise? and again, I shall look for the evening, and shall be filled with sorrows even till darkness. ^job-07-4-douay

5 My flesh is clothed with rottenness and the filth of dust; my skin is withered and drawn together. ^job-07-5-douay

6 My days have passed more swiftly than the web is cut by the weaver, and are consumed without any hope. ^job-07-6-douay

7 Remember that my life is but wind, and my eye shall not return to see good things. ^job-07-7-douay

8 Nor shall the sight of man behold me: thy eyes are upon me, and I shall be no more. ^job-07-8-douay

9 As a cloud is consumed, and passeth away: so he that shall go down to hell shall not come up. ^job-07-9-douay

10 Nor shall he return any more into his house, neither shall his place know him any more. ^job-07-10-douay

11 Wherefore, I will not spare my month, I will speak in the affliction of my spirit: I will talk with the bitterness of my soul. ^job-07-11-douay

12 Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou hast inclosed me in a prison? ^job-07-12-douay

13 If I say: My bed shall comfort me, and I shall be relieved, speaking with myself on my couch: ^job-07-13-douay

14 Thou wilt frighten me with dreams, and terrify me with visions. ^job-07-14-douay

15 So that my soul rather chooseth hanging, and my bones death. ^job-07-15-douay

16 I have done with hope, I shall now live no longer: spare me, for my days are nothing. ^job-07-16-douay

17 What is a man, that thou shouldst magnify him or why dost thou set thy heart upon him? ^job-07-17-douay

18 Thou visitest him early in the morning, and thou provest him suddenly. ^job-07-18-douay

19 How long wilt thou not spare me, nor suffer me to swallow down my spittle? ^job-07-19-douay

20 I have sinned: what shall I do to thee, O keeper of men? why hast thou set me opposite to thee and am I become burdensome to myself? ^job-07-20-douay

21 Why dost thou not remove my sin, and why dost thou not take away my iniquity? Behold now I shall sleep in the dust: and if thou seek me in the morning, I shall not be. ^job-07-21-douay
