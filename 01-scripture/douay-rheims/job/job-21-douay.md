# Job Chapter 21

Job shows that the wicked often prosper in this world, even to the end of their life: but that their judgment is in another world.

1 Then Job answered, and said: ^job-21-1-douay

2 Hear, I beseech you, my words, and do penance. ^job-21-2-douay

3 Suffer me, and I will speak, and after, if you please, laugh at my words. ^job-21-3-douay

4 Is my debate against man, that I should not have just reason to be troubled? ^job-21-4-douay

5 Hearken to me and be astonished, and lay your finger on your mouth. ^job-21-5-douay

6 As for me, when I remember, I am afraid, and trembling taketh hold on my flesh. ^job-21-6-douay

7 Why then do the wicked live, are they advanced, and strengthened with riches? ^job-21-7-douay

8 Their seed continueth before them, a multitude of kinsmen, and of children's children in their sight. ^job-21-8-douay

9 Their houses are secure and peaceable, and the rod of God is not upon them. ^job-21-9-douay

10 Their cattle have conceived, and failed not: their cow has calved, and is not deprived of her fruit. ^job-21-10-douay

11 Their little ones go out like a flock, and their children dance and play. ^job-21-11-douay

12 They take the timbrel, and the harp, and rejoice at the sound of the organ. ^job-21-12-douay

13 They spend their days in wealth, and in a moment they go down to hell. ^job-21-13-douay

14 Who have said to God: Depart from us, we desire not the knowledge of thy ways. ^job-21-14-douay

15 Who is the Almighty, that we should serve him? and what doth it profit us if we pray to him? ^job-21-15-douay

16 Yet because their good things are not in their hand, may the counsel of the wicked be far from me. ^job-21-16-douay

17 How often shall the lamp of the wicked be put out, and a deluge come upon them, and he shall distribute the sorrows of his wrath? ^job-21-17-douay

18 They shall be as chaff before the face of the wind, and as ashes which the whirlwind scattereth. ^job-21-18-douay

19 God shall lay up the sorrow of the father for his children: and when he shall repay, then shall he know. ^job-21-19-douay

20 His eyes shall see his own destruction, and he shall drink of the wrath of the Almighty. ^job-21-20-douay

21 For what is it to him what befalleth his house after him: and if the number of his months be diminished by one half? ^job-21-21-douay

22 Shall any one teach God knowledge, who judgeth those that are high? ^job-21-22-douay

23 One man dieth strong, and hale, rich and happy. ^job-21-23-douay

24 His bowels are full of fat, and his bones are moistened with marrow. ^job-21-24-douay

25 But another dieth in bitterness of soul without any riches: ^job-21-25-douay

26 And yet they shall sleep together in the dust, and worms shall cover them. ^job-21-26-douay

27 Surely I know your thoughts, and your unjust judgments against me. ^job-21-27-douay

28 For you say: Where is the house of the prince? and where are the dwelling places of the wicked? ^job-21-28-douay

29 Ask any one of them that go by the way, and you shall perceive that he knoweth these same things. ^job-21-29-douay

30 Because the wicked man is reserved to the day of destruction, and he shall be brought to the day of wrath. ^job-21-30-douay

31 Who shall reprove his way to his face? and who shall repay him what he hath done? ^job-21-31-douay

32 He shall be brought to the graves, and shall watch in the heap of the dead. ^job-21-32-douay

33 He hath been acceptable to the gravel of Cocytus, and he shall draw every man after him, and there are innumerable before him. **Acceptable to the gravel of Cocytus...** The Hebrew word, which St. Jerome has here rendered by the name Cocytus, (which the poets represent as a river in hell), signifies a valley or a torrent: and in this place, is taken for the low region of death and hell: which willingly, as it were, receives the wicked at their death: who are ushered in by innumerable others that have gone before them; and are followed by multitudes above number. ^job-21-33-douay

34 How then do ye comfort me in vain, whereas your answer is shown to be repugnant to truth? ^job-21-34-douay
