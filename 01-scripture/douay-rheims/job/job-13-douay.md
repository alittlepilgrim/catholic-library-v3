# Job Chapter 13

Job persists in maintaining his innocence: and reproves his friends.

1 Behold my eye hath seen all these things, and my ear hath heard them, and I have understood them all. ^job-13-1-douay

2 According to your knowledge I also know: neither am I inferior to you. ^job-13-2-douay

3 But yet I will speak to the Almighty, and I desire to reason with God. ^job-13-3-douay

4 Having first shown that you are forgers of lies, and maintainers of perverse opinions. ^job-13-4-douay

5 And I wish you would hold your peace, that you might be thought to be wise men. ^job-13-5-douay

6 Hear ye therefore my reproof, and attend to the judgment of my lips. ^job-13-6-douay

7 Hath God any need of your lie, that you should speak deceitfully for him? ^job-13-7-douay

8 Do you accept this person, and do you endeavour to judge for God? ^job-13-8-douay

9 Or shall it please him, from whom nothing can be concealed? or shall he be deceived as a man, with your deceitful dealings? ^job-13-9-douay

10 He shall reprove you, because in secret you accept his person. ^job-13-10-douay

11 As soon as he shall move himself, he shall trouble you: and his dread shall fall upon you. ^job-13-11-douay

12 Your remembrance shall be compared to ashes, and your necks shall be brought to clay. ^job-13-12-douay

13 Hold your peace a little while, that I may speak whatsoever my mind shall suggest to me. ^job-13-13-douay

14 Why do I tear my flesh with my teeth, and carry my soul in my hands? ^job-13-14-douay

15 Although he should kill me, I will trust in him: but yet I will reprove my ways in his sight. ^job-13-15-douay

16 And he shall be my saviour: for no hypocrite shall come before his presence. ^job-13-16-douay

17 Hear ye my speech, and receive with your ears hidden truths. ^job-13-17-douay

18 If I shall be judged, I know that I shall be found just. ^job-13-18-douay

19 Who is he that will plead against me? let him come: why am I consumed holding my peace? ^job-13-19-douay

20 Two things only do not to me, and then from thy face I shall not be hid: ^job-13-20-douay

21 Withdraw thy hand far from me, and let not thy dread terrify me. ^job-13-21-douay

22 Call me, and I will answer thee: or else I will speak, and do thou answer me. ^job-13-22-douay

23 How many are my iniquities and sins? make me know my crimes and offenses. ^job-13-23-douay

24 Why hidest thou thy face, and thinkest me thy enemy? ^job-13-24-douay

25 Against a leaf, that is carried away with the wind, thou showest thy power, and thou pursuest a dry straw. ^job-13-25-douay

26 For thou writest bitter things against me, and wilt consume me for the sins of my youth. ^job-13-26-douay

27 Thou hast put my feet in the stocks, and hast observed all my paths, and hast considered the steps of my feet: ^job-13-27-douay

28 Who am to be consumed as rottenness, and as a garment that is motheaten. ^job-13-28-douay
