# Ephesians Chapter 5

Exhortations to a virtuous life. The mutual duties of man and wife, by the example of Christ and of the Church.

1 Be ye therefore followers of God, as most dear children: ^ephesians-05-1-douay

2 And walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us and hath delivered himself for us, an oblation and a sacrifice to God for an odour of sweetness. ^ephesians-05-2-douay

3 But fornication and all uncleanness or covetousness, let it not so much as be named among you, as becometh saints: ^ephesians-05-3-douay

4 Or obscenity or foolish talking or scurrility, which is to no purpose: but rather giving of thanks. ^ephesians-05-4-douay

5 For know you this and understand: That no fornicator or unclean or covetous person (which is a serving of idols) hath inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God. ^ephesians-05-5-douay

6 Let no man deceive you with vain words. For because of these things cometh the anger of God upon the children of unbelief. ^ephesians-05-6-douay

7 Be ye not therefore partakers with them. ^ephesians-05-7-douay

8 For you were heretofore darkness, but now light in the Lord. Walk then as children of the light. ^ephesians-05-8-douay

9 For the fruit of the light is in all goodness and justice and truth: ^ephesians-05-9-douay

10 Proving what is well pleasing to God. ^ephesians-05-10-douay

11 And have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness: but rather reprove them. ^ephesians-05-11-douay

12 For the things that are done by them in secret, it is a shame even to speak of. ^ephesians-05-12-douay

13 But all things that are reproved are made manifest by the light: for all that is made manifest is light. ^ephesians-05-13-douay

14 Wherefore he saith: Rise, thou that sleepest, and arise from the dead: and Christ shall enlighten thee. ^ephesians-05-14-douay

15 See therefore, brethren, how you walk circumspectly: not as unwise, ^ephesians-05-15-douay

16 But as wise: redeeming the time, because the days are evil. ^ephesians-05-16-douay

17 Wherefore, become not unwise: but understanding what is the will of God. ^ephesians-05-17-douay

18 And be not drunk with wine, wherein is luxury: but be ye filled with the Holy Spirit, ^ephesians-05-18-douay

19 Speaking to yourselves in psalms and hymns and spiritual canticles, singing and making melody in your hearts to the Lord: ^ephesians-05-19-douay

20 Giving thanks always for all things, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, to God and the Father: ^ephesians-05-20-douay

21 Being subject one to another, in the fear of Christ. ^ephesians-05-21-douay

22 Let women be subject to their husbands, as to the Lord: ^ephesians-05-22-douay

23 Because the husband is the head of the wife, as Christ is the head of the church. He is the saviour of his body. ^ephesians-05-23-douay

24 Therefore as the church is subject to Christ: so also let the wives be to their husbands in all things. **As the church is subject to Christ...** The church then, according to St. Paul, is ever obedient to Christ, and can never fall from him, but remain faithful to him, unspotted and unchanged to the end of the world. ^ephesians-05-24-douay

25 Husbands, love your wives, as Christ also loved the church and delivered himself up for it: ^ephesians-05-25-douay

26 That he might sanctify it, cleansing it by the laver of water in the word of life: ^ephesians-05-26-douay

27 That he might present it to himself, a glorious church, not having spot or wrinkle or any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish. ^ephesians-05-27-douay

28 So also ought men to love their wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his wife loveth himself. ^ephesians-05-28-douay

29 For no man ever hated his own flesh, but nourisheth and cherisheth it, as also Christ doth the church: ^ephesians-05-29-douay

30 Because we are members of him, body, of his flesh and of his bones. ^ephesians-05-30-douay

31 For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother: and shall cleave to his wife. And they shall be two in one flesh. ^ephesians-05-31-douay

32 This is a great sacrament: but I speak in Christ and in the church. ^ephesians-05-32-douay

33 Nevertheless, let every one of you in particular love for his wife as himself: And let the wife fear her husband. ^ephesians-05-33-douay
