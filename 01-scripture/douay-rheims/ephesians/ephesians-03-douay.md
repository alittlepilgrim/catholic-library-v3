# Ephesians Chapter 3

The mystery hidden from former ages was discovered to the apostle, to be imparted to the Gentiles. He prays that they may be strengthened in God.

1 For this cause, I Paul, the prisoner of Jesus Christ, for you Gentiles: ^ephesians-03-1-douay

2 If yet you have heard of the dispensation of the grace of God which is given me towards you: ^ephesians-03-2-douay

3 How that, according to revelation, the mystery has been made known to me, as I have written above in a few words: ^ephesians-03-3-douay

4 As you reading, may understand my knowledge in the mystery of Christ, ^ephesians-03-4-douay

5 Which in other generations was not known to the sons of men, as it is now revealed to his holy apostles and prophets in the Spirit: ^ephesians-03-5-douay

6 That the Gentiles should be fellow heirs and of the same body: and copartners of his promise in Christ Jesus, by the gospel ^ephesians-03-6-douay

7 Of which I am made a minister, according to the gift of the grace of God, which is given to me according to the operation of his power. ^ephesians-03-7-douay

8 To me, the least of all the saints, is given this grace, to preach among the Gentiles the unsearchable riches of Christ: ^ephesians-03-8-douay

9 And to enlighten all men, that they may see what is the dispensation of the mystery which hath been hidden from eternity in God who created all things: ^ephesians-03-9-douay

10 That the manifold wisdom of God may be made known to the principalities and powers in heavenly places through the church, ^ephesians-03-10-douay

11 According to the eternal purpose which he made in Christ Jesus our Lord: ^ephesians-03-11-douay

12 In whom we have boldness and access with confidence by the faith of him. ^ephesians-03-12-douay

13 Wherefore I pray you not to faint at my tribulations for you, which is your glory. ^ephesians-03-13-douay

14 For this cause I bow my knees to the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, ^ephesians-03-14-douay

15 Of whom all paternity in heaven and earth is named: **All paternity...** Or, the whole family. God is the Father, both of angels and men; whosoever besides is named father, is so named with subordination to him. ^ephesians-03-15-douay

16 That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened by his Spirit with might unto the inward man: ^ephesians-03-16-douay

17 That Christ may dwell by faith in your hearts: that, being rooted and founded in charity, ^ephesians-03-17-douay

18 You may be able to comprehend, with all the saints, what is the breadth and length and height and depth, ^ephesians-03-18-douay

19 To know also the charity of Christ, which surpasseth all knowledge: that you may be filled unto all the fulness of God. ^ephesians-03-19-douay

20 Now to him who is able to do all things more abundantly than we desire or understand, according to the power that worketh in us: ^ephesians-03-20-douay

21 To him be glory in the church and in Christ Jesus, unto all generations, world without end. Amen. ^ephesians-03-21-douay
