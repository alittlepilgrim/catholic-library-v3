# 1 Maccabees Chapter 1

The reign of Alexander and his successors: Antiochus rifles and profanes the temple of God: and persecutes unto death all that will not forsake the law of God, and the religion of their fathers.

1 Now it came to pass, after that Alexander the son of Philip the Macedonian, who first reigned in Greece, coming out of the land of Cethim, had overthrown Darius, king of the Persians and Medes: ^1-maccabees-01-1-douay

2 He fought many battles, and took the strong holds of all, and slew the kings of the earth: ^1-maccabees-01-2-douay

3 And he went through even to the ends of the earth: and took the spoils of many nations: and the earth was quiet before him. ^1-maccabees-01-3-douay

4 And he gathered a power, and a very strong army: and his heart was exalted and lifted up: ^1-maccabees-01-4-douay

5 And he subdued countries of nations, and princes; and they became tributaries to him. ^1-maccabees-01-5-douay

6 And after these things, he fell down upon his bed, and knew that he should die. ^1-maccabees-01-6-douay

7 And he called his servants, the nobles that were brought up with him from his youth: and he divided his kingdom among them, while he was yet alive. **Divided his kingdom, etc...** This is otherwise related by Q. Curtius; though he acknowledges that divers were of that opinion, and that it had been delivered by some authors, lib. 10. But here we find from the sacred text, that he was in error. ^1-maccabees-01-7-douay

8 And Alexander reigned twelve years, and he died. ^1-maccabees-01-8-douay

9 And his servants made themselves kings, every one in his place: ^1-maccabees-01-9-douay

10 And they all put crowns upon themselves after his death, and their sons after them, many years; and evils were multiplied in the earth. ^1-maccabees-01-10-douay

11 And there came out of them a wicked root, Antiochus the Illustrious, the son of king Antiochus, who had been a hostage at Rome: and he reigned in the hundred and thirty-seventh year of the kingdom of the Greeks. **Antiochus the Illustrius...** Epiphanes, the younger son of Antiochus the Great, who usurped the kingdom, to the prejudice of his nephew Demetrius, son of his elder brother Seleucus Philopater. -- Ibid. **Of the kingdom of the Greeks...** Counting, not from the beginning of the reign of Alexander, but from the first year of Seleucus Nicator. ^1-maccabees-01-11-douay

12 In those days there went out of Israel wicked men, and they persuaded many, saying: Let us go and make a covenant with the heathens that are round about us: for since we departed from them, many evils have befallen us. ^1-maccabees-01-12-douay

13 And the word seemed good in their eyes. ^1-maccabees-01-13-douay

14 And some of the people determined to do this, and went to the king: and he gave them license to do after the ordinances of the heathens. ^1-maccabees-01-14-douay

15 And they built a place of exercise in Jerusalem, according to the laws of the nations: ^1-maccabees-01-15-douay

16 And they made themselves prepuces, and departed from the holy covenant, and joined themselves to the heathens, and were sold to do evil: ^1-maccabees-01-16-douay

17 And the kingdom was established before Antiochus, and he had a mind to reign over the land of Egypt, that he might reign over two kingdoms. ^1-maccabees-01-17-douay

18 And he entered into Egypt with a great multitude, with chariots, and elephants, and horsemen, and a great number of ships: ^1-maccabees-01-18-douay

19 And he made war against Ptolemee king of Egypt; but Ptolemee was afraid at his presence and fled, and many were wounded unto death. ^1-maccabees-01-19-douay

20 And he took the strong cities in the land of Egypt: and he took the spoils of the land of Egypt. ^1-maccabees-01-20-douay

21 And after Antiochus had ravaged Egypt, in the hundred and forty-third year, he returned and went up against Israel. ^1-maccabees-01-21-douay

22 And he went up to Jerusalem, with a great multitude. ^1-maccabees-01-22-douay

23 And he proudly entered into the sanctuary, and took away the golden altar, and the candlestick of light, and all the vessels thereof, and the table of proposition, and the pouring vessels, and the vials, and the little mortars of gold, and the veil, and the crowns, and the golden ornament that was before the temple: and he broke them all in pieces. ^1-maccabees-01-23-douay

24 And he took the silver and gold, and the precious vessels: and he took the hidden treasures, which he found: and when he had taken all away, he departed into his own country. ^1-maccabees-01-24-douay

25 And he made a great slaughter of men, and spoke very proudly. ^1-maccabees-01-25-douay

26 And there was great mourning in Israel, and in every place where they were: ^1-maccabees-01-26-douay

27 And the princes, and the ancients mourned, and the virgins and the young men were made feeble, and the beauty of the women was changed. ^1-maccabees-01-27-douay

28 Every bridegroom took up lamentation: and the bride that sat in the marriage bed, mourned: ^1-maccabees-01-28-douay

29 And the land was moved for the inhabitants thereof, and all the house of Jacob was covered with confusion. ^1-maccabees-01-29-douay

30 And after two full years, the king sent the chief collector of his tributes to the cities of Juda, and he came to Jerusalem with a great multitude. **The chief collector, etc...** Apollonius. ^1-maccabees-01-30-douay

31 And he spoke to them peaceable words in deceit; and they believed him. ^1-maccabees-01-31-douay

32 And he fell upon the city suddenly, and struck it with a great slaughter, and destroyed much people in Israel. ^1-maccabees-01-32-douay

33 And he took the spoils of the city, and burnt it with fire, and threw down the houses thereof, and the walls thereof round about: ^1-maccabees-01-33-douay

34 And they took the women captive, and the children, and the cattle they possessed. ^1-maccabees-01-34-douay

35 And they built the city of David with a great and strong wall, and with strong towers, and made it a fortress for them: **The city of David...** That is, the castle of Sion. ^1-maccabees-01-35-douay

36 And they placed there a sinful nation, wicked men, and they fortified themselves therein: and they stored up armour and victuals, and gathered together the spoils of Jerusalem; ^1-maccabees-01-36-douay

37 And laid them up there: and they became a great snare. ^1-maccabees-01-37-douay

38 And this was a place to lie in wait against the sanctuary, and an evil devil in Israel. **An evil devil...** That is, an adversary watching constantly to do harm, as the evil spirit is always watching and seeking whom he may devour. ^1-maccabees-01-38-douay

39 And they shed innocent blood round about the sanctuary, and defiled the holy place. ^1-maccabees-01-39-douay

40 And the inhabitants of Jerusalem fled away by reason of them and the city was made the habitation of strangers, and she became a stranger to her own seed, and her children forsook her. ^1-maccabees-01-40-douay

41 Her sanctuary was desolate like a wilderness, her festival days were turned into mourning, her sabbaths into reproach, her honours were brought to nothing. ^1-maccabees-01-41-douay

42 Her dishonour was increased according to her glory, and her excellency was turned into mourning. ^1-maccabees-01-42-douay

43 And king Antiochus wrote to all his kingdom, that all the people should be one: and every one should leave his own law. ^1-maccabees-01-43-douay

44 And all nations consented, according to the word of king Antiochus. ^1-maccabees-01-44-douay

45 And many of Israel consented to his service, and they sacrificed to idols, and profaned the sabbath. ^1-maccabees-01-45-douay

46 And the king sent letters by the hands of messengers to Jerusalem, and to all the cities of Juda; that they should follow the law of the nations of the earth. ^1-maccabees-01-46-douay

47 And should forbid holocausts and sacrifices, and atonements to be made in the temple of God. ^1-maccabees-01-47-douay

48 And should prohibit the sabbath, and the festival days to be celebrated. ^1-maccabees-01-48-douay

49 And he commanded the holy places to be profaned, and the holy people of Israel. ^1-maccabees-01-49-douay

50 And he commanded altars to be built, and temples, and idols, and swine's flesh to be immolated, and unclean beasts, ^1-maccabees-01-50-douay

51 And that they should leave their children uncircumcised, and let their souls be defiled with all uncleannesses, and abominations, to the end that they should forget the law, and should change all the justifications of God. ^1-maccabees-01-51-douay

52 And that whosoever would not do according to the word of king Antiochus, should be put to death. ^1-maccabees-01-52-douay

53 According to all these words he wrote to his whole kingdom: and he appointed rulers over the people that should force them to do these things. ^1-maccabees-01-53-douay

54 And they commanded the cities of Juda to sacrifice. ^1-maccabees-01-54-douay

55 Then many of the people were gathered to them that had forsaken the law of the Lord: and they committed evils in the land: ^1-maccabees-01-55-douay

56 And they drove away the people of Israel into lurking holes, and into the secret places of fugitives. ^1-maccabees-01-56-douay

57 On the fifteenth day of the month, Casleu, in the hundred and forty-fifth year, king Antiochus set up the abominable idol of desolation upon the altar of God, and they built altars throughout all the cities of Juda round about: **The abominable idol, etc...** Viz., the statue of Jupiter Olympius. ^1-maccabees-01-57-douay

58 And they burnt incense, and sacrificed at the doors of the houses and in the streets. ^1-maccabees-01-58-douay

59 And they cut in pieces, and burnt with fire the books of the law of God: ^1-maccabees-01-59-douay

60 And every one with whom the books of the testament of the Lord were found, and whosoever observed the law of the Lord, they put to death, according to the edict of the king. ^1-maccabees-01-60-douay

61 Thus by their power did they deal with the people of Israel, that were found in the cities month after month. ^1-maccabees-01-61-douay

62 And on the five and twentieth day of the month they sacrificed upon the altar of the idol that was over against the altar of God. ^1-maccabees-01-62-douay

63 Now the women that circumcised their children were slain according to the commandment of king Antiochus, ^1-maccabees-01-63-douay

64 And they hanged the children about their necks in all their houses: and those that had circumcised them, they put to death. ^1-maccabees-01-64-douay

65 And many of the people of Israel determined with themselves, that they would not eat unclean things: and they chose rather to die, than to be defiled with unclean meats: ^1-maccabees-01-65-douay

66 And they would not break the holy law of God and they were put to death: ^1-maccabees-01-66-douay

67 And there was very great wrath upon the people. ^1-maccabees-01-67-douay
