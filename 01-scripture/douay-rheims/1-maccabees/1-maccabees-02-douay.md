# 1 Maccabees Chapter 2

The zeal and success of Mathathias. His exhortation to his sons at his death.

1 In those days arose Mathathias, the son of John, the son of Simeon, a priest of the sons of Joarib, from Jerusalem, and he abode in the mountain of Modin: ^1-maccabees-02-1-douay

2 And he had five sons: John, who was surnamed Gaddis: ^1-maccabees-02-2-douay

3 And Simon, who was surnamed Thasi; ^1-maccabees-02-3-douay

4 And Judas, who was called Machabeus; ^1-maccabees-02-4-douay

5 And Eleazar, who was surnamed Abaron; and Jonathan, who was surnamed Apphus. ^1-maccabees-02-5-douay

6 These saw the evils that were done in the people of Juda, and in Jerusalem. ^1-maccabees-02-6-douay

7 And Mathathias said: Woe is me, wherefore was I born to see the ruin of my people, and the ruin of the holy city, and to dwell there, when it is given into the hands of the enemies? ^1-maccabees-02-7-douay

8 The holy places are come into the hands of strangers: her temple is become as a man without honour. ^1-maccabees-02-8-douay

9 The vessels of her glory are carried away captive; her old men are murdered in the streets, and her young men are fallen by the sword of the enemies. ^1-maccabees-02-9-douay

10 What nation hath not inherited her kingdom, and gotten of her spoils? ^1-maccabees-02-10-douay

11 All her ornaments are taken away. She that was free is made a slave. ^1-maccabees-02-11-douay

12 And behold our sanctuary, and our beauty, and our glory is laid waste, and the Gentiles have defiled them. ^1-maccabees-02-12-douay

13 To what end then should we live any longer? ^1-maccabees-02-13-douay

14 And Mathathias and his sons rent their garments, and they covered themselves with haircloth, and made great lamentation. ^1-maccabees-02-14-douay

15 And they that were sent from king Antiochus, came thither, to compel them that were fled into the city of Modin, to sacrifice, and to burn incense, and to depart from the law of God. ^1-maccabees-02-15-douay

16 And many of the people of Israel consented and came to them: but Mathathias and his sons stood firm. ^1-maccabees-02-16-douay

17 And they that were sent from Antiochus, answering, said to Mathathias: Thou art a ruler, and an honourable, and great man in this city, and adorned with sons, and brethren. ^1-maccabees-02-17-douay

18 Therefore, come thou first, and obey the king's commandment, as all nations have done, and the men of Juda, and they that remain in Jerusalem: and thou, and thy sons shall be in the number of the king's friends, and enriched with gold, and silver, and many presents. ^1-maccabees-02-18-douay

19 Then Mathathias answered, and said with a loud voice: Although all nations obey king Antiochus, so as to depart every man from the service of the law of his fathers, and consent to his commandments: ^1-maccabees-02-19-douay

20 I and my sons, and my brethren will obey the law of our fathers. ^1-maccabees-02-20-douay

21 God be merciful unto us: it is not profitable for us to forsake the law, and the justices of God: ^1-maccabees-02-21-douay

22 We will not hearken to the words of king Antiochus, neither will we sacrifice and transgress the commandments of our law, to go another way. ^1-maccabees-02-22-douay

23 Now as he left off speaking these words, there came a certain Jew in the sight of all to sacrifice to the idols upon the altar in the city of Modin, according to the king's commandment. ^1-maccabees-02-23-douay

24 And Mathathias saw, and was grieved, and his reins trembled, and his wrath was kindled according to the judgment of the law, and running upon him he slew him upon the altar: ^1-maccabees-02-24-douay

25 Moreover the man whom king Antiochus had sent, who compelled them to sacrifice, he slew at the same time, and pulled down the altar, ^1-maccabees-02-25-douay

26 And showed zeal for the law, as Phinees did by Zamri, the son of Salomi. ^1-maccabees-02-26-douay

27 And Mathathias cried out in the city with a loud voice, saying: Every one that hath zeal for the law, and maintaineth the testament, let him follow me. ^1-maccabees-02-27-douay

28 So he and his sons fled into the mountains, and left all that they had in the city. ^1-maccabees-02-28-douay

29 Then many that sought after judgment, and justice, went down into the desert, ^1-maccabees-02-29-douay

30 And they abode there, they and their children, and their wives, and their cattle: because afflictions increased upon them. ^1-maccabees-02-30-douay

31 And it was told to the king's men, and to the army that was in Jerusalem, in the city of David, that certain men, who had broken the king's commandment, were gone away into the secret places in the wilderness, and that many were gone after them. ^1-maccabees-02-31-douay

32 And forthwith they went out towards them, and made war against them on the sabbath day. ^1-maccabees-02-32-douay

33 And they said to them: Do you still resist? come forth, and do according to the edict of king Antiochus, and you shall live. ^1-maccabees-02-33-douay

34 And they said: We will not come forth, neither will we obey the king's edict, to profane the sabbath day. ^1-maccabees-02-34-douay

35 And they made haste to give them battle. ^1-maccabees-02-35-douay

36 But they answered them not, neither did they cast a stone at them, nor stopped up the secret places, ^1-maccabees-02-36-douay

37 Saying: Let us all die in our innocency: and heaven and earth shall be witnesses for us, that you put us to death wrongfully. ^1-maccabees-02-37-douay

38 So they gave them battle on the sabbath: and they were slain, with their wives, and their children, and their cattle, to the number of a thousand persons. ^1-maccabees-02-38-douay

39 And Mathathias and his friends heard of it, and they mourned for them exceedingly. ^1-maccabees-02-39-douay

40 And every man said to his neighbour: If we shall all do as our brethren have done, and not fight against the heathens for our lives, and our justifications, they will now quickly root us out of the earth. ^1-maccabees-02-40-douay

41 And they determined in that day, saying: Whosoever shall come up against us to fight on the sabbath day, we will fight against him: and we will not all die, as our brethren that were slain in the secret places. ^1-maccabees-02-41-douay

42 Then was assembled to them the congregation of the Assideans, the stoutest of Israel, every one that had a good will for the law. **The Assideans...** A set of men that led a religious life; and were zealous for the law and worship of God. ^1-maccabees-02-42-douay

43 And all they that fled from the evils, joined themselves to them, and were a support to them. ^1-maccabees-02-43-douay

44 And they gathered an army, and slew the sinners in their wrath, and the wicked men in their indignation: and the rest fled to the nations for safety. ^1-maccabees-02-44-douay

45 And Mathathias and his friends went round about, and they threw down the altars: ^1-maccabees-02-45-douay

46 And they circumcised all the children whom they found in the confines of Israel that were uncircumcised: and they did valiantly. ^1-maccabees-02-46-douay

47 And they pursued after the children of pride, and the work prospered in their hands: ^1-maccabees-02-47-douay

48 And they recovered the law out of the hands of the nations, and out of the hands of the kings: and they yielded not the horn to the sinner. **They yielded not the horn, etc...** That is, they suffered not the power of Antiochus, that man of sin, to abolish the law and religion of God. ^1-maccabees-02-48-douay

49 Now the days drew near that Mathathias should die, and he said to his sons: Now hath pride and chastisement gotten strength, and the time of destruction, and the wrath of indignation: ^1-maccabees-02-49-douay

50 Now, therefore, O my sons, be ye zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of your fathers. ^1-maccabees-02-50-douay

51 And call to remembrance the works of the fathers, which they have done in their generations: and you shall receive great glory, and an everlasting name. ^1-maccabees-02-51-douay

52 Was not Abraham found faithful in temptation, and it was reputed to him unto justice? ^1-maccabees-02-52-douay

53 Joseph, in the time of his distress, kept the commandment, and he was made lord of Egypt. ^1-maccabees-02-53-douay

54 Phinees, our father, by being fervent in the zeal of God, received the covenant of an everlasting priesthood. ^1-maccabees-02-54-douay

55 Jesus, whilst he fulfilled the word, was made ruler in Israel. **Jesus...** That is, Joshua. ^1-maccabees-02-55-douay

56 Caleb, for bearing witness before the congregation, received an inheritance. ^1-maccabees-02-56-douay

57 David, by his mercy, obtained the throne of an everlasting kingdom. ^1-maccabees-02-57-douay

58 Elias, while he was full of zeal for the law, was taken up into heaven. ^1-maccabees-02-58-douay

59 Ananias and Azarias and Misael, by believing, were delivered out of the flame. ^1-maccabees-02-59-douay

60 Daniel, in his innocency, was delivered out of the mouth of the lions. ^1-maccabees-02-60-douay

61 And thus consider, through all generations: that none that trust in him, fail in strength. ^1-maccabees-02-61-douay

62 And fear not the words of a sinful man, for his glory is dung and worms: ^1-maccabees-02-62-douay

63 Today he is lifted up, and tomorrow he shall not be found, because he is returned into his earth and his thought is come to nothing. ^1-maccabees-02-63-douay

64 You, therefore, my sons, take courage, and behave manfully in the law: for by it you shall be glorious. ^1-maccabees-02-64-douay

65 And behold, I know that your brother Simon is a man of counsel: give ear to him always, and he shall be a father to you. ^1-maccabees-02-65-douay

66 And Judas Machabeus, who is valiant and strong from his youth up, let him be the leader of your army, and he shall manage the war of the people. ^1-maccabees-02-66-douay

67 And you shall take to you all that observe the law: and revenge ye the wrong of your people. ^1-maccabees-02-67-douay

68 Render to the Gentiles their reward, and take heed to the precepts of the law. ^1-maccabees-02-68-douay

69 And he blessed them, and was joined to his fathers. ^1-maccabees-02-69-douay

70 And he died in the hundred and forty-sixth year: and he was buried by his sons in the sepulchres of his fathers, in Modin, and all Israel mourned for him with great mourning. ^1-maccabees-02-70-douay
