# 1 Maccabees Chapter 6

The fruitless repentance and death of Antiochus. His son comes against Judas with a formidable army. He besieges Zion: but at last makes peace with the Jews.

1 Now king Antiochus was going through the higher countries, and he heard that the city of Elymais in Persia, was greatly renowned, and abounding in silver and gold, ^1-maccabees-06-1-douay

2 And that there was in it a temple exceeding rich; and coverings of gold, and breastplates, and shields, which king Alexander, son of Philip, the Macedonian, that reigned first in Greece, had left there. ^1-maccabees-06-2-douay

3 So he came, and sought to take the city and to pillage it; but he was not able, because the design was known to them that were in the city. ^1-maccabees-06-3-douay

4 And they rose up against him in battle, and he fled away from thence, and departed with great sadness, and returned towards Babylonia. ^1-maccabees-06-4-douay

5 And whilst he was in Persia there came one that told him how the armies that were in the land of Juda were put to flight: ^1-maccabees-06-5-douay

6 And that Lysias went with a very great power, and was put to flight before the face of the Jews, and that they were grown strong by the armour, and power, and store of spoils which they had gotten out of the camps which they had destroyed: ^1-maccabees-06-6-douay

7 And that they had thrown down the abomination which he had set up upon the altar in Jerusalem, and that they had compassed about the sanctuary with high walls as before, and Bethsura also, his city. ^1-maccabees-06-7-douay

8 And it came to pass, when the king heard these words, that he was struck with fear, and exceedingly moved: and he laid himself down upon his bed, and fell sick for grief, because it had not fallen out to him as he imagined. ^1-maccabees-06-8-douay

9 And he remained there many days: for great grief came more and more upon him, and he made account that he should die. ^1-maccabees-06-9-douay

10 And he called for all his friends, and said to them: Sleep is gone from my eyes, and I am fallen away, and my heart is cast down for anxiety: ^1-maccabees-06-10-douay

11 And I said in my heart: Into how much tribulation am I come, and into what floods of sorrow wherein now I am: I that was pleasant and beloved in my power! ^1-maccabees-06-11-douay

12 But now I remember the evils that I did in Jerusalem, from whence also I took away all the spoils of gold, and of silver, that were in it, and I sent to destroy the inhabitants of Juda without cause. ^1-maccabees-06-12-douay

13 I know, therefore, that for this cause these evils have found me: and behold I perish with great grief in a strange land. ^1-maccabees-06-13-douay

14 Then he called Philip, one of his friends, and he made him regent over all his kingdom. ^1-maccabees-06-14-douay

15 And he gave him the crown, and his robe, and his ring, that he should go to Antiochus, his son, and should bring him up for the kingdom. ^1-maccabees-06-15-douay

16 So king Antiochus died there in the year one hundred and forty-nine. ^1-maccabees-06-16-douay

17 And Lysias understood that the king was dead, and he set up Antiochus, his son, to reign, whom he had brought up young: and he called his name Eupator. ^1-maccabees-06-17-douay

18 Now they that were in the castle, had shut up the Israelites round about the holy places: and they were continually seeking their hurt, and to strengthen the Gentiles. ^1-maccabees-06-18-douay

19 And Judas purposed to destroy them: and he called together all the people, to besiege them. ^1-maccabees-06-19-douay

20 And they came together, and besieged them in the year one hundred and fifty, and they made battering slings and engines. ^1-maccabees-06-20-douay

21 And some of the besieged got out: and some wicked men of Israel joined themselves unto them. ^1-maccabees-06-21-douay

22 And they went to the king, and said: How long dost thou delay to execute judgment, and to revenge our brethren? ^1-maccabees-06-22-douay

23 We determined to serve thy father, and to do according to his orders, and obey his edicts: ^1-maccabees-06-23-douay

24 And for this they of our nation are alienated from us, and have slain as many of us as they could find, and have spoiled our inheritances. ^1-maccabees-06-24-douay

25 Neither have they put forth their hand against us only, but also against all our borders. ^1-maccabees-06-25-douay

26 And behold they have approached this day to the castle of Jerusalem to take it, and they have fortified the strong hold of Bethsura: ^1-maccabees-06-26-douay

27 And unless thou speedily prevent them, they will do greater things than these, and thou shalt not be able to subdue them. ^1-maccabees-06-27-douay

28 Now when the king heard this, he was angry: and he called together all his friends, and the captains of his army, and them that were over the horsemen. ^1-maccabees-06-28-douay

29 There came also to him from other realms, and from the islands of the sea, hired troops. ^1-maccabees-06-29-douay

30 And the number of his army was an hundred thousand footmen, and twenty thousand horsemen, and thirty-two elephants trained to battle. ^1-maccabees-06-30-douay

31 And they went through Idumea, and approached to Bethsura, and fought many days, and they made engines: but they sallied forth, and burnt them with fire, and fought manfully. **But they sallied forth...** That is, the citizens of Bethsura sallied forth and burnt them, that is, burnt the engines of the besiegers. ^1-maccabees-06-31-douay

32 And Judas departed from the castle, and removed the camp to Bethzacharam, over against the king's camp. ^1-maccabees-06-32-douay

33 And the king rose before it was light, and made his troops march on fiercely towards the way of Bethzacharam: and the armies made themselves ready for the battle, and they sounded the trumpets: ^1-maccabees-06-33-douay

34 And they showed the elephants the blood of grapes, and mulberries, to provoke them to fight. ^1-maccabees-06-34-douay

35 And they distributed the beasts by the legions: and there stood by every elephant a thousand men in coats of mail, and with helmets of brass on their heads: and five hundred horsemen set in order were chosen for every beast. ^1-maccabees-06-35-douay

36 These before the time wheresoever the beast was they were there: and whithersoever it went, they went, and they departed not from it. **These before the time...** That is, these were ready for every occasion. ^1-maccabees-06-36-douay

37 And upon the beast, there were strong wooden towers which covered every one of them: and engines upon them, and upon every one thirty-two valiant men, who fought from above: and an Indian to rule the beast. ^1-maccabees-06-37-douay

38 And the rest of the horsemen he placed on this side and on that side, at the two wings, with trumpets to stir up the army, and to hasten them forward that stood thick together in the legions thereof. ^1-maccabees-06-38-douay

39 Now when the sun shone upon the shields of gold, and of brass, the mountains glittered therewith, and they shone like lamps of fire. ^1-maccabees-06-39-douay

40 And part of the king's army was distinguished by the high mountains, and the other part by the low places: and they marched on warily and orderly. ^1-maccabees-06-40-douay

41 And all the inhabitants of the land were moved at the noise of their multitude, and the marching of the company, and the rattling of the armour, for the army was exceeding great and strong. ^1-maccabees-06-41-douay

42 And Judas and his army drew near for battle: and there fell of the king's army six hundred men. ^1-maccabees-06-42-douay

43 And Eleazar, the son of Saura, saw one of the beasts harnessed with the king's harness: and it was higher than the other beasts; and it seemed to him that the king was on it: ^1-maccabees-06-43-douay

44 And he exposed himself to deliver his people, and to get himself an everlasting name. ^1-maccabees-06-44-douay

45 And he ran up to it boldly in the midst of the legion, killing on the right hand, and on the left, and they fell by him on this side and that side. ^1-maccabees-06-45-douay

46 And he went between the feet of the elephant, and put himself under it: and slew it, and it fell to the ground upon him, and he died there. ^1-maccabees-06-46-douay

47 Then they seeing the strength of the king and the fierceness of his army, turned away from them. ^1-maccabees-06-47-douay

48 But the king's army went up against them to Jerusalem: and the king's army pitched their tents against Judea and Mount Sion. ^1-maccabees-06-48-douay

49 And he made peace with them that were in Bethsura: and they came forth out of the city, because they had no victuals, being shut up there, for it was the year of rest to the land. ^1-maccabees-06-49-douay

50 And the king took Bethsura: and he placed there a garrison to keep it. ^1-maccabees-06-50-douay

51 And he turned his army against the sanctuary for many days: and he set up there battering slings, and engines, and instruments to cast fire, and engines to cast stones and javelins, and pieces to shoot arrows, and slings. ^1-maccabees-06-51-douay

52 And they also made engines against their engines, and they fought for many days. ^1-maccabees-06-52-douay

53 But there were no victuals in the city, because it was the seventh year: and such as had stayed in Judea of them that came from among the nations, had eaten the residue of all that which had been stored up. ^1-maccabees-06-53-douay

54 And there remained in the holy places but a few, for the famine had prevailed over them: and they were dispersed every man to his own place. ^1-maccabees-06-54-douay

55 Now Lysias heard that Philip; whom king Antiochus while he lived had appointed to bring up his son, Antiochus, and to reign, ^1-maccabees-06-55-douay

56 Was returned from Persia, and Media, with the army that went with him and that he sought to take upon him the affairs of the kingdom: ^1-maccabees-06-56-douay

57 Wherefore he made haste to go, and say to the king and to the captains of the army: We decay daily, and our provision of victuals is small, and the place that we lay siege to is strong, and it lieth upon us to take order for the affairs of the kingdom. ^1-maccabees-06-57-douay

58 Now, therefore, let us come to an agreement with these men, and make peace with them and with all their nation. ^1-maccabees-06-58-douay

59 And let us covenant with them, that they may live according to their own laws, as before. For because of our despising their laws, they have been provoked, and have done all these things. ^1-maccabees-06-59-douay

60 And the proposal was acceptable in the sight of the king, and of the princes: and he sent to them to make peace: and they accepted of it. ^1-maccabees-06-60-douay

61 And the king and the princes swore to them: and they came out of the strong hold. ^1-maccabees-06-61-douay

62 Then the king entered into Mount Sion, and saw the strength of the place: and he quickly broke the oath that he had taken, and gave commandment to throw down the wall round about. ^1-maccabees-06-62-douay

63 And he departed in haste and returned to Antioch, where he found Philip master of the city: and he fought against him, and took the city. ^1-maccabees-06-63-douay
