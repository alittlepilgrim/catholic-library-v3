# Acts Chapter 24

Paul defends his innocence before Felix the governor. He preaches the faith to him.

1 And after five days, the high priest, Ananias, came down with some ancients and one Tertullus, an orator, who went to the governor against Paul. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-1-douay

2 And Paul being called for, Tertullus began to accuse him, saying: Whereas, through thee we live in much peace and many things are rectified by thy providence, ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-2-douay

3 We accept it always and in all places, most excellent Felix, with all thanksgiving. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-3-douay

4 But that I be no further tedious to thee, I desire thee of thy clemency to hear us in a few words. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-4-douay

5 We have found this to be a pestilent man and raising seditions among all the Jews throughout the world: and author of the sedition of the sect of the Nazarenes. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-5-douay

6 Who also hath gone about to profane the temple: whom, we having apprehended, would also have judged according to our law. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-6-douay

7 But Lysias the tribune, coming upon us with great violence, took him away out of our hands; ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-7-douay

8 Commanding his accusers to come to thee. Of whom thou mayest thyself, by examination, have knowledge of all these things whereof we accuse him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-8-douay

9 And the Jews also added and said that these things were so. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-9-douay

10 Then Paul answered (the governor making a sign to him to speak): Knowing that for many years thou hast been judge over this nation, I will with good courage answer for myself. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-10-douay

11 For thou mayest understand that there are yet but twelve days since I went up to adore in Jerusalem: ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-11-douay

12 And neither in the temple did they find me disputing with any man or causing any concourse of the people: neither in the synagogues, nor in the city. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-12-douay

13 Neither can they prove unto thee the things whereof they now accuse me. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-13-douay

14 But this I confess to thee that according to the way which they call a heresy, so do I serve the Father and my God, believing all things which are written in the law and the prophets: ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-14-douay

15 Having hope in God, which these also themselves look for, that there shall be a resurrection of the just and unjust. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-15-douay

16 And herein do I endeavour to have always a conscience without offence, towards God and towards men. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-16-douay

17 Now after many years, I came to bring alms to my nation and offerings and vows. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-17-douay

18 In which I was found purified in the temple: neither with multitude nor with tumult. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-18-douay

19 But certain Jews of Asia, who ought to be present before thee and to accuse, if they had anything against me: ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-19-douay

20 Or let these men themselves say if they found in me any iniquity, when standing before the council, ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-20-douay

21 Except it be for this one voice only that I cried, standing among them: Concerning the resurrection of the dead am I judged this day by you. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-21-douay

22 And Felix put them off, having most certain knowledge of this way, saying: When Lysias the tribune shall come down, I will hear you. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-22-douay

23 And he commanded a centurion to keep him: and that he should be easy and that he should not prohibit any of his friends to minister unto him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-23-douay

24 And after some days, Felix, coming with Drusilla his wife, who was a Jew, sent for Paul and heard of him the faith that is in Christ Jesus. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-24-douay

25 And as he treated of justice and chastity and of the judgment to come, Felix, being terrified, answered: For this time, go thy way: but when I have a convenient time, I will send for thee. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-25-douay

26 Hoping also withal that money should be given him by Paul: for which cause also oftentimes sending for him, he spoke with him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-26-douay

27 But when two years were ended, Felix had for successor Portius Festus. And Felix being willing to show the Jews a pleasure, left Paul bound. ^acts-of-the-apostles-24-27-douay
