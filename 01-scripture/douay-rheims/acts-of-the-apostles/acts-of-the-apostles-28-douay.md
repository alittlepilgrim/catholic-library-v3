# Acts Chapter 28

Paul, after three months' stay in Melita, continues his voyage and arrives at Rome. His conference there with the Jews.

1 And when we had escaped, then we knew that the island was called Melita. But the barbarians showed us no small courtesy. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-1-douay

2 For kindling a fire, they refreshed us all, because of the present rain and of the cold. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-2-douay

3 And when Paul had gathered together a bundle of sticks and had laid them on the fire, a viper, coming out of the heat, fastened on his hand. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-3-douay

4 And when the barbarians saw the beast hanging on his hand, they said one to another: Undoubtedly this man is a murderer, who, though he hath escaped the sea, yet vengeance doth not suffer him to live. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-4-douay

5 And he indeed, shaking off the beast into the fire, suffered no harm. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-5-douay

6 But they supposed that he would begin to swell up and that he would suddenly fall down and die. But expecting long and seeing that there came no harm to him, changing their minds, they said that he was a god. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-6-douay

7 Now in these places were possessions of the chief man of the island, named Publius: who, receiving us for three days, entertained us courteously. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-7-douay

8 And it happened that the father of Publius lay sick of a fever and of a bloody flux. To whom Paul entered in. And when he had prayed and laid his hands on him, he healed him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-8-douay

9 Which being done, all that had diseases in the island came and were healed. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-9-douay

10 Who also honoured us with many honours: and when we were to set sail, they laded us with such things as were necessary. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-10-douay

11 And after three months, we sailed in a ship of Alexandria, that had wintered in the island, whose sign was the Castors. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-11-douay

12 And when we were come to Syracusa, we tarried there three days. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-12-douay

13 From thence, compassing by the shore, we came to Rhegium: and after one day, the south wind blowing, we came the second day to Puteoli: ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-13-douay

14 Where, finding brethren, we were desired to tarry with them seven days. And so we went to Rome. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-14-douay

15 And from thence, when the brethren had heard of us, they came to meet us as far as Appii Forum and the Three Taverns. Whom when Paul saw, he gave thanks to God and took courage. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-15-douay

16 And when we were come to Rome, Paul was suffered to dwell by himself, with a soldier that kept him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-16-douay

17 And after the third day, he called together the chief of the Jews. And when they were assembled, he said to them: Men, brethren, I, having done nothing against the people or the custom of our fathers, was delivered prisoner from Jerusalem into the hands of the Romans. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-17-douay

18 Who, when they had examined me, would have released me, for that there was no cause of death in me. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-18-douay

19 But the Jews contradicting it, I was constrained to appeal unto Caesar: not that I had anything to accuse my nation of. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-19-douay

20 For this cause therefore I desired to see you and to speak to you. Because that for the hope of Israel, I am bound with this chain. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-20-douay

21 But they said to him: We neither received letters concerning thee from Judea: neither did any of the brethren that came hither relate or speak any evil of thee. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-21-douay

22 But we desire to hear of thee what thou thinkest: for as concerning this sect, we know that it is every where contradicted. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-22-douay

23 And when they had appointed him a day, there came very many to him unto his lodgings. To whom he expounded, testifying the kingdom of God and persuading them concerning Jesus, out of the law of Moses and the prophets, from morning until evening. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-23-douay

24 And some believed the things that were said: but some believed not. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-24-douay

25 And when they agreed not among themselves, they departed, Paul speaking this one word: Well did the Holy Ghost speak to our fathers by Isaiah the prophet, ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-25-douay

26 Saying: ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-26-douay

Go to this people and say to them: With the ear you shall hear and shall not understand: and seeing you shall see and shall not perceive.

27 For the heart of this people is grown gross, and with their ears have they heard heavily and their eyes they have shut, lest perhaps they should see with their eyes and hear with their ears and understand with their heart and should be converted: and I should heal them. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-27-douay

28 Be it known therefore to you that this salvation of God is sent to the Gentiles: and they will hear it. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-28-douay

29 And when he had said these things, the Jews went out from him, having much reasoning among themselves. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-29-douay

30 And he remained two whole years in his own hired lodging: and he received all that came in to him, ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-30-douay

31 Preaching the kingdom of God and teaching the things which concern the Lord Jesus Christ, with all confidence, without prohibition. ^acts-of-the-apostles-28-31-douay
