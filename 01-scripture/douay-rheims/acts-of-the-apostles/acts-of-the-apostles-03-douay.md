# Acts Chapter 3

The miracle upon the lame man, followed by the conversion of many.

1 Now Peter and John went up into the temple at the ninth hour of prayer. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-1-douay

2 And a certain man who was lame from his mother's womb was carried: whom they laid every day at the gate of the temple, which is called Beautiful, that he might ask alms of them that went into the temple. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-2-douay

3 He, when he had seen Peter and John, about to go into the temple, asked to receive an alms. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-3-douay

4 But Peter with John, fastening his eyes upon him, said: Look upon us. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-4-douay

5 But he looked earnestly upon them, hoping that he should receive something of them. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-5-douay

6 But Peter said: Silver and gold I have none; but what I have, I give thee. In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, arise and walk. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-6-douay

7 And taking him by the right hand, he lifted him up: and forthwith his feet and soles received strength. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-7-douay

8 And he leaping up, stood and walked and went in with them into the temple, walking and leaping and praising God. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-8-douay

9 And all the people saw him walking and praising God. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-9-douay

10 And they knew him, that it was he who sat begging alms at the Beautiful gate of the temple: and they were filled with wonder and amazement at that which had happened to him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-10-douay

11 And as he held Peter and John, all the people ran to them, to the porch which is called Solomon's, greatly wondering. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-11-douay

12 But Peter seeing, made answer to the people: Ye men of Israel, why wonder you at this? Or why look you upon us, as if by our strength or power we had made this man to walk? ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-12-douay

13 The God of Abraham and the God of Isaac and the God of Jacob, the God of our fathers, hath glorified his Son Jesus, whom you indeed delivered up and denied before the face of Pilate, when he judged he should be released. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-13-douay

14 But you denied the Holy One and the Just: and desired a murderer to be granted unto you. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-14-douay

15 But the author of life you killed, whom God hath raised from the dead: of which we are witnesses. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-15-douay

16 And in the faith of his name, this man, whom you have seen and known, hath his name strengthened. And the faith which is by him hath given this perfect soundness in the sight of you all. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-16-douay

17 And now, brethren, I know that you did it through ignorance: as did also your rulers. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-17-douay

18 But those things which God before had showed by the mouth of all the prophets, that his Christ should suffer, he hath so fulfilled. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-18-douay

19 Be penitent, therefore, and be converted, that your sins may be blotted out. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-19-douay

20 That when the times of refreshment shall come from the presence of the Lord, and he shall send him who hath been preached unto you, Jesus Christ. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-20-douay

21 Whom heaven indeed must receive, until the times of the restitution of all things, which God hath spoken by the mouth of his holy prophets, from the beginning of the world. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-21-douay

22 For Moses said: A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me: him you shall hear according to all things whatsoever he shall speak to you. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-22-douay

23 And it shall be, that every soul which will not hear that prophet shall be destroyed from among the people. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-23-douay

24 And all the prophets, from Samuel and afterwards, who have spoken, have told of these days. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-24-douay

25 You are the children of the prophets and of the testament which God made to our fathers, saying to Abraham: And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-25-douay

26 To you first, God, raising up his Son, hath sent him to bless you: that every one may convert himself from his wickedness. ^acts-of-the-apostles-03-26-douay
