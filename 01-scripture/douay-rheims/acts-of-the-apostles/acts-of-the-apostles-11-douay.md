# Acts Chapter 11

Peter defends his having received the Gentiles into the Church.

1 And the apostles and brethren, who were in Judea, heard that the Gentiles also had received the word of God. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-1-douay

2 And when Peter was come up to Jerusalem, they that were of the circumcision contended with him, ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-2-douay

3 Saying: Why didst thou go in to men uncircumcised and didst eat with them? ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-3-douay

4 But Peter began and declared to them the matter in order, saying: ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-4-douay

5 I was in the city of Joppe praying: and I saw in an ecstasy of mind a vision, a certain vessel descending, as it were a great sheet let down from heaven by four corners. And it came even unto me. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-5-douay

6 Into which looking, I considered and saw fourfooted creatures of the earth and beasts and creeping things and fowls of the air. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-6-douay

7 And I heard also a voice saying to me: Arise, Peter. Kill and eat. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-7-douay

8 And I said: Not so, Lord: for nothing common or unclean hath ever entered into my mouth. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-8-douay

9 And the voice answered again from heaven: What God hath made clean, do not thou call common. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-9-douay

10 And this was done three times. And all were taken up again into heaven. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-10-douay

11 And behold, immediately there were three men come to the house wherein I was, sent to me from Caesarea. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-11-douay

12 And the Spirit said to me that I should go with them, nothing doubting. And these six brethren went with me also: and we entered into the man's house. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-12-douay

13 And he told us how he had seen an angel in his house, standing and saying to him: Send to Joppe and call hither Simon, who is surnamed Peter, ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-13-douay

14 Who shall speak to thee words whereby thou shalt be saved, and all thy house. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-14-douay

15 And when I had begun to speak, the Holy Ghost fell upon them, as upon us also in the beginning. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-15-douay

16 And I remembered the word of the Lord, how that he said: John indeed baptized with water but you shall be baptized with the Holy Ghost. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-16-douay

17 If then God gave them the same grace as to us also who believed in the Lord Jesus Christ: who was I, that could withstand God? ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-17-douay

18 Having heard these things, they held their peace and glorified God, saying: God then hath also to the Gentiles given repentance, unto life. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-18-douay

19 Now they who had been dispersed by the persecution that arose on occasion of Stephen went about as far as Phenice and Cyprus and Antioch, speaking the word to none, but to the Jews only. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-19-douay

20 But some of them were men of Cyprus and Cyrene, who, when they were entered into Antioch, spoke also to the Greeks, preaching the Lord Jesus. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-20-douay

21 And the hand of he Lord was with them: and a great number believing, were converted to the Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-21-douay

22 And the tidings came to the ears of the church that was at Jerusalem, touching these things: and they sent Barnabas as far as Antioch. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-22-douay

23 Who, when he was come and had seen the grace of God, rejoiced. And he exhorted them all with purpose of heart to continue in the Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-23-douay

24 For he was a good man and full of the Holy Ghost and of faith. And a great multitude was added to the Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-24-douay

25 And Barnabas went to Tarsus to seek Saul: whom, when he had found, he brought to Antioch. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-25-douay

26 And they conversed there in the church a whole year: and they taught a great multitude, so that at Antioch the disciples were first named Christians. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-26-douay

27 And in these days there came prophets from Jerusalem to Antioch. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-27-douay

28 And one of them named Agabus, rising up, signified by the Spirit that there should be a great famine over the whole world, which came to pass under Claudius. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-28-douay

29 And the disciples, every man according to his ability, purposed to send relief to the brethren who dwelt in Judea. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-29-douay

30 Which also they did, sending it to the ancients, by the hands of Barnabas and Saul. ^acts-of-the-apostles-11-30-douay
