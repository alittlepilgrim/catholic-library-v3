# Acts Chapter 9

Paul's conversion and zeal. Peter heals Eneas and raises up Tabitha to life.

1 And Saul, as yet breathing out threatenings and slaughter against the disciples of the Lord, went to the high priest ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-1-douay

2 And asked of him letters to Damascus, to the synagogues: that if he found any men and women of this way, he might bring them bound to Jerusalem. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-2-douay

3 And as he went on his journey, it came to pass that he drew nigh to Damascus. And suddenly a light from heaven shined round about him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-3-douay

4 And falling on the ground, he heard a voice saying to him: Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me? ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-4-douay

5 Who said: Who art thou, Lord? And he: I am Jesus whom thou persecutest. It is hard for thee to kick against the goad. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-5-douay

6 And he, trembling and astonished, said: Lord, what wilt thou have me to do? ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-6-douay

7 And the Lord said to him: Arise and go into the city; and there it shall be told thee what thou must do. Now the men who went in company with him stood amazed, hearing indeed a voice but seeing no man. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-7-douay

8 And Saul arose from the ground: and when his eyes were opened, he saw nothing. But they, leading him by the hands, brought him to Damascus. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-8-douay

9 And he was there three days without sight: and he did neither eat nor drink. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-9-douay

10 Now there was a certain disciple at Damascus, named Ananias. And the Lord said to him in a vision: Ananias, And he said: Behold I am here, Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-10-douay

11 And the Lord said to him: Arise and go into the street that is called Strait and seek in the house of Judas, one named Saul of Tarsus. For behold he prayeth. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-11-douay

12 (And he saw a man named Ananias coming in and putting his hands upon him, that he might receive his sight.) ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-12-douay

13 But Ananias answered: Lord, I have heard by many of this man, how much evil he hath done to thy saints in Jerusalem. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-13-douay

14 And here he hath authority from the chief priests to bind all that invoke thy name. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-14-douay

15 And the Lord said to him: Go thy way: for this man is to me a vessel of election, to carry my name before the Gentiles and kings and the children of Israel. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-15-douay

16 For I will show him how great things he must suffer for my name's sake. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-16-douay

17 And Ananias went his way and entered into the house. And laying his hands upon him, he said: Brother Saul, the Lord Jesus hath sent me, he that appeared to thee in the way as thou camest, that thou mayest receive thy sight and be filled with the Holy Ghost. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-17-douay

18 And immediately there fell from his eyes as it were scales: and he received his sight. And rising up, he was baptized. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-18-douay

19 And when he had taken meat, he was strengthened. And he was with the disciples that were at Damascus, for some days. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-19-douay

20 And immediately he preached Jesus in the synagogues, that he is the son of God. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-20-douay

21 And all that heard him were astonished and said: Is not this he who persecuted in Jerusalem those that called upon this name and came hither for that intent, that he might carry them bound to the chief priests? ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-21-douay

22 But Saul increased much more in strength and confounded the Jews who dwelt at Damascus, affirming that this is the Christ. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-22-douay

23 And when many days were passed, the Jews consulted together to kill him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-23-douay

24 But their lying in wait was made known to Saul. And they watched the gates also day and night, that they might kill him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-24-douay

25 But the disciples, taking him in the night, conveyed him away by the wall, letting him down in a basket. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-25-douay

26 And when he was come into Jerusalem, he essayed to join himself to the disciples: and they all were afraid of him, not believing that he was a disciple. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-26-douay

27 But Barnabas took him and brought him to the apostles and told them how he had seen the Lord, and that he had spoken to him: and how in Damascus he had dealt confidently in the name of Jesus. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-27-douay

28 And he was with them, coming in and going out in Jerusalem and dealing confidently in the name of the Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-28-douay

29 He spoke also to the Gentiles and disputed with the Greeks. But they sought to kill him. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-29-douay

30 Which when the brethren had known, they brought him down to Caesarea and sent him away to Tarsus. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-30-douay

31 Now, the church had peace throughout all Judea and Galilee and Samaria: and was edified, walking in the fear of the Lord: and was filled with the consolation of the Holy Ghost. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-31-douay

32 And it came to pass that Peter, as he passed through, visiting all, came to the saints who dwelt at Lydda. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-32-douay

33 And he found there a certain man named Eneas, who had kept his bed for eight years, who was ill of the palsy. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-33-douay

34 And Peter said to him: Eneas, the Lord Jesus Christ healeth thee. Arise and make thy bed. And immediately he arose. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-34-douay

35 And all that dwelt at Lydda and Saron saw him: who were converted to the Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-35-douay

36 And in Joppe there was a certain disciple named Tabitha, which by interpretation is called *Dorcas*. This woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-36-douay

37 And it came to pass in those days that she was sick and died. Whom when they had washed, they laid her in an upper chamber. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-37-douay

38 And forasmuch as Lydda was nigh to Joppe, the disciples, hearing that Peter was there, sent unto him two men, desiring him that he would not be slack to come unto them. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-38-douay

39 And Peter rising up went with them. And when he was come, they brought him into the upper chamber. And all the widows stood about him, weeping and showing him the coats and garments which Dorcas made them. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-39-douay

40 And they all being put forth, Peter, kneeling down, prayed. And turning to the body, he said: Tabitha, arise. And she opened her eyes and, seeing Peter, sat up. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-40-douay

41 And giving her his hand, he lifted her up. And when he had called the saints and the widows, he presented her alive. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-41-douay

42 And it was made known throughout all Joppe. And many believed in the Lord. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-42-douay

43 And it came to pass that he abode many days in Joppe, with one Simon a tanner. ^acts-of-the-apostles-09-43-douay
