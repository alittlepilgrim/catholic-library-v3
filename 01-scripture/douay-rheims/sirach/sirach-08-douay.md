# Sirach Chapter 8

Other lessons of wisdom and virtue.

1 Strive not with a powerful man, lest thou fall into his hands. ^sirach-08-1-douay

2 Contend not with a rich man, lest he bring an action against thee. ^sirach-08-2-douay

3 For gold and silver hath destroyed many, and hath reached even to the heart of kings, and perverted them. ^sirach-08-3-douay

4 Strive not with a man that is full of tongue, and heap not wood upon his fire. ^sirach-08-4-douay

5 Communicate not with an ignorant man, lest he speak ill of thy family. ^sirach-08-5-douay

6 Despise not a man that turneth away from sin, nor reproach him therewith: remember that we are all worthy of reproof. ^sirach-08-6-douay

7 Despise not a man in his old age; for we also shall become old. ^sirach-08-7-douay

8 Rejoice not at the death of thy enemy; knowing that we all die, and are not willing that others should rejoice at our death. ^sirach-08-8-douay

9 Despise not the discourse of them that are ancient and wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs. ^sirach-08-9-douay

10 For of them thou shalt learn wisdom, and instruction of understanding, and to serve great men without blame. ^sirach-08-10-douay

11 Let not the discourse of the ancients escape thee, for they have learned of their fathers: ^sirach-08-11-douay

12 For of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give an answer in time of need. ^sirach-08-12-douay

13 Kindle not the coals of sinners by rebuking them, lest thou be burnt with the flame of the fire of their sins. ^sirach-08-13-douay

14 Stand not against the face of an injurious person, lest he sit as a spy to entrap thee in thy words. ^sirach-08-14-douay

15 Lend not to a man that is mightier than thyself: and if thou lendest, count it as lost. ^sirach-08-15-douay

16 Be not surety above thy power: and if thou be surety, think as if thou wert to pay it. ^sirach-08-16-douay

17 Judge not against a judge: for he judgeth according to that which is just. ^sirach-08-17-douay

18 Go not on the way with a bold man, lest he burden thee with his evils: for he goeth according to his own will, and thou shalt perish together with his folly. ^sirach-08-18-douay

19 Quarrel not with a passionate man, and go not into the desert with a bold man: for blood is as nothing in his sight, and where there is no help he will overthrow thee. ^sirach-08-19-douay

20 Advise not with fools, for they cannot love but such things as please them. ^sirach-08-20-douay

21 Before a stranger do no matter of counsel: for thou knowest not what he will bring forth. ^sirach-08-21-douay

22 Open not thy heart to every man: lest he repay thee with an evil turn, and speak reproachfully to thee. ^sirach-08-22-douay
