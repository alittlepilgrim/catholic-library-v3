# Sirach Chapter 32

Lessons for superiors and inferiors. Advantages of fearing God, and doing nothing without counsel.

1 Have they made thee ruler? be not lifted up: be among them as one of them. ^sirach-32-1-douay

2 Have care of them, and so sit down, and when thou hast acquitted thyself of all thy charge, take thy place: ^sirach-32-2-douay

3 That thou mayst rejoice for them, and receive a crown as an ornament of grace, and get the honour of the contribution. ^sirach-32-3-douay

4 Speak, thou that art elder: for it becometh thee, ^sirach-32-4-douay

5 To speak the first word with careful knowledge, and hinder not music. ^sirach-32-5-douay

6 Where there is no hearing, pour not out words, and be not lifted up out of season with thy wisdom. ^sirach-32-6-douay

7 A concert of music in a banquet of wine is as a carbuncle set in gold. ^sirach-32-7-douay

8 As a signet of an emerald in a work of gold: so is the melody of music with pleasant and moderate wine. ^sirach-32-8-douay

9 Hear in silence, and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee. ^sirach-32-9-douay

10 Young man, scarcely speak in thy own cause. ^sirach-32-10-douay

11 If thou be asked twice, let thy answer be short. ^sirach-32-11-douay

12 In many things be as if thou wert ignorant, and hear in silence and withal seeking. ^sirach-32-12-douay

13 In the company of great men take not upon thee: and when the ancients are present, speak not much. ^sirach-32-13-douay

14 Before a storm goeth lightning: and before shamefacedness goeth favour: and for thy reverence good grace shall come to thee. ^sirach-32-14-douay

15 And at the time of rising be not slack: but be first to run home to thy house, and there withdraw thyself, and there take thy pastime. ^sirach-32-15-douay

16 And do what thou hast a mind, but not in sin or proud speech. ^sirach-32-16-douay

17 And for all these things bless the Lord, that made thee, and that replenisheth thee with all his good things. ^sirach-32-17-douay

18 He that feareth the Lord, will receive his discipline: and they that will seek him early, shall find a blessing. ^sirach-32-18-douay

19 He that seeketh the law, shall be filled with it: and he that dealeth deceitfully, shall meet with a stumbling block therein. ^sirach-32-19-douay

20 They that fear the Lord, shall find just judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light. ^sirach-32-20-douay

21 A sinful man will flee reproof, and will find an excuse according to his will. ^sirach-32-21-douay

22 A man of counsel will not neglect understanding, a strange and proud man will not dread fear: ^sirach-32-22-douay

23 Even after he hath done with fear without counsel, he shall be controlled by the things of his own seeking. ^sirach-32-23-douay

24 My son, do thou nothing without counsel, and thou shalt not repent when thou hast done. ^sirach-32-24-douay

25 Go not in the way of ruin, and thou shalt not stumble against the stones: trust not thyself to a rugged way, lest thou set a stumbling block to thy soul. ^sirach-32-25-douay

26 And beware of thy own children, and take heed of them of thy household. ^sirach-32-26-douay

27 In every work of thine regard thy soul in faith: for this is the keeping of the commandments. **In faith...** That is, follow sincerely thy soul in her faith and conscience. ^sirach-32-27-douay

28 He that believeth God, taketh heed to the commandments: and he that trusteth in him, shall fare never the worse. ^sirach-32-28-douay
