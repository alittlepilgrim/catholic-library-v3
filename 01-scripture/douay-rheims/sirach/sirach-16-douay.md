# Sirach Chapter 16

It is better to have none than many wicked children. Of the justice and mercy of God. His ways are unsearchable.

1 Rejoice not in ungodly children, if they be multiplied: neither be delighted in them, if the fear of God be not with them. ^sirach-16-1-douay

2 Trust not to their life, and respect not their labours. ^sirach-16-2-douay

3 For better is one that feareth God, than a thousand ungodly children. ^sirach-16-3-douay

4 And it is better to die without children, than to leave ungodly children. ^sirach-16-4-douay

5 By one that is wise a country shall be inhabited, the tribe of the ungodly shall become desolate. ^sirach-16-5-douay

6 Many such things hath my eyes seen, and greater things than these my ear hath heard. ^sirach-16-6-douay

7 In the congregation of sinners a fire shall be kindled, and in an unbelieving nation wrath shall flame out. ^sirach-16-7-douay

8 The ancient giants did not obtain pardon for their sins, who were destroyed trusting to their own strength: ^sirach-16-8-douay

9 And he spared not the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for the pride of their word. ^sirach-16-9-douay

10 He had not pity on them, destroying the whole nation that extolled themselves in their sins. ^sirach-16-10-douay

11 So did he with the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their heart: and if one had been stiffnecked, it is a wonder if he had escaped unpunished: **Six hundred thousand footmen, etc...** Viz., the children of Israel, whom he sentenced to die in the wilderness. Num. 14. ^sirach-16-11-douay

12 For mercy and wrath are with him. He is mighty to forgive, and to pour out indignation: ^sirach-16-12-douay

13 According as his mercy is, so his correction judgeth a man according to his works. ^sirach-16-13-douay

14 The sinner shall not escape in his rapines, and the patience of him that showeth mercy shall not be put off. ^sirach-16-14-douay

15 All mercy shall make a place for every man according to the merit of his works, and according to the wisdom of his sojournment. ^sirach-16-15-douay

16 Say not: I shall be hidden from God, and who shall remember me from on high? ^sirach-16-16-douay

17 In such a multitude I shall not be known: for what is my soul in such an immense creation? ^sirach-16-17-douay

18 Behold the heaven, and the heavens of heavens, the deep, and all the earth, and the things that are in them, shall be moved in his sight, ^sirach-16-18-douay

19 The mountains also, and the hills, and the foundations of the earth: when God shall look upon them, they shall be shaken with trembling. ^sirach-16-19-douay

20 And in all these things the heart is senseless: and every heart is understood by him. ^sirach-16-20-douay

21 And his ways who shall understand, and the storm, which no eye of man shall see? ^sirach-16-21-douay

22 For many of his works are hidden, but the works of his justice who shall declare? or who shall endure? for the testament is far from some, and the examination of all is in the end. ^sirach-16-22-douay

23 He that wanteth understanding thinketh vain things, and the foolish, and erring man, thinketh foolish things. ^sirach-16-23-douay

24 Hearken to me, my son, and learn the discipline of understanding, and attend to my words in thy heart. ^sirach-16-24-douay

25 And I will show forth good doctrine in equity, and will seek to declare wisdom: and attend to my words in thy heart, whilst with equity of spirit I tell thee the virtues that God hath put upon his works from the beginning, and I show forth in truth his knowledge. ^sirach-16-25-douay

26 The works of God are done in judgment from the beginning, and from the making of them he distinguished their parts, and their beginnings in their generations. ^sirach-16-26-douay

27 He beautified their works for ever, they have neither hungered, nor laboured, and they have not ceased from their works. ^sirach-16-27-douay

28 Nor shall any of them straiten his neighbour at any time. ^sirach-16-28-douay

29 Be not thou incredulous to his word. ^sirach-16-29-douay

30 After this God looked upon the earth, and filled it with his goods. ^sirach-16-30-douay

31 The soul of every living thing hath shown forth before the face thereof, and into it they return again. **Shown forth...** Viz., the glory and power of God upon the earth. ^sirach-16-31-douay
