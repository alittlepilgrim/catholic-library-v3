# Sirach Chapter 13

Cautions in the choice of company.

1 He that toucheth pitch, shall be defiled with it: and he that hath fellowship with the proud, shall put on pride. ^sirach-13-1-douay

2 He shall take a burden upon him that hath fellowship with one more honourable than himself. And have no fellowship with one that is richer than thyself. ^sirach-13-2-douay

3 What agreement shall the earthen pot have with the kettle? for if they knock one against the other, it shall be broken. ^sirach-13-3-douay

4 The rich man hath done wrong, and yet he will fume: but the poor is wronged and must hold his peace. ^sirach-13-4-douay

5 If thou give, he will make use of thee: and if thou have nothing, he will forsake thee. ^sirach-13-5-douay

6 If thou have any thing, he will live with thee, and will make thee bare, and he will not be sorry for thee. ^sirach-13-6-douay

7 If he have need of thee he will deceive thee, and smiling upon thee will put thee in hope; he will speak thee fair, and will say: What wantest thou? ^sirach-13-7-douay

8 And he will shame thee by his meats, till he have drawn thee dry twice or thrice, and at last he will laugh at thee: and afterward when he seeth thee, he will forsake thee, and shake his head at thee. ^sirach-13-8-douay

9 Humble thyself to God, and wait for his hands. ^sirach-13-9-douay

10 Beware that thou be not deceived into folly, and be humbled. ^sirach-13-10-douay

11 Be not lowly in thy wisdom, lest being humbled thou be deceived into folly. ^sirach-13-11-douay

12 If thou be invited by one that is mightier, withdraw thyself: for so he will invite thee the more. ^sirach-13-12-douay

13 Be not troublesome to him, lest thou be put back: and keep not far from him, lest thou be forgotten. ^sirach-13-13-douay

14 Affect not to speak with him as an equal, and believe not his many words: for by much talk he will sift thee, and smiling will examine thee concerning thy secrets. ^sirach-13-14-douay

15 His cruel mind will lay up thy words: and he will not spare to do thee hurt, and to cast thee into prison. ^sirach-13-15-douay

16 Take heed to thyself, and attend diligently to what thou hearest: for thou walkest in danger of thy ruin. ^sirach-13-16-douay

17 When thou hearest those things, see as it were in sleep, and thou shalt awake. ^sirach-13-17-douay

18 Love God all thy life, and call upon him for thy salvation. ^sirach-13-18-douay

19 Every beast loveth its like: so also every man him that is nearest to himself. ^sirach-13-19-douay

20 All flesh shall consort with the like to itself, and every man shall associate himself to his like. ^sirach-13-20-douay

21 If the wolf shall at any time have fellowship with the lamb, so the sinner with the just. ^sirach-13-21-douay

22 What fellowship hath a holy man with a dog, or what part hath the rich with the poor? ^sirach-13-22-douay

23 The wild ass is the lion's prey in the desert: so also the poor are devoured by the rich. ^sirach-13-23-douay

24 And as humility is an abomination to the proud: so also the rich man abhorreth the poor. ^sirach-13-24-douay

25 When a rich man is shaken, he is kept up by his friends: but when a poor man is fallen down, he is thrust away even by his acquaintance. ^sirach-13-25-douay

26 When a rich man hath been deceived, he hath many helpers: he hath spoken proud things, and they have justified him. ^sirach-13-26-douay

27 The poor man was deceived, and he is rebuked also: he hath spoken wisely, and could have no place. ^sirach-13-27-douay

28 The rich man spoke, and all held their peace, and what he said they extol even to the clouds. ^sirach-13-28-douay

29 The poor man spoke, and they say: Who is this? and if he stumble, they will overthrow him. ^sirach-13-29-douay

30 Riches are good to him that hath no sin in his conscience: and poverty is very wicked in the mouth of the ungodly. ^sirach-13-30-douay

31 The heart of a man changeth his countenance, either for good, or for evil. ^sirach-13-31-douay

32 The token of a good heart, and a good countenance thou shalt hardly find, and with labour. ^sirach-13-32-douay
