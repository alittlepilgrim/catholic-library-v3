# Sirach Chapter 24

Wisdom praises herself: her origin, her dwelling, her dignity, and her fruits.

1 Wisdom shall praise her own self, and shall be honoured in God, and shall glory in the midst of her people, ^sirach-24-1-douay

2 And shall open her mouth in the churches of the most High, and shall glorify herself in the sight of his power, ^sirach-24-2-douay

3 And in the midst of her own people she shall be exalted, and shall be admired in the holy assembly. ^sirach-24-3-douay

4 And in the multitude of the elect she shall have praise, and among the blessed she shall be blessed, saying: ^sirach-24-4-douay

5 I came out of the mouth of the most High, the firstborn before all creatures: ^sirach-24-5-douay

6 I made that in the heavens there should rise light that never faileth, and as a cloud I covered all the earth: ^sirach-24-6-douay

7 I dwelt in the highest places, and my throne is in a pillar of a cloud. ^sirach-24-7-douay

8 I alone have compassed the circuit of heaven, and have penetrated into the bottom of the deep, and have walked in the waves of the sea, ^sirach-24-8-douay

9 And have stood in all the earth: and in every people, ^sirach-24-9-douay

10 And in every nation I have had the chief rule: ^sirach-24-10-douay

11 And by my power I have trodden under my feet the hearts of all the high and low: and in all these I sought rest, and I shall abide in the inheritance of the Lord. ^sirach-24-11-douay

12 Then the creator of all things commanded, and said to me: and he that made me, rested in my tabernacle, ^sirach-24-12-douay

13 And he said to me: Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thy inheritance in Israel, and take root in my elect. ^sirach-24-13-douay

14 From the beginning, and before the world, was I created, and unto the world to come I shall not cease to be, and in the holy dwelling place I have ministered before him. ^sirach-24-14-douay

15 And so was I established in Sion, and in the holy city likewise I rested, and my power was in Jerusalem. ^sirach-24-15-douay

16 And I took root in an honourable people, and in the portion of my God his inheritance, and my abode is in the full assembly of saints. ^sirach-24-16-douay

17 I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree on mount Sion. ^sirach-24-17-douay

18 I was exalted like a palm tree in Cades, and as a rose plant in Jericho: ^sirach-24-18-douay

19 As a fair olive tree in the plains, and as a plane tree by the water in the streets, was I exalted. ^sirach-24-19-douay

20 I gave a sweet smell like cinnamon, and aromatical balm: I yielded a sweet odour like the best myrrh: ^sirach-24-20-douay

21 And I perfumed my dwelling as storax, and galbanum, and onyx, and aloes, and as the frankincense not cut, and my odour is as the purest balm. ^sirach-24-21-douay

22 I have stretched out my branches as the turpentine tree, and my branches are of honour and grace. ^sirach-24-22-douay

23 As the vine I have brought forth a pleasant odour: and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches. ^sirach-24-23-douay

24 I am the mother of fair love, and of fear, and of knowledge, and of holy hope. ^sirach-24-24-douay

25 In me is all grace of the way and of the truth, in me is all hope of life and of virtue. ^sirach-24-25-douay

26 Come over to me, all ye that desire me, and be filled with my fruits. ^sirach-24-26-douay

27 For my spirit is sweet above honey, and my inheritance above honey and the honeycomb. ^sirach-24-27-douay

28 My memory is unto everlasting generations. ^sirach-24-28-douay

29 They that eat me, shall yet hunger: and they that drink me, shall yet thirst. ^sirach-24-29-douay

30 He that hearkeneth to me, shall not be confounded: and they that work by me, shall not sin. ^sirach-24-30-douay

31 They that explain me shall have life everlasting. ^sirach-24-31-douay

32 All these things are the book of life, and the covenant of the most High, and the knowledge of truth. ^sirach-24-32-douay

33 Moses commanded a law in the precepts of justices, and an inheritance to the house of Jacob, and the promises to Israel. ^sirach-24-33-douay

34 He appointed to David his servant to raise up of him a most mighty king, and sitting on the throne of glory for ever. **A most mighty king...** Viz., Christ, who by his gospel, like an overflowing river, has enriched the earth with heavenly wisdom. ^sirach-24-34-douay

35 Who filleth up wisdom as the Phison, and as the Tigris in the days of the new fruits. ^sirach-24-35-douay

36 Who maketh understanding to abound as the Euphrates, who multiplieth it as the Jordan in the time of harvest. ^sirach-24-36-douay

37 Who sendeth knowledge as the light, and riseth up as Gehon in the time of the vintage. ^sirach-24-37-douay

38 Who first hath perfect knowledge of her, and a weaker shall not search her out. **Who first hath perfect knowledge of her...** Christ was the first that had perfect knowledge of heavenly wisdom. ^sirach-24-38-douay

39 For her thoughts are more vast than the sea, and her counsels more deep than the great ocean. ^sirach-24-39-douay

40 I, wisdom, have poured out rivers. ^sirach-24-40-douay

41 I, like a brook out of a river of a mighty water; I, like a channel of a river, and like an aqueduct, came out of paradise. ^sirach-24-41-douay

42 I said: I will water my garden of plants, and I will water abundantly the fruits of my meadow. ^sirach-24-42-douay

43 And behold my brook became a great river, and my river came near to a sea: ^sirach-24-43-douay

44 For I make doctrine to shine forth to all as the morning light, and I will declare it afar off. ^sirach-24-44-douay

45 I will penetrate to all the lower parts of the earth, and will behold all that sleep, and will enlighten all that hope in the Lord. ^sirach-24-45-douay

46 I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and will leave it to them that seek wisdom, and will not cease to instruct their offspring even to the holy age. ^sirach-24-46-douay

47 See ye that I have not laboured myself only, but for all that seek out the truth. ^sirach-24-47-douay
