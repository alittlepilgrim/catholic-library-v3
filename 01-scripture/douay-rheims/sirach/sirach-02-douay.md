# Sirach Chapter 2

God's servants must look for temptations: and must arm themselves with patience and confidence in God.

1 Son, when thou comest to the service of God, stand in justice and in fear, and prepare thy soul for temptation. ^sirach-02-1-douay

2 Humble thy heart, and endure: incline thy ear, and receive the words of understanding: and make not haste in the time of clouds. ^sirach-02-2-douay

3 Wait on God with patience: join thyself to God, and endure, that thy life may be increased in the latter end. ^sirach-02-3-douay

4 Take all that shall be brought upon thee: and in thy sorrow endure, and in thy humiliation keep patience. ^sirach-02-4-douay

5 For gold and silver are tried in the fire, but acceptable men in the furnace of humiliation. ^sirach-02-5-douay

6 Believe God, and he will recover thee: and direct thy way, and trust in him. Keep his fear, and grow old therein. ^sirach-02-6-douay

7 Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy: and go not aside from him lest ye fall. ^sirach-02-7-douay

8 Ye that fear the Lord, believe him: and your reward shall not be made void. ^sirach-02-8-douay

9 Ye that fear the Lord hope in him, and mercy shall come to you for your delight. ^sirach-02-9-douay

10 Ye that fear the Lord, love him, and your hearts shall be enlightened. ^sirach-02-10-douay

11 My children behold the generations of men: and know ye that no one hath hoped in the Lord, and hath been confounded. ^sirach-02-11-douay

12 For who hath continued in his commandment, and hath been forsaken? or who hath called upon him, and he despised him? ^sirach-02-12-douay

13 For God is compassionate and merciful, and will forgive sins in the day of tribulation: and he is a protector to all that seek him in truth. ^sirach-02-13-douay

14 Woe to them that are of a double heart and to wicked lips, and to the hands that do evil, and to the sinner that goeth on the earth two ways. ^sirach-02-14-douay

15 Woe to them that are fainthearted, who believe not God: and therefore they shall not be protected by him. ^sirach-02-15-douay

16 Woe to them that have lost patience, and that have forsaken the right ways, and have gone aside into crooked ways. ^sirach-02-16-douay

17 And what will they do, when the Lord shall begin to examine? ^sirach-02-17-douay

18 They that fear the Lord, will not be incredulous to his word: and they that love him, will keep his way. ^sirach-02-18-douay

19 They that fear the Lord, will seek after the things that are well pleasing to him: and they that love him, shall be filled with his law. ^sirach-02-19-douay

20 They that fear the Lord, will prepare their hearts, and in his sight will sanctify their souls, ^sirach-02-20-douay

21 They that fear the Lord, keep his commandments, and will have patience even until his visitation, ^sirach-02-21-douay

22 Saying: If we do not penance, we shall fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men. ^sirach-02-22-douay

23 For according to his greatness, so also is his mercy with him. ^sirach-02-23-douay
