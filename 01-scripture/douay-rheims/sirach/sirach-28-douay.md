# Sirach Chapter 28

Lessons against revenge and quarrels. The evils of the tongue.

1 He that seeketh to revenge himself, shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance. ^sirach-28-1-douay

2 Forgive thy neighbour if he hath hurt thee: and then shall thy sins be forgiven to thee when thou prayest. ^sirach-28-2-douay

3 Man to man reserveth anger, and doth he seek remedy of God? ^sirach-28-3-douay

4 He hath no mercy on a man like himself, and doth he entreat for his own sins? ^sirach-28-4-douay

5 He that is but flesh, nourisheth anger, and doth he ask forgiveness of God? who shall obtain pardon for his sins? ^sirach-28-5-douay

6 Remember thy last things, and let enmity cease: ^sirach-28-6-douay

7 For corruption and death hang over in his commandments. **In his commandments...** Supply the sentence out of the Greek thus: Remember corruption and death, and abide in the commandments. ^sirach-28-7-douay

8 Remember the fear of God, and be not angry with thy neighbour. ^sirach-28-8-douay

9 Remember the covenant of the most High, and overlook the ignorance of thy neighbour. ^sirach-28-9-douay

10 Refrain from strife, and thou shalt diminish thy sins. ^sirach-28-10-douay

11 For a passionate man kindleth strife, and a sinful man will trouble his friends, and bring in debate in the midst of them that are at peace. ^sirach-28-11-douay

12 For as the wood of the forest is, so the fire burneth, and as a man's strength is, so shall his anger be, and according to his riches he shall increase his anger. ^sirach-28-12-douay

13 A hasty contention kindleth a fire and a hasty quarrel sheddeth blood and a tongue that beareth witness bringeth death. ^sirach-28-13-douay

14 If thou blow the spark, it shall burn as a fire: and if thou spit upon it, it shall be quenched: both come out of the mouth. ^sirach-28-14-douay

15 The whisperer and the double tongue is accursed: for he hath troubled many that were at peace. ^sirach-28-15-douay

16 The tongue of a third person hath disquieted many, and scattered them from nation to nation. ^sirach-28-16-douay

17 It hath destroyed the strong cities of the rich, and hath overthrown the houses of great men. ^sirach-28-17-douay

18 It hath cut in pieces the forces of people, and undone strong nations. ^sirach-28-18-douay

19 The tongue of a third person hath cast out valiant women, and deprived them of their labours. ^sirach-28-19-douay

20 He that hearkeneth to it, shall never have rest, neither shall he have a friend in whom he may repose. ^sirach-28-20-douay

21 The stroke of a whip maketh a blue mark: but the stroke of the tongue will break the bones. ^sirach-28-21-douay

22 Many have fallen by the edge of the sword, but not so many as have perished by their own tongue. ^sirach-28-22-douay

23 Blessed is he that is defended from a wicked tongue, that hath not passed into the wrath thereof, and that hath not drawn the yoke thereof, and hath not been bound in its bands. ^sirach-28-23-douay

24 For its yoke is a yoke of iron: and its bands are bands of brass. ^sirach-28-24-douay

25 The death thereof is a most evil death: and hell is preferable to it. ^sirach-28-25-douay

26 Its continuance shall not be for a long time, but it shall possess the ways of the unjust: and the just shall not be burnt with its flame. ^sirach-28-26-douay

27 They that forsake God shall fall into it, and it shall burn in them, and shall not be quenched, and it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and as a leopard it shall tear them. ^sirach-28-27-douay

28 Hedge in thy ears with thorns, hear not a wicked tongue, and make doors and bars to thy mouth. ^sirach-28-28-douay

29 Melt down thy gold and silver, and make a balance for thy words, and a just bridle for thy mouth: ^sirach-28-29-douay

30 And take heed lest thou slip with thy tongue, and fall in the sight of thy enemies who lie in wait for thee, and thy fall be incurable unto death. ^sirach-28-30-douay
