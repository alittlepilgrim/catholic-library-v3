# Sirach Chapter 29

Of charity in lending money, and justice in repaying. Of alms, and of being surety.

1 He that showeth mercy, lendeth to his neighbour: and he that is stronger in hand, keepeth the commandments. **And he that is stronger in hand...** That is, he that is hearty and bountiful in lending to his neighbour in his necessity. ^sirach-29-1-douay

2 Lend to thy neighbour in the time of his need, and pay thou thy neighbour again in due time. ^sirach-29-2-douay

3 Keep thy word, and deal faithfully with him: and thou shalt always find that which is necessary for thee. ^sirach-29-3-douay

4 Many have looked upon a thing lent as a thing found, and have given trouble to them that helped them. ^sirach-29-4-douay

5 Till they receive, they kiss the hands of the lender, and in promises they humble their voice: ^sirach-29-5-douay

6 But when they should repay, they will ask time, and will return tedious and murmuring words, and will complain of the time: ^sirach-29-6-douay

7 And if he be able to pay, he will stand off, he will scarce pay one half, and will count it as if he had found it: ^sirach-29-7-douay

8 But if not, he will defraud him of his money, and he shall get him for an enemy without cause. ^sirach-29-8-douay

9 And he will pay him with reproaches and curses, and instead of honour and good turn will repay him injuries. ^sirach-29-9-douay

10 Many have refused to lend, not out of wickedness, but they were afraid to be defrauded without cause. ^sirach-29-10-douay

11 But yet towards the poor be thou more hearty, and delay not to show him mercy. ^sirach-29-11-douay

12 Help the poor because of the commandment: and send him not away empty handed because of his poverty. ^sirach-29-12-douay

13 Lose thy money for thy brother and thy friend: and hide it not under a stone to be lost. ^sirach-29-13-douay

14 Place thy treasure in the commandments of the most High, and it shall bring thee more profit than gold. ^sirach-29-14-douay

15 Shut up alms in the heart of the poor, and it shall obtain help for thee against all evil. ^sirach-29-15-douay

16 Better than the shield of the mighty, and better than the spear: ^sirach-29-16-douay

17 It shall fight for thee against thy enemy. ^sirach-29-17-douay

18 A good man is surety for his neighbour: and he that hath lost shame, will leave him to himself. ^sirach-29-18-douay

19 Forget not the kindness of thy surety: for he hath given his life for thee. ^sirach-29-19-douay

20 The sinner and the unclean fleeth from his surety. ^sirach-29-20-douay

21 A sinner attributeth to himself the goods of his surety: and he that is of an unthankful mind will leave him that delivered him. ^sirach-29-21-douay

22 A man is surety for his neighbour: and when he hath lost all shame, he shall forsake him. ^sirach-29-22-douay

23 Evil suretyship hath undone many of good estate, and hath tossed them as a wave of the sea. ^sirach-29-23-douay

24 It hath made powerful men to go from place to place round about, and they have wandered in strange countries. ^sirach-29-24-douay

25 A sinner that transgresseth the commandment of the Lord, shall fall into an evil suretyship: and he that undertaketh many things, shall fall into judgment. ^sirach-29-25-douay

26 Recover thy neighbour according to thy power, and take heed to thyself that thou fall not. ^sirach-29-26-douay

27 The chief thing for man's life is water and bread, and clothing, and a house to cover shame. ^sirach-29-27-douay

28 Better is the poor man's fare under a roof of boards, than sumptuous cheer abroad in another man's house. ^sirach-29-28-douay

29 Be contented with little instead of much, and thou shalt not hear the reproach of going abroad. ^sirach-29-29-douay

30 It is a miserable life to go as a guest from house to house: for where a man is a stranger, he shall not deal confidently, nor open his mouth. ^sirach-29-30-douay

31 He shall entertain and feed, and give drink to the unthankful, and moreover he shall hear bitter words. ^sirach-29-31-douay

32 Go, stranger, and furnish the table, and give others to eat what thou hast in thy hand. ^sirach-29-32-douay

33 Give place to the honourable presence of my friends: for I want my house, my brother being to be lodged with me. ^sirach-29-33-douay

34 These things are grievous to a man of understanding: the upbraiding of houseroom, and the reproaching of the lender. ^sirach-29-34-douay
