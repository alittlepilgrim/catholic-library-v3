# Sirach Chapter 1

All wisdom is from God, and is given to them that fear and love God.

1 All wisdom is from the Lord God, and hath been always with him, and is before all time. ^sirach-01-1-douay

2 Who hath numbered the sand of the sea, and the drops of rain, and the days of the world? Who hath measured the height of heaven, and the breadth of the earth, and the depth of the abyss? ^sirach-01-2-douay

3 Who hath searched out the wisdom of God that goeth before all things? ^sirach-01-3-douay

4 Wisdom hath been created before all things, and the understanding of prudence from everlasting. ^sirach-01-4-douay

5 The word of God on high is the fountain of wisdom, and her ways are everlasting commandments. ^sirach-01-5-douay

6 To whom hath the root of wisdom been revealed, and who hath known her wise counsels? ^sirach-01-6-douay

7 To whom hath the discipline of wisdom been revealed and made manifest? and who hath understood the multiplicity of her steps? ^sirach-01-7-douay

8 There is one most high Creator Almighty, and a powerful king, and greatly to be feared, who sitteth upon his throne, and is the God of dominion. ^sirach-01-8-douay

9 He created her in the Holy Ghost, and saw her, and numbered her, and measured her. ^sirach-01-9-douay

10 And he poured her out upon all his works, and upon all flesh according to his gift, and hath given her to them that love him. ^sirach-01-10-douay

11 The fear of the Lord is honour, and glory, and gladness, and a crown of joy. ^sirach-01-11-douay

12 The fear of the Lord shall delight the heart, and shall give joy, and gladness, and length of days. ^sirach-01-12-douay

13 With him that feareth the Lord, it shall go well in the latter end, and in the day of his death he shall be blessed. ^sirach-01-13-douay

14 The love of God is honourable wisdom. ^sirach-01-14-douay

15 And they to whom she shall show herself love her by the sight, and by the knowledge of her great works. ^sirach-01-15-douay

16 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and was created with the faithful in the womb, it walketh with chosen women, and is known with the just and faithful. ^sirach-01-16-douay

17 The fear of the Lord is the religiousness of knowledge. ^sirach-01-17-douay

18 Religiousness shall keep and justify the heart, it shall give joy and gladness. ^sirach-01-18-douay

19 It shall go well with him that feareth the Lord, and in the days of his end he shall be blessed. ^sirach-01-19-douay

20 To fear God is the fulness of wisdom, and fulness is from the fruits thereof. ^sirach-01-20-douay

21 She shall fill all her house with her increase, and the storehouses with her treasures. ^sirach-01-21-douay

22 The fear of the Lord is a crown of wisdom, filling up peace and the fruit of salvation: ^sirach-01-22-douay

23 And it hath seen, and numbered her: but both are the gifts of God. ^sirach-01-23-douay

24 Wisdom shall distribute knowledge, and understanding of prudence: and exalteth the glory of them that hold her. ^sirach-01-24-douay

25 The root of wisdom is to fear the Lord: and the branches thereof are long-lived. ^sirach-01-25-douay

26 In the treasures of wisdom is understanding, and religiousness of knowledge: but to sinners wisdom is an abomination. ^sirach-01-26-douay

27 The fear of the Lord driveth out sin: ^sirach-01-27-douay

28 For he that is without fear, cannot be justified: for the wrath of his high spirits is his ruin. ^sirach-01-28-douay

29 A patient man shall bear for a time, and afterwards joy shall be restored to him. ^sirach-01-29-douay

30 A good understanding will hide his words for a time, and the lips of many shall declare his wisdom. ^sirach-01-30-douay

31 In the treasures of wisdom is the signification of discipline: ^sirach-01-31-douay

32 But the worship of God is an abomination to a sinner. ^sirach-01-32-douay

33 Son, if thou desire wisdom, keep justice, and God will give her to thee. ^sirach-01-33-douay

34 For the fear of the Lord is wisdom and discipline: and that which is agreeable to him, ^sirach-01-34-douay

35 Is faith, and meekness: and he will fill up his treasures. ^sirach-01-35-douay

36 Be not incredulous to the fear of the Lord: and come not to him with a double heart. ^sirach-01-36-douay

37 Be not a hypocrite in the sight of men, and let not thy lips be a stumbling-block to thee. ^sirach-01-37-douay

38 Watch over them, lest thou fall, and bring dishonour upon thy soul, ^sirach-01-38-douay

39 And God discover thy secrets, and cast thee down in the midst of the congregation. ^sirach-01-39-douay

40 Because thou camest to the Lord wickedly, and thy heart is full of guile and deceit. ^sirach-01-40-douay
