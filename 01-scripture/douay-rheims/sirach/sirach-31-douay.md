# Sirach Chapter 31

Of the desire of riches, and of moderation in eating and drinking.

1 Watching for riches consumeth the flesh, and the thought thereof driveth away sleep. ^sirach-31-1-douay

2 The thinking beforehand turneth away the understanding, and a grievous sickness maketh the soul sober. ^sirach-31-2-douay

3 The rich man hath laboured in gathering riches together, and when he resteth he shall be filled with his goods. ^sirach-31-3-douay

4 The poor man hath laboured in his low way of life, and in the end he is still poor. ^sirach-31-4-douay

5 He that loveth gold, shall not be justified: and he that followeth after corruption, shall be filled with it. ^sirach-31-5-douay

6 Many have been brought to fall for gold, and the beauty thereof hath been their ruin. ^sirach-31-6-douay

7 Gold is a stumbling block to them that sacrifice to it: woe to them that eagerly follow after it, and every fool shall perish by it. ^sirach-31-7-douay

8 Blessed is the rich man that is found without blemish: and that hath not gone after gold, nor put his trust in money nor in treasures. ^sirach-31-8-douay

9 Who is he, and we will praise him? for he hath done wonderful things in his life. ^sirach-31-9-douay

10 Who hath been tried thereby, and made perfect, he shall have glory everlasting. He that could have transgressed, and hath not transgressed: and could do evil things, and hath not done them: ^sirach-31-10-douay

11 Therefore are his goods established in the Lord, and all the church of the saints shall declare his alms. ^sirach-31-11-douay

12 Art thou set at a great table? be not the first to open thy mouth upon it. ^sirach-31-12-douay

13 Say not: There are many things which are upon it. ^sirach-31-13-douay

14 Remember that a wicked eye is evil. ^sirach-31-14-douay

15 What is created more wicked than an eye? therefore shall it weep over all the face when it shall see. ^sirach-31-15-douay

16 Stretch not out thy hand first, lest being disgraced with envy thou be put to confusion. ^sirach-31-16-douay

17 Be not hasty in a feast. ^sirach-31-17-douay

18 Judge of the disposition of thy neighbour by thyself. ^sirach-31-18-douay

19 Use as a frugal man the things that are set before thee: lest if thou eatest much, thou be hated. ^sirach-31-19-douay

20 Leave off first, for manners' sake: and exceed not, lest thou offend. ^sirach-31-20-douay

21 And if thou sittest among many, reach not thy hand out first of all, and be not the first to ask for drink. ^sirach-31-21-douay

22 How sufficient is a little wine for a man well taught, and in sleeping thou shalt not be uneasy with it, and thou shalt feel no pain. ^sirach-31-22-douay

23 Watching, and choler, and gripes, are with an intemperate man: ^sirach-31-23-douay

24 Sound and wholesome sleep with a moderate man: he shall sleep till morning, and his soul shall be delighted with him. ^sirach-31-24-douay

25 And if thou hast been forced to eat much, arise, go out, and vomit: and it shall refresh thee, and thou shalt not bring sickness upon thy body. ^sirach-31-25-douay

26 Hear me, my son, and despise me not: and in the end thou shalt find my words. ^sirach-31-26-douay

27 In all thy works be quick, and no infirmity shall come to thee. ^sirach-31-27-douay

28 The lips of many shall bless him that is liberal of his bread, and the testimony of his truth is faithful. ^sirach-31-28-douay

29 Against him that is niggardly of his bread, the city will murmur, and the testimony of his niggardliness is true. ^sirach-31-29-douay

30 Challenge not them that love wine: for wine hath destroyed very many. ^sirach-31-30-douay

31 Fire trieth hard iron: so wine drunk to excess shall rebuke the hearts of the proud. ^sirach-31-31-douay

32 Wine taken with sobriety is equal life to men: if thou drink it moderately, thou shalt be sober. ^sirach-31-32-douay

33 What is his life, who is diminished with wine? ^sirach-31-33-douay

34 What taketh away life? death. ^sirach-31-34-douay

35 Wine was created from the beginning to make men joyful, and not to make them drunk. ^sirach-31-35-douay

36 Wine drunken with moderation is the joy of the soul and the heart. ^sirach-31-36-douay

37 Sober drinking is health to soul and body. ^sirach-31-37-douay

38 Wine drunken with excess raiseth quarrels, and wrath, and many ruins. ^sirach-31-38-douay

39 Wine drunken with excess is bitterness of the soul. ^sirach-31-39-douay

40 The heat of drunkenness is the stumbling block of the fool, lessening strength and causing wounds. ^sirach-31-40-douay

41 Rebuke not thy neighbour in a banquet of wine: and despise him not in his mirth. ^sirach-31-41-douay

42 Speak not to him words of reproach: and press him not in demanding again. ^sirach-31-42-douay
