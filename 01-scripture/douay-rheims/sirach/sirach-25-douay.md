# Sirach Chapter 25

Documents of wisdom on several subjects.

1 With three things my spirit is pleased, which are approved before God and men: ^sirach-25-1-douay

2 The concord of brethren, and the love of neighbours, and man and wife that agree well together. ^sirach-25-2-douay

3 Three sorts my soul hateth, and I am greatly grieved at their life: ^sirach-25-3-douay

4 A poor man that is proud: a rich man that is a liar: an old man that is a fool, and doting. ^sirach-25-4-douay

5 The things that thou hast not gathered in thy youth, how shalt thou find them in thy old age? ^sirach-25-5-douay

6 O how comely is judgment for a grey head, and for ancients to know counsel! ^sirach-25-6-douay

7 O how comely is wisdom for the aged, and understanding and counsel to men of honour! ^sirach-25-7-douay

8 Much experience is the crown of old men, and the fear of God is their glory. ^sirach-25-8-douay

9 Nine things that are not to be imagined by the heart have I magnified, and the tenth I will utter to men with my tongue. ^sirach-25-9-douay

10 A man that hath joy of his children: and he that liveth and seeth the fall of his enemies. ^sirach-25-10-douay

11 Blessed is he that dwelleth with a wise woman, and that hath not slipped with his tongue, and that hath not served such as are unworthy of him. ^sirach-25-11-douay

12 Blessed is he that findeth a true friend, and that declareth justice to an ear that heareth. ^sirach-25-12-douay

13 How great is he that findeth wisdom and knowledge! but there is none above him that feareth the Lord. ^sirach-25-13-douay

14 The fear of God hath set itself above all things: ^sirach-25-14-douay

15 Blessed is the man, to whom it is given to have the fear of God: he that holdeth it, to whom shall he be likened? ^sirach-25-15-douay

16 The fear of God is the beginning of his love: and the beginning of faith is to be fast joined unto it. ^sirach-25-16-douay

17 The sadness of the heart is every plague: and the wickedness of a woman is all evil. ^sirach-25-17-douay

18 And a man will choose any plague, but the plague of the heart: ^sirach-25-18-douay

19 And any wickedness, but the wickedness of a woman: ^sirach-25-19-douay

20 And any affliction, but the affliction from them that hate him: ^sirach-25-20-douay

21 And any revenge, but the revenge of enemies. ^sirach-25-21-douay

22 There is no head worse than the head of a serpent: ^sirach-25-22-douay

23 And there is no anger above the anger of a woman. It will be more agreeable to abide with a lion and a dragon, than to dwell with a wicked woman. ^sirach-25-23-douay

24 The wickedness of a woman changeth her face: and she darkeneth her countenance as a bear: and showeth it like sackcloth. In the midst of her neighbours, ^sirach-25-24-douay

25 Her husband groaned, and hearing he sighed a little. ^sirach-25-25-douay

26 All malice is short to the malice of a woman, let the lot of sinners fall upon her. ^sirach-25-26-douay

27 As the climbing of a sandy way is to the feet of the aged, so is a wife full of tongue to a quiet man. ^sirach-25-27-douay

28 Look not upon a woman's beauty, and desire not a woman for beauty. ^sirach-25-28-douay

29 A woman's anger, and impudence, and confusion is great. ^sirach-25-29-douay

30 A woman, if she have superiority, is contrary to her husband. ^sirach-25-30-douay

31 A wicked woman abateth the courage, and maketh a heavy countenance, and a wounded heart. ^sirach-25-31-douay

32 Feeble hands, and disjointed knees, a woman that doth not make her husband happy. ^sirach-25-32-douay

33 From the woman came the beginning of sin, and by her we all die. ^sirach-25-33-douay

34 Give no issue to thy water, no, not a little: nor to a wicked woman liberty to gad abroad. ^sirach-25-34-douay

35 If she walk not at thy hand, she will confound thee in the sight of thy enemies. ^sirach-25-35-douay

36 Cut her off from thy flesh, lest she always abuse thee. ^sirach-25-36-douay
