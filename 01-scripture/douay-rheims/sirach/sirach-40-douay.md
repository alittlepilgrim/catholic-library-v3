# Sirach Chapter 40

The miseries of the life of man are relieved by the grace of God and his fear.

1 Great labour is created for all men, and a heavy yoke is upon the children of Adam from the day of their coming out of their mother's womb, until the day of their burial into the mother of all. ^sirach-40-1-douay

2 Their thoughts, and fears of the heart, their imagination of things to come, and the day of their end: ^sirach-40-2-douay

3 From him that sitteth on a glorious throne, unto him that is humbled in earth and ashes: ^sirach-40-3-douay

4 From him that weareth purple, and beareth the crown, even to him that is covered with rough linen: wrath, envy, trouble, unquietness, and the fear of death, continual anger, and strife, ^sirach-40-4-douay

5 And in the time of rest upon his bed, the sleep of the night changeth his knowledge. ^sirach-40-5-douay

6 A little and as nothing is his rest, and afterward in sleep, as in the day of keeping watch. ^sirach-40-6-douay

7 He is troubled in the vision of his heart, as if he had escaped in the day of battle. In the time of his safety he rose up, and wondereth that there is no fear. ^sirach-40-7-douay

8 Such things happen to all flesh, from man even to beast, and upon sinners are sevenfold more. ^sirach-40-8-douay

9 Moreover, death, and bloodshed, strife, and sword, oppressions, famine, and affliction, and scourges: ^sirach-40-9-douay

10 All these things are created for the wicked, and for their sakes came the flood. ^sirach-40-10-douay

11 All things that are of the earth, shall return to the earth again, and all waters shall return to the sea. ^sirach-40-11-douay

12 All bribery, and injustice shall be blotted out, and fidelity shall stand for ever. ^sirach-40-12-douay

13 The riches of the unjust shall be dried up like a river, and shall pass away with a noise like a great thunder in rain. ^sirach-40-13-douay

14 While he openeth his hands he shall rejoice: but transgressors shall pine away in the end. ^sirach-40-14-douay

15 The offspring of the ungodly shall not bring forth many branches, and make a noise as unclean roots upon the top of a rock. ^sirach-40-15-douay

16 The weed growing over every water, and at the bank of the river, shall be pulled up before all grass. ^sirach-40-16-douay

17 Grace is like a paradise in blessings, and mercy remaineth for ever. ^sirach-40-17-douay

18 The life of a laborer that is content with what he hath, shall be sweet, and in it thou shalt find a treasure. ^sirach-40-18-douay

19 Children, and the building of a city shall establish a name, but a blameless wife shall be counted above them both. ^sirach-40-19-douay

20 Wine and music rejoice the heart, but the love of wisdom is above them both. ^sirach-40-20-douay

21 The flute and the psaltery make a sweet melody, but a pleasant tongue is above them both. ^sirach-40-21-douay

22 Thy eye desireth favour and beauty, but more than these green sown fields. ^sirach-40-22-douay

23 A friend and companion meeting together in season, but above them both is a wife with her husband. ^sirach-40-23-douay

24 Brethren are a help in the time of trouble, but mercy shall deliver more than they. ^sirach-40-24-douay

25 Gold and silver make the feet stand sure: but wise counsel is above them both. ^sirach-40-25-douay

26 Riches and strength lift up the heart: but above these is the fear of the Lord. ^sirach-40-26-douay

27 There is no want in the fear of the Lord, and it needeth not to seek for help. ^sirach-40-27-douay

28 The fear of the Lord is like a paradise of blessing, and they have covered it above all glory. ^sirach-40-28-douay

29 My son, in thy lifetime be not indigent: for it is better to die than to want. ^sirach-40-29-douay

30 The life of him that looketh toward another man's table is not to be counted a life: for he feedeth his soul with another man's meat. ^sirach-40-30-douay

31 But a man, well instructed and taught, will look to himself. ^sirach-40-31-douay

32 Begging will be sweet in the mouth of the unwise, but in his belly there shall burn a fire. ^sirach-40-32-douay
