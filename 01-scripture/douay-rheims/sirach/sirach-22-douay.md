# Sirach Chapter 22

Wise sayings on various subjects.

1 The sluggard is pelted with a dirty stone, and all men will speak of his disgrace. ^sirach-22-1-douay

2 The sluggard is pelted with the dung of oxen: and every one that toucheth him will shake his hands. ^sirach-22-2-douay

3 A son ill taught is the confusion of the father: and a foolish daughter shall be to his loss. ^sirach-22-3-douay

4 A wise daughter shall bring an inheritance to her husband: but she that confoundeth, becometh a disgrace to her father. ^sirach-22-4-douay

5 She that is bold shameth both her father and husband, and will not be inferior to the ungodly: and shall be disgraced by them both. ^sirach-22-5-douay

6 A tale out of time is like music in mourning: but the stripes and instruction of wisdom are never out of time. ^sirach-22-6-douay

7 He that teacheth a fool, is like one that glueth a potsherd together. ^sirach-22-7-douay

8 He that telleth a word to him that heareth not, is like one that waketh a man out of a deep sleep. ^sirach-22-8-douay

9 He speaketh with one that is asleep, who uttereth wisdom to a fool: and in the end of the discourse he saith: Who is this? ^sirach-22-9-douay

10 Weep for the dead, for his light hath failed: and weep for the fool, for his understanding faileth. **For the fool...** In the language of the Holy Ghost, he is styled a fool, that turns away from God to follow vanity and sin. And what is said by the wise man against fools is meant of such fools as these. ^sirach-22-10-douay

11 Weep but a little for the dead, for he is at rest. ^sirach-22-11-douay

12 For the wicked life of a wicked fool is worse than death. ^sirach-22-12-douay

13 The mourning for the dead is seven days: but for a fool and an ungodly man all the days of their life. ^sirach-22-13-douay

14 Talk not much with a fool and go not with him that hath no sense. ^sirach-22-14-douay

15 Keep thyself from him, that thou mayst not have trouble, and thou shalt not be defiled with his sin. ^sirach-22-15-douay

16 Turn away from him, and thou shalt find rest, and shalt not be wearied out with his folly. ^sirach-22-16-douay

17 What is heavier than lead? and what other name hath he but fool? ^sirach-22-17-douay

18 Sand and salt, and a mass of iron is easier to bear, than a man without sense, that is both foolish and wicked. ^sirach-22-18-douay

19 A frame of wood bound together in the foundation of a building, shall not be loosed: so neither shall the heart that is established by advised counsel. ^sirach-22-19-douay

20 The thought of him that is wise at all times, shall not be depraved by fear. ^sirach-22-20-douay

21 As pales set in high places, and plasterings made without cost, will not stand against the face of the wind: ^sirach-22-21-douay

22 So also a fearful heart in the imagination of a fool shall not resist against the violence of fear. ^sirach-22-22-douay

23 As a fearful heart in the thought of a fool at all times will not fear, so neither shall he that continueth always in the commandments of God. ^sirach-22-23-douay

24 He that pricketh the eye, bringeth out tears: and he that pricketh the heart, bringeth forth resentment. ^sirach-22-24-douay

25 He that flingeth a stone at birds, shall drive them away: so he that upbraideth his friend, breaketh friendship. ^sirach-22-25-douay

26 Although thou hast drawn a sword at a friend, despair not: for there may be a returning. To a friend, ^sirach-22-26-douay

27 If thou hast opened a sad mouth, fear not, for there may be a reconciliation: except upbraiding, and reproach, and pride, and disclosing of secrets, or a treacherous wound: for in all these cases a friend will flee away. ^sirach-22-27-douay

28 Keep fidelity with a friend in his poverty, that in his prosperity also thou mayst rejoice. ^sirach-22-28-douay

29 In the time of his trouble continue faithful to him, that thou mayst also be heir with him in his inheritance. ^sirach-22-29-douay

30 As the vapour of a chimney, and the smoke of the fire goeth up before the fire: so also injurious words, and reproaches, and threats, before blood. ^sirach-22-30-douay

31 I will not be ashamed to salute a friend, neither will I hide myself from his face: and if any evil happen to me by him, I will bear it. ^sirach-22-31-douay

32 But every one that shall hear it, will beware of him. ^sirach-22-32-douay

33 Who will set a guard before my mouth, and a sure seal upon my lips, that I fall not by them, and that my tongue destroy me not? ^sirach-22-33-douay
