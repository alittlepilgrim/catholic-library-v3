# Sirach Chapter 14

The evil of avarice: works of mercy are recommended, and the love of wisdom.

1 Blessed is the man that hath not slipped by a word out of his mouth, and is not pricked with the remorse of sin. ^sirach-14-1-douay

2 Happy is he that hath had no sadness of his mind, and who is not fallen from his hope. ^sirach-14-2-douay

3 Riches are not comely for a covetous man and a niggard, and what should an envious man do with gold? ^sirach-14-3-douay

4 He that gathereth together by wronging his own soul, gathereth for others, and another will squander away his goods in rioting. ^sirach-14-4-douay

5 He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? and he shall not take pleasure in his goods. ^sirach-14-5-douay

6 There is none worse than he that envieth himself, and this is the reward of his wickedness: ^sirach-14-6-douay

7 And if he do good, he doth it ignorantly, and unwillingly: and at the last he discovereth his wickedness. ^sirach-14-7-douay

8 The eye of the envious is wicked: and he turneth away his face, and despiseth his own soul. ^sirach-14-8-douay

9 The eye of the covetous man is insatiable in his portion of iniquity: he will not be satisfied till he consume his own soul, drying it up. ^sirach-14-9-douay

10 An evil eye is towards evil things: and he shall not have his fill of bread, but shall be needy and pensive at his own table. ^sirach-14-10-douay

11 My son, if thou have any thing, do good to thyself, and offer to God worthy offerings. ^sirach-14-11-douay

12 Remember that death is not slow, and that the covenant of hell hath been shown to thee: for the covenant of this world shall surely die. **Covenant of hell...** The decree by which all are to go down to the regions of death. ^sirach-14-12-douay

13 Do good to thy friend before thou die, and according to thy ability, stretching out thy hand give to the poor. ^sirach-14-13-douay

14 Defraud not thyself of the good day, and let not the part of a good gift overpass thee. ^sirach-14-14-douay

15 Shalt thou not leave to others to divide by lot thy sorrows and labours? ^sirach-14-15-douay

16 Give and take, and justify thy soul. ^sirach-14-16-douay

17 Before thy death work justice: for in hell there is no finding food. ^sirach-14-17-douay

18 All flesh shall fade as grass, and as the leaf that springeth out on a green tree. ^sirach-14-18-douay

19 Some grow, and some fall off: so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born. ^sirach-14-19-douay

20 Every work that is corruptible shall fail in the end: and the worker thereof shall go with it. ^sirach-14-20-douay

21 And every excellent work shall be justified: and the worker thereof shall be honoured therein. ^sirach-14-21-douay

22 Blessed is the man that shall continue in wisdom, and that shall meditate in his justice, and in his mind shall think of the all seeing eye of God. ^sirach-14-22-douay

23 He that considereth her ways in his heart, and hath understanding in her secrets, who goeth after her as one that traceth, and stayeth in her ways. ^sirach-14-23-douay

24 He who looketh in at her windows, and hearkeneth at her door. ^sirach-14-24-douay

25 He that lodgeth near her house, and fastening a pin in her walls shall set up his tent high unto her, where good things shall rest in his lodging for ever. ^sirach-14-25-douay

26 He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches: ^sirach-14-26-douay

27 He shall be protected under her covering from the heat, and shall rest in her glory. ^sirach-14-27-douay
