# Sirach Chapter 3

Lessons concerning the honour of parents, and humility, and avoiding curiosity.

1 The sons of wisdom are the church of the just: and their generation, obedience and love. ^sirach-03-1-douay

2 Children, hear the judgment of your father, and so do that you may be saved. ^sirach-03-2-douay

3 For God hath made the father honourable to the children: and seeking the judgment of the mothers, hath confirmed it upon the children. ^sirach-03-3-douay

4 He that loveth God, shall obtain pardon for his sins by prayer, and shall refrain himself from them, and shall be heard in the prayer of days. ^sirach-03-4-douay

5 And he that honoureth his mother is as one that layeth up a treasure. ^sirach-03-5-douay

6 He that honoureth his father shall have joy in his own children, and in the day of his prayer he shall be heard. ^sirach-03-6-douay

7 He that honoureth his father shall enjoy a long life: and he that obeyeth the father, shall be a comfort to his mother. ^sirach-03-7-douay

8 He that feareth the Lord, honoureth his parents, and will serve them as his masters that brought him into the world. ^sirach-03-8-douay

9 Honour thy father, in work and word, and all patience, ^sirach-03-9-douay

10 That a blessing may come upon thee from him, and his blessing may remain in the latter end. ^sirach-03-10-douay

11 The father's blessing establisheth the houses of the children: but the mother's curse rooteth up the foundation. ^sirach-03-11-douay

12 Glory not in the dishonour of thy father: for his shame is no glory to thee. ^sirach-03-12-douay

13 For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father, and a father without honour is the disgrace of the son. ^sirach-03-13-douay

14 Son, support the old age of thy father, and grieve him not in his life; ^sirach-03-14-douay

15 And if his understanding fail, have patience with him, and despise him not when thou art in thy strength: for the relieving of the father shall not be forgotten. ^sirach-03-15-douay

16 For good shall be repaid to thee for the sin of thy mother. ^sirach-03-16-douay

17 And in justice thou shalt be built up, and in the day of affliction thou shalt be remembered: and thy sins shall melt away as the ice in the fair warm weather. ^sirach-03-17-douay

18 Of what an evil fame is he that forsaketh his father: and he is cursed of God that angereth his mother. ^sirach-03-18-douay

19 My son, do thy works in meekness, and thou shalt be beloved above the glory of men. ^sirach-03-19-douay

20 The greater thou art, the more humble thyself in all things, and thou shalt find grace before God: ^sirach-03-20-douay

21 For great is the power of God alone, and he is honoured by the humble. ^sirach-03-21-douay

22 Seek not the things that are too high for thee, and search not into things above thy ability: but the things that God hath commanded thee, think on them always, and in many of his works be not curious. ^sirach-03-22-douay

23 For it is not necessary for thee to see with thy eyes those things that are hid. ^sirach-03-23-douay

24 In unnecessary matters be not over curious, and in many of his works thou shalt not be inquisitive. ^sirach-03-24-douay

25 For many things are shown to thee above the understanding of men. ^sirach-03-25-douay

26 And the suspicion of them hath deceived many, and hath detained their minds in vanity. ^sirach-03-26-douay

27 A hard heart shall fear evil at the last: and he that loveth danger shall perish in it. ^sirach-03-27-douay

28 A heart that goeth two ways shall not have success, and the perverse of heart shall be scandalized therein. ^sirach-03-28-douay

29 A wicked heart shall be laden with sorrows, and the sinner will add sin to sin. ^sirach-03-29-douay

30 The congregation of the proud shall not be healed: for the plant of wickedness shall take root in them, and it shall not be perceived. ^sirach-03-30-douay

31 The heart of the wise is understood in wisdom, and a good ear will hear wisdom with all desire. ^sirach-03-31-douay

32 A wise heart, and which hath understanding, will abstain from sins, and in the works of justice shall have success. ^sirach-03-32-douay

33 Water quencheth a flaming fire, and alms resisteth sins: ^sirach-03-33-douay

34 And God provideth for him that showeth favour: he remembereth him afterwards, and in the time of his fall he shall find a sure stay. ^sirach-03-34-douay
