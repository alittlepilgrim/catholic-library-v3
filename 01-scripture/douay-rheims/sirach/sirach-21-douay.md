# Sirach Chapter 21

Cautions against sin in general, and some sins in particular.

1 My son, hast thou sinned? do so no more: but for thy former sins also pray that they may be forgiven thee. ^sirach-21-1-douay

2 Flee from sins as from the face of a serpent: for if thou comest near them, they will take hold of thee. ^sirach-21-2-douay

3 The teeth thereof are the teeth of a lion, killing the souls of men. ^sirach-21-3-douay

4 All iniquity is like a two-edged sword, there is no remedy for the wound thereof. ^sirach-21-4-douay

5 Injuries and wrongs will waste riches: and the house that is very rich shall be brought to nothing by pride: so the substance of the proud shall be rooted out. ^sirach-21-5-douay

6 The prayer out of the mouth of the poor shall reach the ears of God, and judgment shall come for him speedily. ^sirach-21-6-douay

7 He that hateth to be reproved walketh in the trace of a sinner: and he that feareth God will turn to his own heart. ^sirach-21-7-douay

8 He that is mighty by a bold tongue is known afar off, but a wise man knoweth to slip by him. ^sirach-21-8-douay

9 He that buildeth his house at other men's charges, is as he that gathereth himself stones to build in the winter. ^sirach-21-9-douay

10 The congregation of sinners is like tow heaped together, and the end of them is a flame of fire. ^sirach-21-10-douay

11 The way of sinners is made plain with stones, and in their end is hell, and darkness, and pains. ^sirach-21-11-douay

12 He that keepeth justice shall get the understanding thereof. ^sirach-21-12-douay

13 The perfection of the fear of God is wisdom and understanding. ^sirach-21-13-douay

14 He that is not wise in good, will not be taught. ^sirach-21-14-douay

15 But there is a wisdom that aboundeth in evil: and there is no understanding where there is bitterness. ^sirach-21-15-douay

16 The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood, and his counsel continueth like a fountain of life. ^sirach-21-16-douay

17 The heart of a fool is like a broken vessel, and no wisdom at all shall it hold. ^sirach-21-17-douay

18 A man of sense will praise every wise word he shall hear, and will apply it to himself: the luxurious man hath heard it, and it shall displease him, and he will cast it behind his back. ^sirach-21-18-douay

19 The talking of a fool is like a burden in the way: but in the lips of the wise, grace shall be found. ^sirach-21-19-douay

20 The mouth of the prudent is sought after in the church, and they will think upon his words in their hearts. ^sirach-21-20-douay

21 As a house that is destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool: and the knowledge of the unwise is as words without sense. ^sirach-21-21-douay

22 Doctrine to a fool is as fetters on the feet, and like manacles on the right hand. ^sirach-21-22-douay

23 A fool lifteth up his voice in laughter: but a wise man will scarce laugh low to himself. ^sirach-21-23-douay

24 Learning to the prudent is as an ornament of gold, and like a bracelet upon his right arm. ^sirach-21-24-douay

25 The foot of a fool is soon in his neighbour's house: but a man of experience will be abashed at the person of the mighty. ^sirach-21-25-douay

26 A fool will peep through the window into the house: but he that is well taught will stand without. ^sirach-21-26-douay

27 It is the folly of a man to hearken at the door: and a wise man will be grieved with the disgrace. ^sirach-21-27-douay

28 The lips of the unwise will be telling foolish things: but the words of the wise shall be weighed in a balance. ^sirach-21-28-douay

29 The heart of fools is in their mouth: and the mouth of wise men is in their heart. ^sirach-21-29-douay

30 While the ungodly curseth the devil, he curseth his own soul. **While the ungodly, etc...** He condemneth and curseth himself: inasmuch as by sin he takes part with the devil, and is, as it were, his member and subject. ^sirach-21-30-douay

31 The talebearer shall defile his own soul, and shall be hated by all: and he that shall abide with him shall be hateful: the silent and wise man shall be honoured. ^sirach-21-31-douay
