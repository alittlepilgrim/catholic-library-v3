# Sirach Chapter 18

God's works are wonderful: we must serve him, and not our lusts.

1 He that liveth for ever created all things together. God only shall be justified, and he remaineth an invincible king for ever. ^sirach-18-1-douay

2 Who is able to declare his works? ^sirach-18-2-douay

3 For who shall search out his glorious acts? ^sirach-18-3-douay

4 And who shall show forth the power of his majesty? or who shall be able to declare his mercy? ^sirach-18-4-douay

5 Nothing may be taken away, nor added, neither is it possible to find out the glorious works of God. ^sirach-18-5-douay

6 When a man hath done, then shall he begin: and when he leaveth off, he shall be at a loss. **Then shall he begin...** God is so great and incomprehensible, that when man has done all that he can to find out his greatness and boundless perfections, he is still to begin: for what he has found out, is but a mere nothing in comparison with his infinity. ^sirach-18-6-douay

7 What is man, and what is his grace? and what is his good, or what is his evil? ^sirach-18-7-douay

8 The number of the days of men at the most are a hundred years, as a drop of water of the sea are they esteemed: and as a pebble of the sand, so are a few years compared to eternity. ^sirach-18-8-douay

9 Therefore God is patient in them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them. ^sirach-18-9-douay

10 He hath seen the presumption of their heart that it is wicked, and hath known their end that it is evil. ^sirach-18-10-douay

11 Therefore hath he filled up his mercy in their favour, and hath shown them the way of justice. ^sirach-18-11-douay

12 The compassion of man is toward his neighbour: but the mercy of God is upon all flesh. ^sirach-18-12-douay

13 He hath mercy, and teacheth, and correcteth, as a shepherd doth his flock. ^sirach-18-13-douay

14 He hath mercy on him that receiveth the discipline of mercy, and that maketh haste in his judgments. ^sirach-18-14-douay

15 My son, in thy good deeds, make no complaint, and when thou givest any thing, add not grief by an evil word. ^sirach-18-15-douay

16 Shall not the dew assuage the heat? so also the good word is better than the gift. ^sirach-18-16-douay

17 Lo, is not a word better than a gift? but both are with a justified man. ^sirach-18-17-douay

18 A fool will upbraid bitterly: and a gift of one ill taught consumeth the eyes. ^sirach-18-18-douay

19 Before judgment prepare thee justice, and learn before thou speak. ^sirach-18-19-douay

20 Before sickness take a medicine, and before judgment examine thyself, and thou shalt find mercy in the sight of God. ^sirach-18-20-douay

21 Humble thyself before thou art sick, and in the time of sickness show thy conversation. ^sirach-18-21-douay

22 Let nothing hinder thee from praying always, and be not afraid to be justified even to death: for the reward of God continueth for ever. ^sirach-18-22-douay

23 Before prayer prepare thy soul: and be not as a man that tempteth God. ^sirach-18-23-douay

24 Remember the wrath that shall be at the last day, and the time of repaying when he shall turn away his face. ^sirach-18-24-douay

25 Remember poverty in the time of abundance, and the necessities of poverty in the day of riches. ^sirach-18-25-douay

26 From the morning until the evening the time shall be changed, and all these are swift in the eyes of God. ^sirach-18-26-douay

27 A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the days of sins will beware of sloth. ^sirach-18-27-douay

28 Every man of understanding knoweth wisdom, and will give praise to him that findeth her. ^sirach-18-28-douay

29 They that were of good understanding in words, have also done wisely themselves: and have understood truth and justice, and have poured forth proverbs and judgments. ^sirach-18-29-douay

30 Go not after thy lusts, but turn away from thy own will. ^sirach-18-30-douay

31 If thou give to thy soul her desires, she will make thee a joy to thy enemies. ^sirach-18-31-douay

32 Take no pleasure in riotous assemblies, be they ever so small: for their concertation is continual. ^sirach-18-32-douay

33 Make not thyself poor by borrowing to contribute to feasts when thou hast nothing in thy purse: for thou shalt be an enemy to thy own life. ^sirach-18-33-douay
