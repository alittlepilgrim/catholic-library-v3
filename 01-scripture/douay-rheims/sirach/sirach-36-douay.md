# Sirach Chapter 36

A prayer for the Church of God. Of a good heart, and a good wife.

1 Have mercy upon us, O God of all, and behold us, and show us the light of thy mercies: ^sirach-36-1-douay

2 And send thy fear upon the nations, that have not sought after thee: that they may know that there is no God beside thee, and that they may show forth thy wonders. ^sirach-36-2-douay

3 Lift up thy hand over the strange nations, that they may see thy power. ^sirach-36-3-douay

4 For as thou hast been sanctified in us in their sight, so thou shalt be magnified among them in our presence, ^sirach-36-4-douay

5 That they may know thee, as we also have known thee, that there is no God beside thee, O Lord. ^sirach-36-5-douay

6 Renew thy signs, and work new miracles. ^sirach-36-6-douay

7 Glorify thy hand, and thy right arm. ^sirach-36-7-douay

8 Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath. ^sirach-36-8-douay

9 Take away the adversary, and crush the enemy. ^sirach-36-9-douay

10 Hasten the time, and remember the end, that they may declare thy wonderful works. ^sirach-36-10-douay

11 Let him that escapeth be consumed by the rage of the fire: and let them perish that oppress thy people. ^sirach-36-11-douay

12 Crush the head of the princes of the enemies that say: There is no other beside us. ^sirach-36-12-douay

13 Gather together all the tribes of Jacob: that they may know that there no God besides thee, and may declare thy great works: and thou shalt inherit them as from the beginning. ^sirach-36-13-douay

14 Have mercy on thy people, upon whom thy name is invoked: and upon Israel, whom thou hast raised up to be thy firstborn. ^sirach-36-14-douay

15 Have mercy on Jerusalem, the city which thou hast sanctified, the city of thy rest. ^sirach-36-15-douay

16 Fill Sion with thy unspeakable words, and thy people with thy glory. ^sirach-36-16-douay

17 Give testimony to them that are thy creatures from the beginning, and raise up the prophecies which the former prophets spoke in thy name. ^sirach-36-17-douay

18 Reward them that patiently wait for thee, that thy prophets may be found faithful: and hear the prayers of thy servants, ^sirach-36-18-douay

19 According to the blessing of Aaron over thy people, and direct us into the way of justice, and let all know that dwell upon the earth, that thou art God the beholder of all ages. ^sirach-36-19-douay

20 The belly will devour all meat, yet one is better than another. ^sirach-36-20-douay

21 The palate tasteth venison and the wise heart false speeches. ^sirach-36-21-douay

22 A perverse heart will cause grief, and a man of experience will resist it. ^sirach-36-22-douay

23 A woman will receive every man: yet one daughter is better than another. **A woman will receive every man...** That is, any man that her parents propose to her to marry, though she does not like him, but marries in obedience to her parents, who make the choice for her. ^sirach-36-23-douay

24 The beauty of a woman cheereth the countenance of her husband, and a man desireth nothing more. ^sirach-36-24-douay

25 If she have a tongue that can cure, and likewise mitigate and show mercy: her husband is not like other men. ^sirach-36-25-douay

26 He that possesseth a good wife, beginneth a possession: she is a help like to himself, and a pillar of rest. ^sirach-36-26-douay

27 Where there is no hedge, the possession shall be spoiled: and where there is no wife, he mourneth that is in want. ^sirach-36-27-douay

28 Who will trust him that hath no rest, and that lodgeth wheresoever the night taketh him, as a robber well appointed, that skippeth from city to city. ^sirach-36-28-douay
