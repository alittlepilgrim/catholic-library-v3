# Sirach Chapter 43

The works of God are exceedingly glorious and wonderful: no man is able sufficiently to praise him.

1 The firmament on high is his beauty, the beauty of heaven with its glorious show. ^sirach-43-1-douay

2 The sun when he appeareth showing forth at his rising, an admirable instrument, the work of the most High. ^sirach-43-2-douay

3 At noon he burneth the earth, and who can abide his burning heat? As one keeping a furnace in the works of heat: ^sirach-43-3-douay

4 The sun three times as much, burneth the mountains, breathing out fiery vapours, and shining with his beams, he blindeth the eyes. ^sirach-43-4-douay

5 Great is the Lord that made him, and at his words he hath hastened his course. ^sirach-43-5-douay

6 And the moon in all in her season, is for a declaration of times and a sign of the world. ^sirach-43-6-douay

7 From the moon is the sign of the festival day, a light that decreaseth in her perfection. ^sirach-43-7-douay

8 The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her perfection. ^sirach-43-8-douay

9 Being an instrument of the armies on high, shining gloriously in the firmament of heaven. ^sirach-43-9-douay

10 The glory of the stars is the beauty of heaven; the Lord enlighteneth the world on high. ^sirach-43-10-douay

11 By the words of the holy one they stand in judgment, and shall never fall in their watches. ^sirach-43-11-douay

12 Look upon the rainbow, and bless him that made it: it is very beautiful in its brightness. ^sirach-43-12-douay

13 It encompasseth the heaven about with the circle of its glory, the hands of the most High have displayed it. ^sirach-43-13-douay

14 By his commandment he maketh the snow to fall apace, and sendeth forth swiftly the lightnings of his judgment. ^sirach-43-14-douay

15 Through this are the treasures opened, and the clouds fly out like birds. ^sirach-43-15-douay

16 By his greatness he hath fixed the clouds, and the hailstones are broken. ^sirach-43-16-douay

17 At his sight shall the mountains be shaken, and at his will the south wind shall blow. ^sirach-43-17-douay

18 The noise of his thunder shall strike the earth, so doth the northern storm, and the whirlwind: ^sirach-43-18-douay

19 And as the birds lighting upon the earth, he scattereth snow, and the falling thereof, is as the coming down of locusts. ^sirach-43-19-douay

20 The eye admireth at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the shower thereof. ^sirach-43-20-douay

21 He shall pour frost as salt upon the earth: and when it freezeth, it shall become like the tops of thistles. ^sirach-43-21-douay

22 The cold north wind bloweth, and the water is congealed into crystal; upon every gathering together of waters it shall rest, and shall clothe the waters as a breastplate. ^sirach-43-22-douay

23 And it shall devour the mountains, and burn the wilderness, and consume all that is green as with fire. ^sirach-43-23-douay

24 A present remedy of all is the speedy coming of a cloud, and a dew that meeteth it, by the heat that cometh, shall overpower it. ^sirach-43-24-douay

25 At his word the wind is still, and with his thought he appeaseth the deep, and the Lord hath planted islands therein. ^sirach-43-25-douay

26 Let them that sail on the sea, tell the dangers thereof: and when we hear with our ears, we shall admire. ^sirach-43-26-douay

27 There are great and wonderful works: a variety of beasts, and of all living things, and the monstrous creatures of whales. ^sirach-43-27-douay

28 Through him is established the end of their journey, and by his word all things are regulated. ^sirach-43-28-douay

29 We shall say much, and yet shall want words: but the sum of our words is, He is all. ^sirach-43-29-douay

30 What shall we be able to do to glorify him? for the Almighty himself is above all his works. ^sirach-43-30-douay

31 The Lord is terrible, and exceeding great, and his power is admirable. ^sirach-43-31-douay

32 Glorify the Lord as much as ever you can, for he will yet far exceed, and his magnificence is wonderful. ^sirach-43-32-douay

33 Blessing the Lord, exalt him as much as you can; for he is above all praise. ^sirach-43-33-douay

34 When you exalt him put forth all your strength, and be not weary: for you can never go far enough. ^sirach-43-34-douay

35 Who shall see him, and declare him? and who shall magnify him as he is from the beginning? ^sirach-43-35-douay

36 There are many things hidden from us that are greater than these: for we have seen but a few of his works. ^sirach-43-36-douay

37 But the Lord hath made all things, and to the godly he hath given wisdom. ^sirach-43-37-douay
