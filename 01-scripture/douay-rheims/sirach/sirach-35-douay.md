# Sirach Chapter 35

What sacrifices are pleasing to God.

1 He that keepeth the law, multiplieth offerings. ^sirach-35-1-douay

2 It is a wholesome sacrifice to take heed to the commandments, and to depart from all iniquity. ^sirach-35-2-douay

3 And to depart from injustice, is to offer a propitiatory sacrifice for injustices, and a begging of pardon for sins. ^sirach-35-3-douay

4 He shall return thanks, that offereth fine flour: and he that doth mercy, offereth sacrifice. ^sirach-35-4-douay

5 To depart from iniquity is that which pleaseth the Lord, and to depart from injustice, is an entreaty for sins. ^sirach-35-5-douay

6 Thou shalt not appear empty in the sight of the Lord. ^sirach-35-6-douay

7 For all these things are to be done because of the commandment of God. ^sirach-35-7-douay

8 The oblation of the just maketh the altar fat, and is an odour of sweetness in the sight of the most High. ^sirach-35-8-douay

9 The sacrifice of the just is acceptable, and the Lord will not forget the memorial thereof. ^sirach-35-9-douay

10 Give glory to God with a good heart: and diminish not the firstfruits of thy hands. ^sirach-35-10-douay

11 In every gift show a cheerful countenance, and sanctify thy tithes with joy. ^sirach-35-11-douay

12 Give to the most High according to what he hath given to thee, and with a good eye do according to the ability of thy hands: ^sirach-35-12-douay

13 For the Lord maketh recompense, and will give thee seven times as much. ^sirach-35-13-douay

14 Do not offer wicked gifts, for such he will not receive. ^sirach-35-14-douay

15 And look not upon an unjust sacrifice, for the Lord is judge, and there is not with him respect of person. ^sirach-35-15-douay

16 The Lord will not accept any person against a poor man, and he will hear the prayer of him that is wronged. ^sirach-35-16-douay

17 He will not despise the prayers of the fatherless: nor the widow, when she poureth out her complaint. ^sirach-35-17-douay

18 Do not the widow's tears run down the cheek, and her cry against him that causeth them to fall? ^sirach-35-18-douay

19 For from the cheek they go up even to heaven, and the Lord that heareth will not be delighted with them. ^sirach-35-19-douay

20 He that adoreth God with joy, shall be accepted, and his prayer shall approach even to the clouds. ^sirach-35-20-douay

21 The prayer of him that humbleth himself, shall pierce the clouds: and till it come nigh he will not be comforted: and he will not depart till the most High behold. ^sirach-35-21-douay

22 And the Lord will not be slack, but will judge for the just, and will do judgment: and the Almighty will not have patience with them, that he may crush their back: ^sirach-35-22-douay

23 And he will repay vengeance to the Gentiles, till he have taken away the multitude of the proud, and broken the sceptres of the unjust, ^sirach-35-23-douay

24 Till he have rendered to men according to their deeds: and according to the works of Adam, and according to his presumption, ^sirach-35-24-douay

25 Till he have judged the cause of his people, and he shall delight the just with his mercy. ^sirach-35-25-douay

26 The mercy of God is beautiful in the time of affliction, as a cloud of rain in the time of drought. ^sirach-35-26-douay
