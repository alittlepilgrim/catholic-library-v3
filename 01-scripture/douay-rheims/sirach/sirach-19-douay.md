# Sirach Chapter 19

Admonition against sundry vices.

1 A workman that is a drunkard shall not be rich: and he that contemneth small things, shall fall by little and little. ^sirach-19-1-douay

2 Wine and women make wise men fall off, and shall rebuke the prudent: ^sirach-19-2-douay

3 And he that joineth himself to harlots, will be wicked. Rottenness and worms shall inherit him, and he shall be lifted up for a greater example, and his soul shall be taken away out of the number. ^sirach-19-3-douay

4 He that is hasty to give credit, is light of heart, and shall be lessened: and he that sinneth against his own soul, shall be despised. ^sirach-19-4-douay

5 He that rejoiceth in iniquity, shall be censured, and he that hateth chastisement, shall have less life: and he that hateth babbling, extinguisheth evil. ^sirach-19-5-douay

6 He that sinneth against his own soul, shall repent: and he that is delighted with wickedness, shall be condemned. ^sirach-19-6-douay

7 Rehearse not again a wicked and harsh word, and thou shalt not fare the worse. ^sirach-19-7-douay

8 Tell not thy mind to friend or foe: and if there be a sin with thee, disclose it not. ^sirach-19-8-douay

9 For he will hearken to thee, and will watch thee, and as it were defending thy sin he will hate thee, and so will he be with thee always. ^sirach-19-9-douay

10 Hast thou heard a word against thy neighbour? let it die within thee, trusting that it will not burst thee. ^sirach-19-10-douay

11 At the hearing of a word the fool is in travail, as a woman groaning in the bringing forth a child. ^sirach-19-11-douay

12 As an arrow that sticketh in a man's thigh: so is a word in the heart of a fool. ^sirach-19-12-douay

13 Reprove a friend, lest he may not have understood, and say: I did it not: or if he did it, that he may do it no more. ^sirach-19-13-douay

14 Reprove thy neighbour, for it may be he hath not said it: and if he hath said it, that he may not say it again. ^sirach-19-14-douay

15 Admonish thy friend: for there is often a fault committed. ^sirach-19-15-douay

16 And believe not every word. There is one, that slippeth with the tongue, but not from his heart. ^sirach-19-16-douay

17 For who is there that hath not offended with his tongue? Admonish thy neighbour before thou threaten him. ^sirach-19-17-douay

18 And give place to the fear of the most High: for the fear of God is all wisdom, and therein is to fear God, and the disposition of the law is in all wisdom. ^sirach-19-18-douay

19 But the learning of wickedness is not wisdom: and the device of sinners is not prudence. ^sirach-19-19-douay

20 There is a subtle wickedness, and the same is detestable: and there is a man that is foolish, wanting in wisdom. ^sirach-19-20-douay

21 Better is a man that hath less wisdom, and wanteth understanding, with the fear of God, than he that aboundeth in understanding, and transgresseth the law of the most High. ^sirach-19-21-douay

22 There is an exquisite subtilty, and the same is unjust. ^sirach-19-22-douay

23 And there is one that uttereth an exact word telling the truth. There is one that humbleth himself wickedly, and his interior is full of deceit: ^sirach-19-23-douay

24 And there is one that submitteth himself exceedingly with a great lowliness: and there is one that casteth down his countenance, and maketh as if he did not see that which is unknown: ^sirach-19-24-douay

25 And if he be hindered from sinning for want of power, if he shall find opportunity to do evil, he will do it. ^sirach-19-25-douay

26 A man is known by his look, and a wise man, when thou meetest him, is known by his countenance. ^sirach-19-26-douay

27 The attire of the body, and the laughter of the teeth, and the gait of the man, show what he is. ^sirach-19-27-douay

28 There is a lying rebuke in the anger of an injurious man: and there is a judgment that is not allowed to be good: and there is one that holdeth his peace, he is wise. ^sirach-19-28-douay
