# Sirach Chapter 37

Of the choice of friends and counsellors.

1 Every friend will say: I also am his friend: but there is a friend, that is only a friend in name. Is not this a grief even to death? ^sirach-37-1-douay

2 But a companion and a friend shall be turned to an enemy. ^sirach-37-2-douay

3 O wicked presumption, whence camest thou to cover the earth with thy malice, and deceitfulness? ^sirach-37-3-douay

4 There is a companion who rejoiceth with his friend in his joys, but in the time of trouble, he will be against him. ^sirach-37-4-douay

5 There is a companion who condoleth with his friend for his belly's sake, and he will take up a shield against the enemy. ^sirach-37-5-douay

6 Forget not thy friend in thy mind, and be not unmindful of him in thy riches. ^sirach-37-6-douay

7 Consult not with him that layeth a snare for thee, and hide thy counsel from them that envy thee. ^sirach-37-7-douay

8 Every counsellor giveth out counsel, but there is one that is a counsellor for himself. ^sirach-37-8-douay

9 Beware of a counsellor. And know before what need he hath: for he will devise to his own mind: ^sirach-37-9-douay

10 Lest he thrust a stake into the ground, and say to thee: ^sirach-37-10-douay

11 Thy way is good; and then stand on the other side to see what shall befall thee. ^sirach-37-11-douay

12 Treat not with a man without religion concerning holiness, nor with an unjust man concerning justice, nor with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous, nor with a coward concerning war, nor with a merchant about traffic, nor with a buyer of selling, nor with an envious man of giving thanks, ^sirach-37-12-douay

13 Nor with the ungodly of piety, nor with the dishonest of honesty, nor with the field laborer of every work, ^sirach-37-13-douay

14 Nor with him that worketh by the year of the finishing of the year, nor with an idle servant of much business: give no heed to these in any matter of counsel. ^sirach-37-14-douay

15 But be continually with a holy man, whomsoever thou shalt know to observe the fear of God, ^sirach-37-15-douay

16 Whose soul is according to thy own soul: and who, when thou shalt stumble in the dark, will be sorry for thee. ^sirach-37-16-douay

17 And establish within thyself a heart of good counsel: for there is no other thing of more worth to thee than it. ^sirach-37-17-douay

18 The soul of a holy man discovereth sometimes true things, more than seven watchmen that sit in a high place to watch. ^sirach-37-18-douay

19 But above all these things pray to the most High, that he may direct thy way in truth. ^sirach-37-19-douay

20 In all thy works let the true word go before thee, and steady counsel before every action. ^sirach-37-20-douay

21 A wicked word shall change the heart: out of which four manner of things arise, good and evil, life and death: and the tongue is continually the ruler of them. There is a man that is subtle and a teacher of many, and yet is unprofitable to his own soul. ^sirach-37-21-douay

22 A skilful man hath taught many, and is sweet to his own soul. ^sirach-37-22-douay

23 He that speaketh sophistically, is hateful: he shall be destitute of every thing. ^sirach-37-23-douay

24 Grace is not given him from the Lord: for he is deprived of all wisdom. ^sirach-37-24-douay

25 There is a wise man that is wise to his own soul: and the fruit of his understanding is commendable. ^sirach-37-25-douay

26 A wise man instructeth his own people, and the fruits of his understanding are faithful. ^sirach-37-26-douay

27 A wise man shall be filled with blessings, and they that see shall praise him. ^sirach-37-27-douay

28 The life of a man is in the number of his days: but the days of Israel are innumerable. ^sirach-37-28-douay

29 A wise man shall inherit honour among his people, and his name shall live for ever. ^sirach-37-29-douay

30 My son, prove thy soul in thy life: and if it be wicked, give it no power: ^sirach-37-30-douay

31 For all things are not expedient for all, and every kind pleaseth not every soul. ^sirach-37-31-douay

32 Be not greedy in any feasting, and pour not out thyself upon any meat: ^sirach-37-32-douay

33 For in many meats there will be sickness, and greediness will turn to choler. ^sirach-37-33-douay

34 By surfeiting many have perished, but he that is temperate, shall prolong life. ^sirach-37-34-douay
