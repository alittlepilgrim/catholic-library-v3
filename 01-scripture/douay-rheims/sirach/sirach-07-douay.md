# Sirach Chapter 7

Religious and moral duties.

1 Do no evils, and no evils shall lay hold of thee. ^sirach-07-1-douay

2 Depart from the unjust, and evils shall depart from thee. ^sirach-07-2-douay

3 My son, sow not evils in the furrows of injustice, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold. ^sirach-07-3-douay

4 Seek not of the Lord a preeminence, nor of the king the seat of honour. ^sirach-07-4-douay

5 Justify not thyself before God, for he knoweth the heart: and desire not to appear wise before the king. ^sirach-07-5-douay

6 Seek not to be made a judge, unless thou have strength enough to extirpate iniquities: lest thou fear the person of the powerful, and lay a stumbling block for thy integrity. ^sirach-07-6-douay

7 Offend not against the multitude of a city, neither cast thyself in upon the people, ^sirach-07-7-douay

8 Nor bind sin to sin: for even in one thou shalt not be unpunished. ^sirach-07-8-douay

9 Be not fainthearted in thy mind: ^sirach-07-9-douay

10 Neglect not to pray, and to give alms. ^sirach-07-10-douay

11 Say not: God will have respect to the multitude of my gifts, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept my offerings. ^sirach-07-11-douay

12 Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one that humbleth and exalteth, God who seeth all. ^sirach-07-12-douay

13 Devise not a lie against thy brother: neither do the like against thy friend. ^sirach-07-13-douay

14 Be not willing to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good. ^sirach-07-14-douay

15 Be not full of words in a multitude of ancients, and repeat not the word in thy prayer. **Repeat not, etc...** Make not much babbling by repetition of words: but aim more at fervour of heart. ^sirach-07-15-douay

16 Hate not laborious works, nor husbandry ordained by the most High. ^sirach-07-16-douay

17 Number not thyself among the multitude of the disorderly. ^sirach-07-17-douay

18 Remember wrath, for it will not tarry long. ^sirach-07-18-douay

19 Humble thy spirit very much: for the vengeance on the flesh of the ungodly is fire and worms. ^sirach-07-19-douay

20 Do not transgress against thy friend deferring money, nor despise thy dear brother for the sake of gold. ^sirach-07-20-douay

21 Depart not from a wise and good wife, whom thou hast gotten in the fear of the Lord: for the grace of her modesty is above gold. ^sirach-07-21-douay

22 Hurt not the servant that worketh faithfully, nor the hired man that giveth thee his life. ^sirach-07-22-douay

23 Let a wise servant be dear to thee as thy own soul, defraud him not of liberty, nor leave him needy. ^sirach-07-23-douay

24 Hast thou cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for thy profit, keep them with thee. ^sirach-07-24-douay

25 Hast thou children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their childhood. ^sirach-07-25-douay

26 Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and show not thy countenance gay towards them. ^sirach-07-26-douay

27 Marry thy daughter well, and thou shalt do a great work, and give her to a wise man. ^sirach-07-27-douay

28 If thou hast a wife according to thy soul, cast her not off: and to her that is hateful, trust not thyself. With thy whole heart, ^sirach-07-28-douay

29 Honour thy father, and forget not the groanings of thy mother: ^sirach-07-29-douay

30 Remember that thou hadst not been born but through them: and make a return to them as they have done for thee. ^sirach-07-30-douay

31 With all thy soul fear the Lord, and reverence his priests. ^sirach-07-31-douay

32 With all thy strength love him that made thee: and forsake not his ministers. ^sirach-07-32-douay

33 Honour God with all thy soul and give honour to the priests, and purify thyself with thy arms. **Thy arms...** That is, with all thy power: or else by arms (brachiis) are here signified the right shoulders of the victims, which by the law fell to the priests. See ver. 35. ^sirach-07-33-douay

34 Give them their portion, as it is commanded thee, of the firstfruits and of purifications: and for thy negligences purify thyself with a few. ^sirach-07-34-douay

35 Offer to the Lord the gift of thy shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things: ^sirach-07-35-douay

36 And stretch out thy hand to the poor, that thy expiation and thy blessing may be perfected. ^sirach-07-36-douay

37 A gift hath grace in the sight of all the living, and restrain not grace from the dead. **And restrain not grace from the dead...** That is, withhold not from them the benefit of alms, prayers, and sacrifices. Such was the doctrine and practice of the church of God even in the time of the Old Testament. And the same has always been continued from the days of the apostles in the church of the New Testament. ^sirach-07-37-douay

38 Be not wanting in comforting them that weep, and walk with them that mourn. ^sirach-07-38-douay

39 Be not slow to visit the sick: for by these things thou shalt be confirmed in love. ^sirach-07-39-douay

40 In all thy works remember thy last end, and thou shalt never sin. ^sirach-07-40-douay
