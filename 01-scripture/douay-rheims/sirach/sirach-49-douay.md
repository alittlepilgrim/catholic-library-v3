# Sirach Chapter 49

The praise of Josiah, of Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and the twelve prophets. Also of Zorobabel, Jesus the son of Josedech, Nehemiah, Enoch, Joseph, Seth, Shem, and Adam.

1 The memory of Josias is like the composition of a sweet smell made by the art of a perfumer: ^sirach-49-1-douay

2 His remembrance shall be sweet as honey in every mouth, and as music at a banquet of wine. ^sirach-49-2-douay

3 He was directed by God unto the repentance of the nation, and he took away the abominations of wickedness. ^sirach-49-3-douay

4 And he directed his heart towards the Lord, and in the days of sinners he strengthened godliness. ^sirach-49-4-douay

5 Except David, and Ezechias and Josias, all committed sin. ^sirach-49-5-douay

6 For the kings of Juda forsook the law of the most High, and despised the fear of God. ^sirach-49-6-douay

7 So they gave their kingdom to others, and their glory to a strange nation, ^sirach-49-7-douay

8 They burnt the chosen city of holiness, and made the streets thereof desolate according to the prediction of Jeremiah. ^sirach-49-8-douay

9 For they treated him evil, who was consecrated a prophet from his mother's womb, to overthrow, and pluck up, and destroy, and to build again, and renew. ^sirach-49-9-douay

10 It was Ezechiel that saw the glorious vision, which was shown him upon the chariot of cherubims. ^sirach-49-10-douay

11 For he made mention of the enemies under the figure of rain, and of doing good to them that showed right ways. ^sirach-49-11-douay

12 And may the bones of the twelve prophets spring up out of their place: for they strengthened Jacob, and redeemed themselves by strong faith. ^sirach-49-12-douay

13 How shall we magnify Zorobabel? for he was as a signet on the right hand; ^sirach-49-13-douay

14 In like manner Jesus the son of Josedec who in their days built the house, and set up a holy temple to the Lord, prepared for everlasting glory. ^sirach-49-14-douay

15 And let Nehemias be a long time remembered, who raised up for us our walls that were cast down, and set up the gates and the bars, who rebuilt our houses. ^sirach-49-15-douay

16 No man was born upon earth like Henoch: for he also was taken up from the earth. ^sirach-49-16-douay

17 Nor as Joseph, who was a man born prince of his brethren, the support of his family, the ruler of his brethren, the stay of the people: ^sirach-49-17-douay

18 And his bones were visited, and after death they prophesied. **They prophesied...** That is, by their being carried out of Egypt they verified the prophetic prediction of Joseph. [[genesis-50-douay|Genesis 50]]] ^sirach-49-18-douay

19 Seth and Sem obtained glory among men: and above every soul Adam in the beginning, ^sirach-49-19-douay
