# Sirach Chapter 26

Of good and bad women.

1 Happy is the husband of a good wife: for the number of his years is double. ^sirach-26-1-douay

2 A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband, and shall fulfil the years of his life in peace. ^sirach-26-2-douay

3 A good wife is a good portion, she shall be given in the portion of them that fear God, to a man for his good deeds. ^sirach-26-3-douay

4 Rich or poor, if his heart is good, his countenance shall be cheerful at all times. ^sirach-26-4-douay

5 Of three things my heart hath been afraid, and at the fourth my face hath trembled: ^sirach-26-5-douay

6 The accusation of a city, and the gathering together of the people: ^sirach-26-6-douay

7 And a false calumny, all are more grievous than death. ^sirach-26-7-douay

8 A jealous woman is the grief and mourning of the heart. ^sirach-26-8-douay

9 With a jealous woman is a scourge of the tongue which communicateth with all. ^sirach-26-9-douay

10 As a yoke of oxen that is moved to and fro, so also is a wicked woman: he that hath hold of her, is as he that taketh hold of a scorpion. ^sirach-26-10-douay

11 A drunken woman is a great wrath: and her reproach and shame shall not be hid. ^sirach-26-11-douay

12 The fornication of a woman shall be known by the haughtiness of her eyes and by her eyelids. ^sirach-26-12-douay

13 On a daughter that turneth not away herself, set a strict watch: lest finding an opportunity she abuse herself. ^sirach-26-13-douay

14 Take heed of the impudence of her eyes, and wonder not if she slight thee. ^sirach-26-14-douay

15 She will open her mouth as a thirsty traveller to the fountain, and will drink of every water near her, and will sit down by every hedge, and open her quiver against every arrow, until she fail. ^sirach-26-15-douay

16 The grace of a diligent woman shall delight her husband, and shall fat his bones. ^sirach-26-16-douay

17 Her discipline is the gift of God. ^sirach-26-17-douay

18 Such is a wise and silent woman, and there is nothing so much worth as a well instructed soul. ^sirach-26-18-douay

19 A holy and shamefaced woman is grace upon grace. ^sirach-26-19-douay

20 And no price is worthy of a continent soul. ^sirach-26-20-douay

21 As the sun when it riseth to the world in the high places of God, so is the beauty of a good wife for the ornament of her house. ^sirach-26-21-douay

22 As the lamp shining upon the holy candlestick, so is the beauty of the face in a ripe age, ^sirach-26-22-douay

23 As golden pillars upon bases of silver, so are the firm feet upon the soles of a steady woman. ^sirach-26-23-douay

24 As everlasting foundations upon a solid rock, so the commandments of God in the heart of a holy woman. ^sirach-26-24-douay

25 At two things my heart is grieved, and the third bringeth anger upon me. ^sirach-26-25-douay

26 A man of war fainting through poverty, and a man of sense despised: ^sirach-26-26-douay

27 And he that passeth over from justice to sin, God hath prepared such an one for the sword. ^sirach-26-27-douay

28 Two sorts of callings have appeared to me hard and dangerous: a merchant is hardly free from negligence: and a huckster shall not be justified from the sins of the lips. **From negligence...** That is, from the neglect of the service of God: because the eager pursuit of the mammon of this world, is apt to make men of that calling forget the great duties of loving God above all things, and their neighbours as themselves. -- Ibid. **A huckster...** Or, a retailer of wine. Men of that profession are both greatly exposed to danger of sin themselves, and are too often accessory to the sins of others. ^sirach-26-28-douay
