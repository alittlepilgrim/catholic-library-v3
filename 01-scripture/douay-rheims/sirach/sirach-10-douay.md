# Sirach Chapter 10

The virtues and vices of men in power: the great evil of pride.

1 A wise judge shall judge his people, and the government of a prudent man shall be steady. **Judge his people...** In the Greek it is, instruct his people. ^sirach-10-1-douay

2 As the judge of the people is himself, so also are his ministers: and what manner of man the ruler of a city is, such also are they that dwell therein. ^sirach-10-2-douay

3 An unwise king shall be the ruin of his people: and cities shall be inhabited through the prudence of the rulers. ^sirach-10-3-douay

4 The power of the earth is in the hand of God, and in his time he will raise up a profitable ruler over it. ^sirach-10-4-douay

5 The prosperity of man is in the hand of God, and upon the person of the scribe he shall lay his honour. **The scribe...** That is, the man that is wise and learned in the law. ^sirach-10-5-douay

6 Remember not any injury done thee by thy neighbour, and do thou nothing by deeds of injury. ^sirach-10-6-douay

7 Pride is hateful before God and men: and all iniquity of nations is execrable. ^sirach-10-7-douay

8 A kingdom is translated from one people to another, because of injustices, and wrongs, and injuries, and divers deceits. ^sirach-10-8-douay

9 But nothing is more wicked than the covetous man. Why is earth, and ashes proud? ^sirach-10-9-douay

10 There is not a more wicked thing than to love money: for such a one setteth even his own soul to sale: because while he liveth he hath cast away his bowels. ^sirach-10-10-douay

11 All power is of short life. A long sickness is troublesome to the physician. ^sirach-10-11-douay

12 The physician cutteth off a short sickness: so also a king is to day, and tomorrow he shall die. ^sirach-10-12-douay

13 For when a man shall die, he shall inherit serpents, and beasts, and worms. ^sirach-10-13-douay

14 The beginning of the pride of man, is to fall off from God: ^sirach-10-14-douay

15 Because his heart is departed from him that made him: for pride is the beginning of all sin: he that holdeth it, shall be filled with maledictions, and it shall ruin him in the end. ^sirach-10-15-douay

16 Therefore hath the Lord disgraced the assemblies of the wicked, and hath utterly destroyed them. ^sirach-10-16-douay

17 God hath overturned the thrones of proud princes, and hath set up the meek in their stead. ^sirach-10-17-douay

18 God hath made the roots of proud nations to wither, and hath planted the humble of these nations. ^sirach-10-18-douay

19 The Lord hath overthrown the lands of the Gentiles, and hath destroyed them even to the foundation. ^sirach-10-19-douay

20 He hath made some of them to wither away, and hath destroyed them, and hath made the memory of them to cease from the earth. ^sirach-10-20-douay

21 God hath abolished the memory of the proud, and hath preserved the memory of them that are humble in mind. ^sirach-10-21-douay

22 Pride was not made for men: nor wrath for the race of women. ^sirach-10-22-douay

23 That seed of men shall be honoured, which feareth God: but that seed shall be dishonoured, which transgresseth the commandments of the Lord. ^sirach-10-23-douay

24 In the midst of brethren their chief is honourable: so shall they that fear the Lord, be in his eyes. ^sirach-10-24-douay

25 The fear of God is the glory of the rich, and of the honourable, and of the poor. ^sirach-10-25-douay

26 Despise not a just man that is poor, and do not magnify a sinful man that is rich. ^sirach-10-26-douay

27 The great man, and the judge, and the mighty is in honour: and there is none greater than he that feareth God. ^sirach-10-27-douay

28 They that are free shall serve a servant that is wise: and a man that is prudent and well instructed will not murmur when he is reproved; and he that is ignorant, shall not be honoured. ^sirach-10-28-douay

29 Extol not thyself in doing thy work, and linger not in the time of distress; ^sirach-10-29-douay

30 Better is he that laboureth, and aboundeth in all things, than he that boasteth himself and wanteth bread. ^sirach-10-30-douay

31 My son, keep thy soul in meekness, and give it honour according to its desert. ^sirach-10-31-douay

32 Who will justify him that sinneth against his own soul? and who will honour him that dishonoureth his own soul? ^sirach-10-32-douay

33 The poor man is glorified by his discipline and fear, and there is a man that is honoured for his wealth. ^sirach-10-33-douay

34 But he that is glorified in poverty, how much more in wealth? and he that is glorified in wealth, let him fear poverty. ^sirach-10-34-douay
