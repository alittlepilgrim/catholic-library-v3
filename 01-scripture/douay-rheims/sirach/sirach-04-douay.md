# Sirach Chapter 4

An exhortation to works of mercy, and to the love of wisdom.

1 Son, defraud not the poor of alms, and turn not away thy eyes from the poor. ^sirach-04-1-douay

2 Despise not the hungry soul: and provoke not the poor in his want. ^sirach-04-2-douay

3 Afflict not the heart of the needy, and defer not to gibe to him that is in distress. ^sirach-04-3-douay

4 Reject not the petition of the afflicted: and turn not away thy face from the needy. ^sirach-04-4-douay

5 Turn not away thy eyes from the poor for fear of anger: and leave not to them that ask of thee to curse thee behind thy back. ^sirach-04-5-douay

6 For the prayer of him that curseth thee in the bitterness of his soul, shall be heard, for he that made him will hear him. ^sirach-04-6-douay

7 Make thyself affable to the congregation of the poor, and humble thy soul to the ancient, and bow thy head to a great man. ^sirach-04-7-douay

8 Bow down thy ear cheerfully to the poor, and pay what thou owest, and answer him peaceable words with mildness. ^sirach-04-8-douay

9 Deliver him that suffereth wrong out of the hand of the proud: and be not fainthearted in thy soul. ^sirach-04-9-douay

10 In judging be merciful to the fatherless as a father, and as a husband to their mother. ^sirach-04-10-douay

11 And thou shalt be as the obedient son of the most High, and he will have mercy on thee more than a mother. ^sirach-04-11-douay

12 Wisdom inspireth life into her children, and protecteth them that seek after her, and will go before them in the way of justice. ^sirach-04-12-douay

13 And he that loveth her, loveth life: and they that watch for her, shall embrace her sweetness. ^sirach-04-13-douay

14 They that hold her fast, shall inherit life: and whithersoever she entereth, God will give a blessing. ^sirach-04-14-douay

15 They that serve her, shall be servants to the holy one: and God loveth them that love her. ^sirach-04-15-douay

16 He that hearkeneth to her, shall judge nations: and he that looketh upon her, shall remain secure. ^sirach-04-16-douay

17 If he trust to her, he shall inherit her, and his generation shall be in assurance. ^sirach-04-17-douay

18 For she walketh with him in temptation, and at the first she chooseth him. **In temptation, etc...** The meaning is, that before wisdom will choose any for her favourite, she will try them by leading them through contradictions, afflictions, and temptations, the usual noviceship of the children of God. ^sirach-04-18-douay

19 She will bring upon him fear and dread and trial: and she will scourge him with the affliction of her discipline, till she try him by her laws, and trust his soul. ^sirach-04-19-douay

20 Then she will strengthen him, and make a straight way to him, and give him joy, ^sirach-04-20-douay

21 And will disclose her secrets to him, and will heap upon him treasures of knowledge and understanding of justice. ^sirach-04-21-douay

22 But if he go astray, she will forsake him, and deliver him into the hands of his enemy. ^sirach-04-22-douay

23 Son, observe the time, and fly from evil. ^sirach-04-23-douay

24 For thy soul be not ashamed to say the truth. ^sirach-04-24-douay

25 For there is a shame that bringeth sin, and there is a shame that bringeth glory and grace. ^sirach-04-25-douay

26 Accept no person against thy own person, nor against thy soul a lie. ^sirach-04-26-douay

27 Reverence not thy neighbour in his fall: ^sirach-04-27-douay

28 And refrain not to speak in the time of salvation. Hide not thy wisdom in her beauty. ^sirach-04-28-douay

29 For by the tongue wisdom is discerned: and understanding, and knowledge, and learning by the word of the wise, and steadfastness in the works of justice. ^sirach-04-29-douay

30 In nowise speak against the truth, but be ashamed of the lie of thy ignorance. ^sirach-04-30-douay

31 Be not ashamed to confess thy sins, but submit not thyself to every man for sin. ^sirach-04-31-douay

32 Resist not against the face of the mighty, and do not strive against the stream of the river. ^sirach-04-32-douay

33 Strive for justice for thy soul, and even unto death fight for justice, and God will overthrow thy enemies for thee. ^sirach-04-33-douay

34 Be not hasty in thy tongue: and slack and remiss in thy works. ^sirach-04-34-douay

35 Be not as a lion in thy house, terrifying them of thy household, and oppressing them that are under thee. ^sirach-04-35-douay

36 Let not thy hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldst give. ^sirach-04-36-douay
