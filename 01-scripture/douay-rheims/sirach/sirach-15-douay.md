# Sirach Chapter 15

Wisdom embraces them that fear God. God is not the author of sin.

1 He that feareth God, will do good: and he that possesseth justice, shall lay hold on her, ^sirach-15-1-douay

2 And she will meet him as an honourable mother, and will receive him as a wife married of a virgin. ^sirach-15-2-douay

3 With the bread of life and understanding, she shall feed him, and give him the water of wholesome wisdom to drink: and she shall be made strong in him, and he shall not be moved. ^sirach-15-3-douay

4 And she shall hold him fast, and he shall not be confounded: and she shall exalt him among his neighbours. ^sirach-15-4-douay

5 And in the midst of the church she shall open his mouth, and shall fill him with the spirit of wisdom and understanding, and shall clothe him with a robe of glory. ^sirach-15-5-douay

6 She shall heap upon him a treasure of joy and gladness, and shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name. ^sirach-15-6-douay

7 But foolish men shall not obtain her, and wise men shall meet her, foolish men shall not see her: for she is far from pride and deceit. ^sirach-15-7-douay

8 Lying men shall be mindful of her: but men that speak truth shall be found with her, and shall advance, even till they come to the sight of God. ^sirach-15-8-douay

9 Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner: ^sirach-15-9-douay

10 For wisdom came forth from God: for praise shall be with the wisdom of God, and shall abound in a faithful mouth, and the sovereign Lord will give praise unto it. ^sirach-15-10-douay

11 Say not: It is through God, that she is not with me: for do not thou the things that he hateth. ^sirach-15-11-douay

12 Say not: He hath caused me to err: for he hath no need of wicked men. ^sirach-15-12-douay

13 The Lord hateth all abomination of error, and they that fear him shall not love it. ^sirach-15-13-douay

14 God made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his own counsel. ^sirach-15-14-douay

15 He added his commandments and precepts. ^sirach-15-15-douay

16 If thou wilt keep the commandments and perform acceptable fidelity for ever, they shall preserve thee. ^sirach-15-16-douay

17 He hath set water and fire before thee: stretch forth thy hand to which thou wilt. ^sirach-15-17-douay

18 Before man is life and death, good and evil, that which he shall choose shall be given him: ^sirach-15-18-douay

19 For the wisdom of God is great, and he is strong in power, seeing all men without ceasing. ^sirach-15-19-douay

20 The eyes of the Lord are towards them that fear him, and he knoweth al the work of man. ^sirach-15-20-douay

21 He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, and he hath given no man license to sin; ^sirach-15-21-douay

22 For he desireth not a multitude of faithless and unprofitable children. ^sirach-15-22-douay
