# Sirach Chapter 51

A prayer of praise and thanksgiving.

1 A prayer of Jesus the son of Sirach. ^sirach-51-1-douay

I will give glory to thee, O Lord, O King, and I will praise thee, O God my Saviour.

2 I will give glory to thy name: for thou hast been a helper and protector to me. ^sirach-51-2-douay

3 And hast preserved my body from destruction, from the snare of an unjust tongue, and from the lips of them that forge lies, and in the sight of them that stood by, thou hast been my helper. ^sirach-51-3-douay

4 And thou hast delivered me, according to the multitude of the mercy of thy name, from them that did roar, prepared to devour. ^sirach-51-4-douay

5 Out of the hands of them that sought my life, and from the gates of afflictions, which compassed me about: ^sirach-51-5-douay

6 From the oppression of the flame which surrounded me, and in the midst of the fire I was not burnt. ^sirach-51-6-douay

7 From the depth of the belly of hell, and from an unclean tongue, and from lying words, from an unjust king, and from a slanderous tongue: ^sirach-51-7-douay

8 My soul shall praise the Lord even to death. ^sirach-51-8-douay

9 And my life was drawing near to hell beneath. ^sirach-51-9-douay

10 They compassed me on every side, and there was no one that would help me. I looked for the succour of men, and there was none. ^sirach-51-10-douay

11 I remembered thy mercy, O Lord, and thy works, which are from the beginning of the world. ^sirach-51-11-douay

12 How thou deliverest them that wait for thee, O Lord, and savest them out of the hands of the nations. ^sirach-51-12-douay

13 Thou hast exalted my dwelling place upon the earth and I have prayed for death to pass away. ^sirach-51-13-douay

14 I called upon the Lord, the father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the day of my trouble, and in the time of the proud without help. ^sirach-51-14-douay

15 I will praise thy name continually, and will praise it with thanksgiving, and my prayer was heard. ^sirach-51-15-douay

16 And thou hast saved me from destruction, and hast delivered me from the evil time. ^sirach-51-16-douay

17 Therefore I will give thanks, and praise thee, and bless the name of the Lord. ^sirach-51-17-douay

18 When I was yet young, before I wandered about, I sought for wisdom openly in my prayer. ^sirach-51-18-douay

19 I prayed for her before the temple, and unto the very end I will seek after her, and she flourished as a grape soon ripe. ^sirach-51-19-douay

20 My heart delighted in her, my foot walked in the right way, from my youth up I sought after her. ^sirach-51-20-douay

21 I bowed down my ear a little, and received her. ^sirach-51-21-douay

22 I found much wisdom in myself, and profited much therein. ^sirach-51-22-douay

23 To him that giveth me wisdom, will I give glory. ^sirach-51-23-douay

24 For I have determined to follow her: I have had a zeal for good, and shall not be confounded. ^sirach-51-24-douay

25 My soul hath wrestled for her, and in doing it I have been confirmed. ^sirach-51-25-douay

26 I stretched forth my hands on high, and I bewailed my ignorance of her. ^sirach-51-26-douay

27 I directed my soul to her, and in knowledge I found her. ^sirach-51-27-douay

28 I possessed my heart with her from the beginning: therefore I shall not be forsaken. ^sirach-51-28-douay

29 My entrails were troubled in seeking her: therefore shall I possess a good possession. ^sirach-51-29-douay

30 The Lord hath given me a tongue for my reward: and with it I will praise him. ^sirach-51-30-douay

31 Draw near to me, ye unlearned, and gather yourselves together into the hours of discipline. ^sirach-51-31-douay

32 Why are ye slow and what do you say of these things? your souls are exceeding thirsty. ^sirach-51-32-douay

33 I have opened my mouth, and have spoken: buy her for yourselves without silver, ^sirach-51-33-douay

34 And submit your neck to the yoke, and let your soul receive discipline: for she is near at hand to be found. ^sirach-51-34-douay

35 Behold with your eyes how I have laboured a little, and have found much rest to myself. ^sirach-51-35-douay

36 Receive ye discipline as a great sum of money, and possess abundance of gold by her. ^sirach-51-36-douay

37 Let your soul rejoice in his mercy and you shall not be confounded in his praise. ^sirach-51-37-douay

38 Work your work before the time, and he will give you your reward in his time. ^sirach-51-38-douay
