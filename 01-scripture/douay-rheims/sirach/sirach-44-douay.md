# Sirach Chapter 44

The praises of the holy father, in particular of Enoch, Noah, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

1 Let us now praise men of renown and our fathers in their generation. ^sirach-44-1-douay

2 The Lord hath wrought great glory through his magnificence from the beginning. ^sirach-44-2-douay

3 Such as have borne rule in their dominions, men of great power, and endued with their wisdom, showing forth in the prophets the dignity of prophets, ^sirach-44-3-douay

4 And ruling over the present people, and by the strength of wisdom instructing the people in most holy words. ^sirach-44-4-douay

5 Such as by their skill sought out musical tunes, and published canticles of the scriptures. ^sirach-44-5-douay

6 Rich men in virtue, studying beautifulness: living at peace in their houses. ^sirach-44-6-douay

7 All these have gained glory in their generations, and were praised in their days. ^sirach-44-7-douay

8 They that were born of them have left a name behind them, that their praises might be related: ^sirach-44-8-douay

9 And there are some, of whom there is no memorial: who are perished, as if they had never been: and are become as if they had never been born, and their children with them. ^sirach-44-9-douay

10 But these were men of mercy, whose godly deeds have not failed: ^sirach-44-10-douay

11 Good things continue with their seed, ^sirach-44-11-douay

12 Their posterity are a holy inheritance, and their seed hath stood in the covenants. ^sirach-44-12-douay

13 And their children for their sakes remain for ever: their seed and their glory shall not be forsaken. ^sirach-44-13-douay

14 Their bodies are buried in peace, and their name liveth unto generation and generation. ^sirach-44-14-douay

15 Let the people show forth their wisdom, and the church declare their praise. ^sirach-44-15-douay

16 Henoch pleased God, and was translated into paradise, that he may give repentance to the nations. ^sirach-44-16-douay

17 Noe was found perfect, just, and in the time of wrath he was made a reconciliation. ^sirach-44-17-douay

18 Therefore was there a remnant left to the earth, when the flood came. ^sirach-44-18-douay

19 The covenants of the world were made with him, that all flesh should no more be destroyed with the flood. ^sirach-44-19-douay

20 Abraham was the great father of a multitude of nations, and there was not found the like to him in glory, who kept the law of the most High, and was in covenant with him. ^sirach-44-20-douay

21 In his flesh he established the covenant, and in temptation he was found faithful. ^sirach-44-21-douay

22 Therefore by an oath he gave him glory in his posterity, that he should increase as the dust of the earth, ^sirach-44-22-douay

23 And that he would exalt his seed as the stars, and they should inherit from sea to sea, and from the river to the ends of the earth. ^sirach-44-23-douay

24 And he did in like manner with Isaac for the sake of Abraham his father. ^sirach-44-24-douay

25 The Lord gave him the blessing of all nations, and confirmed his covenant upon the head of Jacob. ^sirach-44-25-douay

26 He acknowledged him in his blessings, and gave him an inheritance, and divided him his portion in twelve tribes. ^sirach-44-26-douay

27 And he preserved for him men of mercy, that found grace in the eyes of all flesh. ^sirach-44-27-douay
