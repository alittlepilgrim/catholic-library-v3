# Sirach Chapter 27

Dangers of sin from several heads: the fear of God is the best preservative. He who digs a pit, shall fall into it.

1 Through poverty many have sinned: and he that seeketh to be enriched, turneth away his eye. ^sirach-27-1-douay

2 As a stake sticketh fast in the midst of the joining of stones, so also in the midst of selling and buying, sin shall stick fast. ^sirach-27-2-douay

3 Sin shall be destroyed with the sinner. ^sirach-27-3-douay

4 Unless thou hold thyself diligently in the fear of the Lord, thy house shall quickly be overthrown. ^sirach-27-4-douay

5 As when one sifteth with a sieve, the dust will remain: so will the perplexity of a man in his thoughts. ^sirach-27-5-douay

6 The furnace trieth the potter's vessels, and the trial of affliction just men. ^sirach-27-6-douay

7 As the dressing of a tree showeth the fruit thereof, so a word out of the thought of the heart of man. ^sirach-27-7-douay

8 Praise not a man before he speaketh, for this is the trial of men. ^sirach-27-8-douay

9 If thou followest justice, thou shalt obtain her: and shalt put her on as a long robe of honour, and thou shalt dwell with her: and she shall protect thee for ever, and in the day of acknowledgment thou shalt find a strong foundation. ^sirach-27-9-douay

10 Birds resort unto their like: so truth will return to them that practise her. ^sirach-27-10-douay

11 The lion always lieth in wait for prey: so do sins for them that work iniquities. ^sirach-27-11-douay

12 A holy man continueth in wisdom as the sun: but a fool is changed as the moon. ^sirach-27-12-douay

13 In the midst of the unwise keep in the word till its time: but be continually among men that think. ^sirach-27-13-douay

14 The discourse of sinners is hateful, and their laughter is at the pleasures of sin. ^sirach-27-14-douay

15 The speech that sweareth much shall make the hair of the head stand upright: and its irreverence shall make one stop his ears. ^sirach-27-15-douay

16 In the quarrels of the road is the shedding of blood: and their cursing is a grievous hearing. ^sirach-27-16-douay

17 He that discloseth the secret of a friend loseth his credit, and shall never find a friend to his mind. ^sirach-27-17-douay

18 Love thy neighbour, and be joined to him with fidelity. ^sirach-27-18-douay

19 But if thou discover his secrets, follow no more after him. ^sirach-27-19-douay

20 For as a man that destroyeth his friend, so is he that destroyeth the friendship of his neighbour. ^sirach-27-20-douay

21 And as one that letteth a bird go out of his hand, so hast thou let thy neighbour go, and thou shalt not get him again. ^sirach-27-21-douay

22 Follow after him no more, for he is gone afar off, he is fled, as a roe escaped out of the snare because his soul is wounded. ^sirach-27-22-douay

23 Thou canst no more bind him up. And of a curse there is reconciliation: **And of a curse there is reconciliation...** That is, it is easier to obtain a reconciliation after a curse, than after disclosing a secret. ^sirach-27-23-douay

24 But to disclose the secrets of a friend, leaveth no hope to an unhappy soul. ^sirach-27-24-douay

25 He that winketh with the eye forgeth wicked things, and no man will cast him off: ^sirach-27-25-douay

26 In the sight of thy eyes he will sweeten his mouth, and will admire thy words: but at the last he will writhe his mouth, and on thy words he will lay a stumbling block. ^sirach-27-26-douay

27 I have hated many things but not like him, and the Lord will hate him. ^sirach-27-27-douay

28 If one cast a stone on high, it will fall upon his own head: and the deceitful stroke will wound the deceitful. ^sirach-27-28-douay

29 He that diggeth a pit, shall fall into it: and he that setteth a stone for his neighbour, shall stumble upon it: and he that layeth a snare for another, shall perish in it. ^sirach-27-29-douay

30 A mischievous counsel shall be rolled back upon the author, and he shall not know from whence it cometh to him. ^sirach-27-30-douay

31 Mockery and reproach are of the proud, and vengeance as a lion shall lie in wait for him. ^sirach-27-31-douay

32 They shall perish in a snare that are delighted with the fall of the just: and sorrow shall consume them before they die. ^sirach-27-32-douay

33 Anger and fury are both of them abominable, and the sinful man shall be subject to them. ^sirach-27-33-douay
