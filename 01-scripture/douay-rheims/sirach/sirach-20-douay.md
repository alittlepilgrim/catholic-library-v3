# Sirach Chapter 20

Rules with regard to correction, discretion, and avoiding lies.

1 How much better is it to reprove, than to be angry, and not to hinder him that confesseth in prayer. ^sirach-20-1-douay

2 The lust of an eunuch shall deflour a young maiden: ^sirach-20-2-douay

3 So is he that by violence executeth of the unwise. ^sirach-20-3-douay

4 How good is it, when thou art reproved, to show repentance! for so thou shalt escape wilful sin. ^sirach-20-4-douay

5 There is one that holdeth his peace, that is found wise: and there is another that is hateful, that is bold in speech. ^sirach-20-5-douay

6 There is one that holdeth his peace, because he knoweth not what to say: and there is another that holdeth his peace, knowing the proper time. ^sirach-20-6-douay

7 A wise man will hold his peace till he see opportunity: but a babbler, and a fool, will regard no time. ^sirach-20-7-douay

8 He that useth many words shall hurt his own soul: and he that taketh authority to himself unjustly shall be hated. ^sirach-20-8-douay

9 There is success in evil things to a man without discipline, and there is a finding that turneth to loss. ^sirach-20-9-douay

10 There is a gift that is not profitable: and there is a gift, the recompense of which is double. ^sirach-20-10-douay

11 There is an abasement because of glory: and there is one that shall lift up his head from a low estate. ^sirach-20-11-douay

12 There is that buyeth much for a small price, and restoreth the same sevenfold. ^sirach-20-12-douay

13 A man wise in words shall make himself beloved: but the graces of fools shall be poured out. ^sirach-20-13-douay

14 The gift of the fool shall do thee no good: for his eyes are sevenfold. ^sirach-20-14-douay

15 He will give a few things, and upbraid much: and the opening of his mouth is the kindling of a fire. ^sirach-20-15-douay

16 Today a man lendeth, and tomorrow he asketh it again: such a man as this is hateful. ^sirach-20-16-douay

17 A fool shall have no friend, and there shall be no thanks for his good deeds. ^sirach-20-17-douay

18 For they that eat his bread, are of a false tongue. How often, and how many will laugh him to scorn! ^sirach-20-18-douay

19 For he doth not distribute with right understanding that which was to be had: in like manner also that which was not to be had. ^sirach-20-19-douay

20 The slipping of a false tongue is as one that falleth on the pavement: so the fall of the wicked shall come speedily. ^sirach-20-20-douay

21 A man without grace is as a vain fable, it shall be continually in the mouth of the unwise. ^sirach-20-21-douay

22 A parable coming out of a fool's mouth shall be rejected: for he doth not speak it in due season. ^sirach-20-22-douay

23 There is that is hindered from sinning through want, and in his rest he shall be pricked. ^sirach-20-23-douay

24 There is that will destroy his own soul through shamefacedness, and by occasion of an unwise person he will destroy it: and by respect of person he will destroy himself. ^sirach-20-24-douay

25 There is that for bashfulness promiseth to his friend, and maketh him his enemy for nothing. ^sirach-20-25-douay

26 A lie is a foul blot in a man, and yet it will be continually in the mouth of men without discipline. ^sirach-20-26-douay

27 A thief is better than a man that is always lying: but both of them shall inherit destruction. ^sirach-20-27-douay

28 The manners of lying men are without honour: and their confusion is with them without ceasing. ^sirach-20-28-douay

29 A wise man shall advance himself with his words, and a prudent man shall please the great ones. ^sirach-20-29-douay

30 He that tilleth his land shall make a high heap of corn: and he that worketh justice shall be exalted: and he that pleaseth great men shall escape iniquity. ^sirach-20-30-douay

31 Presents and gifts blind the eyes of judges, and make them dumb in the mouth, so that they cannot correct. ^sirach-20-31-douay

32 O Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is not seen: what profit is there in them both? ^sirach-20-32-douay

33 Better is he that hideth his folly, than the man that hideth his wisdom. ^sirach-20-33-douay
