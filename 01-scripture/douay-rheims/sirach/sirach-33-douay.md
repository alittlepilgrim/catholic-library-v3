# Sirach Chapter 33

The fear of God is the best security. Times and men are in the hands of God. Take care of thyself as long as thou livest, and look to thy servants.

1 No evils shall happen to him that feareth the Lord, but in temptation God will keep him and deliver him from evils. ^sirach-33-1-douay

2 A wise man hateth not the commandments and justices, and he shall not be dashed in pieces as a ship in a storm. ^sirach-33-2-douay

3 A man of understanding is faithful to the law of God, and the law is faithful to him. ^sirach-33-3-douay

4 He that cleareth up a question, shall prepare what to say, and so having prayed he shall be heard, and shall keep discipline, and then he shall answer. ^sirach-33-4-douay

5 The heart of a fool is as a wheel of a cart: and his thoughts are like a rolling axletree. ^sirach-33-5-douay

6 A friend that is a mocker, is like a stallion horse: he neigheth under every one that sitteth upon him. ^sirach-33-6-douay

7 Why doth one day excel another, and one light another, and one year another year, when all come of the sun? ^sirach-33-7-douay

8 By the knowledge of the Lord they were distinguished, the sun being made, and keeping his commandment. ^sirach-33-8-douay

9 And he ordered the seasons, and holidays of them, and in them they celebrated festivals at an hour. ^sirach-33-9-douay

10 Some of them God made high and great days, and some of them he put in the number of ordinary days. And all men are from the ground, and out of the earth, from whence Adam was created. ^sirach-33-10-douay

11 With much knowledge the Lord hath divided them and diversified their ways. ^sirach-33-11-douay

12 Some of them hath he blessed, and exalted: and some of them hath he sanctified, and set near himself: and some of them hath he cursed and brought low, and turned them from their station. ^sirach-33-12-douay

13 As the potter's clay is in his hand, to fashion and order it: ^sirach-33-13-douay

14 All his ways are according to his ordering: so man is in the hand of him that made him, and he will render to him according to his judgment. ^sirach-33-14-douay

15 Good is set against evil, and life against death: so also is the sinner against a just man. And so look upon all the works of the most High. Two and two, and one against another. ^sirach-33-15-douay

16 And I awaked last of all, and as one that gathereth after the grapegatherers. ^sirach-33-16-douay

17 In the blessing of God I also have hoped: and as one that gathereth grapes, have I filled the winepress. ^sirach-33-17-douay

18 See that I have not laboured for myself only, but for all that seek discipline. ^sirach-33-18-douay

19 Hear me, ye great men, and all ye people, and hearken with your ears, ye rulers of the church. ^sirach-33-19-douay

20 Give not to son or wife, brother or friend, power over thee while thou livest; and give not thy estate to another, lest thou repent, and thou entreat for the same. ^sirach-33-20-douay

21 As long as thou livest, and hast breath in thee, let no man change thee. **Change thee...** That is, so as to have this power over thee. ^sirach-33-21-douay

22 For it is better that thy children should ask of thee, than that thou look toward the hands of thy children. ^sirach-33-22-douay

23 In all thy works keep the pre-eminence. **The pre-eminence...** That is, be master in thy own house, and part not with thy authority. ^sirach-33-23-douay

24 Let no stain sully thy glory. In the time when thou shalt end the days of thy life, and in the time of thy decease, distribute thy inheritance. ^sirach-33-24-douay

25 Fodder, and a wand, and a burden are for an ass: bread, and correction, and work for a slave. ^sirach-33-25-douay

26 He worketh under correction, and seeketh to rest: let his hands be idle, and he seeketh liberty. ^sirach-33-26-douay

27 The yoke and the thong bend a stiff neck, and continual labours bow a slave. ^sirach-33-27-douay

28 Torture and fetters are for a malicious slave: send him to work, that he be not idle: ^sirach-33-28-douay

29 For idleness hath taught much evil. ^sirach-33-29-douay

30 Set him to work: for so it is fit for him. And if he be not obedient, bring him down with fetters, but be not excessive towards any one, and do no grievous thing without judgment. ^sirach-33-30-douay

31 If thou have a faithful servant, let him be to thee as thy own soul: treat him as a brother: because in the blood of thy soul thou hast gotten him. ^sirach-33-31-douay

32 If thou hurt him unjustly, he will run away: ^sirach-33-32-douay

33 And if he rise up and depart, thou knowest not whom to ask, and in what way to seek him. ^sirach-33-33-douay
