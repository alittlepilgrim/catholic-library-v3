# Romans Chapter 2

The Jews are censured, who make their boast of the law and keep it not. He declares who are the true Jews.

1 Wherefore thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest. For wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself. For thou dost the same things which thou judgest. ^romans-02-1-douay

2 For we know that the judgment of God is, according to truth, against them that do such things. ^romans-02-2-douay

3 And thinkest thou this, O man, that judgest them who do such things and dost the same, that thou shalt escape the judgment of God? ^romans-02-3-douay

4 Or despisest thou the riches of his goodness and patience and longsuffering? Knowest thou not that the benignity of God leadeth thee to penance? ^romans-02-4-douay

5 But according to thy hardness and impenitent heart, thou treasurest up to thyself wrath, against the day of wrath and revelation of the just judgment of God: ^romans-02-5-douay

6 Who will render to every man according to his works. ^romans-02-6-douay

7 To them indeed who, according to patience in good work, seek glory and honour and incorruption, eternal life: ^romans-02-7-douay

8 But to them that are contentious and who obey not the truth but give credit to iniquity, wrath and indignation. ^romans-02-8-douay

9 Tribulation and anguish upon every soul of man that worketh evil: of the Jew first, and also of the Greek. ^romans-02-9-douay

10 But glory and honour and peace to every one that worketh good: to the Jew first, and also to the Greek. ^romans-02-10-douay

11 For there is no respect of persons with God. ^romans-02-11-douay

12 For whosoever have sinned without the law shall perish without the law: and whosoever have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law. ^romans-02-12-douay

13 For not the hearers of the law are just before God: but the doers of the law shall be justified. ^romans-02-13-douay

14 For when the Gentiles, who have not the law, do by nature those things that are of the law; these, having not the law, are a law to themselves. ^romans-02-14-douay

15 Who show the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience bearing witness to them: and their thoughts between themselves accusing or also defending one another, ^romans-02-15-douay

16 In the day when God shall judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ, according to my gospel. ^romans-02-16-douay

17 But if thou art called a Jew and restest in the law and makest thy boast of God, ^romans-02-17-douay

18 And knowest his will and approvest the more profitable things, being instructed by the law: ^romans-02-18-douay

19 Art confident that thou thyself art a guide of the blind, a light of them that are in darkness, ^romans-02-19-douay

20 An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of infants, having the form of knowledge and of truth in the law. ^romans-02-20-douay

21 Thou therefore, that teachest another, teachest not thyself: thou, that preachest that men should not steal, stealest. ^romans-02-21-douay

22 Thou, that sayest men should not commit adultery, committest adultery: thou, that abhorrest idols, committest sacrilege: ^romans-02-22-douay

23 Thou, that makest thy boast of the law, by transgression of the law dishonourest God. ^romans-02-23-douay

24 (For the name of God through you is blasphemed among the Gentiles, as it is written.) ^romans-02-24-douay

25 Circumcision profiteth indeed, if thou keep the law: but if thou be a transgressor of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision. ^romans-02-25-douay

26 If then, the uncircumcised keep the justices of the law, shall not this uncircumcision be counted for circumcision? ^romans-02-26-douay

27 And shall not that which by nature is uncircumcision, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision art a transgressor of the law? ^romans-02-27-douay

28 For it is not he is a Jew, who is so outwardly: nor is that circumcision which is outwardly in the flesh. ^romans-02-28-douay

29 But he is a Jew that is one inwardly and the circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit not in the letter: whose praise is not of men, but of God. ^romans-02-29-douay
