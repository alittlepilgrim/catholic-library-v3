# Romans Chapter 7

We are released by Christ from the law and from the guilt of sin, though the inclination to it still tempts us.

1 Know you not, brethren (for I speak to them that know the law) that the law hath dominion over a man as long as it liveth? **As long as it liveth...** or, as long as he liveth. ^romans-07-1-douay

2 For the woman that hath an husband, whilst her husband liveth is bound to the law. But if her husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband. ^romans-07-2-douay

3 Therefore, whilst her husband liveth, she shall be called an adulteress, if she be with another man: but if her husband be dead, she is delivered from the law of her husband: so that she is not an adulteress, if she be with another man. ^romans-07-3-douay

4 Therefore, my brethren, you also are become dead to the law, by the body of Christ: that you may belong to another, who is risen again from the dead that we may bring forth fruit to God. ^romans-07-4-douay

5 For when we were in the flesh, the passions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members, to bring forth fruit unto death. ^romans-07-5-douay

6 But now we are loosed from the law of death wherein we were detained; so that we should serve in newness of spirit, and not in the oldness of the letter. ^romans-07-6-douay

7 What shall we say, then? Is the law sin? God forbid! But I do not know sin, but by the law. For I had not known concupiscence, if the law did not say: Thou shalt not covet. ^romans-07-7-douay

8 But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law sin was dead. **Sin taking occasion...** Sin, or concupiscence, which is called sin, because it is from sin, and leads to sin, which was asleep before, was weakened by the prohibition: the law not being the cause thereof, nor properly giving occasion to it: but occasion being taken by our corrupt nature to resist the commandment laid upon us. ^romans-07-8-douay

9 And I lived some time without the law. But when the commandment came, sin revived, ^romans-07-9-douay

10 And I died. And the commandment that was ordained to life, the same was found to be unto death to me. ^romans-07-10-douay

11 For sin, taking occasion by the commandment, seduced me: and by it killed me. ^romans-07-11-douay

12 Wherefore the law indeed is holy: and the commandment holy and just and good. ^romans-07-12-douay

13 Was that then which is good made death unto me? God forbid! But sin, that it may appear sin, by that which is good, wrought death in me: that sin, by the commandment, might become sinful above measure. **That it may appear sin, or that sin may appear, viz...** To be the monster it is, which is even capable to take occasion from that which is good, to work death. ^romans-07-13-douay

14 For we know that the law is spiritual. But I am carnal, sold under sin. ^romans-07-14-douay

15 For that which I work, I understand not. For I do not that good which I will: but the evil which I hate, that I do. **I do not that good which I will, etc...** The apostle here describes the disorderly motions of passion and concupiscence; which oftentimes in us get the start of reason: and by means of which even good men suffer in the inferior appetite what their will abhors: and are much hindered in the accomplishment of the desires of their spirit and mind. But these evil motions, (though they are called the law of sin, because they come from original sin, and violently tempt and incline to sin), as long as the will does not consent to them, are not sins, because they are not voluntary. ^romans-07-15-douay

16 If then I do that which I will not, I consent to the law, that it is good. ^romans-07-16-douay

17 Now then it is no more I that do it: but sin that dwelleth in me. ^romans-07-17-douay

18 For I know that there dwelleth not in me, that is to say, in my flesh, that which is good. For to will is present with me: but to accomplish that which is good, I find not. ^romans-07-18-douay

19 For the good which I will, I do not: but the evil which I will not, that I do. ^romans-07-19-douay

20 Now if I do that which I will not, it is no more I that do it: but sin that dwelleth in me. ^romans-07-20-douay

21 I find then a law, that when I have a will to do good, evil is present with me. ^romans-07-21-douay

22 For I am delighted with the law of God, according to the inward man: ^romans-07-22-douay

23 But I see another law in my members, fighting against the law of my mind and captivating me in the law of sin that is in my members. ^romans-07-23-douay

24 Unhappy man that I am, who shall deliver me from the body of this death? ^romans-07-24-douay

25 The grace of God, by Jesus Christ our Lord. Therefore, I myself, with the mind serve the law of God: but with the flesh, the law of sin. ^romans-07-25-douay
