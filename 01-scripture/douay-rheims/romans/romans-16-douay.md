# Romans Chapter 16

He concludes with salutations, bidding them beware of all that should oppose the doctrine they had learned.

1 And I commend to you Phebe, our sister, who is in the ministry of the church, that is in Cenchrae: ^romans-16-1-douay

2 That you receive her in the Lord as becometh saints and that you assist her in whatsoever business she shall have need of you. For she also hath assisted many, and myself also. ^romans-16-2-douay

3 Salute Prisca and Aquila, my helpers, in Christ Jesus ^romans-16-3-douay

4 (Who have for my life laid down their own necks: to whom not I only give thanks, but also all the churches of the Gentiles), ^romans-16-4-douay

5 And the church which is in their house. Salute Epenetus, my beloved: who is the firstfruits of Asia in Christ. ^romans-16-5-douay

6 Salute Mary, who hath laboured much among you. ^romans-16-6-douay

7 Salute Andronicus and Junias, my kinsmen and fellow prisoners: who are of note among the apostles, who also were in Christ before me. ^romans-16-7-douay

8 Salute Ampliatus, most beloved to me in the Lord. ^romans-16-8-douay

9 Salute Urbanus, our helper in Christ Jesus and Stachys, my beloved. ^romans-16-9-douay

10 Salute Apellas, approved in Christ. ^romans-16-10-douay

11 Salute them that are of Aristobulus' household. Salute Herodian, my kinsman. Salute them that are of Narcissus' household, who are in the Lord. ^romans-16-11-douay

12 Salute Tryphaena and Tryphosa, who labour in the Lord. Salute Persis, the dearly beloved, who hath much laboured in the Lord. ^romans-16-12-douay

13 Salute Rufus, elect in the Lord, and his mother and mine. ^romans-16-13-douay

14 Salute Asyncritus, Phlegon, Hermas, Patrobas, Hermes: and the brethren that are with them. ^romans-16-14-douay

15 Salute Philologus and Julia, Nereus and his sister, and Olympias: and all the saints that are with them. ^romans-16-15-douay

16 Salute one another with an holy kiss. All the churches of Christ salute you. ^romans-16-16-douay

17 Now I beseech you, brethren, to mark them who make dissensions and offences contrary to the doctrine which you have learned and avoid them. ^romans-16-17-douay

18 For they that are such serve not Christ our Lord but their own belly: and by pleasing speeches and good words seduce the hearts of the innocent. ^romans-16-18-douay

19 For your obedience is published in every place. I rejoice therefore in you. But I would have you to be wise in good and simple in evil. ^romans-16-19-douay

20 And the God of peace crush Satan under your feet speedily. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. ^romans-16-20-douay

21 Timothy, my fellow labourer, saluteth you: and Lucius and Jason and Sosipater, my kinsmen. ^romans-16-21-douay

22 I, Tertius, who wrote this epistle, salute you in the Lord. ^romans-16-22-douay

23 Caius, my host, and the whole church saluteth you. Erastus, the treasurer of the city, saluteth you: and Quartus, a brother. ^romans-16-23-douay

24 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen. ^romans-16-24-douay

25 Now to him that is able to establish you, according to my gospel and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery which was kept secret from eternity; ^romans-16-25-douay

26 (Which now is made manifest by the scriptures of the prophets, according to the precept of the eternal God, for the obedience of faith) known among all nations: ^romans-16-26-douay

27 To God, the only wise, through Jesus Christ, to whom be honour and glory for ever and ever. Amen. ^romans-16-27-douay
