# Romans Chapter 15

He exhorts them to be all of one mind and promises to come and see them.

1 Now, we that are stronger ought to bear the infirmities of the weak and not to please ourselves. ^romans-15-1-douay

2 Let every one of you please his neighbour unto good, to edification. ^romans-15-2-douay

3 For Christ did not please himself: but, as it is written: The reproaches of them that reproached thee fell upon me. ^romans-15-3-douay

4 For what things soever were written were written for our learning: that, through patience and the comfort of the scriptures, we might have hope. ^romans-15-4-douay

5 Now the God of patience and of comfort grant you to be of one mind, one towards another, according to Jesus Christ: ^romans-15-5-douay

6 That with one mind and with one mouth you may glorify God and the Father of our Lord Jesus Christ. ^romans-15-6-douay

7 Wherefore, receive one another, as Christ also hath received you, unto the honour of God. ^romans-15-7-douay

8 For I say that Christ Jesus was minister of the circumcision for the truth of God, to confirm the promises made unto the fathers: **Minister of the circumcision...** That is, executed his office and ministry towards the Jews, the people of the circumcision. ^romans-15-8-douay

9 But that the Gentiles are to glorify God for his mercy, as it is written: Therefore will I confess to thee, O Lord, among the Gentiles and will sing to thy name. ^romans-15-9-douay

10 And again he saith: rejoice ye Gentiles, with his people. ^romans-15-10-douay

11 And again: praise the Lord, all ye Gentiles: and magnify him, all ye people. ^romans-15-11-douay

12 And again, Isaiah saith: There shall be a root of Jesse; and he that shall rise up to rule the Gentiles, in him the Gentiles shall hope. ^romans-15-12-douay

13 Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing: that you may abound in hope and in the power of the Holy Ghost. ^romans-15-13-douay

14 And I myself also, my brethren, am assured of you that you also are full of love, replenished with all knowledge, so that you are able to admonish one another. ^romans-15-14-douay

15 But I have written to you, brethren, more boldly in some sort, as it were putting you in mind, because of the grace which is given me from God, ^romans-15-15-douay

16 That I should be the minister of Christ Jesus among the Gentiles: sanctifying the gospel of God, that the oblation of the Gentiles may be made acceptable and sanctified in the Holy Ghost. ^romans-15-16-douay

17 I have therefore glory in Christ Jesus towards God. ^romans-15-17-douay

18 For I dare not to speak of any of those things which Christ worketh not by me, for the obedience of the Gentiles, by word and deed, ^romans-15-18-douay

19 By the virtue of signs and wonders, in the power of the Holy Ghost, so that from Jerusalem round about, as far as unto Illyricum, I have replenished the gospel of Christ. ^romans-15-19-douay

20 And I have so preached this gospel, not where Christ was named, lest I should build upon another man a foundation. ^romans-15-20-douay

21 But as it is written: They to whom he was not spoken of shall see: and they that have not heard shall understand. ^romans-15-21-douay

22 For which cause also, I was hindered very much from coming to you and have been kept away till now. ^romans-15-22-douay

23 But now, having no more place in these countries and having a great desire these many years past to come unto you, ^romans-15-23-douay

24 When I shall begin to take my journey into Spain, I hope that, as I pass, I shall see you and be brought on my way thither by you: if first, in part, I shall have enjoyed you. ^romans-15-24-douay

25 But now I shall go to Jerusalem, to minister unto the saints. ^romans-15-25-douay

26 For it hath pleased them of Macedonia and Achaia to make a contribution for the poor of the saints that are in Jerusalem. ^romans-15-26-douay

27 For it hath pleased them: and they are their debtors. For, if the Gentiles have been made partakers of their spiritual things, they ought also in carnal things to minister to them. ^romans-15-27-douay

28 When therefore I shall have accomplished this and consigned to them this fruit, I will come by you into Spain. ^romans-15-28-douay

29 And I know that when I come to you I shall come in the abundance of the blessing of the gospel of Christ. ^romans-15-29-douay

30 I beseech you therefore, brethren, through our Lord Jesus Christ and by the charity of the Holy Ghost, that you help me in your prayers for me to God, ^romans-15-30-douay

31 That I may be delivered from the unbelievers that are in Judea and that the oblation of my service may be acceptable in Jerusalem to the saints. ^romans-15-31-douay

32 That I may come to you with joy, by the will of God, and may be refreshed with you. ^romans-15-32-douay

33 Now the God of peace be with, you all. Amen. ^romans-15-33-douay
