# Joshua Chapter 4

Twelve stones are taken out of the river to be set up for a monument of the miracle; and other twelve are placed in the midst of the river.

1 And when they were passed over, the Lord said to Joshua: ^joshua-04-1-douay

2 Choose twelve men, one of every tribe: ^joshua-04-2-douay

3 And command them to take out of the midst of the Jordan, where the feet of the priests stood, twelve very hard stones, which you shall set in the place of the camp, where you shall pitch your tents this night. ^joshua-04-3-douay

4 And Joshua called twelve men, whom he had chosen out of the children of Israel, one out of every tribe, ^joshua-04-4-douay

5 And he said to them: Go before the ark of the Lord your God to the midst of the Jordan, and carry from thence every man a stone on your shoulders, according to the number of the children of Israel, ^joshua-04-5-douay

6 That it may be a sign among you: and when your children shall ask you tomorrow, saying: What means these stones? ^joshua-04-6-douay

7 You shall answer them: The waters of the Jordan ran off before the ark of the covenant of the Lord when it passed over the same: therefore were these stones set for a monument of the children of Israel forever. ^joshua-04-7-douay

8 The children of Israel therefore did as Joshua commanded them, carrying out of the channel of the Jordan twelve stones, as the Lord had commanded him according to the number of the children of Israel unto the place wherein they camped, and there they set them. ^joshua-04-8-douay

9 And Joshua put other twelve stones in the midst of the channel of the Jordan, where the priests stood that carried the ark of the covenant: and they are there until this present day. ^joshua-04-9-douay

10 Now the priests that carried the ark, stood in the midst of the Jordan, till all things were accomplished, which the Lord had commanded Joshua to speak to the people, and Moses had said to him. And the people made haste, and passed over. ^joshua-04-10-douay

11 And when they had all passed over, the ark also of the Lord passed over, and the priests went before the people. ^joshua-04-11-douay

12 The children of Ruben also, and Gad, and half the tribe of Manasses, went armed before the children of Israel, as Moses had commanded them. ^joshua-04-12-douay

13 And forty thousand fighting men by their troops and bands, marched through the plains and fields of the city of Jericho. ^joshua-04-13-douay

14 In that day the Lord magnified Joshua in the sight of all Israel, that they should fear him, as they had feared Moses, while he lived. ^joshua-04-14-douay

15 And he said to him: ^joshua-04-15-douay

16 Command the priests, that carry the ark of the covenant, to come up out of the Jordan. ^joshua-04-16-douay

17 And he commanded them, saying: Come ye up out of the Jordan. ^joshua-04-17-douay

18 And when they that carried the ark of the covenant of the Lord, were come up, and began to tread on the dry ground, the waters returned into their channel, and ran as they were wont before. ^joshua-04-18-douay

19 And the people came up out of the Jordan, the tenth day of the first month, and camped in Galgal, over against the east side of the city of Jericho. ^joshua-04-19-douay

20 And the twelve stones, which they had taken out of the channel of the Jordan, Joshua pitched in Galgal, ^joshua-04-20-douay

21 And said to the children of Israel: When your children shall ask their fathers tomorrow, and shall say to them: What mean these stones? ^joshua-04-21-douay

22 You shall teach them, and say: Israel passed over this Jordan through the dry channel, ^joshua-04-22-douay

23 The Lord your God drying up the waters thereof in your sight, until you passed over: ^joshua-04-23-douay

24 As he had done before in the Red Sea, which he dried up till we passed through: ^joshua-04-24-douay

25 That all the people of the earth may learn the most mighty hand of the Lord, that you also may fear the Lord your God for ever. ^joshua-04-25-douay
