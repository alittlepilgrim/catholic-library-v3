# Joshua Chapter 19

The lots of the tribes of Simeon, Zabulon, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali and Dan. A city is given to Joshua.

1 And the second lot came forth for the children of Simeon by their kindreds: and their inheritance was ^joshua-19-1-douay

2 In the midst of the possession of the children of Juda: Bersabee and Sabee and Molada, ^joshua-19-2-douay

3 And Hasersual, Bala and Asem, ^joshua-19-3-douay

4 And Eltholad, Bethul and Harma, ^joshua-19-4-douay

5 And Siceleg and Bethmarchaboth and Hasersusa, ^joshua-19-5-douay

6 And Bethlebaoth and Sarohen: thirteen cities, and their villages. ^joshua-19-6-douay

7 And Remmon and Athor and Asan: four cities, and their villages. ^joshua-19-7-douay

8 And all the villages round about these cities to Baalath Beer Ramath to the south quarter. This is the inheritance of the children of Simeon according to their kindreds, ^joshua-19-8-douay

9 In the possession and lot of the children of Juda: because it was too great, and therefore the children of Simeon had their possession in the midst of their inheritance. ^joshua-19-9-douay

10 And the third lot fell to the children of Zabulon by their kindreds: and the border of their possession was unto Sarid. ^joshua-19-10-douay

11 And it went up from the sea and from Merala, and came to Debbaseth: as far as the torrent, which is over against Jeconam. ^joshua-19-11-douay

12 And it returneth from Sarid eastward to the borders of Ceseleththabor: and it goeth out to Dabereth and ascendeth towards Japhie. ^joshua-19-12-douay

13 And it passeth along from thence to the east side of Gethhepher and Thacasin: and goeth out to Remmon, Amthar and Noa. ^joshua-19-13-douay

14 And it turneth about to the north of Hanathon: and the outgoings thereof are the valley of Jephtahel, ^joshua-19-14-douay

15 And Cateth and Naalol and Semeron and Jedala and Bethlehem: twelve cities and their villages. ^joshua-19-15-douay

16 This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Zabulon by their kindreds, the cities and their villages. ^joshua-19-16-douay

17 The fourth lot came out to Issachar by their kindreds. ^joshua-19-17-douay

18 And his inheritance was Jezrael and Casaloth and Sunem, ^joshua-19-18-douay

19 And Hapharaim and Seon and Anaharath, ^joshua-19-19-douay

20 And Rabboth and Cesion, Abes, ^joshua-19-20-douay

21 And Rameth and Engannim and Enhadda and Bethpheses. ^joshua-19-21-douay

22 And the border thereof cometh to Thabor and Sehesima and Bethsames: and the outgoings thereof shall be at the Jordan: sixteen cities, and their villages. ^joshua-19-22-douay

23 This is the possession of the sons of Issachar by their kindreds, the cities and their villages. ^joshua-19-23-douay

24 And the fifth lot fell to the tribe of the children of Aser by their kindreds: ^joshua-19-24-douay

25 And their border was Halcath and Chali and Beten and Axaph, ^joshua-19-25-douay

26 And Elmelech and Amaad and Messal: and it reacheth to Carmel by the sea and Sihor and Labanath, ^joshua-19-26-douay

27 And it returneth towards the east to Bethdagon: and passeth along to Zabulon and to the valley of Jephthael towards the north to Bethemec and Nehiel. And it goeth out to the left side of Cabul, ^joshua-19-27-douay

28 And to Abaran and Rohob and Hamon and Cana, as far as the great Sidon. ^joshua-19-28-douay

29 And it returneth to Horma to the strong city of Tyre, and to Hosa: and the outgoings thereof shall be at the sea from the portion of Achziba: ^joshua-19-29-douay

30 And Amma and Aphec and Rohob: twenty-two cities, and their villages. ^joshua-19-30-douay

31 This is the possession of the children of Aser by their kindreds, and the cities and their villages. ^joshua-19-31-douay

32 The sixth lot came out to the sons of Nephtali by their families: ^joshua-19-32-douay

33 And the border began from Heleph and Elon to Saananim, and Adami, which is Neceb, and Jebnael even to Lecum: ^joshua-19-33-douay

34 And the border returneth westward to Azanotthabor, and goeth out from thence to Hucuca, and passeth along to Zabulon southward, and to Aser westward, and to Juda upon the Jordan towards the rising of the sun. ^joshua-19-34-douay

35 And the strong cities are Assedim, Ser, and Emath, and Reccath and Cenereth, ^joshua-19-35-douay

36 And Edema and Arama, Asor, ^joshua-19-36-douay

37 And Cedes and Edri, Enhasor, ^joshua-19-37-douay

38 And Jeron and Magdalel, Horem, and Bethanath and Bethsames: nineteen cities, and their villages. ^joshua-19-38-douay

39 This is the possession of the tribe of the children of Nephtali by their kindreds, the cities and their villages. ^joshua-19-39-douay

40 The seventh lot came out to the tribe of the children of Dan by their families, ^joshua-19-40-douay

41 And the border of their possession was Saraa and Esthaol, and Hirsemes, that is, the city of the sun, ^joshua-19-41-douay

42 Selebin and Aialon and Jethela, ^joshua-19-42-douay

43 Elon and Themna and Acron, ^joshua-19-43-douay

44 Elthece, Gebbethon and Balaath, ^joshua-19-44-douay

45 And Juda and Bane and Barach and Gethremmon: ^joshua-19-45-douay

46 And Mejarcon and Arecon, with the border that looketh towards Joppe, ^joshua-19-46-douay

47 And is terminated there. And the children of Dan went up and fought against Lesem, and took it: and they put it to the sword, and possessed it, and dwelt in it, calling the name of it Lesem Dan, by the name of Dan their father. ^joshua-19-47-douay

48 This is the possession of the tribe of the sons of Dan, by their kindreds, the cities and their villages. ^joshua-19-48-douay

49 And when he had made an end of dividing the land by lot to each one by their tribes, the children of Israel gave a possession to Joshua the son of Nun in the midst of them, ^joshua-19-49-douay

50 According to the commandment of the Lord, the city which he asked for, Thamnath Saraa, in mount Ephraim: and he built up the city, and dwelt in it. ^joshua-19-50-douay

51 These are the possessions which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the princes of the families, and of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed by lot in Silo, before the Lord at the door of the tabernacle of the testimony, and they divided the land. ^joshua-19-51-douay
