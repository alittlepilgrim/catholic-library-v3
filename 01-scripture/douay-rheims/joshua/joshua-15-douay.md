# Joshua Chapter 15

The borders of the lot of Judah. Caleb's portion and conquest. The cities of Judah.

1 Now the lot of the children of Juda by their kindreds was this: From the frontier of Edom, to the desert of Sin southward, and to the uttermost part of the south coast. ^joshua-15-1-douay

2 Its beginning was from the top of the most salt sea, and from the bay thereof, that looketh to the south. ^joshua-15-2-douay

3 And it goeth out towards the ascent of the Scorpion, and passeth on to Sina: and ascendeth into Cadesbarne, and reacheth into Esron, going up to Addar, and compassing Carcaa. ^joshua-15-3-douay

4 And from thence passing along into Asemona, and reaching the torrent of Egypt: and the bounds thereof shall be the great sea, this shall be the limit of the south coast. ^joshua-15-4-douay

5 But on the east side the beginning shall be the most salt sea even to the end of the Jordan: and towards the north from the bay of the sea unto the same river Jordan. ^joshua-15-5-douay

6 And the border goeth up into Beth-Hagla, and passeth by the north into Beth-Araba: going up to the stone of Boen the son of Ruben. ^joshua-15-6-douay

7 And reaching as far as the borders of Debara from the valley of Achor, and so northward looking towards Galgal, which is opposite to the ascent of Adommin, on the south side of the torrent, and the border passeth the waters that are called the fountain of the sun: and the goings out thereof shall be at the fountain Rogel. ^joshua-15-7-douay

8 And it goeth up by the valley of the son of Ennom on the side of the Jebusite towards the south, the same is Jerusalem: and thence ascending to the top of the mountain, which is over against Geennom to the west in the end of the valley of Raphaim, northward. ^joshua-15-8-douay

9 And it passeth on from the top of the mountain to the fountain of the water of Nephtoa: and reacheth to the towns of mount Ephron: and it bendeth towards Baala, which is Cariathiarim, that is to say, the city of the woods. ^joshua-15-9-douay

10 And it compasseth from Baala westward unto mount Seir: and passeth by the side of mount Jarim to the north into Cheslon: and goeth down into Bethsames, and passeth into Thamna. ^joshua-15-10-douay

11 And reacheth northward to a part of Accaron at the side: and bendeth to Sechrona, and passeth mount Baala: and cometh into Jebneel, and is bounded westward with the great sea. ^joshua-15-11-douay

12 These are the borders round about of the children of Juda in their kindreds. ^joshua-15-12-douay

13 But to Caleb the son of Jephone he gave a portion in the midst of the children of Juda, as the Lord had commanded him: Cariath-Arbe the father of Enac, which is Hebron. ^joshua-15-13-douay

14 And Caleb destroyed out of it the three sons of Enac, Sesai and Ahiman, and Tholmai of the race of Enac. ^joshua-15-14-douay

15 And going up from thence he came to the inhabitants of Dabir, which before was called Cariath-Sepher, that is to say, the city of letters. ^joshua-15-15-douay

16 And Caleb said: He that shall smite Cariath-Sepher, and take it, I will give him Axa my daughter to wife. ^joshua-15-16-douay

17 And Othoniel the son of Cenez, the younger brother of Caleb, took it: and he gave him Axa his daughter to wife. ^joshua-15-17-douay

18 And as they were going together, she was moved by her husband to ask a field of her father, and she sighed as she sat on her ass. And Caleb said to her: What aileth thee? ^joshua-15-18-douay

19 But she answered: Give me a blessing: thou hast given me a southern and dry land, give me also a land that is watered. And Caleb gave her the upper and the nether watery ground. ^joshua-15-19-douay

20 This is the possession of the tribe of the children of Juda by their kindreds. ^joshua-15-20-douay

21 And the cities from the uttermost parts of the children of Juda by the borders of Edom to the south, were Cabseel and Eder and Jagur, ^joshua-15-21-douay

22 And Cina and Dimona and Adada, ^joshua-15-22-douay

23 And Cades and Asor and Jethnam, ^joshua-15-23-douay

24 Ziph and Telem and Baloth, ^joshua-15-24-douay

25 New Asor and Carioth, Hesron, which is Asor. ^joshua-15-25-douay

26 Amam, Sama and Molada, ^joshua-15-26-douay

27 And Asergadda and Hassemon and Bethphelet, ^joshua-15-27-douay

28 And Hasersual and Bersabee and Baziothia, ^joshua-15-28-douay

29 And Baala and Jim and Esem, ^joshua-15-29-douay

30 And Eltholad and Cesil and Harma, ^joshua-15-30-douay

31 And Siceleg and Medemena and Sensenna, ^joshua-15-31-douay

32 Lebaoth and Selim and Aen and Remmon: all the cities twenty-nine, and their villages. ^joshua-15-32-douay

33 But in the plains: Estaol and Sarea and Asena, ^joshua-15-33-douay

34 And Zanoe and Engannim and Taphua and Enaim, ^joshua-15-34-douay

35 And Jerimoth and Adullam, Socho and Azeca, ^joshua-15-35-douay

36 And Saraim and Adithaim and Gedera and Gederothaim: fourteen cities, and their villages. ^joshua-15-36-douay

37 Sanan and Hadassa and Magdalgad, ^joshua-15-37-douay

38 Delean and Masepha and Jecthel, ^joshua-15-38-douay

39 Lachis and Bascath and Eglon, ^joshua-15-39-douay

40 Chebbon and Leheman and Cethlis, ^joshua-15-40-douay

41 And Gideroth and Bethdagon and Naama and Maceda: sixteen cities, and their villages. ^joshua-15-41-douay

42 Labana and Ether and Asan, ^joshua-15-42-douay

43 Jephtha and Esna and Nesib, ^joshua-15-43-douay

44 And Ceila and Achzib and Maresa: nine cities, and their villages. ^joshua-15-44-douay

45 Accaron with the towns and villages thereof. ^joshua-15-45-douay

46 From Accaron even to the sea: all places that lie towards Azotus and the villages thereof. ^joshua-15-46-douay

47 Azotus with its towns and villages. Gaza with its towns and villages, even to the torrent of Egypt, and the great sea that is the border thereof. ^joshua-15-47-douay

48 And in the mountain Samir and Jether and Socoth, ^joshua-15-48-douay

49 And Danna and Cariath-senna, this is Dabir: ^joshua-15-49-douay

50 Anab and Istemo and Anim, ^joshua-15-50-douay

51 Gosen and Olon and Gilo: eleven cities and their villages. ^joshua-15-51-douay

52 Arab and Ruma and Esaan, ^joshua-15-52-douay

53 And Janum and Beththaphua and Apheca, ^joshua-15-53-douay

54 Athmatha and Cariath-Arbe, this is Hebron and Sior: nine cities and their villages. ^joshua-15-54-douay

55 Maon and Carmel and Ziph and Jota, ^joshua-15-55-douay

56 Jezrael and Jucadam and Zanoe, ^joshua-15-56-douay

57 Accain, Gabaa and Thamna: ten cities and their villages. ^joshua-15-57-douay

58 Halhul, and Bessur, and Gedor, ^joshua-15-58-douay

59 Mareth, and Bethanoth, and Eltecon: six cities and their villages. ^joshua-15-59-douay

60 Cariathbaal, the same is Cariathiarim the city of woods, and Arebba: two cities and their villages. ^joshua-15-60-douay

61 In the desert Betharaba, Meddin and Sachacha, ^joshua-15-61-douay

62 And Nebsan, and the city of salt, and Engaddi: six cities and their villages. ^joshua-15-62-douay

63 But the children of Juda could not destroy the Jebusite that dwelt in Jerusalem: and the Jebusite dwelt with the children of Juda in Jerusalem until this present day. ^joshua-15-63-douay
