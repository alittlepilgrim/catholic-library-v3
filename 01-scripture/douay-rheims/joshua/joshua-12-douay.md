# Joshua Chapter 12

A list of the kings slain by Moses and Joshua,

1 These are the kings, whom the children of Israel slew and possessed their land beyond the Jordan towards the rising of the sun, from the torrent Arnon unto mount Hermon, and all the east country that looketh towards the wilderness. ^joshua-12-1-douay

2 Sehon king of the Amorrhites, who dwelt in Hesebon, and had dominion from Aroer, which is seated upon the bank of the torrent Arnon, and of the middle part in the valley, and of half Galaad, as far as the torrent Jaboc, which is the border of the children of Ammon. ^joshua-12-2-douay

3 And from the wilderness, to the sea of Ceneroth towards the east, and to the sea of the wilderness, which is the most salt sea, on the east side by the way that leadeth to Bethsimoth: and on the south side that lieth under Asedoth, Phasga. ^joshua-12-3-douay

4 The border of Og the king of Basan, of the remnant of the Raphaims who dwelt in Astaroth, and in Edrai, and had dominion in mount Hermon, and in Salecha, and in all Basan, unto the borders ^joshua-12-4-douay

5 Of Gessuri and Machati, and of half Galaad: the borders of Sehon the king of Hesebon. ^joshua-12-5-douay

6 Moses the servant of the Lord, and the children of Israel slew them, and Moses delivered their land in possession to the Rubenites, and Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasses. ^joshua-12-6-douay

7 These are the kings of the land, whom Joshua and the children of Israel slew beyond the Jordan on the west side from Baalgad in the field of Libanus, unto the mount, part of which goeth up into Seir: and Joshua delivered it in possession to the tribes of Israel, to every one their divisions, ^joshua-12-7-douay

8 As well in the mountains as in the plains and the champaign countries. In Asedoth, and in the wilderness, and in the south was the Hethite and the Amorrhite, the Chanaanite and the Pherezite, the Hevite and the Jebusite. ^joshua-12-8-douay

9 The king of Jericho one: the king of Hai, which is on the side of Bethel, one: ^joshua-12-9-douay

10 The king of Jerusalem one, the king of Hebron one, ^joshua-12-10-douay

11 The king of Jerimoth one, thee king of Lachis one, ^joshua-12-11-douay

12 The king of Eglon one, the king of Gazer one, ^joshua-12-12-douay

13 The king of Dabir one, the king of Gader one, ^joshua-12-13-douay

14 The king of Herma one, the king of Hered one, ^joshua-12-14-douay

15 The king of Lebna one, the king of Odullam one, ^joshua-12-15-douay

16 The king of Maceda one, the king of Bethel one, ^joshua-12-16-douay

17 The king of Taphua one, the king of Opher one, ^joshua-12-17-douay

18 The king of Aphec one, the king of Saron one, ^joshua-12-18-douay

19 The king of Madon one, the king of Asor one, ^joshua-12-19-douay

20 The king of Semeron one, the king of Achsaph one, ^joshua-12-20-douay

21 The king of Thenac one, the king of Mageddo one, ^joshua-12-21-douay

22 Thee king of Cades one, the king of Jachanan of Carmel one, ^joshua-12-22-douay

23 The king of Dor, and of the province of Dor one, the king of the nations of Galgal one, ^joshua-12-23-douay

24 The king of Thersa one: all the kings thirty and one. ^joshua-12-24-douay
