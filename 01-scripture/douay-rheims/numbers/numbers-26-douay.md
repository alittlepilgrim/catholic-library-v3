# Numbers Chapter 26

The people are again numbered by their tribes and families.

1 After the blood of the guilty was shed, the Lord said to Moses and to Eleazar the son of Aaron, the priest: ^numbers-26-1-douay

2 Number the whole sum of the children of Israel from twenty years old and upward, by their houses and kindreds, all that are able to go forth to war. ^numbers-26-2-douay

3 Moses therefore and Eleazar the priest, being in the plains of Moab upon the Jordan over against Jericho, spoke to them that were ^numbers-26-3-douay

4 From twenty years old and upward, as the Lord had commanded: and this is the number of them: ^numbers-26-4-douay

5 Ruben the firstborn of Israel. His sons were Henoch, of whom is the family of the Henochites: and Phallu, of whom is the family of the Phalluites: ^numbers-26-5-douay

6 And Hesron, of whom is the family of the Hesronites: and Charmi, of whom is the family of the Charmites. ^numbers-26-6-douay

7 These are the families of the stock of Ruben: whose number was found to be forty-three thousand seven hundred and thirty. ^numbers-26-7-douay

8 The son of Phallu was Eliab. ^numbers-26-8-douay

9 His sons, were Namuel and Dathan and Abiron. These are Dathan and Abiron the princes of the people, that rose against Moses and Aaron in the sedition of Core, when they rebelled against the Lord: ^numbers-26-9-douay

10 And the earth opening her mouth swallowed up Core, many others dying, when the fire burned two hundred and fifty men. And there was a great miracle wrought, ^numbers-26-10-douay

11 That when Core perished, his sons did not perish. ^numbers-26-11-douay

12 The sons of Simeon by their kindreds: Namuel, of him is the family of the Namuelites: Jamin, of him is the family of the Jaminites: Jachim, of him is the family of the Jachimites: ^numbers-26-12-douay

13 Zare, of him is the family of the Zarites: Saul, of him is the family of the Saulites. ^numbers-26-13-douay

14 These are the families of the stock of Simeon, of which the whole number was twenty-two thousand two hundred. ^numbers-26-14-douay

15 The sons of Gad by their kindreds: Sephon, of him is the family of the Sephonites: Aggi, of him is the family of the Aggites: Suni, of him is the family of the Sunites: ^numbers-26-15-douay

16 Ozni, of him is the family of the Oznites: Her, of him is the family of the Herites: ^numbers-26-16-douay

17 Arod, of him is the family of the Arodites: Ariel, of him is the family of the Arielites. ^numbers-26-17-douay

18 These are the families of Gad, of which the whole number was forty thousand five hundred. ^numbers-26-18-douay

19 The sons of Juda, Her and Onan, who both died in the land of Chanaan. ^numbers-26-19-douay

20 And the sons of Juda by their kindreds were: Sela, of whom is the family of the Selaites: Phares, of whom is the family of the Pharesites: Zare, of whom is the family of the Zarites. ^numbers-26-20-douay

21 Moreover the sons of Phares were: Hesron, of whom is the family of the Hesronites: and Hamul, of whom is the family of the Hamulites. ^numbers-26-21-douay

22 These are the families of Juda, of which the whole number was seventy-six thousand five hundred. ^numbers-26-22-douay

23 The sons of Issachar, by their kindreds: Thola of whom is the family of the Tholaites: Phua, of whom is the family of the Phuaites: ^numbers-26-23-douay

24 Jasub, of whom is the family of the Jasubites: Semran, of whom is the family of the Semranites. ^numbers-26-24-douay

25 These are the kindreds of Issachar, whose number was sixty-four thousand three hundred. ^numbers-26-25-douay

26 The sons of Zabulon by their kindreds: Sared, of whom is the family of the Saredites: Elon, of whom is the family of the Elonites: Jalel, of whom is the family of the Jalelites. ^numbers-26-26-douay

27 These are the kindreds of Zabulon, whose number was sixty thousand five hundred. ^numbers-26-27-douay

28 The sons of Joseph by their kindred, Manasses and Ephraim. ^numbers-26-28-douay

29 Of Manasses was born Machir, of whom is the family of the Machirites. Machir begot Galaad, of whom is the family of the Galaadites. ^numbers-26-29-douay

30 Galaad had sons: Jezer, of whom is the family of the Jezerites: and Helec, of whom is the family of the Helecites: ^numbers-26-30-douay

31 And Asriel, of whom is the family of the Asrielites: and Sechem, of whom is the family of the Sechemites: ^numbers-26-31-douay

32 And Semida, of whom is the family of the Semidaites: and Hepher, of whom is the family of the Hepherites. ^numbers-26-32-douay

33 And Hepher was the father of Salphaad, who had no sons, but only daughters, whose names are these: Maala, and Noa, and Hegla, and Melcha, and Thersa. ^numbers-26-33-douay

34 These are the families of Manasses, and the number of them fifty-two thousand seven hundred. ^numbers-26-34-douay

35 And the sons of Ephraim by their kindreds were these: Suthala, of whom is the family of the Suthalaites: Becher, of whom is the family of the Becherites: Thehen, of whom is the family of the Thehenites. ^numbers-26-35-douay

36 Now the son of Suthala was Heran, of whom is the family of the Heranites. ^numbers-26-36-douay

37 These are the kindreds of the sons of Ephraim: whose number was thirty-two thousand five hundred. ^numbers-26-37-douay

38 These are the sons of Joseph by their families. The sons of Benjamin in their kindreds: Bela, of whom is the family of the Belaites: Asbel, of whom is the family of the Asbelites: Ahiram, of whom is the family of the Ahiramites: ^numbers-26-38-douay

39 Supham, of whom is the family of the Suphamites: Hupham, of whom is the family of the Huphamites. ^numbers-26-39-douay

40 The sons of Bela: Hered, and Noeman. Of Hered, is the family of the Heredites: of Noeman, the family of the Noemanites. ^numbers-26-40-douay

41 These are the sons of Benjamin by their kindreds, whose number was forty-five thousand six hundred. ^numbers-26-41-douay

42 The sons of Dan by their kindreds: Suham, of whom is the family of the Suhamites: These are the kindreds of Dan by their families. ^numbers-26-42-douay

43 All were Suhamites, whose number was sixty-four thousand four hundred. ^numbers-26-43-douay

44 The sons of Aser by their kindreds: Jemna, of whom is the family of the Jemnaites: Jessui, of whom is the family of the Jessuites: Brie, of whom is the family of the Brieites. ^numbers-26-44-douay

45 The sons of Brie: Heber, of whom is the family of the Heberites: and Melchiel, of whom is the family of the Melchielites. ^numbers-26-45-douay

46 And the name of the daughter of Aser, was Sara. ^numbers-26-46-douay

47 These are the kindreds of the sons of Aser, and their number fifty-three thousand four hundred. ^numbers-26-47-douay

48 The sons of Nephtali by their kindreds: Jesiel, of whom is the family of the Jesielites: Guni, of whom is the family of the Gunites: ^numbers-26-48-douay

49 Jeser, of whom is the family of the Jeserites: Sellem, of whom is the family of the Sellemites. ^numbers-26-49-douay

50 These are the kindreds of the sons of Nephtali by their families: whose number was forty-five thousand four hundred. ^numbers-26-50-douay

51 This is the sum of the children of Israel, that were reckoned up, six hundred and one thousand seven hundred and thirty. ^numbers-26-51-douay

52 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^numbers-26-52-douay

53 To these shall the land be divided for their possessions according to the number of names. ^numbers-26-53-douay

54 To the greater number thou shalt give a greater portion, and to the fewer a less: to every one, as they have now been reckoned up, shall a possession be delivered: ^numbers-26-54-douay

55 Yet so that by lot the land be divided to the tribe and families. ^numbers-26-55-douay

56 Whatsoever shall fall by lot, that shall be taken by the more, or the fewer. ^numbers-26-56-douay

57 This also is the number of the sons of Levi by their families: Gerson, of whom is the family of the Gersonites: Caath, of whom is the family of the Caathites: Merari, of whom is the family of the Merarites. ^numbers-26-57-douay

58 These are the families of Levi: The family of Lobni, the family of Hebroni, the family of Core. Now Caath begot Amram: ^numbers-26-58-douay

59 Who had to wife Jochabed the daughter of Levi, who was born to him in Egypt. She bore to her husband Amram sons, Aaron and Moses, and Mary their sister. ^numbers-26-59-douay

60 Of Aaron were born Nadab and Abiu, and Eleazar and Ithamar: ^numbers-26-60-douay

61 Of whom Nadab and Abiu died, when they had offered the strange fire before the Lord. ^numbers-26-61-douay

62 And all that were numbered, were twenty-three thousand males from one month old and upward: for they were not reckoned up among the children of Israel, neither was a possession given to them with the rest. ^numbers-26-62-douay

63 This is the number of the children of Israel, that were enrolled by Moses and Eleazar the priest, in the plains of Moab upon the Jordan, over against Jericho. ^numbers-26-63-douay

64 Among whom there was not one of them that were numbered before by Moses and Aaron in the desert of Sinai. ^numbers-26-64-douay

65 For the Lord had foretold that they should die in the wilderness. And none remained of them, but Caleb the son of Jephone, and Joshua the son of Nun. ^numbers-26-65-douay
