# Numbers Chapter 34

The limits of Canaan; with the names of the men that make the division of it.

1 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^numbers-34-1-douay

2 Command the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: When you are entered into the land of Chanaan, and it shall be fallen into your possession by lot, it shall be bounded by these limits: ^numbers-34-2-douay

3 The south side shall begin from the wilderness of Sin, which is by Edom: and shall have the most salt sea for its furthest limits eastward: **The most salt sea...** The lake of Sodom, otherwise called the Dead Sea. ^numbers-34-3-douay

4 Which limits shall go round on the south side by the ascent of the Scorpion and so into Senna, and reach toward the south as far as Cadesbarne, from whence the frontiers shall go out to the town called Adar, and shall reach as far as Asemona. **The Scorpion...** A mountain so called from having a great number of scorpions. ^numbers-34-4-douay

5 And the limits shall fetch a compass from Asemona to the torrent of Egypt, and shall end in the shore of the great sea. **The great sea...** The Mediterranean. ^numbers-34-5-douay

6 And the west side shall begin from the great sea, and the same shall be the end thereof. ^numbers-34-6-douay

7 But toward the north side the borders shall begin from the great sea, reaching to the most high mountain, **The most high mountain...** Libanus. ^numbers-34-7-douay

8 From which they shall come to Emath, as far as the borders of Sedada: ^numbers-34-8-douay

9 And the limits shall go as far as Zephrona, and the village of Enan. These shall be the borders on the north side. ^numbers-34-9-douay

10 From thence they shall mark out the grounds towards the east side from the village of Enan unto Sephama. ^numbers-34-10-douay

11 And from Sephama the bounds shall go down to Rebla over against the fountain of Daphnis: from thence they shall come eastward to the sea of Cenereth, **Sea of Cenereth...** This is the sea of Galilee, illustrated by the miracles of our Lord. ^numbers-34-11-douay

12 And shall reach as far as the Jordan, and at the last shall be closed in by the most salt sea. This shall be your land with its borders round about. ^numbers-34-12-douay

13 And Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying: This shall be the land which you shall possess by lot, and which the Lord hath commanded to be given to the nine tribes, and to the half tribe. ^numbers-34-13-douay

14 For the tribe of the children of Ruben by their families, and the tribe of the children of Gad according to the number of their kindreds, and half of the tribe of Manasses, ^numbers-34-14-douay

15 That is, two tribes and a half, have received their portion beyond the Jordan over against Jericho at the east side. ^numbers-34-15-douay

16 And the Lord said to Moses: ^numbers-34-16-douay

17 These are the names of the men, that shall divide the land unto you: Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, ^numbers-34-17-douay

18 And one prince of every tribe, ^numbers-34-18-douay

19 Whose names are these: Of the tribe of Juda, Caleb the son of Jephone. ^numbers-34-19-douay

20 Of the tribe of Simeon, Samuel the son of Ammiud. ^numbers-34-20-douay

21 Of the tribe of Benjamin, Elidad the son of Chaselon. ^numbers-34-21-douay

22 Of the tribe of the children of Dan, Bocci the son of Jogli. ^numbers-34-22-douay

23 Of the children of Joseph of the tribe of Manasses, Hanniel the son of Ephod. ^numbers-34-23-douay

24 Of the tribe of Ephraim, Camuel the son of Sephtan. ^numbers-34-24-douay

25 Of the tribe of Zabulon, Elisaphan the son of Pharnach. ^numbers-34-25-douay

26 Of the tribe of Issachar, Phaltiel the prince, the son of Ozan. ^numbers-34-26-douay

27 Of the tribe of Aser, Ahiud the son of Salomi. ^numbers-34-27-douay

28 Of the tribe of Nephtali: Phedael the son of Ammiud. ^numbers-34-28-douay

29 These are they whom the Lord hath commanded to divide the land of Chanaan to the children of Israel. ^numbers-34-29-douay
