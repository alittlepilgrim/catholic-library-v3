# Numbers Chapter 1

The children of Israel are numbered: the Levites are designed to serve the tabernacle.

1 And the Lord spoke to Moses in the desert of Sinai in the tabernacle of the covenant, the first day of the second month, the second year of their going out of Egypt, saying: ^numbers-01-1-douay

2 Take the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel by their families, and houses, and the names of every one, as many as are of the male sex, ^numbers-01-2-douay

3 From twenty years old and upwards, of all the men of Israel fit for war, and you shall number them by their troops, thou and Aaron. ^numbers-01-3-douay

4 And there shall be with you the princes of the tribes, and of the houses in their kindreds, ^numbers-01-4-douay

5 Whose names are these: Of Ruben, Elisur the son of Sedeur. ^numbers-01-5-douay

6 Of Simeon, Salamiel the son of Surisaddai. ^numbers-01-6-douay

7 Of Juda, Nahasson the son of Aminadab. ^numbers-01-7-douay

8 Of Issachar, Nathanael the son of Suar. ^numbers-01-8-douay

9 Of Zabulon, Eliab the son of Helon. ^numbers-01-9-douay

10 And of the sons of Joseph: of Ephraim, Elisama the son of Ammiud: of Manasses, Gamaliel the son of Phadassur. ^numbers-01-10-douay

11 Of Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gedeon. ^numbers-01-11-douay

12 Of Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammisaddai. ^numbers-01-12-douay

13 Of Aser, Phegiel the son of Ochran. ^numbers-01-13-douay

14 Of Gad, Eliasaph the son of Duel. ^numbers-01-14-douay

15 Of Nephtali, Ahira the son of Enan. ^numbers-01-15-douay

16 These are the most noble princes of the multitude by their tribes and kindreds, and the chiefs of the army of Israel: ^numbers-01-16-douay

17 Whom Moses and Aaron took with all the multitude of the common people: ^numbers-01-17-douay

18 And assembled them on the first day of the second month, reckoning them up by the kindreds, and houses, and families, and heads, and names of every one from twenty years old and upward, ^numbers-01-18-douay

19 As the Lord had commanded Moses. And they were numbered in the desert of Sinai. ^numbers-01-19-douay

20 Of Ruben the eldest son of Israel, by their generations and families and houses and names of every head, all that were of the male sex, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-20-douay

21 Were forty-six thousand five hundred. ^numbers-01-21-douay

22 Of the sons of Simeon by their generations and families, and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names and heads of every one, all that were of the male sex, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-22-douay

23 Fifty-nine thousand three hundred. ^numbers-01-23-douay

24 Of the sons of Gad, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-24-douay

25 Forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty. ^numbers-01-25-douay

26 Of the sons of Juda, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-26-douay

27 Were reckoned up seventy-four thousand six hundred. ^numbers-01-27-douay

28 Of the sons of Issachar, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that could go forth to war, ^numbers-01-28-douay

29 Were reckoned up fifty-four thousand four hundred. ^numbers-01-29-douay

30 Of the sons of Zabulon, by the generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-30-douay

31 Fifty-seven thousand four hundred. ^numbers-01-31-douay

32 Of the sons of Joseph, namely, of the sons of Ephraim, by the generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one, from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-32-douay

33 Forty thousand five hundred. ^numbers-01-33-douay

34 Moreover of the sons of Manasses, by the generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that could go forth to war, ^numbers-01-34-douay

35 Thirty-two thousand two hundred. ^numbers-01-35-douay

36 Of the sons of Benjamin, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-36-douay

37 Thirty-five thousand four hundred. ^numbers-01-37-douay

38 Of the sons of Dan, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-38-douay

39 Sixty-two thousand seven hundred. ^numbers-01-39-douay

40 Of the sons of Aser, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, all that were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-40-douay

41 Forty-one thousand and five hundred. ^numbers-01-41-douay

42 Of the sons of Nephtali, by their generations and families and houses of their kindreds, were reckoned up by the names of every one from twenty years old and upward, were able to go forth to war, ^numbers-01-42-douay

43 Fifty-three thousand four hundred. ^numbers-01-43-douay

44 These are they who were numbered by Moses and Aaron, and the twelve princes of Israel, every one by the houses of their kindreds. ^numbers-01-44-douay

45 And the whole number of the children of Israel by their houses and families, from twenty years old and upward, that were able to go to war, ^numbers-01-45-douay

46 Were six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty men. ^numbers-01-46-douay

47 But the Levites in the tribes of their families were not numbered with them. ^numbers-01-47-douay

48 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^numbers-01-48-douay

49 Number not the tribe of Levi, neither shalt thou put down the sum of them with the children of Israel: ^numbers-01-49-douay

50 But appoint them over the tabernacle of the testimony, and all the vessels thereof, and whatsoever pertaineth to the ceremonies. They shall carry the tabernacle and all the furniture thereof: and they shall minister, and shall encamp round about the tabernacle. ^numbers-01-50-douay

51 When you are to go forward, the Levites shall take down the tabernacle: when you are to camp, they shall set it up. What stranger soever cometh to it, shall be slain. ^numbers-01-51-douay

52 And the children of Israel shall camp every man by his troops and bands and army. ^numbers-01-52-douay

53 But the Levites shall pitch their tents round about the tabernacle, lest there come indignation upon the multitude of the children of Israel, and they shall keep watch, and guard the tabernacle of the testimony. ^numbers-01-53-douay

54 And the children of Israel did according to all things which the Lord had commanded Moses. ^numbers-01-54-douay
