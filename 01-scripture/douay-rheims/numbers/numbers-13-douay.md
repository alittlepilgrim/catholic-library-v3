# Numbers Chapter 13

The twelve spies are sent to view the land. The relation they make of it.

1 And the people marched from Haseroth, and pitched their tents in the desert of Pharan. ^numbers-13-1-douay

2 And there the Lord spoke to Moses, saying. ^numbers-13-2-douay

3 Send men to view the land of Chanaan, which I will give to the children of Israel, one of every tribe, of the rulers. ^numbers-13-3-douay

4 Moses did what the Lord had commanded, sending from the desert of Pharan, principal men, whose names are these: ^numbers-13-4-douay

5 Of the tribe of Ruben, Sammua the son of Zechur. ^numbers-13-5-douay

6 Of the tribe of Simeon, Saphat the son of Huri. ^numbers-13-6-douay

7 Of the tribe of Juda, Caleb the son of Jephone. ^numbers-13-7-douay

8 Of the tribe of Issachar, Igal the son of Joseph. ^numbers-13-8-douay

9 Of the tribe of Ephraim, Osee the son of Nun. ^numbers-13-9-douay

10 Of the tribe of Benjamin, Phalti the son of Raphu. ^numbers-13-10-douay

11 Of the tribe of Zabulon, Geddiel the son of Sodi. ^numbers-13-11-douay

12 Of the tribe of Joseph, of the sceptre of Manasses, Gaddi the son of Susi. ^numbers-13-12-douay

13 Of the tribe of Dan, Ammiel the son of Gemalli. ^numbers-13-13-douay

14 Of the tribe of Aser, Sthur the son of Michael. ^numbers-13-14-douay

15 Of the tribe of Nephtali, Nahabi the son of Vapsi. ^numbers-13-15-douay

16 Of the tribe of Gad, Guel the son of Machi. ^numbers-13-16-douay

17 These are the names of the men, whom Moses sent to view the land: and he called Osee the son of Nun, Joshua. ^numbers-13-17-douay

18 And Moses sent them to view the land of Chanaan, and said to them: Go you up by the south side. And when you shall come to the mountains, ^numbers-13-18-douay

19 View the land, of what sort it is, and the people that are the inhabitants thereof, whether they be strong or weak: few in number or many: ^numbers-13-19-douay

20 The land itself, whether it be good or bad: what manner of cities, walled or without walls: ^numbers-13-20-douay

21 The ground, fat or barren, woody or without trees. Be of good courage, and bring us of the fruits of the land. Now it was the time when the firstripe grapes are fit to be eaten. ^numbers-13-21-douay

22 And when they were gone up, they viewed the land from the desert of Sin, unto Rohob as you enter into Emath. ^numbers-13-22-douay

23 And they went up at the south side, and came to Hebron, where were Achiman and Sisai and Tholmai the sons of Enac. For Hebron was built seven years before Tanis the city of Egypt. ^numbers-13-23-douay

24 And forward as far as the torrent of the cluster of grapes, they cut off a branch with its cluster of grapes, which two men carried upon a lever. They took also of the pomegranates and of the figs of that place: ^numbers-13-24-douay

25 Which was called Nehelescol, that is to say, the torrent of the cluster of grapes, because from thence the children of Israel had carried a cluster of grapes. ^numbers-13-25-douay

26 And they that went to spy out the land returned after forty days, having gone round all the country, ^numbers-13-26-douay

27 And came to Moses and Aaron and to all the assembly of the children of Israel to the desert of Pharan, which is in Cades. And speaking to them and to all the multitude, they showed them the fruits of the land: ^numbers-13-27-douay

28 And they related and said: We came into the land to which thou sentest us, which in very deed floweth with milk and honey as may be known by these fruits: ^numbers-13-28-douay

29 But it hath very strong inhabitants, and the cities are great and walled. We saw there the race of Enac. ^numbers-13-29-douay

30 Amalec dwelleth in the south, the Hethite and the Jebusite and the Amorrhite in the mountains: but the Chanaanite abideth by the sea and near the streams of the Jordan. ^numbers-13-30-douay

31 In the mean time Caleb, to still the murmuring of the people that rose against Moses, said: Let us go up and possess the land, for we shall be able to conquer it. ^numbers-13-31-douay

32 But the others, that had been with him, said: No, we are not able to go up to this people, because they are stronger than we. ^numbers-13-32-douay

33 And they spoke ill of the land, which they had viewed, before the children of Israel, saying: The land which we have viewed, devoureth its inhabitants: the people, that we beheld are of a tall stature. **Spoke ill, etc...** These men, who by their misrepresentations of the land of promise, discouraged the Israelites from attempting the conquest of it, were a figure of worldlings, who, by decrying or misrepresenting true devotion, discourage Christians from seeking in earnest and acquiring so great a good, and thereby securing to themselves a happy eternity. ^numbers-13-33-douay

34 There we saw certain monsters of the sons of Enac, of the giant kind: in comparison of whom, we seemed like locusts. ^numbers-13-34-douay
