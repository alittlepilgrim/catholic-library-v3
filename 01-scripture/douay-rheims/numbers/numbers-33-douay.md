# Numbers Chapter 33

The mansions or journeys of the children of Israel towards the land of promise.

1 These are the mansions of the children of Israel, who went out of Egypt by their troops under the conduct of Moses and Aaron, **The mansions...** These mansions, or journeys of the children of Israel from Egypt to the land of promise, were figures, according to the fathers, of the steps and degrees by which Christians leaving sin are to advance from virtue to virtue, till they come to the heavenly mansions, after this life, to see and enjoy God. ^numbers-33-1-douay

2 Which Moses wrote down according to the places of their encamping, which they changed by the commandment of the Lord. ^numbers-33-2-douay

3 Now the children of Israel departed from Ramesses the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month, the day after the phase, with a mighty hand, in the sight of all the Egyptians, ^numbers-33-3-douay

4 Who were burying their firstborn, whom the Lord had slain (upon their gods also he had executed vengeance), ^numbers-33-4-douay

5 And they camped in Soccoth. ^numbers-33-5-douay

6 And from Soccoth they came into Etham, which is in the uttermost borders of the wilderness. ^numbers-33-6-douay

7 Departing from thence they came over against Phihahiroth, which looketh towards Beelsephon, and they camped before Magdalum. ^numbers-33-7-douay

8 And departing from Phihahiroth, they passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness: and having marched three days through the desert of Etham, they camped in Mara. ^numbers-33-8-douay

9 And departing from Mara, they came into Elim, where there were twelve fountains of waters, and seventy palm trees: and there they camped. ^numbers-33-9-douay

10 But departing from thence also, they pitched their tents by the Red Sea. And departing from the Red Sea, ^numbers-33-10-douay

11 They camped in the desert of Sin. ^numbers-33-11-douay

12 And they removed from thence, and came to Daphca. ^numbers-33-12-douay

13 And departing from Daphca, they camped in Alus. ^numbers-33-13-douay

14 And departing from Alus, they pitched their tents in Raphidim, where the people wanted water to drink. ^numbers-33-14-douay

15 And departing from Raphidim, they camped in the desert of Sinai. ^numbers-33-15-douay

16 But departing also from the desert of Sinai, they came to the graves of lust. ^numbers-33-16-douay

17 And departing from the graves of lust, they camped in Haseroth. ^numbers-33-17-douay

18 And from Haseroth they came to Rethma. ^numbers-33-18-douay

19 And departing from Rethma, they camped in Remmomphares. ^numbers-33-19-douay

20 And they departed from thence and came to Lebna. ^numbers-33-20-douay

21 Removing from Lebna they camped in Ressa. ^numbers-33-21-douay

22 And departing from Ressa, they came to Ceelatha. ^numbers-33-22-douay

23 And they removed from thence and camped in the mountain Sepher. ^numbers-33-23-douay

24 Departing from the mountain Sepher, they came to Arada, ^numbers-33-24-douay

25 From thence they went and camped in Maceloth. ^numbers-33-25-douay

26 And departing from Maceloth, they came to Thahath. ^numbers-33-26-douay

27 Removing from Thahath they camped in Thare. ^numbers-33-27-douay

28 And they departed from thence, and pitched their tents in Methca. ^numbers-33-28-douay

29 And removing from Methca, they camped in Hesmona. ^numbers-33-29-douay

30 And departing from Hesmona, they came to Moseroth. ^numbers-33-30-douay

31 And removing from Moseroth, they camped in Benejaacan. ^numbers-33-31-douay

32 And departing from Benejaacan, they came to mount Gadgad. ^numbers-33-32-douay

33 From thence they went and camped in Jetebatha. ^numbers-33-33-douay

34 And from Jetebatha they came to Hebrona. ^numbers-33-34-douay

35 And departing from Hebrona, they camped in Asiongaber. ^numbers-33-35-douay

36 They removed from thence and came into the desert of Sin, which is Cades. ^numbers-33-36-douay

37 And departing from Cades, they camped in mount Hor, in the uttermost borders of the land of Edom. ^numbers-33-37-douay

38 And Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of the Lord: and there he died in the fortieth year of the coming forth of the children of Israel out of Egypt, the fifth month, the first day of the month, ^numbers-33-38-douay

39 When he was a hundred and twenty-three years old. ^numbers-33-39-douay

40 And king Arad the Chanaanite, who dwelt towards the south, heard that the children of Israel were come to the land of Chanaan. ^numbers-33-40-douay

41 And they departed from mount Hor, and camped in Salmona. ^numbers-33-41-douay

42 From whence they removed and came to Phunon. ^numbers-33-42-douay

43 And departing from Phunon, they camped in Oboth. ^numbers-33-43-douay

44 And from Oboth they came to Ijeabarim, which is in the borders of the Moabites. ^numbers-33-44-douay

45 And departing from Ijeabarim they pitched their tents in Dibongab. ^numbers-33-45-douay

46 From thence they went and camped in Helmondeblathaim. ^numbers-33-46-douay

47 And departing from Helmondeblathaim, they came to the mountains of Abarim over against Nabo. ^numbers-33-47-douay

48 And departing from the mountains of Abarim, they passed to the plains of Moab, by the Jordan, over against Jericho. ^numbers-33-48-douay

49 And there they camped from Bethsimoth even to Ablesatim in the plains of the Moabites, ^numbers-33-49-douay

50 Where the Lord said to Moses: ^numbers-33-50-douay

51 Command the children of Israel, and say to them: When you shall have passed over the Jordan, entering into the land of Chanaan, ^numbers-33-51-douay

52 Destroy all the inhabitants of that land: Beat down their pillars, and break in pieces their statues, and waste all their high places, ^numbers-33-52-douay

53 Cleansing the land, and dwelling in it. For I have given it you for a possession. ^numbers-33-53-douay

54 And you shall divide it among you by lot. To the more you shall give a larger part, and to the fewer a lesser. To every one as the lot shall fall, so shall the inheritance be given. The possession shall be divided by the tribes and the families. ^numbers-33-54-douay

55 But if you will not kill the inhabitants of the land: they that remain, shall be unto you as nails in your eyes, and spears in your sides, and they shall be your adversaries in the land of your habitation. ^numbers-33-55-douay

56 And whatsoever I had thought to do to them, I will do to you. ^numbers-33-56-douay
