# Numbers Chapter 2

The order of the tribes in their camp.

1 And the Lord spoke to Moses and Aaron, saying: ^numbers-02-1-douay

2 All the children of Israel shall camp by their troops, ensigns, and standards, and the houses of their kindreds, round about the tabernacle of the covenant. ^numbers-02-2-douay

3 On the east Juda shall pitch his tents by the bands of his army: and the prince of his sons, shall be Nahasson the son of Aminadab. ^numbers-02-3-douay

4 And the whole sum of the fighting men of his stock, were seventy-four thousand six hundred. ^numbers-02-4-douay

5 Next unto him they of the tribe of Issachar encamped, whose prince was Nathanael, the son of Suar. ^numbers-02-5-douay

6 And the whole number of his fighting men were fifty-four thousand four hundred. ^numbers-02-6-douay

7 In the tribe of Zabulon the prince was Eliab the son of Helon. ^numbers-02-7-douay

8 And all the army of fighting men of his stock, were fifty-seven thousand four hundred. ^numbers-02-8-douay

9 All that were numbered in the camp of Juda, were a hundred and eighty-six thousand four hundred: and they by their troops shall march first. ^numbers-02-9-douay

10 In the camp of the sons of Ruben, on the south side, the prince shall be Elisur the son of Sedeur: ^numbers-02-10-douay

11 And the whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were forty-six thousand five hundred. ^numbers-02-11-douay

12 Beside him camped they of the tribe of Simeon: whose prince was Salamiel the son of Surisaddai. ^numbers-02-12-douay

13 And the whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were fifty-nine thousand three hundred. ^numbers-02-13-douay

14 In the tribe of Gad the prince was Eliasaph the son of Duel. ^numbers-02-14-douay

15 And the whole army of his righting men that were numbered, were forty-five thousand six hundred and fifty. ^numbers-02-15-douay

16 All that were reckoned up in the camp of Ruben, were a hundred and fifty-one thousand four hundred and fifty, by their troops: they shall march in the second place. ^numbers-02-16-douay

17 And the tabernacle of the testimony shall be carried by the officers of the Levites and their troops. As it shall be set up, so shall it be taken down. Every one shall march according to their places, and ranks. ^numbers-02-17-douay

18 On the west side shall be the camp of the sons of Ephraim, whose prince was Elisama the son of Ammiud. ^numbers-02-18-douay

19 The whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were forty thousand five hundred. ^numbers-02-19-douay

20 And with them the tribe of the sons of Manasses, whose prince was Gamaliel the son of Phadassur. ^numbers-02-20-douay

21 And the whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were thirty-two thousand two hundred. ^numbers-02-21-douay

22 In the tribe of the sons of Benjamin the prince was Abidan the son of Gedeon. ^numbers-02-22-douay

23 And the whole army of fighting men, that were reckoned up, were thirty-five thousand four hundred. ^numbers-02-23-douay

24 All that were numbered in the camp of Ephraim, were a hundred and eight-thousand one hundred by their troops: they shall march in the third place. ^numbers-02-24-douay

25 On the north side camped the sons of Dan: whose prince was Ahiezar the son of Ammisaddai. ^numbers-02-25-douay

26 The whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were sixty-two thousand seven hundred. ^numbers-02-26-douay

27 Beside him they of the tribe of Aser pitched their tents: whose prince was Phegiel the son of Ochran. ^numbers-02-27-douay

28 The whole army of his fighting men, that were numbered, were forty-one thousand five hundred. ^numbers-02-28-douay

29 Of the tribe of the sons of Nephtali the prince was Ahira the son of Enan. ^numbers-02-29-douay

30 The whole army of his fighting men, were fifty-three thousand four hundred. ^numbers-02-30-douay

31 All that were numbered in the camp of Dan, were a hundred and fifty-seven thousand six hundred: and they shall march last. ^numbers-02-31-douay

32 This is the number of the children of Israel, of their army divided according to the houses of their kindreds and their troops, six hundred and three thousand five hundred and fifty. ^numbers-02-32-douay

33 And the Levites were not numbered among the children of Israel: for so the Lord had commanded Moses. ^numbers-02-33-douay

34 And the children of Israel did according to all things that the Lord had commanded. They camped by their troops, and marched by the families and houses of their fathers. ^numbers-02-34-douay
