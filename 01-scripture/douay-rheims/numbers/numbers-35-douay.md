# Numbers Chapter 35

Cities are appointed for the Levites. Of which six are to be the cities of refuge.

1 And the Lord spoke these things also to Moses in the plains of Moab by the Jordan, over against Jericho: ^numbers-35-1-douay

2 Command the children of Israel that they give to the Levites out of their possessions, ^numbers-35-2-douay

3 Cities to dwell in, and their suburbs round about: that they may abide in the towns, and the suburbs may be for them cattle and beasts: ^numbers-35-3-douay

4 Which suburbs shall reach from the walls of the cities outward, a thousand paces on every side: ^numbers-35-4-douay

5 Toward the east shall be two thousand cubits: and toward the south in like manner shall be two thousand cubits: toward the sea also, which looketh to the west, shall be the same extent: and the north side shall be bounded with the like limits. And the cities shall be in the midst, and the suburbs without. ^numbers-35-5-douay

6 And among the cities, which you shall give to the Levites, six shall be separated for refuge to fugitives, that he who hath shed blood may flee to them: and besides these there shall be other forty-two cities, ^numbers-35-6-douay

7 That is, in all forty-eight with their suburbs. ^numbers-35-7-douay

8 And of these cities which shall be given out of the possessions of the children of Israel, from them that have more, more shall be taken: and from them that have less, fewer. Each shall give towns to the Levites according to the extent of their inheritance. ^numbers-35-8-douay

9 The Lord said to Moses: ^numbers-35-9-douay

10 Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: When you shall have passed over the Jordan into the land of Chanaan, ^numbers-35-10-douay

11 Determine what cities shall be for the refuge of fugitives, who have shed blood against their will. ^numbers-35-11-douay

12 And when the fugitive shall be in them, the kinsman of him that is slain may not have power to kill him, until he stand before the multitude, and his cause be judged. ^numbers-35-12-douay

13 And of those cities, that are separated for the refuge of fugitives, ^numbers-35-13-douay

14 Three shall be beyond the Jordan, and three in the land of Chanaan, ^numbers-35-14-douay

15 As well for the children of Israel as for strangers and sojourners, that he may flee to them, who hath shed blood against his will. ^numbers-35-15-douay

16 If any man strike with iron, and he die that was struck: he shall be guilty of murder, and he himself shall die. ^numbers-35-16-douay

17 If he throw a stone, and he that is struck die: he shall be punished in the same manner. ^numbers-35-17-douay

18 If he that is struck with wood die: he shall be revenged by the blood of him that struck him. ^numbers-35-18-douay

19 The kinsman of him that was slain, shall kill the murderer: as soon as he apprehendeth him, he shall kill him. ^numbers-35-19-douay

20 If through hatred any one push a man, or fling any thing at him with ill design: ^numbers-35-20-douay

21 Or being his enemy, strike him with his hand, and he die: the striker shall be guilty of murder: the kinsman of him that was slain as soon as he findeth him, shall kill him. ^numbers-35-21-douay

22 But if by chance medley, and without hatred, ^numbers-35-22-douay

23 And enmity, he do any of these things, ^numbers-35-23-douay

24 And this be proved in the hearing of the people, and the cause be debated between him that struck, and the next of kin: ^numbers-35-24-douay

25 The innocent shall be delivered from the hand of the revenger, and shall be brought back by sentence into the city, to which he had fled, and he shall abide there until the death of the high priest, that is anointed with the holy oil. **Until the death, etc...** This mystically signified that our deliverance was to be effected by the death of Christ, the high priest and the anointed of God. ^numbers-35-25-douay

26 If the murderer be found without the limits of the cities that are appointed for the banished, ^numbers-35-26-douay

27 And be struck by him that is the avenger of blood: he shall not be guilty that killed him. ^numbers-35-27-douay

28 For the fugitive ought to have stayed in the city until the death of the high priest: and after he is dead, then shall the manslayer return to his own country. ^numbers-35-28-douay

29 These things shall be perpetual, and for an ordinance in all your dwellings. ^numbers-35-29-douay

30 The murderer shall be punished by witnesses: none shall be condemned upon the evidence of one man. ^numbers-35-30-douay

31 You shall not take money of him that is guilty of blood, but he shall die forthwith. ^numbers-35-31-douay

32 The banished and fugitives before the death of the high priest may by no means return into their own cities. ^numbers-35-32-douay

33 Defile not the land of your habitation, which is stained with the blood of the innocent: neither can it otherwise be expiated, but by his blood that hath shed the blood of another. ^numbers-35-33-douay

34 And thus shall your possession be cleansed, myself abiding with you. For I am the Lord that dwell among the children of Israel. ^numbers-35-34-douay
