# Numbers Chapter 29

Sacrifices for the festivals of the seventh month.

1 The first day also of the seventh month shall be venerable and holy unto you; you shall do no servile work therein, because it is the day of the sounding and of trumpets. ^numbers-29-1-douay

2 And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, one calf of the herd, one ram and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish. ^numbers-29-2-douay

3 And for their sacrifices, three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two tenths to a ram, ^numbers-29-3-douay

4 One tenth to a lamb, which in all are seven lambs: ^numbers-29-4-douay

5 And a buck goat for sin, which is offered for the expiation of the people, ^numbers-29-5-douay

6 Besides the holocaust of the first day of the month with the sacrifices thereof, and the perpetual holocaust with the accustomed libations. With the same ceremonies you shall offer a burnt sacrifice for a most sweet odour to the Lord. ^numbers-29-6-douay

7 The tenth day also of this seventh month shall be holy and venerable unto you, and you shall afflict your souls; you shall do no servile work therein. ^numbers-29-7-douay

8 And you shall offer a holocaust to the Lord for a most sweet odour, one calf of the herd, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-8-douay

9 And for their sacrifices, three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, two tenths to a ram, ^numbers-29-9-douay

10 The tenth of a tenth to every lamb, which are in all seven lambs: ^numbers-29-10-douay

11 And a buck goat for sin, besides the things that are wont to be offered for sin, for expiation, and for the perpetual holocaust with their sacrifice and libations. ^numbers-29-11-douay

12 And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month, which shall be unto you holy and venerable, you shall do no servile work, but shall celebrate a solemnity to the Lord seven days. ^numbers-29-12-douay

13 And you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, thirteen calves of the herd, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-13-douay

14 And for their libations three tenths of flour tempered with oil to every calf, being in all thirteen calves: and two tenths to each ram, being two rams, ^numbers-29-14-douay

15 And the tenth of a tenth to every lamb, being in all fourteen lambs: ^numbers-29-15-douay

16 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-16-douay

17 On the second day you shall offer twelve calves of the herd, two rams and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-17-douay

18 And the sacrifices and the libations for every one, for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall duly celebrate: ^numbers-29-18-douay

19 And a buck goat for a sin offering besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-19-douay

20 The third day you shall offer eleven calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-20-douay

21 And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall offer according to the rite: ^numbers-29-21-douay

22 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice, and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-22-douay

23 The fourth day you shall offer ten calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-23-douay

24 And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate in right manner: ^numbers-29-24-douay

25 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-25-douay

26 The fifth day you shall offer nine calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-26-douay

27 And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^numbers-29-27-douay

28 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-28-douay

29 The sixth day you shall offer eight calves, two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-29-douay

30 And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^numbers-29-30-douay

31 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-31-douay

32 The seventh day you shall offer seven calves and two rams, and fourteen lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-32-douay

33 And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^numbers-29-33-douay

34 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-34-douay

35 On the eighth day, which is most solemn, you shall do no servile work: ^numbers-29-35-douay

36 But you shall offer a holocaust for a most sweet odour to the Lord, one calf, one ram, and seven lambs of a year old, without blemish: ^numbers-29-36-douay

37 And the sacrifices and the libations of every one for the calves and for the rams and for the lambs you shall celebrate according to the rite: ^numbers-29-37-douay

38 And a buck goat for sin, besides the perpetual holocaust, and the sacrifice and the libation thereof. ^numbers-29-38-douay

39 These things shall you offer to the Lord in your solemnities: besides your vows and voluntary oblations for holocaust, for sacrifice, for libation, and for victims of peace offerings. ^numbers-29-39-douay
