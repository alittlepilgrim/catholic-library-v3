# Numbers Chapter 7

The offerings of the princes at the dedication of the tabernacle. God speaks to Moses from the propitiatory.

1 And it came to pass in the day that Moses had finished the tabernacle, and set it up, and had anointed and sanctified it with all its vessels, the altar likewise and all the vessels thereof, ^numbers-07-1-douay

2 The princes of Israel and the heads of the families, in every tribe, who were the rulers of them who had been numbered, offered ^numbers-07-2-douay

3 Their gifts before the Lord, six wagons covered, and twelve oxen. Two princes offered one wagon, and each one an ox, and they offered them before the tabernacle. ^numbers-07-3-douay

4 And the Lord said to Moses: ^numbers-07-4-douay

5 Receive them from them to serve in the ministry of the tabernacle, and thou shalt deliver them to the Levites according to the order of their ministry. ^numbers-07-5-douay

6 Moses therefore receiving the wagons and the oxen, delivered them to the Levites. ^numbers-07-6-douay

7 Two wagons and four oxen he gave to the sons of Gerson, according to their necessity. ^numbers-07-7-douay

8 The other four wagons, and eight oxen he gave to the sons of Merari, according to their offices and service, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest. ^numbers-07-8-douay

9 But to the sons of Caath he gave no wagons or oxen: because they serve in the sanctuary and carry their burdens upon their own shoulders. ^numbers-07-9-douay

10 And the princes offered for the dedication of the altar on the day when it was anointed, their oblation before the altar. ^numbers-07-10-douay

11 And the Lord said to Moses: Let each of the princes one day after another offer their gifts for the dedication of the altar. ^numbers-07-11-douay

12 The first day Nahasson the son of Aminadab of the tribe of Juda offered his offering: ^numbers-07-12-douay

13 And his offering was a silver dish weighing one hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-13-douay

14 A little mortar of ten sicles of gold full of incense: ^numbers-07-14-douay

15 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-15-douay

16 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-16-douay

17 And for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five he goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Nahasson the son of Aminadab. ^numbers-07-17-douay

18 The second day Nathanael the son of Suar, prince of the tribe of Issachar, made his offering, ^numbers-07-18-douay

19 A silver dish weighing one hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles, according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-19-douay

20 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-20-douay

21 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-21-douay

22 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-22-douay

23 And for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Nathanael the son of Suar. ^numbers-07-23-douay

24 The third day the prince of the sons of Zabulon, Eliab the son of Helon, ^numbers-07-24-douay

25 Offered a silver dish weighing one hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles by the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-25-douay

26 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-26-douay

27 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-27-douay

28 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-28-douay

29 And for the sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This is the oblation of Eliab the son of Helon. ^numbers-07-29-douay

30 The fourth day the prince of the sons of Ruben, Elisur the son of Sedeur, ^numbers-07-30-douay

31 Offered a silver dish weighing one hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-31-douay

32 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-32-douay

33 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old, for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-33-douay

34 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-34-douay

35 And for victims of peace offerings two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Elisur the son of Sedeur. ^numbers-07-35-douay

36 The fifth day the prince of the sons of Simeon, Salamiel the son of Surisaddai, ^numbers-07-36-douay

37 Offered a silver dish weighing one hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles after the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-37-douay

38 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-38-douay

39 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-39-douay

40 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-40-douay

41 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Salamiel the son of Surisaddai. ^numbers-07-41-douay

42 The sixth day the prince of the sons of Gad, Eliasaph the son of Duel, ^numbers-07-42-douay

43 Offered a silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles by the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-43-douay

44 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-44-douay

45 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-45-douay

46 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-46-douay

47 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Eliasaph the son of Duel. ^numbers-07-47-douay

48 The seventh day the prince of the sons of Ephraim, Elisama the son of Ammiud, ^numbers-07-48-douay

49 Offered a silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-49-douay

50 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-50-douay

51 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-51-douay

52 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-52-douay

53 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Elisama the son of Ammiud. ^numbers-07-53-douay

54 The eighth day the prince of the sons of Manasses, Gamaliel the son of Phadassur, ^numbers-07-54-douay

55 Offered a silver dish, weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles, according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-55-douay

56 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-56-douay

57 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-57-douay

58 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-58-douay

59 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Gamaliel the son of Phadassur. ^numbers-07-59-douay

60 The ninth day the prince of the sons of Benjamin, Abidan the son of Gedeon, ^numbers-07-60-douay

61 Offered a silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles by the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-61-douay

62 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-62-douay

63 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-63-douay

64 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-64-douay

65 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Abidan the son of Gedeon. ^numbers-07-65-douay

66 The tenth day the princes of the sons of Dan, Ahiezer the son of Ammisaddai, ^numbers-07-66-douay

67 Offered a silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles, according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-67-douay

68 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-68-douay

69 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-69-douay

71 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Ahiezer the son of Ammisaddai. ^numbers-07-71-douay

72 The eleventh day the prince of the sons of Aser, Phegiel the son of Ochran, ^numbers-07-72-douay

73 Offered a silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles, according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-73-douay

74 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-74-douay

75 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-75-douay

76 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-76-douay

77 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Phegiel the son of Ochran. ^numbers-07-77-douay

78 The twelfth day the prince of the sons of Nephtali, Ahira the son of Enan, ^numbers-07-78-douay

79 Offered a silver dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles, a silver bowl of seventy sicles, according to the weight of the sanctuary, both full of flour tempered with oil for a sacrifice: ^numbers-07-79-douay

80 A little mortar of gold weighing ten sicles full of incense: ^numbers-07-80-douay

81 An ox of the herd, and a ram, and a lamb of a year old for a holocaust: ^numbers-07-81-douay

82 And a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-07-82-douay

83 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five buck goats, five lambs of a year old. This was the offering of Ahira the son of Enan. ^numbers-07-83-douay

84 These were the offerings made by the princes of Israel in the dedication of the altar, in the day wherein it was consecrated. Twelve dishes of silver: twelve silver bowls: twelve little mortars of gold: ^numbers-07-84-douay

85 Each dish weighing a hundred and thirty sicles of silver, and each bowl seventy sicles: that is, putting all the vessels of silver together, two thousand four hundred sicles, by the weight of the sanctuary. ^numbers-07-85-douay

86 Twelve little mortars of gold full of incense, weighing ten sicles apiece, by the weight of the sanctuary: that is, in all a hundred and twenty sicles of gold. ^numbers-07-86-douay

87 Twelve oxen out of the herd for a holocaust, twelve rams, twelve lambs of a year old, and their libations: twelve buck goats for sin. ^numbers-07-87-douay

88 And for sacrifices of peace offerings, oxen twenty-four, rams sixty, buck goats sixty, lambs of a year old sixty. These things were offered in the dedication of the altar, when it was anointed. ^numbers-07-88-douay

89 And when Moses entered into the tabernacle of the covenant, to consult the oracle, he heard the voice of one speaking to him from the propitiatory, that is over the ark between the two cherubims, and from this place he spoke to him. ^numbers-07-89-douay
