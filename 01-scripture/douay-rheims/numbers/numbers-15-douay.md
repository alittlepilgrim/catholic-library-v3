# Numbers Chapter 15

Certain laws concerning sacrifices. Sabbath breaking is punished with death. The law of fringes on their garments.

1 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^numbers-15-1-douay

2 Speak to the children of Israel and thou shalt say to them: When you shall be come unto the land of your habitation, which I will give you, ^numbers-15-2-douay

3 And shall make an offering to the Lord, for a holocaust, or a victim, paying your vows, or voluntarily offering gifts, or in your solemnities burning a sweet savour unto the Lord, of oxen or of sheep: ^numbers-15-3-douay

4 Whosoever immolateth the victim, shall offer a sacrifice of fine flour, the tenth part of an ephi, tempered with the fourth part of a hin of oil: ^numbers-15-4-douay

5 And he shall give the same measure of wine to pour out in libations for the holocaust or for the victim. For every lamb, ^numbers-15-5-douay

6 And for every ram there shall be a sacrifice of flour of two tenths, which shall be tempered with the third part of a hin of oil: ^numbers-15-6-douay

7 And he shall offer the third part the same measure of wine for the libation, for a sweet savour to the Lord. ^numbers-15-7-douay

8 But when thou offerest a holocaust or sacrifice of oxen, to fulfil thy vow or for victims of peace offerings, ^numbers-15-8-douay

9 Thou shalt give for every ox three tenths of flour tempered with half a hin of oil, ^numbers-15-9-douay

10 And wine for libations of the same measure, for an offering of most sweet savour to the Lord. ^numbers-15-10-douay

11 Thus shalt thou do ^numbers-15-11-douay

12 For every ox and ram and lamb and kid. ^numbers-15-12-douay

13 Both they that are born in the land, and the strangers ^numbers-15-13-douay

14 Shall offer sacrifices after the same rite. ^numbers-15-14-douay

15 There shall be all one law and judgment both for you and for them who are strangers in the land. ^numbers-15-15-douay

16 And the Lord spoke to Moses, saying: ^numbers-15-16-douay

17 Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt say to them: ^numbers-15-17-douay

18 When you are come into the land which I will give you, ^numbers-15-18-douay

19 And shall eat of the bread of that country, you shall separate firstfruits to the Lord, ^numbers-15-19-douay

20 Of the things you eat. As you separate firstfruits of your barnfloors: ^numbers-15-20-douay

21 So also shall you give firstfruits of your dough to the Lord. ^numbers-15-21-douay

22 And if through ignorance you omit any of these things, which the Lord hath spoken to Moses, ^numbers-15-22-douay

23 And by him hath commanded you from the day that he began to command and thenceforward, ^numbers-15-23-douay

24 And the multitude have forgotten to do it: they shall offer a calf out of the herd, a holocaust for a most sweet savour to the Lord, and the sacrifice and libations thereof, as the ceremonies require, and a buck goat for sin: ^numbers-15-24-douay

25 And the priest shall pray for all the multitude of the children of Israel: and it shall be forgiven them, because they sinned ignorantly, offering notwithstanding a burnt offering to the Lord for themselves and for their sin and their ignorance: ^numbers-15-25-douay

26 And it shall be forgiven all the people of the children of Israel: and the strangers that sojourn among them: because it is the fault of all the people through ignorance. ^numbers-15-26-douay

27 But if one soul shall sin ignorantly, he shall offer a she goat of a year old for his sin. ^numbers-15-27-douay

28 And the priest shall pray for him, because he sinned ignorantly before the Lord: and he shall obtain his pardon, and it shall be forgiven him. ^numbers-15-28-douay

29 The same law shall be for all that sin by ignorance, whether they be natives or strangers. ^numbers-15-29-douay

30 But the soul that committeth any thing through pride, whether he be born in the land or a stranger (because he hath been rebellious against the Lord) shall be cut off from among his people: ^numbers-15-30-douay

31 For he hath contemned the word of the Lord, and made void his precept: therefore shall he be destroyed, and shall bear his iniquity. ^numbers-15-31-douay

32 And it came to pass, when the children of Israel were in the wilderness, and had found a man gathering sticks on the sabbath day, ^numbers-15-32-douay

33 That they brought him to Moses and Aaron and the whole multitude. ^numbers-15-33-douay

34 And they put him into prison, not knowing what they should do with him. ^numbers-15-34-douay

35 And the Lord said to Moses: Let that man die, let all the multitude stone him without the camp. ^numbers-15-35-douay

36 And when they had brought him out, they stoned him, and he died as the Lord had commanded. ^numbers-15-36-douay

37 The Lord also said to Moses: ^numbers-15-37-douay

38 Speak to the children of Israel, and thou shalt tell them to make to themselves fringes in the corners of their garments, putting in them ribands of blue: **Fringes...** The Pharisees enlarged these fringes through hypocrisy ([[matthew-23-douay|Matthew 23:5]]) to appear more zealous than other men for the law of God. ^numbers-15-38-douay

39 That when they shall see them, they may remember all the commandments of the Lord, and not follow their own thoughts and eyes going astray after divers things, ^numbers-15-39-douay

40 But rather being mindful of the precepts of the Lord, may do them and be holy to their God. ^numbers-15-40-douay

41 I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, that I might be your God. ^numbers-15-41-douay
