# Numbers Chapter 22

Balac, king of Moab, sends twice for Balaam to curse Israel. In his way Balaam is rebuked by an angel.

1 And they went forward and encamped in the plains of Moab, over against where Jericho is situate beyond the Jordan. ^numbers-22-1-douay

2 And Balac the son of Sephor, seeing all that Israel had done to the Amorrhite, ^numbers-22-2-douay

3 And that the Moabites were in great fear of him, and were not able to sustain his assault, ^numbers-22-3-douay

4 He said to the elders of Madian: So will this people destroy all that dwell in our borders, as the ox is wont to eat the grass to the very roots. Now he was at that time king in Moab. ^numbers-22-4-douay

5 He sent therefore messengers to Balaam the son of Beor, a soothsayer, who dwelt by the river of the land of the children of Ammon, to call him, and to say: Behold a people is come out of Egypt, that hath covered the face of the earth, sitting over against me. ^numbers-22-5-douay

6 Come therefore, and curse this people, because it is mightier than I: if by any means I may beat them and drive them out of my land: for I know that he whom thou shalt bless is blessed, and he whom thou shalt curse is cursed. ^numbers-22-6-douay

7 And the ancients of Moab, and the elders of Madian, went with the price of divination in their hands. And where they were come to Balaam, and had told him all the words of Balac: ^numbers-22-7-douay

8 He answered: Tarry here this night and I will answer whatsoever the Lord shall say to me. And while they stayed with Balaam, God came and said to him: ^numbers-22-8-douay

9 What mean these men that are with thee? ^numbers-22-9-douay

10 He answered: Balac the son of Sephor king of the Moabites hath sent to me, ^numbers-22-10-douay

11 Saying: Behold a people that is come out of Egypt, hath covered the face of the land: come and curse them, if by any means I may fight with them and drive them away. ^numbers-22-11-douay

12 And God said to Balaam: Thou shalt not go with them, nor shalt thou curse the people: because it is blessed. ^numbers-22-12-douay

13 And he rose in the morning and said to the princes: Go into your country, because the Lord hath forbid me to come with you. ^numbers-22-13-douay

14 The princes returning, said to Balac: Balaam would not come with us. ^numbers-22-14-douay

15 Then he sent many more and more noble than he had sent before: ^numbers-22-15-douay

16 Who, when they were come to Balaam, said: Thus saith Balac the son of Sephor, Delay not to come to me: ^numbers-22-16-douay

17 For I am ready to honour thee, and will give thee whatsoever thou wilt: come and curse this people. ^numbers-22-17-douay

18 Balaam answered: If Balac would give me his house full of silver and gold, I cannot alter the word of the Lord my God, to speak either more or less. ^numbers-22-18-douay

19 I pray you to stay here this night also, that I may know what the Lord will answer me once more. **To stay...** His desiring them to stay, after he had been fully informed already that it was not God's will he should go, came from the inclination he had to gratify Balac, for the sake of worldly gain. And this perverse disposition God punished by permitting him to go (though not to curse the people as he would willingly have done), and suffering him to fall still deeper and deeper into sin, till he came at last to give that abominable counsel against the people of God, which ended in his own destruction. So sad a thing it is to indulge a passion for money. ^numbers-22-19-douay

20 God therefore came to Balaam in the night, and said to him: If these men be come to call thee, arise and go with them: yet so, that thou do what I shall command thee. ^numbers-22-20-douay

21 Balaam arose in the morning, and saddling his ass went with them. ^numbers-22-21-douay

22 And God was angry. And an angel of the Lord stood in the way against Balaam, who sat on the ass, and had two servants with him. ^numbers-22-22-douay

23 The ass seeing the angel standing in the way, with a drawn sword, turned herself out of the way, and went into the field. And when Balaam beat her, and had a mind to bring her again to the way, ^numbers-22-23-douay

24 The angel stood in a narrow place between two walls, wherewith the vineyards were enclosed. ^numbers-22-24-douay

25 And the ass seeing him, thrust herself close to the wall, and bruised the foot of the rider. But he beat her again: ^numbers-22-25-douay

26 And nevertheless the angel going on to a narrow place, where there was no way to turn aside either to the right hand or to the left, stood to meet him. ^numbers-22-26-douay

27 And when the ass saw the angel standing, she fell under the feet of the rider: who being angry beat her sides more vehemently with a staff. ^numbers-22-27-douay

28 And the Lord opened the mouth of the ass, and she said: What have I done to thee? Why strikest thou me, lo, now this third time? **Opened the mouth, etc...** The angel moved the tongue of the ass, to utter these speeches, to rebuke, by the mouth of a brute beast, the brutal fury and folly of Balaam. ^numbers-22-28-douay

29 Balaam answered: Because thou hast deserved it, and hast served me ill: I would I had a sword that I might kill thee. ^numbers-22-29-douay

30 The ass said: Am not I thy beast, on which thou hast been always accustomed to ride until this present day? tell me if I ever did the like thing to thee. But he said: Never. ^numbers-22-30-douay

31 Forthwith the Lord opened the eyes of Balaam, and he saw the angel standing in the way with a drawn sword, and he worshipped him falling flat on the ground. ^numbers-22-31-douay

32 And the angel said to him: Why beatest thou thy ass these three times? I am come to withstand thee, because thy way is perverse, and contrary to me: **Perverse...** Because thy inclinations are wicked in being willing for the sake of gain to curse the people of whom I am the guardian. ^numbers-22-32-douay

33 And unless the ass had turned out of the way, giving place to me who stood against thee, I had slain thee, and she should have lived. ^numbers-22-33-douay

34 Balaam said: I have sinned, not knowing that thou didst stand against me: and now if it displease thee that I go, I will return. ^numbers-22-34-douay

35 The angel said: Go with these men, and see thou speak no other thing than what I shall command thee. He went therefore with the princes. ^numbers-22-35-douay

36 And when Balac heard it he came forth to meet him in a town of the Moabites, that is situate in the uttermost borders of Arnon. ^numbers-22-36-douay

37 And he said to Balaam: I sent messengers to call thee, why didst thou not come immediately to me? was it because I am not able to reward thy coming? ^numbers-22-37-douay

38 He answered him: Lo, here I am: shall I have power to speak any other thing but that which God shall put in my mouth? ^numbers-22-38-douay

39 So they went on together, and came into a city, that was in the uttermost borders of his kingdom. ^numbers-22-39-douay

40 And when Balac had killed oxen and sheep, he sent presents to Balaam, and to the princes that were with him. ^numbers-22-40-douay

41 And when morning was come, he brought him to the high places of Baal, and he beheld the uttermost part of the people. ^numbers-22-41-douay
