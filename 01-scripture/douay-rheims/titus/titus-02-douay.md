# Titus Chapter 2

How he is to instruct both old and young. The duty of servants. The Christian's rule of life.

1 But speak thou the things that become sound doctrine: ^titus-02-1-douay

2 That the aged men be sober, chaste, prudent, sound in faith, in love, in patience. ^titus-02-2-douay

3 The aged women, in like manner, in holy attire, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teaching well: ^titus-02-3-douay

4 That they may teach the young women to be wise, to love their husbands, to love their children. ^titus-02-4-douay

5 To be discreet, chaste, sober, having a care of the house, gentle, obedient to their husbands: that the word of God be not blasphemed. ^titus-02-5-douay

6 Young men, in like manner, exhort that they be sober. ^titus-02-6-douay

7 In all things show thyself an example of good works, in doctrine, in integrity, in gravity, ^titus-02-7-douay

8 The sound word that can not be blamed: that he who is on the contrary part may be afraid, having no evil to say of us. ^titus-02-8-douay

9 Exhort servants to be obedient to their masters: in all things pleasing, not gainsaying: ^titus-02-9-douay

10 Not defrauding, but in all things showing good fidelity, that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things. ^titus-02-10-douay

11 For the grace of God our Saviour hath appeared to all men: ^titus-02-11-douay

12 Instructing us, that, denying ungodliness and worldly desires, we should live soberly and justly and godly in this world, ^titus-02-12-douay

13 Looking for the blessed hope and coming of the glory of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ. ^titus-02-13-douay

14 Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity and might cleanse to himself a people acceptable, a pursuer of good works. ^titus-02-14-douay

15 These things speak and exhort and rebuke with all authority. Let no man despise thee. ^titus-02-15-douay
