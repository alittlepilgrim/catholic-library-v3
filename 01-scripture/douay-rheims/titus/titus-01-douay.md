# Titus Chapter 1

What kind of men he is to ordain priests. Some men are to be sharply rebuked.

1 Paul, a servant of God and an apostle of Jesus Christ, according to the faith of the elect of God and the acknowledging of the truth, which is according to godliness: ^titus-01-1-douay

2 Unto the hope of life everlasting, which God, who lieth not, hath promised before the times of the world: ^titus-01-2-douay

3 But hath in due times manifested his word in preaching, which is committed to me according to the commandment of God our Saviour: ^titus-01-3-douay

4 To Titus, my beloved son according to the common faith, grace and peace, from God the Father and from Christ Jesus our Saviour. ^titus-01-4-douay

5 For this cause I left thee in Crete: that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting and shouldest ordain priests in every city, as I also appointed thee: ^titus-01-5-douay

6 If any be without crime, the husband of one wife having faithful children, not accused of riot or unruly. ^titus-01-6-douay

7 For a bishop must be without crime, as the steward of God: not proud, not subject to anger, nor given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre: ^titus-01-7-douay

8 But given to hospitality, gentle, sober, just, holy, continent: ^titus-01-8-douay

9 Embracing that faithful word which is according to doctrine, that he may be able to exhort in sound doctrine and to convince the gainsayers. ^titus-01-9-douay

10 For there are also many disobedient, vain talkers and seducers: especially they who are of the circumcision. ^titus-01-10-douay

11 Who must be reproved, who subvert whole houses, teaching things which they ought not, for filthy lucre's sake. ^titus-01-11-douay

12 One of them a prophet of their own, said: The Cretans are always liars, evil beasts, slothful bellies. ^titus-01-12-douay

13 This testimony is true. Wherefore, rebuke them sharply, that they may be sound in the faith: ^titus-01-13-douay

14 Not giving heed to Jewish fables and commandments of men who turn themselves away from the truth. ^titus-01-14-douay

15 All things are clean to the clean: but to them that are defiled and to unbelievers, nothing is clean: but both their mind and their conscience are defiled. ^titus-01-15-douay

16 They profess that they know God: but in their works they deny him: being abominable and incredulous and to every good work reprobate. ^titus-01-16-douay
