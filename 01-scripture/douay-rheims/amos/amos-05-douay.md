# Amos Chapter 5

A lamentation for Israel: an exhortation to return to God.

1 Hear ye this word, which I take up concerning you for a lamentation. The house of Israel is fallen, and it shall rise no more. ^amos-05-1-douay

2 The virgin of Israel is cast down upon her land, there is none to raise her up. ^amos-05-2-douay

3 For thus saith the Lord God: The city, out of which came forth a thousand, there shall be left in it a hundred: and out of which there came a hundred, there shall be left in it ten, in the house of Israel. ^amos-05-3-douay

4 For thus saith the Lord to the house of Israel: Seek ye me, and you shall live. ^amos-05-4-douay

5 But seek not Bethel, and go not into Galgal, neither shall you pass over to Bersabee: for Galgal shall go into captivity, and Bethel shall be unprofitable. **Bethel, -Galgal,-Bersabee...** The places where they worshipped their idols. ^amos-05-5-douay

6 Seek ye the Lord, and live: lest the house of Joseph be burnt with fire, and it shall devour, and there shall be none to quench Bethel. ^amos-05-6-douay

7 You that turn judgment into wormwood, and forsake justice in the land, ^amos-05-7-douay

8 Seek him that maketh Arcturus, and Orion, and that turneth darkness into morning, and that changeth day into night: that calleth the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth: The Lord is his name. **Arcturus and Orion...** Arcturus is a bright star in the north: Orion a beautiful constellation in the south. ^amos-05-8-douay

9 He that with a smile bringeth destruction upon the strong, and waste upon the mighty. **With a smile...** That is, with all ease, and without making any effort. ^amos-05-9-douay

10 They have hated him that rebuketh in the gate: and have abhorred him that speaketh perfectly. ^amos-05-10-douay

11 Therefore because you robbed the poor, and took the choice prey from him: you shall build houses with square stone, and shall not dwell in them: you shall plant most delightful vineyards, and shall not drink the wine of them. ^amos-05-11-douay

12 Because I know your manifold crimes, and your grievous sins: enemies of the just, taking bribes, and oppressing the poor in the gate. ^amos-05-12-douay

13 Therefore the prudent shall keep silence at that time, for it is an evil time. ^amos-05-13-douay

14 Seek ye good, and not evil, that you may live: and the Lord the God of hosts will be with you, as you have said. ^amos-05-14-douay

15 Hate evil, and love good, and establish judgment in the gate: it may be the Lord the God of hosts may have mercy on the remnant of Joseph. ^amos-05-15-douay

16 Therefore thus saith the Lord the God of hosts the sovereign Lord: In every street there shall be wailing: and in all places that are without, they shall say: Alas, alas! and they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skilful in lamentation to lament. ^amos-05-16-douay

17 And in all vineyards there shall be wailing: because I will pass through in the midst of thee, saith the Lord. ^amos-05-17-douay

18 Woe to them that desire the day of the Lord: to what end is it for you? the day of the Lord is darkness, and not light. ^amos-05-18-douay

19 As if a man should flee from the face of a lion, and a bear should meet him: or enter into the house, and lean with his hand upon the wall, and a serpent should bite him. ^amos-05-19-douay

20 Shall not the day of the Lord be darkness, and not light: and obscurity, and no brightness in it? ^amos-05-20-douay

21 I hate, and have rejected your festivities: and I will not receive the odour of your assemblies. ^amos-05-21-douay

22 And if you offer me holocausts, and your gifts, I will not receive them: neither will I regard the vows of your fat beasts. ^amos-05-22-douay

23 Take away from me the tumult of thy songs: and I will not hear the canticles of thy harp. ^amos-05-23-douay

24 But judgment shall be revealed as water, and justice as a mighty torrent. ^amos-05-24-douay

25 Did you offer victims and sacrifices to me in the desert for forty years, O house of Israel? **Did you offer, etc...** Except the sacrifices that were offered at the first, in the dedication of the tabernacle, the Israelites offered no sacrifices in the desert. ^amos-05-25-douay

26 But you carried a tabernacle for your Moloch, and the image of your idols, the star of your god, which you made to yourselves. **A tabernacle, etc...** All this alludes to the idolatry which they committed, when they were drawn away by the daughters of Moab to the worship of their gods ([[numbers-25-douay|Numbers 25]]). ^amos-05-26-douay

27 And I will cause you to go into captivity beyond Damascus, saith the Lord, the God of hosts is his name. ^amos-05-27-douay
