# Amos Chapter 8

Under the figure of a hook, which brings down the fruit, the approaching desolation of Israel is foreshowed for their avarice and injustices.

1 These things the Lord showed to me: and behold a hook to draw down the fruit. ^amos-08-1-douay

2 And he said: What seest thou, Amos? And I said: A hook to draw down fruit. And the Lord said to me: ^amos-08-2-douay

The end is come upon my people Israel: I will not again pass by them any more.

3 And the hinges of the temple shall screak in that day, saith the Lord God: many shall die: silence shall be cast in every place. ^amos-08-3-douay

4 Hear this, you that crush the poor, and make the needy of the land to fail, ^amos-08-4-douay

5 Saying: When will the month be over, and we shall sell our wares: and the sabbath, and we shall open the corn: that we may lessen the measure, and increase the sicle, and may convey in deceitful balances, ^amos-08-5-douay

6 That we may possess the needy for money, and the poor for a pair of shoes, and may sell the refuse of the corn? ^amos-08-6-douay

7 The Lord hath sworn against the pride of Jacob: surely I will never forget all their works. ^amos-08-7-douay

8 Shall not the land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein: and rise up altogether as a river, and be cast out, and run down as the river of Egypt? ^amos-08-8-douay

9 And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord God, that the sun shall go down at midday, and I will make the earth dark in the day of light: ^amos-08-9-douay

10 And I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation: and I will bring up sackcloth upon every back of yours, and baldness upon every head: and I will make it as the mourning of an only son, and the latter end thereof as a bitter day. ^amos-08-10-douay

11 Behold the days come, saith the Lord, and I will send forth a famine into the land: not a famine of bread, nor a thirst of water, but of hearing the word of the Lord. ^amos-08-11-douay

12 And they shall move from sea to sea, and from the north to the east: they shall go about seeking the word of the Lord, and shall not find it. ^amos-08-12-douay

13 In that day the fair virgins, and the young men shall faint for thirst. ^amos-08-13-douay

14 They that swear by the sin of Samaria, and say: Thy God, O Dan, liveth: and the way of Bersabee liveth: and they shall fall, and shall rise no more. ^amos-08-14-douay
