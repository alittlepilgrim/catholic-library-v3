# Amos Chapter 6

The desolation of Israel for their pride and luxury.

1 Woe to you that are wealthy in Sion, and to you that have confidence in the mountain of Samaria: ye great men, heads of the people, that go in with state into the house of Israel. ^amos-06-1-douay

2 Pass ye over to Chalane, and see, and go from thence into Emath the great: and go down into Geth of the Philistines, and to all the best kingdoms of these: if their border be larger than your border. ^amos-06-2-douay

3 You that are separated unto the evil day: and that approach to the throne of iniquity; ^amos-06-3-douay

4 You that sleep upon beds of ivory, and are wanton on your couches: that eat the lambs out of the flock, and the calves out of the midst of the herd; ^amos-06-4-douay

5 You that sing to the sound of the [[psalm-00-douay|psaltery]]: they have thought themselves to have instruments of music like David; ^amos-06-5-douay

6 That drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the best ointments: and they are not concerned for the affliction of Joseph. ^amos-06-6-douay

7 Wherefore now they shall go captive at the head of them that go into captivity: and the faction of the luxurious ones shall be taken away. ^amos-06-7-douay

8 The Lord God hath sworn by his own soul, saith the Lord the God of hosts: I detest the pride of Jacob, and I hate his houses, and I will deliver up the city with the inhabitants thereof. ^amos-06-8-douay

9 And if there remain ten men in one house, they also shall die. ^amos-06-9-douay

10 And a man's kinsman shall take him up, and shall burn him, that he may carry the bones out of the house; and he shall say to him that is in the inner rooms of the house: Is there yet any with thee? ^amos-06-10-douay

11 And he shall answer: There is an end. And he shall say to him: Hold thy peace, and mention not the name of the Lord. ^amos-06-11-douay

12 For behold the Lord hath commanded, and he will strike the greater house with breaches, and the lesser house with clefts. ^amos-06-12-douay

13 Can horses run upon the rocks, or can any one plough with buffles? for you have turned judgment into bitterness, and the fruit of justice into wormwood. ^amos-06-13-douay

14 You that rejoice in a thing of nought: you that say: Have we not taken unto us horns by our own strength? ^amos-06-14-douay

15 But behold, I will raise up a nation against you, O house of Israel, saith the Lord the God of hosts; and they shall destroy you from the entrance of Emath, even to the torrent of the desert. ^amos-06-15-douay
