# Amos

Amos prophesied in Israel about the same time as Hosea: and was called from following the cattle to denounce God's judgments to the people of Israel, and the neighbouring nations, for their repeated crimes, in which they continued without repentance. (*For more information, see the article* AMOS *in the Catholic Encyclopedia*.)

[[amos-01-douay|**Chapter 1.**]] The prophet threatens Damascus, Gaza, Tyre, Edom, and Ammon with the judgments of God, for their obstinacy in sin.

[[amos-02-douay|**Chapter 2.**]] The judgments with which God threatens Moab, Juda, and Israel for their sins, and their ingratitude.

[[amos-03-douay|**Chapter 3.**]] The evils that shall fall upon Israel for their sins.

[[amos-04-douay|**Chapter 4.**]] The Israelites are reproved for their oppressing the poor, for their idolatry, and their incorrigibleness.

[[amos-05-douay|**Chapter 5.**]] A lamentation for Israel: an exhortation to return to God.

[[amos-06-douay|**Chapter 6.**]] The desolation of Israel for their pride and luxury.

[[amos-07-douay|**Chapter 7.**]] The prophet sees, in three visions, evils coming upon Israel: he is accused of treason by the false priest of Bethel.

[[amos-08-douay|**Chapter 8.**]] Under the figure of a hook, which bringeth down the fruit, the approaching desolation of Israel is foreshowed for their avarice and injustices.

[[amos-09-douay|**Chapter 9.**]] The certainty of the desolation of Israel: the restoring of the tabernacle of David, and the conversion of the Gentiles to the church; which shall flourish for ever.
