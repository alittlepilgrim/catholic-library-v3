# 1 Thessalonians Chapter 5

The day of the Lord shall come when least expected. Exhortations to several duties.

1 But of the times and moments, brethren, you need not, that we should write to you: ^1-thessalonians-05-1-douay

2 For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord shall so come as a thief in the night. ^1-thessalonians-05-2-douay

3 For when they shall say: Peace and security; then shall sudden destruction come upon them, as the pains upon her that is with child, and they shall not escape. ^1-thessalonians-05-3-douay

4 But you, brethren, are not in darkness, that the day should overtake you as a thief. ^1-thessalonians-05-4-douay

5 For all you are the children of light and children of the day: we are not of the night nor of darkness. ^1-thessalonians-05-5-douay

6 Therefore, let us not sleep, as others do: but let us watch, and be sober. ^1-thessalonians-05-6-douay

7 For they that sleep, sleep in the night; and they that are drunk, are drunk in the night. ^1-thessalonians-05-7-douay

8 But let us, who are of the day, be sober, having on the breast plate of faith and charity and, for a helmet, the hope of salvation. ^1-thessalonians-05-8-douay

9 For God hath not appointed us unto wrath: but unto the purchasing of salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ, ^1-thessalonians-05-9-douay

10 Who died for us: that, whether we watch or sleep, we may live together with him. ^1-thessalonians-05-10-douay

11 For which cause comfort one another and edify one another, as you also do. ^1-thessalonians-05-11-douay

12 And we beseech you, brethren, to know them who labour among you and are over you in the Lord and admonish you; ^1-thessalonians-05-12-douay

13 That you esteem them more abundantly in charity, for their work's sake. Have peace with them. ^1-thessalonians-05-13-douay

14 And we beseech you, brethren, rebuke the unquiet: comfort the feeble-minded: support the weak: be patient towards all men. **The unquiet...** That is, such as are irregular and disorderly. ^1-thessalonians-05-14-douay

15 See that none render evil for evil to any man: but ever follow that which is good towards each other and towards all men. ^1-thessalonians-05-15-douay

16 Always rejoice. ^1-thessalonians-05-16-douay

17 Pray without ceasing. ^1-thessalonians-05-17-douay

18 In all things give thanks for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you all. ^1-thessalonians-05-18-douay

19 Extinguish not the spirit. ^1-thessalonians-05-19-douay

20 Despise not prophecies. ^1-thessalonians-05-20-douay

21 But prove all things: hold fast that which is good. ^1-thessalonians-05-21-douay

22 From all appearance of evil refrain yourselves. ^1-thessalonians-05-22-douay

23 And may the God of peace himself sanctify you in all things: that your whole spirit and soul and body may be preserved blameless in the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ. ^1-thessalonians-05-23-douay

24 He is faithful who hath called you, who also will do it. ^1-thessalonians-05-24-douay

25 Brethren, pray for us. ^1-thessalonians-05-25-douay

26 Salute all the brethren with a holy kiss. ^1-thessalonians-05-26-douay

27 I charge you by the Lord that this epistle be read to all the holy brethren. ^1-thessalonians-05-27-douay

28 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen. ^1-thessalonians-05-28-douay
