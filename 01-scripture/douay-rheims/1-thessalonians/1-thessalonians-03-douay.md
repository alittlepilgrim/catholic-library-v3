# 1 Thessalonians Chapter 3

The apostle's concern and love for the Thessalonians.

1 For which cause, forbearing no longer, we thought it good to remain at Athens alone. ^1-thessalonians-03-1-douay

2 And we sent Timothy, our brother and the minister of God in the gospel of Christ, to confirm you and exhort you concerning your faith: ^1-thessalonians-03-2-douay

3 That no man should be moved in these tribulations: for yourselves know that we are appointed thereunto. ^1-thessalonians-03-3-douay

4 For even when we were with you, we foretold you that we should suffer tribulations: as also it is come to pass, and you know. ^1-thessalonians-03-4-douay

5 For this cause also, I, forbearing no longer, sent to know your faith: lest perhaps he that tempteth should have tempted you: and our labour should be made vain. ^1-thessalonians-03-5-douay

6 But now when Timothy came to us from you and related to us your faith and charity, and that you have a good remembrance of us always, desiring to see us as we also to see you: ^1-thessalonians-03-6-douay

7 Therefore we were comforted, brethren, in you, in all our necessity and tribulation, by your faith. ^1-thessalonians-03-7-douay

8 Because now we live, if you stand in the Lord. ^1-thessalonians-03-8-douay

9 For what thanks can we return to God for you, in all the joy wherewith we rejoice for you before our God, ^1-thessalonians-03-9-douay

10 Night and day more abundantly praying that we may see your face and may accomplish those things that are wanting to your faith? ^1-thessalonians-03-10-douay

11 Now God himself and our Father and our Lord Jesus Christ, direct our way unto you. ^1-thessalonians-03-11-douay

12 And may the Lord multiply you and make you abound in charity towards one another and towards all men: as we do also towards you, ^1-thessalonians-03-12-douay

13 To confirm your hearts without blame, in holiness, before God and our Father, at the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, with all his saints. Amen. ^1-thessalonians-03-13-douay
