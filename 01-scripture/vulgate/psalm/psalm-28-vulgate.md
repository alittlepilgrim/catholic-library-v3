# Psalm 28

*Douay-Rheims:* [[psalm-28-douay|Psalm 28]]

1 Psalmus David, in consummatione tabernaculi. Afferte Domino, filii Dei, afferte Domino filios arietum. ^psalm-28-1-vulgate

2 Afferte Domino gloriam et honorem ; afferte Domino gloriam nomini ejus ; adorate Dominum in atrio sancto ejus. ^psalm-28-2-vulgate

3 Vox Domini super aquas ; Deus majestatis intonuit : Dominus super aquas multas. ^psalm-28-3-vulgate

4 Vox Domini in virtute ; vox Domini in magnificentia. ^psalm-28-4-vulgate

5 Vox Domini confringentis cedros, et confringet Dominus cedros Libani : ^psalm-28-5-vulgate

6 et comminuet eas, tamquam vitulum Libani, et dilectus quemadmodum filius unicornium. ^psalm-28-6-vulgate

7 Vox Domini intercidentis flammam ignis ; ^psalm-28-7-vulgate

8 vox Domini concutientis desertum : et commovebit Dominus desertum Cades. ^psalm-28-8-vulgate

9 Vox Domini præparantis cervos : et revelabit condensa, et in templo ejus omnes dicent gloriam. ^psalm-28-9-vulgate

10 Dominus diluvium inhabitare facit, et sedebit Dominus rex in æternum. ^psalm-28-10-vulgate

11 Dominus virtutem populo suo dabit ; Dominus benedicet populo suo in pace. ^psalm-28-11-vulgate
