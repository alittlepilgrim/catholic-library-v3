# Psalm 81

*Douay-Rheims:* [[psalm-81-douay|Psalm 81]]

1 Psalmus Asaph. Deus stetit in synagoga deorum ; in medio autem deos dijudicat. ^psalm-81-1-vulgate

2 Usquequo judicatis iniquitatem, et facies peccatorum sumitis ? ^psalm-81-2-vulgate

3 Judicate egeno et pupillo ; humilem et pauperem justificate. ^psalm-81-3-vulgate

4 Eripite pauperem, et egenum de manu peccatoris liberate. ^psalm-81-4-vulgate

5 Nescierunt, neque intellexerunt ; in tenebris ambulant : movebuntur omnia fundamenta terræ. ^psalm-81-5-vulgate

6 Ego dixi : Dii estis, et filii Excelsi omnes. ^psalm-81-6-vulgate

7 Vos autem sicut homines moriemini, et sicut unus de principibus cadetis. ^psalm-81-7-vulgate

8 Surge, Deus, judica terram, quoniam tu hæreditabis in omnibus gentibus. ^psalm-81-8-vulgate
