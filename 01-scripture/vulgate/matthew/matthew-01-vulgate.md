# Matthew 1

*Douay-Rheims:* [[matthew-01-douay|Matthew 1]]

1 Liber generationis Jesu Christi filii David, filii Abraham. ^matthew-01-1-vulgate

2 Abraham genuit Isaac. Isaac autem genuit Jacob. Jacob autem genuit Judam, et fratres ejus. ^matthew-01-2-vulgate

3 Judas autem genuit Phares, et Zaram de Thamar. Phares autem genuit Esron. Esron autem genuit Aram. ^matthew-01-3-vulgate

4 Aram autem genuit Aminadab. Aminadab autem genuit Naasson. Naasson autem genuit Salmon. ^matthew-01-4-vulgate

5 Salmon autem genuit Booz de Rahab. Booz autem genuit Obed ex Ruth. Obed autem genuit Jesse. Jesse autem genuit David regem. ^matthew-01-5-vulgate

6 David autem rex genuit Salomonem ex ea quæ fuit Uriæ. ^matthew-01-6-vulgate

7 Salomon autem genuit Roboam. Roboam autem genuit Abiam. Abias autem genuit Asa. ^matthew-01-7-vulgate

8 Asa autem genuit Josaphat. Josaphat autem genuit Joram. Joram autem genuit Oziam. ^matthew-01-8-vulgate

9 Ozias autem genuit Joatham. Joatham autem genuit Achaz. Achaz autem genuit Ezechiam. ^matthew-01-9-vulgate

10 Ezechias autem genuit Manassen. Manasses autem genuit Amon. Amon autem genuit Josiam. ^matthew-01-10-vulgate

11 Josias autem genuit Jechoniam, et fratres ejus in transmigratione Babylonis. ^matthew-01-11-vulgate

12 Et post transmigrationem Babylonis : Jechonias genuit Salathiel. Salathiel autem genuit Zorobabel. ^matthew-01-12-vulgate

13 Zorobabel autem genuit Abiud. Abiud autem genuit Eliacim. Eliacim autem genuit Azor. ^matthew-01-13-vulgate

14 Azor autem genuit Sadoc. Sadoc autem genuit Achim. Achim autem genuit Eliud. ^matthew-01-14-vulgate

15 Eliud autem genuit Eleazar. Eleazar autem genuit Mathan. Mathan autem genuit Jacob. ^matthew-01-15-vulgate

16 Jacob autem genuit Joseph virum Mariæ, de qua natus est Jesus, qui vocatur Christus. ^matthew-01-16-vulgate

17 Omnes itaque generationes ab Abraham usque ad David, generationes quatuordecim : et a David usque ad transmigrationem Babylonis, generationes quatuordecim : et a transmigratione Babylonis usque ad Christum, generationes quatuordecim. ^matthew-01-17-vulgate

18 Christi autem generatio sic erat : cum esset desponsata mater ejus Maria Joseph, antequam convenirent inventa est in utero habens de Spiritu Sancto. ^matthew-01-18-vulgate

19 Joseph autem vir ejus cum esset justus, et nollet eam traducere, voluit occulte dimittere eam. ^matthew-01-19-vulgate

20 Hæc autem eo cogitante, ecce angelus Domini apparuit in somnis ei, dicens : Joseph, fili David, noli timere accipere Mariam conjugem tuam : quod enim in ea natum est, de Spiritu Sancto est. ^matthew-01-20-vulgate

21 Pariet autem filium : et vocabis nomen ejus Jesum : ipse enim salvum faciet populum suum a peccatis eorum. ^matthew-01-21-vulgate

22 Hoc autem totum factum est, ut adimpleretur quod dictum est a Domino per prophetam dicentem : ^matthew-01-22-vulgate

23 Ecce virgo in utero habebit, et pariet filium : et vocabunt nomen ejus Emmanuel, quod est interpretatum Nobiscum Deus. ^matthew-01-23-vulgate

24 Exsurgens autem Joseph a somno, fecit sicut præcepit ei angelus Domini, et accepit conjugem suam. ^matthew-01-24-vulgate

25 Et non cognoscebat eam donec peperit filium suum primogenitum : et vocavit nomen ejus Jesum. ^matthew-01-25-vulgate
