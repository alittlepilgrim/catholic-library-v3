# 1 Chronicles 6

*Douay-Rheims:* [[1-chronicles-06-douay|1 Chronicles 6]]

1 Filii Levi : Gerson, Caath, et Merari. ^1-chronicles-06-1-vulgate

2 Filii Caath : Amram, Isaar, Hebron, et Oziel. ^1-chronicles-06-2-vulgate

3 Filii Amram : Aaron, Moyses, et Maria. Filii Aaron : Nadab et Abiu, Eleazar, et Ithamar. ^1-chronicles-06-3-vulgate

4 Eleazar genuit Phinees, et Phinees genuit Abisue. ^1-chronicles-06-4-vulgate

5 Abisue vero genuit Bocci, et Bocci genuit Ozi. ^1-chronicles-06-5-vulgate

6 Ozi genuit Zaraiam, et Zaraias genuit Meraioth. ^1-chronicles-06-6-vulgate

7 Porro Meraioth genuit Amariam, et Amarias genuit Achitob. ^1-chronicles-06-7-vulgate

8 Achitob genuit Sadoc, et Sadoc genuit Achimaas, ^1-chronicles-06-8-vulgate

9 Achimaas genuit Azariam, Azarias genuit Johanan, ^1-chronicles-06-9-vulgate

10 Johanan genuit Azariam : ipse est qui sacerdotio functus est in domo quam ædificavit Salomon in Jerusalem. ^1-chronicles-06-10-vulgate

11 Genuit autem Azarias Amariam, et Amarias genuit Achitob, ^1-chronicles-06-11-vulgate

12 Achitob genuit Sadoc, et Sadoc genuit Sellum, ^1-chronicles-06-12-vulgate

13 Sellum genuit Helciam, et Helcias genuit Azariam, ^1-chronicles-06-13-vulgate

14 Azarias genuit Saraiam, et Saraias genuit Josedec. ^1-chronicles-06-14-vulgate

15 Porro Josedec egressus est, quando transtulit Dominus Judam et Jerusalem per manus Nabuchodonosor. ^1-chronicles-06-15-vulgate

16 Filii ergo Levi : Gersom, Caath, et Merari. ^1-chronicles-06-16-vulgate

17 Et hæc nomina filiorum Gersom : Lobni, et Semei. ^1-chronicles-06-17-vulgate

18 Filii Caath : Amram, et Isaar, et Hebron, et Oziel. ^1-chronicles-06-18-vulgate

19 Filii Merari : Moholi et Musi. Hæ autem cognationes Levi secundum familias eorum. ^1-chronicles-06-19-vulgate

20 Gersom : Lobni filius ejus, Jahath filius ejus, Zamma filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-20-vulgate

21 Joah filius ejus, Addo filius ejus, Zara filius ejus, Jethrai filius ejus. ^1-chronicles-06-21-vulgate

22 Filii Caath : Aminadab filius ejus, Core filius ejus, Asir filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-22-vulgate

23 Elcana filius ejus, Abiasaph filius ejus, Asir filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-23-vulgate

24 Thahath filius ejus, Uriel filius ejus, Ozias filius ejus, Saul filius ejus. ^1-chronicles-06-24-vulgate

25 Filii Elcana, Amasai et Achimoth ^1-chronicles-06-25-vulgate

26 et Elcana. Filii Elcana : Sophai filius ejus, Nahath filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-26-vulgate

27 Eliab filius ejus, Jeroham filius ejus, Elcana filius ejus. ^1-chronicles-06-27-vulgate

28 Filii Samuel : primogenitus Vasseni, et Abia. ^1-chronicles-06-28-vulgate

29 Filii autem Merari, Moholi : Lobni filius ejus, Semei filius ejus, Oza filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-29-vulgate

30 Sammaa filius ejus, Haggia filius ejus, Asaia filius ejus. ^1-chronicles-06-30-vulgate

31 Isti sunt quos constituit David super cantores domus Domini, ex quo collocata est arca : ^1-chronicles-06-31-vulgate

32 et ministrabant coram tabernaculo testimonii, canentes donec ædificaret Salomon domum Domini in Jerusalem : stabant autem juxta ordinem suum in ministerio. ^1-chronicles-06-32-vulgate

33 Hi vero sunt qui assistebant cum filiis suis, de filiis Caath, Hemam cantor filius Johel, filii Samuel, ^1-chronicles-06-33-vulgate

34 filii Elcana, filii Jeroham, filii Eliel, filii Thohu, ^1-chronicles-06-34-vulgate

35 filii Suph, filii Elcana, filii Mahath, filii Amasai, ^1-chronicles-06-35-vulgate

36 filii Elcana, filii Johel, filii Azariæ, filii Sophoniæ, ^1-chronicles-06-36-vulgate

37 filii Thahath, filii Asir, filii Abiasaph, filii Core, ^1-chronicles-06-37-vulgate

38 filii Isaar, filii Caath, filii Levi, filii Israël. ^1-chronicles-06-38-vulgate

39 Et frater ejus Asaph, qui stabat a dextris ejus, Asaph filius Barachiæ, filii Samaa, ^1-chronicles-06-39-vulgate

40 filii Michaël, filii Basaiæ, filii Melchiæ, ^1-chronicles-06-40-vulgate

41 filii Athanai, filii Zara, filii Adaia, ^1-chronicles-06-41-vulgate

42 filii Ethan, filii Zamma, filii Semei, ^1-chronicles-06-42-vulgate

43 filii Jeth, filii Gersom, filii Levi. ^1-chronicles-06-43-vulgate

44 Filii autem Merari fratres eorum, ad sinistram, Ethan filius Cusi, filii Abdi, filii Maloch, ^1-chronicles-06-44-vulgate

45 filii Hasabiæ, filii Amasiæ, filii Helciæ, ^1-chronicles-06-45-vulgate

46 filii Amasai, filii Boni, filii Somer, ^1-chronicles-06-46-vulgate

47 filii Moholi, filii Musi, filii Merari, filii Levi. ^1-chronicles-06-47-vulgate

48 Fratres quoque eorum Levitæ, qui ordinati sunt in cunctum ministerium tabernaculi domus Domini. ^1-chronicles-06-48-vulgate

49 Aaron vero et filii ejus adolebant incensum super altare holocausti, et super altare thymiamatis, in omne opus Sancti sanctorum : et ut precarentur pro Israël juxta omnia quæ præceperat Moyses servus Dei. ^1-chronicles-06-49-vulgate

50 Hi sunt autem filii Aaron : Eleazar filius ejus, Phinees filius ejus, Abisue filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-50-vulgate

51 Bocci filius ejus, Ozi filius ejus, Zarahia filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-51-vulgate

52 Meraioth filius ejus, Amarias filius ejus, Achitob filius ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-52-vulgate

53 Sadoc filius ejus, Achimaas filius ejus. ^1-chronicles-06-53-vulgate

54 Et hæc habitacula eorum per vicos atque confinia, filiorum scilicet Aaron, juxta cognationes Caathitarum : ipsis enim sorte contigerant. ^1-chronicles-06-54-vulgate

55 Dederunt igitur eis Hebron in terra Juda, et suburbana ejus per circuitum : ^1-chronicles-06-55-vulgate

56 agros autem civitatis, et villas, Caleb filio Jephone. ^1-chronicles-06-56-vulgate

57 Porro filiis Aaron dederunt civitates ad confugiendum Hebron, et Lobna, et suburbana ejus, ^1-chronicles-06-57-vulgate

58 Jether quoque, et Esthemo cum suburbanis suis, sed et Helon, et Dabir cum suburbanis suis, ^1-chronicles-06-58-vulgate

59 Asan quoque, et Bethsemes, et suburbana earum. ^1-chronicles-06-59-vulgate

60 De tribu autem Benjamin, Gabee et suburbana ejus, et Almath cum suburbanis suis, Anathoth quoque cum suburbanis suis : omnes civitates, tredecim, per cognationes suas. ^1-chronicles-06-60-vulgate

61 Filiis autem Caath residuis de cognatione sua dederunt ex dimidia tribu Manasse in possessionem urbes decem. ^1-chronicles-06-61-vulgate

62 Porro filiis Gersom per cognationes suas de tribu Issachar, et de tribu Aser, et de tribu Nephthali, et de tribu Manasse in Basan, urbes tredecim. ^1-chronicles-06-62-vulgate

63 Filiis autem Merari per cognationes suas de tribu Ruben, et de tribu Gad, et de tribu Zabulon, dederunt sorte civitates duodecim. ^1-chronicles-06-63-vulgate

64 Dederunt quoque filii Israël Levitis civitates, et suburbana earum : ^1-chronicles-06-64-vulgate

65 dederuntque per sortem, ex tribu filiorum Juda, et ex tribu filiorum Simeon, et ex tribu filiorum Benjamin urbes has, quas vocaverunt nominibus suis, ^1-chronicles-06-65-vulgate

66 et his, qui erant de cognatione filiorum Caath, fueruntque civitates in terminis eorum de tribu Ephraim. ^1-chronicles-06-66-vulgate

67 Dederunt ergo eis urbes ad confugiendum, Sichem cum suburbanis suis in monte Ephraim, et Gazer cum suburbanis suis, ^1-chronicles-06-67-vulgate

68 Jecmaam quoque cum suburbanis suis, et Bethoron similiter, ^1-chronicles-06-68-vulgate

69 necnon et Helon cum suburbanis suis, et Gethremmon in eumdem modum. ^1-chronicles-06-69-vulgate

70 Porro ex dimidia tribu Manasse, Aner et suburbana ejus, Balaam et suburbana ejus : his videlicet, qui de cognatione filiorum Caath reliqui erant. ^1-chronicles-06-70-vulgate

71 Filiis autem Gersom de cognatione dimidiæ tribus Manasse, Gaulon in Basan, et suburbana ejus, et Astaroth cum suburbanis suis. ^1-chronicles-06-71-vulgate

72 De tribu Issachar, Cedes et suburbanis suis, et Dabereth cum suburbanis suis, ^1-chronicles-06-72-vulgate

73 Ramoth quoque et suburbana ejus, et Anem cum suburbanis suis. ^1-chronicles-06-73-vulgate

74 De tribu vero Aser : Masal cum suburbanis suis, et Abdon similiter, ^1-chronicles-06-74-vulgate

75 Hucac quoque et suburbana ejus, et Rohob cum suburbanis suis. ^1-chronicles-06-75-vulgate

76 Porro de tribu Nephthali, Cedes in Galilæa et suburbana ejus, Hamon cum suburbanis suis, et Cariathaim et suburbana ejus. ^1-chronicles-06-76-vulgate

77 Filiis autem Merari residuis : de tribu Zabulon, Remmono et suburbana ejus, et Thabor cum suburbanis suis : ^1-chronicles-06-77-vulgate

78 trans Jordanem quoque ex adverso Jericho contra orientem Jordanis, de tribu Ruben, Bosor in solitudine cum suburbanis suis, et Jassa cum suburbanis suis, ^1-chronicles-06-78-vulgate

79 Cademoth quoque et suburbana ejus, et Mephaat cum suburbanis suis. ^1-chronicles-06-79-vulgate

80 Necnon et de tribu Gad, Ramoth in Galaad et suburbana ejus, et Manaim cum suburbanis suis, ^1-chronicles-06-80-vulgate

81 sed et Hesebon cum suburbanis suis, et Jezer cum suburbanis suis. ^1-chronicles-06-81-vulgate
