# Canticle of Canticles 2

*Douay-Rheims:* [[song-of-songs-02-douay|Canticle of Canticles 2]]

1 Ego flos campi, et lilium convallium. ^song-of-songs-02-1-vulgate

2 Sponsus. Sicut lilium inter spinas, sic amica mea inter filias. ^song-of-songs-02-2-vulgate

3 Sponsa. Sicut malus inter ligna silvarum, sic dilectus meus inter filios. Sub umbra illius quem desideraveram sedi, et fructus ejus dulcis gutturi meo. ^song-of-songs-02-3-vulgate

4 Introduxit me in cellam vinariam ; ordinavit in me caritatem. ^song-of-songs-02-4-vulgate

5 Fulcite me floribus, stipate me malis, quia amore langueo. ^song-of-songs-02-5-vulgate

6 Læva ejus sub capite meo, et dextera illius amplexabitur me. ^song-of-songs-02-6-vulgate

7 Sponsus. Adjuro vos, filiæ Jerusalem, per capreas cervosque camporum, ne suscitetis, neque evigilare faciatis dilectam, quoadusque ipsa velit. ^song-of-songs-02-7-vulgate

8 Sponsa. Vox dilecti mei ; ecce iste venit, saliens in montibus, transiliens colles. ^song-of-songs-02-8-vulgate

9 Similis est dilectus meus capreæ, hinnuloque cervorum. En ipse stat post parietem nostrum, respiciens per fenestras, prospiciens per cancellos. ^song-of-songs-02-9-vulgate

10 En dilectus meus loquitur mihi. Sponsus. Surge, propera, amica mea, columba mea, formosa mea, et veni : ^song-of-songs-02-10-vulgate

11 jam enim hiems transiit ; imber abiit, et recessit. ^song-of-songs-02-11-vulgate

12 Flores apparuerunt in terra nostra ; tempus putationis advenit : vox turturis audita est in terra nostra ; ^song-of-songs-02-12-vulgate

13 ficus protulit grossos suos ; vineæ florentes dederunt odorem suum. Surge, amica mea, speciosa mea, et veni : ^song-of-songs-02-13-vulgate

14 columba mea, in foraminibus petræ, in caverna maceriæ, ostende mihi faciem tuam, sonet vox tua in auribus meis : vox enim tua dulcis, et facies tua decora. ^song-of-songs-02-14-vulgate

15 Sponsa. Capite nobis vulpes parvulas quæ demoliuntur vineas : nam vinea nostra floruit. ^song-of-songs-02-15-vulgate

16 Dilectus meus mihi, et ego illi, qui pascitur inter lilia, ^song-of-songs-02-16-vulgate

17 donec aspiret dies, et inclinentur umbræ. Revertere ; similis esto, dilecte mi, capreæ, hinnuloque cervorum super montes Bether. ^song-of-songs-02-17-vulgate
