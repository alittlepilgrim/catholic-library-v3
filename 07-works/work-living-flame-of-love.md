# The Living Flame of Love

Work card for *The Living Flame of Love* by St. John of the Cross. **Ratified exception (Peregrinita, 2026-07-21):** the settled rule is that single-edition works do not get a work card (see `book-family-cover-proposal`) — this work exists only in the ICS Collected Works, same as the Minor Works — but Peregrinita chose to give it a card anyway rather than route it through the Minor Works pattern of direct `about` edges to the cover note.
