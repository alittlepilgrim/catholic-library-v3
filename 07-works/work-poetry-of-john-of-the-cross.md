# The Poetry of St. John of the Cross

Work card — the family anchor for St. John of the Cross's **poetry as a body of work**: the part title, introduction, and shorter poems gathered in the ICS *Collected Works* poetry section (the Romances, the Glosses, *Christmas Refrain*, *The Sum of Perfection*, and the other stanzas), excepting the three poems that already anchor to their own prose-treatise work cards. **One card for the poetry; individual short poems do not get their own registry cards** — see Notes.

## Notes

- **Three poems anchor elsewhere and are excluded here, deliberately, to avoid a double join:** Poem 1 and Poem 15 ("The Spiritual Canticle") → [[work-spiritual-canticle]]; Poem 2 ("The Dark Night") → [[work-dark-night-of-the-soul]]; Poem 3 ("The Living Flame of Love") → [[work-living-flame-of-love]]. Their `about:` edges already point there and are not duplicated onto this card.
- Created per the recommendation in `agent-exceptions-queue` (2026-08-10, `jc-cw-3e-build-cowork`): *"one card for the poetry as a body of work... individual cards for each short poem are almost certainly over-registration and should not be created without Peregrinita asking for them."*
- Whether the Sayings of Light and Love, the Letters, and Special Counsels warrant work cards of their own is a separate, open proposal — see `agent-exceptions-queue` (2026-08-10) — and is **not** decided by this card's creation.
- The 1979 edition's poetry sits under `jc-cw-v1-*` and is untouched pending the link-migration pass (`jc-cw-3e-readiness-punch-list` §6).
