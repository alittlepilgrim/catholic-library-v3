# Proper of the Liturgy of the Hours OCD

Work card — the family anchor for the **Discalced Carmelite proper of the Divine Office** as a book. The 53 dated notes in `pursuits-texts/04-formation/propers/` are its parts: each one an individual celebration's Office of Readings, antiphons and collect, transcribed. **The book is the whole; the tables of contents are navigation, not the whole** — see Notes.

**The original is Latin.** The English texts the vault transcribes are the 1993 revision; where the two differ, the Latin governs. Related apparatus in [[pdf-assets-online]]: the format of the offices, and the table of liturgical days.

## Parts

All 53 dated notes in `04-formation/propers/` carry `part_of: "[[work-ocds-propers]]"`. Navigate them by date through [[ocds-propers]]. Precedence between the OCD calendar and the general Roman calendar is set out in [[loth-carmelite-calendar-precedence]].

## Notes

- **This card is the book; [[ocds-propers]] is its index.** It is the dated table of contents every proper's nav line points at (`type: moc`), and it shared the alias *OCDS Propers* with no book behind it, which is why `part_of` had nowhere correct to land until now. A second, duplicate in-folder list — an auto-generated Waypoint — was removed on 2026-09-08: without the Waypoint plugin it was one more thing to maintain by hand, and it held nothing `ocds-propers` did not already have.
- **Each proper is a Layer 4 prayer text; its celebration is a Layer 5 Feast card.** They are never the same note (Peregrinita, 2026-07-10). The proper links to its feast with `prayed_during` and to the person with `commemorates`; the feast card carries the observance itself.
- Where a proper has recorded audio, the episode reaches the person through the proper: `podcast → renders → proper → prayed_during → feast → commemorates → figure`.
