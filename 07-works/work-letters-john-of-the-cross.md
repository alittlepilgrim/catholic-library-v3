# The Letters of St. John of the Cross

Work card for the **Letters** of St. John of the Cross — 33 individually titled pieces to superiors, directors, and religious. **Ratified (Peregrinita, 2026-08-10):** deserves a card of its own — the Letters are cited individually in Carmelite scholarship, not only as a batch, and the shape matches the standing precedent for shorter-form works ([[work-spiritual-testimonies]], [[work-maxims]]).
