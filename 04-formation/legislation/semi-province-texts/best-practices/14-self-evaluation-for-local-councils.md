# Self-evaluation for Local Councils

Original article *Becoming an Effective Council* by Dorothy Mansen, OCDS

Revised April 2018

***This is offered by way of advice and is not considered binding as policy.***

**Questions individual council members should ask themselves periodically.**

Have I respected and appreciated other members of the council?

Am I doing my fair share of council and community work?

Am I prompt and conscientious?

Do I keep council business confidential?

(Am I committed to finding charitable and just solutions to matters before the council? )

Is the language I use supportive and encouraging?

Am I careful not to play favorites?

Do I become emotional or take disagreements personally?

(Do I shy away from saying "no" when discerning a candidate who is not a good fit for the community?

**Questions the council as a whole might consider when evaluating themselves as a team.**

(How are we fulfilling our primary responsibility, which is the formation and Christian ) and Carmelite maturing of the members of the community? (Constitutions

How are we developing leaders for the future?

Do we consistently keep in mind that the council as a whole is the superior of the community and not just the president or the director of formation? (Constitutions 46)

(Have we been able to practice detachment and charity when matters arise and there is ) a difference of opinion among ourselves?

Do we strive to create an atmosphere of cooperation?

Have we established any guidelines that we as a council should follow?

Do any need revising?

Do we regularly review the OCDS legislations and the Ratio?

How have we responded to guidance from the Province and the Order?

Do our members feel they can approach us with their ideas and concerns?

Where do we see our community in ten years?

What are our current and long-term goals?

What have we accomplished?
