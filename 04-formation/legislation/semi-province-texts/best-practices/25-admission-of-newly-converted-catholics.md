# Discernment - Admission of Newly Converted Catholics

To ensure that candidates are well-prepared for the demands of Carmelite life and to protect the integrity of the formation process, it is recommended that newly converted Catholics wait a year after their reception into the Church before making any formal visits to a community in the Secular Order of Discalced Carmelites (OCDS). This period allows them to become firmly rooted in the Catholic faith and sacramental practice before discerning the additional responsibilities of Carmelite life.

Local Councils, exercising prudent discernment, may consider a shorter waiting period, prior to Admission/Clothing, when there is clear evidence that the candidate has been faithfully living as a Catholic --- attending Mass, praying, and studying the faith. This balanced approach both safeguards the integrity of formation and respects the unique faith journey of each candidate, while promoting the spiritual health and stability of the community.
