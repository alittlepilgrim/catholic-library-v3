# Preface

The [[02-constitutions-wn|Constitutions of the Secular Order of Discalced Carmelites]] are the fundamental, general law for its members. Provincial Statutes are to provide a practical application of this general law as directed by the Constitutions [Preface, Art. 58].

The present statutes are the particular law for Carmelite Seculars of the Oklahoma Province. The term "Provincial Council" refers to the OCDS Provincial Council.
