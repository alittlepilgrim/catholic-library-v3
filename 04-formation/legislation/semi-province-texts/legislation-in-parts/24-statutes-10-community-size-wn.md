# Section X: Community Size

Following Holy Mother Teresa's wisdom, communities are to limit their size in order to maintain a strong sense of intimacy and Christ-centered fellowship within the community.

1)        When a community reaches 40 to 50 active professed members, the local Council shall consult with the Provincial Delegate about establishing a second community. [Sec. XII] ^ocds-s-X-1

2)         Formation of the second community shall follow the procedures outlined in the provincial policies. ^ocds-s-X-2
