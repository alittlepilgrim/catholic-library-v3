# Formation Guidelines for the Province of St. Therese

The OCDS Formation Guidelines for the Province of St. Therese (Oklahoma) are published on the OCDS Provincial website.

These guidelines have been approved by the General Definitory of the Order of Discalced Carmelites and are binding on all OCDS of the Province. [cf. Statutes 1.1]

Local Councils are expected to print these out for use in their Communities, Study Groups, and Groups in Development.

Local Councils are expected to use the Ratio Institutionis and the OCDS Constitutions, especially the Preface through Art. 36, in conjunction with the Formation Guidelines.
