# Section XVIII: Transfers Between Provinces

1)         Carmelite Seculars fall under the authority of the province in which they reside. When an OCDS member from another province moves into the Oklahoma Province, the same transfer procedures as in Sec. XVII shall apply. ^ocds-s-XVIII-1

2)          When a member of the Oklahoma Province moves into another province, that member shall contact the Provincial Office of the new province for information and procedures for transferring into that province. The transferring member can obtain contact information for the two other Provinces of the U.S. from the Central Office of the Oklahoma Province. ^ocds-s-XVIII-2
