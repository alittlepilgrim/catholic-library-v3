# Section VI: External Signs of Membership

1)        The large ceremonial brown scapular shall be given as the habit of the Secular Order at the time of admission to Formation I. ^ocds-s-VI-1

2)         A smaller brown scapular or scapular medal is worn for everyday use. ^ocds-s-VI-2

3)        In addition to regular OCDS meetings, the ceremonial scapular may be worn when participating as a group at gatherings and events approved by the local Council. The ceremonial scapular is not worn publicly by OCDS members at other times, nor in an individual capacity. ^ocds-s-VI-3
