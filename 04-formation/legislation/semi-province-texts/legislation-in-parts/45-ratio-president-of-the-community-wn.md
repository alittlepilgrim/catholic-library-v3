# The President of the Community

30\. First among brothers and sisters, the president, together with the Council, directs the community in a spirit of faith and is heard in the same spirit in an atmosphere of dialogue. In exercising the service of authority, the president must not fail to use it, but the role should be one of service rather than that of a control. Let the chief concern be to establish communion in a spirit of charity. ^ocds-ratio-30

31\. It is the task of the president to see that the Council team draws up a suitable program, guide its implementation. The president arranges that Council meet to review the program and consider changes. All of this should be done with prudent regard for the competence and independence of the Director and collaborators. ^ocds-ratio-31
