# Section II: Prayer Life

The members of our province shall strive to:

•      Practice mental prayer for at least one half hour each day in an atmosphere of interior silence and solitude

•      Pray Morning and Evening Prayer daily from the Liturgy of the Hours, and Night Prayer if possible.

•      Attend daily Mass, as able.
