# Council Member's Recusal from Certain Decisions

2022 – OCDS Oklahoma Semi-Province Provincial Policy

Members of the Local Council do not take part in discernments or other Council actions

pertaining to a relative. They are not to be present for any phase of the discernment or other

decision-making processes, including interviews, discussion, and voting. This is to avoid the

appearance of bias and to safeguard the harmony of the Council member’s family should the

person receive a negative vote.

---

> [[07-private-vow|← Previous: Considerations in Making a Private Vow]] | [[00-province-policies-toc|TOC]] | [[09-elements-of-meeting|Next: Required Elements of an OCDS Meeting →]]

Council members should give serious consideration to their objectivity when discerning the

vocation of someone who is not a relative but with whom they are particularly close (e.g., a

friend, protégé, mentor, or member of the same formation group). In these situations, recusal

from the discernment process is strongly advised.

This statement supersedes all previous statements on OCDS Discernments of a Council Member’s relative.

The online document is the only one to be considered official.

December 2022
