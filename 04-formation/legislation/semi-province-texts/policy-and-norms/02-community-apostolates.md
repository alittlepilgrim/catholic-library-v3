Community Apostolates

The primary apostolate of an OCDS Community is to share Carmelite Spirituality. In the most

ideal situation, the apostolate includes personal contact and interaction with the local church,

either on the parochial or diocesan level.

A community may also have a secondary apostolate of serving the poor or the needs of the

local Church.

All members are to contribute in some way, according to their capacity, to the ecclesial

apostolate of the Community.

Examples

• Members can give talks on Carmelite spirituality and Carmelite saints to local parishes or

community groups. Members who are not speaking may participate by publicizing the

event, writing, or editing the talk, attending the talk, providing hospitality at the talk,

praying for the success of the talk.

• The Community can organize special Masses to be said (perhaps by their Spiritual

Assistant) at times near Carmelite holy days. Members personally invite individual

members of the public to attend so that their intentions may be prayed for at the Mass.

As many Community members as are available should attend.

• The Community can invite members of various local parishes to attend the Community’s

annual retreat or day of recollection, as space is available.

• The Community can invite a Carmelite friar to present a talk or talks to the public on

Carmelite spirituality. Members may participate by publicizing the event, attending the

talk, providing hospitality at the talk, praying for the success of the talk, etc.
