# Policy on Married Couples Serving Concurrently on the Local Council

2023 – OCDS Oklahoma Semi-Province Provincial Policy

Policy on Married Couples Serving concurrently on the Local Council.

The concurrent participation of married couples on a local council is prohibited to safeguard the

---

viability of a varied and independent governance of the Community, as well as in respectful and

charitable consideration for the safekeeping of the couple’s primary vocation of marriage, as

preordained by God. As a further safeguard, it is also prohibited for anyone living in the same

household to serve concurrently on a local council.

This statement supersedes all previous statements on OCDS Married Couples and Community Elections.

The online document is the only one to be considered official.

March 2023
