# OCDS Formation Reading Path

**The Province formation manuals are the authority for reading order** (ratified
by Peregrinita, 2026-08-28). This note holds only the WORK-level order; the full
session-by-session curated list it comes from is [[themes-of-ocds-formation]]
— Peregrinita's own index of the whole program. The work-level `prerequisite_for`
edges on the work cards derive from the table below.

| Stage | Handbook | The work read |
|---|---|---|
| Aspirancy | [[themes-of-ocds-formation#Aspirancy]] | *Welcome to the Secular Order of Discalced Carmelites* (Deeney) — no work card; lives as `reference-work` notes |
| Formation I, Year A | f1a handbook | [[work-way-of-perfection]] |
| Formation I, Year B | f1b handbook | *The Carmelite Tradition* (history) · OCDS Constitutions · [[work-book-of-her-life]] |
| Formation II, Year A | f2a handbook | [[work-ascent-of-mount-carmel]] |
| Formation II, Year B | f2b handbook | [[work-interior-castle]] |
| Formation II, Year C | f2c handbook | [[work-story-of-a-soul]] |
| Ongoing Formation, Vol I | fo1 handbook | Elizabeth of the Trinity (Complete Works, Vol. 1) · *The Carmelite Tradition* · Ascent revisited |
| Ongoing Formation, Vol II | fo2 handbook | [[work-dark-night-of-the-soul]] |

Verified against the handbooks' own Required Reading lines, 2026-08-28
(f1b names the Carmelite Tradition pages, the Constitutions articles, and
the Book of Her Life; f2a's sessions walk the Ascent; fo2's sessions walk
the Dark Night book by book).

**What this settles.** "What should someone read after *Interior Castle*?"
— *Story of a Soul*, per Formation II's own order; the *Dark Night* follows
in ongoing formation. The doctrinal `corresponds_to` bridge between Teresa's
dwelling places and John's Night stays unwritten (the 2026-08-11 Group C
parking stands); the reading path runs on the program, not on that bridge.

**Boundary kept.** Session-to-session `prerequisite_for` edges inside a
handbook remain forbidden (Group D of the 2026-08-11 ratification —
"programme sequence masquerading as dependence"). This note and the
work-level edges are the machine-readable home Group D pointed to.
