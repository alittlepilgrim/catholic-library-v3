# Carmelites - A Two-Volume Systematic Study (Curated List)

This note is a first-pass editorial arrangement of the nine OCDS formation handbooks as though they formed a two-volume work. Volume I gathers Carmelite order, community, legislation, formation structure, and history. Volume II gathers Carmelite spirituality, prayer, purification, and the stages of spiritual life. The session files mostly remain in their native sequence, while the supporting materials are regrouped into appendix sections that fit each volume.

## How to Use This

Read **[[#Volume I. Carmelite Order|Volume I]]** if you want the shape of the Order: vocation, identity, formation, legislation, community, governance, and history.

Read **[[#Volume II. Carmelite Spirituality|Volume II]]** if you want the inner doctrine of Carmel: prayer, purification, the stages of spiritual life, and the major Carmelite witnesses.

Use the appendix sections in two ways: as companion reading for the main flow, or as a subject library for talks, study, discussion, retreat preparation, and personal reading.
