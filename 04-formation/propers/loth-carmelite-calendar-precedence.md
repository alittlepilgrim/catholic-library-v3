# Liturgy of the Hours: Carmelite Calendar Precedence

The Discalced Carmelite (OCD) Proper Calendar alters the rank of specific days within the Universal Church calendar. When a Carmelite observance conflicts with a universal observance, the Carmelite rank takes absolute precedence.

## Hierarchy of Feasts and Daily Setup

The rank of the day determines which texts replace the standard 4-Week Psalter.

| Rank  | Celebration             | Setup Rule                                                                                                                                                                   |
| :---- | :---------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | **Solemnity**           | **Total Replacement.** Starts with Evening Prayer I the night before. Use the Proper or Common for all Psalms, antiphons, readings, and prayers. Ignore the weekday psalter. |
| **2** | **Feast**               | **Total Replacement.** Use the Proper or Common for everything. There is no Evening Prayer I unless the Feast is a Feast of the Lord falling on a Sunday.                    |
| **3** | **Obligatory Memorial** | **Merge.** Combine the Saint's specific texts with the standard Weekday texts.                                                                                               |
| **4** | **Optional Memorial**   | **Choice.** Celebrate the standard Weekday or the Memorial. If choosing the Memorial, follow the Merge rules below.                                                          |
| **5** | **Weekday (Feria)**     | **Standard.** Use the current day in the 4-Week Psalter for everything.                                                                                                      |

## The Merge Rules for Memorials

When observing an Obligatory or Optional Memorial, you must weave the Proper of the Saint into the standard Weekday.

* **Psalms (Weekday):** Use the Psalms and their attached antiphons from the current weekday in the 4-Week Psalter. Never use Psalms from the Commons for a Memorial.
* **Concluding Prayer (Saint):** Use the specific prayer from the Proper of the Saint to close the hour.
* **Gospel Antiphons (Saint):** Check the Proper of the Saint for the Canticle of Zechariah (Morning) and Canticle of Mary (Evening) antiphons. If provided, use them. If not, use the weekday antiphon.
* **Middle Elements (Optional):** You may use the hymn, reading, and intercessions from the Common, but it is highly traditional to use the weekday texts for these elements.

## When to Use Festive Psalms

You only leave the 4-Week Psalter for Psalms when the day is elevated to a Feast or a Solemnity.

* **Feasts and Solemnities:** Use the thematic Psalms found in the Commons.
* **Sunday Week 1 Psalms:** Use these as the default festive Psalms for Morning Prayer and Evening Prayer on Feasts and Solemnities if the Proper does not specify otherwise.
