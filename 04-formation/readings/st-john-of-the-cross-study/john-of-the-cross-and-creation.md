# St. John of the Cross and Creation

See also: [[stc-0|Introductory Summary]]

For 2026, the texts chosen are as follows:

1. [[stc-1|Creation as ‘a palace’ (Romances 3; 4:1-38)]]
2. [[stc-2|Creation as 'an icon' (Spiritual Canticle B 5:1-4)]]
3. [[stc-3|Creation as a ‘messenger’ (Spiritual Canticle B 4:1-7)]]
4. [[stc-4|Creation as a quasi-sacrament (Spiritual Canticle B 14-15:1-5, 9, 28-29)]]
5. [[stc-5|The 'awakening' of creation (Living Flame B 4:1-5, 14-15)]]
6. [[stc-6|Creation drawn from nothingness (1 Ascent of Mount Carmel 4, 1-4)]]
7. [[stc-7|Creation as a gift rather than possession (3 Ascent of Mount Carmel 20, 1-4)]]
8. [[stc-8|Creation as the place of our liberation (Prayer of a Soul Enkindled with Love)]]
