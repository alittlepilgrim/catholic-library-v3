# Abbreviations

The following abbreviations will be used in references to Teresa's works: ^tj-way-ics-p4

IC = [[tj-interior-castle-ccel|The Interior Castle]]

F = The Book of Her Foundations

L = [[tj-life-ccel|The Book of Her Life]]

M = Meditations on the Song of Songs

S = [[tj-cw-v1-sol|Soliloquies]]

ST = [[tj-cw-v1-st|Spiritual Testimonies]]

W = The Way of Perfection

Principal divisions, such as chapters and numbers, are separated by a period; references to several parts within the same major division (e.g., several numbers within the same chapter) are separated by a comma. An initial Roman numeral is used to indicate the “dwelling places” in references to [[tj-interior-castle-ccel|The Interior Castle]]. Thus “IC VI.7.8, 9” refers to [[tj-interior-castle-ccel|The Interior Castle]], sixth “dwelling places,” seventh chapter, numbers 8 and 9, while “W 1.2” refers to the second number in the first chapter of the Way of Perfection.

Unless otherwise noted, all quotations from St. Teresa of Avila are taken from The Collected Works of St. Teresa of Avila, trans. Kieran Kavanaugh, O.C.D., and Otilio Rodriguez, O.C.D., 3 vols. (Washington, D.C.: ICS Publications, 1976–1985).
