## About This Page

These are the book's own indexes, copied out word for word. Nothing here was written for the vault: the wording, the order and the spelling are the edition's, and where the edition is odd the oddness is kept.

The long alphabetical Index is the analytical index the 1916 Thomas Baker printing carries at the back, running from Abecedario to Zeal. The five shorter indexes after it belong to the Christian Classics Ethereal Library edition of the same book.

The letters A to Z are dividers added so a reader can jump. The entries under them are untouched.

Every chapter reference is a link to that chapter's page. A reference to one of the Relations stays as plain text, because the Relations are not in the vault yet; so do the references to the Preface, the Annals and St. Teresa's Arguments of the Chapters. "ib." is left as the book prints it — working out what it stands for would be guessing, not copying. A "See" pointing at another entry on this page is left as plain text too.
