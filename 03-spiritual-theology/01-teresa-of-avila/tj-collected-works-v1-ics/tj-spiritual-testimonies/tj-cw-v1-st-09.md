# Testimony 9

**(Place unknown; probably 1570)**

**Public raptures**

Once while wondering why I almost never had raptures in public any more, I heard: “It’s not necessary now; you have enough approval for what I intend;[^1] let us look at the weakness of the suspicious ones.”

---

[^1]: See *Life* 20.5 and note 5 in the same chapter.
