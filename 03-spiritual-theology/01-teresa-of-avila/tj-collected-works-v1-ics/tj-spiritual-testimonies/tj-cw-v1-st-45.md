# Testimony 45

**(Seville, late 1575)**

**The reform of the monastery of Paterna**

I was once thinking about whether they were going to send me to reform a certain monastery, and this troubled me. I heard: “What do you fear? What can you lose but the lives you have so often offered me? I shall help you.” It happened on a certain occasion in such a way that my soul was much satisfied.[^1]

---

[^1]: She is speaking of a monastery of Calced Carmelite nuns in Paterna and its reform by some nuns from her Carmel in Seville.
