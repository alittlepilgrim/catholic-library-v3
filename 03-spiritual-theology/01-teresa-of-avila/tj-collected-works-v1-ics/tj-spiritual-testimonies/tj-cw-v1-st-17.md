# Testimony 17

**(Ávila, St. Joseph, July 22, 1571)**

**Desires for death**

The desires and impulses for death, which were so strong, have left me, especially since the feast day of St. Mary Magdalene; for I resolved to live very willingly in order to render much service to God. There is the exception sometimes when no matter how much I try to reject the desire to see Him, I cannot.
