# Testimony 40

**(Probably Seville, second half of 1575)**

**God’s omnipresence**

One time I understood how the Lord was present in all things, and how in the soul, and I thought of the example of a sponge which absorbs water.[^1]

[^1]: See *Spiritual Testimonies* 14.
