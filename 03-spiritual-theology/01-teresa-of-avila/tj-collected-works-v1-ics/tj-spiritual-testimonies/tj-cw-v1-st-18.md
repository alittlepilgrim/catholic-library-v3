# Testimony 18

**(Ávila, St. Joseph, 1571)**

**Prophecy about St. Joseph’s at Ávila**

Once I heard: “The time will come when many miracles will be worked in this church; they will call it the holy church.” It was in St. Joseph’s at Ávila in 1571.
