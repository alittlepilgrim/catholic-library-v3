# Testimony 56

**(Seville, 1575 or 1576)**

**Infused understanding of a Magnificat verse**

While in prayer one day, I felt my soul to be so deep in God that it didn’t seem there was a world; but while immersed in Him, understanding of that Magnificat verse, *et exultavit spiritus*,[^1] was given to me in such a way I cannot forget it.

---

[^1]: [[luke-01#^luke-01-47|Lk 1:47]]. See *Spiritual Testimonies* 39.
