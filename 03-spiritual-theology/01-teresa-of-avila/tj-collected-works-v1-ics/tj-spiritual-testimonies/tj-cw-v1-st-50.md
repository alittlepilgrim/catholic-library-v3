# Testimony 50

**(Seville, probably 1575)**

**Father Gratian’s health**

Having been so distressed over our father’s health that I couldn’t be at peace, and begging the Lord very emphatically one day after Communion that since He had given our father to me He not allow me to be without him, the Lord told me: “Don’t be afraid.”
