# Testimony 57

**(Seville, spring 1576)**

**Revelation about the survival of her Carmel**

I was once thinking about their desire to suppress this monastery of discalced nuns, and of whether it was their intention little by little to put an end to them all. I heard: “They are attempting this, but will not succeed—on the contrary.”
