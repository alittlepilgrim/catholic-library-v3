# Testimony 7

**(Place unknown; perhaps 1570)**

**A message to be given**

While thinking about what I should say regarding a message the Lord had given me, I didn’t understand anything, even though I prayed to Him. And I thought that the message must have come from the devil. He told me that this was not so, that He would advise me when the time came.[^1]

[^1]: See *Life* 34.10.
