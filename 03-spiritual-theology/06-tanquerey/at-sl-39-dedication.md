# Dedication

The author humbly dedicates this book to the Word Incarnate and to His Blessed Mother, Seat of Wisdom, happy indeed to contribute in some way to the glory of the Most Holy and Adorable Trinity.

Ut in omnibus honorificetur Deus per Jesum Christum.

— AD. TANQUEREY

Issy, France

Feast of the Annunciation, 1923
