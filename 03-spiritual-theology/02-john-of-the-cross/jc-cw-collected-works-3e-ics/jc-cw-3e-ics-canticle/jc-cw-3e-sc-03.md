# Theme

1\. These stanzas begin with a person's initial steps in the service of God and continue until the ultimate state of perfection is reached, which is spiritual marriage. They refer, consequently, to the three states or ways of spiritual exercise (purgative, illuminative, and unitive) through which a person passes in advancing to this state, and they describe some of the characteristics and effects of these ways. ^jc-cw-3e-sc-03-1

2\. The initial stanzas treat of the state of beginners, that of the purgative way. ^jc-cw-3e-sc-03-2

The subsequent ones deal with the state of proficients, in which the spiritual betrothal is effected, that is, the illuminative way.

The stanzas following these refer to the unitive way, that of the perfect, where spiritual marriage takes place. This unitive way of the perfect follows the illuminative way of the proficients.

The final stanzas speak of the beatific state, that sole aspiration of a person who has reached perfection.
