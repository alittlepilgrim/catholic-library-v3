# Letter 30

[To Madre Ana de San Alberto, discalced Carmelite in Caravaca

La Peñuela, August-September 1591]

... You already know, daughter, the trials they are now suffering. God permits it to try his elect. In silence and in hope shall our strength be ([[isaiah-30#^isaiah-30-15|Is. 30:15]]). May God keep you and make you holy. Commend me to God.
