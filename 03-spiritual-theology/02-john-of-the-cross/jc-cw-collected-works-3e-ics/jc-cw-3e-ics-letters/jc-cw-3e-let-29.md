# Letter 29

[To a woman under his direction[^57]

La Peñuela, August 22, 1591]

May God grant us the right intention in all these things and that we might not consent knowingly to sin; for in this way, even though the battery of temptation be great and of many kinds, one will walk safely and everything will be converted into a crown.

Give my greetings to your sister; and to Isabel de Soria my best regards in the Lord, and tell her that I marveled that she is not in Jaén, since there is a monastery there.

The Lord be in your soul, my daughter in Christ.

From La Peñuela, August 22, 1591

Fray John of the Cross

---

[^57]: This was probably one of the Soto sisters, Ana or María, who were friends of Isabel de Soria (mentioned later in the letter) and belonged to a group of persons directed by John in Baeza.
