# Sketch of Mount Carmel by St. John of the Cross

![[jc-cw-3e-sketch.jpg]]

*Saint John of the Cross's own drawing of the Mount, in his hand, in Spanish —

`Monte Carmelo`. Extracted from the source PDF at 1500 × 2028 px; the page

carries no text layer, so the drawing is reproduced as the image it is. The

inscriptions correspond to the maxims that appear as running text in

[[jc-cw-3e-asc-b1c13]]. The facing page translates the terms:
