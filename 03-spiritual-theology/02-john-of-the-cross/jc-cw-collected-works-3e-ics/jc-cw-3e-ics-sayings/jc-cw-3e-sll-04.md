# Sayings 159-175

159\. The further you withdraw from earthly things the closer you approach heavenly things and the more you find in God. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-159

160\. Whoever knows how to die in all will have life in all. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-160

161\. Abandon evil, do good, and seek peace ([[psalm-34#^psalm-34-14|Ps. 34:14]]). ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-161

162\. Anyone who complains or grumbles is not perfect, nor even a good Christian. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-162

163\. The humble are those who hide in their own nothingness and know how to abandon themselves to God. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-163

164\. The meek are those who know how to suffer their neighbor and themselves. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-164

165\. If you desire to be perfect, sell your will, give it to the poor in spirit, come to Christ in meekness and humility, and follow him to Calvary and the sepulcher. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-165

166\. Those who trust in themselves are worse than the devil. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-166

167\. Those who do not love their neighbor abhor God. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-167

168\. Anyone who does things lukewarmly is close to falling. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-168

169\. Whoever flees prayer flees all that is good. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-169

170\. Conquering the tongue is better than fasting on bread and water. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-170

171\. Suffering for God is better than working miracles. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-171

172\. Oh, what blessings we will enjoy in the vision of the Most Blessed Trinity! ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-172

173\. Do not be suspicious of your brother, for you will lose purity of heart. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-173

174\. As for trials, the more the better. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-174

175\. What does anyone know who doesn't know how to suffer for Christ? ^jc-cw-3e-sll-04-175
