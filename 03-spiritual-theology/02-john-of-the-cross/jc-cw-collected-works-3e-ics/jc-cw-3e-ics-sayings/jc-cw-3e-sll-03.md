# Sayings 123-158

123\. If you wish to glory in yourself, but do not wish to appear ignorant and foolish, discard the things that are not yours and you will have glory in what remains. But certainly if you discard all that is not yours, nothing will be left, since you must not glory in anything if you do not want to fall into vanity. But let us descend now especially to those graces, the gifts that make people pleasing in God's sight. It is certain that you must not glory in these gifts, for you do not even know if you possess them. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-123

124\. Oh, how sweet your presence will be to me, you who are the supreme good! I must draw near you in silence and uncover your feet that you may be pleased to unite me to you in marriage ([[ruth-03#^ruth-03-7|Ru. 3:7]]), and I will not rest until I rejoice in your arms. Now I ask you, Lord, not to abandon me at any time in my recollection, for I am a squanderer of my soul. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-124

125\. Detached from exterior things, dispossessed of interior things, disappropriated of the things of God - neither will prosperity detain you nor adversity hinder you. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-125

126\. The devil fears a soul united to God as he does God himself.[^7] ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-126

127\. The purest suffering produces the purest understanding.[^8] ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-127

128\. The soul that desires God to surrender himself to it entirely must surrender itself entirely to him without keeping anything for itself. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-128

129\. The soul that has reached the union of love does not even experience the first motions of sin. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-129

130\. Old friends of God scarcely ever fail him, for they stand above all that can make them fail.[^9] ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-130

131\. My Beloved, all that is rugged and toilsome I desire for myself, and all that is sweet and delightful I desire for you.[^10] ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-131

132\. What we need most in order to make progress is to be silent before this great God with our appetite and with our tongue, for the language he best hears is silent love. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-132

133\. The submission of a servant is necessary in seeking God. In outward things light helps to prevent one from falling; but in the things of God just the opposite is true: It is better for the soul not to see if it is to be more secure. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-133

134\. More is gained in one hour from God's good things than in a whole lifetime from your own. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-134

135\. Love to be unknown both by yourself and by others. Never look at the good or evil of others. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-135

136\. Walk in solitude with God; act according to the just measure; hide the blessings of God. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-136

137\. To lose always and let everyone else win is a trait of valiant souls, generous spirits, and unselfish hearts; it is their manner to give rather than receive even to the extent of giving themselves. They consider it a heavy burden to possess themselves, and it pleases them more to be possessed by others and withdrawn from themselves, since we belong more to that infinite Good than we do to ourselves. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-137

138\. It is seriously wrong to have more regard for God's blessings than for God himself: prayer and detachment. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-138

139\. Look at that infinite knowledge and that hidden secret. What peace, what love, what silence is in that divine bosom! How lofty the science God teaches there, which is what we call the anagogical acts that so enkindle the heart. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-139

140\. The secret of one's conscience is considerably harmed and damaged as often as its fruits are manifested to others, for then one receives as reward the fruit of fleeting fame. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-140

141\. Speak little and do not meddle in matters about which you are not asked. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-141

142\. Strive always to keep God present and to preserve within yourself the purity he teaches you. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-142

143\. Do not excuse yourself or refuse to be corrected by all; listen to every reproof with a serene countenance; think that God utters it. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-143

144\. Live as though only God and yourself were in this world, so that your heart may not be detained by anything human. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-144

145\. Consider it the mercy of God that someone occasionally speaks a good word to you, for you deserve none. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-145

146\. Never allow yourself to pour out your heart, even though it be but for the space of a Creed. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-146

147\. Never listen to talk about the weaknesses of others, and if someone complains of another, you can tell her humbly to say nothing of it to you ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-147

148\. Do not complain about anyone, or ask for anything; and if it is necessary for you to ask, let it be with few words. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-148

149\. Do not refuse work even though it seems that you cannot do it. Let all find compassion in you. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-149

150\. Do not contradict; by no means speak words that are not pure. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-150

151\. Let your speech be such that no one may be offended, and let it concern things that would not cause you regret were all to know of them. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-151

152\. Do not refuse anything you possess, even though you may need it. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-152

153\. Be silent concerning what God may have given you and recall that saying of the bride: *My secret for myself* ([[isaiah-24#^isaiah-24-16|Is. 24:16]]). ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-153

154\. Strive to preserve your heart in peace; let no event of this world disturb it; reflect that all must come to an end. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-154

155\. Take neither great nor little notice of who is with you or against you, and try always to please God. Ask him that his will be done in you. Love him intensely, as he deserves to be loved. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-155

156\. Twelve stars for reaching the highest perfection: love of God, love of neighbor, obedience, chastity, poverty, attendance at choir, penance, humility, mortification, prayer, silence, peace. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-156

157\. Never take others for your example in the tasks you have to perform, however holy they may be, for the devil will set their imperfections before you. But imitate Christ, who is supremely perfect and supremely holy, and you will never err. ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-157

158\. Seek in reading and you will find in meditation; knock in prayer and it will be opened to you in contemplation. [^11] ^jc-cw-3e-sll-03-158
