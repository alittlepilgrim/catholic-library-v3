## About This Page

These are the book's own indexes, copied out word for word from the Christian Classics Ethereal Library edition. Nothing here was written for the vault. This edition prints no subject index, so there is none here: an index is copied from the book, never made up.

Every reference to a chapter is a link to that chapter. The only plain-text references left are to the edition's own front matter — the cover, the frontispiece, the title page, the dedication and the table of contents — which this vault does not hold as separate pages.

When this page was first built on 2026-08-28 it said the vault was missing Book II chapter I and Book III chapter IX, and held Book III chapter V twice. That was wrong, and the eight references left plain because of it are now linked. The vault holds all 188 chapters. Two notes were simply wearing the wrong title: `book-ii-a-fs-tlg` is Book II chapter I, and `book-iii-e-fs-tlg` is Book III chapter IX. Checked against the source edition and corrected the same day.
