# Editorial Notes

_This electronic edition of 'The Twelve Degrees of Humility and Pride' has been prepared_ _from the 1929 translation by Barton R.V. Mills, M.A. This translation, all apparently_ _available for use in the public domain, is by a translator not of the Catholic faith, and he_ _is the author of the footnotes, except in several cases where they have been modified or_ _excised. His other commentary, introductory notes, etc. have been removed from this_ _electronic edition._
