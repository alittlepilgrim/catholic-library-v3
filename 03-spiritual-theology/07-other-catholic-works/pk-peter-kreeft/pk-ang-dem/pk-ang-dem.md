*This book is dedicated to*

*Francis/ces and Frodo*

*my wife's and my guardian angels*

*who have been the real editors and ghost writers of this book*

*through their gentle inspirations*

*and their muted laughter*

*and to*

*Tranquillo (Tony) Massi*

*my father-in-law*

*who sat at the edge of his children's beds*

*lulling them to sleep*

*with stories of St. Michael the Archangel betting with Lucifer*

*and who is now most likely losing*

*a game of poker (as usual) to an angel*
