# 22 A Little Comic Relief

During the troubles in Northern Ireland, a man was out late walking in the dark. Suddenly there was an arm around his neck and a knife to his throat. "Tell me true if ye want to live: are ye a Protestant or are ye a Catholic?"

The man thought quickly. He had a 50 percent chance to live. That seemed too little. So he replied, "I'm an atheist."

The knife did not move. "Are ye a Protestant atheist or a Catholic atheist?"

There are still places in the world where the difference between a Protestant and a Catholic is more severe than the difference between a Christian and an atheist.
