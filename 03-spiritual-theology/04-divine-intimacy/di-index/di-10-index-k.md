# K - Divine Intimacy Analytical Index

## Knowledge (Gift of)

- Makes one understand the nothingness of creatures : [[309-the-gift-of-knowledge#Meditation 1|309,1]], [[309-the-gift-of-knowledge#Meditation 2|309,2]];
- the soul who is enlightened feels the need of withdrawing from creatures : [[309-the-gift-of-knowledge#Meditation 2|309,2]];
- under the influence of — creatures become a ladder to go to God : ibid.
- infuses contrition for sin : [[310-blessed-are-they-that-mourn#Meditation 1|310,1]];
- perfects the virtue of hope: [[310-blessed-are-they-that-mourn#Meditation 2|310,2]];
- to the gift of — corresponds the beatitude of mourning : [[310-blessed-are-they-that-mourn#Meditation 1|310,1]];
- makes the soul understand the joy of sorrow embraced for love of God : [[310-blessed-are-they-that-mourn#Meditation 2|310,2]].
