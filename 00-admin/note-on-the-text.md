# Note on the Text

Every text in this library was transcribed from a stated edition. None of it is retyped from memory, paraphrased, or drawn from an unnamed source. What edition a page came from is recorded at the foot of that page, and the full list is in the [[bibliography|Bibliography]].

## What was corrected, and what was not

**A defect in the printed source is reproduced, not repaired.** Where an edition prints a wrong scripture reference, an endnote with no marker, or a heading that disagrees with its own contents page, the page here prints what the book prints, and the difference is recorded. The alternative — quietly improving the text — would make this library a source you could not check against the book on your shelf.

**A defect introduced in transcription is repaired**, and the repair is recorded too, so that anyone who read the earlier version knows.

Those records — the errata — are kept by the library's editor and are not part of this copy. The pages here are the texts as the editions print them.

## Where a source could not be followed

Some editions simply do not contain what you might expect — no analytical index, no page numbers in a reflowable text, an illustration with no text layer. Where the absence might be read as an omission here, a note says so and points to whatever serves instead.

## Translations and editions

Where more than one translation exists, this library names the one it follows rather than pretending there is only one. Scripture is one canonical study translation per language, recorded on each page. Works that exist in several editions have a **work card** in `07-works` that holds them together, so you can see at a glance which editions are here and move between them.

## What this library is not

It is not a critical edition. It collates nothing, and it establishes no text. It is a faithful transcription of stated editions, with its departures from them written down.
