# Our Lady of Guadalupe

Apparitions of the Blessed Virgin Mary to St. Juan Diego Cuauhtlatoatzin at Tepeyac hill, near modern Mexico City, in December 1531. Mary appeared as a mestiza, speaking Nahuatl, and left her image miraculously imprinted on Juan Diego's tilma (cloak), still venerated at the Basilica of Our Lady of Guadalupe. Credited with the rapid evangelization of Mexico and named Patroness of the Americas by Pope Pius XII. Feast: December 12.
