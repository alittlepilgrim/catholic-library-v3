# Our Lady of Fatima

Series of six apparitions of the Blessed Virgin Mary to three shepherd children — Lucia dos Santos and her cousins Francisco and Jacinta Marto — near Fatima, Portugal, from May to October 1917. Mary's message called for prayer (especially the Rosary), penance, and consecration to her Immaculate Heart, and included the three "Fatima secrets." The apparitions culminated in the public Miracle of the Sun on October 13, 1917. Feast: May 13.
