## Commemorates

**Carmelite Saints** — the saints and blesseds of the Carmelite Order, taken together. This card holds them; there is no separate card.

The canonized and beatified members of the Carmelite family — Ancient Observance and Discalced — considered together as a collective. This day is distinct from the individual feast days of each Carmelite saint, which are carded separately in `pursuits-texts/06-figures/`.
