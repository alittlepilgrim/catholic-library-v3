# Divine Mercy

Devotion centered on the mercy of God as revealed in the private revelations of St. Faustina Kowalska (canonized 2000). Includes the Image of Divine Mercy ("Jesus, I trust in You"), the Chaplet of Divine Mercy, the Hour of Great Mercy (3:00 PM), and Divine Mercy Sunday, established by Pope St. John Paul II as the Second Sunday of Easter in the universal calendar (2000).
