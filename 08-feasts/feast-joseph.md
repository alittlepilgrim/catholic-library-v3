# Saint Joseph, Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary

**One feast, not two.** The OCD proper's own heading for this date reads "SAINT JOSEPH, Spouse of the Blessed Virgin Mary, Protector of our Order — Solemnity," and its introduction states plainly: "The feast of Saint Joseph, celebrated by the entire Church, has special significance for Carmelites, and, in particular, for the Order of Discalced Carmelites. Our Holy Mother Saint Teresa found in Saint Joseph an unparalleled protector, intercessor and friend." This is a single `scope: universal` entity carrying Carmelite significance — not a separate charism-scope card. (An earlier pass in this session split it into two cards in error; both have been merged here and left as redirect stubs per merge-never-delete — see `feast-joseph-spouse-of-the-blessed-virgin-mary` and `feast-joseph-patron-of-our-order`.)
