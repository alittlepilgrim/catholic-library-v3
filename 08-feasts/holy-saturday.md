# Holy Saturday

The day between Good Friday and Easter Sunday, on which Christ's body lay in the tomb and, per the Apostles' Creed, He "descended into hell" (the realm of the dead) to free the righteous who awaited Him. The Church keeps this day in silence and watching, without the Eucharistic sacrifice, until the Easter Vigil begins after nightfall — a distinct liturgical moment from the Vigil itself, which already belongs to Easter.
