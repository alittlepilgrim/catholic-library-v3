# Our Lady of Loreto

Devotion centered on the Holy House of Loreto, Italy — venerated by tradition as the very house of the Holy Family at Nazareth, said to have been miraculously transported (by angels, in popular piety; historically, likely by Crusader-era ship transport) to its current site near Ancona in the 13th century. Pope Francis added the memorial to the universal calendar in 2019. Our Lady of Loreto is patroness of aviators. Feast: December 10.
