# The Holy Innocents

The male children of Bethlehem two years old and under, killed by order of Herod the Great in his attempt to destroy the infant Jesus ([[matthew-02#^matthew-02-16|Mt 2:16-18]]). Venerated from the earliest centuries of the Church as the first martyrs for Christ, though they did not choose martyrdom by will. Feast: December 28.
