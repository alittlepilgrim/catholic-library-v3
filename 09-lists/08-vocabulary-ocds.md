## A

**[[Abandonment]]** — a free surrender of one's will to God (WP 32:4), [[f1a-appendix-a]] · Thérèse felt attracted to the path of love and abandonment, [[theresian-anniversaries-2023-study-guide-06]] · abandonment here is delicious; its force and authenticity come from being combined with passive abandonment, [[theresian-anniversaries-2025-text-03]] · her great freedom and total abandonment, in confidence and love, culminating in "My God… I love you!," [[theresian-anniversaries-2025-text-08]] · we put ourselves in God's hands, who knows better than us what we need (Zelie Martin's letters), [[07-12-sts-louis-martin-and-marie-azelia-zelie-guerin]] ^ocds-ai-abandonment

**Absences** — *see* Attendance at community meetings ^ocds-ai-absences

**[[abstinence]]** — abstain from meat, except as a remedy for sickness or feebleness, [[05-02-rule-st-albert#^ocds-a-17|A §17]] ^ocds-ai-abstinence

**Accompaniment** — to accompany those in formation, to help them put into practice what they learn, [[ocds-fh-06]] ^ocds-ai-accompaniment

**[[adoration|Adoration]]** — to contemplate with deep faith our Beloved in the Sacrament; may adoration and love accompany and beautify everything (Maria Candida's own writings), [[06-14-blessed-maria-barba]] ^ocds-ai-adoration

**age requirements** — must be at least eighteen years of age, [[05-04-statutes#Section Xi: Age Requirements|S §XI]] ^ocds-ai-age-requirements

**[[albert-of-jerusalem-saint|Albert of Jerusalem, Saint]]** — formed the hermit brothers of Mount Carmel into a collegium and wrote a Rule for them, [[09-17-st-albert-of-jerusalem]] ^ocds-ai-albert-of-jerusalem-saint

**[[albert-of-trapani-saint|Albert of Trapani, Saint]]** — renowned as a fervent preacher of the Gospel; died with a reputation for purity and prayer, [[08-07-st-albert-of-trapani]] ^ocds-ai-albert-of-trapani-saint

**allegiance to Jesus Christ** — a life of allegiance to Christ, unswerving in the service of his Master, [[05-02-rule-st-albert#^ocds-a-2|A §2]] ^ocds-ai-allegiance-to-jesus-christ

**[[alphonsus-mary-mazurek-blessed|Alphonsus Mary Mazurek, Blessed, and Companions]]** — Polish priest shot by occupying troops in 1944, beatified with other Polish WWII martyrs, [[06-12-blessed-alphonsus-mary-of-the-holy-spirit-mazurek-and-companions]] ^ocds-ai-alphonsus-mary-mazurek-blessed

**[[andrew-corsini-saint|Andrew Corsini, Saint]]** — gave the Church a wonderful example of love, apostolic zeal, prudence, and love of the poor, [[01-09-st-andrew-corsini]] ^ocds-ai-andrew-corsini-saint

**[[angelus-maria-prat-hostench|Angelus Maria Prat Hostench, Blessed, and Companions]]** — martyrs of the Spanish Civil War, beatified by Benedict XVI in 2007, [[05-04-blesseds-angelus-maria]] ^ocds-ai-angelus-maria-prat-hostench

**[[anne-of-jesus-blessed|Anne of Jesus, Blessed]]** — received into Carmel by Teresa herself; John of the Cross dedicated his Spiritual Canticle commentary to her, [[11-25-blessed-anne-of-jesus]] ^ocds-ai-anne-of-jesus-blessed

**[[anne-of-st-bartholemew-blessed|Anne of St. Bartholomew, Blessed]]** — professed in Teresa's own hands; great zeal for the salvation of souls in France and Belgium, [[06-07-blessed-anne-of-st-bartholomew]] ^ocds-ai-anne-of-st-bartholemew-blessed

**[[apostolate]]** — each member has an Apostolate, in collaboration with others or individually, [[07-constitutions-serving-gods-plan-wn#^ocds-c-25|C ¶25–28]] · the primary community apostolate is to share Carmelite spirituality with others, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-III-1|S §III]] · the Carmelite apostolate is based on a fervent interior life – friendship with God, [[f1a-session-02]] · has an apostolic dimension and overflows with missionary fervor, distinguishing Community and Personal Apostolate, [[session-09-asp]] · [[session-11-asp]] · [[appendix-a-asp]] · the connection between Thérèse and the missionary is not direct but indirect, by the grace of God, [[theresian-anniversaries-2025-text-06]] · a burning hunger and thirst to win souls for the Divine Heart (Mary Teresa Tauscher's Autobiography), [[10-30-blessed-mary-teresa-tauscher]] · "the apostolate, whether it be prayer or action, is my vocation" (Maria Felicia's Personal Diary), [[04-28-blessed-maria-felicia]] · I would have given a thousand lives to save one soul (Teresa, Way of Perfection), [[04-18-blessed-mary-of-the-incarnation]] · by their silent apostolate of suffering, love and prayer, foremost in the benefits they obtain for mankind (Teresa Margaret Redi's Acts of Canonization), [[09-01-st-teresa-margaret-of-the-sacred-heart-of-jesus-redi]] ^ocds-ai-apostolate

**[[Appetites]]** — inordinate affective desires in which the will participates, impeding union with God, [[f2a-session-02]] ^ocds-ai-appetites

**[[Apprehensions]]** — the activity and content of perception, sensory or spiritual, natural or supernatural, [[fo2-ascent-3]] ^ocds-ai-apprehensions

**[[aridity|Aridity]]** — a vigorous effort at stripping herself, passing by a new "desert, arid and without water," [[theresian-anniversaries-2025-text-05]] ^ocds-ai-aridity

**[[prayer-ascetical|Ascetical prayer]]** — the prayer found in the first three dwelling places; relies on human effort, [[f2b-session-01]] ^ocds-ai-ascetical-prayer

**Aspirancy** — twelve-month introductory study of the Carmelite Secular charism, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-I-3|S §I.2–3]] · help the Aspirant become familiar with the Community and adequate discernment of a vocation, [[syllabus-asp]] · [[introduction-asp]] ^ocds-ai-aspirancy

**[[attachments|Attachment]]** — the positive harms to the soul under their five effects: weary, torment, darken, defile, weaken, [[f2a-appendix-a]] ^ocds-ai-attachments

**Attendance at community meetings** — absences allowed only for serious and just reasons, [[06-constitutions-witnesses-wn#^ocds-c-24|C ¶24.c]] · community meets at least monthly, attendance expected, unjustifiable absences addressed by the local Council, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-VIII-3|S §VIII.3]] ^ocds-ai-attendance-at-community-meetings

**Awakening of God in the soul** — the communication of God's excellence to the substance of the soul, [[fo2-living-flame]] · an awakening of God in the soul, brought about in gentleness and love (Living Flame B 4), [[stc-5]] ^ocds-ai-awakening-of-god-in-the-soul

## C

**candidate** — the candidate has the primary responsibility for a "yes" to the call, [[43-ratio-candidate-wn#^ocds-ratio-22|R 22–23]] ^ocds-ai-candidate

**candidate files** — Formation files are kept for each candidate, beginning with admission to aspirancy through to the Definitive Promise, [[appendix-a-fh]] ^ocds-ai-candidate-files

**[[charism]]** — shared with the friars and nuns, each according to their particular state of life, [[04-constitutions-identity-values-commitment-wn#^ocds-c-1|C ¶1]] · the particular way its members are called to follow Christ, giving each religious family its unique identity, [[session-07-asp]] · the distinct spirit that animates a religious order and gives it a particular character and identity, [[ocds-fh-07]] ^ocds-ai-charism

**[[charity|Charity]]** — the pars potior of our vocation is to love and cherish one another, [[f1a-session-03]] · [[f1a-appendix-e]] · [[f1a-appendix-f]] · charity consists in bearing with the faults of others, must not remain hidden in the bottom of the heart, [[f2c-session-08]] · love, love, and yet more love — the more we love God, the more we shall long to love him (Joachina de Vedruna's Letters), [[05-22-st-joachina]] ^ocds-ai-charity

**[[chastity]]** — the promise of chastity reinforces the commitment to love God above all else, [[05-constitutions-following-jesus-wn#^ocds-c-13|C ¶13]] · purity of heart by which alone I can see God, [[f1b-appendix-p]] · reviewed at Definitive-Promise preparation, [[f2c-session-13]] · [[f2b-session-12c]] ^ocds-ai-chastity

**[[church|Church]]** — Way of Perfection Study Edition glossary term, [[f1a-appendix-a]] ^ocds-ai-church

**Coat of Arms** — the order of Discalced Carmelites has its own crest, significant of its rich spirit and antiquity, [[appendix-k-asp]] ^ocds-ai-coat-of-arms

**commitment to the Order** — the Secular Carmelite is moved to commit himself or herself to the Order, [[52-ratio-makes-a-commitment-to-the-order-wn#^ocds-ratio-81|R 81–85]] ^ocds-ai-commitment-to-the-order

**[[community|Community]]** — a place to live and promote personal and communal fellowship with God in Christ, [[06-constitutions-witnesses-wn#^ocds-c-24|C ¶24.a–e]] · the appropriate place for the formation of the candidate, [[44-ratio-community-wn#^ocds-ratio-24|R 24–29]] · growth in holiness is a journey in community, side by side with others, [[appendix-c-asp]] ^ocds-ai-community

**Community Formation** — "Community formation" is time set aside for the entire community, distinct from Candidate Formation and Ongoing Formation, [[fo1-community-formation]] ^ocds-ai-community-formation

**community size** — 40 to 50 active professed members prompts consultation on a second community, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-X-1|S §X]] ^ocds-ai-community-size

**Community / Study Group / Group in Development** — communities are founded by the Order, not by individuals; defines all three statuses, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-XII-1|S §XII]] ^ocds-ai-community-study-group-group-in-development

**[[confidence|Confidence]]** — attracted to the path of love and abandonment, Thérèse took it with total confidence only after Father Alexis, [[theresian-anniversaries-2023-study-guide-06]] · a vital faith in the love which is absolute, infinite, eternal — the Merciful Love of God, [[theresian-anniversaries-2024-text-03]] ^ocds-ai-confidence

**[[contemplation|Contemplation]]** — the perfect life in relation to the highest states of prayer, [[f1a-session-09]] · three signs of readiness to move beyond discursive meditation into contemplative prayer, [[f2a-session-12]] · an inflow of God into the soul, called by contemplatives infused contemplation or mystical theology, [[fo2-the-dark-night-2]] · ten successive steps on the ladder of secret contemplation, elevating the soul step by step to God, [[fo2-the-dark-night-2]] · the celebrated "passage" (Pasch) from meditation to contemplation, a "loving attentiveness," [[stc-3]] · in infused contemplation God is experienced through contact ("touch"), without concepts or mental images, [[stc-4]] · to taste in our hearts something of the power of the divine presence, through purity of heart and perfect love (Institution of the First Monks), [[08-07-st-albert-of-trapani]] · all who wear the holy habit of Carmel are called to prayer and contemplation, this precious pearl (Teresa of Jesus), [[11-14-all-carmelite-sts]] · when the voice of heavenly understanding enters the mind through the grace of contemplation, the whole man is no longer within the cave (Gregory the Great on Elijah), [[07-20-st-elijah]] ^ocds-ai-contemplation

**[[contemplatives|Contemplatives]]** — Way of Perfection Study Edition glossary term, [[f1a-appendix-a]] ^ocds-ai-contemplatives

**[[council|Council]] (local)** — constitutes the immediate authority of the community, [[10-constitutions-organization-government-wn#^ocds-c-46|C ¶46–47]] · immediate, competent authority within the limits of the Constitutions; develops methods for members' growth, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-VIII-2|S §VIII.2]] · it is always the Council that has the right to admit the candidates to each stage of the formation process, [[council-and-responsibilities-fh]] · authority lived "with a mother's love" and with "discretion," [[f1b-session-09]] · reviewed at Definitive-Promise preparation, [[f2c-session-11]] · constitutes the immediate authority of the community; primary responsibility is the formation and maturing of the members, [[fo1-general-principles]] ^ocds-ai-council

**Councilors** — form, with the President, the government of the community, [[10-constitutions-organization-government-wn#^ocds-c-52|C ¶52]] ^ocds-ai-councilors

**[[creation|Creation]]** — creation is above all a mystery which, in revealing itself, reveals to us the mystery of God, [[stc-0]] · by these words the world was created, a palace for the bride made with great wisdom, [[stc-1]] · in them he left some trace of who he is — creatures are like a trace of God's passing, [[stc-2]] · the soul knows creatures through God and not God through creatures — essential knowledge, [[stc-5]] · he clothed creatures wholly in beauty and dignity, leaving in them some trace of who he is (Spiritual Canticle), [[12-14-st-john-of-the-cross]] ^ocds-ai-creation

## D

**[[dark-night|Dark night]]** — the purifying reality of the mystical night — darkness, trials, the cross — in the sixth dwelling places, [[f2b-session-08]] · Thérèse's trial of faith, "invaded by the thickest darkness," [[f2c-session-07]] · *active night of the senses* — the point of departure, purification of sensory desire, [[f2a-session-01]] · [[fo2-formation-guidelines]] · *active night of the spirit* — intellect purified by faith, memory by hope, will by charity, [[f2a-session-08]] · [[fo2-formation-guidelines]] · *passive night of sense* — three signs discerning whether dryness is God's action, [[fo2-the-dark-night-1]] · *passive night of the spirit* — intellect left in darkness, will in aridity, memory in emptiness, [[fo2-the-dark-night-2]] · the necessity to pass through this dark night — the mortification of the appetites and denial of pleasure, [[stc-6]] · Thérèse's "trial of faith" beginning around Easter 1896, [[theresian-anniversaries-2024-text-05]] ^ocds-ai-dark-night

**Definitive Promise** — *see* Promise, the; Ongoing formation ^ocds-ai-definitive-promise

**[[denis-redemptus-blessed|Denis and Redemptus, Blesseds]]** — cartographer turned Carmelite, sent to Sumatra; received the martyr's crown November 29, 1638, [[11-29-blesseds-denis-berthelot]] ^ocds-ai-denis-redemptus-blessed

**[[detachment|Detachment]]** — true humility and the virtue of detachment always go together, two inseparable sisters, [[f1a-session-04]] · [[f1a-appendix-a]] · equivalent of poverty of spirit, a freedom from the appetites, [[f2a-session-01]] · relinquishing what stands in the way of giving oneself to the All without reserve, [[fo1-community-formation]] · the solitude of detachment, a "nest," a "place for God," [[fo2-introduction]] · to be detached from creation means to receive it as a gift from God, [[stc-6]] · detachment sets me free to love — freedom of the heart for God (3 Ascent 20,4), [[stc-7]] ^ocds-ai-detachment

**[[determination|Determination]]** — a great and very determined determination to persevere until reaching the end, [[f1a-session-05]] · [[f1a-appendix-a]] · the same Teresian phrase taken up by Thérèse in pursuit of her vocation, [[f2c-session-04]] ^ocds-ai-determination

**Director of Formation** — responsibility of preparing candidates for first and definitive promises, [[10-constitutions-organization-government-wn#^ocds-c-53|C ¶53]] · the principal task is to accompany the candidate, [[46-ratio-director-of-formation-wn#^ocds-ratio-32|R 32–35]] · has the responsibility of preparing the candidates for first and definitive promises, [[ocds-fh-05]] · reviewed at Definitive-Promise preparation, [[f2c-session-12]] ^ocds-ai-director-of-formation

**Discalced Carmelites** — established as a separate province (1581); Clement VIII established the Discalced Carmelites as an independent religious order (1593), [[f1b-session-05]] ^ocds-ai-discalced-carmelites

**discernment (of vocation)** — three active parties — God, the candidate, and those responsible for formation; qualities and counter-indications listed, [[48-ratio-apx-2-discernment-vocation-to-secular-order-wn#^ocds-ratio-59|R 59–68]] · certain qualities that indicate a person is essentially suited to the vocation of a Secular Carmelite, [[criteria-for-evaluating-candidates-fh]] ^ocds-ai-discernment

**dismissal** — verify the facts, warn the member, allow a reasonable time for repentance, [[06-constitutions-witnesses-wn#^ocds-c-24|C ¶24.e]] · dismissal from the community is dismissal from the Order, [[05-04-statutes#^ocds-s-IX-1|S §IX]] ^ocds-ai-dismissal

**Dwelling Places (seven)** — the seven dwelling places are not seven rooms but seven degrees of life or communion, [[f2b-session-01]] ^ocds-ai-dwelling-places

**[[examination-of-conscience|Examination of Conscience]]** — grow more sensitive to one's own spirit — its longings, its powers, its sources, [[session-06-asp]] ^ocds-ai-examination-of-conscience
