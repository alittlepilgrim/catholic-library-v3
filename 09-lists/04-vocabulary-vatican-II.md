## A

## Abortion

life must be guarded with the greatest care while abortion and infanticide are unspeakable crimes, [[gaudium-et-spes-02#^gs-51|GS ¶51]] — card: [[abortion]]

## Abraham

He called Abraham in order to make of him a great nation, [[dei-verbum#^dv-3|DV ¶3, ¶14]] · with whom the faith of Islam takes pleasure in linking itself, submitted to God, [[nostra-aetate#^na-3|NA ¶3–4]] — card: [[abraham]]

## Almsgiving

they value the moral life and worship God especially through prayer, almsgiving and fasting, [[nostra-aetate#^na-3|NA ¶3]] — card: [[almsgiving]]

## Anointing of the Sick

which may also and more fittingly be called Anointing of the Sick, [[sc-03#^sc-73|SC ¶73–75]] — card: [[anointing-of-the-sick]]

## Apostolate

*lay* — The lay apostolate...is a participation in the salvific mission of the Church itself, [[lumen-gentium-04|LG ¶33]] · *coordination under the bishop* — the coordination and close connection of all apostolic works should be fostered under the direction of the bishop, [[cd-02#^cd-17|CD ¶17]] · [[cd-02#^cd-33|CD ¶33–35]] · *lay, in the missions* — let associations and groups be organized by means of which the lay apostolate will be able to permeate the whole, [[ag-02#^ag-15|AG ¶15]] · All activity of the Mystical Body directed to the attainment of this goal is called the apostolate, [[aa-01#^aa-2|AA ¶2]] — card: [[apostolate]]

## Apostolic succession

the Apostles left bishops as their successors, 'handing over' to them 'the authority to teach', [[dei-verbum#^dv-7|DV ¶7–8]] · *in the East* — possess true sacraments, above all by apostolic succession, the priesthood and the Eucharist, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-15|UR ¶15]] — card: [[apostolic-succession]]

## Assumption

was taken up body and soul into heavenly glory, and exalted by the Lord as Queen of the universe, [[lumen-gentium-08|LG ¶59]] — card: [[assumption]]

## Atheism

atheism must be accounted among the most serious problems of this age, [[gaudium-et-spes-01#^gs-19|GS ¶19–21]] — card: [[atheism]]

## B

## Baptism

*rite of* — Both the rites for the baptism of adults are to be revised, [[sc-03#^sc-66|SC ¶66–70]] · *sacramental bond of unity* — Baptism therefore establishes a sacramental bond of unity which links all who have been reborn by it, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-22|UR ¶22]] — card: [[baptism]]

## Biblical inspiration

written under the inspiration of the Holy Spirit, they have God as their author, [[dei-verbum#^dv-11|DV ¶11]] — card: [[biblical-inspiration]]

## Bishop

*missionary role* — It is the bishop's role, as the ruler and center of unity in the diocesan apostolate, to promote missionary activity, [[ag-05#^ag-30|AG ¶30]] — card: [[bishop]]

## C

## Canon of Scripture

Through the same tradition the Church's full canon of the sacred books is known, [[dei-verbum#^dv-8|DV ¶8]] — card: [[canon-of-scripture]]

## Catechesis

*catechetical instruction* — this instruction is based on Sacred Scripture, tradition, the liturgy, magisterium, and life of the Church, [[cd-02#^cd-14|CD ¶14]] · catechetical instruction, which enlightens and strengthens the faith, nourishes life according to the spirit of Christ, [[gravissimum-educationis#4. Various Aids to Christian Education|GE §4]] — card: [[catechesis]]

## Celibacy (priestly)

is held by the Church to be of great value in a special manner for the priestly life, [[po-03#^po-16|PO ¶16]] · renouncing thereby the companionship of marriage for the sake of the kingdom of heaven, [[optatam-totius#^ot-10|OT ¶10]] — card: [[celibacy]]

## Charism

allotting his gifts to everyone according as He wills, He distributes special graces among the faithful of every rank, [[lumen-gentium-02|LG ¶12]] · *of the faithful* — the Holy Spirit Who sanctifies the people of God through ministry and the sacraments gives the faithful special gifts also, [[aa-01#^aa-3|AA ¶3]] — card: [[charism]]

## Charity

love for God and neighbor is the first and greatest commandment, [[gaudium-et-spes-01#^gs-24|GS ¶24]] — card: [[charity]]

## Chastity

*religious vow* — which religious profess should be counted an outstanding gift of grace, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-12|PC ¶12]] — card: [[chastity]]

## Church

*as universal sacrament* — the Church is in Christ like a sacrament...a sign and instrument...of the unity of the whole human race, [[lumen-gentium-01#^lg-1|LG ¶1]] · *subsists in the Catholic Church* — This Church constituted and organized in the world as a society, subsists in the Catholic Church, [[lumen-gentium-01#^lg-8|LG ¶8]] — card: [[church]]

## Collegiality

one is constituted a member of the Episcopal body in virtue of sacramental consecration and hierarchical communion, [[lumen-gentium-03|LG ¶22–23]] · *episcopal college* — bishops are constituted as members of the episcopal body, [[cd-01#^cd-4|CD ¶4]] — card: [[collegiality]]

## Communion of saints

all in various ways and degrees are in communion in the same charity of God and neighbor, [[lumen-gentium-07|LG ¶49–51]] — card: [[communion-of-saints]]

## Confession

*faculties across rites* — the faculties for hearing confessions duly and without restriction given to priests of any rite, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-16|OE ¶16]] — card: [[confession]]

## Confirmation

The rite of confirmation is to be revised, [[sc-03#^sc-71|SC ¶71]] · *Eastern discipline* — priests validly confer this sacrament, using chrism blessed by a patriarch or a bishop, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-13|OE ¶13–14]] — card: [[confirmation]]

## Conscience

Conscience is the most secret core and sanctuary of a man, [[gaudium-et-spes-01#^gs-16|GS ¶16]] · *freedom of* — he is not to be forced to act in a manner contrary to his conscience, [[dignitatis-humanae#^dh-3|DH ¶3]] — card: [[conscience]]

## Consecration

*religious profession* — This constitutes a special consecration, which is deeply rooted in that of baptism and expresses it more fully, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-5|PC ¶5]] — card: [[consecration]]

## Contemplation

*contemplative life in the missions* — Since the contemplative life belongs to the fullness of the Church's presence, let it be put into effect everywhere., [[ag-02#^ag-18|AG ¶18]] — card: [[contemplation]]

## Covenant

First He entered into a covenant with Abraham... and, through Moses, with the people of Israel, [[dei-verbum#^dv-14|DV ¶14]] — card: [[covenant]]

## D

## Deposit of faith

Sacred tradition and Sacred Scripture form one sacred deposit of the word of God, committed to the Church, [[dei-verbum#^dv-10|DV ¶10]] — card: [[deposit-of-faith]]

## Development of doctrine

there is a growth in the understanding of the realities and the words which have been handed down, [[dei-verbum#^dv-8|DV ¶8]] — card: [[development-doctrinal]]

## Diaconate

the diaconate can in the future be restored as a proper and permanent rank of the hierarchy, [[lumen-gentium-03|LG ¶29]] · *restoration* — the order of the diaconate should be restored as a permanent state of life according to the norms, [[ag-02#^ag-16|AG ¶16]] · *Eastern, permanent* — the office of the permanent diaconate should, where it has fallen into disuse, be restored, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-17|OE ¶17]] — card: [[diaconate]]

## Diocese

A diocese is a portion of the people of God which is entrusted to a bishop, [[cd-02#^cd-11|CD ¶11]] — card: [[diocese]]

## Divine Office

He joins the entire community of mankind to Himself, associating it with His own singing of this canticle, [[sc-04#^sc-83|SC ¶83–101]] — card: [[divine-office]]

## E

## Easter

*common date* — to celebrate the feast of Easter on the same Sunday, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-20|OE ¶20]] — card: [[easter]]

## Eastern Churches

the Churches of the East, as much as those of the West, have a full right and are in duty bound, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-5|OE ¶5–6]] · *ecumenical relation* — there flourish in the East many particular or local Churches, among which the Patriarchal Churches hold first place, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-14|UR ¶14–18]] — card: [[eastern-churches]]

## Ecumenism

*separated brethren* — The Church recognizes that in many ways she is linked with those who...are honored with the name of Christian, [[lumen-gentium-02|LG ¶15]] · *in the missions* — The ecumenical spirit should be nurtured in the neophytes, [[ag-02#^ag-15|AG ¶15]] · The term 'ecumenical movement' indicates the initiatives and activities planned and undertaken, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-4|UR ¶1, ¶4]] — card: [[ecumenism]]

## Episcopate

*bishops as successors of the apostles* — bishops by divine institution have succeeded to the place of the apostles, as shepherds of the Church, [[lumen-gentium-03|LG ¶18–21]] — card: [[episcopal-episcopate]]

## Eschatology

*pilgrim Church* — will attain its full perfection only in the glory of heaven, [[lumen-gentium-07|LG ¶48]] — card: [[eschatology]]

## Eucharist

*Mass* — our Saviour instituted the eucharistic sacrifice of His Body and Blood, [[sc-02#^sc-47|SC ¶47]] · *source and apex of priestly ministry* — the Eucharist shows itself as the source and the apex of the whole work of preaching the Gospel, [[po-02#^po-5|PO ¶5–6]] — card: [[eucharist]]

## Evangelical counsels

The evangelical counsels of chastity dedicated to God, poverty and obedience are based upon the words and examples, [[lumen-gentium-06|LG ¶42–43]] · the pursuit of perfect charity through the evangelical counsels draws its origin from the doctrine and example, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-1|PC ¶1]] — card: [[evangelical-counsels]]

## Evangelization

The proper purpose of this missionary activity is evangelization, and the planting of the Church among those peoples, [[ag-01#^ag-6|AG ¶6]] — card: [[evangelization]]

## F

## Faith

*freedom of the act of* — The act of faith is of its very nature a free act, [[dignitatis-humanae#^dh-10|DH ¶10]] — card: [[faith]]

## Feast days

It belongs only to an ecumenical council or to the Apostolic See to determine, transfer or suppress feast days, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-19|OE ¶19]] — card: [[feast-days]]

## G

## Gospel

*apostolic origin, historicity* — the four Gospels... whose historical character the Church unhesitatingly asserts, [[dei-verbum#^dv-18|DV ¶18–19]] — card: [[gospel]]

## H

## Hierarchy

*relation to lay apostolate* — The hierarchy should promote the apostolate of the laity, provide it with spiritual principles and support, [[aa-05#^aa-24|AA ¶23–25]] — card: [[hierarchy]]

## Hierarchy of truths

in Catholic doctrine there exists a 'hierarchy' of truths, since they vary in their relation to the fundamental Christian faith, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-11|UR ¶11]] — card: [[hierarchy-of-truths]]

## Holy Orders

*ordination rites* — Both the ceremonies and texts of the ordination rites are to be revised, [[sc-03#^sc-76|SC ¶76]] — card: [[holy-orders]]

## Homily

the homily, therefore, is to be highly esteemed as part of the liturgy itself, [[sc-02#^sc-52|SC ¶52]] — card: [[homily]]

## Human person

*dignity of* — all things on earth should be related to man as their center and crown, [[gaudium-et-spes-01#^gs-12|GS ¶12]] · *dignity, foundation of rights* — A sense of the dignity of the human person has been impressing itself more and more deeply on the consciousness, [[dignitatis-humanae#^dh-1|DH ¶1]] — card: [[person-human]]

## I

## Immaculate Conception

the Immaculate Virgin, preserved free from all guilt of original sin, [[lumen-gentium-08|LG ¶56, 59]] — card: [[immaculate-conception]]

## Inerrancy

teaching solidly, faithfully and without error that truth which God wanted put into sacred writings, [[dei-verbum#^dv-11|DV ¶11]] — card: [[inerrancy]]

## Infallibility

his definitions, of themselves, and not from the consent of the Church, are justly styled irreformable, [[lumen-gentium-03|LG ¶25]] — card: [[infallibility]]

## Intercommunion (communicatio in sacris)

Common participation in worship (communicatio in sacris) which harms the unity of the Church or involves formal acceptance of error, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-26|OE ¶26–29]] · some worship in common (communicatio in sacris), given suitable circumstances and the approval of Church authority, is not only possible, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-8|UR ¶8, ¶15]] — card: [[intercommunion]]

## J

## Justice

To satisfy the demands of justice and equity, strenuous efforts must be made, [[gaudium-et-spes-02#^gs-66|GS ¶66]] — card: [[justice]]

## K

## Kingdom of God

the Kingdom is clearly visible in the very Person of Christ, the Son of God and the Son of Man, [[lumen-gentium-01#^lg-5|LG ¶5]] — card: [[kingdom-of-god]]

## L

## Laity

The term laity is here understood to mean all the faithful except those in Holy Orders, [[lumen-gentium-04|LG ¶31]] · *secular character of* — Secular duties and activities belong properly although not exclusively to laymen, [[gaudium-et-spes-01#^gs-43|GS ¶43]] · *in the missions* — unless there is a laity worthy of the name working along with the hierarchy, [[ag-03#^ag-21|AG ¶21]] · *right and duty to apostolate* — The laity derive the right and duty to the apostolate from their union with Christ the head, [[aa-01#^aa-3|AA ¶3]] — card: [[laity]]

## Lent

The season of Lent has a twofold character: primarily by recalling or preparing for baptism and by penance, [[sc-05#^sc-109|SC ¶109–110]] — card: [[lent]]

## Liberty

Only in freedom can man direct himself toward goodness, [[gaudium-et-spes-01#^gs-17|GS ¶17]] — card: [[liberty]]

## Liturgical Year

she unfolds the whole mystery of Christ, from the incarnation and birth until the ascension, [[sc-05#^sc-102|SC ¶102]] — card: [[liturgical-year]]

## M

## Magisterium

*teaching office* — authentically interpreting the word of God... entrusted exclusively to the living teaching office of the Church, [[dei-verbum#^dv-10|DV ¶10]] — card: [[magisterium]]

## Marriage

God Himself is the author of matrimony, endowed as it is with various benefits and purposes, [[gaudium-et-spes-02#^gs-48|GS ¶48]] · *canonical form, mixed rites* — the canonical 'form' for the celebration of these marriages is of obligation only for liceity, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-18|OE ¶18]] — card: [[marriage]]

## Martyrdom

the Church, then, considers martyrdom as an exceptional gift and as the fullest proof of love, [[lumen-gentium-05|LG ¶42]] — card: [[martyr]]

## Mary

*Mother of God* — is acknowledged and honored as being truly the Mother of God and Mother of the Redeemer, [[lumen-gentium-08|LG ¶52–54]] · *Type of the Church* — the Mother of God is a type of the Church in the order of faith, charity and perfect union, [[lumen-gentium-08|LG ¶63–65]] — card: [[mary]]

## Matrimony (rite of)

the grace of the sacrament is more clearly signified and the duties of the spouses are taught, [[sc-03#^sc-77|SC ¶77–78]] — card: [[matrimony]]

## Mission

the Church fosters the missions with care and attention, [[lumen-gentium-02|LG ¶16–17]] · The pilgrim Church is missionary by her very nature, since it is from the mission of the Son, [[ag-01#^ag-2|AG ¶2]] — card: [[mission]]

## Monastic life

The principal duty of monks is to offer a service to the divine majesty at once humble and noble, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-9|PC ¶9]] — card: [[monastic-life]]

## Mystical Body of Christ

By communicating His Spirit, Christ made His brothers, called together from all nations, mystically the components of His own Body, [[lumen-gentium-01#^lg-7|LG ¶7–8]] — card: [[body-of-christ]]

## N

## New Testament

the word of God... shows its power in a most excellent way in the writings of the New Testament, [[dei-verbum#^dv-17|DV ¶17–20]] — card: [[new-testament]]

## O

## Obedience

*priestly* — to dedicate their own will by obedience to the service of God and their fellow men, [[po-03#^po-15|PO ¶15]] · *religious vow* — religious offer the full surrender of their own will as a sacrifice of themselves to God, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-14|PC ¶14]] — card: [[obedience]]

## Old Testament

these books... written under divine inspiration, remain permanently valuable, [[dei-verbum#^dv-14|DV ¶14–16]] — card: [[old-testament]]

## P

## Parish

*erection/suppression* — the same concern for souls should be the basis for determining or reconsidering the erection or suppression of parishes, [[cd-02#^cd-32|CD ¶30–32]] — card: [[parish]]

## Particular church

already rooted in social life and somewhat conformed to the local culture, enjoys a certain firmness and stability, [[ag-03#^ag-19|AG ¶19–20]] — card: [[particular-church]]

## Paschal mystery

He achieved His task principally by the paschal mystery of His blessed passion, resurrection from the dead, [[sc-01#^sc-5|SC ¶5–6]] — card: [[paschal-mystery-sacrifice]]

## Patriarch

the bishop to whom belongs jurisdiction over all bishops, not excepting metropolitans, clergy and people of his own territory, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-7|OE ¶7–11]] — card: [[patriarch]]

## Peace

Peace is not merely the absence of war; nor can it be reduced solely to the maintenance of a balance, [[gaudium-et-spes-02#^gs-78|GS ¶78]] — card: [[peace]]

## Penance (sacrament of)

The rite and formulas for the sacrament of penance are to be revised, [[sc-03#^sc-72|SC ¶72]] — card: [[penance-sacrament-of]]

## People of God

not according to the flesh but in the Spirit. This was to be the new People of God, [[lumen-gentium-02#^lg-9|LG ¶9]] — card: [[people-of-god]]

## Perfection

*priestly holiness* — Priests are bound, however, to acquire that perfection in special fashion, [[po-03#^po-12|PO ¶12–14]] — card: [[perfection]]

## Poverty

*voluntary poverty of priests* — Priests, moreover, are invited to embrace voluntary poverty by which they are more manifestly conformed to Christ, [[po-03#^po-17|PO ¶17]] · *religious/evangelical* — Religious should diligently practice and if need be express also in new forms that voluntary poverty, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-13|PC ¶13]] — card: [[poverty]]

## Primacy (papal)

the Roman Pontiff has full, supreme and universal power over the Church, [[lumen-gentium-03|LG ¶22]] — card: [[primacy]]

## R

## Religious life

*consecrated state* — the state which is constituted by the profession of the evangelical counsels...undeniably belongs to its life and holiness, [[lumen-gentium-06|LG ¶44–46]] · *renewal and adaptation* — includes both the constant return to the sources of all Christian life, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-2|PC ¶2]] · *in the missions* — the religious life should be carefully fostered... precious and absolutely necessary assistance to missionary activity, [[ag-02#^ag-18|AG ¶18]] — card: [[religious-life]]

## Revelation (divine)

God chose to reveal Himself and to make known to us the hidden purpose of His will, [[dei-verbum#^dv-2|DV ¶2–6]] — card: [[revelation]]

## Rites

*individual Churches* — each individual Church or Rite should retain its traditions whole and entire, [[orientalium-ecclesiarum#^oe-2|OE ¶2–3]] — card: [[rites]]

## S

## Sacrament

*nature and purpose* — The purpose of the sacraments is to sanctify men, to build up the body of Christ, [[sc-03#^sc-59|SC ¶59]] — card: [[sacrament]]

## Sacramentals

these are sacred signs which bear a resemblance to the sacraments, [[sc-03#^sc-60|SC ¶60]] — card: [[sacramentals]]

## Sacred Liturgy

*summit and font* — the liturgy is the summit toward which the activity of the Church is directed, [[sc-01#^sc-10|SC ¶10]] — card: [[liturgy]]

## Sacred Scripture

*relation to tradition* — Sacred Scripture is the word of God inasmuch as it is consigned to writing under the inspiration of the divine Spirit, [[dei-verbum#^dv-9|DV ¶9]] — card: [[sacred-scripture]]

## Secular institutes

although not Religious institutes involve a true and full profession of the evangelical counsels in the world, [[perfectae-caritatis#^pc-11|PC ¶11]] — card: [[institute-secular]]

## Sensus fidei

The entire body of the faithful, anointed as they are by the Holy One, cannot err in matters of belief, [[lumen-gentium-02|LG ¶12]] — card: [[sensus-fidei]]

## Sunday (the Lord's Day)

the Lord's day is the original feast day, and it should be proposed to the piety of the faithful, [[sc-05#^sc-106|SC ¶106]] — card: [[sunday]]

## Synod of Bishops

a deliberative body which will be called by the proper name of Synod of Bishops, [[cd-01#^cd-5|CD ¶5]] · [[cd-03#^cd-36|CD ¶36–37]] — card: [[synod]]

## T

## Theology

*Scripture its soul* — the study of the sacred page is, as it were, the soul of sacred theology, [[dei-verbum#^dv-24|DV ¶24]] · *curriculum, magisterium-guided* — should be so taught that the students will correctly draw out Catholic doctrine from divine revelation, [[optatam-totius#^ot-16|OT ¶16]] — card: [[theology]]

## Tradition (sacred)

This tradition which comes from the Apostles develops in the Church with the help of the Holy Spirit, [[dei-verbum#^dv-7|DV ¶7–10]] — card: [[tradition]]

## Typology

*unity of the Testaments* — the New Testament be hidden in the Old and the Old be made manifest in the New, [[dei-verbum#^dv-16|DV ¶16]] — card: [[typology]]

## U

## Unity, Christian

the unity of His Church is both signified and made a reality, [[unitatis-redintegratio#^ur-2|UR ¶2]] — card: [[unity-christian]]

## V

## Vocation

*fostering priestly vocations* — The duty of fostering vocations pertains to the whole Christian community, [[optatam-totius#^ot-2|OT ¶2]] · *missionary* — He inspires the missionary vocation in the hearts of individuals, [[ag-04#^ag-23|AG ¶23]] — card: [[vocation]]

## W

## Word of God

*veneration of* — the bread of life from the table both of God's word and of Christ's body, [[dei-verbum#^dv-21|DV ¶21]] — card: [[word-of-god]]
