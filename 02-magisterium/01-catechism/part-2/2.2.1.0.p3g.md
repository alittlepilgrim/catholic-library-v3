# Article 1. The Sacrament of Baptism

1213 Holy [[baptism|Baptism]] is the basis of the whole Christian [[life-earthly#Index|life]], the gateway to [[life#Index|life]] in the Spirit (vitae spiritualis ianua),<sup>4</sup> and the door which gives access to the other sacraments. Through Baptism we are freed from [[sin#Index|sin]] and reborn as sons of God; we become members of Christ, are incorporated into the Church and made sharers in her mission: "Baptism is the sacrament of [[regeneration#Index|regeneration]] through water in the word."<sup>5</sup> ^ccc-1213

---

**[4]** Cf. Council of Florence: DS 1314: vitae spiritualis ianua.**[5]** Roman Catechism II, 2, 5; Cf. Council of Florence: DS 1314; CIC, can. 204 # 1; 849; CCEO, can. 675 # 1.
