# III. The Sacraments of Faith

1122 Christ sent his apostles so that "repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in his name to all nations."<sup>41</sup> "Go therefore and make disciples of all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and of the Son and of the Holy Spirit."<sup>42</sup> [[mission#Index|The mission]] to baptize, and so the sacramental mission, is implied in the mission to evangelize, because [[sacrament#Index|the sacrament]] is prepared for by the word of God and by the faith which is assent to this word: ^ccc-1122

The People of God is formed into one in the first place by the Word of the living God. . . . The preaching of the Word is required for the sacramental ministry itself, since the sacraments are sacraments of faith, drawing their origin and nourishment from the Word.<sup>43</sup>

1123 "The purpose of [[sacrament#Index|the sacraments]] is to sanctify men, to build up the Body of Christ and, finally, to give [[worship#Index|worship]] to God. Because they are signs they also instruct. They not only presuppose faith, but by words and objects they also nourish, strengthen, and express it. That is why they are called 'sacraments of faith."'<sup>44</sup> ^ccc-1123

1124 The Church's faith precedes the faith of the believer who is invited to adhere to it. When the Church celebrates the sacraments, she confesses the faith received from the apostles - whence the ancient saying: lex orandi, lex credendi (or: legem credendi lex statuat supplicandi according to Prosper of Aquitaine \[5th cent.\]).<sup>45</sup> The law of prayer is the law of faith: the Church believes as she prays. [[liturgy#Index|Liturgy]] is a constitutive element of the holy and living [[tradition#Index|Tradition]].<sup>46</sup> ^ccc-1124

1125 For this reason no sacramental rite may be modified or manipulated at the will of the minister or the community. Even the supreme [[authority#Index|authority]] in the Church may not change [[liturgy#Index|the liturgy]] arbitrarily, but only in the obedience of faith and with religious respect for the mystery of the liturgy. ^ccc-1125

1126 Likewise, since [[sacrament#Index|the sacraments]] express and develop [[communion#Index|the communion]] of faith in the Church, the lex orandi is one of the essential criteria of [[dialogue#Index|the dialogue]] that seeks to restore the unity of Christians.<sup>47</sup> ^ccc-1126
