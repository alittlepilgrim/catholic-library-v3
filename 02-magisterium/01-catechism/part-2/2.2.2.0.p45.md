# Chapter 2. The Sacraments of Healing

1420 Through [[sacrament#Index|the sacraments]] of Christian initiation, man receives the new [[life-earthly#Index|life]] of Christ. Now we carry this [[life#Index|life]] "in earthen vessels," and it remains "hidden with Christ in God."<sup>1</sup> We are still in our "earthly tent," subject to suffering, illness, and death.<sup>2</sup> This new life as a child of God can be weakened and even lost by sin. ^ccc-1420

1421 The Lord Jesus Christ, [[physician#Index|physician]] of our souls and bodies, who forgave the sins of the paralytic and restored him to bodily health,<sup>3</sup> has willed that his Church continue, in the power of the Holy Spirit, his work of healing and salvation, even among her own members. This is the purpose of the two sacraments of healing: [[sacrament#Index|the sacrament]] of Penance and the sacrament of [[anointing-of-the-sick|Anointing of the Sick]]. ^ccc-1421
