# Chapter 1. The Sacraments of Christian Initiation

1212 [[sacrament#Index|The sacraments]] of [[initiation-christian|Christian initiation]] - [[baptism#Index|Baptism]], [[confirmation#Index|Confirmation]], and [[eucharist#Index|the Eucharist]] - lay the foundations of every Christian [[life-earthly#Index|life]]. "The sharing in the divine [[nature#Index|nature]] given to men through the grace of Christ bears a certain likeness to the origin, development, and nourishing of natural [[life#Index|life]]. The faithful are born anew by Baptism, strengthened by the sacrament of Confirmation, and receive in the Eucharist the food of eternal life. By means of these sacraments of Christian initiation, they thus receive in increasing measure the treasures of the divine life and advance toward the perfection of charity."<sup>3</sup> ^ccc-1212
