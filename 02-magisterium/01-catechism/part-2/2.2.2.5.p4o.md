# V. Viaticum, the Last Sacrament of the Christian

1524 In addition to the [[anointing-of-the-sick|Anointing of the Sick]], the Church offers those who are about to leave this [[life-earthly#Index|life]] the Eucharist as [[viaticum|viaticum]]. Communion in the body and blood of Christ, received at this moment of "passing over" to the Father, has a particular significance and importance. It is the seed of eternal [[life#Index|life]] and the power of resurrection, according to the words of the Lord: "He who eats my [[flesh#Index|flesh]] and drinks my blood has eternal life, and I will raise him up at the last day."<sup>140</sup> The sacrament of Christ once dead and now risen, the Eucharist is here the sacrament of passing over from death to life, from this world to the Father.<sup>141</sup> ^ccc-1524

1525 Thus, just as the sacraments of [[baptism#Index|Baptism]], Confirmation, and the Eucharist form a unity called "the sacraments of Christian initiation," so too it can be said that Penance, the [[anointing-of-the-sick|Anointing of the Sick]] and the Eucharist as [[viaticum#Index|viaticum]] constitute at the end of Christian life "the sacraments that prepare for our heavenly [[homeland#Index|homeland]]" or the sacraments that complete the earthly pilgrimage. ^ccc-1525
