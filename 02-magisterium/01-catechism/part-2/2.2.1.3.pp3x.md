# I. The Eucharist - Source and Summit of Ecclesial Life

1324 [[eucharist#Index|The Eucharist]] is "the source and summit of the Christian [[life-earthly#Index|life]]."<sup>134</sup> "The other sacraments, and indeed all ecclesiastical ministries and works of the [[apostolate#Index|apostolate]], are bound up with the Eucharist and are oriented toward it. For in the blessed Eucharist is contained the whole spiritual good of the Church, namely Christ himself, our Pasch."<sup>135</sup> ^ccc-1324

1325 "The Eucharist is the efficacious sign and sublime cause of that communion in the divine life and that unity of the People of God by which the Church is kept in being. It is the culmination both of God's [[action-acting#Index|action]] sanctifying the world in Christ and of [[worship#Index|the worship]] men offer to Christ and through him to the Father in the Holy Spirit."<sup>136</sup> ^ccc-1325

1326 Finally, by the Eucharistic [[liturgy#Index|celebration]] we already unite ourselves with the heavenly liturgy and anticipate eternal life, when God will be all in all.<sup>137</sup> ^ccc-1326

1327 In brief, [[eucharist#Index|the Eucharist]] is the sum and summary of our faith: "Our way of thinking is attuned to the Eucharist, and the Eucharist in turn confirms our way of thinking."<sup>138</sup> ^ccc-1327
