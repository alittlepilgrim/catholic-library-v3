# Article 7. The Sacrament of Matrimony

1601 "The matrimonial covenant, by which a man and a woman establish between themselves a partnership of the whole of life, is by its nature ordered toward [[good#Index|the good]] of the spouses and the procreation and [[education#Index|education]] of offspring; this covenant between baptized persons has been raised by Christ the Lord to [[dignity#Index|the dignity]] of a sacrament."<sup>84</sup> ^ccc-1601
