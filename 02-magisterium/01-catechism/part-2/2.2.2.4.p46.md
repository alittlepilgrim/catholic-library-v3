# Article 4. The Sacrament of Penance and Reconciliation

1422 "Those who approach [[penance-sacrament-of#Glossary|the sacrament of Penance]] obtain pardon from God's [[mercy|mercy]] for the offense committed against him, and are, at the same time, reconciled with the Church which they have wounded by their sins and which by charity, by example, and by prayer labors for their [[conversion#Index|conversion]]."<sup>4</sup> ^ccc-1422
