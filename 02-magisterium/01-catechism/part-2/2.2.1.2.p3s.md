# III. The Effects of Confirmation

1302 It is evident from its celebration that the effect of the sacrament of [[confirmation#Index|Confirmation]] is the full outpouring of [[holy-spirit#Index|the Holy Spirit]] as once granted to the apostles on the day of Pentecost. ^ccc-1302

1303 From this fact, [[confirmation#Index|Confirmation]] brings an increase and deepening of baptismal grace: ^ccc-1303

\- it roots us more deeply in the divine filiation which makes us cry, "Abba! Father!";<sup>115</sup>

\- it unites us more firmly to Christ;

\- it increases the gifts of the Holy Spirit in us;

\- it renders our bond with the Church more perfect;<sup>116</sup>

\- it gives us a special strength of the Holy Spirit to spread and defend the faith by word and action as true witnesses of Christ, to confess the name of Christ boldly, and never to be ashamed of the Cross:<sup>117</sup>

Recall then that you have received the spiritual seal, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of right judgment and courage, the spirit of knowledge and reverence, the spirit of holy fear in God's presence. Guard what you have received. God the Father has marked you with his sign; Christ the Lord has confirmed you and has placed his pledge, the Spirit, in your hearts.<sup>118</sup>

1304 Like [[baptism#Index|Baptism]] which it completes, [[confirmation#Index|Confirmation]] is given only once, for it too imprints on [[soul#Index|the soul]] an indelible spiritual mark, the "character," which is the sign that Jesus Christ has marked a [[christian#Index|Christian]] with [[seal#Index|the seal]] of his Spirit by clothing him with power from on high so that he may be his witness.<sup>119</sup> ^ccc-1304

1305 This "character" perfects the common priesthood of the faithful, received in [[baptism#Index|Baptism]], and "the confirmed person receives the power to profess faith in Christ publicly and as it were officially (quasi ex officio)."<sup>120</sup> ^ccc-1305
