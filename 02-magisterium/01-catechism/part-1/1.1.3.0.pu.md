# Chapter 3. Man's Response to God

142 By his [[revelation#Index|Revelation]], "the invisible God, from the fullness of his love, addresses men as his friends, and moves among them, in order to invite and receive them into his own company."<sup>1</sup> The adequate response to this invitation is [[faith|faith]]. ^ccc-142

143 By faith, [[man#Index|man]] completely submits his intellect and his will to God.<sup>2</sup> With his whole being man gives his assent to God the revealer. Sacred Scripture calls this human response to God, the author of [[revelation#Index|revelation]], "the obedience of faith".<sup>3</sup> ^ccc-143
