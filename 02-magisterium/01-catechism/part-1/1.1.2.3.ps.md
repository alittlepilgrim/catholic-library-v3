# V. Sacred Scripture in the Life of the Church

131 "and such is the force and [[power#Index|power]] of [[word-of-god#Index|the Word of God]] that it can serve the Church as her support and vigour, and the children of the Church as strength for their faith, food for [[soul#Index|the soul]], and a pure and lasting fount of [[spiritual-life#Index|spiritual life]]." <sup>109</sup> Hence "access to [[sacred-scripture#Index|Sacred Scripture]] ought to be open wide to the Christian faithful." <sup>110</sup> ^ccc-131

132 "Therefore, the study of the sacred page should be the very soul of sacred theology. [[ministry#Index|the ministry]] of the Word, too - [[pastoral#Index|pastoral]] [[preaching#Index|preaching]], catechetics and all forms of Christian instruction, among which the liturgical [[homily|homily]] should hold pride of place - is healthily nourished and thrives in holiness through the Word of Scripture." <sup>111</sup> ^ccc-132

133 The Church "forcefully and specifically exhorts all the Christian faithful. . . to learn the surpassing knowledge of Jesus Christ, by frequent reading of the divine Scriptures. [[ignorance#Index|Ignorance]] of the Scriptures is ignorance of Christ.<sup>112</sup> ^ccc-133
