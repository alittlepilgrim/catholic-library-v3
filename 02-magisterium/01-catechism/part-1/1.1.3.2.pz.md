# I. Lord, Look upon the Faith of Your Church

168 It is the Church that believes first, and so bears, nourishes and sustains my faith. Everywhere, it is the Church that first confesses the Lord: "Throughout the world the holy Church acclaims you", as we sing in the hymn Te Deum; with her and in her, we are won over and brought to confess: "I believe", "We believe". It is through the Church that we receive faith and new [[life-earthly#Index|life]] in Christ by [[baptism#Index|Baptism]]. In the Rituale Romanum, the minister of Baptism asks [[catechumen#Index|the catechumen]]: "What do you ask of God's Church?" and the answer is: "Faith." "What does faith offer you?" "Eternal [[life#Index|life]]."<sup>54</sup> ^ccc-168

169 [[salvation|Salvation]] comes from God alone; but because we receive the life of faith through the Church, she is our mother: "We believe the Church as the mother of our [[new-birth#Index|new birth]], and not in the Church as if she were the author of our salvation."<sup>55</sup> Because she is our mother, she is also our teacher in the faith. ^ccc-169

---

**[54]** Roman Ritual, Rite of Baptism of Adults.

**[55]** Faustus of Riez, De Spiritu Sancto 1, 2: PL 62, II.
