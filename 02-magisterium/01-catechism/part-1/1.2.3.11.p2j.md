# Article 11. I Believe in the Resurrection of the Body – In Brief

*1015 "The flesh is the hinge of salvation" (Tertullian, De res. 8, 2: PL 2, 852). We believe in God who is creator of the flesh; we believe in the Word made flesh in order to redeem the flesh; we believe in the resurrection of the flesh, the fulfillment of both [[creation#Index|the creation]] and the redemption of the flesh.* ^ccc-1015

*1016 By death [[soul#Index|the soul]] is separated from the body, but in the resurrection God will give incorruptible [[life-earthly#Index|life]] to our body, transformed by reunion with our soul. Just as Christ is risen and lives for ever, so all of us will rise at the last day.* ^ccc-1016

*1017 "We believe in the true resurrection of this [[flesh#Index|flesh]] that we now possess" (Council of Lyons II: DS 854). We sow a corruptible body in the tomb, but he raises up an incorruptible body, a "spiritual body" (cf [[1-corinthians-15#^1-corinthians-15-42|1 Cor 15:42-44]]).* ^ccc-1017

*1018 As a consequence of original sin, man must suffer "bodily death, from which man would have been immune had he not sinned" (GS # 18).* ^ccc-1018

*1019 Jesus, the Son of God, freely suffered death for us in complete and free submission to the will of God, his Father. By his death he has conquered death, and so opened the possibility of salvation to all men.* ^ccc-1019
