# Article 2. and in Jesus Christ, His Only Son, Our Lord – In Brief

*452 The name Jesus means "God saves". The child born of the Virgin Mary is called Jesus, "for he will save his people from their sins" ([[matthew-01#^matthew-01-21|Mt 1:21]]): "there is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved" ([[acts-of-the-apostles-04#^acts-of-the-apostles-04-12|Acts 4:12]]).* ^ccc-452

*453 The title "Christ" means "Anointed One" (Messiah).Jesus is the Christ, for "God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power" ([[acts-of-the-apostles-10#^acts-of-the-apostles-10-38|Acts 10:38]]). He was the one "who is to come" ([[luke-07#^luke-07-19|Lk 7:19]]), the object of "the hope of [[israel#Index|Israel]]" ([[acts-of-the-apostles-28#^acts-of-the-apostles-28-20|Acts 28:20]]).* ^ccc-453

*454 The title "Son of God" signifies the unique and eternal relationship of Jesus Christ to God his Father: he is the only Son of [[father#Index|the Father]] (cf [[john-01#^john-01-14|Jn 1:14]], [[john-01#^john-01-18|18;]] [[john-03#^john-03-16|3:16,]] [[john-03#^john-03-18|18]]); he is God himself (cf [[john-01#^john-01-1|Jn 1:1]]). To be a Christian, one must believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God (cf [[acts-of-the-apostles-08|Acts 8]]:37; [[1-john-02#^1-john-02-23|1 Jn 2:23]]).* ^ccc-454

*455 The title "Lord" indicates divine sovereignty. To confess or invoke Jesus as Lord is to believe in his [[divinity#Index|divinity]]. "No one can say 'Jesus is Lord' except by the Holy Spirit'" ([[1-corinthians-12#^1-corinthians-12-3|I Cor 12:3]]).* ^ccc-455
