# II. to Judge the Living and the Dead

678 Following in the steps of the prophets and John the Baptist, Jesus announced the judgement of the Last [[day#Index|Day]] in his preaching.<sup>581</sup> Then will the conduct of each one and the secrets of hearts be brought to light.<sup>582</sup> Then will the culpable [[unbelief#Index|unbelief]] that counted the offer of God's grace as nothing be condemned.<sup>583</sup> Our attitude to our neighbour will disclose [[acceptance-accepting#Index|acceptance]] or refusal of grace and divine love.<sup>584</sup> On the Last Day Jesus will say: "Truly I say to you, as you did it to one of the least of these my brethren, you did it to me."<sup>585</sup> ^ccc-678

679 Christ is Lord of eternal [[life-earthly#Index|life]]. Full right to pass definitive judgement on [[works#Index|the works]] and hearts of men belongs to him as redeemer of the world. He "acquired" this right by his cross. The Father has given "all judgement to the Son".<sup>586</sup> Yet the Son did not come to judge, but to save and to give [[life#Index|the life]] he has in himself.<sup>587</sup> By rejecting grace in this life, one already judges oneself, receives according to one's works, and can even condemn oneself for all [[eternity#Index|eternity]] by rejecting the Spirit of love.<sup>588</sup> ^ccc-679
