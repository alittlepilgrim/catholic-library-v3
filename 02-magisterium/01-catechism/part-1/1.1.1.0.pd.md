# Chapter 1. Man's Capacity for God – In Brief

44 [[man#Index|Man]] is by nature and [[vocation#Index|vocation]] a religious being. Coming from God, going toward God, man lives a fully human life only if he freely lives by his bond with God. ^ccc-44

45 [[man#Index|Man]] is made to live in [[communion#Index|communion]] with God in whom he finds happiness: When I am completely united to you, there will be no more sorrow or trials; entirely full of you, my life will be complete (St. Augustine, Conf. 10, 28, 39: PL 32, 795}. ^ccc-45

46 When he listens to the message of creation and to the voice of conscience, [[man#Index|man]] can arrive at certainty about the existence of God, the cause and the end of everything. ^ccc-46

47 The Church teaches that the one true God, our Creator and Lord, can be known with certainty from his works, by the natural [[light#Index|light]] of human [[reason#Index|reason]] (cf. Vatican Council I, can. 2 # 1: DS 3026), ^ccc-47

48 We really can name God, starting from the manifold perfections of his creatures, which are likenesses of the infinitely perfect God, even if our limited language cannot exhaust the mystery. ^ccc-48

49 Without the Creator, the [[creature#Index|creature]] vanishes (GS 36). This is the reason why believers know that the love of Christ urges them to bring the light of the living God to those who do not know him or who reject him. ^ccc-49
