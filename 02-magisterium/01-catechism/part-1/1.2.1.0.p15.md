# Chapter 1. I Believe in God the Father

**198** Our profession of faith begins with [[god|God]], for God is the First and the Last,<sup>1</sup> The beginning and [[end#Index|the end]] of everything. the Credo begins with God [[father#Index|the Father]], for the Father is the first divine person of the Most Holy Trinity; our Creed begins with the creation of [[heaven#Index|heaven]] and [[earth#Index|earth]], for creation is the beginning and the foundation of all God's [[works#Index|works]]. ^ccc-198

---

**[1]** Cf. [[isaiah-44#^isaiah-44-6|Is 44:6.]]
