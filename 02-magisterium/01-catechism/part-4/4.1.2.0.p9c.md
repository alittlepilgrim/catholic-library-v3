# Chapter 2. The Tradition of Prayer

2650 Prayer cannot be reduced to the spontaneous outpouring of interior impulse: in order to pray, one must have the will to pray. Nor is it enough to know what the Scriptures reveal about prayer: one must also learn how to pray. Through a living transmission (Sacred Tradition) within "the believing and praying Church," <sup>1</sup> [[holy-spirit#Index|The Holy Spirit]] teaches [[children-of-god#Index|the children of God]] how to pray. ^ccc-2650

2651 [[tradition#Index|The tradition]] of Christian prayer is one of the ways in which the tradition of faith takes shape and grows, especially through the contemplation and study of believers who treasure in their hearts the events and words of the economy of salvation, and through their profound grasp of the spiritual realities they experience.<sup>2</sup> ^ccc-2651
