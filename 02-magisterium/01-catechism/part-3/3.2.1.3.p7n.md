# I. The Sabbath Day

2168 The third [[commandment#Index|commandment]] of the Decalogue recalls the holiness of [[sabbath#Index|the sabbath]]: "The seventh [[day#Index|day]] is a sabbath of solemn [[rest#Index|rest]], holy to the LORD."<sup>92</sup> ^ccc-2168

2169 In speaking of the sabbath Scripture recalls [[creation#Index|creation]]: "For in six days the LORD made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and rested the seventh [[day#Index|day]]; therefore the Lord blessed the sabbath day and hallowed it."<sup>93</sup> ^ccc-2169

2170 Scripture also reveals in the Lord's [[day#Index|day]] a [[memorial#Index|memorial]] of [[israel#Index|Israel]]'s [[liberation#Index|liberation]] from bondage in Egypt: "You shall remember that you were a servant in the land of Egypt, and the LORD your God brought you out thence with mighty hand and outstretched arm; therefore the LORD your God commanded you to keep the sabbath day."<sup>94</sup> ^ccc-2170

2171 God entrusted the sabbath to [[israel#Index|Israel]] to keep as a sign of the irrevocable [[covenant#Index|covenant]].<sup>95</sup> The sabbath is for the Lord, holy and set apart for [[praise#Index|the praise]] of God, his work of creation, and his saving actions on behalf of Israel. ^ccc-2171

2172 God's action is the model for human action. If God "rested and was refreshed" on the seventh day, man too ought to "rest" and should let others, especially the poor, "be refreshed."<sup>96</sup> The sabbath brings everyday [[work#Index|work]] to a halt and provides a respite. It is a day of protest against the servitude of work and the worship of [[money#Index|money]].<sup>97</sup> ^ccc-2172

2173 The Gospel reports many incidents when Jesus was accused of violating [[sabbath#Index|the sabbath]] law. But Jesus never fails to respect the holiness of this day.<sup>98</sup> He gives this law its authentic and authoritative [[interpretation#Index|interpretation]]: "The sabbath was made for man, not man for the sabbath."<sup>99</sup> With compassion, Christ declares the sabbath for doing good rather than harm, for saving life rather than killing.<sup>100</sup> The sabbath is [[day#Index|the day]] of the Lord of mercies and a day to honor God.<sup>101</sup> "The Son of Man is lord even of the sabbath."<sup>102</sup> ^ccc-2173
