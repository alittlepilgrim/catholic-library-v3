# IV. The Family and the Kingdom

2232 [[family#Index|Family]] ties are important but not absolute. Just as the child grows to maturity and human and spiritual [[autonomy#Index|autonomy]], so his unique [[vocation#Index|vocation]] which comes from God asserts itself more clearly and forcefully. [[parents#Index|Parents]] should respect this [[call#Index|call]] and encourage their [[children#Index|children]] to follow it. They must be convinced that the first vocation of the Christian is to follow Jesus: "He who loves father or mother more than me is not worthy of me; and he who loves son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me."<sup>39</sup> ^ccc-2232

2233 Becoming a disciple of Jesus means [[acceptance-accepting#Index|accepting]] the invitation to belong to God's [[family#Index|family]], to live in conformity with His way of life: "For whoever does the will of my Father in heaven is my [[brother#Index|brother]], and sister, and mother."<sup>40</sup> ^ccc-2233

Parents should welcome and respect with joy and thanksgiving the Lord's call to one of their children to follow him in virginity for the sake of the Kingdom in the consecrated life or in priestly ministry.
