# Article 1. The First Commandment – In Brief

*2133 "You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, and with all your soul and with all your strength" ([[deuteronomy-06#^deuteronomy-06-5|Deut 6:5]]).* ^ccc-2133

*2134 The first commandment summons man to believe in God, to hope in him, and to love him above all else.* ^ccc-2134

*2135 "You shall worship the Lord your God" ([[matthew-04#^matthew-04-10|Mt 4:10]]). Adoring God, praying to him, offering him [[worship#Index|the worship]] that belongs to him, fulfilling the promises and vows made to him are acts of the virtue of [[religion|religion]] which fall under obedience to the first commandment.* ^ccc-2135

*2136 The duty to offer God authentic worship concerns man both as an individual and as a social being.* ^ccc-2136

*2137 "Men of the present day want to profess their [[religion#Index|religion]] freely in private and in public" (DH 15).* ^ccc-2137

*2138 [[superstition#Index|Superstition]] is a departure from [[worship#Index|the worship]] that we give to the true God. It is manifested in [[idolatry-idol#Index|idolatry]], as well as in various forms of [[divination#Index|divination]] and magic.* ^ccc-2138

*2139 Tempting God in words or deeds, [[sacrilege#Index|sacrilege]], and simony are sins of [[irreligion#Index|irreligion]] forbidden by the first commandment.* ^ccc-2139

*2140 Since it rejects or denies the existence of God, [[atheism#Index|atheism]] is a sin against the first commandment.* ^ccc-2140

*2141 The veneration of sacred images is based on the mystery of the Incarnation of the Word of God. It is not contrary to the first [[commandment#Index|commandment]].* ^ccc-2141
