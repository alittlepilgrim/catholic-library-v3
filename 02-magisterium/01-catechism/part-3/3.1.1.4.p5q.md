# Article 4. The Morality of Human Acts

1749 Freedom makes [[man#Index|man]] a moral subject. When he acts deliberately, man is, so to speak, the father of his acts. Human acts, that is, acts that are freely chosen in consequence of a judgment of conscience, can be morally evaluated. They are either good or [[evil#Index|evil]]. ^ccc-1749
