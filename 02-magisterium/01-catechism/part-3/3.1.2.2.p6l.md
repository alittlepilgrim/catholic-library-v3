# III. Responsibility and Participation

1913 "Participation" is the voluntary and generous engagement of a [[person#Index|person]] in social interchange. It is necessary that all participate, each according to his position and role, in promoting [[common-good#Index|the common good]]. This obligation is inherent in the dignity of the human person. ^ccc-1913

1914 Participation is achieved first of all by taking charge of the areas for which one assumes personal responsibility: by the care taken for the education of his [[family#Index|family]], by conscientious [[work#Index|work]], and so forth, man participates in the good of others and of society.<sup>31</sup> ^ccc-1914

1915 As far as possible citizens should take an active part in public life. The manner of this participation may vary from one country or [[culture#Index|culture]] to another. "One must pay tribute to those nations whose systems permit the largest possible number of [[citizens#Index|the citizens]] to take part in public life in a climate of genuine freedom."<sup>32</sup> ^ccc-1915

1916 As with any ethical obligation, the participation of all in realizing [[common-good#Index|the common good]] calls for a continually renewed conversion of the social partners. [[fraud#Index|Fraud]] and other subterfuges, by which some people evade the constraints of the law and the prescriptions of societal obligation, must be firmly condemned because they are incompatible with the requirements of [[justice#Index|justice]]. Much care should be taken to promote [[institutions#Index|institutions]] that improve the conditions of human life.<sup>33</sup> ^ccc-1916

1917 It is incumbent on those who exercise [[authority#Index|authority]] to strengthen the values that inspire the confidence of the members of the group and encourage them to put themselves at the service of others. Participation begins with [[education#Index|education]] and [[culture#Index|culture]]. "One is entitled to think that [[future#Index|the future]] of [[humanity#Index|humanity]] is in the hands of those who are capable of providing the generations to come with reasons for [[life-earthly#Index|life]] and optimism."<sup>34</sup> ^ccc-1917
