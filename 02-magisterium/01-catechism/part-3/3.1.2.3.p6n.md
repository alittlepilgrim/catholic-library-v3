# Article 3. Social Justice

1928 [[society#Index|Society]] ensures [[social-justice|social justice]] when it provides the conditions that allow [[associations#Index|associations]] or individuals to obtain what is their due, according to their nature and their vocation. Social [[justice#Index|justice]] is linked to [[common-good#Index|the common good]] and the exercise of [[authority#Index|authority]]. ^ccc-1928
