# Article 2. Our Vocation to Beatitude – In Brief

*1725 The Beatitudes take up and fulfill God's promises from [[abraham|Abraham]] on by ordering them to the Kingdom of [[heaven#Index|heaven]]. They respond to the desire for happiness that God has placed in the human [[heart#Index|heart]].* ^ccc-1725

*1726 The Beatitudes teach us the final end to which God calls us: the Kingdom, the vision of God, [[participation#Index|participation]] in the divine nature, eternal life, filiation, rest in God.* ^ccc-1726

*1727 [[beatitude#Index|The beatitude]] of eternal life is a gratuitous gift of God. It is [[supernatural#Index|supernatural]], as is the grace that leads us there.* ^ccc-1727

*1728 The Beatitudes confront us with decisive choices concerning earthly [[goods#Index|goods]]; they purify our hearts in order to teach us to love God above all things.* ^ccc-1728

*1729 The beatitude of [[heaven#Index|heaven]] sets the standards for discernment in the use of earthly [[goods#Index|goods]] in keeping with the law of God.* ^ccc-1729
