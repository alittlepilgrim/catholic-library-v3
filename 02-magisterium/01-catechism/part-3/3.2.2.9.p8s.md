# Article 9. The Ninth Commandment – In Brief

^ccc-3

*2528 "Everyone who looks at a woman lustfully has already committed [[adultery]] with her in his [[heart#Index|heart]]" ([[matthew-05#^matthew-05-28|Mt 5:28]]).* ^ccc-2528

*2529 The ninth commandment warns against lust or carnal [[concupiscence#Index|concupiscence]].* ^ccc-2529

*2530 The struggle against carnal lust involves purifying [[heart#Index|the heart]] and practicing temperance.* ^ccc-2530

*2531 [[purity#Index|Purity]] of heart will enable us to see God: it enables us even now to see things according to God.* ^ccc-2531

*2532 [[purification#Index|Purification]] of the heart demands prayer, the practice of chastity, [[purity#Index|purity]] of intention and of vision.* ^ccc-2532

*2533 [[purity#Index|Purity]] of [[heart#Index|heart]] requires [[modesty#Index|the modesty]] which is patience, decency, and discretion. Modesty protects the intimate center of the person.* ^ccc-2533
