# Article 2. Participation in Social Life – In Brief

*1918 "There is no [[authority#Index|authority]] except from God, and those authorities that exist have been instituted by God" ([[romans-13#^romans-13-1|Rom 13:1]]).* ^ccc-1918

*1919 Every human [[community#Index|community]] needs an [[authority#Index|authority]] in order to endure and develop.* ^ccc-1919

*1920 "The political community and public [[authority#Index|authority]] are based on human nature and therefore. . . belong to an order established by God" (GS 74 # 3).* ^ccc-1920

*1921 [[authority#Index|Authority]] is exercised legitimately if it is committed to [[common-good#Index|the common good]] of [[society#Index|society]]. To attain this it must employ morally acceptable means.* ^ccc-1921

*1922 The diversity of political regimes is legitimate, provided they contribute to the good of [[community#Index|the community]].* ^ccc-1922

*1923 [[political-authority-community#Index|Political authority]] must be exercised within the limits of the moral order and must guarantee the conditions for the exercise of freedom.* ^ccc-1923

*1924 [[common-good#Index|The common good]] comprises "the sum total of social conditions which allow people, either as groups or as individuals, to reach their fulfillment more fully and more easily" (GS 26 1).* ^ccc-1924

*1925 [[common-good#Index|The common good]] consists of three essential elements: respect for and promotion of the fundamental rights of the person; prosperity, or the development of the spiritual and temporal goods of society; the peace and security of the group and of its members.* ^ccc-1925

*1926 The dignity of the human [[person#Index|person]] requires the pursuit of [[common-good#Index|the common good]]. Everyone should be concerned to create and support institutions that improve the conditions of human life.* ^ccc-1926

*1927 It is the role of the state to defend and promote [[common-good#Index|the common good]] of civil [[society#Index|society]]. The common good of the whole human family calls for an organization of society on the international level.* ^ccc-1927
