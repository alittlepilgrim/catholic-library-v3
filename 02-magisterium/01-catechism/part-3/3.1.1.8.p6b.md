# III. The Different Kinds of Sins

1852 There are a great many kinds of [[sin|sins]]. Scripture provides several lists of them. The Letter to the Galatians contrasts [[works#Index|the works]] of [[flesh#Index|the flesh]] with the fruit of the Spirit: "Now the works of the flesh are plain: [[fornication#Index|fornication]], impurity, licentiousness, [[idolatry-idol#Index|idolatry]], sorcery, enmity, strife, [[jealousy#Index|jealousy]], anger, selfishness, dissension, factions, envy, drunkenness, carousing, and the like. I warn you, as I warned you before, that those who do such things shall not inherit the Kingdom of God."<sup>127</sup> ^ccc-1852

1853 Sins can be distinguished according to their objects, as can every [[human-act#Index|human act]]; or according to the virtues they oppose, by excess or defect; or according to the commandments they violate. They can also be classed according to whether they concern God, neighbor, or oneself; they can be divided into spiritual and carnal sins, or again as sins in thought, word, deed, or [[omission#Index|omission]]. The root of [[sin#Glossary|sin]] is in [[heart#Index|the heart]] of man, in his free will, according to the teaching of the Lord: "For out of the heart come evil thoughts, murder, [[adultery]], fornication, theft, false witness, slander. These are what defile a man."<sup>128</sup> But in the heart also resides charity, the source of the good and pure [[works#Index|works]], which sin wounds. ^ccc-1853
