# Article 7. The Virtues – In Brief

*1833 Virtue is a habitual and firm disposition to do [[good#Index|good]].* ^ccc-1833

*1834 The human virtues are stable dispositions of the intellect and the will that govern our acts, order our passions, and guide our conduct in accordance with reason and faith. They can be grouped around the four [[cardinal-virtues|cardinal virtues]]: prudence, justice, fortitude, and temperance.* ^ccc-1834

*1835 [[prudence#Index|Prudence]] disposes the practical reason to discern, in every circumstance, our true [[good#Index|good]] and to choose the right means for achieving it.* ^ccc-1835

*1836 Justice consists in the firm and constant will to give God and [[neighbor#Index|neighbor]] their due.* ^ccc-1836

*1837 Fortitude ensures firmness in difficulties and constancy in the pursuit of [[good#Index|the good]].* ^ccc-1837

*1838 [[temperance#Index|Temperance]] moderates the attraction of the pleasures of the senses and provides balance in the use of created [[goods#Index|goods]].* ^ccc-1838

*1839 The moral virtues grow through [[education#Index|education]], deliberate acts, and perseverance in struggle. Divine grace purifies and elevates them.* ^ccc-1839

*1840 The theological virtues dispose Christians to live in a relationship with the Holy Trinity. They have God for their origin, their motive, and their object - God known by faith, God hoped in and loved for his own sake.* ^ccc-1840

*1841 There are three theological virtues: faith, hope, and charity. They inform all the moral virtues and give life to them.* ^ccc-1841

*1842 By faith, we believe in God and believe all that he has revealed to us and that Holy Church proposes for our belief.* ^ccc-1842

*1843 By hope we desire, and with steadfast trust await from God, eternal life and the graces to merit it.* ^ccc-1843

*1844 By charity, we love God above all things and our [[neighbor#Index|neighbor]] as ourselves for love of God. Charity, the form of all the virtues, "binds everything together in perfect harmony" ([[colossians-03#^colossians-03-14|Col 3:14]]).* ^ccc-1844

*1845 The seven gifts of the Holy Spirit bestowed upon Christians are wisdom, understanding, counsel, fortitude, knowledge, piety, and fear of the Lord.* ^ccc-1845
