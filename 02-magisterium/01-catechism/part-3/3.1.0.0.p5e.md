# Section 1. Man's Vocation Life in the Spirit

1699 [[life-earthly#Index|Life]] in the Holy Spirit fulfills [[vocation#Index|the vocation]] of [[man#Index|man]] (chapter one). This [[life#Index|life]] is made up of divine charity and human solidarity (chapter two). It is graciously offered as salvation (chapter three). ^ccc-1699
