# Article 3. The Church, Mother and Teacher

2030 It is in the Church, in communion with all the baptized, that [[christian#Index|the Christian]] fulfills his [[vocation#Index|vocation]]. From the Church he receives the Word of God containing the teachings of "the law of Christ."<sup>72</sup> From the Church he receives the grace of [[sacrament#Index|the sacraments]] that sustains him on the "way." From the Church he learns the example of [[holiness#Index|holiness]] and recognizes its model and source in the all-holy Virgin [[mary#Index|Mary]]; he discerns it in the authentic witness of those who live it; he discovers it in the spiritual tradition and long history of the saints who have gone before him and whom the liturgy celebrates in the rhythms of the sanctoral cycle. ^ccc-2030

2031 The moral [[life-earthly#Index|life]] is spiritual [[worship#Index|worship]]. We "present (our) bodies as a living [[sacrifice#Index|sacrifice]], holy and acceptable to God,"<sup>73</sup> within the Body of Christ that we form and in communion with [[offering#Index|the offering]] of his Eucharist. In the liturgy and the celebration of the sacraments, prayer and teaching are conjoined with the grace of Christ to enlighten and nourish Christian activity. As does the whole of the Christian [[life#Index|life]], the moral life finds its source and summit in the Eucharistic sacrifice. ^ccc-2031
