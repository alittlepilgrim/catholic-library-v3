# Article 1. The Person and Society – In Brief

*1890 There is a certain resemblance between the union of the divine persons and the fraternity that men ought to establish among themselves.* ^ccc-1890

*1891 The human person needs [[life-earthly#Index|life]] in [[society#Index|society]] in order to develop in accordance with his nature. Certain societies, such as the family and the state, correspond more directly to [[nature#Index|the nature]] of man.* ^ccc-1891

*1892 "The human person. . . is and ought to be the principle, the subject, and the object of every social organization" (GS 25 # 1).* ^ccc-1892

*1893 Widespread participation in voluntary [[associations#Index|associations]] and institutions is to be encouraged.* ^ccc-1893

*1894 In accordance with the principle of [[subsidiarity#Index|subsidiarity]], neither the state nor any larger society should substitute itself for the initiative and responsibility of individuals and intermediary bodies.* ^ccc-1894

*1895 [[society#Index|Society]] ought to promote the exercise of virtue, not obstruct it. It should be animated by a just [[hierarchy#Index|hierarchy]] of values.* ^ccc-1895

*1896 Where sin has perverted the social climate, it is necessary to call for [[conversion#Index|the conversion]] of hearts and appeal to the grace of God. Charity urges just reforms. There is no solution to the social question apart from the Gospel (cf CA 3, 5).* ^ccc-1896
