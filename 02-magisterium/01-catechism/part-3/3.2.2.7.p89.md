# Article 7. The Seventh Commandment

You shall not steal.<sup>185</sup>

2401 The seventh [[commandment#Index|commandment]] forbids unjustly taking or keeping [[goods#Index|the goods]] of one's [[neighbor#Index|neighbor]] and wronging him in any way with respect to his goods. It commands [[justice#Index|justice]] and charity in the care of earthly goods and the fruits of men's labor. For the sake of [[common-good#Index|the common good]], it requires respect for the universal destination of goods and respect for the [[right#Index|right]] to private property. Christian life strives to order this world's goods to God and to fraternal charity. ^ccc-2401
