# Chapter 3. God's Salvation Law and Grace

1949 Called to [[beatitude#Index|beatitude]] but wounded by [[sin#Index|sin]], [[man#Index|man]] stands in need of [[salvation#Index|salvation]] from God. Divine help comes to him in Christ through the law that guides him and the grace that sustains him: ^ccc-1949

Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling; for God is at work in you, both to will and to work for his good pleasure.<sup>1</sup>
