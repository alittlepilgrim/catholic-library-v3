# Article 5. The Fifth Commandment

You shall not kill.<sup>54</sup>

You have heard that it was said to the men of old, "You shall not kill: and whoever kills shall be liable to judgment." But I say to you that every one who is angry with his brother shall be liable to judgment.<sup>55</sup>

2258 "Human [[life-earthly#Index|life]] is sacred because from its beginning it involves the creative [[action-acting#Index|action]] of God and it remains for ever in a special relationship with the Creator, who is its sole end. God alone is the Lord of [[life#Index|life]] from its beginning until its end: no one can under any circumstance claim for himself the right directly to destroy an innocent human being."<sup>56</sup> ^ccc-2258

---

**[56]** CDF, instruction, Donum vitae, intro. 5.
