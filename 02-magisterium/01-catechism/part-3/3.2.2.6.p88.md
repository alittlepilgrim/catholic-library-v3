# Article 6. The Sixth Commandment – In Brief

^ccc-3

*2392 "Love is the fundamental and innate [[vocation#Index|vocation]] of every human being" (FC 11).* ^ccc-2392

*2393 By creating the human being [[man#Index|man]] and [[woman#Index|woman]], God gives personal dignity equally to the one and the other. Each of them, man and woman, should acknowledge and accept his sexual identity.* ^ccc-2393

*2394 Christ is the model of chastity. Every baptized person is called to lead a chaste life, each according to his particular state of life.* ^ccc-2394

*2395 Chastity means the integration of [[sexuality#Index|sexuality]] within the person. It includes an apprenticeship in self-mastery.* ^ccc-2395

*2396 Among the sins gravely contrary to chastity are masturbation, fornication, [[pornography#Index|pornography]], and homosexual practices.* ^ccc-2396

*2397 The covenant which spouses have freely entered into entails faithful love. It imposes on them the obligation to keep their marriage indissoluble.* ^ccc-2397

*2398 [[fecundity#Index|Fecundity]] is a good, a gift and an end of marriage. By giving [[life-earthly#Index|life]], spouses participate in God's fatherhood.* ^ccc-2398

*2399 [[regulation-of-births#Index|The regulation of births]] represents one of the aspects of responsible fatherhood and motherhood. Legitimate intentions on the part of the spouses do not justify recourse to morally unacceptable means (for example, direct [[sterilization#Index|sterilization]] or [[contraception#Index|contraception]]).* ^ccc-2399

*2400 [[adultery]], [[divorce#Index|divorce]], polygamy, and free union are grave offenses against the dignity of marriage.* ^ccc-2400
