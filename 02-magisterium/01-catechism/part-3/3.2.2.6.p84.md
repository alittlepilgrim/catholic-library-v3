# I. Male and Female He Created Them

2331 "God is love and in himself he lives a mystery of personal loving [[communion#Index|communion]]. Creating the human race in his own image. . . . God inscribed in the humanity of [[man#Index|man]] and [[woman#Index|woman]] [[vocation#Index|the vocation]], and thus the capacity and responsibility, of love and communion."<sup>114</sup> ^ccc-2331

"God created man in his own image. . . male and female he created them";<sup>115</sup> He blessed them and said, "Be fruitful and multiply";<sup>116</sup> "When God created man, he made him in the likeness of God. Male and female he created them, and he blessed them and named them Man when they were created."<sup>117</sup>

2332 [[sexuality#Index|Sexuality]] affects all aspects of the human [[person#Index|person]] in the unity of his body and [[soul#Index|soul]]. It especially concerns affectivity, the capacity to love and to procreate, and in a more general way the aptitude for forming bonds of [[communion#Index|communion]] with others. ^ccc-2332

2333 Everyone, man and [[woman#Index|woman]], should acknowledge and accept his sexual [[identity#Index|identity]]. Physical, moral, and spiritual difference and complementarity are oriented toward [[goods#Index|the goods]] of marriage and the flourishing of family life. [[harmony#Index|The harmony]] of the couple and of society depends in part on the way in which the complementarity, needs, and mutual support between the sexes are lived out. ^ccc-2333

2334 "In creating men 'male and female,' God gives [[man#Index|man]] and [[woman#Index|woman]] an equal personal [[dignity#Index|dignity]]."<sup>118</sup> "Man is a person, man and woman equally so, since both were created in the image and likeness of the personal God."<sup>119</sup> ^ccc-2334

2335 Each of the two sexes is an image of the power and [[tenderness#Index|tenderness]] of God, with equal dignity though in a different way. [[union#Index|The union]] of man and [[woman#Index|woman]] in marriage is a way of imitating in the flesh the Creator's generosity and [[fecundity#Index|fecundity]]: "Therefore a man leaves his father and his mother and cleaves to his wife, and they become one flesh."<sup>120</sup> All human generations proceed from this union.<sup>121</sup> ^ccc-2335

2336 Jesus came to restore creation to the purity of its origins. In [[sermon-on-the-mount#Index|the Sermon on the Mount]], he interprets God's plan strictly: "You have heard that it was said, 'You shall not commit [[adultery|adultery]].' But I say to you that every one who looks at a [[woman#Index|woman]] lustfully has already committed adultery with her in his [[heart#Index|heart]]."<sup>122</sup> What God has joined together, let not man put asunder.<sup>123</sup> ^ccc-2336

The tradition of the Church has understood the sixth commandment as encompassing the whole of human sexuality.
