# Article 3. Social Justice – In Brief

*1943 [[society#Index|Society]] ensures social justice by providing the conditions that allow [[associations#Index|associations]] and individuals to obtain their due.* ^ccc-1943

*1944 [[respect#Index|Respect]] for the human [[person#Index|person]] considers the other "another self." It presupposes respect for the fundamental rights that flow from the dignity intrinsic of the person.* ^ccc-1944

*1945 The equality of men concerns their dignity as persons and the rights that flow from it.* ^ccc-1945

*1946 The differences among persons belong to God's plan, who wills that we should need one another. These differences should encourage charity.* ^ccc-1946

*1947 The equal dignity of human persons requires the effort to reduce excessive social and economic inequalities. It gives urgency to the elimination of sinful inequalities.* ^ccc-1947

*1948 [[solidarity#Index|Solidarity]] is an eminently Christian virtue. It practices the sharing of spiritual [[goods#Index|goods]] even more than material ones.* ^ccc-1948
