# I. The Universal Destination and the Private Ownership of Goods

2402 In the beginning God entrusted [[earth#Index|the earth]] and its resources to the common stewardship of mankind to take care of them, master them by labor, and enjoy their fruits.<sup>186</sup> [[goods#Index|The goods]] of [[creation#Index|creation]] are destined for the whole [[human-race#Index|human race]]. However, the earth is divided up among men to assure the security of their lives, endangered by poverty and threatened by violence. The appropriation of property is legitimate for guaranteeing [[freedom#Index|the freedom]] and [[dignity#Index|dignity]] of persons and for helping each of them to meet his basic needs and the needs of those in his charge. It should allow for a natural [[solidarity#Index|solidarity]] to develop between men. ^ccc-2402

2403 The [[right#Index|right]] to private property, acquired by work or received from others by inheritance or gift, does not do away with the original gift of [[earth#Index|the earth]] to the whole of mankind. The universal destination of [[goods#Index|goods]] remains primordial, even if the promotion of [[common-good#Index|the common good]] requires [[respect#Index|respect]] for the right to private property and its exercise. ^ccc-2403

2404 "In his use of things man should regard the external [[goods#Index|goods]] he legitimately owns not merely as exclusive to himself but common to others also, in the sense that they can benefit others as well as himself."<sup>187</sup> The ownership of any property makes its holder a steward of Providence, with the task of making it fruitful and communicating its benefits to others, first of all his family. ^ccc-2404

2405 [[goods#Index|Goods]] of production - material or immaterial - such as land, factories, practical or artistic skills, oblige their possessors to employ them in ways that will benefit the greatest number. Those who hold goods for use and consumption should use them with moderation, reserving the better part for guests, for [[sick#Index|the sick]] and the poor. ^ccc-2405

2406 Political [[authority#Index|authority]] has the [[right#Index|right]] and [[duty#Index|duty]] to regulate the legitimate exercise of the right to ownership for the sake of [[common-good#Index|the common good]].<sup>188</sup> ^ccc-2406
