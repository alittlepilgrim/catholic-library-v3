# Article 6. Moral Conscience

1776 "Deep within his conscience man discovers a law which he has not laid upon himself but which he must obey. Its voice, ever calling him to love and to do what is [[good#Index|good]] and to avoid evil, sounds in his [[heart#Index|heart]] at the right moment. . . . For man has in his heart a law inscribed by God. . . . His conscience is man's most secret core and his sanctuary. There he is alone with God whose voice echoes in his depths."<sup>47</sup> ^ccc-1776
