# Chapter 2. The Human Communion

1877 [[vocation#Index|The vocation]] of humanity is to show forth the image of God and to be transformed into the image of the Father's only Son. This vocation takes a personal form since each of us is called to enter into the divine beatitude; it also concerns the human [[community#Index|community]] as a whole. ^ccc-1877
