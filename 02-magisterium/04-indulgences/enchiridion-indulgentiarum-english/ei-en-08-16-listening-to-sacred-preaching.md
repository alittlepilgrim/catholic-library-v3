# 16 Listening to Sacred Preaching (Concession Sixteen)

A plenary indulgence is granted to the faithful who on the occasion of a mission have heard some of the sermons and are present for the solemn conclusion of the mission. A partial indulgence is granted to the faithful who assist with attention and devotion at other occasions of the preaching of the Word of God.
