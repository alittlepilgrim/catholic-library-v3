# 27 A Priest's First Mass and Jubilee Celebrations of Ordination (Concession Twenty-Seven)

## Section One

A plenary indulgence is granted to

1° a priest celebrating his first Mass before the people on a chosen day;

2° the faithful who devoutly assist at such a Mass.

## Section Two

Similarly, a plenary indulgence is granted to

1° priests celebrating the twenty-fifth, fiftieth, sixtieth, and seventieth anniversary of their priestly ordinations, who renew before God their promise of faithfully fulfilling the duties of their vocation;

2° bishops celebrating the twenty-fifth, fortieth, and fiftieth anniversaries of their episcopal ordination, who renew before God their promise of faithfully fulfilling the duties of their office;

3° the faithful who devoutly assist at jubilee Mass celebrations.
