# Spiritual Childhood

**Hub.** St Thérèse of Lisieux's *little way* — the path to holiness she found for "little souls" who cannot scale the heights of the great saints. Instead of climbing, the soul consents to *remain little*: aware of its own weakness and poverty, it abandons itself with total confidence into the arms of God as a child into its father's, expecting everything from His merciful love rather than from its own efforts. "Whoever is a LITTLE ONE, let him come to me" (Prov 9:4). Its marks are littleness embraced, unwavering confidence, and merciful love received and given.
