# Love of God

**Hub.** The soul's answering love for God who first loved us — willed, not felt, and perfected in full conformity to His will. Not a synonym for charity but its God-ward half: charity is love of God and love of neighbor together, inseparable ([[257-fraternal-charity#Meditation 1|DI 257,1]]).
