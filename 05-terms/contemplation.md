# Contemplation

**Hub.** A form of wordless prayer in which mind and heart focus on God's greatness and goodness in affective, loving adoration — a "gaze of faith" fixed on Jesus ([[4.1.1.3.p96#^ccc-2628|CCC §2628]]; [[4.1.3.1.p9m#^ccc-2715|§2715]]). For St. Teresa, a prayer that cannot be acquired: the Lord himself puts the soul at peace by his presence.
