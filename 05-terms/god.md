# God

God is the eternal, infinite source of all being: the One who is, Father, Son, and Holy Spirit ([[1.2.1.0.p15|CCC §198]], [[1.2.1.1.p19|CCC §279]]). Every prayer is addressed to him, and every path through the interior life leads toward union with him.
