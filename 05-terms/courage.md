# Courage

The cardinal virtue of fortitude — the moral strength to persevere in doing good and to face fears and trials for the sake of fidelity to God (CCC synonym: [[fortitude|Fortitude]], [[3.1.1.7.p65#^ccc-1808|§1808]]). In the Carmelite way, the courage to face anything to be faithful to God; one *becomes* courageous by the repeated practice of courageous acts.
