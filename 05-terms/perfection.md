# Perfection

**Hub.** The fulfillment of the call to holiness — not flawless performance but the soul's union with God through charity conformed to His will. Its root is a living, experiential knowledge of Creator and creature; its path is prayer and detachment, by which "the Lord then infuses the virtues." It admits of every state of life: giving our wills to God and forgiving others are elements everyone can practice — "we must do what we can, the Lord receives everything."
