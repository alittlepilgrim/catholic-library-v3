# Spiritual Consolations

**Hub.** Sensible sweetness or contentment in prayer — Teresa's _contentos_ — acquired partly through our own acts of devotion and distinguished from the infused _gustos_ ([[f2b-session-05|Formation 2b, Session 5]]). A help that serves God with greater generosity and strength for the cross, never an end in itself; the soul must learn to love God with a pure act of the will above every feeling and consolation.
