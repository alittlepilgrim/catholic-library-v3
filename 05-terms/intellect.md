# Intellect

**Hub.** The soul's power of knowing, reasoning, and grasping truth — Teresa's "thinking power," also called _reason_ (the "understanding" faculty); one of the three faculties (with memory and will). In prayer the intellect is quieted so that, in contemplation, the soul understands it is close to God in a way wholly unlike knowing through the exterior senses ([[tj-way-ccel-ch-31|Way 31.2]]).
