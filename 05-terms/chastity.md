## The Carmelite Order

### OCDS Vocabulary

![[08-vocabulary-ocds#^ocds-ai-chastity]]

Chastity, as the Secular Carmelite promises to live it, is the freedom to love God above all else and one's neighbor with God's own love—a commitment in personal and social dimensions, whatever one's state of life.
