# Union with God

**Hub.** The goal of the spiritual journey — "a union of likeness through love" ([[f2a-session-09|Formation 2a, Session 9]]) in which the soul is wholly transformed in the Will of God so that his will becomes her own. Attained not in the sweetness of prayer but in the full embrace of God's will, in the measure of charity, purity, and detachment; consummated in spiritual marriage.
