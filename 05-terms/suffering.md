# Suffering

**Hub.** Trial or pain that, offered in union with the Cross of Christ, becomes redemptive — "in my flesh I am filling up what is lacking in the afflictions of Christ on behalf of his body, which is the church" ([[colossians-01#^colossians-01-24|Col 1:24]]). In the Carmelite way, suffering accepted from God's hand is a means of sanctification and, in its passive form, necessary for union with God — the night of the spirit that purifies love.
