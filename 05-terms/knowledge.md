# Knowledge

The CCC Index identifies knowledge in relation to awareness of sin; created and uncreated reality; creation as God's gift; faith and the catechism; good and evil; man's knowledge of Christ and of God; and knowledge of the truth. See the [CCC Index, p. 813](https://assets.alittlepilgrim.org/pdf/ccc/catechism-index.pdf).
