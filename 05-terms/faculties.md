# Faculties

**Hub.** The powers of the soul by which the person knows and loves — the three spiritual faculties of **memory, intellect, and will**, together with the external and internal senses through which we function at the level of sensory creation. The whole spiritual life is the ordering of these powers and, in the higher degrees of prayer, their recollection and suspension, so that God may possess and act in them directly.
