# Humility

Humility acknowledges God as the author of all good and our complete dependence on him ([[4.1.0.0.p8z#^ccc-2559|CCC §2559]], [[3.2.2.10.p8w#^ccc-2546|§2546]]). Saint Teresa calls humility walking in truth ([[tj-ic-ics-06.10#^tj-ic-ics-6-10-7|Interior Castle 6.10.7]]); the Catechism calls it the foundation of prayer ([[4.1.0.0.p8z#^ccc-2559|CCC §2559]]).
