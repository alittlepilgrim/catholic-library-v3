## The Carmelite Order

### OCDS Vocabulary

![[08-vocabulary-ocds#^ocds-ai-poverty]]

Evangelical poverty, as promised in the Secular Order, is the wealth of generosity, self-denial, and interior liberty in dependence on Christ, who "though rich, became poor." It embraces an evangelical use of goods and talents, a commitment to justice, and the confident surrender of personal limitations to God.
