## The Carmelite Order

### OCDS Vocabulary

![[08-vocabulary-ocds#^ocds-ai-self-knowledge]]

Teresa makes self-knowledge the room the soul never leaves: throughout the dwelling places she insists the knowledge of self and the knowledge of God grow together. John teaches that it flows first from the dry night, and from it proceeds the knowledge of God.
