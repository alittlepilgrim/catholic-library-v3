# Charity

Charity is the greatest theological virtue: loving God above all things and our neighbor as ourselves for the love of God ([[3.1.1.7.p66#^ccc-1822|CCC §1822]]; [[3.1.1.7.p66#^ccc-1826|§1826]]). Saint Teresa makes this the measure of prayer—not to think much, but to love much.
