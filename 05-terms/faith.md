# Faith

Faith is God's gift and the soul's free assent to him, the first of the theological virtues ([[3.1.1.7.p66#^ccc-1814|CCC §1814]]; cf. [[1.1.3.0.pu#^ccc-142|§142]], [[1.1.3.1.pw#^ccc-150|§150]]). Saint John of the Cross presents the night of faith as the road by which the soul is led toward union with God.

## St. Teresa of Ávila

### Glossary

![[tj-way-ics-07-glossary#Faith]]

Faith is a gift God pours into the intellect, by which we know and give assent to all that God revealed to us about himself and about the means by which we can reach him. Through trials that test and perfect it, faith brings the soul into an ever-deeper likeness to Christ.
