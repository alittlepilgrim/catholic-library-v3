## The Carmelite Order

### OCDS Vocabulary

![[08-vocabulary-ocds#^ocds-ai-beatitudes]]

The beatitudes are the Secular Carmelite's plan of action for life: the promised way of entering into relationship with the world, neighbors, family, and work, giving evangelical witness that invites others to follow Christ, "the Way, the Truth and the Life."
