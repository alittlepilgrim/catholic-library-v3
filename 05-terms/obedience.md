## The Carmelite Order

### OCDS Vocabulary

![[08-vocabulary-ocds#^ocds-ai-obedience]]

Obedience is the promise to live open to the will of God, imitating Christ, who was "obedient unto death." It is an exercise of faith that seeks God's will in the events of society and one's own life, in free cooperation with the community's council, the Provincial, and the General.
