# Sacred Scriptures

Sacred Scripture: the books which contain the truth of God's [[revelation|Revelation]] and were composed by human authors inspired by the [[holy-spirit|Holy Spirit]] ( [[1.1.2.3.pp|ccc-105]]). The Bible contains both the forty-six books of the [[old-testament|Old Testament]] and the twenty-seven books of the [[new-testament|New Testament]] ( [[1.1.2.3.pr|ccc-120]]).
