description: "Hesitation in believing or trusting. Voluntary doubt about the faith disregards or refuses what God has revealed; involuntary doubt is hesitation and difficulty with objections, which if cultivated can lead to spiritual blindness (CCC 2088). Its other faces are the disciples' doubt before the risen Jesus (CCC 644) and doubt of God's love in tempting him (CCC 2119); prudence overcomes doubt about good and evil (CCC 1806)."
