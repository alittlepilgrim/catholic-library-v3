# Death

The end of earthly life and the separation of the soul from the body, followed immediately by particular judgment — "it is appointed for men to die once, and after that comes judgment" ([[hebrews-09#^hebrews-09-27|Heb 9:27]]). Received in faith it is the "last visit of the Lord," the door that opens into eternal life; it is never sudden for one who is ready, and not feared by one who is always faithful to grace (DI 95,1).
