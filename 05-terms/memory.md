# Memory

**Hub.** One of the three spiritual [[faculties|faculties]] — the power that recalls and keeps before the mind what the soul has come to know, above all God's past blessings and the mysteries of the life of Jesus Christ. In the prayer of quiet it would be occupied with nothing but God; in the prayer of union it falls still, "asleep to the things of the world"; and its purification — the emptying of all that is contrary to God's life — is the work of hope.
