# Grace

Grace is God's free gift of his own life: sanctifying grace, actual graces, sacramental graces, and the graces proper to one's state of life ([[3.1.3.2.p6z#^ccc-1996|CCC §1996]], [[3.1.3.2.p6z#^ccc-2000|§2000]], [[3.1.3.2.p6z#^ccc-2003|§2003]]–[[3.1.3.2.p6z#^ccc-2004|2004]]). It carries the soul from its first response to God toward the fullness of union with him.
