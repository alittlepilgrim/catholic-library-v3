# Virtue

**Hub.** A habitual and firm disposition to do what is good, allowing a person to give the best of themselves ([[3.1.1.7.p64#^ccc-1803|CCC §1803]]). The moral virtues are acquired through human effort aided by God's grace; the theological virtues (faith, hope, charity) are gifts of God.

## The Carmelite Order

### Constitutions

- Arming ourselves with the practice of the virtues, as we live an intense life of faith, hope and charity — one of the founding principles of Carmelite life drawn from the Rule of St. Albert — [[04-constitutions-identity-values-commitment-wn#^ocds-c-6|C ¶6f]].
- St. Teresa of Jesus: evangelical self-denial, disposition to service, and perseverance in the practice of the virtues are a daily guide to living the spiritual life — [[04-constitutions-identity-values-commitment-wn#^ocds-c-7|C ¶7]].
- St. John of the Cross inspires the Secular Carmelite to be vigilant in the practice of faith, hope, and charity — [[04-constitutions-identity-values-commitment-wn#^ocds-c-8|C ¶8]].
