# Detachment

Freedom from every disordered affection for creatures, so the soul can give itself to "the All" without reserve. Its essence is the putting to death of disordered attachments — "a thread suffices to attach the soul to earth." Not an end in itself but the indispensable foundation of the spiritual life and the means to union: it does not leave the soul in emptiness but raises it to God. With humility, one of "two inseparable sisters" of the Carmelite way.
