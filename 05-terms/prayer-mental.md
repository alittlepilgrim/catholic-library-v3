# Mental Prayer

**Hub.** Prayer made in mind and heart rather than aloud — to speak with God knowing and aware that we are with him, understanding who we are who dare speak with so great a Lord (St. Teresa, [[tj-way-ccel-ch-25|Way 25.3]]). The genus of interior prayer that begins in discursive meditation (CCC synonym, partial: [[meditation|Meditation]], [[4.1.3.1.p9l#^ccc-2705|§2705]]) and opens toward [[contemplation|contemplation]].
