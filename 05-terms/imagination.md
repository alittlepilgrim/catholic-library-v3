## St. Teresa of Ávila

### Glossary

![[tj-way-ics-07-glossary#Imagination]]

![[tj-ic-ics-09-glo#imagination]]

[[tj-ic-ccel-04|IC Book 4.1]]* Teresa makes a distinction between the [[mind]] (which she says is the same as the [[imagination|Imagination]]) and the [[intellect|Intellect]]. Teresa laments how much souls suffer through lack of knowledge, even to the point of abandoning prayer. She urges perseverance.
