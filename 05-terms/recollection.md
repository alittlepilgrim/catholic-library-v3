# Recollection

**Hub.** The interior drawing-in of the soul's powers, away from outward distraction, to attend to God present and indwelling in its innermost depths ([[fo2-spiritual-canticle|Spiritual Canticle]]). Teresa's _prayer of recollection_ gathers the faculties and disposes the soul for contemplation ([[152-prayer-of-recollection|Divine Intimacy 152]]); it is compatible with activity and charity, never their enemy.
