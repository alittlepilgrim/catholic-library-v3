# Abandonment

**Hub.** The free surrender of one's own will to God — trustful self-giving to divine providence and to his Will ([[4.1.2.2.p9f#^ccc-2677|CCC §2677]]), modeled on Christ, who in the garden gave his own desire and fear over to the Father ([[tj-way-ccel-ch-30|Way 30.2]]). Its deepest form is abandonment amid the anguish of the night of the spirit, where only trust remains.
