# Will (Of Person)

**Hub.** The rational appetite — the soul's power of loving and of free choice, good or bad; one of the three faculties (with intellect and memory). In Teresa the will is "the power of loving," which the Holy Spirit enkindles and binds with great love and holds captive in the prayer of quiet. The whole aim of prayer is to bring the will, little by little, into conformity with the Will of God, and at last to surrender it wholly — whereupon the Lord gives it back "its own will and his own besides" ([[tj-way-ccel-ch-32|Way 32.12]]).

## St. Teresa of Ávila

### Glossary

![[tj-way-ics-07-glossary#Will]]

The power to make choices, good or bad. The whole aim of the persons who are beginning prayer should be that they work with determination and with every possible effort to bring their will into conformity with God’s will. What cannot be done all at once can be done little by little. The soul should bend its will if it wishes that prayer be beneficial to it.
